# Harvest 2026-09-23

## Highlights

- Live Nation President/CFO Joe Berchtold publicly defended the company's proposed DOJ antitrust settlement at a Sept. 9 Goldman Sachs conference, even as 17 state AGs plus D.C., SeatGeek, AEG, NIVA and TicketNetwork press the reviewing judge to reject or strengthen it; a separate states' remedies proceeding is running in parallel after an April jury verdict Live Nation is seeking to overturn.
- The New York Rangers' new $75/game subscription pass restricts resale to transfers only, a condition that squarely tests NY Section 25.12's sports-membership-pass exemption — which currently applies only to venues over 30,000 seats (MSG holds ~19,500); a bill to lower that threshold to 13,500 remains stalled in committee with no passage date.
