# Harvest 2026-09-02

## Highlights
- **California AB 1349** cleared the legislature Aug. 31 (Senate 29-5, Assembly concurred) and now sits with Gov. Newsom, who must sign or veto by end of September — but NIVA, which helped write the bill and called itself "proud to help architect" it, launched a Sept. 1 veto campaign days after its own CA advocacy page (updated Aug. 29) was still urging "continued support." Groups that initially opposed the bill (Coalition for Ticket Fairness, National Action Network Sacramento, and others) ended up backing the amended version.
- **Federal GHOST Act** (S.5278, introduced Aug. 6) would ban resale without "actual possession" of an issued ticket — FTC/state-AG enforceable, $15,000/day plus per-ticket penalties — with no "constructive possession" carve-out like AB 1349's, risking a bar on resale of tickets that are paid-for and guaranteed but not yet digitally delivered. NIVA backs GHOST despite fighting to kill AB 1349's narrower version.
