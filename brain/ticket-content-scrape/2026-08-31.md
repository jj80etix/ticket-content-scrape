# Harvest 2026-08-31

## Highlights
- **California AB 1349** heads to its final Senate vote on Aug. 31 (the legislature's last day to pass bills) after a late Aug. 28 rewrite added a speculative-ticket ban alongside a new carve-out protecting rights-holder/venue ticket holdbacks (incl. a broad pro-sports safe harbor); NIVA and 28 allied groups that helped write the bill are now urging a "no" vote, having already lost companion bill AB 1720's 10% resale-cap. If it passes, it returns to the Assembly for concurrence.
- **FTC + Connecticut AG** reached a $4M deceptive-fee settlement with a car dealership requiring all-in pricing prominence, per-charge consent, and 6-year compliance reporting — the alert explicitly ties this enforcement pattern to April's $10M FTC/StubHub junk-fee settlement, reinforcing that ticketing-style fee-transparency enforcement remains a bipartisan priority.
