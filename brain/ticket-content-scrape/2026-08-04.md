# Harvest 2026-08-04

## Highlights
- **Ticket holdback transparency (Q9, Q11):** The newly introduced HOLDBACKS Act (H.R. 9762, Rep. Nellie Pou) would require primary sellers to disclose public-onsale ticket counts at least 7 days before tickets go on sale, and bar concealing or misrepresenting availability to inflate prices — FTC-enforced.
- **BOTS Act enforcement (Q11):** FTC's $300K settlement (of $10.7M, largely suspended) with ticket broker Elite Events (SmartScalpers) details the specific circumvention toolkit — fictitious accounts, virtual cards, IP proxies, multi-session browsers — used to bypass purchase limits across 2,400+ events.
