## Highlights

- **California Fans First Act stalled, lobbying surged first** — the bill (fee disclosure, resale cap at face+10%, fee cap at 10% of face) was narrowed to venues under 3,000-cap/indie only and sent to the Senate Appropriations "suspense file" Aug. 3, a common holding pen for bills to die — but not before StubHub's Q2 CA lobbying spend hit $2.56m (3x its prior high) and a previously-dormant "Ticket Policy Forum" spent ~$1.1m opposing it. Watch for a scoped-down revival; California standards tend to propagate nationally.
