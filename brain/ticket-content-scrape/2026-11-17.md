# Harvest 2026-11-17

## Highlights
- **BOTS Act enforcement fight escalates**: Live Nation/Ticketmaster filed a defense in the FTC's Sept. 2025 BOTS Act suit — the law's first-ever use against a ticketing platform — arguing enforcement is selective and that tracing bot-bought resale tickets back to a primary purchase is "virtually impossible." - **Alberta resale regulation takes shape**: NHL, Ticketmaster, SeatGeek and Live Nation have all registered lobbyists on Bill 34 (passed second reading Nov. 3), which would mandate all-in resale price disclosure and give the province authority to set resale-price/fee rules — no cap proposed yet, pending consultation; Ontario's parallel cap has already produced C$67,500+ in fines against SeatGeek Canada and StubHub.
