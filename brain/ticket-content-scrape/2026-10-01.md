# Harvest 2026-10-01

## Highlights

- **California COMPETE Act (AB 1776), effective Jan. 1, 2027:** Newsom signed a law giving AG Bonta a standalone single-firm monopolization claim under state antitrust law, with his office explicitly citing the Live Nation/Ticketmaster verdict as justification — a new parallel state enforcement track against dominant platforms, enforceable only by the AG/DAs (no private right of action).
- **BOTS Act case against Ticketmaster/Live Nation advances to discovery:** A federal judge rejected their motion to dismiss, ruling the BOTS Act applies to platforms, not just individual resellers; the suit (joined by 7 state AGs) also revives all-in-pricing bait-and-switch allegations.
- **UK ticket-resale price-cap bill:** Creative Industries Minister Ian Murray said the government wants to move its draft resale-cap legislation (face value + ≤10% platform fee) faster than planned, though no publication date was committed.

### Q10 — Live Nation/Ticketmaster antitrust litigation
- [[articles/2026-10-01-california-gives-bonta-new-monopoly-enforcement-tool-after-ticketmaster-verdict|California Gives Bonta New Monopoly Enforcement Tool After Ticketmaster Verdict]]: California added a state-law monopolization claim explicitly motivated by the Live Nation/Ticketmaster antitrust verdict.

### Q11 — Resale/transferability, price caps, BOTS Act enforcement
- [[articles/2026-10-01-uk-minister-promises-faster-resale-crackdown-at-industry-backed-labour-conferenc|UK Minister Promises Faster Resale Crackdown at Industry-Backed Labour Conference Panel]]: UK government signaled a faster timeline for its resale price-cap bill, with no independent resale platform represented on the policy-discussion panel.
- [[articles/2026-10-01-bots-act-applies-to-ticketmaster-judge-rules-allowing-prosecution-to-continue|BOTS Act Applies To Ticketmaster, Judge Rules, Allowing Prosecution To Continue]]: Court confirmed BOTS Act liability extends to platforms, clearing the way for discovery into Ticketmaster's broker-enforcement practices.

### Q12 — Competitors shipping (feature launches, fee-model changes, redesigns)
- [[articles/2026-10-01-alexa-find-me-ticket-buyers|Alexa, Find Me Ticket Buyers]]: Ticketmaster is syndicating inventory into Amazon's Alexa+ voice/agentic-ad surface and Plan A Day's venue kiosks rather than building a competing marketplace — a distribution-channel expansion, not a new product.
- [[articles/2026-10-01-california-festival-claims-99-ticketing-fee-cut-with-self-made-platform|California festival claims 99% ticketing fee cut with self-made platform]]: A self-built, contract-free Shopify ticketing app (ShopTickets) is now being commercialized to other organizers at $1.50/ticket or $49/month.

### Q14 — AI deployment beyond fraud (support, forecasting, pricing, personalization)
- [[articles/2026-10-01-alexa-find-me-ticket-buyers|Alexa, Find Me Ticket Buyers]]: Amazon's "Alexa+ Agentic Ads" (launched June 2026) complete a ticket purchase end-to-end inside a single ad/voice interaction, reaching 600M+ Alexa devices — a concrete benchmark for agentic-AI commerce relevant to Etix Assistant.

### Q16 — Digital-only entry, accessibility, refund/insurance flexibility
- [[articles/2026-10-01-ticketing-system-glitch-hits-freya-skye-fans-at-milwaukee-concert|Ticketing-System Glitch Hits Freya Skye Fans at Milwaukee Concert]]: Barcode failures at the venue door highlight the fragility of walled-garden digital-entry/resale-restriction setups when something breaks.

### Q19 — Vendor whitespace (what no vendor serves well)
- [[articles/2026-10-01-california-festival-claims-99-ticketing-fee-cut-with-self-made-platform|California festival claims 99% ticketing fee cut with self-made platform]]: Organizers built their own ticketing stack rather than accept fees they called "disconnected from the actual cost of selling a ticket" — direct evidence of fee-structure whitespace.
