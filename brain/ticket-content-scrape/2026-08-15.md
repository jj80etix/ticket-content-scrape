# Harvest 2026-08-15

## Highlights
- **D.C.'s RESALE Act cleared final Council passage** — caps concert/theater resale at ~120% of face value (10% markup + up to 10% fees), bans speculative sales, requires all-in pricing, and requires resellers moving 50+ tickets/year to register with a $25,000 bond; takes effect **Jan. 1, 2027**. Sports, movies, and primary-market pricing are excluded. [[articles/2026-08-15-vivid-seats-ceo-pushes-back-on-ticket-resale-price-caps-questions-d-c-approach|Vivid Seats CEO Pushes Back on Ticket Resale Price Caps, Questions D.C. Approach]]
- **Vermont's 10% resale cap (Act 109) is already in effect** (since July 1, 2026); California's comparable AB 1720 was shelved this week after lobbying opposition, while Maine's 2025 package remains the third active state front.
