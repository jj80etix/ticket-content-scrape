# Harvest 2026-09-01

## Highlights
- **Brazil ticketing decree** (signed Aug. 31, full effect by end of Sept. 2026) bans bot-driven mass ticket acquisition and mandates price/resale transparency across primary and secondary markets — a sign that speculative-resale and bot enforcement is becoming a global compliance concern, not just a US/EU one.
- **California AB 1349** passed the legislature in the literal final minutes of the session (Aug. 31) after its own venue-industry sponsors (NIVA + 16 allies) flipped to opposing it; the final text bans speculative resale while carving out explicit safe harbors for rights-holder/pro-sports ticket holdbacks. Now awaits Gov. Newsom's signature or veto by Sept. 30, 2026.
