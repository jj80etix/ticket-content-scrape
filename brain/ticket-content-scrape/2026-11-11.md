# Harvest 2026-11-11

## Highlights

- European Commission formalised its Digital Fairness Act proposal, which would ban "drip pricing" by requiring real-time, all-in price display on ticketing queues and resale, plus EU-wide enforcement powers (fines up to 6% of global turnover); not yet law — next stop is European Parliament and Council. [[articles/2026-11-11-eu-s-digital-fairness-act-proposal-advances-ambitious-ticketing-reform|EU’s Digital Fairness Act proposal advances ‘ambitious’ ticketing reform]]
- Garth Brooks’ 2027 Ireland standing tickets cost 108% more than 2022 despite Ireland’s face-value resale cap, showing resale-price regulation does nothing to constrain primary-market pricing — context: Ticketmaster’s historically 90%+ share of Irish primary ticketing. [[articles/2026-11-11-garth-brooks-ireland-ticket-prices-jump-108-despite-face-value-resale-cap|Garth Brooks Ireland Ticket Prices Jump 108% Despite Face-Value Resale Cap]]
