# Ha*Ash Sets 2027 ‘No Me Hablen de Amor’ Tour Across U.S. and Canada

## Summary

Latin pop duo Ha*Ash announced an 18-city "No Me Hablen de Amor – Gira 2027" tour across the U.S. and Canada, opening April 20 in Toronto, following their 2024-25 HAASHVILLE run that sold more than 66,000 tickets.

### Key Points
- Tour continues through Indianapolis, Chicago-area Rosemont, Denver, Seattle, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Houston and New York before closing May 22 in Miami Beach.
- Citi cardmember presale begins Sept. 22; artist presale Sept. 23; Live Nation and select local presales follow.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/HaAsh.webp)

Latin pop duo Ha\*Ash will return to North America in 2027 with an 18-city tour across the U.S. and Canada.

The sisters Hanna and Ashley Pérez Mosa will launch the No Me Hablen de Amor – Gira 2027 on April 20 at Meridian Hall in Toronto. The run will continue through Indianapolis, Chicago-area Rosemont, Denver, Seattle, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Houston, New York and other cities before wrapping May 22 at the Fillmore Miami Beach.

The tour follows Ha\*Ash’s 2024-25 HAASHVILLE Tour, which sold more than 66,000 tickets and included 16 sold-out performances in the U.S. and Canada.

“After everything we experienced with HAASHVILLE, we couldn’t wait to be back with our fans,” Ha\*Ash said in announcing the new run.
