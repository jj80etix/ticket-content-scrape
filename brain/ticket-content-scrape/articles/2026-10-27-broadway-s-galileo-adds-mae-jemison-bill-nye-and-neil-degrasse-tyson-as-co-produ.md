# Broadway’s ‘Galileo’ Adds Mae Jemison, Bill Nye and Neil deGrasse Tyson as Co-Producers

## Summary

Former NASA astronaut Mae Jemison, Bill Nye and Neil deGrasse Tyson are joining as co-producers of the Broadway musical "Galileo," which begins previews Nov. 10, 2026 at the Shubert Theatre and opens Dec. 6.

### Key Points
- Jemison, Nye and Tyson join lead producers Amanda Lipitz, Henry Tisch and Jordan Roth.
- Raúl Esparza stars as Galileo Galilei.
- Previews begin Nov. 10, 2026; opening night Dec. 6.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Galileo.jpg)

Mae Jemison, Bill Nye and Neil deGrasse Tyson will co-produce the Broadway musical “Galileo,” which begins previews November 10, 2026, at the Shubert Theatre. Opening night is scheduled for December 6.

The three join lead producers Amanda Lipitz, Henry Tisch and Jordan Roth. Raúl Esparza will star as Galileo Galilei, the astronomer whose scientific discoveries brought him into conflict with established authorities.

Jemison is a former NASA astronaut and the first woman of color to travel to space. Nye is a scientist, educator and television host. Tyson is an astrophysicist, author and director of the Hayden Planetarium.

In a joint statement, Lipitz, Tisch and Roth said the new co-producers share a commitment to making science accessible to the public.

“Galileo’s story reminds us that scientific discovery begins with the courage to question what we think we already know,” the producers said. “Each has devoted their career to making science more accessible, sparking curiosity, and inspiring millions of people to look at the world with a deeper sense of wonder.”

Jemison said the production connects her longstanding interests in science and the arts. “For me, science provides an understanding of a universal experience, and the arts provide a universal understanding of a personal experience,” Jemison said. “And that is why I could not be more excited to join this team.”

She said the musical presents Galileo’s scientific work and history while exploring his personal story.

Nye said the show examines the astronomer’s contributions to understanding space and motion. “I am excited indeed to join the Galileo musical as a co-producer,” Nye said. “We’re bringing the passion, beauty, and joy of science to the Broadway stage.”

Tyson said Galileo’s experiences remain relevant to discussions about science and authority.

“Galileo remains a seminal figure, not only in the history of science but in the history of civilization,” Tyson said. “A stark reminder that what is discovered to be objectively true in the universe is not always welcomed by cultural, political, or religious dogma.”

“Galileo” has a book by Danny Strong and an original score by Zoe Sarnak and Michael Weiner. Tony Award winner Michael Mayer directs, and Tony nominee David Neumann choreographs.

Esparza stars alongside Jeremy Kushnier and Joy Woods. Esparza and Kushnier previously appeared in the musical’s world premiere at Berkeley Repertory Theatre. Michael Park will alternate with Esparza at certain performances.

The company also includes Bradley Dean, Jordon Lee Garcia, Javier Muñoz, Gabrielle Elisabeth, Adam Halpin, Nicole Kyoung-Mi Lambert, Rico LeBron and Terica Marie.

Brian Usifer is music supervisor and orchestrator. The creative team includes scenic designer Rachel Hauck, lighting designer Kevin Adams, sound designer John Shivers, costume designer Susan Hilferty and video and projection designer Ethan Tobman.

Casting is by Jim Carnahan and Jason Thinger. Aurora Productions serves as production manager, and ShowTown Theatricals is general manager.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [galileomusical.com](http://galileomusical.com/).
