# Bad Bunny Concludes Record-Breaking Tour With Sold-Out Puerto Rico Shows

## Summary

Bad Bunny closed his record-breaking "DeBÍ TiRAR MÁS FOToS World Tour" with a sold-out two-night stand at San Juan's Estadio Hiram Bithorn, capping 55 shows across five continents that grossed more than $438 million from 3.1 million tickets sold without a single U.S. date.

### Key Points
- Both Puerto Rico shows featured guest appearances from artists including Arcángel, Farruko, Ñengo Flow and Rauw Alejandro.
- The tour's $438.6M gross off 3.1M tickets makes it the top-grossing tour of the year so far.
- Included a 10-show, $85.5M run at Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid.

## Transcript

![bad bunny](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bad-bunny.jpg)

Bad Bunny’s “DeBÍ TiRAR MÁS FOToS World Tour” is one of the most successful treks of the year, grossing more than $438 million. (Photo by Cheery Viruet)

Bad Bunny’s global journey came to an end where it all began: Puerto Rico. The megastar wrapped his record-breaking “DeBÍ TiRAR MÁS FOToS World Tour” with a sold-out two-night stand at Estadio Hiram Bithorn in San Juan, performing in front of 70,000 fans across both shows Aug. 22 and 23.

After performing 55 shows in five continents and 22 cities, celebrating Puerto Rican culture with more than 3.1 million fans worldwide, Bad Bunny returned home to celebrate the milestone with his compatriots. During the shows, which were produced by Noah Assad Presents and Move Concerts, the artist reflected on his heritage and expressed his pride and gratitude for what the island has taught him.

Both nights featured special guests who have collaborated with Bad Bunny. The first show featured performances from DEI V, Arcángel, Farruko and Ñengo Flow, and the second night was just as special with DEI V, Victor Manuelle, RaiNao, Jowell & Randy and Rauw Alejandro.

Bad Bunny changed the setlist for the home crowd, performing “Ser Bichote” from his 2018 debut album *X 100PRE* and “Vuelve Candy B” from 2023’s *nadie sabe lo que va a pasar mañana*.

The final two nights of the tour have yet to be reported to Pollstar Boxoffice, but Bad Bunny’s stadium tour is the most successful by any artist so far this year, grossing a whopping $438,634,180 off 3,109,459 tickets sold.

Bad Bunny closes one of the most remarkable chapters of his career, which included a memorable Super Bowl Halftime Show, but what’s most impressive is that he accomplished the touring feats without a single U.S. stop, showing Latin music’s global influence and the potential of Latin American markets as touring destinations. He averaged nearly $8 million per outing and recently played 10 sold-out shows at Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid, Spain, where he grossed $85,547,133 off 639,656 tickets.

Bad Bunny is managed by Noah Assad of Rimas Entertainment and booked by United Talent Agency’s Jbeau Lewis.
