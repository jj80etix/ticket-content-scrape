# Pollstar Live! relocates to Coachella Valley for 2027

## Summary

Pollstar announced that its Pollstar Live! conference, including the Pollstar Awards, will relocate to Indian Wells in California's Coachella Valley for 2027, running April 13-15 between the two Coachella Valley Arts & Music Festival weekends. Organizers say the new location creates a fresh destination for the industry gathering of promoters, agents, managers and venue executives.

### Key Points
- Pollstar Live! 2027: April 13-15, Indian Wells, CA (Coachella Valley).
- Scheduled between Coachella's two festival weekends (Apr 9-11 and Apr 16-18).
- Includes the annual Pollstar Awards.
- Location change from prior venues intended to create a new destination draw.
