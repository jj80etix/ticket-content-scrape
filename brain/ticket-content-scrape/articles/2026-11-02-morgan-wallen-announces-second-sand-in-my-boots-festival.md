# Morgan Wallen Announces Second Sand in My Boots Festival

## Summary

Morgan Wallen announced his "Sand in My Boots" festival will return to Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, 2027, for a second edition after a sold-out debut. Artist lineup and ticketing details were not yet released at the time of this announcement.

### Key Points
- First edition featured Wallen, Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn, HARDY, The War on Drugs, and Three 6 Mafia.
- Wallen says he wants to "elevate the experience" with new stage concepts for the second year.
- Produced in partnership with AEG Presents.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Sand-In-My-Boots-2027.webp)

Morgan Wallen’s Sand in My Boots Festival will return to Gulf Shores, Alabama, from May 21 through May 23, 2027, marking the second edition of the three-day event.

In a statement, Wallen shared, “One thing I’m looking forward to the most about bringing Sand in My Boots Festival back is being able to have new concepts on stage and trying to elevate the experience overall for the fans, myself and other artists on the bill,” Wallen said in a statement.

“I think it’s going to be really top-notch and we’re going to try to do it even better this time.”

The first Sand in My Boots Festival brought Wallen together with a roster that included Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn, HARDY and Ella Langley. The War on Drugs and Three 6 Mafia also performed, giving the debut a lineup that extended beyond country music.

Artists for the 2027 edition have not yet been announced, and ticketing information will be released at a later date. For additional details and to stay up-to-date on the latest information, festival goers can visit the official [Sand In My Boots website](https://sandinmybootsfest.com/).
