# IQ 146 out now: Inside Bad Bunny’s DTMF tour, New Bosses revealed, K-pop’s global growth

## Summary

IQ Magazine's August issue (IQ 146) is now available, featuring coverage of Bad Bunny's record-breaking Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour, the 19th annual "New Bosses" list of 20 rising under-30 talents in international live music, and a look at K-pop's global export strategy. The issue also profiles agent Tom Schroeder's 25th anniversary at The Team and includes market reports on Belgium and Hong Kong.

### Key Points
- Cover feature: Bad Bunny's Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour, reported by James Hanley.
- "New Bosses 2026" spotlights 20 rising talents under 30 in international live music.
- K-pop feature by Lisa Henderson covers direct-to-fan platforms, HYBE's "The City" campaigns, and merchandise value.
- Market reports cover Belgium and Hong Kong; columns from Reeperbahn Festival's Detlef Schwarte, Yvan Boudillet, and Supajam's Gill Tee.
