# Matchbox Twenty Announces 52-Date 30th Anniversary Tour for 2027

## Summary

Matchbox Twenty announced a 52-date North American arena and amphitheater tour marking the band's 30th anniversary, running May 15 to August 12, 2027. Opening acts rotate across the run, including The All-American Rejects, The Wallflowers, David Gray, O.A.R., and Matt Nathanson.

### Key Points
- 52-date "30th Anniversary Tour" runs May 15-Aug. 12, 2027 across the US and Canada.
- Kicks off in Birmingham, AL; closes in Grand Rapids, MI.
- Stops include Red Rocks, Hollywood Bowl, Bridgestone Arena, and RBC Amphitheatre.
- Rotating support acts: The All-American Rejects, The Wallflowers, David Gray, O.A.R., Matt Nathanson.
- Marks 30 years since debut album "Yourself or Someone Like You" (1996).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Matchbox-Twenty-1.webp)

Matchbox Twenty will celebrate three decades of music with an extensive North American tour in 2027, announcing a 52-date run that will bring the band to arenas and amphitheaters across the United States and Canada.

The 30th Anniversary Tour will kick off May 15 at Coca-Cola Amphitheater in Birmingham, Alabama, and conclude August 12 at Acrisure Amphitheater in Grand Rapids, Michigan. The itinerary includes performances at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, Bridgestone Arena in Nashville and RBC Amphitheatre in Toronto.

The tour features several stretches with different opening acts. The All-American Rejects will support the opening four dates, followed by an extended run featuring The Wallflowers through early July. David Gray joins for the July performances, with O.A.R. appearing in early August and Matt Nathanson supporting the final two concerts.

The Lucky will also appear on select West Coast dates alongside The Wallflowers.

The anniversary celebration recognizes 30 years since the release of the band’s debut album, *Yourself or Someone Like You*, which arrived in 1996 and helped establish Matchbox Twenty as one of the most successful rock acts of the era. The album produced a string of hits, including “Push,” “3AM,” “Real World,” “Back 2 Good” and “Long Day.” It went on to achieve diamond certification and has been certified 12-times platinum.

Ahead of the headlining tour, Matchbox Twenty will perform April 25 at the [2027 Stagecoach Festival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/garth-brooks-riley-green-and-zach-top-to-headline-stagecoach-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in Indio, California.
