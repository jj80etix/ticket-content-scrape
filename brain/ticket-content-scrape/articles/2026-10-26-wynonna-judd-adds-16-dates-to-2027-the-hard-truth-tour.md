# Wynonna Judd Adds 16 Dates to 2027 ‘The Hard Truth Tour’

## Summary

Wynonna Judd has added 16 dates to her 2027 "The Hard Truth Tour," extending the run through March across the South, Midwest, and Mid-Atlantic. The expansion follows an earlier 15-date fall 2026 leg and two previously announced Ryman Auditorium shows.

### Key Points
- New dates run Feb. 13-March 21, 2027, across cities including San Antonio, Austin, Kansas City, Indianapolis, and Detroit.
- Tour supports her new album *The Hard Truth*, released Oct. 2 via ANTI-Records.
- Separate March 26-27 Ryman Auditorium shows (announced earlier) are not part of this 16-date expansion.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Wynonna-Judd-The-Hard-Truth-Tour.webp)

Wynonna Judd has added 16 concerts to her 2027 *The Hard Truth Tour*, extending the country singer’s headlining schedule through March with dates across the South, Midwest and Mid-Atlantic.

Announced Monday, Oct. 26, the new run begins Feb. 13 at the Tobin Center for the Performing Arts in San Antonio, followed by a Feb. 16 performance at ACL Live at The Moody Theater in Austin. The February leg also visits Kansas City, Springfield, Missouri, and Hot Springs, Arkansas.

In March, Judd will perform at theaters and casino venues in Indianapolis, Grand Rapids, Detroit, Rockford, Illinois, and Morristown, New Jersey, before heading through West Virginia, Pennsylvania, North Carolina, Georgia and Alabama. The newly added dates conclude March 21 in Dothan, Alabama.

Those shows follow the [15-date fall tour TicketNews covered in August](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/wynonna-judd-sets-fall-2026-u-s-the-hard-truth-tour-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), launched Oct. 8 in New York and continuing into November. Judd’s remaining 2026 itinerary includes stops in Alabama, South Carolina, Florida, North Carolina, Virginia, Pennsylvania, Ohio and Indiana.

Judd released *The Hard Truth* on Oct. 2 through ANTI-Records. The 10-song album marks the first time she has co-written an album, exploring themes of grief, resilience and personal renewal. The tour is expected to mix the new songs with music from her decades-long career, including material familiar to fans of The Judds and her solo work.

Two additional March performances at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium appear on the 2027 schedule, but they are not part of Monday’s 16-date expansion. The March 26-27 shows were [announced Oct. 21](https://musicrow.com/2026/10/wynonna-judd-slates-first-ever-headlining-ryman-shows/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) as Judd’s first headlining concerts at the landmark venue. Judd has said the venue holds special meaning because it hosted a celebration honoring her late mother and musical partner, Naomi Judd.

The Ryman performances are [already on sale](https://www.ryman.com/event/2027-wynonna-judd?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), having opened to the public Oct. 23. For the 16 newly announced dates, Wynation Fan Club presales begin Wednesday, Oct. 28, with general ticket sales beginning Friday, Oct. 30. The announcement did not specify onsale times. Fans can consult [Judd’s official website](https://www.wynonna.com/pages/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and local venues for individual show details.
