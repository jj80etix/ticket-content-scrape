# LL Cool J Announces 11-Show Las Vegas Residency for 2027

## Summary

LL Cool J will headline "Rock The Bells: The Residency," an 11-show Las Vegas engagement at Dolby Live at Park MGM in 2027, split into a five-show May run and a six-show July run.

### Key Points
- May dates: 15, 16, 19, 21, 22; July dates: 9, 10, 11, 14, 16, 17
- Show will blend hits performances with DJs, storytelling, and special guest appearances (unannounced)

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/LL-Cool-J-Rock-the-Bells-Residdency.webp)

LL Cool J will bring Rock The Bells: The Residency to Las Vegas in 2027, scheduling 11 performances at Dolby Live at Park MGM. The engagement will take place in two runs, with five shows in May and six in July.

The opening performance is scheduled for May 15, followed by shows on May 16, 19, 21 and 22. LL Cool J will return to Dolby Live on July 9 for the second stretch. That run continues on July 10, 11, 14, 16 and 17.

The residency will combine performances of the rapper’s hits with DJs, storytelling and appearances by special guests, which have not yet been identified.

“Hip-Hop started in a few neighborhoods in New York, and now it belongs to the whole world. Along the way it changed rock, R&B, pop, soul, dance music, all of it,” LL Cool J said in a statement.
