# DIIV Releases New Album, Announces World Tour

## Summary

DIIV released new album ZIRP! and announced a 47-date world tour spanning North America, Europe, the UK, Australia, and Japan. The album, produced by A.G. Cook, has drawn strong early reviews from Stereogum ("Album of the Week") and NME.

### Key Points
- ZIRP! out now; includes singles "Dare Me" (BBC Radio 1 "Hottest Record"), "Trillions," "Kid," "The Fountain"
- Tour includes festival dates in Australia and Japan plus headline shows across North America, Europe, UK
- Album title/themes reference zero-interest-rate-policy-era startup culture

## Transcript

![unnamed (32)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/unnamed-32-1-2.jpg)

DIIV by Louie Kovatch

DIIV is gearing up for a 47-date world tour in support of the band’s new album, *ZIRP!*, out today. The experimental alternative band has lined up festival appearances in Australia and Japan, along with headline shows across North America, Europe and the UK.  
  
*ZIRP!* includes “Dare Me,” which recently premiered as BBC Radio 1’s “Hottest Record,” as well as the singles “Trillions,” “Kid,” and “The Fountain.” The album – which the band’s PR team notes is “named for the zero interest rate policies that drew us into a world of failed startups and turned us all into atomised ‘users’” – was produced by A. G. Cook (Caroline Polachek, Charli XCX).  
  
[*Stereogum* named *ZIRP!* their “Album of the Week”](https://stereogum.com/2515261/album-of-the-week-diiv-zirp/reviews/album-of-the-week) with a review declaring: “ *ZIRP!* really does feel like a quantum leap. It’s the most stylish, dynamic, endlessly replayable album of DIIV’s career, animated by emotional currents that feel destined to strike a nerve. Buy up as many shares in this band as you can.”  
  
[*NME* gave the album four stars in its review:](https://www.nme.com/reviews/album/diiv-zirp-review-3974219) “pop meets politics on their most adventurous record yet.”

DIIV’s PR team adds that the band’s new sound can be described as “The New Alternative: a new genre for the 21st century. More specifically: a new genre for music’s optimized VC-funded super deluxe remixed AI-enhanced algorithmically perfected wild west 2020s, where a million streams is great, a billion even better, and a trillion well within reach. On their fifth album ZIRP!, DIIV emphatically and ecstatically embrace the underground pedigree and the pop instinct that have anchored the band like magnetic poles for the better part of their career—smashing them together in pursuit of a sound that pre-empts any notion that Zachary Cole Smith, Andrew Bailey, Colin Caulfield and Ben Newman have ever been playing between worlds.”

Following a handful of previously announced European/UK festival performances in November, DIIV’s 2027 tour launches in February with stops at four editions of Laneway Festival in Australia and New Zealand and two nights in Tokyo. The North American leg starts with an April 7 show in Minneapolis, at Varsity Theater, with dates booked through a May 21 show at San Diego’s The Observatory at North Park. DIIV heads back to Europe for a month of gigs scheduled May 31 through June 30.  
  
DIIV is booked by Toutpartout Agency’s Steven Thomassen in Europe and THE·TEAM’s Fred Zahedinia and Andrew Morgan in Asia, North America and South America.

Tickets went on sale today. Check out the routing below and the video for “Dare Me.”
