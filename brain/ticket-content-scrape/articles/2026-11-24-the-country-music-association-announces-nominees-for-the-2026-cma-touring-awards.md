# The Country Music Association Announces Nominees for the 2026 CMA Touring Awards

## Summary

The Country Music Association announced nominees for the 2026 CMA Touring Awards, honoring touring professionals behind the genre's biggest tours, with veteran production expert Chuck Hull receiving the Lifetime Achievement Award. The ceremony, hosted by Parker McCollum, takes place Jan. 26 at Nashville's The Pinnacle, with final voting open Dec. 1–16.

### Key Points
- Chuck Hull (50+ years touring with Fleetwood Mac, Paul McCartney, Keith Urban) receives the Lifetime Achievement Award.
- Crew of the Year nominees include tours for Morgan Wallen, Luke Combs, Chris Stapleton and Cody Johnson.
- STRAUSS returns as presenting sponsor/Workwear Partner for a second year.
- Ceremony is Jan. 26, 2027 at The Pinnacle, Nashville; voting runs Dec. 1–16.

## Transcript

Chuck Hull named 2026 CMA Touring Awards Lifetime Achievement Award recipient. Photo courtesy Chuck Hull

The Country Music Association revealed the nominees for the 2026 CMA Touring Awards, which recognize the professionals behind country music’s biggest tours. Final round voting opens Dec. 1 and runs through Dec. 16 at 6:00 PM/CT. Parker McCollum will host the 21st CMA Touring Awards celebration on Jan. 26 at The Pinnacle in Nashville.

Chuck Hull, veteran live music production expert, audio engineer and retired tour manager, will be honored with the 2026 CMA Touring Lifetime Achievement Award. With more than 50 years of experience, Hull played an instrumental role in shaping the live music industry, overseeing global tours and production logistics for some of music’s biggest acts, including Fleetwood Mac, Paul McCartney and Keith Urban, among others. His career began as an audio engineer with Clair Brothers before expanding into television stage management and international touring across rock, pop and country. During his notable 20-year tenure with Urban, Hull helped build a sophisticated, world-class touring operation and advance a more professional model for country touring teams, contributing to the evolution of the industry as the genre expanded into international markets.

Since retiring from the road in 2024, Hull has continued to champion the touring community through his consultancy and as a board member of ECCHO Live, while mentoring the next generation of touring professionals and advocating for the health, sustainability and well-being of touring professionals worldwide.

“The CMA Touring Awards shine a well-deserved spotlight on the incredible professionals whose expertise, dedication and hard work brings country music to fans through unforgettable live experiences,” stated Brittany Schaffer, CMA incoming Chief Executive Officer in the announcement. “Touring is such an important part of how this music connects with people, and that wouldn’t be possible without the individuals who work tirelessly behind the scenes. We’re grateful for all they bring to this industry and proud to recognize this year’s nominees.”

STRAUSS returns for the second consecutive year as presenting sponsor of the 2026 CMA Touring Awards and official Workwear Partner.

“Every great show starts with the people who build the stage, rig the lights and get every detail right” added Henning Strauss, CEO and co-owner of STRAUSS. “We’re proud to return to the CMA Touring Awards and, together with CMA, put the professionals behind that work on centerstage, where they deserve to be.”
