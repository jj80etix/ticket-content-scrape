# The Maine to Celebrate 20th Anniversary at 8123 Fest

## Summary

Alt-rock band The Maine will headline their own 8123 Festival January 15–16, 2027 in Arizona to mark the band's 20th anniversary, with support from The Band Camino, Andrew McMahon, The Aces and others; tickets are on sale now.

### Key Points
- Festival dates: Jan. 15–16, 2027, celebrating The Maine's 20th anniversary; a pop-up shop and charity day follow on Jan. 18.
- Lineup includes The Band Camino, Andrew McMahon and the Strings Attached Quartet, The Academy Is…, Beach Weather, The Aces, The Summer Set and Broadside.
- Tickets (GA bundles, two-day, single-day passes) available now via the festival's official website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0792.jpeg)

The Maine is returning to Arizona in January 2027 to celebrate their 20-year anniversary.

The alt-rock group, fronted by John O’Callaghan, will take the stage at their 8123 Festival on January 15 and 16. Alongside The Maine, other acts are set to perform across the weekend including The Band Camino, Andrew McMahon and the Strings Attached Quartet, The Academy Is…, Beach Weather, The Aces, and The Summer Set, Broadside, Nick Santino, and more.

Side shows will be announced at a late date, while a pop-up shop and charity day will take place following the festival on January 18.

O’Callaghan described the band’s anniversary in a statement, noting, “Standing at the edge of 20 years as a band and that free-fall feeling of weightlessness is beginning to well up as the roller coaster climbs.”
