# Primavera Sound ‘leaves no stone unturned’ for 25th edition

## Summary

Primavera Sound unveiled its 25th-anniversary 2027 lineup (Barcelona, June 2-6), headlined by Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers, Fontaines D.C. and Massive Attack among more than 150 acts, alongside Latin American and Portuguese sister editions.

### Key Points
- Main Barcelona dates run June 3-5, 2027, plus a free opening party and a closing electronic "Primavera Bits" night.
- General tickets priced at €295 (presale) or €350 (general), VIP €545, plus booking fees.
- Buenos Aires (Nov. 28-29) and São Paulo (Dec. 5-6) Latin American editions also confirmed.
- Discounted presale opens Oct. 8; general onsale Oct. 9.

## Primavera Sound ‘leaves no stone unturned’ for 25th edition

The 'unbelievable' pool of performers set for the Spanish festival includes Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers, Fontaines D.C., and Massive Attack

![Doechii, All Things Go 2025, NYC](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Dave-©-Gabriel-Moses.png)

Doechii © Gabriel Moses

Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers, and Fontaines D.C. are among the names set to perform what organisers call “a universe that cannot be replicated” for the 25th edition of Spain’s Primavera Sound, set for 2-6 June 2027.

The headliner pool also includes Massive Attack, Turnstile, Hayley Williams, FKA twigs, The Chemical Brothers, Robyn, Bad Gyal, Lola Young, Pavement, Skrillex b2b Ninajirachi, ADÉLA, Prospa, and Caroline Polachek.

With over 150 artists lined up, the Spanish institution will return to Parc del Fòrum for its main days, 3-5 June, with a free opening party on the 2nd and a closing electronic party, Primavera Bits, on the 6th. In addition, spaces around Barcelona will host a complementary programme for a week full of music.

With this edition, organisers say the lineup “celebrates a legacy that has been painstakingly crafted over a quarter of a century”, highlighting the “countless extraordinary – and sometimes almost unbelievable – musical moments Barcelona has experienced over the course of the festival”.

Beyond the bill toppers, the lineup also includes King Gizzard & the Lizard Wizard, Bleachers, EsDeeKid, Kim Petras, Tinashe, Shygirl, BICEP, Weyes Blood, Julien Baker, Dove Ellis, and Angine de Poitrine.

To close the event, three electronic pioneers – Richie Hawtin, Four Tet, and Dixon – will headline Primavera Bits, coming together for an eight-hour journey of alternating sets.

“The fact that everyone sees Primavera Sound as the space where these norms and conditions can be reimagined is the result of 24 editions spent diligently leaving no stone unturned,” organisers say.

> “There is still a lot to be revealed about what next year’s festival will bring”

In addition to the festival, Primavera Pro will return for its 18th edition from 2-5 June, moving to DHub with a programme to be announced.

Fans can register for a discounted presale, commencing Thursday (8 October), with general tickets priced at €295 or VIP at €545 (plus booking fees). General ticket sales open the following day, 9 October, with tickets priced at €350 or VIP at €545, plus fees. Ticket prices remain the same as the 2026 edition.

Barcelona metropolitan residents are eligible for a 10% discount on their tickets, while Revolut customers can score a €20 discount on tickets.

“The Primavera lexicon also means knowing that, although this line-up is already an inexhaustible source of musical inspiration, there is still a lot to be revealed about what next year’s festival will bring,” organisers say.

This year will also see the return of the festival’s Latin American editions, one in Buenos Aires (28-29 November) and another in São Paulo (5-6 December), led by names including Gorillaz, The Strokes, FKA twigs, Lily Allen, and Yung Lean.

The festival’s Portuguese edition, Primavera Sound Porto, is expected to return 10-13 June 2027, with details to be announced later.
