# Triangle sets sights on ‘getting Asia’s mid-tier firing again’

## Summary

Singapore-based touring platform Triangle Group, fresh off a $15M funding round and the addition of former Live Nation executive Roger Field, says it aims to reignite Asia's mid-tier touring segment, arguing regional promoters are "starving for content" underserved by multinational operators' one-size-fits-all approach.

### Key Points
- $15M (€13.1M) funding round just closed.
- Former Live Nation exec Roger Field added to the team.
- Company targets the mid-sized/arena segment in East Asia specifically.
- Cites reduced promoter risk appetite and multinational homogenization as the gap it's targeting.
