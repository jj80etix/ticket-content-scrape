# Kustom Entertainment closes $6.1m divestiture of legacy video solutions

## Summary

Kustom Entertainment closed a $6.1m divestiture of its legacy video-solutions business to Cycurion on August 3, exiting a non-core segment to become a "pure-play live entertainment engine" focused entirely on festival production and proprietary ticketing technology. The deal structure (cash, a 36-month secured promissory note at 7% interest, and preferred stock) hands Kustom recurring debt-service cash flow while freeing capital for further scaling — organically and via acquisition — of its festival footprint and ticketing roadmap.

### Key Points
- Divestiture to Cycurion closed August 3 for $6.1m ($1.25m upfront cash incl. a $250k non-refundable payment; $4.25m secured promissory note at 7%/36 months; $600k Cycurion series H preferred stock).
- CEO Stanton E. Ross frames the deal as completing Kustom's transition to a "pure-play live entertainment engine," eliminating legacy operational overhead.
- Kustom says it's now positioned for both organic growth and "targeted strategic acquisitions" across the ~$100bn live-event industry, with a focus on ticketing software and venue partnerships.

### Highlights
- $6.1m divestiture closed Aug. 3, 2026, to Cycurion; consideration split across upfront cash, a yield-bearing note, and preferred stock rather than a clean cash sale.
- Kustom explicitly cites capacity for "targeted strategic acquisitions" going forward — a consolidation signal in the ticketing/festival-production space.

### Industry Problem
Smaller/mid-tier ticketing and live-entertainment companies are actively reshaping their portfolios — shedding non-core lines to fund consolidation — signaling continued M&A activity and roll-up pressure in the ticketing/festival-production space that could produce new competitors or acquisition targets.

### Proposed Solution
No product response needed; this is a #monitor signal. Track whether Kustom's freed-up capital translates into ticketing-platform acquisitions or festival-portfolio expansion that could create new competitive pressure or partnership/consolidation opportunities in adjacent markets.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Kustom Entertainment has successfully closed its previously announced divestiture of its legacy video solutions business assets, to focus on live music festival production and proprietary ticketing operations.**

The divestiture to Cycurion was completed on August 3, with the deal worth $6.1m (£4.5m/€5.3m).

Kustom will now focus 100% of its corporate resources and capital on expanding its festival footprint, and advancing its proprietary ticketing technology platforms.

The transaction includes $1.25m upfront cash consideration, which also features a $250,000 non-refundable cash payment; a $4.25m secured promissory note (a 36-month note bearing 7% annual interest, delivering recurring debt service cash flow to Kustom); and $600,000 high-yield series H preferred stock issued by Cycurion in lieu of previously structured warrants.

“Closing this divestiture marks a historic milestone for Kustom Entertainment,” said Stanton E. Ross, chief executive of Kustom Entertainment, which is headquartered in Overland Park, a city in Kansas in the US.

“By finalising this upgraded $6.1m agreement with Cycurion, we have strengthened our balance sheet, eliminated legacy operational overhead, and secured non-dilutive, yield-bearing capital for our shareholders.

“More importantly, this transaction marks the official completion of our transition into a pure-play live entertainment engine.

“With our legacy video division successfully transferred, we now have the ideal platform to rapidly expand our business – both organically and through targeted strategic acquisitions. Our team is laser-focused on scaling our live event experiences, deepening venue partnerships, and executing on our high-margin ticketing software roadmap across the $100bn live event industry.”
