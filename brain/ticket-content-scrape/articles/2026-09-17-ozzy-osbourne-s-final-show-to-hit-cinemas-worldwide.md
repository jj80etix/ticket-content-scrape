# Ozzy Osbourne’s final show to hit cinemas worldwide

## Summary

Ozzy Osbourne's final concert performance — captured with the original Black Sabbath lineup at Birmingham's sold-out "Back to the Beginning" stadium show, just over two weeks before his death — will be released in cinemas worldwide beginning Oct. 28, produced by Mercury Studios and distributed by Trafalgar Releasing.

### Key Points
- Worldwide cinema release begins Oct. 28.
- Captures Osbourne's final performance with the original Black Sabbath lineup, filmed July 5, 2025 at Villa Park, Birmingham.
- Recorded roughly two weeks before Osbourne's passing (Aug. 25).
- Produced by Mercury Studios, distributed by Trafalgar Releasing; includes behind-the-scenes material.
