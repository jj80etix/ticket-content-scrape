Superstar touring comic John Mulaney has announced the nine-venue, nine-date “Mulaney Takes Manhattan” takeover in New York, as part of his “Mister Whatever” world tour, with dates Jan. 7-15.

The dates kick off Jan. 7 at the Comedy Cellar, from which he will play the Blue Note, Joe’s Pub, Bowery Ballroom Music Box Theatre on Broadway, Beacon Theatre, Carnegie Hall, Radio City Music Hall and the finale at Madison Square Garden Jan. 15.

The announcement follows a hometown Chicago performance at Wrigley Field, where he became the first comedian to headline. The sold-out show included surprise appearances from David Letterman, Fred Armisen and Richard Kind.

Presale information and details on how to secure access to the intimate performances are available at Mulaney’s website, according to promoter Live Nation.

Wed, Jan. 13 – New York, NY – Carnegie Hall  
Thu, Jan. 14 – New York, NY – Radio City Music Hall
