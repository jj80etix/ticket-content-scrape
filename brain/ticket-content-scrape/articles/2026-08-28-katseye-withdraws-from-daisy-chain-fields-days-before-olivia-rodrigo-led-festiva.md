# KATSEYE Withdraws From Daisy Chain Fields Days Before Olivia Rodrigo-Led Festival

## Summary

KATSEYE canceled its appearance at Olivia Rodrigo's inaugural "Daisy Chain Fields" festival (Irvine, CA, Aug. 29) after member Megan sprained her ankle in rehearsal. Devon Again was added to the all-women lineup in KATSEYE's place.

### Key Points
- Announced two days before the festival via Instagram; Megan advised by doctors to rest ahead of KATSEYE's own Sept. 1 tour start.
- Festival headlined by Rodrigo, with Chappell Roan, Doechii, The Breeders and Garbage also on the bill; proceeds support women's/reproductive-health organizations.
- Same story also covered independently by Pollstar (see companion note).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Olivia-Rodrigo-Daisy-Chain-Fields.webp)

KATSEYE has canceled its scheduled appearance at Olivia Rodrigo’s inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival after member Megan suffered an ankle injury during rehearsals.

The girl group announced the cancellation two days before the festival, which is set to take place Saturday in Irvine, California. According to KATSEYE, Megan sprained her ankle while rehearsing for the group’s upcoming tour and was advised by doctors to rest.

“We’re so incredibly sorry to share that we are unable to perform at Daisy Chain Fields this Saturday,” the group wrote in a statement shared through its Instagram Story.

“Megan sprained her ankle yesterday during tour rehearsal and after being evaluated by doctors, she has been advised to stay off it and allow it to properly heal so we can be ready for our upcoming tour,” the statement continued. “We know how much our fans were looking forward to seeing us, and it breaks our hearts to disappoint anyone who made plans to be there.”

Alt-pop singer-songwriter Devon Again has been added to the festival lineup in KATSEYE’s place.

Daisy Chain Fields is a new festival organized and curated by Rodrigo, who will serve as the event’s headliner. The all-women lineup also includes Chappell Roan, Doechii, Rachel Chinouriri, The Breeders and Garbage. Stevie Nicks, Karen O and Sarah McLachlan are also scheduled to appear as special guests.

KATSEYE said the festival’s charitable focus made the decision to withdraw particularly disappointing for the group.

“We were especially excited for Daisy Chain Fields because it’s not just about music, but the greater mission of joy and community,” the statement said. “We’re sending all our love to Olivia and to all of the incredible artists, non-profit organizations and volunteers who have given their time to make this day so special.”

The group added that it is looking ahead to its upcoming tour while Megan recovers.

“It’s going to be such an amazing day,” KATSEYE concluded, “and we can’t wait to see everyone out on tour.”

Festival organizers confirmed the lineup change on Instagram and offered their support to Megan. “Megan is recovering from a sprained ankle, and the group will no longer be able to perform this weekend,” organizers wrote. “We are wishing Megan a smooth and speedy recovery!”
