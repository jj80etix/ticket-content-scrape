# Second Colombia earthquake relief concert set for Vive Claro

## Summary

A second Colombia earthquake relief concert, Voces por la Vida, has added Karol G, Maluma and Grupo Niche to its Aug. 23 Bogotá lineup at the 40,000-capacity Vive Claro venue, as the Latin American live industry continues raising funds following the Aug. 10 earthquake that killed over 250 people. Combined fundraising across recent benefit concerts (including Miami's Unidos por los Nuestros) has passed $4 million, with Global Citizen, Live Nation and other donors contributing directly to relief and rebuilding efforts.

### Key Points
- Aug. 23 Voces por la Vida concert at Bogotá's Vive Claro (40,000 cap); artists and industry donating services free of charge.
- Global Citizen pledged $500,000, matched by Live Nation, plus $250,000 from the Charities Aid Foundation.
- Funds are directed to rebuilding Universidad Tecnológica de Quibdó and building ten new schools.
- Follows a related Aug. 16 Miami benefit concert that expanded its scope to include Colombia relief.

## Transcript

Stars including Karol G, Maluma, and Grupo Niche will lead the event, as the wider Colombian live sector continues to pledge donations

![default](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/08/Vive-Claro-scaled.jpg.webp)

A benefit concert for victims of the recent Colombia earthquake on 10 August has added Karol G, Maluma, and Grupo Niche to its lineup for the 23 August event, as the Latin American sector continues to pledge support to recovery efforts.

The growing lineup of the *Voces por la Vida,* or *Voices for Life* concert comes as Colombian star Shakira announced that international charity Global Citizen has pledged $500,000 (€428,1000), which will be matched by Live Nation, to go towards emergency relief through the Presentes Corporation.

This includes funds to be directed to rebuilding of the Universidad Tecnológica de Quibdó and construction of ten new schools alongside the Buffett Foundation and Pies Descalzos Foundation. The Charities Aid Foundation has also donated $250,000 (€214,000).

*Voices for Life* will be held at Bogotá’s 40,000-cap Vive Claro. The venue confirmed today that fundraising efforts had passed the $4m (€3.4m) mark.

> “The immediate crisis has passed, but the need has not.”

“The need remains great, and the journey is just beginning. Affected families are still waiting for this aid. The immediate crisis has passed, but the need has not,” the venue said.

Also on the bill for the concert are Andrés Cepeda, Luis Alfonso, and Miguel Bosé. Karol G’s set will also be livestreamed in conjunction with the singer’s foundation Con Cora.

The concert will follow a separate concert held on 16 August in Miami’s Kaseya Centre, known as the *Unidos por los Nuestros* concert. This event was initially announced as a benefit concert for the recovery efforts after the 24 June Venezuela earthquakes, before expanding its focus to also include Colombia after 10 August.

Colombia’s live industry initially [sprung into action](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/colombias-live-industry-springs-into-action-following-devastating-earthquake/) following the 7.4-magnitude earthquake that ripped through the western portion of the country, killing over 250 people and injuring thousands more.

The OCESA-operated Vive Claro began operating as a national aid point, and many of the country’s biggest artists urged donations to a variety of local charities working to support victims, including Shakira, Karol G, J Balvin, Maluma, and Feid.

Announcing the *Voces por la Vida* concert, OCESA said: “National and international artists and hundreds of people behind the music industry are donating their work, goods, services, and talent, without charging fees or incurring direct costs, to make this event possible.

“This initiative is inspired by solidarity and the conviction that when a country needs us, we can all choose to be part of the solution.”
