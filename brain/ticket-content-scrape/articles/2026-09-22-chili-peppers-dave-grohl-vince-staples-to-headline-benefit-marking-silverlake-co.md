# Chili Peppers, Dave Grohl, Vince Staples To Headline Benefit Marking Silverlake Conservatory Of Music’s 25th Anniversary

## Summary

Red Hot Chili Peppers, Dave Grohl, and Vince Staples will headline a Hollywood Palladium benefit concert Oct. 17 celebrating the 25th anniversary of the Silverlake Conservatory of Music, the nonprofit school founded by Flea. Comedian Marc Maron will host the show, which opens the school's usual private fundraiser to a wider public ticketed audience aiming to raise $1.5 million.

### Key Points
- RHCP, Dave Grohl, Vince Staples headline Oct. 17 benefit at the Hollywood Palladium.
- Event marks Silverlake Conservatory of Music's 25th anniversary; hosted by Marc Maron.
- Presale begins 10 a.m. Sept. 24; general onsale 10 a.m. Sept. 25 via Ticketmaster.
- Benefit aims to raise $1.5 million, expanding what's normally a private dinner into a public ticketed show.

Silverlake Conservatory of Music, the Los Angeles nonprofit music school founded by the Red Hot Chili Peppers’ Flea and former RHCP touring member Tree Barry, will mark its silver anniversary with a star-studded benefit at the Hollywood Palladium Oct. 17.

Comedian Marc Maron will host the event which features performances from the Chili Peppers, Dave Grohl, Vince Staples and more.

SCM hosts an annual private benefit dinner with the aim of raising $1.5 million. Past performers have included Red Hot Chili Peppers, Patti Smith, Eddie Vedder, Neil Young, KD Lang, Bruno Mars, John Legend, Beck, Kamasi Washington, and Tracy Chapman. This year, to mark the 25th anniversary milestone, the school is opening up its birthday party for a broader celebration at the famed Hollywood venue.

Presales for the show begin 10 a.m. Sept. 24 with general onsale 10 a.m. Sept. 25 via [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/0900653276CE3529). More information, VIP and sponsorship opportunities are available at the event’s website.
