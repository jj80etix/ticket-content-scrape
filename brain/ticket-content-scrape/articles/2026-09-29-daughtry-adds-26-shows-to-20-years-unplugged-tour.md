# Daughtry Adds 26 Shows to ‘20 Years Unplugged’ Tour

## Summary

Daughtry announced a 26-show second leg of its "20 Years Unplugged" acoustic tour, running January 23 – March 7, 2027, following an already-scheduled 22-date fall leg (Oct. 12 – Nov. 14) supported by Ryan Perdz.

### Key Points
- Second leg spans Florida, Texas, California, Colorado, the Midwest, and Northeast stops including Detroit, Hershey and Bethlehem, PA.
- Includes a March 6 stop at Nashville's new venue The Truth, one night before the March 7 Atlanta finale.
- A limited number of $20 tickets will be offered per newly announced show while supplies last.
- Artist presale/VIP/$20 promotion runs Sept. 30 – Oct. 1; general on-sale begins Oct. 2.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Daughtry-Unplugged.webp)

Daughtry will carry its “20 Years Unplugged” tour into 2027 with a newly announced 26-show second leg.

The acoustic run begins January 23 at Saracen Casino Resort in Pine Bluff, Arkansas, and ends March 7 at the Tabernacle in Atlanta. The new itinerary includes Florida shows in Melbourne, Orlando and Clearwater before heading through Texas, California, Colorado and the Midwest.

Later stops include Detroit, Hershey and Bethlehem in Pennsylvania, Orono in Maine, and Charleston in South Carolina. Daughtry will also play Nashville’s The Truth on March 6, one night before the Atlanta finale.

The first leg of the tour is scheduled to begin October 12 at Troy Savings Bank Music Hall in Troy, New York, and closes November 14 at Majestic Theatre in Dallas. Singer-songwriter Ryan Perdz is scheduled to support all 22 fall dates.
