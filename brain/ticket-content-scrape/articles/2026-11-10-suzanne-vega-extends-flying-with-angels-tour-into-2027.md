# Suzanne Vega Extends ‘Flying With Angels Tour’ Into 2027

## Summary

Suzanne Vega added spring 2027 dates to her "Flying With Angels Tour," extending into markets like Atlanta, Nashville, Charlotte and Richmond.

### Key Points
- 11 new dates announced, March 6 – March 20, 2027
- Supports her first new full-length album in over a decade, released on Cooking Vinyl
- Tour has run since June 2025 with 100+ shows worldwide

AUCKLAND, NEW ZEALAND – SEPTEMBER 10: Suzanne Vega performs at Auckland Town Hall on September 10, 2026 in Auckland, New Zealand. (Photo by Dave Simpson/WireImage)

Suzanne Vega will continue “Flying With Angels” in 2027, adding spring dates to her tour in markets like Atlanta, Nashville, Charlotte and Richmond.

The acclaimed folk rock singer-songwriter announced 11 dates in March, kicking off her “Flying With Angels Tour” at Billheimer Capitol Theatre in Clearwater, Florida. The routing includes stops at Ponte Vedra Concert Hall in Florida, Atlanta’s Variety Playhouse, CMA Theater in Nashville, Charlotte’s Neighborhood Theatre, two nights at Haw River Ballroom in Saxapahaw, Greenville’s Peace Center, The Tin Pan in Richmond and Wilmington’s The Queen before wrapping up March 20 at Outpost in the Burbs in Montclair, New Jersey.

Vega has been on the road promoting her latest album, *Flying With Angels*, since June 2025, performing more than 100 shows worldwide in over a year. She recently performed at Salisbury City Hall in the U.K. and grossed $61,045 off 886 tickets, according Pollstar Boxoffice.

Her latest record was released on Cooking Vinyl, and it’s Vega’s first full-length album with new music in over a decade.

“Each song on the album takes place in an atmosphere of struggle,” adds Vega. “Struggle to survive, to speak, to dominate, to win, to escape, to help someone else, or just live.”

Vega is booked by High Road Touring in North America and managed by Mark Spector Company.
