# Lin-Manuel Miranda and Eisa Davis Announce Broadway Cast of ‘Warriors’

## Summary

Lin-Manuel Miranda and Eisa Davis announced the Broadway cast for their new musical "Warriors," starring Jasmine Cephas Jones, Amber Gray and Casey Likes, beginning previews March 9, 2027 at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre with an April 8 opening.

### Key Points
- "Warriors" (Lin-Manuel Miranda/Eisa Davis musical) begins Broadway previews March 9, 2027; opens April 8 at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre.
- Stars Jasmine Cephas Jones (Swan), Amber Gray (Ajax), Casey Likes (Luther), plus a broader announced ensemble.
- Jenny Koons directs; Andy Blankenbuehler co-directs/choreographs.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Broadway-Warriors-.webp)

Jasmine Cephas Jones, Amber Gray and Casey Likes will star in the Broadway premiere of “Warriors,” the new musical from Lin-Manuel Miranda and Eisa Davis.

The production begins previews March 9, 2027, at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre, with opening night scheduled for April 8. Miranda and Davis wrote the book, music and lyrics. Jenny Koons directs, with co-direction and choreography by Andy Blankenbuehler.

Jones will play Swan, with Gray as Ajax and Likes as Luther. The announced company also includes Jaci Calderon as Fox, Aneesa Folds as Cleon, Julia Harriman as Mercy, Sasha Hutchings as Cowgirl, Gizel Jiménez as Rembrandt and Kenita R. Miller as Cochise. Sasha Allen will portray Cyrus, and Donald Webber Jr. will play Masai. Additional casting will be announced later.

Several performers will reprise roles from the musical’s 2024 concept album. Folds, Gray, Harriman, Hutchings, Jiménez, Jones and Miller all appeared on the recording. Calderon takes on the role recorded by Phillipa Soo, while Allen and Webber will play characters voiced by Lauryn Hill and Colman Domingo. Likes, who also appeared on the album, moves into the role of Luther for the stage production.

The company brings together several performers with ties to Miranda’s previous projects. Jones and Hutchings were members of the original Broadway cast of “Hamilton,” while Folds appeared in “Freestyle Love Supreme.” Webber has starred as Alexander Hamilton on Broadway.

Gray earned a Tony nomination for “Hadestown,” and Miller received a nomination for “for colored girls who have considered suicide / when the rainbow is enuf.” Likes previously starred in “Almost Famous” and “Back to the Future: The Musical.”

Based on Walter Hill’s 1979 film “The Warriors” and Sol Yurick’s novel, the musical follows a Coney Island gang falsely accused of killing Cyrus during a gathering in the Bronx. The group must make its way home across New York City while fighting to survive and clear its name.

The stage production’s design team includes David Korins on scenery, Dede Ayite on costumes, Natasha Katz on lighting and Jason Crystal on sound. Kurt Crowley serves as music supervisor, with orchestrations by Crowley, Elizondo and Scott Wasserman.
