# Noah Kahan Announces ‘The Last Of The Bugs World Tour’ For 2027

## Summary

Noah Kahan announced the 31-date "The Last Of The Bugs World Tour" for 2027, a Live Nation-promoted run following his sold-out "The Great Divide World Tour." The North American leg opens April 24 at Sanford Stadium and includes four nights at Boston's Fenway Park.

### Key Points
- North American leg runs major stadiums including Nissan Stadium, Allegiant Stadium and MetLife Stadium
- Four-night Fenway Park run follows his sold-out summer 2026 four-show stand there
- European leg includes Berlin's Waldbühne, Seville's Plaza de España and Paris' Hippodrome ParisLongchamp, plus previously announced Wembley Stadium shows
- Artist presale registration opens Nov. 10, with sign-up required by Nov. 5

Following this year’s sold-out “The Great Divide World Tour” of stadiums, Noah Kahan is continuing his momentum of one of the biggest live draws out there with the 31-date “The Last Of The Bugs World Tour.”

The North American leg of “The Last Of The Bugs World Tour,” promoted by Live Nation, kicks off April 24 at Sanford Stadium in Athens, Georgia, and stops at major stadiums across the U.S. and Canada, including Nashville’s Nissan Stadium, Las Vegas’ Allegiant Stadium, and East Rutherford’s MetLife Stadium. The native New Englander will also do four nights at Boston’s Fenway Park, following his record-breaking, sold-out four-consecutive-show run this summer.

After wrapping in North America, Kahan will head to Europe with stops including Berlin’s Waldbühne, Seville’s Plaza de España, Paris’ Hippodrome ParisLongchamp and more, along with previously announced nights at Wembley Stadium in London.

Fans interested in the artist presale on Tuesday, Nov. 10th at 12 p.m. must sign up before Nov. 5th at 11:5 9.p.m ET. Artist Presales are hosted by multiple ticketing sites, but anyone who signs up can join the sale, according to the announcement. In order to prevent for-profit resale, some fans may be asked to complete a quick selfie check, or in rare cases, provide an ID via Persona to sign up.

The Reserved by Spotify presale will take place on Wednesday, Nov. 11 at 9 a.m. local time. Noah Kahan Reserved by Spotify tickets may be available to eligible U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers through Ticketmaster. Noah Kahan is using Ticketmaster’s Face Value Exchange to help fans get tickets at the original price, according to the announcement. Tickets purchased on Ticketmaster will be non-transferable and can only be resold on Ticketmaster at face value. In New York where laws prevent resale restrictions, tickets can be transferred, but Ticketmaster will still honor Noah Kahan’s terms by keeping resale prices at face value on its site.

Tickets for the European dates will first be available through an artist presale via Laylo, beginning Tuesday, Nov. 10th at 12 p.m. local time. Remaining tickets will be available via the general onsale starting Thursday, Nov. 12 at 12 p.m. local time.

Next week, Kahan kicks off the sold-out U.K. and European leg of “The Great Divide World Tour,” following fully sold-out North American, Australian, and New Zealand dates this summer.

“The Great Divide World Tour” sold more than 1.8 million tickets worldwide and earned Kahan the highest-ranked debut on Pollstar’s LIVE75 chart. Recent boxoffice reports submitted to Pollstar include 64,042 tickets sold over four nights at Afterpay Arena in Sydney, Australia, in early October, along with two sold-out T-Mobile Park dates in Seattle in late August, which sold more than 91,000 tickets and grossed $13.6 million.

Kahan is represented by THE·TEAM and managed by Foundations.
