# David Guetta, Maisie Peters Join The O2’s 20th Anniversary Lineup

## Summary

AEG kicked off countdown celebrations for The O2 London's 20th anniversary, launching in May 2027 and running through summer with multi-night residencies and exclusive shows. The first two confirmed acts are Maisie Peters (debut headline show, May 8, 2027) and David Guetta (Monolith show, June 5, 2027 — his only 2027 UK indoor date).

### Key Points
- 20th-anniversary celebrations run May-summer 2027, featuring a lineup from rising stars to global icons.
- Maisie Peters headlines May 8, 2027, ahead of her album *Florescence* (out May 22).
- David Guetta's Monolith show is his only UK indoor date and only London show in 2027, promoted by independent UK promoter Labyrinth Events.
- Venue is AEG's flagship European arena, opened in 2007.

## Transcript

![The O2 – London](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/6a3ca90f-4ad2-49e7-9d5d-1bc3765addad-The_O2_L-scaled.jpg)

The O2 London, AEG’s flagship in Europe. Courtesy of AEG

AEG has begun the countdown to The O2’s 20th anniversary celebrations next year, which will launch in May 2027 and run throughout the summer.

According to AEG, which owns and operates the iconic building, the 20th anniversary shows, including multi-night residencies, one-off special performances and exclusive shows, “will open a new chapter in the venue’s history, bringing together an exciting and diverse line-up of artists spanning rising stars to global icons across a range of genres.”

The first confirmed artist was British singer-songwriter [Maisie Peters](https://pollstar.com/artists/maisie-peters-328908), who will perform her debut headline show at The O2 on Saturday, May 8, 2027, ahead of the release of her anticipated new album Florescence, due to be released May 22. Peters represents the next generation of talent to take to The O2 stage, ushering in a new era of headliners and reflecting the venue’s ongoing commitment in presenting artists from grassroots to the global stage.

“Being the first artist announced for The O2’s 20th birthday celebration is honestly such an honor. I’ve been a fan of the venue for as long as I can remember and have watched so many shows there, so getting to step onto that stage for my own headline show feels like a real dream come true. The O2 holds so many iconic moments, and to be part of the very start of its next chapter makes this moment even more special and I can’t wait to share it with my fans,” commented Maisie Peters.

The second confirmed artists to perform as part of the O2’s 20th birthday lineup has been a headliner for decades: [David Guetta](https://pollstar.com/artists/david-guetta-41774), who’ll bring his Monolith show to London on Saturday, June 5, 2027, promoted by independent UK promoted Labyrinth Events. The show will mark Guetta’s only UK 2027 indoor show and only London show in 2027.

David Guetta performing at I-Days Milan at Ippodromo Snai San Siro in Italy on Sept. 6, 2026. He will join the O2’s 20th anniversary lineup in 2027. Photo by Sergione Infuso/Corbis via Getty Images

The two-time Grammy winner has been touring Monolith across Europe, topped off by three nights at Stade de France in Paris, where he sold more than 240,000 tickets (the exact figures hadn’t yet been reported to Pollstar Boxoffice at the time of this writing).

David Guetta commented, “I’m super excited to bring The Monolith Show to The O2. It’s been an incredible summer, with some outstanding Monolith shows across Germany, Belgium and Portugal, and I’m still thinking about the crazy memories from those three stadium shows at Stade de France. I can’t wait to bring that energy to London and experience it with everyone at The O2. London has always been a very special city for me, so playing my biggest show there to date means a lot.”

2027 will also mark more than two decades of partnership between the venue and headline sponsor O2, who have worked together in close collaboration since the venue’s inception. The relationship is one of the UK’s longest-standing and most-recognizable naming rights partnerships, and O2 customers continue to benefit from it. Over the past two decades, O2 has sold more than four million tickets to shows at The O2 to its customers via Priority, the platform which offers fans access to tickets 48 hours before general sale and a host of perks from drinks discounts to queue jumps.

O2 customers will also have priority access to the tickets for the anniversary lineup.

Gareth Griffiths, Director, Partnerships and Sponsorship, Virgin Media O2 commented: “We couldn’t be prouder of our partnership with AEG and everything we have achieved together at The O2 in London – countless memories made at shows which have shaped music and entertainment as we know it, putting the fan at the centre of the experience every step of the way. Now, we look towards the future and artists like Maisie Peters who are at the top of their game and will truly define live music for the next twenty years; the perfect first artist to celebrate this milestone with us next year.”

Steve Sayer, Senior Vice President and General Manager of The O2, and [a 2024 Pollstar Impact International honoree](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/11/13/2024-impact-international-uk-euro-steve-sayer/), commented, “Celebrating 20 years of The O2 is a significant milestone for us. Over the past two decades, we’ve hosted more than 3,000 shows, welcomed over 100million visitors, and established The O2 as the world’s leading live entertainment venue with countless iconic live moments to its name.

Our birthday celebrations are about reflecting on our success and legacy whilst continuing to look firmly to the future. Bringing together iconic acts alongside the next generation of talent is central to that vision (…). We have an incredible line-up planned with activity taking place throughout the site, and we look forward to welcoming fans from across the country and beyond to celebrate with us.”
