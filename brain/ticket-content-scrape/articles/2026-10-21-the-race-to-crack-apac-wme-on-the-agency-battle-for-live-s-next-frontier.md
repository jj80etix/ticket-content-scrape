# The race to crack APAC: WME on the agency battle for live’s next frontier

## Summary

IQ talks to WME Asia-Pacific head Brett Murrihy about the agency's decade-plus investment in the region and how local expertise has become a competitive advantage as agencies race to build APAC touring infrastructure.

### Key Points
- WME established its APAC footprint in 2015 via the acquisition of Australian agency Artist Voice, the first major agency with a local Sydney operation.
- Murrihy frames the region's rise as having "fundamentally changed" global touring.
