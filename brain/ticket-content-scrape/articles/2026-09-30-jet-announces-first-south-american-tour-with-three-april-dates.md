# Jet Announces First South American Tour With Three April Dates

## Summary

Australian rock band Jet announced its first South American tour: three dates in April 2027 across Bogotá, Santiago and São Paulo, marking debut performances in Colombia and Brazil.

### Key Points
- April 10: Royal Center, Bogotá, Colombia (Jet's first Colombia show).
- April 13: Teatro La Cúpula, Santiago, Chile.
- April 18: Audio, São Paulo, Brazil (Jet's first Brazil show).
- Comes more than two decades after breakout album "Get Born".

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Jet-South-America-Tour.webp)

Australian rock band Jet has announced its first South American tour, scheduling three concerts across Colombia, Chile and Brazil in April 2027.

The run begins April 10 at Royal Center in Bogotá, Colombia. Jet will then perform April 13 at Teatro La Cúpula in Santiago, Chile, before concluding April 18 at Audio in São Paulo, Brazil.

The announcement brings the band to new markets more than two decades after its debut album, *Get Born*, introduced songs including “Are You Gonna Be My Girl,” “Rollover DJ” and “Look What You’ve Done” to international audiences.

The Bogotá concert will mark Jet’s first performance in Colombia. Its São Paulo appearance will also be the group’s first concert in Brazil, adding another country to a touring history that has included extensive international travel.

Although the April dates constitute Jet’s first South American tour, the band has previously performed in Chile. Jet appeared at the REC festival in Concepción in March 2026, closing the event’s first day. The upcoming Santiago engagement will be its first concert in the Chilean capital.

Tickets for the Santiago show go on sale September 30 at 10 a.m. local time through Puntoticket. For São Paulo, presales begin September 29, followed by a general sale October 10 at 10 a.m. local time through Ticket360.

The South American announcement adds to a schedule that includes Australian concerts throughout October and November 2026. Upcoming stops listed by the band include Canberra, Wollongong, Erina, Hobart, Forth, Toowoomba, Coolum Beach, Traralgon, Ballarat and Frankston.

Jet’s Australian itinerary also includes an October 3 appearance at Night at the Barracks in Manly and an October 25 performance at Phillip Island Grand Prix Circuit.
