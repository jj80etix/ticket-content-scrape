# TVG Hospitality reveals ‘world-class’ club Domino in Brooklyn

## Summary

TVG Hospitality, the Ben Lovett-backed operator, announced it will open a 750-capacity club called Domino inside Brooklyn's landmarked Domino Sugar Refinery next April. It will be the company's first New York City venue, hosting concerts, club nights, comedy, film screenings, live podcasts, and community events.

### Key Points
- 750-capacity, all-ages venue opening April 2027 in Williamsburg, Brooklyn.
- First NYC venue for TVG Hospitality, founded by Mumford & Sons' Ben Lovett.
- Positioned as a multi-use, year-round community gathering space, not just a concert venue.

## TVG Hospitality reveals ‘world-class’ club Domino in Brooklyn

The 750-cap space with an 'independent spirit' will open next April, the Ben Lovett-backed operator announced today

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Domino-rendering.png)

New York City will gain a 750-capacity club with an “independent spirit” next year, with TVG Hospitality announcing the opening of Domino in April today.

Located within the landmarked Domino Sugar Refinery in Brooklyn’s Williamsburg neighbourhood, the multifaceted, all-ages venue will host concerts, club nights, comedy, film screenings, live podcasts, fashion, and arts events, with organisers also positioning it as a year-round gathering place for the local community.

It is the first NYC space for TVG, an independent developer and operator founded by Mumford & Son’s Ben Lovett, which launched a decade ago and has scaled to oversee venues in Los Angeles, London, and Alabama.

“Williamsburg was my introduction to New York City, as it was for so many artists and musicians twenty years ago,” Lovett says of the new space. “The neighbourhood has changed enormously since then, but for decades, people have sought and found community in places like the one we’re building.”

Lovett adds that the venue is designed to be a place of development, discovery, and community. Built within the historic industrial infrastructure, Domino will feature “world-class sound, carefully considered sightlines, a bespoke bar program, and instinctive hospitality”, operators say.

“Neighbourhoods change. The need for a place like this never does,” he adds.

Domino will open its calendar to all promoters, operators affirm, saying every show will be evaluated “on the same terms: strength and distinctiveness”. It will launch as a member of the National Independent Venue Association (NIVA), and with DICE as its ticketing platform.

“Independent venues built Brooklyn’s neighbourhoods and NYC’s nightlife economy,” says Jen Lyon, president of NYIVA (New York Independent Venue Association).

“Our hope is for the city and state to support us as an industry as we welcome TVG Hospitality’s new venue and the independent spirit it brings to Brooklyn,” she adds.

Operators have also committed to not taking a cut of artist merchandise sales.

Venue operators are also looking to establish a local cultural board to steer its programming, support emerging talent, and expand the space’s reach and impact. The first batch of performers will be announced in the new year ahead of the April opening, with a firm date to be confirmed.

It is the latest US space for TVG, which also operates Alabama’s 8,000-cap Orion Amphitheatre and 500-cap Saturn and Los Angeles’ 750-cap Pacific Electric and adjoining 150-cap Mitsi. Additional projects are in development.

The company has also developed and operated venues in London, including the 600-cap Lafayette, the 320-cap Rae’s (FKA Omeara), and the 250-cap The Social.
