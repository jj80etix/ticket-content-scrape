# DICE adds first Australian festival ticketing partner with Wildlands deal

## Summary

DICE has signed as exclusive ticketing partner for Australia's Wildlands Festival (Perth and Brisbane, New Year period) for the 2026/2027 edition, its first Australian festival deal. The tie-up is an early step in DICE's previously announced push into Australia, the Netherlands, Switzerland, and Mexico, and comes about a year after DICE's acquisition by Fever, which has kept the two brands separate.

### Key Points
- Deal covers Wildlands' Perth and Brisbane 2026/2027 editions; Wildlands is produced by Untitled Group and enters its seventh year, headlined by John Summit, Ki/Ki, and Skepta.
- First Australian festival partnership for DICE, part of a stated expansion into Australia, the Netherlands, Switzerland, and Mexico.
- Comes roughly one year after Fever's acquisition of DICE, with both companies maintaining separate brands.
- Untitled Group head of ticketing Courtney Tahar and DICE CCO Andrew Foggin both framed the deal around fan experience and community connection rather than price or fee terms.

### Highlights
- DICE (a Q12 Tier 1 primary-ticketing competitor) is actively converting its stated multi-market expansion plan (Australia, Netherlands, Switzerland, Mexico) into signed festival partnerships.
- The deal is exclusive-ticketing-partner in structure, the same model independent primary platforms like Etix compete on for festival/venue clients.
- Timing ties the expansion to DICE's post-Fever-acquisition strategy of scaling as an "independent live entertainment tech platform."

### Industry Problem
Independent, artist/fan-experience-branded ticketing platforms are actively bidding for exclusive festival contracts in markets outside their home base, intensifying competition for primary-ticketing deals that independents like Etix also pursue.

### Proposed Solution
DICE's response is geographic expansion via exclusive festival partnerships backed by its acquirer's scale; the comparable move for Etix is tracking which markets/festival segments DICE and similar Tier 1 competitors are entering next and where an independent, transparent-pricing pitch could win comparable exclusive deals before larger platforms establish a foothold.

Ticketing firm DICE has announced a deal to become the exclusive ticketing partner for Australia’s Wildlands Festival in 2027, marking the first Australian festival partnership for the company.

Produced by Untitled Group, Wildlands is approaching its seventh year, with organisers calling it “one of Australia’s most anticipated summer festivals”. Like many Australian festivals, its travelling editions take place across Perth and Brisbane over the New Year period. Last week it announced its upcoming edition would be [headlined](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/festival-news-spilt-milk-leads-flurry-of-australian-announcements/) by John Summit, Ki/Ki, and Skepta.

“Wildlands has become one of Australia’s most anticipated summer festivals – built not just on its lineup, but on a loyal community that returns year after year. An independent Australian festival at its core, Wildlands is known for world-class music and has earned a reputation for experiences that feel genuinely connected to the people who show up for them,” DICE said.

For DICE, the tie-up becomes an early addition to its push to launch in multiple new markets, which the company [said in June](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/the-last-year-has-marked-a-turning-point-for-dice/) would see it target Australia, the Netherlands, Switzerland, and Mexico.

> “Australia has one of the most passionate live music audiences in the world, and Wildlands reflects that energy”

The push comes a year on from DICE’s acquisition by Fever, which the company said would aim to build the “world’s leading independent live entertainment tech platform” while maintaining separate brands for the two firms.

“Wildlands has always been about more than the music — it’s about the connections people make and the moments that stay with them long after. We’re obsessive about every part of that experience, and ticketing is no exception. DICE gets that — and that’s exactly why we wanted them involved.” said Courtney Tahar, Untitled Group’s head of ticketing.

DICE CCO Andrew Foggin added that “Australia has one of the most passionate live music audiences in the world, and Wildlands reflects that energy”.

“Partnering with Untitled Group on this is a big moment for DICE, and we’re looking forward to connecting their fanbase through our platform,” Foggin said.
