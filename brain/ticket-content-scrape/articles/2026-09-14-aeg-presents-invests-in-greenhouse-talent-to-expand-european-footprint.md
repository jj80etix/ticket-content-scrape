# AEG Presents Invests in Greenhouse Talent to Expand European Footprint

## Summary

AEG Presents has taken an equity investment in Greenhouse Talent, the largest independent concert promoter in the Benelux region, formalizing roughly two decades of informal partnership; deal size and stake were not disclosed, and Greenhouse retains its Ghent/Breda leadership and operations.

### Key Points
- Greenhouse Talent, founded 2005, employs ~60 people and promotes ~1,500 events/year across music, comedy and other live entertainment in Belgium and the Netherlands.
- Founder Pascal Van De Velde: "After 20 years of working together, it feels like the right moment to take this next step."
- Investment size and ownership stake were not disclosed by either company.

### Industry Problem
Major promoters are consolidating regional market access by taking equity stakes in established independent promoters rather than building local relationships from scratch — a pattern that gradually narrows which promoters and venues remain genuinely independent.

### Proposed Solution
No product response applies; this is a market-structure signal to monitor — specifically whether AEG's Benelux consolidation changes which ticketing systems Greenhouse-promoted venues use going forward.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/AEG-Presents.webp)

AEG Presents has invested in Greenhouse Talent, formalizing a partnership with the independent Benelux concert promoter after approximately two decades of collaboration.

The companies did not disclose the size of the investment or the stake acquired by AEG Presents. Greenhouse Talent will continue operating from its offices in Ghent, Belgium, and Breda, the Netherlands, under its existing leadership.

Greenhouse Talent founder Pascal Van De Velde shared, “We know each other very well, understand how the other works, and most importantly, we genuinely love working together.”

He said the relationship has developed around shared goals involving excellence, innovation and integrity. “After 20 years of working together, it feels like the right moment to take this next step,” Van De Velde added.

Founded in 2005, Greenhouse Talent employs about 60 people across Belgium and the Netherlands. Mathilde Van geertruyden leads the company’s Belgian business, while Wouter de Wilde oversees its Dutch operations.

Greenhouse Talent promotes nearly 1,500 events annually, ranging from club shows to stadium concerts and festivals. Its programming includes music, comedy and other forms of live entertainment, with comedy events presented under the LiveComedy brand.

Greenhouse Talent also produces Gent Jazz in Belgium and Spoorpark LIVE in the Netherlands. The promoter leads the Dutch edition of Country to Country, the country music event created by AEG Presents, SJM Concerts and the Country Music Association.

Adam Wilkes, president and CEO of AEG Presents Europe and Asia-Pacific, called the agreement “hugely exciting for both organisations.”

“We have a longstanding and successful relationship with Pascal and the Greenhouse Talent team,” Wilkes said.

The investment expands AEG Presents’ reach into Belgium and the Netherlands as the live entertainment company grows its European operations. Wilkes pointed to AEG Presents’ recent expansion in the United Kingdom, France, Norway and Germany, saying the Greenhouse Talent deal “further expands our European footprint.”

The partnership will allow the companies to draw on their combined touring experience and create additional opportunities for artists and audiences across the region, according to Wilkes.

“Pascal and Greenhouse Talent fit seamlessly into everything we’re doing across Europe,” he said.

Despite joining AEG Presents’ broader European network, Greenhouse Talent is expected to retain its leadership, local expertise, artist relationships and “entrepreneurial identity.”
