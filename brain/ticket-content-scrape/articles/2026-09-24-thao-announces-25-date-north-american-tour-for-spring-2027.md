# Thao Announces 25-Date North American Tour for Spring 2027

## Summary

Thao has announced a 25-date North American tour for spring 2027, running February 23 (Hamden, CT) through April 11 (Phoenix), with stops including New York, Boston, Chicago, Toronto, Vancouver and Seattle. The tour supports Show Me, Thao's first album in six years, due October 30 via Kill Rock Stars. Six November 2026 warm-up shows precede the larger run. General on-sale is September 25.

### Key Points
- Spring 2027 leg: Feb 23 (Hamden, CT) - Apr 11 (Phoenix), 25 dates across the US and Canada.
- November 2026 warm-up shows (6 dates) begin Nov 3 in Los Angeles.
- Supports new album Show Me (Kill Rock Stars), out October 30 — Thao's first in six years.
- Presales began Sept 23; general on-sale Sept 25, noon local time.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Thao-1.webp)

Thao has announced a 25-date North American tour for spring 2027, with shows across the United States and Canada.

The run begins February 23 at Space Ballroom in Hamden, Connecticut, and ends April 11 at Crescent Ballroom in Phoenix. The itinerary includes stops at Bowery Ballroom in New York on February 24, Paradise Rock Club in Boston on February 25 and Black Cat in Washington, D.C., on February 27.

Thao will continue through the Southeast and Midwest in March, playing Atlanta, Nashville, Chicago and other cities before a March 14 show at The Great Hall in Toronto. After a short break, the tour resumes April 1 at Fox Cabaret in Vancouver. The final stretch includes Portland, Seattle, Salt Lake City, Denver, Santa Fe and Tucson before the Phoenix finale.

Tickets for the 2027 dates go on sale to the general public Friday, September 25, at noon local time. Presales begin Wednesday, September 23, at noon local time.

The tour will support *Show Me*, Thao’s first album in six years. The record is due October 30 through Kill Rock Stars.

Before the larger run, Thao has six shows scheduled for November 2026. Those dates begin November 3 at Lodge Room in Los Angeles and include San Francisco, Kingston and New York City, New York, Providence, Rhode Island, and Brattleboro, Vermont.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Thao’s official website](https://thaothaothao.com/).
