# Kings of Leon Announce 30-Date North American Tour for 2027

## Summary

Kings of Leon announced a 30-date North American tour for 2027 in support of their new album *O My Beloved*, running May 8 through October 15, 2027, across the U.S. and Canada. Band of Horses and Dexter and The Moonrocks join as support acts on select dates.

### Key Points
- Tour opens May 8, 2027 at Jiffy Lube Live (Bristow, VA); closes Oct. 15 at Madison Square Garden.
- 30 dates total, split into a spring leg (through June 6) and a fall leg starting Sept. 11.
- Stops include Blossom Music Center, Pine Knob, Rogers Arena, Kia Forum, and Red Rocks.
- Band of Horses and Dexter and The Moonrocks support on select dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Kings-of-Leon-2027-.webp)

Kings of Leon have announced a 30-date North American tour for 2027, with performances scheduled across the United States and Canada in support of their new album, *O My Beloved*.

The tour will begin May 8 at Jiffy Lube Live in Bristow, Virginia, and conclude October 15 at Madison Square Garden in New York City. Band of Horses and Dexter and The Moonrocks will join as special guests on select dates.

The first leg includes 13 performances through June 6, with stops at Philadelphia’s TD Pavilion at Highmark Mann, Blossom Music Center in Ohio, Pine Knob Music Theatre in Michigan and Toronto’s RBC Amphitheatre. Band of Horses will provide support throughout the spring run.

Following a summer break from North American touring, Kings of Leon will return September 11 at Utah First Credit Union Amphitheatre in West Valley City, Utah. The second leg includes performances at Vancouver’s Rogers Arena, Seattle’s Climate Pledge Arena, the Kia Forum in Los Angeles and Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado.
