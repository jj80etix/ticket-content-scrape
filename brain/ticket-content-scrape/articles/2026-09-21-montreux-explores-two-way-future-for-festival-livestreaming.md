# Montreux explores two-way future for festival livestreaming

## Summary

Montreux Jazz Festival's media arm, Montreux Media Ventures, is piloting two-way "immersive" livestreaming technology meant to turn remote viewers into active participants rather than passive watchers, following a reported 324% year-over-year spike in stream viewership.

### Key Points
- Trial ran during Vulfpeck's set at the festival's 60th edition, part of an R&D project called IFeL (Immersive Festival Live: Remote Presence at Scale).
- Used immersive microphones and remote-presence technology to test two-way audience engagement.
- Comes after MMV reported a 324% jump in livestream viewership the previous year.
