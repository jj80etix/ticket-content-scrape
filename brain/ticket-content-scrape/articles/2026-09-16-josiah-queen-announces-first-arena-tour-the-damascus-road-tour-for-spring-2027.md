# Josiah Queen Announces First Arena Tour, ‘The Damascus Road Tour,’ For Spring 2027

## Summary

Christian artist Josiah Queen announced his first arena tour, "The Damascus Road Tour," for Spring 2027, running April 8 through May 8.

### Key Points
- Kicks off April 8, 2027 in Tulsa, OK; ends May 8 in Fort Worth, TX
- Stops include St. Louis, MO, Charlotte, NC, San Diego, CA, and Kansas City, MO
- Marks Queen's first arena-level tour, tied to his "Damascus Road" album

Josiah Queen has announced his first-ever arena tour, set for Spring 2027. “The Damascus Road Tour” kicks off April 8 in Tulsa, OK. From there, the tour will run through major cities like St. Louis, MO, Charlotte, NC, San Diego, CA, Kansas City, MO, and more. Josiah will conclude the tour on May 8 in Fort Worth, TX.

“Damascus Road is really personal to me because that story is all about being changed by an encounter with God and never being the same after,” Queen said in a statement. “That’s what so many of these songs represent in my own life. My hope is that this tour feels like more than just a concert and that people walk in one way and leave changed by God. I really believe the Damascus Road Tour is going to be something special, and I can’t wait to sing these songs with everyone!”

As per an official release from TPR, “The Damascus Road Tour” begins a major new chapter for Queen as he makes the jump to arenas for the first time. As his reach continues to grow beyond the Christian music community, Damascus Road offers a deeper look at the faith and experiences shaping Queen both as an artist and a person. The album explores what it means to encounter God, be transformed by that  
encounter, and live differently because of it, making the album a natural foundation for his first-ever arena tour.

Tickets available to the public starting Sept. 18th at 10am local time. For tickets and more information, visit [josiahqueen.com](https://www.josiahqueen.com/).
