# Wynonna Judd Sets Fall 2026 U.S.’The Hard Truth’ Tour Dates

## Summary

Wynonna Judd announced her fall 2026 "The Hard Truth Tour," a 15-date U.S. headlining run supporting her upcoming album *The Hard Truth*, out October 2. The tour kicks off October 8 in New York City and wraps November 21 in Gary, Indiana, with Troubadour Blue as special guest on all dates.

### Key Points
- 15-date tour runs Oct. 8 (New York) through Nov. 21 (Gary, IN), with stops including Houston, Tulsa, West Palm Beach and Tampa.
- Fan club pre-sale begins Aug. 4; Live Nation All Access pre-sale Aug. 5; general Ticketmaster onsale Aug. 7.
- Judd is completing a co-headlining tour with Melissa Etheridge before this headlining run begins.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Wynonna-Judd-The-Hard-Truth-Tour.webp)

Wynonna Judd has announced her fall 2026 “The Hard Truth Tour,” a 15-date U.S. headlining run supporting her upcoming solo album, *The Hard Truth*, set for release on October 2.

The tour is set to kick off on October 8 in New York City at Bowery Ballroom. From there, Judd is slated to make stops in cities such as Houston, Tulsa, West Palm Beach and Tampa before wrapping up on November 21 in Gary, Indiana at Hard Rock Live Northern Indiana.

Troubadour Blue will join the tour as the special guest on all announced dates.

Tickets will first be available through the Wynation Fan Club pre-sale, which begins Tuesday, August 4, at 10 a.m. local time.
