# BTS to livestream two ‘Arirang’ shows to thousands of cinemas

## Summary

BTS will livestream its Jakarta (Dec. 29) and Kaohsiung (Nov. 21) Arirang tour stops to roughly 2,800 cinemas worldwide as a ticketed cinema event called "BTS Live," following an earlier Seoul livestream that drew 18 million viewers. Tickets for the cinema broadcasts go on sale Nov. 5 through a dedicated event website.

### Key Points
- Two shows will stream: Kaohsiung, Taiwan (Nov. 21) and the Jakarta finale of the tour's Asian leg (Dec. 29).
- Broadcast reaches ~2,800 cinemas globally, exclusive to theaters.
- A prior livestreamed Seoul Arirang show drew 18 million viewers earlier this year.
- Tickets go on sale Nov. 5 via a dedicated BTS Live website.
