# New 2-Day Venice Beach Music Festival In The Works

## Summary

A new two-day music festival, "1905 Fest," is in the works for Venice Beach, aiming to launch next fall following the cancellation of Santa Monica's Ocean Way festival due to beach erosion. Sam Meyers, former festival director of Redondo Beach's Beachlife Festival, is organizing the event, named for the year Venice, California was founded.

### Key Points
- Two-day beachfront festival planned for Venice Beach, targeting a fall launch.
- Organized by Sam Meyers, previously of Beachlife Festival in Redondo Beach.
- Follows the cancellation of a similar Santa Monica beach festival over erosion concerns.

## Transcript

Los Angeles isn’t giving up on having music events on the beaches. Following the cancellation of Ocean Way in Santa Monica because of beach erosion, Venice Beach is proposing to have its own music festival on its beachfront.

The two-day 1905 Fest in Venice Beach is in the works and aims to launch next fall. Sam Meyers, who worked as festival director on Redondo Beach’s Beachlife Festival, is behind the upcoming event celebrating the origins and spirit of Venice.

The name of the festival, 1905, is the year Venice was founded by Abbot Kinney, and the site will be centered around Winward Plaza near the beach and iconic Venice Skatepark. Producers of the festival plan to welcome 20,000 fans and to feature “a multi-stage lineup built around psychedelic rock, indie, and electronic dance music, alongside emerging and local Southern California artists,” the festival’s website states.

1905 Fest will offer six ticketing options: Venetian, General Admission, VIP, Goofy Stance, Shredder and Bungalows. The Venetian tickets are exclusively for Venice locals.

To sign up for updates on the event, visit [1905fest.com](https://www.1905fest.com/).
