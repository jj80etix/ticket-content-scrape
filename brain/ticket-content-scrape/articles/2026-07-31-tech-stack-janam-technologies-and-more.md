# Tech Stack: Janam Technologies and more…

## Summary

TheTicketingBusiness's weekly tech roundup covers Janam Technologies' (HID) new GTX+ mobile entry pedestal, built for flexible, affordable facial-authentication check-in at scale via Wicket's facial-auth platform while still validating barcodes, QR codes, and Apple/Google Wallet passes; Mizzou Athletics' new fan app/site with WMT Digital's FanID for first-party data; TickPick's rebuilt app leaning on Spotify-listening-habit-driven personalized discovery with a "transparent pricing" focus; Ticketbud's expansion into credentialing/access-control/badge services; and Elon University Athletics moving ticket ops to Taymar.

### Key Points
- Janam/HID's GTX+ pedestal adds flexible, affordable facial-authentication entry while still supporting barcodes, QR, Apple/Google Wallet passes, and wristbands (built on Wicket's facial-auth platform).
- Mizzou Athletics + WMT Digital launch "Mizzou ID" (WMT FanID) for first-party fan data and personalized engagement.
- TickPick's rebuilt app emphasizes transparent pricing alongside a personalized discovery feed driven by favorite artists/teams, Spotify listening habits, browsing activity, and location.
- Ticketbud adds credential printing, access control, and on-site badge services for organizer-side staff/VIP management.
- Elon University Athletics hires Taymar for ticket sales and operations.

### Highlights
- GTX+ pairs facial authentication (via Wicket) with existing barcode/QR/wallet validation in a single entry device.
- TickPick explicitly markets "transparent pricing" alongside its personalization rebuild.

### Industry Problem
Venues need cheaper, more flexible ways to add biometric/identity-based entry security without ripping out existing barcode/QR infrastructure, while resale platforms are using pricing transparency as a competitive differentiator against fee-opaque incumbents.

### Proposed Solution
Janam/Wicket's answer is a single multi-modal entry device (barcode + QR + wallet + face); this is a concrete build/improve signal worth evaluating for high-value client venues, and TickPick's transparent-pricing positioning is a marketing cue Etix could lean into given its own all-in pricing stance.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Our weekly tech round-up from the world of ticketing and associated industries…**

**Janam Technologies**, part of HID, has announced GTX+, a new mobile entry pedestal designed to help sports and live entertainment venues deploy facial authentication more flexibly, affordably and at scale.

GTX+ builds on Janam’s proven GTX platform with a taller form factor optimised to support biometric authentication while continuing to validate barcodes, QR codes, phone-based Apple Wallet and Google Wallet passes, wristbands and more. It is powered by Janam’s XT4 rugged mobile computer and supports Wicket’s facial authentication platform.

**Mizzou Athletics**, the athletics division of the University of Missouri in the US, has launched a new digital experience in partnership with **WMT Digital**, featuring a fresh website, enhanced mobile app and the debut of Mizzou ID, powered by WMT FanID.

The goal is to prioritise direct fan relationships and first-party data while delivering more personalised engagement and strengthening fan loyalty.

**TickPick** has launched a newly rebuilt mobile app, with the aim of delivering a faster, smarter and more personalised way to discover, buy, sell and create live events.

With a focus on transparent pricing, the app also introduces a more personalised way to discover live events. A redesigned home feed shows concerts, games and shows based on favourite artists, teams and venues, Spotify listening habits, browsing activity, tracked events and location.

Elsewhere, **Ticketbud** has expanded its event ticketing and credentialing capabilities to include credential printing, access control, and on-site badge services, helping event organisers to manage attendee, staff, vendor and VIP access with greater flexibility, speed and control.

**Elon University Athletics** has brought in **Taymar** to handle ticket sales and operations.

“We are excited about our partnership with Taymar as we continue investing in the future of Elon Athletics,” Elon’s director of athletics, Jenn Strawley, said. “With a proven track record in ticket sales and operations, Taymar brings the expertise and resources to help us meaningfully grow our ticket revenue, enhance the fan experience and expand our reach in our community.”

Taymar, a US sports revenue partner, has appointed Matt Kaufman as general manager of ticket sales and operations to lead efforts in the Elon community.
