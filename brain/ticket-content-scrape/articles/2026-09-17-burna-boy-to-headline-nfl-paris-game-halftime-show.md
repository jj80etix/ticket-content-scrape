# Burna Boy to Headline NFL Paris Game Halftime Show

## Summary

Burna Boy will headline the halftime show for the NFL's first regular-season game in France (Steelers-Saints, Oct. 25 at Stade de France), returning to the venue where he became the first African artist to headline it in April 2025.

### Key Points
- Halftime show at NFL's first regular-season game in France, Oct. 25, Stade de France.
- Matchup: Pittsburgh Steelers vs. New Orleans Saints, kickoff 9:30 a.m. ET.
- Burna Boy previously became the first African artist to headline Stade de France (April 2025).
- Framed by the artist as representing African music on a historic NFL international stage.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/NFL-Paris-Halftime-Show-Burna-Boy.webp)

Burna Boy will headline the halftime show at the NFL’s first regular-season game in France, returning to Stade de France for the historic event on October 25.

The Grammy Award-winning Nigerian artist will perform during the matchup between the Pittsburgh Steelers and New Orleans Saints. Kickoff is scheduled for 2:30 p.m. Central European Summer Time, or 9:30 a.m. Eastern Time.

“Paris gave me one of the biggest nights of my career, and to walk back into Stade de France for a moment like this means everything,” Burna Boy said. “The NFL is bringing something to France that has never happened here before, and I get to stand in the middle of it representing African music and I intend to give the people a show they will not forget.”

The appearance will bring Burna Boy back to a venue where he previously reached a career milestone. In April 2025, he became the first African artist to headline Stade de France, one of Europe’s largest stadiums.

The performance is part of a record seven halftime shows accompanying the NFL’s 2026 international schedule. The league is staging games and musical performances across seven countries and four continents during its largest international season to date.

Other scheduled performers include Ricky Martin and Pedro Sampaio in Rio de Janeiro on September 27, Ayra Starr in London on October 11, Cage the Elephant in Munich on November 15 and The Warning in Mexico City on November 22. The Jonas Brothers performed during the NFL’s Melbourne game on September 10.

The NFL has staged 62 regular-season games outside the United States. Previous host cities include London, Berlin, Munich, Frankfurt, Madrid, Dublin, São Paulo, Mexico City and Toronto. Paris, Melbourne and Rio de Janeiro are new markets on the league’s 2026 schedule.
