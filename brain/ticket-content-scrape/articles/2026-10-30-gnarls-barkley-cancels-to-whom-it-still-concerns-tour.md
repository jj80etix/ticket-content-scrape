# Gnarls Barkley Cancels ‘To Whom It Still Concerns’ Tour

## Summary

Gnarls Barkley canceled its "To Whom It Still Concerns Tour" less than two months after announcing it; Ticketmaster is refunding buyers within 14-21 days. The cancellation follows reports that Danger Mouse would not appear, despite Cee-Lo Green having rights to perform the duo's catalog.

### Key Points
- Tour was Live Nation-promoted, set to run Nov. 5 (The Masonic, San Francisco) through Nov. 28 (House of Blues, Chicago), with stops in Atlanta, Boston, Toronto and more
- Cancellation comes a month after Danger Mouse's camp said he would not perform on the tour
- Refunds processed automatically to ticket buyers' accounts

Gnarls Barkley has canceled its “Whom It Still Concerns Tour” less than two months after announcing the outing. [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/gnarls-barkley-to-whom-it-still-atlanta-georgia-11-10-2026/event/0E006530D4716CB2) has also notified ticket buyers that they will receive a refund to their “account within 14-21 days.”

The cancellation arrives a month after a representative for Danger Mouse said he would not appear on the tour. However, Cee-Lo Green had permission to perform the duo’s music under an agreement between the artists.

Promoted by Live Nation, the tour was set to kick off Nov. 5 at The Masonic in San Francisco. Throughout the trek, the run was scheduled to stop in Atlanta, Boston, Toronto, and more. The tour was set to conclude on Nov. 28 at the House of Blues in Chicago.

CeeLo Green and Live Nation did not immediately respond to a request for comment.
