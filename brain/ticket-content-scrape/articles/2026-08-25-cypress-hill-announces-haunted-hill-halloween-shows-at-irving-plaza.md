# Cypress Hill Announces ‘Haunted Hill’ Halloween Shows At Irving Plaza

## Summary

Cypress Hill announced the return of their "Haunted Hill" Halloween tradition with two shows at New York's Irving Plaza on Oct. 31 and Nov. 1, featuring support acts including Immortal Technique, Lord Sko, and Mellow Man Ace.

### Key Points
- Two shows: Oct. 31 and Nov. 1 at Irving Plaza, NYC.
- Oct. 31 support: Immortal Technique and Lord Sko; Nov. 1 support: Mellow Man Ace featuring Krooked Treez and Planta Industrial.
- Presale begins Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. ET; general on-sale Friday, Aug. 28.

Cypress Hill have announced the return of their storied Halloween tradition, “Haunted Hill,” with a two-night show at New York’s Irving Plaza on Oct. 31 & Nov. 1. Each night will include an array of supporting acts; Halloween will see Immortal Technique and Lord Sko hitting the stage. The Nov. 1 show will include Mellow Man Ace featuring Krooked Treez and Planta Industrial.

Tickets for both shows will go on sale via presale on Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. ET, ahead of the general sale on Friday, Aug. 28 at 10 a.m. ET at [Ticketmaster.com.](https://www.ticketmaster.com/)

Mellow Man Ace also appears on Cypress Hill’s critically acclaimed new album, *Dios Bendiga,* joining the group on the high-energy standout “Campeones.” Released on July 24 via HYBE, *Dios Bendiga* is the band’s first full-length project in more than four years and is their first-ever Spanish-language album since they exploded on the scene more than three decades ago. Honoring their Latino roots while pushing their iconic West Coast sound forward, the album also features collaborations with Alemán, Trueno, Coyote and Poe Leos.

That evolution is equally evident on stage. Most recently, the LA icons brought their high-energy live show to major festivals across Latin America, including Vive Latino in Mexico City, Lollapalooza Brazil and Tecate Pa’l Norte in Monterrey, commanding some of the biggest audiences of the night at each stop. With Haunted Hill, Cypress Hill returns to New York for two unforgettable nights celebrating a beloved Halloween tradition, decades of iconic music, and the success of *Dios Bendiga*.
