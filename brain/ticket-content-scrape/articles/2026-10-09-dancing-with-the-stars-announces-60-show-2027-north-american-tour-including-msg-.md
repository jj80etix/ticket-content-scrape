# Dancing With the Stars Announces 60-Show 2027 North American Tour, Including MSG Debut

## Summary

*Dancing With the Stars: Live!* announced a 60-show 2027 North American arena tour (Jan. 21–Apr. 11), including the production's first-ever Madison Square Garden performance (Feb. 5), with CoverGirl as its first presenting sponsor.

### Key Points
- Tour runs SNHU Arena (Manchester, NH, Jan. 21) through Gas South Arena (Duluth, GA, Apr. 11), with a new stage production directed by Mandy Moore.
- For the first time, the show's touring troupe (not just pro dancers) joins the production.
- Promoted by Live Nation and NS2; produced by Faculty Productions with BBC Studios LA.
- General onsale begins Oct. 13; the "Mirrorball" fan presale began Oct. 8.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Dancing-with-the-stars.webp)

*Dancing With the Stars: Live!* will return to the road in 2027 with a 60-show North American arena tour featuring professional dancers from the popular ABC competition series, special guests and a new stage production.

The tour begins Jan. 21 at SNHU Arena in Manchester, New Hampshire, and concludes April 11 at Gas South Arena in Duluth, Georgia. Among the highlights is the production’s first-ever performance at New York City’s Madison Square Garden on Feb. 5.

Other major stops include Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia, TD Garden in Boston, Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, American Airlines Center in Dallas, Ball Arena in Denver, Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle and Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.

The production will feature professional dancers Brandon Armstrong, Rylee Arnold, Alan Bersten, Hailey Bills, Sharna Burgess, Val Chmerkovskiy, Jenna Johnson, Emma Slater, Ezra Sosa, Britt Stewart and Adele Zaikman.

Slater will also return as co-host, while Arnold and Burgess will participate during select portions of the tour. Arnold is scheduled for appearances Jan. 21 through Feb. 13, Feb. 23 through March 20 and March 31 through April 11. Burgess is scheduled for Feb. 17-21 and March 22-27.

For the first time, the *Dancing With the Stars* troupe will join the touring production. Troupe members Kamri Peterson, Onye Stevenson, Jaxon Willard and Carter Williams are scheduled to perform alongside the professional cast.

The show will be directed by Emmy Award-winning choreographer Mandy Moore, whose credits include *La La Land* and *Taylor Swift: The Eras Tour*. The production will feature ballroom performances, elaborate choreography and theatrical elements designed to bring the television competition’s atmosphere to live audiences.

“We could not be more excited for the fans of DWTS to attend shows across the country next year and see this all-new spectacle that the truly world class dancers and our brilliant creative team are dreaming up,” said Jared Paul of Faculty Inc., the tour’s production company.

*Dancing With the Stars: Live!* is produced by Faculty Productions in association with BBC Studios Los Angeles Productions and promoted by Live Nation and NS2.

## Dancing With the Stars 2027 Tour Tickets and Presale Information

Tickets for the 2027 tour will go on sale to the general public Tuesday, Oct. 13, at 10 a.m. local time through the [official Dancing With the Stars tour website](https://dwtstour.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The Mirrorball presale began Thursday, Oct. 8, at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register for free Mirrorball membership to receive presale access codes and updates on tour announcements, special guests and ticket availability.

The complete schedule, including individual venue details, ticketing links and any additional announcements, is available through [DWTSTour.com](https://dwtstour.com/dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Fans can check the official tour site for information on cast appearances at individual stops.
