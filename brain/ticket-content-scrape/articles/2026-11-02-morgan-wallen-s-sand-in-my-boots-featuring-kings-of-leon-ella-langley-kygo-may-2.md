# Morgan Wallen’s ‘Sand In My Boots’ Featuring Kings of Leon, Ella Langley, Kygo May 21-23 In Gulf Shores, Alabama

## Summary

The full artist lineup for Morgan Wallen's second "Sand In My Boots" festival (Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, 2027) was revealed, headlined by Kings of Leon, Ella Langley, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, and Nelly alongside Wallen.

### Key Points
- Over 30 acts announced, spanning country, hip-hop, and pop/EDM, continuing the first edition's genre-blending approach.
- Produced again with AEG Presents following a sold-out 2025 debut.
- Multiple ticket tiers offered (GA, Party Pit, VIP, Super VIP, "Livin' the Dream") with varying amenities like in-pool viewing areas.
- Wallen's foundation continues its "Tour Give Back" instrument-donation program tied to the festival.

Following up on Friday’s announcement of dates and location, the lineup has been revealed for Morgan Wallen’s Sand In My Boots festival in Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, which features Kings of Leon, Ella Langley, Kygo, Empire of The Sun, Nelly, Flo Rida, Gavin Adcock, Koe Wetzel and many others.

Following a sold-out inaugural edition in 2025 and again produced in partnership with AEG Presents, Sand In My Boots’ musical lineup also includes Cole Swindell, Third Eye Blind, Midland, Hudson Westbrook, Gucci Mane, Rick Ross and still more.

The announcement says the lineup reflects Wallen’s own musical identity blending traditional country with other genres and intended to create a space for fans to discover the music, personalities, and influences that have shaped one of the biggest stars in music.

“One thing I’m looking forward to the most about bringing Sand In My Boots Festival back is being able to have new concepts on stage and trying to elevate the experience overall for the fans, myself and other artists on the bill,” Wallen says in the announcement. “I think it’s going to be really top-notch and we’re going to try to do it even better this time.”

Morgan will also continue his ongoing commitment to giving back to the community at Sand In My Boots through his Morgan Wallen Foundation (MWF), which supports programs for youth in the areas of sports and music. In 2025, the foundation donated over $200,000 in instruments to five schools in Alabama. Since the start of MWF’s Tour Give Back initiative, the foundation has contributed over $2 million worth of instruments to schools in need across U.S. touring cities. For more information, visit MorganWallenFoundation.org.

Sand In My Boots’ sold-out debut in May 2025 was topped by Morgan, Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn, and HARDY, also featuring top talent like Ella Langley, Riley Green, Bailey Zimmerman, T-Pain, Diplo, Wiz Khalifa, The War on Drugs, 2 Chainz, Three 6 Mafia and more.

Sand In My Boots looks to combine the atmosphere of a major music festival with the laid-back spirit of a Gulf Coast beach vacation, offering a range of pass options, including GA, Party Pit, VIP, Super VIP, and Livin’ The Dream, with exclusive amenities available across each tier.

Depending on pass type, amenities include premium viewing areas and lounges, main stage in-ground swimming pools, complimentary bars and gourmet food options, private restrooms, dedicated festival entrances, exclusive merch bundles, and more.
