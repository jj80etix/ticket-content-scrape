# Shakira Adds 13th Madrid Show After Setting Amazon Music Livestream Record

## Summary

Shakira added a 13th and final Madrid residency show (Oct. 12, "Gran Despedida con Amigos," with guests Maluma, Beéle and Dani Martín) after one of the earlier concerts set an Amazon Music livestream record; the run grew from an originally planned 3 shows to 13 at the purpose-built Estadio Shakira.

### Key Points
- Residency expanded from 3 to 13 shows across the year (March → October additions).
- New finale date Oct. 12 at Estadio Shakira (Iberdrola Music site), with guests Maluma, Beéle and Dani Martín.
- Triggered by an Amazon Music livestream record set during the residency.
- General onsale for the new date begins Oct. 7 via Live Nation, Ticketmaster and El Corte Inglés.

Shakira is extending her Madrid residency one more time, adding a 13th and final performance at the purpose-built Estadio Shakira after one of the run’s concerts set a new livestream record for Amazon Music.

The newly added show is scheduled for Monday, October 12 at the Iberdrola Music complex in Madrid. Billed as “Gran Despedida con Amigos” — “Grand Farewell with Friends” — the concert extends a residency that had previously been scheduled to conclude with its 12th performance on October 11. The finale will also bring several notable guests to the stage with Maluma, Beéle and Spanish singer Dani Martín.

The latest addition continues a remarkable expansion for the Madrid residency. [TicketNews reported in March](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/shakira-expands-madrid-residency-to-nine-nights-with-three-new-october-shows/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that what began as a three-show engagement had grown to nine dates. By April, [the run had expanded to 11 concerts](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/shakira-expands-las-mujeres-ya-no-lloran-tour-with-new-u-s-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), with additional dates ultimately pushing the residency to 12 before the October 12 finale was announced.

All of the shows are being staged at Estadio Shakira, a temporary stadium constructed at Madrid’s Iberdrola Music site as part of the broader “ES Latina” residency concept.

## Madrid Show Sets Amazon Music Record

The additional date follows another milestone from Shakira’s Madrid run.

Her October 3 performance became Amazon Music’s most-viewed livestream performance by a female artist, according to the streaming service. Amazon has not disclosed a viewer total for the broadcast.

The concert was streamed globally across Amazon Music, Prime Video and Twitch and marked Shakira’s first full-concert global livestream. [Amazon Music had announced the broadcast](https://press.aboutamazon.com/2026/9/amazon-music-and-shakira-celebrate-the-women-shaping-latin-music-and-culture?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) as the centerpiece of its 2026 “The Women Shaping Latin Music” campaign.

The October 3 concert also featured Dua Lipa as a surprise guest. The two singers performed an acoustic version of “Antología,” a song from Shakira’s 1995 album *Pies Descalzos*. Other artists who have appeared with Shakira during the Madrid residency include appearances from Alejandro Sanz, Laura Pausini and Manuel Turizo.

Shakira’s broader touring schedule does not end in Madrid. An October 18 performance at Bishkek Arena in Kyrgyzstan is also scheduled, while her official website lists a November 28 concert at the Pyramids of Giza in Egypt.
