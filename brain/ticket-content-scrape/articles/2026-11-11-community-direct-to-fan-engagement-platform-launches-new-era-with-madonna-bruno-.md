# Community, Direct-To-Fan Engagement Platform, Launches ‘New Era’ With Madonna, Bruno Mars, Megan Moroney, Detroit Lions, McDonald’s

## Summary

Direct-to-fan engagement platform Community launched a new wave of interactive, app-like experiences built into Apple Messages (and RCS on non-Apple devices), debuting with partners including Madonna, Bruno Mars, Megan Moroney, the Detroit Lions and McDonald's. Moroney is the first artist to add in-message merchandise checkout powered by Apple Pay, collapsing discovery and purchase into a single conversation.

### Key Points
- Launch partners include Madonna, Bruno Mars, Megan Moroney, Detroit Lions, McDonald's, plus rising artists and the City of West Hollywood
- Built natively into Apple Messages worldwide and RCS for non-Apple devices
- Megan Moroney is first to enable in-message shopping via Apple Pay for merchandise
- Company positions the product as a "next-generation messaging channel" for fan/brand engagement
- More artist, team and brand partners expected to be announced shortly

### Highlights
- First in-message, Apple-Pay-powered checkout tied to a named artist partnership (Megan Moroney)
- Combines personalized messaging and commerce into one always-on channel, bypassing app/website visits entirely

### Industry Problem
Fans increasingly expect commerce to happen inside the channels they already use rather than requiring a separate app or site visit; conventional checkout flows add friction by routing fans away from the conversation they're already in.

### Proposed Solution
Community's answer is wallet-native, in-conversation checkout for merchandise. For Etix this is a signal to watch conversational/wallet-based checkout patterns (Apple Pay, RCS messaging commerce) as a lower-friction complement to standard purchase flows — not yet applied to ticketing, but a plausible adjacent-use pattern to track.

Community, a direct-to-fan engagement platform, has announced the launch of a “new era” in interactive experiences aiming to do nothing less than transform how artists, sports teams, brands and organizations connect with audiences. Its initial launch partners include an impressive line-up with Madonna, Bruno Mars, Megan Moroney, Detroit Lions and McDonald’s

This new interactive engagement experience, powered by Community, enables brands to deliver immersive, app-like experiences directly within the native Apple Messages app on Apple devices worldwide, and through RCS on supported non-Apple devices. The platform is designed with a focus on user experiences, privacy and seamless interaction.

Additional launch participants Include Russell Dickerson, Julia Cole, and the City of West Hollywood, alongside Rising Independent Artists The Burney Sisters, Tessa Schultz and Carrie Brockwell.  
  
Community’s new capabilities combine rich media, personalized engagement and intuitive design to create experiences that the platform say is the “next-generation messaging channels” and is uniquely partnering with artists, sports teams, and brands to create curated, interactive experiences to connect with audiences.

![Community Rich Messaging image 2](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Community-Rich-Messaging-image-2.png)

Community Rich Messaging image 2

According to the company, these “capabilities transform traditional text conversations into always-on, interactive destinations where audiences can explore content, access exclusive opportunities, engage with their favorite artists and brands, and take action whenever they choose.”

Among the first artist to embrace these new capabilities is country star (and [Pollstar cover subject](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/05/02/megan-moroney-the-emo-cowgirl-bad-bitch-of-country-sells-out-radio-city-twice-sheds-arenas-more-cover/) ) Megan Moroney will become the first artist to introduce an in-message shopping experience powered by Apple Pay, allowing fans to discover and purchase merchandise directly within Apple Messages. The experience brings fan engagement and commerce together, creating a seamless path from discovery to purchase without leaving the conversation.

“Megan has always prioritized her genuine connection with her fans, and that relationship is at the heart of everything we do,” said sHayley Corbett, artist manager for Megan Moroney in a statement. “Partnering with Community on this new experience is an exciting opportunity to take that connection even further, with a more personal and seamless experience for her fans. We’re always looking for innovative ways to superserve those who endlessly support her, and we’re excited to be a part of this new chapter with Community,”  

Additional artists, sports teams and brands will be announced shortly, according to Community.
