# Travis Scott, Post Malone and Charli XCX Lead Tecate Pa’l Norte 2027 Lineup

## Summary

Travis Scott, Post Malone, and Charli XCX will headline the 2027 edition of Tecate Pa'l Norte in Monterrey, Mexico, as the festival marks its 15th anniversary with 150+ artists across eight stages over March 26-28.

### Key Points
- Three-day festival returns to Parque Fundidora, Monterrey, March 26-28, 2027, for its 15th anniversary.
- Headliners: Travis Scott, Post Malone, and Charli XCX, all making festival debuts alongside Avenged Sevenfold and Julión Álvarez.
- Lineup spans 150+ artists across eight stages, including Deadmau5, Kasabian, Fontaines D.C., and Arcade Fire (guest appearance).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tecate-Pal-Norte-Festival.webp)

Travis Scott, Post Malone and Charli XCX will headline the 2027 edition of Tecate Pa’l Norte, as the Mexican music festival celebrates its 15th anniversary with more than 150 artists performing across eight stages.

The three-day event will return to Parque Fundidora in Monterrey, Mexico, from March 26-28. Travis Scott, Post Malone, Avenged Sevenfold and regional Mexican music star Julión Álvarez will all be making their Tecate Pa’l Norte debuts.

Scott will lead Friday’s lineup alongside The Neighbourhood and Mexican rock band Caifanes. Friday’s performers also include Latin Mafia, Raye, Milo J, Kasabian, Deadmau5 and Polo & Pan.

Post Malone will top Saturday’s bill alongside Avenged Sevenfold and Martin Garrix, with additional performances from Hombres G, Young Miko, Fontaines D.C., Sofi Tukker and Spoon. Arcade Fire will also make a special guest appearance presenting Santa Pirata.
