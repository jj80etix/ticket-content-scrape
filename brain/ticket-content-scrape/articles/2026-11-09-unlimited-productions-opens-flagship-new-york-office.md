# Unlimited Productions opens ‘flagship’ New York office

## Summary

Dutch production company Unlimited Productions opened a "flagship" New York office to grow its North American presence, pitching a single-vendor model covering audio, video, lighting, staging and site infrastructure after a summer of new U.S. contracts including Martin Garrix's tour and the Elements Music and Arts Festival.

### Key Points
- Recent U.S. work: Martin Garrix's North American tour, Elements Festival (PA), events at LA State Historic Park, HiJinx main stage (Philadelphia).
- Pitch: one team/point-of-contact for production, design, and site build-out instead of separate vendors per discipline.
- Managing director Jan-Maarten Luijnenburg cites Elements coming in under budget and building Garrix's touring rig in six weeks as proof points.

## Transcript

## Unlimited Productions opens ‘flagship’ New York office

The Dutch company will target the US market after a summer of key contracts in the country

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/768638077_18484187956098343_4788833804897786980_n-scaled.jpg.webp)

Fire Stage at Elements Festival © Unlimited Productions

Netherlands’ Unlimited Productions has unveiled a new “flagship” office in New York, telling *IQ* it is aiming to grow in the North American market on the back of a full service offering covering audio, video, lighting, and more under one roof.

The company has been active in the US for several years, but now says after a summer of expanding its account book in the market it is aiming to establish its first permanent US operation.

This expanded workload included Martin Garrix’s North American tour, Pennsylvania’s Elements Music and Arts Festival, and a series of events in Los Angeles State Historic Park. The company previously also worked on the redesign of the main stage at HiJinx in Philadelphia, and is currently working on a new design for the third year in a row, it says.

Managing director Jan-Maarten Luijnenburg told *IQ* the company is pitching itself to potential new US partners as “one of the few” firms that provides all areas of production from one vendor.

“Instead of events juggling separate audio, video, lighting, SFX, and staging vendors, one team owns all aspects and is accountable for the result. As one of the few, we own not only technical production but also site or overlay including temporary infrastructure, scenic and branding, all from a single point of contact,” he said.

> “ This year in the US proved that model travels”

Unlimited said the new office will allow it to offer American promoters, artists, festivals, sports and entertainment brands “the model it built in Europe, acting as architect, general contractor and site supervisor for a show”. This includes the option of bringing the company on as consultants or as a general contractor for the full production. It also has an in-house CAD department.

“This allows us to deliver turn-key stages, areas or complete sites. Every bolt, fence, cable path and scaffold ledger is drawn before load-in, so nothing gets figured out on site, improving efficiency and the end result,” Luijnenburg told *IQ.*“That’s how Elements came in under budget with every stage finished early in UP’s first year running it, and how we built Garrix’s North American touring rig in six weeks.”

“ This year in the US proved that model travels,” the managing director added.
