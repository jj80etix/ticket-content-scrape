# Reece Weaver Extends Broadway Run in ‘Chicago’

## Summary

Reece Weaver has extended her limited Broadway engagement as Roxie Hart in "Chicago" by two weeks, now running through November 1 instead of October 18. She made her Broadway debut in the role on September 7 at the Ambassador Theatre.

### Key Points
- Extension takes Weaver's run from six weeks to eight weeks, through November 1.
- "Chicago" is Broadway's second-longest-running show, behind only "The Phantom of the Opera."
- Current cast includes Lissa deGuzman, Max von Essen, Natasha Yvette Williams and Red Concepción.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Chicago-Broadway.webp)

Reece Weaver will spend two more weeks as Roxie Hart in Broadway’s “Chicago.”

The former Dallas Cowboys Cheerleader has extended her limited engagement through Sunday, November 1. Her run had been scheduled to end October 18. Weaver made her Broadway debut September 7 at the Ambassador Theatre in New York. The extension takes her engagement from six weeks to eight and keeps her in the role through the end of October.

The current Broadway cast also includes Lissa deGuzman as Velma Kelly, Max von Essen as Billy Flynn, Natasha Yvette Williams as Matron “Mama” Morton, Red Concepción as Amos Hart and J. Workman as Mary Sunshine.

The Broadway revival of “Chicago” began as part of New York City Center’s Encores! series before opening at the Richard Rodgers Theatre in 1996. It later moved to the Shubert Theatre, then transferred to the Ambassador Theatre in 2003, where it remains.

The musical was created by John Kander, Fred Ebb and Bob Fosse. Walter Bobbie directed the revival, with choreography by Ann Reinking.
