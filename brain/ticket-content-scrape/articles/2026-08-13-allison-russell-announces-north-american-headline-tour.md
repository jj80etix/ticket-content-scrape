# Allison Russell Announces North American Headline Tour

## Summary

Allison Russell has announced a North American headline tour in support of her new album *In the Hour of Chaos*, beginning October 15 in Asheville, North Carolina. The run includes a Nashville stop at the Ryman Theatre supporting Neko Case.

### Key Points
- Third solo studio album, produced by Russell and Dim Star, featuring guests including Norah Jones and Brittney Spencer.
- Tour runs Oct. 15 – Nov. 15 across the US and Canada, with stops including D.C., New York, Toronto, and San Francisco.
- Tickets are on sale now.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed-5.jpg)

Allison Russell is following up her summer tour with Sarah McLachlan with a North American headline tour in support of her new album, *In The Hour of Chaos*. The Grammy-winning musician, poet and actor also has a show booked at the iconic Ryman Theater in her adopted home of Nashville supporting Neko Case.

Marking her third solo studio album, *In The Hour of Chaos* was produced by Russell and Dim Star. The LP is described as “a fervent plea for connection in this time of alienation, isolation and dread.” An announcement from her team added that “It’s an album that is meant to make you feel GOOD and, in these times, feeling good is a radical act.”

Guest appearances on the album include Norah Jones, Brittney Spencer, Joy Oladokun and Julie Williams, Kara Jackson, Devon Gilfillian, Ahya Simone, Chibueze Ihuoma, Denitia and the Explore! Pop Choir featuring Russell’s daughter, Ida.

Russell’s fall headline tour starts Oct. 15 in Asheville, North Carolina, at The Orange Peel. Stops include the 9:30 Club in D.C., Irving Plaza in New York, Massey Hall in Toronto, The Vic Theatre in Chicago, First Avenue in Minneapolis, The Showbox in Seattle, and The FIllmore in San Francisco and more. Check out the routing below. Tickets are available now.  
  
Russell’s team includes Vector Management’s Nicki Loranger and The Feldman Agency’s Kathleen Reid.
