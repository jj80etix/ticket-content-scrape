# Somerset Sounds Concert Series Set for 2027 Launch

## Summary

Somerset County Cricket Club has partnered with Live Nation-owned promoter Full Fat 360 to launch Somerset Sounds, an 18,000-capacity annual concert series debuting summer 2027 at the Cooper Associates County Ground in Taunton, England.

### Key Points
- The venue previously hosted concerts by Elton John, Rod Stewart and Lionel Richie.
- Plans include a fan village with food, drink and cultural pop-ups along St James Street.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Somerset-Sounds.webp)

Somerset County Cricket Club has partnered with Live Nation-owned promoter Full Fat 360 to launch Somerset Sounds, a new annual concert series scheduled to debut in summer 2027.

The companies said the 18,000-capacity event will bring international touring artists to the Cooper Associates County Ground in Taunton, England. Full Fat 360 will co-promote and deliver the concerts as part of a joint venture with the cricket club.

Outdoor main-stage performances will take place on the venue’s outfield, with premium hospitality offered in the stands. Plans also include a fan village along St James Street featuring food, drinks and cultural pop-ups.

Somerset Sounds is intended to establish Taunton as a regular stop for major summer tours while expanding the use of the 145-year-old cricket venue beyond sports. The County Ground, which has a standing capacity of 20,000 and 8,500 seats in its stands, has previously hosted concerts by Elton John, Rod Stewart and Lionel Richie.

“The Cooper Associates County Ground already has an incredible legacy of hosting world-class live entertainment, and Somerset Sounds is the next ambitious step in that evolution,” Full Fat 360 founder Oli Bartlam said in the official announcement.

Bartlam said Taunton and the wider region have a “massive appetite for major live music,” adding that the partnership could help establish the area as a destination on the national touring circuit.

“We’re thrilled to be working with Somerset County Cricket Club to bring international acts to this iconic venue and build an event series the entire South West can be proud of,” Bartlam said. “Summer 2027 is only the beginning.”

Full Fat 360’s existing portfolio includes the SMK Live festival series at Stadium MK in Milton Keynes. The promoter has also worked on Kings of Leon concerts and the BS3 Festival at Ashton Gate in Bristol.

Somerset County Cricket Club CEO Jamie Cox said cricket would remain central to the organization while the venue pursued a broader role within the region.

“We want to create experiences that bring new audiences to Taunton from across the South West and beyond, while generating meaningful economic and cultural benefits for the County,” Cox said.

An economic impact analysis commissioned by the club in 2025 estimated that Somerset County Cricket Club contributes more than 20 million pounds annually to the local and regional economy. The study also found that the County Ground attracts approximately 150,000 visitors per year and directly supports more than 200 local jobs.

The venue is located near Taunton railway station, which provides direct connections to London, Bristol and Exeter. Organizers said the location could allow Somerset Sounds to serve audiences outside the major metropolitan markets that typically host stadium tours.

The first performers for the inaugural Somerset Sounds series have not yet been announced, but organizers said lineup details will be revealed soon. Fans can register for priority ticket access through the [Somerset Sounds website](https://somersetsounds.co.uk/).
