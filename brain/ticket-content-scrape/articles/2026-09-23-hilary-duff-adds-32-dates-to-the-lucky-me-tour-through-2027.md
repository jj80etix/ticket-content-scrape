# Hilary Duff Adds 32 Dates to ‘The Lucky Me Tour’ Through 2027

## Summary

Hilary Duff is adding 32 dates to "The Lucky Me Tour" through 2027, spanning Europe, Latin America and a 19-date U.S. arena run, on top of an already sold-out Australian and Canadian run. An artist presale for the new shows begins September 29, with general onsale October 2.

### Key Points
- New leg opens May 7, 2027 in Madrid and runs through nine European dates before Latin America in August and a U.S. run Oct. 1–Nov. 20.
- Rebecca Black joins the European leg; Dashboard Confessional and Tegan and Sara support portions of the U.S. leg.
- Nearly all Australian dates, the 2027 Canadian run, and two Mexico City shows are already sold out.
- Artist-presale registration closes Sept. 27, 3 p.m. ET; access is tied to registered accounts rather than a presale code.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Hilary-Duff-the-lucky-me-tour-2027.webp)

Hilary Duff is extending “The Lucky Me Tour” well into 2027, adding 32 performances across Europe, Latin America and the United States following a run of sold-out dates on the singer’s comeback tour.

The newly announced leg begins May 7 at Movistar Arena in Madrid and includes nine European shows before heading to Latin America in August. Duff will then return to the U.S. for a 19-date arena run beginning October1 in Sunrise, Florida, and wrapping November 20 at Honda Center in Anaheim, California.

Rebecca Black will join Duff for the European dates. Dashboard Confessional will support the first portion of the newly added U.S. leg, while Tegan and Sara will appear on the November dates.

The latest expansion substantially extends a tour that has already grown several times since its initial announcement earlier this year.

Duff initially announced the full-scale international tour in February, marking her first global headlining run in nearly two decades. Days later, second performances were added in Los Angeles, New York, Toronto and London after the initial announcement.

Demand has continued as the tour has progressed. Nearly all of Duff’s upcoming Australian dates are listed as sold out, along with the 2027 Canadian run and two February performances at Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City.

Tickets for the 32 newly announced shows will first become available through an artist presale beginning September 29, with additional presales running during the week. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin October 2 at 10 a.m. local time.

Fans seeking access to the artist presale must register by September 27 at 3 p.m. ET. Access will be tied directly to registered accounts rather than distributed through a presale code.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Duff’s official website](https://www.hilaryduff.com/).
