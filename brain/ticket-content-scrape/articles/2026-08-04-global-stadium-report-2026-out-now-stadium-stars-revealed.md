# Global Stadium Report 2026 out now: Stadium Stars revealed

## Summary

IQ Magazine published the second edition of its Global Stadium Report, highlighting a 16.5% year-over-year rise in stadium-headlining artists (912 in 2025) and 12%+ growth in stadium show count, despite the FIFA World Cup displacing 16 North American venues for part of the summer. The report also unveils a 20-venue "Stadium Stars" list and features on production complexity, the collegiate-venue circuit, and industry interviews.

### Key Points
- 912 artists headlined stadiums in 2025, up 16.5% year-over-year; show count grew over 12%.
- FIFA World Cup 2026 removed 16 North American stadiums from the concert calendar for much of the summer.
- Second annual "Stadium Stars" list recognizes 20 top venues worldwide, chosen by the industry.
- Coverage includes 360° production trends, the US collegiate stadium circuit filling FIFA-vacated dates, and interviews with CAA's Emma Banks and other venue/agency executives.
