# DEAG CEO Detlef Kornett to depart at the end of 2026

## Summary

IQ Magazine's version of the DEAG leadership story: CEO Detlef Kornett will depart at year-end after 13 years, succeeded as Executive Board chairman by media executive Markus Breitenecker, who joins November 1, 2026 and takes a shareholding in the company. DEAG frames the move as focused on further international expansion and strengthening market position. (Duplicate coverage of the same transition also reported via Pollstar — see companion note.)

### Key Points
- Breitenecker succeeds Kornett as chairman of DEAG's Executive Board effective Nov. 1, 2026.
- Kornett's departure closes out a 13-year tenure at the company.
- Breitenecker will acquire a shareholding in DEAG as part of the transition.
