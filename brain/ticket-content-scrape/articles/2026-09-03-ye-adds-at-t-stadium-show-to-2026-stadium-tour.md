# Ye Adds AT&T Stadium Show to 2026 Stadium Tour

## Summary

Ye added an AT&T Stadium show in Arlington, Texas on November 7 to his 2026 stadium tour. Presale begins September 8, with general public onsale September 10 through SeatGeek.

### Key Points
- Show is part of a broader stadium run including US, Russia, and Indonesia dates.
- Follows an October 31 Houston show and precedes a November 21 Glendale, AZ date.
- Tickets sold via SeatGeek for general public onsale.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ye-Dallas.webp)

Ye will bring his 2026 stadium tour to North Texas this fall, with a performance scheduled for Saturday, November 7, at AT&T Stadium in Arlington.

The concert will be one of several stadium dates on Ye’s current touring schedule, which includes shows in the United States, Russia and Indonesia. Details about supporting performers and the show’s start time have not been announced.

A ticket presale will begin Tuesday, September 8, at 10 a.m. CT. Tickets will go on sale to the general public Thursday, September 10, at 10 a.m. CT through SeatGeek.

The Arlington stop is scheduled one week after Ye performs at Reliant Stadium in Houston on October 31. He will follow the AT&T Stadium concert with a November 21 appearance at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona.

Ye’s 2026 itinerary also includes two concerts at Soldier Field in Chicago on September 3 and September 4. The international portion of the schedule features two shows at Gazprom Arena in Russia on October 10 and October 11, followed by an October 24 concert at Gelora Bung Karno Stadium in Jakarta, Indonesia.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit Ye’s [official tour website](https://tour.yeezy.com/).
