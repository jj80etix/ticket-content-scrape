# Papa Roach Adds 2027 ‘Rise Of The Roach’ US Tour, Joined by Ice Nine Kills, Ninth Key

## Summary

Papa Roach's Pollstar announcement covers the same 30-date 2027 U.S. "Rise of the Roach" tour with Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key, noting the band spent much of 2026 touring Europe and playing U.S. festival dates like BottleRock Napa Valley and Rocklahoma.

### Key Points
- 30 U.S. dates, May-June 2027, with Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key.
- 2026 activity: European arena dates (UK, Ireland, Germany, Belgium) plus U.S. festivals.
- Spotify Reserved presale Oct. 20, artist presale Oct. 20 noon, general onsale Oct. 23.
- $1 per ticket donated to American Foundation for Suicide Prevention.
- Represented by THE·TEAM; managed by 10th Street Entertainment.

## Transcript

PR I9K ADMAT FEED

Papa Roach has announced a lengthy 2027 US run as part of its global infestation known as the “Rise of the Roach Tour.”

The 30-date across the United States follows up on 2025’s 34-stop of the same title, promising “an unforgettable night of high energy rock and metal spectacle to fans nationwide.” Special guests joining the tour are Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key.

“We can’t wait to get out there and infest America again,” Papa Roach frontman Jacoby Shaddix says in the announcement. “Bringing our horrifyingly entertaining friends Ice Nine Kills is overdue, and we’re excited to introduce our fans to Ninth Key, a band we’re big fans of. These shows are going to be unforgettable”

Kicking off in May at Mississippi Coast Coliseum, the tour will hit amphitheaters and arenas across the country, with notable stops including Barclays Center in Brooklyn, Pine Knob Music Theatre near Detroit, Moody Center in Austin, Toyota Arena in Southern California and Golden 1 Center in the band’s hometown-area Sacramento.

Tickets will be available starting with Spotify Reserved presale on Tuesday, Oct. 20 at 10 a.m. local time followed by the artist presales on Tuesday, Oct. 20, at 12 p.m. local time. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general on sale on Friday, Oct. 23 at 10 a.m. local time. One dollar from every ticket sold is to be donated to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention (AFSP),

The band has spent much of 2026 in Europe, with upcoming arena dates in the UK, Ireland, Germany, Belgium and many other markets, although played some U.S. festival dates this year as well, such as BottleRock Napa Valley in California and Rocklahoma in Pryor, Oklahoma.

The band is represented by THE·TEAM worldwide and managed by 10th Street Entertainment.
