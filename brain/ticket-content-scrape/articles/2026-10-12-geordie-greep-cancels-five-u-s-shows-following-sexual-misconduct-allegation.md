# Geordie Greep Cancels Five U.S. Shows Following Sexual Misconduct Allegation

## Summary

Five North American tour dates for Geordie Greep (former Black Midi frontman) have been canceled following a rape allegation that surfaced during his October 8 Vancouver show, which he publicly denied from the stage. The canceled shows span Boise, Salt Lake City, Boulder, Denver, and Omaha between Oct. 9–14, with no separate statement issued explaining the cancellations beyond the initial denial.

### Key Points
- Five U.S. tour dates canceled (Boise, Salt Lake City, Boulder, Denver, Omaha), Oct. 9–14.
- Cancellations followed a sexual misconduct (rape) allegation made public after an Oct. 8 Vancouver performance.
- Greep denied the allegation on stage but has not separately addressed the cancellations.
- Number of affected shows grew over the weekend following the allegation.

## Greep Addresses Allegation During Vancouver Concert

The controversy began at Greep’s October 8 performance at The Pearl in Vancouver, where an individual distributed flyers accusing the musician of rape.

The flyers alleged that an incident had occurred earlier that morning and encouraged concertgoers to confront Greep about the accusation.

Greep eventually appeared onstage without his accompanying musicians and addressed the situation directly before beginning his performance.

According to a [recording of his remarks published by Stereogum](https://stereogum.com/2514031/geordie-greep-addresses-rape-allegation-made-on-flyers-at-vancouver-show/news?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Greep described an encounter at a house party after spending the previous evening visiting bars with members of his touring group.

He acknowledged making an advance toward someone at the gathering but said he had misunderstood the situation and quickly apologized when the advance was not welcomed.

Greep described the encounter as a misunderstanding, expressed regret for making someone uncomfortable and explicitly denied the allegation of rape.

“I would never rape a woman. I’m not a rapist,” he told the audience.

The musician’s account has not been independently corroborated, and the underlying circumstances of the allegation remain unverified in public reporting.

Greep also addressed the absence of his backing musicians, explaining that he did not want to place them in the position of having to perform amid the controversy.

He told concertgoers they were free to leave and could request refunds if they no longer wished to attend. Greep also pledged to donate the proceeds from the Vancouver performance to an organization supporting victims of violence in British Columbia.

Rather than canceling the concert, he proceeded with a solo performance, reportedly playing 10 songs without his touring band.

The cancellations began the following day.

## Ticket Refunds and Remaining Tour Dates

Although five U.S. performances have now been canceled, neither Greep nor the affected venues have publicly provided a comprehensive explanation of the decisions.

The timing follows the allegations made in Vancouver, but available cancellation notices do not establish whether the decisions originated with Greep, his representatives, individual promoters or other parties involved in the tour.

For ticket holders, the immediate concern is obtaining refunds and determining whether any remaining performances will proceed.

[EVENTIM’s cancellation notice](https://www.eventim.com/eventseries/geordie-greep-canceled-4193667/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for the Omaha concert states that refunds will automatically be issued to the original payment card. The company advises that refunds may take five to seven business days to appear.

Customers who purchased tickets with cash at the box office can request refunds through that location.

Ticket holders for the other canceled performances should consult their original points of purchase for refund instructions, as procedures may vary by ticketing provider.

Greep, who rose to prominence as the frontman of British experimental rock group Black Midi, has been touring behind his 2024 solo debut, *The New Sound*. His North American itinerary included multiple West Coast performances before the Vancouver incident.

His upcoming European performances have not been publicly canceled. A November 1 appearance at [Doornroosje in Nijmegen, Netherlands](https://www.doornroosje.nl/en/event/geordie-greep-knobil/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), remains advertised, with additional European dates scheduled later in November.

The immediate uncertainty surrounds the October 15 Des Moines performance, which remains listed for purchase despite the cancellation of the preceding five concerts.

Fans holding tickets for that event should monitor official communications from First Fleet Concerts, Wooly’s and AXS before making travel arrangements.
