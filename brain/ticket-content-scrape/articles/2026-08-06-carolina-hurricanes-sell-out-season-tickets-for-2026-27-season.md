# Carolina Hurricanes Sell Out Season Tickets for 2026-27 Season

## Summary

The Carolina Hurricanes have sold out every season-ticket tier (full, half, and quarter-season) for the 2026-27 NHL season ahead of their September 29 opener, extending a sellout streak to 168 consecutive regular-season and playoff home games dating back to February 2023. The sellout follows the team's 2025-26 Stanley Cup championship run. A limited allotment of individual-game tickets goes on sale August 20.

### Key Points
- All three season-ticket plan levels (full-season, half-season, quarter-season) sold out following this week's seat-selection process.
- Sellout streak now stands at 168 consecutive regular-season and playoff home games.
- Individual single-game tickets go on sale August 20, per the team.
- Chief ticketing officer Sara Danie credits fan support during the team's Stanley Cup run, including watch parties drawing nearly 20,000 fans to Lenovo Center.
- This marks Carolina's second Stanley Cup title, won in the 2025-26 season.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Carolina-Hurricanes.webp)

The Carolina Hurricanes have sold out all season ticket plans for the 2026-27 National Hockey League (NHL) season ahead of the team’s season opener on September 29.

The franchise announced that full-season, half-season and quarter-season ticket plans have all sold out following the completion of this week’s seat selection process. The Hurricanes have also sold out 168 consecutive regular season and playoff home games, a streak dating back to February 2023.

Chief ticketing officer Sara Danie shared, “We all saw the results of our amazing fan support last spring, as the team made its run to the Stanley Cup championship. The loudest house brought it every night – even when the team was playing thousands of miles away, nearly 20,000 people packed the Lenovo Center for watch parties.

“We’re thrilled to reach this milestone and continue to build on the franchise’s success – on and off the ice,” Danie continued.

The team said a limited number of tickets have been reserved for individual game sales. Those tickets will go on sale August 20.

“The team allocates inventory to three different levels of season ticket plans – full-season plans, half-season plans and quarter-season plans,” the Hurricanes said in a statement. “Following the seat selections that conclude at the end of this week, all three levels have officially sold out.”

The sellout follows a successful 2025-26 campaign in which Carolina finished first in the NHL regular season standings before winning the Stanley Cup. The championship marked the second Stanley Cup title in franchise history.
