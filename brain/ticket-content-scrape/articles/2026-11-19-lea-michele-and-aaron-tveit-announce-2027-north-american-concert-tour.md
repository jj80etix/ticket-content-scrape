# Lea Michele and Aaron Tveit Announce 2027 North American Concert Tour

## Summary

Broadway stars Lea Michele and Aaron Tveit announced a 2027 North American concert tour after strong demand for their previously announced Beacon Theatre show, adding dates in Chicago, Detroit, Philadelphia, Washington D.C. and a Feb. 26, 2027 stop at Radio City Music Hall.

### Key Points
- 2027 North American tour announced following demand from their Beacon Theatre (NYC) show.
- New dates include Chicago, Detroit, Philadelphia, Washington D.C., and Radio City Music Hall (NYC) on Feb. 26, 2027.
- Tour follows the pair's prior "Chess" Broadway co-starring run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Lea-Michele-and-Aaron-Tveit-Live-in-Concert.webp)

Broadway stars Lea Michele and Aaron Tveit have announced a 2027 North American concert tour following demand for their previously announced performance at New York City’s Beacon Theatre.

“The response to our show at The Beacon Theatre has been nothing short of incredible, and we’re beyond grateful for the enthusiasm and support from our fans,” Michele and Tveit said in a joint statement.

“We have had so much fun in rehearsals and are thrilled to extend the tour and bring even more music and unforgettable moments to audiences around the country. We can’t wait to get out there and continue to share this experience with everyone!”

The upcoming tour will bring the former “Chess” co-stars to several major cities, including Chicago, Detroit, Philadelphia and Washington, D.C. The newly announced dates also include a February 26, 2027, performance at Radio City Music Hall in New York City.

The concert series, titled “Lea Michele and Aaron Tveit: Live in Concert,” will feature a combination of Broadway favorites and pop songs drawn from the performers’ respective careers. The shows will include solo performances, duets and opportunities for Michele and Tveit to exchange songs from one another’s musical repertoires.

In addition to live music, the production will feature personal stories from their experiences in theater and television, along with appearances by surprise guests.

A ticket presale for the newly announced dates begins November 19 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general public on-sale November 20 at 10 a.m. local time. Additional tour dates, venues and ticket information are available through the performers’ [official tour website](https://leaandaaron.com/).
