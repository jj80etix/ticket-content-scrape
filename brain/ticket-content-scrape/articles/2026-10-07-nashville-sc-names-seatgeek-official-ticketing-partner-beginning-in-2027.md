# Nashville SC Names SeatGeek Official Ticketing Partner Beginning in 2027

## Summary

Nashville SC has named SeatGeek its official ticketing and "intelligence" partner starting in 2027, covering both the MLS club at GEODIS Park and its MLS NEXT Pro affiliate Huntsville City FC. The deal gives SeatGeek a unified ticketing/data platform across matches and concerts, with fans gaining access to SeatGeek's conversational-AI search tool "Ask SeatGeek," its Deal Score price-comparison feature, seat-view previews, and self-service sell/transfer tools. It extends SeatGeek's existing Nashville footprint (Titans, Nissan Stadium, CMA Fest) and broader MLS/NWSL/USL/Premier League portfolio.

### Key Points
- Multi-year deal begins 2027, covering Nashville SC (GEODIS Park) and Huntsville City FC (Wicks Family Field).
- SeatGeek positioned as "intelligence partner" providing fan data/insights alongside ticketing.
- Fan-facing AI features: "Ask SeatGeek" conversational search and "Deal Score" buyer price-comparison tool.
- Includes seat-view previews, in-platform ticket resale/transfer, and mobile matchday entry.
- Adds to SeatGeek's existing portfolio of MLS, NWSL, USL and Premier League clubs.

### Highlights
- SeatGeek is bundling AI-driven discovery (conversational search) and price-transparency tooling (Deal Score) directly into a primary ticketing deal, not as a bolt-on — a concrete Tier 2 competitor AI deployment to benchmark against.
- The "intelligence partner" framing signals SeatGeek is selling data/analytics capability, not just ticketing infrastructure, as part of its core sports-team pitch.
- Self-service sell/transfer tools are bundled into the primary ticketing contract rather than left to a separate resale product.

### Proposed Solution
SeatGeek's answer is to pair ticketing with conversational AI search and a price-comparison feature as standard parts of a venue/team contract. Etix should treat this as a build/improve signal for its own AI roadmap (an Etix Assistant-style conversational ticket search, plus a transparent price/value indicator for resale or dynamic-priced inventory) and watch whether "intelligence partner" positioning becomes a standard ask from prospective clients evaluating ticketing RFPs.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Seat-Geek-Nashville-SC.webp)

[Nashville Soccer Club](https://www.nashvillesc.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) has selected SeatGeek as its official ticketing partner under a multi-year agreement that will begin in 2027 and span the club’s soccer and live entertainment operations.

Under the partnership, [SeatGeek](https://seatgeek.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) will power ticketing for Nashville SC at GEODIS Park as well as Huntsville City Football Club, the organization’s MLS NEXT Pro affiliate, at Wicks Family Field at Joe Davis Stadium.

The agreement will give Nashville SC a unified ticketing platform and data foundation across matches, concerts and other events. SeatGeek will also serve as what the companies described as an “intelligence partner,” providing technology, data and fan insights intended to help the club manage sales and operations while developing its relationships with fans.

“As we head into our sixth season at GEODIS Park, we are thrilled to partner with SeatGeek to continue providing fans with experiences that are unmatched for soccer, concerts and more,” Nashville SC Chief Business Officer Lindsey Paola said. “Their forward-thinking technology and integrations will help our guests enjoy a seamless experience during their visit to the largest soccer-specific stadium in the United States and Canada.”

For fans, the partnership will move ticket discovery, purchasing and management into SeatGeek’s platform. The company said customers will be able to use Ask SeatGeek, its conversational AI-based search feature, to look for tickets, while its Deal Score tool will allow buyers to compare options.

SeatGeek will also provide seat-view previews and tools allowing ticket holders to sell or transfer tickets through its platform. Mobile ticketing will be used for admission on matchday.

The agreement adds another soccer organization to SeatGeek’s ticketing portfolio. The company works with multiple clubs across MLS, the NWSL and USL, as well as teams in England’s Premier League.

The Nashville SC partnership also expands SeatGeek’s existing presence in the Nashville live entertainment market. The company’s portfolio there already includes the Tennessee Titans, Nissan Stadium and CMA Fest.

“Nashville is one of the most dynamic live event markets in the country, and Nashville SC has built a fan base and ambitions that go well beyond 90 minutes on the pitch,” said Robyn Theodorakis, SeatGeek’s director of business development and sales.

“Together, we’ll build on that momentum, growing the club’s business with the right tools and insights while making every step easier for fans, from finding the right seat to walking through the gates on matchday.”

Nashville SC plays at GEODIS Park, while Huntsville City FC plays its home matches at Wicks Family Field at Joe Davis Stadium. The new SeatGeek arrangement is scheduled to cover both organizations beginning in 2027.
