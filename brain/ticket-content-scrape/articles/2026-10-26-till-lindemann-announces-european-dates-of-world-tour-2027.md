# Till Lindemann Announces European Dates Of ‘World Tour 2027’

## Summary

Rammstein frontman Till Lindemann has announced a solo "World Tour 2027," kicking off in spring 2027 with a European leg of 33 shows across 22 countries starting in October. The tour follows his 2025 "Meine Welt" solo tour, which sold more than 200,000 tickets in Europe, and a 2026 Australian run and self-titled festival.

### Key Points
- "World Tour 2027" comprises 50+ concerts, growing from 18 to 25 to 33 European shows across three successive tour cycles.
- Prior "Meine Welt" tour (2025) sold 200,000+ tickets in Europe; strong Pollstar Boxoffice grosses cited for individual dates.
- German, Austrian, and Swiss tickets go on sale Oct. 28, 2026, via till-lindemann.myticket.de.
- Non-European dates to be announced later.

## Transcript

![Till Lindemann 2 2027 Credit Jens Koch](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Till-Lindemann_2_-2027-Credit-Jens-Koch-scaled.jpg)

Till Lindemann is going back on tour in 2027. Picture by Jens Koch

Rammstein frontman [Till Lindemann](https://pollstar.com/artists/till-lindemann-780682) has announced his solo “World Tour 2027”, kicking off in spring 2027, and coming to Europe the following October, visiting countries including Germany, Finland, the Netherlands, UK, Switzerland, Italy, Turkey and more.

The tour follows Lindemann’s “Meine Welt” solo tour in 2025, which sold more than 200,000 tickets in Europe. Some highlights reported Pollstar Boxoffice include a sold out Romexpo convention center in Bucharest, Romania, Dec. 4, 2025, where he moved 6,531 tickets at a $549,358 gross; or 12,981 tickets sold for his Nov. 29 performance at Wiener Stadthalle in Vienna, Austria, grossing $1,164,893.

In the beginning of 2026, Lindemann ventured to Australia for the first time as a solo artist, and did reasonably well, opening the short run with 2,127 tickets sold and $206,318 grossed at Brisbane’s Fortitude Music Hall, and closing it with 1,596 ticket sold and $157,879 grossed at Metro City in Northbridge. The year was capped off by his own festival [Till Fest, July 3-4, 2026, which sold 25,000 tickets](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/07/09/rammstein-frontman-draws-25000-to-till-fest-germany/) according to promoter handwerker promotions (which just merged fully with DEAG).

The new “World Tour 2027” will comprise more than 50 concerts, and kick off in the spring of 2027, starting on other continents, before traveling through 22 European countries for 33 shows.

Fred Handwerker, Managing Director of Handwerker Promotion e GmbH and promoter of the European tour, said, “This is the third time we’ve been in charge of Till Lindemann’s entire European tour – and each edition has surpassed the last: We started with 18 concerts, grew to 25, and now have 33 venues in 22 countries! The audience’s enthusiasm is palpable every time – the crowd is electrified, and the energy Till generates on stage is simply one-of-a-kind. We’re really looking forward to the ‘World Tour 2027’ – our biggest tour with Till to date.”

Tickets for the German, Austrian and Swiss dates of Till Lindemann’s “World Tour 2027” go on sale Oct. 28, 2026, at 4 p.m. exclusively at till-lindemann.myticket.de.

Dates outside of Europe will be announced at a later date.
