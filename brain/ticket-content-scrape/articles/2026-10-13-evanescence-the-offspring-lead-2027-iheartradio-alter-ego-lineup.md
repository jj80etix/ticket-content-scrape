# Evanescence, The Offspring Lead 2027 iHeartRadio ALTer EGO Lineup

## Summary

Evanescence, The Offspring, and Billy Corgan will headline the 2027 iHeartRadio ALTer EGO festival on January 16 at the Kia Forum in Inglewood. The lineup also includes Death Cab for Cutie, Modest Mouse, and Young the Giant, with Capital One presenting and tickets going on general sale October 23.

### Key Points
- iHeartRadio ALTer EGO set for Jan. 16, 2027 at Kia Forum, Inglewood.
- Headliners: Evanescence, The Offspring; Billy Corgan (Smashing Pumpkins) solo set.
- Full bill includes Death Cab for Cutie, Slightly Stoopid, Modest Mouse, Young the Giant.
- Capital One cardholder presale runs Oct. 20-22; general onsale Oct. 23 via Ticketmaster.
- Separate 24-hour ALT 98.7 VIP sale begins Oct. 22.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/iHeartRadio-ALTer-EGO-Fetsival.webp)

Evanescence, The Offspring and Billy Corgan will perform at iHeartRadio ALTer EGO on January 16, 2027, at the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California.

The lineup also includes Death Cab for Cutie, Slightly Stoopid, Modest Mouse, Young the Giant, The Warning and Temper City. Corgan, frontman of the Smashing Pumpkins, is scheduled for a special solo performance. Capital One will serve as the event’s presenting sponsor.

“This year’s iHeartRadio ALTer EGO delivers a diverse lineup spanning all genres of Alternative and Rock music,” said Lisa Worden, program director for ALT 98.7 and senior vice president of rock and alternative for iHeartMedia.

Woody, host of ALT 98.7’s nationally syndicated “The Woody Show,” will host the concert.

Tickets go on sale to the general public October 23 at noon Pacific through Ticketmaster. Eligible Capital One cardholders can purchase tickets during a presale beginning October 20 at 10 a.m. Pacific and ending October 22 at 10 a.m. Pacific.

A separate ALT 98.7 VIP sale begins October 22 at noon Pacific. That window runs for 24 hours, ending when the general sale opens the following day. Fans can register through the station to participate.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit the official [ALTer EGO Festival website](https://www.iheart.com/alter-ego/).
