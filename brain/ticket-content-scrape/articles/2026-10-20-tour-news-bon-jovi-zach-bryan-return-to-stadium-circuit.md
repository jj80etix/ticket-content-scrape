# Tour news: Bon Jovi & Zach Bryan return to stadium circuit

## Summary

A touring-news roundup led by Bon Jovi's seven new 2027 Forever Tour dates (Australia, London's BST Hyde Park) and Zach Bryan's return to the stadium circuit, plus briefer mentions of Calvin Harris's new North American trek, Kenshi Yonezu's expanded tour, and anniversary runs from Iron Maiden, Toto and Matchbox Twenty.

### Key Points
- Bon Jovi adds 7 new 2027 Forever Tour dates: Melbourne (Mar. 4), Sydney (Mar. 11), London's BST Hyde Park (Jun. 24), among others.
- Zach Bryan returns to the stadium touring circuit in 2027.
- Also notes: a Calvin Harris North American tour, Kenshi Yonezu's tour expansion, and anniversary tour runs from Iron Maiden, Toto, and Matchbox Twenty.
