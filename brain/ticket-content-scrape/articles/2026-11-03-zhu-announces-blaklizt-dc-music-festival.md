# ZHU Announces BLAKLIZT.DC Music Festival

## Summary

Electronic artist ZHU has announced his inaugural BLAKLIZT.DC festival, a one-city-per-year rotating event, with the first edition set for Dec 18-19 in Washington D.C. The three-stage event extends the project around his March album *BLACK MIDAS* and features artists with personal ties to ZHU.

### Key Points
- First edition at BERHTA in D.C., Dec 18-19, across three stages.
- Lineup includes Dansyn, Fahlberg, HNTR, Joezi, KamJam, Kotiér, Nico De Andrea, Parallelle, Riche and TOBEHONEST.
- Tickets go on sale Nov 5 via DICE.
- Framed by ZHU as an "underground warehouse party" counter to mainstream high-production events.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed.png)

ZHU has announced details for his inaugural BLAKLIZT.DC music festival in Washington, D.C., with the electronic music artist planning to host the event in a different city once a year.  
  
The first fest is set for Dec. 18-19 at BERHTA in D.C. Featuring performances across three stages, the lineup includes Dansyn, Fahlberg, HNTR, Joezi, KamJam, Kotiēr, Nico De Andrea, Parallelle, Riche, and TOBEHONEST.  
  
The festival is described as expanding the project and success of his latest album, *BLACK MIDAS,* which was released in March. Today’s announcement notes that “the lineup was curated with a focus on artists who have unique connections to ZHU through shared stages and collaborations.”

“It’s the message of BLACK MIDAS,” ZHU said in a statement. “Putting a focus solely on the music, counterplaying mainstream and high-production events – BLACKLIZT D.C. is a return to the dancefloor inspired by early underground warehouse parties with a club feel in its DNA.”
