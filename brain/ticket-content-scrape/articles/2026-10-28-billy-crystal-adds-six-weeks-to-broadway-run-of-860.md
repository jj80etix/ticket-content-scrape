# Billy Crystal Adds Six Weeks to Broadway Run of ‘860’

## Summary

Billy Crystal's autobiographical solo show "860" is extending its Broadway run six weeks, through Feb. 14, 2027, at the Imperial Theatre, after opening Oct. 21. The show follows Crystal's earlier solo hit "700 Sundays" and explores his family home lost in the 2025 Palisades Fire.

### Key Points
- "860" extended through Feb. 14, 2027 (previously set to close Jan. 3).
- Previews began Oct. 1; opened Oct. 21 at the Imperial Theatre.
- Follows Crystal's Tony-winning solo show "700 Sundays" (2004) and the musical "Mr. Saturday Night" (2022).
- Produced by Janice Crystal, James L. Nederlander, Larry Magid and Face Productions.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Billy-Crystal-860.webp)

Billy Crystal’s autobiographical solo show “860” will remain on Broadway for an additional six weeks, extending its engagement at the Imperial Theatre through February 14, 2027.

The production, which began previews October 1 and officially opened October 21, had previously been scheduled to close January 3, 2027.

“860” takes its name from the address of Crystal’s longtime California home, which was destroyed in the 2025 Palisades Fire. Crystal and his family lived there for 46 years. The show revisits memories of that home, bringing together stories from his career and family life while exploring how loved ones help each other through loss.

The production marks another autobiographical Broadway outing for Crystal, who made his debut with “700 Sundays” in 2004. That solo show examined his relationship with his father, who died when Crystal was 15. Its title referred to the number of Sundays they shared.

“700 Sundays” earned Crystal the Tony Award for Best Special Theatrical Event and returned to Broadway for an encore engagement in 2013.

Crystal later starred in the 2022 Broadway musical “Mr. Saturday Night,” adapted from his 1992 film. He reprised his role as comedian Buddy Young Jr. and co-wrote the musical’s book. The production brought him Tony nominations for his leading performance and the book.

“860” is produced by Janice Crystal, James L. Nederlander, Larry Magid and Face Productions.
