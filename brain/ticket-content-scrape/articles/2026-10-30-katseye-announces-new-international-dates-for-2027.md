# KATSEYE Announces New International Dates for 2027

## Summary

KATSEYE added 11 new international dates to its Wildworld Tour, extending into Asia, Australia, and New Zealand in early 2027, while the group is still mid-way through its first North American arena tour.

### Key Points
- New leg runs January 27 (Yokohama) through March 5 (Auckland), with stops including Manila, Hong Kong, Seoul, Cebu, Singapore, Bangkok, and Taipei
- Current North American tour continues with Detroit, Chicago, Austin, Las Vegas, and Los Angeles dates, plus Jingle Ball (Fort Worth) and Lollapalooza India (Mumbai) appearances

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/KAYSEYE-World-Tour.webp)

KATSEYE has added 11 shows to its Wildworld Tour, bringing the international girl group to Asia, Australia and New Zealand in early 2027.

The new leg begins January 27 at Yokohama Arena in Yokohama, Japan, before heading to Manila, Hong Kong, Seoul, Cebu, Singapore, Bangkok and Taipei. The itinerary includes two stops in the Philippines, with performances in both Manila and Cebu.

After a 10-day break, the group will resume the tour with concerts in Melbourne and Sydney before wrapping up March 5 at Spark Arena in Auckland, New Zealand.

KATSEYE is currently on its first North American arena tour, with upcoming stops in Detroit, Chicago, Austin, Las Vegas and Los Angeles, among other cities. The group will also perform at Jingle Ball in Fort Worth, Texas, on December 1 and Lollapalooza India in Mumbai in January.
