# Latin Music Icon Luis Miguel Teases 2027 Tour

## Summary

Latin music icon Luis Miguel teased a "Tour 2027" via social media, with no further details revealed. His prior comeback run (2023-2025), promoted by Cárdenas Marketing Network, was one of the most successful Latin tours ever: 186 shows in 16 months grossing $415.8 million from 2.86 million tickets per Pollstar Boxoffice. Miguel is booked worldwide by WME.

### Key Points
- Only confirmation so far: a social-media post reading "Luis Miguel Tour 2027," no dates or markets disclosed.
- Previous tour (2023-2025): 186 shows, 16 months, $415,849,128 gross, 2,861,491 tickets sold (Pollstar Boxoffice).
- Miguel has released no new music and made few public appearances in nearly a decade, fueling demand.
- Booked worldwide by WME.

## Transcript

Latin music fans won’t have to wait long for Luis Miguel to make his way back to venues across the globe. The iconic singer recently posted an image of himself on social media with the words “Luis Miguel Tour 2027.”

![Luis Miguel Triumphs In His Concert At The Santiago Bernabeu](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/GettyImages-2160932636-scaled-1.jpg)

Luis Miguel performs during his concert at the Santiago Bernabéu on July 6, 2024, in Madrid, Spain. The Puerto Rican-born Mexican singer is having a successful tour of Spain. (Photo By Francisco Guerra/Europa Press via Getty Images)

No other details were revealed, but it’s more than enough to rile fans, no matter what generation. Miguel, a Puerto Rican-born crooner raised in Mexico, is one of the most beloved figures in Latin music, a modern Frank Sinatra whose charm and talent remain intact despite not making many public appearances or releasing any new music in nearly a decade.

“Because he’s an idol. People love him,” Cárdenas Marketing Network CEO Henry Cárdenas told *Pollstar* in 2024. “The other thing is that he doesn’t go out every year, and people were hungry to see him.”

**[El Sol De México Brilla: Luis Miguel Makes Historic Return To Live](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/12/16/el-sol-de-mexico-brilla-luis-miguel-makes-historic-return-to-live/)**

Cárdenas promoted Miguel’s return to live in 2023—his first trek since 2019—and it became one of the most successful Latin tours ever. The celebrated singer performed in arenas and stadiums across North and South America and Europe, playing 186 shows in 16 months and grossing $415,849,128 from 2,861,491 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

It is unclear who is involved in Miguel’s 2027 tour, but it will likely finish among the top tours on *Pollstar* ’s charts.

Miguel is booked worldwide by WME.
