# Spotify augments live activity in US with Live Nation Urban event

## Summary

Spotify, in partnership with Live Nation Urban, is co-hosting RNB X Live: Dallas, a curated R&B festival featuring Erykah Badu, H.E.R., and others — the latest step in Spotify's expansion into live events following its "Spotify Reserved" tour-ticket programme.

### Key Points
- One-day R&B festival at Dallas' 8,000-cap The Pavilion at Toyota Music Factory, tied to Spotify's RNB X editorial playlist.
- Lineup includes Erykah Badu, H.E.R., kwn, Destin Conrad, Jai'len Josey, and rjtheweirdo.
- Presented in partnership with Live Nation Urban.
- Follows Spotify's "Reserved" tour-ticket programme as part of a broader push into the live-events domain.

### Highlights
- Spotify continues moving beyond streaming into curated, ticketed live events tied to its editorial brands.
- Partnership with Live Nation Urban gives Spotify production/promotion muscle for the format.
- Builds directly on the "Spotify Reserved" ticketing feature launched earlier.

### Industry Problem
A subscription-streaming platform is increasingly blurring the line between content curation and direct ticketed live experiences, signaling that adjacent tech/media platforms see fan-relationship and ticketing access as a growth lever — a competitive dynamic beyond the traditional ticketing-vendor set.

### Proposed Solution
No solution proposed by the article; worth watching whether Spotify's live push (and similar moves by other platforms) evolves into direct ticketing competition or data/fan-relationship encroachment, and whether it points to opportunity in loyalty/membership-tied ticket access as a differentiator.
