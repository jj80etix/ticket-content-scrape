# The Neighbourhood Announce ‘The Wourld Tour II’ Building On Sold-Out Arena Run

## Summary

Pollstar reports The Neighbourhood's newly announced "The Wourld Tour II" 2027 arena dates, beginning March 2 in Denver, as the band continues its sold-out current "Wourld Tour" (including two Barclays Center shows) into December. New markets include Houston, Dallas, Montreal, Boston and multiple Canadian and European stops into summer 2027.

### Key Points
- New 2027 dates kick off March 2 in Denver; current 2026 tour runs through early December with sold-out Barclays Center shows.
- New markets include Houston, Dallas, Montreal, Boston, Indianapolis and several Canadian cities, plus European dates into summer.
- Band represented by Marty Diamond and Ash Mowry at The Team.
- Guitarist Mike Margott described the band's post-hiatus fan-base growth as "unforeseen."

## Transcript

![WT2 Dates](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WT2_Dates-scaled.jpg)

The Neighbourhood have announced “The Wourld Tour II,” with additional arena shows confirmed starting in March 2027 and kicking off March 2 in Denver.

The announcement of these additional shows comes as the band head into the remaining dates of their current global, sold-out “The Wourld Tour,” with dates at major arenas like State Farm Arena in Atlanta, United Center in Chicago, two dates at Barclays Center in New York and others into early December.

New dates hit markets including Houston, Dallas, Montreal, Boston, Indianapolis, multiple Canadian cities and Europe into the summer. See below for full listing.

[As told in a recent *Pollstar* cover feature, the blockbuster tour](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/08/a-glorious-resurgence-the-neighbourhood-return-with-massive-arena-tour-playing-to-new-legions-of-fans-across-the-globe-cover/) is a dream a group of kids from Newbury Park, California couldn’t have imagined, with dates including a sold-out Madison Square Garden. It came after a hiatus that had many thinking the band would never get back together.

“The hiatus was an interesting time because of so much going on and so much in the air that it kind of felt like, ‘Who knows where we’ll be as a career when we get back together?’” assist Mike Margott told *Pollstar*. “We always knew we wanted to get back together because we’re best friends, grew up together, and we knew that would happen. But the rise in our fan base during that time was obviously unforeseen, and we’re just incredibly grateful.”

The California-based band is represented by Marty Diamond and Ash Mowry at The Team.
