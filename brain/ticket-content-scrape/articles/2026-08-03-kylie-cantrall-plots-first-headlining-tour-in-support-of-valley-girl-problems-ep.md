# Kylie Cantrall Plots First Headlining Tour in Support of ‘Valley Girl Problems’ EP

## Summary

Pop singer and actress Kylie Cantrall announced her first headlining tour, "The Valley Girl Problems Tour," a 15-city North American run from September 25 to October 22, 2026, in support of her new EP. The tour follows strong early streaming numbers for the EP and continued visibility from her role in Disney's Descendants franchise.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Sept. 25 at The Fillmore Minneapolis and closes Oct. 22 at The Novo in Los Angeles, spanning 15 cities including Chicago, Toronto, New York, Atlanta, and San Francisco.
- The nine-track "Valley Girl Problems" EP generated more than 2.6 million streams in its first five days and debuted in Spotify's US Top 10 Album Chart.
- Cantrall plays Red in Disney's Descendants franchise; the latest installment, "Descendants: Wicked Wonderland," was the top Disney+ movie globally as of July 19, leading charts in 71 countries.
- Tickets are available via kyliecantrall.com.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/kylie-cantrall.jpg)

Kylie Cantrall is stepping into the spotlight with her first-ever headlining tour.

The singer and actress will launch “The Valley Girl Problems Tour” on Sept. 25 at The Fillmore Minneapolis, beginning a 15-city North American run that concludes Oct. 22 at The Novo in Los Angeles. The itinerary also includes stops at Chicago’s Riviera Theatre, New York’s Terminal 5, Atlanta’s The Eastern, San Francisco’s The Warfield and Toronto’s Queen Elizabeth Theatre.

“I’ve been waiting to bring this project to life onstage and create an epic show where my fans can experience the entire world of *Valley Girl Problems* with me,” Cantrall said in a press release. “This music is so personal, and I can’t wait to sing these songs together every night.”

The tour follows the release of Cantrall’s nine-track *Valley Girl Problems* EP, which generated more than 2.6 million streams during its first five days and debuted inside the Top 10 of Spotify’s U.S. Album Charts.
