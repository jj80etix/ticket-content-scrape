# Co-op Live Unifies Hospitality & Finance Operations

## Summary

Manchester's Co-op Live is unifying food-and-beverage and financial operations on Oracle Cloud, pairing Oracle Simphony for point-of-sale/ordering data with Oracle NetSuite for financial management. The venue says the integration will simplify reconciliation and give operations and finance teams a shared view of performance.

### Key Points
- Oracle Simphony unifies ordering, payments and operational data across bars, concessions, premium spaces and self-service kiosks.
- Oracle NetSuite handles financial management, reconciliation, budgeting and forecasting.
- Venue leadership frames the move as supporting "best-in-class technology behind every aspect of the fan experience."

## Transcript

![Co op Live Hero Image (1)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Co-op-Live-Hero-Image-1.png)

Co-op Live in Manchester, UK is unifying f&b and finance management ops. Courtesy Co-op Live

Co-op Live announced that it is now using Oracle Cloud applications to bring together food and beverage operations with financial management to help “optimize inventory, simplify business processes, and support continued growth,” according to the press announcement.

There’s Oracle Simphony, which “unifies ordering, payments, and operational data across Co-op Live’s bars, concessions, premium event spaces, and self-service kiosks, providing teams with timely insight into sales performance, product demand, and venue operations,” as well as the financial management software Oracle NetSuite.

By bringing together both operational information and financial management, Co-op Live can “simplify financial reconciliation and improve budgeting and forecasting, giving finance and operations teams a shared view of business performance,” the press release continues.

Comments:

Guy Dunstan, senior vice president and general manager, Co-op Live: “Co-op Live was designed to be one of the world’s most advanced live entertainment venues, and that requires best-in-class technology behind every aspect of the fan experience. Oracle plays a vital role in supporting our operations. By connecting our venue and finance data, our teams have better visibility across the business, helping us make more informed decisions while delivering fast, efficient, and reliable service at every event.”

“Live entertainment venues need to serve thousands of guests during short, intense periods while maintaining the speed, quality, and convenience fans expect,” said Kevin Kimber, senior vice president and general manager, UK and Ireland, Oracle. “By bringing food and beverage operations and financial management together with Oracle Simphony and NetSuite, Co-op Live can improve efficiency, inventory management, and decision-making to further enhance the experience for fans.”
