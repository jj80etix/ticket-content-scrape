# Allison Russell Sets North American Tour in Support of ‘In The Hour of Chaos’

## Summary

Allison Russell announced a North American headline tour supporting her album "In The Hour of Chaos," running October 15 (Asheville, NC) through November 15 (Berkeley, CA), including a Ryman Auditorium stop supporting Neko Case. Tickets are on sale now.

### Key Points
- Allison Russell's North American headline tour runs October 15–November 15.
- Tour follows her summer dates supporting Sarah McLachlan.
- Includes a Nashville Ryman Auditorium appearance supporting Neko Case.
- Tickets currently on sale.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Allison-Russell.webp)

Allison Russell is taking her latest album on the road this fall, announcing a North American headline tour in support of *In The Hour of Chaos*.

The new tour is scheduled to begin October 15 at The Orange Peel in Asheville, North Carolina. Russell will then make stops in cities such as Washington D.C., Boston, New York, Toronto, Chicago, Minneapolis, Seattle and San Francisco before wrapping up on November 15 in Berkeley, California at The UC Theatre.

Notably, the fall trek will also include a performance at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium, where Russell will appear in support of Neko Case.

The upcoming run follows Russell’s summer dates with Sarah McLachlan. Tickets are on sale now. For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Russel’s official website](https://allisonrussellmusic.com/).
