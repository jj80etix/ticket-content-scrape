# Get Moments eyes untapped value in festival fan footage

## Summary

Festival-tech company Get Moments says festivals are leaving significant commercial-sponsorship revenue on the table by not leveraging fan-captured photos and videos, and pitches its platform — which lets fans upload content and auto-generates personalized AI-edited "aftermovies" — as a fix worth "dozens of thousands" in added partnership value. The pitch follows its largest activation yet at Tomorrowland 2026 (full article is paywalled beyond the teaser).

### Key Points
- Platform lets festival attendees upload photos/video to an official event app; AI edits those into personalized aftermovies per attendee.
- Vendor claims festivals can add "dozens of thousands" in commercial-partnership value by monetizing this fan content.
- Largest deployment to date was at Tomorrowland 2026; full details are behind IQ Magazine's paywall.

### Highlights
- Explicit framing: festivals (organizer/client segment) are under-monetizing fan-generated content in sponsorship deals.
- AI is the enabling mechanic — personalized, automatically-edited aftermovies per attendee.

### Industry Problem
Festival organizers have an underused asset (fan-captured photo/video) and lack an easy way to turn it into incremental sponsorship/commercial revenue.

### Proposed Solution
Get Moments' answer is an app-based upload-and-AI-edit pipeline tied to sponsor activations. Worth watching as a template for client-facing (organizer) monetization add-ons, though this item has limited detail beyond the vendor's pitch — not direct enough to warrant a build commitment yet.
