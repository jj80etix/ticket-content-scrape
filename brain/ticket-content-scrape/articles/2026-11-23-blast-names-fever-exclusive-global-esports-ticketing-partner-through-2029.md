# BLAST Names Fever Exclusive Global Esports Ticketing Partner Through 2029

## Summary

Esports organizer BLAST named Fever its exclusive global ticketing partner through 2029, integrating Fever's discovery, purchasing, secure-transfer, access-control and analytics technology across BLAST's international tournaments. The deal extends Fever's rapid expansion across sports ticketing, following recent agreements with X Games, Formula 1, LIV Golf and the Premier Jumping League.

### Highlights
- Multi-year deal (through 2029) covers BLAST's global esports tournaments, including on-site box-office and access-control tech.
- Fever will supply ticket-purchasing analytics and audience data to help BLAST plan demand and events.
- Companies plan added anti-fraud/anti-resale measures, though specifics on resale-price restrictions are unspecified.
- BLAST expects ~40 live arena events across 2027–28, building on 190,000+ tickets sold across 15 shows in 10 countries in 2025.

### Industry Problem
Event organizers outside traditional music/sports venues (esports, showjumping, motorsport) need integrated, fraud-resistant ticketing plus audience analytics without building it themselves — a gap full-service primary platforms like Fever are filling across adjacent live-entertainment verticals.

### Proposed Solution
Fever bundles discovery/marketing, ticketing, access control and analytics into one offering; Etix could watch Fever's cross-vertical analytics and anti-fraud bundling as a benchmark for packaging its own box-office/analytics features for niche-event organizers.

Esports tournament organizer BLAST has named Fever its exclusive global ticketing partner under a multi-year agreement running through 2029, expanding the ticketing platform’s presence in international sports and live entertainment.

The partnership will integrate Fever’s ticketing technology across BLAST’s digital platforms, bringing ticket discovery, purchasing, secure transfers and event access together in one system.

Tickets for BLAST events will also be distributed through Fever’s global sales and discovery channels, giving the esports organizer additional opportunities to reach fans beyond its existing audiences.

The agreement covers more than online ticket sales. Fever will provide on-site ticketing infrastructure, including secure access-control technology and box-office terminals, alongside 24/7 multilingual customer support across its sales channels.

The partnership will also create opportunities for additional purchases at selected events, including merchandise, food and beverages, digital products and other upgrades designed to personalize the event experience.

Fever’s analytics technology will give BLAST access to data on ticket purchasing patterns, audience behavior and attendance, helping the company evaluate demand and plan future events.

## Fever Continues Expansion Across Sports Ticketing

The BLAST agreement adds another major international event organizer to Fever’s expanding sports ticketing portfolio, following several recent partnerships covering traditional and emerging sporting competitions.

In October, [X Games expanded its relationship with Fever through a three-year ticketing agreement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/x-games-fever-ticketing-partnership/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) covering events in the United States and select international markets as the organization develops its MoonPay X Games League.

That agreement includes online and on-site ticketing operations, customer support, audience acquisition and analytics services, with Fever supporting the league’s efforts to attract spectators across multiple markets.

More recently, [Fever was named the official ticketing partner of the Premier Jumping League](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/fever-named-official-ticketing-partner-of-premier-jumping-league/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), a new international showjumping competition preparing for its inaugural season in 2027.

Fever has also secured major ticketing relationships with Formula 1 and LIV Golf, extending its reach across multiple categories of professional sports.

For BLAST, the arrangement comes as the company continues expanding its international esports event portfolio. Earlier this year, the organizer said it expected to stage at least 40 live arena events across 2027 and 2028, building on a 2025 calendar that generated more than 190,000 ticket sales across 15 arena shows in 10 countries.
