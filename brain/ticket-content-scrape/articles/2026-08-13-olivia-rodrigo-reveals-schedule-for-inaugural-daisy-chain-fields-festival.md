# Olivia Rodrigo Reveals Schedule for Inaugural Daisy Chain Fields Festival

## Summary

Olivia Rodrigo has revealed the full schedule for her inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival, an all-women lineup taking place August 29 at Irvine, California's Great Park, with 14 acts across two non-overlapping stages including Mitski, Doechii, Chappell Roan, and Bikini Kill. Net proceeds benefit organizations advancing and advocating for women and girls.

### Key Points
- 14 acts across two stages (Dandelion, Marigold), scheduling designed so no sets overlap.
- Lineup includes Santigold, KATSEYE, Mitski, Bikini Kill, Doechii, The Breeders, Garbage, and Chappell Roan; Rodrigo closes with an 80-minute set featuring Stevie Nicks, Karen O, and Sarah McLachlan.
- 100% of net proceeds go to charities supporting women and girls.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Olivia-Rodrigo-Daisy-Chain-Fields.webp)

Olivia Rodrigo has officially revealed the lineup for her inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival — and fans won’t have to make a difficult decision of which acts to see, as no sets will overlap.

The festival is set to take over Irvine, California’s Great Park on August 29, featuring 14 acts across two stages. Quiet Light will kick-off the festival at 11:20 a.m., followed by Eli, Rachel Chinouriri, Santigold, Die Spitz, and KATSEYE. Acts will alternate between the Dandelion Stage and Marigold Stage, with afternoon performances from Mitski, Bikini Kill, Doechii, The Breeders, and Garbage.

Chappell Roan, Garbage, and Not for Radio fill out the night time slots before Olivia Rodrigo closes out the festival with special guests during an 80-minute set. As previously announced, special guests include Stevie Nicks, Karen O, and Sarah McLachlan.

The festival is intended to bring together women in music, nonprofit organizations and fans for a day centered on creativity, connection and community. Net proceeds from the event will be donated to organizations dedicated to advancing and advocating for women and girls.
