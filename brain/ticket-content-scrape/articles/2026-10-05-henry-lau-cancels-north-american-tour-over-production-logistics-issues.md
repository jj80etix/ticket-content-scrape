# Henry Lau Cancels North American Tour Over Production, Logistics Issues

## Summary

Henry Lau canceled all five dates of the North American leg of his "ENJOY THE SHOW" World Tour, citing unspecified production and logistics challenges; organizers say all tickets will be refunded. The cancellation follows, but organizers say is unrelated to, K-pop star Taemin's recent postponement of his own North American tour over U.S. visa delays.

### Key Points
- Canceled dates: Nov. 4 (LA), Nov. 8 (SF), Nov. 12 (Seattle), Nov. 14 (Toronto), Nov. 16 (NYC).
- No replacement dates announced; full refunds promised through the original point of purchase.
- Organizers explicitly deny any visa/immigration connection to Taemin's situation.

Henry Lau has canceled all five U.S. and Canadian dates on his “ENJOY THE SHOW” World Tour, wiping out a November run through Los Angeles, San Francisco, Seattle, Toronto and New York after organizers cited “unforeseen production and logistical challenges beyond our control.”

The North American leg had been scheduled to begin Nov. 4 at The Novo in Los Angeles and conclude Nov. 16 at Terminal 5 in New York. An [official cancellation notice from Seattle’s McCaw Hall](https://www.mccawhall.com/events/detail/henry-lau-1?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) says the decision was made by the event organizer and that all tickets will be refunded.

Ticket buyers are expected to receive refund instructions through the ticketing platform used for their purchase. Unlike a postponement, organizers have not announced replacement dates for any of the five canceled shows.

The cancellation comes just days after [Taemin postponed the entire North American leg of his LiMiNaL World Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/taemin-postpones-north-american-tour-as-u-s-visa-delays-hit-production-crew/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) because a significant number of key touring and production personnel were still awaiting U.S. visas.

The disruption follows a separate schedule change elsewhere on Lau’s tour. His Nov. 28 Bangkok concert was previously moved to May 1, 2027, with local organizer iMe Thailand citing necessary production adjustments. Buyers of tickets for the original Bangkok date are also receiving refunds because those tickets will not carry over to the rescheduled performance.

The broader ENJOY THE SHOW World Tour has not been canceled. The North American decision applies specifically to the five November performances in the U.S. and Canada.

Lau announced the 2026-27 world tour earlier this year, with dates spanning Asia, Australia and North America. The Canadian singer, songwriter, producer and multi-instrumentalist first gained international recognition as a member of Super Junior-M before developing a solo career combining pop, R&B and classical influences, including violin performances that have become a central part of his live shows.

For North American fans, the biggest distinction between Lau’s situation and Taemin’s is what organizers are promising next. Taemin’s announcement described his dates as postponed and said new North American shows and venues were being arranged. Lau’s dates, by contrast, have been canceled with refunds and no replacement North American itinerary announced.
