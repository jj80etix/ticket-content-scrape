# New Cast Members to Join ‘Mamma Mia!’ North American Tour

## Summary

The North American tour of "Mamma Mia!" is adding new cast members as it continues its 25th-anniversary run, with Kayla Ryan Walsh making her national tour debut as Rosie and Rob Hancock moving from ensemble into the role of Harry Bright.

### Key Points

- Jessica Crouch and Juliette M. Ojeda continue as Donna Sheridan and Sophie Sheridan; principal cast also includes Jalynn Steele, Leland Burnett, Victor Wallace, and Grant Reynolds.
- The 25th-anniversary tour launched in Denver in October 2023, paused for a six-month Broadway engagement at the Winter Garden Theatre, and resumed touring in March 2026.
- Original Broadway production ran 14 years across the Winter Garden and Broadhurst theatres after debuting in London in 1999.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/mamma-mia-1-1.webp)

The North American tour of “Mamma Mia!” is welcoming several new and returning performers as the ABBA musical continues traveling across the United States and Canada.

Kayla Ryan Walsh will make her national Broadway tour debut in the role of Rosie, while Rob Hancock will move from the ensemble into the principal role of Harry Bright. The casting changes were announced as the production continues the latest leg of its 25th anniversary tour.

Hancock joined the “Mamma Mia!” tour earlier this year as a member of the ensemble before taking over the role of Harry.

Jessica Crouch and Juliette M. Ojeda will continue leading the touring company as Donna Sheridan and Sophie Sheridan, respectively. The principal cast also includes Jalynn Steele as Tanya, Leland Burnett as Bill Austin, Victor Wallace as Sam Carmichael and Grant Reynolds as Sky. Tony Clements, Sam Harvey and dance captain Ryan Sander are also returning to the company.

The musical premiered in London in 1999 before opening on Broadway in 2001. Its original Broadway production played for 14 years across the Winter Garden Theatre and Broadhurst Theatre.
