# Fontaines D.C. Set Spring and Fall North American Tour Dates

## Summary

Fontaines D.C. announced a 19-date 2027 North American tour supporting new album Dopamine Chamber, split into spring and fall legs running April through October.

### Key Points
- Spring leg opens April 23 in Boston; fall leg runs Sept. 24-Oct. 15, closing at the Kia Forum.
- Dopamine Chamber releases Oct. 16, 2026.
- Includes a Red Rocks Amphitheatre stop Oct. 13, 2027.
- Presale registration closed Oct. 5; general onsale Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Fontaines-D.C.webp)

Fontaines D.C. will return to North America in 2027 for a 19-date tour supporting their upcoming album, *Dopamine Chamber*.

The Irish band will split the itinerary between spring and fall, opening April 23 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston and closing October 15 at the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California. Westside Cowboy will support the spring dates, while Iceage joins the fall run.

The spring leg includes Philadelphia, Detroit, Chicago, Milwaukee and Minneapolis before heading west for performances in Vancouver, Seattle and Sacramento. That portion concludes May 12 at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium in San Francisco.

Following a summer break from the announced North American schedule, the band will resume September 24 at KEMBA! Live Outdoor in Columbus, Ohio. The fall itinerary includes Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, Forest Hills Stadium in Queens, New York, and Merriweather Post Pavilion in Columbia, Maryland.

October performances will take Fontaines D.C. to Nashville, Asheville and Atlanta before an October 13 appearance at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado. The Kia Forum finale follows two days later. The routing spans theaters, outdoor venues and arenas across the United States and Canada.

*Dopamine Chamber* is scheduled for release October 16, 2026. It follows *Romance*, which arrived in August 2024, and adds another album to a catalog that includes *Dogrel*, *A Hero’s Death* and *Skinty Fia*.

Fans seeking presale access must register by October 5 at 6 p.m. Eastern. The presale begins October 6 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general sale October 9 at 10 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [fontainesdc.com](http://fontainesdc.com).
