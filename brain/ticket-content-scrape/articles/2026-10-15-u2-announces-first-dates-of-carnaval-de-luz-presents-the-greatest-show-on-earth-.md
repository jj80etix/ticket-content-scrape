# U2 Announces First Dates Of ‘Carnaval De Luz Presents: The Greatest Show On Earth’ Tour, To Play Mexico, South America

## Summary

U2 announced the first dates of a separate "Carnaval De Luz Presents: The Greatest Show On Earth" tour leg covering Mexico and South America — companion coverage to the broader 2027 world tour announcement.

### Key Points
- Confirms Mexico and South America dates as part of U2's 2027 touring plans.
- Routine tour-announcement coverage; no pricing/platform angle beyond standard presale mechanics covered elsewhere.

## Transcript

![FINAL TOUR ARTWORK](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/FINAL-TOUR-ARTWORK.jpg)

FINAL TOUR ARTWORK

U2 is set to hit the road again with the announcement of the initial dates of the “Carnaval De Luz presents: The Greatest Show On Earth” tour.

Produced by Live Nation, the run is described as “a world tour” that will see all four original U2 members “return to select stadiums in 2027 and beyond.” Dates run Feb. 23 to March 23, with two shows at Estadio GN Seguros in Mexico City, two shows at Estádio MorumBIS\* in São Paulo, Brazil, and two dates at Estadio River Plate in Buenos Aires.

The announcement states the dates are the only three Latin American cities the tour will visit next year, while additional 2027 dates for Europe and North America are set to be announced in the coming weeks, along with plans for the band’s return to its native Ireland. Beck is listed as support on the initial dates.

Always innovative and known for pushing the boundaries of live performance, the tour is set as U2’s first since its debut residency that opened the game-changing Sphere in Las Vegas, where the band played 40 shows from September 2023 to March 2024 and once again set the bar for live music. Those shows grossed an average of more than $6 million per night, as reported to *Pollstar*.

“The Greatest Show on Earth”? Now that’s a throwdown. It’s a dare, to ourselves as much as anyone, to try and have the best night of anybody’s life.
