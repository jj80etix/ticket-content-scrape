# BIGBANG Extends 20th Anniversary World Tour With SoFi Stadium Show

## Summary

BIGBANG has added a March 13, 2027 concert at SoFi Stadium, extending its 20th-anniversary "XX: COSMOS" world tour and making it the new scheduled finale of the U.S. run. Tickets go on sale Nov. 13 through Ticketmaster, with membership, venue, and VIP presales preceding it.

### Key Points
- SoFi Stadium (Inglewood) show becomes the tour's new last U.S. stop, following earlier Oakland and MetLife Stadium dates.
- Tour reunites G-DRAGON, TAEYANG, and DAESUNG for BIGBANG's first major international tour since 2017.
- General onsale Nov. 13 via Ticketmaster; V.I.P. membership presale Nov. 11, venue presale Nov. 12.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Big-Bang-.webp)

BIGBANG has added a March 13, 2027, concert at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California, extending the K-pop group’s 20th anniversary world tour beyond its previously scheduled February finale.

The newly announced performance will be the final U.S. stop on BIGBANG’s *XX: COSMOS* world tour and will feature G-DRAGON, TAEYANG and DAESUNG, who have reunited for the group’s first major international tour since 2017.

The Los Angeles-area addition follows BIGBANG’s earlier U.S. stadium performances in September, including two concerts at Oakland-Alameda County Coliseum in California and a show at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

When the anniversary tour was initially announced in June, the 31-date stadium run was set to wrap up with two performances at Kaohsiung National Stadium in Taiwan on February 27 and 28, 2027. Now, the newly added SoFi Stadium concert extends the schedule by nearly two weeks and is now currently the scheduled last stop of the tour.

The world tour began with three concerts at Goyang Stadium in South Korea in August and has taken the group across North America, Europe, Asia and Australia. Additional performances are scheduled through the remainder of 2026, including stadium and dome concerts in Thailand, Hong Kong and Japan, before the tour continues into 2027.

The reunion marks a significant return for BIGBANG, which debuted in 2006 and became one of the most influential acts in K-pop’s international expansion. The group performed together at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival earlier this year before beginning its first full-scale world tour in nearly nine years.

The anniversary celebrations have also included new music. BIGBANG released *BiiiG* on August 19, marking its first new single in four years. The song arrived on the 20th anniversary of the group’s debut and reached No. 1 on several South Korean music charts, according to the venue’s announcement.

Tickets for BIGBANG’s March 13 concert will go on sale to the general public Friday, November 13, at 10 a.m. PT, through Ticketmaster.

Fans with a BIGBANG V.I.P Membership will have access to an earlier presale beginning Wednesday, November 11, at 10 a.m. PT. To participate, eligible members must complete the required registration through the group’s [official b.stage platform](https://bigbang.bstage.in/contents/6ae99ccc8d38027a8d6b8bb9?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) by Sunday, November 8, at 9:59 p.m. PT.

Registration requires fans to submit their six-digit membership code, which will also serve as their presale code. YG Entertainment said the membership presale will offer a limited allocation of tickets, with purchases restricted to six tickets per show.

A separate venue presale is scheduled to begin Thursday, November 12, at 10 a.m. PT, while VIP package presales will begin November 11. All sales will take place ahead of the November 13 general onsale.
