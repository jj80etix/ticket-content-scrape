# Oak View Group Appoints Diane Neville As Chief Human Resources Officer

## Summary

Oak View Group has hired Diane Neville as Chief Human Resources Officer, based in Denver and reporting to CEO Chris Granger; she joined Oct. 19 from Alterra Mountain Company, where she was Chief People Officer.

### Key Points
- Neville oversees global HR — talent acquisition, compensation, HR compliance, L&D, and culture — for OVG.
- Previously Chief People Officer at Alterra Mountain Company; earlier roles at Shake Shack, P.F. Chang's, McKesson.
- Joined OVG Oct. 19, 2026; reports to CEO Chris Granger.

Oak View Group has announced the hire of Diane Neville as Chief Human Resources Officer. She will be based in Denver and report directly to Chris Granger, CEO of Oak View Group.

This week’s announcement from Oak View Group (parent company to *Pollstar*) notes that Neville will oversee “all human resources responsibilities globally for the company, including teams covering talent acquisition, employee relations, compensation and benefits, HR compliance, onboarding, learning and development, social impact and cultural engagement and more.” She officially joined OVG on Oct. 19.

“We’re very excited to welcome Diane to OVG and tap into her vast experience leading high-performing teams with a focus on collaboration and culture building,” Granger said in a statement. “There is nothing more important to the success of our organization than nurturing a strong foundation with clear values and a robust, people-first culture. As our number of services, markets and employees continues to expand, Diane and her team will help bolster the critical parts of our organization that allow OVG’s people to grow and thrive.”

Neville most recently served as Chief People Officer at Alterra Mountain Company. Neville has more than 25 years “of experience driving business performance through strategic, people-first practices.” In addition to her work at Alterra Mountain Company, she’s held senior leadership roles at Shake Shack Enterprises, The Piada Group, P.F. Chang’s, McKesson Corporation, and Fender Musical Instruments.

“Working in hospitality and guest services for many years, I’ve admired the incredible growth and collected expertise of OVG from afar,” said Neville. “I couldn’t be more excited to join such a dynamic organization at a time of innovation, expansion and transition. I’m looking forward to working alongside Chris and the unparalleled group of leaders at Oak View Group, building a structure and culture that supports the organization for 2027 and beyond.”

Oak View Group is recognized as a global leader in premium live entertainment infrastructure and services, with a platform spanning venue development and end-to-end capabilities across venue management, hospitality, and sponsorship sales. Founded in 2015, the company serves a collection of seven world-class owned venues and a client roster of iconic arenas, stadiums, convention centers, music festivals, performing arts centers, and cultural institutions, spanning four continents.
