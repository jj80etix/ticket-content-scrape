# Superstruct losses grow as costs jump across portfolio

## Summary

Superstruct, the festival conglomerate, reported after-tax losses widening to €115.5m for FY2025 (up from €90.6m in 2024) as cost of sales rose 15% to €619.7m and admin costs jumped over 50%, despite revenue growth. Directors nonetheless described the company as "well placed" on liquidity and said it is "actively" pursuing new acquisitions.

### Key Points
- FY2025 after-tax loss: €115.5m, vs. €90.6m in FY2024.
- Cost of sales up 15% to €619.7m; admin costs up more than 50%.
- Revenue grew year-over-year despite the wider loss.
- Directors state the company is "well placed" to meet liquidity needs.
- Company says it is "actively" seeking new acquisitions.

### Highlights
- Losses grew roughly 28% YoY (€90.6m to €115.5m) even as revenue rose -- cost inflation, not demand, is the driver.
- Admin costs rose over 50%, suggesting scaling/overhead strain across its multi-festival portfolio.
- Despite the widening loss, directors publicly committed to continued acquisitions.

### Industry Problem
A major festival roll-up is absorbing steep cost inflation across a large multi-brand portfolio while still pursuing growth by acquisition -- a sign that consolidation in European live events is continuing even as unit economics worsen, which could pressure ticketing/fee terms as portfolio owners look to offset rising costs.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor for Superstruct, or similarly leveraged roll-ups, renegotiating ticketing/fee terms with platform partners to offset margin pressure, and watch announced acquisition targets for signs of further consolidation in markets Etix serves.
