# Goose Unveils 2027 Spring Tour Dates

## Summary

Goose has announced its first 2027 tour dates, a five-show spring run across Georgia and Florida beginning April 9, with presales starting Oct. 28-29 and general on-sale Oct. 30. One dollar per ticket will benefit the Western Sun Foundation.

### Key Points
- Spring 2027 dates: Alpharetta (GA, 2 nights), Hollywood (FL), St. Augustine (FL, 3 nights), Clearwater (FL).
- Ticket presale tiers: fan sign-up (Oct. 28), Live Nation/Ticketmaster presale (Oct. 29), general on-sale (Oct. 30).
- $1 per ticket benefits Western Sun Foundation.
- Band is also mid-way through a separate November 2026 fall tour and annual Goosemas event.

## Transcript

![Goose spring](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Goose-spring-scaled.jpg)

Goose spring

Goose is going to keep on rocking through spring. The foursome from Wilton, Connecticut, announced its first dates for 2027, with shows in Georgia and Florida.

The band opens its “Spring 2027” trek with a two-night stand at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre in Alpharetta, Georgia, on April 9 and 10, followed by a show at Hard Rock Live in Hollywood, Florida on April 13. Goose then plays three nights, April 15-17, at St. Augustine Amphitheatre in Northeastern Florida before wrapping up April 18 at The BayCare Sound in Clearwater.

Fans have until Oct. 27 to sign up for the presale at goosetheband.com/tour, and those who sign up will receive a unique code that allows them to access the presale beginning Oct. 28 at 10 a.m. ET. The Live Nation and Ticketmaster presale begins Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. ET, and the general onsale begins Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. ET.

One dollar of every ticket sold will benefit Western Sun Foundation, a nonprofit that supports small and mid-sized nonprofits in the communities Goose fans travel through.

Goose recently wrapped its summer tour by performing at Bourbon & Beyond in Louisville. The band’s most successful summer outing was a sold-out two-night stand at the iconic Madison Square Garden in New York, where the band grossed a total of $2,890,271 off 28,455 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

Goose will soon be back on the road for their fall tour, kicking off at The Midland Theatre in Kansas City on Nov. 3. The band will play 14 shows next month and then host its annual Goosemas at Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall in Atlantic City, New Jersey, Dec. 11 and 12.

Goose is represented by ROAM’s Ethan Berlin and Karl Morse in North America.
