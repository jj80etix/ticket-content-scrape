# Los Tucanes De Tijuana Sign With WME For Global Representation

## Summary

Norteño group Los Tucanes de Tijuana signed with WME for global representation, joining the agency's Latin touring roster alongside Shakira, Maluma and J Balvin as the group expands its international footprint.

### Key Points
- WME partners with the group's existing management (Adrian Arce) and ENTOTAL Agency.
- The band has 15M+ monthly Spotify listeners; a recent Pacific Amphitheatre show grossed $447,437 off ~7,000 tickets (per Pollstar Boxoffice).
- Joins WME's Latin roster, including Shakira, Luis Miguel, Maluma, J Balvin and Pitbull.

## Transcript

![Los Tucanes de Tijuana photo by Elfego Becerra](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Los-Tucanes-de-Tijuana-photo-by-Elfego-Becerra.jpg)

Photo by Elfego Becerra

Los Tucanes de Tijuana, one of Mexico’s most popular norteño acts, has signed with WME for worldwide representation. The move marks a new chapter in the group’s career as they look to expand their global presence.

The group’s management team, which is led by Adrian Arce, aligned with ENTOTAL Agency’s Jorge Sanchez and Tom Muzquiz to create new opportunities for Los Tucanes de Tijuana across international markets with help from WME, and the entire team will combine their expertise and industry relationships to strengthen the band’s global footprint.

Over the past three decades, Los Tucanes de Tijuana helped define Mexican music, and many of their hit songs, including “La Chona” and “El Tucanazo,” have become staples at family gatherings. They have amassed 15 million monthly listeners on Spotify with more than a dozen songs reaching nine-digit streaming numbers. The group performed an August show at The Pacific Amphitheatre in Costa Mesa, California, that grossed $447,437 off 6,989 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

The signing bolsters WME’s Latin touring roster, which includes Luis Miguel, Shakira, Carín León, Maluma, Becky G, Wisin, J Balvin, Xavi, Ivan Cornejo, Caifanes, Café Tacvba, Julieta Venegas, Juanes, Pitbull and more.
