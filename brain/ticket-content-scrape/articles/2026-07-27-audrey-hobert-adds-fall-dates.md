Audrey Hobert — working through a summer run of headline dates and a busy festival schedule — has added a half dozen fall headline performances to her schedule.

The “Sue me” singer, touring in support of her full-length debut *Who’s the Clown*, will kick off the fall run, built around already announced ACL performances, with a double at the Brooklyn Paramount Sept. 29 and 30. She’ll perform at the Ryman and put on back-to-back nights at The Riviera Theatre in Chicago before the leg wraps at Oakland’s Fox Oct. 13.

Tickets are on sale to the public July 31 at 10 A.M. local time, with artist presale beginning July 29 at 10 A.M. local time and local presale beginning July 30 at 10 A.M. local time.

In June, Hobert sold out two nights at Washington, D.C.’s Lincoln Theatre, grossing $115,200 on 2,400 tickets.

**Audrey Hobert Fall Tour**

September 29––Brooklyn Paramount––Brooklyn, NY

September 30––Brooklyn Paramount––Brooklyn, NY

October 4––Austin City Limits––Austin, TX

October 6––Ryman Auditorium––Nashville, TN

October 8––The Riviera Theatre––Chicago, IL

October 9––The Riviera Theatre––Chicago, IL

October 11––Austin City Limits––Austin, TX
