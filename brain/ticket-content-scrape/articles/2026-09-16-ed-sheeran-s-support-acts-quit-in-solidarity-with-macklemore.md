# Ed Sheeran’s support acts quit in solidarity with Macklemore

## Summary

All of Ed Sheeran's Loop Tour support acts — Finneas, Aaron Rowe, and Lukas Graham — have withdrawn in solidarity with Macklemore, who was removed from the tour after making on-stage comments supporting Palestine. The acts said venues should not silence artists speaking up for the oppressed.

### Key Points
- Finneas, Aaron Rowe, and Lukas Graham all dropped out as openers
- Macklemore was removed from the tour following pro-Palestine on-stage remarks
- Affects Sheeran's 8 remaining U.S. dates and the South American leg opener
