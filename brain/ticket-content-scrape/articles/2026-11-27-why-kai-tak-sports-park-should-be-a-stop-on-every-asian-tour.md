# Why Kai Tak Sports Park should be a stop on every Asian tour

## Summary

Sponsored IQ advertorial on Kai Tak Sports Park (KTSP), Hong Kong's sports-and-entertainment precinct that opened March 2025. It argues KTSP should be a standard stop on Asian tours, citing 190+ events, 20M+ precinct visits, 2.8M+ Kai Tak Stadium spectators, a #1 ranking on Pollstar's Asia Focus Stadiums chart, and shows from acts like The Weeknd and BIGBANG. The piece frames Hong Kong's connectivity (an "88 million people, one-hour living circle" catchment) and tourism rebound (40.7M visitor arrivals in the first nine months of 2026, +12% YoY) as reasons for promoters to route tours through the venue.

### Key Points
- KTSP: 28-hectare precinct with Kai Tak Stadium, Kai Tak Arena, Kai Tak Youth Sports Ground, plus a mall and outdoor space.
- 190+ events hosted since March 2025 opening; 20M+ precinct visits; 2.8M+ stadium spectators.
- Ranked #1 on Pollstar's Asia Focus Stadiums chart.
- Some events draw 50%+ regional (non-Hong Kong) attendance, underscoring its role as an Asia touring hub.
- Hong Kong saw 40.7M visitor arrivals in Jan–Sep 2026 (+12% YoY) per the Hong Kong Tourism Board.
- Marked content: this is advertorial/promoted content from KTSP, not independent reporting.

## Transcript

Since opening in March 2025, the sports and entertainment destination has become an integral part of Hong Kong's venue landscape

![Kai Tak Sports Park © Simon Kwan & Associates](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Kai-Tak-Sports-Park-©-Simon-Kwan-Associates.png)

Kai Tak Sports Park © Simon Kwan & Associates

Since opening in March 2025, [Kai Tak Sports Park](https://www.kaitaksportspark.com.hk/?utm_source=IQwebsite&utm_medium=PromotedContent&utm_campaign=KTSPNov2026&utm_content=Advertorial) (KTSP) has rapidly established itself as a leading destination for sports and live entertainment in Asia, hosting more than 190 local and international events across sports, culture, and entertainment.

What began as a landmark addition to Hong Kong’s event landscape has grown into a thriving destination, welcoming over 20 million visits across the precinct, including more than 2.8 million spectators at Kai Tak Stadium.

Its growing international profile is reflected in a calendar of major events and performances by local and global stars, including The Weeknd and BIGBANG, whose recent engagements have further strengthened KTSP’s portfolio of major live events.

**A strategic destination  
**Hong Kong has long been one of Asia’s most connected international cities. With KTSP, it now has an added dimension: an event destination capable of attracting audiences from across the region.

Located at the heart of the Greater Bay Area, Hong Kong offers access to a catchment of approximately 88 million people within the “one-hour living circle”. Its proximity to the Chinese Mainland and extensive regional and international air connections make it accessible to audiences across Asia and beyond, while its established tourism and hospitality infrastructure supports visitors travelling for events.

This regional reach is reflected in KTSP’s audience profile, with some events attracting more than 50% of attendees from regional cities. This highlights the role live events can play in connecting markets and bringing audiences together across geographical boundaries.

Kai Tak Stadium’s ranking as the top venue on Pollstar’s Asia Focus Stadiums chart is another reflection of KTSP’s growing profile. Beyond ticketing performance, KTSP has also received international recognition and contributed to Hong Kong’s wider tourism economy. According to the Hong Kong Tourism Board, Hong Kong welcomed 40.7 million visitor arrivals in the first nine months of 2026, representing a 12% year-on-year increase and reflecting the continued recovery and growth of the city’s tourism sector.

**More than a venue  
**Spanning 28 hectares, KTSP is designed as an integrated destination, bringing together three major venues — Kai Tak Stadium, Kai Tak Arena and Kai Tak Youth Sports Ground — alongside outdoor sports facilities, open spaces and Kai Tak Mall to create a diverse precinct for events and everyday experiences.

Its event calendar reflects this versatility. Alongside major concerts, KTSP has hosted family entertainment such as Disney On Ice and Cavalluna, as well as major sporting events including the Hong Kong Sevens, international football matches, the 15th National Games and the 12th National Games for Persons with Disabilities (Hong Kong). These are complemented by a growing programme of cultural and community activities.

This diversity is more than programming variety. It demonstrates how a single precinct can accommodate different event requirements, audiences and experiences, while maintaining a year-round programme of activity.

At the heart of KTSP is Kai Tak Stadium, a 50,000-seat venue designed to accommodate major sporting and entertainment events with flexibility and audience comfort in mind.

Its retractable roof provides greater flexibility in adapting to unpredictable weather conditions, while the district cooling system and individual cooling outlets enhance audience comfort in Hong Kong’s warm and humid climate.

A flexible pitch system enables the stadium to accommodate different sporting and entertainment configurations, while the in-bowl stage pocket streamlines stage loading and provides additional space for concert staging, creating the potential for greater audience capacity at large-scale shows.

Designed with versatility in mind, integrated LED and lighting systems allow event-specific content, branding and messaging to become part of the stadium environment, adding another dimension to the audience experience.

**An extended event experience  
**One of KTSP’s defining characteristics is that the experience does not begin at the entrance. Kai Tak Mall and the wider precinct bring together retail, dining and entertainment, giving audiences more reasons to arrive early and stay longer.

This creates additional opportunities to engage audiences through merchandise, fan experiences, brand activations and retail collaborations. These activities also allow events to connect with fans beyond the performance itself, extending their presence across the precinct and forming part of the overall event experience.

This integrated approach also brings tangible benefits across the precinct. During the Hong Kong Football Festival 2026, Kai Tak Mall recorded nearly 120,000 visits in a single day. One sporting goods retailer reported an approximately fivefold increase in turnover, while a food and beverage merchant saw its business grow by more than 50% during the event period.

Beyond retail and dining, fan exhibitions and themed activations further extend the event experience, creating additional touchpoints for audiences throughout their visit. That’s sportainment in action — transforming a single live event into a full-scale destination experience.

**The next chapter starts here  
**The calendar ahead continues to reflect the scale and diversity of KTSP, with Bruno Mars, Charlie Puth, BTS, Disney On Ice and many other mega events on the horizon.

KTSP has moved from being the new venue on the block to being an established destination – one that combines scale, flexibility, technology, sport, entertainment and a wider destination experience in a way few venues in the world can match.

For those planning their next Asia tour, Hong Kong is ready to take its place on the itinerary.

The next stop? [Kai Tak Sports Park, Hong Kong.](https://www.kaitaksportspark.com.hk/?utm_source=IQwebsite&utm_medium=PromotedContent&utm_campaign=KTSPNov2026&utm_content=Advertorial)
