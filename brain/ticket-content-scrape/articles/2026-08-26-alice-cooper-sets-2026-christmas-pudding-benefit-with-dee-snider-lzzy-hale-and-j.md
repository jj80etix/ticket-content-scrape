# Alice Cooper Sets 2026 Christmas Pudding Benefit With Dee Snider, Lzzy Hale and John 5

## Summary

Alice Cooper's annual Christmas Pudding benefit concert returns to Phoenix's Celebrity Theatre December 12 for its 24th edition, with Dee Snider, Lzzy Hale, and John 5 joining the lineup. Proceeds support Cooper's Solid Rock Teen Centers, which offer free arts and vocational programming for youth ages 12–20. Presales begin August 26, with general onsale August 28.

### Key Points
- 24th annual Christmas Pudding benefit, December 12 at Phoenix's Celebrity Theatre.
- Lineup includes Dee Snider, Lzzy Hale, Joe Hottinger, and John 5.
- Proceeds benefit Solid Rock Teen Centers (Phoenix, Mesa, Goodyear).
- Presale August 26; general onsale August 28.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Alice-Cooper-Christmas-Pudding.webp)

Alice Cooper is bringing his annual Christmas Pudding benefit concert back to Phoenix this December, with Dee Snider, Lzzy Hale, John 5 and several other rock names joining the veteran performer for the event’s 24th edition.

The 2026 Christmas Pudding concert will take place December 12 at Celebrity Theatre in Phoenix, Arizona. Money raised from the show will support Alice Cooper’s Solid Rock Teen Centers, which operate locations in Phoenix, Mesa and Goodyear.

The nonprofit centers offer free programming for young people ages 12 to 20, including opportunities in music, dance and art alongside vocational programs designed to help participants develop practical skills.

This year’s lineup includes Twisted Sister frontman Dee Snider, Halestorm members Lzzy Hale and Joe Hottinger, and John 5 and the Creatures. Cooper will also take part in the annual benefit.

“We are excited to celebrate our 24th year with another incredible lineup of artists who generously give their time and talent to support the teens at our three Teen Centers,” Cooper said.

The singer added that the longtime fundraiser has developed into a holiday tradition in the Phoenix area, promising another evening built around rock music and the theatrical atmosphere associated with his live shows.

The Christmas Pudding announcement comes as Cooper prepares to head back on the road later this year. His fall 2026 North American tour, featuring his “Alice’s Attic” production, is scheduled to open October 27 at Ruth Eckerd Hall in Clearwater, Florida.

The itinerary will take Cooper through a number of U.S. markets, including Richmond, Atlantic City, Hershey, St. Louis, San Diego and San Jose. The run is currently scheduled to conclude November 21 at the Grand Theatre at Grand Sierra Resort + Casino in Reno, Nevada.
