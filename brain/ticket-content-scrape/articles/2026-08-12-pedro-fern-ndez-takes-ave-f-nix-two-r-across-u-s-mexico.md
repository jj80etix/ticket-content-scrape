# Pedro Fernández Takes ‘Ave Fénix Two’r’ Across U.S., Mexico

## Summary

Mexican icon Pedro Fernández announced his "Ave Fénix Two'r" tour across the U.S. and Mexico, spanning Sept. 5 (Mexico City) through Dec. 5 (San Diego), with stops including Madison Square Garden's Infosys Theater and Chicago's Rosemont Theatre. Live Nation-promoted general on-sale begins Aug. 14, following a Citi cardmember presale that started Aug. 11.

### Key Points
- Tour spans Sept. 5 (Auditorio Nacional, Mexico City) through Dec. 5 (Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre, San Diego).
- U.S. leg launches Oct. 15 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood; other stops include San Jose, Las Vegas, Denver, and Houston.
- Live Nation-promoted dates go on general sale Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. local; Citi cardmember presale began Aug. 11.
- Setlist built around four decades of catalog favorites including "Yo No Fui" and "El Aventurero."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/pedro-fernandez.jpg)

Mexican music icon Pedro Fernández is hitting the road with a new international run, announcing his “Ave Fénix Two’r” tour across Mexico and the United States.

The tour will bring Fernández’s four-decade catalog to major venues on both sides of the border, including Auditorio Nacional in Mexico City, YouTube Theater in Inglewood, Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden in New York, Rosemont Theatre near Chicago and 713 Music Hall in Houston.

Fans can expect a revamped production built around career-spanning favorites including “Yo No Fui,” “Amarte a la Antigua,” “Mi Forma de Sentir” and “El Aventurero.”

The announced schedule begins Sept. 5 at Auditorio Nacional before the U.S. leg launches Oct. 15 at YouTube Theater. Additional stops include San Jose, Las Vegas, Denver, Hidalgo, San Antonio, Phoenix and San Diego, with the run currently set to wrap Dec. 5 at Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre.
