# Agent Jon Moskowitz Joins Pyramid Entertainment

## Summary

Veteran booking agent Jon Moskowitz has joined Pyramid Entertainment after 15 years at Universal Attractions Agency, bringing relationships with artists including Black Eyed Peas, Rick Ross and Nelly.

### Key Points
- Moskowitz joins Pyramid Entertainment, founded in 1988 by CEO Sal Michaels.
- Prior client roster at Universal Attractions Agency included George Clinton, Ludacris, Flo Rida and Ashanti.
- Pyramid currently represents Rock & Roll Hall of Fame inductees Kool & the Gang, among others.

## Transcript

![image001 (7)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/image001-7.png)

Jon Moskowitz

Veteran booking agent Jon Moskowitz has joined Pyramid Entertainment, bringing more than two decades of experience in the live entertainment business to the agency.

A New York City Native hailing from Greenwich Village, Moskowitz has spent the past 15 years with Universal Attractions Agency where he worked with artists including, I Love 90s, George Clinton, Ludacris, Black Eyed Peas, Ub40, Flo Rida, Rick Ross, Nelly, Ja Rule and Ashanti, Trey Songz, Mya, Floetry, Morris Day, and Sheila E.

“I’m excited to begin this next chapter with Pyramid Entertainment,” said Moskowitz. “Pyramid has a long and storied history, and I am looking forward to joining the house that Sal, Seth, Doug and Virginia have built.

Pyramid Entertainment Group, Inc, was founded in 1988 by its owner and CEO Sal Michaels. A 40-year veteran of the music business, Sal began his career in the disco era of the 70’s by signing the underground queen of disco Gloria Gaynor. He went on to represent music icons, including Marvin Gaye, Luther Vandross, Rick James, Gladys Knight, KC & the Sunshine Band, the Commodores and countless others. Michaels currently represents Rock n Roll Hall of Fame Inductee Kool & the Gang and dozens of other legendary acts.

Speaking on the addition of Moskowitz, Owner and CEO Sal Michaels says, “Jon is a proven and accomplished agent with many years of experience, and we are glad to have him working on behalf of Pyramid. His tenacity, outgoing personality and expertise in this industry will surely prove to be assets to our company. Welcome aboard.”

Pyramid Entertainment Partner/Agent and 25-year veteran Seth Cohen also added, “I’ve known Jon for nearly two decades and we’ve always seen eye to eye on how business should be conducted. I am excited to have him on my team and look forward to the years ahead.”

Moskowitz can be reached at *JM@pyramid-ent.com*
