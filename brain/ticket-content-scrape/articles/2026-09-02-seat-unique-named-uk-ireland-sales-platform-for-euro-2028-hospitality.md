# Seat Unique Named UK, Ireland Sales Platform for Euro 2028 Hospitality

## Summary

Seat Unique has been appointed the UK/Ireland sales channel for UEFA Euro 2028 hospitality by BEYOND Hospitality, UEFA's exclusive official hospitality provider, handling inquiries and bookings for ticket-inclusive packages (from £900/person + VAT) across the tournament's nine UK/Ireland venues. Sales opened Sept. 1; the deal extends Seat Unique's premium-sports ticketing/hospitality business, which raised $26.5M earlier in 2026 to fund acquisitions and international expansion.

### Key Points
- Seat Unique appointed UK/Ireland sales platform for UEFA Euro 2028 hospitality by BEYOND Hospitality (UEFA's exclusive hospitality provider).
- Handles inquiries, product guidance, and booking assistance for hospitality packages across the tournament's nine England/Scotland/Wales/Republic of Ireland venues.
- Packages start at £900/person + VAT, spanning Supporters Club tiers up to Prestige/Platinum lounges and private Skyboxes; sales opened Sept. 1.
- Extends Seat Unique's premium-sports ticketing footprint following its earlier 2026 $26.5M raise earmarked for acquisitions and international expansion.

### Industry Problem
Major-tournament premium hospitality distribution is consolidating around specialist platforms winning exclusive regional sales mandates — a distribution channel primary/independent ticketing platforms without a dedicated premium-hospitality product are structurally excluded from.

### Proposed Solution
No new product is described beyond the distribution appointment itself. Monitor how Seat Unique's funded international/premium-hospitality expansion progresses as a competitive benchmark for whether a premium/hospitality ticketing tier is worth building for Etix's own major-event or sports clients.

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Seat-Unique-Named-UK-Ireland-Sales-Platform-for-Euro-2028-Hospitality.webp)

Seat Unique has been appointed as an official online sales platform for UEFA Euro 2028 hospitality in the United Kingdom and Ireland, giving the premium ticketing company a distribution role around one of Europe’s largest sporting events.

BEYOND Hospitality, UEFA’s exclusive official hospitality provider for the tournament, selected Seat Unique to serve as a local sales channel for corporate and individual customers seeking ticket-inclusive hospitality packages.

Under the agreement, Seat Unique will handle customer inquiries, provide guidance on hospitality products and assist buyers through the booking process across the tournament’s nine venues in England, Scotland, Wales and the Republic of Ireland.

Official hospitality sales opened September 1, with packages beginning at £900 per person, excluding VAT. Each package includes a guaranteed match ticket along with varying levels of hospitality.

Seat Unique has also expanded through acquisitions, including its purchase of P1 Travel, which combined its premium ticketing platform with an international sports-travel and distribution business. The combined operation has relationships with more than 140 official partners and sells experiences in more than 150 countries.

BEYOND Hospitality received the broader Euro 2028 mandate from UEFA in March. The company is responsible for creating, marketing, selling and delivering the tournament’s official hospitality program across all nine stadiums and can distribute that inventory through its own channels and appointed sales agents.

BEYOND Executive Chairman Jaime Byrom said Seat Unique’s customer relationships and familiarity with the UK and Ireland markets made it a useful addition to that sales network.

“We are delighted that Seat Unique is part of our growing sales network,” Byrom said. “Their proven expertise, strong customer relationships, and deep understanding of applicable markets make them an ideal partner to promote the Official Hospitality programme for UEFA EURO 2028.”

Euro 2028 will run from June 9 through July 9, 2028, with 24 national teams playing 51 matches across nine stadiums. The tournament will open at the National Stadium of Wales in Cardiff, while Wembley Stadium will host both semifinals and the final.

Approximately three million tickets are expected to be made available to fans and the general public during the tournament.
