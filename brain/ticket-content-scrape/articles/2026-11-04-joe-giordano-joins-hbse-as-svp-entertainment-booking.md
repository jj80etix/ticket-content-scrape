# Joe Giordano Joins HBSE As SVP, Entertainment Booking

## Summary

Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment has hired Joe Giordano Jr. as SVP of entertainment booking, overseeing bookings for Prudential Center (Newark) and the newly restored Loew's Jersey Theatre. Giordano previously held stadium/arena/theater alliance roles at Oak View Group and ASM Global.

### Key Points
- Giordano joins HBSE from Oak View Group, where he was VP of Stadium, Arena and Theater Alliances.
- He'll oversee entertainment programming at Prudential Center and Loew's Jersey Theatre.
- HBSE's broader portfolio includes Northwest Stadium (Washington Commanders) and a stake in a new Philadelphia arena for the 76ers/Flyers.

## Transcript

![Joe Giordano Jr. HBSE](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Joe-Giordano-Jr.-HBSE-scaled.jpg)

Joe Giordano

Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment has hired Joe Giordano Jr. as senior vice president of entertainment booking, where he will oversee entertainment booking for properties including Prudential Center in Newark and Loew’s Jersey Theatre in Jersey City.

“Joe’s deep experience in the entertainment industry and proven track record of building strong relationships while attracting major touring acts will be a tremendous asset to our team,” said Sean Saadeh, Chief Programming Officer and Head of Entertainment at HBSE. “Joe has a rare ability to pair creative vision with instinctive talent-spotting, consistently identifying and elevating entertainment experiences that resonate deeply with audiences. As we expand our entertainment footprint across New Jersey and beyond, his expertise will help us continue to deliver world-class events and experiences to our audiences.”

Giordano brings nearly two decades of experience to the role, with extensive knowledge in venue programming and booking. Prior to becoming Oak View Group’s Vice President of Stadium, Arena and Theater Alliances, he spent nearly a decade at ASM Global and went on to serve as assistant general manager at BOK Center in Tulsa.

“I’m excited to hit the ground running at HBSE and continue building on the incredible momentum across its growing portfolio of world-class sports and entertainment properties,” Giordano said in the announcement. “Having grown up in New Jersey and spent a lifetime cheering for the Devils, joining HBSE is both a professional milestone and a personal dream realized. The opportunity to support Prudential Center’s continued success while helping grow entertainment offerings across the broader HBSE portfolio is incredibly special, and I look forward to creating unforgettable experiences for fans and guests for years to come.”

Along with Prudential Center and the newly restored Loew’s Jersey Theatre, HSBE’s properties include Northwest Stadium, home of the Washington Commanders NFL team, and as owner of the Philadelphia 76ers NBA team, is a partner in a new arena underdevelopment for both the 76ers and NHL’s Philadelphia Flyers.
