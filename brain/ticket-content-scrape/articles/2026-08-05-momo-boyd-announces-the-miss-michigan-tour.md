# Momo Boyd Announces “The Miss Michigan Tour”

## Summary

Infinity Song's Momo Boyd announced her first solo headlining tour, "The Miss Michigan Tour," a four-date run kicking off in Atlanta August 13 and hitting Detroit, Los Angeles, and New York, following her debut solo EP *Miss Michigan* released in April.

### Key Points
- Four dates: Atlanta (Aug. 13), Detroit (Aug. 15), Los Angeles (Aug. 19), and New York (Aug. 21).
- Each stop carries a different theme tied to the "Miss Michigan" concept, with Boyd crowning an audience member nightly.
- Tour marks Boyd's first outing separate from her family band, Infinity Song.
- Tickets available via Ticketmaster.

## Transcript

Infinity Song’s Momo Boyd is stepping out on her own to embark on her first solo trek, ‘The Miss Michigan Tour,’ per Roc Nation. According to the social media post, Momo will kick off the four-date tour in Atlanta on Aug 13, then travel to Detroit and Los Angeles, and conclude in New York. Fans can grab tickets [here](https://www.ticketmaster.com/momo-boyd-tickets/artist/4267164). See full tour routing below.

The singer offered insight into her first headlining tour and what it means to step out on her own, outside of her family band, Infinity Song.

“THE MISS MICHIGAN TOUR!!!!! coming to a city near you. OMG everyone, I’m so so excited!!! I’m having so much fun preparing for this tour, and it starts in just over a weeeeeek,” Momo Boyd typed on Instagram. “Get ready to cry and dance and sing and laugh with meeeeeee. My first solo headline tour ever ever ever.”

“This moment is so massive for me, to bring these songs to life for the people who resonate with them. I hope to see so many of you there!!! Each night will be a different theme based on the Miss Michigan world we’re building. Come dressed according to the theme, and I’ll pick someone from the audience to crown for the night!!!! lol I’m so excited,” she added.

Momo Boyd recently released her first solo project, *Miss Michigan*, in April. The EP showcased the artist’s range outside of Infinity Song, with Boyd even describing her solo musicianship as “a completely different artist than the version that’s in Infinity Song,” per an interview with NME.
