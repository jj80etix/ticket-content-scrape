# Cleo Sol Expands 2027 Gentlewoman Tour With Three New Shows

## Summary

Cleo Sol added three dates to her 2027 "Gentlewoman Tour" — Boston, Detroit and the Houston area — expanding her first North American arena headlining run to 10 shows in support of her fifth studio album, "Gentlewoman." New-date tickets go on sale Nov. 20 via Ticketmaster and venue outlets.

### Key Points
- Three new dates added: April 14 (Boston), April 23 (Detroit), April 26 (Houston area) — tour now 10 stops total.
- Tour supports Sol's fifth album, "Gentlewoman," released Aug. 20, 2026; opens April 13 at Madison Square Garden, closes May 1 at Oakland Arena.
- New-date tickets on sale Nov. 20, 9 a.m. local, via Ticketmaster and participating venue outlets.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Cleo-Sol-1.webp)

British singer-songwriter Cleo Sol has added three performances to her 2027 *Gentlewoman Tour*, expanding her first North American arena headlining run to 10 dates.

The newly announced concerts will take place April 14 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston, April 23 at the Fox Theatre in Detroit and April 26 at Smart Financial Centre at Sugar Land in the Houston area. The tour will also visit Baltimore, Toronto, Chicago, Atlanta, Los Angeles and Oakland before wrapping up on May 1 at Oakland Arena.

The additions come less than a month after Sol [announced the original seven-date tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/cleo-sol-announces-north-american-gentlewoman-tour-for-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which is scheduled to begin April 13 at Madison Square Garden in New York City.

The *Gentlewoman Tour* supports Sol’s fifth studio album, *Gentlewoman*, released August 20. Tickets for the three newly announced concerts are scheduled to go on sale Friday, November 20, at 9 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster and participating venue ticketing outlets.
