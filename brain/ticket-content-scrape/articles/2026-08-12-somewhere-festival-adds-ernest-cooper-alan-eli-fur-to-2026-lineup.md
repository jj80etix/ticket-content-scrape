# Somewhere Festival Adds ERNEST, Cooper Alan, Eli & Fur to 2026 Lineup

## Summary

Somewhere Festival, a two-day country/electronic/hip-hop event in downtown Wichita on September 25-26, added ERNEST, Cooper Alan, and Eli & Fur to its 2026 lineup alongside previously announced acts like Bailey Zimmerman and Shaquille O'Neal (as DJ Diesel). Single-day and two-day passes are now on sale across General Admission, VIP, and premium tiers.

### Key Points
- New additions: ERNEST, Cooper Alan, Eli & Fur, plus Logan Mize, Just Jayne, and Jaelen Johnston.
- Friday is led by Bailey Zimmerman, DJ Diesel (Shaquille O'Neal), Ian Munsick, and Evan Honer; Saturday by ERNEST, Two Friends, Bob Moses, Cooper Alan, and Eli & Fur.
- ~25 local artists and the Tribal Roots Beat Bus (Indigenous/regional talent) are also on the bill.
- Produced by Movement Musick with Breakaway; includes the "Be The People" block party connecting attendees to social-service organizations.
- GA, VIP, Somewhere Deck, and Somewhere On Stage tickets are on sale now via the festival website.

Somewhere Festival has expanded its 2026 lineup, adding ERNEST, Cooper Alan, Eli & Fur and more as the downtown Wichita event rolls out daily schedules and puts single-day passes on sale.

Set for September 25-26, the two-day festival will span country, electronic, indie, hip-hop, soul and singer-songwriter music across multiple stages. Friday’s lineup is led by Bailey Zimmerman, DJ Diesel — aka Shaquille O’Neal — Ian Munsick and Evan Honer, with ME N Ü and Night Tales also among the performers. Saturday brings ERNEST, Two Friends, Bob Moses with a club set, Cooper Alan, Eli & Fur, Kaleena Zanders and Cure for Paranoia. Other newly announced names include Logan Mize, Just Jayne and Jaelen Johnston.

Around 25 local artists are also expected to perform during the weekend, while the Tribal Roots Beat Bus returns with a lineup spotlighting Indigenous, regional and emerging talent. Additional artist and programming announcements are expected in the coming weeks.

Produced by Movement Musick in partnership with Breakaway, Somewhere Festival will once again take over downtown Wichita with live music alongside art installations, local food vendors, sponsor activations and community programming.
