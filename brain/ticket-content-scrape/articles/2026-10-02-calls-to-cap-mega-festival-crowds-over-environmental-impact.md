# Calls to cap ‘mega festival’ crowds over environmental impact

## Summary

A new peer-reviewed paper by three UK-based academics calls on festival licensing bodies to consider capping audience numbers at the industry's largest "mega" festivals, arguing that continued growth in scale is incompatible with reducing environmental impact. The paper is set for publication in the journal Event Management; the full IQ article is paywalled beyond the opening paragraphs.

### Key Points
- Paper authored by independent researcher Dr. Adrian Bossey and Dr. Mike Duignan, among others, forthcoming in Event Management.
- Central argument: "degrowth through reducing absolute audience capacity must be on the table for mega festivals."
- Frames recent licensing scrutiny (e.g., Glastonbury-scale events) around environmental sustainability rather than ticketing or safety.
