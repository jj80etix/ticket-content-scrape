# Annie Lennox, Last Dinner Party help Wilderness Festival turn 15

## Summary

The UK's Wilderness Festival marked its 15th edition (July 30–Aug 2) at Cornbury Park, Oxfordshire, with headliners Scissor Sisters, Carl Cox, The Last Dinner Party, and a gender-equity-themed showcase curated by Annie Lennox. The 30,000-capacity boutique festival, promoted by Festival Republic, combined music with food, wellness, and family programming, and has already announced its 16th edition for August 2027.

### Key Points
- Wilderness Festival's 15th edition ran July 30–Aug 2, 2026 at Cornbury Park, promoted by Festival Republic.
- Headliners included Scissor Sisters, Carl Cox, first-time headliners The Last Dinner Party, and a one-off Annie Lennox showcase ("SISTERS") benefiting gender-based-violence charities.
- Festival capacity is roughly 30,000; £5 from each Long Table Banquet seat went to grassroots women-led organizations.
- The 16th edition is already scheduled for Aug. 5–8, 2027.

The UK’s Wilderness Festival capped its milestone 15th edition at the weekend, bringing together established headliners with rising talent as it looks ahead to its next chapter.

The boutique UK festival returned to Oxfordshire’s Cornbury Park last weekend (30 July-2 August), bringing headliners Scissor Sisters and DJ Carl Cox with first-time festival headliners The Last Dinner Party and Annie Lennox, who delivered a world-first showcase centred on gender equity.

Promoted by Festival Republic, the multi-stage, 30,000-capacity festival welcomed thousands of fans during its 15th anniversary edition. Renowned for its diverse and immersive programme, Wilderness’s offerings extend beyond the music realm, with thought leaders, teachers, writers, chefs, and creators all coming together for the secluded retreat.

It served as the setting of SISTERS: Curated by Annie Lennox’s global debut, marketing a one-off collaboration between Lennox and global feminist organisation and festival charity partner The Circle. The main stage takeover featured special performances from Jessie Ware, Benedict Wong, Becky Sikasa, Ríoghnach Connolly, and The Last Dinner Party, in support of raising funds to tackle gender-based violence and economic injustice worldwide.

It also marked the first time English alternative act The Last Dinner Party headlined a festival, which the band called a “perfect send-off” for their *From The Pyre* live campaign that has taken them around the world.

> “The experience of playing this album live has been forged into something we adore”

“Headlining the same festival we first played on the Rising stage four years ago, before we’d released any music at all, felt like someone who loves us has been writing the story,” the band said [online](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dblc1SLjRcz/?img_index=1). “From all the hours agonised over the perfect setlist, to our endlessly inspiring crew and collaborators on the stage design, to all of your beautiful art and gifts, the experience of playing this album live has been forged into something we adore by so many thousands of people, across the world.”

In addition to the multi-generational headliner pool, the festival also hosted Soulwax, Groove Armada, Saint Etienne, Say She She, Bleech 9:3, Chloe Qisha, Eats Everything, and Ella Knight across its days.

Outside of its music offerings, the Audi-sponsored gathering also featured culinary experiences, interactive family entertainment, and wellness perks, showcasing the diversifying palette of festival experiences. Festivalgoers could take part in the Long Table Banquet, with £5 from every seat being directly donated to supporting grassroots women-led organisations worldwide.

And theatre, workshops, games, and circus skills were on offer for younger festivalgoers in The Family Field, while those looking to recharge could indulge at the spa, take part in workouts, or experience a sauna/cold plunge for the first time at the festival.

Wilderness is set to return for its 16th edition from 5-8 August 2027.
