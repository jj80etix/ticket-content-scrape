# Fans hospitalised after stand collapses at Red Hot Summer Tour

## Summary

A grandstand seating roughly 700 people partially collapsed during a performance at the Red Hot Summer Tour's Gold Coast, Australia stop, hospitalizing three people and injuring 22 others; the festival resumed but the band performing at the time, Boom Crash Opera, cut its set short. Queensland Police and Workplace Health and Safety Queensland are investigating, and the Gold Coast mayor says future events at the site will be reviewed.

### Key Points
- Incident occurred during Boom Crash Opera's set at the Coolangatta Beach venue site; 3 hospitalized, 22 treated.
- Festival resumed with other acts (Men At Work, Australian Crawl) after the grandstand was closed.
- Promoter Face to Face Touring has not responded to questions about responsibility for the stand's safety testing.
- Investigation by Queensland Police and Workplace Health and Safety Queensland is ongoing.

## Transcript

The event in Australia's Gold Coast was stopped after a bleacher seating 700 people fell, with investigations now underway

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/red-hot-summer-tour-face-to-face-touring-scaled.jpg.webp)

Red Hot Summer Tour © Face To Face Touring

Three people were hospitalised and 22 treated for injuries after the collapse of a bandstand at Red Hot Summer tour in Australia’s Gold Coast.

The stand collapsed at the event’s Coolangatta Beach venue site during a performance by the band Boom Crash Opera.

The festival resumed later with sets from other acts, including Men At Work and Australian Crawl, though Boom Crash Opera did not finish their performance.

Queensland Police and Workplace Health and Safety Queensland are now investigating, with Gold Coast Mayor Tom Tate suggesting the future holding of events at Coolangatta Beach will be reviewed in the wake of the incident.

“So sorry we had to cut our set short today at Red Hot Summer in Coolangatta. We hope that everyone is ok and we trust that the rest of the day goes off with joy and fun,” Boom Crash Opera said in an online post.

> “Our focus remains on the wellbeing of those affected”

Multiple pieces of footage of the site shows seated bleachers partially collapsed, which reportedly had 700 people out of the 10,000-strong crowd seated in them.

Red Hot Summer Tour and promoter Face to Face Touring did not respond to *IQ’s* request for comment on who was responsible for the safe operation of the stand and what testing was done of the stand prior to the event.

However, a statement provided to *Rolling Stone AU / NZ* attributed to organisers said: “Following an incident involving a grandstand at the Red Hot Summer Tour event in Coolangatta on Sunday, 1 November, the grandstand was closed. A number of patrons received medical assistance on site, with some attending hospital for further assessment.”

“Our focus remains on the wellbeing of those affected. We sincerely thank patrons for their cooperation, and Queensland Police and medical teams for their assistance,” the statement continued.
