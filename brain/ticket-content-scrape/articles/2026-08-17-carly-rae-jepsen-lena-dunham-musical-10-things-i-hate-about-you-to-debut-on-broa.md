# Carly Rae Jepsen, Lena Dunham Musical ‘10 Things I Hate About You’ to Debut on Broadway in 2027

## Summary

Carly Rae Jepsen and Lena Dunham are bringing a new musical adaptation of "10 Things I Hate About You" to Broadway in 2027, with a book by Dunham and Jessica Huang, music/lyrics by Jepsen and Ethan Gruska, and direction/choreography by Christopher Wheeldon.

### Key Points
- "10 Things I Hate About You" musical debuts on Broadway in 2027.
- Book by Lena Dunham and Jessica Huang; music/lyrics by Carly Rae Jepsen and Ethan Gruska.
- Christopher Wheeldon directs and choreographs; Tom Kitt oversees music supervision.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0822.jpeg)

Carly Rae Jepsen and Lena Dunham will bring a new adaptation of “10 Things I Hate About You” to Broadway in 2027.

The new musical is an adaptation of Gil Junger’s 1999 teen comedy film — starring Julia Stiles, Heath Ledger, and Joseph Gordon-Levitt — which was loosely adapted from William Shakespeare’s “The Taming of the Shrew.”

“10 Things I Hate About You” will feature a book from Dunham and Jessica Huang, with music and lyrics from Jepsen and Ethan Gruska. Christopher Wheeldon will both direct and choreograph the show, while Broadway veteran Tom Kitt will oversees music supervision, arrangements, and orchestrations.

The production delves into the story of Padua High, following a tale of crushes, cliques, and teenage angst, with the tag line: “How do I loathe thee? Let me count the ways.”

While this marks Jepsen’s first musical, she previously made her Broadway debut in 2014 as the title role in “Cinderella.” She is set to release her new double album *Day and Night* on September 18.
