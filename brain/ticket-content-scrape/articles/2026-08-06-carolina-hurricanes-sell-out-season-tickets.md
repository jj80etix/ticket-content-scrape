# Carolina Hurricanes sell out season tickets

## Summary

The NHL's Carolina Hurricanes have sold out season tickets across all three of their season-ticket plan tiers (full, half, and quarter-season) for the 2026-27 regular season, extending a streak of 168 consecutive sellouts dating back to February 2023. The team, coming off a second Stanley Cup title, will offer a limited number of individual-game tickets starting Aug. 20.

### Key Points
- Carolina Hurricanes sold out all season-ticket tiers for the 2026-27 season, which begins Sept. 29.
- The sellout streak covers 168 consecutive regular-season and playoff games since February 2023.
- Individual-game tickets from reserved inventory go on sale Aug. 20.
- The team credits strong fan support, including a Stanley Cup title in 2025-26, for sustained demand.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**National Hockey League (NHL) side Caroline Hurricanes has sold out of season tickets for the 2026-27 regular season.**

The team enters the new campaign, which is set to begin on September 29, having sold out 168 consecutive regular season and playoff matches dating back to February 2023.

“We all saw the results of our amazing fan support last spring, as the team made its run to the Stanley Cup championship,” said Hurricanes’ chief ticketing officer Sara Daniel.

“The loudest house brought it every night – even when the team was playing thousands of miles away, nearly 20,000 people packed the Lenovo Center for watch parties.

“We’re thrilled to reach this milestone and continue to build on the franchise’s success – on and off the ice.”

The Hurricanes have also reserved a limited number of seats to be sold on an individual game basis which are set to go on sale on August 20.

“The team allocates inventory to three different levels of season ticket plans – full-season plans, half-season plans and quarter-season plans,” read a team statement.

“Following the seat selections that conclude at the end of this week, all three levels have officially sold out.”

The Hurricanes enjoyed a title-winning season in 2025-26 as they finished on top of the regular season standings before claiming a second Stanley Cup triumph in their history.
