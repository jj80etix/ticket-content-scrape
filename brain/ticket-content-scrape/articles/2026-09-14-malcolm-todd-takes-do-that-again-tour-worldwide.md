# Malcolm Todd Takes ‘Do That Again Tour’ Worldwide

## Summary

Indie pop artist Malcolm Todd, mid-way through a sold-out North American fall tour, announced a new international leg spanning Asia, Europe, the UK and Australia from late 2026 through May 2027, in support of his album "Do That Again."

### Key Points
- North American "Do That Again Tour" (started Sept. 2) includes stops at New York's Radio City Music Hall, Chicago's The Salt Shed, and three nights at LA's Greek Theatre.
- New Live Nation-promoted Asia leg begins Nov. 23 in Taipei, with stops in Yokohama, Manila, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta.
- Europe leg starts Feb. 12, 2027 in Stockholm and runs through a March 2 show in London.
- Todd, who turns 23 this week, had a breakout year including a Tonight Show debut and a Gap campaign.


## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/unnamed-1.jpg)

While in the midst of his sold-out North American fall tour, Malcolm Todd has unveiled new dates for late 2026 through May 2027 in Asia, Europe, UK and Australia.

The platinum-selling indie pop singer/songwriter and guitarist is supporting his second studio album, Do That Again, which was released in June by Columbia Records. The album hit No. 5 on the Billboard 200 and includes the singles “Breathe” and “I Saw Your Face.” Todd, who turns 23 tomorrow, has had a big year including making his debut on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, starring in Gap’s Fall 2026 “Denim on Your Own” campaign alongside actress Inde Navarrette and performing at Outside Lands and Osheaga.

Following the festival appearances, the “Do That Again Tour” kicked off Sept. 2 in Irving, Texas. Major venues on the trek include New York’s Radio City Music Hall, Chicago’s The Salt Shed and three nights at Los Angeles’ Greek Theatre.

The newly announced Live Nation-promoted run begins Nov. 23 at TICC in Taipei. Todd’s travels throughout Asia include stops in Yokohama, Manila, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta.

He’ll begin in 2027 plans with a run throughout Europe that starts Feb. 12 at Stockholm’s Fryshuset Arenan, with dates booked through a Mar. 2 show at London’s O2 Academy Brixton. Gigs in between include Copenhagen, Cologne, Brussels, Berlin, Amsterdam, Paris, Dublin, Glasgow, Birmingham and Manchester.

The Australia leg launches May 12 in Brisbane, followed by stops in Sydney, Melbourne and Perth.

Natanya will support shows across the European and UK leg, with Maden Lane joining the Australian shows.

Todd is booked worldwide by Creative Artists Agency. Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Todd include a Feb. 10, 2026 show at Roundhouse in Kensington, Australia, that sold 2,132 tickets and grossed $112,658.

Check out ticketing information and the routing below.
