# Harry Styles ramps up Together, Together with another dozen cities

## Summary

Harry Styles has added another dozen cities to his "Together, Together" tour, continuing to expand the run.

### Key Points
- A dozen new cities added to the existing tour routing.
- Extends an already large-scale global tour.
