# Robert Glasper Embarks On First Blue Note Residency

## Summary

Grammy-winning pianist Robert Glasper wrapped his first-ever Blue Note residency, a seven-date London run featuring guest appearances by Marsha and Common, and will follow with a December Los Angeles Blue Note residency plus additional US dates in Orlando, Seattle, Oakland, Atlanta and Chicago.

### Key Points
- London Blue Note residency ran Nov. 13–22 with guests Marsha and Common.
- A December Blue Note LA residency (Dec 10–13) follows.
- Additional 2026–27 dates: Orlando, Seattle, Oakland, Atlanta, Chicago.
- Tickets are on sale now via the respective venues.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2296192492](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-2296192492-1-scaled.jpg)

Robert Glasper performs at the Grand Opening of the Blue Note Jazz Club London on September 23, 2026 in London, England. (Photo by Alan Chapman/Dave Benett/Getty Images)

Robert Glasper has announced his first-ever residency at the Blue Note, with completed performances in London and new additional shows set for Los Angeles.

The 5-time Grammy Award-winning artist is wrapping up a seven-date stay at the London Blue Note, which kicked off on Nov. 13. The last show concluded on Nov. 22, and the run featured guest appearances by Marsha and Common. Glasper will then follow up with a multi-night Blue Note Los Angeles residency in December, alongside additional dates in Orlando, Seattle, Oakland, Atlanta and Chicago.
