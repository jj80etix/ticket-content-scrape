# Gang of Youths Set Spring 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Gang of Youths announced a spring 2027 North American tour supporting their fourth album, Compañero, running from Boston (March 17) through two closing Coachella sets (April 9 and 16). Tickets go on sale Nov. 20, with an artist presale starting Nov. 18.

### Key Points
- 18-date North American run, March 17–April 16, 2027
- Closes with the band's Coachella performances rather than a standalone finale date
- Compañero arrives March 12, 2027 via Mosy Recordings/Sony Music Australia/RCA UK/Arista US

Gang of Youths have announced a 2027 North American tour supporting their fourth studio album, *Compañero*. The trek kicks off at the Royale in Boston on March 17. The outing will then conclude at the Swan Dive in Las Vegas on April 14 before the band performs at Coachella on April 16.

Tickets go on sale Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time, with an artist presale beginning Nov. 18.

*Compañero* arrives March 12, 2027, via Mosy Recordings / Sony Music Australia / RCA UK / Arista US.
