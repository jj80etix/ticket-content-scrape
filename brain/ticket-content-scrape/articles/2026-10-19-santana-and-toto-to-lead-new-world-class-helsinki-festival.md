# Santana and Toto to lead new ‘world-class’ Helsinki festival

## Summary

A new two-day "world-class" festival launching in Helsinki will be headlined by Santana and Toto on July 30-31, aiming to fill what organizers Loud'n Live call a "clear gap" in the Finnish capital's festival market. The debut edition targets up to 25,000 attendees with a lineup also featuring Kool & The Gang, Hurts, and Rag'n'Bone Man alongside domestic acts.

### Key Points
- Debut two-day seaside festival, July 30-31, Helsinki; capacity target ~25,000.
- Headliners: Santana, Toto; also Kool & The Gang, Hurts, Rag'n'Bone Man.
- Domestic acts: Erja Lyytinen, SF Blues, Honey B & the T-Bones.
- Organized by Loud'n Live to address a gap in Helsinki's festival landscape.
