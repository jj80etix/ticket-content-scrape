# Tour news: Dave Matthews Band rolls out new dates

## Summary

A roundup of touring news: Dave Matthews Band adds seven arena dates including a two-night Madison Square Garden finale; Brandi Carlile postpones her UK/EU leg to 2027; Morrissey cancels his Las Vegas Encore Theatre run; and Steve Lacy, Phoebe Bridgers, DOMI & JD BECK, Jorja Smith, and Mariah Carey all announce new tour dates or additions.

### Key Points
- Dave Matthews Band adds 7 arena dates beginning Nov. 4, closing with two nights at Madison Square Garden.
- Brandi Carlile postpones her UK/EU *Human Tour* leg to March-April 2027, cancelling Lisbon and adding Wolverhampton and Copenhagen.
- Morrissey cancels a four-show Las Vegas run citing "unforeseen logistical challenges."
- Steve Lacy, Phoebe Bridgers, DOMI & JD BECK, Jorja Smith, and Mariah Carey each announce new or additional tour dates.

## Transcript

Plus: Brandi Carlile pushes EU/UK tour back, Morrissey cancels in Las Vegas, Phoebe Bridgers sets out intimate UK run, plus more...

Leading the latest tour news, **Dave Matthews Band** has announced they will hit the arena circuit this year, adding seven dates to their ongoing run.

The newly announced dates begin on 4 November at Wisconsin’s Kohl Center, with stops lined up in Nashville, Chicago, Providence, Baltimore, and a two-night finale at New York City’s Madison Square Garden on 12-13 November.

The band is currently due out for a handful of amphitheatre shows and festival plays ahead of the new dates. After making stops in Denver, Shakopee, and George, the group will play Oceans Calling Festival, [Farm Aid](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/09/farm-aid-ploughs-ahead-for-40th-edition/), and Bourbon & Beyond, with Matthews playing solo at Maryland’s Power to the People Festival.

American singer **Brandi Carlile** has postponed the UK/EU leg of her *Human Tour* dates to 2027.

The dates, which were set to follow a still-scheduled amphitheatre and arena run in the US in August and September, have now been pushed to next March-April, with some venues changing, the Lisbon show being outright cancelled, and new dates in Wolverhampton and Copenhagen being added.

“I’m aware that this change may be disappointing and inconvenient for many of you, especially those of you who were planning on travelling internationally,” she said online. “I want nothing more than to see my friends across the pond, and I have done everything in my power to avoid rescheduling this tour.”

With a week to go, **Morrissey** has cancelled his four-show run in Las Vegas’ Encore Theatre due to “unforeseen logistical challenges”.

The former Smiths frontman was due to play the 1,480-seat venue at Wynn Las Vegas from 14-15 and 18-19 August. It is the latest cancellation for the singer, who axed over 20 shows last year alone.

Elsewhere, **Steve Lacy** has rolled out a 27-city North American tour, the  *Oh yeah? Tour*.

Promoted by Live Nation, the theatre-level outing launches on 27 September at Minneapolis’ Armory, and will make stops across in cities including Toronto, Miami, Los Angeles and more before wrapping up in Vancouver at Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre on 30 November.

The *Oh yeah? Tour* marks Lacy’s first run of headline shows since his 2022 sold-out *Give You The World Tour* across North America.

Indie singer **Phoebe Bridgers** has lined up a quartet of intimate, phone-free shows in the UK ahead of her *Lost Tour* arena run later this year.

The outing begins on 17 August at London’s Union Chapel, and will run through Kingston’s Circuit (19 August), Brighton’s Chalk (20), and Bristol’s Electric (21).

Jazz fusion duo **DOMI & JD BECK** have unveiled an expansive *Who Asked?* tour, setting out legs in the US, Australia, and Asia for later this year.

The theatre-level outing will begin in Dallas, Texas on 9 September, and will span 26 dates in the US. The pair will then head to Australia for three shows from 10-12 November, and then continue for another 15 shows across Asia for a month.

That leg will span several countries: three cities in India, Thailand, Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Taiwan, Hong Kong, two shows in China, South Korea, and three in Japan.

English singer **Jorja Smith** will also hit the North American theatre circuit this year, lining up five shows for November.

The quick run starts on 14 November at Los Angeles’ Orpheum Theater, followed by stops at San Francisco’s The Castro, Toronto’s Massey Hall, and Brooklyn’s Kings Theater, ending at Chicago’s The Riviera Theatre on 23 November.

She recently wrapped up her support slot for Harry Styles’ *Together, Together* tour in Mexico City, and is due to play a handful of festivals before another tour leg in South America later this year.

Finally, Queen of Christmas **Mariah Carey** has added an extra stadium show in Brazil ahead of the holiday season.

Promoted by 30e, the songstress is now set to play two nights at São Paulo’s Nubank Parque on 17-18 November, followed by a show at Rio de Janeiro’s Área Externa da Farmasi Arena on 21 November.
