# Iconic British Pop Group S Club Announce 2027 UK Tour

## Summary

British pop group S Club announced "Ain't No Party Like A S Club Party Tour," a 13-date UK/Ireland arena reunion run starting October 8, 2027 at Glasgow's OVO Hydro. All five original members are participating.

### Key Points
- 13 UK/Ireland arena shows beginning Oct. 8, 2027 in Glasgow
- Includes AO Arena Manchester (Oct. 9) and two shows at The O2, London (Oct. 16)
- Reunites Bradley McIntosh, Jo O'Meara, Jon Lee, Rachel Stevens and Tina Barrett
- General onsale Nov. 6 at 9:30 a.m. UK time; fan presale begins Nov. 4

## Transcript

[S Club](https://pollstar.com/artists/s-club-56106), one of the most successful British pop groups of all time, have announced the “Ain’t No Party Like A S Club Party Tour”, with 13 UK and Ireland shows starting Oct. 8, 2027 at the OVO Hydro in Glasgow, Scotland.

The tour will see Bradley McIntosh, Jo O’Meara, Jon Lee, Rachel Stevens and Tina Barrett reunite to bring their signature feel-good pop to arenas across the country including AO Arena Manchester Oct. 9, and two shows at The O2, London Oct. 16, one matinee and one evening performance).

Tickets for all shows go on sale at 9.30 a.m. UK time on Friday, Nov. 6. An exclusive fan presale (sign-up required) launches Nov. 4 from 9.30 a.m. local time.

S Club, formerly known as S Club 7, are a British pop group formed by British manager Simon Fuller in 1998. They captured the hearts of a generation with hits including “Bring It All Back”, “S Club Party”, “Reach” and “Never Had a Dream Come True”. Throughout their career, they sold over 10 million albums worldwide, had four UK number-one singles, and four multi-platinum selling studio albums, including 7, which went to Number One in the UK charts. The band have also won two BRIT awards, including British Breakthrough Artist and Best British Single for ‘Don’t Stop Movin’.

The number seven is, of course, a reference to the original lineup, which also included Paul Cattermole and Hannah Spearritt.

The band’s first box office report submitted to Pollstar is from three sold out shows at London’s Wembley Arena (now OVO Arena Wembley), May 29-31, 2001, where the band moved 31,114 tickets at a $917,054 gross.

The band went separate ways in 2003, but reunited for a short tour in 2015, visiting many of the buildings they’ll be returning to next year. Pollstar Boxoffice highlights from their 2015 run include 12,152 ticket sold and $766,427 grossed at Manchester Arena, May 8, 2015; or 26,876 tickets sold across two shows at The O2, London, May 16-17, 2015, grossing $1,680,001.

The band had announced their official reunion for 2023, including a tour, when band member Paul Cattermole passed away at the age of 46. Band member Hannah Spearritt, who used to be in a relationship with Cattermole though not at the time of his passing, subsequently left the band.

Hence they dropped the 7 from the band name and toured as S Club, calling their 2023 arena run the “Good Times Tour” in reference to the song of the same name, on which Cattermole sings the lead vocals. Boxoffice reports submitted for that tour include 10,614 ticket sold, $731,946 grossed at The OVO Hydro in Glasgow, Scotland, Oct. 18, 2023, which is where next year’s tour kicks off.

Commenting on the newly announced “Ain’t No Party Like A S Club Party Tour”, the band released a statement: “We can’t wait to get back out on tour, see you all and celebrate the songs, memories and moments we’ve shared together over the years. It’s going to be one BIG party, and we want you all to be part of it!”

They will be joined by special guests Boyzlife, the supergroup made up of the former Boyzone and Westlife members Keith Duffy and Brian McFadden, on all dates.

The tour is promoted by SJM Concerts. S Club are represented by CAA worldwide.
