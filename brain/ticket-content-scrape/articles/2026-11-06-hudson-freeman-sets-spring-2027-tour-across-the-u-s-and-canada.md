# Hudson Freeman Sets Spring 2027 Tour Across the U.S. and Canada

## Summary

Singer-songwriter Hudson Freeman announced a spring 2027 North American tour in support of his forthcoming album *Doom Country*, following a run of UK and European dates. The tour opens in Pioneertown, California, and continues through the western U.S., three Canadian cities, and on to the East Coast.

### Key Points
- Tour supports forthcoming album *Doom Country*.
- Opens in Pioneertown, CA, after UK/Europe dates; includes San Diego, LA, Portland, Seattle.
- Canadian stops: Vancouver, Toronto, Montreal.
- First leg concludes Phoenix, April 10; second leg resumes Philadelphia, May 1.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Hudson-Freeman.webp)

Hudson Freeman has announced a spring 2027 North American tour in support of his forthcoming album, *Doom Country*, with performances across the United States and Canada.

The New York-based singer-songwriter will begin the run in Pioneertown, California, following a series of concerts in the United Kingdom and Europe.

Following the Pioneertown opener, the western portion of the tour includes appearances in San Diego and Los Angeles before continuing to Portland and Seattle. Freeman will also travel to Vancouver, one of three Canadian cities identified in the announced routing, alongside Toronto and Montreal.

Additional stops during the first stretch include Salt Lake City and Denver. A Phoenix performance on April 10 will conclude that portion of the schedule, followed by a break before Freeman returns to the road on May 1 in Philadelphia.

The May itinerary shifts the tour toward the East Coast, the South and the Midwest. Washington, D.C., Atlanta and Nashville are among the scheduled destinations, with further performances planned in Chicago, Detroit, Cleveland and Pittsburgh.

Freeman will also play Toronto and Montreal during the second stretch before closing the North American run in Boston on May 22.

Sean Solomon, Hiding Places, James Eichman and Sam Tudor are scheduled to support Freeman on select dates.

Tickets will initially be available through an artist presale beginning Monday, November 9, at 10:00 a.m. local time. Fans can register through Seated on Freeman’s official website, selecting the concert dates for which they want presale access. General ticket sales will follow Friday, November 13, at 10:00 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster and [Freeman’s website](https://hudsonfreeman.com/).
