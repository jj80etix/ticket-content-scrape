# Oak View Group to Operate New 5,800-Capacity Sudbury Event Centre

## Summary

Oak View Group has been selected to manage the new 5,800-capacity Greater Sudbury Event Centre in Ontario, opening fall 2028, under a 10-year agreement (with a 10-year option) covering booking, ticketing, premium seating, box office, F&B, sponsorships and maintenance. The venue will be the home of two local franchises, the OHL's Sudbury Wolves and the Basketball Super League's Sudbury Five.

### Key Points
- OVG wins competitive procurement to operate the 5,800-cap. Greater Sudbury Event Centre (opening fall 2028); construction underway since 2025.
- Initial 10-year term with a 10-year renewal option; OVG will also make a capital contribution.
- Venue becomes the long-term home of the Sudbury Wolves (OHL) and Sudbury Five (Basketball Super League).

Oak View Group has been selected to manage and operate Greater Sudbury’s new 5,800-capacity Event Centre in Ontario, with the venue currently scheduled to open in fall 2028.

The city chose OVG following a competitive procurement process for the arena, which has been under construction since 2025. Under the agreement, OVG will oversee the full range of venue operations, including event booking and promotion, ticketing, premium seating, box office services, food and beverage, sponsorships and facility maintenance.

The initial agreement will run for 10 years and includes an option for an additional 10-year term. OVG will also make a significant capital contribution to the facility.

“Selecting an experienced venue operator is one of the most important decisions we can make before opening our doors,” said Shari Lichterman, Greater Sudbury CAO. “OVG brings the industry relationships, operational expertise and experience needed to position Greater Sudbury for success.”

OVG Canada President Tom Pistore said the company sees the project as an opportunity to create a major entertainment destination for Northern Ontario.

“We look forward to partnering with SWSE and the City of Sudbury on creating a world-class, state-of-the-art Event Centre here in Sudbury designed to attract top-notch entertainment, attract more visitors from across Ontario into Sudbury, and ultimately, generate a boost to the economic impact on the region,” Pistore said.

“Sudbury is ready for a new arena and we’re excited to be part of this project.”

Greater Sudbury is located about 248 miles north of Toronto and is one of Northern Ontario’s major regional centers. The city is known for its mining industry and is home to more than 300 lakes.

The management contract adds to OVG’s existing footprint in Canada. The company’s portfolio includes TD Coliseum in Hamilton and Canada Life Place in London, Ontario, while OVG Hospitality also provides food and beverage services at Canadian venues including Rogers Stadium.

With construction underway, an operator selected and long-term sports tenants secured, the Event Centre remains on track for a fall 2028 opening.
