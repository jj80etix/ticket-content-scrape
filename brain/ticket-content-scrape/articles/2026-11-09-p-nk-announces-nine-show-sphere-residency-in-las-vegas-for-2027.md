# P!NK Announces Nine-Show Sphere Residency in Las Vegas for 2027

## Summary

TicketNews' coverage of P!NK's newly announced nine-show Sphere residency in Las Vegas for fall 2027 (Sept. 29-Oct. 30), promoted by Live Nation with tickets listed on Ticketmaster. Duplicate coverage of the same announcement reported via Pollstar (see companion note).

### Key Points
- All nine shows listed at 9 p.m. local time on Ticketmaster.
- Follows a touring run (Summer Carnival, TRUSTFALL, P!NK Live) credited with 4M+ tickets sold across 130+ dates in 15 countries.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Pink-Sphere-announcement-1200-11-9-26.webp)

P!NK has announced a nine-show residency at Sphere in Las Vegas for fall 2027, with performances scheduled from Sept. 29 through Oct. 30. Promoted by Live Nation, the engagement will bring the three-time Grammy winner to the immersive venue for a monthlong run.

The residency opens Wednesday, Sept. 29, followed by five dates in early October and three additional performances near the end of the month. All nine shows are currently listed for 9 p.m. local time on [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/p-nk-tickets/artist/718655?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The announcement follows her extensive 2023 and 2024 touring schedule, which included the Summer Carnival Tour, TRUSTFALL Tour and P!NK Live performances. The announcement credits those runs with selling more than 4 million tickets across more than 130 dates in 15 countries.

Fans can register for the artist presale through [P!NK’s official website](https://www.pinkspage.com/us/presale-info?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The presale begins Wednesday, Nov. 18. Ticketmaster currently lists a 10 a.m. Pacific start for the first six shows and a noon Pacific start for the Oct. 27, 29 and 30 performances.

The general public on-sale begins Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. Pacific for all nine dates, following other presales during the week. The official announcement lists 10 a.m. for the artist presale generally, so fans looking at the three late-October shows should confirm the time on their specific event page before purchasing.

The ticketing platform states that displayed ticket prices are inclusive of fees before any applicable taxes. Individual standard-ticket prices were not provided in the announcement.

Vibee will sell [concert and hotel packages](https://pink.vibee.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) beginning Wednesday, Nov. 11, at 10 a.m. Pacific. Packages include a ticket to a selected performance and a two-night stay at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas, with seating options ranging from general admission floor to reserved seats. Packages without hotel accommodations are also planned.

Separately, a P!NK-themed promotion will temporarily transform the SlotZilla zipline at Fremont Street Experience into “P!NKZILLA.” The activation is scheduled for Nov. 11-19, with music and custom visuals displayed on the Fremont Street canopy at 5, 7 and 9 p.m.

Sphere’s wraparound LED display and immersive sound system provide a different production environment from the stadiums and arenas P!NK has played on previous tours. Her performances have prominently featured aerial acrobatics, including [performer-flying effects on the Summer Carnival Tour](https://www.taittowers.com/work/pink-summer-carnival-tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), but the Sphere announcement did not disclose specific staging plans.

P!NK has a substantial track record as a live draw. [TicketNews previously reported](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/03/pnks-summer-carnival-tour-smashes-ticket-sales-records/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that her Summer Carnival concerts in Australia and New Zealand sold nearly 1 million tickets, setting a regional record for a female headliner.

Earlier this year, P!NK hosted the 79th annual Tony Awards at Radio City Music Hall. Her upcoming Sphere dates also arrive during the 25th-anniversary year of her breakthrough album *Missundaztood*, which produced the singles “Get the Party Started,” “Just Like a Pill” and “Family Portrait.”

She joins a growing roster of major acts booked at Sphere for 2027. [Rush recently announced five May performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/rush-sphere-residency-may-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) at the Las Vegas venue, while John Mayer and Carín León are also scheduled to appear there next year.
