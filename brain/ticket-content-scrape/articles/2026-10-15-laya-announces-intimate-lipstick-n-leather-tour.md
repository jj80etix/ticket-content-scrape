Laya has announced the “Lipstick N’ Leather” tour, an intimate 5-city trek beginning on Nov. 7 at Center Stage Vinyl in Atlanta.

The singer-songwriter will then perform at the Peppermint Club in Los Angeles, Cafe Du Nord in San Francisco, the Bronze Peacock in Houston, and will conclude at The Foundry in Philadelphia. The “Lipstick N’ Leather” tour is in collaboration with Live Nation Urban. The tour announcement follows the singer’s addition to Jai’Len Josey’s “Serial Romantic Tour,” Josey’s first headlining tour. Laya’s last appearance on that trek will be at the Vinyl in Atlanta on Oct. 27.

Laya, a native of Staten Island, NY, has been on a consistent rise since getting the attention of fans with her 2022 single, “Sock It 2 Me.”Since then, the songstress has released two albums, 2022’s *Um, Hello*, and 2023’s *Bet That*. Her latest single, “Yeeha,” which was released in September, has gone viral on TikTok and Instagram, introducing new audiences to her DIY musicianship.
