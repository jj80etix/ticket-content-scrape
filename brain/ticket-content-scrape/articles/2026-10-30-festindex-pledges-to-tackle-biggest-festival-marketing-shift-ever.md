# FestIndex pledges to tackle ‘biggest festival marketing shift ever’

## Summary

IQ reports that FestIndex, a new social-media benchmarking tool founded by Steve Jenner, aims to help festivals adapt to what he calls the industry's biggest-ever marketing shift, as platforms move from follower-based feeds to interest-based algorithmic distribution. The article is paywalled after the opening paragraphs, so Jenner's specific recommendations and FestIndex's product details aren't available in this excerpt.

### Key Points
- FestIndex positions itself as a benchmarking tool for festival social-media performance
- Core thesis: social platforms now surface "content the algorithm thinks you're interested in" rather than content from accounts you follow, undercutting festivals' organic reach
