# Janet Jackson, Airtab Music Announce “Control 40 Live Concert” At Amerant Bank Arena

## Summary

Janet Jackson and Airtab Music announced a one-night "Control 40 Live Concert" on Dec. 12 at Amerant Bank Arena, marking the 40th anniversary of her Control album and doubling as a concert-film shoot for theatrical and streaming release. Tickets go on sale Aug. 21 via SeatGeek, with a new VIP floor experience featuring reserved premium seating and hospitality access.

### Key Points
- One-off show at Amerant Bank Arena, Sunrise FL, Dec. 12; filmed for a Janet Jackson: Control 40 movie.
- Setlist spans Control-era and career-spanning hits ("Miss You Much," "Escapade," "All for You").
- Tickets sold via SeatGeek starting 2 p.m. ET Aug. 21.
- New limited VIP floor tier with reserved premium seating, high-tops and dedicated hospitality access.

## Transcript

Janet Jackson and Airtab Music have joined forces to bring to life a special performance to celebrate the 40th anniversary of the singer’s 1986 critically acclaimed LP, *Control*.

The performance is scheduled for Dec. 12 at Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, FL, which will also be filmed for *Janet Jackson: Control 40 — The Airtab Music Movie*, directed by Leslie Small. Airtab Music will produce and present the show, which honors Janet Jackson’s landmark album. The movie will also feature behind-the-scenes footage of the tour.

The show will be inspired not only by *Control* but also by songs and albums that have positioned Janet Jackson as a global icon and helped shape the music industry. Tracks to be featured in the performance include hits like “Miss You Much,” “Escapade,” “Love Will Never Do (Without You),” “If,” “Again,” “That’s the Way Love Goes,” “Together Again,” and “All for You.”

Tickets for the Dec. 12 event are available starting at 2 p.m. ET at [SeatGeek.com](https://seatgeek.com/?dd_referrer=).

As per an official press release, “the arena floor will feature a first-of-its-kind VIP experience, offering fans an entirely new and elevated way to experience Janet Jackson live with reserved premium seating, VIP tables, high-top seating, and dedicated access to select hospitality areas.”

Furthermore, as per the press release, the project “is currently being positioned for a global theatrical release, followed by worldwide distribution across major streaming platforms, bringing this landmark concert film to audiences around the globe.”

The available VIP floor seating, designed specifically for this event, combines exceptional views of the performance with premium amenities and exclusive, controlled-access spaces. VIP floor experiences will be extremely limited, making this a truly unique and exclusive way to experience Janet Jackson live.

“Few artists have changed the course of popular music the way Janet Jackson has,” said Dennis Cunningham, Founder and CEO of Airtab Music. “ *Control* wasn’t simply a hit album—it was a cultural turning point that empowered an entire generation and redefined what a global superstar could be. We are incredibly proud that Airtab Music is producing this landmark film in partnership with Janet, Randy Jackson and their team. *Janet Jackson: Control 40 — The Airtab Music Movie* represents exactly the kind of ambitious global music entertainment we want Airtab Music to be known for: iconic artists, extraordinary live moments and premium filmed experiences built to reach audiences around the world.”
