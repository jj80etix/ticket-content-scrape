# Bret Michaels Plans October Stage Return Following 2026 Tour Cancellation

## Summary

Bret Michaels will resume his "Live & Amplified" solo tour Oct. 16 in Portage, Indiana, after emergency surgery forced him to cancel earlier 2026 dates, with additional shows booked in Michigan and a Pennsylvania "Hometown Holiday Show." He has also floated a possible Poison reunion with the band's original lineup in 2027 or 2028.

### Key Points
- Michaels resumes touring Oct. 16 at Founders Square, Portage, IN, replacing a canceled Aug. 7 date.
- Additional shows: Island Resort & Casino (MI), Nov. 13-14; Palace Theatre, Greensburg, PA, Nov. 29.
- PA show tickets went on public sale Sept. 21 after a Sept. 18 donor presale.
- Michaels says he's interested in reuniting Poison's original lineup in 2027 or 2028, health permitting.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bret-michaels.jpg)

Bret Michaels is preparing to resume his solo touring schedule in October after emergency surgery forced him to cancel performances earlier this year. The Poison frontman has also expressed interest in reuniting the band’s original lineup in 2027 or 2028, depending on his health.

His Live & Amplified tour is scheduled to return October 16 at the Amphitheater at Founders Square in Portage, Indiana. The performance replaces a concert originally booked for August 7. Michaels will also play Island Resort & Casino in Harris, Michigan, on November 13 and November 14.

Encouraging fans to purchase tickets in a recent Instagram post, Michaels said, “You know I’m gonna give it all I’ve got.”

The singer has also announced a November 29 performance at the Palace Theatre in Greensburg, Pennsylvania, billed as a “Hometown Holiday Show.” Tickets went on sale to the public September 21 at 10 a.m., following a September 18 presale for Westmoreland Cultural Trust donors. The performance begins at 7:30 p.m., according to the venue’s event listing.

Michaels returned to the stage August 21 in Sugar Hill, Georgia. The following day, however, he announced that he and his doctors had decided to cancel the remaining shows on his 2026 schedule so he could continue recovering.

His recent posts describe a renewed goal of performing a limited number of concerts. Writing about living with diabetes, Michaels discussed the challenges of managing the condition and how those experiences have shaped his determination.

Beyond the fall concerts, he is scheduled to appear aboard Rock Legends Cruise XIV, which runs February 21 through February 25, 2027. The sailing will travel from Miami to Costa Maya, Mexico.

Other acts on the cruise lineup include Foreigner, George Thorogood and the Destroyers, Collective Soul, Alan Parsons Live Project, Gin Blossoms and The Fixx.
