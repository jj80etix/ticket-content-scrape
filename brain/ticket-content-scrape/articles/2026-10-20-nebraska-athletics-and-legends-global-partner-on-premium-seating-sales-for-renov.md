# Nebraska Athletics And Legends Global Partner On Premium Seating Sales For Renovated Memorial Stadium

## Summary

Nebraska Athletics partnered with Legends Global, which will lead premium seating sales for the renovated Memorial Stadium under the two-phase "Big Red Rebuild" project (Phase 1 in 2027: new West Suites, Theater Boxes, updated West Club; Phase 2 in 2028: luxury loge boxes, luxury club, Bunker Club). Legends Global was previously named the project's Owner's Representative in April.

### Key Points
- Legends Global to lead premium-seating sales for Nebraska's renovated Memorial Stadium ("Big Red Rebuild").
- Phase 1 (2027): new West Suites, Theater Boxes, updated West Club. Phase 2 (2028): luxury loge boxes, luxury club, Bunker Club.
- Legends Global already serves as the project's Owner's Representative (named April 2026).
- Legends Global also handles similar premium-seating/venue projects at Kansas, Northwestern, Maryland, Notre Dame, Oklahoma, USC, Wisconsin, Utah, Villanova and San Diego State.

## Transcript

![NebraskaMemorialStadium](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/NebraskaMemorialStadium-scaled.jpg)

Memorial Stadium rendering courtesy Nebraska Athletics and Legends Global

Nebraska Athletics and Legends Global announced a new partnership in which Legends Global will lead premium seating sales for the transformed Memorial Stadium. As part of the Big Red Rebuild campaign, the historic stadium will be modernized for current and future generations of Nebraskans.

The premium seating project will be completed in two phases, with Phase 1 scheduled for completion in 2027 and Phase 2 in 2028. Legends Global will sell a distinct portfolio of premium products across the two years. In 2027, fans can expect new West Suites, new Theater Boxes and an updated West Club. In 2028, the offering will expand to include new luxury loge boxes, a new luxury club and a new Bunker Club.

“The Big Red Rebuild is about honoring the traditions that make Memorial Stadium special while ensuring it remains one of the premier venues in college football for generations to come,” said Troy Dannen, Nebraska Director of Athletics in the announcement. “Our partnership with Legends Global is an important part of that effort. They have the experience and expertise necessary to help us deliver the variety of premium seating options and game day experience that Husker fans deserve.”

“Memorial Stadium is one of the most iconic venues in college football, and the Big Red Rebuild is a once-in-a-generation opportunity to honor that tradition while delivering a premium experience that today’s fans expect,” added Tim Statezni, Executive Vice President, Sales, Legends Global. “Our team is focused on matching the passion of Husker Nation with best-in-class seating products and ensuring every fan finds a way to be part of it.”

In April, it was announced that Legends Global would serve as Owner’s Representative for the Big Red Rebuild. As Owner’s Rep, the company will help guide the project from vision to delivery, aligning stakeholders, overseeing execution and ensuring the fan experience remains top of mind. The result will be a fan-first reimagining of the stadium, with improved sightlines and seating, a fully connected 360° concourse, enhanced amenities and greater accessibility throughout.

In addition Memorial Stadium, Legends Global is involved in renovations and new facility projects at the University of Kansas, Northwestern University, the University of Maryland, the University of Notre Dame, the University of Oklahoma, the University of Southern California, the University of Wisconsin-Madison, the University of Utah, Villanova University and San Diego State University, among others.
