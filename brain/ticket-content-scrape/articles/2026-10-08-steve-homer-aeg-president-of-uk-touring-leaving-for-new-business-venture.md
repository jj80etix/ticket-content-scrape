# Steve Homer, AEG President of UK Touring, Leaving For New Business Venture

## Summary

Steve Homer, AEG Presents' President of UK Touring, is leaving the company after 10 years to pursue a new business venture, according to an internal memo from AEG Presents UK & European Festivals CEO Jim King. Homer will continue collaborating with AEG on select co-promoted tours.

### Key Points
- Homer joined AEG Presents UK in 2016 as co-CEO alongside Toby Leighton-Pope (who left in 2022); both previously spent 15 years at Live Nation.
- Homer moved to President, UK Touring when Jim King was promoted to CEO of AEG Presents UK & European Festivals in summer 2025.
- Internal memo says Homer will keep working with AEG on selected co-promoted tours going forward.
- No successor or new venture details were disclosed in the memo.

## Transcript

![Steve Homer](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Steve_Homer.jpg)

Steve Homer in 2019.

Steve Homer, AEG’s President UK Touring, is leaving after 10 years of helping lead the promoter’s UK business, *Pollstar* has learned.

In a memo to the AEG team obtained by *Pollstar*, Jim King, CEO of AEG Presents UK and European Festivals, informed staff that “after a decade at AEG Present UK, Steve Homer will be leaving the company to pursue a new business venture.”

Homer had joined AEG Presents UK in 2016 as co-CEO alongside Toby Leighton-Pope. Both men had joined after having spent 15 years at Live Nation. [Leighton-Pope left AEG Presents in 2022](https://news.pollstar.com/2022/01/04/toby-leighton-pope-leaves-aeg-presents-uk/), an Homer continued to lead the division.

When [Jim King was promoted to](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/07/23/aeg-presents-elevates-jim-king-to-chief-executive-officer-aeg-presents-uk-european-festivals/) the role of Chief Executive Officer of AEG Presents UK and European Festivals last summer, Homer transitioned to a new position as President, UK Touring at the company, as part of King’s team.

The memo continues to state that Homer “will continue to work together” with AEG Presents “on selected co-promoted tours, and we look forward to continuing our collaboration on future projects.

“We thank Steve for his contribution over the last ten years and wish him every success in the future.”

Homer is one of the UK’s most experienced live execs. While at AEG Presents, he is credited with expanding both its events division and UK venue portfolio, including [The Halls Wolverhampton](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/03/21/aeg-presents-appoints-gm-for-the-halls-wolverhampton-uk/), where he went to his first gig ever; and [British Airways ARC, which just opened at London Olympia](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/19/british-airways-arc-london-announces-opening-lineup/?_gl=1%2A1v8emis%2A_ga%2AMTM4MzMyNDM3Ny4xNzgyOTc2ODgw%2A_ga_5B6XRECYRM%2AczE3OTE0NzI5MDQkbzE5NiRnMSR0MTc5MTQ3MzA4MyRqNjAkbDAkaDA.). He also grew the AEG Presents UK promoter team, in addition to continuing to promote his own roster, which includes The Hives, Katy Perry, Kylie, Pet Shop Boys, Billy Joel, and others.

The news of Homer’s departure from AEG Presents follows parent company AEG’s realignment of its international business divisions in the beginning of last year.

As part of this change, AEG implemented a new management structure with Adam Wilkes assuming responsibility for AEG Presents’ regional operations in Asia-Pacific and Europe, in the new position of President and CEO of AEG Presents, Europe and Asia-Pacific, and Alex Hill assuming leadership of all of AEG’s global real estate and venue operations outside of the United States, in the new position of President and CEO, AEG International.
