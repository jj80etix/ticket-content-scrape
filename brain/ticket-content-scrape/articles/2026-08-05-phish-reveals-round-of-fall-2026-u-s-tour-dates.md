# Phish Reveals Round of Fall 2026 U.S. Tour Dates

## Summary

Phish added a new round of fall 2026 U.S. tour dates in October, including a three-night run at Atlantic City's Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall, two Richmond shows, a Jacksonville stop, and two nights at The Orion Amphitheater in Huntsville, Alabama. This follows their previously announced three-night Labor Day Weekend stand at DICK's Sporting Goods Park.

### Key Points
- New October dates: Atlantic City (Oct. 2-4), Richmond (Oct. 6-7), Jacksonville (Oct. 9), and Huntsville (Oct. 10-11).
- Previously announced Labor Day run at DICK's Sporting Goods Park in Commerce City, CO runs Sept. 4-6.
- A ticket request period for the new shows runs through Aug. 10; remaining tickets go on general sale via Ticketmaster starting Aug. 14.
- Limited travel packages are also available separately.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/phish.jpg)

Phish is returning to the road this fall.

The jamband icons revealed a new round of dates this October, including a three-date run at Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall in Atlantic City from October 2 to 4. From there, they’ll head to Richmond for two gigs at Allianz Amphitheater at Riverfront, followed by a gig in Jacksonville. The brief run wraps-up with two consecutive shows at The Orion Amphitheater in Huntsville, Alabama October 10 and 11.

As previously announced, Phish will play three nights at DICK’s Sporting Goods Park Labor Day Weekend from September 4 through 6.

A ticket request period for the newly-announced shows is now underway [here](https://tickets.phish.com), continuing through August 10 at 12 p.m. ET. All remaining tickets will be available to the general public starting on Friday, August 14 at 10 a.m. ET via [Ticketmaster](https://ticketmaster.evyy.net/4aqyB0). A limited number of travel packages are also up-for-grabs [here](https://phishfall.100xhospitality.com).
