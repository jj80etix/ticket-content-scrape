# Zach Bryan Sets Record At Gillette Stadium With 73,538 Fans

## Summary

Zach Bryan set the all-time attendance record at Gillette Stadium on Oct. 3, drawing 73,538 fans during the second of two Foxborough shows on his "With Heaven On Tour." The performance extends a record-setting run for Bryan, who in Sept. 2025 drew 112,408 fans at Michigan Stadium — the largest ticketed concert audience in U.S. history.

### Key Points
- Oct. 3 Gillette Stadium show drew 73,538 fans, the largest crowd for any event in the venue's history.
- Part of a 40+ date "With Heaven On Tour" spanning North America and Europe, launched March 2026.
- Follows Bryan's Sept. 27, 2025 Michigan Stadium show (112,408 fans), the largest ticketed concert audience in U.S. history, surpassing George Strait's 2024 Texas record (110,905) and setting a $5M single-night merch record.
- 2025 stadium run also included three MetLife Stadium shows and a Notre Dame Stadium performance.

Zach Bryan sets Gillette Stadium record on Oct. 3. Photo courtesy of AEG Presents

Zach Bryan broke the Gillette Stadium attendance record Oct. 3 performing for 73,538 fans, the largest crowd for any event in the venue’s history.

The show was the second of two nights at the stadium, as part of Bryan’s “With Heaven On Tour.” The Foxborough shows are part of Bryan’s biggest tour yet, spanning more than 40 dates across North America and Europe, which launched in March 2026. The tour follows a run of sold-out stadium shows in 2025.

The milestone adds to a record-setting year for Bryan.

On Sept. 27, 2025, he became the first artist ever to play Michigan Stadium, drawing 112,408 fans, the largest ticketed concert audience in U.S. history. That show surpassed the previous mark of 110,905, set by George Strait in Texas in 2024, and also set a single-night merchandise sales record of $5 million.

The hallmark capped a 2025 stadium run that included three shows at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, and a performance at Notre Dame Stadium.
