# Dave Chappelle Adds Second ‘Karmageddon’ Madison Square Garden Date

## Summary

Dave Chappelle has added a second Madison Square Garden show (Nov. 7) to his "Karmageddon" tour, in response to demand for the previously announced Nov. 6 date, with the pair now anchoring the New York Comedy Festival.

### Key Points
- New second date: Nov. 7 at MSG, added to the existing Nov. 6 show.
- Both shows fold into the New York Comedy Festival lineup.
- Tour launched with a summer arena run (Baltimore, Detroit, Cleveland, Chicago, San Diego) and kicks off its fall leg Oct. 20 in Nashville.
- General onsale for the new date began Sept. 17 via LiveNation.com.

Dave Chappelle has added a second New York City show in response to overwhelming demand for his previously unveiled show at Madison Square Garden on Nov. 6. Now, Chappelle will also perform at MSG on Nov. 7 as a part of the New York Comedy Festival and his “Karmageddon” tour.

Dave Chappelle previously announced the “Karmageddon” tour for fall 2026 with stops in Toronto, Austin, and Houston. The tour kicks off at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, TN, on Oct. 20. See the full tour routing below. Presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale, which begins Sept. 17 at noon local time at [LiveNation.com.](https://www.livenation.com/) For more information and to purchase tickets, please visit [https://nycomedyfestival.com](https://nycomedyfestival.com).

“Dave is one of the defining comedians of his generation and, without question, one of the greatest stand-up comedians of all time,” said Caroline Hirsch, founder and owner of the New York Comedy Festival. “To have Dave close out his tour by opening our festival is spectacular.”

The fall dates follow a busy summer. Chappelle played a June arena run through Baltimore, Detroit, Cleveland, Chicago, and San Diego, then returned to his hometown of Yellow Springs, Ohio, for the sixth year of Dave Chappelle’s Summer Camp—his series of intimate, outdoor shows at the Wirrig Pavilion. He closed the season abroad, bringing “The Pulling Up Tour” to London for two sold-out nights at the Eventim Apollo before hosting Ms. Lauryn Hill’s inaugural UK “Diaspora Calling!” festival at the Milton Keynes National Bowl.
