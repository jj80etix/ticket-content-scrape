# Festival news: Spilt Milk leads flurry of Australian announcements

## Summary

A roundup of Australian and US festival announcements led by Spilt Milk's four-city 2026 lineup, headlined by Lewis Capaldi and Raye. The piece also covers new lineups and dates from Beyond the Valley, Wildlands Festivals, CMC Rocks' 20th-anniversary 2027 edition, and San Diego's Mission Bayfest.

### Key Points
- Spilt Milk reveals its first lineup tranche for a four-stop tour Dec 12–20, 2026 (Canberra, Gold Coast, Geelong, Perth), headlined by Lewis Capaldi and Raye, following its earlier move from Ballarat to Geelong.
- Beyond the Valley (Barunah Plains, Victoria, Dec 28–Jan 1) headlined by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, Ki/Ki, and Skepta.
- Wildlands Festivals sets Perth (Dec 31) and Brisbane (Jan 2) dates with largely the same top-line headliners.
- CMC Rocks announces its 20th-anniversary edition for March 17–21, 2027 at Willowbank Raceway, Queensland, with artist announcements to follow later in 2026.
- San Diego's Mission Bayfest reveals a genre-themed three-day October lineup spanning reggae, R&B, and punk/rock/ska acts.

## Transcript

Lineup reveals and date announcements also come from Beyond The Valley, Wildlands, CMC Rocks, plus California's Mission Bayfest

Leading a swathe of Australian festival announcements, **Spilt Milk** has revealed its first tranche of artists for its four-stop travelling edition for 2026.

Topping the bill are Lewis Capaldi and Raye, who will both play their only Australian dates at the festival.

They will be joined at the festival by Kettama, Baby Keem, Maisie Peters, Remi Wolf, and DMA’s, among others. The lineup announcement comes a week after Spilt Milk announced it will be [moving](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/festival-news-australias-spilt-milk-to-move-victorian-home/) its Victoria edition to Geelong after four editions in Ballarat, where it is set to continue through 2030.

The festival will play four editions between 12 December and 20 December at Exhibition Park, Canberra, Gold Coast Sports Precinct, Gold Coast, Kardinia Park Stadium and Precinct, Geelong, and Claremont Showground, Perth, respectively.

Also in Australia, **Beyond the Valley** has revealed its 2026 edition will be led by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, Ki/Ki, and Skepta.

The event is set for Barunah Plains in Victoria between 28 December and 1 January, presented by Untitled Group and Triple J.

John Summit, Ki/Ki, and Skepta will also all headline **Wildlands Festivals,** organisers have announced. The festival’s Perth event will take place on 31 December before setting up shop in Brisbane on 2 January. The two one-day events will be separated by a 2,700 mile trip across the country, with organisers setting up three stages at each site.

In Queensland, meanwhile, **CMC Rocks** has revealed dates for its 20th anniversary festival for 2027, shortly after wrapping up this year’s edition. The milestones festival will return to Willowbank Raceway from 17-21 March next year, with organisers saying artists announcements will begin later in 2026.

Festival director and chairman of Chugg Entertainment, Michael Chugg said: “The legacy of CMC Rocks runs deep, as the festival continues to grow and blow even our minds. The sense of community and country spirit we experience each year is like nothing in this world and we’re proud to be able to connect our community with the hottest local and international talent around.

“Bring on 2027! It will be a wonderful memorial and celebration of the late Rob Potts’ vision of country music and Australia.”

Finally in the US, San Diego’s **Mission Bayfest** has also revealed its lineup for its October event, with organisers pledging “a stacked lineup of reggae, pop, and alternative talent that embodies Southern California’s laid-back culture”.

With a three-day schedule, the festival devotes each day to a different genre, with 16 October dubbed American Reggae Friday, and featuring Rebelution, Steel Pulse, The Movement, and Surfer Girl, among others.

Saturday 17 October is set to be a day of R&B lead by artists including Kolohe Kai, Common Kings, and Soja. The festival’s final day is reserved punk, rock, and ska, with a lineup of The Offspring, The Interrupters, Goldfinger, Sublime’s Jacob Nowell, and more.
