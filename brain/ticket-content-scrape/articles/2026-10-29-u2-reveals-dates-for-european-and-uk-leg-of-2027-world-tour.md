# U2 reveals dates for European and UK leg of 2027 world tour

## Summary

U2 revealed the European and UK leg of its Live Nation-promoted "Carnaval De Luz" tour, its first global run in eight years, with US and Spanish dates still to come. The European leg opens in Stockholm in late April 2027.

### Key Points
- European/UK leg opens April 26-28 at Stockholm's Strawberry Arena
- Continues with two nights at Paris' Stade de France and further major-city stadium stops
- First global U2 tour in eight years
- US and Spanish dates to be announced separately
