# Vive Latino 2027 Announces Lineup Featuring Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe and Placebo

## Summary

TicketNews's coverage of Mexico City's Vive Latino festival confirms its 2027 lineup — Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison, and Ed Maverick among over 50 acts — for the March 13-14, 2027 edition at Estadio GNP Seguros. The 27th edition continues the festival's shift from its original Spanish-rock focus toward a broader international, multi-genre bill.

### Key Points
- 27th edition of Vive Latino: March 13-14, 2027, Estadio GNP Seguros, Mexico City.
- Headliners include Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison, Ed Maverick; also Turnstile, Placebo, Kasabian.
- More than 50 acts total on the bill.
- Promoted by OCESA, presented by Amazon; festival running since 1998.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Vive-Latino.webp)

Mexico City’s Vive Latino festival has revealed its 2027 lineup, featuring Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison and Ed Maverick among the leading performers. The 27th edition of the annual festival will take place March 13–14 at Estadio GNP Seguros in Mexico City.

The two-day event will also feature Residente, Turnstile, Placebo, Kasabian, Ximena Sariñana, Kase.O, Los Auténticos Decadentes and 31 Minutos. More than 50 acts are scheduled to perform, bringing together established international rock bands, Latin alternative artists, hip-hop performers and emerging musicians.

The festival, promoted by OCESA and presented by Amazon, has become one of Mexico’s most prominent live music events since its launch in 1998. Originally focused on Spanish-language rock, Vive Latino has steadily expanded its programming to include a broader range of musical styles and international performers.

The 2027 edition continues that approach, pairing hard rock veterans Mötley Crüe and British alternative rock band Placebo with newer artists including Latin Mafia and Argentine rapper Milo J. Baltimore hardcore band Turnstile adds another international name to a lineup that also features British rock group Kasabian and Dutch symphonic metal band Epica.

Mötley Crüe’s appearance comes shortly before the band begins its previously announced [2027 Las Vegas residency](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/motley-crue-2027-las-vegas-residency/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace on March 19. The eight-show engagement will run through April 3.

Among the Latin American performers, Puerto Rican rapper and former Calle 13 frontman Residente joins Mexican alternative and indie artists Ed Maverick, Carla Morrison and Ximena Sariñana. The lineup also includes Mexican rock acts Zurdok, Jumbo, Cuca and La Gusana Ciega, alongside Argentine bands Los Auténticos Decadentes and Attaque 77.

Other performers reflect the festival’s increasingly varied programming, including Colombian reggae group Cultura Profética, ska bands Inspector and The Skatalites, and Chilean children’s musical television project 31 Minutos. The festival will also bring back its Música Pa’ Mandar A Volar series for a third installment.

Banamex cardholders will have access to a presale beginning November 11 at 2 p.m. local time, followed by the general onsale November 12 at 2 p.m. through Ticketmaster Mexico. First-phase two-day general admission passes start at 2,800 Mexican pesos, excluding service fees.
