# Decadence Arizona Unleashes Massive Phase One Lineup for New Year’s Eve 2026

## Summary

Decadence Arizona has announced its phase-one lineup for its New Year's Eve 2026 edition ("Sands of Eternity"), a two-night electronic festival at Phoenix Raceway on Dec. 30-31 headlined by Chris Lake, Eli Brown, I Hate Models, ISOxo, Kettama and Slander. General passes go on sale July 31, with an early-access presale beginning July 30; the festival is produced by Relentless Beats and Global Dance and is now entering its 12th year.

### Key Points
- Phase-one lineup includes Chris Lake, Eli Brown, I Hate Models, ISOxo, Kettama, Slander, and a back-to-back Max Dean/Luke Dean set.
- Two-night event Dec. 30-31, 2026 at Phoenix Raceway; a special countdown performer for 2027 is still to be announced.
- Presale opened July 30 at 10 a.m. PT; general on-sale (GA, GA+, VIP, Platinum) begins July 31 at 10 a.m. PT.
- Event is 18+, with table reservations restricted to guests 21 and older.
- Produced by Relentless Beats and Global Dance, now in its 12th year.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/decadence.jpg)

Decadence Arizona is preparing to send 2026 out with a bang.

The two-night New Year’s Eve festival has revealed the first wave of artists for its 2026 edition, dubbed *Sands of Eternity*, with Chris Lake, Eli Brown, I Hate Models, ISOxo, Kettama and Slander among the names headed to Phoenix Raceway on Dec. 30 and 31.

Also included in the phase one lineup are Beltran, Bunt., Levity, The Masquerade, RØZ and a back-to-back set from Max Dean and Luke Dean. Bass fans will also get Wankdat, a new collaborative set pairing Crankdat and Wooli.

More artists are still to come, including a special countdown performer who will ring in 2027.
