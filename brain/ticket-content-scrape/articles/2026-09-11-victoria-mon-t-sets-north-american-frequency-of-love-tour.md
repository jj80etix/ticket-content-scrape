# Victoria Monét Sets North American ‘Frequency of Love Tour’

## Summary

Victoria Monét announced a 27-city North American "Frequency of Love Tour" running Oct. 27-Dec. 17, 2026, supporting her forthcoming album of the same name, out Oct. 2 via RCA Records. Presales begin Sept. 14, with general on-sale Sept. 16.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Oct. 27 in Raleigh, NC; closes Dec. 17 in San Francisco.
- Stops include New York (MSG's Infosys Theater), Chicago, Toronto and Vancouver.
- Follows Monét's summer run opening for Bruno Mars across European/UK stadiums.
- Citi and Verizon presales start Sept. 14; public on-sale Sept. 16 via Monét's official site.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Victoria-Monet-tour.webp)

Victoria Monét will return to the road this fall for a 27-city North American tour supporting her forthcoming album, *Frequency of Love*.

The run is set to begin October 27 at Red Hat Amphitheater in Raleigh, North Carolina. The route continues through December 17, when Monét will close the run at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium in San Francisco.

The itinerary includes concerts in Atlanta, Boston, Chicago, Houston, Las Vegas, Philadelphia, Seattle and Washington, D.C. Monét will also perform at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California, and Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden in New York. Canadian dates are scheduled for Toronto and Vancouver.

*Frequency of Love* is scheduled for release October 2 through Lovett Music and RCA Records. The album follows Monét’s 2023 debut studio album, *Jaguar II*, and its expanded deluxe edition, released the following year.

The tour follows Monét’s summer run as a special guest on the European and U.K. portion of Bruno Mars’ “The Romantic Tour.” She performed at stadium concerts in Amsterdam, Berlin, London, Madrid, Milan and Paris.

Citi and Verizon presales begin September 14 at 8 a.m. local time. Monét’s artist presale opens the same day at 11 a.m. local time, with additional presales scheduled throughout the week.

Tickets go on sale to the general public September 16 at 10 a.m. local time through [Monét’s official website](https://www.victoriamonet.co/).
