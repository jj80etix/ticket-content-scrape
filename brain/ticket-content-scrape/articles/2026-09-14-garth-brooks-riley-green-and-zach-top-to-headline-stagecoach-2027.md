# Garth Brooks, Riley Green and Zach Top to Headline Stagecoach 2027

## Summary

Garth Brooks, Riley Green and Zach Top will headline the 2027 Stagecoach Festival at Indio's Empire Polo Club (April 23-25), with a Florida Georgia Line reunion set also on the bill.

### Key Points
- Green headlines Friday, Top leads Saturday, Brooks closes Sunday.
- Brooks previously joined Luke Combs at Stagecoach 2026 and is currently touring his "Blame It All On My Roots" arena run.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Stagecoach.webp)

Garth Brooks, Riley Green and Zach Top will headline the 2027 Stagecoach Festival, leading a lineup that also includes a reunion performance from Florida Georgia Line.

The country music festival will return to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, from April 23 through April 25. Green will headline Friday, Top will lead Saturday’s lineup and Brooks will close the festival Sunday.

Brooks’ appearance follows his return to full-scale touring with the 2026 [“Blame It All On My Roots” Arena Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/garth-brooks-expands-2026-blame-it-all-on-my-roots-arena-tour-with-two-salt-lake-city-shows/). He also appeared at Stagecoach in 2026, joining Luke Combs for a performance of “Friends in Low Places.”

“Stagecoach is a guaranteed good time,” Brooks said in a statement. “A MUST if you’re a Country music fan!”

Green returns after performing at the festival in 2026, while Top’s promotion to headliner follows his 2024 appearance on the Palomino Stage.

“When we played Stagecoach in 2024, it was one of the very first festivals where we really drew a crowd for our set,” Top said. “Now to be back in 2027 as a headliner is just something truly special.”

The bill spans multiple generations and genres. Willie Nelson & Family, HARDY, Brothers Osborne, Turnpike Troubadours, Cody Jinks, Charley Crockett, ERNEST, Shaboozey, Jo Dee Messina, Gretchen Wilson and Ingrid Andress are among the country performers scheduled to appear.

Additionally, Kelly Clarkson, Pitbull, Journey, Matchbox Twenty, David Lee Roth, Melissa Etheridge, The Fray and Tedeschi Trucks Band are included in the 2027 lineup. Clarkson’s performance will mark her Stagecoach debut.

Florida Georgia Line will return to the festival as part of the Late Night on Mustang Stage programming. The duo last performed at Stagecoach in 2018, when it headlined the festival. Tyler Hubbard and Brian Kelley announced a break in 2022 before reuniting for a surprise appearance at CMA Fest in June 2026.

“A whole lot of life has happened since then,” the duo said of its previous Stagecoach appearance, adding that it “couldn’t think of a better place” to reunite with fans. Sean Paul and Flo Rida will also lead late-night sets during the weekend.

Stagecoach passes go on sale Friday, September 18, at 11 a.m. PT. For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [stagecoachfestival.com](http://stagecoachfestival.com).
