# More Than 1 Million Bad Bunny Fans Flood Queue for Puerto Rico Finale Tickets

## Summary

More than 1.5 million fans joined the virtual queue for Bad Bunny's final two "DeBÍ TiRAR MáS FOToS" tour shows in Puerto Rico, vastly outstripping the 35,000-capacity Hiram Bithorn Stadium venue. Ticketing platform PR Tickets restricted sales to Banco Popular cardholders with a four-ticket-per-transaction cap, selling 80% of capacity within four hours and reaching full sellout under six hours after sales opened.

### Key Points
- 1.5 million fans in the virtual queue vs. 35,000 total seats across two shows (Aug. 22-23, Hiram Bithorn Stadium).
- PR Tickets limited sales to Banco Popular cardholders, capped at 4 tickets/transaction.
- 80% of capacity sold within 4 hours; fully sold out inside 6 hours of sale opening.
- Fans expressed frustration on social media and questioned whether additional shows would be added to meet demand.

### Highlights
- A single onsale drew a queue roughly 43x larger than available capacity — an extreme case study in demand-vs-supply queue management.
- Card-issuer-exclusive access (Banco Popular) plus a hard per-transaction cap were the primary demand-throttling levers used, alongside standard virtual queuing.

### Industry Problem
Massive demand imbalances at superstar-scale onsales overwhelm queuing systems and leave the vast majority of fans locked out, fueling frustration and pressure for platforms to prove queues are fair and bot-free at extreme scale.

### Proposed Solution
Continue to harden virtual-queue infrastructure for extreme-demand spikes (load-tested queue capacity, transparent queue-position communication, per-transaction/per-card caps) and consider partner-exclusive presale windows (as used here) as a demand-throttling tool for supply-constrained megashows.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bad-bunny.jpg)

Bad Bunny [just revealed](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/bad-bunny-to-close-out-debi-tirar-mas-fotos-world-tour-with-pair-of-puerto-rico-shows/) that he’ll be wrapping-up his massive global run in Puerto Rico this week, though fans hoping to catch one of the performances were met with dismay as over 1 million people joined the virtual ticketing queue.

The final two shows of the “DeBÍ TiRAR MáS FOToS World Tour” are set to take place on August 22 and 23 at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan, bringing the Latin superstar back to Puerto Rico following his sold-out, 31-date residency last year.

According to [*EFE*](https://efe.com/cultura/2026-08-19/bad-bunny-entradas-conciertos-puerto-rico/), 1.5 million people were waiting in the virtual queue on PR Ticket’s website when sales went live Wednesday morning. Limitations were put in place for the sale; tickets were exclusive to Banco Popular cardholders, and fans could only purchase up to four tickets per transaction.

Four hours into the sale, PR Tickets reported that 80% of the available capacity for the concerts had sold, and less than two hours later, the ticketing company confirmed all seats were sold out.
