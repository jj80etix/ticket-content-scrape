# Movers & Shakers: New Boss to lead Arcadia Live promoter team

## Summary

IQ's Movers & Shakers roundup reports Florian Plakolm taking over as head promoter at Vienna's Arcadia Live (an FKP Scorpio subsidiary) from Nov. 1, after nearly five years as a booker there developing festivals including LIDO Sounds and METAStadt Open Air. The item also references other personnel moves across Sziget Festival, London Stadium, Venu, whynow, AEG Presents and Citizen Group.

### Key Points
- Florian Plakolm becomes head promoter at Arcadia Live GmbH (Vienna) effective Nov. 1.
- He previously booked acts including Air, Blood Orange, Chet Faker, Flo Rida, IDLES and Jungle.
- Arcadia Live is a subsidiary of FKP Scorpio.
- Roundup also references appointments at Sziget Festival, London Stadium, Venu, whynow, AEG Presents and Citizen Group (full details paywalled).
