# Broadway’s ‘Harry Potter and the Cursed Child’ Announces Cast Changes

## Summary

Broadway's "Harry Potter and the Cursed Child" is rolling out a wave of cast changes beginning November 10 at the Lyric Theatre, with further swaps through February 2027 including Rupert Grint's previously announced return as Ron Weasley. Jocelyn Maher makes her Broadway debut as Ginny Potter, and Tom Felton continues as Draco Malfoy through early January before Ben Thys takes over.

### Key Points
- Jocelyn Maher and Matt Harrington join the company Nov. 10; Maher debuts as Ginny Potter, Harrington plays Ron Weasley through Jan. 31, 2027.
- Eight additional ensemble members join the production Nov. 10.
- Tom Felton continues as Draco Malfoy through Jan. 3, 2027; Ben Thys (from the North American tour) takes over Jan. 5.
- Rupert Grint begins his previously announced limited run as Ron Weasley Feb. 2, 2027, alongside Cody Combs' Broadway debut as Scorpius.
- The production has run continuously at the Lyric Theatre since its 2021 one-part relaunch.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/Harry-Potter.webp)

Jocelyn Maher and Matt Harrington will join Broadway’s “Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” on November 10 at the Lyric Theatre, beginning a series of cast changes scheduled through February 2027.

Maher will make her Broadway debut as Ginny Potter. Harrington, whose Broadway credits include “Leopoldstadt,” will play Ron Weasley through January 31, 2027, joining the company directly from the production’s North American tour.

Josh Bates, Markcus Blair, Danny Bó, Casey Butler, Erin Chupinsky, Lauryn Hayes, Jennifer Thiessen and Timmy Thompson will also enter the ensemble on November 10.

The production’s January cast changes will follow the departures of Tom Felton and Aidan Close. Felton, who played Draco Malfoy in the “Harry Potter” films, will continue in the same role on Broadway through January 3, 2027. Close will conclude his run as Draco’s son, Scorpius, that day.

“Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” is written by Jack Thorne and directed by John Tiffany, based on an original story by J.K. Rowling, Thorne and Tiffany. The story takes place 19 years after “Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows” and follows the children of Harry Potter and his former Hogwarts classmates.

The play premiered in London in July 2016 and opened on Broadway on April 22, 2018. Originally presented in two parts, the Broadway production earned 10 Tony Award nominations and won Best Play.

A condensed, one-part version opened at the Lyric Theatre on December 7, 2021, following the pandemic shutdown. The production introduced another shortened version in November 2024.

The creative team includes movement director Steven Hoggett, set designer Christine Jones, costume designer Katrina Lindsay, lighting designer Neil Austin and sound designer Gareth Fry. Imogen Heap provides music and arrangements, while Jamie Harrison oversees illusions and magic. Martin Lowe provides music supervision and arrangements.

Jim Carnahan handles U.S. casting for the production, which is produced by Sonia Friedman Productions, Colin Callender and Harry Potter Theatrical Productions.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit Broadway’s official [“Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” website](https://broadway.harrypottertheplay.com/).
