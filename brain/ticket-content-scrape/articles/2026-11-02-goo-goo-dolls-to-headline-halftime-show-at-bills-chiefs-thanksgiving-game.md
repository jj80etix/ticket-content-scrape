# Goo Goo Dolls to Headline Halftime Show at Bills-Chiefs Thanksgiving Game

## Summary

Goo Goo Dolls will headline the halftime show when the Buffalo Bills host the Kansas City Chiefs on Thanksgiving night (Nov. 26), marking a hometown performance for the Buffalo-formed band.

### Key Points
- Performance presented by M&T Bank, staged on the Bills Legends Deck for stadium-wide visibility.
- Other Thanksgiving halftime acts this season: Wu-Tang Clan (Lions-Bears), Miranda Lambert (Cowboys-Eagles), and U2 (Packers-Rams Thanksgiving Eve game).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Goo-Goo-Dolls-Halftime-Show.webp)

Goo Goo Dolls will headline the halftime show when the Buffalo Bills host the Kansas City Chiefs at Highmark Stadium on November 26, 2026. The Thanksgiving night matchup will air nationally on NBC.

The Buffalo band’s performance, presented by M&T Bank, will take place on the Bills Legends Deck. The team said the location will allow fans throughout the stadium to see the show.

**| RELATED:**[**Detroit Lions Announce Wu-Tang Clan as Thanksgiving Halftime Performer**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/detroit-lions-announce-wu-tang-clan-as-thanksgiving-halftime-performer/) **|**

Led by John Rzeznik and Robby Takac, Goo Goo Dolls formed in Buffalo in 1986. The Thanksgiving appearance will bring the group back in front of its hometown audience during the nationally televised game.

Goo Goo Dolls join a lineup of artists set to perform during the NFL’s Thanksgiving games. Miranda Lambert will headline halftime when the Dallas Cowboys host the Philadelphia Eagles at AT&T Stadium on November 26.

Wu-Tang Clan will perform during halftime of the Detroit Lions’ game against the Chicago Bears that same day. U2 will kick off the holiday’s halftime performances on November 25 during the NFL’s first Thanksgiving Eve game, featuring the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams.
