# Mötley Crüe Announces Eight-Show 2027 Las Vegas Residency at Caesars Palace

## Summary

Mötley Crüe will return to Las Vegas for an eight-show residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace, running March 19 through April 3, 2027, reviving its "Carnival of Sins" production under the title "Carnival of Sins in Sin City." It marks the band's fourth Las Vegas residency and first at this venue, following its 2026 "Return of the Carnival of Sins" North American tour with Tesla and Extreme.

### Key Points
- Eight shows, March 19 - April 3, 2027, at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace -- band's first residency at this venue.
- Fourth Las Vegas residency overall, after 2012, 2013 and 2025 engagements.
- General onsale Friday, Oct. 23, via Ticketmaster; S.I.N. Club and Citi presales begin Oct. 20, Caesars Rewards/Live Nation presale Oct. 22.
- A portion of proceeds benefits Nevada Partnership for Homeless Youth.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Motley-Crue.webp)

Mötley Crüe will return to Las Vegas next spring for an eight-show residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace, bringing its revived *Carnival of Sins* production to the Strip from March 19 through April 3, 2027.

The band announced the limited engagement under the title *Carnival of Sins in Sin City*. The performances mark Mötley Crüe’s fourth Las Vegas residency, following engagements in 2012, 2013 and 2025.

The new residency follows the band’s [*The Return of the Carnival of Sins* North American tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/motley-crue-announces-the-return-of-the-carnival-of-sins-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which revisited the group’s 2005-2006 production in celebration of its 20th anniversary and Mötley Crüe’s 45th year together. The 2026 tour traveled across North America from July through September, with special guests Tesla and Extreme.

Notably, the new shows represent a change of venue from Mötley Crüe’s most recent Las Vegas engagement, which took place at Dolby Live at Park MGM in 2025. That residency was [rescheduled from spring to fall](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/03/05/motley-crue-reschedules-las-vegas-residency/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) after frontman Vince Neil required a medical procedure. The band’s previous Las Vegas productions included *Mötley Crüe Takes On Sin City* in 2012 and *Evening in Hell* in 2013. The 2027 shows will mark its first residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace.
