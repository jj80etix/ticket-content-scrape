# 2.3M Fans, $345M Grossed, $2.2M Donated: The Weeknd Wraps 2026 UK/Euro Stadium Tour

## Summary

The Weeknd wrapped the 2026 UK/Europe leg of his "After Hours Til Dawn" stadium tour, grossing more than $345 million and drawing over 2 million fans across 42 dates, including a historic five-night Wembley stand and six sold-out nights at Stade de France. The run also raised $2.2 million for charity and delivered an estimated $618 million in UK/France economic impact.

### Key Points
- 42-date run grossed $345M+ and drew 2M+ fans; The Weeknd has led Pollstar's Artist Power Index for three straight weeks.
- First artist ever to sell six nights at Stade de France on one tour; 13 total French stadium sellouts across the tour.
- Raised $2.2M+ for the UN World Food Programme and Global Citizen.
- Since 2022, the tour has topped $1B in global gross and sold 7.5M+ tickets across 153 shows — the highest-grossing tour ever by a male solo artist.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2288361176](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2288361176.jpg)

The Weeknd performs live onstage during his “After Hours til Dawn tour” at PGE Narodowy in Warsaw, Poland, Aug. 4, 2026. Photo by Pedro Becerra/Getty Images for Live Nation

[The Weeknd](https://pollstar.com/charts/5180/artist-power-index/chart?variant=) wrapped his sold-out 2026 Europe and UK leg of his record-breaking “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour,” culminating with back-to-back shows at Estádio do Restelo in Lisbon, Portugal. The 42-date run marked The Weeknd’s second time bringing the tour to the region, following its first Europe and UK leg in 2023.

The 2026 leg grossed more than $345 million and welcomed more than 2 million fans, according to promoter Live Nation, with multi-night runs in nearly every market, including Manchester (UK), Munich (Germany), Milan (Italy), Stockholm (Sweden), Madrid (Spain), Dublin (Ireland), Paris (France), and more.

Thanks to this performance, The Weeknd has been leading Pollstar’s Artist Power Index for three weeks straight.

Throughout the run, the tour also raised more than $2.2 million in charitable donations benefiting the United Nations World Food Programme (WFP) and Global Citizen. The tour also delivered a major economic boost across Europe, with newly released figures from the UK and France alone showing more than $618 million in economic impact — nearly €532 million.

Kicking off June 11 with two nights at Manchester’s Etihad Stadium, the run included a historic five-night stand at London’s Wembley Stadium. Across Manchester and London, The Weeknd performed seven sold-out UK stadium shows for more than 450,000 fans, including ticket buyers from nearly 150 countries.

The Weeknd also made history in France, becoming the first artist ever to sell out six nights at Stade de France in Paris on a single tour. His 2026 performances drew more than 500,000 fans across France this summer alone. Across the full “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour,” The Weeknd has now sold out 13 stadium shows in France, further demonstrating the scale of his success in the market.

Since launching in 2022, the “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour” has traveled across North America, Europe, the UK, Latin America and Australia, with The Weeknd returning for second runs across nearly every territory.

[Last year, the record-breaking tour surpassed $1 billion in global gross following the addition of 2026 dates across Europe, the UK and Latin America](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/11/20/the-weeknd-makes-history-with-after-hours-til-dawn-tour-surpassing-1b-in-grosses/). With more than 7.5 million tickets sold across 153 shows, “After Hours Til Dawn” became the highest-grossing tour ever by a male solo artist, cementing its place among the biggest global tours in history.

The Weekend’s first visit to Europe on “After Hours Til Dawn” took place between June and August of 2023, when he performed 30 shows at stadiums in 15 countries in Europe, grossing over $158 million from 1.64 million sold tickets. That leg closed with a Aug. 18, 2023 performance at London’s iconic Wembley Stadium [where he set the attendance record for an end-stage configuration](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/08/23/the-weeknd-sets-an-end-stage-wembley-stadium-attendance-record/), selling out 89,179 tickets, and grossing $9,250,620, according to the box office report submitted to *Pollstar* for the show.

This latest leg of the tour featured a brand new 40-foot art sculpture designed by Hajime Sorayama, over two-times the size of the statue showcased on previous legs. First hand-carved from polystyrene and cast in Toyama by Japanese craftsmen, this unique one-of-one sculpture was finished in gold through a meticulous hand-coating process using multiple layers of high-gloss aluminum paint to achieve a luminous metallic surface. Created exclusively for The Weeknd’s “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour,” the sculpture was further customized with laser illumination embedded in the eyes and theatrical smoke effects emerging from the palm, transforming it into a living stage presence.

The After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour heads to Asia for its final leg in just a few weeks, kicking off on Saturday, Sept. 19 in Tokyo, before making stops in Jakarta, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok, Hong Kong and Kuala Lumpur. Originally announced with 11 stadium shows, the Asia run quickly expanded to 17 dates due to overwhelming demand, including additional performances in Tokyo, Kuala Lumpur and Bangkok.
