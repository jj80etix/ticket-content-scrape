# Tomorrowland Breaks Own Livestream Record

## Summary

Tomorrowland Belgium 2026 drew 400,000 on-site attendees across two weekends and set a new livestream record with 233 million views, up from 193 million last year.

### Key Points
- Mainstage lineup included Martin Garrix, Calvin Harris, David Guetta and Charlotte de Witte.
- Full sets are available to rewatch on YouTube, the Tomorrowland App, and Apple Music.

Tomorrowland, like all festivals, are best experiences live, on site, in person. However, if you couldn’t make it, there are now ample options to watch the festival on livestream. Picture by Julien Duval

Tomorrowland Belgium 2026, which went down across two weekends, July 17–19 and July 24–26, welcomed 400,000 People from over 200 countries to the grounds of Boom. Impressive as these visitor numbers are, they are in line with past Tomorrowland editions. Where the festival outdid itself this year was in the live streaming department.

Tomorrowland’s global livestream reached a record-breaking 233 million views across YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and other platforms, surpassing last year’s 193 million and setting a new high watermark in the festival’s history, according to a press release from the promoters at We Are One World.

Tomorrowland 2026 was built around the Consciencia theme, which revolved around six primal emotions: Wonder, Love, Anger, Joy, Desire and Sadness. Across 16 stages, each corner of the festival became “an invitation to feel something real, and to feel it together,” according to promoters.

Visitors experienced two weekends offering the best of Belgium’s summer weather, and got treated to a mainstage lineup led by Martin Garrix, Calvin Harris, Dimitri Vegas, Amelie Lens, David Guetta, Hardwell, Lost Frequencies, a surprise performance by Charlotte De Witte, and much more.

Armin van Buuren, Steve Aoki, who welcomed Latin superstar surprise guests Farruko and Greeicy, Sara Landry, Indira Paganotto, Miss Monique, Afrojack and R3hab uniting for a b2b set, Netsky, John Summit, NERVO, Oscar and the Wolf, and Bebe Rexha also featured on the stacked bill.

Fun fact: The youngest DJ performing at Tomorrowland 2026 was Knox Kind at only 11 years old. The oldest performer was Eko Roosevelt at 83 years old.

Full sets of Tomorrowland 2026 are available to rewatch on YouTube, the Tomorrowland App, as well as Apple Music.
