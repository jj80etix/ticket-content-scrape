# Justice and Moby Added to Simi Fest 2026 Lineup in Mexico City

## Summary

Justice and Moby have been added to the Simi Fest 2026 lineup, joining Snoop Dogg, Miguel and Chet Faker for the December 5 festival at Mexico City's Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez. Justice will perform a DJ set rather than its full live show, and Moby returns to a Mexico City festival stage after pulling out of Vive Latino earlier this year. The festival, organized by Farmacias Similares' Dr. Simi brand, ties ticket sales to a reforestation initiative.

### Key Points
- Justice (DJ set) and Moby join previously announced headliners Snoop Dogg, Miguel and Chet Faker for Simi Fest 2026.
- The festival takes place December 5 at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.
- Moby canceled a March Vive Latino appearance earlier this year, citing unforeseen circumstances; Steve Aoki replaced him there.
- Each ticket sold funds a "Bomba de Vida" reforestation seed ball as part of an environmental tie-in.
- This is the third edition of the Dr. Simi-branded festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Simi-Fest-2026.webp)

Justice and Moby have joined the lineup for Simi Fest 2026, expanding a bill that already includes Snoop Dogg, Miguel and Chet Faker. The festival takes place December 5 at Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez in Mexico City.

Justice will perform a DJ set rather than the French electronic duo’s full live production. Moby’s addition puts him back on a Mexico City festival lineup after he canceled a scheduled appearance at Vive Latino earlier this year.

The musician withdrew from Vive Latino in February, citing unforeseen circumstances, ahead of the festival’s March dates. Steve Aoki subsequently replaced him on the bill.

At Simi Fest, Justice and Moby join a roster that also features Neil Frances, STRFKR, Two Another, Los Blenders, María Barracuda, Los Eclipses, Descartes a Kant and Romoo.
