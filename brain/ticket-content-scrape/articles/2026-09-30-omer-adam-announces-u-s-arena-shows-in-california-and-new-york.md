# Omer Adam Announces U.S. Arena Shows in California and New York

## Summary

Israeli singer Omer Adam will play two U.S. arena shows this fall — Oct. 29 at Intuit Dome (Inglewood) and Nov. 1 at Barclays Center (Brooklyn) — promoted by Live Nation, following his sold-out Madison Square Garden date.

### Key Points
- Live Nation presale begins Sept. 30 at 10am local; general on-sale Oct. 1 at 10am local.
- Adds a West Coast stop (Intuit Dome) to a previously NY-centric U.S. run.
- Follows a sold-out Madison Square Garden performance.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Omer-Adam.webp)

Omer Adam will return to the United States this fall for two arena concerts, with performances scheduled in Southern California and New York.

The Israeli singer will perform October 29 at Intuit Dome in Inglewood, California, followed by November 1 at Barclays Center in Brooklyn. Live Nation is promoting the two-show run.

A Live Nation presale begins Wednesday, September 30, at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public Thursday, October 1, at 10 a.m. local time.

The concerts follow Adam’s sold-out performance at Madison Square Garden, bringing him back to the New York market while adding a West Coast stop to his U.S. schedule.

Adam’s music blends contemporary Western pop and electronic dance influences with Mizrahi styles rooted in Middle Eastern and North African musical traditions. His catalog includes the 2020 album “OMER” and songs such as “Magic,” “Million Kisses” and “Two Crazy People.”

Born in North Carolina and raised in Israel, Adam gained public attention through his appearance on the Israeli singing competition “Kokhav Nolad” in 2009. His participation ended after organizers learned he had entered before reaching the show’s minimum age of 16.

He continued building his career after leaving the competition, releasing his debut album, *Melting Because of You*, in 2010. Early singles included “Lost Spirit” and “Jose Soul.”
