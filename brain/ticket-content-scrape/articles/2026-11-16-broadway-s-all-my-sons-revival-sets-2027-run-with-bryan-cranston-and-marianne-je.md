# Broadway’s ‘All My Sons’ Revival Sets 2027 Run With Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste

## Summary

The Olivier Award-winning West End revival of "All My Sons," starring Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste, will transfer to Broadway in spring 2027, directed by Ivo van Hove. Previews begin March 30 at the August Wilson Theatre, with an official opening April 13. Both leads earned Olivier nominations for the London run, which also had a National Theatre Live filmed release.

### Key Points
- Broadway previews begin March 30, 2027; opening night April 13 at the August Wilson Theatre.
- Reunites Cranston with director Ivo van Hove, who directed his Tony-winning "Network" performance.
- Production won Best Revival at the 2026 Olivier Awards; London run had a filmed theatrical release.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Broadways-All-My-Sons.webp)

The Olivier Award-winning West End revival of Arthur Miller’s “All My Sons,” starring Bryan Cranston and Marianne Jean-Baptiste, will transfer to Broadway in spring 2027 following its run in London.

Directed by Ivo van Hove, the production is scheduled to begin previews on March 30 at the August Wilson Theatre in New York City, ahead of an official opening on April 13. Additional casting will be announced at a later date.

Cranston will reprise his role as Joe Keller, with Jean-Baptiste returning as his wife, Kate Keller. The two actors previously led the production at Wyndham’s Theatre in London, where the revival received critical acclaim and earned several major theater award nominations.

The production won Best Revival at the 2026 Olivier Awards, while Cranston and Jean-Baptiste each received nominations for their performances. A filmed version of the London staging was also released internationally in movie theaters through National Theatre Live.

Originally premiered in 1946, “All My Sons” follows Joe Keller, a successful businessman whose family and reputation are threatened when details of a criminal manufacturing scandal involving his former business partner emerge.

The Broadway transfer will reunite Cranston with van Hove, who previously directed the actor in the 2018 Broadway production of “Network.” Cranston won the Tony Award for Best Actor in a Play for his performance in that production.

Van Hove, a Tony Award-winning director, is returning to Broadway for the first time since his 2020 revival of “West Side Story,” which ended early due to the COVID-19 pandemic. His previous Broadway credits also include “The Crucible” and “A View From the Bridge,” the latter of which earned him Tony and Olivier Awards.

The upcoming revival is being produced by Wessex Grove and Gavin Kalin Productions, who highlighted the play’s continued relevance in a joint statement.
