# Dru Hill Sets 2027 “History Of R&B” Tour

## Summary

Dru Hill announced a 2027 "History of R&B" tour, a multimedia concert experience covering R&B classics from The Temptations through Prince alongside the group's own hits. The trek kicks off Jan. 16 at Tachi Palace Casino Resort in Fresno and runs through a mix of casinos and theaters nationwide, booked exclusively by Universal Attractions Agency.

### Key Points
- Premieres Jan. 16, 2027 at Tachi Palace Casino Resort, Fresno, CA.
- 16+ dates announced across US/Canada casinos and theaters through April 2027, plus a November 2027 Soul Train Cruise stop.
- Booked exclusively by Universal Attractions Agency (Adam Zagor, Joel Brandes).
- Set will blend Dru Hill hits ("Tell Me," "In My Bed") with covers honoring R&B forebears.

Dru Hill has announced their 2027 “The History of R&B Tour.” Universal Attractions Agency’s Adam Zagor and Joel Brandes booked the tour exclusively. The new trek is “a high-impact, multimedia concert experience powered by cinematic production that will bring audiences the iconic hits and era-defining moments of R&B’s musical journey, plus their own hits, in a bold, immersive live experience,” a press release reads. The all-new live experience premieres on Jan. 16, at the Tachi Palace Casino Resort in Fresno, then hits casinos and theaters nationwide, including Yaamava, Foxwoods Resort, Paramount Baltimore, and Texas Trust CU Theatre.

The show will take audiences on a journey through R&B’s evolution, honoring the music that paved the way, shaped generations, and inspired Dru Hill’s legendary sound. Throughout the show, Dru Hill will perform songs made famous by legendary artists such as The Temptations, Marvin Gaye, Stevie Wonder, The O’Jays, Earth, Wind & Fire, The Jacksons, Prince, and many more — showing how one generation of R&B influenced the next. The journey ultimately leads to Dru Hill’s own place in R&B history, as fans will also hear Dru Hill classics including “Tell Me,” “In My Bed,” “Never Make a Promise,” “How Deep Is Your Love,” and “These Are the Times, among others.

“Dru Hill has done our very best to show honor and respect to the myriad of multi-talented conventional artists who have come before us with our very unconventional interpretation of the genre. Meeting seemingly impossible challenges with influential innovations that inspire people around the world from age eight to 80, and proving with every live performance that there are still some things AI can’t do. With our motto, ‘always expect the unexpected,’ we invite audiences to bravely voyage into the future with us on the wings of the Dragon as we soar to new heights of our musical journey with the ‘History of R&B presented by the one and only multi-platinum recording artist Dru Hill,” Sisqó said.

“R&B is a huge part of our lives. Being able to bring ‘The History of R&B’ to the people not only gives us a chance to perform our records to new audiences, but it also gives us a chance to show the love and admiration we have for those who came before us and paved the way,” Nokio, founding member of Dru Hill, said.
