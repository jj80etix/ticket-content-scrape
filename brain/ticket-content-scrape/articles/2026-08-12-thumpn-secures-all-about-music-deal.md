# ThumpN secures All About Music deal

## Summary

Indian live-entertainment platform ThumpN — which raised $3.75M in pre-seed funding last month — has been named official ticketing partner for All About Music X, a Mumbai music-industry conference (Aug 25-27), where it will provide its AI-native discovery and ticketing infrastructure, including its "Shadow" conversational AI assistant that recommends events.

### Key Points
- ThumpN appointed official ticketing partner for All About Music X's 10th edition (Grand Hyatt Mumbai, Aug 25-27).
- ThumpN raised over $3.75M (£2.8m/€3.3m) in a pre-seed round last month.
- Founded by ex-Paytm Insider COO Varun Khare; Insider founder Shreyas Srinivasan is a strategic advisor.
- Platform's AI assistant "Shadow" combines AI with human curators/organizers to recommend concerts, comedy, festivals, and sports events via a conversational interface.

### Highlights
- ThumpN pairs a conversational AI discovery layer ("Shadow") directly with its ticketing infrastructure — an early-stage, India-market example of AI-native ticketing rather than AI bolted onto an existing platform.
- Backed by fresh pre-seed capital ($3.75M) specifically earmarked for AI-native discovery/ticketing expansion.

### Industry Problem
Emerging regional platforms are building AI-first discovery directly into the ticketing purchase flow rather than treating AI as a separate support layer, which raises the bar for what "AI-deployed" ticketing looks like relative to legacy platforms bolting on chat support.

### Proposed Solution
Worth tracking as an early benchmark for conversational-AI-driven discovery-to-purchase flows; not an immediate build trigger given ThumpN's early stage and regional focus, but useful grounding material for Etix Assistant's roadmap on discovery/recommendation.
