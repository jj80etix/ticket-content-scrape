# 2026 IMPACT International: Australia/New Zealand Next-Gen: Josh Paterson

## Summary

Pollstar's 2026 Impact International Australia/New Zealand Next-Gen profile on Josh Paterson, Content Production Lead at Untitled Group, covering his path from nightclub doorperson to live-show content director.

### Key Points
- Began at Melbourne's Xe54 nightclub, run by Untitled Group.
- Career highlight: directing a 15-camera live capture of DJ KI/KI's five-hour set at Melbourne's The Wool Store.
- Moved from photography/content production into full-time work at Untitled.

Joshua Paterson’s first gig was at Melbourne techno/house nightclub Xe54, run by Untitled Group.

“As a teenager discovering the world, music and arts for the first time, I was so amazed by the brand direction and artwork created every week by Sam Hayes (multi-discipline designer in the EDM and festival scenes, and now head designer at Untitled).

He moved from doorperson to photographer/content producer “with the drive to contribute to the creative through photo and video to complement Sam’s weekly flyer designs.”

It brought him further into Untitled’s orbit, and he ended up working at the company full time.

The highlight of his career so far came last September when he directed a live show capture for Dutch DJ and producer KI/KI for her five-hour DJ set at Melbourne’s The Wool Store.

Alongside producer Daina Coxall “and my duo-queue Edd Schlaghecke”, they used 15 cameras – “and many cinema cameras we had never used before and had little idea how to operate.

“Seeing it go live online as such a monumental moment in a surging artist’s career gave me a lot of confidence that I could perform and create at a world-class level.

When it comes to the advantages and challenges of his generation, Paterson says technology is the overwhelming answer.

“I’ve spent my whole life glued to a device, and I wouldn’t want it any other way. Although I skew to the extreme end of the scale I have so much nostalgia growing up with technology and it’s shaped my life and influences in such a unique way compared to any other generation. My hyper-awareness of culture and trends is born from  
the time I’ve spent digesting online. I would be nowhere near as good at my job if I didn’t spend so much time with my laptop. I’m grateful for all the hobbies I’ve had and friends I’ve made because of the internet and technology and the ways I’ve been able to express myself and explore life as an introvert without having to leave my house.”

Looking ahead, Paterson notes that after accomplishing so many goals “in a bit of a speed run,” he’s been trying to learn more about himself outside of work.

“I love what I do and am so grateful to be creative for a living surrounded by such great people and so close to the parts of life that make me happiest. I just want to keep learning and growing and meet more like-minded people.”
