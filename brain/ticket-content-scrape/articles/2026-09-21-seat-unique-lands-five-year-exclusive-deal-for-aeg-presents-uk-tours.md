# Seat Unique Lands Five-Year Exclusive Deal for AEG Presents UK Tours

## Summary

Seat Unique signed a five-year exclusive deal to become AEG Presents' official hospitality and VIP partner across its entire UK touring program, expanding a relationship that previously covered only individual festival partnerships.

### Key Points
- Deal covers AEG's full UK arena/stadium touring slate, not just BST Hyde Park and Roundhay Festival as before.
- More than 7,000 fans have already used Seat Unique hospitality offerings through the prior festival-only partnership.
- AEG frames it as centralizing hospitality/VIP commerce across tours involving artists like Taylor Swift, The Rolling Stones and Zach Bryan.
- Brokered by AEG Global Partnerships; builds on Seat Unique's existing role as the Euro 2028 hospitality sales platform.

### Highlights
- Five-year exclusive term covering AEG Presents' entire UK touring business, not single festivals.
- 7,000+ fans already converted through the prior festival-only hospitality partnership.
- Positions Seat Unique as the sole hospitality/VIP commerce layer across a major promoter's full UK slate.

### Industry Problem
Premium hospitality and VIP packaging is becoming a distinct, high-margin layer of concert inventory that promoters increasingly want centralized and professionally merchandised rather than handled ad hoc per venue or festival.

### Proposed Solution
Seat Unique's answer is an exclusive, promoter-wide hospitality marketplace layered on top of primary ticketing. For a primary platform like Etix, this signals demand for a native or partner-integrated VIP/hospitality upsell layer rather than ceding that revenue and fan-experience touchpoint to a third-party specialist.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Seat-Unique.webp)

Seat Unique has signed a five-year exclusive partnership with AEG Presents to become the promoter’s official hospitality and VIP partner across its entire UK touring program.

The agreement expands an existing relationship between the companies beyond individual festivals, giving Seat Unique a role across AEG Presents’ broader slate of UK arena and stadium tours. Fans will be able to discover and book official hospitality and VIP packages for participating AEG-promoted shows through the Seat Unique platform.

The agreement was brokered by AEG Global Partnerships and builds on Seat Unique’s existing work with AEG Presents at American Express presents BST Hyde Park and American Express presents Roundhay Festival. More than 7,000 fans have used Seat Unique hospitality offerings across those festival partnerships, according to the company.

Seat Unique became an official partner of BST Hyde Park in 2025, operating the Seat Unique VIP Terrace and later becoming the exclusive seller of access to the festival’s Great Oak Roof Garden. The company has also operated premium hospitality products at AEG’s Roundhay Festival in Leeds, including the Seat Unique VIP Grandstand and Seat Unique Club.
