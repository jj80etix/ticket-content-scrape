# Over Ice: Kickstand Cocktails Partners With UBS Arena and the New York Islanders

## Summary

Kickstand Cocktails, the craft vodka soda brand co-founded by Darren Rovell and J.J. Watt, announced a multi-year partnership with UBS Arena and the New York Islanders — its first NHL/arena tie-up — bringing the drinks to concessions and premium spaces plus custom food pairings developed with the venue's executive chef.

### Key Points
- Multi-year partnership begins with the Islanders-Blue Jackets game on Nov. 17; first NHL/arena deal for Kickstand Cocktails.
- Drinks available at arena concessions and all premium spaces; includes signature cocktail/food pairings led by Delaware North's UBS Arena executive chef.
- Four Kickstand flavors featured: Raspberry Habanero, Pineapple Poblano, Peach Chipotle, Cucumber Jalapeño.
- UBS Arena's year-round calendar (hockey, concerts, family shows) framed as the brand's appeal beyond single-game exposure.

## Transcript

![Current image: UBSKickstand2](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/UBSKickstand2-1024x683.jpg)

Photo credit UBS Arena/Dennis DaSilva

Kickstand Cocktails, the line of craft vodka sodas founded by entrepreneur and sports journalist Darren Rovell and football legend J.J. Watt, announced a multi-year partnership with UBS Arena and the New York Islanders – marking the first NHL and arena partnership for the brand.

Beginning with the New York Islanders game against the Columbus Blue Jackets on Nov. 17, Kickstand Cocktails are now available at arena concessions and in all the venue’s premium spaces.

The partnership extends beyond signage and hospitality with one-of-a-kind food pairings and recipes. Led by Delaware North Executive Chef of UBS Arena Ted Donnelly – the mastermind behind the viral Nerds Gummy Cluster Brie Grilled Cheese – the partnership includes signature cocktails infused with some of Kickstand’s four flavors: Raspberry Habanero, Pineapple Poblano, Peach Chipotle and Cucumber Jalapeño.

For Kickstand, the appeal goes beyond any single game or concert. UBS Arena hosts a variety of events annually including New York Islanders hockey, concerts, family shows and special events. Its diverse calendar, premium hospitality and place within Belmont Park’s growing sports, entertainment and fashion campus give the brand opportunities to connect with different audiences throughout the year.

“One of the things that really drew us to UBS Arena is that it’s so much more than a sports and entertainment venue – it’s a culinary destination in its own right,” said Elisa Baker, CEO of Kickstand Cocktails. “From innovative, elevated cuisine that goes far beyond traditional arena fare to the premium hospitality experiences and the shopping and dining surrounding Belmont Park, there’s a real commitment to creating something special for every guest.

“That’s exactly the kind of environment where Kickstand belongs. We’re excited to bring our bold craft vodka sodas to a destination that shares our passion for delivering an experience that’s anything but ordinary.”

Kickstand will also serve as a supporting partner of the UBS Arena Belmont Stakes Pre-Party, with product and branding integrated into the celebration. Plans for influencer events, hospitality experiences and opportunities for appearances by Islanders alumni will create additional ways to introduce guests to the brand.

“We pride ourselves on giving fans the best guest experience when they come to UBS Arena, which includes our food and beverage offerings,” added Kelly Cheeseman, President of Business Operations for the New York Islanders and UBS Arena. “Kickstand Cocktails brings bold flavors and fresh energy to our beverage lineup. From concessions and premium hospitality to fan engagement throughout Belmont Park, we’re excited to welcome Kickstand to the experience we create for New York Islanders fans and concertgoers.”

Kickstand Cocktails pairs premium vodka with fruit and a touch of spice to create its signature flavor profile, each with 110 calories, zero carbs, no added sugar and 5% ABV. Kickstand is gluten-free and contains no artificial sweeteners. The partnership builds on Kickstand’s expanding presence across sports, hospitality and live entertainment, including collaborations with the Orlando Solar Bears, Orlando Pride and Orlando City FC, RFK Racing and Pabst Theater Group.
