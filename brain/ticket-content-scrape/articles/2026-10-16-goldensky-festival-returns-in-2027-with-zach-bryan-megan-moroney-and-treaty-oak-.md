# GoldenSky Festival Returns in 2027 With Zach Bryan, Megan Moroney and Treaty Oak Revival

## Summary

GoldenSky Festival returns to Sacramento's Discovery Park Oct. 9-10, 2027, after a two-year absence, with Zach Bryan, Megan Moroney and Treaty Oak Revival headlining under a new three-year agreement to host the country/Americana festival through 2029. Danny Wimmer Presents announced the full first-wave lineup Oct. 15, with pass sales opening Oct. 16 via SMS and email presales ahead of a noon general onsale.

### Key Points
- Returns Oct. 9-10, 2027 at Discovery Park, Sacramento, under a three-year venue agreement (2027-2029) with the city, county and Visit Sacramento.
- Headliners: Zach Bryan, Megan Moroney, Treaty Oak Revival, plus Ty Myers and Whiskey Myers.
- Produced by Danny Wimmer Presents in partnership with Visit Sacramento, Sacramento County and the City of Sacramento.
- Pass sales: SMS presale Oct. 16 at 10 a.m. PT, email presale at 11 a.m., general onsale at noon; payment plans available.
- Follows three prior editions (2022-2024) that featured artists including Tim McGraw, Keith Urban and Eric Church.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GoldenSky-Festival.webp)

GoldenSky Festival will return to Sacramento’s Discovery Park on October 9 to 10, 2027, with a lineup featuring Zach Bryan, Megan Moroney, Treaty Oak Revival, Ty Myers and Whiskey Myers.

Danny Wimmer Presents announced the lineup on October 15, confirming the country music festival’s return after a two-year absence. Hudson Westbrook, Max McNown and Wyatt Flores are also scheduled to perform during the two-day event.

The announcement brings together established acts and emerging performers, with a bill spanning country, Americana, roots and rock. Additional artists include Vincent Mason, Avery Anna, Braxton Keith, Blake Whiten, Waylon Wyatt and Mack Geiger.

Kaitlin Butts, Carter Faith, Alexandra Kay, Noah Rinker, The Band Perry, Tyler Nance, Graham Barham, Los Hermanos Mendoza and Fey Fili complete the announced lineup. Daily artist assignments and performance times were not included in the announcement.

“This lineup feels like the perfect return, reflecting what’s exciting in country music right now while staying true to what made people fall in love with GoldenSky in the first place.”

The festival’s comeback is part of a three-year agreement to hold GoldenSky at Discovery Park in 2027, 2028 and 2029. Danny Wimmer Presents produces the event in partnership with Visit Sacramento, Sacramento County and the City of Sacramento.

“More importantly, we’re not just coming back for a weekend,” McCurry continued. “We’ve made a long-term commitment to Sacramento, and we’re excited to keep growing GoldenSky and investing in this community for years to come.”

Visit Sacramento President and CEO Mike Testa said the announced performers are expected to draw fans from outside the region.

“This event has a proven track record of bringing music fans from across the country to our region, and we’re confident that the 2027 lineup will inspire even more visitors to travel here for an unforgettable festival weekend.”

Moroney is among the artists returning to GoldenSky after appearing during its first three editions, held from 2022 through 2024. Those festivals featured performers including Tim McGraw, Keith Urban, Eric Church, Luke Bryan, Lainey Wilson, Thomas Rhett and Turnpike Troubadours.

The earlier lineups also included Ashley McBryde, Bailey Zimmerman, Brothers Osborne, Carly Pearce, Parker McCollum, Riley Green and Shaboozey. The 2027 announcement continues the festival’s mix of established country performers and newer artists.

Pass sales begin October 16, with an SMS presale at 10 a.m. Pacific and an email subscriber presale at 11 a.m. General sales open at noon through the GoldenSky Festival website. Payment plans will be available.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [goldenskyfestival.com](http://goldenskyfestival.com).
