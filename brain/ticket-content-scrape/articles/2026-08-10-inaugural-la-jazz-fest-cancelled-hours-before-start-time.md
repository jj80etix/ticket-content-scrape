# Inaugural LA Jazz Fest Cancelled Hours Before Start Time

## Summary

The inaugural LA Jazz Fest was cancelled just before its Aug. 7 opening night, with founder Martin Ludlow citing unanticipated public-safety and municipal permitting costs; ticket buyers are promised refunds within 30 days.

### Key Points
- The festival was originally slated to run Aug. 7-23 across dozens of city venues, plus a flagship "Jazz on the Beach" show Aug. 22-23 with John Legend and Janelle Monáe.
- Ludlow says he intends to relaunch the festival in 2027.
- No information was given regarding payment to performers or vendors.
- The festival's social channels had gone quiet, deepening confusion for ticket holders.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0803.jpeg)

The inaugural LA Jazz Fest was set to kick-off on Thursday, but before anyone took the stage, the festival was mysteriously cancelled.

According to the [*Los Angeles Daily News*](https://www.dailynews.com/2026/08/07/why-the-los-angeles-jazz-festival-was-canceled-on-its-opening-day/), a spokesperson for the festival said that programming would need to be “postponed.” Then, on Friday, founder Martin Ludlow released a statement, confirming that the entire festival was cancelled.

“Due to significant, unanticipated last-minute public safety and municipal costs associated with the final permitting of the On The Beach Festival, we have reached the decision to unfortunately postpone this year’s inaugural LA Jazz Festival,” Ludlow wrote.

He said that while this was a difficult decision to make, “our commitment to the audiences and artists participating in the festival is to have a first-class experience worthy of the great legacy of jazz and our great city.”

This might not be the last of the festival, however, as Ludlow said he looks “forward to presenting all of the components of this incredible cultural opportunity for Los Angeles and the world in 2027 and beyond.”

Ludlow said ticket buyers would receive refunds within 30 days. Any information regarding payment to performers or vendors was not mentioned in his statement.

The festival’s social media pages do not offer any clarity, as their Instagram has not been updated in over a week and comments have been turned-off. Additionally, the festival’s website reads: “August 22-23. Updated event info coming soon.”

The LA Jazz Festival was set to run from August 7 through 23, kicking-off with Lalah Hathaway headlining opening night. From August 8 through 16, a series of concerts were slated to take place, dubbed “Jazz In The Park” at 24 parks around the city, alongside a night concert series, “Jazz After Dark,” appearing at 70+ local spots from August 8 to 23. A “Caribbean Street Carnival” was also set to take place on August 21 for “one big block party in Venice” across four stages.
