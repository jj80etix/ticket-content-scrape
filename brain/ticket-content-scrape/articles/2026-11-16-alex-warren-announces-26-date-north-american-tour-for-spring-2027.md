# Alex Warren Announces 26-Date North American Tour for Spring 2027

## Summary

Alex Warren announced a 26-date North American leg of his "Finding (More) Family on the Road World Tour" for spring 2027, running April 5-May 16 with support from Bebe Stockwell and Faouzia on different legs. Presale registration opens Nov. 18, with general onsale Nov. 20. The tour continues his PLUS1 charitable partnership donating $1 per ticket to children's hospitals.

### Key Points
- 26-date North American tour, April 5-May 16, 2027; presale Nov. 18, general onsale Nov. 20.
- Support acts split: Bebe Stockwell (April 5-28), Faouzia (April 30-May 16).
- PLUS1 partnership has generated over $424,000 for children's hospitals since 2025.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Alex-Warren-Finding-More-Family-on-the-Road.webp)

Alex Warren has announced a 26-date North American leg of his *Finding (More) Family on the Road World Tour*, bringing the Grammy-nominated pop singer back to arenas and amphitheaters across the United States and Canada in spring 2027.

The tour is slated to open April 5 at Paycom Center in Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, before making stops in Florida, Tennessee, New York, Wisconsin, Ontario and several other markets. The North American run will conclude May 16 at Coca-Cola Amphitheater in Birmingham, Alabama.

Bebe Stockwell and Faouzia will join Warren as supporting acts on select dates. Stockwell will appear on the first 15 performances, through April 28 in Columbus, Ohio, while Faouzia will take over beginning April 30 in Hamilton, Ontario, and remain on the tour through its conclusion.

The upcoming North American leg includes several notable arena stops, including Orlando’s Kia Center, St. Louis’ Enterprise Center, Columbus’ Nationwide Arena and UBS Arena in Belmont Park, New York. Warren will also perform in four Canadian cities: Winnipeg, Hamilton, Ottawa and Quebec City.

The final stretch of the tour shifts toward outdoor venues, with performances scheduled at Raleigh’s Coastal Credit Union Music Park at Walnut Creek, Charleston’s Credit One Stadium and Noblesville’s Ruoff Music Center before the Birmingham finale.

Following the North American dates, Warren will return to Europe for a series of previously announced festival appearances and headlining concerts. Those performances include two nights at London’s Crystal Palace Park Live in July.

Warren will also continue his charitable partnership with PLUS1, directing $1 from every ticket sold to children’s hospitals in the communities where he performs. Since the partnership began in 2025, the singer and his fans have helped generate more than $424,000 for organizations supporting children across North America, Live Nation said.
