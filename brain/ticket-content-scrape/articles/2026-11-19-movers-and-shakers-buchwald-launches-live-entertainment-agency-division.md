# Movers and shakers: Buchwald launches live entertainment agency division

## Summary

IQ's movers-and-shakers roundup leads with talent agency Buchwald launching a live entertainment division, to be led by former UTA agent Isaiah Jones. The same roundup notes appointments at d&b Audiotechnik, Live Nation, and other firms.

### Key Points

- Former UTA agent Isaiah Jones will lead the new division.
- Roundup also covers appointments at d&b Audiotechnik and Live Nation (full details behind IQ's paywall).
