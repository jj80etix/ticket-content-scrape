# Angus & Julia Stone Announce New Karaoke Project, North American Tour

## Summary

Angus & Julia Stone announced a new covers project, The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse, following up their 2026 album Karaoke Bar, alongside a North American tour set for May 2027. The duo kicked off the project with a cover of OneRepublic's "Apologize."

### Key Points
- Karaoke Bar (their sixth studio album) hit No. 1 on Australia's ARIA albums chart after its May 2026 release.
- New covers series, The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse, will roll out over the coming months.
- North American tour announced for May 2027.
- Project launched with a cover of OneRepublic's "Apologize."

## Transcript

Australian indie pop/folk band Angus & Julia Stone are following up their album *Karaoke Bar* with a new project, The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse. Plus, the sibling duo has announced a North American tour set for May 2027.

Today’s announcement explains that The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse will feature a series of covers released over the coming months in which Angus & Julia Stone will their take on some of their favorite songs, “breathing new life into familiar classics through their unmistakable harmonies, intimate arrangements and effortless musical chemistry.”

Marking their sixth studio album, *Karaoke Bar* was released in May 2026 and hit No. 1 on the Australian Albums ARIA chart, as well as charting in the UK, Scotland and beyond.

Angus & Julia Stone have kicked off their new project with a cover of OneRepublic’s pop ballad “Apologize.” Their cover can be heard here.

The duo offer the following thoughts about The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse and “Apologize”:

“Our dad plays in a covers band, so we grew up listening to his interpretations of other people’s songs. We were around a lot of live music, and I think that instilled in us a real joy in taking a song and making it your own. As we got older and started singing and playing ourselves, we naturally started doing the same…..There’s something really special about taking a song you grew up listening to, or one that means something to you, and finding your own version of it……We’ve been lucky enough to hear people all around the world doing their versions of our songs, too. And while doing press for Karaoke Bar, we held some really fun karaoke events with fans and got talking about everyone’s favorite songs to sing. The Highway Karaoke Roadhouse felt like a fun way to share some of our own favorites……… ‘Apologize’ is such a sad and beautiful song, lyrically and melodically. It’s one of those pop songs that gets stuck in your head, but when you really listen to the lyrics, it’s deeply tragic. That feeling of knowing you’ve reached the point where you can’t go back. There’s no apology big enough, no words left to say. Just that awful knowing that it’s over.”

The North American leg of their “Karaoke Bar World Tour” starts May 13 at Big Night Live in Boston. Stops on the routing include New York, Montreal, Toronto, Chicago, Denver, Seattle and more. The last gig is May 30 at The Wiltern in Los Angeles.

“North America, we had such a beautiful time with you in September, we’re coming back for more in May 🤍 We’re bringing the Karaoke Bar World Tour to cities across the US and Canada,” the duo posted on Instagram.  
  
Tickets go on sale on Friday Nov. 13 at 10 a.m. local time to the general public but fans can sign up [here](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/AngusandJuliaStone/NorthAmerica) for a ticket presale.

Angus & Julia Stone is booked in North and South America by WME’s Josh Kurfirst and Joe Friel.  
  
Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Angus & Julia Stone include a June 26 show at Stadtpark Open Air in Hamburg Germany that sold 2,970 tickets and grossed $185,940.
