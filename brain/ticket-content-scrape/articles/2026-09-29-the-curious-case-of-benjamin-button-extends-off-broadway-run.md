# ‘The Curious Case of Benjamin Button’ Extends Off-Broadway Run

## Summary

"The Curious Case of Benjamin Button" has again extended its Off-Broadway run at The Public Theater's Newman Theater, now through December 6, adding two weeks; previews begin October 8 with an October 21 opening.

### Key Points
- North American premiere of the Olivier Award-winning London production, adapted from F. Scott Fitzgerald's short story.
- Written/directed by Jethro Compton with music by Darren Clark; Chi-San Howard choreographs.
- Features a company of 15 actor-musicians; Andrew Durand stars as Benjamin Button.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Curious-Case-of-Benjamin-Button-Off-Broadway.webp)

“The Curious Case of Benjamin Button” has extended its upcoming Off-Broadway engagement at The Public Theater again.

The musical’s North American premiere will now run through December 6 at the Newman Theater, adding two weeks of performances. Previews begin October 8, and the production is scheduled to open October 21.

Based on F. Scott Fitzgerald’s short story, the musical follows Benjamin Button, who is born old and grows younger as the people around him age. Jethro Compton wrote the book and lyrics and directs the production. Darren Clark wrote the music and shares lyric credit with Compton.

The two were part of the Olivier Award-winning writing team behind the London production. Chi-San Howard choreographs the New York staging, which features a company of 15 actor-musicians.

Andrew Durand plays Benjamin Button, with Kelsee Kimmel appearing as Elowen Keene. The ensemble also includes Jo Brook, Britney Coleman, Trevor Lindley Craft, Brandon J. Ellis, Katie Horner, Casey Martin Klein, Sachiko Mae, Morgan Morse, Kris Saint-Louis, Claire-Frances Sullivan, Lauren Jeanne Thomas, Spiff Wiegand and Matt Wolpe.

Compton also designed the scenery for the production. Anna Kelsey designed the costumes, Zoe Spurr designed the lighting, and Luke Swaffield designed the sound for Autograph Sound. Clark and Mark Aspinall provide orchestrations and arrangements and serve as music supervisors, with Aspinall also serving as music director.

For more information and ticketing details, theatergoers can visit [PublicTheater.org](http://www.publictheater.org/).
