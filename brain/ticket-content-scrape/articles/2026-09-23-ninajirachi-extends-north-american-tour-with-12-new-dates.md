# Ninajirachi Extends North American Tour With 12 New Dates

## Summary

Ninajirachi has added 12 North American dates to her "I Love My Computer and It Loves Me" tour, extending the run into January 2027 with stops including Seattle, Chicago, Atlanta, D.C., New York, Toronto and Montreal's Igloofest. A local presale runs September 23 ahead of the general onsale September 24.

### Key Points
- New dates begin Nov. 19 in San Francisco, resume Jan. 5 in Portland and close Jan. 23 at Montreal's Igloofest.
- MGNA CRRRTA and iKeda support on the added dates.
- Tour supports her debut album *I Love My Computer* (2025), which won three ARIA Awards including Best Solo Artist.
- Follows festival appearances at Coachella, Primavera Sound and Lollapalooza, plus dates on Tame Impala's Australian "Deadbeat" tour.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ninajirachi-.webp)

Ninajirachi has added 12 North American dates to her “I Love My Computer and It Loves Me” tour, extending the run into January 2027.

The new schedule begins November 19 in San Francisco. After a break, the Australian electronic producer will return January 5 in Portland, Oregon, and travel through the United States and Canada before closing the newly announced stretch January 23 at Montreal’s Igloofest. The itinerary includes shows in Seattle, Chicago, Atlanta, Washington, D.C., New York and Toronto.

MGNA CRRRTA and iKeda are set to provide support on the added dates. A local presale is scheduled for Wednesday, September 23, at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general sale Thursday, September 24, at 10 a.m. local time.

The expansion follows Ninajirachi’s initial seven-date North American tour announcement and a series of added performances. Her upcoming shows include two nights at Boston’s Royale, two at Brooklyn Steel in New York and four at the Fonda Theatre in Los Angeles. A Columbus, Ohio, show was also moved to the larger KEMBA Live! as the tour grew.
