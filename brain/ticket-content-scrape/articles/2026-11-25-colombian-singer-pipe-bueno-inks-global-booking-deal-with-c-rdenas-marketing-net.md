# Colombian Singer Pipe Bueno Inks Global Booking Deal With Cárdenas Marketing Network

## Summary

Colombian singer Pipe Bueno has signed a global booking deal with Cárdenas Marketing Network. The announcement covers the new representation agreement.

### Key Points
- Pipe Bueno signs global booking deal with Cárdenas Marketing Network.
- Routine artist-representation announcement.

## Transcript

![Photo Nov 23 2026, 3 28 01 PM](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Photo-Nov-23-2026-3-28-01-PM-scaled.jpg)

Juan Guillermo Ballesteros (manager), Daniela Domador (CMN), Pipe Bueno and Carolina Daza (CMN).

Colombian singer Pipe Bueno has signed a global booking agreement with Cárdenas Marketing Network (CMN), one of the world’s biggest Latin music promoters, to expand his international touring opportunities.

CMN, which is led by CEO Henry Cárdenas, will oversee booking of Bueno’s performances across the globe. The major promoter currently works with Latin stars Marc Anthony, Ana Gabriel and Christian Nodal, helping them expand their presence in new markets, and it will work to do the same for Bueno.

“Pipe Bueno is an artist with an established career and tremendous international potential,” Cárdenas said in a statement. “We’re excited to join forces to bring his music and live performances to new stages and continue opening doors for Colombian popular music around the world.”

Bueno recently released a 23-track studio album titled *Aquí Estoy Yo*, and his single “Señora” was a hit on Colombian radio stations.

“Working with CMN is a dream come true. I’ve known Henry for many years and have always admired what he has accomplished as a Colombian in the U.S. entertainment industry, working with major artists like Marc Anthony,” Bueno said. “Today, I have the opportunity to join forces with him and a team that shares my vision for growth. This partnership represents a major step toward taking my career to the next level, transcending the boundaries of Colombian popular music, and establishing myself as an increasingly global artist.”

Juan Guillermo Ballesteros, Bueno’s manager, added: “Pipe has built a solid career in Colombia, and this partnership with CMN reflects a vision we’ve been working toward for years: expanding his international presence alongside a team with the experience and reach to make it possible. We want to continue growing without losing the essence that has connected Pipe with his audience from day one.”
