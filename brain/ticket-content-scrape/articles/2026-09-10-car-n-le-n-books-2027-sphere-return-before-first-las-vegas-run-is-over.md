# Carín León Books 2027 Sphere Return Before First Las Vegas Run Is Over

## Summary

Carín León has booked a 2027 return engagement at Sphere in Las Vegas before his current Sphere run has even concluded.

### Key Points
- 2027 Sphere dates booked ahead of the current run wrapping.
- Continues the venue's pattern of extended-run bookings with major artists.

## Transcript

![Carín León performing at Las Vegas Sphere in September. (Photo courtesy The Sphere)](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CarinleonSphere2026AEG-courtesy.webp)

Carín León performing at Las Vegas Sphere in September. (Photo courtesy The Sphere)

Carín León is already planning his return to Sphere, announcing three September 2027 shows at the Las Vegas venue while his historic first engagement there is still underway.

The Mexican superstar will return to Sphere on Sept. 16, 17 and 18, 2027, once again putting his Las Vegas dates around Mexican Independence Day celebrations. The limited engagement will be produced by AEG Presents.

The announcement comes less than a week after León became the first Latin artist to perform at Sphere on Sept. 4. His opening weekend of Sept. 4-6 sold out, and León returned to the venue Thursday for the fourth show of his [seven-date 2026 engagement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/10/carin-leon-seventh-final-show-sphere-run/). The current run continues with performances Sept. 11-13.

The quick return booking extends what has already become a significant relationship between León and the Las Vegas venue. His first three Sphere shows were [initially announced](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/09/carin-leon-history-first-latino-headline-sphere/) for Sept. 11-13, 2026, before three Labor Day weekend performances were added amid increased demand. A seventh show on Sept. 10 followed several weeks later.
