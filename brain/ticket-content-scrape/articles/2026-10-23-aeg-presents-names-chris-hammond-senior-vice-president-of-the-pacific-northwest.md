# AEG Presents Names Chris Hammond Senior Vice President Of The Pacific Northwest

## Summary

AEG Presents has promoted Chris Hammond to Senior Vice President of the Pacific Northwest, relocating him from Las Vegas to Seattle ahead of a new AEG concert venue opening in Portland next year. Hammond will oversee booking and operational strategy for the region, reporting to Brent Fedrizzi.

### Key Points
- Hammond moves from VP of Talent (Las Vegas) to SVP, Pacific Northwest, based in Seattle.
- Move coincides with a new AEG venue opening in Portland next year.
- Hammond spent 8 years with AEG Presents Las Vegas, including work at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas.
- Reports to Brent Fedrizzi, President of North American Regional Offices.

AEG Presents has promoted Chris Hammond to Senior Vice President of the Pacific Northwest from his previous role as VP of Talent at the promoter’s Las Vegas office. Hammond will now be based in Seattle, where he will oversee “booking and operational strategy for AEG Presents’ growing portfolio of Pacific Northwest venues.” He will report to Brent Fedrizzi, President of North American Regional Offices.

“I’m really excited for what lies ahead,” Hammond commented. “The Pacific Northwest’s music scene speaks for itself…it’s globally recognized. And the live business is a critical part of that ecosystem. Add a new venue coming online in Portland next year, and it’s shaping up to be quite an exciting time for us. I can’t wait to dig in with the team and get to work.”

“Chris has demonstrated that he is ready to take this next step with the company,” Fedrizzi said. “Over the past eight years in Las Vegas, his results have consistently spoken for themselves, and he has shown an exceptional ability to cultivate strong artist relationships while helping to drive the business’s financial performance. I look forward to seeing the impact he will make in this expanded role.”

Since joining the AEG Presents Las Vegas team in 2018, Hammond has played a key role in elevating the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas into one of the world’s most successful boutique theaters. Most recently, he led the AEG Presents Las Vegas booking team as regional Vice President of Talent, helping shape programming while managing the agent and artist relationships that underpin one of the most fiercely competitive entertainment destinations on the planet.
