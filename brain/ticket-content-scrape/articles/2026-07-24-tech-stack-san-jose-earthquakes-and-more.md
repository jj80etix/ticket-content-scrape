# Tech Stack: San Jose Earthquakes and more…

## Summary

A weekly tech-industry round-up from TheTicketingBusiness covering three brief items: AI go-to-market platform Apollo's new sponsorship and revenue-operations partnership with MLS's San Jose Earthquakes, Live Nation/Livewire's Roblox game "Music Festival Tycoon" surpassing 3.5 million visits, and a new 10-year ticketing and marketing partnership between Paciolan and University of Texas Rio Grande Valley athletics. Each item is covered in a couple of sentences with no deeper operational or financial detail.

### Key Points
- Apollo, an AI-driven go-to-market platform, becomes San Jose Earthquakes' sleeve sponsor and technology partner for revenue operations, covering group ticket sales, sponsorship pipeline management, inbound lead routing, and season-ticket renewals.
- Apollo describes the deal as the first to combine a pro-sports sponsorship with a full revenue-operations transformation partnership.
- Live Nation/Livewire's Roblox experience "Music Festival Tycoon" has drawn over 3.5 million visits and generated more than 23 million in-game performances, letting players book artists and build their own festivals/stages.
- The game launched its first artist collaboration (K-pop group Cortis) and its first real-world festival tie-in (Lollapalooza).
- University of Texas Rio Grande Valley athletics signed a 10-year ticketing and marketing partnership with Paciolan, including custom email marketing campaigns and a promised "enhanced" ticket-buying experience.

### Highlights
- Paciolan (competitor ticketing platform) locked in a 10-year ticketing + marketing deal with UTRGV athletics — a long-duration client-retention structure.
- Live Nation/Livewire's Roblox "Music Festival Tycoon" has scaled to 3.5M+ visits and 23M in-game performances, with its first artist (Cortis) and first real-world festival (Lollapalooza) tie-ins now live.

### Industry Problem
Both items show ticketing vendors competing on long-horizon client relationships and pre-onsale fan-engagement/marketing reach rather than just transaction fees — a shift from single-event ticketing toward season-long retention and gamified demand-generation.

### Proposed Solution
Paciolan's 10-year structure and Live Nation's gamified engagement layer are both retention/engagement plays; an independent primary platform like Etix could respond by strengthening multi-year client contract incentives and lightweight pre-onsale engagement/marketing tools rather than competing only on per-ticket fees.

**Our weekly tech round-up from the world of ticketing and associated industries…**

AI go-to-market system **Apollo**  has agreed a deal with Major League Soccer’s  **San Jose Earthquakes**, becoming the official sleeve partnership and go-to-market partner.

According to Apollo, the partnership marks the first go-to-market company to combine a professional sports sponsorship with a full-scale partnership for revenue operations transformation.

Apollo will serve both as a brand sponsor and as a technology partner, powering the club’s go-to-market strategy. The Earthquakes will deploy Apollo’s system across key revenue-generating functions, including group ticket sales, sponsorship pipeline management, inbound lead routing and season ticket renewals.

Music Festival Tycoon, a new **Roblox** experience from **Live Nation**  and  **Livewire** where players build and grow their own live entertainment events, has attracted over 3.5 million visits, generating more than 23 million in-game performances.

The experience allows players to book artists, build stages and create their own festivals, and has launched its first featured artist collaboration with South Korean boyband Cortis and welcomed Lollapalooza as its first real-world festival.

Elsewhere, the athletics department of **The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley**  has struck a 10-year partnership with  **Paciolan**, to deliver ticketing and marketing solutions.

Fans will enjoy an enhanced experience throughout the ticket-buying journey, as well as custom-tailored email marketing campaigns.
