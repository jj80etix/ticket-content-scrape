# John Summit Announces Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum Headline Show

## Summary

John Summit announced a December 12 headline show at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum as part of his "CTRL ESCAPE" tour — his biggest headline show to date. General onsale is August 20, with presales for Cash App Visa Card holders and Ticketmaster-routed Spotify Premium subscribers beforehand.

### Key Points
- John Summit will headline LA Memorial Coliseum on December 12, his biggest show yet.
- General onsale August 20; Cash App Visa presale begins August 17.
- Spotify Premium subscribers get an early presale opportunity via Ticketmaster.
- Follows his recent Lollapalooza Chicago headline set.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/John-Summit.webp)

John Summit has announced a new headline show at the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum as part of his “CTRL ESCAPE” tour. The December 12 performance will be Summit’s biggest headline show to date.

The announcement follows Summit’s recent headline appearance at Lollapalooza in Chicago, where the DJ and producer performed in his hometown.

Tickets for the Los Angeles show will go on sale to the general public on August 20 at 10 a.m. PT. A presale for Cash App Visa Card customers will begin August 17 at 10 a.m. PT. Fans can access the presale by using the first nine digits of their Cash App Card.

Summit will also offer an early ticket opportunity to his U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers through Ticketmaster. Eligible fans will receive an email from Spotify with information about the presale.

The new date comes as Summit prepares for a series of international performances before launching a larger tour in October. His upcoming schedule includes shows in England and South America, along with festival appearances.

The North American tour will include stops at several major arenas and stadiums. Summit is scheduled to perform at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C., Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, United Center in Chicago and Oakland Arena in the Bay Area.

He is also set to perform at the Experts Only Festival NYC on September 19 and 20.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Summit’s official website](https://www.johnsummitmusic.com/).
