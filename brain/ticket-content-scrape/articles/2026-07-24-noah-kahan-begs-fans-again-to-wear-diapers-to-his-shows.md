# Noah Kahan Begs Fans — Again —  to Wear Diapers to His Shows

## Summary

Singer Noah Kahan has again publicly asked fans to use venue restrooms rather than relieving themselves during his shows, after a string of incidents on his "The Great Divide" tour. The piece recaps a defecation incident in Philadelphia and a reported public urination incident at Citi Field, plus Kahan's on-stage jokes about the situation in Toronto and Washington, D.C.

### Key Points
- A fan reportedly defecated near the stage during Kahan's Philadelphia show at Citizens Bank Park.
- A separate incident was reported at Citi Field involving a fan allegedly urinating into another fan's purse.
- Kahan addressed the pattern on social media and in on-stage bits during subsequent tour stops in Toronto and Washington, D.C.
- The article is a human-interest piece with no ticketing, venue-operations, or regulatory angle.
- Kahan's "The Great Divide" tour continues through August 2026 with remaining U.S. and Canadian stadium dates listed.

## Transcript

Noah Kahan, Photo via Live Nation

Noah Kahan is — once again — asking fans who can’t control themselves to please wear a diaper to his show.

Just a few weeks ago, a fan apparently [defecated near the stage](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/noah-kahan-bathroom-shows/) during a gig at Philadelphia’s Citizens Bank Park.

“If you have to poop at a show please dear god just go to the bathroom lmao,” Kahan wrote on X after the show. “I’ve pooped my pants as much as the next 29 year old but you guys gotta understand there’s a venue worker out there with a 1000 yard stare after dealing with that.”

Now, the topic has come up once again after reports circulated of a woman who dropped her shorts and allegedly peed in another fan’s purse during Kahan’s gig at Citi Field last weekend.

Following the first incident in Philadelphia, Kahan appeared at Toronto’s Roger’s Stadium and made the crowd take a pledge: “I solemnly swear I will not shit my pants, and If I do, I won’t take it from my pants and put it on the floor.”

The unnamed Congress member told the crowd that legislation has been passed “ordering everyone to wear diapers.”
