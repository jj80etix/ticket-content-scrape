# Movers and shakers: The Circuit Group, Tixel, Music Venue Trust

## Summary

IQ's latest "movers and shakers" roundup covers appointments across the live-events sector, including three additions to fan-to-fan resale platform Tixel's US leadership team — led by former DICE exec Juan Torres as VP of commercial, who framed Tixel's mission as "transforming the resale market from one filled with fraud and price gouging" into a fair fan-to-fan exchange. Also covered: The Circuit Group's new head of artist management, six new Music Venue Trust senior hires, and restructuring at German promoter Mewes Entertainment Group.

### Key Points
- Tixel added Juan Torres (ex-DICE) as VP of commercial, US, plus Alexis Rommel (head of platform partnerships) and Meghan McBreen (business development manager).
- The Circuit Group named Marc Thomas (ex-Red Light Management) head of artist management, UK and Europe.
- UK charity Music Venue Trust made six senior leadership additions as part of a growth phase, building a 21-person team across venue support, fundraising, and campaigns.
- German promoter Mewes Entertainment Group restructured leadership, establishing Munich as a new operational hub alongside Hamburg.
- Legends Global named Beau Sutherland general manager of Peoria Civic Center.

### Highlights
- Tixel's new US commercial lead explicitly positions the company's mission around fixing secondary-market fraud and price gouging via a fair fan-to-fan exchange model.
- Hire brings DICE (Tier 1 primary-platform competitor) experience directly into a resale-platform leadership role, signaling continued cross-pollination between primary and secondary ticketing talent pools.

### Industry Problem
Resale platforms are increasingly marketing themselves explicitly against fraud and price-gouging in the secondary market, a positioning aimed at both consumer trust and closer alignment with venues/promoters who are typically squeezed out of resale revenue.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor Tixel's US expansion under new commercial leadership as a signal of how "fair resale" positioning is being marketed to compete with StubHub/Vivid Seats — informs how Etix frames any resale/transfer features relative to that fairness narrative.

Management agency **The** **Circuit Group** has named Marc Thomas as its head of artist management, UK and Europe, leading the latest round of appointments across the live business.

Based in London, Thomas is charged with overseeing artist management activities across Europe while also “helping drive Circuit’s continued international expansion”, the firm said.

“A respected music industry executive with experience spanning artist management, radio, and label marketing, Thomas joins The Circuit Group following eight years at Red Light Management’s London office,” the company said upon the appointment.

Meanwhile, Dean Wilson, co-founder and CEO of The Circuit Group, commented: “Marc has an outstanding track record of developing artists and building meaningful businesses around their careers.”

In other movers and shakers news, fan-to-fan ticket platform **Tixel** has made several additions to its US leadership team, led by former DICE executive Juan Torres joining as vice president of commercial for the territory.

Torres said he was “excited to join \[…\] the mission of transforming the resale market from one filled with fraud and price gauging, to a fair fan to fan exchange that helps people trust the secondary market, and not have the venues, festivals, and promoters who put them on be squeezed out of the process”.

Torres will be based in Texas, with the company saying his remit will “span go-to-market strategy, anchor partnerships with venues, promoters, and ticketing companies”.

The company has also added Alexis Rommel as its head of platform partnerships for the US, and Meghan McBreen as business development manager.

In the UK, the **Music Venue Trust (MVT)** has made six additions to its senior leadership team, which the charity says will bring it into its “next phase of growth”.

Toni Coe-Brooker becomes campaign and communications manager, Sophie Asquith venue suport manager, and Chris Hurne head of external affairs.

Joining as partnerships manager is Denzil Thomas, while Rebecca Walker is the new live projects manager, and Adrian Norman takes up the role of financial controller.

“Toni Coe-Brooker and Rebecca Walker have both been promoted into departmental leadership roles after joining Music Venue Trust in 2020 and 2021 respectively, while Chris Hurne and Adrian Norman joined the charity earlier this year to strengthen its external affairs and financial leadership,” MVT said.

The charity added: “The appointments complete a wider organisational development programme that has established a 21-strong team across four departments, providing dedicated expertise in venue support, fundraising, partnerships, campaigns, communications, live project delivery, and finance.”

Meanwhile, German booking agency and promoter **Mewes Entertainment Group (MEG)** has revealed a new management structure.

It will see Zuzana Kürsten become director of touring and operations, where she will act as a “link” between chief executives and operational teams. Corinna Wulf has also been appointed director of finance, with MEG saying the restructure aims to create “more specialised teams in Hamburg while establishing Munich as a new operational hub”.

The move also involved the hiring of Tobias Stockmann as senior project manager, who is to be based out of the company’s Munich office. In the role, he will be responsible for market development, booking, budgeting, and project management, MEG said.

Elsewhere, production company **Raw Cereal** has hired Aaron Johnson in the role of executive producer as a part of what the firm calls an expansion of its creative production capabilities.

“Bringing nearly two decades of experience across live entertainment, commercial production, and music videos, Aaron joins the team at an exciting time of growth,” Raw Cereal said. “From Coachella and the Grammys to working with artists Bad Bunny, Sabrina Carpenter, Big Sean, Doechii and more, Aaron has built an incredible body of work across the entertainment industry.”

Finally, Legends Global has appointed Beau Sutherland as general manager of **Peoria Civic Centre**. The Sutherland has served as interim manager of the Illinois venue since June, and has been at the centre since 2000.

In the post, Sutherland will oversee all operations of the Peoria Civic Center, which includes the Prairie Home Alliance Theatre, arena, and convention centre.
