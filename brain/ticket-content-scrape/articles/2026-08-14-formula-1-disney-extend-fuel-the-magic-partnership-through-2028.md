# Formula 1, Disney Extend ‘Fuel the Magic’ Partnership Through 2028

## Summary

Formula 1 and Disney extended their "Fuel the Magic" partnership through the 2028 F1 season and expanded it to include the Cars franchise for the first time, debuting a new merchandise collection at D23 with a wider rollout Aug. 27 and a Lightning McQueen appearance planned at Monza for Cars' 20th anniversary.

### Key Points
- Partnership (launched 2025) extended through 2028; now includes Disney/Pixar's Cars alongside existing Mickey & Friends branding.
- Six-product merch collection debuts at D23's Disney Store booth, online Aug. 14, wider rollout Aug. 27.
- Monza (Italian GP) will host a Lightning McQueen appearance tied to Cars' 20th anniversary.

## Transcript

Formula 1 and Disney are putting more fuel in the tank.

The two brands have extended their global “Fuel the Magic” collaboration through the 2028 Formula 1 season, while expanding the partnership to include Disney and Pixar’s *Cars* franchise for the first time.

Launched in 2025, “Fuel the Magic” has brought Mickey & Friends into the F1 world through merchandise, digital content, race-weekend activations and fan experiences. The new agreement adds another season to the original partnership and opens the door for Lightning McQueen and the *Cars* universe to join the action.

“The success of our partnership with Disney has demonstrated what’s possible when two globally loved brands come together around a shared vision for creativity, storytelling and fan engagement,” Formula 1 Chief Commercial Officer Emily Prazer said in a press release. “Extending our relationship reflects the strength of that collaboration, and our ambition to continue creating new experiences for fans around the world.”

The expanded partnership was announced Friday at D23: The Ultimate Disney Fan Event, where attendees are getting the first look at a new Disney and Pixar *Cars* Collection with Formula 1.

Six products are debuting at the Disney Store booth at D23, including an exclusive hat and fleece along with Mattel die-cast vehicles. The collection also launches online Aug. 14 before a wider global rollout Aug. 27. Products will arrive at the F1 Flagship Store during the Italian Grand Prix weekend at Monza.

Monza will also provide the setting for one of the partnership’s biggest crossover moments yet, with Lightning McQueen appearing in a new look as Disney celebrates 20 years of *Cars*.

“This merchandise collection is a natural next step and brings together the excitement of Formula 1 with Disney and Pixar’s *Cars* in a way that feels both authentic and nostalgic,” Prazer added. “For many fans, *Cars* was an early introduction to the thrill of racing.”

Disney Senior Vice President of Marketing for Consumer Products Joss Hastings said bringing *Cars* into the partnership creates another way to connect two highly engaged fan bases.

“Building on the momentum established with Mickey & Friends, expanding our relationship to include Pixar’s *Cars* at Monza GP presents an exciting opportunity to ‘Fuel the Magic’ and bring together two fandom communities that share the same love of speed, spectacle, and heart,” Hastings said.

The collaboration has already produced activations at several major F1 events. It debuted at the 2025 Las Vegas Grand Prix with a Disney Live Entertainment performance at the Fountains of Bellagio before expanding to Shanghai, Montreal and Silverstone in 2026.

At the British Grand Prix, Formula 1 and Disney also teamed with Make-A-Wish on a custom Mickey Mouse-inspired racing helmet signed by all 20 current F1 drivers. The helmet sold for £151,000, or approximately $201,000, setting a record as the highest-value charitable item sold through F1 Authentics.

A second custom helmet is being displayed at D23, while U.S. fans can enter a sweepstakes for a replica, with net proceeds benefiting Make-A-Wish America.

Formula 1 and Disney said additional details about “Fuel the Magic” activations planned for the 2028 season will be announced in the coming months.
