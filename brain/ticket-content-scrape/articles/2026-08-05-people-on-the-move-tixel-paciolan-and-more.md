# People on the move: Tixel, Paciolan and more…

## Summary

TheTicketingBusiness rounds up personnel moves across the ticketing sector: Tixel built out its US leadership team behind new VP of Commercial Juan Torres, Paciolan brought on a VP/GM for performing arts, and Live Nation, the NFL, and the Premier League each made ticketing/hospitality leadership appointments.

### Key Points
- Tixel added several US hires led by VP of Commercial Juan Torres, plus a head of platform partnerships (ex-AXS), a business development manager (ex-Newport Folk Festival), and a US operations associate (ex-MSG Entertainment).
- Paciolan (a Learfield subsidiary) named Jenifer LaMorte VP/GM of its performing-arts segment.
- The NFL promoted Darren Ali to director of EMEA ticketing and global hospitality sales; the Premier League promoted Kate Bickell to head of hospitality and ticketing.
- Live Nation Entertainment hired Kaine Opere (ex-AO Arena, Glasgow Commonwealth Games) as director of ticketing operations and fan experience for VIP Nation International.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**We round up the latest movements from the ticketing sector and associated industries…**

Tixel has appointed several new hires to its US team, led by fellow new hire **Juan Torres**, vice president of commercial, US.

It has been a year since [Tixel officially launched](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2025/06/tixel-officially-launches-in-the-us/) in the US market, with the fan-to-fan ticket resale platform securing partnerships with primary ticketing businesses Eventim USA, Leap Event Technology, and Opendate.

To continue this growth, **Alexis Rommel** has been appointed as head of platform partnerships, US, joining from AXS where she was director of strategy.

**Meghan McBreen** has joined Tixel as business development manager, US. McBreen most recently held ticketing leadership roles at Newport Folk Festival and Park City Song Summit.

Also joining the team is **Jennifer Lee**, who has taken on the role of US operations associate. Lee has joined from Madison Square Garden Entertainment.

Elsewhere, **Darren Ali** has been promoted to director of EMEA ticketing and global hospitality sales at the National Football League (NFL), and **Kate Bickell** has been promoted to head of hospitality and ticketing at the Premier League.

Paciolan has brought **Jenifer LaMorte** on board as vice president and general manager, performing arts, where she will lead the company’s dedicated performing arts segment, overseeing strategy, client partnerships, and growth.

Paciolan is a ticketing, fundraising, marketing and analytics platform for live entertainment, college sports, professional sports and more. It is a subsidiary of Learfield.

The University of Northern Iowa’s (UNI) athletics department has hired **Jens Jorgensen** as its new athletics ticketing account executive, where he will assist in ticket sales for events and guest experiences for fans.

Additionally, UNI Athletics has promoted **Dylan Petersen** to the role of director of sales.

After two years at Manchester’s AO Arena and a stint working as ticketing manager for the Glasgow Commonwealth Games, **Kaine Opere** has joined Live Nation Entertainment as director of ticketing operations and fan experience – VIP Nation International.
