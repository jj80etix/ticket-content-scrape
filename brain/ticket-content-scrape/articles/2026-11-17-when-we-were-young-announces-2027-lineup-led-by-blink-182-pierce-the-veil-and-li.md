# When We Were Young Announces 2027 Lineup Led by blink-182, Pierce the Veil and Limp Bizkit

## Summary

When We Were Young festival returns to the Las Vegas Festival Grounds on Oct. 16, 2027 after a 2026 hiatus, with blink-182, Pierce the Veil, Limp Bizkit and Third Eye Blind topping a lineup of dozens of pop-punk, emo and nu-metal acts. Organizers, who skipped 2026 to "give the festival the care it deserves," open a presale Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. PT via registration, with GA+, VIP, and hotel packages also available; no second-day or general-onsale date has been confirmed.

### Key Points
- Fifth edition since 2022, returning after a one-year hiatus; single date (Oct. 16, 2027) announced so far.
- Headliners include blink-182, Pierce the Veil, Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, All Time Low, Simple Plan.
- Presale Nov. 20, 10-11 a.m. PT via text-delivered codes; lowest GA pricing advertised during the first hour.
- General public onsale date not yet set; additional lineup/second-day details may follow, per 2025 precedent of an added day.

When We Were Young Festival is officially returning to Las Vegas in 2027, unveiling a massive lineup led by blink-182, Pierce the Veil, Limp Bizkit and Third Eye Blind after taking a one-year hiatus in 2026.

The fifth edition of the emo and pop-punk festival will take place Saturday, October 16, 2027, at the Las Vegas Festival Grounds, with 5 Seconds of Summer, Circa Survive and The Used also among the top-billed performers.

Announced Tuesday, November 17, the festival’s return brings together dozens of acts spanning pop-punk, emo, alternative rock, post-hardcore and nu-metal, continuing the event’s focus on music from the late 1990s and 2000s.

Other prominent names on the bill include All Time Low, Simple Plan, Taking Back Sunday, Sleeping With Sirens, Hot Mulligan, Thrice, Thursday, The Maine, Portugal. The Man, One OK Rock, New Found Glory, Bayside, Senses Fail and Hawthorne Heights.

The lineup also includes Michelle Branch, Ashlee Simpson, Natasha Bedingfield, Story of the Year, Underoath, The Starting Line, Cartel, The Academy Is…, Plain White T’s, Saves the Day and several other acts.

The 2027 festival will be presented by 7-Eleven.

The announcement marks the festival’s return after organizers elected to skip 2026, interrupting a run of four consecutive annual editions that began in 2022.

As [TicketNews previously reported](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/when-we-were-young-year-off-return-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), organizers announced in February that they would take a year off to prepare for the festival’s future. At that point, no dates or lineup had been announced for 2026.

“After an unforgettable run in Las Vegas, we’ve decided to take 2026 off to give this festival the care it deserves and to make sure what comes next feels just as special as what came before,” organizers said at the time.

They promised the festival would return in October 2027, describing the break as a temporary pause rather than an ending.

The 2025 festival was headlined by blink-182 and Panic! at the Disco, with the latter delivering a reunion performance celebrating the 20th anniversary of its debut album, *A Fever You Can’t Sweat Out*. Other past headliners have included My Chemical Romance, Green Day, Paramore and Fall Out Boy.

The festival has also previously expanded its schedule in response to demand. In 2025, organizers [added a second day](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/02/when-we-were-young-second-day-added/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), featuring the same lineup on consecutive dates.

For 2027, only October 16 has been announced so far, with no confirmation of a second festival date.

Tickets for When We Were Young 2027 will be available through a presale beginning **Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. PT**.

Fans can register for access through the [official festival presale website](https://www.whenwewereyoungfestival.com/presale?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). A confirmation message sent to registrants states that the presale window will run from 10 to 11 a.m. PT, with access codes distributed by text message that morning.

The festival’s website advertises its lowest general-admission ticket pricing during the first hour of the presale. Additional ticket options include GA+, VIP and VIP Cabana packages, along with hotel packages.

A separate general public onsale time has not yet been confirmed.
