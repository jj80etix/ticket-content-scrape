# Destroy Boys Set 22-Date North American Tour for Spring 2027

## Summary

Destroy Boys announced a 22-city North American headline tour for March-April 2027, "Destroy Boys Presents FOIE GRAS," supporting their forthcoming album of the same name.

### Key Points
- Tour opens March 13 in Los Angeles, closes April 16 in San Francisco.
- Follows the March 5 release of the album FOIE GRAS on Epitaph Records.
- Sole Canadian date: Toronto's Danforth Music Hall, April 2.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Destroy-Boys-North-American-Tour.webp)

Destroy Boys will launch a North American headline tour in March 2027, bringing their forthcoming album *FOIE GRAS* to 22 cities across the United States and Canada.

Billed as “Destroy Boys Presents FOIE GRAS,” the tour opens March 13 at The Fonda Theatre in Los Angeles and closes April 16 at August Hall in San Francisco. The opening show falls eight days after the album’s scheduled March 5 release through Epitaph Records.

Following the Los Angeles opener, the band will play Phoenix before heading to Texas for performances in Dallas and Houston. The route then moves through Orlando, Atlanta, Carrboro, North Carolina, and Richmond, Virginia, ahead of a March 26 appearance at Warsaw in Brooklyn.

The Northeast stretch continues with Boston on March 29, Philadelphia on March 30 and Washington, D.C., on March 31. Toronto’s Danforth Music Hall will host the tour’s only Canadian performance on April 2.

From there, Destroy Boys return to the United States for shows in Lakewood, Ohio; Detroit; Chicago; and Minneapolis. The final portion of the tour includes Denver and Salt Lake City before the band reaches the Pacific Northwest for Portland and Seattle performances. The San Francisco finale follows three days after the Seattle stop.

Illuminati Hotties and The Rat Utopia Experiment will support the full run.

Artist presales begin October 28 at 10 a.m. local time, with registration available through the band’s website. General ticket sales follow October 30 at 10 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [destroydestroyboys.com](http://destroydestroyboys.com).
