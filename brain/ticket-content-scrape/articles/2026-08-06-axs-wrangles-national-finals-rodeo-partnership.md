# AXS wrangles National Finals Rodeo partnership

## Summary

AXS has been named the exclusive primary and secondary ticketing partner for the Wrangler National Finals Rodeo (Thomas & Mack Center, Las Vegas, Dec 3-12, its 41st year), in a deal with producer Las Vegas Events. The expanded partnership bundles primary ticketing, official fan-to-fan resale, and sponsorship under one agreement, letting season-ticket holders manage tickets and resale through a single AXS account/app. The move is a competitive data point on how a Tier 2 consumer-major platform is consolidating primary and secondary ticketing control around a high-demand annual event.

### Key Points

- AXS becomes exclusive primary and official secondary (fan-to-fan) ticketing partner for the NFR, plus sponsorship/branding assets (onsite, in-arena, digital activations).
- Season tickets and marketplace listings go on sale August 12; general public onsale follows August 13.
- The 2025 NFR drew 175,846 attendees across 10 days and sold out 379 consecutive performances, underscoring the event's consistent demand.
- AXS positions the deal as giving season-ticket holders a single connected account for tickets and resale, backed by its "safety and security protocols."
- Las Vegas Events (a nonprofit funded partly by hotel room tax) has produced or supported 800+ events since 1991.

### Highlights

- Deal scope: primary ticketing + official fan-to-fan resale marketplace + sponsorship, all under one exclusive AXS agreement.
- Key dates: season-ticket/marketplace access August 12; general public onsale August 13; event runs December 3-12, 2026.
- Demand signal: 175,846 attendees and 379 consecutive sold-out performances in 2025.

### Industry Problem

Rights-holders like Las Vegas Events want a single, trusted, integrated ticketing-plus-resale experience for season-ticket holders rather than fragmented primary/secondary systems — and are consolidating that control with one vendor to reduce leakage to unofficial resale channels and gain "richer insights" into fan/ticket-holder behavior.

### Proposed Solution

AXS's answer is a bundled primary + official resale + sponsorship deal delivered through one account and app, capturing resale volume that would otherwise go to third-party marketplaces. Etix could pursue similar anchor-client deals by building or improving a native official resale/marketplace layer and unified account experience for season-ticket and recurring-event clients, strengthening its pitch against Tier 2 platforms for exclusive, multi-year partnerships.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**AXS has been brought in by Las Vegas Events to create a more unified experience for National Finals Rodeo ticket holders.**

The Wrangler National Finals Rodeo will take place at the Thomas & Mack Center from December 3-12, marking its 41st year.

AXS has been named the exclusive primary and secondary ticketing partner, with the expanded relationship bringing ticketing, official resale and sponsorship under one deal. The goal is to create a more integrated experience for NFR season ticket holders and fans, who will be able to manage their tickets and resale activity through their AXS account and mobile app.

Those looking to attend individual performances will also have access to the official marketplace.

Season tickets and tickets listed through the official fan-to-fan marketplace will be available from August 12, while the general public ticket onsale will follow on August 13.

As part of the deal, AXS will receive assets that include onsite, in-arena and in-market branding and exposure opportunities, digital activations and promotions through NFR social and digital channels, and additional opportunities throughout the rodeo season.

“Las Vegas Events is pleased to partner with AXS to provide NFR season ticket holders and fans with a more integrated ticketing experience,” said the company’s president Tim Keener.

“Season ticket holders will be able to manage their accounts through an easy-to-navigate platform supported by AXS’s safety and security protocols. AXS will also become the NFR’s official Fan-to-Fan Ticket Marketplace, giving rodeo fans a trusted place to find and sell tickets as they plan their NFR experience in Las Vegas.”

Tom Andrus, president of North America at AXS, added: “The Wrangler National Finals Rodeo is a bucket-list event with an incredibly dedicated fan base and sustained demand year after year.

“With AXS powering both primary ticketing and official resale, fans get a seamless, trusted experience from a connected ecosystem while NFR gains richer insights and greater control over their own tickets.”

The National Finals Rodeo brings together the top 15 contestants in bareback riding, steer wrestling, team roping, saddle bronc riding, tie-down roping, barrel racing and bull riding. In 2025, the event attracted 175,846 people across 10 days of competition, selling out 379 consecutive performances in Las Vegas.

Las Vegas Events is a private, not-for-profit organisation that is partially funded by hotel room tax. Since 1991, Las Vegas Events has produced, presented or supported more than 800 events.
