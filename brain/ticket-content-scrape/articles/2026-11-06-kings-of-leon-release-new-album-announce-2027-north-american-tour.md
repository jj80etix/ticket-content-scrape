# Kings Of Leon Release New Album & Announce 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Kings of Leon released their 10th studio album, *O My Beloved*, and announced an accompanying 30-city North American tour for 2027, their first self-produced LP. The band describes the record as its most "intimate and unguarded" to date, with the tour running May 8 to October 15, 2027.

### Key Points
- Album *O My Beloved* released via Love Tap Records/Virgin Music; band's 10th LP and first self-produced.
- 30-city North American tour, May 8 (Bristow, VA) through Oct. 15, 2027 (Madison Square Garden).
- Support acts: Band of Horses, Dexter and The Moonrocks.
- Spotify "Reserved" presale begins Nov. 9, followed by Artist, Citi, and Amex presales.

## Transcript

To go along with Kings of Leon’s new studio album, *O My Beloved*, the rock band has announced a 30-city North American tour featuring special guests Band of Horses and Dexter and The Moonrocks.

*O My Beloved* – released today via Love Tap Records, distributed by Virgin Music – marks the band’s 10th full-length studio album and its first self-produced LP. An announcement from the band’s team described the album as a deeply personal collection that “explores themes of family, gratitude, and the everyday moments that have shaped the lives” of Caleb, Nathan, Jared and Matthew Followill.

Fans can expect Kings of Leon’s most “intimate and unguarded record to date,” with the band departing from its expansive sound to “return to the raw, unfiltered spirit of the music they made when they first came to Nashville.”

The North American tour features a mix of amphitheatres and arenas routed across the U.S. and Canada, starting with a May 8 show in Bristow, Virginia at Jiffy Lube Live. Dates are booked through an Oct. 15 performance at New York’s famed Madison Square Garden.

A Reserved by Spotify presale begins Nov. 9 at 9 a.m. local time, followed by Artist, Citi, and Amex presales beginning Tuesday, November 10 at 9 a.m. Additional presales run throughout the week ahead of the general public onsale on Friday, Nov. 13 at 9 a.m.

Kings of Leon is booked by WME in North America.
