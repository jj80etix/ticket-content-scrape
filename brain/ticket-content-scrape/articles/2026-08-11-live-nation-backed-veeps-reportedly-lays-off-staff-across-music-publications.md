# Live Nation-backed Veeps reportedly lays off staff across music publications

## Summary

Veeps, the ticketed livestream and media company majority-owned by Live Nation Entertainment since 2021, has reportedly cut nearly all editorial staff across its music publications — BrooklynVegan, Alternative Press, Revolver, and Goldmine — according to a Pitchfork report. The publications, acquired starting around 2020 by Veeps parent MDDN (founded by Good Charlotte's Joel and Benji Madden) and consolidated under the Veeps umbrella last year, remain online for now despite the cuts.

### Key Points

- Veeps has been majority-owned by Live Nation Entertainment since 2021.
- Nearly all editorial staff reportedly cut across BrooklynVegan, Alternative Press, Revolver, and Goldmine.
- Alternative Press (founded 1985) was Veeps/MDDN's initial publishing acquisition (~2020); BrooklynVegan, Revolver, Goldmine, and Metal Edge joined the Veeps umbrella last year (2025).
- Publications remain online for now despite the staff cuts.
- Cuts reported via Pitchfork; the article notes no independent statement from Veeps itself.

### Highlights

- Live Nation's Veeps subsidiary absorbed five legacy music-media brands (Alternative Press, BrooklynVegan, Revolver, Goldmine, Metal Edge) between 2020 and 2025, then cut nearly all their editorial staff in 2026.
- Majority ownership by Live Nation Entertainment since 2021 places this ticketed-livestream/media rollup inside the dominant incumbent's portfolio, not an independent player's.
- Cuts hit five brands simultaneously, indicating a consolidation-then-retrenchment pattern rather than a single isolated cost-cutting move.

### Industry Problem

Illustrates how the dominant incumbent (Live Nation) continues to expand into adjacent media/livestream properties and then rationalizes costs once acquired, a structural signal of ongoing consolidation in the live-events ecosystem that independent platforms compete against.

### Proposed Solution

No direct product response applies here; the right posture is to monitor how Live Nation's adjacent-market bets (livestream, music media) perform and consolidate, since retrenchment in newly acquired assets can foreshadow broader cost discipline or portfolio-focus shifts worth tracking for competitive positioning and independence messaging.
