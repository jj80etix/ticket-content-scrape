# Florida Georgia Line, Megan Moroney Headline 2027 Minnesota Country Club Festival

## Summary

Florida Georgia Line and Megan Moroney will headline the second annual Minnesota Country Club Festival, returning July 9–10, 2027, to Harriet Island Regional Park in St. Paul. The two-day, two-stage event features nearly 20 performers with no overlapping sets.

### Key Points
- Moroney headlines Friday, July 9; Florida Georgia Line closes Saturday, July 10.
- Supporting acts include Dan + Shay, Shaboozey, Warren Zeiders, Gabby Barrett, and others.
- Organizers guarantee no overlapping sets across the two stages.
- Presale registration opens via the festival's own site beginning Nov. 19.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Minnesota-Country-Club.webp)

Florida Georgia Line and Megan Moroney will headline the second annual Minnesota Country Club Festival, returning July 9-10, 2027, to Harriet Island Regional Park in St. Paul, Minnesota.

The two-day event will feature nearly 20 performers across two stages, with organizers again promising no overlapping sets so attendees can see every artist on the schedule.

Moroney will headline Friday, July 9, joined by Dan + Shay, Stella Lefty, Wyatt Flores, Ernest, Cooper Alan, Kaitlin Butts, the Jack Wharff Band, Ian Harrison and the Sullivan Sisters.

Florida Georgia Line will close Saturday, July 10, with Ty Myers, Shaboozey, Warren Zeiders, Gabby Barrett, Josh Ross, Wade Forster, Belles and Abby Webster also scheduled to perform.
