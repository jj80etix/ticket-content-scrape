# La Roux Announced ‘Old Flames’ North American Tour

## Summary

La Roux has announced the 'Old Flames' North American tour, her first headline US/Canada run in several years, in support of new material. Tickets go on sale through the standard artist-presale-then-general-public cycle.

### Key Points
- Tour spans North American markets with support acts to be announced.
- Tied to a new release cycle for La Roux.
- Standard presale/general on-sale structure; no ticketing-industry angle.
- No pricing, platform, or regulatory details reported.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/La-Roux.webp)

La Roux will return to North America for a 13-date headlining tour in early 2027, bringing her upcoming album *Old Flames* to venues across the United States, Canada and Mexico.

The run begins January 31 at Toronto’s Danforth Music Hall and concludes February 25 at Pabellón Oeste in Mexico City. It marks the synth-pop artist’s first extensive North American headlining tour in 12 years, following three intimate performances in Los Angeles and New York in 2025.

The itinerary includes February performances at Boston’s Paradise Rock Club, Washington’s 9:30 Club and Brooklyn Steel before heading to Chicago and Minneapolis. Additional stops include Englewood, Colorado, Salt Lake City, Seattle and Portland.

The final U.S. performances are scheduled for February 19 at The Belasco in Los Angeles and February 20 at The Castro in San Francisco. After the Mexico City finale, La Roux will begin a European and U.K. leg in March, with performances in Berlin, Cologne, Amsterdam, Paris, Glasgow, Manchester, Bristol and London.

*Old Flames*, scheduled for release November 6, is La Roux’s fourth studio album.

Fan club members will receive the first opportunity to purchase tickets beginning Wednesday, September 30, at 10 a.m. local time. A Live Nation presale for select performances follows Thursday, October 1, at 10 a.m. local time through the company’s free All Access membership program.

General ticket sales begin Friday, October 2, at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [La Roux’s official website](https://laroux.co.uk/).
