# Anyma Headlining Zamna Tulum’s 10th Anniversary, Bringing ‘ÆDEN’ To The Mayan Coast

## Summary

Electronic artist Anyma will bring his immersive "ÆDEN" audiovisual show to Zamna Tulum's 10th-anniversary season (Dec. 31–Jan. 10), performing Jan. 4 with dedicated ticketing. Zamna, founded in Tulum in 2017, now runs events worldwide including India, Brazil and the Middle East.

### Key Points
- Anyma performs "ÆDEN" Jan. 4 as part of Zamna Tulum's 10th-anniversary season; dedicated ticketing for the show.
- Zamna Tulum's anniversary season runs Dec. 31, 2026 through Jan. 10, 2027, with more artists to be announced.
- Anyma previously made history as the first electronic artist to headline a Sphere Las Vegas residency, grossing $31M+ over 12 dates.
- Booked by CAA and managed by Encode Talent.

Electronic music star Anyma is bringing his audiovisual experience “ÆDEN” to the Yucatán Peninsula in Mexico. The DJ will perform his immersive set using cutting-edge technology at Zamna Tulum, one of the world’s biggest electronic music festivals.

Anyma’s ambitious audiovisual tour stop in Mexico is part of Zamna Tulum’s highly anticipated 10 <sup>th</sup> anniversary season, which begins Dec. 31 and runs through Jan. 10. Anyma performs on Jan. 4, and the event will have dedicated ticketing.

Zamna was founded in the jungles of Tulum in 2017 and has become a leader in the production of electronic music experiences, with events all over the globe, including India, New York, Brazil, Indonesia, the Middle East, Hawaii, Montreal, Peru and Thailand.

The announcement of the Anyma show is just a sneak peek of what fans can expect for Zamna Tulum’s 10 <sup>th</sup> anniversary season. Additional artists, experiences, and details about the event will be revealed in the coming weeks. For tickets, hotel packages, VIP table reservations and additional information, visit [ZamnaFestival.com](https://zamnafestival.com/).

Anyma, who has garnered more than 7.1 million monthly listeners on Spotify, has performed all over the globe this year, including shows in Chicago, Mexico City, Lebanon, Poland and London. He also had one of the most talked-about sets at Coachella, with guests LISA of BLACKPINK, Muse’s Matt Bellamy, Joji and Swae Lee.

The standout DJ made history in late 2024 and early 2025 as the first electronic music artist to headline Sphere in Las Vegas. His 12-date residency grossed more than $31 million, averaging about $2.6 million per night, according to Pollstar Boxoffice Reports.

Anyma is booked by Creative Artists Agency and managed by Encode Talent.
