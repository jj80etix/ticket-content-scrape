# ILMC 39 launches: Around the World in 80 Hours

## Summary

The International Live Music Conference (ILMC) returns to London's Royal Lancaster Hotel February 23–26, 2027 for its 39th edition, themed "Around the World in 80 Hours," expecting over 1,700 attendees among roughly 3,000 total across ILMC, Soho Calling, Touring Entertainment Live and the Arthur Awards. This year brings the biggest venue reconfiguration since 2023, including a new ground-floor networking space, plus dedicated Asia Live and Latam Live programming.

### Key Points
- Dates: Feb. 23–26, 2027, Royal Lancaster Hotel, London; Arthur Awards move to London Hilton Park Lane with 600 guests.
- New "ILMC Central" ground-floor networking space debuts as the biggest layout change in four years.
- New programming tracks focus on Asia and Latin America, plus expanded access routes for younger executives.
- Platinum partners: Live Nation and Ticketmaster; gold partners CTS Eventim and Legends Global.

## Transcript

The live industry's largest gathering returns to London's Royal Lancaster Hotel in February with "a race around the live music world"

The world’s largest gathering of live music professionals, the International Live Music Conference (ILMC), has unveiled its theme of ‘Around the World in 8o Hours’ for its 39th edition, ahead of its return to London from 23-26 February 2027.

Under this theme, more than 1,700 of the industry’s leading pioneers and trailblazers will gather at London’s Royal Lancaster Hotel for the start of “an unforgettable race as the touring party crosses borders and seeks out new markets, routes and opportunities”, organisers say.

Altogether, the four-day conference will bring together around 3,000 professionals for the world’s leading gathering of live music executives across ILMC, Soho Calling, Touring Entertainment Live and the Arthur Awards 2027, which this year will move to London Hilton Park Lane.

The Arthur Awards are again expanded in this new location and will be hosted by CAA’s Emma Banks, with 600 guests in attendance.

This year sees the biggest changes to the conference set-up since it moved to the Royal Lancaster four years ago. The spaces have been completely reconfigured to create ILMC Central, a giant ground floor networking space where hundreds of people can meet up at the same time.

> “ILMC 39 provides a reinvigorated space to serve the needs of the most influential execs in the business”

Greg Parmley, head of ILMC, says: “The world’s largest gathering of live music and entertainment professionals is back, ready to provide a reinvigorated space to serve the needs of the most influential execs in the business.”

“This year we are focusing on two of the most compelling areas of the global business with our Asia Live and Latam Live programmes, as well as bringing conversations focussed on younger executives into the heart of the main conference, with a number of routes to enable free or discounted entry to ensure that the next generation of leaders are well-represented,” he adds.

ILMC’s Central London showcase event, Soho Calling, has also become a key part of the conference week and will take place again across eight music venues on the evening of Wednesday 24 February.

In addition to ILMC’s schedule of debates, workshops, keynotes, and events, the conference sees the return of Touring Entertainment Live, dedicated to the global business of big brand live entertainment, exhibitions, and touring family shows, on Thursday 25 February.

“Touring Entertainment Live and Soho Calling are growing year on year, the core ILMC conference remains the place where the most important issues are debated and the biggest deals are done and the Arthur Awards 2027 will be the best yet,” Parmley says.

ILMC’s platinum partners are Live Nation and Ticketmaster, and the event is also supported by gold partners CTS Eventim and Legends Global, silver partners atVenu, Aviva Arena, AXS, BWO Entertainment, DEAG Entertainment Group, Showsec, and Tysers Live.

Legends Global will again support ILMC’s Alia Dann Swift Bursary Scheme, which offers a complimentary conference pass and mentoring opportunities to 30 young professionals.

Full details are available on the ILMC 39 website [here](https://39.ilmc.com/).
