# S Club Announces 13-Show UK and Ireland Arena Tour for 2027

## Summary

S Club announced the "Ain't No Party Like A S Club Party Tour," a 13-show UK/Ireland arena reunion run beginning October 8, 2027 at Glasgow's OVO Hydro, with special guests Boyzlife.

### Key Points
- All five current members — Bradley McIntosh, Jo O'Meara, Jon Lee, Rachel Stevens, Tina Barrett — participating
- Includes Manchester's AO Arena (Oct. 9) and two shows at London's The O2 (Oct. 16, matinee and evening)

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/S-Club.webp)

S Club will return to arenas across the United Kingdom and Ireland in 2027, announcing a 13-show tour featuring special guests Boyzlife.

The “Ain’t No Party Like A S Club Party Tour” begins October 8, 2027, at Glasgow’s OVO Hydro. Bradley McIntosh, Jo O’Meara, Jon Lee, Rachel Stevens and Tina Barrett will perform together on the run, which includes Manchester’s AO Arena on October 9.

The group will also play two performances at London’s The O2 on October 16, with a matinee and an evening concert scheduled for the same day.

Taking to social media, the group shared, “There really Ain’t No Party Like An S Club Party, and we’re so excited to be back for a new tour across the UK & Ireland in October 2027! We can’t wait to celebrate the songs, memories and moments we’ve shared together. It’s going to be one big party and we want you all to be part of it.”

Boyzlife will appear on every date. The duo consists of Keith Duffy, formerly of Boyzone, and former Westlife member Brian McFadden.

The announcement follows S Club’s 2023 “Good Times Tour,” which took place after the death of original member Paul Cattermole at age 46. The group had announced its reunion before Cattermole’s death. Hannah Spearritt, another founding member, subsequently departed, leaving the five performers who will return for the upcoming shows.

Tickets go on sale November 6 at 9:30 a.m. UK time. A fan presale begins November 4 at 9:30 a.m. local time, with advance registration required for access.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [S Club’s official website](https://www.sclub7.co.uk/).
