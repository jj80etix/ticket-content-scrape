# Luke Bryan, Megan Moroney To Headline SoCal’s Coastal Country Jam 2027

## Summary

Coastal Country Jam returns to Huntington Beach, CA in 2027 with Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney headlining, after the festival spent recent years in Long Beach. The two-day event runs May 22–23 with more than a dozen acts.

### Key Points
- Luke Bryan headlines night one (May 22); Megan Moroney headlines night two (May 23).
- Supporting acts include Thomas Rhett, Josh Ross, Parker McCollum, and Avery Anna.
- Produced by Activated Events, returning the festival to its original Huntington Beach site.

Coastal Country Jam is returning to Huntington Beach in 2027 with Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney headlining the SoCal beachside country festival.

Activated Events, the independent live event company behind the festival, announced that after moving the annual event to Long Beach in 2023, Country Coastal Jam is returning to Huntington Beach, the city where it all began, to deliver a unique event along the coastline featuring more than a dozen acts.

“We’ve been working for the last couple of years to bring Coastal Country Jam back to where it all started, and it feels good to be returning to Huntington Beach with Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney,” said Steve Thacher, Founder and CEO of Activated Events. “We really enjoyed our time in Long Beach, and we’re grateful to everyone who joined us there. We’re back, and we can’t wait to see everyone on the sand.”

The festival takes place May 22 and 23, with Bryan closing out the first night following performances from Thomas Rhett, Josh Ross, Ashley Cooke, Myles Morgan, Joe Peters and an act to be announced later.

Moroney headlines the second night, which also features Parker McCollum, Avery Anna, Corey Kent, John Morgan, Colin Lynch and Presley Tennant.

The two-day festival also offers on-site experiences like The Belt Buckle Line Dancing Hall, The Smoke Show BBQ pitmaster cooking demos, Pour Decisions mixology bar, Rosé Rodeo wine bar, On The Wagon mocktail bar, Tailgate Tavern sports bar, cornhole tournament and mechanical bull riding.

Fans can register now at [coastalcountryjam.com](https://www.coastalcountryjam.com/) for presale access to single-day and two-day passes. The presale begins Nov. 12 at 10 a.m. PT, and the general onsale begins Nov. 13 at 10 a.m. PT.
