# Schoolboy Q Plots ‘10 Years of Blank Face LP’ Tour

## Summary

Schoolboy Q announced the "10 Years of Blank Face LP" tour celebrating a decade since his fourth studio album, kicking off October 1 in Atlanta and running through October 17 in Las Vegas, with stops including New York City, Washington D.C., Los Angeles, and Phoenix.

### Key Points
- Ten dates from Oct. 1 (Atlanta) through Oct. 17 (Brooklyn Bowl, Las Vegas).
- Marks the 10th anniversary of *Blank Face LP* (2014), featuring singles "Groovy Tony," "That Part," and "Overtime."
- Artist presale begins Aug. 4; public on-sale follows Aug. 7 via groovyq.com.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0785.jpeg)

Schoolboy Q is celebrating a decade of *Blank Face* *LP*.

The “10 Years of Blank Face LP” tour will kick-off in Atlanta on October 1. From there, he’ll appear in New York City, Washington, D.C., Los Angeles, Phoenix, and Oakland before wrapping-up at the Brooklyn Bowl in Las Vegas on October 17.

Tickets head on sale starting with an artist presale August 4 at 10 a.m. local time [here](https://www.groovyq.com/tour/), followed by a public onsale August 7 [here](https://www.groovyq.com/tour/).

*Blank Face LP*, Schoolboy Q’s fourth studio album, dropped in 2014. The record features singles “Groovy Tony,” “That Part,” and “Overtime.”
