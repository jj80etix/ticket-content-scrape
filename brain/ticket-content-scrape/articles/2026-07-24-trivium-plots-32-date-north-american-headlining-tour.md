# Trivium Plots 32-Date North American Headlining Tour

## Summary

Metal band Trivium announced a 32-date North American headlining run, the "Crown in the Grave Tour," kicking off November 5 in St. Petersburg, FL and wrapping December 19 in Charlotte, NC. The tour features support from In Flames, Orbit Culture, Fit For An Autopsy, and Frozen Soul, and tickets went on sale Friday via the band's official website.

### Key Points
- 32-date tour spans the US and Canada, running Nov. 5 through Dec. 19, 2026.
- Support acts include In Flames, Orbit Culture, Fit For An Autopsy, and Frozen Soul.
- Tickets sold via Trivium's official website (trivium.org).
- Band teased new music but has not announced a follow-up to 2021's *In The Court of the Dragon*.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0762.jpeg)

The metalheads of Trivium will hit the road this fall for a headlining run across North America.

The 32-date “Crown in the Grave Tour” kicks-off in St. Petersburg, Florida at Jannus Live on November 5. From there, they’ll head to New Haven’s College Street Music Hall, The Palladium in Worcester, South Side Ballroom in Dallas, Seattle’s Paramount Theater, and The Masonic in San Francisco, stopping in cities along the way like Nashville, Toronto, Laval, Las Vegas, Seattle, and Omaha. The Fillmore in Charlotte will wrap-up the tour on December 19.

“This fall’s Trivium tour is a huge one for us,” the band noted in a statement. “The lineup is stacked from top to bottom and we’re honored to have special guests In Flames, who not only influenced our early years but continue to prove time and again why they’re one of the greatest metal bands of all time.”

“With Orbit Culture, Fit For An Autopsy and Frozen Soul rounding out the bill, we can’t wait to get out there, kick off this next era of Trivium with Rudi behind the kit and give you a taste of the new music we’ve been working on. We’ll see you soon!”
