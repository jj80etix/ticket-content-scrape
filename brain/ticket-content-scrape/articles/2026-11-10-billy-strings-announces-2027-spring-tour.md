# Billy Strings Announces 2027 Spring Tour

## Summary

Billy Strings announced a 15-date Spring 2027 U.S. arena/amphitheater tour beginning April 6 in Tampa and wrapping June 11 in St. Louis.

### Key Points
- 15 dates across U.S. arenas/amphitheaters, April 6 – June 11, 2027
- GA Pit/Floor tickets only available via Ticketmaster Request, except St. Augustine Amphitheatre (Fair AXS for the full venue)
- Pre-sale begins Nov. 18, general on-sale Nov. 20

## Transcript

The virtuosic Billy Strings has announced the dates for his Spring 2027 run.

The 15-date tour of U.S. arenas and amphitheaters begins April 6 at Tampa’s Benchmark International Arena and includes a five-show run in St. Augustine and doubles in North Charleston, South Carolina; Greensboro, North Carolina; and Birmingham, Alabama, before wrapping with a June 10-11 double in St. Louis.

General Admission Pit/Floor tickets will only be available through Ticketmaster Request, with the exception of St. Augustine Amphitheatre, where tickets for the entire venue will be offered through Fair AXS. Sign-ups are open now and must be completed by 11:59 p.m. Eastern Nov. 11. Tickets for all other sections will be available for pre-sale beginning at 10 a.m. local Nov. 18 with general on-sale at 10 a.m. local Nov. 20.

Strings has been one of the most consistent touring acts of the last decade. In the 363 headline reports in the Pollstar Boxoffice dating back to 2016, he has grossed nearly $95 million on 1.725 million tickets.

Strings is repped in North America by Crossover Touring’s Patrick May and managed by Bill Orner at Hank & Ed Management.
