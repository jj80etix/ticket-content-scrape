# French Montana, D-Block Europe Cancel Seven European Tour Dates

## Summary

French Montana and D-Block Europe canceled seven stops on their Worldwide Wave Tour across the Netherlands, Czech Republic, Poland, Denmark, Germany, Austria and France, running Oct. 30–Nov. 8, without a unifying public explanation. Refund guidance differs by venue and seller — Ticketmaster is emailing Amsterdam buyers, Copenhagen's VEGA is directing fans to their original ticket provider, and Berlin's venue is telling buyers to return tickets to point of purchase. The London O2 and a Madrid festival date remain on sale as of publication.

### Key Points
- Seven dates canceled: Amsterdam (Oct. 30), Prague (Nov. 1), Warsaw (Nov. 2), Copenhagen (Nov. 4), Berlin (Nov. 5), Vienna (Nov. 6), Paris (Nov. 8).
- No substitute dates have been announced for any of the seven shows as of Oct. 28.
- Refund instructions vary by market/seller (Ticketmaster, VEGA, Eventlook, venue box office).
- London's O2 Arena (Oct. 28) and a Madrid Halloween festival date (Oct. 31) remain unaffected.
- Tour supports the Sept. 11 collaborative album "Worldwide Wave."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/wordwide-wave-1200.webp)

French Montana and D-Block Europe have canceled seven concerts across continental Europe on their Worldwide Wave Tour, with venue and ticketing listings confirming that performances in the Netherlands, Czech Republic, Poland, Denmark, Germany, Austria and France are off the calendar.

The affected dates run from Oct. 30 through Nov. 8. While organizers in Amsterdam cited “unforeseen circumstances,” no broader public explanation has been provided for the series of cancellations, and none of the notices reviewed by TicketNews announces a replacement date.

[AllHipHop reported the Amsterdam and Vienna cancellations](https://allhiphop.com/news/french-montanas-amsterdam-vienna-concerts-suddenly-canceled/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) Tuesday, then [reported Copenhagen as another canceled stop](https://allhiphop.com/news/french-montana-d-block-europes-copenhagen-concert-is-canceled-too/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). A review of venue, promoter and ticketing records by TicketNews identified four additional affected concerts in Prague, Warsaw, Berlin and Paris.

The original announcements covered a mix of concert halls and club venues, making the disruption significant for fans who planned travel around individual stops. Warsaw ticketing service eBilet lists the Nov. 2 performance as canceled, while Berlin’s Uber Eats Music Hall and Live Nation’s French operation carry corresponding notices for Nov. 5 and Nov. 8, respectively.

Prague’s Nov. 1 performance at SaSaZu Club is also marked canceled on the current event listings of ticketing platform Eventlook. The Czech date was not included among the three cancellations initially identified in AllHipHop’s reports.

AFAS Live says Ticketmaster will email Amsterdam buyers with further information. Copenhagen’s VEGA says its ticket purchasers will hear from their ticket provider, while the Berlin venue advises customers to return tickets to the original place of purchase. Refund arrangements may therefore differ by market and seller.

The cancellations have not been accompanied by a publicly documented reason tying all seven shows together. Although the dates fall within a short period, the available notices do not establish whether the same circumstances affected each concert.

## London and Madrid Still Listed

The changes have not extended to every performance on the artists’ calendars. [London’s O2 arena](https://www.theo2.co.uk/events/detail/d-block-europe-2026?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) continued to advertise its Wednesday, Oct. 28, show with D-Block Europe headlining and French Montana appearing as a special guest, with tickets offered through AXS as of Wednesday.

[D-Block Europe’s official schedule](https://www.dblockeurope.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) also lists an Oct. 31 appearance at Fabrik in Madrid. That booking is part of a Halloween festival lineup featuring D-Block Europe, rather than a separately advertised joint headline concert with French Montana. The site’s current live calendar lists only London and Madrid, although older tour pages and concert aggregators may still show some of the canceled stops.

The concerts were organized around [Worldwide Wave, the collaborative album](https://music.apple.com/us/album/worldwide-wave/6805887396?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) released Sept. 11 by French Montana and the South London duo, whose members are Young Adz and Dirtbike LB. The 19-track release includes the single “RICO,” and the artists have also worked together on “Fully Loaded.”

For people holding tickets to the canceled dates, the most reliable next step is to check notices from the original ticket seller and event venue. As of Oct. 28, the cancellation notices reviewed by TicketNews had not announced substitute dates for the seven affected concerts.
