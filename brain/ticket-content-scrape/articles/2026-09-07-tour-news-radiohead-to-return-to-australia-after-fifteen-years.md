# Tour news: Radiohead to return to Australia after fifteen years

## Summary

IQ's weekly tour-news roundup leads with Radiohead's first Australian tour in 15 years (May 2027, Perth/Melbourne/Sydney, capacities of 15,000–18,000 suggesting 200,000+ fans across the run), alongside brief mentions of new Simple Minds, Bonobo, James Blunt and Emeli Sandé tour announcements. Full detail on the latter four is behind IQ's paywall.

### Key Points
- Radiohead: first Australian tour in 15 years, May 2027, RAC Arena (Perth), Rod Laver Arena (Melbourne), Afterpay Arena (Sydney).
- Estimated draw of 200,000+ fans across the Australian leg based on venue capacities.
- Also flags new tour announcements from Simple Minds, Bonobo, James Blunt and Emeli Sandé (details paywalled).

Leading the latest tour announcements, Radiohead have revealed their first Australian tour in 15 years, set for May 2027.

The run will consist of four shows at Perth’s RAC Arena between 3-7 May, six shows at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena between 13-20 May, followed by six Sydney performances at the Afterpay Arena between 25 May and 1 June.
