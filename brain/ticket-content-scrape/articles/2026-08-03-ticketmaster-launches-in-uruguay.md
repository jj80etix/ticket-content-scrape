# Ticketmaster launches in Uruguay

## Summary

Ticketmaster has launched in Uruguay, entering the market by acquiring Red UTS, a major local ticketing platform. The new Uruguayan platform gives fans discovery and purchase access with digital ticket delivery via the Quentro wallet app used across Latin America, plus fraud-prevention tooling. The move follows Ticketmaster's announced entry into the Philippines earlier in 2026 via a partnership with SM Prime, continuing a pattern of international expansion through local acquisitions and partnerships.

### Key Points
- Ticketmaster acquired Red UTS to launch its Uruguayan platform, effective Aug. 3, 2026.
- Ticket delivery runs through Quentro, a digital wallet app used across Latin America.
- Javier Parra (market leader, Chile and Uruguay) and Adam Newsam (EVP, Latin America) provided launch statements emphasizing fraud prevention and a "seamless" fan journey.
- Follows Ticketmaster's Philippines market entry (announced May 2026) via a partnership with SM Prime.

### Highlights
- Second Ticketmaster market entry via local acquisition/partnership disclosed in 2026 (Uruguay following Philippines/SM Prime).
- Acquisition target (Red UTS) was described as a "major ticketing platform" in Uruguay, i.e. displacing/absorbing an incumbent rather than greenfield entry.
- Expansion framed explicitly around fraud prevention and secure digital delivery (Quentro wallet).

### Industry Problem
Consolidation by the dominant primary-ticketing player into new geographies via acquisition of local incumbents reduces the number of independent platforms in those markets and extends Ticketmaster's network/data advantages internationally — the same market-structure dynamic under antitrust scrutiny domestically.

### Proposed Solution
No independent-platform response is described in the item; the relevant move for Etix is to monitor Ticketmaster's international roll-up pattern (which markets, which local acquisitions) as a leading indicator of where consolidation pressure could next reach independent/mid-market ticketing operators, and revisit posture if the pattern extends into markets Etix serves or targets.

**Ticketmaster has launched in Uruguay, it has been announced today (August 3).**

As part of its launch, Ticketmaster has also agreed to acquire Red UTS, a major ticketing platform in the South American country.

Ticketmaster’s Uruguayan platform is now live, meaning fans can discover and purchase tickets for live events. Fans will also benefit from secure digital ticket delivery through Quentro (a digital wallet app used across Latin America), tools designed to help prevent fraud and protect ticket purchases and a simple ticket-buying experience.

“We’re thrilled to welcome Uruguay to the Ticketmaster network. Our goal is to give every fan a simple, secure and transparent experience – from discovering an event to walking through the venue gates,” said Javier Parra, market leader, Chile and Uruguay at Ticketmaster.

“We look forward to supporting the continued growth of live entertainment in Uruguay and connecting more fans with the artists and events they love.”

Adam Newsam, executive vice president, Latin America at Ticketmaster, added: “At Ticketmaster, we’re constantly working to make the journey from discovering an event to walking through the venue gates as seamless as possible.

“Our expansion into Uruguay reflects that commitment, combining technology, innovation and local expertise to deliver a better experience for fans while supporting the continued growth of live entertainment across Latin America.”
