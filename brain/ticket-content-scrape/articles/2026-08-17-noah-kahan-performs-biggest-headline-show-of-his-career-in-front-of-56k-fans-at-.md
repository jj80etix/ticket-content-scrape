# Noah Kahan Performs Biggest Headline Show Of His Career In Front Of 56K Fans At Rose Bowl Stadium

## Summary

Noah Kahan played the largest headline show of his career on Aug 15 at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, selling out the 56,000-capacity stadium as part of his "Great Divide World Tour." Chappell Roan made a surprise guest appearance for a duet. The tour has now sold more than 873,000 tickets and grossed nearly $144 million as of Aug. 9, following an earlier four-night sellout run at Fenway Park that grossed over $25.5 million.

### Key Points
- 56,000+ fans at Rose Bowl Stadium, Kahan's biggest headline show to date, part of a sold-out "Great Divide World Tour."
- Chappell Roan made a surprise appearance, dueting on her song "California."
- Tour has sold 873,000+ tickets and grossed ~$144 million as of Aug. 9, 2026.
- Earlier four-night Fenway Park run (July) grossed $25,574,963 off 153,783 tickets — first artist to sell out four Fenway shows.
- Seven more U.S. dates remain before the tour heads to Australia, New Zealand, the UK, and Europe.

## Transcript

![20260815 Noah Kahan LOS ANGELES photo patrick mccormack 19](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/20260815_Noah_Kahan_LOS-ANGELES_photo-patrick-mccormack__19.jpg)

Grammy-nominated star Noah Kahan performed in front of 56,000-plus fans at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California, making it the largest headline concert of his career. (Photo by Patrick McCormack)

Noah Kahan brought his “Great Divide World Tour” to Los Angeles Saturday and performed in front of his largest crowd yet. The multiplatinum superstar played a sold-out show for more than 56,000 fans at the iconic Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena.

Kahan joins artists such as Oasis, Coldplay, RÜFÜS DU SOL, The Rolling Stones and Karol G in playing a sold-out show at the stadium, which is considered hallowed ground by college football fans.

One of the biggest nights of Kahan’s touring career was made even more special with a guest of honor. Pop star Chappell Roan surprised the large L.A. crowd and joined Kahan for a duet of her song “California.”

Gigi Perez and Annabelle Dinda opened for Kahan, whose 25-song set included renditions of streaming hits including “Stick Season,” “Northern Attitude,” “Orbiter” and “The Great Divide.”

“The Great Divide World Tour” is Kahan’s most successful trek, with unprecedented demand across North America, Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom and Europe. He sold more than a million tickets following the initial onsale for North American dates.

![20260815 Noah Kahan LOS ANGELES photo patrick mccormack 8](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/20260815_Noah_Kahan_LOS-ANGELES_photo-patrick-mccormack__8.jpg)

Chappell Roan surprised the L.A. crowd and joined Noah Kahan for a special rendition of her song “California.” (Photo by Patrick McCormack)

Another major milestone Kahan achieved this summer was playing four sold-out nights at Boston’s Fenway Park in July—the first artist to sell out four performances at the baseball stadium—grossing a whopping $25,574,963 off 153,783 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. As of Aug. 9, Kahan’s tour has moved more than 873,000 tickets and nearly $144 million, according to reports submitted to *Pollstar*.

Kahan has seven more U.S. dates this summer in venues such as San Diego’s Petco Park, Oracle Park in San Francisco, BC Place in Vancouver and a two-night stand at T-Mobile Park in Seattle before he embarks for Australia, New Zealand, the U.K. and Europe in the fall.

The folk-pop artist, hailing from Strafford, Vermont, is booked by THE·TEAM and managed by Drew Simmons of Foundations Artist Management.
