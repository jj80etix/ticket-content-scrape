# Jim Glancy, John Moore Depart Bowery Presents in Major Leadership Transition

## Summary

AEG Presents-owned concert promoter The Bowery Presents is restructuring its leadership as Co-Presidents Jim Glancy and John Moore step aside, handing control to four newly promoted senior vice presidents. The reshuffle also splits Bowery's New York and New England operations into more clearly separated regional businesses.

### Key Points
- Josh Bhatti, Charley Magrew, Andrew Martin, and Josh Moore promoted to SVP, assuming collective leadership.
- Bhatti takes standalone control of the Boston-based New England business.
- Magrew to oversee marketing, ticketing, sponsorship, and venue initiatives from New York.
- Martin (Nashville) to oversee business and legal affairs; Josh Moore to lead talent operations.

The Bowery Presents is turning leadership of the AEG Presents-owned concert promotion company over to four longtime executives as Co-Presidents Jim Glancy and John Moore step aside from their roles.

Josh Bhatti, Charley Magrew, Andrew Martin and Josh Moore have each been promoted to senior vice president and will collectively assume leadership of The Bowery Presents, the company announced Monday. The restructuring also creates a clearer separation between Bowery’s New York and New England operations, with Bhatti taking control of the Boston-based regional business as a standalone promoter.

Magrew will oversee marketing, ticketing, sponsorship and venue initiatives from New York, while Josh Moore will lead talent operations from the same office. Martin, based in Nashville, will oversee business and legal affairs. Magrew, Martin and Moore will share responsibility for the strategic direction and day-to-day leadership of the New York operation.

All four executives will report to Brent Fedrizzi, president of North American regional offices for AEG Presents.

The change closes a lengthy chapter for Glancy and John Moore, who led The Bowery Presents through its rise from an independent New York promoter into a major East Coast operation and its eventual integration with AEG.

John Moore co-founded The Bowery Presents in 2004, with Glancy joining as a partner in 2006. [AEG formally announced in January 2017](https://aegworldwide.com/press-center/press-releases/aeg-live-partners-bowery-presents) that it had acquired a stake in the company, saying at the time that Glancy and Moore would continue overseeing Bowery’s operations as the partnership expanded AEG’s reach across New York, Boston, Philadelphia and other East Coast markets.

“Founding The Bowery Presents and partnering together to grow the company has been an incredibly rewarding chapter for us,” Glancy and Moore said in a joint statement. “We’re proud of what we’ve built alongside an extraordinary team and grateful to everyone who has been part of that journey.”

The pair said Bhatti, Magrew, Martin and Josh Moore had each made significant contributions during their tenures and that they were “leaving the company in capable hands.”

The four incoming leaders all have lengthy histories within either Bowery or AEG.

Bhatti joined The Bowery Presents in 2010 as its first employee based outside New York City after earlier work in artist management and at Clear Channel Entertainment (the company which later became Live Nation). His promotion formalizes his control over a New England operation he has helped build for more than a decade. TicketNews reported in 2019 that Bhatti, then vice president of Boston for Bowery, [was overseeing the promoter’s expansion as it prepared a new 3,500-capacity venue at Boston Landing](https://www.ticketnews.com/2019/03/the-bowery-to-build-venue-at-boston-landing/).

Magrew joined Bowery in 2015 after holding roles with Hennepin Theatre Trust, Broadway Across America and Kings Theatre. Martin has been with AEG Presents since 2007 and joined Bowery in 2018, most recently serving as vice president and assistant general counsel.

Josh Moore joined AEG Presents in 2005 and moved to The Bowery Presents in 2007, where he became one of its original talent buyers at Music Hall of Williamsburg. His elevation comes during a period of transition for that venue as well: Bowery said last year that the Williamsburg property’s owner would not renew its lease after 2026, bringing the promoter’s nearly two-decade run at the building to an end unless the venue relocates.

Fedrizzi said AEG wanted Bowery’s next leadership team to come from within the organization.

“The Bowery Presents is a renowned, internationally recognized brand that has been instrumental in so many artists’ careers, and it was important for us to find the right group of people that can steward the company into this important next phase,” Fedrizzi said.

The restructuring leaves Bowery without a direct replacement for the co-president model Glancy and Moore held. Instead, New York responsibilities will be distributed among Magrew, Martin and Josh Moore, while Bhatti gains separate control of the New England business.

The Bowery Presents currently promotes shows and operates or works with venues across New York, Boston, Philadelphia and other markets, with AEG describing the company as a wholly owned subsidiary that presents thousands of shows annually.
