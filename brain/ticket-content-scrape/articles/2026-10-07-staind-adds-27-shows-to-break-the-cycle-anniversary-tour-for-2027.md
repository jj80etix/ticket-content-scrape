# Staind Adds 27 Shows to ‘Break the Cycle’ Anniversary Tour for 2027

## Summary

Staind is extending its "Break the Cycle" 25th anniversary tour into 2027 with 27 new North American dates alongside Seether, Hoobastank and Hinder, running March 4 (Manchester, NH) through April 16 (Cleveland). The new leg follows the band's fall 2026 anniversary shows, with tickets on general sale October 9 after a staggered run of Spotify, Citi and artist presales.

### Key Points
- 27 new North American dates, March 4-April 16, 2027, spanning the U.S. and Canada (Vancouver, Calgary, Edmonton stops included).
- Co-headlining/support bill: Seether, Hoobastank, Hinder (Hinder absent only from the Cleveland finale).
- General onsale Oct. 9; presales include Spotify Reserved (Premium subscribers, Oct. 7) and Citi (Oct. 7-8).
- Follows the band's existing fall 2026 anniversary leg through Texas in October.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Staind-.webp)

Staind will extend its “Break the Cycle” 25th anniversary tour into 2027, announcing 27 additional North American shows with Seether, Hoobastank and Hinder.

The new leg begins March 4 at SNHU Arena in Manchester, New Hampshire, according to the published itinerary, and concludes April 16 at Rocket Arena in Cleveland. Hinder will appear at every newly announced show except the Cleveland finale.

The spring itinerary takes Staind through Baltimore, Pittsburgh and Reading, Pennsylvania, before heading south for performances in Huntington, West Virginia, Tampa and West Palm Beach, Florida. Other stops include Birmingham, Alabama, Oklahoma City, Dallas and San Antonio.

The tour then moves west for shows at Denver’s Ball Arena, Boise’s ExtraMile Arena and Seattle’s Climate Pledge Arena. Canadian performances are scheduled for March 29 at Rogers Arena in Vancouver, March 31 at Scotiabank Saddledome in Calgary and April 1 at Rogers Place in Edmonton.

The final stretch returns to the United States, with stops in Billings, Montana, Sioux Falls, South Dakota, Fargo, North Dakota, Omaha, Nebraska, and Kansas City, Missouri. Performances in Moline, Illinois, Grand Rapids, Michigan, and Evansville, Indiana, lead into the closing Cleveland show.

The new dates will follow the band’s fall 2026 anniversary shows. Its remaining October schedule includes performances in Utah, California, Arizona, New Mexico and Oklahoma before heading to Texas for shows in Houston and Austin.

Tickets go on sale to the general public October 9 at 10 a.m. local time. A Spotify Reserved presale begins October 7 at 11 a.m. Eastern, followed by artist and Citi presales at 1 p.m. Eastern. Additional presales will take place throughout the week.

The Citi presale continues through October 8 at 10 p.m. local time. Spotify’s offering is available to eligible U.S. Premium subscribers, with qualifying fans notified through email and the app. Those fans will have an opportunity to purchase up to two tickets during the Reserved presale.
