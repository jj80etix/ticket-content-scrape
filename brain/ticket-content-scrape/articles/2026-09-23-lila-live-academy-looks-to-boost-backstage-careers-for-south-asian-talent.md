# Lila Live Academy looks to boost backstage careers for South Asian talent

## Summary

UK non-profit Lila has launched Live Academy, an eight-week evening production training course followed by 48 paid placements, aimed at building routes into live-music production careers for South Asian talent. Twelve participants aged 18–30 will be selected; the application window closes September 28.

### Key Points
- Funded by Arts Council England's Supporting Grassroots Music fund.
- Course taught by working industry professionals over eight weeks this autumn.
- 48 paid placements follow the training phase for successful applicants.
- Applications close September 28, 2026.
