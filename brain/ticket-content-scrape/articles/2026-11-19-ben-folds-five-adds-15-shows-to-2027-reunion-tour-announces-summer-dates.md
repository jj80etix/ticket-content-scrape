# Ben Folds Five Adds 15 Shows to 2027 Reunion Tour, Announces Summer Dates

## Summary

Ben Folds Five expanded its 2027 reunion tour with 15 additional shows, bringing the total to 24 dates — its first full tour since 2013. The new summer leg runs July 10-31 across cities including Louisville, Kansas City, Denver, Vail, Salt Lake City and wraps with two shows at The Wiltern in Los Angeles, on top of the April dates announced in October.

### Key Points
- 15 new dates added, bringing the 2027 reunion tour to 24 shows total — first full tour since 2013.
- New summer leg: July 10 (Huber Heights, OH) through July 31 (The Wiltern, Los Angeles, two shows).
- Expands geographic reach into Louisville, Kansas City, Omaha, Denver, Vail, Salt Lake City and the Pacific Northwest/California.
- Builds on the original April tour announced in October 2026 (nine dates across TN, FL, TX, AL).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ben-Folds-Five-On-Tour.webp)

Ben Folds Five has expanded its 2027 reunion tour with 15 additional concerts, extending the band’s first full tour since 2013 into the summer and bringing the total itinerary to 24 shows.

The newly announced dates include an April 25 performance at The Eastern in Atlanta, followed by a 14-show summer leg beginning July 10 in Huber Heights, Ohio. The tour will now continue through July 31, wrapping up with two performances at The Wiltern in Los Angeles.

The expansion follows the band’s [initial reunion tour announcement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/ben-folds-five-sets-2027-return-with-april-tour-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in October, which included nine April concerts across Tennessee, Florida, Texas and Alabama.

The summer run significantly expands the tour’s geographic reach, adding performances in Louisville, Kansas City, Omaha, Denver, Vail and Salt Lake City before heading to the Pacific Northwest and California.

Following a July 21 concert in Salt Lake City, for which the venue has yet to be announced, the band will perform at Remlinger Farms in Carnation, Washington, on July 23. Additional Pacific Northwest stops include McMenamins Edgefield near Portland, Oregon, and the Cuthbert Amphitheater in Eugene.

The final stretch includes shows at Oakland’s Fox Theater on July 27 and Sacramento’s Channel 24 on July 28 before the two-night Los Angeles finale.

Andrew McMahon will join Ben Folds Five as a special guest on select dates, including previously announced April performances and portions of the newly added summer run. Jackie Evans will provide support for the April 12 and 13 concerts in Tennessee and the July 10 show in Ohio.

The reunion brings pianist and vocalist Ben Folds back together with bassist Robert Sledge and drummer Darren Jessee. Formed in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, in 1993, the trio built a following with its piano-driven alternative rock sound, releasing its self-titled debut album in 1995 and the commercially successful *Whatever and Ever Amen* in 1997.

The reunion also follows the September release of an expanded 30th anniversary edition of the band’s debut album.
