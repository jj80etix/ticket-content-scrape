# Mariah Carey Announces Run Of European Festival Appearances

## Summary

Mariah Carey confirmed "Firefly," a run of summer 2027 European festival appearances starting June 24 in Sønderborg, Denmark, and closing at Moon & Stars Festival in Locarno, Switzerland. Select newly announced dates go on sale Nov. 13.

### Key Points
- Stops include four UK dates plus Germany, Italy and Belgium.
- Tickets for newly announced dates on sale Nov. 13 at 9 a.m. local time.
- Tour booked by CAA; managed by Maroon Entertainment.

It’s a Mariah Carey summer. The “Songbird Supreme” has just confirmed what she’s dubbed “Firefly,” a series of summer 2027 festival appearances across Europe.

The fun begins June 24 at Mølleparken in Sønderborg, Denmark. Mariah has lined up four stops in the UK, along with appearances in Germany, Italy and Belgium. The final gig of the “Firefly’ run is at Moon & Stars Festival in Locarno, Switzerland.

Select festival dates on the “Firefly” tour are on sale now. Tickets for the newly announced dates go on sale Nov. 13 at 9 a.m. local time.

Mariah’s most recent album is 2025’s *Here for It All.* Her countless awards include 5 Grammy Awards, a Grammy Global Impact Award, 10 American Music Awards, and an induction into the Songwriters Hall of Fame. In 2023, *Rolling Stone* ranked Mariah No. 5 on its list of [“The 200 Greatest Singers of All Time.”](https://www.rollingstone.com/music/music-lists/best-singers-all-time-1234642307/billie-holiday-2-1234643209/) A write-up praised Carey for her range, declaring “Across five staggering octaves, the Elusive Chanteuse can pivot easily between a biting, taunting growl to an unreal whistle tone so sharply delivered it could cut steel.”

Mariah is managed by Maroon Entertainment’s Michael Richardson and her tour dates are booked by CAA.  
  
Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Mariah include her run of residency dates at Dolby Live at Park MGM Las Vegas, including a Dec. 13, 2025 show that sold 5,276 tickets and grossed $1,156,715.
