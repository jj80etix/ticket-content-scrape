# Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who Lead 2027 New Orleans Jazz Fest Lineup

## Summary

TicketNews's coverage of the same 2027 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival lineup announcement highlights the breadth of genres — rock, pop, country, hip-hop, R&B, jazz, blues, gospel and Louisiana traditional music — spanning two weekends, April 22 through May 2, 2027, at the Fair Grounds Race Course, with over 5,000 performers across 14 stages.

### Key Points
- Headliners: Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile, Gwen Stefani, James Taylor, Jack Johnson, Jack White; also Matchbox Twenty, LL Cool J, Chaka Khan, The Avett Brothers, Wyclef Jean, Buddy Guy and more.
- Lineup unveiled Thursday, Nov. 19, 2026, for the April 22-May 2, 2027 event at Fair Grounds Race Course.
- Over 5,000 musicians performing across 14 stages over the eight-day, two-weekend run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/New-Orleans-Jazz-Heritage-Festival.webp)

Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile, Gwen Stefani, James Taylor, Jack Johnson and Jack White are among the major artists announced for the 2027 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival, which returns to the Fair Grounds Race Course for two weekends from April 22 through May 2.

The festival unveiled its lineup Thursday, November 19, with more than 5,000 musicians expected to perform across 14 stages during the eight-day event.

Additional major performers include Matchbox Twenty, Widespread Panic, The Red Clay Strays, LL Cool J, Chaka Khan, Khruangbin, The B-52s, The War On Drugs, The Avett Brothers, Train, Wyclef Jean, Buddy Guy, Shaggy, Warren Haynes Band, Larkin Poe, Robert Glasper, Trampled by Turtles, Molly Tuttle, Keb’ Mo’, Sheila E. and Rickie Lee Jones.

The expansive lineup spans rock, pop, country, hip-hop, R&B, jazz, blues, gospel and traditional Louisiana music, continuing Jazz Fest’s longstanding emphasis on bringing major touring artists together with the musical traditions of New Orleans.

## Jazz Fest Celebrates the Bahamas in 2027

The 2027 festival will dedicate its Cultural Exchange Pavilion to the Bahamas, highlighting the longstanding musical and cultural connections between New Orleans and the Caribbean nation.

Grammy Award-winning group Baha Men, best known for “Who Let the Dogs Out,” will headline the Bahamian celebration on Sunday, May 2.

Additional Bahamian performers will include K.B., D-Mac, Nishie L.S., Jonny Cake and Lady E, with programming showcasing Junkanoo music and rake-n-scrape, a traditional Bahamian musical style that incorporates percussion made by scraping a carpenter’s handsaw.

The pavilion will also feature gospel artist Cara T. Newton, Ambasah & Da Conchy Band and DJ Sherwood Rhaming, alongside cultural demonstrations and Bahamian food.

A dedicated demonstration area will be transformed into a Junkanoo shack, offering visitors a look at the elaborate costumes traditionally created for Bahamian celebrations. The cultural exchange continues a relationship that has included appearances by Junkanoo groups at Jazz Fest throughout the 1980s and 1990s, as well as during the festival’s 50th anniversary celebration in 2019.

Weekend general admission and VIP passes for the 2027 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival go on sale Friday, November 20, beginning at 10 a.m. through [nojazzfest.com](https://www.nojazzfest.com/tickets/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
