# JAŸ-Z To Headline Apple Music Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show At SoFi Stadium

## Summary

The NFL, Roc Nation and Apple Music announced JAY-Z will headline the Super Bowl LXI Halftime Show at SoFi Stadium on Feb. 14, 2027, under Apple Music's naming partnership with the event.

### Key Points
- Performance set for Feb. 14, 2027 at SoFi Stadium, Inglewood.
- Announced under the NFL/Roc Nation deal dating to 2019.
- Comes days after Jay-Z completed two of his only nine live dates of 2026, at the same venue.
