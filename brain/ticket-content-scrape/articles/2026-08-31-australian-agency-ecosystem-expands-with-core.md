# Australian agency ecosystem expands with CORE

## Summary

Australia's booking-agency landscape continues to evolve as Lonely Lands Agency (LLA) rebrands into CORE Talent Agency, a new venture from Unified Music Group CEO Jaddan Comerford and Lemon Tree Music co-founder Regan Lethbridge focused on rising Australian and New Zealand talent — arriving alongside the separate launch of Lowtide, founded by a former LLA general manager.

### Key Points
- CORE emerges from LLA, founded in 2019 by Comerford, Lethbridge, and artist Tash Sultana; operates within the Unified Music Group ecosystem.
- Roster includes Teen Jesus and the Jean Teasers, The Terrys, Budjerah, Emma Donovan, and South Summit.
- Comes as former LLA general manager Harry Moore separately launches Lowtide, taking part of his roster with him.
- Around 80% of Australian booking agencies are locally owned and independently run, often combining booking, management, and promotion.

Evolving from the Lonely Lands Agency, CORE's launch comes amid the establishment of another new agency, Lowtide

![Desiree Venuto & Adam Montgomery](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Desiree-Venuto-Adam-Montgomery.png)

Desiree Venuto & Adam Montgomery

The Australian booking agency landscape is undergoing a period of change, with Lonely Lands Agency (LLA) evolving into CORE, a new agency dedicated to championing rising Australian and New Zealand talent.

CORE Talent Agency is founded by Unified Music Group CEO Jaddan Comerford and Regan Lethbridge, co-founder of Lemon Tree Music. It emerges from the LLA, which the pair established alongside artist Tash Sultana in 2019.

Operating within the Unified ecosystem, CORE will be led by co-general managers Desiree Venuto and Adam Montgomery. The agency launches with a domestic-focused roster that spans names like Teen Jesus and the Jean Teasers, The Terrys, Budjerah, Emma Donovan, and South Summit.

“Having been a part of this journey since its inception, it is incredibly rewarding to step into this new role to lead the next iteration of the agency,” Venuto says. “Artist creativity has always been our foundation, and we are stepping forward with a unified team backed by decades of shared industry expertise.”

Montgomery added: “CORE is built around putting the artist at the heart of every decision, and we’re excited to bring this fresh energy into each conversation. With the support of Jaddan and Regan, we’re eager to champion our incredible roster and continue to expand our community.”

In addition to its leading managers, CORE’s launch team includes LaBelle Jackson, Victoria Kozbanis, and Jo Syme.

Tash Sultana is represented by One Fiinix Live in Australia/New Zealand, Europe/UK, and Asia and CAA in North & South America.

> “For domestic artists, it’s pivotal to hit regional markets”

The evolution of LLA comes amid the establishment of [Lowtide](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/lowtide-agency-launches-with-mission-to-unlock-australian-regional-touring/), the new agency from former LLA general manager Harry Moore, who brought his roster with him to found the new venture. Lowtide’s first acts established artists Tones and I and Ocean Alley.

Speaking to *IQ* at the time of Lowtide’s launch, Moore said one of the agency’s targets is unlocking the touring potential of Australia’s regional towns to supplement the established city circuit.

“For domestic artists, it’s pivotal to hit regional markets,” he says. “Fans will travel to your metropolitan show — spend money, spend a night in a hotel, but if they’ve had that core experience with their friends in their community, it pays massively.”

The new developments come as Australia’s agency sector continues to operate primarily independently, despite increasing globalisation and broader challenges.

According to *IQ* ‘s [market report](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/07/great-southern-land-australia-market-report-part-2/), around 80% of agencies are locally owned and independently run, with many serving as one-stop shops that combine booking, management, and promotion efforts suitable for the country’s geographically dispersed market.

Standouts of this model include Destroy All Lines, Untitled Group, Select Music, Mushroom Group’s Premier Artists, and Billions Australia.
