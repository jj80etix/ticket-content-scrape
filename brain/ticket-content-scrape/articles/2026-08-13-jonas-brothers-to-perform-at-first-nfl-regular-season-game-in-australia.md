# Jonas Brothers to Perform at First NFL Regular-Season Game in Australia

## Summary

TicketNews' coverage confirming the Jonas Brothers will perform the halftime show at the NFL's first-ever regular-season game in Australia, as the San Francisco 49ers face the Los Angeles Rams at Melbourne Cricket Ground on September 11.

### Key Points
- Part of the NFL's broader push into international markets with its first Australian regular-season fixture.
- Surrounding NFL Kickoff Festival (Sept. 10-12, Melbourne Park) includes a watch party for the Seahawks-Patriots game and local artist performances.

The Jonas Brothers will perform at halftime of the first NFL regular-season game in Australia when the San Francisco 49ers face the Los Angeles Rams at the Melbourne Cricket Ground on September 11.

The performance will mark the band’s appearance at one of the NFL’s international games as the league stages its first regular-season fixture in Australia.

In a statement, the Jonas Brothers shared, “We couldn’t be more excited to perform at halftime during the NFL’s first-ever regular season game in Australia.”

“Playing at the iconic Melbourne Cricket Ground in front of such passionate Australian fans is an incredible honour. We can’t wait to be part of this historic moment and celebrate with everyone in the stadium and watching around the world,” the band continued.

The NFL game will be part of a wider series of events taking place in Melbourne around the league’s Australian debut. The NFL Kickoff Festival will run from September 10-12 at Melbourne Park. The festival will include a watch party for the Seattle Seahawks’ game against the New England Patriots.

The festival will also feature performances from Australian artists Peking Duk, Grinspoon and Sneaky Sound System.

Tim Tubito, senior director of global game presentation and entertainment at the NFL, shared, “Bringing the first-ever NFL regular season game to Australia is a landmark moment for the league, and we are committed to creating an entertainment experience that reflects the scale and significance of the occasion.”

“The Jonas Brothers are global entertainers whose music connects across generations, making them the ideal artists to help us celebrate this historic game in Melbourne,” Tubito said. “We look forward to delivering a memorable halftime performance for our fans in Australia and those watching around the world.”
