# PepsiCo and Live Nation Announce New Multi-Year Food and Beverage Partnership

## Summary

PepsiCo and Live Nation announced a new multi-year partnership expanding PepsiCo beverage and snack offerings — including Pepsi, Mountain Dew, Lay's, Doritos and Cheetos — across more than 110 Live Nation concert venues in the U.S., plus new concert-specific menu items co-developed by both companies' culinary teams.

### Key Points
- Deal covers 110+ Live Nation venues, including Jones Beach Theater (NY), Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre (IL), Hollywood Palladium (CA), and Tabernacle (GA).
- New menu items include Street Corn Doritos Loaded Nachos, Tostitos Queso Blanco dip, and Flamin' Hot-marinated tofu.
- Offerings also include gluten-free and other dietary-accommodating options (e.g., Sabra Hummus).
- Regional snack variants: Cheetos Flamin' Hot Limon, Doritos Dinamita, Funyuns Flamin' Hot, and others.

## Transcript

PepsiCo is expanding its presence at live music venues across the United States through a new multi-year partnership with Live Nation.

The agreement will bring PepsiCo beverages and snacks to more than 110 Live Nation concert venues nationwide. The partnership will also introduce food options developed specifically for concertgoers at select locations.

The expanded offerings will include Pepsi, Pepsi Zero Sugar, Mountain Dew, Starry, Mug Root Beer, Tropicana Lemonade and poppi Strawberry Lemon. PepsiCo snack options will include Lay’s, Doritos, Cheetos and Miss Vickie’s, as well as additional varieties selected for individual markets.

Among the regional offerings will be Cheetos Flamin’ Hot Limon, Doritos Dinamita, Funyuns Flamin’ Hot, Fritos Chili Cheese and Ruffles Cheddar snacks.

The partnership will be available at venues including Northwell at Jones Beach Theater in New York, Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre in Illinois, Hollywood Palladium in California and Tabernacle presented by iTHINK Financial in Georgia.

In addition to expanding the existing food and beverage selection, PepsiCo and Live Nation are introducing new menu items at certain venues. The companies’ culinary teams collaborated on offerings designed around flavors associated with PepsiCo brands.

New food options include Street Corn Doritos Loaded Nachos, Tostitos Queso Blanco cheese dip and tofu marinated in Flamin’ Hot sauce.

The collaboration also gives venues access to additional food and beverage choices intended to accommodate different preferences. Live Nation said offerings will include items such as Doritos and Walking Tacos, gluten-free Sabra Hummus and an expanded selection of soft drinks.

“We are proud to elevate the concertgoing experience through the power of iconic brands, refreshing beverages and memorable culinary experiences,” Scott Finlow, chief marketing officer of PepsiCo Away from Home, said in a statement.

Finlow said the partnership will allow PepsiCo to connect with concert fans through familiar products while also introducing food and beverage options tailored to individual venues.

Andy Yates, head of food and beverage at Live Nation, said the collaboration is intended to make attending concerts a more memorable experience for fans.

“PepsiCo beverages and snacks are the perfect complement to our food and beverage program,” Yates said.

The partnership comes as Live Nation continues to expand food and beverage programs at its entertainment venues. PepsiCo said the collaboration will give venue operators additional ways to respond to changing consumer preferences while creating new opportunities to incorporate its brands into the concert experience.

Hugh Roth, chief customer officer of PepsiCo Away from Home, shared “Together, Live Nation’s entertainment destinations and PepsiCo’s food and beverage portfolio give operators more ways to meet evolving consumer preferences, drive sales and enhance the guest experience.”
