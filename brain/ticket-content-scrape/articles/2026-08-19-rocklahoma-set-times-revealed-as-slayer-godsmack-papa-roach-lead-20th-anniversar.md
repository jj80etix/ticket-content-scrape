# Rocklahoma Set Times Revealed as Slayer, Godsmack, Papa Roach Lead 20th Anniversary

## Summary

Rocklahoma's 20th-anniversary edition (Sept 4-6, Pryor, OK) locked in set times, with Godsmack, Papa Roach, and Slayer headlining across the three days; Slayer's Sunday set doubles as a 40th-anniversary celebration of Reign in Blood. VIP passes are sold out, though GA, camping, RV, and limited glamping packages remain.

### Key Points
- Friday: Godsmack headlines with Stone Temple Pilots, Hollywood Undead, Yelawolf and others.
- Saturday: Papa Roach's first Rocklahoma appearance in over a decade, joined by Cypress Hill, Dropkick Murphys, Pennywise.
- Sunday: Slayer headlines a Reign in Blood 40th-anniversary set alongside The Pretty Reckless, Insane Clown Posse.
- New for 2026: Cowboy Cup Cannabis Championship & Festival integrated into the weekend.
- VIP sold out; glamping deadline Aug 21; Affirm offered as a payment option.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Rocklahoma-1.webp)

Rocklahoma is gearing up for its biggest birthday yet, with set times now locked in for the festival’s 20th anniversary edition.

The three-day rock and metal blowout returns to the Rockin’ Red Dirt Ranch in Pryor, Oklahoma, September 4-6, with Godsmack, Papa Roach and Slayer topping a [lineup featuring more than 40 acts.](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/rocklahoma-2026-celebrates-20-years-with-slayer-godsmack-papa-roach/)

Friday will see Godsmack close out the opening day, with Stone Temple Pilots, Hollywood Undead, Yelawolf, Jet, Crossfade, Buckcherry and Black Label Society among the other names on the bill.

Papa Roach takes over headline duties Saturday, marking the band’s first Rocklahoma appearance in more than a decade. They’ll be joined by a genre-spanning lineup featuring Cypress Hill, Dropkick Murphys, Pennywise, Black Veil Brides, Wolfmother, Living Colour, Molly Hatchet and Militarie Gun.

But one of the weekend’s biggest moments is being saved for Sunday.

Slayer will headline Rocklahoma’s final night with a performance celebrating the 40th anniversary of the band’s landmark 1986 album *Reign in Blood*. The thrash metal heavyweights lead a stacked Sunday lineup that also includes The Pretty Reckless, Insane Clown Posse, Suicidal Tendencies, PRESIDENT, Ugly Kid Joe, Barbarians of California and Ill Niño.

The Slayer celebration gives Rocklahoma two anniversaries to mark at once. *Reign in Blood* turns 40 this year, while Rocklahoma itself is celebrating two decades since first bringing rock fans to Pryor in 2007.

Over those years, the festival has developed into more than a three-day concert, with its campgrounds and annual Labor Day weekend gathering becoming a major part of the Rocklahoma experience.

The 2026 edition will add something new to that mix through a partnership with the Cowboy Cup Cannabis Championship & Festival, presented by Dime Industries and [710labs.com](http://710labs.com/).

The Cowboy Cup will bring vendors, interactive experiences and industry programming to the Ranch throughout the weekend. Its awards ceremony will take place Saturday, September 5, immediately before Cypress Hill’s performance.

Fans hoping to make a last-minute trip still have options, although some ticket categories have already disappeared.

All VIP passes are sold out, while a limited number of General Admission and camping passes remain available. RV Road Trip Packages and a limited number of Glamping Packages are also still on sale.

The deadline to buy a Glamping Package is Friday, August 21. Glamping guests can check in beginning Thursday, September 3, with check-out scheduled for Monday, September 7. Rocklahoma is also offering Affirm as a payment option at checkout for fans looking to spread the cost of their purchase.

Rocklahoma XX takes place September 4-6 at the Rockin’ Red Dirt Ranch in Pryor, Oklahoma. Find tickets and more festival info [here](https://www.rocklahoma.com/).
