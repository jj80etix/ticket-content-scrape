# Lola Young Plots 17-Date North American ‘Everything Begins’ Tour

## Summary

Grammy-winning singer Lola Young will launch a 17-date North American "Everything Begins" tour in spring 2027, opening April 6 at The Pinnacle in Nashville and closing May 4 at LA's Greek Theatre. The run follows a year of major festival appearances (Rock in Rio, All Things Go, Reading, Austin City Limits), with presales beginning October 7 and general onsale October 9.

### Key Points
- 17-date U.S./Canada tour, April 6-May 4, 2027, including Radio City Music Hall, The Salt Shed and a two-night Toronto stand at Massey Hall.
- Follows a 2026 festival run: Rock in Rio, All Things Go (Washington, D.C.), Reading Festival, Austin City Limits.
- Presales begin Oct. 7; general onsale Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. local time.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Lola-Young-1.webp)

Lola Young will return to North American stages next spring with her “Everything Begins” tour, a 17-date run scheduled to visit the United States and Canada throughout April and early May 2027.

The Grammy-winning singer will launch the tour April 6 at The Pinnacle in Nashville and close May 4 at the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles. The itinerary includes New York’s Radio City Music Hall, Chicago’s The Salt Shed and San Francisco’s Bill Graham Civic Auditorium.

Presales begin October 7, followed by the general sale October 9 at 10 a.m. local time.

Young will open the trek with performances in Nashville and Atlanta before heading up the East Coast for shows in Washington, Philadelphia, New York and Boston. She will then play consecutive nights at Toronto’s Massey Hall on April 15 and 16, the only two-night engagement on the announced schedule.
