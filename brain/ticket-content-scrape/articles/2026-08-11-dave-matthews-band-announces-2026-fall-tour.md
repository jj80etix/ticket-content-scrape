# Dave Matthews Band Announces 2026 Fall Tour

## Summary

Dave Matthews Band has announced a seven-show 2026 fall U.S. tour, opening November 4 at the Kohl Center in Madison, WI and closing with a two-night stand at Madison Square Garden on November 12-13, as reported by Pollstar. A Warehouse Fan Association presale is already underway, with general on-sale beginning Friday, August 21 at 10 AM local venue time. The announcement follows on the heels of the band's ongoing 2026 summer tour, which resumes August 28 in Greenwood Village, CO and wraps with the band's traditional Labor Day weekend residency at The Gorge Amphitheatre in George, WA.

### Key Points
- Fall tour dates: Nov. 4 Madison, WI; Nov. 6 Nashville; Nov. 7 Chicago; Nov. 9 Providence; Nov. 10 Baltimore; Nov. 12-13 New York (MSG).
- Presale for Warehouse Fan Association members is live now; general on-sale starts August 21 at 10 AM local time.
- Summer tour resumes August 28 in Greenwood Village, CO and closes with a three-night Labor Day weekend run at The Gorge Amphitheatre (Sept. 4-6).
- Support acts for the Gorge shows include Infinity Song, Stephen Wilson Jr., Jonah Kagen, Watchhouse, Sierra Hull, and Jesse Welles.
- Dave Matthews Band ranked No. 4 on Pollstar's "25 Most Popular Touring Artists of the Millennium" and has sold more than 25 million tickets since debuting in 1991.

## Transcript

![DMB](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DMB.jpeg)

The Dave Matthews Band has revealed its 2026 fall tour, which is set to kick off on Nov. 4 at Kohl Center in Madison, WI. From there, the storied band’s U.S. headline run will make stops in Chicago, Providence, RI, and more. The trek will conclude with a two-night stand at Madison Square Garden on Nov. 12 & 13. See full tour routing below

An online ticket presale for members of the DMB Warehouse Fan Association is underway now here. General on-sale for tickets begins on Friday, August 21, at 10 AM (local venue time).

DMB has been on a break from their summer tour since completing their July 25 show in Hartford, CT, at The Meadows Music Theatre. The band will get back on the road starting Aug. 28 in Greenwood Village, CO at the Fiddler’s Green Amphitheater, with the summer trek concluding with the band’s traditional Labor Day weekend three-night stand in George, WA at The Gorge Amphitheater from Sept. 4, 5, & 6. The supporting acts for the weekend will be Infinity Song and Stephen Wilson Jr. (Friday), Jonah Kagen and Watchhouse (Saturday), and Sierra Hull and Jesse Welles (Sunday).

Dave Matthews Band was a part of Pollstar’s “25 Most Popular Touring Artists of The Millenium” list, with the band placed at No. 4. Since debuting in 1991, Dave Matthews Band has sold more than 25 million tickets, making them the second-largest ticket seller in history, and a combined 38 million CDs and DVDs.
