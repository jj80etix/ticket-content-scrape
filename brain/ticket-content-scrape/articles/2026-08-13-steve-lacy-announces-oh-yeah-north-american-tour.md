# Steve Lacy Announces ‘Oh Yeah?’ North American Tour

## Summary

Steve Lacy has announced his "Oh Yeah?" North American tour, his first headline run since 2022's sold-out "Give You The World Tour," kicking off September 27 in Minneapolis and wrapping November 30 in Vancouver. Tickets go on sale August 14.

### Key Points
- 27-city run supports Lacy's new album *Oh Yeah?*, released July 17.
- Follows *Gemini Rights* (2022), which included the No. 1 hit "Bad Habit" and won a Grammy for Best Progressive R&B Album.
- Tickets on sale Aug. 14 via Lacy's official site.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Steve-Lacy-Oh-Yeah-Tour.webp)

Steve Lacy is getting ready to hit the road with his newly announced “Oh Yeah?” North American tour.

The newly announced run is scheduled to kick off on September 27 in Minneapolis at The Armory. From there, the tour is set to make stops in cities such as Chicago, Toronto, New York, Boston, Atlanta, Nashville, Houston, Las Vegas and Los Angeles before wrapping up on November 30 in Vancouver at the Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Center.

The upcoming tour is Lacy’s first North American headline run since his 2022 “Give You The World Tour,” which sold out venues across the continent.

Lacy released *Oh Yeah?* on July 17. The album is his first full-length release since *Gemini Rights*, which arrived in 2022 and received critical acclaim. *Gemini Rights* included Lacy’s hit single “Bad Habit,” which reached No. 1 on the Billboard Hot 100. The album also won the Grammy Award for Best Progressive R&B Album.

Tickets will go on sale August 14 at 10 a.m. local time. For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Lacy’s official website](https://www.wearesteve.net/).
