# Venu secures $25m in debt financing fo Oklahoma amphitheatre

## Summary

Venu Holding Corporation closed a $25m debt financing transaction to fund construction of its 12,500-seat Regent Bank Amphitheater in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma, due for completion this autumn. The facility bridges Venu to previously announced permanent C-PACE financing expected to close in Q3 2026.

### Key Points
- $25m raised: $12.5m in gross proceeds at closing, with another $12.5m to follow once closing conditions are met.
- Funds go toward construction costs for the 12,500-cap Regent Bank Amphitheater, targeted for completion this autumn.
- The debenture is expected to be repaid in cash but is convertible by the lender into Venu common stock.
- CEO J.W. Roth frames the deal as protecting shareholder value while keeping construction on schedule ahead of the Q3 2026 permanent C-PACE financing close.

**Venu Holding Corporation has closed a $25m (£18.6m/€21.7m) financing transaction.**

The funds will be dedicated to the construction costs for the company’s 12,500-seat Regent Bank Amphitheater in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma which is due to be completed this coming autumn.

The facility bridges Venu to the closing on the previously announced Commercial Property Assessed Clean Energy (C-PACE) permanent financing for Regent Bank Amphitheater, which the company expects to close in the third quarter of 2026.

The financing advances Regent Bank Amphitheater’s construction towards completion without delay.

Venu received $12.5m in gross proceeds at closing with an additional $12.5m before fees and expenses to follow subject to the satisfaction of certain closing conditions for the release of those additional funds.

The debenture is expected to be repaid by Venu in cash, however it is also convertible by the lender into shares of common stock.

“Getting the capital structure right matters as much as getting the construction right,” said J.W. Roth, founder, chairman and chief executiveof Venu.

“This financing keeps Regent Bank Amphitheater moving forward through a structure that’s disciplined, ownership-focused, and built to protect shareholder value. My thanks to the lending team for their diligence throughout this process, and for believing in what we’re building.”
