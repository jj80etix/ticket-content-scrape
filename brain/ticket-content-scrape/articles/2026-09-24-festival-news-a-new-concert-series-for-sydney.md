# Festival News: A new concert series for Sydney

## Summary

A roundup of festival-lineup announcements: The Strokes will headline the new Sydney Summertime series (Feb 20, 2027, Centennial Park) alongside Beck and Courtney Barnett; Afro Nation Portugal reveals its 2027 headliners (Davido, Rema, Fally Ipupa); Barcelona Jazz Festival unveils its 58th-edition program; Open'er (Poland) adds Sombr, Raye, The Neighbourhood and Patti Smith; Norway's Tons of Rock reveals its first 17 acts led by Mötley Crüe and Turnstile; and CMC Rocks QLD and Two Step Inn confirm 2027 dates and lineups.

### Key Points
- Sydney Summertime debuts Feb 20, 2027, at Centennial Park, headlined by The Strokes.
- Afro Nation Portugal returns June 9-11, 2027, led by Davido, Rema and Fally Ipupa.
- Barcelona Jazz Festival's 58th edition opens a three-year campaign toward its 2028 anniversary.
- Open'er (Gdynia, Poland, June 30-July 3) adds Sombr, Raye, The Neighbourhood, Patti Smith alongside previously announced Tame Impala.
- Tons of Rock (Oslo, June 23-26) reveals first 17 acts led by Mötley Crüe, Turnstile and Lynyrd Skynyrd, after 2026's "biggest festival ever in Norway."
- Two Step Inn (Texas, April 17-18, 2027) is led by Ella Langley and Brooks & Dunn.

## Transcript

Plus: Lineup and date announcements from the likes of Afro Nation Portugal, Barcelona Jazz Festival, Open'er, Tons of Rock, and more

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/09/The-Strokes--scaled.jpg.webp)

The Strokes will headline Sydney Summertime © Sydney Summertime

Sydney’s recently revealed new Sydney Summertime concert series has now unveiled a lineup led by The Strokes for its February-March 2027 debut, leading a raft of festival news.

The Strokes will headline the new event in the city’s Centennial Park on 20 February, with Beck, Courtney Barnett, and Promiseland also announced as performers. It will be The Strokes’ first Australian headline show since 2022 and their only outdoor show of the 2027 Australian leg of thier *Reality Awaits* tour.

Presented by Black Peach and supported by Destination NSW, Sydney Summertime is the work ofAustralian live music executives Jessica Ducrou and Elise Huntley, who have previously worked on events including Splendour in the Grass, Falls Festival, Homebake, Harvest Rock, Download Festival, and Sydney City Limits.

“Centennial Park is honestly my favourite place in town, it’s an absolute dream to host Sydney Summertime in arguably the best venue in the country. What a privilege to create a greenfield concert experience here; we are super grateful to Greater Sydney Parklands and Destination NSW for supporting our vision as we roll up our sleeves and get to it,” Ducrous said of the launch.

**Afro Nation Portugal  
**Elsewhere in festival news, the afrobeat and amapiano event will return to Praia Da Rocha, Portimão, for earlier dates between 9-11th June 2027, with the first names now announced for its lineup.

The festival will feature Davido, Rema, and Fally Ipupa as headliners, with other acts including Omah Lay, Shenseea, and Kabza De Small.

**Barcelona Jazz Festival  
**In Spain, the Barcelona Jazz Festival has unveiled the full program for its 58th edition. New names include Zucchero, Michel Camilo, Samara Joy, Vicente Amigo, Andrea Motis, Yamandu Costa, Ramon Mirabet, Branford Marsalis, Dianne Reeves, Kyle Eastwood, Kurt Rosenwinkel, and Las Migas.

The festival had already announced the performances of Van Morrison, his first visit to the festival, as well as Pat Metheny.

Organisers said the upcoming outing will commence a three-year commemorative period leading up to 2028 which will operate under the slogan ‘Barcelona és jazz” (‘Barcelona is jazz’).

**Open’er  
**Returning to Gdynia on Poland’s Baltic coast from 30 June – 3 July, Open’er has revealed headliners Sombr, Raye, The Neighbourhood, and Patti Smith.

The new names join Tame Impala, who were previously announced for the bill, which organisers called “one of the festival’s most exciting and diverse lineups to date”.

**Tons of Rock  
**The first 17 bands have also been announced for Norway’s Tons of Rock, led by Mötley Crüe, Turnstile, and Lynyrd Skynyrd.

The festival will take place at Ekebergsletta in Oslo, following what organisers called the “historic milestone” of the 2026 outing, which they said became the biggest music festival ever held in Norway. 2027’s edition is set for 23-26 June 2027.

**CMC Rocks QLD  
**Back in Australia, CMC Rocks will mark its 20th anniversary edition in 2027, with dates confirmed for 17-21 March. The milestone event will take place at the Willowbank Raceway in Ipswich, Queensland, outside of Brisbane.

**Two Step Inn  
**Finally, Texas country festival Two Step Inn has revealed its lineup for 17-18 April 2027, led by Ella Langley, whose song *Choosin’ Texas* recently became the longest-running number one song on the Billboard Hot 100 in history.

Langley headlines 18 April, while Brooks & Dunn lead the previous day’s bill. Other acts from the lineup include Sheryl Crow, Old Crow Medicine Show, and Stella Lefty.
