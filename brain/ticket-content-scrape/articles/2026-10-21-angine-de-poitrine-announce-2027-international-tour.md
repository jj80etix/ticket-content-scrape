# Angine de Poitrine Announce 2027 International Tour

## Summary

Quebec duo Angine de Poitrine announced a 2027 international tour spanning Australia, South America, North America, and Europe, kicking off March 4 at Forum Melbourne.

### Key Points
- Tour begins March 4, 2027 at Forum Melbourne, running through July.
- Australian leg includes Golden Plains (Meredith), WOMADelaide, and Sydney's Enmore Theatre.
- South American dates include Lollapalooza Argentina, Chile, and Brasil, plus Estéreo Picnic in Colombia.
- Canadian dates open with two shows at Calgary's Grey Eagle Event Centre (March 28-29), followed by Ottawa and Winnipeg stops.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Angine-de-Poitrine.webp)

Angine de Poitrine have announced a 2027 international tour, with headline concerts and festival appearances across Australia, South America, North America and Europe.

The Quebec duo will begin the newly announced schedule March 4 at Forum Melbourne and continue through July. Following the Melbourne opener, the Australian itinerary includes Golden Plains in Meredith, WOMADelaide in Adelaide and a headline performance at Sydney’s Enmore Theatre. The duo will then head to South America for appearances at Lollapalooza Argentina, Lollapalooza Chile, Lollapalooza Brasil and Estéreo Picnic in Colombia.

Canadian dates begin with two shows at Calgary’s Grey Eagle Event Centre on March 28 and 29. Additional two-night engagements are scheduled at History Ottawa on March 31 and April 1 and Winnipeg’s Burton Cummings Theatre on April 6 and 7.

The U.S. leg opens May 2 at The Anthem in Washington, D.C., followed by Boston’s MGM Music Hall at Fenway on May 3. The schedule continues through Pittsburgh, Royal Oak, Michigan, Columbus, Nashville, Dallas and Austin before concluding May 15 at Denver’s Mission Ballroom.
