# Vans Warped Tour Announces 2027 Dates

## Summary

Vans Warped Tour set its 2027 dates and locations following the close of the 2026 Orlando run: Washington D.C. (June 19–20), Long Beach (July 24–25), Mexico City (Sept. 11–12), and Orlando (Nov. 13–14). Presale for the three U.S. stops opens Nov. 20 at 9 a.m. Pacific.

### Key Points
- 2027 Warped Tour cities: Washington D.C., Long Beach, Mexico City, Orlando.
- Presale for the U.S. dates begins Nov. 20 at 9 a.m. Pacific.
- Announcement follows the close of the 2026 Orlando stop.

Following the close of the 2026 run in Orlando last weekend, Vans Warped Tour has set dates and locations for the 2027 edition of the beloved touring festival.

Warped will head to Washington, D.C. June 19 and 20; Long Beach July 24 and 25; Mexico City Sept. 11 and 12; and once again wrap up in Orlando Nov. 13 and 14.

Presale for the three U.S. stops begin 9 a.m. Pacific Nov. 20.
