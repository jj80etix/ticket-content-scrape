# A Perfect Circle Sets 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

A Perfect Circle announced a North American tour running April through June 2027, with Puscifer and The Cult joining separate legs of the run. General on-sale begins October 2, with one date (Elizabethtown, KY) on a later on-sale.

### Key Points
- Tour runs April 13 (Vancouver) through June 13 (Phoenix), spanning Canada and the US.
- Puscifer supports the Canadian dates plus Detroit; The Cult joins from the Boston date through the finale.
- General on-sale is October 2, 10 a.m. local time, except the Elizabethtown show.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/A-Perfect-Circle.webp)

A Perfect Circle will tour North America from April through June 2027, with Puscifer and The Cult joining separate portions of the run.

The tour opens April 13 at Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre in Vancouver and closes June 13 at Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre in Phoenix. Between those dates, A Perfect Circle will play Calgary, Edmonton, Winnipeg and Niagara Falls before heading to U.S. markets including Boston, Houston, Nashville, Chicago, Seattle and Los Angeles.

Puscifer will support the six Canadian performances and the April 25 show at Detroit’s Fox Theatre. That pairing will put Maynard James Keenan onstage with both bands each night. The Cult joins the tour April 27 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston and remains on the bill through the Phoenix finale.

“Very much looking forward to pulling double duty through Canada with A Perfect Circle and Puscifer,” Keenan said in the announcement.
