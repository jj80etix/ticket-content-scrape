# KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour Announces 2027 Dates

## Summary

Netflix and AEG Presents announced the "KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour," an arena production based on the animated film, with more than 150 performances planned across nearly 50 cities in 2027-2028. The North American leg opens June 5, 2027 in Baltimore and closes August 14 in Tacoma, before moving to Europe through an October 23 finale at London's The O2.

### Key Points
- 150+ shows across ~50 cities planned for 2027-2028.
- North American leg: June 5 (Baltimore) to August 14 (Tacoma, WA).
- European leg follows, concluding October 23 at The O2 in London; Asia-Pacific and Latin America dates expected for 2028.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/kpop.jpg)

Netflix and AEG Presents announced the *KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour*, an arena production inspired by the animated film, with more than 150 performances planned across nearly 50 cities in 2027 and 2028.

The tour is slated to open June 5, 2027, at CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore, Maryland. From there, the run is set to make stops in cities such as Newark, Chicago, Pittsburgh, Atlanta, Orlando, Los Angeles and Las Vegas. The final North American stop is scheduled for August 14 in Tacoma, Washington at Tacoma Dome.

Following the North American dates, the tour will head to Europe with stops in cities such as Milan, Amsterdam, Berlin, Oslo, Brussels, Paris and Dublin before wrapping up on October 23 in London at The O2. Additional dates in Asia-Pacific and Latin America are expected to be announced for 2028.

The announcement follows the [initial tour plans revealed by Netflix and AEG Presents in May](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/netflix-kpop-demon-hunters-global-world-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), when the companies confirmed their partnership without disclosing specific dates or venues.

Unlike a traditional concert featuring the recording artists behind a soundtrack, the production will combine animated characters with live performers, stunts, choreography and immersive visual effects. HUNTR/X members Rumi, Mira and Zoey will appear alongside the Saja Boys and Derpy Tiger in a theatrical experience designed around the film’s music and characters.

It remains unclear whether EJAE, Audrey Nuna and Rei Ami, the recording artists who provide HUNTR/X’s singing voices, will participate. The three singers have performed together at major events since the film’s release, but organizers have not confirmed their involvement in the tour.

The production follows the success of *KPop Demon Hunters*, which became Netflix’s most popular movie following its June 2025 release. Its soundtrack generated multiple international hits, including “Golden.”
