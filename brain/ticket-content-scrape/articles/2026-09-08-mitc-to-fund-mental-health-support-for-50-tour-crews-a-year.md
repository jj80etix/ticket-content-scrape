# MITC to fund mental health support for 50 tour crews a year

## Summary

The Music Industry Therapist Collective (MITC) launched a $272,000, three-year "Pay It Forward Fund" to provide free mental health support to 50 touring crews a year, backed by Live Nation, AEG, and The Team.

### Key Points

- Fund targets crew on small and mid-scale tours worldwide, free to access.
- Program is set to run for three years under this funding commitment.
- Source article is largely paywalled beyond the opening summary.

The funding will be free to access for touring teams and is aimed at “crew on small and mid-scale tours” operating anywhere in the world.
