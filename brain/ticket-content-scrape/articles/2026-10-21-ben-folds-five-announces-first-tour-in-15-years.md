# Ben Folds Five Announces First Tour In 15 Years

## Summary

Ben Folds Five has announced initial dates for the alt-rock trio's first tour in 15 years.

### Key Points
- Band (Ben Folds, Robert Sledge, Darren Jessee) formed in 1993 in Chapel Hill, NC.
- First leg runs April 12 (Knoxville, TN) through April 24 (Birmingham, AL), with more U.S. shows promised for 2027.
- Tickets for announced dates go on sale Friday, Oct. 23, 10am local time.
- Announcement follows a 30th-anniversary reissue of the band's debut album, including a previously shelved, newly rediscovered early version.

## Transcript

Ben Folds Five is back! The beloved alt-rock trio has announced initial dates for the band’s first tour in 15 years.  
  
Along with Ben Folds on piano and vocals, the group formed in 1993 in Chapel Hill, North Carolina, includes Robert Sledge on bass and Darren Jessee on drums. As an announcement from the band’s PR team notes, the trio “dubbed their distinctive sound ‘punk rock for sissies’ – a smart-alecky collision of spaz rock, power pop, irresistible 70s AM melodies, pseudo-jazz, and copious lyrical mischief that proved you didn’t need aggro guitars to make a glorious ‘90s noise.”  
  
Last month BFF celebrated the 30th anniversary of its debut, self-titled album by releasing a special anniversary edition featuring a newly remastered version of the original LP, along with “the long-awaited, fan-requested un-vaulting of the band’s mythical shelved first attempt, recently rediscovered on digital audio tape in Folds’ personal archives and specially remastered for this release.”  
  
And now new tour dates are here, with the promise of more shows to be announced across the U.S. in 2027. So far the band has confirmed two nights in Tennessee, three nights each in Florida and Texas and a gig in Birmingham, Alabama.

The fun starts with an April 12 show in Knoxville, Tennessee, at the Tennessee Theatre. Stops include Chattanooga, Jacksonville, Austin and Houston.

Tickets for announced dates go on sale Friday, Oct. 23 at 10 a.m. local time

More details and new exclusive Ben Folds Five merchandise can be found at [www.officialbenfoldsfive.com](http://www.officialbenfoldsfive.com)  
  
Folds is represented by THE·TEAM’s Jonathan Levine, Noah Plotnicki and Seth Malasky worldwide, excluding the UK and Europe.

Highlights from Ben Folds Five’s last full-fledged tour include two shows in December 2012 at O2 Academy Brixton in London that sold 7,421 tickets and grossed $411,675.
