# Brooks & Dunn Bring Neon Moon Tour to Australia in March 2027

## Summary

Country duo Brooks & Dunn will bring their "Neon Moon" tour to Australia in March 2027, marking a new international leg for the pairing. The announcement includes tour dates and venues for the Australian run.

### Key Points
- Brooks & Dunn's "Neon Moon" tour heads to Australia in March 2027.
- New international leg for the duo's current touring cycle.
- Standard tour-announcement item with no ticketing-industry angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Brooks-Dunn.webp)

Brooks & Dunn will return to Australia in March 2027 for their first performances in the country since 2009, with four arena concerts now scheduled alongside a headline appearance at CMC Rocks QLD.

The Australian leg of the duo’s Neon Moon Tour includes Sydney’s Afterpay Arena on March 24 and 25 and Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena on March 27 and 28. The March 25 and 28 performances expand the original two-show announcement. Ashley McBryde and Queensland singer-songwriter Mack Geiger will support the arena dates. General ticket sales begin October 9 at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketek.

Before the arena run, Kix Brooks and Ronnie Dunn will join the 20th-anniversary edition of [CMC Rocks QLD](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/brooks-dunn-eric-church-and-ashley-mcbryde-to-headline-cmc-rocks-qlds-20th-anniversary/), scheduled for March 19–21 at Willowbank Raceway in Ipswich. Eric Church and McBryde also lead the festival lineup, which includes Parker McCollum, Kameron Marlowe, Turnpike Troubadours, Blake Whiten and Lanie Gardner. The appearance will mark Brooks & Dunn’s debut at the festival.

The Australian return follows a year of international festival appearances and U.S. touring. Brooks & Dunn made their first U.K. appearances earlier in 2026, headlining C2C with performances in Belfast, Glasgow and London. They subsequently headlined Stagecoach and joined Morgan Wallen for nearly a dozen dates on his Still The Problem Tour. Their own Neon Moon Tour continues across the United States this fall.
