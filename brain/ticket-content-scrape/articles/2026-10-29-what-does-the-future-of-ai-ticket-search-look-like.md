# What does the future of AI ticket search look like?

## Summary

Leading ticketing firms that have moved early on conversational AI discovery tell IQ that LLM-based ticket search could stay a "modest" slice of the market unless it can "inspire" event choices rather than just serving existing intent. The comments follow a wave of 2026 integrations between major ticketers and chatbot platforms.

### Key Points
- SeatGeek partnered with ChatGPT; Ticketmaster integrated with Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Google AI mode, Meta Muse, Claude and Alexa+
- Ticketmaster EVP of distributed commerce Dan Armstrong is quoted on how discovery is evolving months into these integrations
- Ticketers' concern: conversational AI is good at serving known intent but weaker at inspiring new event discovery
- Framed as an open question for how large a share of ticket search AI assistants will ultimately capture

### Highlights
- Ticketmaster has now integrated with essentially every major LLM assistant (Gemini, ChatGPT, AI mode, Meta Muse, Claude, Alexa+)
- SeatGeek's competing integration is specifically with ChatGPT
- Industry's own early read, months after launch, is that conversational discovery remains "modest" without a way to inspire choice

### Industry Problem
Patron ticket discovery via AI assistants is being built out rapidly by Tier 2 competitors, but even they report it underperforms at inspiring event choice versus serving pre-formed intent — a benchmark data point for where AI-assisted discovery investment pays off versus where it doesn't.

### Proposed Solution
This is direct competitive benchmarking for Etix Assistant: treat broad LLM-platform integrations (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Alexa+) as a now-standard distribution surface for ticket search, but prioritize inspiration/discovery features over simple conversational lookup, since competitors report that's the current product gap even among early movers.
