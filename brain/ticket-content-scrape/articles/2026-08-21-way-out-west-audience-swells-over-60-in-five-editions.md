# Way Out West audience swells over 60% in five editions

## Summary

Sweden's Way Out West festival sold out all pass types for the first time in its 18-year history, drawing 81,500 attendees — a 63% increase over five editions — for its Gothenburg edition headlined by The xx, Gorillaz, The Cure and Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds. Ahead of the festival, Live Nation-owned Nordic promoter Luger introduced "Real Fake Tickets," a program giving fans who had previously been scammed by counterfeit tickets a second chance to attend.

### Key Points
- 81,500 attendees, first-ever complete sellout of all three days/pass types; up 63% over five editions since 2022 (50,000 → 81,500).
- Organized by Live Nation's Nordic subsidiary Luger.
- "Real Fake Tickets" program offers replacement access to fans previously victimized by counterfeit-ticket scams.
- 2027 edition already announced (Aug. 12-14), with early tickets on sale.

### Highlights
- A major European festival built a dedicated remediation program specifically for counterfeit-ticket scam victims — a rare direct response to ticket fraud's patron-facing harm.
- Framed as a trust/goodwill play rather than a detection technology.

### Industry Problem
Counterfeit and scam tickets remain a real, recurring patron pain point even at major, professionally-run festivals — fans who fall victim currently have few recourse options beyond losing their money and missing the show.

### Proposed Solution
A primary platform could build a formal "verified victim" remediation pathway (discounted or comped replacement access, tied to a fraud-report/verification flow) as both a fraud-prevention deterrent and a trust-building feature, distinct from pure bot/counterfeit-detection tooling.

Building on a streak of record attendances, Sweden’s Way Out West is celebrating a complete sell-out for its 18th edition, marking a 63% increase in festivalgoers across five editions.

A total of 81,500 people descended on Slottsskogen park in Gothenburg for the 13-15 August event, with the record figures a result of the event’s Thursday allocation selling out for the first time in its history.

The achievement marks the first complete sell-out of its three-day programme, which was initially introduced in 2012.

Since 2022, the festival has continued bar-setting sales, growing from the 50,000-person mark that year to [78,000](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/08/record-crowds-flock-to-swedens-way-out-west/) people with last year’s edition.

“We are overwhelmed by the response to this year’s festival, and incredibly grateful that we still, quite objectively, have the best audience in the world,” says festival manager Kimmie Winroth. “Thank you for continuing to trust what we do and for coming back year after year.”

Organised by Live Nation’s Nordic subsidiary Luger, the 19th edition featured The xx, Gorillaz, The Cure, and Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds as its headliners.

Ahead of the festival gates opening, organisers also introduced a ticket offer for fans who have fallen victim to fake tickets and scams, called [Real Fake Tickets](https://www.iqmagazine.com/?p=470834&preview=true), giving them a second chance at seeing their favourite artists.

“We usually meet fans at their very best – when they’re inside the festival. This time, we want to meet a few of them at their worst, and turn that moment into something positive,” said Filip Hiltmann, the festival’s head of marketing & PR, with the announcement.

Other artists that performed at this year’s festival included Lorde, Moby, Zara Larsson, Four Tet, sombr, Lily Allen, Geese, Dijon, and more.

Organisers have already announced Way Out West’s return for 12-14 August 2027, with a limited number of advance tickets available now.
