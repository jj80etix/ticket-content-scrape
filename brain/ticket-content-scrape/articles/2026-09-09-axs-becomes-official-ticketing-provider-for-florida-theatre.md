# AXS Becomes Official Ticketing Provider for Florida Theatre

## Summary

The Florida Theatre in Jacksonville named AXS its exclusive ticketing partner, giving the historic nonprofit venue access to AXS Mobile ID, data/analytics tools, integrated marketing and premium seating support. The deal extends AXS's growing portfolio of independent venue clients in the U.S.

### Key Points
- Exclusive agreement covers full ticketing/mobile services plus AXS's broader distribution network.
- Includes AXS Mobile ID for purchase, access, transfer and ticket management.
- Adds to AXS's independent-venue client base, alongside marquee clients like Coachella, Red Rocks and LA28.
- Announced ahead of the Florida Theatre's upcoming centennial.

### Highlights
- New exclusive ticketing deal, announced this week, for a historic independent venue.
- AXS gains data/analytics and premium-seating tools bundled into the agreement.
- Follows AXS's recent exclusive deal for Wrangler NFR, showing continued push into new venue verticals.

### Industry Problem
Independent, nonprofit venues like the Florida Theatre are choosing full-service platforms (AXS) for mobile ticketing, fraud-resistant access, and marketing/analytics tooling — signaling that feature depth (not just ticketing plumbing) is now a competitive requirement to win and retain independent venue clients.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes AXS's win directly: modern mobile ID, data/analytics, integrated marketing and premium seating bundled as a package. For Etix, the competitive response is to keep pace on comparable mobile-ID/access, analytics and marketing-integration features when pitching or retaining similar independent/nonprofit venue clients.

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/axs-florida-theatre.webp)

The Florida Theatre in Jacksonville has selected AXS as its official ticketing partner under a new exclusive agreement announced Wednesday.

AXS will power the historic venue’s ticketing operations and provide its full range of ticketing and mobile services. The platform will allow customers to purchase, access, transfer and manage tickets through AXS Mobile ID.

The agreement also gives the Florida Theatre access to AXS data and analytics tools, integrated marketing capabilities, premium seating support and the company’s broader ticket distribution network.

Kevin Stone, interim president and vice president of programming at the Florida Theatre, said AXS would provide patrons with a “modern, secure, and convenient ticket-buying experience.”

AXS Vice President of Business Development Ali McCloud said the company would provide “flexible technology and tools” to support the theater and its customers. “We look forward to supporting the Theatre as it approaches its centennial and begins this next chapter,” McCloud said.

The deal expands AXS’ portfolio of independent venue clients in the United States. The AEG-owned ticketing company operates primary and secondary marketplaces and works with more than 1,600 sports and entertainment brands across North America, Europe and the Asia-Pacific region. Its clients include the LA28 Olympic and Paralympic Games, Coachella, Red Rocks Amphitheatre, The O2 and Stagecoach.

The Florida Theatre opened in downtown Jacksonville in 1927 as a venue for live performances and motion pictures. The 1,865-seat theater now presents more than 200 events annually, including concerts, comedy, ballet and community programming.

The venue completed an $8 million renovation in 2023 following the opening of a new member lounge in 2022. The project included upgrades to the theater’s seating, lobby, restrooms, lounges and building systems.

The Florida Theatre, the only remaining historic theater on Jacksonville’s Forsyth Street, will celebrate its 100th anniversary in 2027.
