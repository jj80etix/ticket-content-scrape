# Miranda Lambert to Headline Opening Night at Nashville’s The Truth

## Summary

Miranda Lambert will headline opening night at Nashville's new venue, The Truth.

### Key Points
- Miranda Lambert headlines the opening night of Nashville venue "The Truth."
- Routine venue/event announcement, no ticketing/industry-structure angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Miranda-Lambert-The-Truth.webp)

Miranda Lambert will headline the opening night of The Truth, a new 4,400-capacity venue in Nashville’s Wedgewood-Houston neighborhood, on Thursday, October 1.

The three-time Grammy Award winner will perform a special concert titled “Miranda Lambert: One Night Only.” The show will feature a 12-piece string section and include songs from Lambert’s catalog as well as new material from her upcoming album, *Crisco*, which is scheduled to be released at midnight that same night.

Lambert said the strings played an important role during the recording of the new album and that she is looking forward to incorporating them into her live performance.

“It was magical to hear the way strings can transform a song when we were in the studio creating this record, and I can’t wait to bring that to life in an even bigger way with our full setlist,” Lambert said. “We’ve never gotten to play that way on stage, and to have the opportunity to do it for the first time at the opening of a beautiful new venue right here in Nashville is going to be really special.”

The concert will mark the launch of The Truth, which is positioning itself as an intimate Nashville venue focused on live performances from established artists and emerging acts.

Tickets for the opening-night show will go on sale Friday, August 21, at 10 a.m. CT through The Truth’s website.

Sally Williams, president of Nashville Music & Business Strategy at Live Nation, shared, “There couldn’t be a more fitting artist to open The Truth than Miranda Lambert,” Williams said. “She’s a songwriter at her core, a trailblazer who has always followed her own creative instincts, and an artist who has never been afraid to push beyond the boundaries of genre.”

Meanwhile, Grant Lyman, president of Live Nation U.S. Regions East, said, “From the beginning, we envisioned The Truth as an intimate room where artists could create deeper connections with their fans,” Lyman said.

Lambert’s concert will begin a month-long inaugural schedule featuring artists from several genres. The October lineup includes Malcolm Todd on October 3, Role Model on October 4, Freya Skye on October 5 and Amyl and the Sniffers on October 6.

Switchfoot will perform October 7, followed by Bleachers on October 8. Jack White is scheduled for October 9 and 10, while Limp Bizkit will perform October 11 and 12.

Other acts scheduled for the venue’s opening month include Foster The People, Suki Waterhouse, The Chicks, Tedeschi Trucks Band, Polyphia, Sting, Rawayana, The Smashing Pumpkins, Kevin Gates, EsDeeKid, HARDY and Beck.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [The Truth’s official website](https://www.thetruthnashville.com/).
