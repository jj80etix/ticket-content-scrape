# The Neighbourhood Announce 19 New Shows for 2027 Wourld Tour II

## Summary

The Neighbourhood announced 19 additional 2027 "The Wourld Tour II" dates, adding North American arena shows (Denver, St. Louis, Houston, Dallas, then Philadelphia through Edmonton) and summer European dates (Luxembourg, Florence, Vienna) on top of 10 previously confirmed festival dates, building on the band's sold-out 2026 "Wourld Tour." Artist presale begins Oct. 29, general onsale Oct. 30.

### Key Points
- 19 new headlining shows plus 10 previously confirmed festival dates folded into "The Wourld Tour II" for 2027.
- North American arena run: Denver (March 2) through Edmonton (May 15); European dates include Luxembourg, Florence and Vienna in July.
- Current 2026 "Wourld Tour" wraps with two sold-out Kia Forum shows Dec. 4–5.
- Artist presale Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. local; general onsale Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. local.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Neighbourhood-World-toUR.webp)

The Neighbourhood have announced 19 additional headlining concerts for 2027 as part of *The Wourld Tour II*, extending the band’s global touring schedule with new arena dates across North America and summer performances in Europe.

The newly announced headlining shows begin March 2 at Ball Arena in Denver, followed by stops in St. Louis, Houston and Dallas. After previously confirmed festival appearances in South America, the band will resume its North American arena run April 23 at Philadelphia’s Xfinity Mobile Arena.

Additional concerts are scheduled for Boston, Montreal, Ottawa, Columbus, Pittsburgh, Raleigh, Indianapolis and Chicago before the tour heads west for performances in Winnipeg, Calgary and Edmonton in May.

The European portion of the expanded schedule includes newly announced headlining performances July 5 at Luxexpo Open-Air in Luxembourg, July 13 at Arena delle Cornacchie in Florence, Italy, and July 15 at Metastadt Open Air in Vienna, Austria.

The announcement also incorporates 10 previously confirmed festival appearances into the band’s 2027 itinerary. Those include Innings Festival in Arizona, Lollapalooza events in Argentina, Chile and Brazil, Festival Estéreo Picnic in Colombia, and several major European festivals, including Pinkpop, Open’er Festival, NOS Alive and Lollapalooza Berlin.

The expanded schedule follows the band’s ongoing 2026 *Wourld Tour*, which has taken the group through North America, Europe, Australia, New Zealand and Asia.

The remaining 2026 itinerary includes shows in Atlanta, Miami, Nashville, Chicago, Detroit and Brooklyn, among other cities, before wrapping up with two sold-out performances December 4 and 5 at the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California.

Tickets for the newly announced headlining dates will be available through an artist presale beginning Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales begin Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. local time.
