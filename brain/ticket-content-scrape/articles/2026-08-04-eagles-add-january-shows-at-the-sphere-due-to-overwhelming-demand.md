# Eagles Add January Shows At The Sphere ‘Due To Overwhelming Demand’

## Summary

The Eagles are adding four new dates — January 15, 16, 22 and 23, 2027 — to their Sphere residency "due to overwhelming demand," bringing the total run to 72 shows and extending its status as the venue's longest-running residency. All-in ticket pricing starts at $175, with presales beginning August 12.

### Key Points
- New January 2027 dates bring the residency to 72 total shows since its September 2024 debut.
- General onsale Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. PT; artist presale (via Seated) begins Aug. 12; additional presales Aug. 13.
- Tickets start at $175 with all-in pricing (taxes and fees included in the listed price).
- The Eagles have grossed over $1 billion since 2001, per Pollstar's Most Popular Touring Artists of the Millennium list.

The Eagles are officially adding new dates to their “Eagles: Live In Concert At the Sphere” residency, with a slew of shows arriving in January 2027. According to a press release for the band, the new shows have been added “due to an overwhelming demand,” and are set for Jan. 15, 16, 22, & 23. With the new shows added, the iconic rock band will have played a total of 72 shows in total, making it the longest-running residency at the innovative venue, which initially began on Sept. 24, 2024.

General on-sale for the December shows will begin August 14 at 10 a.m. PT [here](https://ticketmaster.com). Advance Artist presale registration is available [now](https://eagles.com), powered by Seated, and the presale begins Aug. 12 at 10 a.m. PT. Additional presale opportunities begin Aug. 13 at 10 a.m. PT. Tickets start at $175 and reflect all-in pricing, meaning the ticket price listed is inclusive of taxes and fees.  
  
Limited VIP Ticket Packages will be available [here](https://eagles.com) and include premium seats, exclusive merchandise, parking, and more.

With grosses exceeding $1 billion since 2001, Eagles are listed on [*Pollstar* ’s list of the Most Popular Touring Artists of the Millennium](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/08/coldplay-u2-sheeran-dmb-top-pollstars-25-most-popular-touring-artists-of-the-millennium/) alongside acts like Elton John, The Rolling Stones, Guns N’ Roses, Bruce Springsteen & The E Street Band and U2.

Eagles is managed by Full Stop Management and the band’s booking agent is Creative Artists Agency’s Rick Roskin.
