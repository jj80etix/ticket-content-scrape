# Off-Broadway’s ‘A History of Paper’ to Welcome Leads Alan Cumming and Shirley Henderson

## Summary

Alan Cumming and Shirley Henderson will star in the U.S. premiere of "A History of Paper," opening for previews Feb. 12, 2027 at Stage 42 Off-Broadway, with tickets going on sale Nov. 17 via Telecharge. The 13-week engagement is directed by Andrew Panton.

### Key Points
- Runs through May 16, 2027; tickets on sale Nov. 17 through Telecharge.
- Marks Shirley Henderson's New York stage debut.
- First New York transfer for both Pitlochry Festival Theatre and Dundee Rep Theatre.
- Written by Oliver Emanuel with music by Gareth Williams.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/A-History-of-Paper-Off-Broadway.webp)

Alan Cumming and Shirley Henderson will star in the U.S. premiere of “A History of Paper,” opening for previews February 12, 2027, at the Shubert Organization’s Stage 42 Off-Broadway.

The musical will run through May 16, with tickets going on sale November 17 through Telecharge. Andrew Panton, artistic director of Dundee Rep Theatre, will direct the 13-week engagement.

The production brings together Scotland’s Pitlochry Festival Theatre and Dundee Rep Theatre and represents the first New York transfer for both companies. Henderson will make her New York stage debut.

Written by Oliver Emanuel with music by Gareth Williams, the two-character musical follows a relationship through the paper objects accumulated over a lifetime.

The project originated as a BBC Radio 4 play by Emanuel in 2017. Emanuel and Williams developed a musical adaptation that was presented as a work-in-progress at Dundee Rep in 2019. It subsequently reached the Edinburgh Festival Fringe in 2024, playing at the Traverse Theatre.

Cumming saw that production and worked with Panton to include the musical in his inaugural season as artistic director of Pitlochry Festival Theatre. Its move to Manhattan advances his plans to establish an exchange between the Scottish company and New York.

“Since taking over as Artistic Director at Pitlochry Festival Theatre, it has been my plan (and dream) to start a theatrical exchange channel between Manhattan and our theatre in the hills,” Cumming said in a statement.

The engagement also reunites Cumming and Henderson, whose working relationship began more than 40 years ago.

“I never imagined it was possible to have the opportunity to step onto the New York stage,” Henderson said. “I am glad it’s with my lovely friend Alan Cumming.”

The creative team includes designer Ana Inés Jabares-Pita, lighting designer Bruno Poet and sound designer Alexandra Faye Braithwaite. Gavin Whitworth serves as musical supervisor, musical director and pianist.

Stephanie Yankwitt and Company Casting will handle U.S. casting, with general management by Pemberley Productions. Sonia Friedman Productions, Jerry Goehring and Pemberley Productions are producing the engagement.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [historyofpapermusical.com](http://historyofpapermusical.com).
