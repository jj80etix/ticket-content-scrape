# Sam Fender Pre-Sale Delayed After ‘Sophisticated Bot Attack’

## Summary

Sam Fender delayed the presale for his Newcastle St. James' Park homecoming stadium show after ticket site Gigantic faced a "sophisticated bot attack," pushing the sale to Friday while presales for his other Proper Massive Stadium Tour dates (Cardiff, London, Edinburgh, Dublin, Manchester) proceeded unaffected. The item notes two other recent bot-driven onsale disruptions this week — Garth Brooks pulling a Kansas City sale and bot-swarmed Raye presale signups — underscoring a broader pattern of bot attacks hitting high-demand onsales.

### Highlights
- Fender's Newcastle presale delayed to Friday after Gigantic reported fighting off a "sophisticated bot attack"; other tour dates unaffected.
- Garth Brooks pulled a Kansas City onsale this week due to a bot swarm.
- Raye's Royal Albert Hall presale signups were similarly swamped by bots.

### Industry Problem
High-demand onsales continue to be disrupted by increasingly sophisticated bot attacks even at mid-size ticketing platforms (Gigantic), delaying access for genuine fans and forcing last-minute onsale changes — a recurring scalability/fraud-defense gap industry-wide.

### Proposed Solution
The article doesn't detail Gigantic's specific mitigation beyond delaying the sale; the broader signal for a primary platform is to continue investing in bot-detection and queue/onsale-scalability defenses (rate-limiting, challenge mechanisms, behavioral detection) ahead of high-demand onsales, given how frequently even well-known artists are hitting this problem.

Sam Fender is the latest artist to halt an on-sale due to bots.

The British singer-songwriter delayed the pre-sale for his upcoming homecoming stadium show at Newcastle’s St. James’ Park due to “sophisticated bot attacks.”

Fender posted on Instagram that the pre-sale for that show would be delayed until Friday as ticket site Gigantic was fighting off bots. Presales for the other shows on his recently announced Proper Massive Stadium Tour in Cardiff, London, Edinburgh, Dublin and Manchester did not appear to be facing the same problems.

Earlier this week, Garth Brooks pulled down the sale for tickets to a show in Kansas City due to a bot swarm. Presale signups for Raye’s Royal Albert Hall show were similarly swamped by bot sign-ups.
