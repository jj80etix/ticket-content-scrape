# The Scepter Performs Metal Show in Hospital Room for Fan

## Summary

Canadian metal band The Scepter performed a live concert inside a Bruyère Health hospital room in Ottawa for 27-year-old fan Tim Berry, whose medical condition prevents him from attending regular shows. The band partnered with local venue Dominion Tavern to bring the free performance to a Bruyère boardroom, with other patients joining the audience.

### Key Points
- Bruyère Health staff arranged the concert after Berry shared his dream of experiencing a heavy-metal show.
- The Scepter partnered with local venue Dominion Tavern to stage the free performance in a hospital boardroom.
- Berry attended dressed in a metal tee with freshly painted nails, joined by other facility patients.
- Bruyère clinical chaplain Roshene Lawson and Scepter guitarist Josh Collins both described the moment as emotional.

## Transcript

The Canadian metal band The Scepter just transformed a hospital room into a metal concert for a fan.

According to *CTV News*, The Scepter were asked by Bruyère Health to perform an in-center concert in Ottawa, Canada for a 27-year-old “metalhead” Tim Berry. The fan faces medical challenges that prevent him from attending concerts.

”Sometime the best medicine is a little live music,” Breyère Health wrote in an [Instagram post](https://www.instagram.com/bruyerehealth?igsh=MjZybGF6MDl0MGp6). “When a patient shared his dream of experiencing a heavy metal concert, our team worked with some incredible community partners to make it happen right here at Bruyère Health.”

The Scepter teamed-up with the local venue Dominion Tavern to help bring the free show to life in a Bruyère Board room. Berry was dressed in a metal tee and freshly-painted nails fr the performance. Additionally, he was joined by other patients of the facility for the show.
