# Aerosmith Announces Two Hollywood Bowl Shows With All-Star Guests

## Summary

Aerosmith will play two shows at the Hollywood Bowl on October 29 and November 1, billed as "California, Dream On: Aerosmith & Friends," marking a return to the stage more than two years after announcing its retirement from touring. The all-star guest lineup includes the Black Crowes, Run-DMC, Heart, Billy Idol, Eric Church, and Sammy Hagar, with Don Was as music director.

### Key Points
- Two Hollywood Bowl dates: Oct. 29 and Nov. 1, 2026.
- Confirmed guests include Slash and Duff McKagan (Guns N' Roses), Jerry Cantrell and Mike Inez (Alice in Chains), Chad Smith (Red Hot Chili Peppers), Nuno Bettencourt (Extreme), and Pat Monahan (Train).
- Nightly lineups differ and will be finalized near showtime, with additional surprise guests planned.
- Don Was serves as music director for the shows.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Aerosmith-Friends.webp)

Aerosmith will perform at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles on October 29 and November 1, returning to the stage more than two years after announcing its retirement.

The concerts, billed as “California, Dream On: Aerosmith & Friends,” will feature the Black Crowes, Run-DMC, Heart, Billy Idol, Eric Church, Sammy Hagar and other guests. Don Was will serve as music director.

The guest lineup will differ between the two performances. The band said final nightly lineups will be announced at or near showtime and remain subject to change. Additional guests and surprise appearances are planned.

Other announced performers include Guns N’ Roses members Slash and Duff McKagan, Alice in Chains’ Jerry Cantrell and Mike Inez, Red Hot Chili Peppers drummer Chad Smith, Extreme guitarist Nuno Bettencourt and Train singer Pat Monahan.

Tom Morello, Andrew Watt, Marcus King, Steve Stevens, Godsmack singer Sully Erna, Adam Wakeman, Audley Freed and Kenny Aronoff are also scheduled to participate.

The announcement follows the cancellation of Aerosmith’s “Peace Out” farewell tour. Tyler suffered a larynx fracture in September 2023, forcing the band to halt the run after three performances. Aerosmith announced its retirement from touring in August 2024.

Tyler has made occasional live appearances since the injury. At the 2025 MTV Video Music Awards, he joined Aerosmith guitarist Joe Perry, Yungblud and Bettencourt for a tribute to Ozzy Osbourne.

Tickets go on sale to the general public October 10 at 10 a.m. Pacific. A Citi cardmember presale runs from October 8 at 10 a.m. through October 9 at 10 a.m. Pacific. Members of Aerosmith’s Aero Force One fan club will have access to an artist presale October 9 from 10 to 11 a.m. Pacific.
