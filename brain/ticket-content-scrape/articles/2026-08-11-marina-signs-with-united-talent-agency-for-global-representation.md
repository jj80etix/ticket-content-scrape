# MARINA Signs With United Talent Agency For Global Representation

## Summary

Pop artist MARINA has signed with United Talent Agency for global representation across live touring, film/TV, and brand partnerships, as she closes out her sold-out "Princess of Power Tour" this weekend at Flow Festival in Helsinki. The 40-plus-show tour sold over 120,000 tickets worldwide, including a $461,441 gross off 5,933 tickets at Radio City Music Hall, and took in major festivals such as Coachella, Lollapalooza, and Primavera Sound.

### Key Points

- UTA signs MARINA for worldwide representation (touring, film/TV, brand partnerships).
- "Princess of Power Tour" wraps this weekend at Flow Festival, Helsinki, after 40-plus shows.
- Tour sold over 120,000 tickets across South America, North America, Europe, and Oceania.
- Radio City Music Hall show grossed $461,441 off 5,933 tickets per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Tour supported her sixth studio album, PRINCESS OF POWER — her first release as an independent artist.
- Played major festivals including Primavera Sound, Lollapalooza, All Things Go, Corona Capital, Outside Lands, Austin City Limits, Coachella, and Governors Ball.

United Talent Agency has signed global pop star MARINA for worldwide representation in all areas to help the artist secure opportunities in live touring, film and television, brand partnerships and more.

The Welsh Greek singer signs with UTA as she wraps up her sold-out “Princess of Power Tour,” and she wraps up the 40-plus-show run at Flow Festival in Helsinki, Finland, this weekend. The tour has sold over 120,000 tickets across the globe, performing in South America, North America, Europe and Oceania, including stops at iconic venues the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles, London’s Alexandra Palace and Mexico City’s Pepsi Center. One of her most successful outings was a sold-out show at Radio City Music Hall in New York, where MARINA grossed $461,441 off 5,933 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

In addition to the headline concerts, MARINA performed sets at major music festivals, including Primavera Sound, Lollapalooza, All Things Go, Corona Capital, Outside Lands, Austin City Limits, Coachella and Governors Ball.

She toured all across the world to promote her sixth studio album, PRINCESS OF POWER, which was her first release as an independent artist.

MARINA is managed by Janelle Lopez Genzink, Bianca Nour and Merce Jessor of Volara Management.
