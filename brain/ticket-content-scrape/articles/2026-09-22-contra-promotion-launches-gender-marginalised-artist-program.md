# Contra Promotion launches gender-marginalised artist program

## Summary

German promoter Contra launched "Claim Your Stage," a 12-month program supporting early-career female and gender-marginalized (FLINTA*) artists across the EU with tour booking, management, and live-setup support, run in cooperation with Summer Breeze Festival, Deichbrand, and Rock for People.

### Key Points
- Contra launches "Claim Your Stage," a 12-month development program for FLINTA* artists in the EU.
- Program covers tour booking, management, and live production support.
- Run in partnership with Summer Breeze Festival, Deichbrand, and Rock for People.
- Aims to address gender balance across the European live-music sector.
