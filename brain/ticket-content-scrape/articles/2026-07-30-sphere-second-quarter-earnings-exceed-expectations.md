# Sphere Second Quarter Earnings Exceed Expectations

## Summary

Sphere Entertainment beat Wall Street expectations in Q2, posting $313.6 million in revenue against a $307.5 million consensus and a narrower-than-expected per-share loss of $1.07, driven largely by strong "The Wizard of Oz" ticket sales at its Las Vegas venue. The company also reaffirmed plans to expand its Sphere venue format globally, including in Abu Dhabi and National Harbor, while its MSG regional sports networks business continued to decline.

### Key Points
- Q2 revenue of $313.6M beat Zacks consensus of $307.5M; per-share loss of $1.07 beat the expected $1.52 loss, pushing shares up nearly 4% intraday.
- "The Wizard of Oz" ticket sales surpassed $400 million all-time; Sphere Experiences segment revenue rose roughly $54M year-over-year.
- Revenue at the Las Vegas Sphere venue reached $226.4M, up $50.8M year-over-year, despite two fewer brand events during the quarter.
- CEO James Dolan reaffirmed plans for additional Sphere venues in Abu Dhabi and National Harbor as part of a "global network" vision.
- The MSG regional sports networks segment saw revenue fall 18% as subscribers dropped 16.5%.

Once again, the quarterly earnings report for Sphere Entertainment surprised Wall Street, exceeding expectations in revenue and on the bottom line.

The operator of the eponymous Vegas venue and the MSG family of networks posted revenues of $313.6 million in Q2, ahead of the Zacks consensus of $307.5 million. Sphere was also a beat on the bottom line, posting a per-share loss of $1.07; Zacks’ consensus was a loss of $1.52.

The report, released ahead of the opening bell, pushed shares up nearly 4% by midday.

“Today’s results reflect our continued execution in Las Vegas, as we remain on track to deliver substantial growth this calendar year. We are also advancing our long-term vision for a global network of Sphere venues, including in Abu Dhabi and National Harbor,” Executive Chairman and CEO James Dolan said.

During the period, ticket sales for *The Wizard of Oz* surpassed $400 million all time. *Oz* continues to be a major revenue driver; the company said year-over-year, revenues for its Sphere Experiences vertical increased nearly $54 million.

Revenue at Sphere itself was $226.4 million, $50.8 million more than the same quarter last year., booned by the aforementioned rise in Experiences revenue, as “event-related revenues” – which includes both concerts and brand events – were down, a dip attributed to two fewer brand events, as concert revenues increased.

The other half of Sphere Entertainment’s business — the MSG family of regional sports networks — saw revenues decrease by 18% as subscribers dropped 16.5%.
