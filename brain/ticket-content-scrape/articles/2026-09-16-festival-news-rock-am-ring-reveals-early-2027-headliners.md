# Festival news: Rock am Ring reveals early 2027 headliners

## Summary

Germany's Rock am Ring and Rock im Park festivals announced Die Toten Hosen and Bring Me The Horizon as 2027 headliners, joining previously announced Blink-182. Organizers say more than 100,000 tickets have already sold for the June 2027 events, part of a broader roundup of festival lineup news.

### Key Points
- New headliners: Die Toten Hosen and Bring Me The Horizon, joining Blink-182
- Other acts include Faith No More, Billy Talent, Evanescence, The Prodigy, and Turnstile
- Over 100,000 tickets already sold for the June 2027 festivals
- Roundup also covers lineup news from Hurricane Festival, Stagecoach, Phillgood, and Rockin' on Sonic
