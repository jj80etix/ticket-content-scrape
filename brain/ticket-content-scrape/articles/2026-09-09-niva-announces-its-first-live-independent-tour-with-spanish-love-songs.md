# NIVA Announces Its First ‘Live Independent Tour’ With Spanish Love Songs

## Summary

NIVA launched its first "Live Independent Tour" certification, with indie rockers Spanish Love Songs playing a 19-city tour exclusively at independent venues. Developed with Spotify, the program is designed to recognize artists who route most of their dates through indie stages and to strengthen the independent venue/promoter ecosystem.

### Key Points
- Certification developed by NIVA in partnership with Spotify; Spanish Love Songs is the first certified tour.
- 19-city tour runs Nov. 4-29, 2026, exclusively at independent venues; tickets go on sale Sept. 11.
- Builds on NIVA's existing "Live Independent" seal with Bandsintown, which has driven 55M+ fan discoveries of indie-venue shows.
- NIVA frames the program as helping independent venues, promoters and artists "thrive" amid market pressure.

### Highlights
- First-ever "Live Independent Tour" certification, launched this week with a named 19-city tour.
- Backed by two major platform partners (Spotify, Bandsintown) for discovery and promotion reach.
- 55M+ fans already reported reached through the existing Live Independent/Bandsintown seal.

### Industry Problem
Independent venues, promoters and artists continue to face an "unpredictable market" (NIVA's own framing) shaped by industry consolidation and unequal access to booking/marketing leverage compared to major promoter networks — the underlying client pain point this program is trying to counteract.

### Proposed Solution
NIVA's own response is a certification/discovery program (with Spotify and Bandsintown) that channels fan discovery and playlist placement toward artists who commit to independent stages. For Etix, the relevant signal is that independent venues value visibility/marketing support as much as ticketing tech — a case for bundling promotional/discovery tools alongside ticketing for indie-venue clients.

The National Independent Venue Association (NIVA) announced its first-ever “Live Independent Tour” certification featuring indie rockers Spanish Love Songs. The band’s 19-city tour will play exclusively at independent venues and is the first under NIVA’s “Live Independent” certification program.

In partnership with Spotify, NIVA developed the certification program to elevate and safeguard independent venues, promoters and festivals across the country by recognizing artists who route the majority of their dates through indie stages.

“Independent venues continue to overcome the challenges of an unpredictable market,” Stephen Parker, NIVA Executive Director, said in a statement. “The Live Independent Tour is a testament to our mission and artists like Spanish Love Songs. Together, the goal is to build and strengthen the independent live entertainment ecosystem where artists, venues, and independent promoters can thrive while creating meaningful fan experiences. We invite every artist to join this movement and choose to route the majority of their tour dates through independent venues.”

Spanish Love Songs are hitting the road to promote their recently announced fifth album *Future Nausea*, which drops Nov. 6 via Pure Noise Records. The tour kicks off Nov. 4 at Songbyrd in Washington, D.C., and the routing includes stops at Philadelphia’s Ruba Club, Bottlerocket Social Hall in Pittsburgh, Chicago’s Cobra Lounge, Globe Hall in Denver, Seattle’s The Vera Project and Sid The Cat Auditorium in South Pasadena before wrapping up Nov. 29 at San Diego’s Soda Bar.

Tickets go on sale Sept. 11 and can be purchased on the band’s website, [spanishlovesongs.com/pages/tour](https://spanishlovesongs.com/pages/tour).

“Our band was born and grew in independent rooms willing to take a chance on us,” said Dylan Slocum, frontman of Spanish Love Songs. “We’re proud to partner with NIVA, the venues, and promoters to put people over profit and work directly with the bands, crew, and fans that keep independent music thriving. Special nights happen in a 250-capacity room where the AC can’t quite keep up, the drinks are cheap, the PA is blaring, and nothing in the world matters outside of the people in the room. We always want to be right in the middle of that.”

NIVA’s “Live Independent Tour” certification program aims to amplify independent events, highlight artists’ tours through monthly playlist features, and recognize independent venues with the organization’s Live Independent seal, launched in partnership with Bandsintown. More than 55 million fans have discovered concerts and bought tickets at independent venues through Live Independent promotion on Bandsintown, and the partnership with Spotify aims to expand NIVA’s outreach and message of supporting independent stages, companies and artists.

“Independent venues are where some of the most meaningful moments in music happen, for artists and fans alike,” said Rene Volker, Senior Director of Live Events at Spotify. “That’s why we partnered with NIVA, and we’re proud to see the first Live Independent Tour certification come to life with Spanish Love Songs.”
