# Congressional Oversight Ahead of the Midterms: What Companies Should Expect

## Summary

A Troutman Pepper podcast episode discusses how congressional committees plan oversight and investigation targets ahead of the midterms, with staff identifying AI, energy, healthcare, pharma, finance and crypto as likely focus industries, and how congressional inquiries can escalate into state AG or federal regulatory scrutiny.

- Episode features former Senate staffer Jason Smith and Troutman Strategies' Lindsay Austin.
- Likely oversight-target industries named: AI, energy, healthcare, pharma, finance, crypto.
- Discusses how congressional inquiry can lead to downstream state AG or federal agency investigations.

With the midterm elections approaching, congressional staff are already building target lists of industries and companies for the next wave of oversight. In this episode of [*Regulatory Oversight*](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/regulatory-oversight/), host [Stephen Piepgrass](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/stephen-c-piepgrass/) is joined by his colleague [Jason Smith](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/jason-a-smith/), a former Senate staffer with more than 12 years on Capitol Hill, and Troutman Strategies principal [Lindsay Austin](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/lindsay-austin/), to discuss how a shift in congressional leadership and committee dynamics could shape investigations in the months ahead.

Lindsay and Jason explain how committees decide whom to investigate and how oversight differs from investigation. They describe how a congressional inquiry can lead to state attorney general or federal scrutiny down the road and identify the industries most likely to draw attention, including AI, energy, healthcare, pharma, finance, and crypto. The episode concludes with a discussion of the constitutional tension between Article I and Article II, and what it means for companies that acted at the administration’s direction.
