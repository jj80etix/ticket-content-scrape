# Ariana Madix Extends Broadway Run in ‘Just in Time’

## Summary

Ariana Madix extended her run as Connie Francis in Broadway's "Just in Time" through November 28 at Circle in the Square Theatre.

### Key Points
- Madix began performances September 12 under an initially announced 10-week engagement set to end November 22.
- Comes amid other cast changes: Matthew Morrison and Tristen Buettel began as Bobby Darin and Sandra Dee on October 20, through November 8.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Just-in-Time-1.webp)

Ariana Madix has extended her engagement in Broadway’s “Just in Time,” keeping her in the role of Connie Francis through November 28 at Circle in the Square Theatre.

Madix began performances September 12 for what was initially announced as a 10-week engagement. The “Love Island USA” host was previously scheduled to finish November 22.

Notably, the extension comes as “Just in Time” moves through several casting changes. Matthew Morrison and Tristen Buettel began performances as Bobby Darin and Sandra Dee on October 20. Both are scheduled to continue through November 8.

Nicholas Christopher will take over as Darin beginning November 10, while Sadie Dickerson returns as Sandra Dee for a three-week engagement. Whitney Leavitt will then play Sandra Dee from December 1 through January 10, 2027.

Developed and directed by Tony Award winner Alex Timbers, “Just in Time” follows Darin’s rise from teen idol to international entertainer. The production places audiences in a nightclub setting with a live band performing songs including “Beyond the Sea,” “Mack the Knife,” “Splish Splash” and “Dream Lover.”

The musical features a book by Warren Leight and Isaac Oliver, choreography by Shannon Lewis, and music supervision and arrangements by Andrew Resnick.
