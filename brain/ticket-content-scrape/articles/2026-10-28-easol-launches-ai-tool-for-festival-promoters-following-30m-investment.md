# Easol launches AI tool for festival promoters following $30m investment

## Summary

IQ Magazine reports Easol has launched Teammates, an agentic AI tool for festival promoters, following a $30M funding round that brings the company's total raised to $60M, as the company aims to equip "experience creators" to operate as AI-native businesses. (IQ's report is paywalled after the opening paragraphs; see Pollstar's fuller coverage of the same funding and product launch for additional detail.)

### Highlights
- $30M new funding round brings Easol's total raised to $60M.
- Teammates is an agentic AI solution drawing on data across a promoter's operations.
- Positioned as part of Easol's push to let "experience creators" operate as AI-native businesses.

### Industry Problem
Festival/experience-business operators lack built-in AI tooling for operational tasks (support, sales, ops), a gap Easol is targeting with agent-based automation layered directly on its platform.

### Proposed Solution
Same signal as the companion Pollstar item: evaluate agentic-AI features for client-facing operations as a build/improve candidate for Etix, and as a competitive benchmark for Etix Assistant's roadmap.
