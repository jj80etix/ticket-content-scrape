# Rolling Loud India Announces 2026 Lineup Led by Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla and Skepta

## Summary

Rolling Loud India announced its 2026 lineup for the Dec. 12-13 Mumbai festival, headlined by Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla and Skepta, with Burna Boy, Metro Boomin and Skepta making their Indian debuts. The second edition's 30-artist bill blends international headliners with regional Indian talent across Punjabi, Haryanvi, Tamil, Marathi and Gujarati rap.

### Key Points
- Two-day festival, Dec. 12-13, 2026, Mumbai; second edition.
- Day 1 (Dec 12): Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Big Sean (debut).
- Day 2 (Dec 13): Karan Aujla (returning headliner), Skepta, ScHoolboy Q.
- 30 artists total, over 10 of Indian origin across multiple regional styles.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Rolling-Loud.webp)

Rolling Loud India has announced its 2026 lineup, with Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla and Skepta leading the festival’s second edition. The two-day event will take place December 12–13 in Mumbai.

Burna Boy, Metro Boomin and Skepta are scheduled to make their first performances in India. Aujla will return as a headliner for the second consecutive year after appearing at the festival’s inaugural Indian edition in 2025.

Burna Boy and Metro Boomin will headline the opening day on December 12. Big Sean, who is also making his India debut, joins a Saturday bill that includes GoldLink and Haryanvi rapper Dhanda Nyoliwala.
