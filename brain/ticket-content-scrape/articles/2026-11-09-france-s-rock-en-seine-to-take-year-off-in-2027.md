# France’s Rock en Seine to take year off in 2027

## Summary

France's Rock en Seine festival will skip 2027, citing "unprecedented constraints" from infrastructure works near its Domaine National de Saint-Cloud site in Paris that would complicate access and hospitality; organizers (AEG Presents) say they plan to return in 2028, possibly in a restructured form.

### Key Points
- Public-transit and motorway works near the historic Paris estate are the stated cause.
- Organizers hint at a "restructured event" for the 2028 comeback.
- AEG Presents-produced festival.
