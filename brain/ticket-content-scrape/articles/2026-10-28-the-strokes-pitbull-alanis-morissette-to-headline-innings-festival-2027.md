# The Strokes, Pitbull, Alanis Morissette To Headline Innings Festival 2027

## Summary

The Strokes, Pitbull and Alanis Morissette will headline Innings Festival 2027, running Feb. 26–28 at Tempe Beach Park & Arts Park in Arizona, alongside a lineup including Pierce the Veil, The Neighbourhood, 5 Seconds of Summer and Spoon. The baseball-themed festival combines music with MLB legend appearances and tiered ticketing from GA through Platinum. Presale begins Oct. 29 via SMS signup, with public onsale to follow.

### Key Points
- Three-day festival (Feb. 26–28, 2027) at Tempe Beach Park & Arts Park, Arizona, with no overlapping sets.
- Headliners: The Strokes, Pitbull, Alanis Morissette; also Pierce the Veil, The Neighbourhood, 5SOS, Train, Death Cab for Cutie, Interpol and more.
- Six ticket tiers (GA, GA+, Center Field Bleachers, Left Field Reserved, VIP, Platinum) plus 1-, 2- and 3-day options.
- SMS-list presale begins Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. PT with a one-hour guaranteed-lowest-price GA window.
- MLB legends (Darryl Strawberry, Nelson Cruz, others) and ESPN's Jessica Mendoza join baseball-themed programming alongside the music.

The Strokes, Pitbull and Alanis Morissette are set to headline Innings Festival 2027, set to take place Feb. 26-28 at Arizona’s Tempe Beach Park & Arts Park. Other artists set to take the stage include artists like Pierce The Veil, The Neighbourhood, 5 Seconds of Summer, Train, Death Cab for Cutie, Interpol, Yellowcard, Hot Mulligan, 4 Non Blondes, Spoon, Shaggy, Passion Pit and more for three days of no overlapping sets.

For more information on all available ticket types and the amenities included in each, visit [InningsFestival.com/Tickets](https://www.inningsfestival.com/Tickets). Fans can sign up now for the festival SMS list at InningsFestival.com to receive an access code for the presale beginning Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. PT, with access to guaranteed lowest-price GA tickets for one hour. Any remaining tickets will go on sale to the public following the presale.

Fans can also enjoy interactive baseball activities at the festival, plus appearances by Major League Baseball legends Darryl Strawberry, Dexter Fowler, David Justice, Nelson Cruz, Rollie Fingers, Joe Kelly and more, as well as Olympic medalist and ESPN analyst Jessica Mendoza. All-Star Jam, hosted by Jake Peavy, returns, along with Off the Mound, with Ryan Dempster back for live interviews and special performances from surprise guests, including artists from the festival lineup.

1-Day and 3-Day GA, GA+, Center Field Bleachers, Left Field Reserved, VIP and Platinum tickets will be available, along with 2-Day GA tickets. GA+ tickets include access to the GA+ Lounge, with a private bar (drinks for purchase), air-conditioned restrooms, relaxed seating and more. Center Field Bleachers offer all GA+ amenities, plus guaranteed bucket seats with prime views of Home Plate Stage. Left Field Reserved tickets also include all GA+ amenities, plus a reserved bucket seating at the dedicated elevated viewing deck overlooking Home Plate Stage. VIP tickets include a dedicated seated viewing platform at Home Plate Stage, plus on-field viewing areas at both stages, unlimited access to the VIP Lounge with air-conditioned restrooms, complimentary beer, dedicated Festival Merch Store and more. Platinum tickets offer exclusive front-of-stage viewing, complimentary full-service bar, coffee service and all-day dining, golf cart transportation between stages, complimentary Tempe Town Lake boat rides on the Platinum Clubhouse Cruiser and more.
