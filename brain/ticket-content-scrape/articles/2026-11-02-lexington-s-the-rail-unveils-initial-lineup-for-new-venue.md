# Lexington’s The Rail Unveils Initial Lineup for New Venue

## Summary

TicketNews' version of The Rail's debut lineup announcement: the new 2,200-capacity Lexington, Kentucky venue (opening early 2027, operated by Peachtree Entertainment) revealed its first 10 shows, led by Lynyrd Skynyrd, Cole Swindell, Umphrey's McGee, and Billy Currington.

### Key Points
- Schedule runs January 23 through June 19, 2027; tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. ET.
- Venue sits within the broader Commons Entertainment District development alongside restaurants and other entertainment.
- CEO Nathan Baugh frames the venue as a driver of district-wide activity, not just a concert hall.

## Transcript

![Rendering of The Rail in Lexington, Kentucky, with expected grand opening in early 2027](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Rail-in-Lexington-Kentucky.webp)

Rendering of The Rail in Lexington, Kentucky, with expected grand opening in early 2027

The Rail, a new 2,200-capacity venue opening in Lexington, Kentucky, in early 2027, has announced its first 10 concerts, with Lynyrd Skynyrd, Cole Swindell, Umphrey’s McGee and Billy Currington among the acts scheduled to perform.

Lynyrd Skynyrd will perform on January 23, marking the earliest date on the announced calendar. Umphrey’s McGee follows on February 5, with Niko Moon scheduled for February 6.

Cole Swindell will take the stage on March 6, followed by Gregory Alan Isakov on March 12. April bookings include Dexter and The Moonrocks on April 9 and BigXthaPlug on April 11.

The Dead South will perform with Amigo the Devil on April 30. BUNT. is scheduled for May 18, while Billy Currington’s June 19 concert rounds out the first wave of announcements.
