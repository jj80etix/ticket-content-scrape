# City Winery Expands To Canada With Toronto Location

## Summary

City Winery confirmed its first Canadian location, opening in Toronto's ROQ City development in early 2028. The venue will pair an intimate live-music listening room (jazz, singer-songwriter, soul, roots, rock, comedy) with a chef-driven restaurant/bar and private event spaces, extending founder Michael Dorf's chain beyond its existing 15 U.S. locations.

### Key Points
- City Winery's first Canadian venue announced for Toronto's ROQ City development, opening early 2028.
- Programming will span jazz, singer-songwriter, soul, roots, rock, and comedy, booked jointly with local talent.
- Venue pairs seated dinner-and-a-show concerts with a chef-driven restaurant and private event spaces.
- Expansion follows CEO Michael Dorf's earlier hints at new locations; company already operates 15 venues across eight U.S. markets.

## Transcript

![unnamed (35)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-35.jpg)

Rendering of City Winery Toronto (courtesy of The Press House)

A few months ago City Winery founder and CEO Michael Dorf hinted at new locations to come, while being interviewed for his [VenuesNow All-Stars profile](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/21/venuesnow-2026-all-star-honoree-michael-dorf/), and now the company’s international expansion is official with details announced for City Winery’s first Canadian location in Toronto.  
  
City Winery Toronto is expected to open in early 2028 and will be located at ROQ City, described as “a music-inspired purpose-built rental development in the St. Lawrence neighbourhood.” The venue will include “an intimate live music listening room with seated, dinner-and-a-show concerts across jazz, singer-songwriter, soul, roots, rock and comedy, programmed by City Winery’s booking team together with Toronto’s own music community,” as well as a full-service restaurant and bar with a seasonal, chef-driven menu designed to pair with wine. Plus, there will be private event spaces available for occasions from corporate gatherings to weddings.

This week’s announcement notes that programming, hiring and private event bookings will be announced in the coming months, but music fans can expect to be wowed by City Winery’s “signature combination of intimate live music, wine and chef-driven dining.”  
  
Dorf founded City Winery in New York in 2008 and it has expanded to 15 venue and restaurant concepts in Chicago, Nashville, Atlanta, Boston, Philadelphia, St. Louis, Pittsburgh and New York’s Hudson Valley.

“It took almost six years of looking for the perfect spot, and I think we found it,” Dorf said in a statement. “We love Toronto. It’s a great music city that has been starved for a sit-down, intimate listening room, the kind of place where the audience is really there for the artist. ROQ City was built around music from the ground up, which makes it the right home for us. And we’re here to be part of Toronto, not just to open in it. That means putting Canadian wine, Canadian producers, and Canadian artists at the centre of what we do.”

ROQ City, located at 261 Queen Street East, features “859 rental suites, 70,000 square feet of commercial space, a 15,000-square-foot public park, and year-round community programming, with completion anticipated in 2027.”

“When we began developing ROQ City, we were inspired by the neighbourhood’s history and longstanding connection to Toronto’s music and creative scene,” said Andrew Joyner, Senior Managing Director and Head of Multi-Family at Tricon Living. “We wanted to create a community that reflects and builds on that culture and energy. City Winery is a beloved destination for live entertainment, hospitality, and events, which makes them a perfect fit for ROQ City and a central part of our vision for the next chapter of the neighbourhood.”

Though we’ll have to wait to check our City Winery Toronto’s programming, this week’s announcement adds that the venue will highlight “Canadian talent and flavours,” from booking celebrated and emerging Canadian musicians and comedians, alongside international performers, and serving a curated drink menu featuring Ontario and Canadian wineries.

“Toronto has plenty of great places to see music and this room is going to be a welcome addition to that list. It’s seated and intimate, and people come for the songs. We just finished a four-night run at City Winery in New York, and it reminded us why we keep coming back. They care about the artist and about the listener. City Winery has been good to us for a long time, and I think Toronto audiences are going to take to it quickly,” said Michael Timmins, primary songwriter, guitarist, and a founding member of Cowboy Junkies.
