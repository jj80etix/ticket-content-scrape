# How Experience Studios is designing the next generation of stadiums for a music-led future

## Summary

Experience Studios, the live-entertainment design division of architecture firm Populous, discusses how a touring boom and the widening gap between a stadium's ~50-year lifespan and rapidly-evolving production technology is forcing venue designers to build production/tech infrastructure into stadiums from the ground up. The piece (paywalled after the intro) previews commentary from Populous principals Mark Murphy and Thomas Hulland on venues the firm has designed.

### Key Points
- Core tension: stadiums have ~50-year shelf lives while touring production tech evolves in months.
- Experience Studios' response is to design production-technology capacity into stadiums/arenas at the foundation stage rather than retrofitting later.
- Speakers: Populous/Experience Studios principals Mark Murphy and Thomas Hulland.
- Full venue examples and technical detail are behind IQ's paywall.
