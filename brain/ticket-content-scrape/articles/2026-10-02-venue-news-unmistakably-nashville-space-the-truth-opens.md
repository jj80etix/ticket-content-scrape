# Venue news: ‘Unmistakably Nashville’ space The Truth opens

## Summary

IQ's venue-news roundup covers five mid-sized venue openings in the US and UK this week: Live Nation's 4,400-capacity The Truth in Nashville and 4,000-capacity Citizens Live at The Wylie in Pittsburgh (both newly opened), a renovated and expanded 2,500-capacity TESTBED reopening in Leeds under AMAAD, AEG's upcoming 1,500-capacity Moonline in Austin (early 2027), and Peachtree Entertainment's forthcoming The Rail in Lexington, Kentucky.

### Key Points
- The Truth (Nashville, 4,400 cap., Live Nation) opened with a sold-out Miranda Lambert show.
- Citizens Live at The Wylie (Pittsburgh, 4,000 cap., Live Nation) is opening with acts including Wiz Khalifa and Modest Mouse.
- TESTBED (Leeds, 2,500 cap., AMAAD) reopens after expanding from 10,000 to 23,000 sq ft.
- AEG's Moonline (Austin, 1,500 cap.) and Peachtree's The Rail (Lexington, 2,200 cap.) are both slated for early 2027.

## Venue news: ‘Unmistakably Nashville’ space The Truth opens

Plus new launches in Pittsburgh and Leeds, along with mid-sized venues on the way in Austin, Texas and Lexington, Kentucky

![The Truth in Nashville](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Truth-in-Nashville.png)

The Truth in Nashville

A swathe of mid-sized venues is popping up in the US, with doors opening in Nashville and Pittsburgh this week, along with new spaces in Austin, Texas and Lexington, Kentucky on the way, in addition to a grand opening in Leeds in the UK.

**The Truth –** Nashville, Tennessee, USA  
*Capacity: 4,400*  
*Operator: Live Nation*

Miranda Lambert opened Nashville’s newest venue with a sold-out show last night (1 October), kicking off a busy first season for the 4,400-capacity space that also includes Jack White, The Chicks, The Smashing Pumpkins, Malcolm Todd, Role Model, GloRilla, Limp Bizkit, and many more.

Designed by Blueprint Studio, Live Nation’s in-house development group, The Truth boasts three tiers that offer an intimate feel with fans no further than 150 feet from the stage. The space also features a lyric-projecting chandelier, nightly setlists printed on an antique letterpress, a two-story Vinyl Room, a whisky lounge, and local cuisine-inspired menus.

“Our aspiration for The Truth is simple: to create a venue worthy of the city that inspired it,” says Sally Williams, Live Nation’s president of Nashville music & business strategy. “We want fans to feel connected to the music and artists to feel genuinely welcome and cared for. We want The Truth to feel unmistakably Nashville – rooted in its extraordinary musical legacy and reflective of the creative community shaping its future.”

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/Citizens-Live-at-The-Wylie-430x269.png.webp)

**Citizens Live at The Wylie** – Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, USA  
*Capacity: 4,000*  
*Operator: Live Nation*

Inspired by the jazz-forward musical legacy of Pittsburgh’s Wylie Avenue and the Lower Hill District, the city’s newest venue honours its past while creating a new stage for artists and fans.

Citizens Live at The Wylie’s opening is underway, with Role Model, Wiz Khalifa, Styx, Foster the People, Modest Mouse, and Boyz II Men all coming through in its first season.

Also designed by Blueprint Studio, the venue features a terrace with city views and a hi-fi listening Vinyl Room, which offers attendees a premium live space to enjoy before, during, and after the show.

It is located in close proximity to PPG Paints Arena and is walkable to downtown restaurants, hotels, and the local arts district, placing it in the heart of Pittsburgh’s cultural offerings.

“We’re proud of what Citizens Live at The Wylie will add to Pittsburgh’s music scene,” says Tom Loudermilk, Live Nation’s Pittsburgh region market president. “The stage will feature artists across genres and at every stage of their career, bringing national tours to the Lower Hill while giving Pittsburgh musicians real opportunities to take the stage in their own city.”

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/TESTBED-in-Leeds-430x269.png.webp)

**TESTBED** – Leeds, UK  
*Capacity: 2,500*  
*Operator: AMAAD  
*

After undergoing a major structural renovation, Leeds’ electronic-forward TESTBED venue will reopen tomorrow (3 Oct) with a launch party that includes Annie Mac, Horse Meat Disco, and Shee.

With the revamp, the venue expanded its footprint from 10,000 sq ft to 23,000 sq ft, with customer-facing upgrades including a new lobby, a 5,000 sq ft mezzanine level with customisable seating, balcony space, and an expanded F&B offering.

Plus, the space now has a custom L-Acoustics L2 system, a fully integrated Chauvet lighting system, and a large LED ceiling installation and video wall.

TESTBED’s founders Laura Wellington and James Abbott-Donnelly remain on board as part of the mission to create an accessible platform for events. Promoters AMAAD partnered on the project to provide investment and expertise, and plans to bring world-class talent to the city.

With its reopening, it will join venues like Brighton’s Quarters and Bristol’s The Prospect Building in AMAAD’s venue portfolio.

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/Moonline-in-Austin-430x269.png.webp)

**Moonline** – Austin, Texas, USA  
*Capacity: 1,500*  
*Operator: AEG Presents  
Opening: Early 2027  
*

AEG Presents has given details of its second live music venue in Austin, to be named Moonline. Following a series of upgrades and renovations of the [former Emo’s building](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/aeg-presents-doubles-down-on-austin-with-new-venue/), including a new sound system and revamped front- and back-of-house spaces, Moonline is set to open in early 2027.

The new space will complement the operator’s bigger building, the 4,000-capacity United Heritage Hall, which is currently under construction one mile down the road from Moonline. It is expected to open in Q2 2027.

Taken together, the pair of new venues will help bolster both AEG’s presence in the market and aid Austin’s talent pipeline by supporting a range of routing and programming options.

“Austin has a long history of artists making music on their own terms,” says Robin Phillips, AEG Presents’ VP Southwest Region. “It’s a city shaped by people who built their own scenes, found their own rooms, challenged what came before them, and made something distinctly Austin long before the rest of the world caught on.”

Additional details, including inaugural performances, ticket information, and venue programming, are to be announced.

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/The-Rail-in-Lexington-430x269.png.webp)

**The Rail** – Lexington, Kentucky, USA  
*Capacity: 2,200*  
*Operator: Peachtree Entertainment  
Opening: Early 2027*

Kentucky will soon be home to a 43-acre mixed-use entertainment district, The Commons, which will be anchored by a state-of-the-art music venue, The Rail.

With a capacity of 2,200 guests and more than 1,000 seats for theatre-style events, The Rail combines scale, flexibility, and contemporary production needs. Behind the scenes, the space also has three dedicated dressing rooms and an artist lounge.

For fans, the Rail features six full-service bars, two outdoor decks, premium VIP spaces, and a flexible floor plan, making it modular for concerts, club nights, corporate functions, special events, and private rentals.

“We are excited and honoured to be involved in this revitalisation of Lexington,” says Dustin Labbe, SVP of booking for Peachtree Entertainment. “Every seat, every sightline, every bar, and every common area has been intentionally designed so fans feel close to the performance.

“Whether you’re on the floor, in the mezzanine, or in a VIP space, the experience stays immersive. The Rail, located within a day’s drive of other major midwest markets, is an ideal routing stop for national tours. That means more opportunities to bring artists, and the economic impact that comes with them, to Central Kentucky.”

The first wave of shows will be announced later this year, with the venue to open in early 2027.

In other Kentucky news, the governor announced plans this week to open an [Office of Music](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/kentucky-to-create-new-office-of-music-for-states-live-sector/), which will introduce new tax rebates for venues and festivals, as well as establish a statewide live industry directory for sector professionals.
