# Kentucky to create new Office of Music for state’s live sector

## Summary

Kentucky Governor Andy Beshear has announced a new state Office of Music that will introduce venue/festival tax rebates, a state-wide live-industry professional directory, and a Music Ambassadors program. (Same announcement as covered by Pollstar; this IQ Magazine version adds detail on the directory and rebate mechanics.)

### Key Points
- New tax rebates for venues and festivals.
- State-wide live-industry directory: registrants (festivals, venues, promoters, artists) verified before access.
- Music Ambassadors program to be introduced.
- Announced at the Bourbon & Beyond festival in Louisville.
