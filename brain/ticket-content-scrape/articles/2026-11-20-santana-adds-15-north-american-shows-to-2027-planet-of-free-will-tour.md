# Santana Adds 15 North American Shows to 2027 Planet of Free Will Tour

## Summary

Santana added 15 North American shows to the 2027 "Planet of Free Will" global tour, running February through April across the US and Canada. The new dates complement an already-announced European leg and an ongoing Las Vegas residency.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Feb. 23 at Yaamava' Theater (Highland, CA) and closes April 10 in Atlantic City.
- Includes a three-night Fillmore San Francisco run and stops at Radio City Music Hall and Caesars Windsor.
- General on-sale began Nov. 20 following a Nov. 18 fan presale.
- Santana also has 16 Las Vegas residency dates at House of Blues/Mandalay Bay in Jan. and May 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Santana-Planet-of-Free-Will-Global-Tour.webp)

Santana has announced 15 North American shows for the *Planet of Free Will Global Tour 2027*, expanding the band’s upcoming international schedule with performances across the United States and Canada from February through April.

The newly announced run begins February 23 at Yaamava’ Theater in Highland, California, followed by a three-night engagement at The Fillmore in San Francisco on February 25, 27 and 28. The shows mark a return to the city where Carlos Santana established his musical career and where the band first gained recognition.

Following the California performances, the tour resumes March 26 at The Hall at Live! in Hanover, Maryland, before moving through Pennsylvania and Ontario. The Canadian dates include March 31 at The Colosseum at Caesars Windsor and April 1 at OLG Stage at Fallsview Casino in Niagara Falls.

The April schedule includes performances at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston on April 3, Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut, on April 4, and New York City’s Radio City Music Hall on April 6. Additional shows are scheduled for Lancaster, Pennsylvania, and Verona, New York, before the North American leg concludes April 10 at Hard Rock Live at Etess Arena in Atlantic City, New Jersey.

The new dates extend Santana’s touring plans ahead of the previously announced European leg of the *Planet of Free Will Global Tour*. That portion begins June 30 at London’s O2 Arena, with additional performances scheduled across France, Spain, Switzerland, Germany, Italy, Austria, the Czech Republic, Poland, Norway and Finland.

As [TicketNews previously detailed](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/santana-2027-european-tour-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the European itinerary features a combination of arena concerts, outdoor performances and festival appearances, with the final scheduled show taking place July 31 at the Kaivopuisto Blues Festival in Helsinki.

In addition to the newly announced tour dates, Santana has an extensive Las Vegas residency schedule planned for 2027. The guitarist will perform eight shows in January and another eight in May at the House of Blues inside Mandalay Bay Resort and Casino. The performances are part of the ongoing *An Intimate Evening with Santana: Greatest Hits Live* residency series.

Tickets for the 15 newly announced North American performances went on sale to the general public Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time, following a fan presale that began November 18.
