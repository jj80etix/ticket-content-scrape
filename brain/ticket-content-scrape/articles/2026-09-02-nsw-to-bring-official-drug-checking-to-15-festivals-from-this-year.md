# NSW to bring official drug checking to 15 festivals from this year

## Summary

New South Wales is investing AUS$4.3M to bring official on-site drug-checking services to up to 15 music festivals starting October 2026, following a year-long trial the government deemed a "successful" harm-reduction strategy; user identities won't be shared with law enforcement.

### Key Points
- NSW government commits AUS$4.3M to official drug-checking (pill testing) services at up to 15 festivals, launching October 2026.
- Follows a year-long trial the government describes as a successful harm-reduction strategy.
- Illicit drugs remain illegal, but participants' identities will not be shared with law enforcement under the scheme.
