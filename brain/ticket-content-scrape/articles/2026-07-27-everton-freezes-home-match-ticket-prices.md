# Everton freezes home match ticket prices

## Summary

Everton has frozen home Premier League ticket prices at Hill Dickinson Stadium for the 2026/27 season (£58-£75) and will trial a new 18-21 pricing tariff bridging junior and adult rates, following supporter-group engagement. The purchase process and Forever Blue+ ballot-priority system remain unchanged.

### Key Points
- Prices held flat year-over-year; new 18-21 tariff added after fan consultation, aimed at young adults transitioning out of junior pricing.
- Previous age tiers were adult, over-65s, under-18s, under-11s, and a 14-21 youth band.
- Forever Blue+ Members retain first-priority ballot access before remaining tickets open to Forever Blue Members.
- First league match of the frozen-price season is August 22 vs. Crystal Palace.

### Highlights
- Rare pricing freeze (not increase) directly attributed to supporter engagement rather than market/demand pressure.
- New age-tier pricing (18-21) targets a specific affordability gap identified through fan consultation.

### Industry Problem
Patron affordability pressure — particularly for young-adult fans aging out of junior pricing — is a recurring pain point clubs are starting to address directly through tiered pricing rather than blanket discounts.

### Proposed Solution
No new technology described; this is a pricing-policy response. Worth monitoring as a comparable for how primary platforms' clients might request more granular age-based pricing tiers as a retention/affordability lever.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Everton has frozen home Premier League match ticket prices for the 2026/27 season and is also set to introduce a new age category.**

The most expensive tickets for games at Hill Dickinson Stadium are currently priced at £75 ($95/€84) while the cheapest are £58, not including promotional offers.

Previously, there were adult, over-65s, under-18s, under-11s, and youth tickets for those aged between 14 and 21 available.

That is set to change in the upcoming season with a new 18-21 tariff which is due to be trialled following discussions with fans.

“Everton can confirm that home Premier League match ticket prices will be frozen for the 2026/27 season and that a new 18–21 tariff will be introduced and trialled, following a review of last season’s approach and continued engagement with supporter groups,” read a club statement.

“Supporters purchasing match tickets at Hill Dickinson Stadium will continue to pay the same prices as last season, while the new 18-21 tariff is designed to bridge the gap between junior and adult pricing and support young adults as they move into further education, apprenticeships or their first jobs.

“The process for purchasing home match tickets will remain unchanged from last season, with Forever Blue+ Members receiving first priority through a ballot before any remaining tickets are made available to Forever Blue Members.”

Everton’s first league match of the season is set to take place on August 22 at home against Crystal Palace.
