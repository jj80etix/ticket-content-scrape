# Canada’s TD Coliseum celebrates ‘tremendous’ first year post-$300m renovation

## Summary

Hamilton, Ontario's TD Coliseum — Oak View Group's first Canadian arena project, following a $300m renovation — marked a strong first year, hosting 850,000+ fans across 110 events. The article is paywalled beyond the lede.

### Key Points
- TD Coliseum hosted 110 events and 850,000+ attendees in its first post-renovation year.
- It's the first arena project in Canada for operator Oak View Group.
- A $300m renovation preceded the reopening.
