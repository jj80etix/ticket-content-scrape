# The Flaming Lips Announce 10 Shows Across U.S. and London for 2027

## Summary

The Flaming Lips announced 10 shows for early 2027 across Nashville, New York, Los Angeles, Chicago and London, timed to the January 22 release of their 17th studio album, The Teardrop Throbs. It's the band's first album of entirely new material in over six years.

### Key Points
- Two-night stands in each city: Nashville (Jan 22-23), NYC (Jan 26-27), LA (Feb 23-24), Chicago (Feb 27-28), London (Apr 2-3).
- Opening Nashville date coincides with the new album's release day.
- Album released via Third Man Records; additional dates still to be announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Flaming-Lips-.webp)

The Flaming Lips have announced 10 concerts for early 2027, scheduling two-night engagements in Nashville, New York, Los Angeles, Chicago and London.

Nashville’s Brooklyn Bowl will host the opening performances January 22 and 23. The band will then head to New York for concerts at Irving Plaza January 26 and 27, completing the first four dates of the newly announced schedule.

The next pair of shows will take place at The Bellwether in Los Angeles February 23 and 24. Chicago follows with performances at Metro February 27 and 28, before the band heads overseas for London’s O2 Shepherd’s Bush April 2 and 3. Additional dates will be announced, according to Third Man Records.

The concerts accompany *The Teardrop Throbs*, arriving January 22, 2027, through Third Man Records, with the first Nashville show falling on release day. The record is the group’s 17th studio album and its first collection of entirely new material in more than six years. The newly released single “God Will Control the Werewolves” introduces the project, which will be available digitally, on CD and on vinyl.
