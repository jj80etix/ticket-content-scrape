# West Ham set all-time EFL Cup first-round attendance record

## Summary

West Ham United set the all-time EFL Cup first-round attendance record with 53,369 fans at London Stadium in a 3-1 win over Portsmouth, beating the previous 2023 Leeds record by over 18,000.

### Key Points
- Attendance was boosted by roughly 9,000 traveling Portsmouth supporters.
- The figure beat the prior record, set by Leeds United in August 2023, by more than 18,000.
- West Ham will learn its second-round opponent from among the 11 Premier League clubs not in Europe this season.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**West Ham United has set the all-time EFL Cup first-round attendance record with 53,369 fans attending the 3-1 win over Portsmouth at London Stadium last Saturday (August 8).**

The figure beat the previous record, set by Leeds United in August 2023, by over 18,000.

“The Claret and Blue Army demonstrated your incredible loyalty yet again by turning out in your thousands for our first competitive game of the 2026/27 season – and were rewarded with a welcome win,” read a club statement.

“The attendance was boosted by nearly 9,000 Portsmouth supporters and the noise generated by both sets of fans made for a lively atmosphere on a warm and sunny afternoon in east London.

“The club would like to thank every fan who attended Saturday’s record-breaking match and look forward to seeing you, home and away, as we target a successful campaign.”

West Ham will find out its next EFL Cup opponent today when the second round draw is made during the build-up to the Plymouth Argyle vs Exeter City match at 7:20pm.

Round two sees the introduction of the 11 Premier League clubs not participating in European competitions.
