# Pitbull Announces 2027 Australia, New Zealand Arena Tour With Five Shows

## Summary

Pitbull has announced a five-date arena tour of Australia and New Zealand for 2027. The announcement covers tour dates and venues.

### Key Points
- Pitbull adds five Australia/New Zealand arena dates for 2027.
- Standard tour announcement with no ticketing-industry angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Pitbull-Im-Back-Tour.webp)

Pitbull is bringing his “I’m Back” Tour to Australia and New Zealand in September 2027, announcing five arena performances across the two countries.

Presented by MG Live and Frontier Touring, the tour will kick off September 11 at RAC Arena in Perth before making stops in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. Pitbull will then head to New Zealand for the final performance of the run at Spark Arena in Auckland on September 24.

The upcoming dates mark Pitbull’s return to Australia following his appearance as a co-headliner on the sold-out Fridayz Live tour in October 2025, which drew more than 150,000 fans across four dates.

The announcement follows a successful year of touring for the Grammy Award-winning rapper, who [announced the North American leg of his “I’m Back” Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/pitbull-announces-im-back-tour-with-special-guest-lil-jon/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) with special guest Lil Jon in January. The run included performances at amphitheaters and stadiums across the United States and Canada, beginning in May in West Palm Beach, Florida..
