# Baltimore’s Camden Yards Scores Renovation

## Summary

Baltimore's Camden Yards will remove roughly 2,000 left-field upper-deck seats to build a new social space ahead of the 2027 season, adding standing-room viewing, drink rails, a central bar, and expanded concessions. The space will be open to all ticketed fans on game days and available for private rental on non-game days.

### Key Points
- Construction begins immediately, targeting completion by Opening Day 2027.
- About 2,000 seats removed to create the new social/gathering space.
- Area will double as a rentable private-event venue on non-game days.

## Transcript

![Current image: BaltimoreCamdenYards](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BaltimoreCamdenYards-1024x576.jpg)

Camden Yards rendering courtesy of Baltimore Orioles

The left-field upper deck at Baltimore’s Camden Yards will be undergoing significant renovations ahead of the 2027 season.

The Orioles announced that their home ballpark will have approximately 2,000 seats removed to build a new social space in the upper deck in left field. The area will include drink rails, standing room viewing areas, gathering zones with tables and chairs, a central bar, a variety of concession offerings and televisions.

The construction will begin immediately, with the space scheduled to be ready in time for Opening Day 2027. It will be open to all ticketed fans, and the area will also be rented out for private events on non-game days. “We’re extremely excited to begin these renovations and bring this space to life at Oriole Park,” said Catie Griggs, Orioles President of Business Operations in a statement. “We’ve listened to our fans, who have expressed a desire for more social spaces that offer a vibrant atmosphere to gather, connect and cheer on the O’s together. We’re confident this will quickly become a popular destination inside our gates next season.”
