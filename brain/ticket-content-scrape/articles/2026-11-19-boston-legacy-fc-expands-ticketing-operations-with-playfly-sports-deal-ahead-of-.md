# Boston Legacy FC Expands Ticketing Operations With Playfly Sports Deal Ahead of Stadium Move

## Summary

NWSL club Boston Legacy FC signed a multiyear deal with Playfly Sports to run ticket sales, premium seating and revenue operations as the team transitions from Centreville Bank Stadium in Pawtucket, RI to a renovated White Stadium in Boston (opening 2028). Playfly's scope includes fan surveys, price benchmarking and seating-plan modeling to manage the ticket-holder transition between venues.

### Key Points
- Multiyear partnership covers ticket inventory, premium experiences, season-ticket memberships and research/consulting (fan surveys, price benchmarking, seating modeling).
- Near-term focus: sales at Centreville Bank Stadium (Pawtucket, RI); longer-term focus shifts to pricing/seating strategy for White Stadium (Boston, opening 2028).
- Playfly team: Jack Luce (VP strategy/special projects) leads research/consulting; Justin Morelli (group VP) oversees the partnership; Chad Cardinal (SVP, ticketing/premium/hospitality) is Playfly's lead on the relationship.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Boston-Legacy-Playfly-Sports.webp)

Boston Legacy FC has entered into a multiyear partnership with Playfly Sports to expand its ticket sales, premium seating and revenue operations as the National Women’s Soccer League (NWSL) club prepares to move into a renovated White Stadium in 2028.

The agreement will see Playfly work alongside the club’s existing staff to develop and execute a customized sales and service strategy covering ticket inventory, premium experiences and season ticket memberships.

Playfly’s work will initially focus on sales at Centreville Bank Stadium in Pawtucket, Rhode Island, before shifting toward the development of pricing strategies, ticket packages and seating arrangements for White Stadium, the club’s planned permanent home in Boston.

The partnership also includes extensive research and consulting services, including fan surveys, premium seating concepts, price benchmarking and customized modeling to help the club manage the transition of its existing ticket holders into the new venue.

“The voice of the fan will be at the heart of this work, with meaningful research, data, and analytics informing the stadium’s pricing, packaging, and overall experience offerings,” Cardinal said.

“This is a unique opportunity to pair long-term strategy with day-to-day execution, and we’re eager to help Boston Legacy FC deepen its relationships with supporters and bring its ambitious vision to life.”

The Playfly agreement represents another step in Boston Legacy’s effort to establish its ticketing infrastructure following the club’s inaugural NWSL season in 2026.

In October, the club [named Jump its exclusive fan experience and ticketing technology partner](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/boston-legacy-fc-names-jump-exclusive-ticketing-partner-ahead-of-2027-season/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) beginning with the 2027 season. That agreement includes a team-branded mobile platform designed to bring ticket purchases, merchandise, concessions and customer engagement into a unified system.

The two partnerships address different components of Boston Legacy’s commercial operations. Jump will supply the technology connecting fans with the club’s ticketing and matchday services, while Playfly will provide sales execution, revenue strategy and research intended to inform pricing and premium inventory decisions.

Together, the agreements establish a structure for developing ticket sales and fan relationships ahead of the club’s anticipated move into White Stadium. The precise division of operational responsibilities between the companies, including oversight of pricing and inventory decisions, has not been publicly detailed.

The stadium transition is already influencing Boston Legacy’s ticketing strategy. On November 16, the club [launched two-year season ticket memberships](https://bostonlegacyfc.com/blogs/press/boston-legacy-fc-launches-two-year-season-ticket-memberships?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) covering the 2027 and 2028 seasons, allowing supporters to secure seats at Centreville Bank Stadium next year while receiving priority access to select seats at White Stadium in 2028.

The memberships include a price lock across both seasons, savings compared with single-match purchases, merchandise discounts and access to exclusive club events. Monthly payment plans are also available.

The arrangement creates an immediate application for Playfly’s planned research and seating migration work, with the club seeking to retain existing supporters while introducing new seating configurations and premium offerings at White Stadium.

Fans can purchase memberships through the [club’s official season ticket website](https://bostonlegacyfc.com/pages/season-ticket-memberships?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
