# AEG Reveals First Moonline Shows With Bad Suns, Chelsea Cutler, Paul Wall

## Summary

AEG Presents has revealed the first 11 concerts for Moonline, its renamed and renovated 1,500-capacity Austin venue (formerly Emo's Austin), kicking off its 2027 season in February with acts including Bad Suns, Chelsea Cutler, and Paul Wall. The venue is reopening in early 2027 after a rebrand announced in September.

### Key Points
- Moonline occupies the former Emo's Austin site, retaining its 1,500-person capacity.
- Inaugural calendar runs Feb. 13-May 26, 2027, spanning rock, pop, hip-hop, and hardcore.
- AEG describes the booking strategy as deliberately genre-diverse.

AEG Presents has announced the first 11 concerts scheduled for Moonline, its new 1,500-capacity live music venue in Austin, Texas, with Bad Suns, Chelsea Cutler, Saves the Day, Paul Wall and Postmodern Jukebox among the artists booked for the venue’s inaugural 2027 season.

The first announced performance is set for February 13, when Talking Heads tribute act HeartByrne takes the stage. The initial calendar extends through May 26 and includes a mix of alternative rock, pop, progressive metal, hardcore, hip-hop and indie music.

Moonline will occupy the former Emo’s Austin location at 2015 East Riverside Drive, which AEG Presents is taking over and renovating ahead of its early 2027 reopening.

The promoter [introduced the Moonline name in September](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/aeg-rebrands-austins-emos-as-moonline-ahead-of-2027-reopening/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), outlining plans to upgrade the existing venue while retaining its 1,500-person capacity. At the time, AEG had not announced any performances or specific opening dates.

The newly revealed lineup provides the first look at the venue’s programming strategy, combining emerging and established touring artists across several genres.

“This first run of shows is exactly what we wanted Moonline to be: a room where a hardcore show, a pop show and a Houston rap show all feel at home in the same month,” said Robin Phillips, vice president of AEG Presents’ Southwest Region. “Austin has always made room for every kind of music, and we built this calendar the same way.”

Among the early bookings are Los Angeles alternative rock band Bad Suns on February 17, singer-songwriter Chelsea Cutler on February 26 and Chicago indie rock band Beach Bunny on March 6.

March also brings progressive metal band Haken, metalcore act Currents and emo veterans Saves the Day, followed by a March 31 hardcore show featuring Gorilla Biscuits and Terror.

The announcement comes as AEG Presents prepares to expand its Austin operations with two venues along the East Riverside corridor.

Approximately one mile from Moonline, the company is developing United Heritage Hall, a new 4,000-capacity venue at the River Park mixed-use development.

AEG [announced the first 12 performances at United Heritage Hall](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/united-heritage-hall-2027-inaugural-shows/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) on October 19, with bookings including ZZ Top, Chaka Khan, deadmau5, Death Cab for Cutie and comedian Taylor Tomlinson. The earliest announced show at that venue is scheduled for March 7.

The two properties will give AEG concert spaces serving different audience sizes, allowing the promoter to accommodate smaller club-level acts at Moonline and larger touring productions at United Heritage Hall.

Moonline also represents a transition for one of Austin’s established concert locations. Emo’s has operated at the East Riverside address since 2011, following its relocation from the city’s downtown entertainment district. The Emo’s name will not transfer to AEG, with the existing venue planning to relocate to a new downtown location under Live Nation’s operation.

AEG’s planned renovations at Moonline include improvements to the sound system, green rooms, backstage facilities and guest-facing amenities. The company has not announced a separate grand-opening event.

## Moonline Ticket Information

Tickets for most of the newly announced performances go on sale Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. CT, with artist presales beginning Thursday, October 29.

Beach Bunny will follow a different schedule, with an artist presale Thursday, November 12, and general ticket sales beginning Friday, November 13, at 10 a.m. CT.

Tickets and additional venue information will be available through [Moonline’s official website](https://moonlineaustin.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

AEG Presents said additional performances and programming details will be announced in the coming weeks.
