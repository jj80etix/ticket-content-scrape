# Amazon Music Live Sets Season 5 With Noah Kahan, Doja Cat and More

## Summary

"Amazon Music Live" is returning for a fifth season, leaving its studio format to broadcast performances pulled from artists' existing tours. The season opens Sept. 24 with Noah Kahan at T-Mobile Park and runs through Doja Cat's Oct. 29 hometown show at the Kia Forum, streaming on Prime Video, Twitch, Amazon Live, Amazon Music and Fire TV right after Thursday Night Football.

### Key Points
- Four episodes this season: Noah Kahan (Sept. 24, Seattle), Don Toliver (Oct. 1, Brooklyn), Lainey Wilson (Oct. 15, Uncasville CT), Doja Cat (Oct. 29, Inglewood).
- Format shift: filming existing tour stops rather than staging dedicated studio concerts.
- Series launched in 2022 tied to Amazon's Thursday Night Football broadcasts.
- Past performers include Ed Sheeran, Foo Fighters, Shaboozey and aespa.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Amazon-Music-Live.webp)

“Amazon Music Live” will return for its fifth season with performances from Noah Kahan, Don Toliver, Lainey Wilson and Doja Cat.

The concert series is changing its format for the upcoming season, leaving the studio and traveling to four tour stops around the United States. Each installment will feature a performance from an artist’s existing tour rather than a concert staged specifically for the program.

Season 5 will begin September 24 with Kahan’s final U.S. stadium performance on “The Great Divide World Tour.” The show will take place at T-Mobile Park in Seattle.

Don Toliver will follow October 1 with the closing performance of the second North American leg of his “Octane Tour.” That concert is scheduled for Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York.

The series will continue October 15 with Wilson’s show at Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut. Doja Cat will close the season October 29 with a hometown performance at the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California. The concert is scheduled for the day after the singer’s birthday.

“I’m playing The Forum the day after my birthday,” Doja Cat said in a statement. She added that having the performance broadcast to fans at home turns the concert into a larger celebration.

Kirdis Postelle, Amazon Music’s global head of artist and fan experiences, said the new format will give viewers a closer look at several of the year’s most prominent tours.

“Amazon Music Live” episodes will stream immediately after “Thursday Night Football.” Fans can watch through Prime Video, Twitch, Amazon Live, Amazon Music and the Amazon Music app on Fire TV.

Amazon launched the weekly series in 2022 as a way to connect live music programming with its NFL broadcasts. Earlier seasons largely featured artists performing before invited audiences at a Los Angeles studio.

Previous “Amazon Music Live” performers have included Ed Sheeran, A$AP Rocky, Foo Fighters, Peso Pluma, Fuerza Regida, Shaboozey and aespa.
