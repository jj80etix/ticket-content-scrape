# Linkin Park, Smashing Pumpkins Among Class of 2027 Artists to Receive Stars on Hollywood Walk of Fame

## Summary

The Hollywood Chamber of Commerce's Walk of Fame Selection Committee has announced its Class of 2027, the group of entertainment figures set to receive stars on the Hollywood Walk of Fame. The 32-person class spans film, television, recording, live performance, sports entertainment, and radio, including musicians Linkin Park, Smashing Pumpkins, The Ramones, Lil Wayne, and Sia.

### Key Points
- The Class of 2027 includes 32 honorees across six categories: Motion Pictures, Television, Recording, Live Theatre/Live Performance, Sports Entertainment, and Radio.
- Recording-category honorees include Linkin Park, Smashing Pumpkins, The Ramones, Lil Wayne, Karol G, David Guetta, Sia, and the posthumous Waylon Jennings.
- Other honorees include Pedro Pascal, Keke Palmer, Kate Hudson, Elle and Dakota Fanning (sharing a double ceremony), and Idris Elba.
- Selection Chairman Peter Roth called the class part of "one of the most enduring symbols of achievement in entertainment."
- Recipients have two years from selection to schedule their star ceremony before it expires; no ceremony dates have been set yet.

The Hollywood Walk of Fame Class of 2027 has arrived.

The Walk of Fame Selection Committee of the Hollywood Chamber of Commerce [revealed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hxYE917ipQk&t=1013s) the new group of entertainment professionals set to receive a star on the Walk of Fame last week. Among the 2027 class are nu-metal icons Linkin Park, the rockers of Smashing Pumpkins and The Ramones, rapper Lil Wayne, and pop’s Sia. Television stars like Pedro Pascal, Keke Palmer, and Bill Lawrence were among the class, as well as movie stars Elle and Dakota Fanning, Kate Hudson, and Sam Elliott.

Walk of Fame Selection Chairman Peter Roth noted in a press release that the committee is “proud to welcome the Class of 2027 to tone of the most enduring symbols of achievement in entertainment.”

“These 32 extraordinary individuals have each made a lasting impact on audiences around the world through their talent, creativity, and dedication to their craft,” Roth said. “We are honored to recognize their remarkable contributions and look forward to celebrating them as they take their place in Hollywood history.”
