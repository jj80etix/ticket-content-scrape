# Rock City UK Awarded For Keeping Customers Safe & Well

## Summary

Rock City in Nottingham, DHP Family's flagship venue, received a Judges' Recognition Award from Best Bar None for its customer welfare program. The award recognizes the venue's dedicated welfare staff, which grew out of its Drink Aware Club Crew initiative.

### Key Points
- Rock City is a 2,000-capacity venue staging about 100 gigs a year
- Best Bar None is the UK's national venue safety and responsible-alcohol accreditation program
- The welfare staff program originated from the Drink Aware Club Crew initiative

Rock City in Nottingham, UK, has been praised for its commitment to customer welfare by Best Bar None, the UK’s national accreditation program assessing venues for their commitment to excellence in safety, customer service, and responsible alcohol management practices.

Rock City in Nottingham is the flagship venue of DHP Family with a storied history stretching back more than four decades. The 2,000-capacity venue stages 100 gigs a year alongside a vibrant program of events and club nights.

DHP Family introduced dedicated welfare staff to help customers feel safe, supported and looked after during event nights all year round. Now their commitment to creating a safe and welcoming environment has been recognized by Best Bar None with a special Judges’ Recognition Award for their outstanding efforts.

Rock City’s award-winning welfare provision grew out of the original Drink Aware Club Crew initiative, created to provide support for those in need including vulnerable customers alongside the venue medic.

Following Covid, the role evolved into a Welfare Team of eight staff led by a welfare supervisor. It forms part of DHP Family’s wider commitment across Nottingham’s night-time economy to create a safe and welcoming environment for residents, visitors and students.

On club nights the team works throughout the venue, maintaining a permanent welfare desk while also carrying out regular welfare patrols. They look out for vulnerable or intoxicated customers, helping people reconnect with friends, charge phones and arrange safe journeys home, providing water, sick bags and ongoing monitoring for customers who are unwell and supporting the venue medic during first aid incidents.

Nottingham itself has been performing great in past safety assessments, retaining the so-called Purple Flag status for 15 consecutive years, a nationally recognized accreditation awarded to places where businesses, local authorities, police and other partners work together to promote safety, wellbeing and a vibrant evening economy.

Rock City’s Best Bar None accreditation will only bolster Nottingham’s status as one of the UK’s safest, vibrant and diverse cities to enjoy a night out in.

Nottingham City Councillor David Mellen, Executive Member for Leisure, Culture and Tourism, said: “This award is a fantastic recognition of the outstanding work being carried out by Rock City’s welfare team and highlights Nottingham’s ongoing commitment to providing a safe, welcoming and inclusive night-time economy.

“Nottingham is proud to be a city that attracts students, residents, and visitors from across the UK and beyond. Ensuring people feel safe when enjoying a night out is essential to maintaining our reputation as a vibrant and welcoming destination.
