# How Semmel brought a Twitch techno festival to life in Berlin

## Summary

German promoter Semmel Concerts brought "Techno Freitag" — an electronic-music format originated on Twitch by streamer HandOfBlood/INSTINCT3 — from livestream to a live Berlin event, selling 9,000 tickets before announcing a single artist by targeting the format's existing online fan community directly.

### Key Points
- Techno Freitag originated as a recurring DJ segment within gaming streamer HandOfBlood's Twitch streams (via his company INSTINCT3).
- Semmel Concerts adapted it into a live Berlin festival event.
- Sold 9,000 tickets before any artist lineup was announced, by marketing directly to the format's pre-existing online fan community.
