# Calle 24 Cancels Remaining U.S. Tour Dates After Visa Revoked

## Summary

Mexican artist Calle 24 canceled the final seven dates of his US "Eterno Tour" after his work visa was revoked, apologizing to fans who had already bought tickets. The cancelled shows spanned Hidalgo, Charlotte, Atlanta, Lake Buena Vista, San Antonio, Houston and Chicago; no rescheduling or refund process was detailed in the report.

### Key Points
- Seven US tour dates cancelled following visa revocation, cited as outside the artist's control.
- Artist statement emphasized compliance with US immigration processes and apologized to ticket-holders.
- No refund or reschedule details were disclosed in the report.
- The tour marked Calle 24's first US run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0849.jpeg)

Mexican music star Calle 24 had to cancel the final seven dates of his U.S. tour after his visa was revoked.

The “BÉSAME” singer took to [social media](https://www.instagram.com/calle24.oficial?igsh=ZHYxb2xocGQxMmpi&igsi=ZHYxb2xocGQxMmpi) on Monday to note that the cancellation was out of his control — and he wasn’t expecting his the revocation of his U.S. work visa.

“This decision is being made out of the utmost respect for the laws, regulations, and processes established by U.S. authorities,” Calle 24 wrote in the statement. “My team and I believe it is essential to act responsibly and in full compliance with all applicable requirements. I deeply regret this situation, especially not being able to be with my fans in each of the cities we were scheduled to visit.”

He added that “I sincerely apologize for any inconvenience this may cause, especially to those who have already purchased tickets and made plans to attend these shows.”
