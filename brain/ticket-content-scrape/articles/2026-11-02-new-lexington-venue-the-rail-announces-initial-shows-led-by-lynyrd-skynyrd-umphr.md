# New Lexington Venue The Rail Announces Initial Shows, Led By Lynyrd Skynyrd, Umphrey’s McGee, Niko Moon

## Summary

The Rail, a new 2,200-cap venue in Lexington, Kentucky's Commons Entertainment District, announced its first wave of shows ahead of an early-2027 opening, led by Lynyrd Skynyrd, Umphrey's McGee, and Niko Moon.

### Key Points
- Ten shows announced running January 23 through June 19, 2027; tickets on sale Nov. 6.
- Operated by Peachtree Entertainment, named co-owner/operator in September.
- Venue offers flexible floor configs, premium VIP spaces, and six bars; more than 1,000 seats in theater-style setup.

The Rail, a new venue in Lexington, Kentucky opening within the Commons Entertainment District, has announced its first wave of concerts ahead of its opening in early 2027, led by Lynyrd Skynyrd, Umphrey’s McGee, Niko Moon and with dates announced into mid-June/

Designed to deliver an elevated live entertainment experience, The Rail will accommodate up to 2,200 guests with flexible floor configurations, premium VIP spaces, six full-service bars and top-notch sound and production capabilities.

“Our vision for The Rail goes beyond bringing great concerts to Lexington,” said Nathan Baugh, CEO of Peachtree Entertainment, [which in September was announced](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/23/peachtree-entertainment-named-as-co-owner-and-exclusive-operator-of-2200-cap-lexington-venue-the-rail-to-open-in-2027/) as operator and co-owner of the venue. “The diversity of this initial lineup reflects our commitment to offering something for every music fan. As part of The Commons Entertainment District, The Rail will play an exciting role in shaping a vibrant destination with new restaurants, entertainment, and community partners that will bring even more energy to the area. We look forward to drawing fans from across the region and contributing to the continued growth of Lexington’s entertainment scene.”

Along with the initial artist announcements, additional shows and updates are promised soon.

Tickets for the newly announced shows are on sale to the general public on Friday, Nov. 6, at 10 a.m. EST.

Located within The Commons, The Rail is designed to bring national touring artists to the region, with a capacity of 2,200 and more than 1,000 seats for theater-style configurations. Additional amenities include two outdoor decks, premium VIP spaces, an exclusive membership program and a flexible floor plan for concerts, club nights, corporate functions, special events, and private rentals.
