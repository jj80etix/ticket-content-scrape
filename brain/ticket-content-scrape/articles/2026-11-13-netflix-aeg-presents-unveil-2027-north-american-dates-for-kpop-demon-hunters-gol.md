# Netflix, AEG Presents Unveil 2027 North American Dates For ‘Kpop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour’

## Summary

Netflix and AEG Presents, presented by Mastercard, unveiled North American tour dates for the "KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour," kicking off June 2027 with an initial 25 shows announced, some markets receiving two same-day performances.

### Key Points
- Initial announcement covers 25 North American shows starting June 2027.
- Tour presented by Mastercard; some markets get two same-day shows.
- Companion to the broader global tour announcement (see TicketNews coverage of the full 2027-2028 run).

Fans will be able to seal the Honmoon in North American arenas next summer. Netflix and AEG Presents announced global tour dates for the highly anticipated “KPop Demon Hunters Golden World Tour.”

The tour presented by Mastercard kicks off in June 2027, with the initial announcement unveiling 25 shows. Some markets will have two shows on the same day: a matinee presentation and another one in the evening. Additional dates will be announced, and the plan is to visit North America, the U.K., Europe, Latin America and the Asia Pacific, with more than 150 performances across 2027 and 2028.

“‘KPop Demon Hunters’ has become a global phenomenon, with characters and music that already mean so much to fans around the world,” said AEG Presents President of Global Touring Rich Schaefer. “We’re excited to work with Netflix to bring that world to life on tour, with the music at the heart of a show that makes fans part of the adventure. The Golden World Tour gives us the chance to create something special for this incredible audience and bring fans of every generation together to experience it.”

The North American run kicks off with two shows on June 5, 2027, at CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore. The routing includes stops at Newark’s Prudential Center, United Center in Chicago, Hamilton’s TD Coliseum, UBS Arena in Belmont Park, Atlanta’s State Farm Arena, American Airlines Center in Dallas and Tacoma Dome in Washington. See full routing below.

A Mastercard presale begins Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. local time, and a fan presale begins Nov. 19 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register at [https://www.kpopdemonhunterstour.com/](https://www.kpopdemonhunterstour.com/). The general onsale begins Nov. 20.

A limited number of VIP experiences and packages will be available, which may include reserved seating, on-stage photo opportunities, limited edition memorabilia, custom merchandise and more. Visit [https://www.kpopdemonhunterstour.com/](https://www.kpopdemonhunterstour.com/) for more information.

The show will feature music from Netflix’s from the HUNTR/X characters—Rumi, Mira and Zoey—who will interact with the audience “through cutting-edge performance innovations that bring this fan-favorite K-pop girl group to life like never before,” according to a release announcing the tour. The Saja Boys and Derpy Tiger characters will also be part of the production, which promises to be “a first-of-its-kind production that immerses fans in the storytelling with live-action stunts and performers, fan-favorite music and choreography, and special performances.”

“KPop Demon Hunters has transformed from a breakout hit into a generational phenomenon — sustaining 63 consecutive weeks in Netflix’s Global Top 10, eclipsing 2 billion Spotify streams for ‘Golden,’ and breaking the all-time record for the longest running soundtrack at No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Soundtracks chart,” said Marian Lee, Netflix’s Chief Marketing Officer. “This fandom was never about a single moment, and the Golden World Tour is how we keep it going – bringing that ‘Takedown’ energy to arenas around the world, where fans can sing, dance and help HUNTR/X seal the Honmoon.”

Amy Tinkham, a seasoned director, writer and show designer with more than 25 years of experience in live entertainment, will serve as creative director of the tour. She recently worked on Disney’s “Descendants/Zombies: Worlds Collide Tour,” Cirque du Soleil’s “Songblazers,” Aerosmith’s “Peace Out Tour” and Bob Marley Hope Road.
