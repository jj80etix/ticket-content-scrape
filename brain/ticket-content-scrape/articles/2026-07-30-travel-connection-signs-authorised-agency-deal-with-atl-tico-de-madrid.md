# Travel Connection signs authorised agency deal with Atlético de Madrid

## Summary

Travel Connection has been named a new authorised agency for LaLiga club Atlético de Madrid, giving fans access to premium seating at Riyadh Air Metropolitano for the 2026-27 season. Inventory includes seats behind the home dugout with exclusive lounge access, plus expanded lower-tier group seating in the Lateral Este, covering LaLiga, Champions League, and domestic cup matches. The deal follows Travel Connection's appointment last month as an official match-breaks partner of Manchester United.

### Key Points
- Travel Connection is now an authorised agency selling premium Atlético de Madrid inventory for all home matches across LaLiga, UEFA Champions League, and domestic cups in 2026-27.
- Premium inventory includes seats directly behind the home dugout with exclusive pre-match/half-time lounge access.
- Expanded lower-tier group seating is available across two blocks in the Lateral Este (East Stand).
- This follows Travel Connection's June 2026 deal as official match-breaks partner of Manchester United, continuing its build-out of authorised club ticket/hospitality agency deals.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Travel Connection has been named a new authorised agency for Spanish LaLiga club Atlético de Madrid, offering fans access to premium seating at Riyadh Air Metropolitano for the 2026-27 season.**

Inventory available to fans via Travel Connection includes limited seats directly behind the home dugout, with access to an exclusive lounge for pre-match and half-time hospitality, as well as expanded lower-tier seating across two blocks in the Lateral Este (East Stand), suitable for group bookings.

“We’re thrilled to evolve our existing relationship with Atlético de Madrid. This is a club with immense history and passionate supporters across the world, located in a magnificent city,” said Charles Hamilton, managing director of Travel Connection.

“As travel destinations go, they don’t come much better. As an Authorised Agency, we’re looking forward to giving travel agents access to secure, premium inventory at one of European football’s biggest clubs and most iconic stadiums.”

Tickets will be available for all Atlético home games across LaLiga, UEFA Champions League and domestic cup competitions.

[Last month](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/06/champions-travel-travel-connection-named-partners-of-manchester-united/), Travel Connection was named an official match breaks partner of English Premier League club Manchester United.
