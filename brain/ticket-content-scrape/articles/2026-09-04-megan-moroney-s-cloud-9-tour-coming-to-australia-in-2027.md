# Megan Moroney’s ‘Cloud 9 Tour’ Coming To Australia In 2027

## Summary

Megan Moroney announced a three-date Australian leg of her Cloud 9 Tour for May 2027, promoted by Frontier Touring, with member presale opening Sept. 8 and general onsale Sept. 9.

### Key Points
- Three Australian shows: Melbourne (May 8), Sydney (May 11), Brisbane (May 14), with Laci Kaye Booth support.
- Frontier member presale Sept. 8; general onsale Sept. 9.
- Tour supports her Cloud 9 album (2025 AMA Album of the Year).
- Follows a U.S. leg that grossed $1.8M off 13,304 tickets at Brooklyn's Barclays Center.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2291496069](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/GettyImages-2291496069.jpg)

Megan Moroney performs onstage during “The Cloud 9 Tour” at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Aug. 21, 2026. (Photo by John Shearer/Getty Images for Megan Moroney)

Megan Moroney is bringing her “Cloud 9 Tour” Down Under next year. The country star announced three dates in Australia with Laci Kaye Booth as a special guest.

The Australian leg of her trek is promoted by Frontier Touring and kicks off May 8 at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne. Moroney then visits Sydney for a show at Afterpay Arena on May 11, and she wraps up her run May 14 at Brisbane Entertainment Centre.

Frontier members have access to a presale beginning Sept. 8 at 11 a.m. local time, and the general onsale begins Sept. 9 at noon local time. For tickets and tour information, visit [frontiertouring.com/meganmoroney](https://www.frontiertouring.com/meganmoroney).

Moroney is on the road promoting her third studio album *Cloud 9*, which won Album of the Year at the 2025 American Music Awards with hits including “Beautiful Things” and “Wish I Didn’t.”

She performed in Australia at the Ridin’ Hearts Festival in Scoresby and Sydney last October. Prior to the festival, Moroney headlined a sold-out show at Brisbane Riverstage, which grossed $272,979 off 8,411 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

Moroney recently wrapped up the U.S. leg of her tour, which included stops at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena, BOK Center in Tulsa, L.A.’s Crypto.com Arena and Brooklyn’s Barclays Center, where she grossed $1,815,129 off 13,304 tickets.

The 28-year-old singer is embarking on the European and U.K. legs of her tour, which begins Sept. 13 at Sentrum Scene in Oslo, Norway. Moroney will also be performing in Stockholm, Paris, London, Glasgow and Belfast.

Moroney is booked by United Talent Agency worldwide and managed by PunchBowl Entertainment.
