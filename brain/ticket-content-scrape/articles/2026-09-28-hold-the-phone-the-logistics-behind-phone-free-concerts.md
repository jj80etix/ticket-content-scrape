# Hold the phone: The logistics behind phone-free concerts

## Summary

IQ Magazine reports on the rising trend of phone-free concerts as the practice reaches arena-level shows, speaking with organizers about the logistical and cultural mechanics of running fully "unplugged" audience experiences. The article (behind IQ's paywall beyond the opening excerpt) frames phone bans as both an exclusivity play and a possible driver of higher perceived value as ticket prices rise.

### Key Points
- Phone-free format is spreading from club/theater shows to arena-scale concerts.
- Framed as creating a more exclusive, interconnected fan experience.
- Article ties the trend to broader conversations about rising ticket prices and perceived ROI for fans.
- Full logistical detail is behind IQ's subscriber paywall; only the introductory excerpt was available to this harvest.
