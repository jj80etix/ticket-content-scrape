# Japan’s ASOBISYSTEM eyes global export with US expansion

## Summary

Japanese entertainment company ASOBISYSTEM is opening its first international office, ASOBISYSTEM USA in Culver City, to support touring, partnerships and business development for its roster of Japanese artists in North America.

### Key Points
- Founder/CEO Yusuke Nakagawa will lead both the US and Japan operations.
- The company represents nearly 100 artists/creators, including Kyary Pamyu Pamyu and Atarashii Gakko!, who return to the US for Bumbershoot in September.
- The company was founded in 2007 in Tokyo's Harajuku district.

## Japan’s ASOBISYSTEM eyes global export with US expansion

The multi-pronged entertainment company is looking to capitalise on "one of the world’s fastest-growing markets for Japanese entertainment"

![Atarashii Gakko!](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Atarashii-Gakko.png)

Atarashii Gakko! © Atarashii Gakko!

Japanese entertainment company ASOBISYSTEM has announced its first international expansion, with plans to set up shop in the US as it aims to capitalise on “one of the world’s fastest-growing markets for Japanese entertainment”.

ASOBISYSTEM USA will be headquartered in Culver City within the Los Angeles metropolitan area, providing a base for business development, partnerships, and artist support for its roster of Japanese artists, creators, entertainment companies, and intellectual property in the market.

The multi-pronged ASOBISYSTEM operation includes artist management, large-scale live events, immersive experiences, hospitality, and branding and intellectual property. Its North American headquarters are intended to help bridge the gap between Japan and the US, supporting touring and promotion in the market while serving as a consultant for additional Japanese entertainment companies looking to enter.

Founder and CEO Yusuke Nakagawa will lead the new USA branch, helping expand ASOBISYSTEM into a global platform for Japanese music, fashion, creators, and culture. He will serve as CEO across both the US and Japan operations.

“We’ve seen incredible growth in global interest for Japanese entertainment over the last several years, and we believe now is the right time to establish a permanent presence in the United States,” Nakagawa says. “Our goal is not only to create more opportunities for our own artists, but to help build lasting connections between Japan and North America by supporting creators, companies, and partners on both sides.”

> The company represents nearly 100 artists, creators, producers, and innovators

Founded in 2007 in Tokyo’s Harajuku district, the company has become one of Japan’s leading entertainment firms, representing nearly 100 artists, creators, producers, and innovators. Its roster includes artists like Kyary Pamyu Pamyu, Atarashii Gakko!, Yasutaka Nakata, and Capsule.

It has also helped its artists tour the market, with singer Kyary Pamyu Pamyu having completed multiple tours and festival plays in North America.

More recently, girl group Atarashii Gakko! sold out a 13-city NA tour in 2024, making an appearance at Coachella along the way. They will return to the US this year, performing at Seattle’s Bumbershoot Arts & Music Festival in September.

Both artists are represented for live activity by The Team.

Based in Tokyo, the company also has offices in Osaka, Fukoka, and Okinowa.
