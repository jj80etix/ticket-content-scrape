Masked country crooner Orville Peck will tour North America in support of his latest album this fall.

The disc — *Mule* – is due Sept. 18, the day of Peck’s scheduled performance at the Hollywood Bowl. The “Man Turned Mule Tour” kicks off in Sacramento at Channel 24 Oct. 28. The 19-date theater run includes a double at the Brooklyn Paramount and stops in Denver, Atlanta, Nashville and more, before wrapping Nov. 22 at The Paramount in Huntington, New York.

Artist presale signups are open now at Orvillepeck.com for the presale which will begin at 10 a.m. local time July 28. VIP packages will be available starting July 27 at 10 a.m. local time and will include premium tickets with early entry, an exclusive VIP Pre-Show Experience with Orville Peck including a soundcheck viewing and a Q&A session, exclusive merchandise and additional perks. General on-sale is July 31 at 10 a.m. local time at Orvillepeck.com/tour.

Orville Peck’s Reserved by Spotify tickets may be available to eligible US Spotify Premium subscribers through Ticketmaster in select markets. Eligible fans will be notified by Spotify via email and app notification and will have the opportunity to purchase up to two tickets before the general on-sale. Citi cardmembers will also have access to presale tickets in select markets beginning July 28 at 10 a.m local time until July 30 at 10 p.m local time through the Citi Entertainment program.
