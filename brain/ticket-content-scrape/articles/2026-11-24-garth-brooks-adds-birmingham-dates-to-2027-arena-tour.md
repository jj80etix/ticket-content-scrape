# Garth Brooks Adds Birmingham Dates to 2027 Arena Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks has added Birmingham dates to his 2027 arena tour. The announcement covers the new stops added to the existing tour.

### Key Points
- Garth Brooks adds Birmingham dates to 2027 arena tour.
- Routine tour-date expansion announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Garth-Brooks-Blame-It-All-On-My-Roots-Arena-Tour.webp)

Garth Brooks will bring his “Blame It All On My Roots” Arena Tour to Legacy Arena at the BJCC in Birmingham, Alabama, on February 19 and 20, 2027. The concerts will be his first at the arena since his 2015 Comeback Tour.

“The Legacy Arena in Birmingham is one of those places where you KNOW it’s gonna be epic!” Brooks said. He first played the venue in 1992 and returned in 1996 during his World Tour.

Tickets for both Birmingham shows go on sale Friday, December 4, at noon Central time through Ticketmaster and its app.

Birmingham is the third city Brooks has confirmed for the 2027 leg, following North Little Rock, Arkansas, and Lexington, Kentucky. A day before announcing Birmingham, Brooks confirmed two shows at Lexington’s Rupp Arena for February 12 and 13. Tickets for the Lexington concerts also go on sale December 4.
