# Koe Wetzel, Parker McCollum Reunite For ‘Naughty or Nice Tour’ This December

## Summary

Koe Wetzel and Parker McCollum reunited for the five-date "Naughty or Nice Tour" across Texas this December, their first joint tour in seven years, partnering with Fandiem for a charity ticket-donation sweepstakes.

### Key Points
- Five Texas dates Dec. 2-10, kicking off at Floore's Country Store and ending at Billy Bob's Texas.
- First joint tour since 2017.
- Raising Cane's is the official tour partner; Fandiem donation sweepstakes offers a flyaway package.
- Artist presale Oct. 7-8 (password NAUGHTY); general onsale Oct. 9.

Koe Wetzel and Parker McCollum have teamed up again for the “Naughty or Nice Tour,” their first joint tour in seven years. The tour will feature a five-date co-headlining jaunt across Texas this December, kicking off Dec. 2 at Floore’s Country Store in Helotes, TX. Wetzel and McCollum will continue the holiday shenanigans until Dec. 10, with the tour ending at Billy Bob’s Texas in Fort Worth, TX. Raising Cane’s joins the tour as an official partner.

Fans can access the artist presale beginning Oct. 7 at 10 a.m. local time through Oct. 8 at 10 p.m. local time using the password NAUGHTY. Promoter and local presales begin Oct. 8 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. CT

“It’s been a long time coming for us to run it back with this tour,” Wetzel said about the new holiday outing. “It felt right to end the year in Texas and make it a party.”

McCollum shares, “Koe and I did this tour for the first time way back in 2017 when we were still just some kids with a dream. Proud to say we’ve both come a long way since then, and getting to bring this back after some years off is really special. Looking forward to us both being back in Texas again, and I’ll do my best to keep Koe from gettin’ too rowdy.”

The Naughty or Nice Tour is also partnering with Fandiem to give fans an opportunity to support causes championed by Wetzel and McCollum. Fans who donate to Koe’s Kids and/or The Ruger Fund will be entered for a chance to win a grand-prize flyaway package to the December 10 show at Billy Bob’s Texas in Fort Worth.

The Naughty or Nice Tour stops include the following:

December 2 — Helotes, TX — Floore’s Country Store  
December 3 — Houston, TX — 713 Music Hall  
December 4 — Corpus Christi, TX — Concrete Street Amphitheater  
December 5 — Round Rock, TX — Round Rock Amp  
December 10 — Fort Worth, TX — Billy Bob’s Texas
