# Gwen Stefani to Headline Opening Concert at Anaheim’s New OCVIBE Concert Hall

## Summary

Gwen Stefani will headline the inaugural concert at Anaheim's new 5,000-capacity OCVIBE Concert Hall on March 17, 2027, part of a $5 billion, 100-acre redevelopment around Honda Center. The hall is designed as a mid-sized venue for touring acts, comedy and other live entertainment between club shows and the larger arena. Presales begin Nov. 19, with general onsale through Ticketmaster on Nov. 20; face-value prices had not been posted at announcement.

### Key Points
- OCVIBE Concert Hall's first show: Gwen Stefani, March 17, 2027, 5,000-capacity venue in Anaheim.
- Part of a $5B, 100-acre OCVIBE district redevelopment around Honda Center, backed by the Samueli family.
- Venue/Live Nation presale Nov. 19; general onsale via Ticketmaster Nov. 20; ticket limit of four per person/card.
- Broader district phases (retail, market hall, offices) slated to open through 2027, full completion 2033.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Gwen-Stefani-OCVIBE-Concert-Hall.jpg)

Gwen Stefani will headline the opening concert at Anaheim’s new 5,000-capacity Concert Hall at OCVIBE on March 17, 2027, bringing a hometown star to one of the first major entertainment openings at the $5 billion development surrounding Honda Center.

The show is the hall’s inaugural concert, introducing a mid-sized performance space designed for touring musicians, comedians and other live entertainment.

Stefani’s selection underscores the project’s Orange County connection. The No Doubt singer grew up in Anaheim and has performed at neighboring Honda Center 10 times, according to OCVIBE. Those appearances include two 1997 concerts recorded for No Doubt’s *Live in the Tragic Kingdom* concert video. She also headlined a sold-out show celebrating the arena’s 30th anniversary in September 2023.

Stefani described the opening as a homecoming. “It’s really special to be part of this exciting opening and to celebrate with the community that means so much to me,” she said.

The Concert Hall is part of OCVIBE’s 100-acre redevelopment of the area around Honda Center, backed by a $5 billion private investment commitment from the Samueli family. The project is intended to build a year-round district of live entertainment, restaurants, public gathering spaces, offices and residences rather than relying solely on arena events.

As [TicketNews reported when the design was unveiled in March](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/ocvibe-design-new-5000-capacity-concert-hall/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the venue was designed by architecture firm Populous as a flexible room with the production infrastructure to host concerts, comedy, award programs, esports and other events. OCVIBE has also promoted premium seating and access to a private Gallagher Club as part of its guest offerings.

With capacity for about 5,000 people, the hall adds an option between intimate club shows and the much larger Honda Center. Its opening gives Anaheim another room for touring acts and promoters to consider when booking Southern California performances, although the opening announcement did not identify any additional concerts at the new hall.

OCVIBE CEO Bill Foltz said the concert would also give the community an early look at the wider development as restaurants, public spaces and other planned attractions prepare to open.

The March concert is one step in a phased opening planned for 2027. Katella Commons, a 50,000-square-foot market hall, is scheduled to open in April, followed by The Weave office building. Restaurants associated with chefs Roy Choi and Tim Hollingsworth, along with Osen Hospitality Group, are expected later in the year. The larger development is slated for completion in 2033.

## Gwen Stefani OCVIBE Tickets and Presales

Concert Hall and Live Nation presales begin Thursday, Nov. 19, at 10 a.m. PST. Ticketmaster also lists an artist presale beginning at the same time. Fans can register for the venue’s Encore Club presale list through the [OCVIBE Concert Hall website](https://www.ocvibe.com/concerthall?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The general public onsale begins Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. PST through [Ticketmaster’s official event page](https://www.ticketmaster.com/gwen-stefani-concert-hall-at-ocvibe-anaheim-california-03-17-2027/event/0900656FC7B77CF1?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Doors are scheduled to open at 6:30 p.m. on March 17. Ticketmaster lists a limit of four tickets per person and credit card; face-value ticket prices had not been posted when the show was announced.
