# Whitney Leavitt Sets Six-Week Broadway Engagement in ‘Just in Time’

## Summary

Whitney Leavitt will join the Broadway production of "Just in Time" for a six-week limited engagement beginning December 1 at Circle in the Square Theatre, portraying Sandra Dee opposite Jeremy Jordan's Bobby Darin. Her second Broadway role following an earlier 2026 debut in "Chicago."

### Key Points
- Whitney Leavitt joins "Just in Time" on Broadway for a six-week run starting December 1.
- She portrays actress/singer Sandra Dee in the Bobby Darin musical.
- Marks her second Broadway role after debuting earlier in 2026 in "Chicago."
- Final performance scheduled for January 10, 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Just-in-Time-1.webp)

Whitney Leavitt is set to return to Broadway this winter, joining the cast of “Just in Time” for a limited engagement at Circle in the Square Theatre.

The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives star will portray actress and singer Sandra Dee in the Bobby Darin musical beginning December 1. Leavitt’s engagement is scheduled to run for six weeks, with her final performance set for January 10, 2027.

Her casting marks her second Broadway production following her debut earlier this year in the long-running revival of “Chicago.”

Jeremy Jordan currently stars as Darin in the production. The cast also includes Olivia Holt, Carrie St. Louis, Tony Award winner Debbie Gravitte, Joe Barbara, Drama Desk nominee Emily Bergl, Lance Roberts, Caesar Samayoa, Tristen Buettel, Christine Cornish, Jaime Foord, Julia Grondin, Jessica Mallare White, Tari Kelly, Matt Magnusson and Jeffrey Schecter.

“Just in Time” opened on Broadway April 26, 2025, and is staged at Circle in the Square under the direction of Tony Award winner Alex Timbers.

The musical was developed by Timbers from an original concept by Ted Chapin. Tony Award winner Warren Leight and Isaac Oliver wrote the book, while Shannon Lewis serves as choreographer. Andrew Resnick provides music supervision and arrangements.

The production began with Tony Award winner Jonathan Groff in the role of Darin. Groff earned a Tony nomination for his performance, and the original cast recording received a 2026 Grammy Award nomination for Best Musical Theater Album.
