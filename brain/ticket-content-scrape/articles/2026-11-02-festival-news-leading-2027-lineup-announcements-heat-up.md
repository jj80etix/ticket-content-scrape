# Festival news: Leading 2027 lineup announcements heat up

## Summary

An IQ roundup of a wave of 2027 festival lineup announcements across Europe, the US, and beyond, including BST Hyde Park, Rock Werchter, Primavera Sound Porto, Welcome to Rockville, and several others.

### Key Points
- BST Hyde Park adds RAYE; Rock Werchter adds Turnstile; Primavera Sound Porto led by Fontaines D.C. and Hayley Williams.
- Welcome to Rockville (Daytona) lineup includes Blink-182, Korn, The Offspring, and The Prodigy.
- Several new or second-year festivals cited strong debut attendance (State Fayre: 50k; Boomtown additions; Innings Fest growth).
- Milan's "Fiera Milano Live" is rebranding to "From Milan Live" starting 2027.

## Transcript

Leading events including BST Hyde Park, Rock Werchter, Primavera Sound Porto, Mighty Hoopla, and Welcome To Rockville all unveil 2027 artists

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/20250703RockWerchtersfeerWerchterjulierommelaere152.jpg)

Belgium's Rock Werchter

Festival lineup announcements have come thick and fast across leading events in Europe and the US in the last week, with events the likes of BST Hyde Park, Rock Werchter, and Primavera Sound Porto leading a raft of events unveiling acts.

**BST Hyde Park  
**Raye has been revealed as the second headliner for the 2027 London concert series, with her performance scheduled for 10 July. The London native, who was recently-crowned Mercury Prize winner, will be supported by Sienna Spiro, with further support acts to be named by AEG Presents soon.

Raye joins a headliner bill that already includes Bon Jovi, with the band’s appearance due on 24 June. The 2027 series will follow this year’s outing, which featured Garth Brooks, Maroon 5, and Ateez, among others.

**Rock Werchter  
**The Belgian festival has named Turnstile among its earliest acts for its edition coming up between 1 and 4 July 2027. The Baltimore band are enjoying strong momentum in their live career, with notable recent performances including drawing 60,000 to an outdoor benefit concert in Washington DC last month.

The festival’s bill already includes SOMBR, Muse, Angèle, Massive Attack, and Sam Fender.

**Primavera Sound Porto  
**Primavera Sound Porto’s bill will also by led by Fontaines D.C. and Hayley Williams, organisers revealed last week. The 10-13 June 2026 will mark the 14th outing for the Portuguese version of the leading festival, and will come a week after the Barcelona flagship edition, which will bring up a quarter century in 2027.

Also on the lineup are artists like FKA Twigs, The Chemical Brothers, and Lola Young.

**Mighty Hoopla  
**The tenth anniversary edition of Mighty Hoopla will feature headline performances from Robyn and The Pussycat Dolls when it returns to London’s Brockwell Park in June 2027.

The bill for the two-day event also features Bebe Rexha, Flor, and Sophie Ellis-Bextor, on Sunday 6 June, and Kelis, Eve, and Nicola Roberts on Saturday 5 June.

**Welcome to Rockville  
**Meanwhile in the US, Welcome To Rockville has confirmed its full lineup as it returns for a sixteenth instalment of its metal and punk weekend in Florida in May 2027.

Blink-182, Korn, The Offspring, and The Prodigy are among the acts for the event at Daytona International Speedway, which is promoted by Danny Wimmer Presents. It will follow a 2026 edition that drew in 210,000 fans across three days.

**Under The Big Sky  
**Elsewhere in the US, Montana’s Under The Big Sky has confirmed its lineup will be led by Zach Bryan, Kacey Musgraves, and Nathaniel Rateliff. They top a bill for the 16-18 July event that also features Brandon Flowers, Sam Barber, and Midland.

**State Fayre  
**In other country news, UK-based State Fayre will be headlined by Shania Twain, with a performance set for the festival’s Saturday night slot on 19 June 2027. It will mark the event’s second year, after its debut edition drew 50,000 fans earlier this year.

**Best Kept Secret  
**The Dutch festival has also unveiled its first wave of artists for June 2027, which is also to be led by Fontaines D.C., alongside Jungle, Phoebe Bridgers, and Angine de Poitrine. The festival will return to Beekse Bergen between 11 and 13 June 2027.

**Kilby Block Party  
**Completing a trio of recent festival slot announcements, Fontaines D.C. will also headline next year’s Kilby Block Party, alongside The Strokes and Slayyyter. The Slat Lake City event will take place between 14-16 May 2027, and had already unveiled Deftones, Jungle, and Lil Yachty as headline performers.

It will follow a [2026 edition](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/from-block-party-to-blockbuster-kilby-block-partys-remarkable-rise/) led by Lorde, the xx, and Turnstile.

**FreQuency Festival  
**The Austrian festival has announced Pitbull as its final headliner, confirming the rapper will perform on the event’s opening day on 19 August next year. The three-day festival will take place at Greenpark in the city of Saint Pölten, and will also feature acts including CRO, Dominic Fike, and Tokio Hotel.

**Boomtown  
**Boomtown has announced the first details of its 2027 event, confirming BICEP will make their debut appearance at the festival to close its Lion’s Den stage. Organisers also revealed NOISIA, whose last UK show was at Boomtown 2022, have reformed and will play the closing set on the HYDRO XL stage. The festival also said its Anara Forest stage will launch a reimagined design for 2027.

**On the Steps**  
The Live Nation-promoted series located on the steps of the Sydney Opera House will feature shows from artists including Australian Crawl, Rise Against, and DMA’S across its November to December span. Australian Crawl will kick off the series on 28 November, and it will wrap up with a show from The Amity Affliction on 12 December.

**Innings Fest  
**Pitbull, The Strokes, and Alanis Morisette have all joined the bill for Arizona’s Innings Fest. They join artists including Shaggy, 5 Seconds of Summer, and Train on the lineup for the return of the music and baseball event to Tempe between 26-28 February 2027.

**From Milano Live  
**Italy’s Fiera Milano Live says it is entering a “new chapter” as it reveals its 2027 edition will open with a set by Alex Warren. From 2027, the multi-genre live music series produced by Vivo Concerti will take the new name ‘From Milan Live’, which organisers say “marks the next stage in its development as a destination international and Italian live music”.

**Drift**  
In other global festival news, Australia’s Untitled Group is to launch Drift, a new one-day electronic festival for Perth and Brisbane in 2027. No artists have been revealed for the event yet, but Drift will land at Ozone Reserve in Perth on 28 Febraury, as well as the Gateway Bridge Underpass in Brisbane on 6 March.

**Home Festival  
**Elsewhere, Arijit Singh and Karan Aujla will headline Home Festival in Vagator, Goa on 29-30 December 2026, a new festival from promoter Team Innovation. The event launches in conjunciton with District by Zomato, and will also feature performances from DJ Chetas and Jasleen Royal.
