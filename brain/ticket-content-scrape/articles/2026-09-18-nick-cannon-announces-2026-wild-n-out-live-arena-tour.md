# Nick Cannon Announces 2026 ‘Wild ’N Out Live’ Arena Tour

## Summary

Nick Cannon is taking "Wild 'N Out Live: The Next Generation" on a 20-city North American arena tour, running November 10 (Oakland) through December 17 (Chicago), ahead of the sketch-comedy series' 22nd TV season.

### Key Points
- 20-city arena run from Nov 10 (Oakland Arena) to Dec 17 (United Center, Chicago).
- Stops include Inglewood, San Diego, Las Vegas, Phoenix, Dallas, Houston, Birmingham, Miami, Tampa, Atlanta, Charlotte, Atlantic City, Hartford, Newark, Baltimore, Toronto, Detroit and Indianapolis.
- Coincides with "Wild 'N Out" entering its 22nd television season since its 2005 debut.
- Artist presale registration open through September 20.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Nick-Cannon-Wildn-Out.webp)

Nick Cannon is taking “Wild ’N Out Live: The Next Generation” back on the road for a 20-city North American arena tour this fall.

The run will begin November 10 at Oakland Arena in Oakland, California. It is scheduled to conclude December 17 at the United Center in Chicago.

The opening portion of the tour includes California stops at Intuit Dome in Inglewood and Viejas Arena in San Diego. Cannon will also bring the production to Michelob Ultra Arena in Las Vegas and Mortgage Matchup Center in Phoenix.

The tour continues through Dallas, Houston and Birmingham before reaching Florida for performances at Kaseya Center in Miami on November 28 and Benchmark International Arena in Tampa on November 30.
