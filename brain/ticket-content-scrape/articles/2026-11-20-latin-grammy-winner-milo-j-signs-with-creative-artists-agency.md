# Latin Grammy Winner Milo J Signs With Creative Artists Agency

## Summary

Latin Grammy-winning Argentine rapper/singer Milo J has signed with Creative Artists Agency, bolstering CAA's Latin touring roster. The 20-year-old swept Record of the Year at the 2026 Latin Grammys and has built a large streaming and touring following across Latin America.

### Key Points
- Milo J won Record of the Year, Best Folk Album, Best Recording Package and Best Urban Performance at the 2026 Latin Grammys.
- A May 2026 Santiago, Chile show grossed $2.5M off nearly 39,000 tickets per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- He plays festivals in 2027 including Vive Latino, Coachella and Estéreo Picnic, plus shows in El Salvador, Costa Rica and Argentina.

Milo J, a Latin Grammy-winning Argentine rapper and singer, has signed with Creative Artists Agency for representation, bolstering the company’s Latin Touring roster.

The 20-year-old rising star is coming off a win for the highly coveted Record of the Year honor at the Latin Grammys for his hit song “Niño,” beating established acts like Rosalía, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Jorge Drexler, Karol G, and Carín León. Milo J also won Best Folk Album, Best Recording Package, and Best Urban Performance.

Born in Morón, Argentina, Milo J began writing music and competing in freestyle battles at 11, and he gained traction in 2021 as part of the Buenos Aires-based artist collective BajoWest, going viral on YouTube and social media platforms.

Milo J earned his first Latin Grammy nomination three years ago in the Best Rap/Hip-Hop category for “DISPARA \*\*\*,” a collaboration with fellow Argentine star Nicki Nicole, and later released his debut studio album, *111*, featuring Peso Pluma, Yahritza y Su Esencia, and Yami Safdie.

His third studio album, *La Vida Era Más Corta*, was released in September 2025 and was praised for combining folk music and urbano, propelling him onto larger stages across Latin America.

Milo J, who has amassed more than 14 million monthly listeners on Spotify, won 13 awards at the 2026 Premios Gardel, Argentina’s prestigious music awards, including the Gardel de Oro given to the artist of the year.

Building on the success of this year—which includes a sold-out show in May at Santiago, Chile’s Estadio Monumental that grossed $2,510,414 off 38,896 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice—Milo J is hitting the festival circuit in 2027, with stops at Mexico City’s Vive Latino, Coachella, Isle of Light, Panama Crossroads and Estéreo Picnic, among others.

He’s wrapping up the year with shows in El Salvador, Costa Rica and his native Argentina, where he will perform at Estadio Mario Alberto Kempes, a soccer stadium in Córdoba.

The young Latin star is managed by his mother, Aldana Ríos, who is an attorney.
