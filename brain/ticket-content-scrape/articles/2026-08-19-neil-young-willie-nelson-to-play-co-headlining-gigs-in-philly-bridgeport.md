# Neil Young, Willie Nelson to Play Co-Headlining Gigs in Philly, Bridgeport

## Summary

Neil Young & The Chrome Hearts and Willie Nelson & Family will co-headline one-off shows in Bridgeport, CT (Sept 22) and Philadelphia (Sept 24), plus a joint Farm Aid appearance Sept 26 in Virginia Beach; tickets go through artist presales before a Live Nation All Access presale and Aug 21 general on-sale.

### Key Points
- Shows: Sept 22 Bridgeport (Hartford HealthCare Amphitheater), Sept 24 Philadelphia (TD Pavilion at Highmark Mann), Sept 26 Farm Aid (Virginia Beach).
- Presale order: Club Luck (Nelson) / Neil Young Archives, then Live Nation All Access (Aug 20), general on-sale Aug 21.
- Ty Myers opens both headline dates.
- Young releases a new Chrome Hearts record Sept 18.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0846.jpeg)

Musical icons Neil Young and Willie Nelson will take the stage together for two one-off performances.

Young, alongside his backing band The Chrome Hearts, will take the stage for a co-headlining show with Nelson & Family at Bridgeport, Connecticut’s Hartford HealthCare Amphitheater on September 22, followed by a gig at Philadelphia’s TD Pavilion at Highmark Mann on September 24. Ty Myers will provide direct support at both concerts.

Tickets for the pair of shows will head on sale starting with artist presales via Nelson’s [Club Luck](https://willienelson.com/pages/club-luck) and [Neil Young Archives](https://neilyoungarchives.com/account?screen=plans), followed by a [Live Nation All Access presale](https://ticketmaster.evyy.net/c/1882189/264167/4272?subId1=willie-nelson&subId2=neil-young-shows&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ticketmaster.com%2Fwillie-nelson-tickets%2Fartist%2F732878) for the September 24 show on Thursday, August 20 at 10 a.m. ET. A general on sale will begin Friday, August 21 at 10 a.m. ET.

Alongside the Bridgeport and Philadelphia shows, Young and Nelson are slated to perform together at the annual Farm Aid benefit concert, set to take place on September 26 at Virginia Beach’s Veterans United Home Loans Amphitheater.
