# The Used Announce West Coast Fall Tour

## Summary

The Used announced an eight-date West Coast fall tour starting Oct. 1 in Sacramento, with stops including Spokane, Boise, and Las Vegas before closing Oct. 30 in Los Angeles supporting My Chemical Romance at the Hollywood Bowl.

### Key Points
- Eight dates, Oct. 1 (Sacramento) through Oct. 30 (Los Angeles).
- Route includes Spokane, Boise, and Las Vegas.
- Closing date is a support slot for My Chemical Romance at the Hollywood Bowl.
- Artist/promoter/Ticketmaster/AXS presales begin Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. local time; general on-sale follows Aug. 27.

The Used announced an eight-date West Coast fall tour kicking off on Oct. 1 in Sacramento, CA. From there, the band will hit Spokane, WA, Boise, ID, Las Vegas, NV, and more before concluding in Los Angeles, CA, where the band will support My Chemical Romance at the Hollywood Bowl on Oct. 30.
