# Noah Kahan Adds Dates to 2027 Last of the Bugs World Tour

## Summary

Noah Kahan has added new dates and opening acts to his 2027 "Last of the Bugs World Tour," an announcement that follows fan complaints about the November 10 presale for his North American stadium shows. Fans in New Jersey and Ohio flagged prices ranging from $300 to over $500 and said the cost led them to skip buying; the backlash renewed attention on Kahan's public support for legislation that would cap resale prices while leaving artists/promoters free to set original prices.

### Key Points
- New tour dates and opening-act additions announced for the 2027 "Last of the Bugs World Tour."
- Announcement follows fan backlash over the Nov. 10 stadium-show presale.
- Fans in NJ and OH cited prices from $300 to $500+; some reported abandoning purchases over cost.
- Complaints renewed focus on Kahan's advocacy for resale (not primary) price-cap legislation.

### Highlights
- Presale prices for North American stadium shows ranged $300–$500+.
- Multiple fans reported opting out of purchase due to cost.
- Artist publicly backs legislation capping resale prices only, leaving primary pricing untouched.

### Industry Problem
Rising primary ticket prices are straining patron affordability and goodwill even for an artist with a fan-friendly reputation, while the resale-cap framing favored by artists and lawmakers leaves the underlying primary-price driver of the complaints unaddressed.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes no platform-side fix; the signal is to keep watching whether artist-driven resale-cap advocacy (Q11) gains legislative traction, and whether primary ticketing platforms face growing pressure to add pricing transparency or tiered options that soften patron sticker shock (Q2).

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Noah-Kahan-The-Last-of-the-Bugs-World-Tour.webp)

Noah Kahan is expanding the supporting lineup for his 2027 *The Last of the Bugs World Tour*, announcing another round of dates ahead of his upcoming stadium tour across North America and Europe.

The additions are June 9 at Ford Field in Detroit, Michigan; June 19 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey; and June 21 and 22 at Fenway Park in Boston, Massachusetts.

The announcement follows the earlier confirmation of several supporting artists, including Remi Wolf, Gregory Alan Isakov, Buffalo Traffic Jam, Tiny Habits and Alyssa Grace.

The North American run begins April 24 at Sanford Stadium in Athens, Georgia. European performances follow in July, including concerts in Germany, Italy, Spain, France, Scotland and Ireland before three nights at London’s Wembley Stadium.
