# Nick Cannon’s ‘Wild ‘N Out Live’ Postpones 14 Arena Shows Following Hospitalization

## Summary

Nick Cannon's "Wild 'N Out Live: The Next Generation" arena tour postponed its remaining 14 shows (of an originally 20-city run) after Cannon, who has lived with lupus since 2012, shared a hospital update; Ticketmaster lists 12 of the dates as postponed, with no replacement dates yet announced and refund arrangements said to vary by venue.

### Key Points
- Postponements begin with a Nov. 21 Houston (Toyota Center) date and run through the Dec. 17 Chicago finale.
- Cannon disclosed the hospitalization via Instagram Nov. 20, referencing his lupus diagnosis (#LupusWarrior).
- The tour is promoted by Live Nation; neither Live Nation nor the venues have confirmed health as the official reason for postponement.
- Refund/rescheduling arrangements vary by venue; no new dates have been announced yet.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Nick-Cannon-Wildn-Out.webp)

Nick Cannon’s *Wild ‘N Out Live: The Next Generation* has postponed 14 arena performances, disrupting the remainder of the tour’s originally announced schedule after the comedian and television host shared a hospital update with fans.

The postponements affect every remaining stop on the original 20-city tour, beginning with a November 21 performance at Houston’s Toyota Center and continuing through the scheduled December 17 finale at Chicago’s United Center.

As of Monday, November 23, [Ticketmaster lists 12 performances as postponed](https://www.ticketmaster.com/artist/2502535?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), while Houston’s Toyota Center and Hartford’s PeoplesBank Arena have separately confirmed the status of their shows through official ticketing channels. Replacement dates have not been announced.

The disruption follows a November 20 Instagram post in which Cannon shared a photograph of himself lying in a hospital bed wearing a face mask.

The tour, promoted by Live Nation, was [announced in September](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/nick-cannon-announces-2026-wild-n-out-live-arena-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) as a 20-city arena run featuring the comedy, freestyle battles, music and audience participation associated with Cannon’s long-running television franchise.

## Ticket Holders Await Rescheduled Dates

Venues have begun issuing notices explaining how ticket holders will be affected, although refund arrangements vary by event and ticketing provider.

Houston’s Toyota Center confirmed that its November 21 performance was postponed without a replacement date.

“At this time, we don’t have information on a new date,” the venue said in its [official postponement notice](https://www.toyotacenter.com/events/detail/wild-n-out-2026?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The arena advised fans to retain their tickets, which will remain valid for the eventual rescheduled performance.

Houston ticket holders who purchased directly through AXS can also request refunds, subject to the venue’s stated conditions. According to Toyota Center, eligible tickets must not have been transferred, listed for sale or sold. Tickets acquired through resale or third-party websites are not eligible for refunds through the venue’s process.

Transferred tickets must be returned to the original purchaser to qualify for the refund option. Approved refunds may take up to 30 business days to process.

[Atlanta’s State Farm Arena](https://www.statefarmarena.com/events/detail/nick-cannon-wild-n-out-live-next-generation-atlanta-georgia-tickets-2026-12-01?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) has similarly confirmed that the December 1 performance has been postponed. The venue said original event tickets and parking purchases will remain valid for the new date, which has not yet been determined.

Other affected performances, including the December 2 show at Charlotte’s Spectrum Center, have also been marked postponed on official venue websites.

For shows sold through Ticketmaster, the company advises that tickets generally remain valid while an event is postponed. Refund eligibility is determined by event organizers and may vary depending on the circumstances of the postponement.

Ticket holders can check their individual orders for available refund options and should monitor official event listings for information about replacement dates.

The extent of the postponements goes beyond the seven shows identified in some initial reports. A review of Ticketmaster, AXS and venue listings accounts for all 14 dates remaining on the original itinerary after the November 19 Dallas performance.

One additional performance, scheduled for December 26 at VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville, Florida, remains listed for sale on Ticketmaster. That date was not included in the original 20-city tour announcement, and its status has not been clarified in connection with the other postponements.

Live Nation has not announced a revised itinerary or a timeline for rescheduling the affected performances.
