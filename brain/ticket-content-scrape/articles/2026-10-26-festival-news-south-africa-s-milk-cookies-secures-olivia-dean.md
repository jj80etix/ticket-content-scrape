# Festival news: South Africa’s Milk + Cookies secures Olivia Dean

## Summary

IQ's festival news roundup covers Olivia Dean headlining South Africa's Milk + Cookies festival in its third edition, plus new lineup announcements for Pinkpop, Sonic Temple, All Points East, Phillgood, Cabaret Vert, Igloofest, and a new Australian event.

### Key Points
- Olivia Dean to headline Milk + Cookies across Cape Town and Johannesburg dates in January 2027, her African debut.
- Additional names confirmed for several European and international festivals in the same roundup.

South Africa’s Milk + Cookies festival will return for its third edition in 2027, with Olivia Dean set to lead the two-city event for her debut on the African continent, leading the latest round-up of festival news.

She will perform across Cape Town’s Sandringham Farm (29 January) and Johannesburg’s Expo Centre (31 Jan), joining previously announced names of Elaine, Chelsea Sloan, Zee Nxumalo, and Jadet.
