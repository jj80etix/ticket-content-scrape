# Raleigh’s Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center Breaks Ground On Expansion

## Summary

Raleigh's Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center broke ground Nov. 18 on a nearly 300,000-square-foot expansion, "Convention Center West," adding a 50,000-square-foot flex hall, an 18,000-square-foot ballroom, and 13 meeting rooms to the downtown facility.

### Key Points
- Groundbreaking held Nov. 18 for the Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center West expansion in Raleigh.
- New building adds ~300,000 square feet, including a flex hall, ballroom, and 13 meeting rooms.
- Flex hall can convert between dining for 3,000, sports courts, exhibits, or general sessions.
- City, county, and corporate sponsor leadership participated in the ceremony.

The Raleigh Convention and Performing Arts Complex hosted a groundbreaking ceremony Nov. 18 to celebrate expansion of the Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center.

The new Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center West adds almost 300,000 square feet to the downtown facility. The new building features versatile spaces, including a 50,000 square foot flex hall, an 18,000 square foot ballroom and 13 additional meeting rooms.

The ballroom provides versatile options for event planners who are looking for something more refined for an elevated occasion, but that can also accommodate more casual event configurations. 

The flex hall features large, panoramic windows with black-out capabilities. The space can be transformed from a dining for 3,000 people, to volleyball courts, exhibits or general sessions. The existing east and new west buildings will be joined via a glass skybridge.

The timing of the expansion is strategic with downtown Raleigh preparing to welcome a new 600 room Omni hotel. The expansion, additional hotel inventory, major improvements happening to the convention center’s sister entertainment venues Red Hat Amphitheater and Martin Marietta Center for the Performing Arts all come together to position the city as an attractive meeting destination.

“This expansion was thoughtfully designed with input and feedback from planners to make sure we added the right spaces they needed” said Kerry Painter, Executive Director of The Complex. “We’re excited to welcome all the groups who wanted to come to Raleigh but needed more space, and we’re booking now so we want people to know if they’ve thought about Raleigh, now is the time so you can be one of the first to enjoy creating something amazing in this beautiful new space.”

The expansion is expected to be completed in 2029 and the venue is now actively booking for 2030.
