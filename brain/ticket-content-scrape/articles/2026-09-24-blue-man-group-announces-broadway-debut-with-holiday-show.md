# Blue Man Group Announces Broadway Debut With Holiday Show

## Summary

Blue Man Group will make its Broadway debut with a new holiday production, "A New Holiday Surprise," running November 12 through January 3 at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre, plus stops in Chicago and Boston. The run returns the group to New York after 33 years and 17,000+ performances at its former Astor Place Off-Broadway home, which closed in February 2025. General on-sale begins September 25.

### Key Points
- Broadway run: Nov 12 - Jan 3, 2027, at Lunt-Fontanne Theatre; opening night Nov 17.
- Also plays Chicago's CIBC Theatre (Nov 24-Dec 13) and Boston's Boch Center-Wang Theatre (Dec 15-23).
- First-ever holiday production and Broadway debut for the group.
- Broadway Direct/Blue Man Group presales underway; general sale Sept 25, 10am ET.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Blue-Man-Group.webp)

Blue Man Group is returning to New York City this holiday season, this time on Broadway.

Its new production, “A New Holiday Surprise,” will begin a limited run at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre on November 12, with opening night set for November 17. Performances are scheduled through January 3, 2027.

The engagement marks two firsts for the group: its Broadway debut and its first holiday production. The show will also travel to Chicago’s CIBC Theatre from November 24 through December 13 and Boston’s Boch Center-Wang Theatre from December 15 through December 23. Additional cities are expected to be announced.

The Broadway run brings Blue Man Group back to the city where it began. The group opened at the Astor Place Theatre in 1991 and remained there for 33 years, giving more than 17,000 performances before closing its Off-Broadway production in February 2025.
