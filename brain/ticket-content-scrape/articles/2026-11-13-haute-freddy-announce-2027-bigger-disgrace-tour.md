# Haute & Freddy Announce 2027 Bigger Disgrace Tour

## Summary

Haute & Freddy announced their 2027 "Bigger Disgrace Tour," adding 16 headlining US dates opening April 24 in Phoenix and wrapping May 21 in Portland, Oregon, with stops including Dallas, Houston, Atlanta, Chicago, and Boise.

### Key Points
- 16-date US headline tour, April 24 (Phoenix) to May 21 (Portland, OR).
- Stops include House of Blues venues in Dallas and Houston, The Tabernacle (Atlanta), and The Salt Shed (Chicago).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Haute-and-Freddy-Bigger-Disgrace-Tour.webp)

Haute & Freddy have announced their 2027 Bigger Disgrace Tour, adding 16 headlining concerts across the United States.

The tour is slated to open on April 24 in Phoenix, Arizona, at The Van Buren. From Phoenix, the tour will head to Texas for performances at House of Blues venues in Dallas and Houston. Additional Southern stops include Orlando’s House of Blues, The Tabernacle in Atlanta and The Ritz in Raleigh, North Carolina.

The itinerary continues through Philadelphia, Boston and Cleveland before reaching The Salt Shed in Chicago. Concerts in Minneapolis, Omaha and Denver will follow, with the final stretch taking the duo to Boise, Idaho, and Seattle. The U.S. headline run will wrap up on May 21 at the Crystal Ballroom in Portland, Oregon.

Notably, before beginning their tour, the duo is set to perform at the [Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/coachella-2027-lineup-tickets-dua-lipa-sza-tame-impala/) in Indio, California, on April 9 and April 16.
