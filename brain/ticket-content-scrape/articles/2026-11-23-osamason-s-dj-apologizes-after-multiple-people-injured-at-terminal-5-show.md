# OsamaSon’s DJ Apologizes After Multiple People injured At Terminal 5 Show

## Summary

OsamaSon's DJ Nino Andretti publicly apologized after inciting a crowd-crush during the rapper's Nov. 19 show at Terminal 5 in New York, when he encouraged fans to rush the merch table for a giveaway. Five concertgoers were injured, two hospitalized, and NYPD confirmed four minor injuries with no arrests made. The incident occurred during a Nike Jordan Brand-sponsored event.

### Key Points
- Crowd crush occurred Nov. 19 at Terminal 5, NYC, during a Nike Jordan Brand-sponsored show.
- DJ Nino Andretti encouraged fans to huddle near the merch table/exit for a giveaway, triggering the crush.
- Five people injured; two hospitalized in stable condition, two refused treatment.
- NYPD reported no arrests as of its statement.
- Andretti apologized publicly on Instagram, saying he'll "prioritize safety over excitement."

OsamaSon’s DJ Nino Andretti has apologized after instigating a crowd-crush that left five concertgoers injured during the rapper’s show at Terminal 5 in New York City. The Nov. 19 event was a partnership with Nike’s Jordan Brand. At one point during the show, Andretti encouraged rowdy fans to huddle around the merch table to win an item from the planned special giveaway.

The result was a crowd-crush incident that injured five people, with two people having to be hospitalized.

“I feel horrible about what happened on Thursday after the show. I’m sorry to all the fans & supporters who got hurt & who were scared. I wasn’t aware that the merch was next to the exit, & most importantly, I wasn’t aware of the power behind my words,” Andretti posted on Instagram. “I’m taking this time to take a step back & reflect & learn from this situation. I’ll prioritize safety over excitement moving forward. & to the 4 people who got hurt, I personally want to apologize to you.”

“Four individuals sustained minor injuries,” the NYPD said in a released statement. “EMS transported two individuals to local hospitals in stable condition, while the remaining two refused medical attention on scene. No arrests have been made at this time.”
