# The Chicks, Shania Twain and Kacey Musgraves Lead State Fayre 2027 Lineup

## Summary

State Fayre has added The Chicks and Kacey Musgraves to its 2027 headlining bill alongside previously announced Shania Twain, making it the first major UK camping festival with an all-female headline lineup. The second edition returns to Hylands Park in Chelmsford, Essex, from June 18-20.

### Key Points
- The Chicks, Shania Twain, and Kacey Musgraves headline; organizers call it the UK's first all-female-headlined major camping festival.
- Second edition runs June 18-20, 2027 at Hylands Park, Chelmsford.
- Lineup spans country, rock, Americana, folk, and blues acts.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/State-Fayre-2027.webp)

State Fayre has announced The Chicks, Shania Twain and Kacey Musgraves as headliners for its 2027 festival. Organizers say it will be the first major camping festival in the United Kingdom to feature an all-female headlining bill.

The festival will return to Hylands Park in Chelmsford, Essex, from June 18 to 20 for its second edition. The Chicks and Musgraves join Twain, who was previously announced, atop a lineup featuring country, rock, Americana, folk and blues artists.

Festival organizers Anna-Sophie Mertens and Alex Simmonds said the headliners reflect the range of artists represented at State Fayre.

“It brings us great pride to announce the full headliner lineup for State Fayre 2027, welcoming The Chicks and Kacey Musgraves to Hylands Park alongside the previously announced Shania Twain,” they said in a joint statement.
