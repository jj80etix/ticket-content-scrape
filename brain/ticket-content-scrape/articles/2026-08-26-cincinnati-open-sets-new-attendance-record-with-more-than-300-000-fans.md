# Cincinnati Open Sets New Attendance Record With More Than 300,000 Fans

## Summary

The Cincinnati Open drew a record 300,288 spectators during its August 11–24 run, up nearly 15,000 from 2025's mark. Attendees came from all 50 states and 62 countries, with 57% traveling from outside the Cincinnati area. Organizers credit the growth to added fan-experience elements like Community Weekend and Finals Weekend.

### Key Points
- New attendance record: 300,288 fans, up from 285,571 in 2025.
- 57% of attendees traveled from outside the Cincinnati region.
- Tournament director credits new fan-experience programming for the growth.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Cincinnati-Open.webp)

The Cincinnati Open reached a new attendance milestone in 2026, welcoming more than 300,000 fans during its two-week run in Mason, Ohio.

A total of 300,288 spectators attended the tournament at the Lindner Family Tennis Center from August 11-24, establishing a new event record. The figure surpassed the previous high of 285,571 set during the 2025 edition, an increase of nearly 15,000 attendees year over year.

The tournament also continued to attract a substantial audience from beyond the Cincinnati region. Ticket buyers represented all 50 U.S. states as well as 62 countries, while organizers said 57% of attendees traveled from outside the Cincinnati area.

Tournament director Bob Moran said crossing the 300,000 mark was a significant achievement for the event following a series of additions designed to expand the experience for spectators.
