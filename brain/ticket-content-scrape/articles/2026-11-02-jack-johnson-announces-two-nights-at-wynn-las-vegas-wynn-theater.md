# Jack Johnson Announces Two Nights At Wynn Las Vegas’ Wynn Theater

## Summary

Jack Johnson booked two shows at the Wynn Las Vegas' Encore Theater (June 2-3, 2027), his first Vegas return in four years, with special guest G. Love, promoting his "SURFILMUSIC" documentary and companion album.

### Key Points
- Tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. PT.
- Follows amphitheater shows across North America and a recent two-night, 31,332-ticket Hollywood Bowl run grossing $4.4M.

Jack Johnson is following up his “SURFILMUSIC” amphitheater shows across North America with two shows booked in Las Vegas in June 2027, marking his first return to the Encore Theater at the Wynn Las Vegas in four years.

Johnson and his band have lined up back-to-back performances at the 1480-capacity venue June 2-3, 2027. He will be joined by special guest G. Love.  
  
An announcement from promoter AEG Presents’ PR team notes that Johnson “will present an expansive set celebrating more than 25 years of making music, pairing chart-topping songs with stories and archival footage from his early days as a surfer and filmmaker, as featured in the documentary SURFILMUSIC, available to stream and rent this December.”

Tickets for both shows go on sale to the public this Friday, Nov. 6, 2026, at 10 a.m. PT.

Along with the documentary, Johnson has recently released a companion double album, SURFILMUSIC Soundtrack and 4-Tracks, via Brushfire Records. A note on Johnson’s website adds the soundtrack features “the film’s original score by Jack Johnson and Hermanos Gutiérrez alongside remastered selections from Jack’s early four-track recordings. 4-Tracks presents a deeper dive into the archival music from Jack’s personal tapes, including raw recordings and previously unreleased gems from his earliest days making music.”  
  
Johnson’s tour dates are booked by Partisan Arts’ Tom Chauncey.  
  
Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Johnson include two nights at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles Oct. 10-11 that sold a total of 31,332 tickets and grossed more than $4.4 million.
