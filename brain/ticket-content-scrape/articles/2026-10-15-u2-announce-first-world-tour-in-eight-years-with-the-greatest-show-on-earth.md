# U2 announce first world tour in eight years with ‘The Greatest Show on Earth’

## Summary

U2 announced its first world tour in eight years, "The Greatest Show On Earth," promoted by Live Nation in support of 16th studio album "Carnaval De Luz," kicking off Feb. 23, 2027 in Mexico City with support from Beck. Only six Latin American dates (two each in Mexico City, São Paulo and Buenos Aires) have been confirmed so far, with European and North American legs to be announced in the coming weeks and the tour expected to run into 2028.

### Key Points
- Opens Feb. 23, 2027 in Mexico City (Estadio GNP Seguros); also two nights each in São Paulo (Estádio MorumBIS) and Buenos Aires (Estadio River Plate).
- Only Latin American dates confirmed so far; Europe/North America dates (including a planned Ireland homecoming) to follow in coming weeks.
- Promoted by Live Nation; tour expected to extend into 2028.
- Marks the band's 50th anniversary; follows a history of landmark productions including the 360° Tour (2009-11) and a 36-show Sphere residency (2023).

## U2 announce first world tour in eight years with ‘The Greatest Show on Earth’

One of the highest-grossing live acts ever will begin a global outing in Mexico City in February 2027, with the full tour rolling into 2028

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/PHOTO-U2-Street-Of-Dreams-cover-image-no-text-CREDIT-Viviane-Sassen-scaled.jpg.webp)

U2 © Viviane-Sassen

U2, one of the highest-grossing live acts in history, have announced their first world tour in eight years, with *The Greatest Show On Earth* sure to be one of the most significant live events of 2027.

While confirming a world tour, which is promoted by Live Nation, the band has only revealed six Latin American dates at this stage, with following legs for Europe and North America confirmed without exact details.

It will feature two nights at each of Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros, São Paulo’s Estádio MorumBIS, and Buenos Aires’ Estadio River Plate, with support coming from Beck. Live Nation suggested the tour will roll into 2028, and left open the possibility of further Latin American dates post-2027.

“ *The Greatest Show On Earth World Tour*  – in support of the band’s forthcoming 16th studio album *Carnaval De Luz* – will kick off in Mexico City on February 23rd, 2027 with support from Beck, with shows also announced for São Paulo and Buenos Aires, the only three Latin American cities the tour will visit next year,” the promoter said.

“Additional 2027 dates for Europe and North America are set to be announced in the coming weeks; with plans for the band’s all-important return home to Ireland in the works,” it added.

The announcement comes as the band celebrates its half-century anniversary, having performed a duo of surprise concert sets in Dublin this month to mark the milestone, including an appearance at their old secondary school.

> “Acknowledged as one of the best live acts in the world, U2 has always consistently pushed the boundaries of live performance”

U2 has a long history of ambitious global stadium tours, often making use of elaborate staging. With their monumental *360° Tour* (2009–2011), the band became the first band to scale an in-the-round stadium production worldwide, going on to sell 7 million tickets worldwide and becoming the highest grossing tour in history at the time.

They returned to arenas in 2016 with *eXPERIENCE + iNNOCENCE,* originally conceived as a two-part outing to be complimented by the *iNNOCENCE + eXPERIENCE* leg, with both representing two sides of a double album project.

A 2017 stadium tour performed 1987 *The Joshua Tree* album in sequence against a giant high-resolution screen displaying desert imagery and Anton Corbijn films. It grossed approximately $317 million in 2017 alone. The 2019 continuation took the show to Oceania and Asia, including U2’s first concert in India, in Mumbai.

In 2023, the band played a [36-show residency at the Las Vegas Sphere](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2023/10/u2-add-11-sphere-shows-amid-unprecedented-demand/), which included 11 initially unplanned dates organised to meet demand.

“Acknowledged as one of the best live acts in the world, U2 has always – since their earliest days, including the industry-defining ZOO TV Tour which broke the mold in 1991 – consistently pushed the boundaries of live performance,” Live Nation said.
