# Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile To Headline 2027 New Orleans Jazz Fest

## Summary

The 2027 New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival unveiled a headliner lineup topped by Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile and Gwen Stefani, spanning two weekends (April 22-25 and April 29-May 2) at the Fair Grounds Race Course with over 5,000 musicians across 14 stages. General admission and VIP passes go on sale Nov. 20, with payment plans and Louisiana-resident discounts available.

### Key Points
- Two-weekend festival, April 22-25 and April 29-May 2, 2027, at Fair Grounds Race Course.
- Headliners include Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile, Gwen Stefani, plus James Taylor, Jack Johnson, Jack White, LL Cool J, Chaka Khan and dozens more across 14 stages.
- GA and VIP passes on sale Nov. 20, 10 a.m. local, via nojazzfest.com; payment plans from $49 down; discounted GA for Louisiana residents.
- Jampack travel packages (hotel + shuttle) offered alongside festival/VIP passes, produced with Festival Productions and AEG Presents.

Next year’s New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival is going to be a big one with Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile and Gwen Stefani headlining the multi-weekend event.

The festival takes place April 22-25 and April 29-May 2 at the historic Fair Grounds Race Course. The spring festival features more than 5,000 musicians across 14 stages, and among those acts are James Taylor, Jack Johnson, Jack White, Matchbox Twenty, Widespread Panic, The Red Clay Strays, LL Cool J, Chaka Khan, Khruangbin, The B-52s, The War On Drugs, The Avett Brothers, Train, Wyclef Jean, The Meters, Trombone Shorty & Orleans Avenue, Mannie Fresh with Juvenile & T.I., Buddy Guy and many more.

Weekend general admission and VIP passes go on sale Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time at [nojazzfest.com](https://www.nojazzfest.com/). Payment plans starting at $49 down are available, and Louisiana residents have access to discounted general admission passes.

The festival, which is produced by Festival Productions, Inc.-New Orleans and AEG Presents, partnered with Jampack to provide travel packages that include festival tickets or VIP passes along with hotel accommodations and roundtrip shuttle service between the festival and hotel. The Hilton New Orleans Riverside is the official hotel of Jazz Fest, which is presented by Shell USA. Visit [nojazzfest.com](https://www.nojazzfest.com/) for more information.

The New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival and Foundation, Inc. invests in Louisiana communities through the foundation’s year-round work in education, economic development, and cultural enrichment. Initiatives include the Don “Moose” Jamison Heritage School of Music, the Allison Miner Series, the Tom Dent Congo Square Lecture Series, and Class Got Brass presented by the Gia Malone Prima Foundation. Economic development programs include Community Partnership Grants, the Community Outreach Jazz Fest ticket program, the Catapult Fund business accelerator and more.

The festivities for the event begin April 21 with the Jazz & Heritage Gala. A variety of gala ticket packages include access to all eight days of the festival, and they go on sale Dec. 1. Visit [jazzandheritagegala.com](https://www.jazzandheritage.org/jazz-fest-gala/) for tickets and sponsorship opportunities.
