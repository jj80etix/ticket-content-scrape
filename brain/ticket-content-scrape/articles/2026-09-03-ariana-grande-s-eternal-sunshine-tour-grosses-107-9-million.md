# Ariana Grande’s Eternal Sunshine Tour Grosses $107.9 Million

## Summary

Ariana Grande's Eternal Sunshine Tour grossed $107.9 million across 41 shows, selling 638,000 tickets in 11 markets across the US, Canada, and UK, concluding September 1 at London's The O2. The run set a per-show revenue record for Grande despite fewer dates than several of her previous tours.

### Key Points
- Averaged $2.6 million gross and 15,600 tickets sold per show.
- Figures reported to Billboard Boxscore.
- Tour concluded with an 11-market, three-month arena run.

Ariana Grande’s Eternal Sunshine Tour generated $107.9 million in ticket sales during its 41-show run, setting several new benchmarks for the singer’s touring career.

The tour, which concluded September 1 at The O2 in London, sold 638,000 tickets, according to figures reported to [*Billboard Boxscore*](https://www.billboard.com/pro/ariana-grande-eternal-sunshine-tour-gross-100-million/). Grande performed in 11 markets across the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom during the three-month arena run.

Despite featuring fewer than half as many shows as several of Grande’s previous tours, the Eternal Sunshine Tour produced her strongest results on a per-show basis. The run averaged $2.6 million in gross revenue and 15,600 tickets sold per concert.

Those nightly averages were 74% and 14% higher, respectively, than Grande’s previous records, which were established during the Sweetener World Tour. That 2019 trek averaged $1.5 million and approximately 13,700 tickets per show.

The Sweetener World Tour remains Grande’s highest-grossing tour overall after earning $146.4 million. However, that run included 101 performances, compared with the 41 shows staged as part of the Eternal Sunshine Tour.

Grande’s 2026 dates now account for all 10 of her highest-grossing single-venue engagements. Eight of the singer’s 10 largest engagements by attendance also came from the latest tour.

The biggest engagement was Grande’s 10-night run at The O2, which generated $27.7 million from 179,000 tickets. The London residency finished with the highest gross and attendance of any single-venue engagement in her career.

Grande’s North American leg included 31 performances and grossed $80.2 million from 459,000 tickets. It became the highest-grossing individual leg of any tour in the singer’s career.

The Eternal Sunshine Tour opened June 6 at Oakland Arena in California. Grande performed multiple nights in a limited number of markets, including Oakland, Los Angeles, Austin, Sunrise, Atlanta, Brooklyn, Boston, Montreal and Chicago, before closing the tour in London.

The trek marked Grande’s first tour since the Sweetener World Tour ended in December 2019. In the years between the two runs, she released the albums *Positions* in 2020 and *Eternal Sunshine* in 2024. She also released her eighth studio album, *Petal*, on July 31 while the tour was underway.
