# Malcolm Todd Expands ‘Do That Again Tour’ With International Dates

## Summary

Malcolm Todd has expanded his "Do That Again Tour" with 24 international dates across Asia, Europe, the UK/Ireland and Australia, running November 2026 through May 2027 in support of his sophomore album. Artist presales for the international leg began September 15, with general onsale to follow.

### Key Points
- 24 new dates across Taipei, Tokyo-area, Manila, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok, Kuala Lumpur and Jakarta (Nov 23–Dec 12).
- European leg resumes Feb 12, 2027 in Stockholm, with stops in Copenhagen, Cologne, Berlin, Brussels, Amsterdam and Paris.
- UK/Ireland leg (Feb 24–Mar 2) opens in Dublin and closes at O2 Academy Brixton in London; Natanya opens.
- Australian leg (May 12–21) hits Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne and Perth with support from Maden Lane.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Malcolm-Todd-Do-That-Again-World-Tour.webp)

Malcolm Todd has expanded his “Do That Again Tour” with 24 concerts across Asia, Europe, the United Kingdom and Australia. The newly announced schedule begins in November and continues through May 2027.

The singer, songwriter and guitarist is touring in support of his sophomore album, *Do That Again*, which Columbia Records released in June.

The Asia leg will begin November 23 at TICC in Taipei. Todd will then perform in Yokohama, Japan; Manila, Philippines; Singapore; Seoul, South Korea; Bangkok; Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia; and Jakarta, Indonesia. The first portion of the international tour will conclude December 12 at GBK Basket Hall in Jakarta.

Todd will resume the tour February 12, 2027, at Fryshuset Arenan in Stockholm. The European itinerary also includes Copenhagen, Denmark; Cologne and Berlin, Germany; Brussels; Amsterdam; and Paris.

Singer-songwriter Natanya will open throughout the European, UK and Ireland dates.

The final announced leg will begin May 12 at Riverstage in Brisbane, Australia. Todd will also visit Sydney’s Hordern Pavilion on May 14, Melbourne’s Festival Hall on May 17 and Perth HPC on May 21. Maden Lane will support the four Australian shows.

Artist presales for the international dates opened September 15. General ticket sales for Europe and the United Kingdom begin September 18 at 9 a.m. local time. Australian tickets go on sale to the general public that day at 11 a.m. local time. Sales schedules for the Asian concerts vary by market.
