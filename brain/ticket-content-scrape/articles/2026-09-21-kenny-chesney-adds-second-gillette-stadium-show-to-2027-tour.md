# Kenny Chesney Adds Second Gillette Stadium Show to 2027 Tour

## Summary

Kenny Chesney has added a second Gillette Stadium show (Aug. 27, 2027) to his Original Vibe Room stadium tour after strong demand for the previously announced Aug. 28 date, extending the run to 20 shows across 19 markets. Eric Church, Sheryl Crow and Greylan James join both Foxborough dates, which go on public sale Friday, Sept. 25.

### Key Points
- Second Gillette Stadium show added for Aug. 27, 2027, one night before the Aug. 28 tour finale.
- Marks Chesney's 25th and 26th performances at Gillette, a venue attendance record.
- Tour now spans 20 shows across 19 markets, opening April 24 at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa.
- First stadium tour since two years of Sphere residency performances in Las Vegas.
- General on-sale for both Gillette dates begins Friday, Sept. 25.

Kenny Chesney has added a second Gillette Stadium concert to his 2027 Original Vibe Room Tour following strong demand for the tour’s Foxborough stop.

The newly announced show is scheduled for August 27, one night before the previously announced August 28 tour finale. Chesney will be joined at both concerts by Eric Church, Sheryl Crow and Greylan James.

The two-night run will mark Chesney’s 25th and 26th performances at Gillette Stadium, extending his record for the most concerts by an artist in the venue’s history. The stadium is home to the NFL’s New England Patriots and Major League Soccer’s New England Revolution.

The added concert expands the Original Vibe Room Tour to 20 shows across 19 markets. The all-stadium run will begin April 24 at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, before traveling to venues in Arlington, Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, Chicago, Nashville, Denver, Atlanta and other markets.

“When we started to talk about touring next year, all I could think about was how good those stadiums feel — how loud, how fast, how much energy comes hurling at us,” Chesney said when announcing the tour. “I don’t think I knew how much I missed it.”

The 2027 run will be Chesney’s first stadium tour following two years of performances at Sphere in Las Vegas.

Tickets for both Gillette Stadium shows will go on sale to the general public Friday, September 25. Additional ticket information is available through [KennyChesney.com](http://kennychesney.com).
