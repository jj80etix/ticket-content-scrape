# UK country audience ‘speaks for itself’: The Long Road on a record year

## Summary

UK country/Americana festival The Long Road posted 30% attendance growth in 2026, its "biggest year ever," with creative director Baylen Leonard crediting a rising international following for country music and an easier pitch to top international talent.

### Key Points
- The Long Road Festival's 2026 edition (27-30 Aug, Leicestershire) saw 30% year-over-year audience growth.
- Creative director Baylen Leonard ties the growth to country/Americana's rising international profile, including Nashville's growing reputation abroad.
- Organizers say securing top international country talent now requires "not a lot of convincing."
