# AXS Names Stephanie Streeter to Lead Partnership Sales

## Summary

AEG Global Partnerships and AXS have named Stephanie Streeter to a newly created VP of Partnership Sales role, tasked with expanding brand and technology partnerships across AXS's ticketing platform and AEG's live-entertainment portfolio. Streeter previously led AXS's Theaters & Residencies division and will be based in the Bay Area to build relationships with tech companies.

### Key Points
- New VP of Partnership Sales role created at AEG Global Partnerships/AXS.
- Comes as AXS expects to process nearly $5 billion in ticketing transactions in 2026.
- Focus is brand sponsorship and partnership revenue, not ticketing product or pricing changes.
- Role complements existing AXS sponsorship-sales leadership under Erin Zinser.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/10/AXS.webp)

AEG Global Partnerships and AXS announced Tuesday the appointment of Stephanie Streeter as vice president of partnership sales, a newly created position focused on expanding brand partnerships across the ticketing platform and AEG’s broader live entertainment portfolio.

Streeter, who most recently led AXS’s Theaters & Residencies division, will report to Nick Baker, president and COO of AEG Global Partnerships. Based in the San Francisco Bay Area, she will also work to strengthen the company’s relationships with technology companies and other potential commercial partners in Silicon Valley.

The appointment comes as AXS expects to process nearly $5 billion in ticketing transactions in 2026, with the company looking to translate the growing reach of its ticketing platform into additional sponsorship and brand partnership opportunities.

Streeter will work with brands and agencies to develop partnerships that leverage AXS’s ticketing technology, digital platforms and relationships with venues and event organizers across sports, music and entertainment. The initiative is designed to give advertisers and sponsors opportunities to engage consumers at multiple points, from the discovery and purchase of tickets through attendance at live events.
