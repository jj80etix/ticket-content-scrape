# Def Leppard Announces 45-Date 2027 Tour With Goo Goo Dolls and The Struts

## Summary

Def Leppard announced a 45-date 2027 North American arena tour with the Goo Goo Dolls and The Struts, running March 2 (Allentown, PA) through July 2 (Indianapolis). The tour follows a February 2026 Caesars Palace residency and the recent single "Rejoice."

### Key Points
- 45-date tour spans major arenas: Madison Square Garden, United Center, Scotiabank Arena, Chase Center, and the Hollywood Bowl.
- Lineup pairs Def Leppard with four-time Grammy-nominated Goo Goo Dolls and British rockers The Struts.
- Follows Def Leppard's Feb. 2026 residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas.
- Band has continued recording between live dates in 2026, including the single "Rejoice."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Def-Leppard-2027-tour.webp)

Def Leppard has announced a 45-date North American tour for 2027, bringing the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame band back to arenas across the United States and Canada alongside Goo Goo Dolls and special guests The Struts.

The extensive run will begin March 2 at PPL Center in Allentown, Pennsylvania, and conclude July 2 at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. The itinerary includes performances at major venues such as Madison Square Garden in New York, United Center in Chicago, Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, Chase Center in San Francisco and the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles.

The tour brings together three acts representing different generations of rock music. Def Leppard, known for hits including “Pour Some Sugar on Me,” “Photograph” and “Love Bites,” will headline the outing, while four-time Grammy-nominated Goo Goo Dolls and British rock band The Struts round out the lineup.

The announcement follows another active touring year for Def Leppard, including [a February 2026 residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/07/def-leppard-sin-city-residency-2026/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The group released the single “Rejoice” earlier in 2026 and has continued recording between live performances.
