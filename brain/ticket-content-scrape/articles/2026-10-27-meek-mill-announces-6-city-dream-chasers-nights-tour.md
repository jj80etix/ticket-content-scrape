# Meek Mill Announces 6-City ‘Dream Chasers Nights’ Tour

## Summary

Meek Mill extended his "Dream Chasers Nights" run, produced with Live Nation Urban, with six new fall dates including his annual Philadelphia hometown show. Tickets go on artist presale Oct. 27 and public sale Oct. 30.

### Key Points
- Run opens Nov. 24 at Philadelphia's Xfinity Mobile Arena.
- Additional dates in Los Angeles, Boston, Baltimore, Chicago and Atlanta through Dec. 20.
- Presented in partnership with Live Nation Urban.

Meek Mill has extended his “Dream Chasers Nights” show, in partnership with Live Nation Urban, with six new dates this fall. The run begins Nov. 24 with the previously announced “Meek Mill & Friends” set at Philadelphia’s Xfinity Mobile Arena for his annual hometown show. From there, the run continues with five headlining dates in major cities, including Los Angeles, Boston, Baltimore, Chicago and Atlanta.

Tickets will be available starting with artist presale beginning Oct. 27 at 12 p.m. local time. The artist presale password is DREAMCHASERS.

The public on sale begins Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. local time. For tickets and more information, visit meekmill.com.

DREAM CHASERS NIGHTS 2026 DATES:

Tue, Nov. 24 – Philadelphia, PA – Xfinity Mobile Arena

Sun, Nov. 29 – Los Angeles, CA – Hollywood Palladium

Sat, Dec. 5 – Boston, MA – MGM Music Hall at Fenway

Fri, Dec. 11 – Baltimore, MD – CFG Bank Arena

Fri, Dec. 18 – Chicago, IL – Aragon Ballroom

Sun, Dec. 20 – Atlanta, GA – Coca-Cola Roxy
