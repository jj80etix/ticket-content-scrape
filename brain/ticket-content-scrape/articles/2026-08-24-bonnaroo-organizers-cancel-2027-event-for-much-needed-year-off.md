# Bonnaroo Organizers Cancel 2027 Event for ‘Much-Needed Year Off’

## Summary

Bonnaroo organizers announced the festival will not be held in 2027, citing the need for The Farm to recover after back-to-back years of severe weather damage, including a heavy rainstorm during the 2026 edition. Despite recent investments in drainage, roadways, and grass to improve site resiliency, organizers say more recovery time is needed before the venue can host again.

### Key Points
- 2026 edition ran June 11-14 after 2025's event was cut short by severe weather.
- Organizers had spent heavily on 4.5 miles of new roadways and 135 acres of new grass ahead of 2026.
- No confirmation yet on a 2028 return; fans had been surveyed on shifting the festival later in the year.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Bonnaroo.webp)

Bonnaroo festivalgoers will have to wait at least another year for a music festival on The Farm.

Bonnaroo organizers released an official statement on their website and social media channels confirming that the festival will not be held next year “to recuperate from back to back year of storms, including the heavy rainstorm on Sunday this year.” The 2026 event, held from June 11-14, [featured improvements](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/07/bonnaroo-2026-dates-festival-site-changes/) after the 2025 edition was [cut short due to severe weather.](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/06/bonnaroo-cancels-2025-festival-due-to-severe-weather/)

“As you know, we spent a great deal of effort and money working to improve the campgrounds and Centeroo in the months leading into the 2026 festival,” organizers said. “That included improved drainage, 4.5 miles of new roadways, and 135 acres of new grass, all which strengthened The Farm’s resiliency to Mother Nature. With limited time to recuperate from back to back years of storms, including the heavy rainstorm on Sunday this year, The Farm, particularly the main venue, needs a break to rest and recover.”

“So, here’s what we’ve decided: Bonnaroo will be taking a much-needed year off in 2027. This wasn’t an easy decision, and not one we take lightly, but in our hearts, we know this break will only make Bonnaroo better. We hope you’ll continue radiating positivity in your community throughout the year and thank you for loving this festival as much as we do.”

The news isn’t entirely surprising for fans; there has been a lot of speculation over Bonnaroo’s return to The Farm over the past few weeks. Additionally, organizers surveyed fans in the past over whether they would prefer a Bonnaroo event later in the summer, or as late as September/October.

Organizers have not confirmed nor denied a festival in 2028.
