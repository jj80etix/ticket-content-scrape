# Flo Rida and Mitchell Tenpenny Announce 2027 Whistle & Whiskey Tour

## Summary

Flo Rida and Mitchell Tenpenny announced the "Whistle & Whiskey Tour" for February 2027, with Jay Webb opening.

### Key Points
- Tour kicks off February 3 at AMSOIL Arena in Duluth, MN.
- Additional dates: Sioux City, IA (Feb. 4); Grand Forks, ND (Feb. 6); Rochester, MN (Feb. 7).
- Pairs Flo Rida's pop/hip-hop catalog with Tenpenny's country hits.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/FloRida-Whistle-Whiskey.webp)

Flo Rida and Mitchell Tenpenny are teaming up for the Whistle & Whiskey Tour in February 2027, with Jay Webb joining the lineup as the opening act.

The limited tour is slated to kick off February 3 at AMSOIL Arena in Duluth, Minnesota. The schedule continues February 4 at Tyson Events Center in Sioux City, Iowa, and February 6 at Alerus Center in Grand Forks, North Dakota, before reaching Mayo Civic Center Arena in Rochester, Minnesota, on February 7.

The shows will pair Flo Rida’s pop and hip-hop catalog with Tenpenny’s country hits.

“Whistle & Whiskey isn’t about choosing between country and hip-hop — it’s about bringing everybody together for a party,” the announcement said. “Fans can expect a night packed with recognizable songs, sing-along moments and high-energy performances from the first song through the final encore.”

Additional dates are expected to be announced.

A complete list of tour dates can be found below:

## Whistle & Whiskey Tour Dates

February 3, 2027 — Duluth, Minnesota — AMSOIL Arena  
February 4, 2027 — Sioux City, Iowa — Tyson Events Center  
February 6, 2027 — Grand Forks, North Dakota — Alerus Center  
February 7, 2027 — Rochester, Minnesota — Mayo Civic Center Arena
