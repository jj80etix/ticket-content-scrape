# Tim McGraw Announces Five New Dates at Caesars Palace

## Summary

Tim McGraw announced five new Las Vegas performances at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace for May 21–22 and 27–29, 2027, following his sold-out 2026 residency run and Country Music Hall of Fame induction.

### Key Points
- Tickets go on sale Nov. 9, 2026 at 10 a.m. PT.
- McGrawONE+ fan-club presale begins Nov. 5; Citi cardmember and Caesars Rewards/Live Nation All Access presales run Nov. 5–6 through Nov. 8.
- Setlist spans three decades of McGraw's catalog in an intimate Colosseum setting.

## Transcript

![Current image: TimMcGraw P 0501 072820 RobbyKlein MgmtOwns](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/TimMcGraw_P_0501_072820_RobbyKlein_MgmtOwns-1024x683.jpg)

Country Music Hall of Fame member Tim McGraw returns to Vegas in 2027. Photo courtesy Robby Klein Management

Following his sold-out 2026 engagement, Tim McGraw announced five new performances of “Time McGraw: Live in Las Vegas” returning on May 21-22, 27-29, 2027, at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace.

The announcement comes on the heels of McGraw’s induction into the Country Music Hall of Fame. The only-in-Vegas shows offer fans an opportunity to experience McGraw in a more intimate setting featuring with a setlist that spans three decades of hits.

Tickets go on sale Nov. 9 at 10:00 AM/PT. McGraw fans will have first access to a pre-sale via the McGrawONE+ fan club beginning Nov. 5 at 10:00 AM/PT.

Citi is the official card of Time McGraw at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace. Cardmembers will have access to presale tickets beginning Nov. 5 at 10:00 AM/PT until Nov. 8 at 10:00 PM/PT through the Citi Entertainment program.

In addition, Caesars Rewards Members as well as Live Nation All Access customers will have access to a presale beginning Nov. 6 at 10:00 AM/PT. All presales will end Nov. 8 at 10:00 PM/PT.
