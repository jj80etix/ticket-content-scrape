# Lisa Expands ‘Viva La Lisa’ Las Vegas Residency to Six Shows

## Summary

Lisa (Blackpink) expanded her "Viva La Lisa" residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace from four to six shows after the original four sold out in under 10 minutes, adding Nov. 12 and Nov. 29 dates.

### Key Points
- Original Nov. 13–14 and Nov. 27–28 shows sold out in under 10 minutes.
- Added Nov. 12 (precedes the first weekend) and Nov. 29 (extends the second weekend).
- Billed as the first Las Vegas residency by a K-pop artist.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/BLACKPINKs-Lisa-at-Las-Vegas-Residency-at-Caesars-Palace.webp)

Lisa has added two performances to her “Viva La Lisa” residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace, expanding the November engagement from four shows to six.

The Blackpink member announced the expansion after the four original performances sold out in under 10 minutes. The November 12 show will precede the previously announced November 13 and 14 dates, while the November 29 performance extends the second weekend beyond its November 27 and 28 shows. All six concerts will take place at the Caesars Palace venue.

“Viva La Lisa” marks Lisa’s first Las Vegas residency. Caesars Entertainment described her as the first K-pop artist to stage a residency in the city when it announced the original dates in March.

The concerts follow an active stretch of solo releases and performances for Lisa. Her debut solo album, *Alter Ego*, arrived in 2025 and reached No. 7 on the Billboard 200 and No. 1 on Billboard’s Top Album Sales chart. Its singles include “Rockstar” and “New Woman,” her collaboration with Rosalía.

Lisa also made her solo Coachella debut in 2025 and more recently, Lisa released “SaWaDiKa,” the first single from her forthcoming EP *Press Play*, which is scheduled for October 23.

The new dates are November 12 and 29, with tickets going on sale Wednesday, September 30, at 10 a.m. Pacific time.
