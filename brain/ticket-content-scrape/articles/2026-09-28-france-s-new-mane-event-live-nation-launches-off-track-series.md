# France’s new mane event: Live Nation launches Off Track series

## Summary

Live Nation France is launching a new open-air concert series, Off Track, at the Hippodrome ParisLongchamp horse racing track in Paris, aiming to build an artist- and experience-led outdoor event brand in the French capital. Alex Warren is the first confirmed headliner for the 2027 debut.

### Key Points
- Series debuts 9-20 July 2027 with seven event nights at Hippodrome ParisLongchamp.
- Alex Warren headlines 13 July as the first confirmed act.
- Venue sits in Bois de Boulogne Park, western Paris.
- Positioned as a boutique, experience-led outdoor series distinct from Live Nation's existing French festival slate.
