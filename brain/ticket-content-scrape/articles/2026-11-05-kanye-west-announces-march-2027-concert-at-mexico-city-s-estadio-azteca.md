# Kanye West Announces March 2027 Concert at Mexico City’s Estadio Azteca

## Summary

Kanye West announced a March 19, 2027 concert at Mexico City's Estadio Banorte (formerly Estadio Azteca), adding another stadium date to his busy 2026–2027 touring schedule. It marks his return to the Mexican capital just over a year after his January 2026 shows there.

### Key Points
- March 19, 2027 show at the newly renovated Estadio Banorte (Estadio Azteca), renovated for the FIFA World Cup.
- Follows West's January 30–31, 2026 Mexico City shows, his first in the country since 2008.
- Precedes two confirmed November 2026 US stadium shows (Arlington, TX and Glendale, AZ).

Kanye West, also known as Ye, has announced a March 19, 2027, concert at Mexico City’s Estadio Banorte, formerly known as Estadio Azteca, adding another stadium performance to his upcoming schedule.

The March performance will mark West’s return to the Mexican capital just over a year after he played two concerts at Monumental Plaza de Toros México on January 30 and 31, 2026. Those shows were his first in the country since 2008, ending an absence of more than 17 years.

The venue, one of Mexico’s largest stadiums, reopened in 2026 following extensive renovations ahead of the FIFA World Cup.

The Mexico City announcement follows a series of stadium bookings for West in 2026, including two upcoming U.S. performances. He is scheduled to appear November 7 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, followed by a November 21 show at State Farm Stadium in Glendale, Arizona.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [tour.yeezy.com](https://tour.yeezy.com).
