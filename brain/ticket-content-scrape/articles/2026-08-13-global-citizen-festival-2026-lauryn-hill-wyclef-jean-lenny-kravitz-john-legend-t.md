# Global Citizen Festival 2026: Lauryn Hill, Wyclef Jean, Lenny Kravitz, John Legend, TOMORROW X TOGETHER

## Summary

Global Citizen Festival returns to New York's Central Park on September 26 with a lineup led by Ms. Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean (celebrating the Fugees' *The Score* 30th anniversary), Lenny Kravitz, John Legend, Lainey Wilson, Shaboozey, and Tomorrow X Together, hosted by Hugh Jackman. Tickets remain free via an advocacy-action app, with a new option this year to secure a ticket instantly via a $25 charitable donation.

### Key Points
- Sept. 26, 2026, Central Park Great Lawn; hosted by Hugh Jackman with Rachel Brosnahan and Gayle King appearing.
- Tickets earned via advocacy actions in the Global Citizen app, or new this year, a $25 donation for instant access (limited supply).
- Campaign goals: education access for 2M children, support for 1.5M smallholder farmers, electricity for 2.5M homes.
- Follows Global Citizen's involvement in the FIFA World Cup Final Halftime Show, which raised $60M for its Education Fund.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/global-citizen.jpg)

Global Citizen Festival is returning to New York City this fall with a stacked lineup led by Ms. Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean, Lenny Kravitz, John Legend, Lainey Wilson, Shaboozey and TOMORROW X TOGETHER.

The 2026 edition will take over Central Park’s Great Lawn on Saturday, September 26, with Hugh Jackman returning as host. Rachel Brosnahan and Gayle King are also slated to appear, with additional guests still to be announced.

One of the night’s biggest draws will see Hill and Jean celebrate the 30th anniversary of the Fugees’ landmark album *The Score*.

As always, Global Citizen Festival tickets won’t be sold through a traditional on-sale. Fans can earn free tickets by completing advocacy actions through the [Global Citizen app](https://www.globalcitizen.org/en/festival/nyc/2026/) and entering a ticket lottery.
