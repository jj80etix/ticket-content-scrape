# Nominations Open For Pollstar’s 2026 Impact International: UK/Euro

## Summary

Pollstar opened nominations for its 2026 Impact International: UK/Euro list, honoring leaders in the UK/European live industry, with submissions due September 2.

### Key Points
- Editorial team selects recipients with industry-submitted nominations.
- Honorees announced in Pollstar's November 2026 issue.
- Submit nominations to editorial@pollstar.com.

## Transcript

![impact ukero](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/impact-ukero.png)

impact ukero

*Pollstar* is accepting nominations for the 2026 edition of Impact International: UK/Euro, which honors the leaders in the UK and Europe having the greatest impact on today’s live industry.  
  
This list is made up of individuals who have shown a commitment to excellence, creative problem solving, strategic management, financial success, top negotiating skills, artist development and a vision for the future.  
  
*Pollstar’* s editorial team will select this year’s recipients with guidance from the industry’s nominations.  
  
Submissions are accepted through Wednesday, Sept. 2 at 5 p.m. BST. The 2026 Impact International: UK/Euro honorees will be announced in the November 2026 issue of *Pollstar.*

Barrie Marshall MBE, founder and chairman of Marshall Arts Ltd., was featured on the cover of *Pollstar* ‘s 2025 Impact International: UK/Euro issue along with Sir Paul McCartney.

Check out the full list of 2025 honorees [here](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/11/07/pollstars-2025-impact-international-uk-euro-honors/).

Please submit nominations for Impact International: UK/Euro to editorial@pollstar.com.
