# Treefort Music Fest Reveals 2027 Lineup Led by Modest Mouse, Orville Peck

## Summary

Treefort Music Fest announced its first-wave 2027 lineup, headlined by Modest Mouse, Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles and Courtney Barnett, for its 15th-anniversary edition in downtown Boise, Idaho, March 31-April 4. The five-day festival will feature more than 180 artists from 18 countries and 28 U.S. states, with additional lineup announcements expected ahead of the event.

### Key Points
- 2027 edition runs March 31-April 4 in downtown Boise, Idaho, marking the festival's 15th anniversary.
- First-wave headliners: Modest Mouse (Treefort debut), Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles, Courtney Barnett.
- More than 180 artists from 18 countries and 28 U.S. states expected across the five days.
- Additional scheduling and artist announcements still to come.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Treefort-Music-Fest.webp)

Treefort Music Fest has announced the first wave of artists for its 2027 edition, with Modest Mouse, Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles and Courtney Barnett among the headliners scheduled to perform in downtown Boise, Idaho, from March 31 through April 4.

The five-day festival will celebrate its 15th anniversary with more than 180 artists representing 18 countries and 28 U.S. states.

The initial lineup also features Slow Pulp, horsegiirL, Waylon Wyatt, LP Giobbi, All Them Witches, Joy Oladokun, Kenny Mason, Enter Shikari, Eartheater, Sleaford Mods, Iceage, Balance and Composure, Colby Acuff, Thee Sinseers and The Altons.

Modest Mouse will make its Treefort debut, while Thee Sacred Souls return to the festival after first appearing in 2022. The 2027 event will also feature Australian singer-songwriter Courtney Barnett and Jesse Welles, whose socially conscious folk music has gained a substantial following.

Additional scheduling information and artist announcements are expected ahead of the festival.

For more information and to stay up-to-date on the latest details, festivalgoers can visit [treefortmusicfest.com](http://treefortmusicfest.com).
