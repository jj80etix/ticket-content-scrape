# A$AP Rocky Adds Australia and New Zealand Dates to ‘Don’t Be Dumb’ Tour

## Summary

A$AP Rocky has added Australia and New Zealand dates to his "Don't Be Dumb" tour. The piece covers the newly added international leg.

### Key Points
- A$AP Rocky adds Australia and New Zealand dates to the "Don't Be Dumb" tour.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AAP-Rocky.webp)

A$AP Rocky has added five arena concerts in Australia and New Zealand to his “Don’t Be Dumb” World Tour, announcing his first performances in the region in eight years.

The December extension includes four Australian shows and one New Zealand stop. The run opens December 5 at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena and concludes December 17 at Perth’s RAC Arena. The announcement follows the completion of Rocky’s European and U.K. dates.

Following the Melbourne opener, Rocky will perform at Sydney’s Afterpay Arena on December 9. He then heads to Auckland’s Spark Arena for a December 12 concert before returning to Australia for Brisbane Entertainment Centre on December 14 and the closing Perth show.

Tickets go on general sale October 16 at 1 p.m. local time. For the Australian dates, artist, Vodafone and Mastercard presales begin October 14 at noon local time. Live Nation and Westfield presales follow October 15 at noon.

The new dates extend a touring campaign supporting *Don’t Be Dumb*, Rocky’s fourth studio album and his first full-length release since 2018’s *Testing*.
