# HUM Announces 12-Date U.S. Tour for 2027

## Summary

Post-rock band HUM has announced a 12-date U.S. tour for 2027, its first full tour since 2015, opening March 3 in Atlanta and closing July 17 in San Francisco. The Life and Times will support on all dates, with additional support from Pelican and Spotlights on select shows.

### Key Points
- Dates span Texas (3 shows), Denver, Kansas City, four East Coast cities in May, and two West Coast dates in July.
- Follows HUM's Slide Away festival appearances earlier this year, its first live performances in nearly seven years.
- The tour includes live debuts of songs from the band's 2020 album *Inlet*.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/HUM-1.webp)

HUM has announced a 12-date United States tour for 2027, beginning March 3 at The Eastern in Atlanta.

After the Atlanta opener, HUM will head to Texas for three consecutive performances. The band will play House of Blues in Houston on March 5, The Bomb Factory in Dallas on March 6 and United Heritage Hall in Austin on March 7. The tour resumes April 22 at Mission Ballroom in Denver, followed by an April 24 concert at The Midland Theatre in Kansas City, Missouri.

In May, HUM will play four East Coast cities over four nights. The run begins May 20 at Franklin Music Hall in Philadelphia, followed by The Anthem in Washington, D.C., on May 21 and Terminal 5 in New York on May 22. The band will then perform May 23 at Roadrunner in Boston.

HUM will head to the West Coast in July for a performance at Showbox SoDo in Seattle on July 13. The tour is set to wrap up on July 17 at The Warfield in San Francisco.

The Life and Times will support HUM on all dates. Pelican will join the band for four East Coast shows in May, and Spotlights will appear at the two West Coast concerts in July.

The 2027 dates will be HUM’s first full tour since 2015. Its Slide Away appearances earlier this year marked the band’s first performances in nearly seven years and included the live debut of songs from its 2020 album, *Inlet*.
