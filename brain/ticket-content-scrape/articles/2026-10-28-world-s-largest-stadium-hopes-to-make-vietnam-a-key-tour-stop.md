# World’s largest stadium hopes to make Vietnam a key tour stop

## Summary

Venue operator Legends Global has partnered with Vingroup to operate the 135,000-seat VinFast–Trong Dong Stadium in Hanoi, set to become the world's largest stadium when it opens in July 2027, as part of a push to position Vietnam as a key international touring market.

### Key Points
- Legends Global and Vingroup partnership to operate VinFast–Trong Dong Stadium in Hanoi.
- Stadium will seat 135,000 and become the world's largest when it opens July 2027.
- Legends Global aims to attract major international touring events to the venue.
