# Jessie Reyez Reveals 13-Date North American ‘A Little Vengeance Tour’

## Summary

Jessie Reyez announced her 2026 "A Little Vengeance Tour," a 13-date North American headlining run supporting her fourth studio album. It's her largest headlining tour to date, running November 3 (Seattle) through November 24 (Toronto).

### Key Points
- 13 dates across the US and Canada, opening in Seattle, closing in her hometown of Toronto.
- Supports fourth studio album *A Little Vengeance*, released June 12.
- A portion of proceeds benefits the Jessie Reyez Fund, supporting underrepresented communities in music.
- Artist presale ran Aug. 25; general public onsale began Aug. 28 via Ticketmaster.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Jessie-Reyez-A-Little-Vengeance-Tour.webp)

Jessie Reyez is hitting the road this fall for her largest headlining tour to date. The Canadian singer-songwriter has announced the 2026 “A Little Vengeance Tour,” a 13-date North American trek supporting her recently released fourth studio album, *A Little Vengeance*.

The tour will begin November 3 in Seattle and travel throughout the United States and Canada before concluding November 24 in Reyez’s hometown of Toronto. Following the opening night in Seattle, Reyez will head down the West Coast for performances in Sacramento, Oakland, San Diego and Los Angeles. The itinerary also includes stops in Phoenix, Houston, Dallas and Chicago.

The final stretch will take Reyez to Washington, D.C., Brooklyn and Boston before she returns to Canada for the tour finale at Coca-Cola Coliseum.

The run represents Reyez’s biggest headlining outing to date and follows the June 12 release of *A Little Vengeance*. The project marks the fourth full-length album of her career.

Notably, the singer is using the upcoming tour to support initiatives aimed at creating greater access within the music business. A portion of proceeds from the “A Little Vengeance Tour” will benefit the Jessie Reyez Fund.

The organization works to provide opportunities for women and people from historically underrepresented communities seeking careers in the music industry. The fund also supports additional programs and initiatives connected to music.

An artist presale for the tour began Tuesday, August 25 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets become available to the general public Friday, August 28 at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Reyez’s official website](https://alittlevengeance.com/tour).
