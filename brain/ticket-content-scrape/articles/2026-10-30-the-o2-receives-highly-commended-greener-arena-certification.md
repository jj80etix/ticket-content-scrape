# The O2 Receives ‘Highly Commended’ Greener Arena Certification

## Summary

London's The O2 received a "Highly Commended" rating under A Greener Future's Greener Arena Certification program, its highest sustainability accreditation to date, following four years of emissions-reduction work since joining the program in 2022.

### Key Points
- 27% reduction in carbon emissions per show and 26% per fan since 2022
- Food-service emissions down 31.5% since 2019; recycling/composting rate up 15% in 2026
- Improvements driven by energy efficiency upgrades, a renewable power purchase agreement with Ecotricity, EV transition for on-site vehicles, and closer tenant waste coordination

## Transcript

The O2 has received a “Highly Commended” rating under A Greener Future’s Greener Arena Certification program, marking the highest sustainability accreditation the London venue has achieved.

The recognition follows four years of work to reduce emissions and improve environmental practices across the venue. Since joining the certification programme in 2022, The O2 has moved up two accreditation tiers. During that period, it reported a 27% reduction in carbon emissions per show and a 26% reduction in emissions per fan.

A Greener Future, an independent not-for-profit organisation known as AGF, awarded the certification as The O2 continues initiatives involving energy use, transport, food service and waste management.

“Achieving a Highly Commended Greener Arena Certification is a fantastic milestone for The O2 and reflects years of commitment, innovation and collaboration across the business,” said Sam Booth, director of sustainability at AEG International.

“Sustainability is embedded into how we operate as a venue, and this recognition demonstrates the progress the team have made in reducing our environmental impact while continuing to deliver world-class live entertainment for millions of visitors every year.”

The venue attributed its emissions reductions to several operational changes. Those include energy efficiency improvements carried out by its facilities team and the introduction of a renewable electricity power purchase agreement with Ecotricity. The O2 has also transitioned to electric vehicles for on-site use.

Food service represents another area of reported improvement. Emissions associated with that part of the operation have fallen 31.5% since 2019, according to the venue. That figure covers a longer period than the four-year comparison used for its emissions per show and per fan.

The O2 also reported a 15% increase in its recycling and composting rate in 2026. It credited closer work with on-site tenants, improved waste segregation and a reduction in overall waste streams for that progress.

Booth said the certification marked an achievement while further environmental work remained a priority. “We’ve come a long way since beginning this journey in 2022, and while we’re proud of what has been achieved, we’re equally focused on continuing to drive progress and finding new ways to reduce emissions, minimise waste and support a more sustainable future for the live events industry.”

AGF chief executive and co-founder Claire O’Neill said the venue’s efforts could have an influence beyond its own operations.

“When an iconic venue like The O2 strives for excellence in sustainability, the impact ripples throughout the sector.”

O’Neill also highlighted the broader responsibilities reflected in the venue’s approach, including its relationship with employees, audiences and the surrounding community.

“We’re really happy to see The O2 achieve ‘Highly Commended’ in the Greener Arena Certification, showing continuous improvements and an unwavering commitment to protect the environment, to care for their staff and visitors, and to be good neighbours in their community. Congratulations to the whole team.”
