## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/unnamed.jpg)

Photo by Travis Shinn

A Perfect Circle has a new single out this week, “To Whom It May Concern,” and new tour dates set for 2027. The New North American headline dates feature Puscifer and The Cult on select shows.  
  
“To Whom It May Concern” is A Perfect Circle’s second new single this year and the follow-up to “Starless,” which just reached #1 at Active Rock radio.  
  
The new tune was recorded at Billy Howerdel’s Lankershim Ranch Studio in Studio City, California. Howerdel produced the single and it was mixed by Matty Green (U2, Florence + the Machine, TV on the Radio), with longtime collaborator Josh Freese playing drums.  
  
“It’s exciting to finally get some new music out into the world, and seeing ‘Starless’ reach #1 at rock radio this week has made it even sweeter! The outpouring of support is so very much appreciated. ‘To Whom It May Concern’ is next up! More to come,” Howerdel said in a statement.

The 2027 North American trek launches April 13 in Vancouver, British Columbia, at Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Centre and runs through a June 13 show in Phoenix at Talking Stick Resort Amphitheater. Stops on the tour include Detroit, Boston, Houston, St. Louis, Nashville, Chicago, Seattle, Los Angeles, Las Vegas and the famed Red Rocks in Morrison, Colorado, outside of Denver.

Puscifer will join A Perfect Circle for all Canadian performances as well as Detroit while The Cult will support on the remaining U.S. dates (April 27 to June 13).

“Very much looking forward to pulling double duty through Canada with A Perfect Circle and Puscifer,” Maynard James Keenan says. “Been way, way too long. New tunes, new dance moves, new hair hats. Figure it out. #figureitout”

A Perfect Circle also has previously announced fall dates booked with Puscifer, as well as Dave Hill, including gigs across Latin America, Australia, New Zealand, Japan and Hawaii. Tickets for all previously announced shows are available now via [APerfectCircle.com](http://aperfectcircle.com).  
  
Presales for the new dates are ongoing, with the general onsale on Friday, Oct. 2 at 10 a.m. local time.

A Perfect Circle is managed by Shelter Music Group’s Dino Paredes and the band’s tour dates are booked by WME.
