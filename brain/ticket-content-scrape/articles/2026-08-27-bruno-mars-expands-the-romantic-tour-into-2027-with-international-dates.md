# Bruno Mars Expands ‘The Romantic Tour’ Into 2027 With International Dates

## Summary

Bruno Mars extended "The Romantic Tour" into 2027 with 26 additional dates across Japan, Australia, New Zealand, and Southeast/East Asia, plus a fifth Mexico City show added to the existing 2026 leg. Tyla and Anderson.Paak (as DJ Pee.Wee) continue as support.

### Key Points
- 2027 leg runs Jan. 4 (Nagoya, Japan) through May 26 (Goyang, South Korea): Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Philippines, South Korea.
- Fifth Mexico City date added Dec. 11, 2026 at Estadio GNP Seguros; on sale Aug. 31.
- Australia/NZ artist presale (Ticketmaster-registration) begins Sept. 2; general onsale Sept. 4. Asian-market presale registration via artist site through Sept. 7.
- Tyla joins for Australia/NZ dates; Anderson.Paak continues as DJ Pee.Wee throughout.

## Transcript

![Bruno Mars The Romantic Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Bruno-Mars-tour-dates.webp)

Bruno Mars The Romantic Tour

Bruno Mars is extending “The Romantic Tour” into 2027, adding 26 dates across Australia, New Zealand and Asia as the global trek continues to grow.

The newly announced run will take Mars to Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines and South Korea. The 2027 leg of the tour will begin January 4 at Vantelin Dome in Nagoya, Japan. Mars will continue through a string of major international markets before wrapping up May 26 at Goyang Stadium in South Korea..

Tyla will join Mars as a special guest for the Australian and New Zealand dates. Anderson.Paak will also remain part of the tour, appearing at all of the newly announced concerts under his DJ Pee.Wee moniker.

The expansion also adds a fifth Mexico City concert at Estadio GNP Seguros on December 11, 2026. Tickets for the newly added performance are scheduled to go on sale August 31 at 2 p.m. local time.

Ticket sales for the Australia and New Zealand concerts will begin with an artist presale September 2. Fans must register through Ticketmaster by August 31 at 11:59 p.m. to participate. Remaining tickets are scheduled to go on sale to the general public September 4.

Fans seeking tickets for the newly announced Asian concerts can register for the artist presale through Mars’ official website through September 7. Presale and general public onsale schedules will differ depending on the individual market.

Mars is currently making his way through the North American portion of the tour, with stops still to come in Pittsburgh, Philadelphia, Foxborough, Indianapolis, Tampa, New Orleans, Inglewood and Santa Clara. The leg is scheduled to conclude with a five-show run at BC Place in Vancouver on October 14, 16, 17, 20 and 21.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Mars’ official website](https://www.brunomars.com/).

A complete list of tour dates can be found below:
