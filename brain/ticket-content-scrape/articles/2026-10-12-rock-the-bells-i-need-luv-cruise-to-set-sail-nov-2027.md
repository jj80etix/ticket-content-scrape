# Rock The Bells ‘I Need Luv’ Cruise To Set Sail Nov. 2027

## Summary

Rock the Bells' "I Need Luv" themed cruise will set sail in November 2027, with presale registration windows running through late October 2026. The announcement covers booking windows and cabin availability for the voyage.

### Key Points
- Rock the Bells "I Need Luv" cruise departs November 2027.
- Presale registration windows run through Oct. 18 and Oct. 26, 2026.
- Niche themed-cruise announcement with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

Long-running hip-hop brand Rock The Bells continues to expand its live experience reach, with the announcement of Rock The Bells Presents: I Need Luv Cruise — described as a new R&B vacation in partnership with Sixthman.

Sailing Nov.15-19, 2027 from Miami to Nassau, Bahamas aboard Norwegian Jewel, the inaugural I Need Luv cruise lineup features Tank, Xscape, Ginuwine, Mýa, K. Michelle, Kenny Lattimore, Eric Benét, Kindred the Family Soul and Eric Bellinger, with Jagged Edge performing a special port show, according to the announcement. Bryan-Michael Cox and DJ Aktive will also join the voyage, with additional artists and special guests to be announced.

To go along with the music, I Need Luv will “bring the theme of love to life” with matchmaking, candid conversations with relationship experts, wellness experiences and more.

“R&B has always given people the words for what they feel but don’t always know how to say,” Rock The Bells founder LL Cool J says in the announcement. “It’s how a lot of us learned to tell somebody we love them, to make things right, to start over. With I Need Luv, we wanted to take that connection people already have with R&B and build an entire vacation around it. Whether you come with your partner, your friends or on your own, come see what happens when love sets sail baby…”

The launch builds on the momentum of the Rock The Bells Cruise: A Hip-Hop Experience, now in its fourth year with [Sixthman, which recently celebrated 25 years in business.](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/19/rocking-the-boat-sixthman-celebrates-25-years-of-living-loud-with-community-inspired-festivals-at-sea/)

Potential guests can sign up for the first round of presale access now through Oct. 18 at 11:59 p.m. ET. The final opportunity to register for presale access ends Oct. 26 at 11:59 p.m. ET. Any remaining cabins will become available to the public beginning Oct. 28 at 2:00 p.m. ET.
