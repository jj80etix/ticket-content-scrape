# Metallica Announces 12-Show North American Stadium Tour for 2027

## Summary

Metallica extended its M72 World Tour with a 12-date North American stadium run in 2027, joined by Limp Bizkit and Avatar, using its signature "in the round" stage configuration.

### Key Points
- Begins May 8, 2027 at BC Place, Vancouver; ends June 19 at Rice-Eccles Stadium, Salt Lake City
- Stops include San Diego, El Paso, San Antonio, Fayetteville, Kansas City, Raleigh, Indianapolis, Buffalo, Madison, and Cleveland
- Fan Club presale begins Sept. 21; general onsale Sept. 25
- Fifth year of the M72 World Tour, launched in support of "72 Seasons"

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Metallica-m72-world-tour.webp)

Metallica will extend its M72 World Tour into a fifth year in 2027, announcing a new 12-date North American stadium run with Limp Bizkit and Avatar.

The latest leg begins May 8 at BC Place in Vancouver. From there, the band is slated to make stops in cities such as San Diego, El Paso, San Antonio, Fayetteville, Kansas City, Raleigh, Indianapolis, Buffalo, Madison and Cleveland before wrapping up on June 19 in Salt Lake City at Rice-Eccles Stadium.

The shows will once again feature Metallica’s distinctive “in the round at the 50 yard line” configuration, placing the band’s circular stage at midfield with the Snake Pit in its center. The production has been a defining feature of M72 since the tour launched in support of the band’s 2023 album *72 Seasons*.

**| RELATED:**[**Metallica Expands Sphere Residency to 24 Shows**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/metallica-expands-sphere-residency-to-24-shows/) **|**

Tickets will first become available through Metallica Fan Club presales beginning Monday, September 21 at 10 a.m. local time. Citi cardmembers can access a presale beginning Tuesday, September 22 at 10 a.m. local time, while Verizon customers will have access to a presale for select U.S. dates beginning at the same time. Local presales are scheduled for Thursday, September 24, with the general public onsale beginning Friday, Sept. 25 at 10 a.m. local time.

The 2027 stadium dates will also come less than two months after Metallica completes its lengthy Life Burns Faster residency at Sphere in Las Vegas. The 24-show run stretches from October 2026 through March 13, 2027, after Metallica repeatedly expanded the residency with additional dates.

**| RELATED:**[**Metallica to Celebrate Mohegan Sun Arena’s 25th Anniversary with Two Shows**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/metallica-to-celebrate-mohegan-sun-arenas-25th-anniversary-with-two-shows/) **|**

The band has continued adding other performances around the M72 schedule as well. Metallica is scheduled to play two shows at Mohegan Sun Arena in Connecticut in November as part of the venue’s 25th anniversary celebration before beginning the bulk of its Sphere residency dates.

Metallica similarly expanded M72 back into North American stadiums in 2025, when its schedule included markets such as Toronto, Nashville, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Tampa, Santa Clara and Denver. Limp Bizkit was among the supporting acts on that leg alongside Pantera, Suicidal Tendencies and Ice Nine Kills.

For more information and additional details, fans visit [Metallica’s official website](https://www.metallica.com/).
