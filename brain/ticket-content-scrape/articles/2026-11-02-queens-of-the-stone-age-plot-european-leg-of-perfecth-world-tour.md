# Queens Of The Stone Age Plot European Leg Of ‘Perfecth World Tour’

## Summary

Pollstar's report on Queens of the Stone Age's newly announced European leg of the "Perfecth World Tour," running June 13 (Hanover) to June 30 (Pula, Croatia), which follows the previously announced North American arena dates.

### Key Points
- North American leg (with Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp) runs May 1-24, 2027.
- Band previously co-headlined the "Take Cover Tour" with Foo Fighters, including a $5.1M-grossing Nashville stop.
- Full European routing includes stops in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, and Croatia.

## Transcript

Queens of the Stone Age have announced the European leg of their “Perfecth World Tour.” The additional dates kick off on June 13 in Hanover, Germany, at the Gilde Parkbühne. The band will then wrap things up on June 30 at the Pula Arena in Croatia. See full tour routing below.

The band [previously announced](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/22/queens-of-the-stone-age-unveil-2027-perfecth-world-tour/) the North American leg of the tour in October, with the first date scheduled for May 1 at San Diego’s Viejas Arena and concluding May 24 at Boston’s TD Garden. The Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp will support the band on all dates. See full routing below.

Queens of the Stone Age previously teamed up with the Foo Fighters for the North American “Take Cover Tour,” running through the summer and the fall. The sold-out tour produced numerous highlights for the two legendary bands, including a stop at Nissan Stadium in Nashville that grossed $5,125,488, per Pollstar Boxoffice Reports.

Fans looking to catch a stop on the “Perfecth World Tour” can head to the band’s [website](https://qotsa.com/tour/).
