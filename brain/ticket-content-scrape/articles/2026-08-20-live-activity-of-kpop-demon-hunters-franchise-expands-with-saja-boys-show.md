# Live activity of KPop Demon Hunters franchise expands with Saja Boys show

## Summary

Andrew Choi and Neckwav, the singing voices behind KPop Demon Hunters' fictional Saja Boys, will play their first live show together at London's 2,000-capacity Outernet Live on Sept. 6, building on the animated franchise's expansion into real-world touring. The show follows AEG Presents and Netflix's announcement of a full KPop Demon Hunters arena tour, with venue and dates still to be confirmed.

### Key Points
- First-ever live Saja Boys performance, London's Outernet Live, Sept. 6.
- KPop Demon Hunters has passed 600M completed Netflix views; its soundtrack made K-pop and film-soundtrack chart history.
- AEG Presents/Netflix's arena tour for the franchise was announced in May, dates TBA; may use virtual performers/holograms for the fictional band.
- The related Huntr/x trio has already performed live at iHeartRadio Jingle Ball and Coachella.

Two of the boy band's voices have announced a standalone show at London's Outernet Live, adding to the franchise's growing live activity

![Andrew Choi & Neckwav](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Andrew-Choi-Neckwav.png)

Andrew Choi & Neckwav

Two of the voices of *KPop Demon Hunters* ‘ fictional boy band, the Saja Boys, have announced a standalone show in London, adding to the franchise’s growing live activity.

On 6 September, South Korean solo artists Andrew Choi and Neckwav – the voices of Jinu and Abby in the smash-hit Netflix phenomenon – will perform at the 2,000-capacity Outernet Live.

Prior to the show, fans will be immersed in the world of the series through an immersive artist experience across the wider Outernet London district, which contains the four-storey-high, floor-to-ceiling screens spanning 23,000 square feet.

“The passion and support we’ve seen from the Saja Boys fandom across the UK and Europe has been incredible, and so this performance is truly a dream come true,” says artist manager Rich Ting. “To make that moment a reality at Outernet Live, in one of the most spectacular and immersive venues in the world, makes it even more special.”

Earlier this year, Outernet CEO Philip O’Ferrall told *IQ* that it is aiming to capitalise on its [digital offerings](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/outernet-hails-new-era-for-live-with-venue-and-programming-refresh/) with live performances, which represent an “infinite creative and commercial opportunity” in a “new era for music” for the [Legends Global](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/01/legends-global-partners-with-cutting-edge-outernet-london/) -backed venue.

Outernet’s CMO, Jessica Dracup-Holland, adds: “Having relaunched Outernet Live with a renewed ambition to bring the world’s biggest artists to the heart of London, it’s a huge moment for us to welcome Saja Boys for their first-ever UK show. Their music has become a global phenomenon. What makes Outernet Live unique is the combination of an exceptional live venue, world-class sound and the immersive screens of Outernet London. We’re excited to create something that feels truly special for Saja Boys and their fans that couldn’t be done anywhere else.”

> “We’re excited to create something that feels truly special for Saja Boys”

Released in June 2025, *KPop Demon Hunters* quickly shattered Netflix’s all-time viewership record, capturing over 600 million completed views. The animated film centres on a K-pop girl group – Huntr/x – doubling as demon hunters, whose role becomes complicated when a demon-member boy band – the Saja Boys – rises to fame.

The film’s soundtrack is also historic, with *Golden* becoming the first K-pop song to win an Oscar and a Grammy, and the first film soundtrack to place four songs simultaneously in the *Billboard* Hot 100’s Top 10. On Spotify alone, the Saja Boys boast over 10 million monthly listeners.

This show marks the first live performance from the Saja Boys. The film’s core trio Huntr/x, voiced by Ejae, Audrey Nuna, and Rei Ami, have done several live performances together, including a slot at [iHeartRadio’s Jingle Ball](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/10/kpop-demon-hunters-trio-to-extend-into-live-space/) in Los Angeles and as a special guest during Katseye’s Coachella slot.

Back in May, AEG Presents and Netflix announced plans to stage an arena tour, the [*KPop Demon Hunters World Tour*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/05/kpop-demon-hunters-set-to-tour-arenas-globally/), though the dates and venues are still to be announced.

How the tour will come to fruition is still to be understood, with virtual performers or holograms a potential route for the animated characters.

A sequel for the film was confirmed in March and is projected for 2029.
