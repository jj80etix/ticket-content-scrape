# Garth Brooks Adds North Little Rock Dates to Blame It All On My Roots Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks added two North Little Rock, Arkansas dates (Feb 5–6, 2027) to his Blame It All On My Roots Tour, the first confirmed US market for next year's leg. Tickets go on sale Nov. 20 via Ticketmaster; the shows continue the tour's end-stage/in-the-round seating format.

### Key Points
- First 2027 US tour market confirmed; follows Brooks' current Seattle and Hamilton, Ontario dates
- Tour continues with Stagecoach (April 25) and a 10-date Ireland/Northern Ireland run (May–June 2027)
- Related coverage this run also flagged Brooks' Ireland ticket prices jumping 108% despite that market's face-value resale cap

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Garth-Brooks-Little-Rock-AR.jpg)

Garth Brooks will begin the 2027 leg of his Blame It All On My Roots Tour in Arkansas, announcing two concerts at Simmons Bank Arena in North Little Rock. The Little Rock area is the first U.S. market confirmed for the tour next year.

The Arkansas engagement is slated to open on Friday, February 5, with a second performance scheduled for Saturday, February 6. The concerts will bring Brooks back to the venue where he last performed for Central Arkansas audiences more than a decade ago.

The concerts will feature end-stage and in-the-round seating, continuing the seating arrangements used throughout the Blame It All On My Roots Tour.

In a statement accompanying the news, Brooks shared, “What I love about playing Arkansas is…it’s just the land of common sense. And they know their country music inside and out,” Brooks said. “I couldn’t pick a better place to start the 2027 leg of the Blame It All On My Roots Tour!”
