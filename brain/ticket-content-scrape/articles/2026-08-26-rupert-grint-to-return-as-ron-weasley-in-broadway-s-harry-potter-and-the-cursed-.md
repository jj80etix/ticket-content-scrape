# Rupert Grint to Return as Ron Weasley in Broadway’s ‘Harry Potter and the Cursed Child’

## Summary

Rupert Grint will return to Broadway's "Harry Potter and the Cursed Child" as Ron Weasley for a limited 17-week run at New York's Lyric Theatre, beginning February 2, 2027. He joins Tom Felton, who continues as adult Draco through January 2027. Registration for first ticket access opens through August 31, with presale September 1 and general onsale September 2.

### Key Points
- Grint's run: February 2 – May 30, 2027, Lyric Theatre, NYC.
- Reunites him on stage with fellow film cast member Tom Felton.
- First-access registration closes August 31; presale September 1; general onsale September 2.
- Grint calls the timing "full-circle," noting he and the character have both become fathers.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Rupert-Grint-Ron-Weasley-Harry-Potter-Broadway.webp)

Rupert Grint is returning to the role that made him a global star, with the actor set to play Ron Weasley in Broadway’s “Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” beginning in February 2027.

Grint, who portrayed Ron throughout all eight *Harry Potter* films, will join the production at New York’s Lyric Theatre for a limited 17-week engagement. His run will begin February 2 and continue through May 30, 2027.

The casting brings another familiar face from the film franchise to the Broadway production. Tom Felton, who played Draco Malfoy alongside Grint in the movies, is currently appearing as the adult Draco in “Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” and is scheduled to remain with the show through January 3, 2027.

Fans hoping to see Grint’s return to the Wizarding World can register for first access to tickets through August 31 at 5:59 p.m. ET. A presale will begin September 1 at 11 a.m. ET, followed by the general public on-sale September 2 at 11 a.m. ET.

“Ron Weasley has been a part of me since I was 11 years old and I can’t wait to meet him again in this stage of life,” Grint said in a statement.

The actor said the timing of his return gives the role a new meaning now that both he and Ron have become fathers.

“There is something so full-circle and so special about stepping back into Ron’s shoes now when we are both fathers; it is somehow both very familiar and completely new,” Grint said. “And the joy of being back on Broadway, this time in a role that defined so much of my life is truly exciting.”

Grint also recalled his reaction to seeing the stage production for the first time.

“The first time I saw ‘Cursed Child’ onstage, I was blown away and I can’t wait to set foot back on Platform 9 ¾ and pick up where I left off,” he said. “I am overjoyed to join this exceptional cast and crew at the Lyric Theatre this winter.”

Producers Sonia Friedman and Colin Callender said Grint’s performance in the original films helped define Ron Weasley for generations of fans.

“Rupert Grint’s performance as Ron Weasley in the eight Harry Potter films defined for generations what it meant to be a loyal and brave friend even in the face of adversity and hardship,” the producers said in a joint statement.

They added that his Broadway casting extends a character journey that began when Grint was a child.

“Rupert is not simply revisiting a role from his past,” Friedman and Callender said. “He is continuing a journey he began as a child and carrying it forward with all the life and experience he has gained since filming concluded.”

Grint previously made his Broadway debut in “It’s Only a Play,” appearing alongside Matthew Broderick and Nathan Lane. In London, he made his West End debut in Jez Butterworth’s “Mojo,” opposite Ben Whishaw.

Set 19 years after the events of “Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows,” “Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” follows the children of Harry Potter and his former Hogwarts classmates. Jack Thorne wrote the play from an original story by J.K. Rowling, Thorne and director John Tiffany.

The production premiered in London in 2016 before opening on Broadway in April 2018. It received 10 Tony Award nominations and won five, including Best Play.

Following Broadway’s reopening after the COVID-19 shutdown, “Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” returned as a condensed one-part production in December 2021. Another shorter version of the show was introduced at the Lyric Theatre in November 2024.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit Broadway’s official [“Harry Potter and the Cursed Child” website](https://broadway.harrypottertheplay.com/).
