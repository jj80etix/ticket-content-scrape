# Tour news: Charli xcx & Carín León extend outings

## Summary

IQ's tour-news roundup is led by Charli xcx's newly announced UK/EU arena run for next February on the Music, Fashion, Film Tour, following her North American dates, with Audrey Hobert supporting. Mexican star Carín León also announced a new run off the back of his Sphere residency debut. The roundup additionally flags new tour announcements from The Warning, Stevie Wonder, Andrea Bocelli, Victoria Monét, Cage The Elephant, Malcolm Todd, and Empire of the Sun.

### Key Points
- Charli xcx's UK/EU arena run begins Feb. 13 at Dublin's 3Arena, running through Glasgow, Manchester, London, and Amsterdam, wrapping Feb. 24 at Paris' Accor Arena.
- Audrey Hobert will support the European leg.
- Carín León announced a new tour following his Sphere residency debut (full details behind the roundup's excerpt cutoff).
- Additional acts covered in the same roundup: The Warning, Stevie Wonder, Andrea Bocelli, Victoria Monét, Cage The Elephant, Malcolm Todd, and Empire of the Sun.
