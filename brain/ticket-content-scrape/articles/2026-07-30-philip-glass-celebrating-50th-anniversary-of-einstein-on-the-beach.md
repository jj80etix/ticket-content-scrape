# Philip Glass Celebrating 50th Anniversary Of ‘Einstein On The Beach’

## Summary

Composer Philip Glass is marking his 90th birthday with a global slate of performances, including a concert version of Einstein on the Beach for its 50th anniversary and a new fully staged production premiering in Manchester in June 2027. Pollstar rounds up the extensive calendar of Glass tributes running from mid-2026 through early 2027 across opera houses, symphonies, and festivals worldwide.

### Key Points
- July 25, 2026 marked the 50th anniversary of Einstein on the Beach, Glass and Bob Wilson's narrative-free theater work.
- A new fully staged production (English National Opera, Factory International, Park Avenue Armory, Improbable; directed by Phelim McDermott) premieres June 2027 at Aviva Studios, Manchester.
- Concert versions of Einstein on the Beach are touring, including Narva Opera Festival (Aug. 15, 2026) and a run at Brooklyn Academy of Music (Nov. 19-21, 2026).
- Glass's Symphony No. 15 ("Lincoln") is premiering across multiple cities starting July 5, 2026 at Tanglewood.
- The 90th-birthday season runs into early 2027, culminating in a Carnegie Hall concert on Jan. 31, 2027.

Celebrated composer Philip Glass is celebrating his 90 <sup>th</sup> birthday with performances across the globe, including a show commemorating the 50 <sup>th</sup> anniversary of Glass and Bob Wilson’s theater work, *Einstein on the Beach*.

The Philip Glass Ensemble returns to venues across the globe with a concert version of Einstein on the Beach, which broke the conventions of the opera by not having a narrative and utilizing image, movement, repetition and music. A major new fully staged experiential production created by English National Opera, Factory International, Park Avenue Armory and Improbable, directed by Phelim McDermott, will premiere in June 2027 at Aviva Studios in Manchester. Narva Opera Festival presents a one-evening-only new staged production in Narva, Estonia on Aug. 15, and ICTUS and Collegium Vocale Gent continue their concert version tour in fall 2026. On the date of the 50th Anniversary, July 25, the late Bob Wilson’s annual benefit at the Watermill Center honored the work with special excerpts and “inspired by” performances. Additional special moments to be announced.

Philip’s 90th birthday seasons of notable performances and events also include: the world premiere performances of Glass’ “Symphony No. 15: Lincoln,” the first of which was held in Boston at Tanglewood on July 5, with following premieres in Santa Cruz July 31, and in Newport News, Virginia in September; global celebrations across opera, theater, dance, symphony and chamber concerts, film and special performances; and special releases from Orange Mountain Music. Find a full calendar of announced performances below.
