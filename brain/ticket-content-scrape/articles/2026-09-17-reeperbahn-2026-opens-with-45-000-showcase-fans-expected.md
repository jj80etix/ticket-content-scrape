# Reeperbahn 2026 opens with 45,000 showcase fans expected

## Summary

Reeperbahn Festival's 21st edition has opened in Hamburg, expecting 45,000 attendees and 6,000 industry delegates across hundreds of concerts at 70+ venues, with this year's conference theme — "Forever Legit?" — examining the cultural legitimacy and sustainability of the music industry amid technological change.

### Key Points
- 21st edition; 45,000 festival attendees, 6,000 industry-conference delegates expected.
- 70+ venues hosting "several hundred" concerts, including Dry Cleaning and English Teacher.
- Domestic-artist "Wunderkinder Artists" showcase spotlights emerging German acts.
- Conference programming covers generational change, AI, sustainability, and community building.

## Transcript

The event's 21st edition is set to welcome 6,000 industry figures to its conference in addition to its live show attendees

The 21st edition of Reeperbahn Festival has kicked off in Hamburg, set to welcome 6,000 industry figures and 45,000 attendees to its artist showcases.

With the launch, organisers hailed the festival and industry conference’s growing international profile, saying it “has grown over the past 21 years to make it one of the world’s most important music conferences”.

A combination of industry conference and music festival, the event will feature “several hundred” concerts across more than 70 venues, with notable acts including Madra Salach, Dry Cleaning, and English Teacher.

Forming part of its domestic artist lineup, the *Wunderkinder Artists* showcases will also see emerging German acts including Agatha is Dead!, Dolphin Love, and Dreamboys The Band take the stage.

“This year’s festival theme for 2026 is ‘Forever Legit?’, which explores the question of the cultural legitimacy, relevance, and sustainability of the creative and music industries in times of profound social and technological change,” organisers said as the festival kicked off.

The conference side of the event is set to host sessions on the themes of generational change, artificial intelligence, sustainability, and community building, with an additional strand focusing on “c ultural relevance and future viability of the creative and music industries”.

> “Reeperbahn has grown over the past 21 years to make it one of the world’s most important music conferences”

The event opened yesterday (16 September) with an address from State Secretary at the Federal Ministry of Finance Björn Böhning, in which the minister warned of the threat of funding cuts to the German live sector.

For the first time this year, the festival has also introduced a guest ‘Country of Honour’ with Ukraine as its focus. Electronic artist Christopher von Deylen, also known as Schiller, introduced the theme in a keynote speech, with organisers saying: “Drawing on his experiences from several visits and numerous conversations with Ukrainian cultural figures, he described how the human urge to create and express oneself persists even in wartime conditions, and called on the international music industry to continue giving Ukrainian acts a platform on their stages and at their festivals.”

He then performed the song *My Silence* alongside the Ukrainian singer Julia Sanina, frontwoman of the rock band The Hardkiss.

Also speaking at the opening event, Dr Carsten Brosda, Hamburg’s senator for culture and media “emphasised the immense importance of music and the arts as defining features of free societies”, organisers said.

They added that “festival director Detlef Schwarte also explained this year’s festival motto, ‘Forever Legit?’, to the approximately 600 invited guests from the worlds of politics and the international music and cultural industries, describing it as a metaphor for the music industry’s ongoing process of transformation”.

The festival will run until 19 September, with showcases spread across genres across rap, jazz, and electronic, alongside the ‘Heavy Reeperbahn’ genre strand as well as 17 emerging artists from Hamburg.
