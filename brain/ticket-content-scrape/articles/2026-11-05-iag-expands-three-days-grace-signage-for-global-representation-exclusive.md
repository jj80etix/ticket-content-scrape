# IAG Expands Three Days Grace Signage For Global Representation (Exclusive)

## Summary

Independent Artist Group (IAG) has expanded its representation of rock band Three Days Grace to global scope, building on a domestic (US/Mexico) deal signed in June 2025.

### Key Points
- IAG previously signed Three Days Grace for US/Mexico representation in June 2025; the deal now covers global representation.
- Agents Dennis Arfa, Pete Pappalardo, and Nick Storch will lead the international expansion.
- Band lineup: Adam Gontier and Matt Walst (co-lead vocals), Barry Stock, Brad Walst, Neil Sanderson.

## Transcript

Independent Artist Group (IAG) has signed Three Days Grace for global representation, expanding its relationship with one of modern rock’s most successful acts. [IAG previously signed the band in June 2025](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/06/20/three-days-grace-signs-with-iag-for-representation-in-the-us-and-mexico-exclusive/) for domestic representation. Following that successful initial partnership, both sides have agreed to expand it, further strengthening the agency’s global music touring business.

Additionally, Dennis Arfa, Pete Pappalardo and Nick Storch, who have represented the band to date, will also spearhead the international representation and build upon the group’s extraordinary global success.

Three Days Grace features Adam Gontier and Matt Walst on co-lead vocals, alongside Barry Stock (guitar), Brad Walst (bass and backing vocals) and Neil Sanderson (drums, keyboards and backing vocals). Gontier’s return to the band after more than a decade has ushered in a new chapter for the group, bringing together two distinctive voices from its celebrated history.

The expanded representation underscores IAG’s continued commitment to growing its international touring business and developing the worldwide careers of leading rock artists.

*Q Prime continues to manage Three Days Grace.*
