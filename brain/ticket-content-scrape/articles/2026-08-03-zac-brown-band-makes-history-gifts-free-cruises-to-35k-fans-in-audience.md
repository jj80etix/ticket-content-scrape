# Zac Brown Band Makes History, Gifts Free Cruises to 35K Fans in Audience

## Summary

Zac Brown Band surprised all 35,000 attendees at its 15th consecutive Fenway Park show with free Margaritaville at Sea cruise tickets for two, a gift the band's representatives valued at more than $40 million — described as the largest audience gifting in live entertainment history. The giveaway, tied to Margaritaville at Sea's sponsorship of the band's 27-date "Love & Fear Tour," will reportedly continue at a rate of four cruises per show through the tour's run into late November.

### Key Points
- Gift given at Fenway Park during the band's record 15th consecutive performance there.
- 35,000 attendees each received cruise tickets for two; total value cited as $40M+.
- Cruises redeemable on Margaritaville at Sea sailings over the next 12 months, including the new ship Beachcomber (debuting January 2027).
- Margaritaville at Sea is presenting sponsor of the 27-date "Love & Fear Tour," running through late November with stops including Red Rocks and Little Caesars Arena.
- Promotion tied to the song "Same Boat," a 2022 collaboration with the late Jimmy Buffett.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Zac-Brown-Band.jpg)

Zac Brown Band gave the crowd a huge surprise during a show over the weekend: free cruise tickets.

During the group’s record-breaking 15th consecutive performance at Boston’s Fenway Park this past Sunday, the band revealed that each member of the 35,000-attendee audience will receive free tickets to the Margaritaville at Sea cruise.

“Tonight we’re going to do something that even Jimmy would say is crazy,” Brown said on stage before performing “Same Boat” — the 2022 collaboration with the late Jimmy Buffett. “I’m buying every single person here a cruise for two people on the boat. This is the biggest giveaway in history ladies and gentlemen, right here tonight at Fenway Park!”

Brown’s representatives confirmed that the surprise gift is valued at more than $40 million, making this the largest audience gifting in live entertainment history.

The 27-date “ [Love & Fear Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/zac-brown-band-unveils-2026-love-and-fear-tour/) ” is set to run through late November, making stops along the way at venues like Columbia’s Merriweather Post Pavilion, Forest Hills Stadium in New York, Morrison’s Red Rocks Amphitheatre, and Little Caesars Arena in Detroit.
