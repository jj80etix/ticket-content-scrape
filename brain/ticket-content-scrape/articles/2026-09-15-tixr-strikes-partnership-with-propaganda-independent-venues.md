# Tixr strikes partnership with Propaganda Independent Venues

## Summary

Tixr announced an exclusive UK ticketing partnership with grassroots venue operator Propaganda Independent Venues (PIV), covering the newly reopened Camden Barfly plus XOYO London, XOYO Birmingham, Cardiff's Tramshed, and The Globe. As part of the deal, PIV retains full ownership of its fan relationship data, with Tixr framing the tie-up as bringing modern ticketing infrastructure to UK grassroots music venues.

### Key Points
- Deal covers five London/Birmingham/Cardiff grassroots venues under the PIV umbrella.
- PIV retains ownership of its fan data and relationships as an explicit deal term.
- Tixr UK/EMEA managing director Wayne Grierson commented on grassroots venues' needs (full remarks behind IQ's paywall).
- Follows Tixr's earlier coverage of the Camden Barfly's reopening after a decade-long closure.

### Highlights
- Tixr (Q12 Tier 1 primary-platform competitor) secures a multi-venue UK grassroots ticketing deal.
- Data-ownership guarantee (PIV keeps its fan data) is the explicit selling point in the announcement.

### Industry Problem
Independent/grassroots venues want ticketing partners without surrendering control of their fan-relationship data — a client pain point around data portability and ownership that primary platforms increasingly compete on.

### Proposed Solution
Tixr's answer bundles a data-ownership guarantee into its ticketing partnership terms; worth benchmarking Etix's own data-ownership terms for independent-venue clients against this positioning, since it's becoming an explicit competitive differentiator in grassroots-venue deals.
