# Tove Lo Announces 2027 ‘Estrus World Tour’

## Summary

Tove Lo has announced the "Estrus World Tour," a spring 2027 U.S. run starting April 19 in Dallas and closing May 7 at Red Rocks Amphitheatre, with Coco & Clair Clair supporting throughout. It follows her recent North American run and upcoming UK/Europe dates for the *ESTRUS* album cycle.

### Key Points
- 9 confirmed U.S. dates from Dallas to Red Rocks; artist presale begins Nov 4, general onsale Nov 6.
- COBRAH joins as a special guest at the Red Rocks finale.
- The tour continues into 2027 with Electric Avenue Festival in New Zealand and an Australian headline run before the U.S. dates.

Tove Lo has announced the “Estrus World Tour,” starting April 19 in Dallas at the South Side Ballroom. Red Rocks Amphitheater in Morrison, Colorado, will be the tour’s final stop on May 7. Coco & Clair Clair will support across all dates, with COBRAH joining as a special guest at Red Rocks. See full tour routing below.

Artist presale begins Nov. 4 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general onsale on Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. local time. For tickets and more information, visit [tove-lo.com/#tour](https://tove-lo.com/#tour).  
  
The announcement follows the conclusion of Tove’s recent North American run, which brought *ESTRUS* to stages in Nashville, Chicago, Brooklyn, Toronto, Los Angeles and Mexico City. This week, she heads to the UK and Europe for her largest headline shows in the region to date, joined by Rose Gray.

The tour continues into 2027 with an appearance at Electric Avenue Festival in Christchurch, New Zealand, followed by headline performances across Australia with support from Salty, before returning to the U.S. for the newly announced spring dates.
