# Sphere and Disguise to Develop New Video Technology for Live Events

## Summary

Sphere Entertainment and visual-technology company Disguise have signed a multi-year partnership to develop new video production tools and workflows for Sphere's Las Vegas venue and planned expansion. Disguise has supplied media-server and playback software for Sphere since it opened in 2023; the new deal extends that relationship into longer-term technology development.

### Key Points
- Partnership builds on three years of Disguise supplying real-time visual playback/sync technology at Sphere.
- Scope expands from running content during events to developing the systems and workflows used to prepare it.
- Framed as supporting Sphere's plans for additional venues beyond Las Vegas.

## Transcript

Sphere Entertainment and visual technology company Disguise announced a multi-year partnership Wednesday to develop video tools for Sphere’s live productions and planned expansion.

Disguise has worked with Sphere since the Las Vegas venue opened in 2023. Its media servers and software help process, synchronize and play back visual content in real time for residencies and corporate events. The new agreement extends that work into the development of technology and production workflows designed specifically for Sphere.

“Every artist who has performed at Sphere has benefited from Disguise’s technology, and we’re looking forward to developing future iterations of immersive productions to support our long-term vision of offering these experiences to audiences in new Sphere venues around the world,” said Ryan Dolan, Sphere’s executive vice president and head of event and interactive technology.

Faster playback capabilities could give production teams more flexibility as they build visual sequences for the venue, though the companies have not detailed how the planned improvements will work.

Disguise provides visual technology for live events, location-based entertainment and virtual production. CEO Fernando Küfer said the partnership builds on three years of work with Sphere and will focus on expanding the venue’s video capabilities.
