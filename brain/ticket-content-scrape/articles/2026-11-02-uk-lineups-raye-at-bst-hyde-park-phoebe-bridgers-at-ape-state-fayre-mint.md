# UK Lineups: Raye At BST Hyde Park, Phoebe Bridgers At APE, State Fayre & Mint

## Summary

A Pollstar UK lineup roundup covering RAYE's BST Hyde Park headline slot, Phoebe Bridgers' first All Points East headline appearance, State Fayre's second-year Shania Twain booking, and Mint Festival's first stage lineup in Leeds.

### Key Points
- Bridgers headlines APE on Aug. 27, 2027, after a sold-out "Lost Weekend" arena tour (e.g., 40,238 tickets/$4.4M across three Barclays Center shows).
- State Fayre 2027 (Chelmsford, June 18-20) follows a 50,000-fan debut year; early-bird tickets already sold out.
- Mint Festival (Leeds, May 1-2, 2027) announced a three-stage electronic-music lineup; tickets on sale Nov. 4.

**Raye Is Coming Home**

[Raye](https://pollstar.com/artists/raye-220012), the newly crowned Mercury Prize winner for her current album, This Music May Contain Hope, is BST Hyde Park’s second headliner, taking to the stage on Saturday, July 10, 2027.

Sienna Spiro, who’s [featured in Pollstar’s 2026 Hotstar Special](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/30/hotstar-special-2026-shades-of-adele-sienna-spiro-breaks-out-of-uk-to-tour/?_gl=1%2Amuh5uw%2A_ga%2AMzkwOTgxNDkwLjE3ODgzNTY2MTg.%2A_ga_5B6XRECYRM%2AczE3OTM2MjY4NzUkbzM2JGcxJHQxNzkzNjI2OTEzJGoyMiRsMCRoMA..), joins the bill, with more acts to be announced, according to a press release from promoter AEG Presents.

South London-born Raye has had a remarkable few years, growing from one of the UK’s most in-demand songwriters into one of the biggest names in music, racking up more than 10 billion Spotify streams and 21 UK Top 40 singles.

Her independent debut album, My 21st Century Blues, changed the course of her career, earning her six BRIT Awards in one night in 2024, including Album of the Year and Songwriter of the Year, as well as an Ivor Novello for Songwriter of the Year and three GRAMMY® nominations.

Since then, Raye has performed major festivals like Coachella, Glastonbury, and All Points East, and took her music beyond the UK, joining Bruno Mars on his stadium tour The Romantic Tour this summer through October.

She released her second album, This Music May Contain Hope, in March, which went straight to No. 1 in the UK, while reaching the Top 10 in 16 territories around the world.

At the start of 2026, Raye became a Grammy®-winning artist after being honored with the Recording Academy’s “Best Song for Social Change” award for “Ice Cream Man.” In October, Raye was named the winner of the 2026 Mercury Prize for Album of the Year, adding another major milestone.

After a year of sold-out shows, major festival appearances, international awards and a return to the Royal Albert Hall in October for a symphonic performance of her critically acclaimed album, she now headlines London’s Hyde Park for the very first time.

The announcement of RAYE follows the first headliner to be unveiled for BST Hyde Park 2027, which was Bon Jovi, who return to London’s famous park 14 years after opening the first edition of the festival.

Tickets for the Raye day at BST Hyde Park go on sale Nov. 10 at 10 a.m. British Standard Time. Raye is repped by The Team worldwide.

**Phoebe Bridgers Plays APE For The First Time**

All Points East, another AEG Presents festival at a famous London park, just announced [Phoebe Bridgers](https://pollstar.com/artists/phoebe-bridgers-199298) as its first headliner. The four-time Grammy winner will take the stage at Victoria Park on Friday, Aug. 27, 2027, joined by a curated supporting line up including Caroline Polachek, Kelsey Lu, Danielle Ponder, jasmine.4.t, and more artists to be confirmed.

Bridgers’ third solo album Lost Weekend earned her first solo Official Number 1 Album Award upon release in August. Lost Weekend’s arena tour of the U.S., UK and EU sold out within hours and sees Bridgers play to the largest audiences of her career to date.

Recent highlights from the tour reported to Pollstar Boxoffice include two shows at TD Garden Boston, MA, Oct. 6-7, where she moved 25,244 tickets at a $2,952,054 gross; or three sold-out nights at Barclays Center Brooklyn, NY, Sept. 24-26, moving 40,238 tickets, and grossing $4,394,002.

Tickets for Phoebe Bridgers at All Points East London will be available from 10 a.m. local British time on Thursday, Oct. 29.

**Stat Fayre Returns**

State Fayre has revealed the first headliner for its second edition, returning to Hylands Park in Chelmsford, England, June 18-20, 2027: Shania Twain, who will take the stage on the Saturday night.

Shania Twain has been blazing the trail and releasing numerous hits across her 30-plus year career, including ‘Man, I Feel Like A Woman’, ‘That Don’t Impress Me Much’ and ‘You’re Still The One’. Dubbed the “Queen Of Country Pop”, her latest album Little Miss Twain released in 2026 saw Shania embracing the roots and bars that shaped the superstar she would become. Her headline set at State Fayre 2027 is primed to be a celebration stacked with singalongs.

Speaking about headlining State Fayre 2027, Shania Twain said:  
“We all know I love the UK and my UK fans are the best. Last summer everyone said that State Fayre was a blast so I can’t wait to experience it for myself and it’s an honour to headline, of course! Summer will be here before we know it, see you all soon for my hits from ’93 and the first record to “Little Miss Twain”, my latest release. Let’s go!”

Speaking about Shania Twain’s headline slot and the return of State Fayre in 2027, festival organisers Anna-Sophie Mertens and Alex Simmonds said:  
“We’re over the moon to reveal the first details of State Fayre 2027 and our first headliner in the form of the one and only: Shania Twain. An icon not just in the history of country music but also in global culture itself, Shania is the perfect fit for what State Fayre is all about. A true celebration in every sense of the word, singing along is going to be a must by the time June comes around. With two more headliners still to be revealed and an incredible lineup still to be announced, we can’t wait to show the world what we’ve been working on. We’ll see you in June”.

State Fayre, which premiered in 2026, couldn’t have had a better start, welcoming over 50,000 fans to Hylands Park for this brand-new festival embracing Americana culture. More headliners to be announced soon.

Early bird tickets sold out. The first wave of Weekend and Saturday tickets for State Fayre 2027 go on-sale Friday, Nov. 6 at 9 a.m. local time. PayPal customers will be able to access an exclusive presale from 9 a.m. on Wednesday, Nov. 4.

**Mint Festival Announces First Stage Lineup**

Mint, the two-day electronic music festival, returns to Newsam Green Farm in Leeds, UK May 1-2, 2027, for a celebration of electronic music, underground sounds and festival atmosphere.

Set against the distinctive countryside surroundings of Newsam Green Farm, MiNT Festival 2027 will feature three stages, The Bunker, The Orchard and The Barn, each showcasing a varied selection of artists spanning electronic music and underground club culture.

Saturday’s program features performances from Captain Wallop, Lili, L.P. Rhythm, Kepler, Rossi., RTK Tarantino, Annie Errez and Bobby O’Donnell, alongside appearances from MiNT residents. The Orchard will host Ellia Jaya and Gaskin, while The Barn welcomes Morgan Seatree.

Sunday continues with Boss Priester, Dr Banana, Enzo Siragusa, Annie Errez and Bobby O’Donnell, alongside MiNT residents at The Bunker. Over at The Orchard, Cam Stockman, Hidde van Wee and Sidney Charles lead the announced names, while The Barn brings together Burnski, Julian Anthony, Luke Dean and MJ Cole.

Tickets go on sale Nov. 4 at 11 a.m. local time.
