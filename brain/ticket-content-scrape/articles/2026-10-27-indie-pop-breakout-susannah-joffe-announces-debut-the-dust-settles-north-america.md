# Indie-Pop Breakout Susannah Joffe Announces Debut ‘The Dust Settles’ North America Tour

## Summary

Indie-pop artist Susannah Joffe has announced her debut headline tour, "The Dust Settles Tour," running North American clubs from late March to mid-April 2027. The run supports her recent LP and follows a breakout year that included festival slots and streams in the hundreds of millions.

### Key Points
- Tour opens March 23 in Phoenix, closes April 16 in Washington, D.C.
- Artist presale begins Oct. 28; stops include New York, LA and Chicago.
- Also booked for High Water Festival (Charleston, SC) April 18.
- Grown to 2.2M monthly Spotify listeners; prior support slots for King Princess and Paris Paloma.

## Transcript

![dates (2)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/dates-2.png)

Breakout indie pop singer/songwriter Susannah Joffe has announced her debut “The Dust Settles Tour” that will take the Austin-born, NYC-based artist to clubs across North America. Supporting her recent LP Dust Settles And So Will I, the tour kicks off at the Rebel Lounge in Phoenix on March 23 and will stop in cities including New York, Los Angeles, Chicago, and more before wrapping in Washington, D.C. on April 16.

Artist presale begins on Wednesday, Oct. 28 at 10 a.m. local time. In addition to headline tour dates, Joffe is on the bill at High Water Festival in Charleston, South Carolina, April 18. This year, she made her festival debut at All Things Go D.C. and joined Paris Paloma on her “Fatal Flaw” North American tour.

Joffe’s style is described as “emotional indie pop that pulls equally from Austin’s soft-rock roots and NYC’s bright, chaotic queer scene,” this year growing to 2.2M monthly Spotify listeners and selling out headline dates in major markets building up to this debut tour. Her 2024 single “Die Your Daughter” has surpassed 135 million Spotify streams. In the past year she’s supported King Princess on her Europe tour and previously has toured with artists including spill tab, Alix Page, Indigo De Souza, NIKI and others.

Joffe is managed by Fiona Dolan and Jack Gallagher at Open Management and represented by WME’s Natalie Otto and Michael Coughlin.
