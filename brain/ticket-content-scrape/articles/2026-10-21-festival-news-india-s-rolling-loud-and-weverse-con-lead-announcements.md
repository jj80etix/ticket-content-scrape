# Festival news: India’s Rolling Loud and Weverse Con lead announcements

## Summary

IQ's festival-news roundup leads with Rolling Loud returning to Mumbai in December with Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, and Skepta, plus HYBE's Weverse Con Festival announcing first names for its international edition in New Delhi, alongside other Australian, UK, and European festival lineup news and the return of the US's GoldenSky.

### Key Points
- Rolling Loud Mumbai (Dec. 12-13), produced with District by Zomato, returns for a second edition after 65,000+ attendance at its 2025 debut.
- Lineup also includes Karan Aujla, Big Sean, Schoolboy Q, Seedhe Maut, and others.
- Weverse Con Festival announces its first international edition, in New Delhi.
- Roundup also covers Australia's Sydney Summertime & On The Banks and various UK/European festival announcements.

## Transcript

## Festival news: India’s Rolling Loud and Weverse Con lead announcements

Plus: New names for Australia's Sydney Summertime & On The Banks and a host of UK and European events, and the return of the US' GoldenSky

![Karan Aujla at Rolling Loud India](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Karan-Aujla-at-Rolling-Loud-India-©-Business-Wire.png)

Karan Aujla will return to Rolling Loud India © Business Wire

**Rolling Loud** will return to Mumbai for its second edition in December, bringing three hip-hop heavyweights – Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, and Skepta – for their market debuts, leading the latest festival news.

The trio of stars will be joined by Indian rapper Karan Aujla, who will return as an event headliner for the second consecutive year.

Produced in partnership with District by Zomato, the 12-13 December festival will bring together an international lineup alongside some of India’s leading homegrown artists. Other confirmed names include Big Sean, Schoolboy Q, Seedhe Maut, Dhanda Nyoliwala, Slayr, and BabyChiefDoit.

“Hip-hop in India has always moved through cyphers, small shows, and people putting each other on, and that’s where we looked when we built this year’s bill,” says District CEO Rahul Ganjoo. “At the ﬁrst edition, the crowd didn’t thin out between the international sets and the Indian ones, and that told us the audience was already a step ahead of the industry. This lineup is built on what we saw.”

The world’s largest hip-hop festival brand attracted over 65,000 people for its [maiden edition in India](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/12/rolling-loud-debuts-in-india-we-made-history/) last November at a purpose-built venue, Loud Park, in Kharghar, Navi Mumbai.

Over in New Delhi, the first names for HYBE’s flagship global event, **Weverse Con Festival**, have been announced, bringing together leading K-pop, Indian, and global artists for the festival’s [first international edition](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/hybe-zeroes-in-on-india-with-weverse-con-festival-move/).

The lineup for the 27-28 March event spans names like Tomorrow x Together, Enhyphen, Le Sserafim, Lara of Katseye, Rhea Raj, Lee Hyun, Bumzu, Hwasa, Eric Nam, and Dayoung, along with Indian artists Gini, King, Ranj x Clifr, Reble, and DJ Zelo.

Together, the diverse lineup brings artists from different musical backgrounds onto one global stage, reflecting HYBE’s commitment to connecting locally rooted artists with audiences worldwide,” the company says.

A venue is to be announced, with tickets going on sale next week (27 October) exclusively through BookMyShow.

> “Three of the most exciting forces shaping the sound of a new generation \[will be\] at the UK’s biggest dance festival”

**Australia  
**The second headliner for **Sydney Summertime** ‘s debut year has been revealed, with Kings of Leon to join The Stokes in topping the bill of the new series.

The American rock band will top the bill at Sydney’s Centennial Park series on 28 February 2027, with support from Australian outfit Ocean Alley. Sydney Summertime is presented by Black Peach, and supported by Destination NSW.

They join previously announced headliners The Strokes, who are set for a 20 February show with a bill that includes Beck, Courtney Barnett, and Promiseland.

In the concert series realm, Brisbane’s **On The Banks** series has lined up a host of talent for 2027, building on its debut year this year.

The Queensland Performing Arts Centre-led and based series, which runs from 24 February to 13 March, will be led by names including Kasabian, Amyl and The Sniffers, Geese, Leon Bridges, Madness, Fat Freddy’s Drop, The Avalanches, Fatboy Slim, Chase & Status, and Pete Murray.

**UK/Europe  
**Following a triple Finsbury Park sell-out, Josh Baker B2B KETTAMA B2B Prospa have been announced for the UK’s **Creamfields*.***

The back-to-back billing will unite “three of the most exciting forces shaping the sound of a new generation at the UK’s biggest dance festival,” organisers say. They will lead the bill on 27 August at the Cheshire event as part of the festival’s 30-year celebrations. The festival runs from 26-29 August.

Sweden’s own Robyn will return to headline Gothenburg’s **Way Out West** in 2027, the first name to be confirmed for the Luger-organised event. The festival returns from 12-14 August.

In France, **We Love Green** ‘s first artist announcements are led by Angèle, Fontaines D.C., Lola Young, and Angine de Poitrine. Additional names include Amelie Lens, Adèle Castillion, wallows, Boy Harsher, Marlon Hoffstadt, and Underscores, with the festival to return to Paris from 4-6 June.

> BICEP is the first act confirmed for Labyrinth on the Thames, while GALA will host Four Tet next year

Norway’s major rock festival **Tons of Rock** has also announced a slate of artists for 2027, with Mötley Crüe, Turnstile, Helloween, Faith No More, Pierce The Veil, Scorpions, Judas Priest, Kreator, Lynyrd Skynyrd, and more lined up for the 23-26 June event in Oslo.

And in London, **BST Hyde Park** has also confirmed its first name, with Bon Jovi to headline the series on 24 June.

BICEP is the first act lined up for the **Labyrinth on the Thames** series at London’s Old Royal Naval College, taking over the riverside venue on 31 July. And down in Peckham Rye Park in the UK capital, **GALA Festival** will be led by Four Tet, who will make his “long-awaited debut”, organisers say.

He’ll be joined by DJ Harvey, Antal & HUNEE, Shy FX, Skin On Skin, Blawan, HAAi, Egyptian Lover, and Chez Damier for the 28-30 May festival.

Over in Portugal, **NOS Alive** has lined up a handful of major acts for 2027, including The Neighbourhood, Sombr, Myles Smith, Beirut, and The Strokes. They join the previously announced Tame Impala for the 8-10 July event.

And Jack Johnson is the first artist confirmed for **Ageas Cooljazz**. The American singer-songwriter will take to the Hipódromo Manuel Possolo in Cascais on 15 July, his first performance in Portugal since 2008. The festival will run from 1-31 July 2027.

> “We knew if we were bringing it back, we needed to come back big”

**North America  
**Danny Wimmer Presents has announced the return of **GoldenSky Festival**, set for 9-10 October at Sacramento’s Discovery Park.

Next year’s edition will be led by Zach Bryan, Megan Moroney, and Treaty Oak Revival, with additional names spanning Ty Myers, Whiskey Myers, Hudson Westbrook, Max McNown, Wyatt Flores, Vincent Mason, Avery Anna, Braxton Keith, Blake Whiten, Waylon Wyatt, Mack Geiger, Kaitlin Butts, Carter Faith, and many more.

“We’ve missed GoldenSky, and after two years away, we knew if we were bringing it back, we needed to come back big,” says Chamie McCurry, DWP general manager. “This lineup feels like the perfect return, reflecting what’s exciting in country music right now while staying true to what made people fall in love with GoldenSky in the first place.

“More importantly, we’re not just coming back for a weekend. We’ve made a long-term commitment to Sacramento, and we’re excited to keep growing GoldenSky and investing in this community for years to come,” she concludes.

Finally, C3 Presents’ **Boardwalk Rock** will be led by Iron Maiden, Korn, and Bad Omens, which will return to Ocean City, Maryland, from 14-16 May after taking 2026 off.

Alice in Chains, Breaking Benjamin, Good Charlotte, Marilyn Manson, The Prodigy, Judas Priest and Cypress Hill are also slated to perform at the event, which initially launched in 2025.
