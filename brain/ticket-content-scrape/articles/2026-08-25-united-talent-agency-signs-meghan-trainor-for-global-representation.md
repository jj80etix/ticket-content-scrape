United Talent Agency announced it has signed Grammy-winning pop singer Meghan Trainor for global representation, working with the artist on live touring, filmed entertainment, digital media, theatre, brand partnerships and more.

Trainor made her on-screen acting debut in the FX series “The Beauty” earlier this year, and she also released her seventh studio album *Toy With Me* in April.

The Nantucket native broke onto the scene in 2015 with her breakthrough major label debut Title, which featured “All About That Bass,” “Dear Future Husband” and “Like I’m Gonna Lose You.”

She went on to win the 2016 Grammy Award for Best New Artist, and she has established herself as a pop hitmaker since, garnering more than 20 million monthly listeners on Spotify.

Trainor was also a judge, coach and mentor on music television series including “The Voice,” “The Voice UK,” “The Four,” “Australian Idol” and more. She also made her debut as an author in 2023 with her motherhood memoir “Dear Future Mama: A TMI Guide to Pregnancy, Birth, and Motherhood from Your Bestie.”

The multihyphenate star is managed by Nikola Barisic of Bond Artist Management. She last toured in 2024 with her “Timeless Tour,” a 26-show run that included sold-out stops at Madison Square Garden and the Kia Forum. That trek sold 265,276 tickets and grossed $14.5 million, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.
