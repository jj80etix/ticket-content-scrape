# Australian Open set to limit number of ground passes

## Summary

Tennis Australia will cap daily ground-pass allocations at the 2027 Australian Open for the first time, after record 2026 attendance (1.37M) led to long queues and passes selling out within an hour on some days. The A$49 ground-pass price stays unchanged since 2019.

### Key Points
- 2027 Australian Open (Jan 11-31) will cap daily ground-pass allocations for the first time.
- 2026 edition set an all-time attendance record of 1,368,042 across three weeks, up from 1,218,831 in 2025.
- Ground passes sold out within the first hour on some 2026 days, turning fans away.
- Ground-pass price (A$49) hasn't risen since 2019 despite record demand.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Daily limits are set to be placed on ground passes at the Australian Open for the first time in 2027 after long queues and crowding marred this year’s edition in January.**

Organiser Tennis Australia said it would cap allocations of the A$49 (£25/$35/€30) tickets during the first week of the main draw in 2027 as demand is expected to reach unprecedented levels.

“This decision reflects the growing popularity of the AO and will help ensure fans are able to make the most of everything on offer across the precinct,” the governing body said in a statement.

The move comes after a record attendance of 1,368,042 across three weeks of this year’s iteration of the tennis grand slam in Melbourne.

While numbers will be restricted for the 2027 Australian Open, set to take place on January 11-31, Tennis Australia’s chief commercial officer Cedric Cornelis said the ground pass would be “one of the best value sporting tickets in the world.”

“The price hasn’t increased since 2019 and we expect demand for AO 2027, particularly week one of the main draw, to be stronger than ever,” he added.

“With this in mind, the daily allocations will be capped for the first time.”

This year’s total was the [highest attendance in Australian Open history](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/02/australian-open-2026-delivers-all-time-attendance-highs/), beating the 2025 figure of 1,218,831.

The numbers resulted in some [fans being turned away](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/01/long-queues-mar-record-day-at-australian-open/) after sales of ground pass tickets were halted within the first hour of play due to overwhelming demand.
