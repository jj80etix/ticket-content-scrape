# ‘Location is unmatched’: Arena MDP readies October opening in Argentina’s biggest resort

## Summary

A new 6,000-square-meter venue, Arena Mar del Plata, is set to open in October 2026 in Argentina's largest beach resort city, developed by Ref Group after a three-year property search. The complex includes a main adaptable floor, 14 VIP boxes, a smaller ~800-capacity secondary space (La Sala Sub), and a food court, with an opening lineup led by Los Fabulosos Cadillacs.

### Key Points
- Opens October 24, 2026 in Mar del Plata, Argentina; developed by brothers Fernando and Federico Roca of Ref Group.
- Opening acts include Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, Ke Personajes, Babasónicos, Diego Torres, Alex Ubago and Sergio Dalma.
- Secondary space "La Sala Sub" (~800 capacity) targets independent/smaller-scale content.
- Positioned to capture shows the city currently misses due to lack of suitable infrastructure.

## ‘Location is unmatched’: Arena MDP readies October opening in Argentina’s biggest resort

With the coastal resort fan experience as the strategic core, arena bosses say the project will bring the city shows it currently misses out on

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/arena-MDP.jpg)

Arena Mar del Plata (impression) © REF Group

**Mar del Plata, Argentina’s tourist coastal city, is preparing to add a new key venue to its cultural and entertainment infrastructure in the shape of a 6,000-square-meter complex is scheduled to open in October 2026.**

Brothers Fernando and Federico Roca, heads of the production company Ref Group, are leading the development of the new Arena Mar del Plata (AMP). Situated at the central intersection of Pedro Luro Avenue and San Juan Street in Argentina’s biggest beach resort city, the venue is aiming to join the city’s current offerings for major national and international productions.

The production team has already confirmed the first acts that will be part of the complex’s lineup, featuring a diverse calendar of national and international shows scheduled starting October 24. The opening will be headlined by Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, followed by Ke Personajes, Babasónicos, Diego Torres, Alex Ubago, and Sergio Dalma (the latter three in a theatre-style format).

The lineup will continue with Cruzando el Charco, Vilma Palma e Vampiros in an 80s and 90s themed party that same month, Flavio Mendoza during the summer season with his show *7 Auras*, and will extend into the future with a performance by No Te Va Gustar.

The choice of the location answers to a rigorous strategy for logistical accessibility for both the local and regional public. “We spent nearly 3 years trying to secure that property. We are certain that its location is unmatched for this project; it is ideal for reaching the venue from all areas of the city and for those coming from elsewhere by train, bus, or even by car via Route 2. We believe we are going to bring back to that area the strength and drive it had in former times,” explains Fernando Roca.

> “ Arena Mar del Plata will provide the infrastructure the city was missing so that certain events that weren’t coming to Mar del Plata can now do so”

The complex was designed under a concept of maximum adaptability. The micro-arena’s main floor will be able to adapt dynamically to different types of events and disciplines, complemented by a premium sector of 14 VIP Boxes distributed across two wings on the first floor, which will feature private parking in the basement.

For independent or smaller-scale content, the development adds a second space in the basement: La Sala Sub, intended for various activities with an approximate capacity of 800 people.

The commercial area will be boosted by the Food Court AMP, a gastronomic patio open to the general public. “We have no restrictions. We believe we will achieve a mix between local businesses and national ones. We are going to provide a nice gastronomic proposal that allows attendees to have different options,”details Fernando.

**Technological Alliance and a Nationwide Advertising Hub**

Ref Group closed an exclusivity agreement with the ticketing platform Venti for the management and commercialisation of ticketing for all shows. The platform will also host wider fan experience features. beyond gate tickets, including, among other things, the purchase of food from the food court in advance.

Starting from ticket sales, producers will be able to receive real-time data from the spectators. The platform is not just transaction-based, however, as it also allows the venue to create a profile for different users based on their activity in the venue, which it says will enhance the communication experience.

The arena will also looking to make digital displays and activations a central component of its strategy to generate sponsor activity. For this, a technological deployment includes a large outdoor LED screen (marquee) measuring 14 meters by 5 meters on Pedro Luro Avenue, alongside 70 linear meters of indoor LED screens running along the first-floor balconies. This is paired with the Lounge room next to the boxes for corporate experiences and high-quality activations.

The Arena will feature its own streaming channel with a street-facing studio, a national profile, and recognised personalities, expanding sponsors’ reach beyond the physical space.

> “ long holiday weekends are a great draw for tourism and for bringing in big shows”

“It will be a free channel that won’t necessarily be focused only on the Mar del Plata audience. Nowadays, these channels are watched all over the world. We are setting up a programming grid with personalities, mostly from Mar del Plata, but who transcend the city. We can’t give names yet, but we imagine many Argentines and locals listening to our programs anywhere in the world,” Fernando Roca says.

**Year-round operation**

Arena AMP seeks to add a new standard of technical services to the marketplace without directly competing with pre-existing venues, but rather complementing the options for large-scale spectacles.

Roca says that “long holiday weekends are a great draw for tourism and for bringing in big shows,” adding that “Arena Mar del Plata will provide the infrastructure the city was missing so that certain events that weren’t coming to Mar del Plata can now do so”.

“There won’t be differences in production or quality in the shows that would make the local audience feel at a ‘disadvantage’. Culture is the same for everyone. That’s why AMP was born in Mar del Plata, but it is for everyone,” Roca continues.

Regarding the operational model, he adds that management analysed the engineering of top-tier international venues.

“We looked at places not only in Argentina. Everything we learned over these years helped us know what things we needed. Then you find yourself limited by space and construction possibilities, but we have tried to put together the nicest and most comfortable place we could according to the possibilities,” the executive notes.

> “ We are certain that its location is unmatched for this project”

**Urban impact**

The complex’s structural design prioritises controlling the impact on the central urban environment, Ref Group says.

Part of this is delegating acoustic management to industry specialists, with Roca saying: “Regarding environmental sustainability, an essential point is acoustic care, for which we are working very closely with a specialised company from Buenos Aires that handles this type of work for major halls across the country.

“The ecosystem must be affected as little as possible; it must change for the better, we are going to beautify it and generate movement around it.”

As for alternative energy sources, the producer points out that they are evaluating efficient technologies. “Alternative technologies are not yet proven everywhere; for example, solar panels do not guarantee the same performance in Mar del Plata as in other zones due to technical reasons that are not worth emphasising right now. What we are trying to do is acquire a device that generates friction-free wind energy, but for that, we must first wait for the studies to certify that it will work for our project.”

Given its proximity of just 50 metres to the train and bus terminal, traffic flow and parking on maximum-capacity show days will require direct coordination with municipal authorities.

He explains will need to be “a joint effort among all parties involved, as happens with other municipal spaces”. He continues: “The arena has a small underground parking lot and another space at the intersection of Pedro Luro Avenue and La Pampa Street. But besides that, we have a large stretch along the avenue without needing to drive into the neighbourhood’s small streets.”

“On top of all that, there are negotiations to obtain the use of one of the train station’s warehouses, but we haven’t been able to resolve that yet. Either way, we are sure that between the authorities and us, we will resolve this situation perfectly,” Roca says.

Regarding the final construction timelines, the executives concludes by saying: “The core goal is to have the stadium operational by early October. It is likely that other spaces like the food court or the small hall will take a bit longer, but we believe that by December we will be 100% operational.”
