# First Day Of Oceans Calling Canceled Due To ‘Impacts Of This Week’s Continued Weather’

## Summary

Oceans Calling canceled Friday of its Ocean City, Maryland festival — scrapping the Dave Matthews Band/Hootie & the Blowfish night — citing beach erosion and safety risk from a continued weather system with gusts to 50mph. Front Gate Tickets buyers get automatic refunds (full refund for 1-day Friday tickets, 33% for 3-day passes) within about 30 days; Saturday (Twenty One Pilots, Gwen Stefani) and Sunday (Mumford & Sons, Matchbox Twenty) remained on as organizers worked to salvage the weekend. Pollstar notes this is the latest in a run of 2026 festival weather disruptions, following Riot Fest, Shaky Knees, Louder Than Life and the outright cancellation of Goldenvoice's debut Ocean Way Festival.

### Highlights
- Cause: beach erosion plus high winds (up to 50mph) prevented safe completion of festival-site construction.
- Refunds: 100% for 1-day Friday tickets, 33% for 3-day passes, via Front Gate Tickets, ~30-day processing.
- Part of a broader pattern: Riot Fest, Shaky Knees and Louder Than Life were hit by weather the prior weekend; Ocean Way Festival was canceled outright weeks before its debut.
- Saturday/Sunday lineups (Twenty One Pilots/Gwen Stefani; Mumford & Sons/Matchbox Twenty) remained scheduled as of the announcement.

### Industry Problem
Recurring severe-weather disruption to outdoor festivals is forcing repeated, ad hoc partial-refund decisions — a pattern significant enough this season (multiple major festivals affected) to be an operational and patron-trust risk rather than an isolated incident.

### Proposed Solution
No structural fix is discussed in the piece beyond per-event refund policies. Worth monitoring whether this season's pattern of weather cancellations pushes primary platforms/festivals toward standardized weather-contingency refund terms communicated up front at purchase.

Oceans Calling festival has announced that day one of the festival, headlined by Dave Matthews Band and Hootie & The Blowfish, will not take place due to continued weather in the vicinity, “including beach erosion that affected the festival site.”

“Despite the extraordinary efforts of our production, site, safety teams, and the city staff/officials, the impacts of this week’s continued weather, including beach erosion that affected the festival site, have made it impossible to safely complete the build in time for Friday,” the festival in Ocean City, Maryland, announced on its social channels today. The festival is produced by C3 Presents.

“As a result, Friday will not move forward as planned. The safety of our fans, artists, crew, and community must come first. 1-Day Friday Tickets and 33% of 3-Day Tickets purchased directly through Front Gate Tickets will be automatically refunded in as little as 30 days to the original method of payment.”

The announcement says, “Please continue to follow our official channels for updates. We are working very hard to get the show up for the remainder of the weekend, and hoping the conditions improve.”

Weather surrounding the resort town has been severe, with wind gusts reportedly reaching 50mph and coastal flooding as the East Coast braces for a powerful nor’easter.

Saturday’s lineup is topped by Twenty One Pilots and Gwen Stefani, with additional artists including Goo Goo Dolls, Third Eye Blind, and many others. Sunday is headlined by Mumford & Sons and Matchbox Twenty, with additional musical talent including CAAMP, My Morning Jacket, The Head And The Heart and many others.

[Last weekend saw significant weather disruption to multiple major music festivals,](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/21/weather-disrupts-major-us-festivals-sporting-events-over-weekend/) affecting events including Riot Fest in Chicago, Shaky Knees in Atlanta and Louder Than Life in Louisville. [Goldenvoice’s Ocean Way Festival in Santa Monica, California, was canceled](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/11/goldenvoices-ocean-way-festival-in-santa-monica-canceled-due-to-beach-erosion/) weeks before its debut, citing the effects of Hurricane Marie and beach erosion. That event was to be headlined by The Killers and Olivia Dean, also featuring Jack White, Khruangbin, Sublime and more.
