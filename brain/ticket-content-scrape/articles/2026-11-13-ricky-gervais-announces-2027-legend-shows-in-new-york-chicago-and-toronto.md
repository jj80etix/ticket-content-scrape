# Ricky Gervais Announces 2027 ‘Legend’ Shows in New York, Chicago and Toronto

## Summary

Ricky Gervais has announced three North American "Legend" stand-up tour dates for 2027, opening April 9 at New York's Radio City Music Hall, followed by Chicago's United Center on April 12 and Toronto's Scotiabank Arena on August 14. The dates follow a sold-out UK run that continues internationally through the end of 2027.

### Key Points
- Three dates: New York (April 9), Chicago (April 12), Toronto (August 14).
- Follows a sold-out UK run of "Legend" continuing internationally through 2027.
- Gervais' prior Hollywood Bowl show holds a Guinness World Record for highest-grossing comedy gig.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Ricky-Gervais-Legend.webp)

Ricky Gervais has announced three North American dates for his “Legend” stand-up tour, with performances scheduled in New York, Chicago and Toronto in 2027.

The newly announced run is slated to open April 9 in New York, New York, at Radio City Music Hall. Gervais will then perform at the United Center in Chicago, Illinois, on April 12.

A Canadian appearance follows later in the summer, with a show at Scotiabank Arena in Toronto, Ontario, scheduled for August 14. The dates follow a sold-out U.K. run of “Legend,” which will continue internationally through the end of 2027.

Gervais’ previous touring work includes a 2023 appearance at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles that earned a Guinness World Record as the highest-grossing comedy gig.
