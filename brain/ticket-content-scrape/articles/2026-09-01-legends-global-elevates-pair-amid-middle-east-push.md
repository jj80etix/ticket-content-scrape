# Legends Global elevates pair amid Middle East push

## Summary

Legends Global announced two Middle East leadership promotions effective September 2026: Mark Jan Kar becomes SVP, MENA and APAC Strategic Projects, while Santiago Olea transitions to General Manager of Dubai's Coca-Cola Arena.

### Key Points
- Jan Kar retains oversight of Coca-Cola Arena while taking on broader MENA/APAC strategic-project responsibilities.
- Olea moves from the King Abdullah Financial District Conference Centre in Riyadh to the GM role in Dubai.
- Comes amid Legends Global's continued Gulf-region growth push, including its joint venture with Saudi Arabia's Event Investment Fund.
