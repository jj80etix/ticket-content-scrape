# The Weeknd becomes first artist to sell out six nights at Stade de France for a single tour

## Summary

The Weeknd has become the first artist ever to sell out six nights at Stade de France on a single tour, extending his "After Hours Til Dawn" run which has now sold over 7.5 million tickets across 153 shows worldwide since 2022, making it the highest-grossing tour ever by a male solo artist. The tour continues through Europe and the UK before concluding in Asia this autumn.

### Key Points
- The Weeknd sold out 13 total stadium shows in France across the tour's run since 2022, including six nights at Stade de France.
- More than 500,000 fans attended French dates this summer alone, per Republic Records.
- Tour has grossed over $1bn globally and sold 3 million-plus tickets for $440m+ in 2026 alone.
- Remaining dates include Milan, Frankfurt, Warsaw, Stockholm, five nights at Wembley Stadium (London), Dublin, Barcelona, and Lisbon, before wrapping in Asia (Japan, Indonesia, Singapore, South Korea, Thailand, Hong Kong, Malaysia).

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Weeknd has become the first artist ever to sell out six nights at Stade de France on a single tour, with his ‘After Hours Til Dawn’ tour.**

The Canadian artist first played two nights at the Saint-Denis, Paris venue in July 2022, before returning for four nights earlier this month.

Across the entire tour, which first started in 2022, The Weeknd has now sold out 13 stadium shows throughout France. This summer alone, more than 500,000 fans across France were entertained by the Canadian singer according to Republic Records.

This latest European run marks the second time the ‘After Hours Til Dawn’ stadium tour has travelled the region, following a sold-out 2023 leg. Last year, the tour surpassed $1bn (£750m/€879m) in global gross, following the addition of 2026 dates across Europe, the UK and Latin America.

More than 7.5 million tickets have been sold across 153 shows, making it the highest-grossing tour ever by a male solo artist. In 2026 alone, the tour has sold over three million tickets and earned over $440m.

The Weeknd is still set to perform in Milan tonight (July 24), tomorrow and on Sunday before heading to Frankfurt, Warsaw, Stockholm, London (for five nights at Wembley Stadium), Dublin, Spain, Barcelona, and Lisbon.

[In May](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/05/the-weeknd-announces-asia-dates-to-round-off-after-hours-til-dawn-world-tour/), The Weeknd announced the long-running tour would conclude in Asia this autumn, with stops in Japan, Indonesia, Singapore, South Korea, Thailand, Hong Kong, and Malaysia.
