# MVT & Audoo launch ‘essential’ UK performance royalty study

## Summary

Audio tech firm Audoo and Music Venue Trust are expanding into a three-month national UK study of live-performance royalty tracking, after a pilot found only 21% of performances in grassroots venues matched known songs in existing licensing data. The study aims to build a clearer, data-driven picture of how accurately current music-licensing models reflect what's actually performed in UK grassroots venues.

### Key Points
- Pilot covered two months and 600+ hours of live audio across UK grassroots venues.
- Only 21% of performances matched known songs against existing licensing/royalty data in the pilot.
- Next phase: a three-month national study, described as "essential" to modernizing venue licensing and royalty distribution.
