# Bresh marks biggest Ibiza season with Latin-led programming

## Summary

Argentinian live-event brand Bresh reported its biggest-ever Ibiza season, crediting an increase in Latin live programming — including a closing residency with Amnesia for its 50th anniversary — as a "different model" for the island's dancefloor scene.
