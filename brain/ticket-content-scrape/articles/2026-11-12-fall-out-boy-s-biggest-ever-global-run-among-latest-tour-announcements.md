# Fall Out Boy’s biggest-ever global run among latest tour announcements

## Summary

Fall Out Boy will headline their biggest-ever global tour in 2027, the 36-date "Twilight of the Punks" run across Europe, the UK, and North America, opening May 17 at Prague's O2 Arena. The IQ roundup also covers newly announced dates from Billy Strings, Brandy and Monica, and The Funeral Portrait, plus a returning Anyma/Jean-Michel Jarre pyramid show.

### Key Points
- Fall Out Boy's 36-date "Twilight of the Punks" tour runs May 17-June 13, 2027, hitting Prague, London's Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and Łódź.
- A Day To Remember and Neck Deep support the European/UK leg; The Offspring and Good Charlotte join elsewhere.
- Roundup also includes new tour dates from Billy Strings, Brandy and Monica, and The Funeral Portrait.

Among the latest tours announced across the industry, Fall Out Boy will embark on their biggest-ever global headline tour in 2027, with the 36-date *Twilight of the Punks* run spanning Europe, the UK and North America.

**Fall Out Boy** – *Twighlight of the Punks* revealedThe European and UK leg of the band’s global run begins at Prague’s O2 Arena on 17 May, before reaching London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on 5 June and concluding in Łódź on June 13. A Day To Remember and Neck Deep will support, while The Offspring, Good Charlotte and The Story So Far join the North American dates.

The 21-date North American leg runs from 13 July to 26 August, taking in major stadiums including Toronto’s Rogers Stadium, Boston’s Fenway Park, New York’s Citi Field, Los Angeles’ Dodger Stadium and Chicago’s Soldier Field. The tour follows the release of “ACES”, the band’s first original material in more than three years, and coincides with their 25th anniversary.

**Anyma** – Return to Pyramids of Giza with Jean-Michel Jarre  
Anyma will return to Egypt’s Great Pyramids of Giza for two headline performances in spring 2027, joined by electronic music veteran Jean-Michel Jarre. The shows follow Anyma’s sold-out, two-part performance at the site in 2025, which promoters say attracted fans from 92 countries.

The new production, *ÆDEN*, will feature staging developed with Egyptologist Dr Ashraf Mohie El-Din, while Jarre’s appearance follows his landmark 1999 performance, *Twelve Dreams of the Sun*. Further acts are to be announced for all dates.

**Billy Strings –** 2027 US tour pegged for spring  
Billy Strings will embark on a spring 2027 US tour, beginning in Tampa on 6 April with a run of Florida dates that includes a five-show residency at St Augustine Amphitheatre from 8 – 13 April.

The itinerary continues through the Carolinas and Alabama before concluding with two nights at St Louis’ Chaifetz Arena on June 10 and 11. Tickets will be available through Ticketmaster Request and Fair AXS.

**Brandy and Monica –** First-ever Las Vegas residency announced  
Brandy and Monica will stage their first-ever Las Vegas residency in 2027, with *The Boy Is Mine* Vegas Residency set for 10 performances at Resorts World Theatre.

Co-produced by Black Promoters Collective and AEG Presents Las Vegas, the run begins on 29 May, with five dates running until 5 June, and a second block of five shows from 9-17 July.

**The Funeral Portrait-** Band casts *A Hex On The Nation*  
The Funeral Portrait will headline a 20-date North American tour, *A Hex On The Nation*, beginning 2 March at The Orange Peel in Asheville and ending 28 March 28 at Brooklyn Bowl Nashville.

The Live Nation-promoted run will feature support from Archers, Creeper, If Not For Me, and FKA Rayne on select dates.

**Haute & Freddy** – *Bigger Disgrace* North American tour for 2027  
Haute & Freddy will bring their Bigger Disgrace tour to North America in spring 2027, with headline dates across the US and Canada, alongside two debut Coachella appearances on 9 and 16 April.

The run will take in theatres and mid-sized live music venues, including Phoenix’s The Van Buren, before concluding in Portland on 21 May. The dates follow the duo’s current European and UK tour and precede a seven-show run across Australian theatres in March, and well as a London show at the O2 Forum Kentish Town on 24 August.

**Gang Of Youths** – North American dates confirmed  
Gang Of Youths will return to North America in 2027 for a headline tour supporting their fourth album, *Compañero*, due on 12 March.

The itinerary begins in Boston on 17 March 2027 and travels across the US and Canada, with two nights in Toronto, before reaching Las Vegas on 14 April. The band will also make two appearances at Coachella, on 9 and 16 April.

**Tiffany Day** – North America and Asia on the list for 2027  
Tiffany Day has announced 2027 dates across Asia and North America, including shows in Seoul and Tokyo in February, before a US and Canadian headline run in March and April.

The North American dates will take in clubs and smaller live music venues, including Boston’s Brighton Music Hall and two Coachella performances. The announcement follows her sold-out European and UK *HALO* tour, with Australia and New Zealand dates also scheduled for February.
