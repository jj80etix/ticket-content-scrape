# Jewel Nightclub At Aria Las Vegas Shutting Down After F1 Weekend

## Summary

Jewel Nightclub at Aria Resort Las Vegas will permanently close Nov 21 after a 10-year run, with operator Tao Group Hospitality and owner MGM Resorts agreeing to shut the 24,000-sq-ft venue; MGM says it has no immediate plans for the space. The closure coincides with F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend, with the final show featuring local DJ Kid Conrad.

### Key Points
- Venue opened in May 2016 and hosted artists including Usher, Bad Bunny, Karol G, Steve Aoki and The Chainsmokers.
- MGM points nightlife guests to Marquee at The Cosmopolitan as an alternative.
- No successor use for the space has been announced.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 610371658](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-610371658-scaled.jpg)

Jewel Nightclub at Aria Resort Las Vegas will close its doors permanently Nov. 21 following a 10-year run. MGM Resorts International announced that club operator and partner Tao Group Hospitality agreed to close the venue, and the resort owner said there are “no immediate plans for the space.”

The 24,000-square-foot venue opened in May 2016 and attracted notable hip-hop artists, DJs and many more, including Usher, Bad Bunny, Karol G, Steve Aoki, Lil Uzi Vert, Playboi Carti, Kaskade, Steve Aoki and The Chainsmokers.

Jewel was also a popular venue for celebrities and athletes to host parties.

“Aria and Tao Group Hospitality have enjoyed a strong and collaborative partnership, and we remain committed to exploring future opportunities together,” MGM Resorts Senior Vice President Mio Danilovic said in a statement, according to the *[Las Vegas Review Journal](https://neon.reviewjournal.com/kats/famed-las-vegas-nightclub-shutting-down-after-a-decade-3345258/)*. “… We continue to offer our MGM Resorts guests a variety of nightlife options, highlighted by Marquee at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas, steps from Aria.”

The final show at Jewel Nightclub will feature local DJ Kid Conrad performing Nov. 21 during the F1 Las Vegas Grand Prix weekend.
