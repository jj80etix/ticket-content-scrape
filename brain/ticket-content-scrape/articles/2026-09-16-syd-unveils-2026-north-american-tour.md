# Syd Unveils 2026 North American Tour

## Summary

Syd announced a North American tour supporting her album "Beard," running November-December 2026 across the U.S. and Canada, including two Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival dates.

### Key Points
- Begins Nov. 7 at the Commodore Ballroom, Vancouver; ends Dec. 16 at The Wiltern, LA
- Includes stops in Portland, Seattle, Sacramento, San Diego, Houston, Dallas, Austin, and Atlanta
- Two Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival appearances Nov. 14-15 in LA
- Presales begin Sept. 16; general onsale Sept. 18

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Syd-Beard-Tour.webp)

Syd will return to the road this fall for a North American tour supporting her third solo album, *Beard*.

The singer, songwriter and producer announced a run of November and December concerts that will visit cities across the United States and Canada. The itinerary begins November 7 at the Commodore Ballroom in Vancouver, British Columbia, and wraps up December 16 with a hometown performance at The Wiltern in Los Angeles.

The tour will include concerts in Portland, Seattle, Sacramento, San Diego, Houston, Dallas, Austin and Atlanta during November. Syd will then travel through the eastern United States and Canada before finishing the run with shows in Chicago, Las Vegas, Phoenix and San Francisco.

Her schedule also includes two appearances at the Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival in Los Angeles on November 14 and 15. The festival dates will be followed by a November 16 performance at Observatory North Park in San Diego.
