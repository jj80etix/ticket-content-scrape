# Arenametrix announces launch of Arenametrix Pulse as part of its two-phase AI plans

## Summary

Audience-data platform Arenametrix has launched Arenametrix Pulse, an AI layer that moves its CRM platform from data aggregation into analysis and strategic guidance for sports/entertainment organizations, as phase one of a two-phase plan to become a broader AI-enablement data layer.

### Key Points
- Pulse is in beta with a focus group of customers ahead of a wider release.
- Phase one connects five previously siloed stages: aggregation, analysis, strategy, segmentation, activation.
- Positioned as context-aware guidance rather than a generic chatbot — it analyzes each organization's own data.
- Phase two: become a trusted, "agent-ready" data layer underpinning customers' own AI use cases.

### Highlights
- Arenametrix Pulse launched in limited beta in July 2026, extending AI from data aggregation into analysis and strategy recommendations.
- Explicit two-phase roadmap: CRM intelligence now, broader AI-enablement data layer later.

### Industry Problem
Sports and entertainment organizations collect audience data across ticketing, access control, apps, and merchandise systems that don't communicate, leaving insight trapped and decision-making reactive ("what happened?") rather than prescriptive ("what should we do next?").

### Proposed Solution
Arenametrix is building AI directly into its CRM to unify data and recommend actions; this is a direct AI-deployment benchmark for Etix Assistant — worth tracking how quickly competitors move from descriptive analytics to prescriptive, agent-ready data layers.

**Audience data platform Arenametrix has announced the initial launch of Arenametrix Pulse, a new AI-powered capability designed to help sports and entertainment organisations understand their audience and business data.**

The goal is to translate this data into clearer strategic action.

Arenametrix Pulse has launched with a focus group of customers, allowing Arenmetrix to develop it alongside users, validate recommendations in different operational contexts and refine the experience before a broader release.

It marks an important step in Arenametrix’s two-phase product vision, with phase one focusing on using data and AI to improve the complete CRM process. The ambition is to then build off this work, and provide the agent-ready data architecture organisations what they require to develop their own AI-powered use cases.

Additionally, the first phase will focus on connecting five stages that are frequently separated by different systems, functionalities and workflows, including aggregation, analysis, strategy, segmentation and activation.

Sports clubs, venues and live entertainment organisations collect information across ticketing, access control, mobile applications, merchandise, customer feedback and digital engagement – though this data does not always move easily between these systems.

Arenametrix already centralises information from across the customer journey, and once consolidated, this data can be analysed, used to create tailored B2C and B2B segments, and activated through marketing channels or connected technologies. Not only this, but it helps entertainment organisations navigate the changing world of data compliance and security.

AI already supports two parts of this process: data aggregation and capping design. Arenmetrix Pulse extends it into analysis and an initial level of strategic guidance.

Rather than operating as a generic chatbot, Arenametrix Pulse analyses each organisation’s information within its own context. It has been designed to help users identify important developments, what could be driving this, and determine which audience or CRM opportunities deserve attention.

“The real value of AI is not adding another standalone feature,” said Kevin Vitoz, co-founder at Arenametrix.

“It is removing the friction between data, insight and activation, so organisations can build stronger fan and audience strategies without being held back by disconnected systems, tools or workflows.”

**Moving from ‘what happened?’ to ‘why does this matter?’ and ‘what should we do next?’**

Arenametrix Pulse will support users in interpreting information and identifying potential directions. Organisations will still be responsible for defining their objects, validating recommendations and deciding which actions to implement.

“AI should not simply sit in the corner of a CRM as a generic chatbot,” added Vitoz. “It should help connect the entire decision chain – from understanding performance to identifying the right audience and taking the next action.”

During the beta phase, Arenametrix will work with the focus group to assess how the capability performs across different organisations and uses. The next stage of the first phase will create a stronger connection between analysis, segmentation and campaign activation, reducing manual work between identifying an opportunity, selecting the right audience and designing the appropriate communication.

**Building the bridge to Phase 2**

Organisations will eventually be able to use Arenametrix not only as a CRM platform, but as a trusted data and intelligence layer behind the broader range of AI-powered applications.

“The organisations that benefit the most from AI will be those with the strongest data foundations,” said Vitoz.

“Our priority today is to make the CRM process more intelligent and accessible. Over time, that same work will allow customers to build broader use cases on trusted, well-structured audience data.”

Phase two encompasses Arenametrix’s ambition to become a dedicated AI-enablement data solution for sports and entertainment venues.
