# World Supercross expands revenue opportunities with Recast

## Summary

World Supercross is expanding its direct-to-consumer content and monetization strategy for the 2026 season by deepening its use of Recast, a platform enabling pay-per-view content sales and automatic revenue splits. Having generated over $500,000 from roughly 23,000 customers across 70+ countries via Recast in 2025, the championship will introduce a wider bundle of digital passes for 2026 and explicitly link in-person ticket purchases to discounted digital content access.

### Key Points
- World Supercross earned $500,000+ in direct content purchases from ~23,000 customers in 70+ countries via Recast in 2025.
- For the 2026 season (starting August 8 in Canada), Recast's bundle technology will power a Championship Season Pass, race-weekend passes, a Bonus Content Pass, a Highlights Pass, and standalone PPV access via "W+".
- In-person ticket buyers at any of the six race weekends will receive a discount code for W+ digital content.
- Recast CEO Andy Meikle framed the model as connecting content, ticketing, brands, riders, and distribution partners into one integrated commercial system.

### Highlights
- $500K+ in direct content purchase revenue from ~23,000 customers across 70+ countries (2025, via Recast).
- 2026 season adds a tiered digital-pass structure (Season Pass, race-weekend passes, Bonus Content Pass, Highlights Pass, PPV) layered on top of physical ticketing.
- Physical ticket buyers get a built-in discount funnel into the digital content ecosystem via a bundled discount code.

### Industry Problem
Rights-holders and ticketing partners increasingly need revenue and engagement to extend beyond the single physical-event transaction, but most primary ticketing platforms have no native mechanism to bundle digital content access, PPV, or tiered passes with the ticket purchase itself, leaving that value on the table or ceded to third-party platforms like Recast.

### Proposed Solution
World Supercross's approach pairs its ticketing funnel with Recast's content-bundle and revenue-split infrastructure to convert one-time attendees into repeat digital-content customers. An independent primary platform like Etix could explore similar bundling — e.g. season-pass or membership-style digital add-ons issued alongside ticket purchases, or a partner integration for PPV/highlights content — to extend client revenue relationships beyond the gate and strengthen retention data.

**The World Supercross Championship has revealed plans to rev up its direct-to-consumer distribution and monetisation strategy for the 2026 season.**

The news comes after the motorcycle racing championship generated more than $500,000 (£372,000/€435,000) in direct content purchases from approximately 23,000 customers across more than 70 countries using Recast in 2025.

Building on these results, for the 2026 season World Supercross will expand the way customers can buy content, connect event ticketing with digital consumption, and increase the commercial contribution of riders and motorsport publishers.

Recast enables automatic revenue splits and instant, pay-per-view access anywhere at any time. The strategy will create additional revenue opportunities alongside the series’ existing broadcast agreements.

For the 2026 season, which will begin on August 8 in Canada, World Supercross will use Recast’s content bundle technology to introduce a wider range of content access through W+. This includes a Championship Season Pass, individual race-weekend passes, a Bonus Content Pass and a Highlights Pass, as well as direct, Pay-Per-View access to specific pieces of content.

The different options will allow World Supercross to serve each level of demand, creating more purchasing opportunities throughout the season. Fans purchasing in-person tickets for any of the championship’s six race weekends will also receive a discount code for access to content on W+.

Connecting ticketing with digital access will enable World Supercross to increase the value of each customer relationship, extend engagement beyond the physical event, and create additional digital revenue. It will also give World Supercross a clearer view of the relationship between physical attendance and digital consumption.

“We’re delighted to be expanding our partnership with World Supercross, which continues to demonstrate how forward-thinking sports properties can unlock greater value for their audiences and wider commercial ecosystem,” said Andy Meikle, founder and chief executive of Recast.

“By connecting content, ticketing, brands, riders and distribution partners through Recast, World Supercross can build a more integrated commercial model that converts demand into revenue and shares that value across the network.”
