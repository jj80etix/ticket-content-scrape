# Pair of metropolitan European festivals shelved: ‘We can’t go on’

## Summary

Czechia's Metronome Prague and Denmark's Syd For Solen festivals will not return in 2027, with both organizers citing difficulty sustaining metropolitan-scale events "in today's market." Metronome Prague had just marked its 10th anniversary with a new Live Nation Czech partnership before the shutdown.

### Key Points
- Both festivals confirmed no 2027 edition.
- Metronome Prague's 10th edition (June 2026) hosted Nick Cave & The Bad Seeds, Sting, and Tom Odell at Letňany Airport.
- Metronome had revealed a strategic partnership with Live Nation Czech ahead of its final edition.
- Syd For Solen's lineup had included Lorde.
