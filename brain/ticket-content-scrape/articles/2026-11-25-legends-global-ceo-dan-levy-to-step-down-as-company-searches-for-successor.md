# Legends Global CEO Dan Levy to Step Down as Company Searches for Successor

## Summary

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down after more than two and a half years leading the venue-management and live-entertainment services company, during which it completed and integrated its acquisition of ASM Global. Levy will stay on until a successor is named; longtime executive Chad Estis has been named president to help oversee the transition.

### Key Points
- Levy joined Legends in March 2024, became CEO in April 2024, and oversaw completion/integration of the ~$2.3B ASM Global acquisition.
- No timetable set for the transition; Levy remains in place until a successor is hired.
- Chad Estis named president, will help manage the transition.
- Prior to Legends, Levy spent 14 years at Meta in senior leadership roles.

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down after more than two and a half years at the helm of the sports and live entertainment services giant, a tenure that included the completion of the company’s acquisition of ASM Global and the subsequent integration of the two businesses into one of the world’s largest venue management and entertainment services operations.

Levy will remain in his position until a successor is hired, according to [a report Monday from Bret McCormick of Sports Business Journal](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/11/23/legends-global-ceo-is-stepping-down-replacement-search-is-underway/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Levy informed employees of his decision in an internal memo, with the move made in conjunction with the company’s board of directors. No timetable has been established for the leadership transition.

Longtime Legends executive Chad Estis has been named president of the company and will help oversee the transition, retaining his new position following the appointment of Levy’s successor.

Legends Global has not publicly announced the leadership changes. The company’s website continued to list Levy as CEO and Estis as executive vice president and chief revenue officer as of Tuesday.

Levy joined Legends in March 2024 as interim president and a member of its board of directors before [being elevated to CEO the following month](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/04/former-meta-exec-dan-levy-appointed-to-head-of-legends/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), succeeding Shervin Mirhashemi. His appointment came as Legends was working to finalize its acquisition of ASM Global, a transaction that substantially expanded its presence in the global live entertainment industry.

Before joining Legends, Levy spent 14 years at Meta, formerly Facebook, where he held several senior leadership positions, including oversight of the company’s global advertising business and efforts to generate revenue from messaging platforms WhatsApp and Messenger. His experience also included an early venture into the ticketing business, co-founding electronic ticketing service Justarrive while attending Stanford University.

## Leadership Change Follows ASM Global Integration

The most consequential development of Levy’s tenure was the completion of the approximately $2.3 billion acquisition of ASM Global in August 2024. Although the transaction was announced before Levy joined Legends, he oversaw its completion and the subsequent combination of the two companies’ operations.

The businesses [formally relaunched as Legends Global in September 2025](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/09/legends-asm-global-relaunch-as-integrated-legends-global/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), bringing together a portfolio of more than 450 venues worldwide that collectively host approximately 20,000 events and welcome more than 165 million guests annually, according to the company.

The acquisition transformed Legends from a company known primarily for its work in sports hospitality, premium seating sales and venue development into a major global venue operator with an extensive presence across professional and collegiate sports, concerts, theaters and convention centers.

Its expanded capabilities have positioned Legends Global to compete for business with other major live entertainment companies, including Oak View Group, across multiple aspects of venue development, management and revenue generation.

Legends Global is majority-owned by investment firm Sixth Street, alongside ownership interests associated with the New York Yankees and the Jerry Jones family, owners of the Dallas Cowboys.

Estis has been involved with Legends since its founding in 2008 and has played a central role in the expansion of its commercial operations. In February 2025, the company [expanded his responsibilities as chief revenue officer](https://legendsglobal.com/press-release-chad-estis-expands-role-at-legends-to-become-executive-vice-president-chief-revenue-officer/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), placing him in charge of sales, corporate partnerships, collegiate sports services, market research and business development.

His promotion to president provides a measure of leadership continuity for the company as it pursues several major venue development and commercialization projects.

In July, the Dallas Mavericks selected Legends Global to represent the franchise’s interests in the development of its future arena and oversee premium seating sales at the facility. The Kansas City Royals followed in October, hiring the company as their representative for the development of a new downtown ballpark.

Internationally, Legends Global announced a 10-year partnership with Coventry City Football Club and Coventry Building Society Arena in August, supported by a joint investment of more than £15 million over three years that includes improvements to premium seating areas, hospitality, technology and venue infrastructure.

Levy’s successor will inherit a significantly larger and more diversified business than the one he was appointed to lead in 2024, with the challenge of capitalizing on the combined company’s reach across the increasingly competitive markets for venue management, premium ticketing and live entertainment services.
