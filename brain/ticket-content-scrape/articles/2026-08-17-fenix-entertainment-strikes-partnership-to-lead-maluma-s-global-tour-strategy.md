# Fenix Entertainment strikes partnership to lead Maluma’s global tour strategy

## Summary

Argentinian independent promoter Fenix Entertainment has struck a partnership to lead the global touring strategy of Colombian star Maluma across 2027–2028. Fenix, which has previously worked with Luis Miguel, Ricardo Montaner, and Shakira, called it an "enormous responsibility" given Maluma's stadium-level draw.

### Key Points
- Fenix Entertainment partners with Maluma to lead his global touring strategy for 2027–2028.
- Fenix's prior roster includes Luis Miguel, Ricardo Montaner, Ricardo Arjona, and Shakira.

## Fenix Entertainment strikes partnership to lead Maluma’s global tour strategy

The independent promoter welcomed the 'enormous responsibility' of overseeing the artist, who is already established at stadium and arena level

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Maluma.png)

Maluma © Fenix Entertainment

Independent Argentinian promoter Fenix Entertainment has announced a partnership with Colombian singer and rapper Maluma to lead the strategy behind his upcoming global touring activity across 2027 and 2028.

The deal will bring the stadium-level artist into the fold at the independent promoter, which Fenix founder Marcelo Figoli described as a “challenge and an enormous responsibility”. The artist has previously played to crowd of 54,000 at Atanasio Girardot Stadium in Medellín, Colombia, with other notable performances including a headline slot at the O2 in London last March.

At this large arena to stadium level, Maluma has drawn crowds comparable to artists already in Fenix’s portfolio, such as Luis Miguel.

The deal with Maluma is similar in scope to Fenix’s strategy of establishing partnerships for full-service career development of Colombian artists, following arrangements with artists including Jorge Celedón to develop his international touring footprint.

Fenix recently told *IQ* it had seen growth from six events to 60 in the space of twelve months, with plans to expand internationally around Colombia as a “regional entertainment hub”.

> “Working with an artist with Maluma’s career and international reach is both a challenge and an enormous responsibility”

At the time, the company cited a “consolidation” of the company as a factor behind its international expansion, saying this has “allowed us to access artists, content, and projects of international scope”. Examples of this are the ten concerts on Ricardo Montaner’s *El Último Regreso* tour operated from Colombia and the regional coordination of Junior H’s Latin American tour.

“With more than a decade of international success, Maluma has established himself as one of the most influential figures in Latin music, with a career that has taken him to major markets across Latin America, the United States, and Europe,” Fenix said, announcing the Maluma deal.

The company added that Maluma’s upcoming tour, details of which are set to be releases soon, will mark the “next evolution of his ever-growing career, bringing this new artistic chapter to major stages around the world throughout 2027 and 2028”.

“Working with an artist with Maluma’s career and international reach is both a challenge and an enormous responsibility. We believe in his vision, his talent, and the exciting moment he is experiencing in his career,” said Fenix’s Figoli. “We are incredibly proud to be part of this new chapter and to put our team and experience behind a project of this scale.”
