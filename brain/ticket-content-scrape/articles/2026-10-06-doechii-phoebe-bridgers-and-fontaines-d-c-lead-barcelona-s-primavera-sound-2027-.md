# Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers and Fontaines D.C. Lead Barcelona’s Primavera Sound 2027 Lineup

## Summary

TicketNews' coverage of the same Primavera Sound 25th-anniversary lineup announcement (Barcelona, June 3–5, 2027) covered above, adding registration details for the festival's Fan Sale (through Oct. 7) and general onsale (Oct. 9).

### Key Points
- Same lineup as reported by Pollstar: Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers, Fontaines D.C., Hayley Williams, FKA twigs and 151 total acts, June 3–5, 2027, Parc del Fòrum, Barcelona.
- Fan Sale registration open through Oct. 7, 11:59 PM CEST; access instructions emailed Oct. 8.
- General onsale begins Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Barcelona-Primavera-Sound-.webp)

Primavera Sound has announced the lineup for its 25th anniversary edition, bringing Doechii, Phoebe Bridgers and Fontaines D.C. to Barcelona alongside an extensive roster of artists spanning pop, rock, hip-hop and electronic music.

The festival’s main performances will take place June 3 to 5, 2027, at Parc del Fòrum. The broader celebration runs June 2 to 6, beginning with a free opening day and ending with the Primavera Bits electronic music program.

Hayley Williams, FKA twigs, Massive Attack, Turnstile, The Chemical Brothers and Robyn are also among the leading names announced. The upper portion of the bill includes Bad Gyal, Lola Young, Adéla, Caroline Polachek and Prospa, along with a back-to-back performance from Skrillex and Ninajirachi.

Organizers have announced 151 artists and bands for the anniversary edition, assembling a program that pairs established acts with newer performers across the three main festival days.

Beyond the headliners, the lineup includes Pavement, Tinashe, King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, Underworld, ANOHNI and Dinosaur Jr. Weyes Blood, Julien Baker, Bleachers and Bonnie “Prince” Billy are also scheduled to perform.

The electronic and experimental selections include Arca, Snow Strippers, Shygirl and Kim Petras. Other announced artists include Vashti Bunyan, Juana Molina, Mabe Fratti, Converge and Atlas Sound.

Fans planning to attend can register for the festival’s Fan Sale through October 7 at 11:59 p.m. Central European Summer Time. Registered users will receive an email October 8 with instructions for accessing the sale, which begins at 11 a.m. CEST.

The Fan Sale will remain open for 24 hours or until its ticket allocation sells out. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin October 9 at 11 a.m. CEST.
