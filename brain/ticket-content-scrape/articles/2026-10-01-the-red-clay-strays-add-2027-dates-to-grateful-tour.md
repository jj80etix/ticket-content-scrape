FINAL 2027 Grateful Tour Square

The Red Clay Strays are feeling extra grateful and extending their tour into 2027. The Alabama-born band is on the road promoting its latest album, *Grateful*, performing arenas and amphitheaters across North America.

The band kicks off the 2027 leg of their “Grateful Tour” Jan. 22 at Lenovo Center in Raleigh, and the routing includes stops at Rosemont’s Allstate Arena near Chicago, Omaha’s CHI Health Center, Vystar Veterans Memorial Arena in Jacksonville, Winnipeg’s Canada Life Centre, Pine Knob Music Theatre in Clarkston, Los Angeles’ Greek Theatre and Oklahoma City’s Paycom Center before wrapping up Sept. 19 at Moody Center in Austin.

The Red Clay Strays will also perform a three-night stand in their hometown at Regions Arena in Mobile March 25-27.

Avery Anna, The Bends and St. Paul & The Broken Bones will support the new dates.

A fan club presale begins Oct. 5 at 10 a.m. local time, and additional presales will run throughout the week leading up to the general onsale, which begins Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. For ticket information, visit [redclaystrays.com/tour](https://www.redclaystrays.com/tour).

The band’s third studio album, *Grateful*, was released in June via HBYCO Records/RCA Records and produced once again by noted musician and producer Dave Cobb. The album’s first single, “Demons In Your Choir,” is described as “A deeply personal spiritual rumination unlike anything in the Mobile, AL-based rock band’s previous body of work.”

The Red Clay Strays are represented by WME and managed by Cody Payne at All For One MGMT.
