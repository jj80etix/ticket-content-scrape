# Luke Bryan, Megan Moroney Set To Perform At 60th Annual CMA Awards

## Summary

Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney join the performer lineup for the 60th Annual CMA Awards, airing live Nov. 18 from Nashville's Bridgestone Arena.

### Key Points
- Additional performers include Vince Gill, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton and Lainey Wilson
- Brothers Osborne, Bryan, Halestorm, HARDY and Marcus King will celebrate Brooks & Dunn's Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award
- Lainey Wilson returns as host for a third year, with three nominations

Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney are among the newly announced slate of artists set to perform at the 60th Annual CMA Awards. Other performers set to take the stage include Vince Gill, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton, Lainey Wilson and Stephen Wilson Jr. to the lineup. Brothers Osborne, Luke Bryan, Halestorm, HARDY and Marcus King will come together to celebrate Brooks & Dunn, this year’s recipients of the CMA Willie Nelson Lifetime Achievement Award.

The CMAs air live from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Nov. 18 at 8 p.m. on ABC and Disney+ and are available the next day on Hulu.

Returning as host for her third consecutive year, reigning Entertainer of the Year Lainey Wilson earns three nominations this year, including Entertainer, Female Vocalist, and Single of the Year.

“The 60th Annual CMA Awards” is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as Executive Producer, Alan Carter is Director, and Jon Macks is Head Writer.
