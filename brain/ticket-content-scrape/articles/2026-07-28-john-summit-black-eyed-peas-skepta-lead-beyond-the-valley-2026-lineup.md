# John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, Skepta Lead Beyond The Valley 2026 Lineup

## Summary

Australian festival Beyond The Valley announced its 2026 lineup, headlined by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, and Skepta. Standard festival lineup announcement.

### Key Points
- Beyond The Valley 2026 lineup announced, headlined by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, and Skepta.
- Coverage includes standard ticketing/onsale details for the festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/beyond-the-valley.jpg)

Beyond The Valley will ring in 2027 with John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, KI/KI and Skepta at the top of a stacked lineup for the Australian festival’s 11th edition.

The four-day event returns to Barunah Plains in Victoria from Dec. 28, 2026, through Jan. 1, 2027, following a 2025 edition that reportedly sold out in less than an hour.

John Summit will command the festival’s marquee New Year’s Eve countdown slot, while six-time Grammy winners Black Eyed Peas are set to play an exclusive Australian performance. Dutch DJ and producer KI/KI will deliver a three-hour set, and British grime heavyweight Skepta will make his first Beyond The Valley appearance since the 2019/20 edition.

The bill also features Ocean Alley, Overmono, Disco Lines, Nia Archives, Armand Van Helden, The Veronicas, Vince Staples, Frost Children, SPFDJ, Jayda G, salute, southstar, Odd Mob and Boys Noize.

Summit arrives at Beyond The Valley after releasing his latest album, “CTRL ESCAPE,” and headlining Tomorrowland. The dance star has generated more than 1 billion global streams and recently mounted three sold-out shows at Los Angeles’ Kia Forum.

Black Eyed Peas bring a catalog packed with global hits including “Where Is The Love?,” “Boom Boom Pow” and “I Gotta Feeling.” The group has sold more than 35 million albums and 120 million singles worldwide.

Beyond The Valley will also welcome several major Australian acts. Ocean Alley will make its festival debut following the release of chart-topping album “Love Balloon,” while The Veronicas return for the first time since their widely celebrated 2019 performance.

Elsewhere, Nia Archives will bring her live show to Australia for the first time, Vince Staples will add a dose of sharp-edged hip-hop, and electronic veteran Armand Van Helden will perform three decades into his influential career.

The expanded electronic lineup includes Hannah Laing, Ewan McVicar, Funk Tribu, Anetha, Hamdi, Job Jobse, Layton Giordani, Oppidan, Tommy Holohan and Jordan Brando B2B Luuk Van Dijk.

Beyond the music, the festival’s Lounge Room program will return with live podcasts, conversations, comedy and interactive events. Attractions include Cupid’s Court Live Dating Show, “In Pour Taste: Comedy Wine Tasting,” Fast Fashun and appearances from Kath Ebbs, Wintana Kidane and Ross and Will Weightman.

The festival’s Valley Stage, Dance Dome and Dr Dan’s venues will all return, joined by a new hidden camping destination called The Woods. Other experiences include The Palms Pool House, POOF DOOF’s Cirque Du Slay, The Sanctuary and the members-only Valley Club.

Tickets will be offered in four-day, three-day, two-day, New Year’s Eve-only and single-day formats, with multiple camping, hotel and transportation packages available. The Daily Aus presale begins Aug. 4 at 10 a.m. AEST, followed by the official presale Aug. 6 at 6 p.m. AEST. General ticket sales begin Aug. 7 at noon AEST [here](https://www.beyondthevalley.com.au).
