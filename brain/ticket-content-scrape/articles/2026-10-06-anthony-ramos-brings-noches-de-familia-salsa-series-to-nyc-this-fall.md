# Anthony Ramos Brings ‘Noches de Familia’ Salsa Series to NYC This Fall

## Summary

Anthony Ramos will headline a three-night "Noches de Familia" salsa residency at NYC's Nublu club (Nov. 25, Dec. 2, Dec. 9), continuing his recent pivot into salsa music alongside producer Sergio George. Tickets are on sale now via Ramos' official ticket page.

### Key Points
- Three Wednesday-night shows at Nublu (East Village): Nov. 25, Dec. 2, Dec. 9.
- Built around Ramos' growing salsa catalog, produced with Sergio George.
- Newest single "Wasn't Even Lookin'" released Sept. 25.
- Tickets on sale now via Ramos' own ticketing page.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Anthony-Ramos.webp)

Anthony Ramos will turn his recent push into salsa music into a three-night New York City residency this fall, setting a trio of “Noches de Familia” performances at East Village live music club Nublu.

The singer, actor and producer will perform on three consecutive Wednesdays — November 25, December 2 and December 9 — with the shows built around his growing catalog of salsa-influenced music.

The three-show run arrives as Ramos continues leaning further into the Latin music influences that have accompanied his better-known work on Broadway and in film. Born and raised in Brooklyn to Puerto Rican parents, Ramos has increasingly put salsa at the center of his recording work.

His newest single, “Wasn’t Even Lookin’,” was released September 25 through Ramos Music/The Orchard and produced by salsa veteran Sergio George. George has also worked with Ramos on recent tracks “New York City” and “I Was the One.”
