# Fever Named Official Ticketing Partner of Premier Jumping League

## Summary

Fever has been named the official ticketing partner of the new Premier Jumping League, an international team showjumping competition launching April 2027 in Las Vegas with a nine-event calendar and $100 million in guaranteed prize money per season. Fever will supply demand-generation technology and its distribution network to help the league convert broadcast/media awareness (including a "Drive to Survive"-style docuseries deal) into ticketed attendance.

### Key Points
- Fever named official ticketing partner for Premier Jumping League's inaugural 2027 season (nine events, North America/Europe/Middle East).
- League backed by McCourt Global/Frank McCourt; FEI-approved; $300M guaranteed prize money across first three seasons.
- Fever's role centers on audience-development/demand-generation tech and premium ticket offers, not just transactional ticketing.
- Partnership follows a production deal with Box to Box Films (makers of F1's "Drive to Survive") aimed at building awareness ahead of ticket sales.

### Highlights
- Fever secures exclusive ticketing for a new nine-event, three-continent sports league backed by $300M in guaranteed prize money.
- Deal pairs ticketing with audience-development/demand-gen technology rather than pure transaction processing.

### Industry Problem
New sports/entertainment properties need to convert broad media and docuseries-driven awareness into actual ticket buyers — a demand-generation problem, not just a transaction problem.

### Proposed Solution
Build/improve: competitors like Fever are winning new-property deals by bundling demand-generation and audience data on top of ticketing; a primary platform could pursue similar packaged demand-gen offerings for emerging leagues/properties to compete for this type of business.

Fever has been appointed the official ticketing partner of the Premier Jumping League, a new international team showjumping competition scheduled to begin April 15–17, 2027, in Las Vegas.

The agreement covers a nine-event calendar spanning North America, Europe and the Middle East. Fever will provide its demand-generation technology and global distribution network as the league seeks to attract spectators to its inaugural season.

The partnership adds ticketing and audience development services to a launch strategy that includes free-to-view broadcasts and a production agreement with Box to Box Films, the company behind Formula 1’s “Drive to Survive.”

Premier Jumping League chief executive Neil Moffitt said the deal would support efforts to expand showjumping’s audience and encourage more people to attend competitions.

“Growing the audience for showjumping is fundamental to what we are building at the PJL. That means making the sport easier to discover, easier to follow and, importantly, easier to experience live.”

The first season will comprise 16 teams competing through a regular season before a semi-final and final. A midseason transfer window is also part of the format, and eligibility is open to riders ranked within the world’s top 250 on ranking points.

The league says it has guaranteed $100 million in prize money per season for its first three seasons, equivalent to a total commitment of $300 million over that period.

The FEI, equestrian sport’s international governing body, approved the series earlier this year. That approval formally sanctioned its competition format, team structure and distribution of prize money.

McCourt Global and its executive chairman, Frank McCourt, have backed the venture.

For Fever, the agreement includes connecting the competition with prospective customers through its platform, alongside ticketing and premium offers. Hannah Joos, Fever’s general manager and managing director for the DACH region, said the partnership aligned with the league’s emphasis on spectators.

“The PJL is bringing showjumping to an extended fan base, building a sustainable future for the sport and putting the fan experience at its centre.

“That’s exactly where Fever can help: our platform connects the league with new audiences, our ticketing and premium offers strengthen its commercial foundation, and our data-driven approach makes every event day more personal.”

Moffitt said Fever’s role would extend beyond reaching existing equestrian audiences, with the league looking to translate broader awareness into attendance at its events.

“Fever brings proven technology, global reach and a deep understanding of how to identify and engage audiences beyond traditional fanbases. This partnership gives us the infrastructure to turn that reach into attendance across our global calendar, introducing more people to the extraordinary athletes and competition at the heart of our sport.”

The ticketing appointment follows the regulatory approval as preparations continue for the Las Vegas opener. The April competition will begin a season designed to combine a team-based sporting format with international events and wider broadcast access.

“As we build towards our first season in 2027, Fever will be an important partner in delivering our ambition to make elite showjumping more accessible to audiences around the world,” Moffitt said.
