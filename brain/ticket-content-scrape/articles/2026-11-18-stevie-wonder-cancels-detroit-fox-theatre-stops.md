# Stevie Wonder Cancels Detroit Fox Theatre Stops

## Summary

Stevie Wonder has canceled his Dec. 8–9 Detroit Fox Theatre stops on the "Songs In The Key of Life — 50th Anniversary Tour" due to "production restrictions," with organizers planning to announce a new venue for the hometown shows. Ticketmaster notified fans of automatic refunds within 14–21 days.

### Key Points
- Dec. 8–9 sold-out Fox Theatre (Detroit) dates canceled, citing production restrictions.
- Organizers plan to announce a replacement venue for the Detroit shows.
- Ticketmaster is issuing automatic refunds to the original payment method, expected within 14–21 days.
- The tour marks the 50th anniversary of Wonder's *Songs In The Key of Life* album.

## Transcript

Stevie Wonder has officially canceled his Detroit dates at the Fox Theatre on Dec. 8 & 9 for the “Songs In The Key Of Life Performances” Tour, marking the 50th Anniversary. Organizers of the sold-out dates plan to announce a new venue for the hometown shows. In emails sent by Ticketmaster, fans were notified that the shows at Fox Theatre couldn’t go on due to “production restrictions.”

“Unfortunately, the Event Organizer has had to cancel your event,” the statement on the [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/stevie-wonder-songs-in-the-key-of-life-performances-50th-anniversary-detroit-12-08-2026/event/080065261D18D5BC) website reads. “You don’t need to do a thing. We’ll issue a refund to the original payment method used at the time of purchase as soon as we receive funds from the Event Organizer. It should appear on your account within 14-21 days.”

Originally released in 1976, *Songs In The Key of Life* earned Stevie Wonder the third of his three GRAMMY® Awards for Album of the Year. The album went Diamond (10+ million copies sold) and was inducted into the Library of Congress’ National Recording Registry. Featuring some of Wonder’s most beloved and enduring songs, it continues to inspire generations of artists and fans around the world.
