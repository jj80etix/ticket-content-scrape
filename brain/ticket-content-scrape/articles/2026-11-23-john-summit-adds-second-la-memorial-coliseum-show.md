# John Summit Adds Second LA Memorial Coliseum Show

## Summary

DJ John Summit added a second Dec. 11 show at the LA Memorial Coliseum after his Dec. 12 date sold out, closing his 2026 CTRL ESCAPE Tour with a holiday toy drive benefiting the LA Fire Department. Tickets go on public sale Nov. 25 through Ticketmaster, with artist, Cash App and Spotify Reserved presales preceding it.

### Key Points
- The added Dec. 11 show follows a sold-out Dec. 12 Coliseum date; Summit is only the sixth artist with 2+ Coliseum shows.
- Partnership with the LA Fire Department toy drive at both shows; Jazwares is donating Squishmallows.
- Presale tiers: Cash App Visa (Nov. 23), artist presale (Nov. 24, 10am PT), general on-sale (Nov. 25, 2pm PT) via Ticketmaster.
- Eligible Spotify Premium subscribers get access via Ticketmaster's "Reserved by Spotify" program.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/John-Summit-.webp)

John Summit will headline the Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum on Friday, December 11, after his December 12 concert at the stadium sold out. The added show gives the DJ and producer two nights in Los Angeles to close his 2026 CTRL ESCAPE Tour.

“Ending the tour in LA with my biggest headline show ever was already insane; now adding a second night is unreal,” Summit said in a statement.

“We’re also teaming up with the LA Fire Department for a holiday toy drive to give back and spread some holiday cheer. Can’t think of a better way to close out the year.”

Summit and the Los Angeles Fire Department will hold a holiday toy drive at both Coliseum concerts. Fans can drop off new, unwrapped toys at any stadium gate before entering. Toymaker Jazwares will also donate Squishmallows.

Summit will also play Chicago’s United Center on November 24 and 25, followed by a November 27 show at the Liacouras Center in Philadelphia and two nights at Barclays Center in Brooklyn on November 28 and 29.
