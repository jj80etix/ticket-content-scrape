# Arie Kaduri, A Concert Promoter Who Helped Establish Major Latin Touring Acts In The US, Dies At 90

## Summary

Concert promoter Arie Kaduri, founder of NYK Productions and a pioneering figure in bringing Latin music acts to major U.S. markets, has died at 90. Industry figures including Live Nation, CMN and artists such as Julio Iglesias and Maná paid tribute to his decades-long role shaping the U.S. Latin touring business. The piece is a tribute/obituary with no bearing on industry pain points, regulation, technology or market structure.

### Key Points
- Arie Kaduri, founder of NYK Productions, died Nov. 12 at age 90.
- Credited with establishing the U.S. as a major market for Spanish-language touring artists since the 1970s.
- Tributes from Live Nation, Cárdenas Marketing Network, Julio Iglesias, Maná and others.

## Transcript

![88220201 e8ae 4c40 9d1e 2d71fb3560ac](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/88220201-e8ae-4c40-9d1e-2d71fb3560ac.jpg)

Arie Kaduri Courtesy NYK Productions

Many in the touring business are mourning the death of influential promoter Arie Kaduri, who founded NYK Productions and helped make Latin music a force at box offices across the U.S. Kaduri died Nov. 12 at the age of 90, Fusion 4 Media and his daughter Natalie announced on [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/p/DfscjG1NIN6/).

“He is a legend in the concert industry, and his contribution to the music industry is unmatched,” read the caption to the photo announcing Kaduri’s death. “He was the first promoter to bring acts such as Julio Iglesias, Luis Miguel, Ana Gabriel, Rocio Durcal, Paloma San Basilio, Raul Di Blasio, Danny Rivera, José Luis Perales, Dyango, Maná, too many to name! Thank you for the music, Kaduri!”

Born in Baghdad, Iraq, Kaduri grew up in Israel and later settled in Miami. He played the accordion before going into the music business as a concert promoter in the 1970s. He founded NYK Productions and recognized the potential of Latin acts in the States. He was instrumental in establishing the U.S. as a major concert market for Spanish-language artists such as José José, Juan Gabriel, Rocío Dúrcal, Roberto Carlos, Shakira and Ricky Martin, all of whom became Latin music icons.

For decades, he helped pave the road that has led to great success for today’s promoters like Live Nation, Loud And Live and Cárdenas Marketing Network (CMN).

“Arie was a genuine trailblazer, one of my mentors and partners, a great human being, a gentleman with a great heart,” said Henry Cárdenas, CEO and founder of CMN.

Phil Rodriguez, CEO of Move Concerts, added: “Arie was a lovable character. He definitely marked an era for Latin music, particularly in Miami.”

Mexican rock icons Maná said working with Kaduri was “an adventure,” and they were able to visit Israel working with the promoter.

“He was a man who loved music and grew genuinely fond of the artists,” Maná said in a statement. “We even traveled with him to Israel—back when things were much calmer there. He took us on a magnificent tour throughout the Holy Land, including Jerusalem and beyond. He was a very hard worker, a skilled negotiator, and one of the last of the old-school impresarios—someone who didn’t just value the business side of things, but also truly cared about the music and the artists themselves.”

Julio Iglesias submitted a statement thanking Kaduri for everything and praising his professionalism and kindness.

“You were always a very special businessman to me. First you were my friend, and then my business partner. And when you have a business partner who is a true friend, everything is so much more beautiful, everything is more generous,” Iglesias said. “I adored you, Arie, and I always had such great respect for your professionalism and for the warmth you showed me. We will see each other again. I am certain that we will meet once more.”

Iconic Spanish singer Raphael, who was named the Latin Recording Academy’s Person of the Year in 2025, also wrote words expressing his grief: “I write these lines with deep sadness to bid farewell to Arie Kaduri—a great professional and a friend—with whom I was fortunate enough to share some of the most significant concerts of my career in the United States and Puerto Rico. Together, we experienced unforgettable moments that, decades later, remain vivid in my memory. Arie was a master of his craft, a man of impeccable integrity; working with him was always synonymous with trust, honesty, and success. I will always cherish the moments we shared and the many fond memories. I send my heartfelt condolences to his entire family. Rest in peace, dear Arie.”

Emily Simonitsch, Live Nation’s senior vice president of talent booking on the West Coast, worked with Kaduri on several co-promotions. She said his sharp insight and attention to pricing guided her to learn the business more thoroughly.

“Ari and I co-promoted shows on Juan Gabriel and Luis Miguel in markets like Atlanta; he provided me with talent to promote in Los Angeles,” Simonitsch said. “One year, we traveled to Monterrey, Mexico, for an end-of-tour celebration for Maná. He enjoyed promoting artists in Israel, and many times Ari invited me to join them. An icon in the Latin promoting scene who will be deeply missed. My thoughts are with his family.”

Michel Vega, CEO of Magnus Media, a boutique agency that provides business management for artists, referred to Kaduri as “one of the greats.”

“He cared deeply for his artists and would spare no detail,” Vega said. “I remember he would go to the radio stations early in the morning to bring Cuban coffee to all the receptionists; he knew every assistant’s name. And all the while one of the kindest souls one could encounter. He nurtured so many careers and touched so many people. We have lost an OG.”

Javier Romero, the first Cuban American to be inducted into the Radio Hall of Fame and the former co-host of the popular variety show “Sábado Gigante,” also mourned the promoter: “I remember my ‘jermano’ (hermano), as he used to call me, because he opened the doors for Latin American artists in the United States and served as a teacher or mentor to many who are now working in the industry.”
