# Karol G, Jay Wheeler, Diljit Dosanjh, Bad Gyal & More Join Latin Grammys Performers List

## Summary

The Latin Recording Academy announced additional performers for the 27th Latin Grammy Awards, including Karol G, Jay Wheeler, Diljit Dosanjh, and Bad Gyal, airing Nov. 12 from Las Vegas' MGM Grand Garden Arena. The telecast will include a tribute to the late Willie Colón.

### Key Points
- New performers added: Karol G, Jay Wheeler, Diljit Dosanjh, Bad Gyal, Bizarrap, DJ Premier, and others.
- Tribute to the late salsa pioneer Willie Colón, reuniting his original 13-member band.
- Previously announced performers include Rosalía, Anitta, Feid, and Daddy Yankee (also honored as Latin Recording Academy Person of the Year).

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-1.png)

The Latin Recording Academy announced additional stars gracing this year’s Latin Grammy Awards stage at MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas on Nov. 12. Global megastar Karol G, Jay Wheeler, Diljit Dosanjh, and Bad Gyal are among those added to the ceremony’s stacked lineup.

Other performers announced include Baby Ranks, Bizarrap, Miguel Colón, Divino, DJ Premier, Grupo Niche, Lil Supa, Luis Enrique, Nathy Peluso and Ángel “Cucco” Peña.

The three-hour telecast includes a special tribute to the late Willie Colón, a legendary Nuyorican trombonist, singer, composer, producer and bandleader who pioneered the salsa movement. The Latin Recording Academy honored him in 2004 with the Lifetime Achievement Award, and the artist helped bring the Latin Grammys to New York in 2006. Ennio Gatti, who worked alongside Colón, will gather the original 13-member band to honor Colón’s legacy.

Previously announced performers for the 27 <sup>th</sup> Annual Latin Grammy Awards include Rosalía, Carín León, Anitta, Mon Laferte, Grupo Frontera, The Warning, Xavi, Cristian Castro, Feid, Jorge Drexler, Elena Rose, Niña Pastori, Rawayana, Kany García, Fonseca, Reik, Silvana Estrada, Camila Fernández, Luis Figueroa, Milo J, Sofía Reyes, Vivir Quintana, Yami Safdie and Daddy Yankee, who is also being honored as the Latin Recording Academy’s Person of the Year.

The awards ceremony airs live on Univision, UNIMÁS, Galavisión and streaming on ViX beginning at 8 p.m. ET/PT (7 p.m. CT). A one-hour pre-show will also start at 7 p.m. ET/PT (6 p.m. CT). For more information about the event, visit [LatinGrammy.com](https://www.latingrammy.com/es/) or follow @LatinGrammys on socials.
