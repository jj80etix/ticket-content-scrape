# Marty Diamond To Be Honored As International Music Person Of The Year At Musexpo 2027

## Summary

Musexpo will honor Marty Diamond, EVP & Managing Executive at THE·TEAM, with its "International Music Person of the Year" award at its 27th global edition, March 21–24, 2027, in Burbank, California. The award recognizes Diamond's career as a booking agent representing artists including Coldplay, Ed Sheeran, RAYE, and Tyla.

### Key Points
- Award ceremony: March 23, 2027, VIP Awards Luncheon at Castaway in Burbank.
- Musexpo 2027 runs March 21–24 and includes the Global Sync & Brands Summit and A&R Summit.
- Diamond previously served as Head of Global Music at Paradigm Talent Agency and has won multiple Pollstar "Agent of the Year" and Impact 50 honors.
- Prior award recipients include Kirk M. Sommer (WME), Jacqueline Saturn (Virgin Music Group), and Harvey Goldsmith (Live Aid, Live Earth).

Musexpo announced that Marty Diamond, EVP & Managing Executive at THE·TEAM, will be presented with its prestigious “International Music Person of the Year” award.

Musexpo will celebrate its 27th global edition, March 21–24, 2027, returning to Burbank, California, for a week of industry events, including the Global Sync & Brands Summit, the A&R Summit, and other program points.

The awards will take place on March 23, during a special VIP Awards Luncheon at the iconic Castaway in Burbank, bringing together distinguished executives, artists, managers, agents, promoters, and industry leaders from around the world to celebrate Diamond’s extraordinary contributions to the live global music business.

Official proclamations will also be presented by representatives from the State of California, the County of Los Angeles, and the City of Burbank in recognition of Marty’s exceptional career, enduring influence, and remarkable impact on the worldwide music industry.

Prior to the luncheon, Musexpo will host an exclusive keynote conversation with Marty Diamond, offering attendees an insightful look into his remarkable journey as one of the most respected and influential booking executives in modern music history. The conversation will explore his philosophy on artist development, the evolution of the global touring business, and the leadership principles that have defined his extraordinary career.

Marty Diamond is widely recognized as one of the music industry’s most legendary live entertainment executives and among its most influential booking agents. He has earned an unparalleled reputation for identifying generational talent, building enduring artist careers, and creating the touring strategies that have transformed emerging acts into global superstars.

Prior to joining THE·TEAM, Diamond served as Head of Global Music at Paradigm Talent Agency, where he played an instrumental role in expanding the agency’s worldwide presence and artist roster. His visionary approach to artist representation, combined with an unwavering commitment to long-term career development, has earned him multiple Pollstar “Agent of the Year” honors and widespread recognition as one of the industry’s most accomplished executives.

Diamond also won Pollstar’s annual Impact 50 honors multiple times.

Diamond has played a pivotal role in THE·TEAM’s establishment and accelerated growth while overseeing a roster that includes some of music’s biggest touring artists, such as Coldplay and Ed Sheeran, alongside today’s most exciting global stars, including RAYE, Tyla, and FLO.

His distinguished client roster also features acclaimed artists including Alessia Cara, Bastille, Blur, Damien Rice, David Gray, Liam Gallagher, Lorde, Louis Tomlinson, Sia, Sigur Rós, and Wet Leg, as well as rising talents including Niko Rubio, Cat Burns, Dylan, Elyanna, Lovejoy, Ryan Beatty, and Slayyyter.

Sat Bisla, President & Founder of A&R Worldwide and Musexpo, stated, “Marty Diamond has helped define the modern global touring business through his exceptional vision, integrity, and unwavering commitment to artists. Throughout his remarkable career, he has played an instrumental role in developing many of the world’s most successful live acts while continually championing emerging talent destined for international success. His leadership, innovation, and lasting impact on the worldwide music community make him an exceptionally deserving recipient of MUSEXPO’s “International Music Person of the Year” honor. We are delighted to celebrate his extraordinary achievements and enduring contributions to the global music industry.”

Past recipients of MUSEXPO’s “International Music Person of the Year” honor include some of the music industry’s most influential global executives, including Kirk M. Sommer (WME), Monte and Avery Lipman (Republic Records), Jacqueline Saturn (Virgin Music Group), Mandar Thakur (Times Music Group, India), Daniel Glass (Glassnote Entertainment), Alexandra Patsavas (Netflix), Steve Schnur (Electronic Arts), and Harvey Goldsmith (Live Aid, Live Earth), Lisa Worden (iHeartMedia), amongst others.  
For more details about MUSEXPO 2027, speaker updates, showcase opportunities, and event registration, please visit www.musexpo.net.
