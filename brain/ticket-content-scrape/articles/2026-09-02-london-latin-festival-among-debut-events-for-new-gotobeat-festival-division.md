# London Latin festival among debut events for new Gotobeat festival division

## Summary

London promoter Gotobeat has launched a festival division, led by former Boardmasters/NASS director of music Mike Lightford, debuting with a six-show series at Beckenham Place Park in July 2027 that includes a new Latin American music festival called Unión — coming less than two months after Gotobeat also added an arena touring unit as part of its expansion.

### Key Points
- Gotobeat launches a festival division, headed by Mike Lightford (former Boardmasters/NASS director of music), as its head of festivals.
- Debut event: a six-show series at Beckenham Place Park (southeast London) in July 2027.
- Flagship event within the series: new Latin American music festival "Unión."
- Comes under two months after Gotobeat added an arena touring unit, part of a broader expansion push.
