# Broadway’s ‘Just in Time’ to Welcome Nicholas Christopher

## Summary

Tony nominee Nicholas Christopher will take over the lead role of Bobby Darin in Broadway's "Just in Time" starting November 10 at Circle in the Square Theatre, following Matthew Morrison's three-week return engagement and Jeremy Jordan's final performance on October 18.

### Key Points
- Nicholas Christopher assumes the Bobby Darin role Nov. 10 at Circle in the Square Theatre.
- Jeremy Jordan's final performance is Oct. 18; Matthew Morrison returns Oct. 20–Nov. 8 before Christopher's run begins.
- Jonathan Groff originated the role when the show opened on Broadway in March 2025.
- Standard casting-change announcement with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Just-in-Time-1.webp)

Nicholas Christopher will take over as Bobby Darin in Broadway’s “Just in Time” beginning November 10 at Circle in the Square Theatre.

The Tony Award nominee joins the musical following his performance as Anatoly Sergievsky in the Broadway revival of “Chess.”

Christopher’s arrival follows two previously announced changes in the leading role. Jeremy Jordan will give his final performance on October 18. Matthew Morrison will then return for a three-week engagement running October 20 through November 8, before Christopher begins his run two days later. The production’s official website confirms all three dates.

Jonathan Groff originated the role of Darin in the musical, which began Broadway performances in March 2025. Christopher will join the roster of actors who have led the production as it continues its run at Circle in the Square.

Developed and directed by Alex Timbers, “Just in Time” follows Darin’s rise from a young recording artist to an international entertainer. The production places audiences in a nightclub setting, with a live band accompanying songs drawn from his catalog.

The creative team for “Just in Time” includes book writers Warren Leight and Isaac Oliver, choreographer Shannon Lewis, and music supervisor and arranger Andrew Resnick. Derek McLane designed the scenery, with costumes by Catherine Zuber, lighting by Justin Townsend and sound by Peter Hylenski.

Other cast changes are scheduled around Christopher’s debut. Ariana Madix continues as Connie Francis through November 22. Whitney Leavitt will join the company as Sandra Dee beginning December 1, with performances scheduled through January 10, 2027.
