# Tour news: Megadeth expands farewell outing with EU/UK dates

## Summary

IQ's tour-news roundup covers Megadeth's 24-date 2027 UK/EU farewell arena tour, plus UK arena dates for Thomas Rhett and Tamer Hosny, expanded Daniel Caesar and Natanael Cano runs, a new EsDeeKid tour, Bailey Zimmerman's EU/UK cancellation, North West's tour cancellation, and added Van Morrison UK dates.

### Key Points
- Megadeth's Live Nation-promoted "Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations" runs 24 dates, March-April 2027, across the UK and mainland Europe.
- Thomas Rhett adds three UK arena dates in April 2027; Tamer Hosny plays OVO Arena Wembley Oct. 31, his first UK date in eight years.
- Bailey Zimmerman cancelled his EU/UK theatre leg citing family matters; North West (age 13) cancelled her debut co-headlining club tour with Molly Santana.
- Daniel Caesar and Natanael Cano both extend their outings into Australia/Latin America and Mexican stadiums, respectively.

Thrash metal band **Megadeth** have charted a 24-date UK/EU arena tour for 2027, dubbed *Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations*, as part of their multi-year global farewell tour.

The Live Nation-promoted outing launches on 9 March at Belfast’s O2 Belfast (cap. 11,200), running through stops including London, Nottingham, Manchester, Cardiff, and Glasgow before travelling to mainland Europe. That part picks up at Paris’s Zénith Paris – La Villette (6,800) on 21 March, with the band to make stops across the likes of Amsterdam, Berlin, Kraków, and Madrid, with the leg set to conclude at Lisbon’s Portugal Sagres Campo Pequeno (9,000) on 21 April.

“This isn’t about saying goodbye,” says frontman Dave Mustaine. “It’s about celebrating everything we’ve built together over the last four decades. Every city has its own story, every crowd has given us unforgettable memories, and we want to come back to celebrate that with the fans who made it all possible.”

The band is currently touring in North America, and will continue through Australia & New Zealand before the year is out.

Country star **Thomas Rhett** will also play the UK arena circuit next year, as part of  *The Soundtrack To Life* *Tour* that will take him across North America this year.

He is due to play three shows in the market: Manchester’s AO Arena (22 April), London’s The O2 (23 April), and Glasgow’s OVO Hydro (25 April).

Also coming to a London arena is Arabic music icon **Tamer Hosny**, who will bring his talents to OVO Arena Wembley (12,500) on 31 October.

The Egyptian singer will make his return to the market for the first time in eight years, offering what is set to be one of the biggest Arabic live music events of the year.

> “It’s about celebrating everything we’ve built together”

Elsewhere in the world, R&B singer **Daniel Caesar** has extended his *Son of Spergy Tour* with Australia and Latin American dates, expanding his biggest-ever global outing.

Following North American and EU/UK legs, Caesar will make a three-city run in Australia, hitting Sydney’s Afterpay Arena (5 November), Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena (8 Nov.), and Brisbane Entertainment Centre (12 Nov.), before carrying on to Latin America.

That leg launches at Bogotá’s Vive Claro Music Hall (6,000) on 29 November, and will make stops in São Paulo, Buenos Aires, and Lima before ending at Santiago’s Movistar Arena (15,000) on 10 December.

Mexican idol **Natanael Cano** is set to hit a trio of stadiums in his native country later this year, in promotion of his upcoming album *Natanael Cano Vol. 1*.

The OCESA-backed outing kicks off on 18 September at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros (65,000), also stopping at Guadalajara’s new [Coliseo GNP Seguros](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/ocesa-unveils-coliseo-gnp-seguros-in-guadalajara/) (30,000) on 25 September and Monterrey’s Estadio Walmart Park (22,000) on 2 October.

Additional dates are expected to be announced.

British rapper **EsDeeKid** has laid out a 48-date *Council House Rat Tour* for this year, set to take him across North America, the UK, and Europe.

It kicks off on 12 September at Charlotte’s Fillmore (2,000), running through theatres in cities like Brooklyn, Montreal, Chicago, Los Angeles, Dallas, and Atlanta before venturing to Europe.

The EU dates begin on 2 November at House of Culture (1,500) in Helsinki, with stops lined up across cities like Copenhagen, Berlin, Warsaw, Vienna, Paris, and Amsterdam, before it moves to the UK. That leg opens at London’s OVO Wembley Arena (12,500) on 3 December, with shows in Dublin and Glasgow before a hometown finale at Liverpool’s Blackstone Street Warehouse (10,000) on 10-11 December.

> “This was not an easy decision and it kills me to have to cancel”

On the opposite end of the spectrum, country singer **Bailey Zimmerman** has cancelled the EU/UK leg of his *Different Night Same Rodeo Tour.*

He was due to start the theatre run in Glasgow on 27 August, making stops in Dublin, Belfast, Manchester, London, Amsterdam, Hamburg, Stockholm, and Oslo.

“This was not an easy decision and it kills me to have to cancel because I know how excited everyone is… but right now I need to take this time to focus on important family matters,” he wrote online. “I will still be playing my shows in North America in August, but after that I will be taking some more to focus on me, my family, and new music.”

Kanye West (Ye) and Kim Kardashian’s 13-year-old daughter, **North West**, has cancelled her first co-headlining tour, the 14-date *Kimokawaii Tour* with rapper Molly Santana.

The North American club outing was due to kick off on 5 August in Dallas, Texas, and end in Los Angeles on 27 August, making stops in cities including Atlanta, Philadelphia, New York, Chicago, and San Francisco along the way.

Back in the UK, **Van Morrison** has added to his [British Airways ARC](https://www.iqmagazine.com/?p=466767&preview=true) residency with a handful of new dates.

Following five nights at the London venue (and another at London’s New Wimbledon Theatre in the mix), the crooner will now perform across Liverpool, Swansea, Bristol, Plymouth, Torquay, Brighton, Glasgow, and Edinburgh from 8 October to 3 November.
