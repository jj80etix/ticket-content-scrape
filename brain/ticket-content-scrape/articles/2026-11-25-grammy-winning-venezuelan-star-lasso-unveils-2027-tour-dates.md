# Grammy-Winning Venezuelan Star Lasso Unveils 2027 Tour Dates

## Summary

Grammy-winning Venezuelan artist Lasso has unveiled 2027 tour dates. The announcement covers the new tour's stops.

### Key Points
- Lasso announces 2027 tour dates.
- Routine tour-date announcement.

Venezuelan singer-songwriter Lasso is embarking on a global tour next year, announcing 24 dates across Europe, Latin America and the U.S.

The “Una Más Y Nos Vamos World Tour” kicks off March 2 at Sala Apolo 1 in Barcelona, and the intimate tour includes stops at Berlin’s Bi Nuu, Hard Club in Porto, Arena 1 in Lima, Santiago’s Teatro Teletón, Pacific Electric in Los Angeles, New York’s HK Hall, Midline in Miami and Mexico City’s Pepsi Center before wrapping up June 12 at C3 Stage in Guadalajara.

The general onsale for U.S. shows begins Nov. 25, and it begins Nov. 26 in Peru and Mexico. Tickets to the general public in Colombia go on sale Nov. 25, and the other LATAM markets are already on sale. Visit [lassomusica.com/tour](https://www.lassomusica.com/tour) for more information.

Lasso is hitting the road to promote his latest album, *Objetos Frágiles en Ambientes Hostiles*. He performed at SummerStage in New York City this year as well as at the iconic Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles for “A Concert for Venezuela,” a benefit show that also featured Beck, Carlos Vives, and Natalia Lafourcade on the bill.

The Grammy-winning artist is managed by Walter Kolm Entertainment.
