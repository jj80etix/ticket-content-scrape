# Conference news: Riga Music Week returns to convene growing Baltic industry

## Summary

A short conference preview on Riga Music Week, which returns to convene the Baltic live-music industry, highlighting the region's growing conference and networking scene.

### Key Points
- Riga Music Week returns as a gathering point for the Baltic live-music industry.
- Coverage focuses on conference logistics/programming rather than ticketing, technology, or regulatory topics.

## Transcript

Plus the latest announcements from Trapital Summit, Amsterdam Dance Event, and Hong Kong's Asia Music Event

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/08/Riga-Music-Week-scaled.jpg.webp)

Riga Music Week © Riga Music Week

**Riga Music Week** will return for its second edition from 4–6 November 2026, with organisers confirming the showcase lineup for the Latvian conference.

The event received more than 1,100 applications from artists to play at its showcase, 87% of which organisers said had never played in Latvia before. The most represented genres are indie rock, followed by rock and metal, with the showcases completed with electronic, pop, and jazz artists.

Organisers say they are aiming for the event to be a “meeting point between the fast-developing Baltic music markets and the wider European industry”. Confirmation of the speaker lineup is expected soon, the conference said.

This year’s programme will take over Riga’s Tallinas Street Quarter, with the conference set for three days of live music to accompany “panels, masterclasses, meetings and networking between artists, labels, managers, festivals, promoters, media and other music professionals”.

“In 2026, Riga Music Week will once again combine a showcase festival, international music conference and professional networking programme,” organisers said.

The third annual **Trapital Summit** returns to Los Angeles on September 15, with a newly completed lineup of speakers to be led by Ticketmaster global president Saumil Mehta and The Team’s co-president of Music Kevin Shivers.

With an aim of bringing together leaders across music, media, technology, investing, and the creator economy, the conference is set for the Sunset Room Hollywood in Los Angeles.

> “Riga Music Week creates a meeting point between the fast-developing Baltic music markets and the wider European industry”

“The one-day summit brings together executives, entrepreneurs, investors and creators shaping the future of entertainment, with an intimate, single-stage format designed around in-depth conversations rather than traditional conference panels” organisers said.

Also making appearances are Larry Mestel, founder and CEO, Primary Wave Music, Erin Teague, EVP, product management, Disney, and Tati Cirisano, VP music strategy, MIDiA Research, among others.

“ Unlike traditional industry conferences built around concurrent tracks and large panels, Trapital Summit runs on one stage with no panels, only fireside chats. Every attendee sees the same conversations, with the event intentionally capped to create a more connected room of senior executives, founders, investors, creators and advisors,” organisers added.

Elsewhere, the **Amsterdam Dance Event (ADE)** has announced it will launch the first edition of its Nighttime Culture Index (NCI), a new “global measure of the vitality of nighttime culture”, at its upcoming 30th anniversary event.

The first edition is co-ordinated by bookers and promoters from around the world, including Dutch producer Rocky van Jaaren, Brazilian booker and strategist Monique Dardenne, and Australia’s LJ White, a curator, booker, and producer with over 20 years in Sydney’s dance and music scenes across clubs and festivals.

“ADE has always been a global destination for the electronic music industry, but there is no business without culture. The Nighttime Culture Index puts culture at the centre of how we understand the value of the night,” said ADE managing director Jan-Willem van de Ven.

“As we celebrate 30 years of ADE, it’s a good moment to reflect: ADE gave me a platform to develop as a creative professional, and the Nighttime Culture Index is how I give back, turning local nighttime scenes into a connected, global advocacy network,” added Mirik Milan, co-founder of VibeLab and Amsterdam’s former Night Mayor.

Finally, the **Asia Music Event** has also announced its first edition, set for later this year. Taking place in Hong Kong on November 26–27, organisers describe AME as “Asia’s very own international conference for the electronic music industry”.

“This will be city’s first-ever conference dedicated to the electronic music industry. We are bringing together more than 2,000 attendees, 100+ speakers and industry leaders, and over 100+ companies from over 15 markets to unpack the people, ideas, and partnerships shaping electronic music’s next chapter across Asia,” organisers said.
