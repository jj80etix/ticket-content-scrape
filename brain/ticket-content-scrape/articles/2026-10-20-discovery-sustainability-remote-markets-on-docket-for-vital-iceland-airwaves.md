# Discovery, sustainability & remote markets on docket for ‘vital’ Iceland Airwaves

## Summary

Iceland Airwaves returns to Reykjavík Nov. 5-7 for its 27th year, with headline programming from Shygirl, The Streets, and Daði Freyr & The Iceland Symphony Orchestra at the Harpa opera house, alongside its industry conference focused on discovery, sustainability and remote-market development.

### Key Points
- Iceland Airwaves 27th edition: Nov. 5-7, Reykjavík.
- Headline acts include Shygirl, The Streets, and Daði Freyr & The Iceland Symphony Orchestra at Harpa.
- Industry-conference themes this year: cultural discovery, sustainability, and reaching remote markets.
