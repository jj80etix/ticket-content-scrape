# United Heritage Hall Unveils Inaugural Lineup Including Chaka Khan, ZZ Top & More

## Summary

AEG Presents revealed the inaugural 2027 lineup for United Heritage Hall, its new 4,000-capacity Austin venue, featuring Chaka Khan, ZZ Top, deadmau5 and others across genres. The 65,000-square-foot venue, developed with Westfield, Partners Group and Presidium, emphasizes premium hospitality, luxury suites and VIP seating.

### Key Points
- Inaugural lineup spans March-April 2027: Hum, Taylor Tomlinson, deadmau5, ZZ Top, La Mafia, Death Cab for Cutie, The Plot in You, Fonseca, Chaka Khan, Sullivan King.
- 4,000-capacity venue at Austin's Riverside neighborhood 109-acre River Park development.
- Features premium hospitality, luxury suites and VIP seating.
- Fans can sign up at UnitedHeritageHall.com for updates.

## Transcript

![United Heritage View 1 2](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/United-Heritage-View-1-2-scaled.png)

Courtesy of AEG Presents

United Heritage Hall, AEG Presents’ new state-of-the-art 4,000-capacity venue in Austin, Texas, has unveiled its inaugural 2027 performances. The venue’s opening-year lineup includes Hum, comedian Taylor Tomlinson, deadmau5, ZZ Top, La Mafia, Death Cab for Cutie, The Plot in You, Fonseca, Chaka Khan, DJ Sullivan King, Roger Waters Presents LEGACY and Jimmy Carr.

The 65,000-square-foot venue brings together influences across generations in a way that feels fresh, distinctive, and unmistakably Austin. The venue will feature state-of-the-art sound and lighting, exceptional sightlines, premium hospitality offerings, luxury suites, VIP seating and thoughtfully designed artist spaces, delivering an intimate, fan-first environment curated to immerse audiences from the moment they arrive.

United Heritage Hall was named through a partnership between United Heritage Credit Union and AEG Presents and developed by AEG Presents in collaboration with Westfield, Partners Group and Presidium. The 109-acre mixed-use development is in Austin’s rapidly growing Riverside neighborhood.
