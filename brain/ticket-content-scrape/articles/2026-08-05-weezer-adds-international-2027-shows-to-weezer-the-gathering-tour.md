# Weezer Adds International 2027 Shows to ‘Weezer: The Gathering’ Tour

## Summary

Weezer expanded its "Weezer: The Gathering" world tour with new international dates across Japan, the UK, and Europe in early 2027, following a previously announced 32-date North American run (Sept. 8-Oct. 24) with The Shins and Silversun Pickups. The international leg, supported by Taking Back Sunday, kicks off February 12 in Tokyo and wraps June 1 in Helsinki, arriving alongside Weezer's next studio album, *Weezer*, out August 21.

### Key Points
- North American leg (32 dates, Sept. 8-Oct. 24) features The Shins and Silversun Pickups; international leg (Feb. 12-June 1) features Taking Back Sunday.
- International stops span Tokyo, Kyoto, Osaka, Nagoya, Dublin, Newcastle, Manchester, Leeds, Glasgow, Birmingham, London, Cardiff, Paris, Amsterdam, Oslo, Stockholm, and Helsinki.
- Tour announcement coincides with Weezer's next self-titled studio album release on August 21.
- North American tour opens Sept. 8 at Sacramento's Golden 1 Center; international tour opens Feb. 12 at Tokyo's Garden Theater.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Weezer.webp)

Weezer has announced an expanded itinerary for its upcoming “Weezer: The Gathering” world tour, adding a new series of performances across Japan, Europe, and the United Kingdom in early 2027.

The run is slated to kick off on February 12 and 13 in Tokyo at Garden Theater. From there, Weezer is scheduled to make stops in cities such as Dublin, Newcastle, Glasgow, London, Paris, Amsterdam and Oslo before their final performance on June 1 in Helsinki at Veikkaus.

The newly announced international leg will feature support from Taking Back Sunday. The expansion follows the group’s previously announced North American run, which is scheduled to take place this fall.

**| RELATED:** [**Weezer Plot 32-City ‘The Gathering’ Tour Across North America**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/weezer-plot-32-date-the-gathering-tour-north-america/) **|**

The North American portion of the “Weezer: The Gathering” tour includes 32 dates across the United States and Canada and is set to begin on September 8 in Sacramento, CA, at Golden 1 Center. Other stops on the run include cities such as San Francisco, Vancouver, Denver, Chicago, Boston, San Diego and Las Vegas. The North American leg of the tour is slated to wrap up on October 24 in Los Angeles, CA at Crypto.com Arena.

Joining Weezer on those concerts will be special guests The Shins and Silversun Pickups, with the lineup differing from the newly announced overseas performances.

Additionally, the tour news arrives ahead of the release of Weezer’s next studio album, *Weezer*, which is scheduled to arrive on August 21. The record marks the band’s latest full-length release and will coincide with the extensive touring schedule planned through 2026 and into 2027.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Weezer’s official website](https://weezer.com/).
