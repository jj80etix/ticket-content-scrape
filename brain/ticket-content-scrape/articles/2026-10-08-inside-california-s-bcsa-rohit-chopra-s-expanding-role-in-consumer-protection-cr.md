# Inside California’s BCSA: Rohit Chopra’s Expanding Role in Consumer Protection — Crossover Episode With Regulatory Oversight Podcast

## Summary

A Troutman Pepper "Regulatory Oversight"/"Consumer Finance Podcast" crossover episode discusses California Gov. Gavin Newsom's appointment of former CFPB Director Rohit Chopra to lead the state's newly created Business and Consumer Services Agency (BCSA), overseeing the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI). The hosts cover Chopra's enforcement track record, DFPI's supervisory powers, and speculation about his political trajectory, with no ticketing-specific content.

### Key Points
- Newsom appointed former CFPB Director Rohit Chopra to lead California's new Business and Consumer Services Agency (BCSA).
- Chopra will also oversee the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI), which has broad supervisory/enforcement authority alongside the state AG.
- Hosts discuss Chopra's history of aggressive, systemic enforcement at the CFPB and FTC.
- Episode speculates whether the role positions Chopra for a future federal Cabinet post under a Democratic administration.
- Discussion is general consumer-finance regulation; no ticketing, resale, or junk-fee specifics are addressed.

In this special crossover episode of [*The Consumer Finance Podcast*](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/the-consumer-finance-podcast/) and [*Regulatory Oversight*](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/regulatory-oversight/), [Chris Willis](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/chris-j-willis/), [Stefanie Jackman](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/stefanie-jackman/), and [Mike Yaghi](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/michael-yaghi/) discuss California Governor Gavin Newsom’s appointment of former CFPB Director Rohit Chopra to lead the state’s newly created Business and Consumer Services Agency (BCSA).

The group discusses Chopra’s oversight of the Department of Financial Protection and Innovation (DFPI), his broad authority alongside the California attorney general, and what his track record of aggressive, systemic enforcement at the CFPB and FTC might mean for California’s consumer financial services industry going forward. They also get into DFPI’s wide-reaching supervisory and enforcement powers, what companies should be doing to get ready, and whether this move sets Chopra up for a bigger Cabinet-level role down the road in a future Democratic administration.

The episode wraps up with a bigger-picture look at whether federal regulators could swing hard back toward consumer protection enforcement under a future Democratic president.
