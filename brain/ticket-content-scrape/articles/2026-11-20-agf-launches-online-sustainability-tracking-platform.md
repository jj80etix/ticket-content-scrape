# AGF launches online sustainability tracking platform

## Summary

The AGF sustainability framework has launched an online platform to manage assessments, certification, and CO2 analysis for events, which organisers describe as a "new phase" of the framework. We Love Green is cited as a festival already holding AGF certification.

### Key Points
- AGF Framework's new online tool handles sustainability assessments, certification, and CO2 analysis for events.
- Organisers describe the launch as marking a "new phase" for the framework.
- We Love Green festival is referenced as an existing AGF-certified event.
- Full detail on the platform's features is behind IQ's subscriber paywall.
