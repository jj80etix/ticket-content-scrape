# AEG Presents Acquires Auguri Productions In France

## Summary

AEG Presents has acquired Auguri Productions, a leading French artist-development and production company founded in 1997 by Charles Bensmaine. The deal extends AEG Presents' European growth strategy, following a recent Belgian promoter partnership and a new German office, with Auguri's existing artist roster (including Stromae and Angèle alumni-era credits) gaining access to AEG's international network.

### Key Points
- AEG Presents acquires Auguri Productions, a French artist-development/production company founded in 1997.
- Auguri's past work includes developing Stromae, Angèle, Louane, Julien Doré, and David Guetta, among others.
- Part of AEG Presents' broader European expansion: a Belgian promoter partnership and new German office both announced within the past month.

## Transcript

![AEG Presents Auguri](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/AEG-Presents-_Auguri.png)

Arnaud Meersseman, MD of AEG Presents (left), and Charles Bensmaine, president and founder of Auguri Productions, one of France’s leading artist development companies.

AEG Presents has announced the acquisition of Auguri Productions, a leading company in artist production and development in France. Founded in 1997 by president and founder Charles Bensmaine, Auguri Productions has established itself as one of France’s leading companies in artist development over the years.

Bensmaine and his team have played a key role in launching and developing the careers of numerous major artists from the French and French-speaking music scene, including Stromae, Angèle, Louane, Julien Doré, David Guetta, Dominique A, Philippe Katerine, -M-, Gazo, Sofiane Pamart, Tiakola, Gaël Faye, Vanessa Paradis, Thomas Dutronc, Yodelice and Jain.

Following the acquisition, Auguri Productions will leverage the scale, expertise and international network of AEG Presents, unlocking new development opportunities for its artists and helping connect local talent with global audiences.

As AEG Presents France MD Arnaud Meersseman told Pollstar recently, “developing local artists is a strategic priority for AEG Presents France. We are committed to identifying talent early, supporting artists as they grow and helping them build sustainable careers over the long term. That means investing not only in established names but also in the next generation of French artists who have the potential to reach larger audiences both in France and internationally.”

The acquisition of Auguri Productions supports this mission in a market, that has an incredibly strong local scene driven by domestic talent. Plus, as Meersseman told Pollstar, “French audiences have a genuine appetite for homegrown artists.”

The acquisition of Auguri Productions marks the next step in AEG Presents’ growth strategy within the French market, “further strengthening its expertise in identifying, developing and supporting local talent,” according to today’s announcement.

Beyond France, AEG Presents continues to expand its European footprint. Just last month, the company [solidified its relationship with leading Belgian promoter Greenhouse Talent](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/14/aeg-presents-greenhouse-talent-announce-partnership-in-europe/), a long-standing local partner in Benelux; and [opened its first German office](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/04/aeg-presents-opens-german-office/), led by [2026 Pollstar Women of Live Honoree Sina Hall](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/16/2026-women-of-live-sina-hall/).

“Through its network of strategic partnerships, AEG Presents leverages local expertise, industry relationships and global reach to deliver greater opportunities for artists and audiences,” today’s announcement concludes.

Comments:

Adam Wilkes, President and CEO of AEG Presents Europe, “France has an extraordinary depth of artistry, a vibrant live music culture and a generation of artists with real international potential. Charles and the Auguri team have earned their reputation by recognising those artists early on and helping them build careers with lasting relevance. Bringing Auguri into AEG Presents deepens our roots in France and creates a meaningful bridge between that local expertise and our global platform. I’m thrilled to welcome Charles and the team as we build this next chapter together.”

Charles Bensmaine, President and Founder of Auguri Productions, “I am very pleased to be joining AEG Presents with my team and to open this new chapter in Auguri’s history. This partnership represents a tremendous opportunity to accelerate our development in the French market while giving the artists we work with even greater access to AEG Presents’ international network and opportunities. We share the same ambition: to identify, develop and support artists over the long term.”

Arnaud Meersseman, President of AEG Presents France, “The addition of Auguri Productions to AEG Presents represents a major opportunity to strengthen our growth in the French market. Charles Bensmaine and his team bring unique expertise in artist development and career building. Their know-how is a perfect complement to our existing activities and will enable us to further strengthen our ability to identify and develop local talent, while offering artists new opportunities both in France and internationally.”
