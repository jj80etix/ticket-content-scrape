# Remi Wolf Releases New Album, Announces North American Headline Tour

## Summary

Remi Wolf released her new album *Mud* and announced a North American headline tour, "The Mud Tour," kicking off Feb. 23, 2027 in Washington, D.C., alongside previously announced Australian dates in December.

### Key Points
- *Mud* (11 tracks, Island Records) follows singles "Twiggy," "Bottle" and "I Won't Cry"; Wolf performed on Jimmy Kimmel Live! the night before release.
- Two intimate "underplay" shows precede the tour: LA's Fonda Theatre (Oct. 10) and NYC's Webster Hall (Oct. 13).
- North American leg runs through major markets (Chicago, Nashville, Atlanta, Austin, Denver, Seattle) into May 2027; Emma Ogier and Annabelle Dinda support on select dates.
- Artist presale starts Oct. 13; Verizon Shine members get an exclusive U.S. presale Oct. 12; general onsale begins Oct. 16.

## Transcript

![Remi Wolf press photo](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Remi-Wolf-press-photo.png)

Remi Wolf press photo

Remi Wolf has announced dates for a North American headline tour to go along with her new album, *Mud*, out today via Island Records.

![MUD cover (1)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MUD-cover-1-scaled.jpg)

cover of Remi Wolf’s Mud album

*Mud* includes the previously released singles “Twiggy,” “Bottle,” and “I Won’t Cry,” which Remi performed last night on Jimmy Kimmel Live! The 11-song collection is described as refining “Wolf’s boundless musical imagination into a collection of tracks that are warmer, more spacious, and deeply human without sacrificing the adventurous spirit that has always defined her work.”

Wolf is celebrating the album release with two intimate underplay gigs booked Saturday at Los Angeles’s The Fonda Theatre and next Tuesday at New York City’s Webster Hall.  
  
Her tour schedule includes a run of dates in Australia in December including all stops of the Spilt Milk Festival.

The newly confirmed dates for “The Mud Tour” kick off Feb. 23 at Washington, D.C., The Anthem. Stops on the tour include Toronto, Chicago, Nashville, Atlanta, Austin, Denver, Seattle and more. Wolf will be joined by Emma Ogier and Annabelle Dinda as support on select dates.

It’s been a big year for Wolf, including making her on-screen debut in Amazon Prime Video’s hit series “Off Campus.” Along with a cameo appearance by the pop star in the premiere episode, the show features multiple songs from her catalog.

An artist presale for the new dates starts Tuesday, Oct. 13th at 10 a.m.local time and ends Thursday, Oct. 15th at 10 p.m. local time. Verizon is hosting an exclusive presale for The Mud Tour in the U.S. as part of its loyalty program, Verizon Shine. That presale starts Monday, Oct. 12th at 10 a.m. local time. The general public onsale begins Friday, Oct. 16 at 10 a.m. local.

Wolf is represented by TBA Agency’s Avery McTaggart and Josh Mulder.
