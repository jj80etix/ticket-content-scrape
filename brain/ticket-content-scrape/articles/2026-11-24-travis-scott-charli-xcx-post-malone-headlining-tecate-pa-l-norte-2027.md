# Travis Scott, Charli xcx, Post Malone Headlining Tecate Pa’l Norte 2027

## Summary

Tecate Pa'l Norte 2027, the Monterrey, Mexico festival, has announced Travis Scott, Charli XCX and Post Malone as headliners. The lineup announcement covers dates and other supporting acts for the 2027 edition.

### Key Points
- Travis Scott, Charli XCX and Post Malone to headline Tecate Pa'l Norte 2027.
- Festival is held in Monterrey, Mexico.
- Routine lineup announcement; no ticketing-industry angle.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-1-scaled.jpg)

Tecate Pa’l Norte, one of Mexico’s premier music festivals, is going all out for its 15 <sup>th</sup> anniversary with a stacked, diverse three-day lineup headlined by Travis Scott, Charli xcx, Post Malone, Empire of the Sun and Leon Bridges.

The highly anticipated festival takes place March 26-28 at Parque Fundidora in Monterrey, Mexico,and features more than 150 artists across eight stages. Other acts on the bill include Avenged Sevenfold, Julión Álvarez, The Neighbourhood, Kasabian, Fontaines D.C., Martin Garrix, Caifanes, Joji, Milo J, Young Miko, Latin Mafia, Kenia Os and many more.

Travis Scott will be making his Pa’l Norte debut along with regional Mexican icon Julión Álvarez, Post Malone and Avenged Sevenfold.

The lineup includes some of awards season’s favorites, including Latin Grammy winner Milo J and British singer-songwriter Raye, who was recently nominated for 10 Grammy Awards.

Early bird passes sold out the same day they went on sale on Nov. 14, and the general onsale begins Dec. 1 at 2 p.m. Mexico Time at Ticketmaster and the Auditoria Banamex box office, where sales do not include a fee. Passes will be sold in phases, and prices will increase as tickets in each phase sell out.

Visit [tecatepalnorte.com](https://www.tecatepalnorte.com/en/) for more information.
