# Kennedy Center votes for closure after latest block on Trump renaming plan

## Summary

President Trump reiterated that the Kennedy Center will close after a federal judge blocked his latest attempt to rename the venue, suggesting planned renovations won't proceed unless the renaming is adopted. The Center's board has again voted to move toward closure.

### Key Points
- A U.S. judge blocked Trump's latest renaming attempt on Sept. 15, 2026
- Trump says renovations are contingent on the renaming plan being adopted
- The Kennedy Center board has voted "almost unanimously" to close the building
