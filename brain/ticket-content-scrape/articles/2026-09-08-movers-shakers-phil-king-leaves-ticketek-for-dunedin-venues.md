# Movers & Shakers: Phil King leaves Ticketek for Dunedin Venues

## Summary

IQ's Movers & Shakers roundup reports Phil King departing as Ticketek Australia's managing director to become CEO of New Zealand's Dunedin Venues Management Limited, plus other industry appointments (source article largely paywalled beyond the lead item).

### Key Points

- King will lead DVML, which operates Forsyth Barr Stadium, the Dunedin Centre, and Dunedin Town Hall.
- Additional appointments mentioned at DHP Family, Merkur Spiel-Arena, Paladin Artists, WME, and Union Chapel were not accessible due to the paywall.
