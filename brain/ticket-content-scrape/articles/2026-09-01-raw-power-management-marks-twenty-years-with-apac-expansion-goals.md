# Raw Power Management marks twenty years with APAC expansion goals

## Summary

Hard rock/alternative artist management company Raw Power Management is marking its 20th anniversary with plans for further APAC expansion, following the May appointment of Don Jenkins and Matt Ash as co-CEOs and founder Craig Jennings's move to executive chairman. The company is also being recognized at the 2026 Artist and Manager Awards.

### Key Points
- Leadership transition: Jenkins and Ash became co-CEOs in May 2026; founder Craig Jennings moved to executive chairman.
- Raw Power previously partnered with agency ATC in 2024.
