# Olivia Rodrigo’s Daisy Chain Fields Festival Raises $20 Million

## Summary

Olivia Rodrigo's debut Daisy Chain Fields festival raised $20 million for charity in its first outing, with organizers confirming the event will continue in future years.

### Key Points
- Inaugural edition raised $20 million, split among charities supporting women and girls.
- Festival confirmed to continue in subsequent years.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Olivia-Rodrigo-Daisy-Chain-Fields.webp)

Olivia Rodrigo’s inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival has generated $20 million for organizations supporting women and girls after Melinda French Gates pledged to match the event’s initial fundraising total.

Festival organizers announced that Daisy Chain Fields had raised $10 million for its nonprofit beneficiaries. French Gates subsequently committed another $10 million through her company, Pivotal, doubling the festival’s charitable contribution.

French Gates praised Rodrigo and the festival in an Instagram post, sharing, “What @daisychainfields set in motion will last far beyond a single night,” French Gates wrote. “I was so inspired by @oliviarodrigo’s vision, which is why I couldn’t be prouder to contribute $10 million through @pivotaldotcom, doubling the amount this extraordinary community already raised for organizations supporting women & girls.”

The 23-year-old singer brought French Gates onstage and credited the philanthropist for her longtime work supporting women and girls.

“Melinda is one of the most inspiring women I’ve ever met. She’s devoted her life to empowering women and girls that might have otherwise been forgotten,” Rodrigo told the audience.

Rodrigo then announced that French Gates would match the money already generated by Daisy Chain Fields.

“She’s generous and brave and has done so much good in this world, and I’m totally floored because as well as being in attendance here tonight, she’s pledged to double every dollar that we made here today to a grand total of funds being donated to $20 million,” Rodrigo said. “I’m so grateful!”
