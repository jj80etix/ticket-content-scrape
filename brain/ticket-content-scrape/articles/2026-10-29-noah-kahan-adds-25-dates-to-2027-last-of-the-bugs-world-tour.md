# Noah Kahan Adds 25 Dates to 2027 Last of the Bugs World Tour

## Summary

Noah Kahan added 25 dates to his 2027 "The Last of the Bugs World Tour," expanding it to 31 shows across North America and Europe including stops at major NFL/college football stadiums. The tour runs April 24 through a three-night Wembley Stadium close in London.

### Key Points
- 20 new North American dates plus 5 in continental Europe, joining 6 previously announced UK/Ireland shows
- Tour opens April 24 at Sanford Stadium (Athens, GA), runs through June 18 at MetLife Stadium
- Major stadium stops include Nissan Stadium, Bank of America Stadium, Allegiant Stadium, Ford Field
- Concludes with three shows at London's Wembley Stadium in July

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/kahan-tour-2027-1200.webp)

Noah Kahan has revealed 25 additional dates for his 2027 *The Last of the Bugs World Tour*, expanding the run with 20 North American performances and five concerts in continental Europe. The newly announced shows join six previously scheduled performances in the United Kingdom and Ireland, bringing the tour to 31 dates.

The expanded itinerary, shared Thursday, October 29, includes performances at major NFL and college football stadiums across the United States, along with stops in Canada, the Netherlands, Germany, Italy, Spain and France.

The tour will begin April 24 at Sanford Stadium in Athens, Georgia, before continuing through North America until June 18 at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey. Kahan will then head to Europe in July, concluding the currently announced schedule with three performances at London’s Wembley Stadium.

The North American routing features several of the country’s largest sports venues, including Nashville’s Nissan Stadium, Charlotte’s Bank of America Stadium, Houston’s Reliant Stadium, Las Vegas’ Allegiant Stadium, Detroit’s Ford Field and Buffalo’s Highmark Stadium.

Kahan will also perform at college football venues, including the University of Georgia’s Sanford Stadium, the University of Oklahoma’s Gaylord Family-Oklahoma Memorial Stadium, the University of Texas’ Darrell K Royal-Texas Memorial Stadium, Wisconsin’s Camp Randall Stadium and Ohio State’s Ohio Stadium.

One of the most notable stops is Boston, where Kahan has scheduled four shows at Fenway Park on May 29-30 and June 1-2. The singer previously made history with four sold-out performances at the ballpark during his 2026 *The Great Divide World Tour*, becoming the first artist to headline four sold-out concerts at the venue during a single engagement.

The new schedule follows a series of coordinated social media teasers from major stadiums on Wednesday, October 28. Venues including Ford Field, MetLife Stadium, Levi’s Stadium and Camp Randall Stadium posted imagery featuring insects and other visual references associated with Kahan’s latest album.

Kahan’s 2026 touring schedule marked a significant expansion of his live performances, including major baseball stadiums and a headline appearance at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California, attended by more than 56,000 fans.

The 2027 tour takes its name from *The Great Divide: The Last of the Bugs*, the expanded edition of Kahan’s fourth studio album, *The Great Divide*, released earlier this year.

Following the North American leg, Kahan will perform at Stadspark in Groningen, the Netherlands, on July 1, followed by Berlin’s Waldbühne on July 2, Milan’s I-Days on July 4, Seville’s Plaza de España on July 7 and Paris’ Hippodrome ParisLongchamp on July 10.

Those dates lead into the previously announced stadium shows in Glasgow, Dublin and London.

As [TicketNews reported in August](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/noah-kahan-sets-u-k-ireland-dates-for-2027-the-last-of-the-bugs-world-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Kahan initially unveiled the U.K. and Ireland portion of the tour with performances at Hampden Park, Aviva Stadium and Wembley Stadium. Additional shows were subsequently added in Dublin and London.

## Noah Kahan 2027 Tour Tickets and Registration

The newly announced North American and continental European dates are listed on [Kahan’s official tour website](https://noahkahan.com/pages/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), with registration links and additional information available for each performance.

The listings also advertise VIP and travel packages for the North American dates, with travel packages available for the continental European stops.

Exact presale and general onsale times for the newly announced dates have not yet been independently confirmed.

Mastercard is also offering preferred-ticket access for eligible World Elite and World Legend cardholders during the general onsale. The reserved inventory will be available through Ticketmaster, subject to availability, with eligible cardholders entering the same purchasing queue as other buyers.

Tickets for the previously announced U.K. and Ireland dates have already gone on sale, with several performances listed as sold out.
