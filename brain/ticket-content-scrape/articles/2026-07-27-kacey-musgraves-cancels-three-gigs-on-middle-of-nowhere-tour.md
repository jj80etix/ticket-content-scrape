# Kacey Musgraves Cancels Three Gigs on ‘Middle of Nowhere’ Tour

## Summary

TicketNews reports the same Kacey Musgraves cancellations covered by Pollstar: three dates (Chicago, Boston, Brooklyn) called off with automatic refunds processed to the original payment method within 7-10 business days.

### Key Points
- Confirms cancellation of Aug. 20 Chicago, Aug. 29 Boston, and Sept. 2 Brooklyn dates.
- Refunds automatic to original point of purchase, 7-10 business day processing window.
- No stated reason from Musgraves or her team at time of reporting.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Kacey-Musgraves.webp)

Kacey Musgraves has called-off three upcoming shows on her “ [Middle of Nowhere](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/kacey-musgraves-adds-five-dates-to-2026-middle-of-nowhere-tour/) ” tour.

According to reports on social media, fans were notified of the cancellation via emails from Ticketmaster.

“Please be advised that the Kacey Musgraves concert scheduled for Saturday, August 29, 2026 at TD Garden has been canceled,” a notice from a fan read. “All tickets will be automatically refunded to fans at their original point of purchase. Please allow 7-10 business days for refunds to process.”

Alongside the August 29 show, Musgraves’ tour stops at Chicago’s United Center on August 20 and Brooklyn’s Barclays Center on September 2 were also called-off.

Musgraves has not commented on the cancelled gigs at this time, nor was there an explanation for the cancellations.

The “Middle of Nowhere” tour is now scheduled to kick-off at the United Center in Chicago on August 21, followed by shows in New York, Baltimore, Kansas City, Nashville, Dallas, Los Angeles, and Oakland before wrapping-up with two nights in Seattle on October 26 and 27. The tour supports Musgraves’ latest album, *Middle Of Nowhere*, which was released May 1.
