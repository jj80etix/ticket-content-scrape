# Gov’t funding awarded for AI booking and promotion tool trial in UK venues

## Summary

UK government funding has been awarded to trial Gig Lab, an AI-driven booking and promotion tool for venues, aimed at reducing guesswork in booking shows, making more shows commercially viable, and widening the UK's touring map beyond established hubs. Detail on the funding amount, the venues involved, and how the tool works is behind IQ's paywall.

### Key Points
- Gig Lab is an AI tool targeting show-booking and promotion decisions for UK venues.
- Stated goals: cut booking guesswork, make more shows viable, expand the UK touring map to more places.
- Funded via UK government grant; specifics (amount, pilot venues, timeline) not available in the accessible teaser text.

### Highlights
- A government-funded AI pilot is targeting booking/promotion decision-making for live venues — an adjacent-industry AI use case, not consumer-facing ticketing AI.
- No named ticketing platform involvement in the available text; this is venue/promoter-side tooling.

### Industry Problem
Promoters and venues struggle to judge which shows will sell and where touring gaps exist, leading to under-booked rooms and smaller markets being skipped by touring acts — a demand-forecasting problem adjacent to the pricing/personalization AI use cases ticketing platforms are also exploring.

### Proposed Solution
Gig Lab proposes AI-assisted booking/promotion decisioning to de-risk show bookings for venues. For Etix, this is a watch item for AI-driven demand forecasting and dynamic-pricing signals that could inform or benchmark Etix Assistant's forecasting features — worth monitoring Gig Lab's pilot results before any build decision.
