# ‘Dolly’ Musical Announces Broadway Leads Ahead of December Debut

## Summary

"Dolly: A True Original Musical" announced its Broadway cast ahead of a Dec. 7 preview start and a Jan. 19, 2027 opening (Parton's 81st birthday), with three actresses portraying Parton at different life stages.

### Key Points
- Previews begin Dec. 7 at the St. James Theatre; opens Jan. 19, 2027.
- Katie Rose Clarke, Carrie St. Louis and Norah Nunes play Dolly at different ages.
- Follows a 2025 Nashville world premiere at the Fisher Center.
- Directed by Tony winner Bartlett Sher.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Hello-Im-Dolly.webp)

Katie Rose Clarke, Carrie St. Louis and Norah Nunes will portray Dolly Parton at different stages of her life in the upcoming Broadway production of “Dolly: A True Original Musical.”

The musical will begin previews December 7 at the St. James Theatre, with an opening night scheduled for January 19, 2027, on what would have been Parton’s 81st birthday. Producers said the late singer personally selected the five principal actors announced for the production.

Clarke will reprise her Nashville performance as “Dolly Parton,” while St. Louis will play “Dolly” during the singer’s early pursuit of a career in Nashville. Nunes, an 11-year-old finalist from the production’s online “Search for Dolly” casting call, will portray “Little Dolly.”

John Behlmann will return as Carl Dean, Parton’s husband of nearly 60 years. Tony Award winner Gabriel Ebert will play Porter Wagoner, the country star whose television program helped introduce Parton to a national audience.

Directed by Tony Award winner Bartlett Sher, “Dolly: A True Original Musical” follows Parton’s journey from her childhood in the Smoky Mountains to international stardom. Parton wrote the music and co-wrote the book with Maria S. Schlatter.

The score combines songs written specifically for the production with familiar hits, including “Jolene,” “I Will Always Love You,” “Coat of Many Colors” and “9 to 5.”

Alongside the casting announcement, producers unveiled a partnership with the New York City Department of Education to provide 30,000 free tickets to public school middle and high school students during the musical’s first Broadway year.

Schools will be able to apply for student visits, while individual students can request two tickets to attend with a parent or guardian. Organizers said further details about the initiative will be released in the coming weeks.

Tickets for the Broadway production are currently on sale, with performances initially available through November 21, 2027.

For more information and to stay up-to-date on the latest details theatergoers can visit [dollymusical.com](http://dollymusical.com/).
