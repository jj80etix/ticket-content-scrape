# Oak View Group Names Female Executives At Rupp Arena In Lexington And Texas Convention Center

## Summary

Oak View Group has named Laura Alexander as the first woman to serve as top executive over Rupp Arena, Central Bank Center, Lexington Opera House, and the OVG Concert Series at Gatton Park, and Melanie Granado as the first general manager (and first woman) of the newly named Caney Pines Convention Center in Texas, opening in December.

### Key Points
- Laura Alexander promoted to VP overseeing four Lexington, KY venues — first woman in the role.
- Melanie Granado named GM of Caney Pines Convention Center (New Caney, TX), opening December 2026.
- Caney Pines will be the third-largest convention center in the Houston area and seventh-largest in Texas.
- Both appointments are framed by OVG as milestones amid its "growing venue management portfolio."​

Oak View Group (OVG) announced two key leadership appointments across its growing venue management portfolio. Laura Alexander has been promoted to Vice President of Rupp Arena, Central Bank Center, Lexington Opera House and the OVG Concert Series at Gatton Park in Lexington, Kentucky; and Melanie Granado has been appointed General Manager of the newly named Caney Pines Convention Center in New Caney, Texas.

Both appointments represent historic milestones for their respective venues.

Alexander’s promotion makes her the first woman to serve as the top executive overseeing Rupp Arena and Central Bank Center, following Tom Minton, William Owens and Brian Sipe.

Granado is the first general manager, and first woman, to lead the Caney Pines Convention Center, the 210,070-square-foot facility scheduled to open in December. The convention center will be the third largest in the Houston area and the seventh largest in Texas, which is experiencing a boom in convention center development and major renovations.

“Laura and Melanie are both accomplished leaders who have demonstrated an exceptional ability to build strong teams, drive operational excellence and create meaningful opportunities for the communities they serve,” stated Greg O-Dell, President of Venue Management OVG, *Pollstar* ’s parent company. “As our portfolio continues to grow, investing in experienced, collaborative leadership is essential to the success of our venues and our partners. It’s exciting to see their hard work and dedication recognized through these important opportunities, and we look forward to the impact their leadership will have on their respective venues.”

Alexander, who is currently serving as Assistant General Manager, will oversee all venue operations across the Lexington venue portfolio, including event management, sales and marketing, facility operations, guest experience, financial performance and booking activity, as well as national touring acts at Gatton Park on the Town Branch. Her appointment is effective on Oct. 3.

Since arriving in Lexington in 2022, Alexander has played a significant role in establishing the operational framework for the transformed convention center following a $300 million renovation and helping position the venue campus for a growing calendar of conventions, entertainment, sporting events and community gatherings. Working closely with clients, community stakeholders, tourism partners and the Lexington Center Corporation Board, the Kentucky native is uniquely positioned to steward that continued success.

Under her leadership, the venue campus has hosted hundreds of events, earned industry recognition and further strengthened Lexington’s reputation as a destination for meetings, conventions and live entertainment.

Prior to joining Oak View Group in Lexington, Alexander built an accomplished career in venue management in Owensboro, Kentucky, where she developed extensive experience in convention center operations, event management and community engagement. Her proven track record of leadership and operational excellence led to her recruitment to Lexington.

In Texas, Granado will oversee day-to-day operations at the Caney Pines Convention Center, including scheduling, operations, finance, advertising, sponsorship and hospitality. An experienced convention center executive, Granado is relocating to New Caney from the OVG-managed Hilliard Center in Corpus Christi, where she most recently served as Assistant General Manager. Granado previously held leadership roles as OVG Hospitality General Manager and Vice President of Convention Center Sales & Operations. Her appointment is effective immediately.

During her time at Hilliard Center, Granado led the hospitality team in creating the Community Incubator Program, an initiative designed to create opportunities and support for local businesses and entrepreneurs. Prior to joining Oak View Group, Granado served as Sales Manager for Visit Corpus Christi and Director of Sales at Ortiz Center.
