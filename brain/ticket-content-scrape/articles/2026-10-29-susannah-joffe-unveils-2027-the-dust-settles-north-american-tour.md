# Susannah Joffe Unveils 2027 ‘The Dust Settles’ North American Tour

## Summary

Singer-songwriter Susannah Joffe announced her debut headline run, "The Dust Settles Tour," a North American club tour in March-April 2027 supporting her album *Dust Settles And So Will I*. The tour caps a year in which she built a following as a support act.

### Key Points
- Debut headline tour: March 23 (Phoenix) through April 16 (Washington, D.C.)
- Stops include Los Angeles, Chicago and New York
- Adds a April 18 High Water Festival (Charleston, SC) date after the headline run
- Prior touring credits as support for King Princess, spill tab, Alix Page, Indigo De Souza and NIKI

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Susannah-Joffe-.webp)

Susannah Joffe has announced her debut “The Dust Settles Tour,” a North American club run scheduled for March and April 2027 in support of her recent album, *Dust Settles And So Will I*.

The Austin-born, New York City-based singer-songwriter will open the tour on March 23 at the Rebel Lounge in Phoenix. The itinerary includes performances in Los Angeles, Chicago and New York before the headline run concludes on April 16 in Washington, D.C.

Joffe will also perform at High Water Festival in Charleston, South Carolina, on April 18, two days after the tour’s final headline date. The festival appearance adds another engagement to her spring schedule following her festival debut at All Things Go D.C. in 2026.

While “The Dust Settles Tour” is billed as her debut tour, Joffe has already performed extensively as a supporting artist. Over the past year, she opened for King Princess in Europe. Her earlier touring credits include dates with spill tab, Alix Page, Indigo De Souza and NIKI.
