# Riley Green Announced as First Act for Cincinnati’s Farmer Music Center

## Summary

Cincinnati's $160 million, 20,000-capacity Farmer Music Center has announced Riley Green as its first confirmed performer, booked for June 24, 2027, ahead of the venue's planned June 2027 opening. The amphitheater, built on the former Coney Island grounds, is owned by the Cincinnati Symphony Orchestra and Music & Event Management.

### Key Points
- Riley Green's "That's Just Me Tour" stop is the first of three bookings the venue plans to announce this week.
- $160M, 20,000-capacity venue under construction since fall 2025; opening expected June 2027.
- Proximity to Riverbend Music Center and PNC Pavilion could enable coordinated large-scale festival use.
- Tickets go on sale Nov. 6, with a presale Nov. 5.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Riley-Green-The-Farmer-Music-Center.webp)

Cincinnati’s new Farmer Music Center has announced Riley Green as its first confirmed performer, with a concert scheduled for June 24, 2027.

Green will appear as part of his That’s Just Me Tour, joined by Muscadine Bloodline, Jake Worthington and Hannah McFarland. The announcement is the first of three bookings the venue plans to reveal this week. The amphitheater is expected to open in June 2027, although Green’s performance has not been identified as its opening concert.

Under construction since fall 2025, the $160 million facility will accommodate 20,000 people on the former Coney Island grounds. It is owned and operated by the Cincinnati Symphony Orchestra and Music & Event Management, Inc.

Project organizers envision the amphitheater as a central attraction for the redeveloped property. Its proximity to Riverbend Music Center and PNC Pavilion could also allow the grounds to host large-scale music festivals in coordination with those venues.

The tour takes its name from Green’s fourth studio album, *That’s Just Me*, released in September. Its announcement includes a fundraising partnership with participating schools, which are contributing band performances, artwork and other student-created material inspired by Friday night football traditions. A portion of ticket proceeds will support school programs and Green’s Buford Bonds Charitable Fund.

Tickets go on sale Friday, November 6, at 10 a.m., following a presale Thursday, November 5, at 10 a.m.
