# AFEM launches Promoter Good Practice Guide for electronic genre strategy

## Summary

The Association For Electronic Music (AFEM) has launched a Promoter Good Practice Guide for promoters working across electronic/EDM genres, developed with CAA, WME, Primary Talent and Resident Advisor. The guide responds to "growing market consolidation and shrinking funding" in the genre and focuses initially on grassroots venues and touring circuits even as DJ-led music's commercial profile keeps rising.

### Key Points
- AFEM's new guide formalizes best-practice advice for promoters across electronic-music genres.
- Developed in association with CAA, WME, Primary Talent, and Resident Advisor.
- Cited drivers: "growing market consolidation and shrinking funding" in the EDM promoter segment.
- Initial focus is grassroots venues and touring circuits, even as the genre's commercial appeal grows.
- Full guide content is paywalled; only the launch summary is available via this feed.
