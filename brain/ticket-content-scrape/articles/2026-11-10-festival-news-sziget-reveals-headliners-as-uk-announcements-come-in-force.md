# Festival news: Sziget reveals headliners as UK announcements come in force

## Summary

A roundup of summer 2027 festival lineup announcements, led by Hungary's Sziget Festival (Fontaines D.C., Bicep, KATSEYE, Lola Young among first acts) alongside BST Hyde Park, Roundhay, Y Not and other UK/US festival news.

- Sziget Festival's first 2027 lineup wave includes Fontaines D.C., Bicep, KATSEYE, Lola Young, Royel Otis and Tove Lo
