# Tove Lo Adds Nine U.S. Shows to 2027 ESTRUS World Tour

## Summary

Tove Lo added nine U.S. dates to her "ESTRUS World Tour" for spring 2027, running April 19 to May 7 and closing at Colorado's Red Rocks Amphitheatre, supporting her sixth album "ESTRUS." Coco & Clair Clair open all nine shows, with COBRAH joining as a special guest at Red Rocks.

### Key Points
- New leg spans Dallas, Houston, Raleigh, D.C., Philadelphia, Boston, Madison, St. Paul, and Red Rocks.
- Artist presale Nov. 4; general onsale Nov. 6 via Ticketmaster.
- Follows an initial North American leg (Nashville, Chicago, Brooklyn, Toronto, LA, Mexico City) and precedes UK/Europe (Nov.) and Australia/NZ (early 2027) dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tove-Lo-Estrus-Tour.webp)

Tove Lo has announced nine additional U.S. dates for her “ESTRUS World Tour,” extending the trek into spring 2027.

The new leg begins April 19 in Dallas, followed by a second Texas show in Houston. Tove Lo will then visit Raleigh, Washington, D.C., Philadelphia, and Boston before heading to Madison and St. Paul. The run ends May 7 at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado.

Coco & Clair Clair will open all nine concerts, with COBRAH appearing as a special guest at the tour’s Red Rocks Amphitheatre stop. The tour supports *ESTRUS*, Tove Lo’s sixth studio album, released in September.

An artist presale opens Wednesday, November 4, at 10:00 a.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, November 6, at 10:00 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

The announcement follows an initial North American tour schedule that included Nashville, Chicago, Brooklyn, Toronto, Los Angeles and Mexico City. The spring additions bring the tour to another group of U.S. markets following previously announced performances overseas.

Tove Lo is scheduled to tour the United Kingdom and Europe throughout November with support from Rose Gray. She will then perform in New Zealand and Australia in early 2027 before returning to the United States for the April and May concerts.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [tove-lo.com](http://tove-lo.com).
