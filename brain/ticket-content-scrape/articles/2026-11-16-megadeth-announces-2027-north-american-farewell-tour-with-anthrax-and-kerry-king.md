# Megadeth Announces 2027 North American Farewell Tour With Anthrax and Kerry King

## Summary

Megadeth announced the North American leg of its farewell tour, "Outbreak: Vaccination of the Nations," running two legs — May 14-June 23 and Sept. 6-30, 2027 — with Anthrax and Kerry King's solo band as support. General onsale is Nov. 20, with a fan-club presale Nov. 17 and several card/streaming-tied presales (Citi, Live Nation, Spotify "Reserved by Spotify") in the days prior. The tour follows the band's European farewell leg and its first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200.

### Key Points
- Two-leg North American run: May 14-June 23 and Sept. 6-30, 2027; general onsale Nov. 20.
- Support from Anthrax and Kerry King; part of Dave Mustaine's announced multiyear farewell campaign.
- Presale layers include fan-club (Nov. 17), Citi, Live Nation (Nov. 18), and a Spotify-listening-based "Reserved by Spotify" presale on select Ticketmaster events.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Megadeth-North-American-Tour-.webp)

Megadeth has announced a 2027 North American leg of its global farewell tour, bringing Anthrax and Slayer guitarist Kerry King’s solo band along for a Live Nation-produced outing titled *Outbreak: Vaccination of the Nations*. The two-part run will take the thrash metal veterans across the United States and Canada next spring and fall.

The tour is slated to open May 14 at First Financial Credit Union Amphitheater in Albuquerque, New Mexico. The first stretch also includes the Kia Forum in the Los Angeles area on May 19, Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, on May 26, Toronto’s RBC Amphitheatre on June 10 and Madison Square Garden in New York on June 15.

Megadeth will finish the spring portion June 23 at Germania Insurance Amphitheater in Austin, Texas. After a summer break, the tour resumes September 6 at Simmons Bank Arena in North Little Rock, Arkansas, before moving through the South, Northeast and Midwest. The currently announced routing concludes September 30 at Numerica Veterans Arena in Spokane, Washington.

The lineup puts Megadeth and Anthrax, two of thrash metal’s so-called Big Four, on the same tour. Kerry King, a founding member of Slayer, will appear with his solo project. The announcement lists both Anthrax and King as special guests at each of the published dates.

## Megadeth 2027 Tickets and Presales

General public ticket sales are scheduled for Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local venue time. The band’s Cyber Army fan-club presale is advertised to begin Tuesday, November 17, at 10 a.m. local time. Citi cardmember presales also begin Tuesday at participating venues, though their opening times differ: some event listings show 10 a.m. and others 2 p.m. local. Live Nation presales are scheduled for Wednesday, November 18, at 10 a.m. local on participating Ticketmaster dates.
