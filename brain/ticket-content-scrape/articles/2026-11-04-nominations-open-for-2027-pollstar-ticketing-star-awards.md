# Nominations Open For 2027 Pollstar Ticketing Star Awards

## Summary

Pollstar opened nominations for its 2027 Ticketing Star Awards, which honor box-office professionals, with submissions due Nov. 13 and voting running Nov. 20-Dec. 4. The 2026 awards, presented in partnership with INTIX, honored six winners from venues including Live Nation's Venue Nation, The Orion Amphitheater, and the Nashville Predators' Bridgestone Arena.

### Key Points
- Nominations open now through Nov. 13, 2026, 5 p.m. PT; voting Nov. 20-Dec. 4.
- Presented in partnership with INTIX; 2026 winners were recognized at INTIX's February conference in Las Vegas.
- 2026 winners spanned box-office, pricing/ticketing, and patron-services roles across six different organizations.
