# Pearl Jam Condemns ICE Detentions at Seattle Airport, Joining Growing Chorus of Music Industry Critics

## Summary

Pearl Jam publicly condemned U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement after a father and daughter were detained at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport, joining a growing list of musicians criticizing ICE actions. The statement follows social-media circulation of video of the detention.

### Key Points
- Pearl Jam issued a statement condemning the ICE detention of two passengers at Sea-Tac.
- Part of a broader pattern of musicians speaking out against ICE enforcement actions.
- Social/political commentary; no ticketing-industry relevance.

Pearl Jam has become the latest major musical act to publicly criticize U.S. Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE), issuing a statement Monday condemning the detention of a father and daughter at Seattle-Tacoma International Airport over the weekend.

The Seattle-based rock band took to social media following the circulation of videos showing masked federal agents detaining two passengers as they attempted to board a flight to California on Saturday, November 21.

“It is heartbreaking and infuriating to watch the human toll of a system driven by cold cruelty,” the band wrote in a statement [reported by Billboard](https://www.billboard.com/music/music-news/pearl-jam-slams-ice-detainments-seattle-airport-1236388933/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Pearl Jam characterized the treatment of the family as inhumane and pointed to the results of November’s elections as evidence of public opposition to the Trump administration’s immigration enforcement practices.

The passengers, Ricardo Larrondo Mendoza and his daughter, Sofia Hernandez Mejia, were traveling to California for a family baptism, according to [The Associated Press](https://apnews.com/article/c2898391e526ebed8472c190e90726d4?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). ICE said the two had overstayed their visas, while an attorney representing the family said both had entered the country legally and were pursuing immigration applications. The attorney also disputed ICE’s allegation that Hernandez Mejia had assaulted officers by throwing hot coffee.

The [Port of Seattle announced Monday](https://www.portseattle.org/news/port-commission-statement-november-23?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that it was conducting a comprehensive review of the incident and called for an end to domestic ICE enforcement operations at the airport.

Pearl Jam has a long history of political activism, but its latest statement comes at a time when the intersection of politics and the live music business has become particularly visible.

The controversy surrounding Ed Sheeran’s Loop Tour earlier this year demonstrated how political expression can have substantial commercial consequences. Rapper Macklemore was removed from the tour following objections to his pro-Palestinian advocacy from stadium operators, prompting other supporting artists to withdraw and generating disputes over ticket refunds.

The controversy also coincided with [growing resale inventory and softening ticket prices for several Sheeran performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/ed-sheeran-refunds-ticket-market-softens/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), though TicketNews found no evidence establishing that the political dispute caused those market movements.

Political expression has continued to intersect with Sheeran’s tour in other ways. Over the weekend, concertgoers in Santiago, Chile, [reported being temporarily denied entry](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/ed-sheeran-palestino-jerseys-chile-concert/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) because they were wearing apparel associated with Club Deportivo Palestino, a Chilean soccer club founded by Palestinian immigrants.

While taking public positions on contentious issues can alienate some potential ticket buyers, it can also strengthen the connection between artists and fans who share their views. For promoters, venues and ticket sellers, the commercial impact of such statements can be difficult to anticipate and even harder to isolate from other factors affecting demand.

Pearl Jam has not announced any changes to its live performance plans in connection with Monday’s statement, and there is no evidence at this point of an impact on the band’s ticket demand.
