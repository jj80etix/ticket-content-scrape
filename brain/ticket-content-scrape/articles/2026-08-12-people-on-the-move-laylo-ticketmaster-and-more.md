# People on the move: Laylo, Ticketmaster and more…

## Summary

TheTicketingBusiness's latest "people on the move" roundup: Laylo — an artist drop/presale platform used by Ariana Grande and Outside Lands — hired ex-Perplexity exec Brian Shin as head of growth to expand its AI capabilities, building on tools it shipped over the past year including automated Ticket Sales and Abandoned Cart agents and an AI Tour Builder for multi-city presales. Also noted: a Ticketmaster EMEA promotion, a new TicketSwap director of engineering, and Theatres Trust board reappointments.

### Key Points
- Laylo hired Brian Shin (ex-Perplexity) as head of growth to expand AI capabilities; recently shipped automated Ticket Sales/Abandoned Cart agents and an AI Tour Builder for multi-city presales.
- Laylo also named Taz Sharp director of artists and events, EMEA, to lead European expansion.
- Scott Pountney promoted to director, event delivery, EMEA at Ticketmaster.
- Pauline Jepp promoted to director of engineering at TicketSwap.
- UK's DCMS reappointed Annie Hampson and Katie Town to the Theatres Trust board.

### Highlights
- Laylo's shipped AI feature set (automated sales/cart-recovery agents, AI-driven multi-city presale builder) is a concrete, live example of AI deployment adjacent to ticketing/fan engagement.
- New growth hire from Perplexity signals continued AI investment specifically targeting presale/conversion automation.

### Industry Problem
Fan-engagement and presale tooling is where AI is being operationalized fastest in the live-events adjacent space (ahead of core ticketing checkout AI), suggesting artist/fan-data platforms like Laylo are moving quicker on AI than many ticketing platforms themselves.

### Proposed Solution
Track Laylo's AI Tour Builder and cart-recovery agents as a benchmark for what a primary ticketing platform's own presale/checkout AI tooling should eventually match or exceed — grounding material for Etix Assistant's roadmap rather than an immediate build trigger.

**We round up the latest movements from the ticketing sector and associated industries…**

Laylo, a drop platform for artists, live events and festivals, has appointed **Brian Shin** as head of growth, with the aim of expanding its AI capabilities.

The platform is utilised by artists such as Ariana Grande, Rosalía, IDLES, Suki Waterhouse and more, as well as events such as Outside Lands. Over the last year, the company shipped automated Ticket Sales and Abandoned Cart agents, an AI Tour Builder that lets management teams launch multi-city presales in seconds, and email templates that automatically optimises layouts for conversion.

Shin will look to take this further, having joined from AI search company Perplexity.

Earlier this year, the businesses also appointed **Taz Sharp** as director of artists and events, EMEA, as it looks to expand across Europe.

Based in London, Sharp will lead Laylo’s growth across the region, working closely with artist managers, promoters, festivals, and label teams to capture first-party fan data and reward fans who take action.

**Scott Pountney** has been promoted to director, event delivery, EMEA at Ticketmaster.

Pountney has spent more than a decade with Ticketmaster at two different stages, and was most recently director, event delivery.

Elsewhere, **Tom Newton** has started a new position as a ticketing executive at English Premier League club Nottingham Forest.

**Pauline Jepp** has been promoted to director of engineering at TicketSwap, having previously served as head of engineering.

Jepp has spent nearly four years at TicketSwap, having joined in early 2023.

The UK’s Secretary of State for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport has reappointed **Annie Hampson**  and  **Katie Town** as trustees of the Theatres Trust board for six months from September 13.

Hampson was formerly the chief planning officer at the City of London, and has held a series of senior local government roles over four decades. She is currently chair of the diocese of London Advisory Committee.

Town is chief executive of Theatre Royal Wakefield, leading a period of significant creative and organisational transformation since joining in 2015.
