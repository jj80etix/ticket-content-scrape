# Jack Johnson and Brandi Carlile to Headline Zootown Festival 2027

## Summary

Jack Johnson and Brandi Carlile will headline Zootown Music Festival's third edition in Missoula, Montana on June 18-19, 2027, with Johnson making his first-ever Montana performance. The 24-act lineup follows a sold-out advance "First Cast" ticket offering released before any artists were announced.

### Key Points
- Carlile headlines Friday (with Alabama Shakes, Whiskey Myers, Courtney Barnett); Johnson headlines Saturday (with Caamp, Sierra Ferrell)
- 24 total acts across two days at the Missoula Fairgrounds
- Pre-lineup "First Cast" ticket allotment sold out before performers were revealed
- Next presale for single-day and two-day passes begins Oct. 30

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Zootown-Festival.webp)

Jack Johnson and Brandi Carlile will headline Zootown Music Festival when it returns to Missoula, Montana, on June 18 and 19, 2027.

The event’s third edition will bring 24 acts to the Missoula Fairgrounds, with Johnson scheduled to perform in Montana for the first time.

Carlile leads the Friday, June 18, lineup alongside Alabama Shakes, Whiskey Myers and Courtney Barnett. Sammy Rae & The Friends, The Hip Abduction, Winyah and Bertha: Grateful Drag are also scheduled that day. The Sways, Leon Majcen, The Timber Rattlers and Desperate Electric round out Friday’s announced performers.

Johnson headlines Saturday, June 19, with Caamp, Sierra Ferrell and Kitchen Dwellers also on the bill. The second day will feature The Felice Brothers, Arcy Drive, Cimafunk, Steph Strings, The Takes, Izaak Opatz, Dammit Lauren! and The Sullivan Sisters. Each day’s announced lineup includes 12 acts.

The announcement follows an advance ticket offering that went on sale before organizers revealed any performers. That initial allotment, called “First Cast,” sold out ahead of the lineup announcement.

The next presale for single-day and two-day passes begins October 30 at 10 a.m. Mountain Time. General sales follow at noon that day. A limited number of tickets will also be sold in person at Missoula record store Rockin Rudy’s at a special rate for local residents.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [zootownfestival.com](http://zootownfestival.com).
