# Iron Maiden Announces 2027 North America Dates For ‘Run For Your Lives Tour’

## Summary

Iron Maiden announced a 16-date 2027 North American leg of its "Run For Your Lives Tour," running April 14 (Tacoma) through May 11 (Belmont Park, NY), plus festival headlining slots at Sonic Temple and Boardwalk Rock. The run is billed as the definitive final North American leg of the band's 50th-anniversary tour, covering markets, particularly the Pacific Northwest and Southeast, not reached on the prior leg.

### Key Points
- 16 North American dates, April 14-May 11, 2027, opening in Tacoma, WA and closing at UBS Arena, Belmont Park, NY.
- Band says this will "DEFINITELY" be the last North American leg of the Run For Your Lives Tour, celebrating its 50th anniversary.
- Artist presale begins Oct. 19; general onsale Oct. 23, both at 10 a.m. local.
- Added festival headlining appearances: Sonic Temple (Columbus, OH) and Boardwalk Rock (Ocean City, MD), both May 14-16.
- Tour overall has played 107 shows across 36 countries, including a $1.78M-grossing sold-out Tinley Park, IL stop per Pollstar Boxoffice.

Iron Maiden is making its triumphant return to North America, announcing a 2027 run of the “Run For Your Lives Tour.” The NA leg will kick off at the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, WA, on April 14, run for 16 dates, and conclude at UBS Arena in Belmont Park, NY, on May 11. See full tour routing below.

Tickets will be available starting with the artist presale beginning on Oct. 19 at 10 a.m. local time. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general on sale beginning on Oct. 23 at 10 a.m. local. For festival on-sales check their respective websites. Go to [www.ironmaiden.com](http://www.ironmaiden.com/) for the latest info.

An official press release for the band reveals that these new legs will “DEFINITELY be the last of the already legendary RUN FOR YOUR LIVES TOUR” and will mark the end of a tour where Iron Maiden played 107 shows across 36 countries in celebration of the iconic band’s 50th anniversary. A recent show from the “Run For Your Lives Tour” included a sold-out stop at Tinley Park, IL, at the Credit Union 1 Amphitheater, grossing $1,775,774 on Sept. 22, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/30375), along with other highlights including sold-out crowds at the Alamodome in San Antonio and two nights at BMO Stadium in Los Angeles.

“As many of our fans know, Maiden were planning on taking a break from live performances in 2027, but we’ve been enjoying this last leg in the USA and Canada so much that we all had a chat and decided we should take the show to parts we missed, particularly the North West and South East,” Iron Maiden founder and bassist Steve Harris said in an official announcement regarding the North American dates. “It just seems fair to our many fans there who have given us tremendous support for so many years. We can’t get everywhere but we can try! Also, we have always enjoyed playing the many great festivals that are in Europe and wanted to experience a couple more in the USA which we will do at the end of the tour in Columbus and Maryland. At festivals you are not just playing to your own fans so it’s always a challenge and we all love a challenge!”

“The first leg was terrific, huge sell-out crowds of all ages spanning multi-generations, some of the biggest shows we have ever played in North America with rave fan and media reactions, and just a great all round vibe and camaraderie from our fans, including in our Eddie’s Dive Bars everywhere. And I know as I go to every one of them! In fact it was so good we just have to come back and play some places we missed and bring Eddie’s Dive Bars with us!” Manager Rod Smallwood said.

The announcement also includes the first artist announcement for two festivals, as headliner for Sonic Temple festival in Columbus, Ohio, on May 14, produced by Danny Wimmer Presents, and the band is also to appear at Boardwalk Rock in Ocean City, Maryland (May 14-16), produced by C3 Presents.
