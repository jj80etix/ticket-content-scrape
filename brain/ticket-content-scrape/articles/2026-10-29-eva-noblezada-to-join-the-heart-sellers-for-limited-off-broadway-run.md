# Eva Noblezada to Join ‘The Heart Sellers’ for Limited Off-Broadway Run

## Summary

Eva Noblezada will make her Off-Broadway debut in "The Heart Sellers" at MCC Theater, opposite Juyeon Song, starting previews January 8, 2027. The limited engagement is directed by May Adrales and closes February 14.

### Key Points
- Previews begin Jan. 8, 2027; opens Jan. 21; closes Feb. 14
- Staged at the Susan and Ronald Frankel Theater inside the Robert W. Wilson MCC Theater Space
- Noblezada plays Luna; Song (also making her Off-Broadway debut) plays Jane
- Lloyd Suh's play is set on Thanksgiving 1973 and follows two immigrant women

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Heart-Sellers.webp)

Eva Noblezada will make her Off-Broadway debut in “The Heart Sellers,” starring opposite Juyeon Song in the New York premiere of Lloyd Suh’s play at MCC Theater. May Adrales will direct the limited engagement, which begins performances in January.

Previews start January 8, 2027, at the Susan and Ronald Frankel Theater inside the Robert W. Wilson MCC Theater Space. Opening night is January 21, and the production is scheduled to close February 14.

Noblezada will portray Luna, while Song will play Jane. Set on Thanksgiving in 1973, “The Heart Sellers” follows two immigrant women whose chance meeting leads them to spend the holiday together. MCC describes the play as “a warm and funny story of a chance encounter and quintessentially American connection.”

Song will also make her Off-Broadway debut with the production.

“The Heart Sellers” premiered at Milwaukee Repertory Theater in 2023. The company commissioned the work through its John (Jack) D. Lewis New Play Development Program. Adrales subsequently directed productions at the Huntington and Guthrie before the upcoming New York engagement. Suh’s other plays include “The Chinese Lady” and “The Far Country,” a Pulitzer Prize finalist.

The MCC creative team includes scenic designer Wilson Chin, costume designer Jennifer Moeller, lighting designer Jiyoun Chang and sound designer Fabian Obispo. Natalie Carney will supervise props. Alyssa K. Howard serves as production stage manager, with Tyler Crow as assistant stage manager. Casting is by The Telsey Office.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [MCCTheater.org](http://www.mcctheater.org/).
