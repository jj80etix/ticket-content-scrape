# Marketing platform Hive receives $27m infusion to scale AI assets

## Summary

IQ Magazine's (paywalled) coverage of the same Hive $27M Frontier Growth raise confirms the funding targets building "AI-powered assistants" to close the seller/buyer data gap for live events, with Frontier Growth also having backed atVenu previously.

### Key Points
- $27M (€24m) private-equity injection from Frontier Growth, which previously invested $30M (€27m) in atVenu (2022).
- Hive says the capital will accelerate its "product roadmap," deepen ticketing/data integrations, and expand its global presence.
- Primary target of the raise is building AI-powered assistants (same core detail as the TicketNews coverage of this raise).
- Article is paywalled past the opening summary; no additional detail beyond the lede available in the excerpt.

### Highlights
- Confirms Hive's $27M raise (reported alongside TicketNews' fuller coverage of the same story) is earmarked for AI-assistant development and deeper ticketing-data integration.

### Industry Problem
Same underlying client-side pain point as the fuller TicketNews coverage: ticketing and marketing data are siloed for venues/promoters, and vendors like Hive are raising capital specifically to close that gap with AI tooling.

### Proposed Solution
See companion note on this raise for the fuller analysis; no new action beyond continuing to monitor Hive's AI roadmap given its existing Etix integration.
