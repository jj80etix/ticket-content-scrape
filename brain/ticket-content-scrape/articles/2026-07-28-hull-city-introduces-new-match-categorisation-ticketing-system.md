# Hull City introduces new match categorisation ticketing system

## Summary

Premier League newcomer Hull City will introduce an A/B/C match-categorisation ticketing system for 2026/27, pricing fixtures by demand (£40-£55 for sideline seats depending on category) alongside a new peer-to-peer ticket exchange platform for season-ticket holders who can't attend a game. Routine single-club operations news.

### Key Points
- Hull City will price 2026/27 Premier League matches into categories A, B, and C.
- Category A sideline seats start at £55; Category C starts at £40.
- New ticket-exchange platform lets members release unused seats for resale to fellow supporters.
- Marks Hull's first Premier League season since 2016/17.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Hull City is set to introduce a new match categorisation system for the 2026/27 campaign following the club’s promotion to the Premier League.**

It is aimed at helping to manage match-by-match ticket sales fairly and transparently for games at the MKM Stadium.

“Matches throughout the season will be categorised as A, B or C,” read a club statement.

“This approach allows the Club to price fixtures appropriately while continuing to offer a range of affordable opportunities for supporters to experience Premier League football at the MKM Stadium.

“The category for each fixture will be confirmed as part of the match-specific ticketing information released ahead of each home game.”

The most expensive tickets for Category A fixtures, which encompass sideline seats, will begin at £55 ($73/€65) while Category C in the same zone will start at £40.

Prior to the 2026/27 season, the club is also introducing a new ticket exchange platform, giving members who are unable to attend selected fixtures the opportunity to release their seat for resale to fellow supporters.

The upcoming campaign, which is set to begin next month, marks Hull’s first in the Premier League since 2016/17 after the Tigers were promoted from the Championship in the last season.
