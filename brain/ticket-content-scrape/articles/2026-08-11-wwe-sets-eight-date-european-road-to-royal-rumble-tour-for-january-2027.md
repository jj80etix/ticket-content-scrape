# WWE Sets Eight-Date European Road to Royal Rumble Tour for January 2027

## Summary

WWE announced an eight-show "2027 Road to Royal Rumble" European tour running January 15-25, covering seven countries including first-ever stops in Dublin (Raw), Belgium (SmackDown), and Gliwice, Poland. The run also marks returns to Amsterdam, Sweden, and Hungary after multi-year absences. Ticket access begins with an exclusive presale registered through WWE's site, with the full roster including CM Punk, Cody Rhodes, and Rhea Ripley advertised across the dates.

### Key Points
- Tour spans Belgium, Poland, the Netherlands, the UK, Sweden, Hungary, and Ireland from January 15-25, 2027.
- First-ever WWE events in Dublin (Raw), Belgium (SmackDown), and Gliwice, Poland; Manchester's Co-op Live gets its first WWE televised event.
- Notable returns: Amsterdam (first in 2+ years), Sweden (first in 8+ years), Hungary (first in nearly a decade).
- Presale access is available now through WWE's own registration portal ahead of the general onsale.
- Eight dates total across nine venues, closing with Raw at Dublin's 3Arena on January 25.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/wwe.jpg)

WWE is taking the 2027 Road to Royal Rumble across Europe, announcing an eight-show January tour spanning seven countries and featuring a string of firsts for the company.

The 2027 Road to Royal Rumble European Tour will run from January 15-25, bringing Raw, SmackDown and non-televised supershows to Belgium, Poland, the Netherlands, the UK, Sweden, Hungary and Ireland.

Among the biggest milestones, WWE will stage Raw in Dublin for the first time, bring SmackDown to Belgium for the first time and hold its first-ever event in Gliwice, Poland. Manchester’s Co-op Live will also host its first WWE televised event when SmackDown arrives on January 22.

The run marks several notable European returns for WWE. The company will head back to Amsterdam for the first time in more than two years, Sweden for the first time in more than eight years, and Hungary for the first time in nearly a decade.

The tour opens with SmackDown at Forest National in Brussels on January 15 before moving to PreZero Arena in Gliwice and Amsterdam’s Ziggo Dome for back-to-back Road to Royal Rumble Supershows. Raw then heads to Glasgow’s OVO Hydro on January 18.

Following SmackDown at Co-op Live in Manchester, WWE will stage supershows at Avicii Arena in Stockholm on January 23 and MVM Dome in Budapest on January 24 before closing the European run with Raw at Dublin’s 3Arena on January 25.

WWE is advertising a roster featuring Undisputed WWE Champion CM Punk, Women’s World Champion Liv Morgan, Cody Rhodes, Seth Rollins, Rhea Ripley, Becky Lynch, Finn Bálor, Jey Uso, LA Knight, Oba Femi, GUNTHER, Jade Cargill, Trick Williams and World Tag Team Champions The Vision, among others.
