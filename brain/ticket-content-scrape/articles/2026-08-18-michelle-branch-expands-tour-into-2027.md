# Michelle Branch Expands Tour Into 2027

## Summary

Grammy-winning singer-songwriter Michelle Branch announced a 17-date second leg that takes her “Everywhere and Back Again Tour” into 2027.

The newly announced shows in U.S. theaters begin Feb. 25 in Charleston, South Carolina, and include stops at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium, Los Angeles’ The Wiltern on March 13th and New York City’s Beacon Theatre before wrapping March 27 at Franklin Music Hall in Philadelphia.

Artist presale begins Aug. 19 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by Live Nation and Ticketmaster presales at noon local time. Local and venue presales begin Aug. 20 at 10 a.m. local time, with all presales endingat 10 p.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public Aug. 21 at 10 a.m. local time.

Branch is repped by WME’s Chris Hrovat, Jay Williams, Travis Wolfe and Andy Mack. She’s managed by Jason Henke and Steve Schweidel at Vector Management.
