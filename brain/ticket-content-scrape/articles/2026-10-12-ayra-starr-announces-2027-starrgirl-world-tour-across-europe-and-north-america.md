# Ayra Starr Announces 2027 ‘Starrgirl World Tour’ Across Europe and North America

## Summary

Nigerian artist Ayra Starr announced her 2027 "Starrgirl World Tour," covering Europe and North America in support of her latest album. General on-sale follows a staggered presale schedule starting Oct. 13 with a Verizon presale.

### Key Points
- Ayra Starr's "Starrgirl World Tour" announced for 2027, covering Europe and North America.
- Tour supports her latest album release.
- Presale schedule: Verizon presale Oct. 13, Spotify Reserved presale, artist presale Oct. 14.
- Standard tour-announcement item with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ayra-Starr-.webp)

Ayra Starr will bring her “Starrgirl World Tour” to Europe and North America in 2027, announcing 20 shows in support of her latest album.

The itinerary begins January 20 at Live Music Hall in Cologne, Germany, and concludes April 14 at The Fillmore in San Francisco. The seven-show European and U.K. schedule takes Starr from Cologne to Amsterdam on January 21, Paris on January 24, Milan on January 27, Zurich on January 29 and Brussels on January 31. That portion of the tour ends February 2 at London’s O2 Shepherd’s Bush Empire.

Following a break, Starr will open the North American leg March 18 in Houston, followed by Dallas on March 19 and Atlanta on March 21. The schedule then heads to Silver Spring, Maryland, on March 24, Boston on March 25 and Philadelphia on March 26.

A March 28 performance in Brooklyn precedes two Canadian dates, with Toronto scheduled for March 29 and Montreal for March 30. Starr returns to the United States for Chicago on April 1, Minneapolis on April 2 and Denver on April 5 before the San Francisco finale. The announced North American leg includes 13 performances across the United States and Canada.

Released August 14, *Starrgirl* is Starr’s third studio album. The 16-track release features collaborations with Wizkid, Rema, ZAYN, Leon Thomas, Theodora, kwn and Danny Ocean.

The announcement follows Starr’s October 11 halftime performance at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London during the NFL game between the Philadelphia Eagles and Jacksonville Jaguars. The Nigerian singer will return to London for a headline performance during the tour’s opening leg.

North American ticket sales begin October 13 with a Verizon presale at 10 a.m. local time, followed by a Spotify Reserved presale at 2 p.m. An artist presale opens October 14 at 10 a.m. local time. Additional presales are scheduled ahead of the general sale on October 16 at 10 a.m. local time.

For the European and U.K. dates, artist and Mastercard presales begin October 14 at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales follow October 16 at 10 a.m. local time through Live Nation.
