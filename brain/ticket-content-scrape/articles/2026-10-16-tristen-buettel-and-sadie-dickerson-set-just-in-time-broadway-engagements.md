# Tristen Buettel and Sadie Dickerson Set ‘Just in Time’ Broadway Engagements

## Summary

Broadway's "Just in Time" will have Tristen Buettel and Sadie Dickerson alternate as Sandra Dee this fall/winter, filling the gap between Carrie St. Louis's Oct. 18 departure and Whitney Leavitt's Dec. 1 engagement, with the role changes coinciding with new actors stepping into the Bobby Darin lead opposite them. The casting also marks Nicholas Christopher's arrival as Darin alongside Dickerson's Nov. 10 start.

### Key Points
- Buettel (currently a swing) plays Sandra Dee Oct. 20-Nov. 8, opposite Matthew Morrison's return as Bobby Darin.
- Dickerson plays the role Nov. 10-29, opposite Nicholas Christopher's debut as Darin; Leavitt (of "The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives") takes over Dec. 1-Jan. 10, 2027.
- St. Louis gives her final performance Oct. 18, the same day Jeremy Jordan concludes his run as Darin.
- Directed by Alex Timbers; show traces Bobby Darin's career with songs including "Mack the Knife" and "Beyond the Sea."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Just-in-Time-1.webp)

Tristen Buettel and Sadie Dickerson will take turns playing Sandra Dee in Broadway’s “Just in Time” this fall, filling the role between Carrie St. Louis’s departure and Whitney Leavitt’s previously announced engagement.

Buettel, currently a swing in the production, will begin her three-week run October 20 and continue through November 8 at Circle in the Square Theatre. Dickerson will return to the musical November 10 for an engagement running through November 29. St. Louis will give her final performance October 18.

The announcement establishes the production’s Sandra Dee casting schedule through the end of November. Leavitt, known for “The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives,” is scheduled to take over December 1 and remain with the show through January 10, 2027.

The two newly announced engagements will coincide with changes in the musical’s leading role. Buettel will appear opposite Matthew Morrison as Bobby Darin during his three-week return to the production. Dickerson’s first performance will also mark Nicholas Christopher’s arrival as Darin on November 10.

Jeremy Jordan, who currently plays Darin, will conclude his engagement October 18, the same day St. Louis departs. Morrison will begin October 20 and perform through November 8. Christopher joins the production following his appearance in “Chess.”

Developed and directed by Tony Award winner Alex Timbers, “Just in Time” follows Darin’s life and career, tracing his rise from teen idol to international performer. The production uses a nightclub setting and a live onstage band to present songs including “Beyond the Sea,” “Mack the Knife,” “Splish Splash” and “Dream Lover.”

The current company also includes Ariana Madix as Connie Francis, Debbie Gravitte as Polly, Joe Barbara as Steve Blauner and other characters, Lance Roberts as Ahmet Ertegun and others, Caesar Samayoa as Don Kirshner and others, and Emily Bergl as Nina and Mary.
