# WWE Launches $99 Club WWE Membership With Ticket Presales, Exclusive Merch

## Summary

WWE launched Club WWE, a $99/year ($8.25/month) membership program bundling early ticket access to select events, members-only lounges at premium live events, exclusive merchandise drops, a premium content library, live match chats, and a points-based rewards system redeemable for merch and experiences. It launches in the U.S. first, with international expansion planned later.

### Key Points
- $99/year membership tier; early-bird "Founding Members" get a limited-edition welcome kit.
- Perks span ticketing (early access, members-only lounges), commerce (exclusive WWE Shop drops), and content (classic matches, behind-the-scenes footage, live chats during broadcasts).
- Points-based loyalty mechanic: earn via purchases, logins, content-watching, chat participation, and games; redeem for merch/experiences.
- Positioned as a "year-round destination," explicitly tying loyalty membership to ticketing perks rather than treating them separately.

### Highlights
- Flat $99/year price point bundling early ticket access with content and commerce loyalty.
- International expansion explicitly deferred to a later phase — U.S.-only at launch.

### Industry Problem
Entertainment brands with passionate fanbases increasingly bundle ticketing access into paid membership/loyalty programs rather than leaving early access as a standalone perk, raising the bar for what "loyalty" means in ticketing.

### Proposed Solution
WWE's model — paid membership gating early ticket access plus content/commerce loyalty — is a concrete Q17 comparable. For Etix, the near-term move is to track adoption/engagement data as it emerges (not build now), since Club WWE tests whether fans will pay upfront for ticketing-adjacent loyalty at flat annual pricing.

WWE is rolling out a new membership program promising fans earlier ticket access, exclusive merchandise, premium content and members-only experiences.

Club WWE officially launches Friday in the United States, offering a centralized destination for ticketing perks, rewards, community features and original programming. Membership costs $99 per year, equivalent to $8.25 per month.

“As we continue to evolve the WWE fan experience, Club WWE creates a year-round destination that rewards our most passionate fans,” 17-time WWE Champion John Cena said in a press release.

Members will receive early access to tickets for select WWE events, along with entry to members-only lounges at certain Premium Live Events and live shows.
