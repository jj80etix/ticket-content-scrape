# Helene Fischer’s Open Air Tour draws nearly 750,000 fans

## Summary

German singer Helene Fischer wrapped her Open Air Tour with a sold-out finale at Munich's Allianz Arena, bringing total attendance across 15 stadium shows in Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and the Netherlands to nearly 750,000. The tour, built around a 360-degree stage, was produced by Live Nation GSA in its second major collaboration with Fischer following her prior arena tour.

### Key Points
- Helene Fischer's Open Air Tour ran June 10-July 17, 2026 across 15 stadium shows in four countries.
- Total attendance reached nearly 750,000 fans, capped by a sold-out Allianz Arena finale in Munich.
- The tour used a 360-degree stage design and was produced/organized by Live Nation GSA.
- This is Live Nation GSA's second major tour collaboration with Fischer, after a previous arena tour.
- The article is an attendance/success recap with no pricing, litigation, or ticketing-platform-specific content.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**German singer** **Helene Fischer has concluded her Open Air Tour with a sold-out show at Munich’s Allianz Arena to bring the total attendance across 15 shows to nearly 750,000.**

The Munich finale capped a run of concerts across Germany, Austria, Switzerland, and the Netherlands from June 10 to July 17.

Built around a 360° stage, the show was designed to bring Fischer as close as possible to fans throughout each stadium.

“Every tour changes you – me included,” Fischer said.

“I’m taking countless memories, emotions, and encounters with me, but above all, new energy and inspiration. I’m grateful for everything we’ve experienced together.

“And if this tour has shown one thing, it’s that the most beautiful moments happen when people forget everything around them for a few hours and simply enjoy the moment together.”

The 360° tour was produced and organised by Live Nation, marking the company’s second major tour collaboration with Fischer following her arena tour.

“We are immensely proud of this milestone in Helene Fischer’s success story, as she has once again demonstrated her unique popularity and outstanding quality,” said Marek Lieberberg, chief executive of Live Nation GSA.

“The 360° tour also showed how to deliver incomparable, magical moments to fans that will remain in their memories.

“That was our ambition from the very beginning.”
