# Dolly Parton’s Estate Announces Dollyfest Celebrations in Nashville and London for 2027

## Summary

Dolly Parton's estate and Team Dolly announced DOLLYFEST, a new two-city tribute festival to be held across successive weekends in Nashville and London in 2027, following the country icon's death.

### Key Points
- Two-city celebration spanning consecutive weekends in Nashville, TN and London, England.
- Organized by Dolly Parton's estate in partnership with Team Dolly.
- Specific dates and lineup not yet announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/DollyFest.webp)

Dolly Parton’s legacy will be celebrated on both sides of the Atlantic in 2027 with DOLLYFEST, a new two-city festival organized by the late music icon’s estate and Team Dolly.

The celebration will take place across two consecutive weekends in Nashville, Tennessee, and London, England. Specific dates, participating artists and ticket information have not yet been announced.

DOLLYFEST developed from an event Parton herself had been planning before her death. The original concept called for a multi-artist celebration in Nashville in January 2026, timed around the country legend’s 80th birthday. Parton was expected to host and perform during the event.

Those plans were put on hold after Parton stepped back from some commitments following health challenges in August 2025. Her team will now expand the idea into a two-day, two-weekend celebration spanning two continents.

“What Dolly started, we will ensure is brought to life,” Danny Nozell, founder and CEO of CTK Enterprises, said in announcing the event.

Nozell said DOLLYFEST had been one of Parton’s personal ambitions and preparations for the original Nashville gathering were already well underway.

“Many people behind the scenes know that DOLLYFEST is a dream of Dolly’s that was intended to take place on her 80th birthday in Nashville,” Nozell said. “We had the venue booked and we had the announcement ready to go.”

Rather than organize a traditional memorial service, Parton’s estate plans to carry out the festival concept she envisioned. Organizers said the delay will also give fans and artists around the world time to participate in other tributes before the larger DOLLYFEST events arrive in 2027.

“We are not discouraging people from celebrating Dolly in the ways they choose,” Nozell said. “However, as far as a traditional memorial goes, that is not something Dolly wanted.”

He added that continuing with Parton’s festival idea felt more fitting than staging a conventional memorial.

“This is something that is authentic to her, something that feels right to me, and something that artists and fans around the world can participate in, while also giving everyone time to grieve, and giving everyone time to plan,” Nozell said.

The Nashville and London locations will place the celebration in two cities closely associated with the global music industry. Parton’s estate acknowledged that additional events, performances and programs honoring the singer are expected to take place this fall. DOLLYFEST, however, is being positioned as the official Team Dolly-produced celebration based on Parton’s own plans.

Additional information, including the 2027 dates, performers, venues and ticket details, will be announced at a later date. Fans can sign up through [Dolly Parton’s official website](https://www.dollyparton.com/) to receive updates.
