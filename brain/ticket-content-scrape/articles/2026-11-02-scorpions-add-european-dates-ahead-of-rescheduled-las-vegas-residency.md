# Scorpions Add European Dates Ahead of Rescheduled Las Vegas Residency

## Summary

Scorpions announced a 2027 "Love, Peace, and Rock N' Roll Tour" opening April 29 in Barcelona, running through Europe before culminating in rescheduled Mexico and Las Vegas dates after postponing their remaining 2026 shows (including an 8-show Vegas residency) for "unforeseen circumstances."

### Key Points
- European leg spans Spain, Germany, Poland, the Baltics, Central Europe, Scandinavia, the UK, France, and Italy through mid-July.
- Rescheduled Las Vegas residency at PH Live runs Sept. 9-25, 2027.
- Rock Zone Community members get early ticket access Nov. 4; general sales start Nov. 6.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Scorpions-2027.webp)

Scorpions will launch their 2027 “Love, Peace, and Rock N’ Roll Tour” on April 29 in Barcelona, beginning a spring and summer run through the United Kingdom and Europe before rescheduled performances in Mexico and Las Vegas.

The German rock band postponed its remaining 2026 concerts in September, citing “unforeseen circumstances.” Those dates included an eight-show Las Vegas residency. At the time, the group said its return in 2027 would also include European shows.

Following the Barcelona opener, the tour will visit Coruña, Bilbao and Madrid before heading to Germany for May concerts in Stuttgart, Dortmund, Frankfurt and Munich.

The schedule resumes in June with performances in Kraków, Vilnius, Prague and Zagreb. Additional stops include Vienna, Zürich and Rotterdam before the band travels to Sweden and Norway. The Oslo appearance is scheduled for June 26 at Tons of Rock.
