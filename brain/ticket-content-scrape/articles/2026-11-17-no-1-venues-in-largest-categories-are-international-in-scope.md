# No. 1 Venues In Largest Categories Are International In Scope

## Summary

Pollstar's Venues Pulse chart for shows Oct. 24-Nov. 6 shows internationally diverse venues topping each capacity tier: Hong Kong's Kai Tak Stadium (The Weeknd, 179,547 tickets across 4 shows) leads venues over 30,000 capacity; Mexico City's Palacio de los Deportes tops the 15,001-30,000 tier; Bogotá's El Corazón de Bogotá Arena (formerly Movistar Arena) leads 10,001-15,000; and Colorado's Red Rocks Amphitheatre tops the 5,001-10,000 tier with a 10-show, 82,314-ticket total led by deadmau5 and Fuerza Regida.

### Key Points
- Kai Tak Stadium, Hong Kong: No. 1 in the 30,000+ tier on The Weeknd's tour-closing Asia dates (179,547 tickets; the tour sold roughly 9M tickets and grossed $1.2B over four years).
- Palacio de los Deportes, Mexico City: No. 1 in 15,001-30,000, led by Omar Courtz (52,217 tickets, $3.9M gross across 3 shows).
- El Corazón de Bogotá Arena, Colombia: No. 1 in 10,001-15,000, led by a sold-out Def Leppard show (11,829 tickets).
- Red Rocks Amphitheatre: No. 1 in 5,001-10,000 (82,314 tickets/10 shows), led by deadmau5 and Fuerza Regida.
- Rankings span three continents across all four largest size tiers, reflecting broad international touring strength.

## Transcript

![Current image: KaiTekStadiumGettyImages 2304452980](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/KaiTekStadiumGettyImages-2304452980-1024x576.jpg)

Aerial view of Kai Tak Stadium, the main stadium of Hong Kong Sports Park on February 4, 2026 in Hong Kong. (Photo by Derek Yung/Nexpher Images via Getty Images)

Three continents are represented on the Venues Pulse chart listing the No. 1 venues in the four largest size categories. A stadium in Hong Kong, arenas in Mexico and Colombia and a Colorado amphitheater each score the top ranking in their respective groups determined by venue capacity. Rankings are based on ticket sales totals reported to *Pollstar* for shows occurring Oct. 24 through Nov. 6.

Ranked highest among the venues in the Over 30,000-capacity group is Kai Tak Stadium in Kowloon, Hong Kong based on reported ticket sales totaling 179,547 from four performances on The Weeknd’s “After Hours til Dawn Tour.” The concerts were part of the final leg of the tour that featured 17 shows at seven stadiums in Asian countries. The R&B star sold a total of 834,387 tickets in Asia, bringing his overall ticket sales from the entire run of the tour to just over 9 million. Altogether, The Weeknd grossed $1.2 billion on his world tour that spanned more than four years, from July 14, 2022 through Nov. 5, 2026.

The venues ranked highest in the 15,001-30,000 category are large arenas, also with an international scope. Six of the top 10 are located in North America, yet two more are in European countries, while Asia and Oceania are both represented by one venue. The No. 1 arena is Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City with ticket sales totaling 129,148 at eight concerts. The top draw at the venue was Puerto Rican artist Omar Courtz who performed for 52,217 fans at three concerts, Oct. 28, 29 and Nov. 3. The OCESA-promoted event grossed $3.9 million. Also, with shows there during the two-week eligibility time period were Panteón Rococó, Fobia and Morat with the first night of a three-show run.

Along with the Mexico City venue, other North American arenas in the top 10 are Madison Square Garden, Barclays Center and UBS Arena, all in the New York City metropolitan area, along with Boston’s TD Garden and Moody Center in Austin, Texas. European buildings are Manchester, England’s Co-op Live and Lanxess Arena in Cologne, Germany, with K-Arena Yokohama in Japan and Afterpay Arena in Sydney, Australia rounding out the list.

The former Movistar Arena in Bogotá, Colombia, now named El Corazón de Bogotá Arena as of Nov. 1, remains a consistent contender for No. 1 in the category for venues with capacities of 10,001-15,000. On this chart, the arena earns the top ranking with a total of 52,214 tickets sold at eight performances. The venue’s top draw was the band Def Leppard with a sellout on Nov. 2 attended by 11,829 fans. Then, Bunbury and Hombres G both sold over 10,800 tickets for their headlining concerts on Oct. 29 and 24, respectively.

Also in the second largest venue group, five European venues make a top 10 appearance including Utilita Arena Newcastle and Utilita Arena Sheffield in the U.K., Hallenstadion Zürich in Switzerland and two arenas in Germany: Hamburg’s Barclays Arena and Frankfurt’s Festhalle. The U.S. arenas included are CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore, Dignity Health Arena in Bakersfield, California and Bryce Jordan Center in University Park, Pennsylvania, while Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne, Australia also lands on the list.

The 5,001-10,000-capacity is represented at No. 1 by Denver-area outdoor venue, Red Rocks Amphitheatre at Morrison, Colorado. A 10-show ticket total of 82,314 earns the top ranking for the venue. Two headliners appeared at the venue on back-to-back nights including deadmau5 with the highest ticket total of 16,988 from shows on Oct. 30 and 31. The San Bernardino, California band Fuerza Regida follows with a ticket count of 16,652, also from two performances.

Four events topped 8,000 in ticket sales from a one-night engagement at Red Rocks. One was a concert featuring Cypress Hill along with Method Man and Redman and others, attended by a crowd of 8,907 on Oct. 29. And on the three previous nights, Evanescence, Tucker Wetmore and Koe Wetzel each appeared at the shed selling tickets totaling 8,870; 8,819 and 8,332, respectively. The other nine venues ranking with the highest ticket sales in the 5,001-10,000 group are Radio City Music Hall in New York followed by three Mexican theaters: Mexico City’s Auditorio Nacional, Monterrey’s Auditorio Banamex and Zapopan’s Auditorio Telmex. Palapartenope in Naples, Italy is next at No. 6 with The Anthem in Washington, DC and Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut following in the ranking. Then, Porsche-Arena in Stuttgart, Germany and Laval, Quebec’s Place Bell complete the top 10.
