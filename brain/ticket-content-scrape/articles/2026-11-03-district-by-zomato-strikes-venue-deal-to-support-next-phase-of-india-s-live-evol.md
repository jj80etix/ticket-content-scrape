# District by Zomato strikes venue deal to support ‘next phase’ of India’s live evolution

## Summary

India's District by Zomato has struck a naming-rights and infrastructure-investment deal with telecom Airtel for Bengaluru's 17,000-capacity Terraform Arena, to be renamed Airtel Arena. The companies frame the deal, which funds digital-connectivity upgrades for concertgoers, as a signal of India's live-entertainment market maturing beyond brand-association-only sponsorships.

### Key Points
- Venue renamed Terraform Arena to Airtel Arena; Airtel invests in high-capacity digital infrastructure across the 16-acre site.
- District's CEO cites India's acute shortage of purpose-built venues over 10,000 capacity (fewer than ten nationwide) as the industry's biggest growth constraint.
- Planned upgrades (digital payments, livestreaming, mobile ordering, cellular coverage) are targeted for deployment before the next concert season.
- Deal is described as the first of several partnerships planned for the venue's expansion.

## District by Zomato strikes venue deal to support ‘next phase’ of India’s live evolution

Telecommunications firm Airtel and District say the investment and naming rights deal signals India's live music market maturation

India’s District by Zomato has announced a “first-of-its-kind” strategic collaboration with telecommunications firm Airtel for the naming rights and investment in the digital infrastructure of one of the country’s only purpose-built venues.

Under the agreement, the 17,000-capacity outdoor space in Bengaluru will be renamed from Terraform Arena to Airtel Arena, with the company investing in high-capacity digital infrastructure across the 16-acre site.

The plug-and-play venue opened in November 2024, with District taking over operations last September. The entertainment platform will continue with its programming, fan engagement, and operational duties.

The companies say the deal signals India’s live music market maturation, as the country’s circuit has had “limited examples of large ecosystem brands committing capital investment to venue infrastructure, rather than brand association alone”.

“India has fewer than ten purpose-built venues that can host more than 10,000 people,” says District CEO Rahul Ganjoo. “The live entertainment industry has outgrown that base, and the shortfall is now the single biggest constraint on what fans here get to experience.”

He says that while corporate naming rights are common in some international markets, capital investments into physical infrastructure are a positive sign for India’s developing ecosystem.

> “Seamless digital experiences have become an integral part of every concert”

“The industry needs more collaborations of the kind Airtel has committed to: brands from the wider ecosystem identifying where the foundations need investment, and putting capital behind closing the gap. That is what it will take to deliver world-class experiences to fans in India,” Ganjoo says.

The partnership is poised to usher in the “next phase of India’s live entertainment evolution”, the companies say, with connectivity for fans and organisers an increasingly important facet of the experiential economy.

“As live entertainment continues to evolve in India, seamless digital experiences have become an integral part of every concert – from sharing moments in real time and making digital payments to simply staying connected with friends and family,” says Airtel chief customer officer Suchita Salwan.

Planned network upgrades will enable attendees to utilise digital payments, livestream shows, access mobile ordering, and enjoy cellular coverage. They are scheduled to be deployed ahead of the next concert season.

Opening less than two years ago, the large-scale space was “built to international production standards” by Go-Golf Entertainment and boasts a modular main stage, roof truss and IMAG extensions, on-site box office, artist village, ample parking, and dedicated service roads.

> Airtel’s alliance with District is also expected to be the “first of several” partnerships

In its brief history, the venue has hosted shows by Arijit Singh, Anirudh Ravichander, and Bryan Adams.

Airtel’s alliance with District is also expected to be the “first of several” partnerships lined up for the venue’s expansion of what it can “offer to fans, artists and the wider industry”, the companies say.

Bengaluru is one of India’s largest metropolitan cities and is an increasingly appealing destination for international tours, making investment in its live entertainment infrastructure a significant step forward.

Earlier this year, BookMyShow’s Ashish Hemrajani described India as a rapidly growing “ [anchor market](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/emerging-touring-models-making-india-an-anchor-market-bookmyshow-says/) ” for international acts, reflecting the country’s expanding importance on the global touring circuit.

Financial details were not disclosed.
