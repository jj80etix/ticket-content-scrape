# Sam Fender Announces 2027 Stadium Tour

## Summary

Sam Fender announced his 2027 "Proper Massive Stadium Tour" across the UK and Ireland, including Wembley and a Rock Werchter headline slot.

### Key Points
- Tour opens June 3 and 5 at Newcastle's St. James' Park, Fender's hometown venue.
- Further stops include Cardiff's Principality Stadium, Wembley (June 18, 20), Dublin's Marlay Park, and Edinburgh's Murrayfield.
- Headlines Belgium's Rock Werchter festival July 3; closes with two Manchester Etihad Stadium shows July 15 and 17.
- Wunderhorse support all eight UK stadium dates; follows Fender's Mercury Prize-winning No. 1 album "People Watching."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sam-Fender-2027-Tour.webp)

Sam Fender has announced his Proper Massive Stadium Tour for 2027, with dates across the United Kingdom and Ireland.

The tour opens June 3 and June 5 at Newcastle’s St. James’ Park. Following the opener, Fender will visit Cardiff’s Principality Stadium before playing Wembley on June 18 and June 20.

July appearances include Dublin’s Marlay Park and Edinburgh’s Scottish Gas Murrayfield Stadium. Fender is also scheduled to headline Belgium’s Rock Werchter festival on July 3. The tour is slated to wrap up with two shows at Manchester’s Etihad Stadium on July 15 and 17.

Wunderhorse will support all eight UK stadium performances.
