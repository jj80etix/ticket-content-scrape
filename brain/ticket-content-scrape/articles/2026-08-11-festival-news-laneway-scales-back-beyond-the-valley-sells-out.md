# Festival news: Laneway scales back & Beyond The Valley sells out

## Summary

A roundup of Australian, US, and UK festival programming news: Australia's Laneway is trimming its 2027 run to four cities (dropping Adelaide and Perth) citing logistical strain, while Destroy All Lines' Good Things Festival and Untitled Group's Beyond The Valley (a second consecutive sell-out) both report strong lineups and demand. In the US, Atlanta's ONE Musicfest is pausing for 2027 after 16 years, and Utah's debut Yours Always festival was quietly cancelled with refunds issued. In the UK, Long Road Festival recruited Ronnie Wood to replace a cancelling headliner, and Bloodstock announced its 2027 lineup.

### Key Points

- Laneway is cutting Adelaide and Perth from its 2027 route, reducing to four cities (Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Auckland) due to logistical demands of touring Australia's geography.
- Good Things Festival (Destroy All Lines) will tour three Australian cities Dec. 4-6, 2026 with acts including Limp Bizkit, Simple Plan, and Bad Religion; presales open Aug. 18.
- Beyond The Valley (Untitled Group) sold out at presale for its 11th edition, a second consecutive sell-out, headlined by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, and The Veronicas.
- Atlanta's ONE Musicfest is pausing for 2027 after 16 years of operation; organizers frame it as a reset rather than a permanent end.
- Utah debut festival Yours Always was cancelled ahead of its October debut, with refunds issued to ticketholders and no formal explanation given.
- UK's Long Road Festival added Ronnie Wood to replace a cancelled headline slot, and Bloodstock announced 27 acts for its 2027 edition.

## Transcript

Elsewhere in the world, two US festivals have been called off, while a UK festival is already looking ahead to 2027

Australia’s festival announcements are picking up speed ahead of the country’s festival season, with both sell-out successes and scale-backs for the vast marketplace.

Long-standing touring event [**Laneway**](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/01/laneway-at-21-tastemaking-instinct-and-chappell-roan/) is scaling back its stops for its next edition, cutting Adelaide and Perth from its 2027 run.

The Queensland-based event will now visit four cities – Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, and Auckland – with dates and a lineup to be announced. Organisers pointed to the logistical demands of taking a major event across the vast landmass as part of its rationale.

“Running so many shows, so close together, across so much ground is a logistical feat we’re proud of, but it also asks a lot of our lineup and crew,” they said online. “Taking a pause allows us to focus on making the festival the best it can be, while freeing up time for more artists to bring headline shows to cities beyond our festival stops.”

Meanwhile, Destroy All Lines’ **Good Things Festival** is set to bring the likes of Limp Bizkit, Simple Plan, Spiritbox, Polaris, The All-American Rejects, and Bad Religion to three Australian cities later this year.

The touring event is set to hit Melbourne’s Flemington Racecourse, Sydney Showgrounds, and Brisbane Showgrounds from 4-6 December this year, with presale tickets starting next week (18 August).

And Untitled Group’s **Beyond The Valley** sold out at presale for its 11th edition, marking its second consecutive sell-out.

The Victoria festival, set for Barunah Plains, will be headlined by John Summit, Black Eyed Peas, The Veronicas, and Vince Staples, and will also feature Skepta, Ocean Alley, Overmono, Hannah Laing, Disco Lines, Nia Archives, and many more.

> “To sell out for a second consecutive year is extraordinary and something we never take for granted”

“We feel grateful to the amazing BTV community who have grown with us over the past decade – they continue to bring their passion and energy year after year,” says Nicholas Greco, Untitled’s co-founder and managing partner. “To sell out for a second consecutive year is extraordinary and something we never take for granted.

“It’s incredibly rewarding to see audiences continue to embrace a lineup that celebrates both global stars and Australia’s outstanding local talent,” he concludes.

Over in the US, two festivals have called off editions this year.

Atlanta’s **ONE Musicfest** will take a year off in 2027 after 16 years in operation.

In its history, the urban music festival has welcomed the likes of Doechii, Kendrick Lamar, The Roots, Future, Megan Thee Stallion, and Janet Jackson.

“We’ve pushed culture forward, brought generations together, and built something that didn’t exist before we created it,” said organisers online. “Starting with 1,500 of you at our very first festival, and growing into a homecoming for 100,000+ every year since.

“That’s why we’re pausing ONE Musicfest in 2026. Not stepping back, but building forward. This year is about reimagining what’s next: new experiences, new energy, new ideas worthy of everything this community has built together,” they say.

And a two-day October festival in Utah, **Yours Always**, was quietly called off ahead of its debut at Salt Lake City’s Utah State Fairpark.

It was due to feature headliners Benson Boone and Sombr, alongside a lineup that included Stephen Sanchez, Labrinth, Joshua Bassett, Daniel Seavey, Bella Kay, and BØRNS.

Organisers have not issued a formal explanation for the cancellation, but have issued refunds to ticketholders.

> “This year is about reimagining what’s next”

And across the pond, **Long Road Festival** has called up legendary guitarist Ronnie Wood to fill a headliner slot left by Bailey Zimmerman [cancelling](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/tour-news-megadeth-expands-farewell-outing-with-eu-uk-dates/) his EU/UK tour.

Wood and his band will be joined by Irish star Imelda May to close out the main stage of the leading country, roots & Americana festival, which is set for 27-30 August in Leicestershire. He joins a pool of artists that include Emmylou. Harris, Maren Morris, Steve Earle, The Shires, Shaky Graves, Josh Ross, and The 502s.

He will pull from a life of music that includes time with The Rolling Stones, Faces, Jeff Beck Group, The Birds, The Creation, Rod Stewart, Ronnie Lane, alongside his solo work.

Off the back of this year’s event, **Bloodstock** has announced 27 names for 2027, including headliners Mercyful Fate, Electric Callboy, and Motionless In White.

Acid Bath, Green Lung, Children of Bodom, Madball, Gaerea, Kanonenfieber, Dethklok, Devildriver, and many more are also confirmed for the metal and rock event, which will take place at Catton Park, Derbyshire from 5-8 August 2027.
