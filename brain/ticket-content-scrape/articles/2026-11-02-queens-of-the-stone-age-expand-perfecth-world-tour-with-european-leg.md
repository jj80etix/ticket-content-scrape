# Queens of the Stone Age Expand ‘Perfecth World Tour’ With European Leg

## Summary

Queens of the Stone Age added a nine-date European leg (June 2027) to their "Perfecth World Tour," running from Hanover to Pula, Croatia, alongside their previously announced North American arena run with Pixies.

### Key Points
- European leg: June 13 (Hanover) through June 30 (Pula, Croatia), including stops in France, Spain, and Italy.
- Dutch multi-instrumentalist ZEP will support the European dates; general tickets on sale Nov. 6.
- North American leg runs May 1 (San Diego) to May 24 (Boston), with Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp.
- Full itinerary also includes Asia-Pacific dates in Nov/Dec 2026 and South American festival stops in March 2027.

## Transcript

Queens of the Stone Age have added nine European concerts to their 2027 “Perfecth World Tour,” extending their schedule with a June run through Germany, France, Spain, Italy and Croatia.

The newly announced leg begins June 13 at Gilde Parkbühne in Hanover, Germany, and closes June 30 at Pula Arena in Croatia. After opening in Hanover, the band will head to France for a June 15 concert at Arkéa Arena in Bordeaux.

Three Spanish appearances follow, beginning June 16 at Bilbao Arena. Queens of the Stone Age will then play Icónica Sevilla on June 20 and Roig Arena in Valencia on June 21. The group then returns to France on June 23 for Festival de Nîmes before traveling to Italy for a June 27 performance at Parco San Valentino in Pordenone. A June 28 appearance at Munich’s Tollwood Festival precedes the closing concert in Pula.

Dutch multi-instrumentalist ZEP will support the European run. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin November 6 at 10 a.m. local time.

The European performances follow a previously announced North American arena tour, consisting of 16 U.S. performances. It opens May 1 at Viejas Arena in San Diego and concludes May 24 at TD Garden in Boston. Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp are scheduled to appear throughout that portion of the tour.

The U.S. routing includes concerts in Inglewood, Oakland, Sacramento, Seattle and Portland before moving east. Additional stops include Salt Lake City, Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, Saint Paul, Milwaukee and Rosemont, Illinois. The final stretch takes the band through Indianapolis, Columbus, Pittsburgh and Detroit before the Boston finale.
