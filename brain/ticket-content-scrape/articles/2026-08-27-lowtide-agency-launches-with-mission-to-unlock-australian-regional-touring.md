# Lowtide agency launches with mission to unlock Australian regional touring

## Summary

New Australian booking agency Lowtide has launched with a mission to unlock touring potential in the country's regional towns, aiming to supplement the established East Coast city circuit with 20-30 date regional routes.

### Key Points
- Founded by former Lonely Lands Agency general manager Harry Moore.
- Targets regional/commuter-town markets with existing venue infrastructure and strong demand, beyond the major city hubs.

Lowtide, a new Australian booking agency has launched, with its founder saying one of its targets is “unlocking” the touring potential in the country’s regional towns to supplement the established city circuit.
