# When We Were Young 2027 Headlined By Blink-182 And Pierce The Veil

## Summary

When We Were Young festival returns to the Las Vegas Festival Grounds for a fifth edition on Oct. 16, 2027, after a 2026 hiatus, headlined by Blink-182 and Pierce the Veil with Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, and dozens of pop-punk/emo acts across four stages. Presented by 7-Eleven and promoted by C3 Presents, registered fans get a presale Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. PT for lowest-price GA tickets, with GA+, VIP, and hotel packages also on sale.

### Key Points
- Fifth edition of the one-day festival, Oct. 16, 2027, Las Vegas Festival Grounds; 50+ acts across four stages.
- Headliners: Blink-182, Pierce the Veil; also Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, All Time Low, and others.
- Presale (guaranteed lowest GA price) Nov. 20, 10 a.m. PT via registration; general onsale follows at 11 a.m. PT if tickets remain.
- GA+ and VIP tiers add lounge access, dedicated entry, and amenities; hotel/ticket packages offered via Jampack.

After 2026’s hiatus, When We Were Young is headed back to the Las Vegas Festival Grounds in 2027 with a lineup topped by Blink-182 and Pierce The Veil. The one-day festival boasts tons of pop-punk, emo rock and a dose of nu-metal, among other genres with more than 50 performances across four stages.

Presented by 7-Eleven and promoted by C3 Presents, When We Were Young will mark its fifth edition on Oct. 16, 2027.  
  
The lineup includes Limp Bizkit, Third Eye Blind, The Used, All Time Low, Simple Plan, 5 Seconds of Summer, Circa Survive, Taking Back Sunday, Sleeping With Sirens, The Psychedelic Furs, Hot Mulligan, New Found Glory, Movements and more.  
  
A presale starts Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. PT, offering the guaranteed lowest price GA tickets.

Fans may sign up now to receive a passcode to the presale at WhenWeWereYoungFestival.com. If any tickets remain after the hour, the general onsale will follow at 11 a.m. PT.

GA+ and VIP tickets will also be available, along with VIP Cabana packages and official festival hotel and ticket packages from Jampack.  
  
GA+ tickets amenities include “access to a private lounge, air-conditioned restrooms, dedicated entry lane at main entrance and more,” according to today’s announcement from C3. VIP tickets take the experience to the next level with “preferred viewing areas across all stages and shaded seating in the VIP Lounges to relax and recharge, charging stations, dedicated festival merch tent, and more.”  
  
Visit [WhenWeWereYoungFestival.com](http://whenwewereyoungfestival.com) for more information.
