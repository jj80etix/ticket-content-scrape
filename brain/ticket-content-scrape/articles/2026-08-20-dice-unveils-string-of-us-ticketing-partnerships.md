# DICE unveils string of US ticketing partnerships

## Summary

Ticketing platform DICE announced five new US partnerships with independent venues and promoters — including Brooklyn's Saint Vitus Bar, Miami's Studio 60, and promoters ViViD Presents, Phase 3 Presents and The Originals — framing the deals as evidence of independent operators shifting toward "fan-first ticketing." The announcement comes roughly a year after DICE's acquisition by Fever, which it called a "record-breaking" period of growth.

### Key Points
- New venue partners: Saint Vitus Bar (Brooklyn, reopening in Bushwick) and Studio 60 (Miami, opened July 2026).
- New promoter partners: ViViD Presents (Seattle nightlife), Phase 3 Presents (Miami Music Week/Art Basel events), The Originals (HYDE Beach at SLS Miami).
- DICE pitches partners on no hidden fees, waiting lists, mobile-first anti-tout technology, and audience-insight/discovery tools via its app.
- Announcement follows the one-year mark since DICE's acquisition by Fever.

### Highlights
- DICE is explicitly positioning "fan-first ticketing" (transparent pricing, anti-bot tech, fan-to-fan resale) as a competitive differentiator to win independent venues/promoters.
- Growth is concentrated in electronic/dance and metal/alt scenes — a genre-specific expansion strategy.

### Industry Problem
Independent venues and promoters are actively seeking ticketing partners that promise transparent, no-hidden-fee pricing and built-in anti-scalping tools — signaling continued client-side dissatisfaction with legacy fee structures elsewhere in the market.

### Proposed Solution
Track DICE's fee-transparency and anti-tout feature set as a benchmark; an independent primary platform can compete for similar client segments by matching or exceeding DICE's "no hidden fees" positioning and audience-insight tooling.

## Transcript

The moves largely involve electronic music venues and promoters, with the company saying the deals show independents turning toward 'fan-first ticketing'

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Studio-60-Miami.png)

Studio 60 Miami © Studio 60 Miami

Ticketing platform DICE has announced a wave of five new partnerships with US-based independent venues, promoters, and festivals, saying the deals mark a “key milestone” in its growth in the national market.

The company has reached agreements with venues Saint Vitus Bar in Brooklyn and Miami’s Studio 60. It called Saint Vitus Bar “one of the most storied rooms in American live music”, saying the metal venue has hosted “era-defining shows”. Its deal with DICE comes as it prepares to reopen at a new location in Bushwick later this year. EDM venue Studio 60 opened this July in Miami’s Allapattah Entertainment District.

The promoters to sign partnerships with DICE are ViViD Presents, with the ticketer crediting the company with “spearheading the resurgence of Seattle nightlife” with immersive events focussed on melodic dance music, as well as Phase 3 Presents. The Miami-based firm was described by DICE as having produced “some of the most desired events in Miami during Miami Music Week, Art Basel, and beyond”.

> “These partnerships reflect a broader shift among independent, culturally driven operators toward fan-first ticketing”

The final partnership is with The Originals, producer of the HYDE Beach events at SLS Miami, with DICE saying “curates spaces, sounds, and environments at the forefront of nightlife culture”.

“The new partners span the breadth of US live culture, including heavy metal to melodic house, from grassroots rooms, to large-scale experiential production, with each occupying a distinct place in its city’s cultural fabric,” DICE said.

It comes shortly after the company marked [one year since its acquisition by technology company Fever](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/the-last-year-has-marked-a-turning-point-for-dice/), which it called a “record-breaking twelve months”.

“These partnerships reflect a broader shift among independent, culturally driven operators toward fan-first ticketing. With DICE prioritising developing trust between fans, venues, and promoters. Partnering with DICE offers partners fan-to-fan returns, waiting lists, no hidden fees, mobile-first anti-tout technology, audience insights and discovery through the app,” the ticketer said.
