# Live Nation reveals plans for South America’s largest indoor arena in São Paulo

## Summary

Live Nation has announced plans for a new 21,000-capacity purpose-built arena in São Paulo, Brazil, developed via a joint venture with the Cidade Center Norte district and set to open in 2028. The project is projected to generate significant regional economic impact and extends Live Nation's already substantial footprint in the Brazilian live entertainment market.

### Key Points
- 21,000-capacity São Paulo Arena announced; billed as set to become the largest indoor arena for live entertainment in South America.
- Joint venture between Live Nation and the Cidade Center Norte district; construction begins in H2 2026, with opening slated for 2028.
- Expected to host 200+ events annually; projected to generate ~$160M in attendee spending and $311M in total economic impact by its fifth year.
- Designed in-house by Live Nation's Blueprint Studio, with three seating levels, a dedicated VIP suite level, and climate control.
- Live Nation is already a leading promoter in Brazil (majority stakeholder in Rock in Rio and The Town/Rock World, producer of Lollapalooza Brasil) and recently signed a booking deal at São Paulo's Nubank Parque.
- A separate, earlier Live Nation/Oak View Group/GL Events arena proposal for Anhembi District (20,000-cap, first reported 2021) remains stalled and is distinct from this new venue.

## Transcript

## Live Nation reveals plans for South America’s largest indoor arena in São Paulo

The 21,000-capacity venue is set to open in 2028 within the Cidade Center Norte district

![São Paulo Arena rendering](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Sao-Paulo-Arena-rendering-©-Live-Nation.png)

São Paulo Arena rendering © Live Nation

Live Nation has announced plans to develop Brazil’s first purpose-built music venue, the 21,000-capacity São Paulo Arena, which will reportedly become the largest indoor arena for live entertainment in South America.

The São Paulo Arena will be located within the Cidade Center Norte district, Brazil’s most comprehensive planned urban living neighbourhood. The venue is being developed through a joint venture with the district, and is poised to create a leading entertainment and cultural hub.

Construction on the multi-purpose venue is expected to begin in the second half of 2026, with the arena scheduled to open in 2028. Once operational, the arena is expected to host over 200 events annually.

The venue is projected to generate around $160 million (€139m) in attendee spending, and more than $311m (€270m) in total economic impact by its fifth year of operation.

“São Paulo deserves an arena that reflects the energy, passion and cultural influence of one of the world’s great cities,” says Michael Rapino, Live Nation’s president/CEO. “São Paulo Arena will be the new home for Brazilian and international artists and the fans who make this city one of the most exciting live entertainment markets. We hope this arena will be a source of pride for the city and a destination for unforgettable live experiences.”

Cidade Center Norte’s CEO, Flavio Fernandes, adds: “São Paulo Arena is a dream project coming to life, and we are proud to partner with Live Nation to bring Brazil’s first world-class arena to our neighbourhood.

> “São Paulo Arena is a dream project coming to life”

“It reinforces Cidade Center Norte’s long-term commitment to São Paulo’s North Zone, adding a world-class live entertainment venue to a district that integrates malls, a center of exhibitions and events, hospitality, residential living, commercial, education and healthcare hubs,” he adds.

Live Nation’s in-house design and development group, Blueprint Studio, will design the venue. Designed with both fans and artists experience in mind, the arena will feature three seating levels, a dedicated VIP suite level, and superior acoustics to enhance every performance while minimising external noise impact. Plus, it will be climate-controlled with high accessibility and sustainability standards.

The international entertainment leader is one of the leading promoters in Brazil, bringing the likes of Madonna, Coldplay, Bruno Mars, Shakira, and The Weeknd to the country. It also recently signed a booking deal with [São Paulo’s Nubank Parque](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/05/live-nation-pens-booking-deal-at-brazils-nubank-parque/). Beyond concerts, Live Nation is also a majority stakeholder in Rock in Rio and The Town creator/producer Rock World, and is also the producer of Lollapalooza Brasil.

Back in 2021, Live Nation was reportedly working with Oak View Group (OVG) and GL events on the 20,000-capacity [Arena São Paulo](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2021/10/live-nation-ovg-gl-events-sao-paulo-arena/), which was set to land in the city’s Anhembi District entertainment district as part of a redevelopment effort. It was slated to open in 2024, but the project’s status remains unclear.

The Anhembi District is roughly 4 kilometres west of Cidade Center Norte in Brazil’s financial center, which is home to over 21 million people in the metropolitan area.
