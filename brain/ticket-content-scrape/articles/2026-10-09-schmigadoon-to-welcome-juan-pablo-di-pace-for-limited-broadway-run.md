# ‘Schmigadoon!’ to Welcome Juan Pablo Di Pace for Limited Broadway Run

## Summary

Juan Pablo Di Pace will make his Broadway debut in "Schmigadoon!" at the Nederlander Theatre, covering the role of Doc for a four-week stretch (Oct 24–Nov 22) while original cast member Ivan Hernandez is on leave. The show, adapted from the Apple TV series, won four 2026 Tony Awards including Best Musical. Broadway tickets are on sale through April 4, 2027, and a North American tour launches September 2027 in Baltimore.

### Key Points
- Di Pace (known for Netflix's "Fuller House") takes over as Doc Oct 24–Nov 22; Hernandez returns Nov 24.
- "Schmigadoon!" won Best Musical, Best Book, Best Original Score and Best Orchestrations at the 2026 Tonys.
- Broadway run is booked through April 4, 2027.
- A North American tour starts September 2027 at Baltimore's Hippodrome Theatre, with stops including Chicago, LA and D.C.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Schmigadoon.webp)

Juan Pablo Di Pace will make his Broadway debut in “Schmigadoon!” this fall, joining the musical at the Nederlander Theatre for a limited engagement from October 24 through November 22.

The actor, known for playing Fernando on Netflix’s “Fuller House,” will take over the role of Doc while original Broadway cast member Ivan Hernandez takes a scheduled four-week leave. Hernandez is set to resume performances on November 24.

Alex Brightman and Sara Chase lead the production as Josh and Melissa. The company also includes Jennifer Simard as Mildred Layton, Ann Harada as Florence Menlove, Brad Oscar as Mayor Menlove and Isabelle McCalla as Emma Tate. Max Clayton plays Danny Bailey, with McKenzie Kurtz as Betsy, Ayaan Diop as Carson and Maulik Pancholy as the Reverend.

Christopher Gattelli directs and choreographs the production, which features a book and score by Cinco Paul. Doug Besterman and Mike Morris provide orchestrations, with music supervision and arrangements by David Chase and music direction by Steven Malone.

“Schmigadoon!” adapts the Apple television series for the stage, following New York doctors Josh Skinner and Melissa Gimble as they attempt to repair their relationship during a backpacking retreat.

The musical opened on Broadway on April 20 and won four 2026 Tony Awards: Best Musical, Best Book of a Musical, Best Original Score and Best Orchestrations.

Tickets are currently on sale for Broadway performances through April 4, 2027. A North American tour is scheduled to launch in September 2027 at Baltimore’s Hippodrome Theatre, with engagements planned in cities including Chicago, Los Angeles and Washington, D.C.
