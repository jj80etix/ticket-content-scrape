# Lake Street Dive Announce 2027 Tour

## Summary

Lake Street Dive announced the first leg of a 2027 North American tour supporting their upcoming live-recorded album "What About Flamingos?," with 15 dates beginning Feb. 26 in Waukee, Iowa.

### Key Points
- 15-city tour begins Feb. 26, 2027 (Waukee, IA); includes Nashville (Mar. 5) and Brooklyn Paramount (Mar. 12).
- Album "What About Flamingos?" (out Feb. 19) is the band's first self-released record, recorded live in one room.
- Presale/VIP begins Oct. 28; general onsale Oct. 30.

Lake Street Dive announced the first leg of their 2027 North American tour in support of the upcoming album *What About Flamingos?*.

The 15-city run begins Feb. 26 in Waukee, Iowa, with dates in Nashville at The Truth on March 5 and Brooklyn Paramount on March 12. Special guest Snacktime will open every night except Greenville, South Carolina.

Artist pre-sale tickets and VIP package sales begin Oct. 28 at 10:00 AM local time. All tickets will be available with the general on-sale Oct. 30 at 10:00 AM local.

*What About Flamingos?* is out Feb. 19 and available for pre-order on vinyl and CD. This is the first release on the band’s own Dive Not Drive Records. A band of friends for 22 years, the New York City-based group includes lead vocalist Rachael Price, drummer Mike Calabrese, keyboardist Akie Bermiss and guitarist James Cornelison.

Inspired by appeals for a live album from the fan base, Lake Street Dive recorded *What About Flamingos?* live in a room together – a first for the band.

“When we set out to record this album, our focus was simple: to play live in a room together – human and alive and perfect in its imperfection,” stated Lake Street Dive in the tour announcement. “We arrived without knowing what songs the others had been writing, with an open invitation to bring anything to the session. Each of us brought our own stories, loves, losses and philosophies. As the album came together, a common thread.”

The record was produced by two different producers on two coasts: Philip Weinrobe (Adrianne Lenker, Big Thief, Cass McCombs) at Spillway Sounds in West Hurley, New York, and Jonathan Wilson (Father John Misty, Angel Olsen, Dawes, Margo Price) at his Topanga Canyon Five Star Studios in Los Angeles. Over the summer, before the album was announced, Lake Street Dive released the first single, “You’re Never Around.” The song has 1.5 million streams to date, with 2 million views across platforms.
