# VenuWorks Names Michael Silva President And Chief Operating Officer As Founder Steve Peters Prepares For 2028 Retirement

## Summary

VenuWorks named Michael Silva President and COO effective September 7, 2026, expanding his role as part of a planned leadership transition ahead of founder/CEO Steve Peters' March 2028 retirement. Silva, VenuWorks' COO since April 2024, will run day-to-day operations while Peters focuses on long-term strategy and key relationships.

### Key Points
- Silva's expanded remit: corporate departments, operational/financial performance, client retention, service delivery, and new business development.
- Peters retains long-term vision, strategic planning, client/industry relationships and mentoring the leadership team through his 2028 retirement.
- As COO, Silva previously led risk-management/OSHA compliance initiatives and employee-ownership programs.
- VenuWorks is structured as an employee-owned company.

## Transcript

![Michael Silva Paramount Theatre Photo](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Michael-Silva-Paramount-Theatre-Photo.png)

Michael Silva (Courtesy VenuWorks)

  
VenuWorks has appointed Michael Silva President and Chief Operating Officer, effective Sept. 7, 2026, and as part of a planned leadership transition as founder and CEO Steve Peters focuses on long-term strategy ahead of his March 2028 retirement.

Silva has served as the company’s Chief Operating Officer since April 2024.

As President and Chief Operating Officer, Silva will lead the day-to-day operations of VenuWorks, with responsibilities including oversight of corporate departments, operational and financial performance, client retention, service delivery, organizational systems and new business development.

Peters will continue to guide VenuWorks’ long-term vision and strategic planning while focusing on key client and industry relationships, contract retention, external partnerships and mentoring the company’s leadership team.

“Mike has been instrumental in keeping our organization moving forward, strengthening our safety and risk management practices, and advancing strategies that support the long-term growth and stability of VenuWorks,” Peters said in the announcement. “This expanded role recognizes the leadership he is already providing and allows me to concentrate on the company’s long-term direction, our most important relationships and a successful transition through my planned retirement in 2028. I have tremendous confidence in Mike, our leadership team and our employee-owners. VenuWorks is in excellent hands.”

As Chief Operating Officer, Silva directed corporate services supporting operations, food and beverage, and client retention across VenuWorks’ managed portfolio. He also participated in new-business pursuits and the onboarding of new management accounts, strengthened the company’s risk-management and OSHA compliance programs, and introduced new initiatives supporting VenuWorks’ employee-ownership culture, according to the announcement.

“I am honored to take on this role and lead the day-to-day operations of VenuWorks,” Silva said. “Our greatest strength is our employee-owners, both at our corporate office and in venues across the country. They bring experience, initiative and a personal commitment to every client partnership. I look forward to working alongside them to strengthen those long-term relationships, pursue thoughtful growth and deliver the transparent, accountable and first-class service our clients and communities expect.”

Silva joined the VenuWorks corporate leadership team after serving as Executive Director of the company’s Cedar Rapids, Iowa, portfolio, where he oversaw the Alliant Energy PowerHouse, Paramount Theatre, McGrath Amphitheatre, ImOn Ice Arena and the Cedar Rapids Tourism Office.

Earlier in his career with VenuWorks, Michael served as Interim Executive Director of the Topeka Performing Arts Center and General Manager of the California University of Pennsylvania Convocation Center. He also held management positions at Cambria County War Memorial Arena, Petersen Events Center at the University of Pittsburgh and at Nationwide Arena in Columbus, Ohio.

Peters founded the company in 1996 and over the past 30 years has led VenuWorks’ growth into a nationally recognized venue management company serving 29 communities across the United States.

Peters began his venue management career in 1976 and later served as the opening General Manager of Target Center in Minneapolis and as a Vice President with Ogden Entertainment. A past president of the International Association of Venue Managers, he has received the association’s Charles A. McElravy Award for lifetime achievement and the IAVM Foundation Legacy Award.
