# World Darts Championship tickets to be allocated by ballot for the first time

## Summary

The Paddy Power World Darts Championship will allocate tickets via a ballot for the first time in its history, as the event moves from the West Hall to the larger Great Hall at Alexandra Palace and expands capacity to 180,000 across its three-week run (Dec 11–Jan 3, 2026-27). The ballot runs in two stages: a priority window for PDCTV annual subscribers (Sept 1–7), followed by a general public ballot (Sept 8–14). Organizers expect no remaining inventory after the ballot closes given demand.

### Key Points
- First-ever ballot allocation for World Darts Championship tickets, replacing (or supplementing) standard on-sale, as the event moves to Alexandra Palace's Great Hall.
- Total tournament capacity increases to 180,000 across the three-week 2026-27 event (Dec 11–Jan 3).
- Two-stage ballot: PDCTV annual-member priority window (must hold active subscription by 23:59 BST Aug 30; ballot open Sept 1–7, one entry per member), then general public ballot Sept 8–14.
- All applicants are notified by email in September regardless of outcome; organizers say demand will likely exhaust inventory entirely via the ballot.
- The venue deal with the Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) runs through at least 2031, announced in December of the prior year alongside the capacity expansion.

### Highlights
- Ballot (lottery-style allocation) replaces first-come queue/on-sale mechanics for a high-demand, capacity-constrained event (180,000 tickets over three weeks).
- Two-tier eligibility structure (paid-subscriber priority window vs. general public) is used to manage demand ahead of a fixed on-sale moment.
- Organizers explicitly frame this as a response to demand that would otherwise overwhelm a standard on-sale ("unlikely to be any remaining ticket inventory following the ballot process").

### Industry Problem
This is a queue-meltdown/high-demand-onsale scalability case: when demand vastly exceeds inventory, a traditional first-come on-sale creates bot pressure, site strain, and a poor fan experience, pushing organizers toward randomized ballot/lottery allocation instead.

### Proposed Solution
The event's solution is a two-stage ballot (loyalty-tier priority window + general lottery) rather than a live queue-based on-sale. Etix could build or improve a native ballot/lottery on-sale mode (with tiered priority windows for members/subscribers) as an alternative fulfillment path for its highest-demand client events, reducing bot incentives and server load compared to a traditional timed on-sale.

**Tickets for the Paddy Power World Darts Championship will be accessed via a ballot for the first time, with the event’s capacity to increase for the 2026-27 edition as it moves into the Great Hall at Alexandra Palace.**

The London venue will continue to host the World Darts Championship until at least 2031, after a deal was struck with the Professional Darts Corporation (PDC) in December [last year](https://www.thestadiumbusiness.com/2025/12/04/capacity-to-increase-as-ally-pally-extends-world-darts-championship-deal/). The renewal was announced alongside plans to move from the West Hall to the Great Hall, increasing the capacity across the three-week tournament to 180,000.

Now, organisers have announced that tickets will be allocated through a ballot for the first time in the event’s history.

The ballot will take place in two stages, with PDCTV annual members receiving an exclusive priority window ahead of the general ballot.

Members must have an active subscription in place by 23:59 BST on August 30, with the PDCTV annual members ballot open from September 1-7. One ballot application is permitted per member. Anyone that secures a subscription after this deadline will only be eligible for the general ballot.

The general ballot will then open at 12:00 BST on September 8, closing at 23:59 on September 14. It is open to all fans.

Once the ballot has closed, all applicants will be notified by email during September to confirm the outcome of their application, regardless of whether they have been successful or not.

Organisers added that due to demand, there is unlikely to be any remaining ticket inventory following the ballot process.

The 2026-27 World Darts Championship will take place at Alexandra Palace from December 11 to January 3.
