# Father’s Legacy, Son’s Band: Pink Floyd Show Created By Roger Waters To Tour North America & Europe

## Summary

AEG Presents and ICON LIVE announced a new touring production, "Roger Waters Presents LEGACY – A Pink Floyd Show," led by a band fronted by Waters' son Harry Waters. The tour launches February 2027 and will visit more than 100 theaters across North America and Europe, performing music from Pink Floyd's 1971-1979 era.

### Key Points
- Tour launches Feb. 15, 2027; over 100 North American and European theater dates.
- 10-piece band led by Harry Waters (organ, piano, vocals).
- Repertoire drawn from The Dark Side of the Moon, Wish You Were Here, Animals, and The Wall.
- Staging features visuals created by Roger Waters, referencing the original Pink Floyd artwork.

## Transcript

This photo was taken at London’s Earls Court during the famous Wall performances in 1980, when Roger Waters was 37 and Harry was 3.

AEG Presents and ICON LIVE announced Roger Waters Presents LEGACY – A Pink Floyd Show, a new touring live production created under the direction of Roger Waters with a band led by his son Harry Waters, a longtime touring musician, pianist, and composer.

An extensive tour will launch Feb. 15, 2027, and visit theaters in more than 100 cities in North America and Europe. The show celebrates the defining music and visual world of Pink Floyd’s golden era from 1971-1979.

Harry Waters will lead a 10-piece band performing selections from *The Dark Side of the Moon*, *Wish You Were Here*, *Animals*, and *The Wall*. He’ll perform on B3 organ, piano, and vocals.

The new stage presentation features a pictorial retrospective of stunning imagery inspired by the original [Pink Floyd](https://pollstar.com/artists/pink-floyd-15919) work, with visuals that Roger Waters created during his tenure as the creative force in Pink Floyd, all presented on hi-def LED video screens.

Tickets for the North American tour dates go on sale Oct. 2. European dates will be announced soon.

Roger Waters commented, “These songs have meant so much to so many people for so many years, and I’m excited for audiences to come together and experience them live again. Harry has assembled a terrific band, and I can’t wait for fans around the world to see the special show we’ve created together.”

Harry Waters added, “I fell in love with the music immediately. It really had an impact on me, irrespective of any bias because it was Dad’s music. I still love it deeply to this day. Collaborating with Dad on this new production is such an honor, and I’m so grateful to have the opportunity to bring this music to fans old and new, around the world.”

Harry Waters’ connection to the music began long before he became a professional musician. As a child, his voice appeared on Pink Floyd’s The Wall on “Goodbye Blue Sky,” and he recalls witnessing the original The Wall production while still very young. He later spent more than 15 years performing alongside his father on major tours, including “The Dark Side of the Moon” and “The Wall,” as well as the 2016 Desert Trip concerts.

Musically, the production aims to recreate the original recordings as closely as possible. “We are going to authentically recreate the albums as close to note-for-note as we possibly can,” Harry Waters said, adding that LEGACY was intended to reconnect audiences with the ideas at the heart of the music.

The show spanning more than two hours reaches deep into Pink Floyd’s catalog. Harry Waters and drummer Graham Broad shaped the set list together, paying tribute to the best loved music from the golden era of Pink Floyd, and building an intentional arc across the evening. For Harry Waters, some selections carry particular meaning. He calls Animals his favorite Pink Floyd album and “Shine On You Crazy Diamond” his favorite song to play.

The search for someone to recreate Roger Water’s share of the Pink Floyd lead vocals drew extraordinary global interest. Both father and son launched a worldwide online talent search to find that voice. Harry Waters said he personally listened to more than 3,000 audition submissions before the role was selected. Ultimately, Clifton David Broadbridge emerged as the successful candidate. Originally from Canada and now based in Nashville, Broadbridge is an accomplished vocalist who secured his place in the band following an in-person audition with Harry Waters.
