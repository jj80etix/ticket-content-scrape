# Barry Manilow Cancels Pair of Las Vegas Residency Shows Amid ‘Technical, Vocal’ Problems

## Summary

Barry Manilow canceled a pair of Las Vegas residency shows at Westgate Las Vegas after technical issues halted an Aug. 20 performance roughly 40 minutes in, citing ongoing "technical and vocal problems." Tickets will be refunded at point of purchase.

### Key Points
- Follows lung cancer surgery last year and a string of recent postponements/cancellations, including a last-minute Kentucky show cancellation earlier this month.
- Manilow, 83, is still scheduled to perform Aug. 27-29 and additional dates through December.
- No specific technical cause was disclosed.

## Transcript

![Barry Manilow (image via ScoreBig)](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Barry-Manilow.jpg)

Barry Manilow (image via ScoreBig)

Barry Manilow has faced a slew of postponements and cancellations over the past few months following lung cancer surgery last year.

The 83-year-old was performing his “The Hits Come Home” residency at Westgate Las Vegas on Thursday, August 20 when technical issues halted the show about 40 minutes into the performance, the *Las Vegas Review-Journal* reported. Then, the following day, Manilow noted in an Instagram post that a pair of shows at the venue over the weekend would be called-off.

“We’re very sorry but we have to cancel tonight’s and tomorrow’s performances,” Manilow wrote. “Due to technical and vocal problems, we’re unable to put on our show.”

No specific technical issues were mentioned. Tickets will be refunded via point of purchase.
