Dutch DJ and music producer Natte Visstick has signed with WME for representation in North America, South America, Australia and Asia, the agency announced Thursday.

Visstick’s blend of hard techno, jumpstyle, industrial chaos and internet culture breaks boundaries of the electronic music scene. He is one of the prominent figures in the meme techno scene and brings a sense of humor to electronic music by incorporating internet culture, including memes and viral clips, into his tracks, resonating with millions of underground and mainstream music fans across the globe.

He has garnered nearly 3 million monthly listeners on Spotify and over 160 million independent streams. Nate Visstick, which is Dutch for wet fish sticks, broke out with the record “Visstick Gooi Die KK Kick,” which became a rave phenomenon, and “POLSKA JUMPSTYLE” was also a big hit with a presence on TikTok and more than 40 million streams on Spotify. Visstick’s track “CRUSH” became an international hit after being featured on the film “Babygirl,” which starred Nicole Kidman, introducing his music to new audiences across the globe.

Visstick launched the “4 THE PEOPLE” movement this year, which was built around community, rave culture, and emotional connection. He’s performed at major European festivals and events, including Tomorrowland, Verknipt Festival, Teletech Festival, Hive Festival, Free Your Mind Festival, Intents Festival, Rebirth Festival and Sunrise Festival.
