# Calvin Harris To Embark On North American Tour Next Spring

## Summary

Calvin Harris announced his first-ever North American headlining tour, an 18-date Live Nation-promoted arena run beginning April 1, 2027 in Boston and closing May 27 at the Kia Forum. Tickets route through Reserved by Spotify, an artist presale, and a general onsale beginning October 22.

### Key Points
- First North American headline tour; 18 arena dates, April 1-May 27, 2027.
- Stops include Barclays Center, United Center, and Rogers Arena.
- Reserved by Spotify presale (Oct. 19) precedes artist presale (Oct. 20) and general onsale (Oct. 22), all via Ticketmaster.
- Follows sold-out hometown shows in Glasgow (100K+ fans) and Ireland (210K+ tickets).
- Also booked for an Australian headline tour in 2027.

Fresh off his performance at Head Trip festival in Indio, California, Calvin Harris announced he’s returning to North American arenas next spring.

It marks the first time the Scottish DJ is headlining a North American tour. The 18-date Live Nation-promoted trek kicks off April 1at Boston’s TD Garden, and the routing includes stops at CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore, Brooklyn’s Barclays Center, Ball Centre in Montreal, Hamilton’s TD Coliseum, Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, Chicago’s United Center and Rogers Arena in Vancouver before wrapping up May 27 at the legendary Kia Forum in Inglewood, California.

Fans can sign up for access to the presale at [CalvinHarris.com](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/CalvinHarris/2027), and tickets will be available starting Oct. 19 via Reserved by Spotify, followed by the artist presale on Oct. 20 at noon local time. The general onsale begins Oct. 22 at noon local time at [CalvinHarris.com](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/CalvinHarris/2027).

Reserved by Spotify tickets are available to eligible U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers through Ticketmaster. Those fans will be notified by Spotify via the app and email, and they will have an opportunity to purchase up to two tickets.

It’s been a remarkable year for Harris, who performed in front of 100,000 fans across two sold-out shows in his hometown of Glasgow at Hampden Park. He followed that up with three sold-out concerts in Ireland, moving more than 210,000 across the U.K. and Ireland.

The DJ, who has more than 74 million monthly listeners on Spotify, will also be visiting Australia in 2027, and that headline tour sold more than 330,000 tickets across 11 dates.

Harris has also broken records in Latin America. The global superstar set a Guinness World Record in February when he performed in front of 1.6 million people in São Paulo, Brazil, setting the record for the largest attended DJ set by a single artist.

The artist’s two-night stand at Hampden Park in Glasgow in August grossed $8,842,480 off 95,306 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. Harris is booked by THE·TEAM’s Brent Smith and managed by Mark Gillespie of Three Six Zero.
