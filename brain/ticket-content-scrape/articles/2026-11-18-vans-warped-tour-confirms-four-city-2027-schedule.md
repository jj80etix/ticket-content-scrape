# Vans Warped Tour Confirms Four-City 2027 Schedule

## Summary

Vans Warped Tour confirmed its full 2027 four-city schedule — Washington D.C., Long Beach, Mexico City, and Orlando — dropping Montreal from the 2026 rotation as the revived festival enters its third consecutive year. Washington D.C. and Long Beach dates were previously announced with earlier presales; the full slate is now locked in.

### Key Points
- 2027 cities: Washington D.C., Long Beach, Mexico City, Orlando (Montreal dropped from 2026's five-city run).
- Washington D.C. return and Long Beach date were announced earlier in 2026 with their own presales.
- Festival enters its third consecutive year since reviving in 2025 for its 30th anniversary.
- Announcement follows the close of the 2026 Orlando stop.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Vans-Warped-Tour-2027.webp)

Vans Warped Tour has confirmed its full 2027 festival schedule, returning to Washington, D.C., Long Beach, Mexico City and Orlando for four two-day events as the revived festival prepares for its third consecutive year.

The announcement comes just days after the festival wrapped its 2026 season in Orlando and continues the momentum of its return in 2025, when Warped Tour celebrated its 30th anniversary with a new destination-based format.

The 2027 schedule includes four of the five cities that hosted Warped Tour in 2026, with Montreal absent from next year’s itinerary.

While the announcement establishes the complete 2027 schedule, some of the dates were previously confirmed. [Warped Tour announced its 2027 Washington, D.C., return](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/vans-warped-tour-announces-washington-d-c-return-in-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in June, with an initial ticket sale that month. The Long Beach event was announced in July, with presales held later that month.

Founded in 1995, Warped Tour became a major touring platform for punk, pop-punk, alternative rock and emerging artists before ending its traditional cross-country run in 2018. A limited anniversary edition followed in 2019 before the festival returned in 2025 with a new destination-based format.

The 2026 events featured performers including Jimmy Eat World, Simple Plan, All Time Low, The Used, New Found Glory and numerous other acts across the five cities.

No artists have been announced for the 2027 festival dates. Additional lineup announcements and event details are expected in the coming months.

Presales for the three U.S. stops are scheduled to begin Friday, November 20, at 9 a.m. PT/noon ET.
