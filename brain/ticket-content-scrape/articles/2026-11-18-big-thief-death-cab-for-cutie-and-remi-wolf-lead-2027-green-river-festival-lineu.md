# Big Thief, Death Cab for Cutie and Remi Wolf Lead 2027 Green River Festival Lineup

## Summary

Big Thief, Death Cab for Cutie, and Remi Wolf will headline the 2027 Green River Festival, a three-day, three-stage event June 18–20 at the Franklin County Fairgrounds in Greenfield, Massachusetts. Over 30 additional acts round out the initial lineup, with more artists to be announced.

### Key Points
- Headliners: Big Thief, Death Cab for Cutie, Remi Wolf.
- Three-day festival (June 18–20, 2027) across three stages at Franklin County Fairgrounds.
- Initial lineup includes Sylvan Esso, Built to Spill, Julia Jacklin, and 25+ other acts.
- Additional artists to be announced later; details via greenriverfestival.com.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Green-River-Festival.webp)

Big Thief, Death Cab for Cutie and Remi Wolf will headline the 2027 Green River Festival in Greenfield, Massachusetts. The three-day festival will take place June 18–20 at the Franklin County Fairgrounds, with performances across three stages.

The lineup includes 30 acts beyond the headliners. Sylvan Esso, Built to Spill, Julia Jacklin, Mon Rovîa and Slow Pulp are among those scheduled to perform, along with This Is Lorelei, Caroline Rose, Racing Mount Pleasant, Nick Shoulders, Friko and Jess Williamson.

The bill also features Son Rompe Pera, Ibibio Sound Machine, Hudson Freeman, Twen, W.I.T.C.H., Wishy, Annie & The Caldwells, Charlotte Cornfield and Brennan Wedl.

Sabine McCalla, Sam & Louise Sullivan, Star Moles, Saha Gnawa, Afrosound, Dogo du Togo, Jess Woodland, Orchestra Gold, Kotoko Brass and Lions & Legends round out the announcement.

Additional artists will be announced at a later date. For more information and to stay up-to-date on the latest details, festivalgoers can visit [greenriverfestival.com](http://greenriverfestival.com).
