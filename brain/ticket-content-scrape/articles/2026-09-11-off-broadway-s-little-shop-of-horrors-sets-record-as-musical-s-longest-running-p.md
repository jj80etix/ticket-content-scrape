# Off-Broadway’s ‘Little Shop of Horrors’ Sets Record as Musical’s Longest-Running Production

## Summary

The Off-Broadway revival of "Little Shop of Horrors" at the Westside Theatre has become the musical's longest-running production, surpassing the 2,210-performance run of the original 1982-87 Orpheum Theatre staging. The revival, which paused for the COVID-19 shutdown and resumed in September 2021, is marking the milestone with a month of anniversary events.

### Key Points
- Surpassed the 2,210-performance run of the original 1982 Orpheum Theatre production.
- Current revival began previews September 2019; resumed post-COVID in September 2021.
- Current cast includes Ethan Slater as Seymour and Betsy Wolfe as Audrey.
- Anniversary events (singalong, post-show discussions, themed partnerships) run through September, centered on "Little Shop Day" Sept. 21.

The current Off-Broadway revival of “Little Shop of Horrors” has become the longest-running production in the musical’s history.

The revival at the Westside Theatre has surpassed the 2,210-performance run of the show’s original Off-Broadway production. That staging opened at the Orpheum Theatre in 1982 and continued for five years, establishing “Little Shop of Horrors” as one of Off-Broadway’s biggest commercial successes.

The record-breaking revival began previews in September 2019 before officially opening the following month. Its run was interrupted by the COVID-19 shutdown, but performances resumed in September 2021. The production has since welcomed a rotating lineup of Broadway and Hollywood performers while remaining at the Westside Theatre.

The musical currently stars Ethan Slater as Seymour and Betsy Wolfe as Audrey. Claybourne Elder plays dentist Orin Scrivello, with Reg Rogers as Mr. Mushnik and Christian McQueen providing the voice of Audrey II.

Christine Wanda, Savannah Lee Birdsong and Morgan Ashley Bryant play Ronnette, Crystal and Chiffon, respectively. The company also includes Teddy Yudain, Weston Chandler Long, Mecca Hicks, Jeff Sears, Aveena Sawyer, Christopher Swan, Alloria Frayser, Chani Maisonet, Johnny Newcomb, Jon Hoche, Jonothon Lyons and Noel MacNeal.

The revival is directed by Michael Mayer and features music by Alan Menken and a book and lyrics by Howard Ashman.

Producers are marking the performance record with a series of events throughout September, timed around Little Shop Day on September 21.

A special singalong with members of the company will follow the September 15 performance. From September 22 through September 26, the theater will host post-show discussions examining the musical’s history, its characters and puppetry, and its place in Black theater history. Members of the current company and special guests are expected to participate.

The production has also partnered with Starbright Floral Design in Manhattan to transform the business into a real-life version of Mushnik & Son through the end of September. Other promotions include a limited-edition Playbill cover inspired by the original Orpheum production and a themed cookie at Schmackary’s.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [Off-Broadway’s “Little Shop of Horrors” website](https://littleshopnyc.com/#home).
