# Ariana Grande to ‘Take Step Back from Visibility’ After Tour, Withdraws from West End Show

## Summary

Ariana Grande announced she will step back from public visibility after her "Eternal Sunshine Tour" concludes in London on September 1, 2026, and has withdrawn from the West End revival of "Sunday in the Park with George," in which she was set to co-star alongside Jonathan Bailey. Her representatives cite the physical demands of touring and ongoing public scrutiny of her appearance.

### Key Points
- Eternal Sunshine Tour wraps Sept. 1, 2026 in London; her team says she will then take "a much-deserved break" from public-facing work.
- Grande has withdrawn from the West End production of "Sunday in the Park with George," previously announced with Jonathan Bailey.
- She had previously called the tour a possible "last hurrah" in a prior podcast appearance.
- She is next set to appear on screen in "Focker-in-Law," alongside Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro.

Ariana Grande is taking a step back from the limelight.

The pop icon and actress is currently performing on her “Eternal Sunshine Tour,” which is set to wrap-up in London on September 1. However, following the tour, fans will no longer see Grande consistently in the public eye, her representatives tell *[PEOPLE](https://people.com/ariana-grande-taking-step-back-after-tour-amid-public-health-scrutiny-rep-exclusive-12032395)*.

“Ariana will be taking a step back from visibility after she completes the Eternal Sunshine Tour,” Grande’s representative told the publication. “She looks forward to finishing the tour and ending it on a high note, both healthily and happily, and then taking a much-deserved break from public-facing work and appearances, which has led to endless, ongoing public scrutiny. This tour has been a beautiful experience for her. She loves her fans and has loved every minute of this tour so much.”

A source close to Grande told *[PEOPLE](https://people.com/ariana-grande-taking-step-back-after-tour-amid-public-health-scrutiny-rep-exclusive-12032395)* that Grande “performs a very physical show, and there is a lot of athleticism involved.”

Additionally, Grande will no longer be performing in the [West End revival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/ariana-grande-jonathan-bailey-reunite-for-west-end-sunday-in-the-park-with-george/) of Stephen Sondheim’s “Sunday in the Park with George,” where she was set to appear alongside *Wicked* costar Jonathan Bailey next year.

Grande previously warned fans that the “Eternal Sunshine Tour” [might be her last hurrah](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/ariana-grande-upcoming-tour-last-hurrah/). She spoke on the *Good Hang with Amy Poehler* podcast last year, noting that the tour is her only scheduled outing for the time being.

“I’m very excited to do this small tour,” she said on the podcast. “But I think it might not happen again for a long, long, long, long time.”

She added that “I’m going to give it my all, and it’s going to be beautiful, and I’m so grateful.”

“I think that’s why I’m doing it,” she said. “Because I’m like, ‘One last hurrah.’ For now.”

While Grande is widely known for her music, she previously shared in an interview with *Variety* that she plans to explore “different forms of art” over the next few years, and “I think acting is feeling like home right now.” Grande has previously starred on TV — in shows like  *Victorious* and *Sam & Cat*  — but she has recently gained critical acclaim for her starring role in  *Wicked* and its sequel *Wicked: For Good.*

She’s set to appear next on the screen in *Focker-in-Law*, the latest installment in the *Meet the Parents* franchise, where she’ll appear alongside stars Ben Stiller and Robert De Niro.
