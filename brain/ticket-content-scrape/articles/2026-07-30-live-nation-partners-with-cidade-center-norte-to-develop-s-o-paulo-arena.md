# Live Nation Partners With Cidade Center Norte To Develop São Paulo Arena

## Summary

Live Nation is partnering with developer Cidade Center Norte to build São Paulo Arena, a roughly 21,000-capacity venue and Brazil's first purpose-built arena to modern live-entertainment standards. Construction starts later in 2026 with an opening targeted for 2028, and the venue is projected to host 200+ concerts and sporting events annually. The deal extends Live Nation's decade-long push to grow its footprint across Latin America, building on its existing majority stake in Rock World (Rock in Rio, The Town, Lollapalooza Brazil).

### Key Points
- São Paulo Arena will have ~21,000 capacity, making it the largest indoor arena in South America, with three seating levels and a dedicated VIP suite tier.
- Live Nation's in-house Blueprint Studio is designing the venue; construction is slated to begin later in 2026 with a 2028 opening.
- Oxford Economics projects an annual economic impact of $311.2 million across Brazil by the arena's fifth year of operation.
- This revives Live Nation's earlier (2021) attempt to build a São Paulo arena with Oak View Group and GL Events, which stalled before breaking ground.
- The project sits within Live Nation's broader Latin American expansion strategy of building, renovating, and acquiring stakes in venues and companies across the region.

Live Nation is teaming up with Cidade Center Norte to develop an arena in São Paulo, Brazil, bringing more music acts and sporting events to Latin America’s largest city.

The arena will be the country’s first purpose-built arena designed to meet the modern standards of the live entertainment business. With a capacity of about 21,000, São Paulo Arena would be the largest indoor arena in South America.

Construction is expected to begin later this year, and the arena is scheduled to open in 2028. The cost of developing the arena was not revealed by Live Nation.

“São Paulo deserves an arena that reflects the energy, passion and cultural influence of one of the world’s great cities,” Michael Rapino, president and CEO of Live Nation Entertainment, said in a statement. “São Paulo Arena will be the new home for Brazilian and international artists and the fans who make this city one of the most exciting live entertainment markets. We hope this arena will be a source of pride for the city and a destination for unforgettable live experiences.”

The venue will be located within the Cidade Center Norte District, a planned urban living neighborhood. It is being designed by Blueprint Studio, Live Nation’s in-house design and development group, which has worked on other projects like Moody Center in Austin.

“São Paulo Arena is a dream project coming to life, and we are proud to partner with Live Nation to bring Brazil’s first world-class arena to our neighborhood,” said Flavio Fernandes, CEO of Cidade Center Norte. “It reinforces Cidade Center Norte’s long-term commitment to São Paulo’s North Zone, adding a world-class live entertainment venue to a district that integrates malls, a center of exhibitions and events, hospitality, residential living, commercial, education and healthcare hubs.”

The live entertainment giant plans to host over 200 concerts and sporting events each year, boosting the local economy and tourism. Oxford Economics expects the arena to generate an annual economic impact of $311.2 million across Brazil by its fifth year of operation.

São Paulo Arena will feature three seating levels and a dedicated VIP suite level. The venue will boast optimal acoustics while minimizing noise pollution. It is being built with sustainability in mind, and the project includes a 2,000-space parking garage. Accessibility is also being considered with dedicated rideshare and taxi zones as well as connections to public transportation and roadways.

Live Nation has been expanding its footprint in Latin America over the past decade, making deals to develop new buildings or renovate them as well as acquiring a stake in some of the region’s top buildings and companies.

São Paulo’s population exceeds 21 million, making it the largest city in Latin America and one of the most prominent music markets in the world. Live Nation had shown interest in developing an arena in the city in 2021 when it was announced that the entertainment company was partnering with Oak View Group, *Pollstar* ’s parent company, and GL Events to build a 20,000-capacity arena in São Paulo. But that project encountered some roadblocks and never broke ground.

The major promoter already has a presence in the country through Rock World. Live Nation owns a majority stake in the company that produces Rock in Rio, The Town and Lollapalooza Brazil.
