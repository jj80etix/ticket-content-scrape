# South Africa’s Delicious Festival breaks from Heritage Day tradition with sponsor change

## Summary

South Africa's Delicious Festival is moving off its traditional Heritage Day weekend slot to April 2027, as its 12-year headline sponsorship with broadcaster DStv ends and SABC steps in as the new official broadcast partner.

### Key Points
- Festival moves to April 10-11, 2027 at Kyalami Grand Prix Circuit, Midrand.
- Ends a 12-year sponsorship relationship with DStv; SABC becomes new broadcast partner.
