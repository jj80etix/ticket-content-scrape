# Ticketmaster Sport Names François Westcombe Managing Director for EMEA

## Summary

Ticketmaster Sport named François Westcombe (ex-IMG) as managing director for EMEA, succeeding Chris Gratton, as the unit continues expanding sports-ticketing deals across the region, including its first SafeTix deployment in the Gulf via the Arabian Gulf Cup partnership. Westcombe framed the opportunity around audience data, CRM integration, and more seamless purchase/entry experiences rather than ticket sales alone.

### Highlights
- First SafeTix (digital/anti-fraud ticketing) deployment in the Gulf region, via the Arabian Gulf Cup (Khaleeji 27) partnership.
- Other recent EMEA sport deals: 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup (Qatar), Dubai Racing, Go Ahead Eagles, 2026 Commonwealth Games.
- Westcombe's stated focus: audience data, CRM/membership-system integration, streamlined purchase and entry — positioning ticketing as a fan-relationship layer, not just transactions.
- Ticketmaster Sport now serves 100+ clients across 15 sports in EMEA.

### Industry Problem
Sports rights-holders increasingly want ticketing platforms to double as fan-data and CRM infrastructure, not just a point of sale — a capability gap for providers still focused on transaction throughput.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster is responding by deepening CRM/membership integrations and extending its SafeTix anti-fraud tech into new regions; Etix could track whether comparable audience-data and CRM-integration features are worth building for its own primary-platform clients, particularly sports/venue clients likely to make similar asks.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Francois-Westcombe-Ticketmaster-Sport.webp)

Ticketmaster Sport has appointed François Westcombe as managing director for Europe, the Middle East and Africa (EMEA). Westcombe succeeds Chris Gratton, who has left the company to pursue other opportunities.

Westcombe joins Ticketmaster Sport from IMG, where he served as vice president of corporate development, digital, helping lead the growth and international expansion of its digital business.

He brings more than 11 years of experience in the sports industry, having previously held roles at Seven League, Mailman and IMG. During that time, he worked with major sports organizations including FIFA, UEFA, the Bundesliga, the NFL, MLB, FIBA, Concacaf and the International Olympic Committee.

The appointment comes as Ticketmaster Sport continues to expand its operations across EMEA. The company currently works with more than 100 clients across 15 sports, including organizations such as the NFL, Premier League and RFL Championship.

Recent business developments include ticketing partnerships for the 2027 FIBA Basketball World Cup in Qatar, Dubai Racing, Dutch football club Go Ahead Eagles and the Arabian Gulf Cup (Khaleeji 27). Ticketmaster also supported the 2026 Commonwealth Games.

The Arabian Gulf Cup partnership will include Ticketmaster’s first deployment of its SafeTix digital ticketing technology in the Gulf region.

“Sport is one of the clearest growth stories in our EMEA business, and François brings exactly the perspective we need for the next phase,” said Andrew Parsons, regional vice president of Ticketmaster EMEA. “Chris has built Ticketmaster Sport into a business that clients actively seek out, and we’re looking forward to building on that momentum as François takes the business forward.”

Westcombe said his focus will include strengthening Ticketmaster Sport’s relationships with existing clients and expanding the role of ticketing technology in sports organizations.

“Ticketing is evolving rapidly. It is no longer just about selling tickets or providing a platform,” Westcombe said. “It increasingly sits at the heart of the relationship between sports organisations and their fans, connecting audience data and insight with the wider technology ecosystem, and helping build trust, loyalty and more seamless fan experiences.”

In an [interview published by Ticketmaster on October 6](https://business.ticketmaster.co.uk/meet-ticketmaster-sports-new-managing-director-for-emea/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Westcombe outlined several areas where he sees opportunities for sports ticketing technology, including improved audience data, integration with customer relationship management and membership systems, and more streamlined ticket purchasing and event entry.

He said his first few months in the position will focus on meeting with clients, getting to know the Ticketmaster Sport team and identifying opportunities to improve the company’s services.

“The one thing I most want to hear from clients is, ‘You understand what matters to us,'” Westcombe said. “That tells me we’ve listened, asked the right questions, understood their business and earned their trust.”

Westcombe replaces Gratton, who was appointed executive vice president and managing director of Entertainment Partners’ UK business earlier this month.

During his tenure at Ticketmaster Sport, Gratton oversaw commercial expansion and new partnerships, including a league-wide agreement with Premiership Rugby that established a centralized rugby ticketing marketplace.

Entertainment Partners, which provides production technology, payroll and financial services to the entertainment industry, announced Gratton’s appointment October 5.
