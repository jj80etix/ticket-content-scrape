# Arny Granat Signs 17-Year-Old Blues/Soul Artist Meaza Joy As Management Client

## Summary

Jam Productions co-founder Arny Granat has signed 17-year-old blues-rock/soul artist Meaza Joy as a management client under his Grand Slam Productions banner, with plans to pursue domestic and international opportunities across music, live performance, and media. Joy's debut album, Free To Be Me, is out now, with upcoming Chicago club dates at Rosa's Lounge and Bourbon and Brass in late August. The piece also recaps Granat's decades-long career building Jam Productions into one of North America's largest independent concert promoters.

### Key Points
- Arny Granat (Jam Productions co-founder, Tony Award winner) signs 17-year-old artist Meaza Joy to his Grand Slam Productions management roster.
- Joy's debut album Free To Be Me is streaming now; she has Chicago dates at Rosa's Lounge (Aug. 21) and Bourbon and Brass (Aug. 22).
- Granat co-founded Jam Productions with Jerry Mickelson, presenting more than 100,000 live events with artists like The Rolling Stones, Madonna, and Whitney Houston.
- Granat's other ventures include Jam Theatricals (10 Tony Awards), Grand Slam Productions (founded 2012), and MPAG (founded 2021) for large-scale international live experiences.

## Transcript

![1782773882](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/1782773882.jpg)

Arny Granat and Meaza Joy, his newest management client.

Jam Productions co-founder and Tony-award winner producer Arny Granat has signed 17-year-old blues-rock/soul artist Meaza Joy as a management client to his own Grand Slam Productions.

Granat will represent the artist in pursuing domestic and international opportunities across music, live performance, media and other entertainment, according to the announcement.

“I’ve had the privilege of working with some of the biggest names in entertainment over the last five decades, and I don’t make comparisons lightly,” said Granat in the announcement. “What makes Meaza special isn’t just her voice—it’s her authenticity, her musicianship, and her ability to connect with audiences across generations. The future of blues and soul music depends on artists who can honor the roots of the genre while bringing something fresh and relevant to today’s listeners. Meaza has that rare combination. She has the talent, the work ethic, and the artistic vision to introduce this music to an entirely new audience, and I’m excited to be part of her journey.”

Joy’s debut album, *Free To Be Me* is available now on all major streaming platforms and through her official website. Upcoming dates listed on her website include Chicago venues Rosa’s Lounge Aug. 21 and Bourbon and Brass Aug. 22.

Granat, as co-founder of Chicago-based Jam Productions, alongside partner Jerry Mickelson, helped build one of North America’s largest independent concert-promotion companies, presenting more than 100,000 live events featuring legendary artists including The Rolling Stones, Madonna, Frank Sinatra, Whitney Houston, Sting, and Diana Ross.

Granat has also produced and developed touring attractions and cultural events, including the inaugural Farm Aid concert in Champaign, Illinois; the grand opening of Chicago’s Millennium Park; the nationally touring BODIES: The Exhibition; and a touring exhibition presented in partnership with the National Baseball Hall of Fame.

With Jam Theatricals, productions under Granat’s co-direction have earned 10 Tony Awards. He launched Grand Slam Productions in In 2012, followed in 2021 by MPAG, expanding his work into large-scale live experiences and international productions.

“I’m thrilled to be working with Arny Granat,” said Meaza Joy. “His experience, accomplishments, and passion for live music are truly inspiring. I’m grateful for the opportunity to learn from someone who has helped shape the entertainment industry while bringing my music to new audiences around the world.”
