# Zara Larsson smashes New Zealand arena attendance record

## Summary

Zara Larsson broke New Zealand's arena attendance record twice on consecutive nights at Auckland's Spark Arena, closing out her debut Australia/New Zealand headline run and surpassing a mark previously set by Linkin Park.

### Key Points
- 12,796 and 12,798 attendees on back-to-back nights (Oct. 25-26), ~25,600 combined.
- Record previously held by Linkin Park, set in March.
- Closes out Larsson's debut headline tour of the region.
