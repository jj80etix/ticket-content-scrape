# Esther Perel Readies ‘Strangers, Friends, and Lovers’ Tour For 2027

## Summary

Psychotherapist and author Esther Perel announced an 8-date 2027 speaking tour, "Strangers, Friends, and Lovers," running April 13-May 2 across North America.

### Key Points
- Opens April 13 at the Dolby Theatre, LA; closes May 2 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway, Boston.
- Citi presale Oct. 6; artist presale Oct. 7; general onsale Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2266528278](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-2266528278.jpg)

Esther Perel speaks onstage during “Where Should We Begin? Love, Loneliness and AI” at the Vox Media Podcast Stage at SXSW on March 14, 2026 in Austin, Texas. (Photo by Rick Kern/Getty Images for Vox Media)

Esther Perel, the New York Times bestselling psychotherapist and author, is bringing “Strangers, Friends, and Lovers: A Night Out with Esther Perel” to stages across the country in 2027. The tour begins April 13 at the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles, CA, and runs for 8 dates before ending at the MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston, MA, on May 2.

Tickets will be available starting with Citi presale beginning on Oct. 6 at 10 am. Artist presale will take place Oct. 7 at 10 a.m. with additional presales running throughout the week ahead of the general on-sale beginning on Oct. 9 at 10 a.m.

“We know that strong, meaningful relationships are essential to a healthy and happy life. So why are we increasingly isolating ourselves, avoiding one another, and finding it harder to connect?” asks Perel in an official statement. “That’s the paradox at the heart of this show. Relationships are not a private luxury. They are a matter of public health, with the power to shape our individual well-being and the vitality of our communities. Together, we’ll explore how to bring the juice back into our relationships — with more joy, energy and choice — finding the parts of ourselves that come alive through others.”
