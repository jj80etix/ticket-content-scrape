# Morgan Wallen’s Sand In My Boots Festival Returns To Gulf Shores May 21-23

## Summary

Morgan Wallen's Sand In My Boots festival will return to Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, 2027, for its second edition following a sold-out 2026 debut. The 2027 lineup has not yet been announced.

### Key Points
- Promoted by AEG Presents
- 2026 debut was headlined by Wallen, Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn, and HARDY, with a broad supporting lineup (Ella Langley, Riley Green, Bailey Zimmerman, T-Pain, Diplo, Wiz Khalifa, and others)
- Wallen says the 2027 edition will introduce new stage concepts and an elevated fan experience

Following last year’s inaugural edition, Morgan Wallen’s Sand In My Boots festival is set to return to the beaches of Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21-23, 2027. With promoter AEG Presents, the music festival and beach vacation promises to bring together Wallen’s favorite artists, musical influences and personally curated experiences against the backdrop of the Alabama coast. Lineup for the 2027 is promised soon.

“One thing I’m looking forward to the most about bringing Sand In My Boots Festival back is being able to have new concepts on stage and trying to elevate the experience overall for the fans, myself and other artists on the bill,” Morgan shares in the announcement. “I think it’s going to be really top-notch and we’re going to try to do it even better this time.”

Sand In My Boots’ sold-out debut in May 2025 was topped by Morgan, Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn, and HARDY, also featuring top talent like Ella Langley, Riley Green, Bailey Zimmerman, T-Pain, Diplo, Wiz Khalifa, The War on Drugs, 2 Chainz, Three 6 Mafia and more.

The 2027 edition looks to build on the vision of the event, bringing together artists and audiences who might not otherwise share the same festival experience and combining a major music festival environment with the laid-back spirit of a Gulf Coast beach vacation. Premium experiences are to be revealed in the lead up to the event, along with addiitonal information including artist lineup and ticket information.
