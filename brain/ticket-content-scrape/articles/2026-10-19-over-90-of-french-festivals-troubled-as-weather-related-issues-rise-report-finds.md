# Over 90% of French festivals troubled as weather-related issues rise, report finds

## Summary

A preliminary barometer report from France's Centre National de la Musique (CNM), conducted with Ekhoscènes, France Festivals and the SMA, finds that more than 90% of French music and comedy festivals faced difficulties this year despite strong national attendance, citing economic pressure and a "very sharp increase" in weather-related hazards, with extreme temperatures the leading risk.

### Key Points
- Over 90% of surveyed French festivals (1,000+ self-reporting) faced operational difficulties this year.
- Extreme heat was the leading hazard, with a sharp year-over-year increase in weather-related issues.
- Strong national festival attendance persisted despite the difficulties.
- Report produced jointly by CNM, Ekhoscènes, France Festivals and SMA.
