# CTS Eventim Reports Third Quarter Results

## Summary

CTS Eventim reported nine-month 2026 consolidated revenue up 8.1% to €2.322 billion, with adjusted EBITDA up 6.3% to €359.1 million, though third-quarter revenue fell 5.3% year over year because a major 2025 international tour (Ed Sheeran) boosted the prior-year comparison. The company's ticketing segment grew 13.1% over nine months on higher retail ticket volume (123.2 million tickets sold) and price mix, and management reaffirmed its full-year 2026 forecast.

### Key Points
- Nine-month revenue: €2.322bn (+8.1%); adjusted EBITDA: €359.1m (+6.3%); margin 15.5%.
- Q3 revenue fell 5.3% to €809.1m, largely due to tour-timing comparisons against a high-revenue Q3 2025 (Ed Sheeran tour).
- Ticketing segment nine-month revenue up 13.1% to €708.7m; retail ticket volume rose from 121.3m to 123.2m tickets.
- Live Entertainment segment nine-month EBITDA margin rose to 5.7% on high-revenue festivals and expanded consolidation scope.
- CEO Klaus-Peter Schulenberg reaffirmed the full-year 2026 forecast.

### Highlights
- Nine-month ticketing revenue +13.1% to €708.7m on 123.2m tickets sold; Q3 group revenue -5.3% on tour-timing effects, not demand weakness.
- Full-year 2026 guidance reaffirmed despite the Q3 dip.

### Industry Problem
As a direct primary-platform and consumer-major peer, CTS Eventim's results show how exposed ticketing/event-management revenue is to the timing of a handful of marquee tours landing in or out of a given quarter — a volatility risk other platforms, including independents, share.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor CTS Eventim's quarterly results as a bellwether for European ticketing demand and margin trends; no immediate product action, but a data point for forecasting models that account for tour-timing seasonality.

CTS Eventim reported its financials for the first nine months of 2026. Consolidated revenue climbed by 8.1% compared with the prior-year period to €2.322 billion ($2.57 billion). Adjusted EBITDA rose by 6.3% to €359.1 million ($397.7 million). The adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 15.5%.

In the third quarter, consolidated revenue amounted to €809.1 million ($896 million), which was 5.3% lower than in the prior-year period. Adjusted EBITDA came to €133.8 million, a fall of  
2.6%. The adjusted EBITDA margin was 16.5%.

Looking at CTS Eventim’s ticketing business in the first nine months of 2026, revenue rose by 13.1% compared with the prior-year period to €708.7 million ($784.8 million). Adjusted EBITDA advanced by 3.0% to €265.5 million ($294 million). The adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 37.5%. The main drivers of growth were a change in the price mix and an increase in retail sales from 121.3 million tickets to 123.2 million tickets. The company stated that “earnings performance continues to be affected by expenses in connection with the Operational Excellence program.”

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue grew by 11.5% compared with the prior-year period to €235.4 million ($260.7 million). Adjusted EBITDA for July to September rose by 2.2% compared with the third quarter of 2025, reaching €93.0 million ($103 million). The adjusted EBITDA margin was 39.5%. On a like-for-like basis, this equates to a mid-single-digit percentage increase for both revenue and adjusted EBITDA.

Compared with the prior-year period, revenue in the Live Entertainment segment went up by 5.7% in the first nine months of 2026, reaching €1.646 billion ($1.823 billion). Adjusted EBITDA climbed by 17.0% to €93.7 million ($103.8 million), causing the adjusted EBITDA margin to rise to 5.7%. The revenue growth was predominantly attributable to well-attended, high-revenue events and festivals and to the inclusion of further entities in the scope of consolidation.

In the third quarter of 2026, revenue in the Live Entertainment segment declined by 11.7% compared with the prior-year period to €585.6 million ($648.5 million). Adjusted EBITDA came to €40.8 million and was thus 12% down compared with the third quarter of 2025. The adjusted EBITDA margin stood at 7.0%.

CTS Evetim states that its live entertainment segment’s performance in the third quarter was “primarily a reflection of the timing of major tours and events. Moreover, the third quarter of 2025 had been boosted by a particularly high-revenue international tour,” which was Ed Sheeran *Pollstar* was told upon request.

CTS Eventim’s Executive Board is upholding the forecast published for 2026 and is optimistic about business in the remaining months of the year. Klaus-Peter Schulenberg, CEO, CTS Eventim, commented, “CTS EVENTIM is remaining firmly on its path of profitable growth. We are dynamically expanding our business internationally and, at the same time, strengthening our earnings power. The results for the first nine months of 2026 confirm our strategic direction.”
