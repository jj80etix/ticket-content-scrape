# Bowen Yang to Star in Broadway’s ‘Oh, Mary!’

## Summary

SNL star Bowen Yang will take over the lead role of Mary Todd Lincoln in Broadway's "Oh, Mary!" for a 12-week engagement starting September 15 at the Lyceum Theatre, succeeding Meg Stalter. The Tony-winning comedy, written by and formerly starring Cole Escola, transferred to Broadway in 2024 after an extended Off-Broadway run.

### Key Points
- Yang begins a 12-week run Sept. 15 at the Lyceum Theatre, succeeding Meg Stalter (who began July 6).
- Tony Macht rejoins the cast alongside Yang as Mary's husband's assistant.
- "Oh, Mary!" won two 2025 Tony Awards (Lead Actor in a Play, Direction of a Play) after an extended Off-Broadway run and a 2024 Broadway transfer.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0781.jpeg)

Bowen Yang is officially the new Mary Todd Lincoln.

The SNL star and Wicked actor will begin a 12-week engagement in Broadway’s “Oh, Mary!” on September 15 at the Lyceum Theatre. He will succeed Meg Stalter in the leading role, who [began her run on July 6](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/meg-stalter-to-make-broadway-debut-in-oh-mary/).

“My friendship with Cole has brought me many places over the years: late-night pizzerias, cross-borough social-distance walks, a tough production of *Les Misérables*  sung in Dutch,” Yang said in a statement. “But being part of their deranged and profound masterpiece, *Oh, Mary!*, is my favorite one by far. I will spend the rest of my life thanking them for the best Broadway debut a cis gay guy with limited singing ability could ask for.”

Each will join current cast members Phillip James Brannon as Mary’s husband, Bianca Leigh as Mary’s chaperone, and Barrett Foa as Mary’s teacher. Tony Macht will re-join the company alongside Yang on September 15, stepping-in as Mary’s husband’s assistant after Ryo Kamibayashi.

The production premiered Off-Broadway at the Lucille Lortel Theatre in 2024, where its run was extended three times. It later transferred to Broadway the same year. At the 2025 Tony Awards, “Oh, Mary!” won two honors: Lead Actor in a Play for Escola and Direction of a Play for Sam Pinkleton.
