# 2027 Coachella Lineup: Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA, Daddy Yankee Headlining The Mega Festival

## Summary

Goldenvoice unveiled the 2027 Coachella lineup, headlined by Dua Lipa, Tame Impala, SZA and Daddy Yankee, with passes going on sale Nov. 13.

### Key Points
- Dua Lipa headlines Friday (with a Madonna dance-tent set), Tame Impala headlines Saturday, SZA closes both Sundays
- General admission passes already sold out in May; GA+shuttle (Tier 2) passes available at $779, plus VIP add-ons
- YouTube will exclusively livestream both festival weekends

## Transcript

![Coachella Lineup](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Coachella-Lineup.jpg)

Coachella Lineup

After weeks of speculation (and a recent tease from SZA), Goldenvoice has unveiled its Coachella lineup.

The 2027 edition of the megafestival, which Goldenvoice referred to as “The return to innocence,” will be headlined by global pop star Dua Lipa, Australian act Tame Impala and alternative R&B standout SZA. Daddy Yankee, who is considered the King of Reggaetón, also receives top billing with a special production in the desert.

Daddy Yankee isn’t the only icon to grace the stage. Madonna will perform a set in the dance tent on Friday night, which is headlined by Dua Lipa. Other acts performing on the first day of the festival include Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy, JISOO, Diljit Dosanjh, Peggy Gou, Kehlani, Solid Grooves, Giorgio Moroder, Social Distortion, Pinegrove, Angine de Poitrine, Leon Thomas, Drain gang, Snow Strippers, Mike D 5D, CHRIS STASSY, Milo J and many more.

Tame Impala is the headliner for a stacked Saturday night that features Phoebe Bridgers, Weezer, Tiësto, Martin Garrix, Robyn, Role Model, ADÉLA, Måneskin, Solomun, NCT, Enigma, Underworld, Charlotte de Witte, Leon Bridges, Cloonee, Magdalena Bay, Barry Can’t Swim, Boy Harsher, KI/KI, Mariah the Scientist, Bad Gyal and more.

SZA will close out the final day of each weekend, and other acts gracing the Sunday stage include Don Toliver, DOM DOLLA, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, Four Tet, The Neighbourhood, Becky G, BABYMONSTER, Amelie Lens, Sara Landry, Remi Wolf, Tove Lo, Victoria Monét, EsDeeKid, Caroline Polachek, Chase & Status, MUNA, Boris Brejcha, Jorja Smith, the Avalanches and dozens more.

Passes go on sale Nov. 13 at noon PT, and YouTube will livestream both weekends of the festival exclusively. General admission passes sold out in May, but GA passes that include shuttle service, with Tier 2 pricing, will be available for $779. VIP passes and premium add-on experiences will also be available. Visit [Coachella.com](https://coachella.com/) for more information.

This year’s festival stole headlines for its diverse acts and memorable sets, including Karol G’s festive production celebrating Latin culture, Sabrina Carpenter’s homage to old Hollywood and Justin Bieber’s intimate set, with the artist bringing his laptop on stage to perform his hit songs.
