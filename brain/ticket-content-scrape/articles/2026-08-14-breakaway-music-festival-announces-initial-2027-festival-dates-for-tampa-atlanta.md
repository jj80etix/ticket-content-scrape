# Breakaway Music Festival Announces Initial 2027 Festival Dates For Tampa, Atlanta, Columbus, St. Paul

## Summary

Breakaway Music Festival announced its first four 2027 tour stops — Tampa, Atlanta, Columbus and St. Paul — with general on-sale Aug. 19, as the brand wraps a record 2026 season that has drawn nearly 160,000 attendees across its first seven weekends.

### Key Points
- 2027 season opens with Tampa (April), followed by Atlanta, Columbus and St. Paul; more cities to be announced this fall.
- General on-sale Aug. 19, 10am ET, with a one-day-early presale for signups.
- 2026 season, still ongoing, has drawn ~160,000 attendees across seven festival weekends so far, with six more remaining including first-time stops in Utah and Houston.

Breakaway, the brand behind the multi-city Breakaway Music Festival, has announced its initial festival dates and locations for 2027, kicking off in April with Breakaway Tampa, to be followed into the early summer by Breakaways in Atlanta, Columbus and St. Paul.

“As the U.S. touring festival series approaches the conclusion of a record-breaking 2026 season, Breakaway is making its biggest investment yet in each market, raising the bar across talent caliber, production quality, and the overall fan experience,” the announcement states. Dates and locations are noted on the festival flier. Additional cities are to be announced later this year.

“Our festivals will always be the foundation of Breakaway, and in 2027 we’re investing more than ever to make each stop on the tour bigger and better than ever before,” said Adam Lynn, Breakaway Co-Founder & CEO. “This is a sneak peek at what’s ahead, with much more to be announced this fall.”

Tickets for the initial Phase One markets will go on sale to the general public on Aug. 19 at 10 a.m. ET., with fans who sign up for presale access having the opportunity to purchase tickets one day early.

With the 2026 season still ongoing, Breakaway Music Festival has already welcomed nearly 160,000 attendees across its first seven event weekends, according to organizers, with six festivals still to come including Breakaway Michigan (Aug. 14-15), Breakaway Mass (Aug. 21-22), Breakaway Philadelphia (Sept. 11-12) and Breakaway Carolina (Sept. 25-26), before concluding with Breakaway Utah (Oct. 2-3) and Breakaway Houston (Nov. 13-14), both first-time stops for the festival brand.

This year also marked Breakaway Music Festival’s landmark debut in New York City at Under The K Bridge Park earlier this summer. This year’s lineups included top electronic touring talent like FISHER, John Summit, Subtronics, KETTAMA, Tiësto and more.
