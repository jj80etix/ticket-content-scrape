# WWE Sets Hawaii Return for First Live Event Since 2019

## Summary

WWE returns to Honolulu on October 16 for its first Hawaii live event since 2019, bringing a card led by Seth Rollins, The Usos, Jacob Fatu, Oba Femi and Liv Morgan to Blaisdell Arena, plus a talent-scouting tryout ahead of the show.

### Key Points
- First WWE live event in Hawaii since Sept. 22, 2019; venue is Blaisdell Arena, Honolulu.
- Presale Aug. 12, general on-sale Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. HST.
- WWE will hold a talent tryout at the venue ahead of the event to scout new wrestlers.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0797.jpeg)

WWE is heading back to Hawaii.

The wrestling giant will return to Honolulu on Friday, October 16, bringing its 2026 Live Event Tour to Blaisdell Arena and ending a seven-year absence from the islands.

The event will mark WWE’s first live show in Hawaii since September 22, 2019, when the company also took over Blaisdell Arena.

WWE is already lining up a star-studded roster for the October return, with Seth “Freakin” Rollins, The Usos, Jacob Fatu, “The Ruler” Oba Femi and Liv Morgan among the names announced for the card. Additional WWE Superstars are expected to appear.
