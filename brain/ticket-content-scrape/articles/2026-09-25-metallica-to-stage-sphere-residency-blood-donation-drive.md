# Metallica to stage Sphere residency blood donation drive

## Summary

Metallica will run an immersive Red Cross blood-donation experience in Las Vegas bookending its "Life Burns Faster" Sphere residency (Oct. 1–March 13), building on a partnership that has produced over 93,000 donations worldwide since April.

### Key Points
- Pop-up donation centers open Sept. 28 at Fashion Show Las Vegas, running through March 19, 2027.
- Donors get a "backstage-inspired" experience with a Lars Ulrich drum-kit replica and limited-edition T-shirt.
- Marks Metallica's first residency format; the band also added a dozen new North American M72 Tour dates.

## Transcript

The drive bookends the heavy metal band's upcoming Las Vegas stint, which spans 24 dates from 1 October to 13 March

![Metallica on M72 World Tour](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Metallica-on-M72-World-Tour-©-Tom-Grut.png)

Adding to their blood donation efforts, Metallica will offer an ‘immersive’ blood drive experience around their *Life Burns Faster* residency at the Sphere in Las Vegas in partnership with the Red Cross.

Inspired by the venue, the pop-up donation centres will bring donors into a ‘backstage-inspired experience’, offering a unique option for fans. Participants will be able to sit behind a replica drum set similar to that of Lars Ulrich and will also receive a limited-edition T-shirt.

The Red Cross x Metallica Blood Donation Experience, sponsored by Delta Air Lines, launches on 28 September at Fashion Show Las Vegas on the Strip, and will be open until 19 March 2027, bookending the band’s 24-date residency.

It is the next step for the ongoing partnership between the Red Cross, Metallica, and the band’s foundation, All Within My Hands, which launched last [April](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/04/metallica-blood-donation-drives-gigs/). Since then, fans have contributed more than 93,000 donations worldwide, including 25,000 donations in the US.

“What makes this partnership with Metallica so special is its ability to turn the passion their fans have for their music into lifesaving action. Together, we’re giving fans a memorable way to show up for patients who need them,” says Darren Irby, American Red Cross’ executive director of national partnerships.

Metallica’s Sphere residency runs from 1 October to 13 March, with the band also recently adding a [dozen new North American dates](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/tour-news-metallica-to-pass-100-dates-on-m72-tour/) to their  *M72 World Tour* to follow the stint.

Veterans of the road, it will mark the first time Metallica has pursued a residency format. The band’s North American agent, IAG’s Dennis Arfa, previously told *IQ* that the demand was seismic – “ [They could have sold out 100 of them](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/for-whom-the-sphere-tolls-metallicas-next-live-frontier/),” he said.

*Life Burns Faster*  will follow the *M72 World Tour’s* ‘No Repeat Weekend’ format, wherein the band plays different setlists each night to create an all-consuming stop for Metallica fans, with the blood donation drive adding a charitable expansion of that immersion.

Metallica is represented by IAG in North America and K2 Agency in the rest of the world.
