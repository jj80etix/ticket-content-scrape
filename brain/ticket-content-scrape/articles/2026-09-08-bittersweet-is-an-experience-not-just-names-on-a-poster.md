# ‘BitterSweet is an experience, not just names on a poster’

## Summary

Poland's BitterSweet Festival, promoted by independent outfit Good Taste Production, drew nearly 150,000 attendees in 2026 — double its debut-year turnout — with a lineup led by Gorillaz, Robbie Williams, Twenty One Pilots and Lorde. The piece (paywalled after the opening) frames the festival's challenge as balancing its nostalgia-leaning brand identity while scaling appeal to international audiences.

### Key Points
- Held 13–15 August at Poznań's Park Cytadela.
- Attendance grew from ~70,000 (debut edition) to nearly 150,000 in its second year.
- Headliners included Gorillaz, Robbie Williams, Twenty One Pilots, Lorde, Major Lazer, Tom Odell.
