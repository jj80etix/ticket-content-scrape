# Greta Van Fleet Announce ‘Into the Beginning Tour for 2027

## Summary

Greta Van Fleet announced the 59-city "Into the Beginning" tour across North America and Europe for 2027, running February through July ahead of their new album "Palace for the People" (out Oct. 9). Presales begin October 13 (North America) and October 7 (Europe), with eligible Spotify Premium subscribers also getting early access via Reserved by Spotify.

### Key Points
- 59-date tour spans North America (Feb. 3-Mar. 30, resuming July 5) and Europe (May 15-June 4).
- North American general on-sale is Oct. 15; European general on-sale is Oct. 9.
- Spotify Premium subscribers get a separate "Reserved by Spotify" presale access path.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Greta-Van-Fleet.webp)

Greta Van Fleet will return to the road in 2027 with the “Into the Beginning” tour, a 59-city run across North America and Europe.

The tour is set to begin February 3 at Enmarket Arena in Savannah, Georgia, and concludes July 31 at Acrisure Amphitheater in Grand Rapids, Michigan. The announcement comes ahead of the band’s new album, *Palace for the People*, arriving October 9 through Republic Records.

The opening North American stretch includes appearances in Nashville, Rosemont, Minneapolis and Pittsburgh before continuing through Detroit, Boston, Brooklyn and Philadelphia in March. Western dates include Anaheim, Seattle, Portland and Boise, with the leg ending March 30 at Denver’s Ball Arena.

The European portion begins May 15 in Munich and includes Milan, Berlin, Amsterdam, Brussels and Paris. Greta Van Fleet will play London’s O2 Arena on May 28 before visiting Birmingham, Manchester and Glasgow. The European leg closes June 4 in Dublin.

North American performances resume July 5 in Vancouver, followed by Canadian and U.S. stops leading to the Michigan finale.

Support will vary throughout the itinerary. Arc De Soleil, The Kooks and Portugal. The Man are among the acts joining North American dates, while Larkin Poe will accompany the European run. Additional openers include Edgehill, Luna Li, Sana Sana Musica, Sun Room and Friko.

Before the international tour, Greta Van Fleet will celebrate the album with a smaller-scale residency at Nashville’s The Basement East on the first three Saturdays in October. The performances will precede an itinerary dominated by arenas, with amphitheater appearances also scheduled during the summer.

North American fan presales begin October 13 at 9 a.m. local time, followed by the general sale October 15 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register for presale access through the band’s official website.

European ticket sales will begin earlier, with a fan presale October 7 at 10 a.m. local time and the general sale October 9 at 10 a.m. local time. Additional presales are scheduled ahead of both regional on-sale dates.

Eligible North American Spotify Premium subscribers may also receive access through Reserved by Spotify. Those selected will be notified by email and through the app, with the opportunity to purchase up to two tickets during that presale.

For more information and additional details fans can visit [gretavanfleet.com](http://gretavanfleet.com).
