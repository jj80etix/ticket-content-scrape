# Quentin Tarantino’s ‘The Popinjay Cavalier’ Sets West End Opening Date

## Summary

Quentin Tarantino's play "The Popinjay Cavalier" has set its West End opening date. The piece covers the production's scheduling announcement.

### Key Points
- "The Popinjay Cavalier," written by Quentin Tarantino, sets its West End opening date.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Popinjay-Cavalier-1.jpg)

Sacha Baron Cohen will star in Quentin Tarantino’s “The Popinjay Cavalier” when the filmmaker’s first stage play premieres in London’s West End next year.

Written and directed by Tarantino, the production will begin previews at the Phoenix Theatre on February 23, 2027, ahead of an official opening on March 18. The engagement is currently booking through June 13, 2027. Additional casting will be announced later.

Sony Pictures Entertainment and Sonia Friedman Productions are presenting the production, which will also mark Baron Cohen’s West End debut.

Set in Europe during the 1830s, “The Popinjay Cavalier” follows an actor named Rupert Sebastian Thomas Jr., whose offense against the French leads to a dangerous journey to Paris. The comedy incorporates sword fighting, acrobatics and the adventure conventions of classic Hollywood swashbucklers.

The production’s influences include writers George Bruce, Ernst Vajda and Jesse Lasky Jr. Its story centers on deception and disguise, drawing from adventure works created for both theater and film.

Producer Sonia Friedman and Tom Rothman, chairman and CEO of Sony Pictures Entertainment’s Motion Picture Group, described the development process in a joint statement. They said Tarantino has been working with actors, fencers, acrobats and musicians as the production takes shape in the rehearsal room.

The creative team includes Tom Pye handling scenic and costume design, Natasha Chivers overseeing lighting and Ian Dickinson providing sound design. Philip d’Orleans serves as fight director, with Sam Lyon-Behan as action coordinator.

Tim Phillips is composing the music, Jim Carnahan is responsible for casting and Jack Bradfield serves as associate director.
