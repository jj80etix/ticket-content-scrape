# For Quality, Environmental And Health & Safety Management: Superstruct Entertainment Earns Three Major ISO Certifications

## Summary

Superstruct Entertainment says it has become the first live-entertainment company to hold all three major ISO certifications simultaneously — quality (9001), environmental (14001), and health & safety (45001) — across its 80-plus festival portfolio.

### Key Points
- Certifications awarded by UKAS-accredited body Amitvo (British Assessment Bureau)
- Certifications cover Superstruct at group level across its full festival portfolio
- The company worked with consultancy PCR Global on the certification process
- COO Rebecca Kane Burton frames it as a shared operational-standards benchmark across the group

Superstruct Entertainment, by its own admission, has become “the first live entertainment company in the world to hold all three major ISO certifications at once: for quality management (ISO 9001), environmental management (ISO 14001) and occupational health and safety management (ISO 45001).

“Achieving all three certifications involved bringing together existing practices from across different teams and markets, while developing new Group-level standards, processes and assurance mechanisms within one shared management framework. Teams across Superstruct’s businesses took part throughout the process,” according to today’s press announcement from Superstruct.

“The group-wide approach also means teams can now share what they learn more easily, bringing together operational performance data, incident learning, assurance findings and audience feedback to identify trends, inform management decisions and spread good practice across the business,” it continues.

The certifications, awarded by UKAS accredited certification body Amitvo (British Assessment Bureau), cover Superstruct at group level, recognizing the management systems behind a diverse portfolio that includes some of the world’s most respected and culturally significant live events.

Superstruct worked with PCR Global throughout this process, a business consultancy that provides specialist support on ISO management systems and certification. PCR helped assess existing systems against the relevant ISO standards, identify gaps and support the development and implementation of the Group level framework needed to prepare for certification.

The certifications themselves were independently awarded by UKAS accredited certification body Amitvo (British Assessment Bureau).
