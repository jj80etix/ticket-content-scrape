# Inside the New Jersey AG’s Office With AG Jennifer Davenport

## Summary

In a Troutman Pepper "Regulatory Oversight" podcast episode, New Jersey Attorney General Jennifer Davenport discusses her office's junk-fees campaign and broader affordability agenda as one of her top priorities since being sworn in as the state's 63rd AG in February 2026. She also addresses recent child-safety settlements with social media companies and human-trafficking enforcement tied to the World Cup, closing with a call for clear rules and fair competition in dealings with industry.

### Key Points
- Davenport was sworn in as NJ's 63rd AG in February 2026; prior roles include DEA intelligence analyst, assistant U.S. attorney, and utility-company deputy general counsel
- Names a junk-fees campaign and broader affordability agenda among her top five priorities
- Also discusses child-safety settlements with social-media companies and World Cup-linked trafficking enforcement
- Her message to counsel emphasizes clear regulatory guidance and hearing from industry before decisions are made

### Highlights
- New Jersey's junk-fees campaign is an active, named priority of the state's top enforcer as of this September 2026 interview
- Davenport frames affordability broadly, not limited to ticketing, but junk fees are explicitly named as a focus

### Industry Problem
State AG junk-fee enforcement continues to expand as a compliance vector alongside the federal FTC rule, meaning primary platforms face a patchwork of state-level pricing-disclosure obligations rather than a single federal standard.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor for New Jersey-specific junk-fee guidance or enforcement actions emerging from this office; no immediate product change is indicated, but Etix should track whether the campaign produces formal rules or settlements that would require pricing-disclosure changes.

In this episode of [*Regulatory Oversigh* t](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/regulatory-oversight/), [Clay Friedman](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/clayton-s-friedman/) and [Matt Berns](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/matthew-j-berns/) are joined by New Jersey Attorney General (AG) Jennifer Davenport, who was sworn in as the state’s 63rd AG in February 2026.

Davenport brings an unusually broad background to the role, having served as a DEA intelligence analyst, assistant U.S. attorney, first assistant AG under three AGs, and deputy general counsel at a utility company. She shares how her years in corporate compliance reshaped her thinking about root cause analysis, clear regulatory guidance, and the value of hearing from industry before decisions are made.

In this episode, they discuss Davenport’s five biggest priorities, the office’s junk fees campaign and broader affordability agenda, recent settlements holding social media companies accountable for child safety, and human trafficking work tied to the World Cup. They close with her message for in-house and outside counsel about the importance of clear rules, fair competition, and building compliance into business operations.
