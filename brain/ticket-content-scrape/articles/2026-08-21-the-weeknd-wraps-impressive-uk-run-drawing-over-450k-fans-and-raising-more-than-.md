The Weeknd wrapped an impressive run throughout the United Kingdom with a sold-out five-night stand at Wembley Stadium in London, performing in front of hundreds of thousands of fans and raising money for charity.

The global superstar’s U.K. run, which included two shows at Manchester’s Etihad Stadium before five nights in London, drew more than 450,000 fans and raised more than $350,000 in charitable donations for the United Nations World Food Programme and Global Citizen, according to promoter Live Nation.

It’s the second U.K. leg for The Weeknd’s record-breaking “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour,” which is the most successful trek by a solo male artist, and it boosted his numbers on music platforms, generating more than 11.1 million audio streams over the past week (a 55% increase compared to the previous four-week average).

Mayor of London Sadiq Khan said the five-night stand was a success for the city.

“London has been hugely proud to welcome The Weeknd for more shows than any other city in the world this year,” Khan said in a statement. “His five performances have brought hundreds of thousands of fans to Wembley Stadium, providing a huge boost to our tourism and hospitality industries, and showing why London is the music capital of the world.”

The “After Hours Til Dawn Stadium Tour” marks the first time a trek from a solo male artist surpassed $1 billion in grosses. The Weeknd won the Pollstar Award for Best R&B Tour earlier this year, and his managers Amir “Cash” Esmailian and Wassim “Sal” Slaiby were recently recognized as [2026 *Pollstar* Impact 50 honorees](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/06/22/2026-impact-50-honoree-amir-cash-esmailian-wassim-sal-slaiby/) for their work with the artist.

And there’s still plenty more history to be made for the Canadian singer. He’s currently in Dublin, Ireland, for two shows at Croke Park before embarking for Madrid, Spain, to perform three nights at Riyadh Air Metropolitano. He also has a concert in Barcelona and two nights at Lisbon, Portugal’s Estadio do Restelo in September.

The Weeknd announced earlier this year that the final leg of the remarkable tour will take place in Asia this fall, with shows in Tokyo, Jakarta, Singapore, Seoul, Bangkok, Hong Kong and Kuala Lumpur. The leg was originally announced as an 11-date stadium run, but it has since expanded to 17 shows due to overwhelming demand.
