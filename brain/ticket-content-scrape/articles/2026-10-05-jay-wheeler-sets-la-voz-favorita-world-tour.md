# Jay Wheeler Sets ‘La Voz Favorita’ World Tour

## Summary

Jay Wheeler announced a 34-city "La Voz Favorita" World Tour spanning Spain, the U.S. and Latin America from December 2026 through June 2027.

### Key Points
- Opens Dec. 4 in Tenerife, Spain; a 16-show U.S. leg begins March 7 in Fort Myers, FL.
- Latin American leg runs April 29-June 5, closing in Santo Domingo.
- Presale Oct. 7; general onsale Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Jay-Wheeler.webp)

Jay Wheeler has announced a 34-city “La Voz Favorita” World Tour that will take the Puerto Rican singer across Spain, the United States and Latin America beginning in December 2026 and continuing through June 2027.

The tour opens December 4 at Palmetum in Tenerife, Spain, followed by six more Spanish dates before Wheeler heads to the U.S. for a 16-show run beginning March 7 at Hertz Arena in Fort Myers, Florida. The itinerary then continues through 11 Latin American markets, wrapping June 5 at Palacio de los Deportes in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic.

The U.S. leg includes a combination of arenas, theaters and other large concert venues. Wheeler will play Kaseya Center in Miami on March 11 before stops at Coca-Cola Roxy in Atlanta, DAR Constitution Hall in Washington, D.C., Hard Rock Live in Atlantic City and Premier Theater at Foxwoods in Connecticut.

The tour reaches Barclays Center in Brooklyn on March 24, followed by Santander Arena in Reading and MGM Music Hall in Boston. April dates include Rosemont Theatre near Chicago, Fillmore in Denver, Toyota Music Factory in Dallas, 713 Music Hall in Houston, YouTube Theater in Los Angeles and Kia Center in Orlando.

“This La Voz Favorita tour is going to be very special,” Wheeler said in announcing the run.

After completing the U.S. leg in Orlando on April 15, Wheeler will begin the Latin American portion of the tour April 29 in Quito, Ecuador. Stops include Lima, Mexico City, Buenos Aires, Santiago, Panama City, San José and Bogotá before the Santo Domingo finale.

Produced by Dynamic Records in association with AUL Worldwide and UTA, the tour takes its name from Wheeler’s latest album, *La Voz Favorita*.

Presales for the newly announced dates begin Wednesday, October 7 at 11 a.m. local time, followed by the general onsale Friday, October 9 at 11 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Wheeler’s official website](https://www.jaywheelermundial.com/index.html).
