# LOS MENOR3S Ink Management Deal With Walter Kolm Entertainment

## Summary

Venezuelan trio LOS MENOR3S signed a management deal with Walter Kolm Entertainment following the release of their EP "CALLAÍTO SAFARI" and supporting dates with Rawayana, including a three-night Movistar Arena (Santiago) run that grossed $1.97M on 41,921 tickets per Pollstar Boxoffice.

### Key Points
- LOS MENOR3S signed with Walter Kolm Entertainment (WKE).
- Recently released EP "CALLAÍTO SAFARI."
- Opened for Rawayana at a three-night Movistar Arena (Santiago) stand grossing $1.97M on 41,921 tickets.
- Group also runs a community initiative, Movimiento Camina, supporting children in Naiguatá, Venezuela.

## Transcript

Walter Kolm Entertainment (WKE) recently bolstered its artist roster by signing LOS MENOR3S, a rising Venezuelan group that recently released a new EP, *CALLAÍTO SAFARI*.

The trio inked a management deal as it continues to expand internationally and grow artistically. LOS MENOR3S recently debuted in Argentina and Chile as special guests of Rawayana, a Venezuelan band that has sold out shows across the globe. LOS MENOR3S opened for Rawayana at Movistar Arena in Santiago, Chile, in August, a three-night stand that grossed $1,973,186 off 41,921 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

“Latin music has never been more global, and its future is in the hands of a new generation of artists unafraid to write their own rules. LOS MENOR3S are that future,” said Walter Kolm, CEO of WKE. “They have the talent, the authenticity and the ambition we look for, but most importantly, they have their own identity. At WK Entertainment, we have always believed in building long-term careers, and this signing reflects our commitment to identifying and developing the next generation of Latin music stars. We could not be more excited for everything we’re going to build together.”

LOS MENOR3S are also making an impact off the stage through their Movimiento Camina initiative. The three young members—Big Bowe, Sheba and Nego—partnered with Bajo el Árbol to organize community events supporting children in Naiguatá.

With a new manager, new music, performances on larger stages and community work at home, LOS MENOR3S feel they are closer to realizing their goals.

“We have been working since day one to make our dreams come true, and steps like these make us realize that they aren’t so far away,” the group said in a statement. “Thank you to the WK family for accompanying us on this journey. Today we are one step closer to what we always dreamed of.”
