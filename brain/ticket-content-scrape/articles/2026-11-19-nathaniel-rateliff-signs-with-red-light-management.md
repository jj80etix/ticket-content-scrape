# Nathaniel Rateliff Signs With Red Light Management

## Summary

Nathaniel Rateliff has signed with Red Light Management for representation, managed by Will Botwin and Coran Capshaw, coinciding with his newly announced 2027 "Endless Horizon Tour" theater run and his unanimous appointment to Farm Aid's board of directors.

### Key Points
- Rateliff signs with Red Light Management (Will Botwin, Coran Capshaw).
- Announces "Endless Horizon Tour 2027," playing theaters including Kansas City's Uptown Theater, D.C.'s Warner Theatre, NYC's Beacon Theatre, and venues in Tampa, Nashville, Atlanta, Detroit and Minneapolis.
- Recently completed a 10th consecutive year performing at Farm Aid and was appointed to its board of directors, joining founders Willie Nelson, Neil Young and John Mellencamp.

Award-winning artist Nathaniel Rateliff has signed with Red Light Management for representation. Will Botwin and Coran Capshaw will manage the artist’s career.

Rateliff recently performed a 10 <sup>th</sup> consecutive year at Farm Aid after being unanimously appointed to the benefit concert’s board of directors. He will work with founders Willie Nelson, Neil Young and John Mellencamp, as well as fellow artist board members Dave Matthews and Margo Price and the Farm Aid team.

Rateliff made an appearance at this year’s Americana Honors & Awards, where he presented the Album of the Year award. Nathaniel Rateliff & The Night Sweats won the prestigious award the previous year for their album *South of Here*.

News of Rateliff’s signing comes on the heels of his “Endless Horizon Tour 2027” announcement. He will perform in theaters across the country, including Kansas City’s Uptown Theater, Warner Theatre in D.C., New York’s Beacon Theatre, BayCare Sound in Tampa, Nashville’s The Truth, The Eastern in Atlanta, Detroit’s Fox Theatre, and State Theatre in Minneapolis. Visit [https://www.nathanielrateliff.com/tour](https://www.nathanielrateliff.com/tour) for more information.

The Colorado Americana star recently performed at Tom Morello’s inaugural Power to the People festival, playing alongside Bruce Springsteen, Dave Matthews and Morello. He headlined the Edmonton Folk Festival and played in front of 20,000 people. Rateliff also closed the Newport Folk Music Festival alongside Matthews, Joan Baez and more during his star-studded Nathaniel Rateliff & Friends set. He was the first artist appointed to the festival’s Stewardship program since Pete Seeger.

Rateliff headlined a special concert with the Colorado Symphony Orchestra at the iconic Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, with the sold-out Aug. 18 show grossing $742,291 off 8,729 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

The artist is booked by High Road Touring’s Dave Rowan in North America, and WME’s Josh Javor represents Rateliff in the U.K.
