# Cristian Castro, Grupo Frontera, The Warning Join List Of Latin Grammy Awards Performers

## Summary

The Latin Recording Academy announced additional performers for the Nov. 12 Latin Grammy Awards, including Cristian Castro, Feid, Grupo Frontera and The Warning, plus a tribute segment honoring songwriter José Alfredo Jiménez's centennial.

### Key Points
- New performers: Cristian Castro, Feid, Grupo Frontera, The Warning, Reik and others.
- Tribute segment marks the 100th anniversary of José Alfredo Jiménez's birth, produced by Edgar Barrera.
- Awards ceremony scheduled for Nov. 12, 2026.

## Transcript

The Latin Recording Academy unveiled additional performers for the upcoming Latin Grammy Awards ceremony on Nov. 12.

Beloved singer Cristian Castro will take the stage for Latin music’s biggest night, as well as Feid, Grupo Frontera, The Warning, Reik, Camila Fernández, Fonseca, Luis Figueroa, Sofía Reyes, Yami Safdie, Niña Pastori and Milo J.

The awards show will also feature a segment honoring the legacy of José Alfredo Jiménez, a legendary Mexican singer-songwriter known as “The King of Ranchero Music” who composed iconic hits like “Ella,” “El Rey” and “Media Vuelta.” The Latin Grammys will celebrate the 100 <sup>th</sup> anniversary of Jiménez’s birth, and renowned music producer Edgar Barrera, who has won 29 Latin Grammys, will serve as the tribute’s producer and help breathe new life into Jiménez’s iconic catalog that defined Mexican music.

Barrera is tied with Residente for most Latin Grammy victories, and he leads this year’s nominees with 10 nominations for his work with Latin music stars like Karol G, Grupo Frontera, Xavi and Carín León.

Roselyn Sánchez will return to host the 27 <sup>th</sup> annual Latin Grammys, which airs live from MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas. The three-hour telecast will be broadcast on Univision beginning at 8 p.m. ET/PT (7 p.m. Central), and there will be a one-hour pre-show starting at 7 p.m. ET/PT (6 p.m. Central).
