# Charli xcx Announces UK, Europe Fall ‘Music, Fashion, Film Tour’ Dates

## Summary

Charli xcx announced new UK/Europe dates for her "Music, Fashion, Film Tour," kicking off Feb. 13, 2027 in Dublin and running through Paris, ahead of the tour's North American leg beginning Sept. 11 in Philadelphia. UK/Europe presale sign-ups are open now through Sept. 15, with general on-sale Sept. 18.

### Key Points
- New UK/Europe leg: Feb. 13 (Dublin) through Feb. 24, 2027 (Paris), including Glasgow, Manchester, London and Amsterdam.
- North America leg runs Sept. 11-Oct. 23, 2026, including two Brooklyn nights and a Las Vegas close.
- Audrey Hobert opens all newly announced UK/Europe dates.
- Artist presale opens Sept. 16; general on-sale Sept. 18 at 9 a.m. BST/10 a.m. CET.

Charli xcx has announced new dates for the “Music, Fashion, Film Tour,” with the pop star making the trek to the UK and Europe in 2027. The new set of shows kicks off on Feb. 13 in Dublin, Ireland, at the 3Arena, followed by stops in Glasgow, Manchester, London, Amsterdam, and concluding at the Accor Arena in Paris. All newly added dates will feature singer Audrey Hobert as the opening act. See full tour routing for the new dates below.

The ‘Music, Fashion, Film’ tour is about to begin, with the first show kicking off on Sept. 11 in Philadelphia, PA, at the Xfinity Mobile Arena. From there, Charli will play two nights at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, then hit Toronto, Boston, Washington, DC, Austin, Atlanta, and more. The North America leg will then conclude on Oct. 23 in Las Vegas, NV, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena.

Sign-ups for presale access are live now through Sept. 15 [here](https://www.charlixcx.com/musicfashionfilm-uk-eu-tour), with artist presale tickets going live on Sept. 16 at 11 a.m. BST/12 p.m. CET.

General on-sale begins Sept. 18 at 9 a.m. BST/10 a.m. CET.

**Charli xcx “Music, Fashion, Film Tour” UK/Europe Dates**  
Feb. 13 — Dublin, Ireland at 3Arena  
Feb. 15 — Glasgow, U.K. at OVO Hydro  
Feb. 17 — Manchester, U.K. at Co-Op Live  
Feb. 19 — London, U.K. at the O2  
Feb. 22 — Amsterdam, The Netherlands at Ziggo Dome  
Feb. 24 — Paris, France at Accor Arena
