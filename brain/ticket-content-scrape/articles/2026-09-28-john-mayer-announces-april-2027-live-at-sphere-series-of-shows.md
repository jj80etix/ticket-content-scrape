# John Mayer Announces April 2027 ‘Live At Sphere’ Series Of Shows

## Summary

John Mayer announced a new run of "Live At Sphere" shows in Las Vegas for April 2027, with six dates spanning solo, full-band, and John Mayer Trio performance formats. General on-sale begins Oct. 9 via Ticketmaster, with an artist presale starting Oct. 7 through Seated.

### Key Points
- Six Sphere dates: April 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, 18, 2027.
- Three performance formats per engagement: solo, full band, and the John Mayer Trio (with Steve Jordan and Pino Palladino).
- General on-sale Oct. 9, 10 a.m. PT via ticketmaster.com; presale registration open now via JohnMayer.com (Seated).
- Artist presale begins Oct. 7, 10 a.m. PT.

John Mayer has announced that he will perform live at the Sphere in Las Vegas, NV, in April 2027. The acclaimed singer-songwriter will hit the stage in the desert on April 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, and 18, with each performance enhanced by the immersive sound, visual spectacle, and sensory feedback the Sphere has become known for.

As for the music, each show will feature John’s music in three forms: solo, full band, and the John Mayer trio. According to an official press release, the solo portion will feature Mayer alone on stage, leaning heavily on his acoustic guitar work, with special performances on piano and electric guitar. The John Mayer Trio features John Mayer, Steve Jordan, and Pino Palladino.

The general on-sale will begin Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. PT at [https://ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/). Artist pre-sale registration is available now at [JohnMayer.com](https://johnmayer.com/), powered by seated, with the pre-sale beginning Oct. 7 at 10 a.m. PT.

SiriusXM listeners will have special pre-sale access to John Mayer’s newly announced dates at Sphere in Las Vegas. The exclusive SiriusXM presale begins Oct. 8, giving listeners an early opportunity to purchase tickets before they go on sale to the general public. SiriusXM listeners can tune in to Life with John Mayer for details on how to access the presale and get their chance to experience John Mayer live at Sphere.  
  
Additionally, a limited number of VIP ticket packages will be available at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/) starting Sept. 30, at 10 AM PT. Select packages include premium seats, a date-specific lithograph signed by John, exclusive merchandise, and more. Tickets for each show will be auctioned off through charityauctionstoday.com/. 100% of proceeds from the auctions will benefit the Heart and Armor Foundation – a foundation for veterans’ health that Mayer founded in 2012.  
  
Tickets start at $129. $1 from each ticket sold will go to Mayer’s Back To You Fund, which has supported many charities, including programs supporting at-risk youth and the homeless.
