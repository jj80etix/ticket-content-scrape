# The Doobie Brothers Announce 10 Canadian Shows for 2027 Walk This Road Tour

## Summary

The Doobie Brothers have added a 10-show Canadian leg across seven provinces to their Walk This Road Tour, opening August 5, 2027, in Halifax with Michael McDonald joining the band's current lineup.

### Key Points
- Canadian leg runs 10 shows across seven provinces starting August 5, 2027, in Halifax.
- Lineup includes Michael McDonald alongside Tom Johnston, Pat Simmons, and John McFee.
- Laval, Quebec is the tour's only Quebec date (August 9); two Ontario shows include Ottawa (Aug 10) and St. Catharines (Aug 12).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Doobie-Brothers.webp)

The Doobie Brothers have added a Canadian leg to their Walk This Road Tour, with 10 concerts scheduled across seven provinces in August 2027.

The tour is slated to open August 5 in Halifax, Nova Scotia, at Scotiabank Centre. Michael McDonald will join Tom Johnston, Pat Simmons and John McFee for the run.

After Halifax, the band will play Avenir Centre in Moncton, New Brunswick, on August 6. Laval will host the only Quebec date, an August 9 concert at Place Bell.

The first of two Ontario shows follows August 10 at Canadian Tire Centre in Ottawa. The band will then head to the Niagara region for an August 12 performance at Meridian Centre in St. Catharines. That show marks the halfway point of the Canadian leg.

The tour resumes August 16 at Canada Life Centre in Winnipeg, the band’s only Manitoba stop. Edmonton is the sole Alberta date, with a performance scheduled for August 19 at Rogers Place.

British Columbia will host the final three shows, beginning August 21 at South Okanagan Events Centre in Penticton. The band will travel to Vancouver Island for an August 23 concert at Save-On-Foods Memorial Centre in Victoria. The Canadian leg wraps August 24 at Rogers Forum in Abbotsford.

The Canadian tour falls between two sets of dates for The Doobie Brothers’ nine-show residency at BleauLive Theater at Fontainebleau Las Vegas. The band will make its debut at the venue on February 26, with additional shows on February 27 and March 5 and 6. Three days after the Abbotsford concert, the group will return to Las Vegas for shows on August 27 and 28, followed by September 1, 3 and 4.
