# Boston Legacy FC Names Jump Exclusive Ticketing Partner Ahead of 2027 Season

## Summary

Boston Legacy FC has named Jump its exclusive fan-experience and ticketing partner starting with the NWSL club's 2027 season, building a team-branded app that unifies ticket purchases, merchandise and concessions and that Jump says uses AI on first-party fan data to drive ticket/membership sales and personalize the matchday experience. The partnership is timed ahead of the club's planned 2028 move into the renovated White Stadium, giving Boston Legacy direct ownership of fan data from its inaugural season onward.

### Key Points
- Jump named exclusive ticketing/fan-experience partner for Boston Legacy FC beginning 2027 season.
- Single branded app combines ticketing, merchandise and concessions; Jump emphasizes club ownership of fan data vs. third-party platforms.
- Jump describes using AI on that data to drive ticket/membership sales and personalize matchday offers.
- Timed ahead of 2028 move to renovated White Stadium; club currently splits games between Gillette Stadium and Centreville Bank Stadium.
- Deal framed partly as a membership/fan-relationship play, not just transactional ticketing.

### Highlights
- Jump is pitching "club ownership of first-party fan data" as the core differentiator against incumbent ticketing platforms — a direct positioning challenge to vendors who retain that data themselves.
- AI is marketed specifically for driving ticket/membership sales and personalizing matchday spend, not just support automation — a concrete non-fraud AI use case to benchmark.
- A new NWSL franchise building its entire fan-data stack from scratch is a greenfield deal structure worth tracking as a model for other expansion franchises.

### Proposed Solution
Jump's offering bundles ticketing, commerce and AI-driven personalization under club-owned data as the sales pitch. Etix should treat this as a build/improve signal: clients increasingly want contractual assurance that fan/transaction data stays theirs, plus AI tooling layered on top of (not instead of) that data — both worth validating as explicit points in Etix's own platform pitch to new and expansion franchises.

Boston Legacy FC has named Jump its exclusive fan experience and ticketing partner beginning with the 2027 season, with plans to bring ticket purchases, merchandise shopping and concession orders together through a team-branded mobile app.

The partnership will establish a new digital platform for the National Women’s Soccer League club as it builds its supporter base and prepares for a planned move into the renovated White Stadium in 2028.

For fans, the app is intended to provide a central place to manage purchases and access club offerings. For Boston Legacy, the agreement will support a more direct connection with supporters through customer data and personalized experiences.

“We’re committed to being the most fan-friendly team in professional sports, and providing a frictionless ticketing experience is an important part of that,” said Sam Barlow, vice president of ticket sales and service for Boston Legacy FC.
