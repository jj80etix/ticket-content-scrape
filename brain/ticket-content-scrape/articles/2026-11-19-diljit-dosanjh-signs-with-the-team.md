# Diljit Dosanjh Signs With THE·TEAM

## Summary

Diljit Dosanjh has signed with THE·TEAM for worldwide representation, continuing a breakout year that includes a 2027 Coachella booking, a No. 1-trending YouTube collaboration with Daddy Yankee, and becoming the first South Asian artist to headline Wembley Stadium in 2026.

### Key Points
- Dosanjh signs with THE·TEAM (agents Sam Hunt, James Rubin, Julia May, Stephanie Miles) for global representation.
- Billed for the 2027 Coachella lineup (April 9 and 16), positioned near top-line headliners Dua Lipa and Madonna.
- "Pokito," his collaboration with Daddy Yankee, hit No. 1 on YouTube's Spanish-language music chart and trended No. 1 in 12 countries.
- Became the first South Asian artist to headline Wembley Stadium in September 2026.

Diljit Dosanjh’s big year continues with news that he’s signed with THE·TEAM for representation worldwide.  
  
Dosanjh’s agents at THE·TEAM include Sam Hunt, James Rubin, Julia May and Stephanie Miles.  
  
The news follows last week’s reveal of the 2027 Coachella lineup with Dosanjh’s name listed high on the poster for Friday, April 9 and 16, just behind Dua Lipa, Madonna, Zara Larsson, Steve Lacy and JISOO.

Last week also saw the Indian superstar hit YouTube’s charts with “Pokito,” his collaboration with Daddy Yankee. The song debuted as the No. 1 Spanish-language music video on YouTube’s Music category, as well as trending No. 1 in 12 countries: United Arab Emirates, Australia, Canada, Costa Rica, Ecuador, Guatemala, Honduras, Nicaragua, Norway, Puerto Rico, Qatar and the United Kingdom.

And in September Dosanjh became the first South Asian artist to headline Wembley Stadium, capping the biggest summer of music in the iconic London venue’s history. [As previously reported](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/15/diljit-dosanjh-closes-out-wembley-stadiums-biggest-summer-of-music/), across 98 nights between June and September, Wembley Stadium hosted a record-breaking 34 concerts and welcomed more than 2.5 million music fans through its turnstiles.
