# Sting Returns to Caesars Palace With New ‘STING 3.0’ Las Vegas Residency

## Summary

Sting will launch a new "STING 3.0" Las Vegas residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace on March 4, 2027, with 11 shows split across March and October. General tickets go on sale October 17 through Ticketmaster and the venue box office, preceded by fan-club and Caesars Rewards presales.

### Key Points
- New "STING 3.0" residency at The Colosseum, Caesars Palace, opens March 4, 2027.
- 11 total performances: five in March, six in October.
- Trio format with guitarist Dominic Miller and drummer Chris Maas.
- Fan-club presale Oct. 14-16; Caesars Rewards/Live Nation presale Oct. 15-16.
- General onsale Oct. 17 via Ticketmaster and the box office.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sting-The-Colosseum.webp)

Sting will launch a new Las Vegas residency at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace on March 4, 2027, with 11 performances scheduled across March and October.

The residency marks Sting’s return to The Colosseum following his “My Songs” residency, which ran from 2021 through 2023 and included 22 sold-out performances. The new engagement will open with five shows in March before returning for six performances in October.

The “Sting 3.0” engagement will feature longtime guitarist Dominic Miller and drummer Chris Maas, whose credits include Mumford & Sons and Maggie Rogers. The three-piece lineup will perform songs from Sting’s solo career and his years with The Police, using the format developed for his “Sting 3.0” World Tour.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Saturday, October 17, at 10 a.m. Pacific time through Ticketmaster and The Colosseum box office.
