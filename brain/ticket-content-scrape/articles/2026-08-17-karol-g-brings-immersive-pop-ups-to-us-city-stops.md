# Karol G brings immersive pop-ups to US city stops

## Summary

Karol G has rolled out five immersive pop-up locations across US tour stops (New York, Miami, Dallas, El Paso, Los Angeles) tied to her "Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour," offering themed exhibitions, concessions, and merchandise around each show. Part of a broader trend of artists using multimedia pop-ups for fan immersion on tour routes.

### Key Points
- Karol G launched five city pop-ups accompanying her US tour dates.
- Pop-ups feature themed exhibits, a "passport" stamping activity, and merchandise tied to her album concept.
- First pop-up opened around her Sofi Stadium show.
- Reflects a broader trend of tour-linked immersive fan-experience activations.

## Transcript

The tour is the latest to use multimedia pop-up events to create fan immersion in cities on the tour route

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/karol-g.jpg)

Karol G

Karol G has begun a rollout of five immersive pop-up locations across the US to accompany her *Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour,* with the first having opened around her recent Sofi Stadium show this weekend.

The five venues will open in New York, Miami, Dallas, El Paso, and Los Angeles, each around the singer’s concerts in the cities. They offer concession stands, immersive exhibition-style tours, and merchandise shops.

The pop-ups include areas themed on global destinations referenced on the singer’s *Viajando Por El Mundo* album, letting visitors stamp ‘passports’ as they travel through them. Fans are also invited to stamp tour-themed T-shirts to create what organisers call a “one-of-a-kind interactive collectible”.

The first of the pop-ups to open was LA, coming as Karol G played the city’s Sofi Stadium this weekend (15 August). The show saw the singer bring Bruno Mars onstage to sing their collaboration *Still*, with the performance filmed for the song’s official music video.

“I’m so excited for my fans to step inside the world of ‘Viajando Por El Mundo’ and feel like they’re traveling through all the beautiful places I imagined when I made the song,” Karol G said. “With these pop-ups, they’ll get to visit every stop and take a little piece of the journey home.”

> “With these pop-ups, they’ll get to visit every stop and take a little piece of the journey home.”

Karol G is the latest artist to explore pop-up experiences around concerts, a trend punctuated by [Justin Bieber utilising a pop-up shop at Coachella](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/justin-bieber-rakes-in-5m-in-coachella-merch-sales/) in April, with reported gross of around $5m. Prior to this, artists including Sabrina Carpenter, Dua Lipa, and Coldplay staged takeover events around London shows in 2025.

The strategy has also been deployed more widely in the K-pop genre, with BTS recently unveiling installations in around their [London Tottenham Hotspur Stadium concerts](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/hybe-hails-tottenham-attendance-record-with-two-night-bts-stand/) which included a themed River Thames boat experience, a pop‑up at Outernet, and a British Museum gallery trail linking Korean artefacts to themes from their album *Arirang*.

The singer’s ongoing tour will see her take in 39 stadiums over the next year. It follows her two [headline performances at Coachella earlier this month](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/coachellas-25th-anniversary-edition-proves-momentous/)  and marks her first global outing since the 2023–24  [*Mañana Será Bonito* World Tour](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/06/karol-g-scores-largest-latin-music-european-tour/), which sold more than 2.3 million tickets across 62 shows.

The run opened on 24 July at Chicago’s Soldier Field Stadium, followed by dates in Toronto’s Rogers Stadium (29 July), Las Vegas’ Allegiant Stadium (7 August), before SoFi Stadium this weekend. The tour will next visit San Francisco’s Levi’s Stadium (21 August), and Seattle’s Lumen Field (26 August).

A Latin American stretch begins 6 November at Estadio BBVA in Monterrey, continuing through Mexico City, Costa Rica, Colombia, Ecuador, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Brazil, the Dominican Republic, and Puerto Rico between November 2026 and February 2027.
