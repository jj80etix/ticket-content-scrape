# PRODI, In Partnership With UdeG, To Develop New Soccer Stadium In Guadalajara

## Summary

Ownership group PRODI unveiled plans for a new 40,000+ capacity stadium in Guadalajara, "Ciudad de UdeG," developed with Universidad de Guadalajara and designed by Populous, to house soccer clubs Atlas F.C. and Leones Negros plus 200+ events a year.

### Key Points
- ~$5 billion pesos (~$270M USD) investment, funded entirely by PRODI with no public funds; targets a 2030 first match.
- Mixed-use site (~40 acres) includes a community park, retail/dining, 56 suites, 16 boxes and 3,600+ premium seats.
- Comes weeks after OCESA launched the rival Coliseo GNP Seguros in the same city.
- Designed by Populous (also behind Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and Yankee Stadium); walking distance to the local train station.

Guadalajara will soon have yet another large venue at its disposal. Weeks after OCESA launched Coliseo GNP Seguros, the owners of storied soccer club Atlas F.C. unveiled plans for a new stadium in Jalisco’s capital.

Ownership group PRODI (Grupo Promoter de Desarrollo e Infraestructura) is developing a 40,000-plus-capacity venue in partnership with Universidad de Guadalajara, and it will be designed by Populous, a global firm that has worked on major projects such as Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London and New York’s Yankee Stadium.

The mixed-use development, which is called Ciudad de UdeG, won’t only serve as the home of Atlas and Leones Negros, which is the university’s professional soccer team. The site within Universidad de Guadalajara is nearly 40 acres, and the plan for the complex is to make it a cultural hub for students and families. It will include tree-lined public spaces, including a community park, sports courts, and playgrounds. There will also be retail and dining spaces.

Concerts will be a major part of operations at the facility, and the ownership group plans to host more than 200 events annually, including soccer matches, cultural activities and other live events. The stadium will boast 56 private suites, 16 boxes and more than 3,600 premium, lower-tier and preferred seats, giving guests multiple options when attending events. It will also have plenty of space for activations and dining.

José Miguel Bejos, director of PRODI, said that they plan to invest nearly $5 billion Mexican pesos (more than $270 million USD) into the project with the university.

“Not a single peso of public funds will be invested in the stadium, and the university’s assets are protected,” he added. “We will be partners with the University of Guadalajara in a project designed to positively transform the surrounding area and generate value for the community for decades to come.

“For us, construction doesn’t end when a project is inaugurated; those who build stay involved and stand accountable,” Bejos said. “That is why we will work hand-in-hand with the Government of Jalisco, the Municipality of Zapopan, the University, and local residents to address challenges such as mobility and to integrate Ciudad UdeG into its surroundings. Today marks the start of this journey, grounded in dialogue, with the goal of playing the first match at the new stadium in 2030.”

The venue is also very accessible and within walking distance of the local train station.
