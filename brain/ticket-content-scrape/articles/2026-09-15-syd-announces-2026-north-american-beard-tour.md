# Syd Announces 2026 North American ‘Beard Tour’

## Summary

Syd announced the "Beard Tour," a 23-date North American run supporting her latest album Beard, promoted by Live Nation and kicking off Nov. 7 in Vancouver, wrapping Dec. 16 in Los Angeles.

### Key Points
- Citi cardmember presale begins Sept. 16; general on-sale starts Sept. 18.
- Tour includes two Los Angeles dates at Camp Flog Gnaw (Nov. 14-15).
- VIP packages available via VIP Nation, including pre-show soundcheck access.
- 23 dates span Vancouver, Portland, Seattle, Sacramento, LA, San Diego, Houston, Dallas, Austin, Atlanta, Charlotte, Raleigh, Brooklyn, DC, Philadelphia, Boston, Toronto, Chicago, Las Vegas, Phoenix, San Francisco, and LA again.

Syd has announced a tour to support her latest album, *Beard*. Live Nation will promote the tour, kicking off in Vancouver on Nov. 7 at the Commodore. The ‘Beard Tour’ will run for 23 dates, concluding on Dec. 16 in Los Angeles at The Wiltern. See full tour routing below.

Tickets will be available starting with a CITI presale (details below) on Sept. 16. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale beginning Sept. 18 at 10 a.m. local time. For full tour routing and details on upcoming dates, see below or visit [https://www.syds.world/tour](https://www.syds.world/tour).

Citi cardmembers will have access to presale tickets beginning Sept. 16 at 10 a.m. local time until Sept. 17 at 10 p.m. local time through the Citi Entertainment program. For complete presale details, visit www.citientertainment.com.

The tour will also offer a variety of different VIP packages and experiences for fans to take their concert experience to the next level. Packages vary but include premium tickets, access to the exclusive pre-show VIP soundcheck with SYD, exclusive VIP gift item, early entry & more. VIP package contents vary based on the offer selected. For more information, visit vipnation.com.
