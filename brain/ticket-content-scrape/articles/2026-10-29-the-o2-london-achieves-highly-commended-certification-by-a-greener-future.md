# The O2, London Achieves ‘Highly Commended’ Certification By A Greener Future

## Summary

The O2 in London achieved a "Highly Commended" rating in the Greener Arena Certification from A Greener Future, its highest sustainability accreditation to date. The venue has cut carbon emissions per show by 27% and per fan by 26% since 2022.

### Key Points
- Rating marks a rise of two accreditation tiers since joining the program in 2022
- Reductions driven by energy-efficiency upgrades, a renewable-electricity PPA with Ecotricity, electric on-site vehicles and reduced food-service waste
- 27% reduction in carbon emissions per show, 26% reduction per fan since 2022
- Follows The O2's 2038 naming-rights extension with AEG

The O2 has achieved its highest sustainability accreditation to date, receiving a “Highly Commended” rating in the prestigious Greener Arena Certification from independent sustainability specialists A Greener Future (AGF).

The certification recognizes The O2’s continued commitment to environmental and social sustainability and marks a major milestone in its sustainability journey, having risen two accreditation tiers since first entering the program in 2022.

The O2’s efforts so far have resulted “in a 27% reduction in carbon emissions per show and a 26% reduction in emissions per fan since 2022,” by the building’s own admission.

**[The O2 Until 2038: AEG & O2 Extend Landmark Naming Rights Partnership](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/07/the-o2-until-2038-aeg-o2-extend-landmark-naming-rights-partnership/)**

The reductions have been driven through a series of initiatives including energy efficiency improvements led by The O2’s facilities team, the introduction of an industry-leading renewable electricity Power Purchase Agreement (PPA) with Ecotricity, the transition to electric on-site vehicles and a 31.5% reduction in food service emissions since 2019.

The venue has also made substantial progress in waste management, increasing its recycling and composting rate by 15% in 2026 through closer collaboration with on-site tenants, enhanced waste segregation practices and a reduction in overall waste streams.

The certifications are awarded by A Greener Future (AGF), an independent not-for-profit organization that assesses festivals, venues and events against a wide range of environmental and social sustainability criteria. Assessments cover areas including energy use, waste management, travel, food and beverage operations, procurement and community engagement.

As The O2 approaches its 20th birthday in 2027, the certification marks another significant milestone in the venue’s continued evolution. Welcoming more than 11 million visitors each year, The O2 has established itself not only as one of the world’s most successful live entertainment destinations, but also as a recognized industry leader in sustainable venue operations.

“We’ve come a long way since beginning this journey in 2022, and while we’re proud of what has been achieved, we’re equally focused on continuing to drive progress and finding new ways to reduce emissions, minimise waste and support a more sustainable future for the live events industry.”

We’re really happy to see The O2 achieve “Highly Commended” in the Greener Arena Certification, showing continuous improvements and an unwavering commitment to protect the environment, to care for their staff and visitors, and to be good neighbours in their community. Congratulations to the whole team.”
