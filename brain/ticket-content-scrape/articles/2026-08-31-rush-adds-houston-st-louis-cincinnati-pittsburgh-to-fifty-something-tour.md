# Rush Adds Houston, St. Louis, Cincinnati, Pittsburgh To ‘Fifty Something’ Tour

## Summary

Rush has added four U.S. dates — Houston, St. Louis, Cincinnati, and Pittsburgh — to its "Fifty Something" comeback tour for October and November, continuing the multi-year, multi-continent run with new drummer Anika Nilles.

### Key Points
- New dates added in Houston, St. Louis, Cincinnati, and Pittsburgh for Oct/Nov.
- Part of the ongoing "Fifty Something" tour featuring Alex Lifeson, Geddy Lee, and new drummer Anika Nilles.
- Tour has already spanned multiple continents.

## Transcript

![IMG 9303](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_9303.jpg)

IN THE LIMELIGHT: Rush’s Alex Lifeson and Geddy Lee during the “Fifty Something Tour.” (Photo: Richard Sibbald)

In the middle of its triumphant, multi-year and multi-continent “Fifty Something” comeback tour with new drummer Anika Nilles, prog-rock legends Rush have added four U.S. dates for October and November, ensuring fans in multiple markets get a chance to see the influential band that many thought would never tour again.

Rush, led by frontman and bassist Geddy Lee and guitarist Alex Lifeson, will play now make stops at Houston’s Toyota Center on Oct. 1, St Louis’s Enterprise Center on Oct. 21, Cincinnati’s Heritage Bank Center on Oct. 23 and Pittsburgh’s PPG Paints Arena on Nov. 15.

“When we initially booked this tour we didn’t know how well we would hold up to the rigours of the road – so we played it safe with lots of days off to recover – but life is nothing if not surprising and we’re doing fine and having a blast – so why not try to hit a few more of the cities that have always treated us so well,” Lifeson and Lee shared in the announcement.

Tickets and VIP for these four new dates will go on sale on Friday, Sept. 4 at 10 a.m. local time with presales beginning Tue, Sept. 1.
