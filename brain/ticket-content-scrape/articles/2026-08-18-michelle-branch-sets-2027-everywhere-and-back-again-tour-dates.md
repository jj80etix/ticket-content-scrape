# Michelle Branch Sets 2027 ‘Everywhere and Back Again’ Tour Dates

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/michelle-branch-tour-cover.webp)

Michelle Branch has announced a new series of 2027 dates for her “Everywhere and Back Again Tour.”

The 2027 tour leg will begin February 25 at Charleston Music Hall in Charleston, South Carolina. Branch will then perform in several cities across the United States, including Atlanta, Houston, Los Angeles, St. Louis and New York. The tour is set to wrap up on March 27 at Franklin Music Hall in Philadelphia.

The shows will support her upcoming EP, “ *Everywhere and Back Again*,” which features reworked versions of songs from throughout her career.

Tickets will be available through a series of presales beginning this week. The artist presale starts Wednesday, August 19, at 10 a.m. local time. Live Nation and Ticketmaster presales will begin at noon local time the same day.

**| RELATED:** [**Michelle Branch Announces Fall 2026 Tour, Reworked Classics EP**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/michelle-branch-announces-fall-2026-tour-reworked-classics-ep/) **|**

Local and venue presales will start Thursday, August 20, at 10 a.m. local time. All presales will end at 10 p.m. that night. The general public can purchase tickets beginning Friday, August 21, at 10 a.m. Eastern Time through Ticketmaster.

Previously announced 2026 dates are set to begin on September 27 in Seattle at The Showbox. From there, Branch is scheduled to make stops in cities such as San Francisco, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Boston, New York City and Chicago before wrapping up the first leg of the tour on November 13 in Lexington at Manchester Music Hall.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Branch’s official website](http://michellebranch.com).
