# AEG acquires Auguri Productions to ‘strengthen’ French market growth

## Summary

AEG Presents has acquired French artist development and production company Auguri Productions, aiming to "deepen its roots in France" and connect Auguri's roster to AEG's international touring network. The deal is the latest in a string of AEG European partnership moves; most of the article is behind IQ's paywall, so financial and operational details beyond the headline are limited.

### Key Points
- AEG Presents acquires Auguri Productions (France) to access overseas live opportunities for Auguri's artist roster.
- Framed as part of a broader pattern of AEG European partnership/investment moves.
