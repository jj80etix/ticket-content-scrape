# Man Charged with Second-Degree Assault After Urinating on Family at Morgan Wallen Concert

## Summary

A North Carolina man was charged with four counts of second-degree assault and public urination after allegedly urinating on a family, including an 8-year-old, during a Morgan Wallen concert at Baltimore's M&T Bank Stadium; he is awaiting extradition to Maryland.

### Key Points
- Jayson Reed, 33, faces four counts of second-degree assault plus public urination and related charges.
- Incident occurred at a Morgan Wallen show at M&T Bank Stadium, Baltimore; victim's family included an 8-year-old.
- Police initially said they couldn't arrest the suspect on-scene but reopened the case after identifying additional witnesses.

A North Carolina man has been arrested after urinating on a family during a Morgan Wallen concert, according to Maryland police.

The Maryland Transportation Authority Police noted in a [news release](https://www.facebook.com/search/top?q=mdta%20police) that Jayson Reed, 33, has been charged with four counts of second-degree assault, public urination, and other related offenses. He was arrested by deputies in Wilson County following an investigation by Maryland police and consultation with Baltimore City State’s Attorney’s Office, which led to an arrest warrant.

Per [*TMZ*](https://www.tmz.com/2026/07/20/morgan-wallen-concert-pee-incident/), Troy Grevelding was in the audience for Wallen’s concert at M&T Bank Stadium in Baltimore with his wife, brother, and eight-year-old niece when a man behind them allegedly began urinating on him and his family. Grevelding told police that the man appeared intoxicated and belligerent, and he was exposing himself. The incident led to a brief confrontation before Grevelding alerted security and left the concert.

Initially, officers told Grevelding that they could not arrest the alleged offender because they had not personally witnessed the incident. However, the Maryland Transportation Authority Police opened an investigation into the incident and “identified witnesses who were not present during the initial on-scene investigation.”
