# Coastal Country Jam Announces Huntington Beach Return With Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney

## Summary

Coastal Country Jam returns to Huntington Beach, California, for its May 22-23, 2027 edition after relocating to Long Beach in 2023, headlined by Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney. Producer Activated Events has spent several years working to bring the festival back to its original host city.

### Key Points
- Dates: May 22-23, 2027, back in Huntington Beach after a stint in Long Beach since 2023.
- Headliners: Luke Bryan (May 22), Megan Moroney (May 23).
- Supporting lineup includes Thomas Rhett, Parker McCollum, Josh Ross, Ashley Cooke, Corey Kent, and others.
- Produced by Activated Events, led by founder/CEO Steve Thacher.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Coastal-Country-Jam.webp)

Coastal Country Jam will return to Huntington Beach, California, on May 22 and 23, 2027, with Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney headlining. The festival’s producer, Activated Events, announced the move back to its original city after relocating the event to Long Beach in 2023.

The two-day lineup also includes Thomas Rhett, Parker McCollum, Josh Ross, Ashley Cooke and Corey Kent. Organizers have announced 13 performers, with another act still to be added to the opening-day schedule.

Bryan will headline May 22, with Rhett, Ross, Cooke, Myles Morgan and Joe Peters also scheduled to perform. The artist yet to be announced will join that bill.

Moroney will close the festival on May 23. McCollum, Avery Anna, Kent, John Morgan, Colin Lynch and Presley Tennant round out the second-day lineup.

The return to Huntington Beach follows several years of efforts to bring the festival back, according to Steve Thacher, founder and CEO of Activated Events. The independent live event company announced the location change as part of its plans for the 2027 edition.

“We’ve been working for the last couple of years to bring Coastal Country Jam back to where it all started, and it feels good to be returning to Huntington Beach with Luke Bryan and Megan Moroney,” Thacher said.

Thacher also addressed the festival’s time in Long Beach, where it moved in 2023.

“We really enjoyed our time in Long Beach, and we’re grateful to everyone who joined us there. We’re back, and we can’t wait to see everyone on the sand.”

In addition to the music, Activated Events announced several attractions for the festival grounds. The plans include a line dancing hall, barbecue cooking demonstrations, themed beverage bars and recreational activities.
