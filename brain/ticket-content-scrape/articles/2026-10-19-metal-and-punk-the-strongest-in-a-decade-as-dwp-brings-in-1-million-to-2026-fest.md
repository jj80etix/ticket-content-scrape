# Metal and punk the ‘strongest in a decade’ as DWP brings in 1 million to 2026 festivals

## Summary

Danny Wimmer Presents reports its 2026 festival season drew more than one million attendees, with GM Chamie McCurry citing metal and punk demand as the "strongest in a decade." Sacramento's Aftershock Festival has grown roughly 25% over five years and reached its largest attendance yet, as DWP tests new ways to capitalize on the genres' growing draw, including internationally.

### Key Points
- DWP's 2026 festivals drew over 1 million total attendees.
- Metal/punk demand described as strongest in a decade.
- Aftershock Festival (Sacramento) grew ~25% over five years, hitting a record attendance this year.
- DWP exploring new avenues to capitalize on genre growth, including global expansion.
