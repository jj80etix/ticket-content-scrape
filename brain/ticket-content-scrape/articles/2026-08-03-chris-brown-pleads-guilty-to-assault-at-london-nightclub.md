# Chris Brown Pleads Guilty to Assault at London Nightclub

## Summary

Chris Brown pleaded guilty to an assault charge stemming from a 2023 incident at a London nightclub, changing his earlier not-guilty plea in exchange for prosecutors dropping more serious charges. He faces sentencing in October and remains on tour with Usher across North America through December.

### Key Points
- Plea entered at Southwark Crown Court; charges of attempted grievous bodily harm and possession of an offensive weapon were dropped in exchange.
- Co-defendant and vocal coach Omololu Akinlolu also pleaded guilty; both stand trial in October and face sentencing October 26.
- Underlying incident: a February 19, 2023 altercation at Tape nightclub in which Brown allegedly struck producer Abraham Diaw with a bottle.
- Brown faces a maximum of three years in prison; he had been free on $6.7 million bail, which allowed him to tour Europe and North America in 2025-26.
- Brown is currently co-headlining "The R&B (Raymond & Brown) Tour" with Usher, running June through December 2026.

Chris Brown pleaded guilty to an assault that took place at a London nightclub in 2023.

The rapper, who previously denied the charges, changed his plea during a hearing at London’s Southwark Crown Court last week. In exchange for his plea, prosecutors dropped charges of assault, attempting to cause grievous bodily harm, and possession of an offensive weapon. His friend and vocal coach Omololu Akinlolu also pled guilty.

Both men are set to stand trial in October.

The charges stem from an incident in 2023 that took place at Tape nightclub, where prosecutors claimed Brown attacked producer Abraham Diaw with a bottle. Additionally, prosecutors claimed Akinlolu joined in and “punched the victim in the head.” Prosecutors showed the fight caught on surveillance cameras.
