# ESNS reveals dedicated young professional track Next Gen

## Summary

Dutch conference and showcase Eurosonic Noorderslag (ESNS) announced a new "Next Gen" track dedicated to young industry professionals, running throughout its 41st edition in Groningen (13-16 January 2027). The track adds sessions, meetings, and networking aimed at newer entrants, alongside a revamped Wednesday schedule. Full article is paywalled beyond the opening paragraphs.

### Key Points
- ESNS 2027 runs 13-16 January in Groningen, its 41st edition.
- New "Next Gen" track runs throughout the four-day conference and showcase.
- Last year's event drew roughly 40,000 industry professionals and fans.
