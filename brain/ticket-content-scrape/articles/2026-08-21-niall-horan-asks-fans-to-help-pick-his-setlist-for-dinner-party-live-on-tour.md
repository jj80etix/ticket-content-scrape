# Niall Horan Asks Fans to Help Pick His Setlist for ‘Dinner Party Live on Tour’

## Summary

Niall Horan is inviting fans to help build his setlist for the "Dinner Party Live on Tour" via an Instagram callout, citing the difficulty of choosing from four albums' worth of material. A fan-engagement PR story with no ticketing-platform relevance.

### Key Points
- Niall Horan asks fans via Instagram to help pick songs for his tour setlist.
- Framed as a response to having four albums of material to draw from.
- Artist-driven fan-engagement stunt; no ticketing content.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/niall-horan.jpg)

Have you ever wanted to build a dream setlist for your favorite artist? Well, now, Niall Horan is giving fans that opportunity.

“Lovers!” Horan wrote in an Instagram story, speaking to his fans. “I’m almost done with the tour setlist but it’s gotten so much tougher with 4 albums now so I need your help.”

He directed fans to an interactive page, allowing concertgoers to build their dream setlist. Fans can choose up to 20 songs they want to hear on tour; the site allows them to search for tracks from Horan’s four records: *Flicker*, *Heartbreak Weather*, *The Show*, and *Dinner Party*. Fans can click on a box next to the song title to add it to their 20-song selection, then,, they are prompted to select the show they are set to attend.
