# Sam Fender returns to live with Proper Massive Stadium Tour

## Summary

Sam Fender announced the "Proper Massive Stadium Tour" for 2027 — nine UK & Ireland headline shows across six cities plus a headline slot at Belgium's Rock Werchter — his biggest headline run to date and first live dates since 2025, including his largest-ever shows at Wembley Stadium (cap. 90,000) on June 18 & 20.

### Key Points
- 2027 "Proper Massive Stadium Tour": 9 UK/Ireland dates across 6 cities, plus a Rock Werchter (Belgium) headline slot.
- Tour opens with two Newcastle hometown shows at St. James' Park, June 3 & 5.
- Includes his largest-ever shows: two nights at Wembley Stadium (cap. 90,000), June 18 & 20.
- First live shows since 2025; biggest headline run of his career to date.
