# Eric Prydz Headlining Club Darc’s First Show Of The 2026 Fall Season

## Summary

Goldenvoice's Club Darc series announced Eric Prydz will headline its 2026 fall-season opener on Halloween night (Oct. 31) at Pier 48 in San Francisco, with support from Tiga and a Kelly Lee Owens DJ set; tickets go on sale next week following artist and venue presales.

### Key Points
- Eric Prydz headlines Club Darc's fall-season opener, Oct. 31, at Shed A, Pier 48, San Francisco.
- Presales: artist presale Aug. 20, venue presale one hour later, general on-sale Aug. 21.
- Club Darc's inaugural season sold out, with past lineups including Chris Lake, Peggy Gou and Underworld.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed-6.jpg)

Goldenvoice announced the first show of Club Darc’s fall season will be headlined by Eric Prydz, taking place on Halloween night, Oct. 31, at Shed A at Pier 48 in San Francisco.

Prydz makes his Club Darc debut, which will be part of his fall North American tour, and will have Tiga and a Kelly Lee Owens DJ set supporting him for the Bay Area show. The Grammy-nominated Swedish headliner is known for hits like “Call on Me,” “Pjanoo” and “Opus.”

Tickets go on sale next week, with the artist presale beginning Aug. 20 at 10 a.m. PT, followed by a venue presale one hour later. All presales end Aug. 21 at 8 a.m. PT, and the general onsale begins the same day at 10 a.m. PT. Visit [www.clubdarc.com](http://www.clubdarc.com) for more information.

Club Darc sold out its inaugural season, which featured EDM stars such as Chris Stussy, Chris Lake, Peggy Gou, Mau P, Kaskade, Michael Bibi, Underworld, Solomun and more.
