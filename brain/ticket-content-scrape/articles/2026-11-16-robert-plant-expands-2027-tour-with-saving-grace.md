# Robert Plant Expands 2027 Tour With Saving Grace

## Summary

Robert Plant and his band Saving Grace added more 2027 European tour dates following the digital release of their EP "All That Glitters." The expanded itinerary runs from two sold-out London shows in December through a September 2027 close in Yerevan, Armenia, with stops across the UK, continental Europe and Central Asia. Tickets are already on sale for the newly added dates.

### Key Points
- New dates added through Sept. 3, 2027 (Yerevan, Armenia), following December's sold-out Royal Albert Hall shows.
- Tour supports the EP "All That Glitters" and the band's 2025 self-titled album.
- Itinerary spans UK, Ireland, continental Europe, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan and Armenia.

NASHVILLE, TENNESSEE – SEPTEMBER 16: Robert Plant performs onstage during the 25th Annual Americana Honors & Awards at Ryman Auditorium on September 16, 2026 in Nashville, Tennessee. (Photo by Erika Goldring/Getty Images for Americana Music Association)

Robert Plant and his band Saving Grace have added more dates to their European itinerary following last month’s digital release of the EP *All That Glitters*.

The EP, which was previously released on vinyl for Record Store Day, as well as the band’s 2025 self-titled album, feature vocalist Suzi Dian “and a group of local musicians from the English countryside that Robert Plant calls home.”

An announcement from label Nonesuch notes that “The EP’s four tracks further explore the folk and Americana songs that Plant and the band love: the traditional tunes ‘The Blackest Crow’ and ‘Two Coats,’ Gillian Welch’s ‘Orphan Girl,’ and Bert Jansch’s ‘Poison.’ … This group of distinguished musicians—vocalist Suzi Dian, drummer Oli Jefferson, guitarist Tony Kelsey, banjo and string player Matt Worley, and cellist Barney Morse-Brown—explore the evolution of roots music both vintage and modern. The band members were drawn together by a shared love of roots music—blues, folk, gospel, country, and the sounds that lay in between.”

In September Plant was honored by the Americana Music Association with its Lifetime Achievement Award at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium and then the Led Zeppelin legend and his band hit the road for the “Up the Sharp End” U.S. tour.

Up next, Plant and Saving Grace have two sold-out shows booked at London’s Royal Albert Hall Dec. 2-3. The 2027 tour starts June 19 back in London at Hampton Court Palace. Dates are booked through a Sep 3 show at Karen Demirchyan Concert Hall in Yerevan, Armenia.
