# The Warning Announce ‘The Everything’s Falling World Tour’

## Summary

The Warning announced the "Everything's Falling World Tour," kicking off Feb. 5 with their first headlining stadium show at Mexico City's Estadio GNP Seguros, running through Europe and wrapping the North American leg May 23 in Boston. The band will also headline the 2026 NFL Mexico City Game halftime show on Nov. 22 ahead of the tour.

### Key Points
- General on-sale begins Sept. 18; artist presale runs Sept. 16-17, with additional local presales Sept. 17.
- European leg spans Madrid, Barcelona, Prague, Manchester, and London, ending April 2 at OVO Arena Wembley.
- North American leg runs 18 dates from Las Vegas (April 17) through Boston (May 23).
- Tour supports the band's fifth studio album, Everything's Falling, recorded in their hometown of Monterrey, Mexico.

THEWARNING TOUR fechas

The Warning have announced the ‘Everything’s Falling World Tour ’, set to begin on Feb. 5 with their first-ever headlining stadium show at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros. The band will then take their tour to Madrid, Barcelona, Prague, Manchester, and London, with their last show overseas concluding at the OVO Arena on April 2. The North American leg of the tour will be in Las Vegas, NV, at the Pearl Concert Theater at Palms Casino Resort on April 17. This leg of the tour will run for 18 dates before ending on May 23 in Boston, MA, at the MGM Music Hall at Fenway. But before the trio embarks on their world tour, the band is set to headline the halftime show, presented by American Express, at the 2026 NFL Mexico City Game between the Minnesota Vikings and San Francisco 49ers on Nov. 22. See full tour routing below.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Sept. 18 at 10 a.m. local time. Patreon presale and VIP packages begin Sept. 15 at 10 a.m. EDT / 7 a.m. PDT and run through Sept. 17 at 10 p.m. local time. Artist presale begins Sept. 16 at 10 a.m. local time and runs through Sept. 17 at 10 p.m. local time. Additional local presales will take place Sept. 17 from 10 a.m. to 10 p.m. local time. Canada and EU/UK will follow the same on-sale timeline. For tickets and to sign up for the artist pre-sale, visit [https://thewarningband.com/pages/tour](https://thewarningband.com/pages/tour).

The Warning recently released their fifth studio album, *Everything’s Falling*, out now via Lava/Republic Records. *Everything’s Falling* captures a period of growth, self-discovery, and resilience for Daniela “Dany” Villarreal, Paulina “Pau” Villarreal, and Alejandra “Ale” Villarreal, while showcasing the fearless songwriting and explosive musicianship at the core of the band. The album was recorded in their hometown of Monterrey, Mexico, with longtime producer Anton DeLost after the band made the conscious decision to step off the road in 2025 and focus fully on writing.
