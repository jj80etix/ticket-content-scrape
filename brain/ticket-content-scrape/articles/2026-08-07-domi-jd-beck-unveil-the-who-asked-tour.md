# DOMI & JD BECK Unveil The “Who Asked?” Tour

## Summary

Fusion jazz duo Domi & JD Beck announced the "Who Asked?" tour supporting their new album of the same name, kicking off Sept. 9 in Dallas. The trek runs 26 US dates before heading to Australia and Asia through November.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Sept. 9 at The Echo in Dallas, TX; 26 US dates before Australia (from Nov. 10) and Asia (from Nov. 17) legs.
- Supports new album *Who Asked?*, a 15-track follow-up to 2022's *Not Tight* with no guest features.
- Tickets on sale via the duo's official site.

Domi & JD Beck, the fusion jazz duo consisting of French keyboardist Domi Louna and American drummer JD Beck, are hitting the road this fall for the ‘Who Asked?’ tour.

The trek will begin in the United States on Sept. 9 at The Echo in Dallas, TX. After 26 dates in the US, the tour will head to Australia for three dates starting on Nov. 10, and then conclude in Asia, with that leg of the tour kicking off on Nov. 17. See full tour routing above. Tickets for the tour can be purchased [here](https://www.domiandjdbeck.com/tour/).

The new tour was put together to support the duo’s newly released album, *Who Asked?* Unlike their 2022 debut album, *Not Tight*, *Who Asked?* includes no special guests across 15 carefully arranged (and very busy) compositions. In an interview with *[NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/31/nx-s1-5867705/domi-jd-beck-talk-about-their-second-album-who-asked)*, the musicians talked about the concept of “time” being a through line on this project.

“I think it’s because we’re around a lot of people who care so much about age,” Domi said. “Personally, I think I’ve heard so much, like, oh, man, age, time is going so fast and stuff. And I don’t necessarily agree or think we should care about it that much. But it’s always lingering, you know? Also, we’re growing older because we started playing instruments so young.”
