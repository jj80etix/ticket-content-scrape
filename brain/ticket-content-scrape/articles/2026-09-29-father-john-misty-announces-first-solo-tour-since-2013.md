# Father John Misty Announces First Solo Tour Since 2013

## Summary

Father John Misty (Josh Tillman) announced his first solo tour since 2013, an eight-date run beginning December 9 in Boulder, Colorado and closing with two shows in Carmel-by-the-Sea, California on December 18-19, alongside a new single, "The X-panded Universe." Damien Jurado joins as support for the December dates.

### Key Points
- Tour spans Colorado, New Mexico, Arizona and California; Dec. 9 (Boulder) through Dec. 19 (Carmel-by-the-Sea).
- Closing shows are part of the fourth annual Big Sur Park School Benefit.
- New single released Sept. 29 via Sub Pop, his third single of 2026 following "The Old Law" and "The Payoff."
- Follows sixth studio album *Mahashmashana* (Nov. 2024); also has a previously announced Oct. 6 LA show at the Fonda Theatre.
- Presale begins Sept. 30 at 10 a.m. local time.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Father-John-Misty.webp)

Father John Misty has announced his first solo tour since 2013 and released a new single, “The X-panded Universe.”

The eight-date tour begins December 9 in Boulder, Colorado, at Boulder Theatre and ends with two shows in Carmel-by-the-Sea, California, on December 18 and 19. Damien Jurado will join the December dates as a special guest.

The singer-songwriter, whose real name is Josh Tillman, is set to perform in Colorado, New Mexico, Arizona and California. Following the opening show at Boulder Theatre, he will perform in Fort Collins, Santa Fe, Tucson, Napa and Grass Valley. The two closing performances at Sunset Cultural Center are part of the fourth annual Big Sur Park School Benefit.

Tillman also has a previously announced solo show at the Fonda Theatre in Los Angeles on October 6, with Omar Velasco as the guest. That performance falls ahead of the newly announced December tour.
