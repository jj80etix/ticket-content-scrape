# Mastering AI in ticketing: Vivenu on connecting and activating data

## Summary

In a promoted/sponsored IQ piece, vivenu's VP of global partnerships, Keal Blache, argues that owning ticketing data is only the first step — the real payoff of AI in ticketing comes from connecting and activating that data operationally. The article opens with an illustrative example: a box-office manager asking vivenu's AI assistant why section 118 sales are tracking behind last year and getting an instant, live-sales-data-driven answer that feeds directly into a targeted marketing response.

### Key Points
- Framing: "owning your ticketing data was the easy part... now, how do you best connect and activate it" — positions AI value as contingent on integration/activation, not just data ownership.
- Concrete example given: a natural-language query about underperforming section sales answered instantly from live sales data, triggering a marketing response.
- Piece is promotional/sponsored content from vivenu, published via IQ Magazine's "Promoted By IQ" content arm.
- Full article is paywalled beyond the opening anecdote — no further technical or ROI detail is available in the harvested content.

### Highlights
- Example use case: AI assistant answers "why is section 118 behind last year?" from live sales data in seconds.
- Framing explicitly separates "owning data" from "activating data" as the harder, more valuable AI problem.

### Industry Problem
Ticketing platforms are increasingly expected to offer conversational/AI-assistant access to live operational data (sales pacing, inventory) for internal staff like box office managers — not just customer-facing chatbots or dynamic pricing — a benchmark for what "AI in ticketing" is expected to mean.

### Proposed Solution
Useful competitive benchmark for Etix Assistant: the concrete use case (natural-language query → live sales-data answer → triggers a marketing action) is a good pattern to compare against Etix Assistant's current capabilities for internal/operator-facing AI features, not just patron-facing ones.
