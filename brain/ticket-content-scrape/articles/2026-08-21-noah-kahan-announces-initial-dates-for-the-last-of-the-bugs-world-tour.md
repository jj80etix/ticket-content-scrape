# Noah Kahan Announces Initial Dates For ‘The Last Of The Bugs World Tour’

## Summary

Noah Kahan has announced initial dates for "The Last Of The Bugs World Tour," following the record-setting run of his "The Great Divide World Tour." Standard tour-announcement news with no ticketing-platform angle.

### Key Points
- Noah Kahan announces initial dates for "The Last Of The Bugs World Tour."
- Follows the record-breaking "The Great Divide World Tour."
- Pure tour-announcement news.

Noah Kahan has been busy setting records during “The Great Divide World Tour” and now he’s got more gigs lined up with the initial routing revealed for what he’s calling “The Last Of The Bugs World Tour.”  
  
Last weekend the acclaimed singer/songwriter played his biggest headline show to date with 65,000 fans filling the Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California. This followed a historic moment in July as Kahan became the first artist ever to headline four consecutive sold-out nights at Boston’s Fenway Park.

Kahan has been supporting his fourth studio album, *The Great Divide.* The new tour takes its name from the expanded edition of the LP subtitled *The Last of the Bugs.*

Promising that more tour dates are to come, the initial routing for “The Last Of The Bugs World Tour” takes place in July 2027 including stops at Glasgow’s Hampden Park, Dublin’s Aviva Stadium and London’s Wembley Stadium.

Kanah announced the news on his Instagram on Thursday, saying: “We’ll be heading to Glasgow, Dublin, and London to play some big old rooms for the bugs across the Atlantic Ocean on The Last Of The Bugs World Tour in 2027.

“You can sign up on my tour site for the presale on Tuesday, August 25th at 10am local time. All tickets will be on sale Friday, August 28th at 10am local time.

“This is only the first of the last of the bugs… stay tuned for more”
