# ‘Heathers: The Musical’ Moves Up Off-Broadway Closing Date

## Summary

The Off-Broadway revival of "Heathers: The Musical" will close early, on Oct. 11 instead of the previously announced Nov. 8, with no reason given for the change; a North American tour launches May 2027 in Baltimore.

### Key Points
- The revival began performances June 22, 2025 and opened July 10 at New World Stages.
- Cast includes Isabella Esler as Veronica and John Cardoza as J.D.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Heathers-the-musical.webp)

The Off-Broadway revival of “Heathers: The Musical” will close earlier than previously announced, ending its run at New World Stages on October 11.

The production had previously extended performances through November 8. A reason for the revised closing date was not announced. Following the New York engagement, “Heathers” will launch a North American tour in May 2027, beginning in Baltimore.

The revival began performances on June 22, 2025, and officially opened on July 10. The musical features a book, music and lyrics by Kevin Murphy and Laurence O’Keefe. Andy Fickman directs the current revival, with choreography by Gary Lloyd and additional choreography by Stephanie Klemons.

Isabella Esler stars as Veronica, opposite John Cardoza as J.D. The principal cast also includes Zan Berube as Heather Chandler, Morgan Dudley as Heather Duke, Ava DeMary as Heather McNamara, Anneliese van der Pol as Ms. Fleming and Veronica’s mother, Aniya Thompson as Martha Dunnstock, Chris Marsh Clark as Ram Sweeney and Nicholas A. Wilkinson as Kurt Kelly.
