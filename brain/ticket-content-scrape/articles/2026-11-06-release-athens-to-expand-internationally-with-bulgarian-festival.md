# Release Athens to expand internationally with Bulgarian festival

## Summary

Release Athens, the long-running Greek festival, will expand internationally for the first time with Release Sofia, launching in Bulgaria in 2027 at Sofia Live Park. The new edition will carry over the Athens event's concept and programming philosophy, with lineup and dates still to be announced.

### Key Points
- New festival: Release Sofia, launching 2027 at Sofia Live Park, Bulgaria.
- First international expansion for the decade-old Release Athens brand.
- Venue sits within Vasil Levski Airport park, one of the region's newest festival sites.
- Lineup and specific dates not yet announced.
