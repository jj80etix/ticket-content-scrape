# Rolling Loud Back To Cali: NOS Events Center, San Bernardino, Dec. 2027

## Summary

Rolling Loud returns to California with a new three-day festival at the 120-acre NOS Events Center in San Bernardino, Dec. 10-12, 2027, its first California edition in three years. Presales begin Sept. 24, alongside confirmations of Rolling Loud Florida (Orlando, May 2027) and Rolling Loud India (Dec. 2026), plus a "Rolling Loud: The Movie" theatrical release Oct. 2.

### Key Points
- Rolling Loud California returns Dec. 10-12, 2027, at NOS Events Center, San Bernardino (new venue, 120 acres).
- First California edition in three years; festival expands to a third day.
- Ticket presales begin Sept. 24; Rolling Loud Florida (Orlando) and Rolling Loud India also confirmed.
- "Rolling Loud: The Movie" hits theaters Oct. 2.

Rolling Loud is returning to California, with a three-day festival set for NOS Events Center in San Bernardino on Dec. 10-12, 2027.

Ticket presales are set to begin Thursday, Sept. 24, with various ticket packages and VIP options available.

Having taken the last two years off in the state, the taste-making hip-hop festival returns to California, 10 years since its first West Coast event took place in the city.

New for California along with the new venue, the sprawling, 120-acre NOS Event Center is a third day of Rolling Loud.

“California has always been a huge part of Rolling Loud’s story, and it feels good to be back,” said Matt Zingler, CEO and Co-Founder of Rolling Loud. “We’ve built an incredible community in Southern California over the years, and that’s something we never take for granted. We’re excited to bring three days of Rolling Loud to the NOS Events Center and give California something to remember.”

Also confirmed are with Rolling Loud Florida, returning to Orlando May 7-9 after this year’s event, and Rolling Loud India, set for Dec. 13-14 before the end of 2026.

Also of note, Rolling Loud makes its way to the big screen with “Rolling Loud: The Movie,” hitting theaters Oct. 2, and with a cast of heavy hitters including director Jeremy Garelick and starring Owen Wilson, Matt Rife, Christine Ko, Sexyy Red, Ski Mask The Slump God, Ty Dolla $ign, Henry Winkler, Christian Convery and Travis Scott.

Although too early for a lineup, Rolling Loud promises to “continue its run as the world’s largest Hip-Hop festival” while “showcasing the most exciting names in Rap while providing a global platform for the next generation of innovative and groundbreaking talent.”
