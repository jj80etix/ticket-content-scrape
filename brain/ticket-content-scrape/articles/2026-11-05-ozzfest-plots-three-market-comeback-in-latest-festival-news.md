# OzzFest plots three-market comeback in latest festival news

## Summary

OzzFest will return in 2027 with UK, US, and Mexico stops, led by Sharon Osbourne curating in memory of Ozzy Osbourne, who passed away in 2025. The roundup also covers new announcements from Resurrection Fest, Electric Castle, Rock For People, Quilmes Rock, and other festivals.

### Key Points
- OzzFest 2027 dates: Milton Keynes (July 10), San Bernardino's Glen Helen Amphitheater (Sept. 11), and Mexico City's Estadio GNP Seguros (Sept. 18, OzzFest's Mexico debut).
- Live Nation-backed; tickets go on sale Nov. 6; lineup TBA.
- Last held annually 1996–2018, concluding at the Kia Forum.

## Transcript

Plus: Announcements from Resurrection Fest, Electric Castle, Rock For People, Quilmes Rock, Sand In My Boots, Flytime Fest, and more...

![Ozzy Osbourne](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Ozzy-Osbourne-©-Ross-Halfin.png)

Ozzy Osbourne

Ozzy and Sharon Osbourne’s touring hard rock festival OzzFest will make its return next year, with organisers announcing UK, US, and Mexico stops today, leading the latest slate of festival announcements.

The Live Nation-backed event will first take place at Milton Keynes’ National Bowl on 10 July 2027, a change in location from the originally planned Villa Park in Birmingham, where Osbourne played his ultimate *Back To The Beginning* show before passing away in 2025.

The UK stop will be followed by a return to its 1996 birthplace, the Glen Helen Amphitheater in San Bernardino, California, on 11 September. OzzFest will then head to Mexico for the first time, to be staged at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros on 18 September.

“Ozzy was the first to launch his own travelling metal festival, and Ozzfest will always remain an important part of Ozzy’s legacy,” says Sharon, who will curate the event.

Lineup details are to be announced, with ticket sales starting tomorrow (6 November).

The touring event was held almost annually from 1996 to 2018, with the last edition staged at Inglewood’s Kia Forum.

**Resurrection Fest  
**Spain’s biggest rock festival will bring Mötley Crüe, Parkway Drive, Evanescence, and Lorna Shore as headliners for its 22nd edition next year.

They will be joined by names like Enter Shikari, Cradle of Filth, Fever 333, Leprous, Blood For Blood, Speed, King Buffalo, Daughtry, Coldrain, and Kylesa.

The event will run from 30 June-3 July in Viveiro.

**Electric Castle**  
Romania’s Electric Castle has revealed its first names for 2027, including Limp Bizkit, Sombr, 5 Seconds of Summer, Noisia, and Joey Valence & Brae.

The 24-hour event will return to Bánffy Castle in Bonțida from 14-18 July, with dozens of additional acts to be announced.

**Rock for People  
**Adding to its previously announced headliner pool of blink-182 and Faith No More, Parkway Drive will also take over Czechia’s Rock for People in 2027.

Organisers announced 45 new names for next year’s event, including Evanescence, Simple Plan, Bastille, Kreator, Carpenter Brut, Joost, Basht., Cowboy Hunters, Rico Nasty, The Snuts, and many more.

The event returns to Hradci Králové’s Park 360 from 2-5 June.

**Quilmes Rock  
**Argentina’s premier rock festival Quilmes Rock has announced its programme for 2027, to be led by Abasonicos, Divididos, Fito Páez, Jet, Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, Ratones Paranoicos, Los Auténticos Caligaris, Jorge Drexler, Las Pelotas, and Henry & La Palangana.

The Pop Art-led festival will return to Buenos Aires from 16-18 April.

**Sand in My Boots  
**Morgan Wallen’s curated Sand in My Boots festival will return in 2027, with Wallen, Ella Langley, and Kings of Leon leading the multi-genre bill.

Also lined up to perform are country acts including Koe Wetzel, Cole Swindell, Avery Anna, Midland, Gavin Adcock, Kameron Marlowe, Hudson Westbrook, Carter Faith, along with hip-hop and electronic names like Nelly, Gucci Mane, Rick Ross, Juicy J, Flo Rida, Kygo, Empire of the Sun, and Third Eye Blind.

The AEG Presents-backed affair is set for 21-23 May on the beaches of Gulf Shores, Alabama.

**Flytime Fest  
**Latto, Sexyy Red, and A Boogie Wit Da Hoodie are lined up to perform at this year’s Flytime Fest in Nigeria.

The three-day event is set for 22-24 December at Lagos’ Eko Convention Centre, West Africa’s largest multipurpose entertainment destination.

**Sydney Summertime  
**Diana Ross is the latest name confirmed for the new Sydney Summertime series, set to be a market exclusive for the star.

She will headline the show on 5 March, with Groove Armada (DJ set) joining in support. The concert, which will take place on the eve of the Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras Parade, marks Diana Ross’s first Sydney performance in more than two decades.

The Strokes and Kings of Leon are also confirmed for the Black Peach Touring-led series, which will be staged at Sydney’s Centennial Park.

**Open’er Festival**  
Nine names have been added to Alter Art’s Open’er Festival: Robyn, Royal Blood, Foals, Suki Waterhouse, Bleachers, Mike D 5D, Dinosaur Jr., POND and Twisted Teens.

They join previously announced headliners Tame Impala, The Chemical Brothers, RAYE, and The Neighbourhood.

The festival will take place from June 30-July 3 in Gdynia, Poland.

**Orange Warsaw  
**Another Alter Art event, Orange Warsaw, has added Christina Aguleria, Tove Lo, and David Kushner to its lineup, joining previously announced headliners Katseye.

The Polish event returns on 13-14 August at Warsaw’s Służewiec Horse Racetrack.

**Love Saves the Day  
**Four Tet, Armand Van Helden, and Damian Marley lead the first wave of artists announced for the UK’s Love Saves the Day.

They will be joined by acts like hy FX, Groove Armada, Oppidan, MPH, and ALISHA, with more to be confirmed for the festival at Bristol’s Ashton Court, set for 29-30 May.
