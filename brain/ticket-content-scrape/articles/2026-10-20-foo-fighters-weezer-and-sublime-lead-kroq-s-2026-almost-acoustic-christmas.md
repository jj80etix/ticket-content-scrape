# Foo Fighters, Weezer and Sublime Lead KROQ’s 2026 Almost Acoustic Christmas

## Summary

Foo Fighters, Weezer and Sublime will headline the 35th annual KROQ's Almost Acoustic Christmas at the Kia Forum on Dec. 12, an eight-act bill also including Interpol, Joyce Manor, Julia Wolf, almost monday and The Linda Lindas, with proceeds benefiting LA-area youth and community organizations.

### Key Points
- KROQ's Almost Acoustic Christmas (35th edition): Dec. 12, Kia Forum, Inglewood, CA.
- Headliners: Foo Fighters, Weezer, Sublime; full bill also includes Interpol, Joyce Manor, Julia Wolf, almost monday, The Linda Lindas.
- Proceeds support Para Los Niños and the Al Wooten Jr. Youth Center, among other LA-area community/education programs.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KROQs-Almost-Acoustic-Christmas-1-1.webp)

Foo Fighters, Weezer and Sublime will lead the lineup for KROQ’s 2026 Almost Acoustic Christmas, returning to the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California, on December 12.

The annual holiday concert will mark its 35th edition with an eight-act bill that also includes Interpol, Joyce Manor, Julia Wolf, almost monday and The Linda Lindas. The event brings established alternative rock bands and newer performers together for a single-night show.

Beyond the performances, proceeds will help support education and community services in Los Angeles. Founded in the city’s Skid Row neighborhood in 1980, Para Los Niños serves children, young people and families through education, wellness, advocacy and other support programs.

The Al Wooten Jr. Youth Center provides free after-school programming and low-cost summer activities. Its work focuses on giving young people a safe environment that encourages academic achievement and good citizenship.

Tickets go on sale October 30 at noon Pacific. Fans can find ticket information through KROQ’s Almost Acoustic Christmas event page.
