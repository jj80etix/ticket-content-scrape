# KATSEYE Postpones Detroit Concert Minutes Before Showtime, Sparking Fan Backlash

## Summary

KATSEYE postponed its Nov. 3 Detroit concert roughly 30 minutes before showtime — after fans were already seated — because member Megan Skiendiel was ill, triggering fan backlash over the late notice, unrecovered travel/lodging costs, and merchandise sales that continued almost up to the cancellation. Venue operator 313 Presents and Ticketmaster say existing tickets remain valid for a rescheduled date and refund/credit options will appear in customers' Ticketmaster accounts, but no new date has been announced and ancillary costs (travel, parking, lodging) aren't covered.

### Key Points
- Postponement announced ~30 minutes before the 8 p.m. show, with many fans already in their seats.
- Cause: illness of member Megan Skiendiel; existing tickets stay valid for a rescheduled (not-yet-announced) date.
- Fans report financial impact beyond ticket cost — travel, lodging, and parking that can't be recovered even with a valid rescheduled ticket.
- Merchandise pre-sale ran via QR code shortly before the cancellation, fueling perceptions the late call was avoidable.
- Next tour stop (Chicago, Nov. 5) proceeded as scheduled; this follows a pattern of illness-related disruptions earlier in the Wildworld Tour.

### Highlights
- ~30-minute pre-show cancellation notice left thousands of already-seated fans stranded with unrecoverable ancillary costs.
- Ticketmaster/313 Presents response: valid ticket for reschedule + in-account refund/credit option, no compensation for travel/lodging.

### Industry Problem
Last-minute cancellations expose a structural patron pain point: ticket-validity guarantees don't address the larger, unrecoverable costs (travel, lodging, time) fans incur, and slow/ambiguous communication amplifies backlash.

### Proposed Solution
Improve/Build: a primary platform could offer clearer, earlier show-status alerts (push notification thresholds ahead of showtime) and optional ancillary-cost protection (travel/lodging insurance add-on) as a differentiator and goodwill/AI-context grounding case for support interactions.

## Transcript

![Katseye via Wikimedia Commons](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/KATSEYE_in_2024.jpg)

Katseye via Wikimedia Commons

KATSEYE postponed its November 3 concert at Detroit’s Little Caesars Arena approximately 30 minutes before the scheduled start, leaving thousands of fans who had already arrived at the venue disappointed and sparking widespread criticism over the last-minute announcement.

The global girl group was scheduled to perform at 8 p.m. as part of its Wildworld Tour, but fans were informed shortly before showtime that the performance would not take place because member Megan Skiendiel was ill.

Many attendees were already inside the arena, with social media photos and videos showing fans in their seats when the postponement announcement appeared on the venue’s large screens.

In a statement shared through Instagram Stories, KATSEYE apologized for the postponement, explaining that Megan was unwell and unable to perform and needed time to rest and recover.

“We know so many of you have traveled, made plans, and been looking forward to tonight,” the group acknowledged in its message, expressing disappointment that the performance could not proceed.

Venue operator [313 Presents](https://www.313presents.com/events/detail/katseye?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) also apologized and instructed attendees to follow directions from venue staff. The company confirmed that existing tickets would remain valid for a rescheduled performance and said ticket holders would receive additional information by email.

No replacement date was immediately announced.

The timing of the decision prompted an angry response online, particularly from fans who had traveled considerable distances, arranged accommodations or made the concert part of birthday celebrations and other special occasions.

Earlier Tuesday afternoon, KATSEYE had posted a [message on X reading “know before you go!!”](https://x.com/katseyeworld/status/2117717353916232023?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), providing fans with information ahead of the concert.

After the postponement, the post became a focal point for frustrated attendees.

“Ya it would of been nice to know before we go,” [replied Angie Cicchitti](https://x.com/angieheartpink/status/2117835993130967081?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

“My daughter’s heart was broken for her birthday because your company decided it was ok to cancel a show 30 minutes before showtime after everyone has spent their money and time.”

Other responses criticized the apparent disconnect between pre-show preparations, merchandise promotions and the eventual cancellation.

One commenter questioned why fans had reportedly been given QR codes to purchase merchandise more quickly before learning the performance would not proceed. That account of the merchandise arrangements has not been independently verified.

The group’s [official merchandise instructions](https://weverse.io/katseye/notice/40589?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) had advertised early sales beginning at 2 p.m. Tuesday, several hours before the concert.

Additional accounts documented the financial and emotional impact on attendees.

One [parent posting on X](https://x.com/Lynn_NicoleK/status/2117789684839690646?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) said she had spent $889 and wanted her money returned after her daughter was left disappointed. Another fan [said they had driven three hours](https://x.com/adelasmin/status/2117777639792627855?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) to Detroit and spent their remaining travel money to attend the performance.

A [TikTok video shared by concertgoer Jessica Guerrero Canta](https://www.tiktok.com/@jessicaxxguerrero/video/7704109990524685598?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) showed visibly upset children inside the arena. The parent described the atmosphere as an arena filled with crying young fans.

The complaints highlighted the costs of a concert postponement that extend beyond the tickets themselves. Even when tickets remain valid for another date, travel expenses, transportation, parking, lodging and time away from work may not be recoverable.

The response also raised questions about when tour management knew the performance might not proceed and whether fans could have been warned earlier. KATSEYE has not publicly explained when the decision to postpone became unavoidable, and there is no verified evidence that organizers deliberately withheld an earlier decision from attendees.

The Detroit disruption follows another recent illness-related adjustment during the tour. At the group’s Boston stop, an [official Weverse notice](https://weverse.io/katseye/notice/40502?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) informed fans that Megan and Daniela Avanzini would miss scheduled VIP activities, including the soundcheck and Q&A session, because of illness. Both were expected to participate in the main concert, which proceeded as scheduled.

KATSEYE has also been touring with four of its six members, with Sophia Laforteza and Manon Bannerman taking breaks from group activities for health and well-being reasons.

The group’s Wildworld Tour has expanded substantially in recent months. As [TicketNews reported in October](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/katseye-announces-new-international-dates-for-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), KATSEYE added 11 international performances across Asia, Australia and New Zealand for early 2027.

The next scheduled concert is Thursday, November 5, at Chicago’s United Center. No change to that performance had been announced as of Wednesday morning.

For Detroit ticket holders, [Ticketmaster’s updated event listing](https://www.ticketmaster.com/katseye-the-wildworld-tour-detroit-michigan/event/080064A9C8CDBB55?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) confirms that the concert is postponed and instructs customers to retain their tickets.

Ticketmaster says refund or credit options, where available, will appear in the event details within customers’ accounts. The organizer has not yet announced a rescheduled Detroit date or detailed any arrangements for fans’ additional expenses.
