# American focus set for Edinburgh International Festival

## Summary

The Edinburgh International Festival opens August 7, 2026 under the theme "All Rise," marking the 250th anniversary of American independence with the festival's largest-ever roster of American artists. The 24-day event runs through August 30 and features over 2,000 artists from 46 countries across 147 performances. Programming highlights include a Brazilian-directed theatre opener, the festival's first-ever jazz residency with the Lincoln Center Orchestra, and an international exhibition from the Equal Justice Initiative's Legacy Museum.

### Key Points
- Festival runs August 7–30, 2026, themed "All Rise," spotlighting American artists for the 250th anniversary of U.S. independence.
- More than 2,000 artists from 46 countries will perform in 147 shows across Edinburgh.
- Festival director Nicola Benedetti frames the programme as a call for artistic collaboration and tolerance amid complexity.
- First-ever jazz residency at the festival features the Lincoln Center Orchestra.
- The Legacy Museum (Equal Justice Initiative) presents its first international exhibition as part of the programme.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Edinburgh International Festival is set to begin tomorrow (August 7) with the theme “All Rise” which is said to be a rallying cry for collaboration, resilience, and ascendance.**

Marking the 250 <sup>th</sup> anniversary of American independence, the 2026 programme aims to offer an examination of the United States featuring the festival’s largest-ever representation of American artists.

In total, more than 2,000 artists from 46 countries are set to take part in 147 performances across the city in the 24-day event which concludes on August 30.

“Our 2026 Edinburgh International Festival is an invitation to All Rise,” said festival director Nicola Benedetti.

“It’s a rallying cry to artists and audiences to stand with us, in our belief that through artistic endeavour we will see each other more truthfully and more tolerantly.

“As we mark 250 years since the Declaration of Independence, we’ve put America centre stage across our programme. Over 2,000 artists from across the world in opera, music, theatre and dance bring their voices to the stage, meeting complexity with curiosity rather than certainty.

“Over the next three weeks, this city becomes a place to gather, to question, and to imagine differently. We’ll rise together, through the dark and the dazzling, the difficult and the transformative, in celebration of artistic excellence and the resilience of the human spirit.”

Opening the 2026 theatre programme, Brazilian director Christiane Jatahy teams up with compatriot Wagner Moura while the festival will also see its first-ever jazz residency with Lincoln Center Orchestra.

Furthermore, complementing several of the American stories told on stages, The Legacy Museum brings its first-ever international exhibition in an open presentation of the Equal Justice Initiative’s work confronting the history and legacy of slavery in America.
