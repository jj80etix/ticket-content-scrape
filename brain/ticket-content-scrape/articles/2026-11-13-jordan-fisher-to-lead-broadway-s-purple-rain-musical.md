# Jordan Fisher to Lead Broadway’s ‘Purple Rain’ Musical

## Summary

Jordan Fisher will originate the role of The Kid in Broadway's adaptation of "Purple Rain," based on the 1984 Prince film, with previews beginning March 12, 2027 at the Majestic Theatre and opening night April 12. Director Saheem Ali praised Fisher's performance; an alternate will cover Tuesday evenings and Wednesday matinees.

### Key Points
- Jordan Fisher originates The Kid, previews March 12, opening April 12, 2027 at the Majestic Theatre.
- First time Fisher originates a role in a new Broadway musical.
- Tickets on sale through Telecharge; an alternate covers select performances.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Purple-Rain.webp)

Jordan Fisher will star as The Kid in the Broadway adaptation of “Purple Rain,” producer Orin Wolf announced. The casting marks Fisher’s first time originating a role in a new Broadway musical, taking on the character Prince portrayed in the 1984 film.

The production begins previews March 12, 2027, at New York’s Majestic Theatre, with opening night set for April 12. Tickets are on sale through Telecharge. An alternate will perform as The Kid on Tuesday evenings and at Wednesday matinees. Additional casting will be announced later.

Director Saheem Ali praised Fisher’s interpretation of the young musician and his work with the company. “We know that Jordan Fisher is an extraordinary talent, but what’s been thrilling about watching him inhabit The Kid is discovering the fire beneath the surface: the swagger, vulnerability, and restless creative spirit we recognize in Prince,” Ali said in a statement.

“Jordan brings all of that to the role, along with an infectious generosity that makes everyone around him better,” he continued. “He’s finding something raw, surprising, and deeply human in this iconic character, and I can’t wait for Broadway audiences to experience it.”

Set in Minneapolis in 1984, “Purple Rain” follows an aspiring musician navigating a difficult home life, competition from another band and a developing romance. As music television creates new opportunities for performers, The Kid works to establish himself and his band while confronting personal struggles that threaten his future.

The musical premiered in Minneapolis in 2025 with a book by Branden Jacobs-Jenkins and direction by Lileana Blain-Cruz. For Broadway, Peter Duchan, whose credits include “Dogfight,” has taken over the book, while Ali, known for “Buena Vista Social Club,” directs.
