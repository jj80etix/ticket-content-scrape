# Domestic events fund can ‘unlock full potential’ of New Zealand

## Summary

New Zealand announced an expanded major-events funding scheme, financed through its International Visitor Levy, aimed at giving regional authorities more long-term certainty when bidding to host international concerts and cultural events. It extends the government's existing $40M Events Attraction Package.

### Key Points
- Funded via the International Visitor Conservation and Tourism Levy (NZD$100 per traveller).
- Extends the existing $40M (€20.1M) Events Attraction Package.
- Goal is to give regional authorities long-term security when competing for major touring events.
- Follows earlier moves such as boosting Eden Park's show allowance.
