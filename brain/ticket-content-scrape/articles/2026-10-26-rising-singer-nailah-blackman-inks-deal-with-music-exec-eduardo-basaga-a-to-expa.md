# Rising Singer Nailah Blackman Inks Deal With Music Exec Eduardo Basagaña To Expand Her Global Presence

## Summary

Rising Trinidadian singer Nailah Blackman has signed with international music executive Eduardo Basagaña to expand her global touring and commercial opportunities while retaining her Caribbean roots.

### Key Points
- Basagaña, founder of EB Producciones, will support international partnerships and touring.
- Blackman recently released single "Watch Me Work (W.M.W.)" and has performed at Coachella and WNBA events.

## Transcript

![Nailah Blackman x Eduardo Basagaña](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Nailah-Blackman-x-Eduardo-Basagana-scaled.jpeg)

Nailah Blackman x Eduardo Basagaña

Rising Trinidadian singer-songwriter Nailah Blackman, whose music is influenced by soca, dancehall, pop and R&B, has signed with international music executive Eduardo Basagaña to expand her global presence.

In working with Basagaña, Blackman hopes to develop her career with strategic partnerships, international touring, collaborations and commercial opportunities while staying true to her Caribbean roots.

“Nailah has an authentic artistic identity and a genuine cultural connection,” says Basagaña. “Our goal is to build the international infrastructure around her artistry and create opportunities for long-term global growth.”

Blackman recently released her latest single, “Watch Me Work (W.M.W.)” via Anson Productions. She has collaborated with artists like Adekunle Gold, Skinny Fabulous, Kes and Shenseea. The artist has also performed on international stages, including Coachella and the WNBA.

Basagaña is the founder of EB Producciones, an entertainment company founded in Latin America that has gone global. He is a promoter, artist manager and booker with extensive experience in international market development and strategic partnerships.
