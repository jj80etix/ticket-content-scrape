# Mischief Brings ‘The Comedy About Spies’ to Broadway’s Music Box Theatre in 2027

## Summary

Mischief Theatre is bringing "The Comedy About Spies" to Broadway's Music Box Theatre for a limited 18-week run starting spring 2027, following two sold-out West End runs in London.

### Key Points
- Previews begin March 27, 2027; opening night April 14; final performance July 25.
- Written by Mischief co-founders Henry Lewis and Henry Shields, who also performed in the original 2025 West End premiere.
- Directed by Matt DiCarlo; individual Broadway casting to be announced.
- Tickets are already on sale for the limited engagement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Comedy-About-Spies.webp)

Mischief Theatre will bring “The Comedy About Spies” to Broadway in spring 2027 after two sold-out runs in London’s West End.

The spy farce, written by Mischief co-founders Henry Lewis and Henry Shields, begins previews March 27 at the Music Box Theatre. Opening night is April 14.

Tickets are on sale for the limited engagement, which will run 18 weeks, with a final performance scheduled for July 25. Matt DiCarlo directs the production. Members of the original Mischief company will appear on Broadway, with individual casting to be announced.

Lewis and Shields both performed in the play when it premiered at London’s Noël Coward Theatre in April 2025. The cast also included Dave Hearn, Chris Leask, Charlie Russell, Greg Tannahill, Nancy Zamit and Adele James. That first engagement ran through September 2025, and the play earned an Olivier Award nomination for Best New Entertainment or Comedy Play.

For its second West End engagement, the production moved to the Adelphi Theatre, where it ran from August 1 through September 26, 2026. Broadway is one of two engagements planned for 2027. A United Kingdom tour is scheduled to begin in the summer.

New York audiences first saw Mischief on Broadway in 2017 with “The Play That Goes Wrong.” That comedy had premiered in London in 2012 and went on to win the Olivier Award for Best New Comedy following its move to the West End. Its New York run continued beyond Broadway when the production moved to New World Stages in 2019, where it continues to play Off-Broadway. Mischief returned to Broadway in 2023 with “Peter Pan Goes Wrong” at the Ethel Barrymore Theatre.

Set in 1960s London, the play follows CIA and KGB spies as they search for a British agent who stole plans for a new weapon. Their paths cross at the Piccadilly Hotel, where guests include a young British couple and an older actor auditioning for the first James Bond film.

For “The Comedy About Spies,” David Farley designed the sets, Deborah Andrews designed the costumes and Johanna Town designed the lighting. Shelley Maxwell serves as movement director. Jon Fiber of Jollygoodtunes composed the music and designed the sound.
