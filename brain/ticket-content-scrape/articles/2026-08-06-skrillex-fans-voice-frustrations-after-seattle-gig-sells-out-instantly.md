# Skrillex Fans Voice Frustrations After Seattle Gig Sells-Out Instantly

## Summary

TicketNews reports that Skrillex fans in Seattle were frustrated after tickets to the August 29 Gas Works Day Party (Gas Works Park, ~10,000 capacity, also featuring Porter Robinson, Dylan Brady, Nick León, and underscores) sold out almost immediately once the general onsale opened Thursday, following a Wednesday presale. The article compiles fan reactions from X and Threads voicing frustration at being shut out, and notes this is Skrillex's first Seattle show in 14 years. It's a patron-sentiment piece illustrating real-time frustration with instant sellouts rather than a technical or regulatory story.

### Key Points

- Gas Works Day Party takes place August 29 at Seattle's Gas Works Park, headlined by Skrillex with support from Porter Robinson, Dylan Brady, Nick León, and underscores; expected attendance ~10,000; 21+ event.
- Presale ran Wednesday, general sale Thursday; fans reported tickets were sold out immediately after the general sale opened.
- The article embeds multiple fan posts from X/Threads expressing frustration at missing out.
- This marks Skrillex's first Seattle performance in 14 years (last played Bumbershoot 2012; last played Washington state at the Gorge in 2019), which likely amplified demand.

### Highlights

- Instant sellout occurred immediately after Thursday's general onsale, following a Wednesday presale window, for a ~10,000-capacity outdoor event.
- Real fan reactions (X/Threads) document frustration and perceived unfairness of the onsale process.
- Scarcity driver: first Seattle Skrillex show in 14 years, concentrating demand into a single onsale moment.

### Industry Problem

This is direct patron-sentiment evidence of a common buyer pain point: high-demand onsales that sell out near-instantly leave presale-eligible fans still unable to secure tickets, fueling frustration and a perception of unfair or opaque access even absent any bot/fraud allegation in this case.

### Proposed Solution

The article describes no vendor-side fix; it's raw fan reaction. Etix could improve patron experience for high-demand onsales by adding queue/position transparency, clearer "sold out" communication with real-time inventory visibility, and a verified waitlist or official resale hand-off so shut-out fans have a legitimate next step instead of turning to unofficial markets.
