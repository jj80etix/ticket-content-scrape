# Phish Announces Fall Run

## Summary

Phish announced an eight-show fall run for October, including three nights in Atlantic City and two-night stands in Richmond and Huntsville, following a summer tour that included a five-show Madison Square Garden stint. A ticket request period runs through August 10 at tickets.phish.com, with remaining tickets going on public sale August 14.

### Key Points
- Fall dates: Oct. 2-4 Atlantic City, Oct. 6-7 Richmond, Oct. 9 Jacksonville, Oct. 10-11 Huntsville.
- Ticket request period open through noon ET Aug. 10; general public onsale begins Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. ET.
- Summer tour included the band's 96th total Madison Square Garden show since its 1994 MSG debut.
- Phish's 10th annual Riviera Maya destination event returns to Moon Palace Cancún Jan. 27-30, 2027.

As Phish gears up to wrap up its summer tour, concluding with the band’s annual Labor Day weekend three-night stand in Commerce City, Colorado, Trey Anastasio & Co. are looking ahead to the fall with dates announced for an eight-show October run.

Highlights from Phish’s summer tour included a five-show run at Madison Square Garden (with the band now at 96 total shows at the famed venue since making its debut at MSG in 1994) and two nights at Boston’s Fenway Park.

The new fall dates start with three nights at Atlantic City, New Jersey’s Jim Whelan Boardwalk Hall Oct. 2-4. The itinerary includes two-night stands at Richmond, Virginia’s Allianz Amphitheater at Riverfront (Oct. 6-7) and Huntsville, Alabama’s The Orion Amphitheater (Oct. 10-11), along with a one-night-only gig at Jacksonville, Florida’s VyStar Veterans Memorial Arena Oct. 9.

A ticket request period is underway at tickets.phish.com and will be open until 12:00 noon (ET) on Monday, August 10. All remaining tickets go on sale to the general public starting Friday, August 14 at 10:00 AM (ET). A limited number of travel packages for the Atlantic City dates (including hotel and tickets) will go on sale Wednesday, August 5 at 12:00 noon (ET) exclusively through [http://phishfall.100xhospitality.com/](http://phishfall.100xhospitality.com/).

Phish’s upcoming schedule also includes the 10th anniversary of their annual Phish: Riviera Maya all-inclusive concert vacation. The band will once again bring their fans together at Mexico’s Moon Palace Cancún on January 27-30, 2027. The getaway, which is presented by 100x Hospitality, will feature eight live sets from Phish across four nights, along with late night DJ sets and comedy, as well as immersive activities, art, and more.  

The lineup includes Fred Armisen, Rory Scovel, Beth Stelling, Ali Macofsky, and Phil Hanley, with DJ sets from LCD Soundsystem’s James Murphy and The Roots’ Questlove. Additional names will be announced.

Phish is managed by Red Light Management and the band is represented on the agency side by THE·TEAM ‘s Mike Greisch.
