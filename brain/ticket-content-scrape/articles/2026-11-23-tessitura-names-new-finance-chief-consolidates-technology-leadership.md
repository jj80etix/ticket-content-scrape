# Tessitura Names New Finance Chief, Consolidates Technology Leadership

## Summary

Arts/culture ticketing provider Tessitura named Matt McIntire chief financial and administrative officer and promoted Jeff Oliver to vice president of technology, consolidating engineering and cloud services under one executive as the member-owned nonprofit cooperative — serving 800 organizations across 10 countries — continues a multi-year platform investment push.

### Highlights
- McIntire (ex-Microsoft Xbox P&L, ex-CFO/COO at Virtual Therapeutics) now oversees finance, people and legal.
- Oliver's promotion merges Engineering and Cloud Services under one leader reporting to the Chief Innovation Officer.
- Tessitura has invested "tens of millions" over five years in cloud databases, onsale throughput, analytics and payment processing, per its April 2026 roadmap update.
- Follows June's addition of a chief growth officer and a January ticket-refund-protection partnership with TicketPlan.

### Industry Problem
Competing ticketing platforms are consolidating technology leadership and investing heavily in onsale throughput, cloud reliability and payment processing, raising the baseline for platform performance that primary competitors are expected to match.

### Proposed Solution
No new feature was announced alongside the reorg, but it signals where a Tier-1 competitor is directing engineering investment (throughput, cloud reliability, analytics); Etix should track Tessitura's resulting roadmap deliverables as a benchmark for its own platform-reliability and analytics investment.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/MattMcIntire_JeffOliver-tessitura-1200.webp)

Tessitura has named Matt McIntire chief financial and administrative officer and Jeff Oliver vice president of technology, bringing its engineering and cloud services teams under a single executive as the arts and culture technology provider seeks to streamline operations and further invest in its platform.

The appointments, [announced Monday, Nov. 23](https://tessitura.reportablenews.com/pr/tessitura-invests-in-operational-excellence-and-technology-innovation-with-new-leadership-roles?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), represent two different leadership changes. McIntire joined Tessitura on Sept. 1 in a newly created role. Oliver, who has worked at the organization since 2021, is taking on expanded responsibilities after leading its cloud services operation and serving as interim head of engineering.

Tessitura provides ticketing, admissions, fundraising, membership, marketing and customer relationship management technology to arts and cultural organizations. The member-owned nonprofit cooperative says its platform serves 800 organizations across 10 countries, making the leadership changes relevant to a wide range of performing arts venues, museums and other cultural institutions that rely on its software.

“Arts and culture organizations are tasked to do more with less while finding new ways to reach audiences and serve their communities,” Tessitura President and CEO Andrew Recinos said in the announcement. “Our cooperative nonprofit model means every dollar earned goes into the platform and services that support our members.”

Recinos said the appointments are intended to help the organization direct those resources more effectively, with an emphasis on operational efficiency, technology development and service to its members.

McIntire will oversee Tessitura’s finance, people and legal departments, including financial planning and resource allocation. He brings more than two decades of finance and operations experience in technology and digital health. Before joining Tessitura, he was chief financial officer and chief operating officer at Virtual Therapeutics, where he helped support the company’s $35 million acquisition of Akili Interactive Labs.

He also spent more than 12 years at Microsoft. His responsibilities included managing the multibillion-dollar profit-and-loss operation for Xbox digital and physical products, supporting decisions involving more than $100 million in annual investments and working with teams connected to the Forza and Fable gaming franchises, according to Tessitura.

Oliver’s promotion combines Tessitura’s Engineering and Cloud Services teams. He reports to Chief Innovation Officer John Jakovich and will oversee both the development of the software platform and the infrastructure used to operate it. Tessitura said the change is designed to improve coordination between product development and delivery, as well as platform reliability and scalability.

Oliver was most recently vice president of cloud services and spent the past three months as interim vice president of engineering. Tessitura credits him with improvements in platform uptime and performance, though the announcement did not provide new reliability statistics. His earlier roles included chief information officer at the National Association of State Boards of Accountancy and leadership positions at Kroll and Dollar General.

The technology restructuring follows an extended period of platform investment. In an [April 2026 technology update](https://www.tessitura.com/items/articles/insights-innovation/2026-technology-roadmap?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Tessitura said it had invested tens of millions of dollars over five years and was prioritizing improvements to cloud databases, throughput during high-demand ticket onsales, analytics and payment processing. The company also highlighted continued development of its browser-based Tessitura v16 platform and tools designed to help organizations manage ticket sales, fundraising and patron relationships.

Those priorities make the consolidation of engineering and cloud operations a significant part of Tessitura’s organizational strategy, although the new appointments do not themselves constitute a product launch. The company has not tied the personnel changes to a specific new feature, release date or change to member pricing.

The appointments also follow Tessitura’s [June appointment of Aleassa Schambers as chief growth officer](https://www.tessitura.com/items/articles/news/2026/new-chief-growth-officer?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), a role focused on expanding adoption of the platform while increasing engagement with existing members. The company said the three leadership appointments fit its broader plans to grow its member community and direct more resources toward technology and services.

Tessitura has also continued expanding its ticketing offerings through partnerships. In January, [Tessitura and TicketPlan announced an integrated ticket refund protection offering](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/tessitura-ticketplan-deliver-ticket-refund-protection/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for member organizations. The current leadership changes place responsibility for building and operating the underlying platform within one technology executive team as those services continue to develop.
