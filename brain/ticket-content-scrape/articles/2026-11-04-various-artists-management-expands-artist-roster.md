# Various Artists Management Expands Artist Roster

## Summary

Boutique management company Various Artists Management (VAM) announced a wave of new signings across its management, writer/producer, publishing, and recording divisions, including 10 new artists to its management roster. The additions follow a year of expansion that included opening a Sydney office and launching VAM's 23 Recordings label.

### Key Points
- 10 artists added to VAM's management roster, including Perrie (of Little Mix) and James Vincent McMorrow.
- Songwriter Jo Hill (BTS, Lauv) and two LA-based producers also signed.
- VAM now operates offices in London, Los Angeles, New York, and Sydney.

## Transcript

![various](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/various.jpg)

Various Artists Management (VAM), a global boutique management company, announced a new wave of signings across its artist management, writer and producer, publishing, and recording divisions.

The company added 10 artists to its management roster: Perrie, Lennixx, Hannah Grae, Karina, James Vincent McMorrow, Jake Marsh, Jessica Doolan, Bennett Coast, Vicky Sometani and Jos Rivers.

VAM also signed songwriter and topliner Jo Hill, who has worked with BTS and Lauv and added Los Angeles producers Cowboy Lansky and Dillon Reed to its writer and producer management roster.

Alternative Songs, VAM’s publishing joint venture with Stellar Songs/Tim & Danny Music, added Poppy Baskcomb and Ava Joe, and Swedish-Ukrainian alt-pop artist Olga Myko signed to VAM-affiliated label 23 recordings.

![4 photo collage L to R Perrie, Lennix, Bennett Coast, Poppy Baskcomb](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/4-photo-collage-L-to-R_-Perrie-Lennix-Bennett-Coast-Poppy-Baskcomb-scaled.jpg)

Perrie, Lennixx, Bennett Coast and Poppy Baskcomb

“This group of signings reflects exactly what VAM is about: backing artists, writers and producers with a real point of view and building their careers globally,” Matt Luxon, CEO of Various Artists Management USA, said in a statement. “From Perrie Edwards stepping into a new chapter as a solo artist to breakthrough talent like Lennixx, Jake Marsh and Olga Myko, we’re proud to welcome every one of them to the VAM family across London, Los Angeles, New York and Sydney.”

The additions to the roster come following a year of expansion for VAM, which opened an office in Sydney in January 2025 and launched 23 Recordings. VAM also has offices in Los Angeles, London and New York.
