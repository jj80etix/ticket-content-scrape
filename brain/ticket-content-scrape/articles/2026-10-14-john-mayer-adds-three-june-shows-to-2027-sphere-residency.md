# John Mayer Adds Three June Shows to 2027 Sphere Residency

## Summary

John Mayer has added three new June 2027 dates to his "Live at Sphere" residency in Las Vegas. The addition extends an already-running residency at the venue.

### Key Points
- Three new June 2027 dates added to the Sphere residency.
- Continues Mayer's ongoing "Live at Sphere" run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/John-Mayer-Sphere-added-June-dates.webp)

John Mayer has added three more performances to his 2027 residency at Sphere in Las Vegas, extending the engagement with concerts on June 9, 11 and 12.

Mayer has now [expanded the residency twice](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/john-mayer-expands-2027-sphere-residency-to-18-shows/), with the latest announcement citing “overwhelming demand.” The John Mayer Trio portion will reunite Mayer with drummer Steve Jordan and bassist Pino Palladino. Full-band performances will complete the evening’s three-part presentation.

Tickets for the newly announced performances go on sale to the general public Friday, October 16, at 10 a.m. PT through Ticketmaster. An artist presale, powered by Seated, begins Wednesday, October 14, at 10 a.m. PT through Mayer’s website.

Fans who previously registered for the artist presale will have access to the added shows. Previously issued presale codes will remain valid for the new dates, according to the announcement.
