# AC/DC Cancels Toronto Concert After Storm Damages Rogers Stadium

## Summary

Live Nation Canada cancelled AC/DC's September 16 Toronto concert at Rogers Stadium after an engineering assessment found storm damage required sections of the venue to be removed and rebuilt, with no replacement venue found in time. Fans who bought through Ticketmaster get automatic full refunds; resale buyers must seek refunds from their original point of sale.

### Key Points
- September 2 storm (described as a "100-year storm"/suspected microburst) damaged Rogers Stadium, forcing removal and rebuild of affected sections.
- Live Nation Canada cancelled the show after being unable to find an alternate Toronto venue.
- Ticketmaster primary-market buyers receive automatic full refunds with no action required.
- Resale-market buyers must contact their original point of sale for refunds — a materially different, more manual process.
- Rogers Stadium targets reopening for the 2027 concert season; AC/DC's tour continues with Winnipeg, East Rutherford, and Philadelphia dates.

### Highlights
- Primary-market (Ticketmaster) refunds are automatic and require no fan action.
- Resale-market buyers face a manual, seller-dependent refund process on cancellation.
- Venue rebuild targeted for summer 2027, following just one full season (700,000+ fans, 14 shows) in 2025.

### Industry Problem
Cancellation events expose a real gap in patron experience between primary and secondary ticket purchases: primary buyers get frictionless automatic refunds, while resale buyers face inconsistent, seller-dependent processes — a recurring pain point during severe-weather-driven cancellations.

### Proposed Solution
The article doesn't propose a fix, but the contrast underscores an opportunity for a primary platform to market automatic, no-action refunds as a clear differentiator versus resale marketplaces, and to keep refund/insurance-flexibility messaging visible during weather-driven disruptions.

AC/DC’s upcoming concert at Rogers Stadium in Toronto has been canceled after severe weather caused extensive damage to portions of the venue.

The rock band was scheduled to perform at the stadium on September 16 as part of its Power Up Tour. Live Nation Canada announced the cancellation Wednesday following an engineering assessment conducted with the City of Toronto.

The review found that sections of the stadium affected by the September 2 storm must be removed and rebuilt. The damage left the venue unable to safely accommodate fans for the concert, according to Live Nation.

Live Nation said it explored possible ways to hold the show in Toronto but was unable to find an alternative venue capable of accommodating the event. “Safety has to come first,” Wayne Zronik, president of business operations for Live Nation Canada, said in a statement.

The September 2 storm brought extreme winds and heavy rainfall to the Toronto area. Kevin Mahoney, an on-site meteorologist for Rogers Stadium, said a suspected microburst made it a “100-year storm for Toronto.” A forensic meteorological analysis remained underway at the time of the cancellation announcement.

Fans who purchased tickets for the canceled AC/DC show through Ticketmaster will automatically receive full refunds to their original payment methods. No action is required. Those who bought resale tickets through another marketplace must contact their original point of sale for refund information.
