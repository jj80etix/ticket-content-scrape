# Spiritbox’s Courtney LaPlante Defends Decision to Kick Out Unruly Concertgoer From Show

## Summary

Spiritbox vocalist Courtney LaPlante publicly defended her decision to have an unruly concertgoer removed from a recent show, addressing fan reaction to the incident on social media.

### Key Points
- LaPlante defended ejecting a disruptive fan from a Spiritbox performance.
- Response addresses social-media criticism/support following the incident.

## Transcript

Spiritbox’s frontwoman Courtney LaPlante made headlines over the past week after she asked security to remove a member of the crowd.

During a show in Phoenix on July 15, LaPlante asked the audience to point out the man who was allegedly getting violent with other fans at the show. She told security to direct him out of the venue.

“This isn’t a damn gold-medal karate match,” LaPlante said. “This is a rock ‘n’ roll show… let that be a lesson to anyone out there who fucks around tonight.”

After videos of the event circulated online, metalheads called-out LaPlante, saying that she shouldn’t have kicked someone out of the show simply for moshing.

”It was old drunk man punching people in \[the\] head who were facing the stage in small amphitheater vip pit, several times while we were playing,” LaPlante said in [response](https://x.com/corklezlaplante/status/2080370698795761962?s=61) to a post on X. “Not appropriate or fun for anyone else there!”

She added that “3 times we told security about him so that was the final straw and I’m glad I did it, he wasn’t moshing.”
