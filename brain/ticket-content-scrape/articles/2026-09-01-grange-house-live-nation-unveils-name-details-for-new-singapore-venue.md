# Grange House: Live Nation Unveils Name & Details For New Singapore Venue

## Summary

Live Nation announced the name and details of its new 3,000-capacity venue on Singapore's Orchard Road: Grange House, opening mid-2027. Developed with the Singapore Tourism Board and Lendlease, the purpose-built venue is designed to fill the gap between Singapore's small clubs and its arenas/stadiums, with all-day hospitality and community programming alongside its show calendar.

### Key Points
- 3,000-capacity venue, designed by Live Nation's in-house Blueprint Studio, opens mid-2027 on Orchard Road.
- Built in partnership with the Singapore Tourism Board and Lendlease Global Commercial REIT as part of a broader Orchard Road redevelopment.
- Open to all promoters and event organizers, not exclusive to Live Nation-booked shows.
- Follows Live Nation's 2024 announcement of its first Singapore venue; the company has operated in the market since 2006.

Live Nation today announced that its new purpose-built, 3,000-capacity venue in the heart of Orchard Road, Singapore’s major sopping and entertainment boulevard, will be known as Grange House and is set to open by mid-2027.

The venue, which is being developed with support from the Singapore Tourism Board, will “create new opportunities to bring more artists, tours and cultural experiences to Singapore, filling an important gap between the city’s smaller venues and its large-scale arenas and stadiums,” Live Nation stated in a press announcement released Aug. 31.

Designed by Blueprint Studio, Live Nation’s in-house design and development team, Grange House will pair exceptional acoustics and sightlines with bespoke hospitality, curated food and drink options, and artist facilities designed to meet international touring standards.

Grange House will be an inviting space beyond show nights, as indoor and outdoor spaces have been designed for people to gather throughout the day, including a landscaped terrace, and an all-day café and restaurant that can host community talks, podcasts and public events. A walkway linking 313@Somerset and Grange House will feature art, exhibitions and pop-ups, and a foyer serving as a welcoming space to dine, unwind and come together before and after shows.

In collaboration with Lendlease Global Commercial REIT, Grange House will also contribute to the ongoing rejuvenation of Orchard Road. According to a statement from Live Nation, “year-round live entertainment, community programming and cultural experiences will create new reasons for locals and visitors to spend time in the precinct, while supporting the retailers, restaurants and businesses around it.”
