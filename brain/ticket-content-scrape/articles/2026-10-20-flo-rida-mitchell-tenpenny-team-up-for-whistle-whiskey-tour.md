# Flo Rida, Mitchell Tenpenny Team Up For ‘Whistle & Whiskey’ Tour

## Summary

Flo Rida and Mitchell Tenpenny announced the co-headlining "Whistle & Whiskey Tour," kicking off February 2027 with dates including Duluth, Sioux City, Grand Forks and Rochester, with support from Jay Webb.

### Key Points
- "Whistle & Whiskey Tour" co-headlined by Flo Rida and Mitchell Tenpenny, starting Feb. 2027.
- Initial dates: DECC (Duluth, MN), Tyson Events Center (Sioux City, IA), Alerus Center (Grand Forks, ND), Mayo Civic Center (Rochester, MN); more dates promised.
- Support act: Jay Webb.

Billed as “A country party, a hip-hop legend, one unforgettable night,” “Whistle & Whiskey Tour” is coming to select markets in early 2027, with additional dates promised.

Dates kick off in February 2027, with gigs so far including DECC in Duluth, Minnesota, Tyson Events Center in Sioux City, Alerus Center in Grand Forks and Mayo Civic Center in Rochester.

Support on the dates is Jay Webb.

With the two artists seeming to come from different worlds sonically, both are responsible for major hits, with Flo Rida’s seven-times platinum “Low” a staple for millennials and permeating popular culture, along with a slew of other hits and more than 100 million singles sold worldwide. Tenpenny, meanwhile, counts more than 2.6 billion global streams to his name, with ubiquitous hits like “Drunk Me,” certified four-times platinum.

“Whistle & Whiskey isn’t about choosing between country and hip-hop — it’s about bringing everybody together for a party,” the announcement reads. “Fans can expect a night packed with recognizable songs, sing-along moments and high-energy performances from the first song through the final encore.”

Booking contact for the tour is Geoffrey Pitch at Howard Pitch Entertainment.
