# Aziz Ansari Adds Fall 2026 Leg To ‘Hypothetical Tour’

## Summary

Comedian Aziz Ansari is extending his Live Nation-promoted "Hypothetical Tour" with a new fall leg covering 10 U.S. cities in October 2026. The run kicks off October 7 at Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles and includes stops in Hollywood/Orlando (Hard Rock Live), New York (Ulster PAC, Beacon Theatre), and Washington, D.C. (Warner Theatre). Artist presale begins August 4, with general onsale following August 6.

### Key Points
- New leg runs October 7-22, 2026, across 10 cities, mostly Live Nation-promoted (a few independent dates noted).
- Opens Oct. 7 at Dolby Theatre, Los Angeles; closes Oct. 22 with two shows at Warner Theatre, Washington, D.C.
- Artist presale starts August 4 at 10 a.m. local time; general onsale starts August 6 at 10 a.m.
- Ansari is booked by WME and managed by 3 Arts Entertainment.
- Prior tour leg included a sold-out Boston show grossing $330,520 on 3,350 tickets (Wang Theatre – Boch Center), per Pollstar Boxoffice.

Multi-hyphenate star Aziz Ansari is extending his “Hypothetical Tour” and bringing his popular show to 10 cities across the U.S. in October.

The award-winning comedian, director, actor and writer kicks off the new leg of his Live Nation-promoted tour Oct. 7 at Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles. The routing includes stops at Hard Rock Live in Hollywood and Orlando, New York Shows at Ulster PAC and Beacon Theatre, and Washington, D.C.’s Warner Theatre.

Fans can purchase tickets via the artist presale beginning Aug. 4 at 10 a.m. local time, and additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale, which begins Aug. 6 at 10 a.m. Visit [AzizAnsari.com](https://www.azizansari.com/) for more information.

Ansari is booked worldwide by WME and managed by 3 Arts Entertainment. He had a successful trek on the road last year that included a sold-out show at Wang Theatre – Boch Center in Boston, where he grossed $330,520 off 3,350 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.
