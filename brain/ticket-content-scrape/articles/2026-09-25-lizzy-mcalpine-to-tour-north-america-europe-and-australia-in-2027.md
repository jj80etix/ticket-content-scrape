# Lizzy McAlpine to Tour North America, Europe and Australia in 2027

## Summary

Lizzy McAlpine announced "The Over Country Tour," a 2027 run spanning Australia, New Zealand, Europe, the UK and North America in support of her new album *Angel*. The tour runs from February 3 in Adelaide through July 17 in Seattle, with major North American stops including Madison Square Garden, United Center and Red Rocks.

### Key Points
- Tour supports McAlpine's fourth album, *Angel*, released September 18.
- North American leg runs April 30–July 17, with stops in Dallas, Nashville, NYC, Toronto, Chicago and LA.
- Artist presales begin September 29–30 depending on region; general onsales start October 2.
- Support acts vary by market and include Flyte, SRCHR and Post Animal.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Lizzy-McAlpine.webp)

Lizzy McAlpine will take her new album *Angel* on the road in 2027 with “The Over Country Tour,” a run spanning Australia, New Zealand, Europe, the United Kingdom and North America.

The tour opens February 3 in Adelaide, Australia, and is scheduled to conclude July 17 in Seattle. McAlpine will play six dates in Australia and New Zealand in February before heading to Europe in March. That leg includes Barcelona, Paris, Munich, Vienna, Prague, Warsaw, Berlin, Oslo, Stockholm, Copenhagen, Cologne and Amsterdam. Shows in London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow and Dublin follow in late March and April.

The North American leg begins April 30 at The Bomb Factory in Dallas. Its first stretch visits Houston, Atlanta, Durham, Columbus, Detroit, Indianapolis, Milwaukee, Minneapolis, St. Louis, Kansas City and Oklahoma City through May 18. McAlpine returns to the road June 16 at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena for a larger-venue run that includes Philadelphia, Boston, New York, Toronto and Chicago.

Among the tour’s biggest stops are Madison Square Garden in New York on June 24, Scotiabank Arena in Toronto on June 27, the United Center in Chicago on June 29 and the Kia Forum in the Los Angeles area on July 10. The final stretch also includes Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, Chase Center in San Francisco and Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle.

Support will vary by market. Announced guests include Mila Bea, Worthitpurchase, Flyte, SRCHR, Sydney Ross Mitchell, Searows and Post Animal.

The announcement follows the September 18 release of *Angel*, McAlpine’s fourth album. “I am so excited to play these songs live for you,” she said in the tour announcement.

In Australia and New Zealand, the artist presale begins September 29 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the public sale September 30 at noon. The artist presale for Europe and the U.K. starts September 30 at 10 a.m. local time, with general sales beginning October 2 at 10 a.m. local time.

North American artist presales will open in two waves on September 30, at 10 a.m. and 2 p.m. local time. General sales begin October 2 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register for artist presale access through [McAlpine’s tour website](https://www.lizzymcalpine.com/).
