# Lollapalooza India Announces Headliners Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, KATSEYE and Fontaines D.C.

## Summary

Lollapalooza India revealed its fifth-edition lineup, headlined by Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, KATSEYE and Fontaines D.C., returning to Mumbai's Mahalaxmi Race Course Jan. 23-24, 2027. General ticket sales are underway in phases after early-bird passes sold out.

### Key Points
- Fifth edition, Jan. 23-24, 2027, Mahalaxmi Race Course, Mumbai.
- All four headliners making their Indian debut, along with several other artists.
- Four stages plus food and art installations.
- Early-bird passes sold out; general sales now underway in phases.

Lollapalooza India has unveiled the lineup for its fifth edition, led by Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, KATSEYE and Fontaines D.C. The two-day festival will return to Mahalaxmi Race Course in Mumbai on January 23 and 24, 2027.

Stewart Copeland, the drummer and co-founder of The Police, will take the stage alongside three-time Grammy winner and Indian composer Ricky Kej.
