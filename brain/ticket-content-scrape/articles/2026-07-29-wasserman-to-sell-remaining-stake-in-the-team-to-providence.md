# Wasserman to sell remaining stake in The Team to Providence

## Summary

Casey Wasserman will sell his remaining stake in talent agency The Team (formerly Wasserman) to Providence Equity, completing the private-equity firm's takeover; company president Mike Watts becomes CEO.

### Key Points
- Providence beat bids from United Talent Agency/EQT, Excel Sports Management, Primera, and Partners Group.
- Sale follows Wasserman's February exit announcement after Epstein-files revelations prompted several artists to cut ties.
- Deal expected to close in H2 2026; Providence also holds stakes in Superstruct, TAIT, Populous, and ATG Entertainment.

## Transcript

The private equity firm will complete its takeover of the major agency before the end of the year

![Casey Wasserman](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Casey-Wasserman.png)

Casey Wasserman

Ending months-long speculation of who would acquire Casey Wasserman’s talent agency, Providence Equity Partners has confirmed it will procure the founder’s remaining stake in The Team, completing the private investment firm’s takeover of the agency formerly known as Wasserman.

The former chairman and CEO confirmed yesterday (28 July) that he will sell his remaining stake in The Team, [which rebranded in March](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/03/wasserman-agency-rebrands-to-theteam/), to Providence. The investment firm has held a [majority stake](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2022/11/providence-makes-strategic-investment-in-wasserman/) in the agency since 2022, and also has [stakes](https://www.provequity.com/portfolio?page=2) in Superstruct, TAIT, Populous, ATG Entertainment, d&b audiotechnik, and Rock-It Cargo.

Providence said it beat multiple rounds of bids from prospective investors, which [reportedly](https://www.sportsbusinessjournal.com/Articles/2026/06/12/team-work-sales-process-narrows-for-wassermans-agency/) included United Talent Agency and its backer EQT, Excel Sports Management, Primera, and Partners Group.

With the sale, Wasserman says: “The truest measure of anything you build is whether it can thrive beyond you – and that was always my goal when I founded what was then called the Wasserman Media Group 24 years ago. What began as a small agency with big ambitions is today a global leader.

“The Team is powered by extraordinary people and trusted by the world’s greatest talent, brands, and properties. I leave with pride in what we created together, gratitude for those who made it possible, and confidence in what comes next. Sports, music, and entertainment are the languages the whole world speaks, and The Team’s future is bright,” he concluded.

The name change followed Wasserman’s February announcement that he would [sell his namesake music and sports agency](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/casey-wasserman-to-sell-agency/) after reports emerged that he had appeared in the Epstein files, leading to a stream of artists – including Chappell Roan, Laufey, Odesza, Weyes Blood, and Gigi Perez – to [cut ties](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/we-will-not-stand-by-idly-more-acts-leave-wasserman/) with the company.

Company president Mike Watts will be named CEO of The Team upon the transaction’s closure, which is expected to be in the second half of this year. He has been an integral member of the leadership since The Team was founded in 2002.

Earlier this year, The Team’s leadership was strengthened with the elevation of [Kevin Shivers](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/the-team-elevates-kevin-shivers-to-music-group-co-president/) to co-president of the music group, to work alongside music group president Lee Anderson to oversee the group’s strategy, operations, and growth across the global live music business.

As The Team’s core investor, Providence says it will provide capital to expand The Team’s capabilities across sports, music, and entertainment verticals. The Team provides representation and marketing services to talent, brands, and properties across 28 countries and more than 70 cities worldwide.

“We’re incredibly excited to further strengthen our partnership with The Team,” said Davis Noell, senior managing director, co-head of North America at Providence. “The robust interest in the Company throughout this process only reinforced our belief in the business and enthusiasm for its next phase of growth. Our additional investment also reflects our conviction in the long-term growth in demand for in-person experiences across sports, music, and entertainment and the brands, talent, and partnerships that bring those moments to life.”

Financial terms were not disclosed.
