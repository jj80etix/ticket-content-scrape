# HYBE revenue doubles year-on-year in ‘all-time high’ quarter

## Summary

HYBE reported an all-time-high Q2, with revenue up 105.5% year-over-year to KRW 1.45 trillion (€874M) and operating profit nearly tripling quarter-over-quarter, driven by BTS's Arirang tour and record Weverse engagement.

### Key Points
- 119 shows played in Q2; more than 200 additional shows are slated for the second half of 2026.
- Weverse MAUs rose 8% QoQ to 14.43 million; ARPPU rose 24%.
- ENHYPEN, LE SSERAFIM and BOYNEXTDOOR tour launches also cited as revenue drivers.

## HYBE revenue doubles year-on-year in ‘all-time high’ quarter

The second quarter saw the entertainment giant behind BTS nearly triple its operating profit, citing the band's blockbuster Arirang tour as a major driver

![BTS in Tampa on The Arirang World Tour](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BTS-in-Tampa-on-The-Arirang-World-Tour-©-BIGHIT-MUSIC-.png)

BTS in Tampa on The Arirang World Tour

HYBE has reported record revenues for the second quarter of this year, with turnover more than doubling year-on-year to €874 million (KRW 1.45 trillion) in what the company called an “all-time high” quarter.

This number propelled the entertainment giant to an operating profit of €103 million (KRW 170.9 billion) for the period, a nearly threefold jump [quarter-on-quarter](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/bts-helps-power-hybe-to-highest-ever-q1-revenue/). The revenue figure for the quarter also amounts to a 105.5% year-on-year growth, up from a figure of KRW 705.6 billion in the same period last year.

HYBE said BTS’s ongoing *[Arirang](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/01/bts-shift-2-4-million-tickets-add-extra-dates-to-world-tour/)* world tour was at the heart of the success, citing this alongside revenue generated from other “artist activities”, including concerts, recorded music, and advertising from across its roster of entertainment acts.

The company said of the blockbuster tour: “BTS maintained its global momentum with its album *Arirang* and the ongoing *BTS World Tour Arirang”*.

> “\[We are\] on track to significantly exceed last year’s total of 279 performances”

The firm added that, according to Luminate’s 2026 Midyear Report, the *Arirang* album ranked No. 1 in U.S. vinyl and CD album sales during the first half of the year.

“The album contributed to South Korea becoming the world’s third-largest music exporter, behind only the United States and the United Kingdom,” HYBE said.

“Artist touring activity accelerated during the first half of the year,” the company reported, adding that HYBE Music Group artists held 119 performances during the quarter, with more than 200 additional shows scheduled for the second half of the year. The firm added this puts the company “on track to significantly exceed last year’s total of 279 performances”.

Within this, the firm named among the key drivers ENHYPEN’s expansion into South America with sold‑out stadium dates, as well as LE SSERAFIM and BOYNEXTDOOR’s launches of new world tours. This expanded touring calendar is expected to further lift merchandise and licensing revenue through concert sales and THE CITY projects, HYBE said.

The company also noted its fan platform Weverse posted record engagement in the quarter, with monthly active users rising 8% quarter-on-quarter to 14.43 million, while total payment volume and ARPPU increased 12% and 24% respectively. The platform also added new P‑pop communities for BINI and SB19, bringing its total to more than 200.
