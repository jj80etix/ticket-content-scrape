# FTC Live Investing In Ecuador’s Live Entertainment Ecosystem With Arena Bicentenario

## Summary

Ecuadorian entertainment company FTC Live acquired a venue structure in Quito's Parque Bicentenario park, which will operate as Arena Bicentenario, an outdoor venue with 8,500 to 18,000 capacity opening December 2026. The move expands FTC Live's promotion and venue portfolio across Latin America, where the company already produces shows for artists including Shakira, Ozuna, Def Leppard, and The Strokes.

### Key Points
- Arena Bicentenario: base capacity 8,500, expandable to 18,000; tent roughly 50 feet tall and 197 by 131 feet; scheduled to open December 2026.
- FTC Live's inaugural "Al Parque" festival at the same park is set for Aug. 29, 2026, featuring Maroon 5, Yandel Sinfónico, and Myke Towers.
- The company's 2026 LATAM concert calendar spans Ecuador, El Salvador, Guatemala, Nicaragua, Honduras, Peru, Mexico, and Costa Rica through December.
- CEO Juan Carlos Dominguez also founded ticketing company Feel The Tickets.

FTC Live, an Ecuadorian entertainment company that has produced some of the country’s biggest shows, is adding a venue to its portfolio and expanding the country’s live entertainment infrastructure.

The company acquired a structure at Quito’s Parque Bicentenario, a large public park covering over 300 acres, and the privately owned venue will operate as Arena Bicentenario, hosting international concerts, festivals, and large-scale live events. It is scheduled to open in December 2026.

The new venue offers a weather-protected environment and boasts flexibility, with capacity capable of expanding from 8,500 to as many as 18,000 guests through outdoor configurations. Arena Bicentenario features a large tent that is about 50 feet tall and covers approximately 197 by 131 feet.

Arena Bicentenario is a significant move from FTC Live. It allows the entertainment company to expand its ability to develop and produce a broader range of experiences while strengthening the Quito market.

“Arena Bicentenario represents much more than a new venue. It reflects our long-term commitment to Ecuador and our belief that artists, fans and the live entertainment industry deserve world-class infrastructure,” Juan Carlos Dominguez, CEO of FTC Live, said in a statement. “This investment allows us to bring more of the world’s biggest productions to Quito while strengthening Ecuador’s position as one of the region’s leading destinations for live entertainment.”

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed.jpg)

In addition to developing a venue, FTC Live promotes major shows in Ecuador, including its inaugural FTC Live Al Parque festival on Aug. 29 at Parque Bicentenario, which features Maroon 5, Yandel Sinfónico, Myke Towers, Los Frequencies and Fruko y sus Tesos. The event also features local artists Machaka, Cris Chil, Miel, Homero Gallardo and Armendaris.

FTC Live has had a hand in the growing influence of global touring in Latin America, promoting shows for major acts such as Shakira. The company is producing shows across the LATAM region for artists like Ozuna, Romeo Santos and Prince Royce, Def Leppard, HUGEL, Hardwell and The Strokes. See full schedule below.

“FTC Live continues to grow across Latin America, with Ecuador at the heart of our operations and the foundation from which we have expanded,” said Dominguez, who also founded ticketer Feel The Tickets. “We’re proud to continue bringing major productions to audiences in Ecuador while strengthening our presence throughout Central America and other key markets in the region. With a strong 2026 calendar underway, we’re also looking ahead to an exciting lineup for 2027.”
