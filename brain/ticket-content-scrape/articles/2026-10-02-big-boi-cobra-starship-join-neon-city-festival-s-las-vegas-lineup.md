# Big Boi, Cobra Starship Join Neon City Festival’s Las Vegas Lineup

## Summary

Las Vegas' Neon City Festival expanded its free, third-annual lineup for its November 20-22 run, adding Big Boi, Cobra Starship, Freddie Gibbs, Joyce Manor, and Three 6 Mafia alongside previously announced headliners Kaskade, Subtronics, and The Fray. The all-ages, no-ticket-required event spans four stages across downtown Las Vegas' Events Center and Fremont Street Experience.

### Key Points
- Festival runs November 20-22, 2026, free and open to all ages, no ticket required.
- New additions include Big Boi, Cobra Starship, Freddie Gibbs, Joyce Manor, Three 6 Mafia, and Diplo.
- Produced in partnership with the Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority, Fremont Street Experience, and Audacy.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Neon-City-Festival.webp)

Neon City Festival has expanded its 2026 lineup, adding Big Boi, Cobra Starship, Freddie Gibbs, Joyce Manor and Three 6 Mafia to its free weekend of music in downtown Las Vegas.

The third annual festival takes place November 20 to 22, with Diplo also joining a bill that includes previously announced performers Kaskade, Subtronics and The Fray. The event is open to all ages, and no festival ticket is required.

The expanded roster brings together electronic music, hip-hop and rock acts alongside local performers, giving attendees a mix of established names and emerging talent.

“This year’s lineup brings together artists people have grown up listening to and new voices they may be discovering for the first time,” festival CEO Jeff Victor said.

Friday, November 20, will feature Diplo, Cobra Starship and Freddie Gibbs, with additional performances from The Paradox, Babyface Ray, Summerlin House Mafia and Split Persona.

Saturday, November 21, brings Subtronics, The Fray, Three 6 Mafia, Slick Rick and Parachute. LUCKY, The Far Worst, Hoity-Toity and Kings/Kings are also scheduled, along with a Unity Events Neon Showcase featuring Marsik, VLITO, Carson Wysocki, Allergic, Rainbolt and Shawn.

Kaskade and Big Boi will perform Sunday, November 22, alongside Joyce Manor, Rich the Kid, almost monday, CID, Maesic, Quinn Ayers, King Cherry, Pure Sport and VOXXX. Organizers plan to announce additional artists, set times and stage assignments later.

Performances will take place across four stages at the Downtown Las Vegas Events Center and Fremont Street Experience. The latter’s Main Street, First Street and Third Street stages sit beneath its canopy, placing the festival throughout the downtown entertainment district.
