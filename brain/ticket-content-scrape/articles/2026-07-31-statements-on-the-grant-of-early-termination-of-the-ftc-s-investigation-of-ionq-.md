# Statements on the Grant of Early Termination of the FTC’s Investigation of IonQ’s Proposed Acquisition of SkyWater

## Summary

The FTC granted early termination of its antitrust review of quantum-computing firm IonQ's proposed acquisition of chip fabricator SkyWater Technologies, with Chairman Ferguson and Commissioner Meador issuing separate statements. This is a routine merger-clearance notice unrelated to ticketing or live events.

### Key Points
- FTC ended its waiting-period review early, clearing IonQ's acquisition of SkyWater Technologies to proceed.
- Both FTC Chairman Andrew Ferguson and Commissioner Mark Meador issued individual statements (linked but not detailed in this release).
- No ticketing-industry relevance; included in the harvest because it came through the FTC press-release feed subscribed for Q9/Q10 monitoring.

## Transcript

The Federal Trade Commission granted early termination of its review of quantum computing developer IonQ Inc.’s proposed acquisition of SkyWater Technologies Inc., a quantum chip fabrication company.

[FTC Chairman Andrew N. Ferguson](/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/public-statements/statement-chairman-andrew-n-ferguson-matter-ionq-inc-skywater-technology-inc) and [Commissioner Mark R.
