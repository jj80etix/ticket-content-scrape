# White Rocks Entertainment creates $10m arts endowment

## Summary

White Rocks Entertainment is building a permanent philanthropic endowment directly into the business model of its planned 10,000-capacity White Rocks Amphitheater in Austin, directing 10% of every ticket's sale price to arts, health, and community organizations — an estimated $10M annually once the venue (modeled on Colorado's Red Rocks) opens, targeted for 2030.

### Key Points
- 10% of every ticket sold at the future venue funds the endowment, rather than relying on separate fundraising.
- Projected to distribute ~$10M annually in year one, potentially doubling as the event calendar expands.
- Venue: 10,000-capacity amphitheater on a 71-acre mixed-use site in Austin, modeled on Red Rocks.
- Construction begins next year; completion targeted for 2030.
- Funds will support performing/visual arts, artist health, first responders, and public media organizations in Austin.

**White Rocks Entertainment has created a permanent philanthropic endowment that is expected to provide approximately $10m (£7.4m/€8.6m) through every ticket sold at the future White Rocks Amphitheater in Austin.**

The money is set to be distributed to arts, health, and community organisations in the Texan city.

Rather than relying on fundraising or charitable contributions, the endowment is built directly into the venue’s long-term operating model with 10% of the sales price of every ticket sold to be directed to the fund.

“Based on projected attendance during the venue’s first full year of operation, White Rocks estimates the endowment could distribute approximately $10m annually, with funding expected to as much as double as the amphitheater expands its event calendar,” read a White Rocks Entertainment statement.

“The endowment is expected to support a broad cross-section of Austin organizations, including the performing arts, visual arts, artist health, first responders, and public media which has for so long supported the music culture of our community.”

The planned amphitheatre is set to have a capacity of 10,000, and is located on a 71-acre site that is also due to house a mixed-use development.

Work is set to begin on the project, which aims to emulate Colorado’s Red Rocks Amphitheater, next year and is estimated to be completed in 2030.
