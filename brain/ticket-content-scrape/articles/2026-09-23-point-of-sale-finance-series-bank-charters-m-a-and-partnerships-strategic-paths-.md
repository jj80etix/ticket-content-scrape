# Point-of-Sale Finance Series: Bank Charters, M&A, and Partnerships — Strategic Paths for Fintech and Payment Companies — Crossover Episode With The Consumer Finance Podcast

## Summary

A crossover episode of Troutman Pepper Locke's *Payments Pros* and *The Consumer Finance Podcast* covers why fintech and point-of-sale finance companies are increasingly pursuing bank charters, bank acquisitions or bank partnerships, citing a more receptive OCC posture toward de novo charter applications. Attorneys James Stevens and Sarah Hanna compare the three paths on timing, cost and regulatory complexity and advise engaging regulators and compliance counsel early.

### Key Points
- OCC is described as actively encouraging new (de novo) bank-charter applications under the current administration.
- Interest spans crypto, lending and payments companies weighing charters, acquisitions or bank partnerships.
- Bank-partnership enforcement activity has reportedly cooled, shifting industry focus.
- Guidance: build compliance infrastructure early and engage regulators/counsel before launch.

In this crossover episode of [*Payments Pros*](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/payments-pros-podcast/) and [*The Consumer Finance Podcast*](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/the-consumer-finance-podcast/), [Taylor Gess](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/taylor-r-gess/) is joined by colleagues [James Stevens](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/james-w-stevens/) and [Sarah Hanna](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/sarah-hanna/) from Troutman Pepper Locke’s [Corporate](https://www.troutman.com/services/practices/corporate/) and [Consumer Financial Services](https://www.troutman.com/services/practices/consumer-financial-services/) practices to discuss the surge of interest among fintech and point-of-sale finance companies in obtaining bank charters, acquiring existing banks, and forming bank partnerships. With the current administration signaling an open-for-business posture at the federal banking agencies, and the Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) actively encouraging de novo applications, payment and fintech companies are weighing their options more seriously than they have in years. The conversation covers what is driving the spike in charter applications, including from companies in the crypto, lending, and payments spaces, and how specialty charters are factoring into that trend. James and Sarah also walk through the bank partnership landscape, where enforcement activity has cooled and focus has shifted, and explain how acquiring an existing bank charter compares to starting from scratch on timing, cost, and regulatory complexity. The episode closes with practical guidance for point-of-sale finance companies considering any of these three paths, including why building internal compliance infrastructure early, engaging regulators informally, and retaining regulatory counsel sooner rather than later can make or break a successful launch.
