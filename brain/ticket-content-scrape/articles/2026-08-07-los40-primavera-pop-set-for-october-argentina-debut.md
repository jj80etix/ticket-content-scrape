# LOS40 Primavera Pop set for October Argentina debut

## Summary

Spanish festival brand LOS40 Primavera Pop, in its 60th year, is expanding outside Spain for the first time with a debut Argentina edition on Oct. 28 at Buenos Aires' Teatro Gran Rex, via a three-year rights partnership with promoter EB Producciones (which will move the event to the 14,000-cap Movistar Arena in 2027).

### Key Points
- First-ever non-Spain edition of the 60-year-old festival, launching Oct. 28 in Buenos Aires.
- Three-year partnership between LOS40 and EB Producciones CEO Eduardo Basagaña; 2027 edition moves to Movistar Arena (14,000 cap).
- Lineup includes Luis Fonsi, Abel Pintos, Luck Ra, Ángela Torres and others.

## Transcript

The stalwart Spanish event will occur outside of Spain for the first time in its 60 year history after a deal with Argentinian promoter EB Producciones

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Eduardo-Basagna-.png)

Eduardo Basagaña © EB Producciones

Spanish festival LOS40 Primavera Pop is to expand into Latin America with a debut edition in Argentina this October, after the rights to the brand were acquired by EB Producciones CEO Eduardo Basagaña.

The new event will be the festival’s first ever destination outside of Spain, and comes as part of a three-year partnership agreed between EB Producciones and LOS40. The inaugural Argentinian festival will take place on 28 October at Buenos Aires’ Teatro Gran Rex.

The promoter also said the 14,000-cap Movistar Arena in Buenos Aires will hold the 2027 edition.

LOS40 Primavera Pop is a multi-artist concert event put on by Spanish radio station LOS40, and is held each April, typically with a one-night edition in Madrid and Barcelona each year. This year’s showcase, held at Madrid’s Movistar Arena and Barcelona’s Olímpic Arena on consecutive nights, marked the event’s 60th anniversary.

> “The expansion marks a major milestone for the brand”

“One of Spain’s leading and long-running festival will make an historic Latin American debut on 28 October,” EB Proucciones said, adding: “For over two decades, Primavera Pop has hosted global titans including Lady Gaga, Take That, OneRepublic, Jason Derulo, Clean Bandit, Enrique Iglesias and Karol G, establishing itself as one of Europe’s premier live pop events.”

Festival organisers said Basagaña’s “bold leadership and strategic drive through EB Producciones secured a landmark three-year partnership with LOS40”, which they said “solidifies his status as a power player in the LatAm entertainment industry”.

The promoter also revealed the first wave of artists that will play the event, saying: “With an elite lineup featuring *Despacito* global icon Luis Fonsi, plus Argentine icons Abel Pintos, Luck Ra, Ángela Torres, Emanero, Coti, and rising stars Olivia Wald, Roze, TULI and Juli Obregon. The expansion marks a major milestone for the brand and is spearheaded by Eduardo Basagaña and EB Producciones.”

In recent years, EB Producciones has been pursuing expansion beyond its Argentinian base, having launched operations in Costa Rica and Guatemala in 2023.
