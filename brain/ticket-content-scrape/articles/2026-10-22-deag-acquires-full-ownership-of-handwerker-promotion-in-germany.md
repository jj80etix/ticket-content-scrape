# DEAG Acquires Full Ownership Of Handwerker Promotion In Germany

## Summary

German promoter DEAG acquired the remaining 25.1% stake in regional promoter handwerker promotion from founder Fred Handwerker, becoming sole owner as part of DEAG's ongoing buy-and-build consolidation strategy.

### Key Points
- DEAG had held a majority stake in handwerker promotion since 2013; Handwerker founded the company over 40 years ago.
- Handwerker will stay actively involved, continuing to lead the company alongside management-team member Johanna Holthöwer.
- handwerker promotion is a leading North Rhine-Westphalia promoter, with past credits including The Rolling Stones, Beyoncé and Dolly Parton, plus Riverdance and Disney on Ice.
- Follows DEAG's similar full buyout of Wizard Live earlier in 2026, continuing its pattern of reducing minority stakes across group companies.

### Highlights
- DEAG becomes sole owner of handwerker promotion by buying out the founder's last 25.1% stake, its latest buy-and-build consolidation move after a similar Wizard Live buyout in January 2026.

### Industry Problem
Mid-size regional promoters facing succession or liquidity needs are being absorbed by larger consolidators (DEAG) building vertically integrated promotion networks across Europe — a consolidation trend that narrows the field of independent promotion partners.

### Proposed Solution
No new product response needed; worth tracking as a signal of continued promoter-side consolidation in the European market (Q13 monitor), which can affect which promoters/venues are open to switching primary ticketing providers.

DEAG Deutsche Entertainment AG (DEAG) is acquiring the remaining 25.1% stake in handwerker promotion e. gmbh from its founder and managing director, Fred Handwerker. Following the acquisition, DEAG will become the sole shareholder of the company.

DEAG has held a majority stake in handwerker promotion since 2013; since then, the companies have celebrated numerous joint successes. The acquisition is part of DEAG’s growth and buy-and-build strategy, and is reducing minority interests in its group companies as planned.

**[See: DEAG Acquires Remaining Shares In Wizard Live](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/01/27/deag-acquires-remaining-shares-in-wizard-live/)**

Fred Handwerker, who founded handwerker promotion more than 40 years ago, will continue to be actively involved in the company following the sale of his remaining stake. Together with Johanna Holthöwer, who joined the company’s management team in 2024, Fred will continue to lead the company.

Handwerker promotion is one of the leading local promoters in North Rhine-Westphalia. For decades, the company has staged concerts of every size, ranging from intimate club shows to large venues and arenas and stadium events. Highlights have included concerts by numerous leading national and international artists, including Justin Bieber, Iron Maiden, KISS, The Rolling Stones, Peter Maffay, Lionel Richie, Beyoncé, Dolly Parton, Neil Young, Romeo Santos, Prince Royce, Ina Müller and the Scorpions.

At the same time, handwerker promotion has continuously expanded its business activities in recent years and has also promoted tours across Europe including tours by Tokio Hotel and, [most recently, Till Lindemann](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/05/08/lindemann-tour-sells-80000-tickets-in-48-hours/).

Beyond the concert business, the agency’s portfolio encompasses a broad range of additional live entertainment formats from shows such as Riverdance and Nutten, Koks & frische Erdbeeren, and Family Entertainment productions such as Disney on Ice and Monster Jam to comedy featuring artists such as Kevin Hart and John Cleese.
