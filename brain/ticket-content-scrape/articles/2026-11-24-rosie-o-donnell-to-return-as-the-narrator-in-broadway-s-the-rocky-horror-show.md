# Rosie O’Donnell to Return as the Narrator in Broadway’s ‘The Rocky Horror Show’

## Summary

Rosie O'Donnell will return as the Narrator in Broadway's "The Rocky Horror Show." The announcement covers casting details for the production.

### Key Points
- Rosie O'Donnell returns as Narrator in Broadway's "The Rocky Horror Show."
- Routine casting announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Rocky-Horror-Show-Broadway.webp)

Rosie O’Donnell will return to Broadway’s “The Rocky Horror Show” on January 12, 2027, taking over as the Narrator at Studio 54 after Margaret Cho gives her final performance on January 10.

O’Donnell is scheduled to remain with the production through March 28. She first played the Narrator for two weeks in the fall, appearing from October 6 through October 18 while Rachel Dratch, who originated the role in the current revival, was away.

In Richard O’Brien’s musical, the Narrator recounts what happens to Brad and Janet, a couple who take shelter from the rain at the home of Frank-N-Furter, a scientist. Kevin McHale and Lorna Courtney currently play Brad and Janet, with Jake Shears as their host. The score includes “Dammit Janet,” “Sweet Transvestite” and “The Time Warp.”
