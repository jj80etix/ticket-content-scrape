# Diana Ross Sets First Australian Concert in More Than 20 Years

## Summary

Diana Ross will headline the inaugural Sydney Summertime concert series with her first Australian show in more than 20 years, playing Centennial Park on March 5, 2027, the night before Sydney's Mardi Gras parade. She joins The Strokes and Kings of Leon as the series' third announced international headliner.

### Key Points
- First Australian performance since 2006; presented by Black Peach Touring, backed by the NSW tourism agency Destination NSW.
- Presale opens Nov. 9; Groove Armada will DJ, with more support acts to be announced.
- Part of a new, government-supported concert series aimed at driving tourism.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Diana-Ross-.webp)

Diana Ross has announced an exclusive Australian concert at Sydney’s Centennial Park on March 5, 2027, marking her first performance in the country in more than two decades.

The concert, billed as Diana Ross Freedom in Motion, will headline the inaugural Sydney Summertime series. Scheduled for the night before the Sydney Gay and Lesbian Mardi Gras Parade, the performance will be Ross’s only Australian show.

British electronic duo Groove Armada will perform a DJ set, with additional artists to be announced. Presales open Monday, November 9, at 9 a.m. local time through Black Peach Touring.

Ross joins The Strokes and Kings of Leon as the third international headliner announced for Sydney Summertime’s first season. The all-ages concert series is supported by Destination NSW, the New South Wales government agency responsible for the state’s visitor economy.

The announcement marks Ross’s first Australian concert booking since November 2006, when she performed at Melbourne’s Regent Theatre and Flemington Racecourse and Sydney’s Capitol Theatre. Her return will bring the singer to an outdoor venue for a single performance rather than a multi-city Australian run.

Black Peach Touring, which is presenting the show, launched last year through Jessica Ducrou, co-founder and former CEO of Secret Sounds Group. Ducrou and fellow Black Peach co-founder and director Elise Huntley announced the booking in a joint statement.

“Diana Ross is an absolute icon and to have her choose Sydney Summertime for her only Australian show, one night only in Centennial Park, on the eve of Mardi Gras is exactly the kind of unforgettable moment we created this series for,” they said. “It’s going to be something very special.”

Ross established her career as the lead singer of the Supremes before achieving further success as a solo artist. Her recordings across both periods have made her catalogue familiar to several generations of listeners, a point highlighted by New South Wales Minister for Jobs and Tourism Steve Kamper.

Kamper described Ross as “one of the most iconic performers in music history, with a career spanning decades and a catalogue of hits loved by generations of fans.”

“To have an international superstar of her caliber headline at Sydney Summertime for an Australian-exclusive performance is a major coup for NSW,” he said.

“Events like this are a powerful driver of tourism. They bring more visitors to our state, generating economic benefits for local businesses and jobs, and reinforce NSW’s reputation as a destination for world-class live music and entertainment.”
