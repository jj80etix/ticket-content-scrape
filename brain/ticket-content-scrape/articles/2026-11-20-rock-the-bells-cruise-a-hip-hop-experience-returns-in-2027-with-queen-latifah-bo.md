# Rock The Bells Cruise: A Hip-Hop Experience Returns In 2027 With Queen Latifah & Bone Thugs-N-Harmony Headlining

## Summary

Rock The Bells Cruise returns for its fifth year, a hip-hop-themed cruise co-produced with Sixthman, headlined by Queen Latifah and Bone Thugs-N-Harmony, with MC Lyte, Rakim, Trina, Talib Kweli and others. The Norwegian Joy sails Miami to Belize and Mexico Oct. 27-Nov. 1, 2027, following four prior sold-out sailings.

### Key Points
- Fifth annual sailing; Norwegian Joy, Oct. 27-Nov. 1, 2027, Miami → Harvest Caye, Belize → Cozumel, Mexico.
- Headliners: Queen Latifah, Bone Thugs-N-Harmony; also MC Lyte, Rakim, Trina, Talib Kweli, Ying Yang Twins, Jungle Brothers and more, with additional acts to be announced.
- Co-produced by Rock The Bells (founded by LL Cool J) and Sixthman; prior four sailings all sold out.

The ultimate hip-hop cruise returns next year with a diverse lineup that honors hip-hop’s legacy. Rock The Bells, a platform founded by LL Cool J to preserve the hip-hop movement and its history, and Sixthman are teaming up again for Rock The Bells Cruise: A Hip-Hop Experience, which features Rock and Roll Hall of Fame inductee Queen Latifah and Bone Thugs-N-Harmony as headliners, as well as MC Lyte, Rakim, Trina, Talib Kweli and many more.

The Norwegian Joy sets sail Oct. 27-Nov. 1, from Miami to Harvest Caye, Belize, and Cozumel, Mexico. Other acts on the bill include Ying Yang Twins, Devin the Dude, Camp Lo, Yo-Yo, Philadelphia Freeway, Jungle Brothers, Mia X, Shawnna and Pastor Try, with more artists to be announced.

“Four sold-out sailings later, we’re raising the bar again for year five,” Rock The Bells founder LL Cool J said in a statement. “There’s nothing like being out on the water with Hip-Hop all around you: the performances, the energy on deck, the late nights, the people. From the moment you board to the moment you step off, it’s a full Hip-Hop experience, and our team is making this one truly unforgettable.”

In addition to the music, there will be specially curated programming for fans, music from DJs such as DJ Premier, Mannie Fresh, Just Blaze, Grandmaster Flash and Technician the DJ, and comedy performances from Tisha Campbell, Tony Roberts, Daphnique Springs and Lance Woods. Roxanne Shante and Torae will be hosting Rock The Bells Cruise.

The five-day sailing delivers a complete 360-degree lifestyle experience that includes wellness sessions led by fitness pioneer Big Lez, themed parties, intimate artist conversations, exclusive experiences, meetups, mixers and unexpected moments throughout the ship.

Fans can sign up now for first-round presale access to Rock The Bells Cruise: A Hip-Hop Experience at [RockTheBellsCruise.com](https://www.rockthebellscruise.com/). The deadline to sign up for the first-round presale is Dec. 2 at 11:59 p.m. ET, and the deadline for the final round presale is Dec. 9 at 11:59 p.m. ET. Fans can save $100 per cabin when booking through the presale, and they can reserve a cabin with just $100 down when booking before Feb. 27 or while cabins last. The general onsale begins Dec. 11 at 2 p.m. ET.
