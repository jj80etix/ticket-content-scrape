# Eagles Extend Sphere Residency Through 2027

## Summary

The Eagles are extending their Las Vegas Sphere residency through January 2027 with four newly added dates, citing "unprecedented demand." The extension brings the band's total Sphere show count to 72, making it the longest-running residency at the venue since it launched in September 2024.

### Key Points
- New dates added: January 15, 16, 22 and 23, 2027, bringing the residency total to 72 shows.
- Presale windows: artist presale Aug. 12, Live Nation All Access presale Aug. 13, general onsale Aug. 14 (all 10 a.m. PT).
- Residency began September 2024 and remains the longest-running show run at Sphere to date.

## Transcript

![The Eagles residency at The Sphere in Las Vegas](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Eagles-Sphere.webp)

The Eagles residency at The Sphere in Las Vegas

The rock icons of the Eagles have extended their residency once again at the Las Vegas Sphere through 2027, citing “unprecedented demand.”

”Live in Concert at Sphere” will now run through January 2027, with additional dates on January 15, 16, 22, and 23. This marks a total of 72 shows and makes the Eagles’ residency the longest-running residency at the Sphere.

Tickets for the newly-announced gigs head on sale starting with an [artist presale](https://eagles.com/) Wednesday, August 12 at 10 a.m. PT; fan can register for the presale [here](https://eagles.com/). Additionally, tickets will be available during a [Live Nation presale](https://ticketmaster.evyy.net/c/1882189/264167/4272?subId1=the-eagles&subId2=sphere-residency-january-2027&u=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ticketmaster.com%2Feagles-tickets%2Fartist%2F734977) for [all access members](https://www.livenation.com/allaccess) on Thursday, August 13 at 10 a.m. PT. A general sale will follow Friday, August 14 at 10 a.m. PT [here](https://eagles.com/).

The Eagles first kicked-off their Sphere residency in September 2024, followed by farewell shows on “The Long Goodbye Act III” tour. The residency is set to resume in September, with shows in November and December.
