# Soundgarden to Reunite for Seahawks Halftime Show With Taylor Momsen

## Summary

Soundgarden will reunite for the Seattle Seahawks' NFL halftime show, with Taylor Momsen handling lead vocals in place of the late Chris Cornell.

### Key Points
- Soundgarden's surviving members will reunite for a Seattle Seahawks NFL halftime performance.
- Taylor Momsen (The Pretty Reckless) will sing lead vocals.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Soundgarden-halftime-show.webp)

Soundgarden will reunite for a special halftime performance during the Seattle Seahawks’ NFL season opener on Wednesday, September 9 at Lumen Field in Seattle.

Taylor Momsen, frontwoman of The Pretty Reckless, will handle lead vocals for the performance, while Pearl Jam guitarist Mike McCready will also join the band onstage.

The appearance will mark another rare performance by the surviving members of Soundgarden following the death of lead singer Chris Cornell in 2017. Cornell died while the band was on tour, effectively bringing Soundgarden’s regular touring activity to an end.

The Seahawks will face the New England Patriots in a rematch of Super Bowl LX. Seattle defeated New England 29-13 in February to win the championship.

The performance will bring together several figures closely associated with Seattle’s rock history. Soundgarden members Kim Thayil, Ben Shepherd and Matt Cameron will share the stage with Momsen and McCready, both of whom have previously performed alongside the group.

Momsen’s connection to Soundgarden stretches back years. The Pretty Reckless served as an opening act on Soundgarden’s final tour in 2017, and she has subsequently joined the surviving members for performances honoring late frontman Chris Cornell.

Momsen and McCready were both part of Soundgarden’s performance at the 2025 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction ceremony in Los Angeles. The pair joined Thayil, Shepherd and Cameron during the celebration of the band’s career, including a performance of “Rusty Cage.”

Notably, McCready will have an additional role during the September 9 event. The Pearl Jam guitarist is scheduled to perform the national anthem before kickoff on behalf of the Washington Innocence Project, which is a Seahawks Spirit of 12 partner.
