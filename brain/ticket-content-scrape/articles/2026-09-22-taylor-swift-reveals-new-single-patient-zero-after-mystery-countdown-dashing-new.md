# Taylor Swift Reveals New Single ‘Patient Zero’ After Mystery Countdown, Dashing New Tour Hopes

## Summary

Taylor Swift ended a mystery website countdown by announcing a new single, "Patient Zero," out Sept. 25 with limited-edition CDs available for a 24-hour preorder window -- not the new tour some fans had speculated the teaser would reveal.

### Key Points
- Swift's countdown teaser revealed new single "Patient Zero," releasing Sept. 25.
- Limited-edition physical CDs open for a roughly 24-hour preorder window.
- No new tour was announced, despite fan speculation.

**UPDATE: 2 p.m.** Tuesday, September 22

Taylor Swift has revealed that her mysterious Tuesday website countdown was leading to new music — though not the new tour some fans had been hoping for.

Swift announced shortly after the timer expired at 2 p.m. ET that her new single, “Patient Zero,” will be released Sept. 25, with a collection of limited-edition physical CDs available to preorder through her website for approximately 24 hours.

“I’ve been impatiently waiting to tell you that my brand new single ‘Patient Zero’ will be out on September 25,” Swift wrote in a post to her verified Instagram account minutes after the countdown ended.

Swift’s online store now promotes Collector’s Edition CDs associated with “Patient Zero,” displaying three different physical editions and an option to purchase the collection together. According to the store, the limited offer is scheduled to remain available until 1:59 p.m. ET Wednesday, Sept. 23, while supplies last.

The limited sales window applies to the physical preorder promotion. Swift’s announcement says the single itself arrives Sept. 25.

What Tuesday’s reveal did not include was another Taylor Swift tour.

No concert dates, venues, presale registration, primary ticketing information or other live-event plans accompanied the “Patient Zero” announcement.

Before the reveal, Swift’s countdown immediately generated speculation among fans about what could be coming next. Online theories before the reveal ranged from additional *The Life of a Showgirl* material and another Taylor’s Version project to a possible return to touring. Reddit discussions Tuesday show fans debating many of those possibilities before the clock expired.

It is also not yet clear from Swift’s announcement whether “Patient Zero” will stand alone or form part of a larger release. Despite the visual connections fans have drawn to her existing material, Swift’s initial announcement identifies it simply as a “brand new single.” Any connection to a deluxe *Life of a Showgirl* release remains speculation unless Swift provides additional details.

The possibility of another Swift tour nevertheless attracted attention because her last live run remains the commercial benchmark for the concert business. The Eras Tour concluded in Vancouver in December 2024 after 149 shows, [generating $2.077 billion in ticket sales and drawing more than 10 million attendees](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/12/taylor-swift-brought-in-over-2-billion-on-the-eras-tour/).

For now, however, Tuesday’s countdown belongs to “Patient Zero,” not another stadium run. The single arrives Friday, Sept. 25, while the Collector’s Edition CD preorder window is set to close Wednesday afternoon.
