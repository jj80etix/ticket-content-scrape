# FTC Live Al Parque sees Ecuador ‘make its case’ for international touring

## Summary

Ecuador's debut FTC Live Al Parque festival drew 17,000 fans to Quito, with promoter Feel The Club calling it a milestone step for the country as a destination for international touring acts, headlined by Maroon 5.

### Key Points

- One-day festival held at Parque Bicentenario in Quito, with a bill including Myke Towers and Lost Frequencies alongside Maroon 5.
- Promoter Feel The Club (FTC) is also building a permanent outdoor venue in the same park and has been expanding its footprint across neighboring Latin American countries.
