# Ticketmaster Users Report Site Down Friday

## Summary

Ticketmaster's website and app went down for an unspecified period Friday, July 31, with Downdetector showing a spike in user reports and frustrated fans (trying to buy hockey and Calvin Harris tickets, among others) venting on social media. Ticketmaster had not issued a public explanation as of publication, and the outage's cause and duration remain unconfirmed.

### Key Points
- Downdetector showed a spike in Ticketmaster outage reports Friday afternoon.
- Multiple users reported being blocked from purchasing tickets (hockey, Calvin Harris) via app and web.
- Ticketmaster had not publicly acknowledged or explained the outage at time of writing.

### Highlights
- Outage blocked ticket purchases across web and mobile app during active demand.
- No public root-cause or duration disclosed by Ticketmaster.

### Industry Problem
Even a routine (non-onsale-spike) reliability failure at the dominant primary platform directly blocks patrons from completing purchases, reinforcing the platform-reliability pain point patrons already associate with Ticketmaster.

### Proposed Solution
No fix is described in the article; for Etix, the takeaway is to keep uptime and fast incident-communication as a differentiator — publicly acknowledging outages quickly is a low-cost trust-building response competitors aren't reliably providing.
