# Earth, Wind & Fire Postpone San Francisco Show Amid ‘Medical Emergency’

## Summary

Earth, Wind & Fire postponed their Aug. 6 San Francisco co-headline show with Lionel Richie at Chase Center, citing a band member's medical emergency shortly before showtime.

### Key Points
- Existing tickets will be honored at the rescheduled date; refunds are available at point of purchase for those who can't attend.
- No rescheduled date or details on the medical emergency were given.
- The "Sing a Song All Night Long" tour continues with Phoenix (Aug. 11) and a final Austin date (Aug. 14).

## Transcript

![Earth, Wind & Fire](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Earth-Wind-Fire.jpg)

Earth, Wind & Fire

Earth, Wind & Fire had to postpone its show with Lionel Richie in San Francisco on Thursday, citing a medical emergency.

The funk band was set to take the stage at San Francisco’s Chase Center on August 6. However, just before the concert was set to begin, the group wrote on Instagram that they are “unable to perform as scheduled” amid “a medical emergency involving a member of our band.”

Ticketholders are encouraged to hold onto their tickets, as they will be honored at the new show date. Those who would rather receive a refund can reach out via point of purchase.

“We sincerely apologize for the unexpected change and appreciate everyone’s understanding,” the band added.
