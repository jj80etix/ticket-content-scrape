# Int’l Lineups: Billie Eilish At Roskilde, Jungle At Latitude, Boardmasters, FOCUS Wales & More

## Summary

A roundup of international festival lineup announcements, led by FOCUS Wales revealing its first 60 names (Yard Act, Kate Nash, Peaches) for its May 2027 Wrexham edition, alongside briefer items covering Billie Eilish at Roskilde, Jungle at Latitude, and Boardmasters.

### Key Points
- FOCUS Wales 2027 (May 6-8, Wrexham) announces first 60 acts, headlined by Yard Act, Kate Nash, and Peaches.
- Additional international lineup notes cover Billie Eilish at Roskilde, Jungle at Latitude, and Boardmasters festivals.
- Roundup-style piece aggregating several unrelated festival announcements.

**FOCUS Wales Reveals First 60**

FOCUS Wales has announced the first 60 names for its 2027 edition, returning to Wrexham, May 6-8.

The first announcement brings together established names, emerging talent and adventurous projects from Wales and around the world, reflecting the breadth of contemporary music and the festival’s role in putting Welsh creativity on the international stage.

Leading the announcement are Yard Act, Kate Nash and Peaches. Further highlights include British-Nigerian poet and musician Joshua Idehen, Birmingham folk singer-songwriter Katherine Priddy, Bristol’s Grandmas House, and a host of Welsh talent including Melin Melyn, The Family Battenburg, Lonely Mountain, Mace The Great, GWEN, the solo project of Gwenllian Anthony, HØST, and Cerys Hafana.

Also joining the line-up are Scottish-Nigerian singer-songwriter Aleena, Canadian indie-folk artist Charlotte Cornfield, Rwandan-Belgian hip-hop and soul artist Fred Gata, Cardiff rap duo Nathan Misra and Patchwork Picasso, and North Wales psychedelic pop outfit Gintis.

FOCUS Wales 2027, now in its 17th year, rightfully describes itself as “a celebration of musical diversity, cultural exchange and independent creativity, bringing together established international names, new discoveries and the next generation of Welsh talent for three days of music and industry activity in Wrexham.”

FOCUS Wales 2027 takes place May 6-8, 2027 across multiple venues in Wrexham, North Wales. A standard festival pass starts at £90 ($117.70) plus fees.

**Kendal Calling’s Headliners**

Kendal Calling announced a 2027 line-up with main stage headliners The Chemical Brothers, Black Eyed Peas, Blossoms and Jamie T, who will perform when the festival returns to Lowther Deer Park in the UK’s Lake District, July 29–Aug. 1.

More than 100 acts have been announced, including James, The Wombats, Dizzee Rascal, Declan McKenna, Razorlight, DMA’s, The Lottery Winners, The K’s, Scouting For Girls, Vengaboys, Pale Waves, Nubian Twist, Esmerelda Road, Saint Clair, and many more.

As usual, Kendal Calling boasts a rich comedy lineup, led by Lucy Beaumont, Hannah East, Rhys Darby, and also featuring Dick & Dom, The Lancashire Hotpots, Sh\*t Indie Disco and Dave Masterman’s All My Friends.

Andy Smith, co-founder of Kendal Calling, commented, “We’re incredibly excited to reveal next summer’s Kendal Calling line-up. We’ve worked harder than ever on this program, but being able to bring some long-requested legends, Will.i.am being a personal highlight, to the Lakes alongside old friends makes it all worthwhile!

“The stars have finally aligned and The Chemical Brothers will be treating us to a techno spectacle whilst the boys in Blossoms are back where they belong in the fields, having been part of the Kendal Calling family for over a decade now. Oh, and then there’s Black Eyed Peas… Honestly, we’re still pinching ourselves at this booking! They’ll be getting the party started along with hundreds of other acts and artists announced for next summer and there’s even more to come. We cannot wait!”

The presale kicks off Thursday, Nov. 26. A registration is required. If tickets remain, those will go on sale Friday, Nov. 27 at 10 a.m. local British time.

**Jungle At Latitude**

Latitude Festival, which returns to Henham Park, Suffolk, UK, July 22-25, 2027, has announced its first headliner, which will be Jungle.

Jungle take the stage at Latitude Festival’s Obelisk Arena, bringing their fifth studio album Sunshine with them, which marked a new chapter for the BRIT Award-winning trio, with Lydia Kitto now an official member alongside founders J Lloyd and Tom McFarland.

Latitude founder and director Melvin Benn commented, “Latitude has always been about what comes next, and 2027 will be bolder than anything we’ve done. Part of that is backing artists early and bringing them back bigger. Jungle played the Second Stage here in 2014. Now they headline. That’s what Latitude is about: giving artists the platform to take the next step, and welcoming them back as they reach new heights. But we’re also looking at the festival as a whole, continuing to push its boundaries and create something that feels distinctive, ambitious and full of surprises. We want Latitude to be a place where audiences can discover something extraordinary around every corner. This is just the beginning.”

General weekend tickets for Latitude, including camping, start at £320 ($418.50).

The announcement follows another announcement from the band itself, which stated that Jungle will bring their live show to Bristol for an outdoor show at Queen Square on Saturday, June 19, as part of their ongoing arena tour, spanning North America, Europe, the UK, Australia and Latin America.

**Lineup For Multi-Venue Desertfest London**

Desertfest London unveiled hardcore pioneers Converge as its final headliners for the 2027 edition, which takes place across several venues in London’s Camden Town district, May 21-23.

Converge will perform their seminal 2001 album *Jane Doe* in its entirety at the legendary Roundhouse.

Joining the growing bill are North Carolina’s dirt-weed heroes Weedeater, Greek psychedelic explorers Naxatras, and the experimental sounds of One Leg One Eye. Also confirmed are LLNN, Oryx, Black Bile, Walk Through Fire, DDENT & Owlcrusher, Codex Serafini, The Hebridean Sheep, Bosco Sacro, Matador, Hades Labyrinth, S.U.G.A.R & Magick Ritual, Tayne, Greet, Witchthroat Serpent, Riders of Rohan, Jim Ghedi, and more.

Early & Bird Phase One Weekend Tickets for the event are SOLD OUT, with Phase Two tickets now on sale via [www.desertfest.co.uk](http://www.desertfest.co.uk), starting at £169.44 ($221.60), including fees.

Aside from the Roundhouse, the venues taking part in Desertfest London are The Electric Ballroom, The Underworld, The Black Heart, and The Devonshire Arms.

**Boardmasters’ Wave One**

Spanning the clifftops of Watergate Bay and the shores of Fistral Beach in the UK, Boardmasters returns Aug. 4–8, 2027, with a lineup led by Chase & Status (Live), Disclosure (Live) and Wolf Alice.

Also announced in wave one: Dutch DJ Chris Stassy, who will make history as Boardmasters’ first ever Thursday headliner; Becky Hill, Ezra Collective, Two Door Cinema Club, Skye Newman, Blossoms, Giggs, Eve, Declan McKenna, La Roux, Holly Humberstone, and many more.

The indie and alternative program, for instance, features The Vaccines, The Fratellis, The Big Moon, Overpass, Scouting for Girls, Red Rum Club, Matilda Mann, Newton Faulkner, Seb Lowe, Arkayla and Cowboy Hunters on the bill.

Set against the Atlantic coastline of Cornwall, UK, Boardmasters brings together world-class music, contemporary culture as well as the Boardmasters OPEN surf competition.

Rob Spring, Festival Director at Boardmasters, commented, “This is one of the strongest first waves we’ve ever announced. Chase & Status, Disclosure and Wolf Alice are three of the most exciting live acts in the country, and around them you’ve got everything from Ezra Collective and Becky Hill to Two Door Cinema Club and Giggs. That range is what Boardmasters is all about.

“Opening the Main Stage on Thursday is something we’ve wanted to do for a long time. It gives people a full extra day of live music on the clifftops, and having Chris Stassy as our first ever Thursday headliner, alongside BBC Radio 1 Dance, sets the tone perfectly. Boardmasters started as a surf competition on these beaches over forty years ago, and Cornwall is still at the heart of everything we do. Every festival has a line-up, but not every festival has Cornwall and we can’t wait to welcome everyone back to Watergate Bay and Fistral next August.”

Tickets for Boardmasters 2027 go on pre-sale on Thursday Nov. 26 at 10 a.m. for those who have signed up, with general sale opening on Friday Nov. 27 at 10 a.m.

**Billie Eilish At Roskilde**

Billie Eilish is set to perform at two of Europe’s biggest events next year: BST Hyde Park in London, as previously announced, and Roskilde Festival in Denmark, where she will take to the main stage on Wednesday, June 30.

Roskilde’s 2027 roster also includes Tame Impala, Patti Smith Quartet, Caroline Polachek, Ezra Collective, Kacey Musgraves, Lambrini Girls, Underscores and more, with plenty of acts still to be announced.

Roskilde Festival takes place June 26–July 3, 2027 in Roskilde, Denmark. Tickets are on sale. A full festival ticket for the entire week costs DKK 2,650 ($387.90), while a one-day ticket costs DKK 1,530 ($223.90). Two-day tickets, which allow festival participants to combine two of the final four festival days on the same ticket, cost DKK 2,230 ($326.40). All ticket prices include fees.

*Belgium*

**11 More For Rock Werchter**

Royal Blood, WILLOW, mgk and more are coming to Rock Werchter, July 1-4, 2027, at Festivalpark Werchter, Belgium.

11 acts were added since Pollstar ran the last lineup update on Belgium’s biggest festival. The bill for Thursday, July 1, is expanded with WILLOW, DIIV & Boys Noize. New additions for Friday, July 2, are Royal Blood and Warhaus. On Saturday, July 3, promoters welcome mgk, Tricky, KALEO, Wilco and Belgian star Pommelien Thijs. And for Sunday, July 4 there is a new name too: Keo!

Rock Werchter 2027 will take place from Thursday 1st to Sunday 4th July at the Festivalpark in Werchter. Tickets are available via ticketmaster.be. One-day, two-day and four-day tickets are available.
