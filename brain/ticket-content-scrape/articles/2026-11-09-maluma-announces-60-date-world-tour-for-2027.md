# Maluma Announces 60-Date World Tour for 2027

## Summary

Maluma announced the 60-date "Loco X Volver World Tour" for 2027, spanning Latin America, North America, Europe and Africa, opening Feb. 5 in Bogotá, Colombia.

### Key Points
- Opens Feb. 5 at Heart of Bogotá Arena; two Buenos Aires shows Feb. 19-20 at Movistar Arena.
- Additional Latin American stops: Mexico City, Santiago, Lima.
- North America: Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, New York, Chicago; Europe: Madrid, Paris, Berlin, Milan.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Maluma.webp)

Maluma has announced a 60-date world tour for 2027. The “Loco X Volver World Tour” will include arena performances across Latin America, North America, Europe and Africa, with the first three shows confirmed in Colombia and Argentina.

The tour is scheduled to begin February 5 at the Heart of Bogotá Arena in Bogotá, Colombia. Maluma will then perform two nights at Movistar Arena in Buenos Aires, Argentina, on February 19 and February 20.

The announcement confirms several additional destinations across Latin America, including Mexico City, Santiago and Lima. North American stops will include Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, New York and Chicago. In Europe, Maluma has announced plans to visit Madrid, Paris, Berlin and Milan.

In a statement accompanying the announcement, Maluma said the tour would give him an opportunity to reconnect with audiences who have supported his career. “There are places that shaped my story and people who made every one of my dreams possible,” he said.

“Loco X Volver was born from a deep desire to go back to where it all began, to reunite with those who have grown up alongside me, and to reach new corners of the world so we can keep writing our story together.”

The singer also described the concerts as a way to recognize his fans’ role in his success.

“Because Maluma wouldn’t exist without my fans, without their love and without every voice that has sung my songs,” he said. “This tour is my way of thanking them and reminding them that no matter how far we go, wherever my people are, I’ll always be home.”

The tour takes its name from Maluma’s seventh album, *Loco X Volver*, released in May.
