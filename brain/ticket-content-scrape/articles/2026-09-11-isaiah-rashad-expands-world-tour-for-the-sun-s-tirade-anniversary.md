# Isaiah Rashad Expands World Tour for ‘The Sun’s Tirade’ Anniversary

## Summary

Isaiah Rashad added a second leg of his "Lil Sunny's Awful Road Trip World Tour," announcing 34 new dates across South Africa, North America, Europe, the UK, Australia and New Zealand to mark the 10th anniversary of his album *The Sun's Tirade*. General on-sale for the new dates began Sept. 11.

### Key Points
- New North American leg opens Nov. 4 in Portland, OR; includes a Nov. 15 Camp Flog Gnaw appearance in LA.
- European/UK leg begins Jan. 10, 2027 in Dublin; Australia/New Zealand leg opens Feb. 23, 2027 in Honolulu and runs through Perth.
- Extension follows the tour's ongoing first North American leg supporting Rashad's new album *It's Been Awful*.
- Marks the 10th anniversary of *The Sun's Tirade*, which featured the Kendrick Lamar collaboration "Wat's Wrong."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Isaiah-Rashad-awful-road-trip.webp)

Isaiah Rashad has announced a second leg of his “Lil Sunny’s Awful Road Trip World Tour,” adding 34 appearances across South Africa, North America, Europe, the United Kingdom, Australia and New Zealand.

The expanded schedule celebrates the 10th anniversary of Rashad’s 2016 album *The Sun’s Tirade*. The platinum-certified project includes “Free Lunch,” “4r Da Squaw” and “Wat’s Wrong,” his collaboration with Kendrick Lamar.

The newly announced dates begin with a second North American leg that opens November 4 at the Crystal Ballroom in Portland, Oregon. The routing includes stops in Seattle, Oakland, Sacramento, San Diego, San Antonio and other cities before concluding November 25 at The Signal in Rashad’s hometown of Chattanooga, Tennessee. He will also appear at Camp Flog Gnaw in Los Angeles on November 15.

The tour resumes in 2027 with a European and U.K. leg beginning January 10 at the Academy in Dublin. Rashad will perform in Manchester, London and Bristol before visiting Amsterdam, Brussels, Milan, Paris, Zurich, Berlin, Cologne and Warsaw.

A final stretch opens February 23 at The Republik in Honolulu. Rashad will then make his first headlining appearances in Australia and New Zealand, including concerts in Wellington, Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane. The announced itinerary concludes March 7 at Metro City in Perth.

The tour extension follows the first North American leg of “Lil Sunny’s Awful Road Trip,” which is currently underway with support from Alemeda. That run supports *It’s Been Awful*, Rashad’s third studio album and first full-length release since *The House Is Burning* arrived in 2021.

General ticket sales for the newly announced concerts begin September 11 at 10 a.m. local time.
