# Night Ranger Adds 2027 Dates To Ongoing Tour

## Summary

Night Ranger added 2027 tour dates, extending its ongoing run into the new year starting Jan. 14 at the Keswick Theatre in Glenside, PA, and continuing through a four-night "80s Cruise" stop in New Orleans in late February/early March 2027.

### Key Points
- New 2027 dates begin Jan. 14 in Glenside, PA; extend through March 2, 2027 in New Orleans.
- Band is currently on its 2026 leg, including a Sept. 11 stop at the Madera District Fair.
- 2026 dates include the Ryman Auditorium (Nashville) and a multi-night Las Vegas "KISS Kruise" run.
- Tickets available via nightranger.com.

## Transcript

Night Ranger is keeping the party going into 2027, adding new dates to their ongoing tour. The 2027 dates kick off in Glenside, PA, at the Keswick Theatre on Jan. 14. From there, the ‘80s rockstars will hit cities like Stroudsburg, PA, Jackson, MI, Oxford, AL, Orlando, FL, and then four nights in New Orleans, LA as a part of the ‘80s Cruise from Feb. 27 to Mar. 2.

The band is currently on tour, trekking through the United States with a show at Madera, CA, at the Madera District Fair on Sept. 11. Additional dates and stops include North Tonawanda, NY, on Nov. 5 at the Riviera Theatre, the Ryman Auditorium in Nashville, TN, and ending in Englewood, NJ, at the Bergen Performing Arts Center on Dec. 13. See full tour routing below.
