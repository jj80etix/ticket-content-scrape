# Chief Keef Adds Two New Dates For ‘Live 2026’ Tour

## Summary

Chief Keef added two dates — Pittsburgh (Oct. 12) and Milwaukee (Oct. 14) — to his "Chief Keef Live 2026" tour, which opens Sept. 12 in his hometown Chicago (already sold out) and runs through Oct. 28 in Las Vegas.

### Key Points
- New dates: Oct. 12 Pittsburgh (Roxian Theatre), Oct. 14 Milwaukee (Landmark Credit Union Live).
- Tour opener (Sept. 12, Chicago) already sold out — his first headline run since 2024.
- 18 total U.S. stops before wrapping Oct. 28 in Las Vegas.

Chief Keef is adding two new dates to his “Chief Keef Live 2026” tour, with the rapper now making stops on Oct. 12 in Pittsburgh, PA, at the Roxian Theatre and on Oct. 14 in Milwaukee, WI, at the Landmark Credit Union Live. Tickets for Chief Keef concerts are available [HERE](https://chiefkeef.com/). See full tour routing below.

  
The news arrived on the heels of Chief Keef previously selling out the opening night of his “Chief Keef Live 2026” tour. The tour will kick things off in his hometown of Chicago at Huntington Bank Pavilion at Northerly Island on Sept. 12.

From there, Keef will perform 18 times across the United States, with stops in Detroit, Houston, Indianapolis, New Orleans, Philadelphia, Virginia Beach, and more. The tour will conclude on Oct. 28 at the Brooklyn Bowl in Las Vegas, NV. The ‘Chief Keef Live 2026’ tour is the first time that the Chicago artist will be embarking on his first headlining run since 2024.

The set will span Chief Keef’s expansive catalog, from *Finally Rich* staples like “Love Sosa” to material from his latest mixtape, *Skeletor*, with surprise guest appearances expected throughout the tour.

In 2026, Chief Keef released the viral tracks “Shake Dat” and “Rooms,” collaborated with Katy Perry on the “Legendary Lovers Remix,” and released *Skeletor*, setting the stage for the current tour.
