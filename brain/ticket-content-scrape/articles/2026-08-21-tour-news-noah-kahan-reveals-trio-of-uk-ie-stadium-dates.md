# Tour news: Noah Kahan reveals trio of UK/IE stadium dates

## Summary

IQ Magazine's tour-news roundup: Noah Kahan has announced a trio of UK/Ireland stadium dates ahead of wrapping his North American stadium tour, alongside briefer mentions of Foo Fighters opening for AC/DC and Chris Brown/Usher and Jonas Brothers tour expansions. Roundup of tour-date announcements with no ticketing-platform or pricing angle.

## Transcript

Plus: Foo Fighters to open for AC/DC, and Chris Brown & Usher expand R&B Tour, and Jonas Brothers unveil 45-date arena run

Ahead of wrapping up his debut North American stadium tour, Noah Kahan has announced he’ll play a trio of stadiums in the UK and Ireland in 2027, his biggest-ever shows in the market.

Next year, he’ll launch *The Last of the Bugs* tour, with dates lined up at Glasgow’s Hampden Park (13 July), Dublin’s Aviva Stadium (17 July), and London’s Wembley Stadium (23 July).

The singer-songwriter is currently touring in support of his album, *The Great Divide,* with arena dates still to come in Australia and the UK/IE later this year. On this run, he has sold [1.5 million tickets](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/noah-kahan-sells-out-debut-stadium-tour-with-1m-tickets/), and recently played his largest show to date at California’s Rose Bowl to 50,000 fans.

Elsewhere in stadiums, **AC/DC** have lined up **Foo Fighters** as the openers of their show at Missouri’s The Dome at America’s Center on 8 September.

Stadium headliners in their own right, the band are stepping in for The Pretty Reckless, who have opened for the Australian band throughout the North American leg of the *PWR/UP Tour*.

Frontman Dave Grohl wrote online: “My band Foo Fighters will share a stage with the gentlemen who are single handedly responsible for my determination to give every audience everything I have, every night. This is literally a dream come true. So…..if you want blood….you’ve got it.”

> “We knew we couldn’t stop at New York”

Elsewhere in the US, **Chris Brown & Usher** have added eight shows to their  *R&B Tour*, expanding their stadium co-headlining tour.

The newly announced dates include first-time stops in Baltimore, Cincinnati, Columbia, and Indianapolis, along with a fifth show in Atlanta, fourth in Los Angeles, and third in both East Rutherford and Miami.

Produced by Live Nation, *The R&B Tour* now spans 59 shows and is set to conclude at Tampa’s Raymond James Stadium with shows on 11-12 December.

In response to three sold-out Madison Square Garden shows, boyband stalwarts **The Jonas Brothers** will head back out for *The Burning Up Tour All Over Again*, which is set to take the Disney Channel stars to 45 cities in North America.

Promoted by Live Nation, the tour launches on 25 September at Boston’s TD Garden, with stops lined up in Toronto, Washington, D.C., Miami, Los Angeles, Seattle, Denver, and more. It is currently set to wrap up on 21 December at New Jersey’s Prudential Center.

“Our last tour reminded us just how much this music means to us and to the fans who’ve been with us from the start,” the Jonas Brothers say. “The response was so overwhelming announcing the MSG shows that we knew we couldn’t stop at New York. We’re taking *The Burning Up Tour All Over Again* on the road so we can relive it with all of you, city by city. We can’t wait to run it back with all of you.”

> “We can’t wait to run it back with all of you”

An allocation of tickets will be reserved for the band’s top Spotify listeners through [Reserved](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/who-owns-the-fan-relationship-agents-raise-questions-over-spotify-reserved/), eligible to both US and Canadian fans in a regional expansion of the service.

Alongside the release of her album *What Are The Odds* today, R&B singer **Jorja Smith** has revealed a 2027 tour across the EU, UK, and Australia.

The run launches on 26 May at Amsterdam’s Ziggo Dome, with stops in Paris, Brussels, Düsseldorf, Berlin, Copenhagen, Stockholm, and Oslo, concluding with a massive homecoming show at London’s The O2 on 17 July.

She’ll then travel to Australia for three shows in Brisbane, Sydney, and Melbourne in November 2027.

Rising pop star **Adéla** has extended her  *Red Bottoms Tour* into 2027, adding dates in the UK/EU.

Following a US run later this year, the Slovakian artist will pick up at Dublin’s 3Olympia Theatre (14 January), running through stops like Manchester, Glasgow, London, Paris, Copenhagen, and Warsaw until 3 February.

Two stars showing no signs of slowing, **Willie Nelson and Neil Young** have united for a pair of shows next month.

The first will be staged at Connecticut’s Hartford Healthcare Amphitheater on 22 September, followed by the second at Philadelphia’s Highmark Mann Center for the Performing Arts on 24 September.

Young – who cancelled [most of his live dates](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/neil-young-axes-2026-run-across-uk-eu/) this year – will be backed up by The Chrome Hearts, and Ty Myers will support both shows.
