# Manhattan Theatre Club Completes Cast for Broadway’s ‘Awake and Sing!’

## Summary

Manhattan Theatre Club completed casting for its Broadway revival of Clifford Odets' "Awake and Sing!," adding four actors ahead of December 15 previews. The production, opening January 7, 2027 at the Samuel J. Friedman Theatre, will feature the play's first entirely Jewish Broadway cast in nearly 100 years.

### Key Points
- New cast additions: Michael Aronov, Benjamin Pelteson, Richard Schiff, Stephen Schnetzer
- Joins previously announced cast including Danny Burstein, Micaela Diamond, Jessica Hecht
- Marks the play's fifth Broadway revival; the 2006 staging won the Tony for Best Revival of a Play
- Director Tyne Rafaeli frames the all-Jewish cast as resonant with current questions of identity and belonging

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Broadways-Awake-and-Sing.webp)

Michael Aronov, Benjamin Pelteson, Richard Schiff and Stephen Schnetzer have joined Manhattan Theatre Club’s Broadway revival of “Awake and Sing!”

The announcement completes casting for the Clifford Odets play, directed by Tyne Rafaeli. Previews are scheduled to begin December 15 at the Samuel J. Friedman Theatre. Opening night is January 7, 2027.

Aronov will play Moe Axelrod. Pelteson will portray Sam Feinschreiber, with Schiff as Jacob Berger and Schnetzer as Schlosser.

They join previously announced cast members Danny Burstein as Uncle Morty, Micaela Diamond as Hennie Berger, Jessica Hecht as Bessie Berger, Will Pullen as Ralph Berger and Jeremy Shamos as Myron Berger.

Set in a Bronx tenement during the 1930s, “Awake and Sing!” follows three generations of the Berger family living together in cramped quarters. The play premiered on Broadway in 1935, establishing Odets as a leading playwright, and has since received numerous productions in New York and elsewhere.

**| RELATED:**[**Clifford Odets’ ‘Awake and Sing!’ to Return to Broadway in 2027**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/clifford-odets-awake-and-sing-to-return-to-broadway-in-2027/) **|**

Manhattan Theatre Club’s production will mark the work’s fifth Broadway revival. Its 2006 staging received the Tony Award for Best Revival of a Play.

Rafaeli, whose directing credits include “Data,” said the new production will feature the play’s first entirely Jewish Broadway cast in nearly 100 years. In a statement, she connected the revival to current questions about identity, assimilation and belonging.

“Of course every member of our cast has a different relationship to their American and Jewish identity, but there is a shared cultural foundation which will inform the specificity of Odets’ story,” Rafaeli said.

She also said she hopes to recreate the intimacy of the Group Theatre ensemble that originated the play. “At a time when questions of belonging, assimilation, and antisemitism have acquired renewed urgency, returning to the particular world from which this play emerged feels especially meaningful and I hope its specificity will illuminate universal questions about what it means to fight for a life of dignity,” she said.

The creative team includes David Zinn as scenic and costume designer, Isabella Byrd as lighting designer and Daniel Kluger as composer and sound designer. Kate Wilson will serve as dialect and vocal coach. Casting is by Caparelliotis Casting and Kelly Gillespie. Jennifer Rae Moore is the production stage manager.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [ManhattanTheatreClub.com](http://www.manhattantheatreclub.com/).
