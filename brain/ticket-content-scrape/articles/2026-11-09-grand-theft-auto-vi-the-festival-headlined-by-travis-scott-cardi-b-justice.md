# ‘Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival’ Headlined By Travis Scott, Cardi B, & Justice

## Summary

Rockstar Games announced "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival," a one-off launch celebration Nov. 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami headlined by Travis Scott, Cardi B and Justice, tied to GTA VI's Nov. 19 release. Tickets are distributed through a presale lottery requiring an active DICE account, capped at two tickets per lottery code.

### Key Points
- Presented with PlayStation; produced by the team behind III Points festival.
- Lineup: Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, Sofaygo; Cardi B; Justice (DJ set); Metro Boomin; others.
- Ticket access is lottery-based via DICE account, lottery closed 4 p.m. ET Nov. 10, two-ticket cap per code.

### Highlights
- DICE is the ticketing/access platform for a major non-music-industry brand activation (video-game launch event), showing the platform extending beyond traditional concert ticketing into gaming/entertainment crossover events.
- Access model is an account-gated lottery rather than open sale — a demand-management mechanic for a high-interest, limited-capacity event.

### Industry Problem
High-demand, brand-driven one-off events need a fair, bot-resistant way to allocate scarce capacity without an open onsale rush.

### Proposed Solution
DICE's account-gated lottery format is the mechanism here; for Etix, it's a reminder that lottery/registration-gated access (not just traditional presale codes) is an established tool worth having available for marquee single-date or branded activations.

GTAVI The Festival Lineup

Rockstar Games has announced a special one-of-a-kind Grand Theft Auto 6 launch event — “Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival.” Taking place on Nov. 21 at Mana Wynwood in Miami, this GTAVI release celebration gives fans a chance to experience “the music of Vice City and Leonida in person, with a star-studded lineup of artists from the game and the official soundtrack,” as per an official press release.

The festival is presented in partnership with PlayStation and produced by the creators of III Points; the event will feature two stages of nonstop music. Performances include Cactus Jack presents Travis Scott, Don Toliver, Kodak Black, and Sofaygo; Cardi B; Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso; Justice (DJ set); Metro Boomin; Nick León B2B DJ Python; Prospa B2B Skream; Rauw & Friends; Suero; Tainy; The Dare B2B Fcukers (DJ set).
