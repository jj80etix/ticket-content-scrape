# Interpol Announces Nine-Date U.S. Tour

## Summary

Interpol will tour the US for nine shows in March 2027 supporting their album "This Mirror Weighs a Ton," following three Mexico dates (including a Mexico City show supporting Queens of the Stone Age).

### Key Points
- US tour: March 16 (New Orleans) through March 27 (Sayreville, NJ), including stops in Texas, Memphis, D.C. and Philadelphia.
- Deafheaven supports eight of the nine US shows.
- Precedes three Mexico dates alongside The Black Angels.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Interpol-Tour.webp)

Interpol will return to U.S. stages in March 2027 for a nine-show tour supporting its latest album, *This Mirror Weighs a Ton*.

The tour is slated to kick off on March 16 in New Orleans at Saenger Theatre. From there, the run includes three Texas cities: San Antonio, Hidalgo and Dallas. Following the Texas dates, Interpol will take their music to Memphis, Asheville, Washington D.C., and Philadelphia before wrapping up on March 27 in Sayreville at the Starland Ballroom.

Deafheaven will appear as the special guest at eight performances, with the Memphis concert serving as the only stop without the band.

Before returning to the United States in March, Interpol will play three concerts in Mexico alongside The Black Angels. One of those engagements, in Mexico City, will feature Interpol supporting Queens of the Stone Age. The Mexico appearances precede the newly announced dates with Deafheaven.
