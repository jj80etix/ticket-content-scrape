# Flag Football, Squash Set for Historic Olympic Debuts at LA28

## Summary

Flag Football and Squash will make their Olympic debuts at the LA28 Games, and both sports sold through their entire Drop 1 ticket allocation, prompting LA28 to release additional Drop 2 inventory on a first-come, first-served basis. Squash will be staged at the Comcast Squash Center at Universal Studios, leaning into a Hollywood theme, while Flag Football's inclusion builds on the NFL's youth-program investment and a wave of nations competing internationally.

### Key Points
- Both Flag Football and Squash sold out their initial Drop 1 ticket allocation for LA28.
- Drop 2 inventory is now on sale for both sports, first-come first-served.
- Squash will be held at the new Comcast Squash Center at Universal Studios (Back to the Future's Courthouse Square).
- Flag Football's Olympic debut follows nearly a century after a 1932 LA demonstration appearance; NFL stars may compete for their countries.
- Follows other recent LA28 stories: 12,000 affordable tickets set aside for LA schools, and 12 million ticket registrations two years out.

Two sports are getting their first shot at Olympic gold in Los Angeles — and fans are already buying in.

Flag Football and Squash will make their Olympic debuts at the LA28 Games, with both sports selling through all ticket inventory made available during the first ticket drop. Additional inventory is now being released as part of Drop 2, while supplies last.

For Flag Football, the Olympic stage represents a major milestone for a sport that has exploded in popularity in the U.S. and around the world. More than 40 nations compete in the IFAF World Championships each cycle, while the NFL has helped fuel the game’s growth through investment in youth and community programs.
