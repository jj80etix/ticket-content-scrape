# IQ 147 out now: IFF special issue, The Weeknd’s expansive outing, Neil Warnock’s pioneering path

## Summary

IQ Magazine's September issue (IQ 147) is out, featuring an International Festival Forum special section, a behind-the-scenes look at The Weeknd's five-continent, five-year "After Hours Til Dawn" tour, a profile of agent Neil Warnock, the 2026 Green Guardians sustainability list, and market reports on Austria and Tokyo.

### Key Points
- Issue is built around an IFF (International Festival Forum) special section covering the European festival season.
- Feature story: The Weeknd's "Til Dawn" tour spanned 175 concerts, 8 million tickets, and over $1 billion in gross revenue across five years.
- Neil Warnock named IQ's latest "Architect" profile subject.
- Also includes 30-years-of-ADE retrospective content and the 2026 Green Guardians sustainability recognitions.
