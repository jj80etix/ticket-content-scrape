# Electric Avenue eyeing site expansion with growing international profile

## Summary

New Zealand's Electric Avenue festival is exploring a long-term site expansion in Christchurch's Hagley Park before 2030, aiming to grow capacity to 60,000–70,000 as it has maxed out its current footprint. Organizers say the growth now allows the festival to target top-level international headliners.

### Key Points
- Expansion target window is 2028–2029, driven by successive years of attendance growth.
- Current site within Hagley Park has reached capacity, prompting exploration of additional park areas.
