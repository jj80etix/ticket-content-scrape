# ‘Houdini: A Magical Musical’ Plans 2028 U.S. Tour

## Summary

"Houdini: A Magical Musical" will tour the U.S. in 2028 with producers seeking a limited Broadway run afterward. The show, which premiered in Italy and moved through Madrid, is timed to the 100th anniversary of Harry Houdini's death.

### Key Points
- No dates, cities, or venues announced yet for either the tour or Broadway engagement.
- Written/directed by Federico Bellone with music by Giovanni Lori; won Spain's PTM Award for Best New Musical.
- Announcement coincides with the Oct. 31, 1926 centenary of Houdini's death.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Houdini-A-Magical-Musical.webp)

“Houdini: A Magical Musical” is scheduled to tour the United States in 2028, with producers seeking a limited Broadway engagement following the national run. Dates, cities and venues have not been announced for either engagement.

The production, written and directed by Federico Bellone with music by Giovanni Lori, will make its way to American stages after engagements in Italy and Spain. The musical premiered in Italy in 2025, performed in English, before opening an open-ended run in Madrid. It also received Spain’s PTM Award for Best New Musical.

The U.S. tour announcement coincides with the 100th anniversary of Harry Houdini’s death on October 31, 1926. The famed escape artist’s life and career are the basis of the musical, which moves between his final performance in 1926 and events from his past.

In a statement, producers shared, “In a world where people struggle for recognition—especially on social media—without seeing the cost that pursuit can have on those they love most, this story feels more relevant than ever.”
