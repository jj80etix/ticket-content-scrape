# Houston Dynamo and Dash to give away 10,000 free tickets

## Summary

The Houston Dynamo and Houston Dash are giving away 10,000 free tickets to first-time attendees during the second half of the 2026 season, in partnership with Houston Mayor John Whitmire. The initiative aims to convert World Cup-driven soccer enthusiasm in Houston into lasting fandom by distributing tickets through community organizations, nonprofits, and youth programs.

### Key Points
- 10,000 complimentary tickets will go to first-time attendees across remaining 2026 Dynamo/Dash matches at Shell Energy Stadium.
- The program is a partnership between the clubs and Houston Mayor John Whitmire, timed to capitalize on 2026 World Cup host-city momentum.
- Distribution channels include community organizations, nonprofit partners, and youth programs rather than open public claims.
- Club leadership frames the effort as building a new generation of local soccer fans post-World Cup.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Houston Dynamo and Houston Dash football clubs have launched an initiative that will see 10,000 free tickets given to first-time attendees during the second half of the 2026 season.**

The scheme is in partnership with Houston Mayor John Whitmire and aims to capitalise on the interest in football from the World Cup, in which Houston was a host city.

“This summer, the world came to Houston, and countless Houstonians experienced the excitement and passion of soccer in a whole new way,” said Mayor Whitmire.

“Now it is our turn to make sure that excitement does not end when the World Cup leaves town.

“By welcoming 10,000 first-time fans to Houston Dynamo and Houston Dash matches, we are giving families the opportunity to experience professional soccer right here in their own backyard.

“Every child deserves the chance to fall in love with the game, and every Houstonian should know these clubs belong to them.”

The complimentary tickets will be distributed from now for matches throughout the remainder of the 2026 regular season via community organisations, nonprofit partners, youth programmes, and to Houstonians who have never attended a match.

“One of the greatest legacies the World Cup can leave behind is a new generation of soccer fans,” said Jessica O’Neill, president of business operations for Houston Dynamo FC and Houston Dash.

“We are incredibly grateful to Mayor Whitmire for making that vision a reality.

“There is nothing like experiencing a match at Shell Energy Stadium for the first time, and thanks to this initiative, 10,000 people will have the opportunity to discover what makes Houston Dynamo and Houston Dash soccer so special.

“We cannot wait to welcome them home.”
