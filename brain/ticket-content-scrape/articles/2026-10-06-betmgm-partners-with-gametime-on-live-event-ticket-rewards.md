# BetMGM Partners With Gametime on Live Event Ticket Rewards

## Summary

BetMGM is letting its sports-betting and iGaming loyalty members redeem BetMGM Rewards Points for Gametime credits, which can then be used to buy live-event tickets through Gametime's mobile marketplace. The partnership extends BetMGM's rewards program — previously focused on gaming, hospitality and travel — into live entertainment, and BetMGM says members have already redeemed credits for high-demand concerts and a soccer-tournament final. For Gametime, it's a new acquisition channel that routes a betting company's loyalty points directly into ticket purchases.

### Key Points
- BetMGM customers redeem Rewards Points for Gametime credits via the BetMGM Rewards Store; credits buy tickets on Gametime.
- First live-event redemption option added to a rewards program previously limited to gaming, hospitality and travel.
- BetMGM operates mobile/retail in 31 markets; cites responsible-gambling program (GameSense) as it expands.
- Gametime did not disclose specific high-demand events redeemed for, citing confidentiality.

### Highlights
- A non-ticketing loyalty program (sports betting/iGaming) is now a direct funnel into live-event ticket purchases — a cross-industry points-to-tickets redemption model.
- Gametime gains a new, large-scale customer-acquisition channel outside traditional ticketing marketing.

### Industry Problem
Ticketing platforms largely compete on price, inventory and checkout friction rather than loyalty; this shows an adjacent industry (sports betting) treating ticket access itself as a premium loyalty reward, which could reshape how platforms court repeat, high-value fans.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes a redemption partnership, not a technology; the applicable response for Etix is to watch whether points-based or third-party loyalty redemption into live tickets becomes a broader distribution channel, and whether it's worth exploring similar redemption partnerships (e.g., with hospitality, airline or card-rewards programs) to acquire fans at lower cost than paid marketing.

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/BETMGM-GAMETIME.webp)

BetMGM has partnered with mobile ticketing platform Gametime to give members of its rewards program another way to turn loyalty points into tickets for sports, concerts and other live entertainment.

Under the partnership, BetMGM customers can redeem BetMGM Rewards Points for Gametime credits through the BetMGM Rewards Store. Those credits can then be used to purchase tickets available through [Gametime](https://gametime.co/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The arrangement expands BetMGM’s rewards offering beyond gaming, hospitality and travel by adding live-event tickets as another redemption option.

“Loyalty isn’t just about earning and spending points, it’s about giving players opportunities to enjoy special moments they can’t find anywhere else,” BetMGM Chief Revenue Officer Matt Prevost said. “Our partnership with Gametime gives customers more flexibility in how they use their points while connecting them to the events they care about most.”
