# John Mulaney Plots 9-Date ‘Mulaney Takes Manhattan’ at Iconic New York Venues

## Summary

Comedian John Mulaney announced "Mulaney Takes Manhattan," a nine-night run across iconic NYC venues (Comedy Cellar, Blue Note, Joe's Pub, Bowery Ballroom, Music Box Theatre, Beacon Theatre, and others) in January 2027, building toward a Madison Square Garden finale. Onsale dates weren't yet announced; fans can sign up for presale access via his website.

### Key Points
- Nine-date "Mulaney Takes Manhattan" run across distinct NYC venues, January 2027.
- Presale signup available now via johnmulaney.com; general onsale dates not yet set.
- Run builds toward a Madison Square Garden show, following a previously filmed Radio City special.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/john-mulaney.jpg)

Comedian and actor John Mulaney is heading to New York City to perform at nine of the city’s iconic venues.

The limited run, dubbed “Mulaney Takes Manhattan,” will kick-off the new year on January 7, 2027 at Comedy Cellar. From there, he’ll appear at Blue Note, Joe’s Pub, Bowery Ballroom, The Music Box Theatre, Carnegie Hall, and Radio City Music Hall before wrapping-up at Madison Square Garden on January 15.

“Each show will feature special guests and each one will have a whole lot of different,” Mulaney said in an announcement post. “Going to all 9 might seem extreme but I bet you’d enjoy it. I’ll be going to all of them myself albeit for my own reasons.”

On *Today*, the former SNL writer added that “this is really a great way to do the intimate small places.”
