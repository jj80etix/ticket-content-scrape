# Band Apologizes for Injuring Fan After Throwing Guitar in Audience

## Summary

Japanese punk band Otoboke Beaver publicly apologized after guitarist Yoyoyoshie threw her guitar into the crowd at an April 9 Osaka show, injuring a fan who needed seven stitches; the band acknowledged a four-month delay in addressing the incident and pledged to work with the injured fan and prevent recurrence.

### Key Points
- Incident occurred April 9 at Umeda Club Quattro, Osaka; fan suffered a forehead laceration requiring seven stitches.
- Band apologized on Instagram, citing poor internal communication for the four-month delay in addressing it.
- Band says it will support the fan's recovery and take steps to prevent similar incidents.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/otoboke-beaver.jpg)

Japanese punk-rock band Otoboke Beaver has apologized after a guitar was thrown into the audience and injured a fan.

During a show on April 9 at Umeda Club Quattro in Osaka, Otoboke Beaver guitarist Yoyoyoshie threw her axe guitar into the crowd. The guitar his an audience member, who was taken to the hospital for a laceration to the forehead and a sprain, requiring seven stitches.

Otoboke Beaver took to Instagram this week, noting that they apologize for waiting four months to address the incident, citing that communication between the band and staff following the show was not handled appropriately.

“We accept the incident seriously and deeply apologize,” the band wrote in a post on Instagram.
