# Remi Wolf, Sierra Ferrell and Underscores Added to All Things Go DC 2026 Lineup

## Summary

Remi Wolf, Sierra Ferrell, and Underscores have been added to the All Things Go DC 2026 festival lineup.

### Key Points
- Three new acts added to the festival's DC edition.
- Standard lineup-expansion announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/All-Things-Go-1.webp)

All Things Go has expanded the lineup for its Washington, D.C.-area festival with the addition of Remi Wolf, Sierra Ferrell and Underscores.

The festival will take place September 25 to 27 at Merriweather Post Pavilion in Columbia, Maryland. The newly announced performers will appear across the first and final days of the three-day event.

Underscores will perform Friday, September 25, joining a lineup led by Mitski and Ethel Cain. The artist’s genre-blurring music combines elements of hyperpop, electronic music, rock and dubstep.

Remi Wolf and Sierra Ferrell will take the stage Sunday, September 27. Brandi Carlile and Lola Young are scheduled to headline the final day.

Saturday’s lineup will be topped by Hayley Williams, Muna and Zara Larsson. Other artists previously announced for the festival include Tinashe, She & Him, Suki Waterhouse, the Beths, Slayyyter, CMAT, the Beaches, Wolf Alice, Rico Nasty, Rebecca Black and Rainbow Kitten Surprise.

The 2026 event marks the 12th edition of All Things Go in the Washington region. All Things Go began in Washington in 2014 and has developed a reputation for lineups that prominently feature women and LGBTQ+ artists. The festival moved from Union Market in Washington to Merriweather Post Pavilion in 2021 before expanding from one day to multiple days.

The brand has since grown beyond the D.C. market. A New York edition will run during the same September 25 to 27 weekend at Forest Hills Stadium in Queens. Brandi Carlile, Zara Larsson, Muna, Lola Young and Carly Rae Jepsen are among the artists scheduled to appear in New York.

All Things Go also staged a Toronto festival at RBC Amphitheatre in June. The Canadian edition featured Lorde, Kesha and Wet Leg, marking the event’s first expansion outside the United States.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit the [official All Things Go website](https://allthingsgofestival.com/).
