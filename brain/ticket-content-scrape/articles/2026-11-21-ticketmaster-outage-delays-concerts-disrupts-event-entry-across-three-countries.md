# Ticketmaster Outage Delays Concerts, Disrupts Event Entry Across Three Countries

## Summary

Widespread Ticketmaster mobile-ticket outages on Nov 20–21, 2026 disrupted entry at major events across the US, Mexico, and Australia, stranding fans outside venues and delaying start times. The incidents followed a similar outage earlier in the week that delayed entry at a South Dakota high school volleyball tournament. Ticketmaster restored service by early Saturday but did not disclose a root cause, outage scope, or safeguards against recurrence, leaving open questions about venues' ability to independently verify tickets when centralized digital delivery fails.

### Key Points
- Outages affected an Usher/Chris Brown concert at Caesars Superdome (New Orleans), the Corona Capital festival and a Los Ángeles Azules show (Mexico City), and AFLW playoff games (Melbourne).
- Fans who had already downloaded tickets to mobile wallets were unaffected; those still retrieving tickets from Ticketmaster's servers were locked out.
- Venues fell back to manual workarounds — box-office verification via order-confirmation emails — with some entrance gates temporarily closed.
- A separate Ticketmaster outage the prior day delayed entry at a South Dakota high school volleyball championship; TicketNews has not established whether the incidents share a cause.
- Ticketmaster announced restoration around 12:34 a.m. Nov 21 without naming the technical cause, affected market count, or outage duration.

### Highlights
- Outage spanned three countries and at least five separate events over two days (Nov 20–21, 2026).
- Mobile-wallet-saved tickets kept working while server-dependent ticket retrieval did not, pointing to a backend/retrieval failure rather than a wallet-display bug.
- No disclosed root cause, scope, or duration as of publication; Ticketmaster says it is "investigating."

### Industry Problem
Admission increasingly depends on real-time, centralized access to a single vendor's mobile-ticket backend, so an outage in that one layer can simultaneously strand patrons and halt doors at unrelated venues worldwide, with venues largely unable to independently verify tickets when it happens.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster's own fallback was manual box-office/confirmation-email verification — a stopgap, not a system fix. For Etix, this reinforces the case for offline-capable ticket caching/display (so a wallet-saved ticket stays scannable without a live backend call) and a documented box-office fallback-verification procedure, positioned as a reliability differentiator against centralized competitors.

## Thousands Delayed Outside Caesars Superdome

Some of the most significant problems occurred Friday evening at the Caesars Superdome, where thousands of fans reportedly faced extended waits while attempting to enter the first of two scheduled performances by Usher and Chris Brown on The R&B Tour.

According to [local reporting from WBRZ](https://lucee.wbrz.com/news/fans-stuck-outside-caesars-superdome-as-ticketmaster-outage-delays-usher-and-chris-brown-concert?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), fans began accumulating outside the stadium after experiencing problems retrieving their mobile tickets. Doors had been scheduled to open at 6 p.m., but the disruption reportedly delayed the concert’s start.

## Ticketmaster Problems Affect Major Events in Mexico

Similar problems disrupted major live music events in Mexico City on Friday.

At the Auditorio Nacional, the scheduled 8:30 p.m. performance by Los Ángeles Azules was delayed by nearly an hour as fans experienced difficulty accessing digital tickets.

According to [reporting by Jacqueline Ponce León of Agencia Reforma](https://www.eldiariodechihuahua.mx/espectaculos/2026/nov/20/retrasan-show-de-los-angeles-azules-por-caida-de-sistema-859545.html?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), published by El Diario de Chihuahua, the group did not take the stage until 9:24 p.m.

The venue acknowledged the problem in an Instagram story posted at 9:14 p.m., advising customers holding digital tickets to visit its box offices because Ticketmaster’s systems were experiencing intermittent problems.

Long lines reportedly formed outside the venue as staff worked to assist affected attendees.

Ticketmaster access problems were also reported at Corona Capital, the three-day music festival taking place Nov. 20-22 at the Autódromo Hermanos Rodríguez.

Mexican news outlet [N+ reported](https://www.nmas.com.mx/entretenimiento/usuarios-reportan-fallas-en-ticketmaster-previo-a-conciertos-de-este-fin-de-semana/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) widespread complaints from users who could not download or display their previously purchased tickets, including attendees preparing to enter the festival.

Other local reports described temporary difficulties at festival entrances. [La Crónica de Hoy reported](https://www.cronica.com.mx/tendencias/2026/11/20/portazo-en-el-corona-capital-reportan-fallas-en-ticketmaster/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that entrance gates were temporarily closed as attendees struggled to retrieve tickets.

The publication also described reports of confrontations at the entrances, although the precise circumstances and any direct relationship between those incidents and the ticketing outage have not been independently established.

Corona Capital continues Saturday and Sunday, making the reliability of Ticketmaster’s services an immediate concern for additional festivalgoers.

## Australian Football Fans Face Long Entry Lines

The effects extended to Australia, where a Ticketmaster disruption interfered with admission to the Australian Football League Women’s preliminary-final doubleheader at Ikon Park in Melbourne on Saturday afternoon local time.

[The Brisbane Times reported](https://www.brisbanetimes.com.au/sport/afl/global-ticketmaster-outage-impacts-the-aflw-preliminary-finals-20261121-p61g5m.html?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that fans remained in long lines outside the stadium even after the opening match between North Melbourne and Sydney had begun.

Some spectators were still waiting to enter midway through the first quarter, with venue personnel reportedly asking attendees to present their ticket confirmation emails as an alternative method of verifying their purchases.

An AFL spokesperson confirmed the widespread Ticketmaster problem to the publication, while a Ticketmaster representative said the company was investigating and would provide updates.

The Australian disruption provides further evidence that Friday’s ticket-access problems were not confined to U.S. ticketing operations or a single venue’s entry systems.

## Ticketmaster Says Services Restored, but Questions Remain

By early Saturday, Ticketmaster was publicly reporting that the affected services had returned to normal.

In a post published at 12:34 a.m. Nov. 21, Ticketmaster Fan Support said tickets were displaying properly again and that other services that had experienced intermittent issues had also been restored.

The company thanked fans for their patience and acknowledged the efforts of venue box-office teams that worked with Ticketmaster to get customers into events.

Earlier during the outage, Ticketmaster Fan Support had advised customers unable to display their tickets to visit venue box offices with their order confirmation details.

The company’s restoration announcement did not identify the technical issue responsible for the disruptions, how many markets or events were affected, or the precise duration of the service interruption.

Nor did it establish whether all reported problems were caused by the same underlying failure.

The disruption came just one day after TicketNews [reported problems at the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/ticketmaster-outage-volleyball-tournament/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in Sioux Falls, South Dakota, where spectators attending the state high school volleyball tournament experienced entry delays Thursday evening.

That earlier incident also coincided with customer complaints involving Ticketmaster ticket retrieval, transfers and purchasing functions. Whether the disruptions on consecutive days were related has not been established.

The incidents highlight the operational challenges that can arise when admission depends on real-time access to a centralized digital ticketing platform.

Fans who had already saved eligible tickets to their mobile wallets were generally able to enter the Superdome despite the outage. Those who had not yet retrieved their tickets, however, were dependent on the restoration of service or assistance from venue staff.

The distinction also raises questions about the ability of venues to independently verify valid purchases, particularly when the disruption affects a large number of attendees arriving shortly before an event.

With another major concert scheduled at the Superdome on Saturday and additional performances planned at affected venues in Mexico City, assurances about service restoration will be especially important for customers preparing to attend events.

TicketNews has contacted Ticketmaster, Live Nation and Caesars Superdome representatives seeking additional information about the outage, its scope, the impact on event admission and the measures being taken ahead of Saturday’s events.
