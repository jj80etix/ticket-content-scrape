# Languages on The O2 stage triple in eight years

## Summary

The number of languages represented on stage at London's The O2 arena has tripled since 2018, reflecting surging demand for international artists.

### Key Points
- 2026 is The O2's most linguistically diverse year yet, with 18 languages across its programming.
- That's a roughly 30% increase since 2024 and a 200% increase since 2018.
- Languages span Spanish, Catalan, French, Korean, Japanese, Yoruba, Lingala and several South Asian languages (Hindi, Punjabi, Haryanvi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu).
