# Giggly Squad Announces 2027 Tour Across North America and Europe

## Summary

Comedy podcast duo Giggly Squad (Paige DeSorbo and Hannah Berner) announced their 2027 "Gehl's Gehl Tour" across North America and Europe, including expanded Madison Square Garden and Dublin dates.

### Key Points
- Tour opens January 15, 2027 in Miami; stops include Toronto, Chicago, Dallas, Seattle, Nashville, Atlanta, London and Dublin.
- Four consecutive Madison Square Garden shows Feb. 18–21 after adding two more nights to the original two-night run.
- Dublin show moved from Vicar Street to the larger 3Arena due to demand; date unchanged.
- Added April 28 show at Hollywood's Dolby Theatre extends the tour into late April.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Giggly-Squad-Live.webp)

Paige DeSorbo and Hannah Berner are taking “Giggly Squad” back on the road in 2027 with their Gehl’s Gehl Tour, bringing the podcast’s conversations to audiences across North America and Europe.

The tour begins January 15 in Miami and includes stops in Toronto, Chicago, Dallas, Seattle, Nashville and Atlanta, along with the duo’s first performances in London and Dublin. An added April 28 show at Hollywood’s Dolby Theatre now extends the announced schedule into late April.

Notably, the itinerary has expanded since the initial announcement with four consecutive performances at Madison Square Garden on February 18–21, adding February 20 and 21 to the previously announced two-night engagement.

There has also been a venue change overseas. The March 12 Dublin performance, initially announced for Vicar Street, has moved to 3Arena because of ticket demand. The date remains unchanged and follows the March 10 performance at the London Palladium.
