# Adéla bolsters global run with North American dates in latest tour news

## Summary

A roundup of new tour announcements led by Slovakian singer Adéla, who has added a ten-date North American headline run for April 2027 bookending the two Coachella weekends. Also covered: P!nk extending her Las Vegas Sphere residency to 18 shows through January 2028 on "overwhelming demand," plus briefer mentions of Jhené Aiko, Pitbull, and Diana Krall tour news.

### Key Points
- Adéla adds 10 North American tour dates for April 2027, following prior European/UK legs.
- P!nk's Sphere residency extended to 18 total shows, now running into late January 2028.
- Live Nation (Sphere promoter) cites "overwhelming demand" for the extension.
- Additional briefer tour news items for Jhené Aiko, Pitbull, and Diana Krall.

## Transcript

Plus announcements on P!nk's Sphere residency and Jhené Aiko's North American arena run, as well as from Pitbull, Diana Krall, and more

![Adéla](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Adela-©-Andrew-Angel.png)

Adéla © Andrew Angel

Leading the latest batch of tour announcements, Adéla has revealed a ten-date North American headline run, which will follow her previously-announced European and UK run.

The ten North American engagements in April 2027 will take the break-out Slovakian singer across theatres, beginning at The Warfield in San Francisco for two nights. These will be bookended by performances at both Coachella weekends in California.

Following this, she will visit Chicago, Brooklyn for two nights, Washington D.C, and Toronto for two nights. The new run fits among a stretch of global dates, coming after a Latin American festival leg, and preceding her return to Europe for festival slots, including Primavera Sound and Glastonbury.

**P!nk** – Sphere run extended  
The singer has added a further six nights at the Las Vegas venue, bringing her residency to a total of 18 shows. The new additions extend the run into late January 2028.

Promoter Live Nation said “overwhelming demand” was behind the decision to extend the residency, which was first announced earlier this month as a nine-date stand set to start in September 2027. Three further dates were then added, before the addition of the latest six concerts. P!nk is also the first solo female pop act to headline the Las Vegas venue.

**Jhené Aiko** – North American arenas announced  
The R&B singer has revealed 37 dates for North American arenas for spring 2027.

Beginning on 10 March at St Louis’ Enterprise Center (22,000-cap), the tour will go on visit notable venues including Chicago’s United Center (23,500-cap), Philadelphia’s XFinity Mobile Arena (21,000-cap), and Los Angeles’ Crypto.com Arena (20,000-cap) for its closing night on 21 May.

“The tour builds on the remarkable success of Aiko’s 2024 sold-out, 27-market Magic Hour Tour, which marked her first headlining tour in seven years,” promoter Live Nation said.

**Pitbull –** Australia and New Zealand run for 2027  
September 2027 will see the singer and rapper play four dates in Australia and one in New Zealand.

He will play arenas in Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane dates between 11 and 21 September, before a night at Auckland’s Spark Arena on 24 September.

**Lasso** – *UNA MAS Y NOS VAMOS WORLD TOUR* unveiled  
The Venezuelan singer will embark on a tour across Europe, Latin America, and the US, totalling 24 nights. Beginning in Europe in early March 2027, the run will take the singer through cities including Barcelona, Madrid, Valencia, Porto, Berlin, and Milan, before setting off for South America. Next, Lasso will perform in Bogotá, Lima, Buenos Aires, Montevideo, La Paz, Santiago, and Caracas.

The tour will then run through a selection of music halls in US cities including New York and Los Angeles, before ending with three Mexican shows in Mexico City, Monterrey, and Guadalajara.

**Dianna Krall** – European and UK tour set for autumn 2027  
The Canadian pianist and singer will play 18 concert hall and theatre dates across Europe and the UK in October 2027. Booked by agent Kim Worsoe of All Things Live, notable stops include London’s Royal Albert Hall and Cologne’s Kölner Philharmonie, as well as the tour’s final two nights at Paris’ L’Olympia.

The tour is set to mark the 25th anniversary of Krall’s *The* *Look Of Love* album, and will also visit cities including Reykjavik, Brussels, Rotterdam, Haarlem, Oslo, Wroclaw, and Prague.

**Gabriel Jacoby** – *Gutta Boy* tour to begin in February  
The R&B artist will play 19 theatre dates across the US and Canada, leading into spring 2027, promoted by Live Nation.

Notable stops include the Roxy Theatre in Los Angeles and Irving Plaza in New York. The run kicks off on 16 February at Neumos in Seattle, with stops following in Houston, Toronto, Boston, and more, before wrapping up on 16 March at The Underground in Charlotte.

**The Twang** – Intimate UK headline tour announced  
Finally, The indie rock band will play seven dates across the UK in intimate venues throughout April 2027. Promoted by SJM Concerts, the run will will see the band perform at London’s The Garage, Manchester’s Band on the Wall, and Liverpool’s Arts Club Theatre, as well as visiting Nottingham, Bristol, and Edinburgh.
