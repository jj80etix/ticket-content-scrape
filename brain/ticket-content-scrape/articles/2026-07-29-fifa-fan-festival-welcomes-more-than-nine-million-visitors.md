# FIFA Fan Festival welcomes more than nine million visitors

## Summary

FIFA Fan Festival sites drew more than nine million visitors across 13 host cities during the World Cup, the highest turnout since the program began in 2006.

### Key Points
- Peak daily attendance of 543,020 occurred on June 27.
- Mexico City led with 2.57 million total visitors across 36 operating days.

## Transcript

![TheTicketingBusiness News](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TBN-LOGO-reversed-white.png)

TheTicketingBusiness News

**More than nine million visitors attended FIFA Fan Festival sites during the World Cup in 13 host cities across the United States, Canada, and Mexico.**

The turnout marked the highest attendance since the programme started during the 2006 tournament in Germany.

Daily turnout hit a peak on June 27 when 543,020 people visited festival venues on the final day of group play.

Mexico City recorded the highest total attendance, welcoming 2.57 million supporters across 36 operating days.

“The FIFA Fan Festival showed the extraordinary power of football to unite and inspire fans from all over the world, creating an unforgettable atmosphere that was accessible to all,” said FIFA president Gianni Infantino.

“We wanted the 2026 edition to be the biggest and most inclusive expression of the FIFA Fan Festival spirit yet, and the response from supporters shows that we achieved this in style.

“We thank every fan, host city, volunteer, performer and partner who helped make it possible – without them, it would not have been the same.”

This year’s FIFA World Cup, which took place from June 11 to July 19, was the biggest in the tournament’s history with 48 teams.

The 13 FIFA Fan Festival locations operated for a combined 348 days across the 39 days of the event.

The celebration culminated on July 19, during Spain’s final victory over Argentina, with 263,972 fans viewing the match across eight remaining active festival locations.
