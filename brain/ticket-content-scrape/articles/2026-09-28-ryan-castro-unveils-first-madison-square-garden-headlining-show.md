# Ryan Castro Unveils First Madison Square Garden Headlining Show

## Summary

Reggaeton artist Ryan Castro announced his first headlining show at Madison Square Garden, part of a new "Los Chorros Con Richy Tour" that also includes a Miami arena date. The announcement follows his first two Latin Grammy nominations and continues his progression through the New York market's major venues.

### Key Points
- MSG headline date: December 12; second arena date at Miami's Kaseya Center December 19.
- Follows prior NY-area shows at Palladium Times Square (2023), Prudential Center (2024), Barclays Center (2025).
- Announced mid-tour on his ongoing "SENDÉ THE LAST DANCE Tour," with dates through Bogotá Oct. 31.
- First two Latin Grammy nominations announced around the same time (with Kapo and Gangsta on "La Villa").

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Ryan-Castro.webp)

Ryan Castro will headline Madison Square Garden for the first time on December 12, bringing his “Los Chorros Con Richy Tour” to New York City. A second arena date follows December 19 at Miami’s Kaseya Center.

The Garden show marks Castro’s largest headlining appearance yet in the New York market. He played Palladium Times Square in 2023, New Jersey’s Prudential Center in 2024 and Brooklyn’s Barclays Center in 2025. The December concert continues that progression through the region’s major venues.

Castro announced the arena dates while continuing the second leg of his “SENDÉ THE LAST DANCE Tour.” His remaining listed stops include Tempe, Arizona, on September 30; San Jose, California, on October 2; Fresno, California, on October 3; and Denver on October 6. A Bogotá performance is scheduled for October 31 before the New York and Miami shows in December. The Madison Square Garden and Kaseya Center concerts are billed under the separate “Los Chorros Con Richy Tour” name.

The announcement also follows Castro’s first two Latin Grammy nominations. “La Villa,” recorded with Kapo and Gangsta, is nominated for best urban performance. “Omerta,” his collaborative album with J Balvin, is nominated for best urban music album.
