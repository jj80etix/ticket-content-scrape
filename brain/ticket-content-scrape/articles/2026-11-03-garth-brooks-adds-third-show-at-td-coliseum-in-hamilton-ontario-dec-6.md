# Garth Brooks Adds Third Show At TD Coliseum In Hamilton, Ontario, Dec. 6

## Summary

Garth Brooks has added a third TD Coliseum date in Hamilton, Ontario (a Dec 6 matinee) to his "Blame It All On My Roots" tour, joining the previously announced Dec 4-5 shows. All Hamilton tickets, priced uniformly at CAD$199.96, go on sale Nov 6 with no presale and an eight-ticket purchase limit.

### Key Points
- Third Hamilton date added "due to demand"; TD Coliseum is a $300M renovation of the former Copps Coliseum that opened last fall.
- The show will feature both end-stage and in-the-round seating configurations.
- Tour has already stopped at Indianapolis, Denver, Rosemont, Washington D.C., Orlando, Salt Lake City and Tulsa; next up is Kansas City, then four Seattle shows before Hamilton.
- Brooks will also headline the Stagecoach festival in April 2027.

The rollout of Garth Brooks’ “Blame It All On My Roots” arena tour continues, with a third date added for TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario, for Dec. 6.

Due to demand, the third date makes it three in a row for the country music star, Dec. 4-6, at the state-of-the-art arena that opened last fall [as a $300 million renovation of the arena formerly known as Copps Coliseum.](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/12/a-new-era-td-coliseum-opening-transforms-ontario-concert-landscape-raises-the-bar-for-mid-sized-city/)

Tickets for the TD Coliseum shows go on sale Friday, Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. local time. In classic Garth fashion, all tickets are priced at CAD$199.96, and the show will feature both end-stage and in-the-round seating. All tickets will go on sale at once, with no previous presale. Limit per customer is eight tickets per purchase. The Dec. 6 date is billed as a Matinee Show.

Brooks’ “Blame It All On My Roots’ tour began at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis in August and has continued with multiple stops at venues including Ball Arena in Denver, Allstate Arena in Rosemont, Capital One Arena in Washington DC, Kia Center in Orlando, Delta Center in Salt Lake City, and BOK Center in Tulsa. The next stop is T-Mobile Center in Kansas City, to be followed by four dates at Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle, before the Hamilton dates in December.

Adding to the mix is a headline slot at Stagecoach festival in April.
