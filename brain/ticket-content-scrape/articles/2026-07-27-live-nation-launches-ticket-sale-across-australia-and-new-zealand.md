# Live Nation launches ticket sale across Australia and New Zealand

## Summary

Live Nation launched a five-day "Christmas in July" promotional ticket sale across Australia and New Zealand, offering 30% discounts, A$50 tickets, and expanded allocations across a range of shows and festivals, timed to a seasonally quiet sales period.

### Key Points
- Five-day sale (through July 31) spanning artists including 5 Seconds of Summer, Guns N' Roses, Charlie Puth, Westlife, and others.
- Discounts include 30% off selected shows and flat A$50 tickets.
- Explicitly designed to drive bookings during a typically slow sales period.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Live Nation has launched a five-day “Christmas in July” sale offering discounted tickets, limited ticket releases and special offers across a range of concerts and live entertainment events in Australia and New Zealand.**

Running from today until Friday (July 31), the promotion includes 30% discounts on selected shows, A$50 (£26/$35/€30) tickets and access to additional ticket allocations for a range of upcoming national and international tours.

It includes events from artists such as 5 Seconds of Summer, Guns N’ Roses, Charlie Puth, Chet Faker, BABYMONSTER, Westlife, Rise Against, Wolf Alice, Robyn, Joji, MAMAMOO, Jungle, The Amity Affliction and Emma Memma, alongside festival offerings such as Strummingbird Festival.

“Christmas in July is a great opportunity for fans to lock in their next live experience, with offers spanning some of the biggest international tours and brilliant local artists,” said Mark Vaughan, senior vice president of touring at Live Nation Australia and New Zealand.

“Whether it’s an artist you’ve been waiting to see or a show you’ve just discovered, there’s something across the sale for every kind of live entertainment fan.”

The sale is designed to encourage fans to buy tickets during what is typically a quieter period of the year.
