# Colorado State Taps Oak View Group to Bring More Concerts, Live Events to Canvas Stadium

## Summary

Colorado State University is expanding its partnership with Oak View Group (OVG) to add concert and event booking at the 36,500-seat Canvas Stadium, alongside a 10-year extension of OVG Hospitality's food and beverage services across CSU Athletics venues and new performance dining for the football program.

### Key Points
- OVG will now handle live-event booking for Canvas Stadium in Fort Collins, aiming to bring more concerts and non-football events to the venue.
- OVG Hospitality's F&B deal with CSU Athletics is extended 10 years and expanded to include performance dining for the football program.
- The expansion follows CSU's move to the Pac-12 and ongoing stadium/hospitality upgrades.
- OVG already runs similar university event-booking relationships at NC State, Tennessee, Tulsa, Iowa State and Arkansas.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ovg.jpg)

Colorado State University is looking to turn Canvas Stadium into a bigger player on Northern Colorado’s live entertainment circuit, expanding its partnership with Oak View Group to include concert and event booking at the 36,500-seat venue.

The new agreement will see Denver-based Oak View Group work to secure additional live programming for the Fort Collins stadium, while also extending OVG Hospitality’s food and beverage deal across CSU Athletics venues for another 10 years.

The partnership will also expand into performance dining for the Rams football program.

Colorado State Director of Athletics John Weber said that they’re “thankful for our partnership with Oak View Group.”

“As we continue to expand the profile of Canvas Stadium as the premier destination in Northern Colorado for large-scale live entertainment, we look forward to collaborating with OVG and its outstanding reputation in the live event industry to help us secure additional events,” Weber said in a press release.

The move comes as Colorado State continues investing in Canvas Stadium and its broader fan experience following the university’s move to the Pac-12. Alongside facility upgrades and premium hospitality additions, CSU is aiming to put the stadium to work beyond football Saturdays with more concerts, sports and community events throughout the year.

That could give promoters another sizable outdoor option in the Fort Collins market.

“Northern Colorado has demonstrated a strong appetite for live entertainment, and we’ve seen that firsthand through our work in the market,” Becky Colwell, OVG’s President of Programming and Content, said in a statement. “Expanding our partnership with Colorado State University gives us the opportunity to bring concerts and other live events to Canvas Stadium, creating new experiences for students, alumni, fans and the broader Fort Collins community.”

OVG already handles similar live-event booking relationships with universities including NC State, Tennessee, Tulsa, Iowa State and Arkansas.

On the hospitality side, the 10-year extension keeps OVG in place across CSU Athletics venues, where the company oversees food, beverage and game-day service. Its expanded role will now include athlete-focused dining for the football program.

“Our partnership with Colorado State University has always been centered on the fan experience, from showcasing local flavors to delivering exceptional hospitality on game day,” OVG Hospitality President Ken Gaber added. “We’re excited to extend that commitment while expanding our support through performance dining that helps fuel student-athletes and elevates every aspect of the Rams experience.”

OVG Hospitality also operates across a growing roster of college properties, including Penn State, USC, Kansas, Arizona, Texas Tech, Virginia Tech and UConn.
