# Thomas Rhett Announces 2027 ‘Soundtrack to Life’ UK Tour

## Summary

Thomas Rhett has announced a three-date UK arena tour for spring 2027, titled "Soundtrack to Life UK," with Kameron Marlowe as support. The run follows his existing 2026 North American tour and a January 2027 Crash My Playa Festival headline slot. Tickets go on sale via a Live Nation pre-sale on Aug. 4 and general on-sale Aug. 7.

### Key Points
- UK tour opens April 22, 2027 at Manchester's AO Arena, followed by London's The O2 (April 23) and Glasgow's OVO Hydro (April 25).
- Live Nation pre-sale begins Aug. 4 at 10 a.m. BST; general on-sale Aug. 7 at 10 a.m. BST.
- North American leg resumes Sept. 12 in Cuyahoga Falls, OH and runs through Oct. 24 in Omaha, NE, with stops including Dallas, Los Angeles, Sacramento, and Phoenix.
- Rhett also headlines Luke Bryan's Crash My Playa Festival, Jan. 13–16, 2027, at Moon Palace Cancún.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Thomas-Rhett-Soundtrack-to-Life-Tour.webp)

Thomas Rhett will return to the United Kingdom next spring with his newly announced “Soundtrack to Life UK” tour dates.

The three-date arena run begins April 22 at Manchester’s AO Arena before making stops in London and Scotland. Kameron Marlowe will join Rhett as the special guest on all dates.

A Live Nation pre-sale will begin Aug. 4 at 10 a.m. BST (5 a.m. ET) for fans who sign up in advance. General ticket sales open Aug. 7 at 10 a.m. BST (5 a.m. ET).

Announcing the tour, Rhett shared, “Our U.K. fans have always shown us so much love,” Rhett said. “Getting back for our own arena tour has been something we’ve been working on for a really long time. We’ve got a lot of songs to catch up on together, and I can’t wait to see everybody next spring.”
