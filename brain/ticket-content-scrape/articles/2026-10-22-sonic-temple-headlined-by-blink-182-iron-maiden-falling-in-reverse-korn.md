# Sonic Temple Headlined By Blink-182, Iron Maiden, Falling In Reverse & Korn

## Summary

Sonic Temple Art + Music Festival returns to Columbus, Ohio for 2027 with Blink-182, Iron Maiden, Falling in Reverse and Korn headlining across five stages.

### Key Points
- Dates: May 13–16, 2027 at Historic Crew Stadium, following a record-attendance 2026 edition.
- Nearly 150 bands across five stages, including Iron Maiden's 50th-anniversary tour set and a rare Faith No More US reunion.
- 2026 edition drew a record 184,000 total attendees per producer Danny Wimmer Presents.
- Additional billed acts include The Offspring, Turnstile, A Perfect Circle, Alice in Chains and Beartooth.

Promising its biggest event yet, Sonic Temple Art + Music Festival returns as planned on May 13-16, 2027, at Historic Crew Stadium in Columbus, Ohio, following up on 2026’s record attendance, with nearly 150 bands across five stages.

Along with each night’s headliners, the lineup also includes top billing for artists including The Offspring, Turnstile, A Perfect Circle, Alice In Chains, A Day To Remember, Beartooth, $UICIDEBOY$, Faith No More and many others. Additional notable performances include being part of Iron Maiden’s “Run For Your Lives 50th Anniversary Tour” featuring classic catalog, a rare Faith No More U.S. reunion, hometown show for Beartooth, 40th anniversary for Mesghuggah, and still more.

“Sonic Temple has become one of those special weekends where the whole rock community comes together,” says Chamie McCurry, general manager of festival producer Danny Wimmer Presents. “We build each lineup to celebrate the artists who shaped this music, the bands driving it forward, and the next generation that will define it. There’s incredible energy in rock right now, and Sonic Temple reflects that. Columbus has embraced this festival in a way that means a lot to us, and we’re ready to give fans another unforgettable four days.”

The 2026 event attracted a record 184,000 total attendees to Sonic Temple, according to DWP, with experiences and music turned up extra for 2027 to go along with art taking over the founds at Historic Crew Stadium as it does every year with towering art pillars, murals, art walls and an Art Mart giving fans a curated marketplace to bring home something unique. More details about 2027 art programming will be announced soon.

Ticket passes are available now, with options for single-day, four-day, VIP and various premium experiences on sale.

In 2025, DWP announced a five-year extension of its partnership with Historic Crew Stadium, securing Sonic Temple Art + Music Festival in Columbus through at least 2030, with the promoter saying the agreement underscores its investment in the region and the festival’s role as an economic and cultural driver to the region.

The Sonic Temple lineup follows the recent lineup announcement and return of [GoldenSky festival in Sacramento, California,](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/15/goldensky-festival-topped-by-zach-bryan-megan-moroney-treaty-oak-revival-oct-9-10-in-sacramento/) and recent [Aftershock rock and metal festival in Sacramento,](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/07/aftershock-sells-out-capping-record-breaking-festival-year-for-danny-wimmer-presents-with-more-than-1-million-attendees/) which capped a year of record festival attendance for DWP, which promises new rock and country events in the near future.
