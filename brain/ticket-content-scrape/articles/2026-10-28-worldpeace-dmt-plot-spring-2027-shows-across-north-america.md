# Worldpeace DMT Plot Spring 2027 Shows Across North America

## Summary

British indie-pop duo Worldpeace DMT announced a 20-date spring 2027 North American tour supporting their new album "Stick Around for Tea," running March 12 (Boston) through April 9 (Toronto). Artist presale begins Oct. 29, with general onsale Oct. 30 via Ticketmaster and Laylo.

### Key Points
- 20 dates, March 12–April 9, 2027, across the US and Canada including Philadelphia, NY, DC, Atlanta, Austin, LA, Denver, Chicago and Cleveland.
- Supports second album "Stick Around for Tea" (released Oct. 16 via Big Girl Records).
- Duo comprises Leo Fincham and Rowan Please.
- Tickets sold via Ticketmaster and Laylo; artist presale Oct. 29, general onsale Oct. 30.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Worldpeace-DMT.webp)

Worldpeace DMT will return to North America in spring 2027 for a 20-date tour supporting their new album, *Stick Around for Tea*.

The tour is slated to kick off on March 12 in Boston at The Rockwell. From there, the British indie-pop duo is set to make stops in cities such as Philadelphia, New York, Washington D.C., Atlanta, Austin, Los Angeles, Denver, Chicago and Cleveland. The run is scheduled to wrap up on April 9 in Toronto at The Drake..

Comprised of Leo Fincham and Rowan Please, Worldpeace DMT released *Stick Around for Tea* on October 16 through Big Girl Records. The second album follows their 2025 debut, *The Velvet Underground & Rowan*, and the *Flowers Powers* mixtape released in July.

An artist presale begins Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. local time, with advance registration available. General ticket sales follow Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster and Laylo.
