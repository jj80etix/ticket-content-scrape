# Queens Of The Stone Age Unveil 2027 ‘Perfecth World Tour’ With Pixies

## Summary

Queens of the Stone Age announced a 2027 "Perfecth World Tour" with the Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp, adding 16 US dates to a run that already includes international stops.

### Key Points
- US leg runs May 1 (San Diego) to May 24 (Boston), supporting the band's upcoming album "Perfecth" (out Oct. 30 on Matador).
- Follows previously announced dates in Iceland, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Hong Kong and Singapore.
- Comes off the band's sold-out "Take Cover Tour" with Foo Fighters, which included a $5.1M Nashville stop per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- General on-sale Oct. 29; artist presale Oct. 28; presale registration closes Oct. 25.

Queens of the Stone Age have announced the 2027 “Perfecth World Tour” to support their upcoming album. Starting May 1, the band will play 16 dates across the United States, beginning at San Diego’s Viejas Arena and concluding May 24 at Boston’s TD Garden. The Pixies and Wheelchair Sports Camp will support the band on all dates. See full routing below.

The newly announced dates will follow previously announced stops in Iceland, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Hong Kong and Singapore.

Queens of the Stone Age previously teamed up with the Foo Fighters for the North American Take Cover Tour, running trough the summer and the fall. The sold-out tour produced numerous highlights for the two legendary bands, including a stop at Nissan Stadium in Nashville that grossed $5,125,488, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/14135).

Tickets for the “Perfecth World Tour” go on sale to the general public on Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. local time, with pre-sale beginning Oct. 28 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans who want to participate in the Queens of the Stone Age artist pre-sale must sign up here by Oct. 25 at 11:59 p.m. ET.

Produced by Homme and band member Michael Shuman, *Perfecth* is set to release on Oct. 30 on Matador Records.
