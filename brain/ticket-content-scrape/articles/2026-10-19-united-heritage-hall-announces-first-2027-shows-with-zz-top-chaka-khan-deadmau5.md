# United Heritage Hall Announces First 2027 Shows With ZZ Top, Chaka Khan, deadmau5

## Summary

AEG Presents unveiled the first 12 shows for United Heritage Hall, its new 4,000-capacity venue opening in Austin, Texas in 2027, with a lineup spanning rock, electronic, R&B, Latin music and comedy. Confirmed acts include ZZ Top, Chaka Khan, deadmau5, Death Cab for Cutie and comedian Taylor Tomlinson, with no formal grand-opening event yet identified.

### Key Points
- 12 shows announced, March-October 2027; earliest is Hum on March 7.
- Venue: ~65,000 sq ft, 4,000-cap, at River Park in Austin's Riverside neighborhood near the airport.
- Developed by AEG Presents with Westfield, Partners Group and Presidium.
- Lineup includes ZZ Top, Chaka Khan, deadmau5, Death Cab for Cutie, Taylor Tomlinson, Jimmy Carr.

AEG Presents has unveiled the first 12 performances scheduled for United Heritage Hall, its new 4,000-capacity venue in Austin, Texas.

Among the artists booked for 2027 are ZZ Top, Chaka Khan, deadmau5, Death Cab for Cutie and comedian Taylor Tomlinson.

Alternative rock band Hum is scheduled for March 7, the earliest date announced, while British comedian Jimmy Carr is booked for Oct. 6. AEG has not separately identified a formal grand-opening event.

The lineup spans rock, electronic music, R&B, Latin music and comedy. Texas rock band ZZ Top will perform March 18, followed by Tejano group La Mafia on March 19. Death Cab for Cutie is scheduled for March 23, Colombian singer-songwriter Fonseca for April 1 and Chaka Khan for April 23. Electronic performers deadmau5 and Sullivan King, metalcore band The Plot in You and the Pink Floyd-focused production Roger Waters Presents LEGACY are also on the calendar.

United Heritage Hall will occupy approximately 65,000 square feet at River Park, a 109-acre mixed-use development in Austin’s Riverside neighborhood, east of downtown and near Austin-Bergstrom International Airport. The venue is being developed by AEG Presents with Westfield, Partners Group and Presidium.
