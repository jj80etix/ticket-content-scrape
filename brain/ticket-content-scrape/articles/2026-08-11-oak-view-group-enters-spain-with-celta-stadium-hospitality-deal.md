# Oak View Group Enters Spain With Celta Stadium Hospitality Deal

## Summary

Oak View Group is expanding its hospitality business into Spain through a new partnership with LaLiga club Celta, taking over food, beverage and hospitality operations at ABANCA Balaídos in Vigo. The deal marks Celta as OVG Hospitality's first Spanish client and sits alongside the stadium's wider modernization push, with an eye toward making the venue a year-round destination beyond matchdays. Both sides frame the tie-up as a template that could later extend into Celta360, the club's planned training-complex redevelopment.

### Key Points
- OVG Hospitality will overhaul food, beverage and hospitality at ABANCA Balaídos as part of Celta's stadium modernization.
- Celta is OVG Hospitality's first client in Spain; OVG already runs hospitality at venues like Allegiant Stadium (Las Vegas) and Manchester City's Etihad Campus.
- The approach pairs OVG's operational playbook with local suppliers and a regional-identity/sustainability angle specific to Vigo.
- Celta frames the deal as addressing "infrastructure demands of modern football" and building long-term commercial stability for the club.
- OVG's Amy Latimer ties the upgrade to broader regional economic impact — tourism and local business opportunity beyond the stadium itself.
- The partnership could expand into Celta360, the club's planned hub for sports innovation, health, education and high-performance training.

Photo via Celta

Oak View Group is making its hospitality debut in Spain through a new partnership with LaLiga club Celta, taking on a major overhaul of the food, beverage and hospitality experience at ABANCA Balaídos.

The agreement makes Celta the first Spanish client for OVG Hospitality and will see the companies develop a new food and beverage concept across the Vigo stadium as its wider modernization continues. The project is aimed at upgrading the experience for supporters, corporate guests and other visitors while helping turn Balaídos into a venue capable of attracting activity beyond football matchdays.

OVG Hospitality already operates at major sports and entertainment properties internationally, including Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas and Manchester City’s Etihad Campus. The company said its approach in Vigo will combine operational expertise with local suppliers, sustainability and regional identity.

“We are particularly proud to launch OVG Hospitality’s operations alongside Celta,” Toni Ortega, President for Southern Europe at OVG, said in a press release. “This project reflects our commitment to Spain and to a hospitality model that combines excellence, sustainability and close collaboration with the local business community.”

The partnership comes as Celta looks to position ABANCA Balaídos as a broader year-round destination and strengthen the commercial potential of its infrastructure.

Amy Latimer, Chief Business Officer at OVG, said improved hospitality could also have an impact beyond the stadium.

“A world-class venue such as ABANCA Balaídos can be transformative for Vigo and the wider region,” Latimer said. “A high-quality food and beverage offering will help boost tourism, create new opportunities for local businesses and generate a positive and sustainable economic impact over the long term.”

For Celta, the deal is another step in a broader push to modernize its facilities and create additional long-term revenue opportunities.
