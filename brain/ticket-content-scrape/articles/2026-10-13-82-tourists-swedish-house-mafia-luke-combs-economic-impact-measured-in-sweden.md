# 82% Tourists: Swedish House Mafia & Luke Combs Economic Impact Measured In Sweden

## Summary

A Live Nation Sweden-commissioned study found that two summer stadium concerts by Swedish House Mafia and Luke Combs at Gothenburg's Ullevi stadium generated roughly $56 million in local economic activity, with 82% of the combined 126,798 attendees traveling as tourists. Average visitor spending reached about $555 per person across accommodation, dining, and other local spend.

### Key Points
- Swedish House Mafia (Aug. 29) and Luke Combs (July 4) shows drew 126,798 combined attendees.
- Visitor spending totaled roughly SEK 562M ($56M), per Lysio Research analysis.
- 82% of attendees were tourists; average spend was SEK 5,569 ($555) per person.
- Luke Combs' show grossed $5.97M on 60,806 tickets sold.
- About half of visitors stayed in paid accommodation, averaging 1.8 nights.

## Transcript

![Foto PaoDuell](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Foto_PaoDuell-scaled.jpg)

Ullevi stadium in Gothenburg, Sweden, during the concert by Swedish House Mafia on Aug. 29. Picture by Pao Duell

The two biggest concerts of the summer at Ullevi stadium in Gothenburg, Sweden, had “a significant impact on the local economy,” according to a press release from Live Nation Sweden.

In connection with both the Aug. 29 [Swedish House Mafia](https://www.pollstar.com/artists/swedish-house-mafia-70167) concert, and [Luke Combs](https://www.pollstar.com/artists/luke-combs-203617) ‘ gig on July 4, visitors spent more than half a billion in Swedish Krona, SEK 562 million or $56,081,924 as per the conversion rate at the time of this writing.

The average consumption per visitor reached SEK 5,569 ($555) per person. The analysis was done by Lysio Research and commissioned by Live Nation and Got Event, the City of Gothenburg’s events and venue department.

According to the promoters, both the Swedish House Mafia concert on Aug. 29 and Luke Combs’ gig on July 4 attracted a combined 126,798 visitors. Only Combs’ box office report was in at press time, showing that the U.S. country star moved 60,806 tickets at a $5,974,550 gross.

The vast majority of visitors, an average of 82%, according to the press release, were tourists.

Concertgoers spent an average of SEK 3,094 ($309) per day and the total consumption per person in connection with the concerts amounted to SEK 5,569 ($555). Consumption includes money spent on accommodation, restaurants, cafés, bars, shopping, transport and other expenses.

On average, just over half of visitors stayed in hotels, hostels or other paid accommodation in Gothenburg, and stayed an average of 1.8 nights in connection with the Swedish House Mafia and Luke Combs concerts.
