# Weezer Adds International Dates To ‘The Gathering’

## Summary

Weezer is taking "The Gathering" worldwide in 2027, adding 19 international dates across Japan, Ireland, the UK, and continental Europe after wrapping its 32-date North American leg in October. Taking Back Sunday will support the UK/Europe shows, with fan-club presale starting August 11 and general on-sale following August 14 (UK/EU) and September 5 (Japan).

### Key Points
- International leg opens with two Tokyo shows (Feb. 12-13) and runs through five Japan dates, then UK/Ireland/Europe from May 12 to June 1 in Helsinki.
- Taking Back Sunday supports the UK and European dates.
- Fan-club presale begins Aug. 11; UK album-preorder presale opens Aug. 10-11; general on-sale is Aug. 14 (UK/EU, 10am BST/11am CEST) and Sept. 5 (Japan).
- Weezer is represented by International Talent Booking (outside North America) and WME (North America).

Weezer is taking “The Gathering” worldwide in 2027.

Following the 32-date North American leg that wraps in October, the 19 international dates begin with a double at Tokyo’s Garden Theater Feb. 12 and 13, the first of five dates in Japan. The tour heads to Dublin, Ireland’s 3Arena May 12, before crossing to the UK for seven shows and then crossing the Channel for a Continental leg that includes plays in Paris, Amsterdam, Oslo and Stockholm, before the tour wraps in Helsinki June 1.

Taking Back Sunday will support on the UK and Europe dates.

First access to tickets on Aug. 11 at 9am BST / 10am CEST is available for fan club members. In the UK, those who pre-order the band’s new album. before 3 p.m. BST Aug. 10 will have presale access at at 10 am BST Aug. 11. In Europe, those who join the mailing list will have presale access at 11 a.m. local Aug. 11.

General on sale for UK/EU begins 10am BST / 11am CEST Aug. 14. Japan general on-sale begins 10 a.m. local Sept. 5.

Weezer is repped by International Talent Booking’s Rod MacSween outside North America and WME’s Kirk Sommer in North America**.**
