# Def Leppard Announces 45-Date ‘2027 Tour’ With Goo Goo Dolls

## Summary

Def Leppard announced a 45-date 2027 arena tour across the U.S. and Canada with the Goo Goo Dolls and The Struts as support. The run kicks off March 2 and wraps July 2, with tickets going on sale October 16 and $1 per ticket benefiting City of Hope cancer research.

### Key Points
- 45-date North American arena tour, March 2–July 2, 2027, with Goo Goo Dolls and The Struts.
- Tickets on sale Oct. 16; $1/ticket donated to City of Hope.
- Follows Def Leppard's 2026 run of sold-out Las Vegas residency dates and shows across the UK, Europe, South America, and Mexico.
- Stops include Little Caesars Arena (Detroit), Capital One Arena (DC), Madison Square Garden (NYC), United Center (Chicago), and Moody Center (Austin), among others.

Genre-defining global touring juggernaut Def Leppard will embark on a major arena tour in 2027, joined by ‘90s hit makers and touring force in their own right the Goo Goo Dolls and with The Struts as special guest.

The 45-date tour kicks off on March 2 and to July 2, making stops in all major markets across the U.S. and Canada. Tickets go on sale on Oct. 16, with $1 of every ticket to be donated to City of Hope, benefiting groundbreaking and innovative cancer research and treatment.

Earlier this year, Def Leppard completed its third sold-out residency in Las Vegas at Caesars Palace, with the remainder of 2026 seeing the British hit-makers selling out shows across the UK, Europe, South America and Mexico.

The announcement follows the Goo Goo Dolls’ extensive run of Summer Tour dates, which included lauded performances at iHeartRadio Festival and Sea.Hear.Now Festival. Following their viral success, the band’s Diamond-certified (10,000,000 in sales) hit “Iris” has garnered over 6 billion global streams, amassing over 1 billion streams in 2025 alone.

Dates include arenas like Little Caesars Arena in Detroit, Capital One Arena in DC, Prudential Center in New Jersey, Madison Square Garden in New York, United Center in Chicago, Ball Arena in Denver, Moody Center in Austin, Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles and more.

Def Leppard is represented by Independent Artist Group. Goo Goo Dolls are represented by WME.
