# Sara Evans Announces Return Of Winter ‘Holiday Road Tour’

## Summary

Sara Evans announced her 2026 "Holiday Road Tour," a 10-city run beginning December 2 in Cranston, Rhode Island, and concluding December 18 in Des Moines, Iowa, with support from Braden Hull. Presale begins August 26, with general onsale August 28.

### Key Points
- 10-city holiday tour, December 2–18, 2026.
- Support act: Braden Hull.
- Presale August 26; general onsale August 28.

Sara Evans, the Grammy-nominated country singer, has announced her 2026 ‘Holiday Road Tour,’ with supporting performances from Braden Hull. The 10-city tour will begin on Dec. 2 at the Historic Park Theatre & Event Center in Cranston, Rhode Island, and travel to Newton, New Jersey; Green Bay, Wisconsin; and more before concluding on Dec. 18 at the Hoyt Sherman Place in Des Moines, Iowa.

Pre-sale begins Aug. 26 with general onsale Aug. 28 at 10 a.m. local time. Visit [www.saraevans.com](https://www.saraevans.com/) to sign up for pre-sale access and more ticket information.

The shows will feature a fusion of Evans’ timeless classics and beloved holiday hits. The limited-engagement run has become a seasonal tradition, enchanting audiences across the country with her stunning vocals and festive spirit.

“There’s always something so magical and special about Christmastime and the holidays every year! I love getting to hit the road with my band and bring our versions of your favorite holiday songs to your hometown. The Holiday Road Tour is really about celebrating all of those things that make this time of year so special: family, tradition, memories, music, and just being together. I hope people can come spend the evening with us, sing along, have a few laughs, and leave feeling a little more in the Christmas spirit. I can’t wait to celebrate with everyone!” says Evans.
