# Neil Patrick Harris to Star in Broadway Revival of ‘Damn Yankees’

## Summary

Neil Patrick Harris will star as Applegate in a Broadway revival of "Damn Yankees," the musical's first Broadway revival in over 30 years, beginning previews March 24, 2027 at the Marquis Theatre with an April 20 opening. Julianne Hough plays Lola and Austin Scott plays Young Joe, in a "revisal" directed by Sergio Trujillo that resets the story to the early 2000s with the Baltimore Orioles.

### Key Points
- Sergio Trujillo returns from a 2025 Arena Stage production that won 8 Helen Hayes Awards, including Outstanding Musical.
- New book adaptation by Will Power and Doug Wright retains the original Adler/Ross score plus additional lyrics by Lynn Ahrens.
- Original 1955 Broadway production won 7 Tony Awards including Best Musical.
- Story reframed around a fan's deal to help the Baltimore Orioles win, replacing the original Washington Senators setting.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Broadways-Damn-Yankees.webp)

Neil Patrick Harris will play Applegate in a new Broadway production of “Damn Yankees,” which begins previews March 24, 2027, at the Marquis Theatre.

Julianne Hough will play Lola, and Austin Scott will portray Young Joe. Opening night is scheduled for April 20.

The production marks the musical’s first Broadway revival in more than 30 years. Sergio Trujillo, who directed and choreographed a 2025 staging at Arena Stage in Washington, D.C., will return in both roles. That production won eight Helen Hayes Awards, including outstanding musical.

This version moves the story to the early 2000s and gives Joe Boyd a new team to root for: the Baltimore Orioles. Joe is a devoted fan who makes a deal with Applegate to help the Orioles win. Applegate turns him into the young player portrayed by Scott, while Lola enters the picture as Joe weighs what he has given up.

Will Power and Doug Wright adapted the original book by George Abbott and Douglass Wallop. The production retains Richard Adler and Jerry Ross’ score, with additional lyrics by Lynn Ahrens. Songs from “Damn Yankees” include “Heart,” “Whatever Lola Wants” and “Shoeless Joe From Hannibal, Mo.”

“Damn Yankees has always been one of our great American musicals, but I wasn’t interested in simply reviving it,” Trujillo said in the production announcement. He described the new staging as a “revisal” that preserves the show’s score and humor while updating its story.

The original “Damn Yankees” opened on Broadway in 1955 and won seven Tony Awards, including best musical. Based on Wallop’s novel “The Year the Yankees Lost the Pennant,” it follows a baseball fan whose bargain with the devil offers his team a shot at defeating the Yankees. The show’s last Broadway revival opened in 1994, also at the Marquis Theatre.
