# AEG Presents Opens German Office

## Summary

AEG Presents launched a new Berlin office, AEG Presents Germany, with promoter Sina Hall as Senior Promoter. The office's initial focus is growing the German country-music market, alongside broader strategic opportunities in the region.

### Key Points
- Sina Hall, a 25-year live-industry veteran, will lead the new Berlin office, reporting to Adam Wilkes (President/CEO, AEG Presents Europe and Asia-Pacific).
- Hall founded Sound of Nashville in 2018 and previously led expansion of the Country to Country (C2C) Festival in Berlin with AEG.
- The office complements AEG's existing 2024 partnership with German agency MCT Agentur.

## Transcript

![Hall Sina](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Hall_Sina.jpg)

Sina Hall will spearhead AEG Presents new German office.

AEG Presents today announced the launch of AEG Presents Germany, a dedicated office in Berlin, with respected promoter Sina Hall joining the company as Senior Promoter.

Hall, [a 2026 Pollstar Women of Live Honoree](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/16/2026-women-of-live-sina-hall/), will spearhead the new office’s initial focus on growing the country music market in Germany, alongside a broader mandate to pursue strategic opportunities across the market. She will report to Adam Wilkes, President and CEO, AEG Presents Europe and Asia-Pacific.

Hall, who brings 25 years of experience to AEG Presents, began her career at Trinity Concerts in Berlin, later moving on to senior positions at Dirk Becker Entertainment and Semmel Concerts.

In 2018, she founded Sound of Nashville, an umbrella brand for touring country artists with a focus on strengthening the presence of country music in Germany and changing the perception of the genre within the German media landscape.

Later, in partnership with AEG Presents, Hall led efforts to expand the Country to Country (C2C) Festival in Berlin. The festival launched in March 2019 and quickly established itself as a leading festival brand in the market.

Hall is a member of the international CMA Task Force and the CMA Board of Directors. In 2024, she was awarded CMA’s Jo Walker Meador International Award for her work on promoting country music internationally and was recognized in Pollstar’s Women Of Live in 2026.

The launch of AEG Presents Germany builds on the momentum of the company’s partnership with MCT Agentur, the German concert promotion company founded by Scumeck Sabottka, which has strengthened the company’s presence in Germany and Europe since the partnership was forged in 2024. Sabottka and the MCT team remain AEG Presents’ key partner in Germany, continuing to lead on concert promotion and other opportunities in the market.
