# DIIV Sets 2027 Tour Across North America, Europe and Asia

## Summary

Shoegaze band DIIV has announced a 47-date 2027 world tour in support of new album *ZIRP!*, spanning Australia/NZ festival dates, Japan, North America, and Europe/UK. The North American leg runs April 7-May 21; the European/UK leg follows May 31-June 30.

### Key Points
- Tour opens with Laneway Festival dates in Australia/NZ, plus two Tokyo shows, before the April North American leg.
- North American leg covers 24 dates from Minneapolis to San Diego.
- European/UK leg covers 23 dates, including Primavera Sound and Best Kept Secret festival slots.
- Tour supports the band's fifth studio album.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/DIIV-2027-Tour-.webp)

DIIV has announced a 47-date world tour in support of its fifth album, *ZIRP!* The band’s schedule includes headline shows across North America, Europe and the United Kingdom, along with appearances in Australia, New Zealand and Japan.

The 2027 itinerary begins in February with performances at four editions of Laneway Festival in Australia and New Zealand. DIIV will also play two nights in Tokyo before bringing the tour to North America in April.

The North American leg opens April 7 at Varsity Theater in Minneapolis. Shows will continue through the spring, with that portion of the tour wrapping up May 21 at The Observatory at North Park in San Diego.

Following the North American dates, DIIV will head back to Europe for a month of performances. That run begins May 31 and continues through June 30, with the broader tour announcement including headline engagements in both continental Europe and the United Kingdom.
