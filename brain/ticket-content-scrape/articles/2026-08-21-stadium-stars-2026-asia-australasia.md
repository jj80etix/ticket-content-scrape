**Accor Stadium** | Sydney, Australia

*Concert capacity:* 83,500  
*Operator:* Venues NSW

A core part of Australia’s touring circuit, Accor Stadium combines world-class event infrastructure with a strong focus on sustainability. The second-largest stadium in the country boasts an open-bowl design, partially covered by a sweeping roof supported by two 295-metre arches, maximising sightlines while minimising direct sunlight on the pitch. The roof also harvests rainwater to irrigate the field, while the venue incorporates natural ventilation and energy-efficient climate management. Complementing its impressive production capabilities is the world’s longest straight-run video screen, stretching 120 metres by 10 metres.

Purpose-built to host the world’s biggest events, the Sydney stadium has welcomed many of the world’s leading artists, alongside international sporting fixtures throughout its 25-year history. For ticket buyers, integrated public transport is often included with event tickets, making access to the venue simple and sustainable.

Operated by Venues NSW, Accor Stadium benefits from the backing of a state government agency with extensive experience in delivering major events. Recently, Venues NSW partnered with Ticketmaster Australia to further streamline ticketing across the portfolio. Combining first-class infrastructure with operational expertise, Accor Stadium remains one of the Asia-Pacific region’s supreme stops on the global touring circuit.

**Eden Park** | Auckland, New Zealand

*Concert capacity*: 60,000  
*Operator*: Eden Park Trust

With a legacy that spans more than 125 years, Auckland’s Eden Park continues to raise the bar for touring in the Southern Hemisphere. As New Zealand’s only stadium with a capacity of more than 50,000, it occupies a unique position on the global touring map, hosting major sports alongside blockbuster concerts. Recent changes to planning regulations have significantly expanded its live entertainment offering, increasing its annual concert allowance from 12 to as many as 32 events and giving promoters greater flexibility when routing tours.

The stadium’s West Stand stage configuration and natural bowl shape deliver excellent sightlines and impressive acoustics, while efficient logistics are supported by internal service roads, a 3.9-metre-wide vehicle tunnel, and dedicated staging access. The venue also prioritises accessibility, with extensive accessible seating, facilities for guests with mobility requirements, and on-site sensory rooms.

Located just minutes from central Auckland, Eden Park is exceptionally well-connected by public transport. And with the recent annual concert allowance boost, the stadium is now better positioned than ever to attract the world’s biggest tours to New Zealand.

**Goyang Stadium** | Goyang, South Korea

*Concert capacity*: 50,000  
*Operator*: Goyang City Facilities Management

Located just 20km northwest of Seoul, Goyang Stadium has become South Korea’s premier venue for large-scale stadium shows. The venue has balanced launching K-pop acts like BTS, BLACKPINK, and Seventeen with hosting major international names like Coldplay, Oasis, and Travis Scott.

Purpose-built for football and athletics, the stadium’s open-air design gives productions exceptional creative freedom, accommodating everything from large-scale pyrotechnics and drone displays to sizeable stages and immersive installations. Excellent transport links, including a nearby commuter rail station, place the venue within easy reach of both central Seoul and Incheon International Airport, while the surrounding KINTEX district provides extensive hotel, retail, and dining options.

The venue’s prominence has grown significantly in recent years, hosting 18 major concerts and welcoming around 700,000 fans last year. As some of Seoul’s stadiums undergo redevelopment, Goyang has become the country’s go-to destination for blockbuster live events. Its growing reputation among international promoters has firmly established the venue as an essential stop on the global circuit and one of Asia’s leading concert destinations.

**Kai Tak Stadium** | Hong Kong

*Concert capacity*: 50,000  
*Operator*: Kai Tak Sports Ltd / Legends Global

Opening just last year, Kai Tak Stadium has rapidly established itself as one of Asia’s foremost destinations for major international touring productions. Located at the heart of Hong Kong’s Kai Tak Sports Park, the venue combines world-class infrastructure with direct access to the Greater Bay Area’s 86m residents, making it a strategic gateway for regional and international tours.

Purpose-built for live entertainment, the stadium features a retractable roof, modular pitch system, and a dedicated stage pocket, enabling efficient load-ins, fast turnarounds, and all-weather operation. Extensive back-of-house facilities, plug-and-play AV infrastructure, flexible dark fibre connectivity, an in-house Spidercam system, and a stadium-wide LED network ensure productions can be delivered at the highest standards. Premium hospitality offerings, including suites and lounges, sit alongside excellent sightlines and a vibrant entertainment precinct with retail and dining options.

Ranking among the world’s best-performing venues, Kai Tak Stadium’s prominence and performance capabilities can only go up from here.

---

**Marvel Stadium** | Melbourne, Australia

*Concert capacity*: 70,000  
*Operator*: Australian Football League

Fans and touring crews all marvel at Melbourne’s Marvel Stadium, which has established itself as one of the region’s leading venues for major live experiences. Welcoming more than 47m fans since opening in 2008, it has hosted global superstars like Taylor Swift, Ed Sheeran, Harry Styles, Adele, and Coldplay, all while doubling as an elite sports domain.

Purpose-built for flexibility, Marvel Stadium features a retractable roof that can open or close in just eight minutes, ensuring events can proceed regardless of weather conditions. Retractable lower-tier seating further enhances the fan experience, bringing audiences to within 10 metres of the stage or field to create a more intimate atmosphere for concerts and sporting events alike. Extensive digital infrastructure, including more than 1,500 TV screens and venue-wide Wi-Fi, supports seamless event delivery and fan engagement.

Sustainability is also central to the venue’s operation, with a rainwater harvesting system capable of storing 1m litres collected from the stadium roof for pitch irrigation and amenities, reducing water consumption by around 25%. Combined with Melbourne’s reputation as Australia’s events capital and its central location, Marvel Stadium continues to offer promoters a versatile, technologically advanced, and weather-proof destination for world-class touring productions.

---

**National Stadium** | The Kallang, Singapore

*Concert capacity*: 55,000  
*Operator*: The Kallang Group

Strategically positioned between Singapore’s international airport and the city centre, the National Stadium remains the centrepiece of Singapore’s premier live entertainment precinct, recently rebranded as The Kallang. The evolving district continues to strengthen its appeal for international touring, with plans for a new 18,000-capacity indoor arena alongside ongoing investments in infrastructure and fan experience.

The 55,000-capacity stadium is purpose-built for large-scale productions, with dedicated load-in/load-out access, an exclusive ring road for production vehicles, extensive on-site parking for trucks and containers, a retractable roof, expansive back-of-house facilities, and multiple merchandise and activation spaces. An experienced in-house team delivers integrated support across operations, hospitality, marketing, and ticketing, streamlining delivery for international promoters.

The wider precinct is also set for major enhancements, including upgrades to Kallang Wave Mall, new public spaces, and improved amenities, reinforcing its position as a year-round destination for world-class experience seekers.
