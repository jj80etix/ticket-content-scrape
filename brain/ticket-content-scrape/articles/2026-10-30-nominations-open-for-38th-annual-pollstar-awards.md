# Nominations Open For 38th Annual Pollstar Awards

## Summary

Pollstar opened nominations for its 38th Annual Pollstar Awards, with a three-week nomination window closing November 20. The peer-voted awards cover 40+ categories spanning tours, venues, promoters, agents, and production.

### Key Points
- Nomination period: Oct. 30 - Nov. 20, 5:00 p.m. PT
- Categories include Tour of the Year, genre-specific tours (Country, Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock, Latin, Comedy, R&B), Artist Residency, Promoter, Manager, Agent, Talent Buyer, Venue Executive, and more
- Only Pollstar subscribers can nominate and vote

Pollstar today (Oct. 30) announced the opening of nominations for the 38 <sup>th</sup> Annual Pollstar Awards, the live industry’s most prestigious honors. The nomination period is open for three weeks and closes Nov. 20 at 5:00 p.m. PT.  
  
The Pollstar Awards, which are peer-voted, include over 40 categories, including: Tour of the Year as well as the top Country, Pop, Hip-Hop, Rock, Latin, Comedy and R&B Tours; the Year’s top Artist Residency, Promoter, Manager, Agent, Talent Buyer, Venue Executive, Marketer and Road Warrior; Venue Awards classified by venue size and type; along with awards for Production, New Headliner, Transportation, and more.  
  
Pollstar [subscribers](https://www.pollstar.com/subscribe) can nominate and vote in the Pollstar Awards. Subscribers can login to their account at [Pollstar.com](https://pollstar.cmail19.com/t/j-l-ydayhjt-hdhulijrht-j/)  and follow the prompt at the top of the webpage to nominate. If you are already logged in, you can also click  [HERE](https://pages.pollstar.com/pollstar-awards-nominations?utm_source=eblast&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=psa_noms) to vote.
