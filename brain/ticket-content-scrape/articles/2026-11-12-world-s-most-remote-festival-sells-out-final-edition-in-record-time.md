# ‘World’s most remote festival’ sells out final edition in record time

## Summary

Australia's Birdsville Big Red Bash, billed as the "world's most remote festival," sold out its final edition in record time ahead of its July farewell event in the Queensland outback.

- Final edition of the Birdsville Big Red Bash sold out in record time.
