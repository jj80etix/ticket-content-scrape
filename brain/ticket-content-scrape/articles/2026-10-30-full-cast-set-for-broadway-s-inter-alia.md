# Full Cast Set for Broadway’s ‘Inter Alia’

## Summary

Broadway's "Inter Alia," starring Rosamund Pike in her Broadway debut, announced its full supporting cast ahead of previews starting November 10 at the Music Box Theatre; the Suzie Miller play officially opens December 1, 2026 and runs through February 21, 2027.

### Key Points
- New ensemble: Reece Boyle, Charlemagne Fadner, Olive Ross-Kline, Ever Brave Savage, Greta Schaefer, Corinne Sweeney, plus standbys Suzy Jane Hunt, Christian Conn, Alex Serino
- Joins previously announced principals Pike, Jamie Glover, and Cormac McAlinden, all reprising their London roles and making Broadway debuts

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Inter-Alia-Broadway.webp)

“Inter Alia” has announced its complete Broadway cast ahead of its first performance at the Music Box Theatre on November 10.

Rosamund Pike will make her Broadway debut in the Suzie Miller play, which officially opens December 1, 2026, and runs through February 21, 2027.

The newly announced ensemble includes Reece Boyle, Charlemagne Fadner, Olive Ross-Kline, Ever Brave Savage, Greta Schaefer and Corinne Sweeney. Suzy Jane Hunt, Christian Conn and Alex Serino will serve as standbys for the production.

They join the previously announced principal cast of Pike as Jessica Parks, Jamie Glover as Michael Wheatley and Cormac McAlinden as Harry Wheatley. All three actors will reprise their London roles and make their Broadway debuts. Hunt covers Jessica, Conn covers Michael and Serino covers Harry.

Directed by Justin Martin, “Inter Alia” follows Jessica, a London Crown Court judge whose professional responsibilities intersect with the demands of motherhood and family life. She wants to improve the legal system while managing the competing expectations placed on her at home and at work.

The Broadway creative team includes Miriam Buether as set and costume designer, Natasha Chivers as lighting designer, and Ben and Max Ringham as sound designers. Willie Williams for Treatment Studios handles video design, with music by Erin LeCount and James Jacob, known as Jakwob.

Lucy Hind serves as movement and intimacy director, and Nick Pinchbeck is music supervisor. Casting is handled by Alastair Coomer and Naomi Downham in the United Kingdom and Jim Carnahan in the United States.
