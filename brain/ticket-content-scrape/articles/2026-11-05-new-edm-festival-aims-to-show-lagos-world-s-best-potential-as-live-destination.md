# New EDM festival aims to show Lagos’ ‘world’s best’ potential as live destination

## Summary

Promoter M:E Entertainment is launching This Is Me, a new one-day EDM festival in Lagos on Dec. 19, positioning Nigeria as capable of delivering world-class production values for electronic music events. Black Coffee headlines.

### Key Points
- One-day festival set for Dec. 19 in Lagos during the Detty December season.
- Headlined by Black Coffee.
- Organizers cite growth in local production expertise and on-shored equipment supply as enabling the event.
