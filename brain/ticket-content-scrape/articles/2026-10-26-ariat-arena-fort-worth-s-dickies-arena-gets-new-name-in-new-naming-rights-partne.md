# Ariat Arena: Fort Worth’s Dickies Arena Gets New Name In New Naming Rights Partnership

## Summary

Fort Worth's Dickies Arena has been renamed Ariat Arena under a new 10-year naming rights partnership with Western workwear brand Ariat and venue operator Trail Drive Management Corp. The deal designates Ariat as the exclusive Western wear, work wear, and boot category partner and includes a new charitable giving program for Fort Worth-area organizations.

### Key Points
- Venue formerly known as Dickies Arena is now Ariat Arena, effective immediately.
- 10-year naming rights deal; terms undisclosed.
- Ariat has had regional HQ and flagship store in Fort Worth since a 2020 $70M investment.
- Deal includes a new charitable giving program for the Fort Worth area.
- Permanent signage expected by year-end, ahead of the Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo's 130th anniversary.

## Transcript

![Ariat Arena](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ariat-Arena.jpg)

Rendering courtesy Ariat Arena

The home of the Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo is now known as Ariat Arena, effective immediately and following a partnership with the Western workwear brand and Trail Drive Management Corp., which operates the 14,000-capacoty arena formerly known as Dickies Arena.

“With Ariat, the team at Trail Drive Management Corp. and the Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo combining our collective talents, innovative leadership, and brand reputations, new and exciting synergies are evolving that will take the guest experience to new heights in Ariat Arena,” said FWSSR President and General Manager Matt Carter. “We are fortunate to already have a longstanding relationship with Ariat as a FWSSR corporate partner, and we’re honored that they are growing their support of FWSSR and Fort Worth’s amazing multipurpose arena.”

Ariat established its regional headquarters in Fort Worth in 2020 along with the opening of a flagship store in the city as well as a $70 million investment and expansion. The 10-year naming rights partnership designates Ariat as the Arena’s official and exclusive partner for Western Wear and Work Wear and Boot categories. Terms of the deal were not disclosed. Under the partnership, Ariat and Trail Drive will also establish a charitable giving program to support Fort Worth-area organizations and further deepen Ariat’s involvement in the local community.

“This arena has become an icon in Fort Worth for fans to experience all forms of entertainment, including concerts, sporting events, and family shows,” said Matt Homan, President of Trail Drive Management Corp. and general manager of the venue. “We look forward to continuing our story and embracing our Western culture and history with Ariat.”

New Ariat signage will be visible immediately, according to the announcement, with permanent signage expected to be installed by the end of the year in time to celebrate the 130th anniversary of the Fort Worth Stock Show & Rodeo beginning Jan. 15.

Upcoming concerts at the arena include Morrissey, Teddy Swims, John Summit and many others.
