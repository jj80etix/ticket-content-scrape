# Tom Hanks, Kelli O’Hara to Bring ‘This World of Tomorrow’ to Broadway in 2027

## Summary

Tom Hanks and Kelli O'Hara will bring "This World of Tomorrow," Hanks' own stage adaptation of his short stories, to Broadway in fall 2027 after its 2025 Off-Broadway premiere at The Shed.

### Key Points
- Hanks co-wrote the play with James Glossman; Kenny Leon directed the original production.
- Off-Broadway run at The Shed's Griffin Theater ran Oct. 30–Dec. 21, 2025.
- Story follows a scientist near the end of the 21st century revisiting the 1939 World's Fair in Queens.
- Both Hanks and O'Hara reprise their original roles (Bert Allenberry and Carmen) for Broadway.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/This-World-of-Tomorrow.webp)

Tom Hanks and Kelli O’Hara will star in a Broadway production of “This World of Tomorrow” in fall 2027, bringing the play to Broadway following its 2025 Off-Broadway premiere.

Hanks co-wrote the work with James Glossman, adapting his own short stories for the stage.

The two actors previously led the production at The Shed’s Griffin Theater in Manhattan. That engagement began previews October 30, 2025, opened November 18 and concluded December 21. Kenny Leon directed the world premiere.

“This World of Tomorrow” follows Bert Allenberry, a scientist living near the end of the 21st century who looks to the past in search of love. His travels repeatedly bring him back to a particular day at the 1939 World’s Fair in Queens, New York. The story explores his attachment to that earlier world and the choices he faces about where to build his life. Hanks played Bert in the original production, with O’Hara appearing as Carmen.

The Broadway engagement will mark Hanks’ return after his 2013 debut in Nora Ephron’s “Lucky Guy.” His performance earned a Tony Award nomination. O’Hara’s recent Broadway work includes “Fallen Angels,” opposite Rose Byrne, for which she received her ninth Tony nomination.

O’Hara won the 2015 Tony Award for best leading actress in a musical for “The King and I.” Her other Broadway credits include “The Light in the Piazza,” “South Pacific” and “Days of Wine and Roses.”

The original Off-Broadway company also featured Kerry Bishé, Kayli Carter, Paul Murphy, Jamie Ann Romero, Lee Aaron Rosen, Jay O. Sanders, Ruben Santiago-Hudson, Donald Webber Jr. and Michelle Wilson. Those performers appeared alongside Hanks and O’Hara during the play’s world-premiere engagement at The Shed.
