# Don Toliver O2 concert rescheduled after Co-op Live evacuation

## Summary

Manchester's Co-op Live arena evacuated over 12,000 fans after Don Toliver's show was halted mid-performance; his rescheduled O2 London concert follows. The article is paywalled beyond the lede, and the cause of the evacuation is not detailed in the available content.

### Key Points
- The evacuation affected 12,000+ attendees at Co-op Live, Manchester.
- Don Toliver's show stopped mid-way through on a Sunday date.
- His O2 (London) concert has since been rescheduled.
