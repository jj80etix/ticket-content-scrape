# Dylan Gossett Announces 2027 UK/Euro Dates

## Summary

Country/singer-songwriter Dylan Gossett has announced his "Ramblin' EU/UK Tour 2027," covering Utrecht, Brussels, London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow, and Dublin. The tour follows the announcement of his sophomore album Ramblin', due September 25 via Big Loud Texas/Mercury Records, and its new single "Loving You Like That." The piece recaps Gossett's rapid rise, including prior tour support slots and box-office figures from recent US shows.

### Key Points
- "Ramblin' EU/UK Tour 2027" dates run February 15-27, 2027 across the Netherlands, Belgium, UK, and Ireland, including stops at London's O2 Academy Brixton and Dublin's 3Arena.
- Sophomore album Ramblin' releases September 25 via Big Loud Texas/Mercury Records, led by single "Loving You Like That."
- Gossett has toured with George Strait, Zach Top, Mumford & Sons, The Lumineers, Noah Kahan, Morgan Wallen, and HARDY.
- Recent US box-office data cited: 4,839 tickets/$167,412 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway (Sept. 2025) and 1,461 tickets/$43,316 at McMenamins Crystal Ballroom, Portland (Aug. 2025).
- Gossett has amassed nearly 1.5 billion career streams, led by double-platinum (US) / multi-platinum (international) single "Coal"; he is repped by UTA and managed by TMWRK.

Dylan Gossett has announced his “Ramblin’ EU/UK Tour 2027,” which will take the multi-platinum singer, songwriter, and producer to Utrecht (Netherlands), Brussels (Belgium), London, Manchester, Birmingham, Glasgow (UK), and Dublin (Ireland).

The news follows the reveal of Dylan’s sophomore album, Ramblin’, which will be released on Sept. 25 via Big Loud Texas/Mercury Records, as well as the album’s newest single, “Loving You Like That.”

Ramblin’ marks the next chapter for one of country music’s fastest-rising artists, building on the foundation of his 2025 debut album, Westward.

Part of the inspiration for Ramblin’ came from years of relentless touring around the world. He’s toured alongside artists including George Strait, Zach Top, Mumford & Sons, The Lumineers, Noah Kahan, Morgan Wallen, and HARDY.

Some headline date highlights taken from Pollstar Boxoffice include a Sept. 26, 2025 performance at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston, MA, where Gossett moved 4,839  
tickets at a $167,412; or his Aug. 6, 2025 show at McMenamins Crystal Ballroom in  
Portland, OR (1,461 tickets, $43,316 grossed).

Hailing from Austin, Texas, the 27-year-old singer, songwriter, and producer has amassed nearly 1.5 billion career streams, led by his breakthrough anthem “Coal,” now certified double-platinum by the RIAA in the U.S. and multi-platinum internationally.

Gossett is repped worldwide by UTA and managed by TMWRK.
