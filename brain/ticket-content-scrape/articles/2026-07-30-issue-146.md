# Issue 146

## Summary

IQ Magazine's Issue 146 is a themed content roundup covering its "New Bosses 2026" list of emerging executives, a profile of agent Tom Schroeder's 25-year career, a Belgium live-music market report, a Hong Kong market feature, and a K-pop special on international promoters, merchandise psychology, and HYBE's "THE CITY" fan-engagement concept.

### Key Points
- "New Bosses 2026" is IQ's annual list of top emerging live-industry executives.
- The K-pop special covers international promoters staging K-pop shows and why K-pop fans consistently outspend other genres on merchandise.
- Includes a piece on direct-to-fan platforms ("WTF is DTF?") reshaping fan engagement.

This is a link roundup with only teaser-length blurbs for each linked story; no single item has enough substantive detail here to summarize further.
