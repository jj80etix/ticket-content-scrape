# Live Nation Backs Startup Creator Nation, Acquisitions Expected In ‘Coming Months’

## Summary

Live Nation announced it is bankrolling Creator Nation, a new creator-economy startup founded by Scott and Alexis Fisher that will acquire and grow creator-management companies, with its first acquisitions expected within months. The Fishers previously built and sold Select Management Group and founded Timeline Management (itself Live Nation-backed since 2024); Creator Nation will operate separately from Timeline. The move extends Live Nation's strategy of buying influence over the talent-management layer that feeds its venues and promotion business, rather than acquiring ticketing or venue assets directly.

### Key Points
- Live Nation provides capital and strategic support; Fishers retain operating control and their own team.
- First acquisitions of creator-management companies expected "in the coming months."
- Scott Fisher sold Select Management Group (500+ creators, 1B+ combined audience) to Propagate Content in 2025.
- Alexis Fisher's Timeline Management received separate Live Nation backing in 2024 and continues operating independently.
- CEO Michael Rapino frames creators as building "entertainment businesses" spanning content, live, brands and commerce.

### Highlights
- New entity, not a direct acquisition by Live Nation itself — capital/strategic-support structure, acquisitions to follow "in coming months."
- Second creator-economy vehicle Live Nation has backed (after Timeline Management in 2024), signaling a deliberate roll-up strategy in talent management adjacent to live events.

### Industry Problem
Live Nation is moving up the supply chain to secure the talent and creator relationships that ultimately drive ticket demand, reflecting a broader consolidation trend where dominant promoters extend control beyond venues and ticketing into artist/creator management.

### Proposed Solution
No ticketing product is proposed here; this is a market-structure signal to monitor. The trigger that would change posture for an independent platform like Etix: if Creator Nation's roster acquisitions start steering creator-led live events toward Ticketmaster/Live Nation venues and away from independent primary platforms.

Live Nation announced it is bankrolling Creator Nation, a creator-economy startup founded by Scott and Alexis Fisher that aims to acquire and grow creator-management companies, with the first acquisitions expected in “the coming months.”

“Creators have proven they can build enormous audiences, but the infrastructure around them has not always kept pace,” said Scott Fisher, co-founder of Creator Nation. “We’re building Creator Nation to give creators and the teams supporting them the resources to turn that influence into businesses designed to last.”

The Fishers will lead the company with their own team with Live Nation providing capital and strategic support, with Creator Nation drawing on Live Nation’s “experience and resources where they can help creators and portfolio companies grow.”

“Creators are increasingly building careers that look a lot like broader entertainment businesses, with opportunities across content, live, brands and commerce,” said Michael Rapino, President and CEO, Live Nation Entertainment. “Scott and Alexis understand how to help talent build those businesses for the long term, and we see a lot of opportunity to support that growth.”

The Fishers are considered pioneers in the creator economy, having first become involved 15 years ago.

Scott founded Select Management Group and led it through its acquisition by Propagate Content in 2025. Select’s roster spans more than 500 creators with a combined audience of more than 1 billion. Select also co-produces the Hulu show *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives*.

Alexis founded Timeline Management, building it across music and creator management, branding and touring. She brought the Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders, *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives* cast and Gracie’s Corner to the stage. Timeline, which received its own backing from Live Nation in 2024, will continue to operate separately from Creator Nation.

“Bringing creator-led shows to the stage showed us how a strong relationship with fans can open the door to entirely new opportunities,” said Alexis Fisher, co-founder of Creator Nation. “Creator Nation gives us a platform to do that for many more creators, across every part of their business.”
