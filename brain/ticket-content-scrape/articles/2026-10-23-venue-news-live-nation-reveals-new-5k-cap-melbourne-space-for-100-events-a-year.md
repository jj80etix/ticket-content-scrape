# Venue news: Live Nation reveals new 5k-cap Melbourne space for 100 events a year

## Summary

IQ Magazine's venue-news roundup leads with Live Nation's planned 5,000-capacity Arden Central venue in North Melbourne, part of a broader A$7bn mixed-use development, alongside shorter items on AEG venue partnerships and Loew's Jersey Theatre's reopening.

### Key Points
- Live Nation's new Arden Central venue (5,000 cap., North Melbourne) is designed for 100+ events/year by Live Nation's Blueprint Studio and Populous.
- Venue is part of a wider A$7bn precinct including 5,400+ homes and community space, developed by Hamton and Hostplus.
- Live Nation is also launching a Community Events Fund tied to the venue.
- Roundup also covers AEG venue partnerships across six US venues and Loew's Jersey Theatre nearing reopening.

## Transcript

Plus: Another launch for Melbourne with the help of Foo Fighters, AEG extends partnerships across six US venues, Loew's Jersey Theatre nears reopening, and more

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/unnamed-21.jpg)

Arden Music Hall (impression) © Live Nation

Leading the latest round-up of venue news, Live Nation has revealed plans for a new 5,000-capacity music venue in North Melbourne, with the purpose-built space at Arden Central expected to host more than 100 events a year and bring additional international tours to the city.

**Arden Central** – North Melbourne, Australia  
*Capacity: 5,000*  
*Operator: Live Nation*

The venue will also create new performance opportunities for Australian artists, as part of a wider A$7bn cultural and parkland precinct being developed by Hamton and Hostplus, the company said.

Located two minutes from Arden Station and 1.5km from Melbourne’s CBD, the venue will be designed by Live Nation’s in-house Blueprint Studio in collaboration with Populous.

“This venue is being designed around the live experience, creating an intimate connection between artists and fans while delivering everything expected of a best-in-class touring venue,” says Guy Ngata, Live Nation’s senior vice president of venue development.

The project will form part of the first phase of Arden Central, a mixed-use development set to include more than 5,400 homes, retail and hospitality spaces, community facilities and 5.5 hectares of open space. Live Nation has also announced a Community Events Fund to support local artists and community use of the venue.

**Crown LIVE** – Melbourne, Australia  
*Capacity: 1,250*  
*Operator: Crown Melbourne*

Also in Melbourne, the city’s new mid-sized venue Crown LIVE will open its doors to one of rock’s biggest acts, with Foo Fighters set to play an exclusive show on 16 November. The 1,250-capacity space will host the band for a one-night-only performance to mark the opening, which will close the Melbourne stop on their *Take Cover* Australian tour with an intimate show for a fraction of their typical audience.

**AEG Presents expands partnership with Marathon Live –** Six US venues

AEG Presents has expanded its content partnership with venue operator and promoter Marathon Live, covering six venues across the US as the latter’s flagship Nashville venue, Marathon Music Works, celebrates its 15th anniversary.

The agreement spans The Truman (1,200-cap) in Kansas City, The Hawthorn (1,300-cap) in St. Louis, The Hall (1,300-cap) in Little Rock, Marathon Music Works (1,800-cap) in Nashville, The Signal (1,500-cap) in Chattanooga, and FIVE (1,266-cap) in Jacksonville.

The partnership builds on an existing relationship that began with The Truman, with AEG Presents aiming to bring more live content to Marathon Live’s venues, the companies said. “We’re thrilled to continue building our relationship with Mark and the entire team at Marathon Live,” said Brent Fedrizzi, president of North American regional offices at AEG Presents. “Our collaboration began with the Truman, and I’m excited to see where we can take this partnership in the years ahead.”

**Loew’s Jersey Theatre –** New Jersey, USA  
*Capacity: Up to 4,000  
Operator: Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment (HBSE)*

New Jersey’s Loew’s Jersey Theatre unveiled a sneak peek of its newly restored marquee, designed to reflect the style and appearance of the original marquee when the theatre first opened in 1929. The up to 4,000-cap theatre has been undergoing renovations and will reopen on 13 November with a concert from Trey Anastasio Band.

**CASTELLO** – Düsseldorf, Germany  
*Capacity: 3,300*  
*Operator: D.LIVE*

Düsseldorf’s CASTELLO has upgraded its facilities to accommodate a wider range of live entertainment, adding a permanent on-site stage, improved lighting and modernised backstage areas. The changes will enable the venue to host more concerts, shows, live podcasts, spoken-word performances and other productions alongside its existing sports and corporate events.

With a capacity of up to 3,300 and a 1,620-square-metre floor, CASTELLO has received approval for standing audiences on its main floor. The permanent stage also removes the need to transport and install one for each event, which bosses said will reduce costs and simplify production planning.

**Forest Hills Stadium** – Queens, New York, US  
*Capacity: 13,000*  
*Operator: Tiebreaker Productions*

Forest Hills Stadium has reached an agreement with Forest Hills Gardens Corporation (FHGC) and West Side Tennis Club (WSTC) to resolve all pending litigation and secure the future of the New York venue.

The agreement establishes an initial seven-year term, with renewal options for up to a further seven years. The stadium will be permitted to host up to 28 concerts per season during the initial term, rising to 29 during renewal periods. Shows will remain subject to operating conditions, including amplified-sound curfews and real-time audio monitoring in line with city guidelines.

**Unit 45 – Redhill, UK  
***Capacity: 200*  
*Operator: Redstart Community*

Finally in the UK, Redhill’s new grassroots music venue, Unit 45, officially opened on 21 October, with the aim of creatingman all-ages space for live music in The Belfry Shopping Centre. Established by local charity Redstart Community, the venue aims to support emerging artists and young people, bosses said.

The venue launch featured psychedelic rock band Pack of Animals and local sludge metal outfit Godsplit, with Simon Williams, founder of Fierce Panda Records and a Redstart patron, among those attending.
