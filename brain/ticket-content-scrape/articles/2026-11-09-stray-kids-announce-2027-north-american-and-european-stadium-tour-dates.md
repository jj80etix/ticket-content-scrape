# Stray Kids Announce 2027 North American and European Stadium Tour Dates

## Summary

Stray Kids announced 21 new 2027 "Run It" World Tour dates across North America and Europe (May-July), expanding their global schedule to 29 shows in 12 countries. Tickets for the new dates go on sale Nov. 19 following a Nov. 18 presale window that includes both a STAY fan-club membership presale and a Live Nation-registered Artist Presale, the latter screened by Ticketmaster for fraudulent registrations.

### Key Points
- New dates: 14 in North America (Seattle's Lumen Field to MetLife Stadium), 7 in Europe/UK, running May 16-July 31.
- Member Lee Know sits out the North American/European leg for mandatory military service; remaining members continue.
- STAY Membership Presale (Nov. 18, 11 a.m.) is open only to registered sixth-gen fan-club members; non-members can still use the Artist Presale via Live Nation registration (deadline Nov. 15).
- Ticketmaster's Artist Presale ties access directly to the registrant's account in North America/UK/most of Europe, removing the need for a separate presale code; France and Italy still use separate co:brand registration.
- Organizers say Ticketmaster is actively screening Artist Presale registrations for fraud.

### Highlights
- Account-tied presale access (no separate code needed) is positioned as a Ticketmaster differentiator versus platforms that still require presale codes.
- Explicit fraud-screening of presale registrations called out as an active anti-bot measure for this high-demand on-sale.

### Industry Problem
High-demand K-pop/global-fandom on-sales are prime targets for bots and scalper registration abuse, and fans face friction when presale access requires juggling separate codes across platforms/regions.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster addresses this by tying presale eligibility directly to a registered account (removing the code step) and screening registrations for fraud before the sale. For Etix, this is a concrete competitor pattern worth benchmarking for any high-demand artist presale: account-based eligibility plus pre-sale registration screening reduces both bot risk and patron friction simultaneously.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Stray-Kids-Run-It.webp)

Stray Kids have announced 21 new concerts across North America, Europe and the United Kingdom as part of their 2027 *RUN IT* World Tour, with a series of major stadium performances scheduled from May through July.

The newly announced dates begin May 16 at Lumen Field in Seattle and conclude July 31 at San Siro Stadium in Milan. The tour includes stops at SoFi Stadium in Los Angeles, AT&T Stadium in Arlington, MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, and Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in London, among other major venues.

Tickets for the newly announced shows go on sale Thursday, Nov. 19, at 3 p.m. local time, following a series of presales on Nov. 18.

The expanded itinerary includes 14 North American concerts and seven European performances, adding to eight previously announced 2027 shows in Thailand, Singapore and Japan. The full 2027 schedule now includes 29 performances across 12 countries.

Fans will have several opportunities to purchase tickets before the general onsale.

The STAY Membership Presale begins Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 11 a.m. local time and is available to eligible sixth-generation STAY fan club members who complete their presale registration by Sunday, Nov. 15, at 3 p.m. ET.

Membership registration is currently closed to new applicants, but fans without a membership can still participate in the Artist Presale.

The Artist Presale begins Nov. 18 at 3 p.m. local time. Fans must [register through Live Nation](https://www.livenation.com/feature/straykids?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) before the Nov. 15 deadline. Registration is free and allows fans to select up to three cities in North America and three cities in Europe and the UK.

Artist Presale access on Ticketmaster is generally tied to the registrant’s account, eliminating the need for a separate presale code in North America, the UK and portions of Europe. Other ticketing platforms may require codes, while France and Italy have separate presale registration arrangements through co:brand.

The tour is also introducing additional safeguards for artist presale registration. According to the [official announcement](https://www.mercedesbenzstadium.com/news/global-superstars-stray-kids-today-announced-their-highly-anticipated-2027-world-tour-run-it?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Ticketmaster is working with the organizers to screen potentially fraudulent registrations.
