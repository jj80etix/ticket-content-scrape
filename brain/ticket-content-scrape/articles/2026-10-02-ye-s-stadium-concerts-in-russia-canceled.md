# Ye’s Stadium Concerts In Russia Canceled

## Summary

Moscow-based promoter Say Agency confirmed that Ye's (Kanye West) two sold-out stadium concerts at St. Petersburg's Gazprom Arena, originally set for October 10-11, will not take place, following weeks of confusion after the venue said it had no agreement with the organizer. Refunds to ticket buyers are scheduled for October 12.

### Key Points
- Shows were set for Oct. 10-11 at the 70,000-capacity Gazprom Arena, St. Petersburg.
- Confusion began in August when the venue publicly disputed having an agreement with Say Agency.
- Refunds will be issued to buyers on Oct. 12; Ye has upcoming shows in Jakarta, Houston, Arlington, and Phoenix.

## Transcript

After weeks of confusion, Moscow-based event producer Say Agency confirmed it is not moving forward with the two sold-out Ye concerts in St. Petersburg.

The rapper formerly known as Kanye West was scheduled to perform at Gazprom Arena in St. Petersburg on Oct. 10 and 11. Say Agency recently sent a message [via Telegram](https://t.me/s/say_agencyy) that the stadium concerts “cannot take place on the scheduled dates.”

“Say Agency is deeply grateful to everyone who has stood by us over the past month and a half—offering support, sharing our concerns, and—just like us—eagerly awaiting these concerts with all their hearts,” the company wrote.

Refunds will be issued to ticket buyers on Oct. 12.

Confusion arose when the shows at the 70,000-capacity stadium were announced in August because the [venue said it had no agreement](https://www.instagram.com/p/DcbUGlBN1y7/?igsi=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ%3D%3D) with the event organizer.

Ye’s has performed in stadiums across the globe this year, including a two-night stand at Chicago’s Soldier Field that grossed a whopping $27.98 million across both nights, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

The rapper has upcoming shows in Jakarta, Houston, Arlington and Phoenix.
