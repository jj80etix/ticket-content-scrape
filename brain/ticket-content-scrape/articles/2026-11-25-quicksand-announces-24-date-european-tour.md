# Quicksand Announces 24-Date European Tour

## Summary

Post-hardcore band Quicksand has announced a 24-date European and UK tour for spring 2027 in support of its latest album, "Bring On The Psychics," opening April 28 in Vienna and wrapping May 27 in Umeå, Sweden.

### Key Points
- 24-date tour runs April 28 - May 27, 2027, across Austria, Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Luxembourg, France, the UK, Belgium, the Netherlands, the Czech Republic, and Scandinavia.
- Four-date UK leg (London, Manchester, Glasgow, Leeds) runs May 8-11.
- Tour supports the band's new studio album, "Bring On The Psychics."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Quicksand-2027.webp)

New York City post-hardcore band Quicksand has announced a 24-date European and United Kingdom tour for spring 2027 in support of its latest studio album, *Bring On The Psychics*.

The headlining tour will begin April 28 at Flex in Vienna, Austria, with subsequent performances in Germany, Italy, Switzerland, Luxembourg and France. A four-date UK run will follow from May 8-11, with stops in London, Manchester, Glasgow and Leeds.

The trio will then return to mainland Europe for performances in Belgium, the Netherlands, Germany and the Czech Republic before heading to Scandinavia. The final stretch includes concerts in Copenhagen, Gothenburg, Oslo and Stockholm, concluding May 27 at Stage in Umeå, Sweden.

Artist presale tickets became available Wednesday, November 25, at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales will begin Friday, November 27, at 10 a.m. local time through [Quicksand’s official website](https://quicksandnyc.com/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Supporting acts have not yet been announced.

The newly announced performances expand Quicksand’s international touring schedule for 2027. The band will begin the year with a January 22 performance alongside Thursday and Titus Andronicus at Starland Ballroom in Sayreville, New Jersey, before traveling to Japan for two concerts in February. Additional performances are scheduled in Australia and New Zealand.

The European announcement follows Quicksand’s [2026 North American co-headlining tour with Bane](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/quicksand-bane-reveal-2026-co-headlining-tour-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which featured support from Soul Blind and included 26 performances across the United States and Canada.

Released July 17 through Equal Vision Records, *Bring On The Psychics* marks Quicksand’s first studio album in five years and its debut release with the label.
