# Mexican Pop Star Kenia Os Unveils US Tour Dates

## Summary

Mexican pop star Kenia Os announced a six-date "K De Karma U.S. Tour" in February 2027, hitting Dallas, Chicago, New York, Houston, El Cajon and Inglewood in support of her fourth studio album, K DE KARMA. Presale is underway ahead of the Oct. 8 general onsale via LiveNation.com.

### Key Points
- Six U.S. dates, Feb. 10–27, 2027: Dallas, Chicago, New York, Houston, El Cajon, Inglewood.
- Promoted by Live Nation; general onsale Oct. 8, with a Citi cardmember presale running through Oct. 7.
- Tour supports new album K DE KARMA; prior Monterrey show grossed $1.19M on 12,267 tickets (Pollstar Boxoffice).
- Artist has ~19M Instagram, 28M+ TikTok followers.

Kenia Os is making her way back to the States early next year. The Mexican pop star announced her “K De Karma U.S. Tour,” a six-date trek that includes shows in New York, Los Angeles and Chicago.

The Live Nation-promoted tour kicks off Feb. 10 at Majestic Theatre in Dallas, and the routing includes stops at The Auditorium in Chicago, New York’s Beacon Theatre, 713 Music Hall in Houston and El Cajon’s The Magnolia before wrapping up Feb. 27 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California.

The presale began today and runs throughout the week ahead of the general onsale, which begins Oct. 8 at 10 a.m. local time at [LiveNation.com](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ917_WHf/kenia-os-events). Citi cardmembers have access to a presale now through Oct. 7 at 11:59 p.m.

Os is on the road promoting her fourth studio album, K DE KARMA, which dropped earlier this year and includes hit songs “Belladona,” “Una y Otra Vez,” “En 4” and “Tokome.”

The artist shone during Baja Beach Fest in August and headlined a successful show at Estadio Banorte in Monterrey, Mexico, in May. The concert grossed $1,190,351 off 12,267 tickets sold at the concert, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

Os is influential in the fashion scene, attending New York Fashion Week and sitting alongside Anna Wintour at Tommy Hilfiger, and she took her style to Paris Fashion Week.

The Mexican star has nearly 19 million followers on Instagram and more than 28 million on TikTok. She also has more than 9 million monthly listeners on Spotify.
