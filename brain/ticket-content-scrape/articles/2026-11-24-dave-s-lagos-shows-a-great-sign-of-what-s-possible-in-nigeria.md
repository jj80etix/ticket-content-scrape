# Dave’s Lagos shows a ‘great sign of what’s possible’ in Nigeria

## Summary

UK artist Dave's recent shows in Lagos, Nigeria are being cited as a positive signal for the growth of the live-music market in Nigeria and West Africa more broadly. Industry commentary frames the shows as evidence of untapped demand in the region.

### Key Points
- Dave's Lagos concerts seen as proof of concept for touring in Nigeria.
- Commentary focuses on West African live-music market potential.
