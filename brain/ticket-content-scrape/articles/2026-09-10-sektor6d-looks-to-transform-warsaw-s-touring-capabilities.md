# Sektor6D looks to transform Warsaw’s touring capabilities

## Summary

Sektor6D, a modular post-industrial warehouse venue with configurable capacity up to 15,000, opened in Warsaw, becoming the Polish capital's largest indoor concert venue and aiming to fill a gap in the city's touring infrastructure amid strong demand for electronic music in Eastern Europe.

### Key Points
- Capacity up to 15,000, far exceeding Warsaw's prior largest indoor venue (4,824-cap Torwar Hall).
- Positioned to capture growing regional demand for electronic music.
- Opened September 11, 2026.
