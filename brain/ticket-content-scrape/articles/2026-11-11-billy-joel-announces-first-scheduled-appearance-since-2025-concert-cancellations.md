# Billy Joel Announces First Scheduled Appearance Since 2025 Concert Cancellations

## Summary

Billy Joel will make his first scheduled public appearance since cancelling his 2025 concerts, a December 14 Q&A session at Florida Atlantic University exclusive to enrolled students. The event will not be a traditional concert — just brief musical demonstrations alongside conversation about his career.

### Key Points
- Event: "Billy Joel: An Evening of Questions & Answers … And a Little Music," Dec 14, FAU, presented with Live Nation
- Exclusive to currently enrolled FAU students; tickets sold in person via Student Union Box Office starting Nov 12
- Comes ~19 months after Joel cancelled remaining dates following a normal pressure hydrocephalus diagnosis
- Joel has since had shunt-implant surgery and reports some symptom improvement

Billy Joel will return to the stage for a special appearance at Florida Atlantic University in December, marking his first formally scheduled event since canceling his concerts in 2025 following a neurological diagnosis. However, the appearance will be a question-and-answer session rather than a traditional concert.

The 77-year-old singer-songwriter is scheduled to appear Monday, December 14, at 7 p.m. at the Dorothy F. Schmidt College of Arts & Letters University Theatre in Boca Raton, Florida. The event, titled “Billy Joel: An Evening of Questions & Answers … And a Little Music,” is being presented in partnership with Live Nation and will be available exclusively to currently enrolled FAU students.

According to [The Palm Beach Post](https://www.aol.com/articles/billy-joel-returns-stage-south-212405000.html?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), a spokesperson for Joel clarified that the event will primarily feature conversations about music, songwriting, recording and the entertainment business. Musical elements will consist of brief demonstrations intended to illustrate his answers rather than complete performances. There will be no backing band or traditional concert setlist.

The representative said the hope is that “it will be an educational evening for those interested in how Billy’s lifelong occupation has been done.”

Tickets for the FAU event go on sale Thursday, November 12, at 9 a.m. ET, exclusively through the Student Union Box Office. Students must present a valid FAU ID/OWL card and purchase tickets in person using a physical credit card.

The appearance comes nearly 19 months after Joel [canceled his remaining concert dates](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/05/billy-joel-cancels-2025-26-stadium-dates-after-neurological-diagnosis/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) following a diagnosis of normal pressure hydrocephalus, a neurological condition affecting his hearing, vision and balance.

At the time, Joel apologized to fans, saying, “I’m sincerely sorry to disappoint our audience, and thank you for understanding.”

In an [October interview with The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/music/music-features/billy-joel-health-update-motorcycle-auction-hydrocephalus-1236730847/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Joel explained why returning to full-scale concerts remained uncertain.
