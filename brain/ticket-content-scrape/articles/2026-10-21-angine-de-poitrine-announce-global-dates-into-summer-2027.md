# Angine de Poitrine Announce Global Dates Into Summer  2027

## Summary

Angine de Poitrine added further sold-out dates to their 2027 global tour, extending the schedule well into summer with headline shows and festival dates across multiple continents (companion coverage to the TicketNews item on the same tour announcement).

### Key Points
- Tour opens March 4, 2027 at Forum Melbourne (Australian debut), then runs through South America, North America, Europe, and the UK into late July.
- Notable stops include Toronto's 16,000-cap RBC Amphitheatre (July 18) and Chicoutimi, Quebec's 15,000-cap Zone Portuaire (July 30).
- Two-night stands added at Calgary's Grey Eagle Event Centre (March 28-29) and Ottawa's History venue.

## Transcript

![Angine TOUR 2027 GLOBAL](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Angine-TOUR-2027-GLOBAL-scaled.jpg)

Angine TOUR 2027 GLOBAL

Everyone’s favorite extraterrestrial math-rock duo, Angine de Poitrine, have added to its sold-out tour schedule, with global dates well into summer 2027, with major headline plays and high profile festival appearances.

The “microtonal cardboard duo” kicks off newly scheduled dates with an eagerly awaited Australian debut, beginning March 4 at Forum Melbourne, after which they will play South America, North America, Europe, and the United Kingdom through late July.

Notable venues include homecoming headline shows for the Canada-based duo at Toronto’s 16,000-capacity RBC Amphitheatre (July 18) and Chicoutimi, Quebec’s 15,000-capacity Zone Portuaire (July 30), two-night stands at Calgary’s Grey Eagle Event Centre (March 28-29), Ottawa’s History Ottawa (March 31-April 1), Winnipeg’s Burton Cummings Theatre (April 6-7) and London, UK’s O2 Academy Brixton (June 28-29). International festival dates include WOMADelaide in South Australia (March 7), Lollapalooza Argentina (March 13), Lollapalooza Chile (March 14), Lollapalooza Brasil (March 19), and Primavera Sound in Barcelona (June 3-5).

Presale signups for all new headline dates are available exclusively via Laylo now through Sunday, Oct. 25 at 3:00 pm (ET), with registered fans receiving a unique code on Monday, Oct. 26, allowing access to the artist presale beginning Tuesday, Oct. 27 at 10:00 a.m. (local). Presales end Thursday, Oct. 29 at 10:00 p.m., with all remaining tickets going on sale to the general public on Friday, October 30 at 10:00 am (local).

Currently, Angine de Poitrine are playing Europe and North America on a sold-out headline run with upcoming North America dates including Toronto’s History, a three-show run at Brooklyn Bowl as well as two-night stands at The Salt Shed, Minneapolis’ First Avenue and The Wiltern in Los Angeles, among others.

[The band was recently featured on a Pollstar weekly cover feature](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/15/angine-de-poitrine-313-year-old-space-aliens-melt-faces-sell-out-first-global-tour/), including a lengthy interview with the band’s booking agents about the intentional and gradual process of building the quirky, captivating duo’s unique appeal that is leading to viral moments, high-profile media moments and shout-outs from contemporaries across music from Jack White to deadmau5.
