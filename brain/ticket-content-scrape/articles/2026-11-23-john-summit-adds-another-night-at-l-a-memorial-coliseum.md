# John Summit Adds Another Night At L.A. Memorial Coliseum

## Summary

John Summit added a second Dec. 11 Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum show after his Dec. 12 date sold out, becoming only the sixth artist to headline the venue twice; presale access runs through Cash App, Ticketmaster's Reserved by Spotify program and an artist presale beginning Nov. 24, ahead of the Nov. 25 general on-sale.

### Key Points
- A second LA Coliseum date (Dec. 11) was added after the Dec. 12 sellout; paired with an LA Fire Department toy drive.
- Presale tiers: Cash App Visa, Spotify Reserved (via Ticketmaster), artist presale (Nov. 24, 10am PT).
- General on-sale is Nov. 25, 2pm PT via Ticketmaster.
- Summit's Oct. 31 Capital One Arena show grossed $1.85M off 17,556 tickets (per Pollstar Boxoffice).

One night in Los Angeles wasn’t enough for John Summit and his fans. Following overwhelming demand, the dance music star announced a second date at the L.A. Memorial Coliseum to make it back-to-back nights at the historic venue.

Summit added a Dec. 11 show ahead of his sold-out concert on Dec. 12, making him only the sixth artist to perform two or more shows at the L.A. Memorial Coliseum.

“Ending the tour in LA with my biggest headline show ever was already insane; now adding a second night is unreal,” Summit said in a statement. “We’re also teaming up with the LA Fire Department for a holiday toy drive to give back and spread some holiday cheer. Can’t think of a better way to close out the year.”

Presale for “CNTRL ESCAPE TOUR” stop in L.A. is now available for Cash App Visa Card. Reserved by Spotify tickets will be available to eligible U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers via Ticketmaster, and eligible fans will be notified via email and app notification.

There will also be an artist presale beginning Nov. 24 at 10 a.m. PT, and fans can sign up on the artist’s [co:brand page](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/JohnSummit/la-coliseum-added-date) ahead of the general onsale, which begins Nov. 25 at 2 p.m. PT at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/0A00651177664037?referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.ticketmaster.com%2Fevent%2F0A00651177664037).

It’s been a remarkable year for Summit. He performed sold-out shows at iconic venues across the globe, including one at Washington, D.C.’s Capital One Arena on Halloween that grossed $1,851,981 off 17,556 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

Summit is booked by THE·TEAM.
