# Bookingkit and NaviPartner form strategic partnership

## Summary

Attractions-booking platform Bookingkit and Denmark's NaviPartner formed a strategic partnership pairing NaviPartner's on-site operations/entry-management platform with Bookingkit's channel management, aiming to give shared clients (including Tivoli Gardens) a seamless path from first sale through on-site redemption across a growing number of online sales channels.

### Key Points
- NaviPartner (Microsoft Solutions Partner for retail/attractions/hospitality) supplies the on-site backbone via its NP Attraction platform; Bookingkit supplies channel distribution to OTAs like GetYourGuide, Viator, and Google Things to Do.
- Partnership combines Bookingkit's strength in the German/European market with NaviPartner's established Nordic footprint.
- The two companies already share clients, including Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen.
- NaviPartner CEO Mark Stewart Pederson frames the goal as reducing complexity for attractions operators managing ticketing, access control, retail, and financial processes together.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Bookingkit has joined forces with Denmark’s NaviPartner to offer a one-stop-shop for leisure experiences and attractions.**

NaviPartner is a Microsoft Solutions Partner for retail, attractions and hospitality, while Bookingkit is a platform for booking, marketing and managing leisure experiences. By combining NaviPartner’s on-site operations and entry management expertise with Bookingkit’s channel management offering, the partnership aims to create a seamless process from the first sale through to on-site redemption.

Attractions operate an increasingly complex technology landscape, and ticketing, access control, retail and financial processes need to work together, while sales have to reach visitors across a growing number of online channels.

The partnership will bring these two worlds together: NaviPartner will deliver the unified on-site backbone through its NP Attraction platform, while Bookingkit provides one of the world’s most powerful channel managers to distribute those experiences across leading sales channels.

Meanwhile, NaviPartner customers will gain access to high-reach distribution channels, thanks to Bookingkit’s technical integrations with online travel agencies such as GetYourGuide, Viator, and Google Things to do.

The partnership will also boost each company’s regional strengths, with Bookingkit contributing in-depth knowledge of the German and wider European market, while NaviPartner brings an established footprint across the Nordics to the table.

The two companies already share clients such as Tivoli Gardens in Copenhagen.

“Attractions today need more than just a ticketing system. They need connected solutions that help them sell across multiple channels, manage visitor flows, and create engaging experiences,” said Mark Stewart Pederson, chief executive of NaviPartner.

“Through our partnership with Bookingkit, our customers can benefit from a more integrated approach where online sales, distribution, ticket validation, and operational processes work together seamlessly. This partnership enables attractions to reduce complexity, improve efficiency, and focus on delivering memorable experiences for their visitors.”
