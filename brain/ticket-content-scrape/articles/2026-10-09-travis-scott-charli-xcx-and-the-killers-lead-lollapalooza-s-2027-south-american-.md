# Travis Scott, Charli xcx and The Killers Lead Lollapalooza’s 2027 South American Lineups

## Summary

Lollapalooza unveiled its 2027 South American lineups for Argentina, Chile and Brazil, led by Travis Scott, Charli xcx, The Killers, David Guetta, Queens of the Stone Age and The Neighbourhood, plus regional acts specific to each country.

### Key Points
- Argentina (Hipódromo de San Isidro) and Chile (Parque O'Higgins) run March 12–14, 2027; Brazil (Autódromo de Interlagos, São Paulo) runs March 19–21.
- Shared international bill also includes The Prodigy, Fontaines D.C., sombr, RAYE, Lola Young and Cage the Elephant.
- Each country adds local acts: Argentina (TINI, Dillom), Chile (Ana Tijoux, Alex Anwandter), Brazil (Rodrigo Amarante, Criolo).
- Comes alongside Lollapalooza's newly announced Berlin 2027 lineup (Olympiapark, July 17–18).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Lollapalooza-South-America.webp)

Lollapalooza has announced its 2027 lineups for Argentina, Chile and Brazil, with Travis Scott, Charli xcx, The Killers, David Guetta, Queens of the Stone Age and The Neighbourhood leading all three festivals.

The Argentina and Chile editions will run March 12 to 14. Argentina’s event returns to Hipódromo de San Isidro outside Buenos Aires, while Chile’s festival takes place at Parque O’Higgins in Santiago. Lollapalooza Brasil follows March 19 to 21 at São Paulo’s Autódromo de Interlagos.

The shared international lineup also includes The Prodigy, Fontaines D.C., sombr, Rawayana, Dominic Fike, RAYE, Lola Young, Geese, Four Tet and Cage the Elephant.

Each festival will also feature its own selection of regional performers. Argentina’s lineup includes TINI, Dillom and Usted Señalemelo. Chile will welcome Ana Tijoux, Alex Anwandter, Javiera Mena and Gondwana with Quique Neira, among others.

Lollapalooza has also begun announcing its 2027 [Berlin lineup](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/robbie-williams-the-neighbourhood-and-sombr-announced-for-lollapalooza-berlin-2027/), with Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood and sombr among the first confirmed acts.
