# Westlife Announces 2027 North American Tour with O-Town

## Summary

Westlife announced a 12-date North American leg of its 25th-anniversary world tour for March 2027, its largest global touring undertaking to date with more than 90 shows announced worldwide. The run opens March 1 in Inglewood and closes March 19 with the group's first-ever Madison Square Garden show. Citi and Amex cardholder presales begin Nov. 17, ahead of a Nov. 20 general onsale.

### Key Points
- 12-date North American leg, March 1-19, 2027; first-ever MSG performance closes the run.
- Part of a 90+ show, multi-continent 25th-anniversary tour following a sold-out 42-date UK/Ireland run.
- Citi (US) and Amex (Canada) cardholder presales run Nov. 17-18; general onsale Nov. 20.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Westlife-North-American-Tour.jpg)

Irish pop group Westlife has announced a 12-date North American leg of its *Westlife 25: The Anniversary World Tour*, bringing the group back to the United States and Canada in March 2027 with special guest O-Town.

The tour is slated to open March 1 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California, before traveling through major markets including San Francisco, Boston, Toronto and Chicago. The run will conclude March 19 with Westlife’s first-ever performance at New York City’s Madison Square Garden.

The March itinerary includes four Canadian performances, with stops in Edmonton, Calgary, Vancouver and Toronto. Westlife will also perform at Allstate Arena in Rosemont, Illinois, on March 17, bringing the Irish group’s anniversary celebration to the Chicago area on St. Patrick’s Day.

Westlife’s 25th anniversary world tour is being billed as the largest global touring undertaking of the group’s career, with more than 90 shows announced across multiple continents. The group recently completed a sold-out, 42-date run across the United Kingdom and Ireland, with additional performances scheduled throughout Europe, Asia, Mexico, Australia and New Zealand.

## Westlife 2027 Tour Tickets and Presales

Tickets for the North American tour will go on sale to the general public Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time through the [official Westlife website](https://www.westlife.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Presales begin Tuesday, November 17, at 10 a.m. local time, with Citi cardmembers receiving early access to U.S. performances and American Express cardmembers receiving presale access for Canadian dates. Both cardholder presales are scheduled to run through Wednesday, November 18, at 10 p.m. local time.
