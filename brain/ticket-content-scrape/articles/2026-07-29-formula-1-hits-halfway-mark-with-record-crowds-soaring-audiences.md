# Formula 1 Hits Halfway Mark With Record Crowds, Soaring Audiences

## Summary

Formula 1 reports record H1 2026 attendance (3.7M spectators across 11 sold-out race weekends) and rising global TV/digital audiences, alongside expanded licensing and hospitality business.

### Key Points
- Silverstone drew a record 564,000 fans; attendance in Japan rose 18%.
- Social media following surpassed 125 million, up 19% YoY; total fan base tops 830 million.
- Signed 20 new licensing partners; hospitality guests topped 60,000.

## Transcript

![Photo courtesy Formula 1](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/f1-5.webp)

Photo courtesy Formula 1

Formula 1 is racing through 2026 with record attendance, rising TV audiences, and a rapidly expanding global fanbase.

All 11 race weekends held during the first half of the season sold out, attracting nearly 3.7 million spectators — up 6% from the same events in 2025.

Formula 1 noted in a press release that circuits broke attendance records, including Silverstone with 564,000 fans, Australia with 484,000 and Belgium with 400,000. Attendance also climbed 18% in Japan and 13% at the British Grand Prix.

TV audiences are accelerating too. Brazil recorded race-weekend viewership as much as five times higher than last year, while the British Grand Prix drew 18 million Brazilian viewers. Audiences in Italy increased 27%, Chinese Grand Prix weekend viewership more than doubled and Monaco reached nearly 80 million viewers worldwide.

F1’s digital footprint is also growing fast. The sport now has more than 125 million social followers, up 19%, while video views surged 54%. Its overall fanbase has climbed beyond 830 million people, with women making up 42% of the audience and fans under 35 accounting for 43%.

Fan reaction to the racing has improved, the company noted, with 66% rating the action “Good” or “Excellent,” compared with 60% last season. Canada led the way with an 84% approval rating.

Commercial growth remains strong as well. Formula 1 signed 20 new licensing partners, welcomed more than 60,000 hospitality guests and expanded its calendar footprint with new or extended agreements involving Malaysia, Türkiye, Las Vegas, Barcelona, Spa and Portugal.

The F1 Sim Racing World Championship also posted a 72% increase in live viewers, while the sport reported a 35% reduction in its carbon footprint compared with 2018.
