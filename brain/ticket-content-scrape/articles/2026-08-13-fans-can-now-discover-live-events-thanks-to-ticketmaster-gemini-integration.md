# Fans can now discover live events thanks to Ticketmaster-Gemini integration

## Summary

Ticketmaster has integrated with Google's Gemini chatbot, letting US users discover events, compare seating and prices, and get redirected to Ticketmaster's marketplace to complete purchase — joining existing integrations with Claude, ChatGPT, and Amazon Alexa+. Users connect Ticketmaster as an app within Gemini and ask conversational questions like "what concerts are happening in Chicago this weekend?" The rollout is US-only for now, with more languages/locations planned.

### Key Points
- Announced Aug. 12, 2026; Gemini becomes the fourth major conversational-AI platform connected to Ticketmaster (after Claude, ChatGPT, Alexa+).
- Users ask natural-language questions (venue, city, event type) and are handed off to Ticketmaster's marketplace to finish the purchase.
- US-only at launch; Ticketmaster says additional languages/markets are coming.
- Framed by Ticketmaster as meeting fans where discovery increasingly starts — conversational AI alongside social, search, and streaming.

### Highlights
- Fourth AI-assistant integration for Ticketmaster (Gemini, joining Claude/ChatGPT/Alexa+) — signals a multi-platform conversational-discovery strategy, not a one-off partnership.
- Purchase still completes on Ticketmaster's own marketplace; AI assistants function as discovery/comparison layers only, not transacting parties.

### Industry Problem
Fans increasingly start their event-discovery journey in conversational AI rather than search or a ticketing site directly — a primary platform that isn't discoverable inside those assistants risks losing the top of the funnel entirely.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster's response is to plug into every major AI assistant as a connected app so it stays the transaction endpoint regardless of where discovery starts. For Etix, this is a direct AI-deployment benchmark (Q14) worth tracking: evaluate whether a similar connected-app/API surface for conversational assistants (or leaning into Etix Assistant as the discovery layer for its own clients) is warranted before AI-mediated discovery becomes a default expectation.
