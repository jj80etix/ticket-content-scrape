# Margaret Cho to Make Broadway Debut in ‘The Rocky Horror Show’

## Summary

Margaret Cho will make her Broadway debut as the Narrator in "The Rocky Horror Show," joining the revival at Studio 54 beginning November 3.

### Key Points
- Succeeds Rachel Dratch, whose final performance is November 1; Dratch originated the role and earned a Tony nomination.
- Rosie O'Donnell had played the role in a limited engagement Oct. 6-18 while Dratch was away.
- Current cast includes Jake Shears (Frank-N-Furter), Lorna Courtney (Janet), Amber Gray (Riff Raff), Kevin McHale (Brad), and Jimin Moon (Rocky).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Rocky-Horror-Show-Broadway.webp)

Margaret Cho will make her Broadway debut in “The Rocky Horror Show” this fall, joining the revival as the Narrator beginning November 3 at Studio 54.

The comedian, actor and musician will succeed Rachel Dratch, who is scheduled to give her final performance November 1. Dratch originated the role in the current production and earned a Tony Award nomination for her performance.

Cho’s arrival follows another recent change in the Narrator role. Rosie O’Donnell played a limited engagement from October 6 through October 18 while Dratch was away from the production.

The current cast also includes Jake Shears as Frank-N-Furter, Lorna Courtney as Janet, Amber Gray as Riff Raff, Kevin McHale as Brad and Jimin Moon as Rocky. George Salazar plays Eddie and Dr. Scott, with Sherie Rene Scott as Magenta and Valentina as Columbia.

Directed by Tony Award winner Sam Pinkleton, the revival began previews March 26 and officially opened April 23. The production received nine Tony Award nominations, including Best Revival of a Musical, and is scheduled to continue through February 28, 2027.

Written by Richard O’Brien, “The Rocky Horror Show” follows Brad and Janet, an engaged couple whose car breaks down outside a mansion. Their search for help brings them into the world of Dr. Frank-N-Furter and the mansion’s unusual residents.

The musical premiered at London’s Royal Court Theatre in 1973 and inspired the 1975 film “The Rocky Horror Picture Show.” Its score includes “Time Warp,” “Dammit Janet” and “Hot Patootie.”

The Broadway creative team includes choreographer Ani Taj and music supervisor Kris Kukul. Scenic design is by dots, with costumes by David I. Reynoso, lighting by Jane Cox and sound by Brian Ronan.
