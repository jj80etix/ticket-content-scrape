# Live Music Society Toolbox Grant Applications Open

## Summary

Nonprofit Live Music Society opened its 2026 Toolbox grant cycle, offering up to $10,000 per small U.S. venue (50-300 capacity) for operational projects like accessibility, ticketing/acoustics consulting, or staff training; applications close Sept 16, with grantees announced in December.

### Key Points
- Grants up to $10,000; the program has awarded $844,000+ to 109 venues since 2023.
- Eligibility: U.S. physical venue, 50-300 capacity, operating since Jan 1, 2023, 30+ annual live-music shows; promoters/festivals ineligible.
- Application window closes Sept 16, 2026; funded projects run Dec 15, 2026-June 30, 2027.

Live Music Society, a nonprofit supporting small music venues and listening rooms across the United States, announced the opening of its 2026 Toolbox grant applications.

Applications will be accepted fhrough September 16, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Pacific, with grantees to be announced in December.

The Toolbox program provides grants of up to $10,000 to help small venues tackle practical projects that address operational challenges and improve the live music experience for artists, audiences, and staff. Since launching the program in 2023, LMS has awarded more than $844,000 to 109 Toolbox grantees, supporting critical upgrades and improvements at independent venues across the country.

Visit the Live Music Society website to learn more about the [Toolbox grant applications](https://www.livemusicsociety.org/toolboxgrant).

Grants have supported initiatives including improving safety and accessibility, incorporating innovative technology to maximize operations, engaging consultants to assist with a practical challenge such as acoustics, ticketing, fundraising and branding; and fostering staff development through training and education.

General eligibility requirements include location of a physical venue and related business entity within the United States or its territories, a legal capacity of 50 to 300 individuals (ncluding standing room, operational status on or before January 1, 2023, a primary focus on live music featuring solo artists or bands, and a minimum of 30 annual music performances, with management or ownership by the applicant (promoters and festivals are not eligible). Funding opportunities allow applicants to request up to $10,000 per submission, with prospective grantees providing a detailed budget outlining utilization.

Grants will be evaluated based on two criteria: the project’s vision for enhancing the live music experience for artists, audiences, or workers at the venue, and the project’s feasibility based on submission of the timeline and implementation plan. Funded projects must be implemented between December 15, 2026 and June 30, 2027.
