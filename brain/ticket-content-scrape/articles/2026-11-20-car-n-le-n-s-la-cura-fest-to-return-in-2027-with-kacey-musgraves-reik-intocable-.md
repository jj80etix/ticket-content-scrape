# Carín León’s La Cura Fest To Return In 2027 With Kacey Musgraves, Reik, Intocable & Hombres G

## Summary

Carín León's La Cura Fest returns March 13-14, 2027 in Sonora, Mexico, with León headlining alongside Reik and Kacey Musgraves. The 2026 inaugural edition drew more than 70,000 fans.

### Key Points
- First wave of acts also includes Intocable, Hombres G and Remmy Valenzuela.
- León became the first Latin act to headline a Sphere Las Vegas residency.
- More artists to be announced; sign-ups open at lacurafest.com.

## Transcript

![la cura](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/la-cura.jpg)

Carín León is bringing La Cura Fest back in 2027, announcing dates and the first wave of artists performing at the festival that bridges Mexican and country music. León will headline the two-day festival alongside Reik and Kacey Musgraves.

La Cura Fest takes place March 13 and 14 at Expogan Hermosillo in Sonora, Mexico. Other acts on the bill include Tejano standouts Intocable, rock legends Hombres G and Remmy Valenzuela. Additional artists will be announced. Visit [lacurafest.com](https://www.lacurafest.com/) for more information and to sign up for news about the onsale.

León, a global star who became the first Latin act to headline a residency at Sphere in Las Vegas, launched La Cura Fest earlier this year, drawing more than 70,000 fans across both days. The inaugural festival featured Spanish pop icon Alejandro Sanz, Grupo Frontera, indie-folk star Kevin Kaarl, Puerto Rican singer Kany García, Matisse, Gabito Ballesteros, Silvestre Dangond, Xavi, Bobby Pulido and more.
