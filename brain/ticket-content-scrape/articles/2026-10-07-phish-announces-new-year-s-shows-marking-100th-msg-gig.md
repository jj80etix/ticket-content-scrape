# Phish Announces New Year’s Shows, Marking 100th MSG Gig

## Summary

Phish will return to Madison Square Garden for a four-night New Year's run (Dec. 30-Jan. 2) marking the band's 100th performance at the venue since 1994, which it is commemorating with a video honoring longtime MSG staff. Tickets are allocated through a request-period system at tickets.phish.com (through Oct. 12), with general public onsale October 16 and travel packages available separately.

### Key Points
- Four-night NYE run at MSG, Dec. 30-Jan. 2, marks Phish's 100th MSG show since its 1994 debut there.
- Ticket request period open now through Oct. 12 at noon ET via the band's own site, tickets.phish.com; general public onsale Oct. 16.
- Travel packages (hotel + tickets) go on sale separately Oct. 8.
- Band also has additional January 2027 shows booked in Riviera Cancun, Mexico.

## Transcript

![Phish MSG2627](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Phish_MSG2627.jpg)

Phish MSG2627

Phish has confirmed the beloved jam band will return to Madison Square Garden to ring in the new year – and the shows are extra special because they culminate in the group’s 100th performance at MSG.

Phish – which features Trey Anastasio (guitar, vocals), Jon Fishman (drums, vocals), Mike Gordon (bass, vocals), and Page McConnell (keyboards, vocals) – first played the iconic New York arena on Dec. 30, 1994. Over the past three decades, the band has made countless memories including 17 New Year’s Eve celebrations and 2017’s Baker’s Dozen, a 13-show run that featured Phish performing 237 songs with no repeats. This summer Phish treated fans five nights featuring tunes from the band’s early and mid-90s era.

And now the guys will be back to celebrate New Year’s Eve with a four-night run scheduled Dec. 30 – Jan. 2.  
  
To commemorate the 100th show at the Garden, Phish premiered a new video celebrating the longtime Madison Square Garden employees who have helped make the shows happen over the years. An announcement from Phish’s PR team adds, “Told through the memories and experiences of ushers, bartenders, concession and merchandise staff, and others, the video reflects on decades of Phish shows through the eyes of the people who have welcomed the band and its fans year after year.”

A ticket request period for the NYE shows is currently underway at tickets.phish.com and ends Monday, October 12th at Noon ET. Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, October 16 at Noon ET. A limited number of travel packages (hotel + tickets) will go on sale Thursday, October 8 at Noon ET at https://phishnye.100xhospitality.com/.

Phish has a handful of previously announced shows lined up including a show tonight at Allianz Amphitheater at Riverfront in Richmond, Virginia.
