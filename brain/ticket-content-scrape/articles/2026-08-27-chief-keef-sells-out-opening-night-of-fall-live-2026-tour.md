# Chief Keef Sells Out Opening Night Of Fall ‘Live 2026’ Tour

## Summary

Chief Keef sold out the opening night of his fall "Live 2026" tour, kicking off a run that underscores continued strong demand for the Chicago rapper's live shows.

### Key Points
- Opening night of Chief Keef's fall "Live 2026" tour sold out.
- Signals continued strong ticket demand for the artist heading into the tour's remaining dates.

## Transcript

Chief Keef Tour Flyer 2026 Final D1

Chief Keef has sold out the opening night of his “Chief Keef Live 2026” tour, which will find the rapper kicking things off in his hometown of Chicago at Huntington Bank Pavilion at Northerly Island on Sept. 12. From there, Keef will perform 18 times across the United States, with stops in Detroit, Houston, Indianapolis, New Orleans, Philadelphia, Virginia Beach and more. The tour will conclude on Oct. 28 at the Brooklyn Bowl in Las Vegas, NV. The ‘Chief Keef Live 2026’ tour is the first time that the Chicago artist will be embarking on his first headlining run since 2024.

The set will span Chief Keef’s expansive catalog, from *Finally Rich* staples like “Love Sosa” to material from his latest mixtape, *Skeletor*, with surprise guest appearances expected throughout the tour.

In 2026, Chief Keef released the viral tracks “Shake Dat” and “Rooms,” collaborated with Katy Perry on the “Legendary Lovers Remix,” and released Skeletor, setting the stage for the current tour.
