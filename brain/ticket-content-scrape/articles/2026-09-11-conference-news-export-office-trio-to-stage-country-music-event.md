# Conference news: Export office trio to stage country music event

## Summary

An IQ Magazine roundup covers three upcoming live-music business conferences: a new "Country Connections Live" showcase during AmericanaFest in Nashville (Sept. 19), plus program updates from Radar Madrid's debut edition and Entrio's second "Live & Loud" conference in Croatia. The article is paywalled beyond the opening section.

### Key Points
- Country Connections Live: Sept. 19 in Nashville, organized by Australian, Canadian and UK export offices, timed to AmericanaFest (Sept. 15-19).
- Radar Madrid and Entrio's "Live & Loud" (Croatia) also detailed in the roundup; specifics are paywalled.
- Full program details require an IQ Magazine subscription.
