# System of a Down to Headline One-Night-Only Rio de Janeiro Concert With Faith No More

## Summary

System of a Down will headline a standalone Jan 15, 2027 show at Rio de Janeiro's Maracanã Stadium with Faith No More, part of a broader 2027 South American/Australia/New Zealand run following their recent European/U.K. tour.

### Key Points
- Jan 15, 2027 at Maracanã Stadium, Rio de Janeiro; Faith No More as special guest.
- Presale began Aug 18 for the band's email-list subscribers; general on-sale Aug 21 at noon.
- Follows a completed European/U.K. tour and an April Sick New World Festival headline slot in Las Vegas.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/System-of-a-Down-Rio-De-Janeiro.webp)

System of a Down will return to Brazil next year for a special one-night concert at Rio de Janeiro’s Maracanã Stadium.

The concert is scheduled for January 15, 2027, and will mark System of a Down’s return to Rio de Janeiro for a standalone performance. Faith No More set to join the band as a special guest.

The newly announced show is part of a larger South American schedule planned for 2027. Following the Rio concert, System of a Down will continue its international touring plans before heading to Australia and New Zealand with Faith No More.

The two bands will share the stage again for the upcoming run after System of a Down recently completed a European and U.K. tour. The group also headlined Sick New World Festival in Las Vegas in April.
