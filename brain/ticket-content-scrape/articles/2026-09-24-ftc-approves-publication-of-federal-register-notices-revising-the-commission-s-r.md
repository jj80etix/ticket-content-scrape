# FTC Approves Publication of Federal Register Notices Revising the Commission’s Rules of Practice

## Summary

The FTC amended its internal Rules of Practice to align with the FTC Act and reflect the new American Competition Enforcement Division, also correcting minor errors in the prior version. Two Federal Register notices cover 16 CFR Parts 0-4 and Parts 4-5 respectively. The Commission approved the changes 2-0.

### Key Points
- Reflects the newly created American Competition Enforcement Division in agency rules.
- Corrects minor errors/omissions in the prior Rules of Practice.
- Two Federal Register notices published, covering different CFR part ranges.
- Commission vote: 2-0.

## Transcript

The Federal Trade Commission amended the agency’s Rules of Practice to better align with the FTC Act and reflect organizational changes, including the creation of the American Competition Enforcement Division. The amendments also correct a number of minor errors and omissions in the previous version of the Rules.

A detailed discussion of these and all rule amendments can be found in this [Federal Register notice (16 CFR Parts 0-4)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/24/2026-19597/rules-of-practice) and this [Federal Register notice (16 CFR Parts 4 and 5)](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/09/24/2026-19598/rules-of-practice)**.**

The Commission vote to approve the proposed changes to the Rules of Practice was 2-0.
