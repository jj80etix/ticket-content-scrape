# Ty Segall Announces 2027 North American Tour with Full Band

## Summary

Ty Segall announced a 2027 North American tour in support of his album "Chrome," running April 13 through June 6 with 17 shows, including eight in Canada. Tickets for the new dates go on sale Nov. 20, with select cities (including Toronto) getting an earlier Live Nation presale on Nov. 18. The tour follows Segall's first South American run and a 2025/2026 album cycle.

### Key Points
- 17-date tour, April 13-June 6, 2027, across the U.S. and Canada; general onsale Nov. 20.
- Toronto gets a Live Nation presale Nov. 18 ahead of a Nov. 20 general sale.
- In support of Segall's August-released album "Chrome."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Ty-Segall-2027.webp)

Garage rock musician Ty Segall has announced a 2027 North American tour in support of his latest album, *Chrome*, bringing his full band to venues across the United States and Canada next spring.

Segall will be joined by longtime collaborators Ben Boye, Evan Burrows, Mikal Cronin and Emmett Kelly for the tour, which is slated to open April 13 at Anchor Rock Club in Atlantic City, New Jersey. The newly announced itinerary features 17 shows, including eight Canadian performances, before concluding June 6 at the Palace Theatre in Calgary, Alberta.

The announcement follows the band’s first run of South American concerts and comes several months after the August release of *Chrome*. Segall also has two previously announced performances scheduled for Jan. 29 and 30 at The Chapel in San Francisco.

The April leg will focus on the northeastern United States, with stops in Connecticut, Maine, Rhode Island, Vermont, New York and Pennsylvania. Following a monthlong break, the tour resumes May 25 in Moncton, New Brunswick, before traveling through Quebec City, Montreal and Toronto.

The itinerary also includes U.S. performances in Grand Rapids, Michigan, and Minneapolis before returning to Canada for four final dates in Winnipeg, Saskatoon, Edmonton and Calgary.

Released in August, *Chrome* followed Segall’s 2025 album *Possession* and marks another chapter in the prolific singer-songwriter’s catalog. The album was developed collaboratively with his band, with several tracks featuring songwriting contributions from multiple members.

Tickets for the newly announced dates go on sale Friday, Nov. 20, with most venues beginning sales at 10 a.m. local time. Select performances have earlier presales or different general onsale times, including Toronto, where a Live Nation presale begins Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. ET and the general sale opens Nov. 20 at noon ET. Fans can find ticket information through [Segall’s official website](https://ty-segall.com/pages/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and individual venue ticketing pages.
