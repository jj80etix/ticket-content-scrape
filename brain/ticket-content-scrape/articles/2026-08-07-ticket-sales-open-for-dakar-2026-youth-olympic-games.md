# Ticket sales open for Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games

## Summary

Ticket sales have opened for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games, the first Olympic sporting event held in Africa, running October 31–November 13. Organizers are prioritizing affordability and access, pricing competition tickets from roughly $1.75 and distributing over 300,000 tickets to young people, schools, and community organizations. The Games will feature around 2,700 athletes across 25 competition and 10 engagement sports at eight venues in three host sites.

### Key Points
- Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games run October 31–November 13, 2026, the first Olympic event ever held in Africa.
- Competition tickets start at XOF 1,000 (~$1.75); day passes from XOF 1,500; Opening Ceremony tickets range XOF 5,000–9,000; fan zones are free.
- More than one million tickets are being made available in total.
- Organizers plan to distribute over 300,000 tickets to young people, schools, sports clubs, and community organizations in Senegal.
- The Games will host ~2,700 athletes aged up to 17, across 25 competition sports and 10 engagement sports.
- Events span eight venues across three host sites: Dakar, Diamniadio, and Saly.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Ticket sales for the Dakar 2026 Youth Olympic Games have officially opened, with more than one million set to be available.**

Dakar 2026 is scheduled to take place from October 31 to November 13 in the Senegalese capital and is set to be the first Olympic sporting event held in Africa.

“The opening of ticket sales for Dakar 2026 marks an important milestone in bringing the Games to life,” said Abdou Diaw, Dakar 2026’s executive director of operations.

“Every ticket purchased is an invitation to be part of an historic moment for Senegal and for Africa.

“Dakar 2026 has chosen to offer affordable ticket prices to allow as many people as possible to experience this unique celebration of sport, youth and excellence.”

Organisers are aiming to make the Games as accessible as possible and, as a result, are set to distribute more than 300,000 tickets to young people, schools, sports clubs and community organisations in Senegal.

Competition tickets will start from XOF 1,000 (£1.30/$1.75/€1.50), while day passes, allowing access to multiple competitions on the same day, will be available from XOF 1,500 CFA francs.

Opening Ceremony tickets will be priced at XOF 9,000 in Category A, while Category B tickets will cost XOF 5,000 and fan zones will be free to enter.

Dakar 2026 will welcome around 2,700 athletes aged up to 17 from around the world.

The programme features 25 competition sports and 10 engagement sports, with events and an array of cultural and engagement activities taking place across eight venues in the three host sites of Dakar, Diamniadio, and Saly.
