# Talkin’ Bout The Bowl: Industry & Artists Pay Tribute

## Summary

As part of Pollstar's November 2026 cover story marking the 35th anniversary of promoters Andy Hewitt and Bill Silva's tenure booking the Hollywood Bowl, artists and industry colleagues (including Sting) share tributes and memories of their work at the venue.

### Key Points
- Companion piece to Pollstar's Nov. 2026 cover story on Hewitt/Silva's 35-year Hollywood Bowl booking tenure.
- Includes tributes from artists/colleagues, including Sting, recalling memorable Hollywood Bowl shows.

“Devo playing the iconic Hollywood Bowl in 2025 was a dream 34 years in the making. And it reunited us with Andy Hewitt, introduced to us by Bill Gerber in 1981 when we had tapped Bill to join Elliot Roberts’ Lookout Management to supercharge our artistic aspirations. Together we witnessed Prince shock the dense-packed, young crowd as he dazzled in nothing but a trench coat, bikini undies, a garter belt and high heeled boots. Starting that night a bond developed between Andy, Bill and Devo. Andy as it turned out was an “influencer” 30 years before the term was ever hatched. He had a natural instinct for promoting new music. His parties were legendary, always curated with a spicy mix of old and new Hollywood and a big dollop of “beautiful people”. Recounting those pre-aides fast times now seem apocryphal – a parallel reality to the devolved present. As Devo soared professionally so did Andy and Bill. They enabled the best concerts Devo ever performed. But the Hollywood Bowl, for whatever reason having nothing to do with Andy’s efforts, remained elusive. Whether it was because a theatrical, Multi-media Devo required playing after dark, or touring schedules around the world refused to align with the Bowl’s available dates, or filling 18 thousand seats seemed daunting as Devo drifted apart from main stream culture, Devo at the Bowl never happened – until November 18th and 19th 2025 headlining with the B-52’s. Those two epic, sold out shows were a real homecoming for Devo -full circle. Having joined forces with Bill Silva and Live Nation decades earlier Andy was finally able to lasso DEVO and put us on stage at the BOWL! The incredible after party seemed like a time warp as Bill Gerber, Andy Hewitt and DEVO celebrated with a champagne toast. It tasted like 1981!”
