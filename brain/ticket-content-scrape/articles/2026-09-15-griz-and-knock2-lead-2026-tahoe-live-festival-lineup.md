# GRiZ and Knock2 Lead 2026 Tahoe Live Festival Lineup

## Summary

Tahoe Live's fifth edition returns to Palisades Tahoe Dec. 11-13 with GRiZ, Knock2, and Chris Lake leading a three-day electronic-music lineup on a single stage. Tickets go on sale Sept. 17.

### Key Points
- Single-stage format lets attendees see every artist without overlapping sets.
- T-Pain performs under his electronic alias, Teddy Pain; Madeon, Walker & Royce, and Noizu also on the bill.
- Festival grew from a 2022 two-day event at Boreal/Woodward Tahoe to a sold-out three-day 2025 edition.
- Located at ~6,200 ft in the Sierra Nevada, adjacent to The Village at Palisades Tahoe.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Tahoe-Live-1.webp)

Tahoe Live will return to Palisades Tahoe this winter with GRiZ, Knock2 and Chris Lake leading a three-day electronic music lineup at the California ski resort.

The fifth edition of the festival is scheduled for December 11 to 13 in Olympic Valley. Performances will take place on a single stage at the base of Palisades Tahoe, giving attendees the chance to see every artist without overlapping set times.

This year’s lineup also includes T-Pain performing under his electronic alias, Teddy Pain. French producer Madeon, house duo Walker & Royce and British producer Noizu are among the other established acts scheduled to appear.

Additional performers include BUNS, Cole Knight, Control Freak, Daniel Allan, Devault, DJ UNI, HerShe, Klo, Mary Droppinz, Nala, KHØDA LEON, WINK x Nikko, YDG and Zaddy B. Organizers said more artists will be announced.

Tahoe Live’s location sits about 6,200 feet above sea level in the Sierra Nevada, with the stage positioned near ski lifts and The Village at Palisades Tahoe. Restaurants, bars and lodging are within walking distance of the performance area.

Tahoe Live began in 2022 as a two-day event at Boreal/Woodward Tahoe. Its first lineup featured Steve Aoki, Louis the Child and Whethan. The festival relocated to Palisades Tahoe in 2023, when REZZ and DJ Diesel, the performance name used by Shaquille O’Neal, topped the bill.

Attendance reached about 15,000 over two days in 2024. That edition featured Diplo, RL Grime, Loud Luxury, Disco Lines and Boogie T. The event grew to three days in 2025 and sold out with a lineup led by Zeds Dead, Porter Robinson, Alison Wonderland, LSZEE and DJ Diesel.

Tickets for Tahoe Live 2026 are set to go on sale Thursday, September 17, at 10 a.m. Pacific time through the [festival’s official website](https://tahoelive.net/).
