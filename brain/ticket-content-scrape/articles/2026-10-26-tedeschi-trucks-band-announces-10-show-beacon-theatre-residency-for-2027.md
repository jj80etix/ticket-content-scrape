# Tedeschi Trucks Band Announces 10-Show Beacon Theatre Residency for 2027

## Summary

Tedeschi Trucks Band will return to New York's Beacon Theatre for a 10-show residency in March-April 2027, marking the venue's 75th through 84th performances by the group. Tickets release through a fan-club presale, local presale, and general public sale through Ticketmaster starting Oct. 28-30.

### Key Points
- 10-show residency March 16-April 3, 2027, bringing total Beacon shows to 84.
- Presale tiers: fan club (Oct. 28), local presale (Oct. 29), general public sale via Ticketmaster (Oct. 30).
- Band will also play Sun, Sand, and Soul (FL), Red Rocks (CO), and Big Blues Bender (NV) later in 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Tedeschi-Trucks-Band-Beacon-Theatre.webp)

Tedeschi Trucks Band will return to New York’s Beacon Theatre for a 10-concert residency in 2027, opening March 16 with a milestone: the group’s 75th performance at the venue.

The engagement runs through April 3 and will bring the band’s total number of Beacon performances to 84. Led by singer and guitarist Susan Tedeschi and guitarist Derek Trucks, the group is presenting the residency as a “Love Letter to New York,” extending its longstanding relationship with the theater.

The schedule places eight performances in March and two in April, spreading the engagement across three weeks. Following the March 16 opener, the band will perform March 17 before returning for shows March 19 and March 20.

The next portion begins March 24, followed by performances March 26 and March 27. A March 31 concert closes out the month, with the final two shows scheduled for April 2 and April 3.

Members of the TTB Swamp Family Fan Club will receive the first opportunity to purchase tickets on Wednesday, October 28, at 10 a.m. Eastern.
