# Global Latin Star Feid Announces 2027 North American Arena Tour

## Summary

Latin star Feid announced a 31-date "FERXXO – The Green Star Tour" across North America, promoted by Live Nation, running February 5 through May 21, 2027, and wrapping in Mexico City. Presale begins November 10, with the general onsale on November 12 via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- 31-date tour, Feb. 5, 2027 (Orlando) through May 21, 2027 (Mexico City).
- Stops include Washington D.C., Houston, Sacramento, Phoenix, and Anaheim.
- Presale Nov. 10; general onsale Nov. 12 via Ticketmaster.com; Citi cardmember presale also available.
- Promoted by Live Nation.

Global Latin star Feid is returning to North American arenas next year with his “FERXXO – THE GREEN STAR TOUR,” which promises to bring a “bold new chapter” for the energetic artist, says promoter Live Nation.

The 31-date tour kicks off Feb. 5 at Orlando’s Kia Center, and the routing includes stops at Gas South Arena in Duluth, Capital One Arena in D.C., Hartford’s PeoplesBank Arena, Ball Centre in Montreal, Houston’s Toyota Center, Golden 1 Center in Sacramento, Phoenix’s Mortgage Matchup Center and Honda Center in Anaheim. He wraps up the run May 21 at Mexico City’s Palacio de los Deportes.

Presale tickets will be available Nov. 10 at 9 a.m. local time, followed by the general onsale beginning Nov. 12 at 10 a.m. local time at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/feid-tickets/artist/2826011). Citi cardmembers have access to the presale through the Citi Entertainment program.

The artist’s presale will be done via Ticketmaster’s Artist Sign-up (ASU), and fans can register now through Nov. 9 at 8 p.m. PT. The ASU presale begins Nov. 11 at 10 a.m. local time and ends the same day at 10 p.m.

Presales for the three Mexico concerts begin Nov. 9 at 9 a.m. local time, and Banamex Cardmembers can purchase tickets beginning Nov. 10 at 11 a.m. local time through Nov. 11 at 9 a.m. local time. Those shows will also be utilizing Ticketmaster ASU.

VIP packages and experiences will be available and include premium tickets, exclusive access to the pre-show Greenprint VIP lounge, a specially designed VIP gift item, early entry into the venue and more. Visit [vipnation.com](https://www.vipnation.com/) for more information.

The new arena comes after the Colombian rapper performed intimate shows in theaters this year, including Brooklyn Paramount in New York, where the artist grossed $211,606 off 2,748 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. He also performed a special show at the iconic Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles alongside Marcello Hernandez of “Saturday Night Live” for the Netflix Is A Joke festival.

Feid is booked worldwide by Creative Artists Agency.
