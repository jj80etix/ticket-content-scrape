# WME Signs Rising Pop Singer Hailey Picardi

## Summary

WME signed rising 20-year-old pop singer Hailey Picardi for worldwide representation. Picardi broke out with 2025 debut single "car crash" and the 23-million-stream hit "double edged sword," built an 820,000-follower TikTok following, and recently made her touring debut supporting Bella Kay's "The Reckless Tour."

### Key Points
- WME signs Hailey Picardi for global representation across all areas.
- Breakout hits: "car crash" (2025 debut) and "double edged sword" (23M+ Spotify streams, 700K+ TikTok fan videos).
- 820,000+ TikTok followers; EP "scars to prove it."
- Touring debut as support on Bella Kay's "The Reckless Tour."

## Transcript

![Hailey Picardi photo by Tanner Deutsch](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Hailey-Picardi-photo-by-Tanner-Deutsch-scaled.jpg)

Rising singer Hailey Picardi signed with WME for worldwide representation in all areas, the agency announced today.

The 20-year-old pop singer known for her radical vulnerability broke into the scene with her 2025 debut single “car crash,” introducing audiences to her cinematic storytelling and amassing millions of streams on music platforms. She followed that up with her biggest hit yet, “double edged sword,” which was released in January and has more than 23 million streams on Spotify.

Picardi possesses the ability to transform deeply personal moments into something sincere that listeners can connect with. Her EP *scars to prove it* solidifies the artist’s ability to transform vulnerability into strength and shared understanding.

The young singer’s storytelling ability goes beyond her music. With more than 820,000 TikTok followers, Picardi has built a relationship with her devoted followers with her “big sister” presence. And many creators are using her music on the platform. Her breakout hit “double edged sword” has inspired more than 700,000 fan-created videos on TikTok.

Her success on streaming platforms led Picardi to her touring debut supporting Bella Kay’s “The Reckless Tour,” bringing her emotionally resonant songs to music fans across the country.
