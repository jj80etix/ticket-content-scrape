# D.LIVE unveils dedicated touring exhibition space in Düsseldorf

## Summary

German venue operator D.LIVE is opening Story Hall, a dedicated touring-exhibition space in Düsseldorf, launching in December with "Titanic: An Immersive Journey." It becomes D.LIVE's 10th venue and is pitched as closing an infrastructure gap for the growing touring-exhibition segment.

### Key Points
- Story Hall opens December 2026 with Titanic: Eine immersive Reise as its debut exhibition.
- 10th venue in D.LIVE's Düsseldorf portfolio (alongside Merkur Spiel-Arena, PSD Bank Dome, Open Air Park Düsseldorf).
- CEO Michael Brill frames it as filling a gap: the city previously couldn't accommodate touring-exhibition enquiries.
- Positioned alongside other recent dedicated exhibition spaces like Belgium's Terminal One.

German venue operator D.LIVE has unveiled a touring exhibition space in Düsseldorf, poised to help bridge the infrastructure gap for the content type.

Story Hall will open in December with *Titanic: Eine immersive Reise (Titanic: An Immersive Journey),* the first of a changing programme of national and international exhibitions.

It will be the 10th venue in D.LIVE’s portfolio, which also includes [Merkur Spiel-Arena](https://www.iqmagazine.com/featured-list/stadium-stars-2026/merkur-spiel-arena-de/), PSD Bank Dome, and Open Air Park Düsseldorf, all of which are also in Düsseldorf.

Michael Brill, D.LIVE CEO, has called the venue’s opening a “new chapter” for both the company and the city.

“We spent a long time looking for a suitable hall to bring the growing touring exhibition segment to our city. Until now, we have been unable to accommodate many enquiries – now that is changing,” Brill says.

“Touring exhibitions bring major topics to audiences in an engaging, entertaining and experience-led way. I would like to thank the City of Düsseldorf for its trust and support, as well as COFO and CTP for their collaborative partnership,” he adds.

Infrastructure is a key hurdle for touring entertainment content, though recent years have seen the development of dedicated spaces like Belgium’s [Terminal One](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/10/fkp-scorpio-brings-belgian-immersive-complex-to-life/) and Germany’s [OBEX](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/fkpes-oberhausen-venue-obex-hails-successful-first-year/). With Story Hall, D.LIVE is looking to capitalise on the growth of the sector.

> “Düsseldorf is gaining an attractive new cultural offering while also opening up a new area of events”

“With Story Hall, Düsseldorf is gaining an attractive new cultural offering while also opening up a new area of events: touring exhibitions,” says Miriam Koch, Deputy Mayor for Culture, Integration and Health of the City of Düsseldorf.

The venue was redeveloped from an industrial site, which discontinued steel tube production in 2023. Over the past year, D.LIVE has refurbished the training workshop for its new purpose.

“The hall is another asset for our city and strengthens Düsseldorf’s position as a destination for international exhibition experiences,” Koch adds. “The project supports the development of the Rath district and shows how former industrial sites can be given a new lease of life through new uses.”

Organisers of a one-day event dedicated to the sector, Touring Entertainment Live, recently confirmed its return for 2027. Find information [here](https://tel.ilmc.com/), or read the [Touring Entertainment Report](https://www.iqmagazine.com/reports/touring-entertainment-report-2026/).
