# Backstreet Boys To Ring In 2027 With 3 New Sphere Dates

## Summary

Backstreet Boys added three New Year's Sphere shows (Dec. 31-Jan. 2) to their "Into the Millennium" residency, with Vibee-branded VIP hotel packages and fan-club/artist presales running Aug. 18-19 ahead of an Aug. 21 general onsale.

### Key Points
- Vibee packages include Venetian Resort stays, tiered Sphere seating, and the returning "Backstreet Boys Terminal" fan activation.
- The residency's average gross per Pollstar Boxoffice is $2.85 million on 16,669 tickets.

With demand continuing to soar, Backstreet Boys have added a set of New Year’s weekend performances of “Into The Millennium” at Sphere.

The Live Nation-produced shows are set for Dec. 31 and Jan. 1 and 2.

Tickets for the newly added dates will first be available via the Backstreet Boys Fan Club Presale beginning August 18 at 10am PT. The Artist Presale will follow August 19 at 10am PT. The general onsale will begin August 21 at 10am PT at backstreetboys.com.

Vibee is the official Concert & VIP Hotel Experience Package partner for Backstreet Boys’ newly announced dates Vibee packages go on sale August 12 at 10 a.m. PT. All Vibee packages include a choice of GA floor or reserved seating at Sphere, a two-night stay at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas and a collectible BSB laminate and lanyard, with a special three-night stay option available for the New Year’s Eve show. Vibee VIP package holders also have priority entry and exclusive lounge space at the returning ‘Backstreet Boys Terminal’ fan experience at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas, premium reserved seating and priority entry into Sphere, luxury motorcoach transportation to and from Harry Reid International Airport, an exclusive BSB curated gifting kit, Vibee’s on-site concierge service and more. For more information and early access to tickets through Vibee’s packages, please visit backstreetboys.vibee.com.

The newest iteration of the immersive fan activation, BSB Terminal, returns to The Venetian Resort in Las Vegas in conjunction with the winter performances.

The average gross reported to Pollstar’s Boxoffice during the Sphere run is $2.85 million on 16,669 tickets.
