# Apple Music to open first live venue in London

## Summary

Apple Music is opening its first publicly accessible permanent venue, a 600-capacity space called Apple Music Hall inside London's Battersea Power Station, extending the streaming giant's push into live music beyond one-off events.

### Key Points
- Venue is positioned around close artist-to-audience intimacy and heightened production control.
- Marks Apple's first standalone public venue after years of hosting live events elsewhere.
- Part of a broader pattern of streaming/tech companies moving into physical live entertainment.

### Highlights
- 600-cap venue inside Battersea Power Station, London.
- First permanent, publicly accessible Apple Music-branded venue.
- Framed by Apple as closing the gap between intimacy and production value.

### Industry Problem
Tech and streaming platforms are moving downstream into venue ownership and live production, blurring the line between content distributor and live-event operator and creating entry points that bypass traditional promoter/venue relationships.

### Proposed Solution
The article doesn't detail a ticketing angle. For Etix, the signal to watch is whether Apple Music Hall (and similar tech-owned venues) builds or buys its own ticketing/box-office stack — a new category of potential client or competitor at the venue-ownership layer.
