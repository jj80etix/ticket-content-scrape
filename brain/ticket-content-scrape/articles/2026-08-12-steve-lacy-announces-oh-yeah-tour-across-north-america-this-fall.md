# Steve Lacy Announces ‘Oh Yeah?’ Tour Across North America This Fall

## Summary

Steve Lacy announced his "Oh Yeah? Tour," a 27-city North American run supporting his new self-produced album Oh Yeah? (out July 17, featuring SZA and Erykah Badu), running Sept. 27 in Minneapolis through Nov. 30 in Vancouver. It's his first headline tour since 2022's "Give You The World Tour"; tickets go on sale Aug. 14.

### Key Points
- 27-city North American tour, Sept. 27 (Minneapolis) through Nov. 30 (Vancouver), tickets on sale Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. local.
- Supports Oh Yeah?, Lacy's self-produced follow-up to 2022's Gemini Rights, featuring SZA, Erykah Badu, and Cecile Believe.
- First headline run since 2022's "Give You The World Tour," which averaged $326K-$366K per Australian show per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Stops include Radio City Music Hall, the Hollywood Bowl, and Red Rocks Amphitheater.

Steve Lacy has prepared a tour to support his latest album with the “Oh yeah? Tour,” which will run through North America beginning on Sept. 27 at the Armory in Minneapolis. From there, the eccentric musician will hit 26 more cities, with stops in Toronto, Miami, Los Angeles and more before wrapping up in Vancouver at Doug Mitchell Thunderbird Sports Center on Nov. 30. See full tour routing below. Tickets will be available starting on Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. local time [here](https://www.wearesteve.net/).

The “Oh yeah? Tour” marks Lacy’s first run of headline shows since his 2022 sold-out “Give You The World Tour” across North America. According to [Pollstar Boxoffice reports,](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/750613) the “Give You The World Tour,” which also had an Australia leg, made stops at Hisense Arena in Melbourne, Australia on Nov. 22, 2022 and Brisbane Riverstage in Brisbane, Australia on Nov. 23, amassing an average of $326,549 & $365,562, respectively.

Steve Lacy released his highly anticipated album, *Oh yeah?* on July 17, four years after his critically acclaimed *Gemini Rights*. *Oh yeah? i* s entirely self-produced; the 10-track album features collaborations with SZA, Erykah Badu, and Cecile Believe. It’s also paired with carefully engineered visuals, like the “doom” music video, which seamlessly pairs with the album’s themes, identity, and sonic foundation.
