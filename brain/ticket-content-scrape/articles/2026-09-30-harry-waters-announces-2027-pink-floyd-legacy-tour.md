# Harry Waters Announces 2027 Pink Floyd ‘Legacy’ Tour

## Summary

Harry Waters will front "Roger Waters Presents LEGACY – A Pink Floyd Show," a new production directed by his father Roger Waters, launching a 2027 North American tour (100+ cities planned across North America and Europe) presented by AEG Presents and ICON LIVE.

### Key Points
- Tour begins Feb. 15, 2027 in Columbus, GA; opening stretch covers Florida, then Atlanta, Montgomery, Nashville, Huntsville.
- March leg covers Minneapolis, Chicago, Indianapolis, Louisville and Texas markets before the Northeast/Mid-Atlantic.
- Organizers plan 100+ cities across North America and Europe; European dates TBA.
- Presented by AEG Presents and ICON LIVE.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Pink-Floyd-‘Legacy-Tour.webp)

Harry Waters will bring Pink Floyd’s music to stages across North America in 2027 with “Roger Waters Presents LEGACY – A Pink Floyd Show,” a new production developed under the direction of his father.

Presented by AEG Presents and ICON LIVE, the tour begins February 15 in Columbus, Georgia. Organizers say the production will eventually visit more than 100 cities across North America and Europe, with European dates still to be announced.

The opening stretch includes Florida performances in Clearwater, Fort Myers, St. Augustine, Fort Lauderdale and Orlando before heading to Atlanta, Montgomery, Nashville and Huntsville.

March stops include Minneapolis, Chicago, Indianapolis, Louisville and several Texas markets. The spring itinerary continues through the Northeast and Mid-Atlantic, with appearances in Pittsburgh, Newark, Atlantic City, Baltimore and Rochester before reaching Wilmington, North Carolina, on May 2.

The North American schedule resumes August 31 in Vancouver. September dates include San Francisco, Los Angeles, San Diego, Las Vegas and Denver, with the currently listed run ending September 25 in St. Louis.

Harry Waters will serve as musical director, performing on B3 organ, piano and vocals alongside a 10-piece band. The production focuses on Pink Floyd’s music from 1971 through 1979, including selections from “The Dark Side of the Moon,” “Wish You Were Here,” “Animals” and “The Wall.”

The concerts will run more than two hours, combining performances with high-definition LED screens displaying imagery drawn from the band’s visual history. Roger Waters’ team shaped the presentation, which includes visuals he created during his years with Pink Floyd.

“These songs have meant so much to so many people for so many years,” Roger Waters said in the announcement.
