# Chris Stapleton to Headline Buckeye Country Superfest 2027

## Summary

Chris Stapleton will headline the 2027 Buckeye Country Superfest at Ohio Stadium in Columbus on July 10, joined by Cody Johnson, Parker McCollum and three additional acts. Registration for a presale beginning September 29 is open now, with an American Express cardmember presale running the same week ahead of a general onsale.

### Key Points
- Six-act lineup: Stapleton, Cody Johnson, Parker McCollum, Buffalo Traffic Jam, Blake Whiten, Laci Kaye Booth.
- Event returns to Ohio Stadium, Columbus, on Saturday, July 10, 2027.
- Registered-fan presale opens Sept. 29, 10 a.m. ET; Amex cardmember presale runs Sept. 29–Oct. 1.
- All presales close October 1 at 10 p.m. ET; no set times announced yet.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Buckeye-Country-Superfest.webp)

Chris Stapleton will headline Buckeye Country Superfest when the one-day country music event returns to Ohio Stadium in Columbus on Saturday, July 10, 2027.

Cody Johnson and Parker McCollum are also on the bill, along with Americana and indie-folk duo Buffalo Traffic Jam, South Carolina country-rock artist Blake Whiten and country-pop singer Laci Kaye Booth.

The announcement brings Stapleton, a 12-time Grammy Award winner, to the top of a six-act lineup. Johnson and McCollum add two more established country names to the event, while Buffalo Traffic Jam, Whiten and Booth round out the day’s performances. Organizers have not announced set times.

Fans can register now through [Buckeye Country Superfest’s website](https://www.buckeyecountrysuperfest.com/) for access to a presale beginning Tuesday, September 29, at 10 a.m. Eastern. Registered fans will receive a unique code and purchase link before the presale starts.

American Express card members will also have early access from September 29 at 10 a.m. Eastern through Thursday, October 1, at 10 p.m. Eastern. All presales end October 1 at 10 p.m. Eastern.
