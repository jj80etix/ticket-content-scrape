# Sugarland Announces The Ride Or Die Tour This Fall

## Summary

Sugarland has announced "The Ride or Die Tour," a 26-city US run celebrating the 20th anniversary of their album *Enjoy the Ride*, kicking off October 1 in St. Augustine, Florida and wrapping November 21 in Dallas.

### Key Points
- Tour marks the 20th anniversary of *Enjoy the Ride*, which included hits "Stay," "Settlin'," and "Baby Girl."
- 26 dates run Oct. 1 – Nov. 21 across mostly mid-size US venues.
- Duo Jennifer Nettles and Kristian Bush have also pursued solo projects, including Nettles' off-Broadway musical *Giulia: The Poison Queen of Palermo*.

Grammy-winning duo Sugarland announced “The Ride or Die Tour,” a 26-city U.S. run celebrating the 20th anniversary of *Enjoy the Ride*, the album that cemented Jennifer Nettles and Kristian Bush as one of music’s most admired duos. The tour kicks off Oct. 1 in St. Augustine, Florida, and wraps Nov. 21 in Dallas, Texas.

*“Enjoy the Ride* was such a turning point album,” stated Nettles in the announcement. “It literally catapulted us onto the ride of our lives with a carnival of hits and a life on The Road. We couldn’t let its anniversary pass without marking it as something special with our fans, live and on tour.”

“There is truly nothing more satisfying than the lights going up on the Sugarland stage and feeling our songs rumble out of the speakers,” added Bush. “I’m excited just thinking about it and even more thrilled to be celebrating the anniversary of *Enjoy The Ride*.”

Since the band launched in 2002, Sugarland has sold nearly 10 million albums, earned seven No. 1 singles and reached nearly 2 billion streams. Some of their most popular hits include the Platinum+-certified “All I Want to Do,” “Stay” and “Stuck Like Glue” as well as Gold-certified “Baby Girl,” “It Happens,” “Settlin’,” “Something More” and “Want To.”

Industry acclaim includes five consecutive CMA Awards for Vocal Duo of the Year (2007- 2011), five ACM Awards (including Song of the Year and Single of the Year) and Grammy Awards for Best Country Performance by a Duo or Group with Vocals and Best Country Song for “Stay.”

Both Nettles and Bush have experienced success with their solo work, as well. Recently, Nettles wrote the book, lyrics and score for the new off-Broadway musical *Giulia: The Poison Queen of Palermo.* Nettles also starred as the title character. The show premiered July 10 at the Perelman Performing Arts Center (PAC NYC) at the World Trade Center in New York, directed by Tony winner Mary Zimmerman.

Bush co-founded the folk-rock duo Billy Pilgrim with Andrew Hyra, making their Grand Ole Opry debut in 2024. He’s one of Nashville’s most in-demand creative collaborators. As a producer, he has earned multiple CMA and ACM award nominations as well as topping *Billboard* ”s country producers chart producing projects for Megan Moroney, Lanie Gardner, K. Michelle and Runaway June.
