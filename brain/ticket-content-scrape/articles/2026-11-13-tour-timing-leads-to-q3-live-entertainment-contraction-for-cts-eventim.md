# Tour timing leads to Q3 live entertainment contraction for CTS Eventim

## Summary

A companion IQ report on CTS Eventim's results notes that despite nine-month revenue and profit growth, the third quarter contracted because major tours and events fell outside the period compared with a high-revenue Q3 2025. (Full financial detail is paywalled; see the companion Pollstar report for the complete nine-month and Q3 figures.)

### Key Points
- Confirms Q3 2026 live-entertainment contraction was a tour-timing effect, not a demand decline.
- Full nine-month results showed revenue and profit growth for 2026 overall.
- CEO Klaus-Peter Schulenberg quoted on the company's growth strategy.

### Highlights
- Q3 contraction attributed explicitly to tour-timing, with full-year growth intact — corroborates the fuller Pollstar report on the same results.

### Industry Problem
Same tour-timing revenue volatility noted for this quarter's results across peer ticketing/event-management companies (see companion Q3 Eventim summary).

### Proposed Solution
Monitor only; no new information beyond the fuller report filed the same day.
