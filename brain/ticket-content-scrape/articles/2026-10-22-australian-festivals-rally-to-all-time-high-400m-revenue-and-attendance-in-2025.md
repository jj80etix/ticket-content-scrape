# Australian festivals rally to all-time high $400m revenue and attendance in 2025

## Summary

Australia's contemporary music festival sector hit a record ~A$400M in revenue and over 2 million attendees in 2025, per industry body Live Performance Australia.

### Key Points
- Revenue grew over 20% and attendance nearly 5% year-on-year.
- Average ticket price rose 14.1% to a record A$206.17.
- Figures counter a narrative of an Australian festival sector in crisis.
- Article is paywalled past the opening stats; full report breakdown not available in the excerpt.
