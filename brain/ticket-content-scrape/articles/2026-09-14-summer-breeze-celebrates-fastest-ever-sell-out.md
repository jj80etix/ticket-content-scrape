# Summer Breeze celebrates ‘fastest ever’ sell-out

## Summary

Germany's Summer Breeze metal festival sold out its 2027 30th-anniversary edition faster than any prior year, with 75% of tickets moving within 24 hours of the early-bird onsale opening — just weeks after the 2026 edition wrapped on August 15.

### Key Points
- 2027 marks Summer Breeze's 30th anniversary, held in Dinkelsbühl, Bavaria.
- 75% of tickets sold within 24 hours of the early-bird onsale opening.
- Sell-out came less than two weeks after the 2026 edition ended.
- Organizers called it a "historic moment" for the festival.
