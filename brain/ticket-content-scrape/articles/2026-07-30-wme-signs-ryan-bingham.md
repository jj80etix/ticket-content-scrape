# WME Signs Ryan Bingham

## Summary

Singer-songwriter Ryan Bingham has signed with WME for worldwide music representation, while continuing film/TV representation with Jack Whigham at Range Media Partners. Bingham, an Academy Award, Golden Globe, Grammy, and Critics' Choice Award winner for "The Weary Kind" (from "Crazy Heart") and a recurring cast member on "Yellowstone," is touring behind his album with The Texas Gentlemen, "They Call Us The Lucky Ones."

### Key Points
- WME now represents Bingham worldwide for music; Range Media Partners' Jack Whigham continues to handle film/TV.
- Pollstar Boxoffice reporting shows a June 13 Lincoln Theatre (Washington, D.C.) date sold 1,124 tickets and grossed $55,638 as part of his "Still Gettin' Away With It Tour '26."
- Bingham is currently supporting Zach Bryan's stadium tour, including two nights at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego (July 31–Aug. 1).
- He returns to headlining his own shows in October.

Ryan Bingham’s latest career move is singing with WME for worldwide representation in music. The award-winning singer, songwriter, guitarist and actor has been supporting his first full-length with his backing band The Texas Gentlemen, *They Call Us The Lucky Ones*.

Bingham will continue to be represented by Jack Whigham at Range Media Partners for film and television.

The artist got his start with his critically acclaimed debut studio album, *Mescalito*, in 2007 and three years later he won an Academy Award, a Golden Globe, a Grammy Award and a Critics’ Choice Award for his song “The Weary Kind” from the film “Crazy Heart.” He was also named Artist of the Year by the Americana Music Association in 2010.

His original music can also be heard in the Paramount TV show “Yellowstone,” which features Bingham in the recurring role as the ex-con and singer Walker.

Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Bingham include a June 13 show at Lincoln Theatre in Washington, D.C., that sold 1,124 tickets and grossed $55,638. The stop was part of his headline trek, “ Still Gettin’ Away With It Tour ‘26.”

Bingham’s upcoming tour schedule includes a run of gigs supporting Zach Bryan on his stadium tour with stops in Salt Lake City, Denver and more. Up next is two nights joining Bryan at Snapdragon Stadium in San Diego July 31 and Aug. 1. Bingham returns to the road to headline his own shows in October.
