# ‘Small Mouth Sounds’ Moves Up Broadway Dates With Julia Roberts, Paul Rudd and Sarah Paulson

## Summary

Broadway's "Small Mouth Sounds," starring Julia Roberts, Paul Rudd, and Sarah Paulson, has moved up its preview and opening dates to February 10 and March 11, 2027, respectively, at the Ethel Barrymore Theatre. The Bess Wohl play reunites Roberts and Rudd two decades after their first Broadway pairing.

### Key Points
- Previews now begin Feb. 10 (eight days earlier); opening moved up one week to March 11.
- Cast also includes Martin Freeman, Greta Lee, and Isaac Powell, directed by Joe Mantello.
- Marks the play's Broadway debut after an Off-Broadway premiere in 2015.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Broadways-Small-Mouth-Sounds.webp)

“Small Mouth Sounds” will arrive on Broadway sooner than previously announced, with the production starring Julia Roberts, Paul Rudd and Sarah Paulson moving its first performance to February 10, 2027. The Bess Wohl play will officially open March 11 at the Ethel Barrymore Theatre.

The revised schedule advances the start of previews by eight days and opening night by one week. Performances had originally been scheduled to begin February 18, with a March 18 opening. Martin Freeman, Greta Lee and Isaac Powell complete the six-person cast, directed by Joe Mantello.

Wohl’s comedy follows six strangers who arrive at an upstate spiritual retreat hoping to step away from their troubles and make changes in their lives. The retreat requires silence, but its participants struggle to leave their personal conflicts behind. Their attempts at reflection become complicated by friction within the group and unexpected attractions.

Roberts will play Joan, with Rudd as Ned and Paulson as Judy. Freeman takes the role of Jan, Lee plays Alicia and Powell portrays Rodney. The engagement reunites Roberts and Rudd following their appearance together in the 2006 Broadway production of “Three Days of Rain,” which marked Roberts’ Broadway debut.

“Small Mouth Sounds” first premiered Off-Broadway at Ars Nova in 2015. Its Broadway arrival follows Wohl’s success with “Liberation,” which received the 2026 Pulitzer Prize for Drama and Tony Award for Best Play. Mantello also won a 2026 Tony for directing “Death of a Salesman.”

The production’s design team includes Chloe Lamford for scenery, An D’Huys for costumes, Jack Knowles for lighting and Tom Gibbons for sound. Pulitzer Prize-winning composer Caroline Shaw supplies the music, with movement by JA Collective.

Producers Scott Rudin and Barry Diller announced the Broadway production in September. “Small Mouth Sounds” is one of two plays Mantello is directing during the season, alongside Bruce Norris’s “Downstate.”
