# Gram Parsons Tribute Featuring Emmylou Harris, Elvis Costello & More Coming To The Troubadour Ahead Of Rock Hall Induction

## Summary

Ahead of Gram Parsons' posthumous Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction (Nov. 14, Peacock Theater), an all-star tribute concert featuring Emmylou Harris, Elvis Costello, The Black Crowes and others will be held Nov. 11 at the Troubadour in Los Angeles, benefiting the Friendly House Foundation and Backline. Tickets go on sale Oct. 22.

### Key Points
- Gram Parsons tribute concert: Nov. 11, The Troubadour, Los Angeles; benefits the Friendly House Foundation and Backline.
- Lineup includes Emmylou Harris, Elvis Costello, The Black Crowes, Margo Price, The Head and the Heart, Grace Potter, and others.
- Precedes Parsons' posthumous Rock and Roll Hall of Fame induction, Nov. 14 at the Peacock Theater.
- Tickets on sale 10am Pacific, Oct. 22.

Ahead of his long-awaited posthumous induction into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame, Gram Parsons will be feted by an all-star lineup at a tribute show at The Troubadour in Los Angeles.

The Nov. 11 concert will feature performances by Parsons’ collaborator and duet partner Emmylou Harris, Elvis Costello, The Black Crowes, Margo Price, The Head and the Heart, Grace Potter, Jesse Welles, Madison Cunningham, Grahame Lesh, Harper Simon and Jim Lauderdale, with “cosmic accompaniment” by Beachwood Sparks. The concert will benefit the Friendly House Foundation and Backline.

Tickets go on sale at 10 a.m. Pacific Oct. 22.

Parsons will be inducted into the hall of fame Nov. 14 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. Parsons, along with Celia Cruz, Fela Kuti, Queen Latifah and MC Lyte, is an “early influence” inductee. Phil Collins, Billy Idol, Iron Maiden, Joy Division and New Order, Oasis and Sade are the performers inductees. Linda Creed, Arif Mardin, Jimmy Miller and Rick Rubin will be inducted in the musical excellence category. Ed Sullivan is the Ahmet Ertegun Award inductee.
