# Foreigner Sets 2027 New York Homecoming Shows for 50th Anniversary

## Summary

Foreigner announced four New York/New Jersey shows in spring 2027 — Beacon Theatre, The Wellmont Theater, The Capitol Theatre, and Westbury Music Fair — as part of its continued 50th-anniversary celebration, marking the band's return to the city where founder Mick Jones formed it in 1976.

### Key Points
- Run begins April 27, 2027 at the Beacon Theatre and closes May 2 at Westbury Music Fair.
- Anniversary marks 50 years since Foreigner's 1977 self-titled debut album.
- General public onsale is September 3; presales begin September 1-2 depending on venue/offer.
- Follows Foreigner's 2026 "Double Trouble Double Vision Tour" co-headline run with Lynyrd Skynyrd.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Foreigner-50th-anniversary.webp)

Foreigner will bring its 50th anniversary celebration back to the New York area in spring 2027, announcing four shows across New York and New Jersey as the band marks five decades since the release of its self-titled debut album.

The run begins April 27 at the Beacon Theatre in New York City, followed by an April 28 performance at The Wellmont Theater in Montclair, New Jersey. Foreigner will then play The Capitol Theatre in Port Chester, New York, on April 30 before closing the four-show stretch May 2 at Flagstar at Westbury Music Fair in Westbury, New York.

The New York-area dates carry added significance for the anniversary celebration. Founder Mick Jones formed Foreigner in New York in 1976, with the band’s self-titled debut arriving the following year on March 8, 1977.

“New York is where Foreigner began, so there could be no better place to celebrate 50 years of our music,” Jones said in announcing the shows.

Foreigner has spent much of 2026 celebrating the 50th anniversary of the band’s formation, beginning with a pair of acoustic performances in Beaver Creek, Colorado, in February. The group subsequently scheduled an extensive slate of concerts in North America and Europe, with the celebration continuing into 2027 to coincide with the debut album’s anniversary.

The band’s 2026 itinerary also included the “Double Trouble Double Vision Tour” with Lynyrd Skynyrd, a co-headlining amphitheater run that traveled across the United States during July and August.

The newly announced shows extend the anniversary schedule into a concentrated run of theater dates around the market where Foreigner originated.

Presales for selected shows begin September 1 and September 2, depending on the venue and offer. Tickets for all four dates go on sale to the general public September 3 at 10 a.m. local time.
