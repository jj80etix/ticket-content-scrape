# Brandy and Monica Announce 10-Show Las Vegas Residency for 2027

## Summary

Brandy and Monica announced a 10-show Las Vegas residency, "The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency," beginning May 29, 2027 at Resorts World Theatre. The engagement marks Black Promoters Collective's first-ever residency, co-produced with AEG Presents Las Vegas.

### Key Points
- Follows their prior co-headlining "The Boy Is Mine Tour," whose audience response prompted the residency
- Co-produced by Black Promoters Collective (its first residency) and AEG Presents Las Vegas
- General onsale begins November 20, 10am Pacific, with several presales ahead of it
- No setlist or further production details announced yet

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Brandy-and-Monica-The-Boy-Is-Mine.webp)

Brandy and Monica have announced a 10-show Las Vegas residency, set for 2027.

“The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency” is scheduled to begin May 29 at Resorts World Theatre at Resorts World Las Vegas. Black Promoters Collective and AEG Presents Las Vegas will co-produce the engagement, which marks Black Promoters Collective’s first residency.

The residency will feature music from both singers’ individual catalogs alongside material reflecting their shared musical history. Organizers have not announced a setlist or additional production details.

The engagement follows “The Boy Is Mine Tour,” which brought Brandy and Monica together as co-headliners. Black Promoters Collective President Shelby Joyner said the audience response to that run helped inform the decision to continue working with the singers.

“BPC is proud to be working with Brandy and Monica again. We saw on The Boy Is Mine tour how much it meant to fans to see them share a stage, and I’m excited to build on what we created together,” Joyner said.

“Bringing The Boy Is Mine to Las Vegas as Black Promoters Collective’s first residency is a big moment for us,” Joyner continued. “We’ve built BPC by betting on artists we believe in and listening to their fans.”

“This residency takes that work into a new arena for our company, and I can’t think of two artists I’d rather take that step with.”

General ticket sales for “The Boy Is Mine Vegas Residency” begin November 20 at 10 a.m. Pacific time, with several presales scheduled ahead of the public sale.
