# Goldenvoice’s Ocean Way Festival washed out ahead of launch

## Summary

Goldenvoice and the City of Santa Monica cancelled the debut Ocean Way Festival, set for Sept. 26-27 and headlined by The Killers and Olivia Dean, after Hurricane Marie caused severe beach erosion that made the site unsafe just two weeks before showtime. Ticketholders will receive automatic full refunds, and both partners have committed to relaunching the festival in 2027.

### Key Points
- Cancellation announced Sept. 11, two weeks ahead of the scheduled dates.
- GA two-day passes started at $399; premium pit passes started at $794.
- Refunds are automatic and require no claim from ticketholders.
- Festival was slated as the first major beach festival in Santa Monica, part of AEG/Goldenvoice's portfolio (Coachella, Stagecoach, and others).
- Both the city and Goldenvoice confirmed a 2027 relaunch.

### Highlights
- Automatic full refunds issued to holders of a ~35,000-capacity event with no claim process required.
- Cancellation came just two weeks before the event, driven by force-majeure weather damage rather than low demand.

### Industry Problem
Late-breaking, weather-driven cancellations leave large numbers of patrons needing fast, friction-free refunds precisely when trust is most at risk — and how a platform handles that moment shapes goodwill ahead of a planned relaunch.

### Proposed Solution
Goldenvoice's automatic, no-claim refund model is a clean benchmark; Etix's cancellation workflow should support one-click, fully-automated bulk refunds plus proactive patron notification so organizers can move at the same speed during a crisis.

## Goldenvoice’s Ocean Way Festival washed out ahead of launch

Olivia Dean and The Killers were due to headline the debut edition, which was cancelled due to the site's post-hurricane deterioration

![Santa Monica Pier](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Santa-Monica-Pier-©-John-Allen-Ampong.png)

Santa Monica Pier © John Allen Ampong

Goldenvoice and the City of Santa Monica have together cancelled the debut edition of California’s Ocean Way Festival with two weeks to go, after “a large portion of the festival site” deteriorated in the aftermath of a recent hurricane.

Set for 26-27 September, the [two-day, dual-stage](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/the-killers-olivia-dean-to-lead-new-goldenvoice-festival/) event was due to feature the likes of The Killers, Olivia Dean, Jack White, Khruangbin, Sublime, bringing up to 35,000 people for what was billed as the [first major festival](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/12/goldenvoice-to-launch-new-santa-monica-festival/) on the Los Angeles coastal city’s beach.

But the recent Hurricane Marie in the Pacific Ocean led to sea swells slamming into the California coastline, causing “significant beach erosion” from both the surf and runoff from a storm drain that made the site unsuitable for the festival to go ahead, organisers said Friday (11 September). The adjacent Santa Monica Pier was not damaged.

Of the decision, Mayor Caroline Torosis said: “We’ve spent more than a year on this festival, and I’ve spent most of it telling anyone who would listen that music was coming back to this beach. I wanted to stand on the sand, see the Pier lit up behind that stage, and sing along with our community \[…\] But the surf took the beach we planned this on, and we cannot put tens of thousands of festival-goers on that sand until it’s safe.”

Goldenvoice’s COO Melissa Ormond added: “While we share everyone’s disappointment that the show can’t go on this year, we fully support the City’s decision to prioritise safety, and we believe cancelling for 2026 is the right call.”

Both parties confirmed plans to stage Ocean Way Festival in Santa Monica next year.

“What didn’t wash away is the partnership,” Torosis said. “Goldenvoice has been a responsible partner from day one, and that work continues. Next year, the show goes on.”

Ticketholders are to recieve full refunds automatically. Two-day general admission passes started at $399 (€350), with premium pit passes were priced from $794 (€654).

Developed as a partnership between the City of Santa Monica and Goldenvoice, Ocean Way was to be the latest piece of the AEG subsidiary’s festival portfolio, which also includes Coachella, Stagecoach, Head in the Clouds, Portola, and Camp Flog Gnaw.

The debut edition was also set to welcome Duran Jones & The Indications, Alvvays, Hiatus Kaiyota, SG Lewis, 54 Ultra, BLOND:ISH, Poolside, Hot Chip (DJs), DJ Harvey, Carlia, Good Neighbours, and more.

Further down the coast, Laguna Beach, Long Beach, and Dana Point all declared local [emergencies](https://www.accuweather.com/en/hurricane/will-2026-go-down-with-no-atlantic-hurricanes/1932308) due to the threat to local homes and infrastructure because of the compromised seawall and receeding sand.
