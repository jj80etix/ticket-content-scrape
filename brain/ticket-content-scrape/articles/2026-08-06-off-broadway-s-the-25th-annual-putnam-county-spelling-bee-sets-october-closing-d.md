# Off-Broadway’s ‘The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee’ Sets October Closing Date

## Summary

The Off-Broadway revival of "The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee" will close at New World Stages on October 11, 2026, after a third and final extension since opening November 16, 2025. The show is undergoing several cast changes in its final weeks, including internet comedian Julian Sewell (Paloma Diamond) joining as William Barfée, Tony winner Alex Newell taking over as Rona Lisa Peretti, and other replacements across the ensemble.

### Key Points
- Final closing date set for October 11, 2026, at New World Stages, following a third extension of the run that began November 16, 2025.
- New cast members joining before closing: Julian Sewell (Paloma Diamond) as William Barfée (from August 18), Alex Newell as Rona Lisa Peretti (from August 7), Nina White as Olive Ostrovsky (from August 10), and Laura Marano as Logainne Schwartzandgrubenierre.
- Remaining original/current cast includes Jon Cryer, Justin Cooley, Philippe Arroyo, Leana Rae Concepcion, and Matt Manuel.
- The musical, with a Tony Award-winning book by Rachel Sheinkin and music/lyrics by William Finn, won two Tony Awards after its 2005 Broadway transfer.
- Produced by Barbara Whitman, Aaron Glick, and Timothy Bloom, with a large co-producer roster including James L. Nederlander.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Spelling-Bee.webp)

The Off-Broadway revival of “The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee” will end its run at New World Stages on October 11. Producers have announced a third and final extension for the musical, which opened on November 16, 2025.

The production will also welcome a new cast member during its final weeks. Internet comedian Julian Sewell, also known as Paloma Diamond, will join the company as William Barfée beginning August 18. Sewell succeeds Kevin McHale, whose final performance in the role is scheduled for August 16.

Sewell’s arrival is one of several cast changes taking place this summer. Laura Marano recently joined the production as Logainne Schwartzandgrubenierre. Tony Award winner Alex Newell will begin performances as Rona Lisa Peretti on August 7, replacing Lilli Cooper, while Nina White will take over the role of Olive Ostrovsky from Jasmine Amy Rogers beginning August 10.

The current cast also includes Jon Cryer as Vice Principal Douglas Panch, Justin Cooley as Leaf Coneybear, Philippe Arroyo as Chip Tolentino, Leana Rae Concepcion as Marcy Park, and Matt Manuel as Mitch Mahoney.

“The 25th Annual Putnam County Spelling Bee” features a Tony Award-winning book by Rachel Sheinkin, adapted from a play by Rebecca Feldman, with music and lyrics by the late William Finn. The musical follows a group of middle school students competing in a spelling bee, with audience members invited to participate in each performance.

The show premiered Off-Broadway before transferring to Broadway in 2005. It received a Tony Award nomination for Best Musical and won two Tony Awards, including Best Book of a Musical for Sheinkin and Best Featured Actor in a Musical for original cast member Dan Fogler.
