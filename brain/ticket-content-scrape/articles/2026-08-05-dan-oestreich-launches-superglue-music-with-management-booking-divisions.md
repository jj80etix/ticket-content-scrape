# Dan Oestreich Launches Superglue Music With Management & Booking Divisions

## Summary

Dan Oestreich, a veteran of New York's punk and hardcore scene, publicly launched Superglue Music's management and booking divisions, built on 20 years of experience across venue operation, tour management, and artist management. The roster includes management clients like Angel Du$t and Tim Heidecker and booking clients like Gorilla Biscuits and Judge.

### Key Points
- Superglue's management roster includes Angel Du$t, Béton Armé, Dillinger Four, Home Front, Poison Ruin, STERÖID, and Tim Heidecker.
- Booking clients include Béton Armé, Bold, CIV, Cold World, Dark Thoughts, Gorilla Biscuits, Judge, and Straight Ahead.
- Oestreich previously co-owned Brooklyn venue The Acheron, ran Saint Vitus production, and spent five years at Another Management Company working with acts like Snail Mail and Chelsea Wolfe.
- Company was founded in 2025 but formally announced this week, with a small core team including a GM and two booking agents.

## Transcript

![SG Music1](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/SG_Music1.png)

Dan Oestreich, a prominent figure in New York’s punk and hardcore scene, launched booking and touring divisions for his company, Superglue Music, building on his 20 years of experience helping artists build their careers.

The company was established in 2025 but officially announced this week, and Superglue’s roster reflects the depth of Oestreich’s relationships and his vision for the future. The management roster includes Angel Du$t, Béton Armé, Dillinger Four, Home Front, Poison Ruin, STERÖID and Tim Heidecker, and Superglue books shows for Béton Armé, Bold, CIV, Cold World, Dark Thoughts, Gorilla Biscuits, Judge and Straight Ahead.

![Evan Hunter McKnight 01](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Evan-Hunter-McKnight_01-1.jpg)

Superglue Founder Dan Oestreich Photo By Evan Hunter McKnight

“The artists I work with aren’t just clients; they’re people I’ve known, respected and believed in for years,” Oestreich said in a statement. “That’s the foundation of this company. I’ve spent most of my adult life finding ways to support artists and the community that’s given so much to me. Starting Superglue isn’t about doing something different; it’s about putting everything I’ve learned over the last twenty years into helping artists build sustainable careers.”

Oestreich has worked in nearly every role in the independent music world, from venue operator to tour manager to festival production. He has promoted hundreds of shows and was a partner at Brooklyn venue The Acheron, which opened in 2010 and closed six years later. He was also a partner of a van and backline company and Saint Vitus’s production manager from 2018-2023.

With so many roles over the years, it was only natural for Oestreich to gravitate toward management. He worked five years at Another Management Company and worked with indie acts including Mdou Moctar, Snail Mail, Chelsea Wolfe, Tim Heidecker and more. Following that stint, Oestreich launched Superglue Music to focus on the community he helped build over two decades.

The Superglue Music team includes General Manager Holly Cartwright, Greg Benedetto and John Scanlon as booking agents and Jim Carroll as day-to-day manager.
