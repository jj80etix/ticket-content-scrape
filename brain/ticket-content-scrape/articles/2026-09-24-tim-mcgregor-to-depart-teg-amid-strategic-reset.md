# Tim McGregor to depart TEG amid ‘strategic reset’

## Summary

Tim McGregor, TEG's global head of touring, will depart the Australasian live-entertainment giant after 20 years as part of a "strategic reset" led by CEO Cameron Hoy. Mark Dodds will take over as managing director of TEG Live while continuing to lead Handsome Tours, and TEG announced a refreshed promoter leadership team — a month after citing a "refocused growth strategy." The source article is paywalled beyond these opening details.

### Highlights
- McGregor departs after 20 years; Mark Dodds becomes TEG Live managing director, retaining his Handsome Tours managing-partner role.
- Change is framed as part of a broader "strategic reset" under CEO Cameron Hoy, sharpening focus on APAC touring.
- Follows a "refocused growth strategy" TEG cited about a month earlier.
- TEG also announced a refreshed wider promoter leadership team alongside the touring-division change.

### Industry Problem
Leadership churn and repeated strategic resets at a major regional live-entertainment/ticketing group (TEG owns Ticketek) signal organizational instability that can ripple into booking relationships and market focus for a period.

### Proposed Solution
No solution is proposed; this is a peer-company organizational development. Worth monitoring TEG's APAC strategy shift for competitive/market-structure implications.
