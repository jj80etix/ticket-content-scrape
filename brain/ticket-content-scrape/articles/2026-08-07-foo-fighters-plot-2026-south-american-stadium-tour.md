# Foo Fighters Plot 2026 South American Stadium Tour

## Summary

Foo Fighters announced a four-date South American stadium run, the "Take Cover Tour," running Feb. 23–Mar. 3, 2027 across Montevideo, Buenos Aires, Santiago and Lima, with tickets on sale Aug. 13.

### Key Points
- Dates: Feb. 23 Montevideo (Estadio Centenario), Feb. 25 Buenos Aires (River Plate), Feb. 28 Santiago (Parque Estadio Nacional), Mar. 3 Lima (Estadio San Marcos).
- Tickets go on sale Aug. 13 via the band's official website.
- Precedes a Sept. 4, 2026 Rock in Rio appearance.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0800.jpeg)

The rock icons Foo Fighters have announced a massive run of stadium shows across South America next year.

The brief, four-date run, dubbed “Take Cover Tour,” kicks-off on February 23 at Estadio Cenenario in Montevideo. From there, they’ll appear at Buenos Aires’s Estadio River Plate and Parque Estadio Nacional in Santiago before wrapping-up at Lima’s Estadio San Marcos on March 3.

Each date will include support from a handful of artists, varying per date, including Niña Lobo, Gamepad, Usted Señalemelo, Pacifica, Adelaida, Aurora Voraz, Cometa a la Deriva, and Lucy Perez.

Tickets for the newly-announced dates head on sale starting Thursday, August 13 at 10 a.m. local time via the Foo Fighters’ official website.
