# Fever Expands Dutch Operations With E&A Events Partnership

## Summary

Live-entertainment platform Fever has opened an Amsterdam office and struck a strategic partnership with Dutch dance-event promoter E&A Events as it expands its Netherlands operations, appointing Bas Ouwehand as general manager for the Netherlands and Nordic region. The partnership will focus on new ways to connect Fever's reach with E&A's domestic and international dance-event audiences.

### Key Points
- New Fever office at A'DAM Tower in Amsterdam; Bas Ouwehand named GM for Netherlands and Nordic region.
- Partnership with E&A Events (portfolio includes Don't Let Daddy Know, TikTak Events) aims to grow audience reach for E&A's event brands.
- Framed by Fever as leveraging its "global reach" to support local and international promoters.
- Follows a related, separately reported three-year Fever ticketing partnership expansion with X Games.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Fever-EA-Events.jpg)

Fever has opened an office in Amsterdam and reached a strategic partnership with Dutch promoter E&A Events as it expands its operations in the Netherlands.

The office is located in A’DAM Tower and will serve as a local base for the live entertainment technology platform. Fever has also appointed Bas Ouwehand as general manager for the Netherlands and the Nordic region.

“The Netherlands is an important market for Fever, with a highly creative and internationally connected live-entertainment ecosystem,” said Sander Verhof, senior market strategy and partnerships manager at Fever.

“Establishing our office at A’DAM Tower marks a new chapter in our growth, placing us in an iconic building at the heart of Amsterdam’s music scene. We’re really excited about this next phase for the team here, and the opportunities that it will bring.”
