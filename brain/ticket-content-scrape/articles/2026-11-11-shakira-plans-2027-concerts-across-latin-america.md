# Shakira Plans 2027 Concerts Across Latin America

## Summary

Shakira announced six additional 2027 Latin American dates for her "Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour," extending the run into another year. The expansion marks her first Venezuela performance in 16 years and first Guatemala show in roughly 17 years.

### Key Points
- New stops: Venezuela, Brazil, Guatemala, Costa Rica, Puerto Rico and Panama
- Last played Venezuela in 2011 (Caracas) and Guatemala/Puerto Rico similarly around 2011
- Comes weeks after completing a Madrid residency that drew 700,000+ fans across 13 shows
- Tour originally launched in Rio de Janeiro, February 2025

Shakira has announced six Latin American destinations for the 2027 expansion of her “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour.”

The upcoming run will include Venezuela, marking her first performance in the country in 16 years. Shakira last performed in Venezuela on March 27, 2011, during her “Sale el Sol World Tour.” That concert took place at the Universidad Simón Bolívar stadium in Caracas as part of the city’s Pop Festival. Other stops include Brazil, Guatemala, Costa Rica, Puerto Rico and Panama.

The news comes less than a month after Shakira completed her Madrid residency. The additional performances will extend the international tour into another year, following its launch in Brazil in February 2025.

Guatemala is also set to host Shakira after a lengthy gap between visits. The planned performance would be her first in the country in approximately 17 years. Puerto Rico last welcomed the singer during her “Sale el Sol World Tour” in 2011 and is included in the newly announced itinerary.

Brazil, by comparison, has hosted several significant appearances during her current touring cycle. Shakira launched the “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour” in Rio de Janeiro in February 2025. She returned to the city in May 2026 for a free concert at Copacabana Beach.
