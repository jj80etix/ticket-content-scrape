# Liverpool to gain ‘extraordinary’ immersive Beatles experience

## Summary

Apple Corps is quadrupling the size of The Beatles Story, Liverpool's immersive Beatles attraction, to a 100,000-square-foot experience opening next year.

### Key Points
- New Liverpool experience will include recreations of The Cavern, Abbey Road, Shea Stadium, and Savile Row.
- Follows plans for a separate permanent Beatles experience at the real Savile Row location in London, due to open 2027.
