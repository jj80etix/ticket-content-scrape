# Wynonna Judd Announces ‘The Hard Truth Tour’

## Summary

Wynonna Judd has announced "The Hard Truth Tour," a 15-date North American fall run in support of her upcoming solo album *The Hard Truth*, out Oct. 2 via ANTI- Records. The tour follows her recent co-headline run with Melissa Etheridge and runs Oct. 8 through Nov. 21.

### Key Points
- 15 dates from Oct. 8 (Bowery Ballroom, New York) to Nov. 21 (Hard Rock Live Northern Indiana, Gary, IN), including stops in Houston, Tulsa, St. Louis, and Bristol, VA.
- Special guest Troubadour Blue joins the full run.
- Wynation Fan Club presale runs Aug. 4-6 (10 a.m.-10 p.m. local); general on-sale begins Aug. 7 at 10 a.m. local via wynonna.com/tour.
- $1 per ticket sold will be donated to nonprofits via The Only Love Fund, in partnership with the Music Community Foundation.
- New album *The Hard Truth* releases Oct. 2, 2026.

Following her co-headline tour with Melissa Etheridge, Wynonna Judd has unveiled dates for “The Hard Truth Tour” with 15 dates booked across North America this fall in support of her upcoming solo album.

*The Hard Truth* is due out Oct. 2 via ANTI- Records.

“As we were making this record, I kept finding myself thinking about you. Every song we recorded, every story we told… I couldn’t help but wonder what it would feel like to stand onstage and share it with you face to face,” Wynonna said in a statement. “That’s the wonderful thing about music. A song may start in a writing room or a studio, but it doesn’t really come alive until it’s shared. *The Hard Truth* holds some of the most honest moments I’ve ever put to music. It’s about the things that shape us, break us, heal us, and keep us moving forward. Bringing these songs to the stage feels like inviting you into that journey with me.”

An announcement promises that Wynonna’s fall solo tour will bring Wynonna’s “unmistakable voice, powerhouse performances, and deeply personal new music to audiences across North America.” The run starts Oct. 8 with a gig at the Bowery Ballroom in New York and includes stops in Houston, Tulsa, St. Louis, and more, concluding Nov. 21 in Gary, Indiana, at the Hard Rock Live Northern Indiana. The Grammy Award-winning country star will be joined on the road by special guest Troubadour Blue.

A Wynation Fan Club Presale begins Tuesday, Aug. 4, at 10:00 a.m. local time and runs through Aug. 6 at 10 p.m. local time. The General on-sale starts Friday, August 7, at 10:00 a.m. local time at [wynonna.com/tour](http://wynonna.com/tour).

Wynonna Judd is working with the Music Community Foundation to donate $1 per ticket to nonprofits via The Only Love Fund.
