# Luxembourg’s Rockhal grows outdoor series amid major venue ‘reimagining’

## Summary

Luxembourg's Rockhal venue is expanding its Open Air Belval outdoor concert series for a fourth edition in June 2027 while undertaking a four-month "reimagining" of its indoor arena to improve the audience experience, CEO Olivier Toth tells IQ. Confirmed 2027 outdoor acts include Fontaines D.C., Sting, Angèle and Die Fantastischen Vier.

### Key Points
- Open Air Belval returns for its fourth edition since launching in 2018.
- The indoor arena will close for four months in 2027 for renovation work.
- Acts already booked for the outdoor series: Fontaines D.C., Sting, Angèle, Die Fantastischen Vier.
- Further renovation details are behind IQ's paywall.
