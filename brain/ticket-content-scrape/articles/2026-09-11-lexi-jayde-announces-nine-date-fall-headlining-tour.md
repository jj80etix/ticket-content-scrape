# Lexi Jayde Announces Nine-Date Fall Headlining Tour

## Summary

Lexi Jayde announced "The Lover Girl Tour," her first headlining run in four years — nine North American dates running Oct. 18-Nov. 3, supporting her debut album *Lover Girl* (released Aug. 21 via Columbia Records).

### Key Points
- Tour opens Oct. 18 in Philadelphia; closes Nov. 3 in Los Angeles.
- Includes two Canadian dates: Montreal (Oct. 25) and Toronto (Oct. 26).
- Supports debut album *Lover Girl*, featuring "you're still mine" and "bubblebath."
- Tickets and info via Jayde's official website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Lexi-Jayde.webp)

Lexi Jayde will return to the road this fall for “The Lover Girl Tour,” her first headlining run in four years.

The nine-date North American tour will begin October 18 at World Stage in Philadelphia. Jayde will then visit New York, Washington, Boston, Montreal, Toronto and Chicago before heading to California for the final two performances.

The tour concludes with a November 2 show at The Independent in San Francisco and a November 3 performance at the El Rey Theatre in Los Angeles.

“The Lover Girl Tour” supports Jayde’s debut studio album, *Lover Girl*, which was released August 21 through Columbia Records. The album includes “you’re still mine,” “bubblebath,” “i’ve missed you longer” and “misunderstood.”

In addition to the United States dates, the tour includes two Canadian performances. Jayde will appear at Le Ministère in Montreal on October 25 and Toronto’s Mod Club on October 26. The Chicago stop at Chop Shop on October 28 will close the tour’s first stretch before Jayde travels to California.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Jayde’s official website](https://www.lexi-jayde.com/).
