# Venue news: Doors open at Colombia’s newest arena

## Summary

IQ's venue roundup covers four new mid-size venues opening or breaking ground across the Americas and Europe. Colombia's 17,200-cap DAVIarena opened in Medellín with a Juanes homecoming show; a 5,000-cap OCVIBE Concert Hall in Anaheim will open next March with Gwen Stefani; a 4,200-cap Arena Mar del Plata has opened in Argentina; and Live Nation broke ground on a 6,200-cap amphitheatre in Cleveland, Ohio.

### Key Points
- DAVIarena (Medellín, Colombia, 17,200-cap, operator BeatHub Entertainment) opened with Juanes and already has Martin Garrix, Carlos Vives, and Ana Gabriel booked.
- OCVIBE's Concert Hall (Anaheim, CA, 5,000-cap) opens next March with Gwen Stefani, part of a 100-acre mixed-use district around Honda Center due to complete in 2033.
- Arena Mar del Plata (Argentina, 4,200-cap main hall plus an 800-cap secondary space) opened with Los Fabulosos Cadillacs, aimed at drawing tourism-driven bookings.
- Live Nation/Bedrock/Rock Entertainment Group broke ground on a 6,200-cap Cleveland Riverfront Amphitheater, projected at $59m in annual economic impact when it opens by summer 2028 — the city's first major new music venue in 20+ years.

**DAVIarena –** Medellín, Colombia  
*Capacity: 17,200*  
*Operator: BeatHub Entertainment*

Colombia’s newest arena opened its doors last Saturday with a homecoming show by rockstar Juanes, widening the net for tours coming through the Antioquia region.

Located in Sabaneta, in the southern part of the market’s second-largest city, DAVIarena is entering the mid-to-high-capacity multi-purpose venue market with infrastructure built to international standards and the agility of a dedicated entertainment ecosystem.

“DAVIarena arrives to fulfil a specific market need: to offer an indoor, multi-purpose and flexible venue in Antioquia, specifically designed to host large-scale shows under international standards,” Tomás Uribe, the venue’s commercial manager, [previously told *IQ*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/colombias-daviarena-opening-a-message-of-confidence-to-promoters/).

The venue features a robust rigging grid with a load capacity of up to 140 tonnes, adaptable architectural design for clear audio (complete with a L-Acoustics K1 and K2 line array system), a modular rolling stage, and five loading docks.

DAVIarena’s calendar has already started filling up, with shows from acts including Martin Garrix, Carlos Vives, and Ana Gabriel scheduled in the coming months.

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/OCVIBE-Concert-Hall-rendering-736x460.png.webp)

**OCVIBE’s Concert Hall** – Anaheim, California, USA  
*Capacity: 5,000*  
*Operator: OCVIBE*

Gwen Stefani will headline the opening night of the new California concert hall in the OCVIBE development next March.

Designed by Populous, OCVIBE’s Concert Hall will be a state-of-the-art venue located in the 100-acre mixed-use district surrounding Honda Center, projected to turn the area into a year-round accessible destination.

Purpose-built to accommodate a diverse range of programming, including concerts, comedy, special events and programming, the new venue offers world-class experiences for both artists and audiences.

“Having Gwen Stefani come home to Anaheim to open OCVIBE’s Concert Hall is an incredibly special moment,” says Bill Foltz, OCVIBE CEO. “Her performance will be the first opportunity for the community to experience a piece of what we’ve been working toward, and a preview of the music, culture, dining, and experiences that will bring this district to life. We can’t wait to welcome everyone.”

The entire OCVIBE project is slated to be completed in 2033 and will feature three entertainment venues, a public plaza, office space, residences, dining experiences, and 20 acres of open space.

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/arena-MDP.jpg)

Arena rendering © REF Group

**Arena Mar del Plata** – Mar del Plata, Argentina  
*Capacity: 4,200*  
*Operator: Ref Group*

Located in a tourist city on Argentina’s eastern coast, [Arena Mar del Plata](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/location-is-unmatched-arena-mdp-readies-october-opening-in-argentinas-biggest-resort/) (AMP) has also opened its doors with a show from Los Fabulosos Cadillacs.

The 6,000-square-metre complex features a main hall, which can fit around 4,200 people, intended to be a modular site to fit productions of all types and sizes.

For independent or smaller-scale content, the development has a second space in the basement: La Sala Sub, intended for various activities with an approximate capacity of 800 people.

Brothers Fernando and Federico Roca, heads of the production company Ref Group, led the development, with Roca previously telling *IQ* that “long holiday weekends are a great draw for tourism and for bringing in big shows,” adding that “Arena Mar del Plata will provide the infrastructure the city was missing so that certain events that weren’t coming to Mar del Plata can now do so”.

The town is located roughly 400 km south of Buenos Aires.

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Cleveland-Riverfront-Amp-rendering-©-DLR-Group.png)

Cleveland Riverfront Amp rendering

**Cleveland Riverfront Amphitheater** – Cleveland, Ohio, USA  
*Capacity: 6,200*  
*Operator: Live Nation*

Ground has been broken on another Live Nation amphitheatre project, this time in Ohio’s Cleveland.

The project is being developed by real estate company Bedrock and Rock Entertainment Group, which oversees the NBA’s Cleveland Cavaliers and their home venue, the 19,000-capacity Rocket Arena.

Partners say it is the first large music venue to be built in the city in over 20 years. It is projected to generate around $59 million (€53.8m) in annual economic impact when it opens, expected by summer 2028.

It comes as part of a [redevelopment](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/05/are-amphitheatres-a-key-to-us-urban-development/) of a 35-acre plot of land along the Cuyahoga River, dubbed The Riverfront.

“This investment not only honours the city’s rock ‘n’ roll roots, it positions Cleveland to compete with other regional markets for the biggest tours in the country,” said Live Nation Ohio market president Michael Belkin with its February announcement.
