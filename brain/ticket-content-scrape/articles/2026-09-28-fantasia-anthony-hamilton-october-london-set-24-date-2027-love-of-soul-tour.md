# Fantasia, Anthony Hamilton, October London Set 24-Date 2027 Love of Soul Tour

## Summary

Fantasia, Anthony Hamilton, and October London announced a 24-date "Love of Soul Tour" of North American arenas for 2027, running February through May. Tickets go on general sale Oct. 2, with promoter and venue presales beginning Sept. 30.

### Key Points
- 24 dates, Feb. 5 (Washington, D.C.) through May 22 (Newark, NJ).
- Stops include UBS Arena, Smoothie King Center, Toyota Center, State Farm Arena, MGM Grand Garden Arena, United Center.
- General on-sale Oct. 2, 10 a.m. local time; presales begin Sept. 30.
- Routing published by North American Entertainment Group.

Fantasia, Anthony Hamilton and October London are teaming up for a 24-date U.S. arena run in 2027, with the newly announced Love of Soul Tour scheduled to visit major markets from Washington, D.C., to Las Vegas, Chicago and Newark.

The tour opens Feb. 5 at Capital One Arena in Washington and runs through May 22 at Prudential Center in Newark, [according to a full routing published by North American Entertainment Group.](https://www.northamericanentertainment.com/the-love-of-soul-tour) Other stops include UBS Arena outside New York City, Smoothie King Center in New Orleans, Toyota Center in Houston, State Farm Arena in Atlanta, MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas and United Center in Chicago.

Tickets are scheduled to go on sale to the general public Friday, Oct. 2 at 10 a.m. local time. Promoter and venue presales begin Wednesday, Sept. 30 at 10 a.m. local time in multiple markets. Ticketmaster listings for the tour identify Fantasia, Hamilton and London as the three-artist lineup.

The new tour continues an established touring partnership between Fantasia and Hamilton, who have shared bills repeatedly in recent years.

The 2027 routing brings the pair back to several familiar arena markets while adding October London to the touring package. London, who records for Death Row Records, has built his profile around a contemporary take on classic soul, including songs such as “Back to Your Place” and “Mulholland Drive.”

Fantasia has remained a major live draw more than two decades after winning the third season of “American Idol.” Her recording career includes “I Believe” and the Grammy-winning “Bittersweet,” while her acting work expanded with her return to the role of Celie in the film adaptation of “The Color Purple.”

Hamilton has likewise maintained a long touring career built around a catalog that includes “Charlene,” “Comin’ From Where I’m From” and “The Point of It All.” He and Fantasia have appeared together across a series of arena and theater dates rather than being paired for the first time on the new run.

The Love of Soul Tour will largely play arenas, beginning with a run through the East and South in February before heading west in March and April. The final stretch returns to the Southeast, Midwest and Northeast in May.
