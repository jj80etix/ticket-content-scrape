# Pass The Hat: Red Hat Amphitheater Closes Time Capsule In Raleigh

## Summary

The Raleigh Convention and Performing Arts Complex sealed a time capsule on Oct. 5 marking the closure of the original Red Hat Amphitheater, which is being relocated to an upgraded permanent structure one block south after the 2026 season. The capsule preserves memorabilia from nearly 1,000 events at the venue, including a ticket from its first Live Nation concert (Backstreet Boys, June 6, 2010), and is slated to be reopened in 2076.

### Key Points
- Time capsule sealed Oct. 5, 2026, at the new venue location.
- Original Red Hat Amphitheater closes after the 2026 season and relocates one block south to an upgraded facility.
- Contents include signage, concert posters, apparel, vinyl records, and a ticket from the venue's first Live Nation show.
- To be opened in 2076.

The Raleigh Convention and Performing Arts Complex sealed and buried the Red Hat Amphitheater time capsule in the venue’s new location on Oct. 5. The time capsule contains items preserving the history of the original venue, which is being relocated to an upgraded permanent structure one block south following the 2026 season.

Items being preserved in the time capsule commemorate nearly 1,000 events hosted at the original location including signage, concert posters, apparel, vinyl records and a ticket from the first Live Nation concert at the venue featuring the Backstreet Boys on June 6, 2010.

“It’s amazing to get to share the history of this venue that means so much to Raleigh with future generations” said Kerry Painter, Executive Director of The Complex. “So many special moments have happened here, from people seeing their favorite rockstar, to ice skating with their families during the holidays, to seeing their favorite team bring a championship back to our city. And now, this original version of Red Hat Amp is giving Raleigh one more meaningful moment when people years from now get to open it up and connect with their community’s history in a unique way.”

The time capsule will be opened in 50 years, in 2076.
