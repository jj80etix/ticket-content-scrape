# Isaiah Rashad Announces Leg Two Of ‘Lil Sunny’s Awful Road Trip’ World Tour

## Summary

Rapper Isaiah Rashad announced Leg Two of his "Lil Sunny's Awful Road Trip" world tour, adding dates in South Africa, North America, Europe/UK, and the Pacific/Australia/New Zealand through March 2027, marking his first South Africa performances and first headline shows in Australia and New Zealand.

### Key Points

- Leg Two celebrates the 10th anniversary of Rashad's platinum project "The Sun's Tirade."
- Artist presale begins Sept. 10, general onsale Sept. 11, via itsbeenawful.com.
- VIP packages (meet-and-greet, pre-show Q&A, early entry) are offered through VIP Nation alongside general admission tickets.
- Rashad is finishing the North American leg of his current tour, culminating Sept. 29 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood.
