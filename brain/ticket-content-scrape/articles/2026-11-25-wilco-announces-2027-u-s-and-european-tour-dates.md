# Wilco Announces 2027 U.S. and European Tour Dates

## Summary

Wilco has announced a 17-date spring 2027 U.S. tour and a run of European headline shows for June/July, highlighted by a five-night residency at San Francisco's The Fillmore.

### Key Points
- U.S. leg opens April 20, 2027, in Huber Heights, Ohio, and runs 17 dates with nearly half the shows in California.
- Includes a five-night Fillmore (San Francisco) residency and stops in Texas, Colorado, and Arizona.
- Separate European headline dates follow in June-July 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Wilcoworld-Tour.webp)

Wilco has announced a 17-date U.S. tour for spring 2027, highlighted by a five-night residency at The Fillmore in San Francisco, along with a series of European headline concerts scheduled for June and July.

The Chicago rock band will open its spring tour April 20 at Rose Music Center in Huber Heights, Ohio, followed by a performance in Cincinnati. The group will then travel through Texas, Colorado and Arizona before embarking on an extensive run of California concerts.

Nearly half of the U.S. performances will take place in California, beginning May 2 at the Joshua Tree Retreat Center. The band will also visit Paso Robles and Los Angeles before settling in San Francisco for five performances at The Fillmore on May 9, 10, 11, 13 and 14. The spring tour will conclude May 15 at Channel 24 in Sacramento.

Fans attending the San Francisco residency will have the option of purchasing individual concert tickets or a ticket package covering all five performances.

All 17 U.S. dates are billed as “An Evening With Wilco,” continuing the performance format featured prominently during the band’s 2026 summer tour.

Wilco’s European schedule includes 11 newly announced headline concerts across eight countries. The band will be joined by Pratt & Moody at select performances, including stops in Spain, France, Germany, Denmark, the Czech Republic and Luxembourg.

The band’s European schedule also includes previously announced appearances at Primavera Sound Porto in Portugal, Azkena Rock Festival in Spain and Rock Werchter in Belgium.

Before embarking on its spring tour, Wilco will travel to Marrakech, Morocco, for a previously announced destination concert event taking place March 25–27. The residency will mark the band’s first performances in Africa.
