# Kylie Cantrall Announces “The Valley Girl Problems Tour”

## Summary

Kylie Cantrall announced her first headline tour, "The Valley Girl Problems Tour," a 15-city North American run from September 25 to October 22, following the release of her EP *Valley Girl Problems*. The EP debuted in Spotify's U.S. Top 10 Albums chart with over 2.6 million streams in its first five days.

### Key Points
- 15-city tour runs Sept. 25 (Minneapolis) through Oct. 22 (Los Angeles), with stops including Chicago, Toronto, New York and Atlanta.
- EP *Valley Girl Problems* produced by Tricky Stewart and co-written with Tayla Parx; debuted Top 10 on Spotify's U.S. Album Charts.
- Cantrall also currently stars as Red in Disney's "Descendants: Wicked Wonderland."

[Kylie Cantrall](https://pollstar.com/artists/kylie-cantrall-772143) announced her first headline tour, “The Valley Girl Problems Tour.” The 15-city North American run kicks off in Minneapolis, Sept. 25 and wraps in Los Angeles, Oct. 22 with stops in Chicago, Toronto, New York, Atlanta, San Francisco and more. Full routing below.

The tour arrives on the heels of her new EP Valley Girl Problems, which amassed over 2.6 million streams within the first five days of release, according to he label Republic Records, and debuted in the Top 10 of Spotify’s U.S. Album Charts, with “Space Between” and currently sitting at #7 on the Genius Weekly Song Chart.

Produced by Tricky Stewart (Rihanna, Tyla) and written alongside Tayla Parx, the nine-track project was accompanied by an official music video for “Closet” and a collection of visualizers.

Ahead of her new music releases, Kylie Cantrall also returned as Red, the daughter of the Queen of Hearts, portrayed by Rita Ora, in Disney’s “Descendants: Wicked Wonderland.”
