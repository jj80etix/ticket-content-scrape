# DFB to reintroduce standing areas for Germany matches

## Summary

Germany's football association (DFB) will reintroduce standing areas for men's, women's, and U21 international matches, starting with upcoming UEFA Nations League fixtures in Augsburg and Munich — the first standing sections at German internationals in 25 years. The move follows UEFA's decision to expand a standing-area observation programme, previously limited to men's club competitions, to all UEFA competitions for 2026-27.

### Key Points
- Standing sections return for DFB internationals for the first time in 25 years, despite already being permitted at Bundesliga matches.
- Change follows UEFA's expansion of a standing-observation programme (running since 2022-23) to all competitions, involving 10 national associations.
- First matches with standing areas: upcoming UEFA Nations League fixtures in Augsburg and Munich.

**The German Football Association (DFB) has detailed plans to introduce standing areas for international matches, starting with the upcoming UEFA Nations League matches in Augsburg and Munich.**

Fans attending men’s, women’s and U21 national team matches will be able to watch from standing areas, as the DFB looks to promote the development of Germany’s “lively and diverse” fan culture.

Standing is permitted at Bundesliga matches but it has not been in place at international fixtures for 25 years. The DFB has taken the decision after the UEFA Executive Committee opted to expand an observation programme that has been in place since the 2022-23 season.

The programme involves 10 participating national associations and will extend to all UEFA competitions for the 2026-27 season, having previously been limited to men’s club competitions.
