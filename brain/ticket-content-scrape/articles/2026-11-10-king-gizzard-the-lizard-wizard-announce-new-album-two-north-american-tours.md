# King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard Announce New Album, Two North American Tours

## Summary

King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard announced a new album, *Entropy* (out Dec. 4), and two 2027 North American tour legs (August and October), with tickets going on sale Nov. 13.

### Key Points
- *Entropy* is the band's 29th album, a "companion" to earlier 2026 release *Alien Metal*
- August North American leg launches Aug. 5-6 at LA's Hollywood Forever Cemetery; October leg runs Oct. 6-22 ending in Chicago
- Both legs build on an existing 2027 European tour routing announced earlier

## Transcript

![unnamed (33)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-33.jpg)

photo by Maclay Heriot

Following the surprise release earlier this year of King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard’s *Entropy,* the experimental Australian band has announced details of another LP, out in December, and two 2027 North American tours.  
  
Marking their 29th album, *Entropy* is due out Dec. 4 via the band’s own (p)doom records label. The LP is described as “a companion album to their 28th album, their dark, ecstatic rave masterpiece from earlier this year, *Alien Metal*.” To go along with the announcements, the band has released the lead single from *Entropy,* “Rogue Wave.”  
  
Today’s news from the band’s team goes on to say that the two albums are “rooted in the group’s fascination with Eurorack, a modular synthesiser system involving diodes and cables that looks like something out of vintage sci-fi and delivers sounds that rewire how you make music – and even what music can be.” King Gizzard has incorporated its Eurorack setup, which they call “Nathan,” into their live show and consider it a seventh member of the band.

The band initially planned to release *Alien Metal* and *Entropy* at the same time but King Gizzard’s Mackenzie explains “ *Entropy* began to feel like its own project, something that could stand on its own, and needed a little longer to simmer. We were like, ‘Let’s commit – let’s dig deeper, mine this sound further, and challenge ourselves a bit more. *Entropy* ’s definitely coming from its own place.”

He adds: “Just trying to make music in ways we’ve never tried before is the excitement for us.”

The North American tour launches with two nights at Los Angeles’ Hollywood Forever Cemetery Aug. 5-6. Stops on the August run include San Diego, Santa Fe and Minneapolis, along with several gigs in Canada including Vancouver, Calgary, Saskatoon and Winnipeg.  
  
The October leg kicks off Oct. 6 in Dallas at Longhorn Backyard Amphitheater, followed by gigs in Austin, New Orleans, Atlanta, Montreal, Toronto, Cleveland and beyond. The last confirmed show is Oct. 22 at Chicago’s Wintrust Arena.  
  
Tickets go on sale Friday, November 13th at 9am PT/Noon ET.

King Gizzard is booked by Panache’s Michelle Cable in North America. The band is also managed by Cable.
