# Cypress Hill Sets Halloween Weekend ‘Haunted Hill’ Shows in New York

## Summary

Cypress Hill announced two "Haunted Hill" Halloween shows at Irving Plaza in New York City on October 31 and November 1, continuing its long-running Halloween tradition, alongside several other fall festival dates.

### Key Points
- Oct. 31 lineup: Immortal Technique, Lord Sko; Nov. 1 lineup: Mellow Man Ace ft. Krooked Treez, Planta Industrial.
- General onsale began Aug. 28 via Ticketmaster.
- Group also playing Rocklahoma (Sept. 5), Aftershock (Oct. 2) and Power To The People (Oct. 3) festivals.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Cypress-Hill-Haunted-Hill.webp)

Cypress Hill is bringing its longtime Halloween tradition back to New York City this fall with two nights of “Haunted Hill” shows at Irving Plaza.

The hip-hop group will take over the Manhattan venue on October 31 and November 1, with a different lineup of supporting performers scheduled for each night.

The Halloween show on October 31 will feature Immortal Technique and Lord Sko as opening acts. The following night, November 1, will include performances from Mellow Man Ace featuring Krooked Treez and Planta Industrial.

Tickets for both performances were made available through a presale beginning August 26 at 10 a.m. ET. The general public sale is scheduled to begin Friday, August 28 at 10 a.m. ET through Ticketmaster.

In addition to the Halloween shows, Cypress Hill has several festival appearances scheduled this year, including [Rocklahoma Festival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/rocklahoma-2026-celebrates-20-years-with-slayer-godsmack-papa-roach/) in Pryor, Oklahoma, on September 5, [Aftershock Festival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/aftershock-festival-tool-my-chemical-romance-limp-bizkit/) in Sacramento, California, on October 2, and [Power To The People](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/bruce-springsteen-foo-fighters-dave-matthews-to-headline-power-to-the-people-festival/) in Columbia, Maryland, on October 3.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [cypresshill.com](http://cypresshill.com).
