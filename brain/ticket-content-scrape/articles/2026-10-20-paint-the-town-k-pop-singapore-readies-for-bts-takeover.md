# Paint the Town K-pop: Singapore readies for BTS takeover

## Summary

HYBE and the Singapore Tourism Board are staging a month-long, island-wide "The City Arirang" fan activation around BTS's sold-out four-night National Stadium run (Dec. 17-22), expected to draw 200,000+ attendees and billed as Singapore's largest fan activation for any recording artist. Singapore is the only Southeast Asian stop on the circuit.

### Key Points
- BTS plays 4 sold-out shows at Singapore's National Stadium, Dec. 17-22, expected to draw 200,000+ attendees.
- HYBE + Singapore Tourism Board partnership produces a month-long, island-wide "City Arirang" takeover through December.
- Billed as the country's largest-ever fan activation for a single artist.
- Singapore is the only Southeast Asian stop on the global City Arirang circuit.
