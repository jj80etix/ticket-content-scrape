# Legendary Mexican Band Maná To Stream Red Rocks Concert On YouTube

## Summary

Maná will stream a free Red Rocks concert from its "Vivir Sin Aire Tour" globally via YouTube on Nov. 6, in partnership with Warner Music. The performance will remain available on demand afterward.

### Key Points
- Free global stream Nov. 6, 8:30 p.m. ET on Maná's YouTube channel.
- Partnership between the band, YouTube and Warner Music.
- Comes amid an active touring year including upcoming Latin American stadium dates.

## Transcript

![mana](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/mana.jpg)

Maná, one of Mexico’s most iconic bands, is partnering with YouTube and Warner Music to stream a special concert held at the beloved Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado.

“Maná ‘Vivir Sin Aire Tour’ (Live From Red Rocks) will stream globally for free Nov. 6 at 8:30 p.m. ET, exclusively on the [band’s YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC3cSX-0y3IZzr2F2J1dFlUg). The performance will remain available to watch on demand after the stream.

The quartet from Guadalajara, who are known for massive hits such as “Oye Mi Amor,” “Clavado en Un Bar” and “Rayando el Sol,” performed at Red Rocks in September, and it was a grand return for lead singer Fher Olvera, who lived in Denver when he was 21 and worked as a dishwasher and often gazed at the venue wondering, “What would it feel like to play here?”

Decades later, Olvera performed at the iconic amphitheater and said, “Ay cabrón….llegaste,” which translates to “Damn…you made it.”

It’s another milestone in what has been an epic year for Maná. The band has four remaining shows for the American leg of its tour before visiting Latin America, where they will perform in stadiums including Bogotá’s Vive Claro, Estadio River Plate in Buenos Aires and Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros.

The band also recently announced an exclusive U.S. residency at Yaamava’ Resort & Casino in Highland, California. Maná will perform intimate shows at Yaamava’ Theater in 2027, marking the venue’s first-ever residency and the quartet’s lone performances in the States next year.

Maná was also a major draw during the FIFA World Cup. They headlined the opening ceremony in Mexico City, which was viewed by 1.2 billion people worldwide, and performed a free concert at La Minerva monument in Guadalajara in front of 250,000 fans.
