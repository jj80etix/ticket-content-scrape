# Issue 148

## Summary

IQ Magazine's Issue 148 landing page lists the October 2026 print issue's contents: a feature on Diljit Dosanjh's record-breaking Aura tour, a German live-market report, AXS's growth to a $5bn business under CEO Bryan Perez, and a look at Evanescence's biggest-ever tour, among other pieces on festivals, VIP ticketing, and AEG's festival division.

### Key Points
- Cover feature: Diljit Dosanjh's "Aura" tour, framed as his breakout to stadium-scale global success.
- "The success of AXS" profiles CEO Bryan Perez on building AXS into a $5bn/year ticketing business over 15 years.
- Market Report: Germany's top-tier live business is strong, but mid-tier promoters face rising costs and pickier audiences.
- Other pieces cover Evanescence's touring run, AEG's festival portfolio (Coachella, BST, All Points East), and a VIP-ticketing trend piece ("Golden Tickets").
