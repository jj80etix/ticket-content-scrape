# Sting Expands Brooklyn Paramount Run to Eight Shows With Two New Dates

## Summary

Sting expanded his "Sting 3.0" tour's Brooklyn Paramount engagement from six to eight shows, adding Nov. 20-21 dates alongside the previously announced Nov. 11-18 run, with Fiction Plane supporting.

### Key Points
- New dates: Nov. 20 and 21, joining previously announced Nov. 11, 12, 14, 15, 17, 18 shows.
- Fan-club presale begins Tuesday, Sept. 29, 10 a.m. ET; general on-sale Friday, Oct. 2, 10 a.m.
- Part of a broader fall North American leg of the "Sting 3.0" tour.
- Fiction Plane serves as support act for the Brooklyn run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Sting-.webp)

Sting has added two more performances at Brooklyn Paramount to his 2026 “Sting 3.0” tour, expanding what was originally announced as a six-show engagement at the venue to eight nights.

The newly added concerts are scheduled for November 20 and November 21, following previously announced Brooklyn shows on November 11, 12, 14, 15, 17 and 18. Fiction Plane will support Sting across the Brooklyn run.

Members of Sting’s fan club will receive first access to tickets for the new dates beginning Tuesday, September 29 at 10 a.m. ET, with additional presales scheduled throughout the week. The general onsale begins Friday, October 2 at 10 a.m.

The expanded Brooklyn stand builds on a fall North American leg first announced earlier this year. Sting is set to return to the road beginning October 5 in Vancouver, with the original itinerary including six Brooklyn Paramount performances as one of the tour’s largest multi-night stops.

The itinerary has continued to grow since that initial announcement. The current schedule includes additional multi-night runs in Minneapolis, Nashville, Indianapolis, Montreal, Akron and Pittsburgh before Sting reaches Brooklyn in November. After the eight-show New York engagement, the North American run is scheduled to close with performances November 24 in Orlando and November 25 at Seminole Hard Rock in Hollywood, Florida.
