# Top Tours: Hayley Williams Debuts On Pollstar Charts With Amphitheater Tour

## Summary

Hayley Williams debuted on Pollstar's LIVE75 and Global Concert Pulse charts off her 21-show headlining tour, with her seven October concerts alone selling 103,210 tickets (avg. 14,744/night) and landing at No. 7 on LIVE75 with "Hot Shot" status for the week's top chart debut.

### Key Points
- Hollywood Bowl two-night stand sold 33,130 tickets for $2.9 million in revenue, the strongest stop of the run.
- Shoreline Amphitheatre (Mountain View) drew 19,639 for a single show, the tour's highest single-night total.
- Tour ran Sept. 3-Oct. 12, 21 dates total, closing with seven outdoor-venue shows in October.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2220240953](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-2220240953.jpg)

[Hayley Williams](https://pollstar.com/artists/hayley-williams-150922) has become a solo touring force to be reckoned with as she makes her debut on the weekly [LIVE75](https://pollstar.com/charts/5289/live-75/chart) and [Global Concert Pulse](https://pollstar.com/charts/5288/global-concert-pulse/chart) charts after box-office results were reported from her headlining tour that kicked off in September. With the title “The Hayley Williams Show,” the tour began with a 21-show trek through U.S. cities starting Sep. 3 and running through Oct. 12. The last seven concerts were October performances set in six outdoor venues, including the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, where she appeared for two performances. With ticket sales at her seven concerts totaling 103,210, she sold an average of 14,744 tickets each night to land at No. 7 on [LIVE75](https://pollstar.com/charts/5289/live-75/chart), while also earning “Hot Shot” status as the touring artist with the top chart debut of the week.

As the only one of the six venues with a two-show engagement, the Hollywood Bowl moved the most tickets, selling a total of 33,130 on Oct. 5 and 6, while ticket revenue from the L.A. concerts totaled $2.9 million. California is also home to two more of the six venues, and both of them hosted more than 19,000 fans for single performances. Williams played Shoreline Amphitheatre in Mountain View in the San Francisco Bay Area on Oct. 2, selling 19,639 tickets, the most for one show. Then, the following evening she appeared on stage at Chula Vista’s North Island Credit Union Amphitheatre in the San Diego market with 19,010 fans in attendance.

After leaving the West Coast, she performed on the 9 <sup>th</sup> in the Houston area, selling 16,123 tickets for her performance at the Cynthia Woods Mitchell Pavilion in The Woodlands. An outdoor performance in New Orleans at Champions Square followed with a crowd of 8,032 present on Oct. 10, and then on the 12 <sup>th</sup>, she headlined a performance in the Memphis market at Southaven, Mississippi’s BankPlus Amphitheater at Snowden Grove with 7,276 tickets sold.

Prior to the fall tour, the Paramore lead singer was also on the road with an earlier solo tour during the first half of the year. Her “Hayley Williams at a Bachelorette Party” tour kicked off in March and continued through the end of June with dates booked in both North America and Europe. She did not play arenas, instead opting for smaller venues such as Atlanta’s Tabernacle, New York’s Hammerstein Ballroom, Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium and The Wiltern in L.A. Then, in Europe she played nine rooms including Paradiso in Amsterdam, Tempodrom in Berlin and London’s Roundhouse, before closing out the tour at Dublin’s National Stadium music venue.

Along with her debut on [LIVE75](https://pollstar.com/charts/5289/live-75/chart), Williams also makes her first appearance on the [Global Concert Pulse](https://pollstar.com/charts/5288/global-concert-pulse/chart) chart with grosses reported from shows that occurred during the past three months. With a per-night gross average of $694,101 from all 21 headlining performances on the opening leg of “The Hayley Williams Show,” she enters the lineup at No. 42. She is third among debuting artists on the chart, appearing behind California alt rock band [The Neighbourhood](https://pollstar.com/artists/the-neighbourhood-168717), ranked highest at No. 26, and Canadian R&B artist [Daniel Caesar](https://pollstar.com/artists/daniel-caesar-219548), who follows at No. 28.

The Neighbourhood’s appearance on [Global Concert Pulse](https://pollstar.com/charts/5288/global-concert-pulse/chart) comes during the band’s ‘Wourld Tour” that began last spring in Austin, Texas. Tour dates were planned in North America, Europe, Oceania and Asia through the end of 2026. It is their seventh tour since 2013 and first as an arena headliner. Grosses recorded at 11 recent concerts topped $10.9 million for an average of $990,934 per night.

Caesar’s chart appearance is based on 13 concerts on his “Son of Spergy Tour,” also a first-time arena trek. It began in Asia in May and June, followed by North America in July and August, while the most recent concerts were set in European venues from Aug. 29 through Sep. 11.

Ranked at the top on [Global Concert Pulse](https://pollstar.com/charts/5288/global-concert-pulse/chart) is [Karol G](https://pollstar.com/artists/karol-g-316860) with her gross average of $9.7 million per show, based on 16 concerts during her “Viajando Por El Mundo Tropitour” that launched at Chicago’s Soldier Field in July. Then on [LIVE75](https://pollstar.com/charts/5289/live-75/chart), [BTS](https://pollstar.com/artists/bts-205021) remains in the No. 1 position with the highest ranked ticket sales average of the week. The South Korean group averaged 64,292 in ticket sales per night at three stadiums on the “Arirang World Tour.”
