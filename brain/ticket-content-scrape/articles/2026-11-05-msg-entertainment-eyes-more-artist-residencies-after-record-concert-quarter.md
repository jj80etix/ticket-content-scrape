# MSG Entertainment Eyes More Artist Residencies After Record Concert Quarter

## Summary

Madison Square Garden Entertainment is looking to expand its artist-residency business after a record concert quarter, with fiscal Q1 revenue up 37% to $217.1m. Executives confirmed on the Nov. 4 earnings call that discussions are underway for additional residencies in fiscal 2028 and beyond.

### Key Points
- Q1 fiscal 2027 revenue: $217.1m, up from $158.3m a year earlier.
- MSGE hosted 1.3m+ guests across 160 live events in the quarter; the significant majority of concerts sold out.
- Growth driven largely by Harry Styles' 30-show residency and higher revenue per concert.
- Entertainment-offerings revenue climbed 33% to $174.9m.
- Executives are already discussing additional artist residencies for fiscal 2028+.

## Transcript

![Madison Square Garden in New York City](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/MSG-handout-photo.jpg)

Madison Square Garden in New York City

Madison Square Garden Entertainment is looking to expand its artist residency business following a record-setting quarter for concerts at Madison Square Garden, as the company reported a 37% increase in revenue and signaled continued strong demand across its live entertainment venues.

The company generated $217.1 million in revenue during its fiscal first quarter ended September 30, 2026, up from $158.3 million a year earlier. The results were driven largely by increased concert activity at the Garden, including the beginning of Harry Styles’ 30-show residency, along with higher revenue per concert.

During its November 4 earnings call, MSG Entertainment executives emphasized the importance of extended artist engagements to the company’s future growth, confirming that discussions are already underway with performers about additional residencies in fiscal 2028 and beyond.

MSG Entertainment hosted more than 1.3 million guests across 160 live events during the quarter, with Madison Square Garden setting a new record for the number of concerts held in any single quarter at the arena. The company said the significant majority of concerts across its venues sold out.

## MSG Sees More Opportunities for Artist Residencies

Extended artist engagements have become an increasingly important part of MSG Entertainment’s concert business, allowing the company to secure multiple performances from major acts while creating greater certainty around its event calendar.

During the [earnings call](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/msge/transcripts/731433-q1-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Chief Financial Officer David Collins said residencies help establish a recurring base of business and provide greater visibility into future bookings.

Styles’ recently completed 30-night residency illustrated the strategy’s potential. The singer performed 16 of those concerts during MSG Entertainment’s fiscal first quarter, with the remaining performances taking place in October. His run concluded October 31.

The Garden also hosted extended engagements by Bon Jovi and Phish during the summer, while MSG Entertainment’s broader venue portfolio has featured composer Joe Hisaishi at Radio City Music Hall and recurring performances from Jerry Seinfeld, Seth Meyers and John Oliver at the Beacon Theatre.

Collins indicated that the company intends to pursue similar opportunities across its properties rather than concentrating exclusively on Madison Square Garden.

Although Styles’ residency contributed substantially to the quarter’s concert calendar, Collins cautioned that its precise incremental financial contribution was difficult to quantify. He said the Garden also experienced strong underlying concert growth beyond the Styles performances.

The residency strategy has also become a prominent part of the responsibilities of Josephine Vaccarello, who was [named president of MSG Entertainment in September](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/josephine-vaccarello-president-msg-entertainment/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Vaccarello oversees the company’s entertainment bookings and productions businesses, with a mandate to maximize its event calendar and ticket sales.

Her experience includes helping develop Billy Joel’s decade-long Madison Square Garden residency, which concluded after 150 performances, along with the Styles residency and concert programming at Sphere in Las Vegas.

## Concert Bookings and Ticket Demand Remain Strong

MSG Entertainment’s outlook for the remainder of fiscal 2027 suggests that its concert business is maintaining momentum even after the completion of the Styles residency.

Collins said the company has already slightly exceeded its full-year concert-booking target at Madison Square Garden and is more than 80% of the way toward its booking goal across the broader venue portfolio.

The company expects the number of concerts to increase for the full fiscal year at both the Garden and its theaters. Although bookings are currently pacing lower in certain periods during the second half of the year, executives attributed those differences to the timing of performances rather than a deterioration in overall demand.

Advance ticket sales also remain encouraging. Collins said concert sell-through rates for the next two fiscal quarters are running ahead of the comparable period last year, and MSG Entertainment has added performances for several upcoming acts in response to demand.

Those trends contrast with concerns about consumer spending pressure affecting parts of the live entertainment business. While acknowledging broader economic uncertainty, management said it has not observed a meaningful weakening of demand across its portfolio.

Strong primary-market attendance, however, does not necessarily translate into continuously elevated resale prices. During Styles’ New York residency, [TicketNews documented substantial declines in secondary-market asking prices](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/harry-styles-kicks-off-msg-residency-as-ticket-prices-sharply-fall-from-early-high-points/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) compared with the extraordinary levels seen around the initial ticket sales.

That distinction is particularly relevant to extended residencies, where additional performances can provide more opportunities for fans to attend while spreading demand across a larger number of dates.

For MSG Entertainment, the financial results demonstrate the value of filling its calendar with high-demand events while generating additional revenue through concessions, merchandise and premium hospitality.

## Christmas Spectacular Adds to Revenue Outlook

Beyond concerts, MSG Entertainment is preparing for another major holiday season at Radio City Music Hall.

The annual Christmas Spectacular opened its 2026 run November 4, with 231 performances planned compared with 215 during the previous season. The company expects to welcome more than 1.2 million guests and generate another year of record revenue for the production.

Advance ticket revenue is pacing ahead of the comparable period last year. Collins said the company continues to actively manage ticket inventory and pricing to maximize revenue across the production’s performances.

MSG Entertainment also reported continued strength in premium hospitality, with suite license renewals exceeding 95% for the fiscal year.

Meanwhile, the company is continuing negotiations surrounding the potential transfer of the Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden as part of the Penn Station redevelopment. Collins indicated that reinvesting potential proceeds in another venue could be an option, with New York remaining the company’s preferred market, although no decisions have been made.

The company’s next fiscal quarters will provide a clearer picture of how its concert business performs after the Styles residency and whether additional long-term artist engagements can extend the booking momentum established during its record-setting summer.
