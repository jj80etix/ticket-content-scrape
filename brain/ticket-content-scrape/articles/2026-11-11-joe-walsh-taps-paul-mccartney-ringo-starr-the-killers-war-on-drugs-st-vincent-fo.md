# Joe Walsh Taps Paul McCartney & Ringo Starr, The Killers, War On Drugs, St. Vincent For VetsAid 2027 In Las Vegas

## Summary

Joe Walsh's VetsAid 2027 benefit concert, marking its 10th anniversary, will feature Paul McCartney & Ringo Starr, The Killers, The War On Drugs and St. Vincent on January 29 at Las Vegas' MGM Grand Garden Arena. Tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 13 via AXS, starting at $200 before fees.

### Key Points
- McCartney and Starr are expected to perform together in a substantial set — a rare pairing for the two surviving Beatles
- Partners with fundraising platform Fandiem and discovery platform Bandsintown for a donate-to-win VetsAid trip giveaway
- All net proceeds go to veterans' services charities vetted via the Combined Arms Institute; VetsAid has disbursed $5M+ in grants to date
- 2027 grants will go exclusively to Nevada-based or Nevada-operating organizations

## Transcript

![image](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image.png)

The lineup for and onsale info is out for the Joe Walsh-led VetsAid 2027 benefit concert, which is set to feature Paul McCartney & Ringo Starr, The Killers, The War On Drugs, St. Vincent, Lucious and more, including a promised “super-set” from Walsh, McCartney and Ringo and surprise special guests.

It’s all going down Jan. 29 at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, with tickets on sale Friday, Nov. 13 through Axs, starting at $200 before fees.

“Having spent the past two years at the Sphere and working with the hardworking people of Las Vegas, I want to show them my gratitude and respect,” Walsh says in the announcement. “This city has taken such good care of me and my family that it would be my privilege to give back to the large veteran community here and to their families. The VetsAid rock and roll circus is pulling into the MGM Grand Garden and for our tenth anniversary we have our biggest show ever! Join me on stage with my musical heroes Paul and Ringo (and more surprise guests), Vegas’s hometown icons The Killers, the sublime St Vincent and my beloved The War on Drugs and Lucius who are the only acts I’ve ever invited back to VetsAid. Hit the jackpot with us on January 29th! Support our veterans and support each other.”

The McCartney & Starr billing and press release language suggests the two will perform together in a substantial manner, which would be a rare occasion. The two remaining members of the original Fab Four have only rarely performed together publicly in recent decades, including with a surprise appearance from Ringo during a McCartney “Got Back” tour stop at The O2 Arena in late 2024, where they performed “Sgt. Pepper’s Lonely Hearts Club Band” and “Helter Skelter.”

VetsAid is also partnering once again with digital fundraising platform Fandiem and live music discovery platform Bandsintown to inspire fans to give back and ‘Be Joe Walsh’s Guest at VetsAid 2027′. Fans can Donate-to-Win online via fandiem.com/vetsaid for a chance at an all-expenses paid trip to VetsAid 2027 in Las Vegas which will include: round trip airfare and lodging, two seats in the Friends and Family section and VIP access to the Friends and Family Lounge, and a coffee date with Joe Walsh the day before the show.

Now in its 10th year, VetsAid hosted its inaugural show Sept. 20, 2017, with a concert at EagleBank Arena in Fairfax, Virginia, featuring Walsh, Zac Brown Band, Keith Urban and Gary Clark Jr. A host of other notable names have taken part in VetsAid events in cities including Tacoma, Houston, Columbus and Wichita. Other performers have included Don Henley, James Taylor, Chris Stapleton, Haim, ZZ Top, Jason Isbell, Sheryl Crow, Eddie Vedder, James Hetfield, Gwen Stefani and many others.

All net proceeds from the concert will go directly to the veterans’ services charities selected through a vetting process coordinated in tandem with the Combined Arms Institute, according to the announcement. To date, VetsAid has disbursed more than $5,000,000 in grants. In 2027, VetsAid plans to disburse grants exclusively to organizations based in Nevada or with operations on the ground in Nevada; grant applications are open now via the VetsAid website (www.vetsaid.org/grants).

Walsh is a Gold Star son himself and his his father was a flight instructor for the first US operational jet powered aircraft, the Lockheed F-80 Shooting Star, and died while stationed and on active duty on Okinawa when Walsh was 20 months old.
