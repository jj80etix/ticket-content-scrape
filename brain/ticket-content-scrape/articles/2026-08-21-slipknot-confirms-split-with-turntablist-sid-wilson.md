Rumors have been circulating for weeks regarding Sid Wilson’s involvement with Slipknot, and now, the metal band has made the news official.

On July 31, *[TMZ](https://www.tmz.com/2026/07/31/sid-wilson-kicked-out-of-slipknot/)* first reported that Wilson had been permanently kicked-out of the group, though there were no further details. Then, Slipknot’s Jim Root shared a cryptic message on his Instagram story shortly after the rumors, writing: “Don’t believe everything you read. Stop. Think. Take a breath. Sit with it for awhile. Just maybe even wait until there’s more information.” Additionally, Wilson’s ex-fiancée Kelly Osbourne posted and deleted several Instagram stories directed at Wilson.

Slipknot formally acknowledged the rumors on August 14 in a now-deleted Instagram post.

“Effective immediately, Slipknot will no longer be associated with Sid Wilson,” the post read. “We wish him the best in his future endeavors.”

No more context or information regarding the split has been announced at this time.

Wilson, who joined the band in 1998 as the group’s turntablist, became known as “No. 0.” He was featured on all seven of Slipknot’s studio albums from 1999 through 2022.

This certainly isn’t the first time Slipknot has quietly pushed members out of its band. The [late Joey Jordison](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/10/joey-jordison-estate-settles-slipknot-lawsuit/), a founding member of Slipknot known as “No. 1,” was dismissed from the group in 2013. Additionally, Slipknot parted ways with one of their longest-standing members, Craig Jones, in 2023, followed by [drummer Jay Weinberg](https://www.ticketnews.com/2023/11/jay-weinberg-responds-to-removal-from-slipknot-feels-blindsided/) the same year. Notably, Chris Fehn left the group in 2019 after performing with the band for over two decades.
