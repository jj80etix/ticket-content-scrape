# SLAYYYTER Announces 2027 Madison Square Garden Headline Show

## Summary

SLAYYYTER announced a June 21, 2027 headline show at Madison Square Garden, her biggest headline performance to date, with general tickets and VIP packages going on sale October 16. The news caps the North American leg of her "Wor$t Girl in the World Tour," which continues into a fully sold-out UK/EU run.

### Key Points
- MSG headline show set for June 21, 2027; tickets and VIP packages on sale Oct. 16.
- VIP packages include floor GA, early entry, lanyard/laminate, and exclusive merch.
- Caps the North American leg of the "Wor$t Girl in the World Tour."
- UK/EU leg (Paris, London, and more) is entirely sold out through late October.

SLAYYYTER has announced a headline show at Madison Square Garden in New York City set for June 21, 2027. Tickets go on sale Oct. 16 at 10 a.m. EST, and fans can register now on her website [HERE](https://msg.slayyyter.com/) for a reminder when they go on sale. The upcoming show marks her biggest headline performance to date.

A limited number of exclusive VIP packages will also be available starting Oct. 16, at 10 am EST. These exclusive offers may include a general admission floor ticket, VIP priority check-in and early floor entry, a commemorative VIP tour lanyard and laminate, an exclusive merch item, and more.

The announcement follows the wrap of the North American leg of SLAYYYTER’s “WOR$T GIRL IN THE WORLD TOUR.” The tour continues with the sold-out UK/EU leg, which includes stops in Paris, London, and more. See full tour routing below.
