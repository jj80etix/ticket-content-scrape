# UK Lineups: Alt-j Join O2 Anniversary Lineup, Download Headliners Unveiled

## Summary

A Pollstar UK-lineups roundup reports alt-J will headline The O2's 20th-anniversary celebrations in summer 2027 with its "Then Again Tour 2027," marking a decade since the band's first O2 headline show, alongside newly unveiled Download Festival headliners.

### Key Points
- alt-J plays The O2's 20th-birthday celebration July 14, 2027, a decade after its first O2 headline date.
- That 2017 O2 show sold 14,294 tickets and grossed $548,101, per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Roundup also covers newly unveiled Download Festival headliners.

**alt-j Join The O2 Party**

The O2 has announced that [alt-J](https://pollstar.com/artists/alt-j-168845) will perform at the venue as part of its 20th birthday celebrations, taking place throughout summer 2027. The Mercury Prize-winning British band will bring its “Then Again Tour 2027” to the iconic London venue July 14, 2027, marking a decade since its headline performance at The O2 in 2017.

That June 16, 2017 performance, promoted by AEG Presents, sold 14,294 tickets, grossing $548,101, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

One of the most successful British bands of the millennium, alt-J have sold more than two million albums and amassed more than two billion streams. Their Mercury Prize and Ivor Novello Award-winning debut, An Awesome Wave, was followed by UK number one album This Is All Yours, while 2017’s RELAXER became the band’s second album to be nominated for the Mercury Prize.

alt-J join a growing line-up of international artists and performers celebrating two decades of live entertainment at the iconic London venue. Confirmed 20th birthday artists include:

- Maisie Peters, Saturday, May 8
- Shreya Ghoshal, Friday, June 4
- David Guetta, Saturday, June 5
- SOMBR, June 21, 22, 24, 25 and 29
- Brad Paisley, Saturday, June 26
- Billie Jean King and John McEnroe, Sunday, June 27
- Santana, Wednesday, June 30
- Kings of Leon, Friday, July 2
- Jack Johnson, Sunday, July 4
- Don Omar, Monday, July 5
- Pet Shop Boys, July 9-10
- alt-J, Wednesday, July 14
- Jamiroquai, July 15, 16 and 19
- Jorja Smith, Saturday, July 17

Emma Bownes, Senior Vice President of Venue Programming, AEG International, commented, “alt-J are one of the most distinctive and successful British bands of their generation, so it feels particularly fitting to welcome them back to The O2 exactly ten years after their first headline show here. Our 20th birthday is about celebrating the artists and performances that have been part of The O2’s story over the past two decades, and we’re delighted to have alt-J joining what is already an incredible summer of live music.”

Priority Tickets will be available exclusively to O2 and Virgin Media broadband customers via the Priority app from 10 a.m. on Nov. 18. Tickets will go on general sale at 10 a.m. on Friday Nov. 20 via The O2 website.

**Download Unveils More Than 100 Names**

Download Festival returns for its 24th edition to Donington Park, the spiritual home of rock, June 9-13, 2027. More than 100 acts were revealed today, among them the three headliners [blink-182](https://pollstar.com/artists/blink-182-47896), [Bring Me The Horizon](https://pollstar.com/artists/bring-me-the-horizon-59272) and [Evanescence](https://pollstar.com/artists/evanescence-37635).

While it is the Download debut for blink-182, Bring Me The Horizon are doing a victory lap after their 2023 headline set.

The stacked line-up also features rock legends Mötley Crüe, as well as UK and international heavyweights Turnstile, The Prodigy, Faith No More, I Prevail, Pierce The Veil, PRESIDENT, 5 Seconds Of Summer, Basement, Soft Play, High Vis, Static-X, Murderdolls, Body Count ft. Ice-T, Malevolence, Converge, Comeback Kid, Terror, The Hives, Dayseeker, Bilmuri, Holding Absence, PVRIS, Hawthorne Heights, Carcass, Napalm Death and many more.

The DLXXIV line-up was launched at Alexandra Palace Theatre Nov. 11 at a banquet for invited guests, who were treated to a feast in four acts cooked over fire by MATBLAK x Woodfire Canteen, before the unveiling of the launch film and a Q&A with festival director Kam Haq.

More acts as well as surprises are still to come. Early Bird tickets sold out within two weeks making it officially the fastest selling Download ever,” according to promoter Live Nation. Tickets are on course to sell out for the fifth year in a row, a Paypal presale is currently ongoing with a five-night camping ticket starting at £375 ($497).
