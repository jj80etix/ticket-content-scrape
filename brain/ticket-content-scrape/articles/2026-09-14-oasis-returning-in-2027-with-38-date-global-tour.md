# Oasis Returning In 2027 With 38-Date Global Tour

## Summary

Pollstar's report on the Oasis "Live '27" 38-date tour announcement emphasizes the commercial context: the 2025 reunion tour grossed $405 million from 2.2 million tickets and won Pollstar's Major Tour of the Year, with US pricing for 2027 fixed at $122.65-$541.05 and registration required by Sept. 17 via oasisnet.com. (See the TicketNews report on this same announcement, tagged #q9 #q11 #q12 #q18, for the regulatory and onsale-process detail.)

### Key Points
- The 2025 reunion was the second-highest-grossing tour of the year.
- Companion documentary "Don't Look Back in Anger" grossed $8.2 million in its opening weekend.
- Registration process is described as designed to "reduce queuing times and get tickets to genuine fans."

Oasis is going to give it another go. Following a successful reunion tour in 2025, brothers Liam and Noel Gallagher announced 38 dates in 2027 across the United Kingdom, Ireland, Europe and the U.S.

Rumors had been swirling that the British rock band would hit the road again after a massively successful tour last year, the second-highest-grossing trek of 2025, hauling in more than $405 million from 2,228,471 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. The sold-out tour received the Major Tour of the Year honor at the 37 <sup>th</sup> annual Pollstar Awards.

“Oasis Live ’27” kicks off May 21 with a five-night stand at Glasgow’s Celtic Park, followed by 11 nights at Etihad Stadium in their hometown of Manchester. The band will also visit Allianz Arena in Munich, Barcelona’s Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys, Johan Cruijff ArenA in Amsterdam, Stade de France in Paris and Rome’s Stadio Olimpico. Oasis make their way back to the U.S. next August with concerts at Boston’s Gillette Stadium and Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas and conclude their run with two nights at Slane Castle in Ireland and four shows at Knebworth in England.

**[Oasis’ Reunion Tour Hits No. 2 On Worldwide Tours Chart: ‘The Outpouring, The Emotion, You Could Cut It With A Knife’](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/12/oasis-reunion-tour-hits-no-2-on-worldwide-tours-chart-the-outpouring-the-emotion-you-could-cut-it-with-a-knife/)**

“START THE CELEBRATIONS!!!! After a period of reflection ‘The Most Damaging Pop Cultural Force In Recent British History’ has declared itself fit for purpose and will once again return to lift the spirits of the people. Come witness the fitness. It shall be a sight to behold,” the band said in a statement.

To purchase tickets, fans must register at [oasisnet.com](https://oasisinet.com/home/) by 11 a.m. on Sept. 17. Demand is expected to be extremely high. The registration process is designed to reduce queuing times and get tickets to genuine fans. It is the only way to buy tickets for the tour, and standard ticket prices are fixed, ranging from $122.65 to $541.05, including fees and taxes.

The tour announcement comes fresh off the release of “Oasis: Don’t Look Back in Anger,” which chronicles Liam and Noel Gallagher’s return to the stage in 2025. The critically acclaimed documentary premiered at the Venice Film Festival and received an eight-minute standing ovation before it was released in theaters worldwide, grossing $8,225,000 last weekend.
