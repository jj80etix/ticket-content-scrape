# Tour news: Rod Stewart unveils farewell Final Encore tour

## Summary

IQ's tour-news roundup highlights Rod Stewart's newly announced farewell tour, "The Final Encore," a 21-date 2027 run opening April 23 at Dublin's 3Arena and spanning the UK, Netherlands, Belgium, Germany, Denmark, Finland, Sweden, Norway, Hungary and Czechia — alongside other major announcements including a new Sphere headliner (John Mayer) and a Slipknot stadium run.

### Key Points
- Rod Stewart's "Final Encore" tour is billed as the capstone of a six-decade live career.
- Tour opens April 23, 2027 at Dublin's 3Arena across 21 dates in 11+ European countries.
- Same roundup also notes John Mayer heading to the Sphere and Slipknot staging a stadium run (details paywalled beyond the lead).
