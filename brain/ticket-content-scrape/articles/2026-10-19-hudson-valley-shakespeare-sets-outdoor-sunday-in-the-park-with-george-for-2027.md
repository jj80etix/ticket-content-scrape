# Hudson Valley Shakespeare Sets Outdoor ‘Sunday in the Park With George’ for 2027

## Summary

Hudson Valley Shakespeare will stage an outdoor production of "Sunday in the Park With George" from June 6 to Sept. 4, 2027, as part of its 40th-anniversary season at the Samuel H. Scripps Theater Center in Garrison, NY. The staging is independent of a separately canceled West End revival that had starred Ariana Grande and Jonathan Bailey.

### Key Points
- Runs June 6 - Sept. 4, 2027, at Garrison, NY, outdoor venue overlooking the Hudson River.
- Part of the company's 40th-anniversary season; directed by Amanda Dehnert.
- Unrelated to the now-canceled West End revival (Ariana Grande, Jonathan Bailey had withdrawn).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sunday-in-the-Park-with-George-Hudson-Valley-Shakespeare.webp)

Hudson Valley Shakespeare will stage a new outdoor production of Stephen Sondheim and James Lapine’s “Sunday in the Park With George” in summer 2027, with performances scheduled from June 6 through Sept. 4 at the Samuel H. Scripps Theater Center in Garrison, New York.

Directed by Amanda Dehnert, the Pulitzer Prize-winning musical will be part of the theater company’s 40th anniversary season, marking its second summer at a permanent home overlooking the Hudson River.

Featuring music and lyrics by Sondheim and a book by Lapine, “Sunday in the Park With George” follows painter Georges Seurat as he works to complete his masterpiece, *A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte*, in 1880s Paris. His dedication to his art strains his relationship with Dot, the woman he loves, while the musical’s second act moves forward a century to explore artistic identity and the connections between generations.

Notably, a [West End production of “Sunday in the Park With George”](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/ariana-grande-and-jonathan-baileys-sunday-in-the-park-with-george-revival-delayed/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) starring Ariana Grande and Jonathan Bailey was postponed after both performers withdrew. Producers subsequently canceled the scheduled 2027 engagement at London’s Barbican Centre while leaving open the possibility of staging the production in 2028.
