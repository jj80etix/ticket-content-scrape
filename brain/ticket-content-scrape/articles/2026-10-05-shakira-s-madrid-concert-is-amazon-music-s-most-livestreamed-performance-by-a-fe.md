# Shakira’s Madrid Concert Is Amazon Music’s Most Livestreamed Performance By A Female Artist

## Summary

Shakira's Oct. 3 Madrid show on her "Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour" became Amazon Music's most-viewed livestream by a female artist, featuring a surprise duet with Dua Lipa, and was broadcast on Amazon Music, Prime Video and Twitch. The show anchored Amazon Music's 2026 "Music Connects Us" campaign honoring female trailblazers in Latin music.

### Key Points
- Oct. 3 Madrid show featured a surprise Dua Lipa appearance for a duet of "Antología"; streamed live on Amazon Music, Prime Video and Twitch.
- Part of a 12-date Madrid residency (extended from an originally planned 3 nights due to demand) at a custom-built 50,000+ capacity "Shakira Stadium."
- Tour has grossed $491.6M from 3,778,276 tickets across 112 shows per Pollstar Boxoffice, making it the most successful trek of Shakira's career.
- Remaining dates include three more Madrid shows (Oct. 9-11), then Bishkek Arena (Kyrgyzstan) and the Pyramids of Giza (Egypt).

## Transcript

![V8A6727 copy](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/V8A6727-copy.jpg)

Dua Lipa surprised the crowd and performed alongside Shakira during the Latin superstar’s concert in Madrid, Spain, on Oct. 3. (Photo by Nicolas Gerardin)

Shakira has achieved yet another milestone with her record-breaking “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour.” The Colombian star’s show in Madrid, Spain, on Oct. 3 was the most-viewed livestream by a female artist on Amazon Music, the major streamer announced Sunday.

Saturday’s show featured a surprise appearance from Dua Lipa, who joined the Latin superstar on stage for a special performance of fan-favorite “Antología,” and it was broadcast on Amazon Music, Prime Video and Twitch.

Amazon Music did not disclose streaming numbers, but Shakira’s record-breaking broadcast served as the centerpiece of The Women Shaping Latin Music, the 2026 edition of Amazon Music’s annual Music Connects Us campaign celebrating artists and cultural movements shaping the genre. This year’s program honors female trailblazers and celebrates those moving the music forward and creating opportunities for the next generation.

Shakira is in the final days of her 12-date residency in Madrid, where she had a custom-built Shakira Stadium for the special event. In addition to the temporary venue, which can accommodate more than 50,000 fans, the site featured a wide range of activities, from exhibitions to workshops, curated by the artist that celebrate Latin culture.

The residency was initially announced as a three-night stand, but it was extended to 12 nights due to demand. Shakira has three more nights in Spain, Oct. 9-11, before performing at Bishkek Arena in Kyrgyzstan and at the Pyramids of Giza in Al Haram, Egypt.

Shakira’s “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour” is the most successful trek of her storied career, with the singer grossing a whopping $491.6 million off 3,778,276 tickets across 112 shows, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

The artist is booked by WME worldwide and managed by Nadine Eliya.
