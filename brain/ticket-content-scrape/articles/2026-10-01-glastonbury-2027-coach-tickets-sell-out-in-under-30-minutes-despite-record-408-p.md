# Glastonbury 2027 Coach Tickets Sell Out in Under 30 Minutes Despite Record £408 Price

## Summary

The first Glastonbury 2027 ticket batch (coach packages) sold out in under 30 minutes despite a record £408 admission price, 7.8% above 2025's price. A demand/pricing data point about one of the world's highest-profile festivals, with no ticketing-platform or policy content — this is a bare market-demand observation, not a substantive pain point or industry-structure signal.

### Key Points
- Coach-ticket allocation for Glastonbury 2027 sold out in ~30 minutes at a record £408 price point.
- Price is 7.8% above 2025 and over 50% above pre-pandemic (2019) pricing.
- General-admission sale (larger allocation) follows separately.
- No platform, fee-structure, or regulatory content reported.

The first batch of tickets for Glastonbury Festival 2027 [sold out in less than 30 minutes Thursday evening](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c0klzvdn8epo), providing an early indication that the festival’s record £408 admission price has done little to diminish competition for access to Worthy Farm.

Ticket-and-coach packages went on sale at 6 p.m. BST Thursday, Oct. 1, with the allocation exhausted within roughly half an hour. Glastonbury subsequently confirmed that all tickets in the coach sale had been sold and directed unsuccessful buyers toward Sunday’s much larger general admission sale at 9 a.m. BST.

The rapid sellout came less than a month after Glastonbury announced that admission for its 2027 return would cost £408, including a £5 booking fee, plus postage and packing. That is £29.50, or about 7.8%, above the £378.50 charged for the 2025 festival.

As [TicketNews reported when the new price was announced](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/glastonbury-2027-ticket-prices-record-408/), the increase takes Glastonbury admission above £400 for the first time and puts the asking price 51% above the £270 ticket price established for the planned 2020 festival before its pandemic cancellation. The price is also 61% higher than the £253 charged for the last festival actually held before the pandemic in 2019.

The higher price did not noticeably slow the first ticket tranche. Coach packages for the 2025 festival [similarly sold out in about 30 minutes when they went on sale in November 2024](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/11/first-round-of-glastonbury-festival-tickets-sell-out-following-new-queue/), the first year Glastonbury used a system that randomly assigned buyers positions in an online queue.

That comparison does not necessarily establish that overall demand is unchanged or stronger, since the festival does not publicly disclose the size of each coach allocation vs. how many tickets have yet to be made available for purchase. But it does show that breaking the £400 threshold was not enough to prevent another near-immediate sellout of the first tickets made available.

Glastonbury’s ticket structure also leaves little opportunity for conventional secondary-market price discovery after the initial sale. Tickets are personalized to the registered buyer, include the ticket holder’s photograph and are “strictly non-transferable,” according to the festival. See Tickets is the only authorized seller, while canceled or returned inventory is eventually offered again through the festival’s own resale process.

For Thursday’s coach sale, buyers could reserve up to six festival tickets with a £100 deposit per person but were required to pay the full coach fare at the time of purchase. The remaining £308 festival balance is due during the first week of April. Coach transportation is offered from locations around the U.K., with the travel component intended in part to reduce the festival’s transportation emissions.

The sale once again produced the familiar scramble among registered fans. Jessi Dimmock of the Glasto Cast podcast told BBC Somerset that ticket weekend is “the most stressful weekend of my year,” while acknowledging that some buyers may balk at a ticket price that now begins with a four rather than a three.

Most of the festival’s general admission inventory remains to be sold. That sale begins at 9 a.m. BST Sunday, Oct. 4, exclusively through See Tickets. Anyone hoping to participate needed to complete Glastonbury’s advance registration before it closed Sept. 25; registration will not reopen until after the October sales. Buyers can purchase as many as six tickets per transaction.

Glastonbury 2027 will run June 23-27 at Worthy Farm following the festival’s scheduled fallow year in 2026. No lineup has yet been announced.

The Sunday sale will provide the much larger test of demand at the new record price. For the first allocation, however, Glastonbury’s move past £400 did not keep its coach packages on the market for even half an hour.
