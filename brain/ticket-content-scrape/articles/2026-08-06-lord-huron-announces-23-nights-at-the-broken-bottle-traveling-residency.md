# Lord Huron Announces ‘23 Nights At The Broken Bottle’ Traveling Residency

## Summary

Lord Huron announced the "23 Nights At The Broken Bottle" traveling residency, an intimate theater tour across North America running December 2026 through January 2027. The tour follows the band's arena and amphitheater shows and will feature an "An Evening With" format at vintage venues including Ryman Auditorium and The Wiltern. Presale is underway and general on-sale begins Friday, August 7 at 10am local time per venue.

### Key Points
- Tour spans December 1, 2026 through January 11, 2027 across North American cities.
- Opens at Ryman Auditorium (Nashville) and closes with two nights at The Wiltern (Los Angeles).
- Format is a single-act "An Evening With" show mixing hits, fan favorites, and deep cuts.
- General on-sale starts Friday, August 7 at 10am local time; presale already in progress.
- Stops include Chicago, Montréal, Brooklyn, Vancouver, Seattle, and other markets.

Lord Huron is inviting fans to join him at “The Broken Bottle,” as stages on his intimate “traveling residency” are transformed into the desert honky-tonk bar in the group’s fictional universe. The dates for the “23 Nights At The Broken Bottle” Tour are booked across North America in December and early 2027.  
  
An announcement notes that after the indie rock band, led by Ben Schneider, has sold out arenas and amphitheaters, the trek will give fans a chance to see Lord Huron in a more intimate setting at vintage theatres. Lord Huron is the only act on the bill and the “An Evening with” experience promises “a set consisting of fan favorites, hits, and rarely played deep cuts.”

The “Broken Bottle” journey starts off at the famed Ryman Auditorium in Nashville on December 1, followed by stops in Chicago, Montréal, Brooklyn, Vancouver, Seattle, and more. THe tour concludes with two nights at The Wiltern in Los Angeles, CA on January 10-11, 2027.

The official Lord Huron pre-sale is ongoing and the general on-sale starts on Friday, August 7 at 10am local time.

For more information visit https://www.lordhuron.com/tour
