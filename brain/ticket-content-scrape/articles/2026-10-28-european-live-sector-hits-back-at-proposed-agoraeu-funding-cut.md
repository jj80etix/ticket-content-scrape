# European live sector hits back at proposed AgoraEU funding cut

## Summary

A coalition of European live-music bodies (Yourope, LiveEurope, ESNS, Live DMA, European Music Managers Alliance) is pushing back on a proposed 30% budget cut to the EU's AgoraEU cultural-industries funding scheme, warning of a "dramatic cost" to the sector, and calling for ring-fenced music funding in the EU's 2028–2034 budget.

### Key Points
- Proposed AgoraEU budget cut of roughly 30% drew coalition pushback.
- Coalition members: Yourope, LiveEurope, ESNS, Live DMA, European Music Managers Alliance.
- Coalition calls for dedicated, ring-fenced music funding in the EU's 2028–2034 budget cycle.
