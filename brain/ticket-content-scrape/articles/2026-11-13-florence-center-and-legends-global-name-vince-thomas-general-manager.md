# Florence Center And Legends Global Name Vince Thomas General Manager

## Summary

The Florence Center and Legends Global named Vince Thomas, an 18+ year venue-management veteran who previously served as the center's Assistant General Manager, as the facility's new General Manager.

### Key Points
- Vince Thomas named General Manager of the Florence Center (Florence, South Carolina).
- Previously served as the venue's Assistant General Manager and director of events.
- Brings 18+ years of experience across arenas, stadiums, and performing arts centers.

## Transcript

![Current image: Vince Thomas Headshot](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Vince-Thomas-Headshot-683x1024.jpg)

Vince Thomas courtesy Legends Global

The Florence Center and Legends Global announced the appointment of Vince Thomas as General Manager of the Florence Center in Florence, South Carolina.

Thomas is a seasoned venue management executive with 18+ years of experience leading operations across arenas, stadiums, performing arts centers, convention facilities and major entertainment destinations. He brings expertise in strategic leadership, financial oversight, facility management and the execution of high-profile sports and entertainment events.

Thomas previously served as Assistant General Manager and director of events at the Florence Center, where he provided leadership across event operations, security, production and facility initiatives. His leadership has also been instrumental in preparing the facility for the return of professional hockey to the Pee Dee region.

Prior to joining the Florence Center, Thomas held senior leadership positions at several prominent venues nationwide, including the historic Paramount Theatre in Oakland, California; Bert Ogden Arena and H-E-B Park in South Texas; MainGate merchandising and hospitality; and the NCAA Hall of Champions and Convention Center in Indianapolis.

His professional portfolio as an event manager includes experience supporting major national and international events and organizations, including The Masters, Super Bowl LII, the NCAA, NBA, Wrestlemania, Live Nation, AEG, and other leading sports and entertainment properties.

“Having had the opportunity to work with Vince in his role as Assistant General Manager at the Florence Center, we are thrilled to see him step into the General Manager position,” stated Julie Maxham, Chairman of the Florence City-County Civic Center Commission, which owns and oversees the Florence Center. “While we will certainly miss Rex Berman, we are confident that the strong foundation he built during his time as General Manager and the knowledge he has shared with Vince will ensure a smooth transition and continued success for the Florence Center. We look forward to all that Vince will accomplish in this new role.”

“Vince brings an exceptional track record in venue management and a deep understanding of the Florence Center and the Pee Dee community,” added Josh Kritzler, President of North American Venues & Content, Legends Global, which manages the venue. “His extensive experience and proven operational expertise will serve the Florence Center and the region well. We look forward to his leadership as the venue continues to grow and deliver outstanding experiences for guests and clients.”

As General Manager, Thomas will lead the Florence Center’s strategic direction, operational performance, business development, and community engagement efforts. His leadership will focus on expanding the venue’s competitive position, strengthening regional partnerships, elevating guest experience and maximizing the facility’s economic and cultural impact throughout Florence and the region.

“I am honored to step into this role at the Florence Center and build on the incredible momentum this venue has created for the Pee Dee region,” continued Thomas in the announcement. “With the return of professional hockey and a growing calendar of world-class events, I look forward to working alongside our team and community partners to deliver exceptional experiences and elevate the Florence Center as a premier destination.” Located in Florence, South Carolina, the Florence Center is the largest convention, entertainment and exhibition facility in northeastern South Carolina. Home of the Pee Dee IceCats, it features a 10,000-seat arena and more than 79,000 square feet of event space hosting concerts, sporting events, conventions, trade shows, family entertainment and community gatherings.
