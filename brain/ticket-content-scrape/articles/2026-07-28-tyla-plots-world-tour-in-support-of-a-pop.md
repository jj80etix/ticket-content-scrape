# Tyla Plots World Tour in Support of A*POP

## Summary

Singer Tyla announced a world tour in support of her album A*POP. Standard artist tour-announcement coverage with presale/resale boilerplate.

### Key Points
- Tyla announced a world tour supporting her album A*POP.
- Coverage includes standard presale-signup and onsale information.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/tyla.jpg)

South African star Tyla is officially bringing her sophomore record *A\*PO* P across the globe.

The 34-date “A\*POP World Tour” kicks-off at the Zenith Paris, followed by gigs in Amsterdam, Brussels, Oslo, and Copenhagen. She’ll head to North America next, stopping in cities like San Francisco, Chicago, Toronto, Houston, and Los Angeles before appearing in Lagos and Cape Town. The massive run wraps-up in Johannesburg at the Expo Centre, Nasrec on January 9, 2027.

Tickets head on sale starting with an artist presale Wednesday, July 29, followed by a general on sale Friday, July 31. Fans can sign up for early access [here](https://www.tylaworld.com/).

Tyla previously plotted a world tour back in 2024 to support her debut, self-titled record, however, the run was ultimately called-off due to an injury.
