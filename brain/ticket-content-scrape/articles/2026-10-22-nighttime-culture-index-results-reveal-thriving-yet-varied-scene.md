# Nighttime Culture Index results reveal ‘thriving’ yet varied scene

## Summary

Amsterdam Dance Event and VibeLab launched the first Nighttime Culture Index, measuring the cultural (not just economic) value of nightlife across six global cities.

### Key Points
- Index covers Amsterdam, Lagos, New York, São Paulo, Seoul and Sydney, launched during ADE's 30th edition.
- Methodology combines research, interviews, data, a public survey and focus groups.
- Results describe the nighttime culture scene as "thriving" overall but varied significantly by city.
- Article is paywalled past the opening summary; city-by-city scores not available in the excerpt.
