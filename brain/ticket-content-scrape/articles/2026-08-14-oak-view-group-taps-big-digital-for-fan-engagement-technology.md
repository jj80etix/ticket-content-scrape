# Oak View Group Taps BIG Digital for Fan Engagement Technology

## Summary

Oak View Group named BIG Digital its preferred partner for portable fan-engagement displays and sustainable (solar/ePaper) signage across its North American venues. The deal covers wayfinding, live event info, contests, sponsorship activations and venue communications, with BIG Digital's ePaper tech claimed to use up to 99% less energy than standard digital displays.

### Key Points
- OVG names BIG Digital preferred Portable Fan Engagement Technology and Sustainable Display Technology partner.
- Portable displays/kiosks can be deployed without permanent infrastructure in entrances, concourses, lounges and plazas.
- Focus areas: fan experience, sponsorship opportunities, and modernized venue communications.
- ePaper displays are pitched as a low-energy alternative to printed signage, remotely updatable.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/Oak-View-Group-e1728427226180.webp)

Oak View Group is teaming up with BIG Digital as it looks to bring more flexible, interactive technology into its North American venues.

OVG has named BIG Digital its preferred Portable Fan Engagement Technology and Sustainable Display Technology partner, giving the companies a framework to explore new digital experiences across the venue operator’s portfolio.

The partnership will focus on three areas: improving the fan experience, creating new sponsorship opportunities and modernizing venue communications.

BIG Digital’s portable displays and interactive kiosks can be placed throughout venues without the need for permanent infrastructure. That means screens can pop up in entrances, concourses, premium lounges, outdoor plazas and other high-traffic areas depending on the event.

The technology can be used for wayfinding, live event information, contests, gamification and sponsor activations.

“OVG is always looking for technologies that improve the guest experience while creating meaningful value for venues and partners,” said Dan Griffis, President, Global Partnerships at Oak View Group. “BIG Digital’s combination of fan engagement capabilities, sponsorship activation solutions and sustainability-focused technologies aligns well with our vision for the future of venue operations.”

Sustainability is another major piece of the deal. BIG Digital will also serve as OVG’s preferred Sustainable Display Technology partner, with the company touting portable and solar-powered ePaper displays as an alternative to temporary printed signage. According to BIG Digital, its remotely managed ePaper technology can use up to 99% less energy than traditional digital displays while allowing venues to update messaging on the fly.

“BIG Digital is bringing OVG the future of sustainable event signage, featuring portable and solar-powered ePaper solutions designed to eliminate print waste and redefine sustainable venue communications,” said BIG Digital CEO Jake Neiman.

For sponsors, the technology could also create more ways to reach fans beyond traditional static signage. BIG Digital’s systems can support interactive campaigns and measurable engagement, giving brands additional activation opportunities throughout an event.

The company has previously worked with organizations across sports and entertainment including the NFL, NBA, PGA TOUR, Live Nation, Maple Leaf Sports & Entertainment and Tennis Canada.

“Partnering with Oak View Group represents an exciting opportunity to help shape the future of fan engagement across some of the industry’s most respected venues,” said Matthew Diamond, Chief Growth Officer of BIG Digital.

He added that the partnership is aimed at delivering “better experiences for fans, stronger opportunities for sponsors and more flexible tools for venue operators.”

OVG and BIG Digital will now look for opportunities to deploy the technology across OVG venues and properties throughout North America, with an eye toward making venue communications more dynamic — and giving fans and sponsors more ways to interact once they walk through the doors.
