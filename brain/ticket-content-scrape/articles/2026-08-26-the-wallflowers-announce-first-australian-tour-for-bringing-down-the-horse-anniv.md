# The Wallflowers Announce First Australian Tour for ‘Bringing Down the Horse’ Anniversary

## Summary

The Wallflowers will make their Australian debut this December with a five-date tour celebrating the 30th anniversary of their album "Bringing Down the Horse," performing it in full at each stop. The run begins at Melbourne's Palais Theatre December 4 and concludes in Adelaide December 9. A promoter presale starts August 26, with general onsale August 28.

### Key Points
- Five Australian dates, December 4–9, across Melbourne, Thirroul, Sydney, Brisbane, and Adelaide.
- Full album performance of the 1996 breakthrough "Bringing Down the Horse."
- Presale August 26; general onsale August 28.
- Shows will also feature a complete Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers set.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/The-Wallflowers-Bringing-Down-the-Horse-.webp)

The Wallflowers will make their Australian debut this December with a five-date tour celebrating the 30th anniversary of their breakthrough album, *Bringing Down the Horse*.

The tour will see the band perform the 1996 album in its entirety at each Australian show. The run will begin at Melbourne’s Palais Theatre on Friday, December 4, before continuing to Anita’s Theatre in Thirroul on December 5 and Liberty Hall in Sydney on December 6.

The band will then travel to Brisbane for a performance at Fortitude Music Hall on December 8 before concluding the tour at Adelaide’s Hindley Street Music Hall on December 9.

A promoter presale is scheduled to begin Wednesday, August 26, with tickets going on sale to the general public Friday, August 28.

Released May 21, 1996, *Bringing Down the Horse* became a defining release for The Wallflowers and helped establish the band as a major act during the 1990s. The album includes songs such as “One Headlight,” “6th Avenue Heartache” and “The Difference.”

The Australian shows will also feature a complete performance of Tom Petty and the Heartbreakers’ 1982 album *Long After Dark*. The band has described Petty as an important influence on frontman Jakob Dylan, who inducted the late singer into the Rock and Roll Hall of Fame in 2002.

The upcoming Australian dates follow The Wallflowers’ recent touring activity in the United States. The group supported Eric Clapton during his 2025 U.S. tour, adding to a history of opening for major artists that also includes Clapton, The Who and The Rolling Stones.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [The Wallflowers’ official website](https://www.wallflowersmusic.com/).
