# Tech Stack: Vivenu, XS2Event and more…

## Summary

TheTicketingBusiness's weekly tech round-up covers four vendor deals: Vivenu signed with University of Indianapolis athletics; Ticketmaster was named official ticketing partner for the 2027 World Bowls Championship; XS2Event became Sunderland AFC's official sportsbreak-ticketing supplier; and The Kallang (Singapore) extended a five-year digital-experience partnership with Deloitte.

### Key Points
- Vivenu ↔ University of Indianapolis athletics: streamlined mobile ticketing plus sales/event-management tools.
- Ticketmaster named official ticketing partner of the 2027 World Bowls Championship (Royal Leamington Spa, England).
- XS2Event ↔ Sunderland AFC (Premier League): multi-year deal covering sportsbreak/travel-bundled ticketing; XS2Event also works with LaLiga, FC Barcelona, Dutch Grand Prix.
- The Kallang (formerly Singapore Sports Hub) extended a five-year digital partnership with Deloitte Singapore, already delivering a mobile app and refreshed website.

### Highlights
- Four distinct competitive-platform wins/partnerships in a single week signal continued vendor land-grab across university athletics, niche sports federations, top-flight football clubs, and multi-venue precincts.
- XS2Event's travel-industry integrations (TUI, Viator, Trip.com) point to bundled ticketing+travel as a differentiator worth tracking.

### Industry Problem
Competing primary-ticketing vendors are winning deals by pairing core ticketing with adjacent value — mobile-first UX for athletics departments, travel-bundle integration for sports clubs, and digital-experience overhauls for multi-venue precincts — areas where a straightforward ticketing stack alone is no longer sufficient to win or retain accounts.

### Proposed Solution
Track Vivenu and XS2Event as mid-tier primary-platform competitors expanding via vertical-specific bundles (collegiate athletics, sportsbreak travel); consider whether Etix's own account wins would benefit from similar adjacent bundling (travel partners, event-management tooling) rather than ticketing alone.
