# Conference news: SoAlive and Radar Madrid reveal lineups for 2026

## Summary

IQ Magazine rounds up conference programming announcements across the European and international live-music circuit: SoAlive (Sofia), Radar Madrid's inaugural edition, a new Los Angeles edition of Italy's Hit Week, Nürnberg Pop, Beyond the Music (Manchester), and Music Cities Convention Alberta. Each item lists newly confirmed speakers, dates, and thematic sessions rather than any ticketing, technology, or regulatory development. It's a routine trade-press digest aimed at industry professionals tracking the conference calendar.

### Key Points
- SoAlive returns to Sofia with speakers including Warner Music Group's Scott Cohen and Beggars Group's Ruth Barlow, riding a wave of attention from Bulgaria's 2026 Eurovision win.
- Radar Madrid debuts 19-21 October at Movistar Arena with ~50 confirmed industry figures, including festival programmers from Coachella and Montreux Jazz Festival.
- Hit Week stages its first Los Angeles edition (5-10 October), backed by the Italian Trade Agency, to connect Italian and US industry professionals.
- Nürnberg Pop (8-10 October) will run sessions on entering the German market and on "Social Music and Artificial Intelligence."
- Beyond the Music (Manchester, October) lines up speakers including LIVE CEO Jon Collins, UK PM Andy Burnham, and actor Steve Coogan.
- Music Cities Convention Alberta (Calgary, 29 September-1 October) adds a second cohort of speakers from city music offices and the Recording Academy.

Bulgaria-based industry conference **SoAlive** has revealed its “strongest lineup yet” for its return to Sofia, which organisers said reflects its “growing position within the wider European music industry calendar”.

As part of the first wave of international speakers, the conference will welcome Scott Cohen, chief innovation officer at Warner Music Group, UK-based label executive Nick Halkes, Ruth Barlow, director of live licensing at Beggars Group, and Paul Schäfer, head of brand and festival experience at Lollapalooze Berlin and Superbloom.

The event will also host a session with X-Ray Touring’s Jake Nevens and Toucan Talent Agency’s Dave Jennings on the international live booking sector.

Organisers said the Balkan music industry has been placed in the spotlight since Bulgaria’s victory at this year’s Eurovision Song Contest

“In May, Bulgaria captured its first-ever Eurovision Song Contest victory when DARA’s *Bangaranga* won both jury and public vote in Vienna — the first entry to achieve that double triumph since 2017,” the conference said. “The result places Bulgaria and the wider Balkan region firmly at the centre of Europe’s music conversation ahead of the 2027 Eurovision Song Contest, which will be hosted in Bulgaria.”

Also coming up in October, **Radar Madrid** has announced a slate of 50 industry figures that will lead its programme for its upcoming inaugural event at the city’s Movistar Arena between 19-21 October.

Precise programming details, including session topics and mastercalss speakers, will be published soon, organisers said. However, confirmed speakers and panelists include festival professionals including Pilar Collado of Lollapalooza Argentina, Henry Huerta of Coachella, and David Torreblanca of Montreux Jazz Festival Foundation.

Organisers say the conference aims to become “a strategic meeting point between the Spanish music industry and the global market”.

At the same time, the conference has revealed its first wave of strategic partnership from across the music industry, with the conference saying: “Partners include organisations such as WOMEX, WBM, SoAlive Festival, and the UK & European Guild of Music Supervisors, as well as industry collaborators like ACCESS, PROVENUE, UFI, SGAE, and Madrid en Vivo,” the event said.

Elsewhere, **Hit Week** has revealed it will stage a Los Angeles edition of its annual festival and conference from 5-10 October.

The event is supported by the Italian Music Federation and the Italian Trade Agency, and calls itself a “synchronisation mission” to connect Italian and international industry professionals and promote Italian music.

The event aims to “bring together in Los Angeles managers of major Italian record labels and leading US operators in audiovisual, advertising, and gaming, in important vertical networking sessions to present the Italian repertoire, fostering direct contacts and potential commercial partnerships between the markets,” organisers said.

At the same time between 8-10 October, **Nürnberg Pop** will return with its just-announced first wave of programming. The event will include sessions titled ‘How to Enter the German Music Market’ and ‘Social Music and Artificial Intelligence’, among others, as well as market focuses on Austrian and Faroese music.

Meanwhile, Manchester-based music and media conference **Beyond the Music** has revealed speakers and programme details for its October edition.

Speakers include managers such as Kneecap’s Dan Lambert and Yungblud’s Tommas Arnby, as well as LIVE CEO Jon Collins, as well as Louder Than War’s John Robb, and Generator and Interval Records’ Josh Daniel.

Meanwhile, actor Steve Coogan and UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham will each speak at wide-ranging creative industry sessions.

“The 2026 programme, titled *Life, Liberty and the Pursuit of Happiness,* will embody Beyond The Music ’s core ethos: to raise, discuss, and solve the biggest challenges impacting the creative industries in the present day,” organisers said.

Finally, **Music Cities Convention Alberta** has also revealed its second cohort of speakers ahead of its event in Calgary between 29 September and 1 October.

The lineup includes Chip Adams (Texas Music  Office), Cory Crossman (London  Music Office and UNESCO City of Music), Moira Demorest (Kingston, Ontario Music Office), Andrea Dempster-Chung (Kingston Creative, Jamaica), Todd Dupler (Recording Academy), and Juanita Hardy (Tiger Management Consulting Group).
