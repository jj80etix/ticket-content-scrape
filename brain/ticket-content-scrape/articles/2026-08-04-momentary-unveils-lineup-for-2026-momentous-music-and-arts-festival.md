# Momentary Unveils Lineup For 2026 Momentous Music And Arts Festival

## Summary

The Momentary unveiled its day-by-day lineup for Momentous 2026, its fourth annual two-day electronic music and arts festival in Bentonville, Arkansas, November 6-7. The lineup is headlined by Sofi Tukker, The Faint and Cut Copy, with single-day and two-day tickets now on sale.

### Key Points
- Nov. 6 lineup (indoor, RØDE House/galleries): The Faint, Hiroko Yamamura, Cut Copy (DJ set), J.Worra, VNSSA, plus local Arkansas acts.
- Nov. 7 lineup (outdoor, the Green): headliner Sofi Tukker, Channel Tres, Sextile, X-Coast and others.
- Curated in partnership with Arkansas promoters On the Map and Solsets; builds on Momentary's 2026 outdoor series (Chris Lake, Zedd).

The Momentary unveiled the day-by-day lineups for Momentous 2026, the venue’s annual two-day electronic music and arts festival returning to Bentonville, Arkansas, Nov. 6-7.

Now in its fourth year, the festival brings together international electronic artists and rising talent for a weekend celebrating music and culture. Single day and two-day tickets are now on sale.

On Nov. 6, the festival kicks off inside the Momentary as music takes over the state-of-the-art RØDE House and transforms the museum’s galleries into dance floors. The lineup features The Faint, Hiroko Yamamura, Cut Copy (DJ set), J.Worra and VNSSA along with local artists St. Bends, Starflyer, Messy Sparkles, Anoc and KSoul.

On Nov. 7, Momentous expands outdoors to the Momentary’s Green, adding an open-air stage for an evening headlined by Grammy-nominated duo Sofi Tukker. The lineup also includes Channel Tres (DJ set), Sextile, X-Coast, Annie-Claude Deschênes, Carlitos and Trustory. The night concludes with an after-party by Lucino and ANTIMATTER, and a special after-party by Channel Tres available to premium ticketholders. This year’s lineup was curated in partnership with Arkansas-based promoters On the Map and Solsets.

Rapidly becoming one of Arkansas’s most popular music festivals, Momentous blends bass, house and techno into an immersive weekend celebrating sound, art and flavor.

This year, the festival expands its sonic scope with the addition of bands incorporating electronic elements, creating the event’s most diverse lineup to date.

The festival reflects Momentary’s continued investment in electronic music programming. Alongside Momentous, the venue’s 2026 outdoor music series featured performances by Chris Lake and Zedd, building on a growing legacy that has welcomed EDM icons including deadmau5 and Kaskade.
