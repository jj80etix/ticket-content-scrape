# AXS, FC Dallas Extend Ticketing Partnership After More Than a Decade

## Summary

AXS and Major League Soccer club FC Dallas have renewed their ticketing partnership, extending a relationship that has already run more than a decade. Under the new deal AXS remains FC Dallas's official ticketing provider, continuing to supply mobile ticket management, data and analytics tools, integrated marketing capabilities, and access to its distribution network, with AXS Mobile ID staying central to the fan-facing experience.

### Key Points
- AXS and FC Dallas extended a ticketing partnership that has lasted more than 10 years.
- The renewal covers mobile ticket management, data/analytics, integrated marketing, and AXS's distribution network.
- AXS Mobile ID remains the primary mechanism for fans to buy, manage, and access tickets.
- FC Dallas VP of ticket sales Kris Katseanes and AXS VP David McElwee both framed the deal as reinforcing a secure, modern matchday experience.
- The renewal adds to AXS's broader footprint across soccer, sports, and live entertainment ticketing.

AXS and FC Dallas are staying together.

The ticketing company and Major League Soccer club have renewed a partnership that has already lasted more than a decade, extending AXS’ role as the team’s official ticketing provider.

Under the new agreement, AXS will supply FC Dallas with mobile ticket management, data and analytics tools, integrated marketing capabilities and access to its distribution network.

“Creating a strong matchday experience begins well before fans arrive at the stadium, and ticketing plays an important role in that journey,” Kris Katseanes, vice president for ticket sales at FC Dallas, said in a press release. “AXS has been a trusted partner for FC Dallas for more than 10 years, and we look forward to continuing our work together to provide fans with reliable, secure and convenient access to our matches and other live events.”
