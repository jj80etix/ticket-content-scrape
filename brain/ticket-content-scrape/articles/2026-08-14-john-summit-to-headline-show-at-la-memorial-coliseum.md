# John Summit To Headline Show At LA Memorial Coliseum

## Summary

DJ John Summit added a Dec. 12 stadium date at the LA Memorial Coliseum — his biggest headline show yet — to his "CTRL ESCAPE" tour, with general on-sale Aug. 20. Access is tiered through multiple presale channels: a Cash App Visa Card presale (Aug. 17) unlocked via card digits, and a Ticketmaster-run early-access presale for U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers notified directly by Spotify.

### Key Points
- Dec. 12 show at LA Memorial Coliseum is Summit's biggest headline date to date; general on-sale Aug. 20, 10am PT.
- Cash App Visa Card presale Aug. 17, unlocked using the first nine digits of the card number.
- Spotify Premium subscribers get a Ticketmaster-run early-access window (two tickets) ahead of general on-sale, notified via Spotify email.
- Booked as part of a broader October tour hitting Capital One Arena, Dickies Arena, United Center and Oakland Arena.

### Highlights
- Two distinct affinity-gated presale channels stack ahead of general on-sale: a payment-card-linked presale (Cash App Visa) and a streaming-subscription-linked presale (Spotify Premium, run through Ticketmaster).
- Spotify directly emails eligible subscribers, showing a platform (not artist/venue) driving presale distribution — a template for subscription-tied ticket access.

### Industry Problem
Presale access is increasingly layered and vendor-mediated (payment brand, streaming subscription, fan club, venue, artist), which can improve targeted marketing reach for artists/platforms but adds complexity patrons must navigate to find their best access window.

### Proposed Solution
Evolve/Monitor: track whether subscription- and payment-brand-linked presales (Spotify/Cash App-style) become a standard secondary access tier beyond fan clubs; if Etix clients want to replicate this, a build response would be lightweight presale-partner integrations (payment brand or loyalty-program gated codes) rather than a full subscription product.

On the heels of a headline set at Lollapalooza in his hometown of Chicago, John Summit is ready to perform in front of another large crowd. The DJ added a stadium date to his “CTRL ESCAPE” tour at the historic Los Angeles Memorial Coliseum, his biggest headline show to date.

The show takes place Dec. 12 and goes on sale to the general public Aug. 20 at 10 a.m. PT. Presale tickets will be available for Cash App Visa Card customers on Aug. 17 at 10 a.m. PT, and they can unlock access to tickets using the first nine digits of their Cash App Card. Visit [cash.app/card/presales](https://cash.app/card/presales) for more information.

There will also be tickets available for Summit’s U.S. Spotify Premium subscribers through Ticketmaster. Eligible fans will be notified by Spotify via email and will have an opportunity to purchase two tickets before the general onsale. Fans can sign up for early access by visiting [johnsummit.lnk.to/lacoliseum](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/14/john-summit-to-headline-show-at-la-memorial-coliseum/).

Following shows in England and South America and festival appearances, Summit is embarking on a major tour this October, visiting venues like Hamilton’s TD Coliseum, Capital One Arena in D.C., Fort Worth’s Dickies Arena, United Center in Chicago and Oakland Arena in the Bay Area.

The shows promise to create “a larger-than-life atmosphere and immersive experience” for Summit’s next creative era. “Fans can expect a high-impact production to elevate every moment of the show,” according to a release announcing the Coliseum show.

Summit is booked by Ben Shprits, Daisy Hoffman and Nick Cave of the THE·TEAM.
