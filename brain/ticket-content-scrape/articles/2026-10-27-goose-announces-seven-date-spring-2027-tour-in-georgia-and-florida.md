# Goose Announces Seven-Date Spring 2027 Tour in Georgia and Florida

## Summary

Goose announced seven spring 2027 shows across Georgia and Florida amphitheaters, marking the jam band's first confirmed 2027 headlining dates.

### Key Points
- Run begins April 9 at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre, Alpharetta, GA (two nights).
- Continues to Hollywood, FL and a three-night St. Augustine Amphitheatre stand.
- Closes April 18 at The BayCare Sound, Clearwater, FL.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Goose-2027-tour-dates-1200.webp)

Goose has announced seven concerts for spring 2027, with the jam band planning a run through Georgia and Florida that includes two-night and three-night engagements at outdoor amphitheaters.

The newly announced dates begin April 9 at Ameris Bank Amphitheatre in Alpharetta, Georgia, where the band will perform two consecutive nights. Goose will then travel to Florida for a show at Hard Rock Live in Hollywood on April 13, followed by three performances at St. Augustine Amphitheatre from April 15-17. The brief tour concludes April 18 at The BayCare Sound in Clearwater.

The announcement marks the Connecticut-based band’s first confirmed headlining dates for 2027, extending a touring schedule that has included increasingly prominent venues and multiple-night engagements across the country.

The April schedule includes five performances across two multi-night stops, continuing the band’s emphasis on extended engagements that allow fans to attend consecutive shows in the same market.

The band is also continuing its charitable partnership with Western Sun Foundation. One dollar from each ticket sold for the spring tour will benefit the volunteer-run nonprofit, which provides support to smaller charitable organizations in communities visited by Goose fans.

## Goose Spring 2027 Tickets and Presales

Fans can register for the artist presale through Tuesday, October 27, at [Goose’s official tour website](https://www.goosetheband.com/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Registered fans will receive a unique access code for the artist presale, which begins Wednesday, October 28, at 10 a.m. ET.

Live Nation, Ticketmaster and local presales begin Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. ET. All presales conclude that evening at 10 p.m. ET.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. ET.
