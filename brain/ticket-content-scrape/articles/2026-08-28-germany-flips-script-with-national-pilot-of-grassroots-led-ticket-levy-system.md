# Germany flips script with national pilot of grassroots-led ticket levy system

## Summary

Germany's live-music industry is piloting a nationwide expansion of Club Euro, a voluntary "solidarity" ticket levy that funds the country's venue ecosystem, after viability concerns dogged the national scheme launched in January. The rollout, funded by Initiative Musik and led by the Federal Foundation for Live Culture, builds on a model first tested in Cologne.

### Key Points
- Club Euro pilot expands nationally, inviting clubs, venues, and organizers to participate.
- Follows a January national rollout of a voluntary levy that industry figures said had stalled by May.
- Funded by Initiative Musik; led by the Federal Foundation for Live Culture.
