# EXIT vows Serbia return as country’s live scene celebrates election result

## Summary

EXIT Festival has pledged a return to Serbia after a student-led election result unseated President Aleksandar Vučić, whose administration had been accused by Serbia's live industry of cracking down on artistic freedom and blacklisting artists who supported student protests. Serbian live-sector leaders welcomed the political change.

### Key Points
- EXIT Festival had paused its home-country event amid alleged government crackdowns on artists.
- A parliamentary election on Oct. 25 ended Vučić's administration.
- Serbian live-industry figures see the result as removing political barriers to the festival's return.
