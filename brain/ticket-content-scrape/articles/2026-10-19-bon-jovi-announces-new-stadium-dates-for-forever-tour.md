# Bon Jovi Announces New Stadium Dates For ‘Forever Tour’

## Summary

Bon Jovi is adding six stadium shows plus a BST Hyde Park festival date to its 2027 Forever Tour, following a 2026 comeback run that grossed $31.4 million across nine sold-out Madison Square Garden shows. The new dates span Australia, the UK and North America, with Rogers Stadium in Toronto closing the run Aug. 4.

### Key Points
- 2026 MSG run: 9 shows, $31.4M gross, 126,097 tickets (Pollstar Boxoffice).
- New 2027 dates: Melbourne, Sydney, BST Hyde Park London, MetLife Stadium, Soldier Field, Gillette Stadium, Rogers Stadium Toronto.
- North American presale opens 10 a.m. local Oct. 28; general onsale Oct. 30.
- Booked by Christopher Dalston (CAA); managed by Full Stop.

## Transcript

![image (3)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/image-3.png)

Rock and Roll Hall of Famers Bon Jovi will bring the “Forever Tour” to six more stadiums — and BST Hyde Park — next year.

The iconic band returned to the stage in 2026 with a sold-out run of nine shows at Madison Square Garden, three at Wembley Stadium and one-offs at both Dublin’s Croke Park and Edinburgh’s Murrayfields. The nine Garden shows grossed a combined $31.4 million on 126,097 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice reports.

The 2027 dates begin March 4 at Melbourne’s Marvel Stadium, followed by a play at Accor Stadium in Sydney March 11. Following an appearance at BST Hyde Park June 24, Bon Jovi heads to North America for a July 15 show at MetLife Stadium, a July 21 play at Soldier Field, a July 28 performance at Gillette Stadium with the finale coming at Toronto’s Rogers Stadium Aug. 4.

For the North American dates, presale registration is open now at BonJovi.com for the presale that begins at 10 a.m. local Oct. 28 and general onsale 10 a.m. Oct. 30.

Ticket information for the UK and Australian dates are available at BonJovi.com.

Bon Jovi is booked by Christopher Dalston at CAA and managed by Full Stop.
