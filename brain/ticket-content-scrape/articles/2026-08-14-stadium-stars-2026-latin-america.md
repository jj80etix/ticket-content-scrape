# Stadium Stars 2026: Latin America

## Summary

IQ Magazine's 2026 Stadium Stars series continues with three Latin American venue profiles: Bogotá's Estadio El Campín (soon to be rebuilt at 50,000 capacity with a retractable roof), Mexico City's redeveloped Estadio GNP Seguros (65,000 capacity, reopened 2024), and Nubank Parque. The piece is an industry-recognition feature on top-tier touring infrastructure rather than a news event.

### Key Points
- El Campín (Bogotá, 60,000 cap.) will be demolished and rebuilt at 50,000 capacity with a retractable roof; projected annual attendance could rise from 4m to 10m.
- Estadio GNP Seguros (Mexico City, 65,000 cap., operator OCESA) reopened in 2024 with permanently integrated production infrastructure after a major redevelopment from the former Foro Sol.
- Selections are industry-nominated as part of IQ's Global Stadium Report 2026.

**Estadio El Campín** | Bogotá, Colombia

*Concert capacity*: 60,000  
*Operator*: Instituto Distrital de Recreación y Deporte

Estadio Nemesio Camacho El Campín (El Campín for short) has been a defining landmark of Bogotá since its inauguration in 1938. It was originally conceived as a modernist concrete structure, but over time, it has evolved into a 39,500-capacity venue that balances football fanfare and major concerts.

Located in the urban district of Teusaquillo, El Campín is deeply integrated into Bogotá’s culture. The stadium itself features four main stands, VIP boxes, press areas, concessions, and modern broadcast infrastructure, with multiple entry gates designed to accommodate high-capacity crowds. Despite its legacy, El Campín is now scheduled for demolition as part of a redevelopment plan to capitalise on Bogotá’s cultural cachet.

A new 50,000-capacity El Campín will be constructed on an adjacent site and is set to feature a retractable roof and year-round capability for all kinds of events. Once that venue is live, the existing stadium will be demolished (with the available land to be transformed into a mixed-use cultural and commercial complex) meaning there will be no lapse in concert-hosting capacity. With projected annual attendance expected to rise from 4m to as many as 10m, the redevelopment is set to transform El Campín into one of Latin America’s most significant cultural and entertainment hubs.

**Estadio GNP Seguros** | Mexico City, Mexico

*Concert capacity*: 65,000  
*Operator*: OCESA

Reopening its doors just two years ago, Estadio GNP Seguros has swiftly reestablished itself as one of the world’s premier live music destinations. Formerly the Foro Sol, the venue underwent a major redevelopment that transformed its concert-hosting abilities, reopening in 2024 with a renewed focus on production efficiency and the fan experience.

The stadium now features permanently integrated infrastructure, including stages, dressing rooms, and press facilities, eliminating the need for temporary builds and streamlining event operations. A large retractable roof provides reliable weather protection while also supporting rainwater collection and reuse, an important sustainability feature in the local environment. Engineered for major international productions, the venue allows simultaneous loading and unloading of more than 40 trucks, enabling fast turnarounds between shows. Its structure is designed to support heavy touring rigs while maintaining unobstructed sight lines from every seat, maximising both capacity and the audience experience.

With these upgrades, Estadio GNP Seguros has quickly cemented its position as a key stop on the global touring circuit, attracting the world’s biggest artists and reinforcing Mexico City’s status as one of the most important live entertainment markets in the world.

**Nubank Parque** | São Paulo, Brazil

*Concert capacity*: 50,000  
*Operator*: WTorre Entretenimento

In the heart of São Paulo, Nubank Parque has established itself as one of Brazil’s premier stadium destinations for live music and sport. Purpose-built with concerts in mind, the stadium features a plug-and-play infrastructure with distributed power and connectivity points, acoustics tailored for large-scale music events, and modular back-of-house areas designed for efficient load-ins and rapid changeovers. Dedicated dock access further streamlines touring logistics, ensuring compatibility with major international productions.

Nubank Parque has hosted a steady run of global tours and festivals, to be strengthened by a recent ten-year deal with Live Nation, with the entertainment giant to bring roughly 150 shows across the next ten years from both international and Brazilian artists. With the Live Nation deal, which complements the pre-existing deal with independent promoter 30e, the venue (FKA Allianz Parque) is now entering a new era with its new, long-term naming rights partnership with Nubank, which will introduce new premium fan experiences, including exclusive lounges and VIP access points.

With its future looking bright, the stadium looks to remain a cornerstone of Brazil’s live entertainment landscape.
