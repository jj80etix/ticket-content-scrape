# The Maine Celebrating 20th Anniversary At 8123 Fest Along With Headliners The Band Camino & Andrew McMahon

## Summary

The Maine's 8123 Festival returns to Arizona's Mesa Amphitheater January 15-16, 2027, marking the band's 20th anniversary with headliners The Band Camino and Andrew McMahon and the Strings Attached Quartet, plus a charity day and pop-up shop January 18. General-admission bundles and two-day passes are already on sale, with single-day passes launching August 7.

### Key Points
- Two-day lineup includes The Band Camino, Andrew McMahon and the Strings Attached Quartet, The Academy Is…, Beach Weather, The Aces, The Summer Set, and more.
- Festival marks The Maine's 20th anniversary as a band; single-day passes go on sale Aug. 7 via 8123fest.com.
- Additional side shows will be announced later, alongside a charity day and pop-up shop Jan. 18.
- The Maine is also touring the West Coast this month, Europe in September, and the U.S. again in November ahead of the festival.

The Maine’s 8123 Festival will return to Arizona’s Mesa Amphitheater in January, and it promises to be the biggest one yet as the band celebrates its 20 <sup>th</sup> anniversary.

Curated by The Maine, comprising John O’Callaghan, Kennedy Brock, Jared Monaco, Garrett Nickelsen and Pat Kirch, the 2027 edition of 8123 Festival taking place Jan. 15-16 is headlined by the host band and will also feature performances by The Band Camino, Andrew McMahon and the Strings Attached Quartet, The Academy Is…, Beach Weather, The Aces, The Summer Seat and more.

In addition to the main festival, there will be side shows set to be announced at a later date. There will also be a pop-up shop and charity day taking place Jan. 18.

“Standing at the edge of 20 years as a band and that free-fall feeling of weightlessness is beginning to well up as the roller coaster climbs,” O’Callaghan said in a statement. “To say I’m excited would be the biggest understatement, and now we can finally answer the most-asked question of the year so far: 8123 FEST is a GO. So it’s time to shed the parkas for a light cardigan, clean off your golf clubs (I heard that’s a sport some people play) and mentally prepare for the most beautifully manic weekend that The Maine can provide. Lineup is juiced. Vibes are sky high. And YEAR 20 + 8123 FEST will make for some historic shit. Giddy up!”

General admission bundles and two-day tickets for 8123 Fest are now available, and single-day passes go on sale Aug. 7. Visit [8123fest.com](https://www.8123fest.com/) for more information.

Earlier this year, the band wrapped its first U.S. headline tour in nearly two years, which included a show at Chicago’s The Salt Shed that grossed $143,645 off 3,265 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. The quintet ready to hit the road again this month with a brief run of West Coast shows before embarking on a European trek in September. The Maine makes their way back to U.S. venues in November. Visit [themaineband.com/tour](https://www.themaineband.com/tour) for tickets.

The Maine are booked by Creative Artists Agency in all territories excluding the U.K. and Europe. X-Ray Touring reps the band in the U.K. and Europe, and Eighty One Twenty Three manages the band.
