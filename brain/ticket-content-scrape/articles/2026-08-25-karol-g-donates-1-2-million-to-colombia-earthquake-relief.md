# Karol G Donates $1.2 Million to Colombia Earthquake Relief

## Summary

Karol G donated $1.2 million to Colombia earthquake relief through her Con Cora Foundation, announced at the "Colombia: Voces por la Vida" benefit concert in Bogotá. The event, featuring over 30 artists including Maluma and Sebastián Yatra, raised roughly $3.6 million total for families affected by the August 10 earthquake.

### Key Points
- Karol G's Con Cora Foundation donated $1.2 million to Colombian earthquake relief.
- Benefit concert "Colombia: Voces por la Vida" held at Vive Claro Distrito Cultural in Bogotá raised ~$3.6 million (11B+ Colombian pesos).
- More than 30 artists performed, including Maluma, Eladio Carrión, Greeicy, Draco Rosa, and Sebastián Yatra.
- Karol G performed remotely from California, playing "Coleccionando Heridas" and "Milagros."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Con-Cora-Foundation-Karol-G.webp)

Karol G has donated $1.2 million to earthquake relief efforts in Colombia through her Con Cora Foundation.

The donation was announced during *Colombia: Voces por la Vida*, a benefit concert held at the Vive Claro Distrito Cultural in Bogotá. The event raised more than 11 billion Colombian pesos, or approximately $3.6 million, for families affected by the 7.4-magnitude earthquake that struck the country on August 10.

More than 30 artists took part in the concert, including Maluma, Eladio Carrión, Greeicy, Draco Rosa and Sebastián Yatra. Karol G participated remotely from California, where she performed “Coleccionando Heridas” and “Milagros.”

The singer spoke about the difficulty of watching the disaster unfold from outside Colombia and said many people had struggled emotionally with the situation.

“Many of us — most of us, maybe all of us — have found these days incredibly difficult, seeing how our people are going through such a tough situation,” Karol G said during the broadcast. “We feel frustrated; it feels selfish to sleep, it feels wrong to be far away.”

She also praised the response from Colombians who have supported communities affected by the earthquake.

“At the same time, it’s been incredible to see how, in moments like these, we show who we are and what we Colombians are made of: love, solidarity, empathy, tolerance, the ability to come together, to support one another, to unite and to live as a community,” she said.
