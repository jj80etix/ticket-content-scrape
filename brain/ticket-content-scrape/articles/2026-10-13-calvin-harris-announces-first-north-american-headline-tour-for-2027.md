# Calvin Harris Announces First North American Headline Tour for 2027

## Summary

Calvin Harris announced his first-ever North American headlining tour, 18 arena shows across the US and Canada beginning April 1, 2027 in Boston and closing May 5 in Los Angeles. The run follows a record-setting sold-out Australian tour and sold-out hometown shows in Glasgow and Ireland earlier this year.

### Key Points
- First North American headline tour: 18 dates, April 1-May 5, 2027.
- Opens at TD Garden (Boston); closes at the Kia Forum (LA).
- Stops include Philadelphia, Brooklyn, Toronto, Austin, Chicago, Seattle, San Diego.
- Australian tour sold 330,000+ tickets across 11 dates ahead of this announcement.
- Earlier in 2026, drew 100,000+ fans in Glasgow and 210,000+ tickets across UK/Ireland.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Calvin-Harris-North-America-Tour-2027.webp)

Calvin Harris has announced his first headlining tour of North America, with the Scottish DJ and producer set to perform 18 arena shows across the United States and Canada in spring 2027.

The tour will begin April 1 at TD Garden in Boston. From there, Harris is scheduled to make stops in cities such as Baltimore, Philadelphia, Brooklyn, Toronto, Austin, Chicago, Seattle and San Diego. The run is slated to wrap up on May 5 in Los Angeles at the Kia Forum.

Notably, Harris enters the tour following a series of major international performances and strong ticket sales for his upcoming Australian shows. His recently announced Australian headline tour has sold more than 330,000 tickets across 11 dates, which the promoter describes as the largest headlining tour by an electronic artist in that market.

The producer also performed two sold-out hometown concerts at Hampden Park in Glasgow this summer, drawing more than 100,000 fans combined. Three additional sold-out shows in Ireland brought his reported UK and Ireland ticket sales to more than 210,000.

Harris received a Guinness World Records title for the largest attendance at a DJ set by a single artist after an estimated 1.6 million people attended his free performance during [São Paulo’s Carnival](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/calvin-harris-sao-paulo-carnival-set-breaks-attendance-record/) celebrations in February.

Harris has also continued making North American festival and nightclub appearances, including Head Trip in Indio, California, with additional performances scheduled in Las Vegas and Latin America ahead of the arena tour.

Tickets for the North American tour will first become available Monday, October 19, at 10 a.m. local time through Spotify’s Reserved by Spotify program.

An artist presale will follow Tuesday, October 20, beginning at noon local time.
