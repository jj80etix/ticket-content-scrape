# New UK Concert Series By Labyrinth Launches In 2027

## Summary

Independent UK promoter Labyrinth has announced a new open-air concert series, "Labyrinth at the Palace," launching in June 2027 at Fulham Palace in West London, with Australian band The Temper Trap as the first confirmed act.

### Key Points
- New open-air concert series launches June 2027 at Fulham Palace, London.
- The Temper Trap first act announced, playing London and a Manchester date.
- Independent promoter Labyrinth is the organizer; no ticketing-platform angle disclosed.

## Transcript

![FP FLY THROUGH BOVINGDONS 2025 frame at 1m26s](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/FP-FLY-THROUGH-BOVINGDONS-2025-frame-at-1m26s.jpg)

Fulham Palace, in West London, UK, site of a new concert series by independent promoter Labyrinth, set to launch next year.

Independent UK promoters Labyrinth announced a new open-air music series called Labyrinth at the Palace, taking place across a series of dates in mid June 2027, and set within the historic grounds of Fulham Palace, in West London, UK.

Australian band The Temper Trap are the first act announced, taking the stage Saturday June 12, 2027 for their first ever London open-air headline show, following a June 11, 2027 performance at Manchester’s Albert Hall.

The band stated, “The UK has always felt like a second home to us after spending eight years living in London. It’s a place that means a lot to us, so we’re really looking forward to coming back and playing these shows.”

Fulham Palace offers a rare escape from the pace of London’s city life, inviting visitors to slow down, explore and discover. With a history stretching back more than 1,300 years, the partly Tudor palace is surrounded by an enchanting walled garden, historic fruit orchards and London’s second-oldest botanical garden.

“A living landscape where history, nature and community have connected for centuries, the Palace offers a remarkable setting for a new kind of open-air music experience,” according to promoters, who explained that “the open-air stage and dancefloor will sit on the main lawn, bringing audiences closer to the artists while preserving the sense of discovery and escapism that defines Labyrinth’s events. From sun-drenched afternoons in the garden to performances beneath the evening sky, Labyrinth at the Palace promises a new way to experience live music in London with performances running throughout the afternoon and evening until 10:30 p.m.”

Michael Dicks & Nick Castleman, co-founders of Labyrinth said: “We’re beyond excited to announce Labyrinth at the Palace. Finding unique spaces to bring artists and fans together is at the heart of what we do. Fulham Palace offers something truly special: a historic setting with a distinctive sense of character, surrounded by beautiful gardens, right in the heart of London. We’ve been working hard behind the scenes to curate a world-class series of artists and look forward to revealing the full line-up over the coming months. We can’t wait to welcome audiences to this remarkable 16th-century setting next summer and are hugely grateful to Fulham Palace for their collaboration.”

Further artists will be announced over the coming months. The presale for tickets for The Temper Trap on June 12 launches Friday, Nov. 27, at 10 a.m. UK time. A [sign-up](https://www.labyrinthevents.com/temper-trap-sungazer-ldn?ref=pr) is required.
