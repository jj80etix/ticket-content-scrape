# Sienna Spiro Pulls Out Of All Things Go; Ethel Cain Added In NY

## Summary

English singer-songwriter Sienna Spiro has withdrawn from her scheduled All Things Go festival performances in New York and Washington, D.C. (Sept. 25-27), citing unforeseen circumstances; Ethel Cain has been added as a Saturday NY co-headliner alongside Brandi Carlile.

### Key Points
- Spiro also previously dropped out of Newport Folk this year.
- All Things Go NY headliners now include Zara Larsson (her first festival headline), MUNA, Lola Young, and Carly Rae Jepsen.
- Spiro's separate theater tour later this year sold out during presale.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2291622992](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/GettyImages-2291622992.jpg)

English singer-songwriter Sienna Spiro has pulled out of scheduled performances at All Things Go in New York and the Washington, D.C. area the weekend of Sept. 25-27.

“Hello everyone, I am so sad to say that I won’t be able to perform at All Things Go this year. I was so excited for these performances but due to unforeseen circumstances I won’t be able to make it this year. I’m so sorry to anyone that was planning to be there, I love you all and I hope to see you soon,” she wrote on social media.

Ethel Cain has been added as a co-headliner for Saturday in New York alongside Brandi Carlile.

All Thing Go NY headliners also include Zara Larsson, headlining her first festival, and MUNA, Lola Young, and Carly Rae Jepsen.

Spiro earlier dropped out of Newport Folk. She has a theater tour — which sold out during the presale — scheduled for later this year.
