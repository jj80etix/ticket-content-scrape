# Iron Maiden Announces Surprise 2027 North American Tour, Sonic Temple Headlining Show

## Summary

Iron Maiden reversed its planned 2027 touring break, announcing a surprise 16-show final North American leg of its "Run For Your Lives World Tour," including headlining festival slots at Sonic Temple (Columbus) and Boardwalk Rock (Ocean City, MD). The run kicks off April 14 in Tacoma and closes May 16, with tickets available via a Fan Club presale Oct. 19 ahead of the Oct. 23 general onsale.

### Key Points
- 16 added North American dates, April 14-May 16, 2027, focused on Pacific Northwest and Southeast markets missed in 2026.
- Headlines Sonic Temple (Columbus, OH, May 14) and Boardwalk Rock (Ocean City, MD, May 16), with Bullet For My Valentine and The Raven Age as support on headlining dates.
- Fan Club presale Oct. 19; general onsale Oct. 23, both 10 a.m. local.
- Sonic Temple 2026 drew 184,000+ attendees across four days; festival passes already on sale, four-day GA starting at $400 including fees.
- Tour previously ruled out dynamic pricing for its original 2025 dates; Friday's announcement did not specify whether that policy extends to the new 2027 shows.
- Overall tour expected to finish with 107 shows across 36 countries and 3M+ fans.

Iron Maiden has announced a surprise return to North America in 2027, adding 16 performances to its *Run For Your Lives World Tour*, including headlining appearances at Sonic Temple in Columbus, Ohio, and Boardwalk Rock in Ocean City, Maryland.

The announcement, made Friday, October 16, reverses the British heavy metal band’s previously announced plans to take a break from touring in 2027. Instead, the group will embark on a third and final North American leg of its 50th anniversary tour, beginning April 14 in Tacoma, Washington, and concluding May 16 at Boardwalk Rock.

“Maiden were planning on taking a break from live performances in 2027,” bassist and founding member Steve Harris said.

Harris explained that the enthusiastic reception to the band’s recent North American performances convinced the members to extend the tour and visit additional markets. He also welcomed the opportunity to headline two American music festivals, where the band can reach audiences beyond its established fan base.

The 2027 tour will feature fellow British acts Bullet For My Valentine and The Raven Age as special guests on the headlining dates. Bullet For My Valentine is preparing to release its album *Social Apocalypse* on March 19, 2027, while The Raven Age previously supported Iron Maiden on dates in Central and South America.

Iron Maiden’s *Run For Your Lives Tour* celebrates the band’s first five decades, featuring songs from its first nine studio albums, spanning its 1980 self-titled debut through 1992’s *Fear of the Dark*. The band plans to bring its full stage production to all 16 newly announced performances, including the festival appearances.

Manager Rod Smallwood said the tour is expected to finish with 107 performances attended by more than 3 million fans across 36 countries.

The extension also follows Iron Maiden’s selection for the [2026 Rock & Roll Hall of Fame class](https://rockhall.com/2026-inductees/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), recognizing the band’s influence on heavy metal and its continued success as a touring act.

## Iron Maiden Named First Sonic Temple 2027 Headliner

Iron Maiden will headline the Friday, May 14, edition of Sonic Temple Art + Music Festival, taking place May 13-16 at Historic Crew Stadium in Columbus.

Festival producer Danny Wimmer Presents confirmed the booking Friday, making Iron Maiden the first announced headliner for the 2027 event.

The four-day festival is expected to feature nearly 150 bands, with its complete lineup scheduled for release next week.

Previously announced performers include A Perfect Circle, Primus, Sleeping With Sirens, Clutch, Imminence, Saosin, Crowbar, Emmure, Escape The Fate, PeelingFlesh, PRESIDENT, Showing Teeth and Vana.

Iron Maiden’s booking comes after another record-breaking year for Sonic Temple. According to organizers, the sold-out 2026 edition drew more than 184,000 in attendance across four days, with nearly 145 bands performing on five stages.

The festival has continued expanding its footprint in Columbus, where Danny Wimmer Presents secured a five-year extension of its partnership with Historic Crew Stadium in 2025, keeping Sonic Temple at the venue through at least 2030.

Iron Maiden previously headlined another Danny Wimmer Presents festival, Louder Than Life, in September 2026. The band’s return to Sonic Temple gives the promoter another major metal headliner as it prepares to announce the remainder of its 2027 lineup.

The band’s final scheduled *Run For Your Lives* performance will follow two days later, on May 16, at Boardwalk Rock in Ocean City, Maryland.

## Iron Maiden 2027 Tickets and Presale Information

Tickets for the newly announced Iron Maiden headlining concerts will first become available through an exclusive Iron Maiden Fan Club presale beginning **Monday, October 19, at 10 a.m. local time**.

Additional presales will take place throughout the week, with the general public onsale scheduled for **Friday, October 23, at 10 a.m. local time**.

VIP packages will also be available, including premium seating, access to the official Iron Maiden TROOPER pre-show party and exclusive merchandise.

TicketNews [previously reported](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/09/iron-maiden-confirms-dynamic-pricing-will-not-be-used-on-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that Iron Maiden ruled out dynamic pricing for its original 2025 *Run For Your Lives* dates. Friday’s announcement did not specify whether the same policy will apply to the newly added 2027 North American concerts.

Ticketing for the two festival appearances follows separate schedules.

**Sonic Temple passes are already on sale**, including single-day and four-day general admission, GA+, VIP and premium packages.

As of October 16, the [festival’s official ticketing page](https://sonictemplefestival.com/passes/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) lists four-day stadium general admission passes starting at $400, including fees.

Fans primarily interested in Iron Maiden’s May 14 performance can purchase Friday-only passes, with stadium general admission listed at $165 and field general admission at $265, both including fees.

The festival also advertises layaway plans beginning with a $1 down payment. That amount represents an initial payment rather than the total admission price, and pass prices may increase as successive pricing tiers become available.

Fans attending the final tour date at Boardwalk Rock can find additional information through the [official festival website](https://www.boardwalkrockfest.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
