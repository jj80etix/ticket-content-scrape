# Jimmy Eat World, The Format To Celebrate Anniversaries Of Landmark Albums At The Hollywood Bowl

## Summary

Jimmy Eat World and The Format will co-headline a Nov. 7 show at the Hollywood Bowl marking the 25th and 20th anniversaries of *Bleed American* and *Dog Problems* respectively — the first time the two Arizona bands have shared a bill.

### Key Points
- Jimmy Eat World celebrates 25 years of *Bleed American*; The Format marks 20 years of *Dog Problems*.
- First-ever joint performance between the two bands.
- Citi presale begins July 30 at 8 a.m. PT; general presale July 30 at 11 a.m. PT; public onsale July 31.
- Tickets sold via Ticketmaster.

Two seminal rock albums from the 2000s are going to be celebrated at the iconic Hollywood Bowl this fall. Arizona bands Jimmy Eat World and The Format are joining forces to co-headline a special show commemorating anniversaries of their landmark albums in Los Angeles on Nov. 7.

Alt-rock darlings Jimmy Eat World will celebrate the 25 <sup>th</sup> anniversary of their beloved album, *Bleed American*, which includes their breakout hit “The Middle” and “Sweetness.” The band is currently on tour performing the album and graced the Vans Warped Tour stage in Long Beach last weekend.

The Format, which reunited last year and includes vocalist Nate Ruess and multi-instrumentalist Sam Means, is celebrating the 20 <sup>th</sup> anniversary of their sophomore LP *Dog Problems*, which includes “She Doesn’t Get It” and “The Compromise.”

The exclusive event marks the first time the two bands hailing from Arizona perform together and one of two full album performances scheduled by The Format this year.

Citi cardmembers have access to presale tickets beginning July 30 at 8 a.m. PT until 10 p.m. PT. Additional presales will run July 30 at 11 a.m. PT ahead of the general onsale, which begins July 31 at 10 a.m. PT.

Fans can purchase tickets via [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/jimmy-eat-world-the-format-sing-hollywood-california-11-07-2026/event/0B0064F5C75F3BBB).
