# ‘Must-come destinations’: Income growth sets up Baltics for live boom

## Summary

Lithuanian promoter Stay Live says rising incomes and under-tapped venue infrastructure are positioning the Baltic states (Lithuania, Latvia, Estonia) as a growing touring market that international promoters have underestimated.

### Key Points
- Stay Live cites EU income data and existing venue infrastructure as drivers.
- Framed as an "under-catered" touring market relative to demand.
- General market-trend piece; no ticketing-platform or regulatory angle.
