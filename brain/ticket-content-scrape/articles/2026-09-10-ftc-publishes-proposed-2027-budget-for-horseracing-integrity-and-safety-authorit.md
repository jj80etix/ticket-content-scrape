# FTC Publishes Proposed 2027 Budget for Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority

## Summary

The FTC is seeking public comment on the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority's proposed 2027 budget, as required by the FTC's oversight rule for the Authority. This is a horse-racing regulatory matter unrelated to ticketing.

### Key Points
- FTC oversight rule requires publication of HISA's proposed budget for public comment.
- 14-day comment window from Federal Register publication.
- Commission will approve or disapprove the budget after the comment period.

The Federal Trade Commission is seeking comment on the Horseracing Integrity and Safety Authority’s proposed 2027 budget as submitted to the Commission and published in the .

The [FTC’s rule regarding](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-1/subpart-U) oversight of the Authority’s budget process requires the publication of the Authority’s proposed budget in the Federal Register for public comment. The public will have 14 days from the date of publication to submit comments, after which the Commission will approve or disapprove the proposed budget. Information about how to submit a comment is included in the Federal Register notice. Comments will be published on Regulations.gov after they are submitted.
