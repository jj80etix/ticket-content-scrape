# Ticketmaster scoops up Red UTS amid Uruguay entry

## Summary

Ticketmaster is entering Uruguay by acquiring leading local ticketing firm Red UTS, extending its Latin American footprint to eight countries and its total global market count past 35. The new pathway is live now, pairing Red UTS' local market expertise with Ticketmaster's marketplace, high-demand onsale technology, Quentro digital delivery, and fraud-prevention tools.

### Key Points
- Acquisition target: Red UTS, Uruguay's leading local ticketing firm, which also operates in neighboring Paraguay.
- Ticketmaster now operates in over 35 markets globally and in eight Latin American countries (adding Uruguay to Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, Peru).
- Local incumbents in Uruguay include Passline, Tickantel, Tikzet, and resale channel Bein.
- Ticketmaster's Chile/Uruguay market leader Javier Parra and LatAm EVP Adam Newsam both framed the deal around end-to-end fan experience.
- Per IQ's Global Promoters Report 2025, promoters view Uruguay as a profitable extension of Southern Cone tour routing alongside Argentina, Chile, and Paraguay.

### Highlights
- Ticketmaster's acquire-the-local-leader playbook (Red UTS) is now applied to an eighth Latin American market, continuing consolidation of the region's ticketing supply.
- Deal brings Ticketmaster's high-demand onsale tech, Quentro digital delivery, and fraud-prevention tools into a market previously served by smaller local/regional players.
- Uruguay/Paraguay overlap via Red UTS gives Ticketmaster simultaneous entry into two Southern Cone markets through one acquisition.

### Industry Problem
Consolidation by a dominant global primary-ticketing platform is steadily narrowing the field of independent local ticketing providers across Latin America, reducing the number of alternative primary-ticketing vendors available to regional promoters and venues.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster's approach is buy-and-integrate local incumbents to gain instant market share and local expertise; the relevant posture for an independent platform like Etix is monitoring which regional markets Ticketmaster targets next via similar tuck-in acquisitions, since each one signals reduced vendor choice for promoters in that market and a shrinking pool of potential independent-platform partners or acquisition targets.

Ticketmaster is strengthening its foothold in Latin America by expanding into Uruguay, scooping up the leading local firm Red UTS as it enters the market.

The acquisition will combine Red UTS’ local market expertise and experience with Ticketmaster’s global technology, innovation, and scale, with the global firm now operating in over 35 markets.

Through the new pathway, which is now live, fans in the country will gain access to an official marketplace, technology to support high-demand onsales, digital delivery through Quentro, and tools to prevent fraud.

“Our goal is to give every fan a simple, secure and transparent experience – from discovering an event to walking through the venue gates,” says Javier Parra, Ticketmaster market leader for Chile and Uruguay. “We look forward to supporting the continued growth of live entertainment in Uruguay and connecting more fans with the artists and events they love.”

Ticketmaster is a dominant player in the regional ticketing market, also operating in Argentina, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Mexico, and Peru. By expanding into Uruguay, the firm is extending its reach in the [booming entertainment market](https://www.iqmagazine.com/iq-magazine/latam-live-2026/).

Other firms operating in the market include Passline, Tickantel, and Tikzet, alongside resale channel Bein. Red UTS also operates in nearby Paraguay, with firms often overlapping operations in the four [Southern Cone](https://www.iqmagazine.com/publications/international-ticketing-report-2025/southern-cone/) countries of Argentina, Chile, Paraguay, and Uruguay.

While the country’s scale is smaller than some of the neighbouring markets, promoters see Uruguay as a profitable extension of Southern Cone routing, per the [*Global Promoters Report 2025*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/publications/global-promoters-report-2025/southern-cone/).

“At Ticketmaster, we’re constantly working to make the journey from discovering an event to walking through the venue gates as seamless as possible,” says Adam Newsam, Ticketmaster EVP, Latin America.

“Our expansion into Uruguay reflects that commitment, combining technology, innovation and local expertise to deliver a better experience for fans while supporting the continued growth of live entertainment across Latin America,” Newsam adds.
