# Harry Styles Announces 2027 Tour Dates With Kylie Minogue, LCD Soundsystem and More

## Summary

Harry Styles announced 2027 tour dates with support from Kylie Minogue, LCD Soundsystem, and other acts.

### Key Points
- New 2027 tour dates announced.
- Support lineup includes Kylie Minogue and LCD Soundsystem, among others.

Harry Styles is extending his blockbuster “Together, Together” tour deep into 2027, adding 25 shows across North America and Europe even as his massive Madison Square Garden residency remains in full swing.

The newly announced run begins April 2 in Arizona and stretches through Aug. 28 at London’s Wembley Stadium, taking Styles to 12 markets with a rotating group of supporting artists including Kylie Minogue, LCD Soundsystem, Tears for Fears, Lainey Wilson, Tinashe and Caroline Polachek.

The announcement significantly expands Styles’ North American footprint for the current touring cycle. His [30-night Madison Square Garden residency](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/harry-styles-kicks-off-msg-residency-as-ticket-prices-sharply-fall-from-early-high-points/) is the only remaining U.S. stop in 2026, with Jamie xx supporting the entire New York run. The residency began Aug. 26 and continues every Wednesday, Friday and Saturday through Halloween.

In 2027, Styles will play two-night engagements in the Phoenix, Dallas, Los Angeles, Toronto, Atlanta and Chicago markets before heading overseas for shows in Berlin, Paris, Madrid, Rome and Dublin. Wembley will receive three additional dates, extending Styles’ relationship with the London stadium after his record-setting 12-show run there earlier this year.

## When Do Harry Styles 2027 Tickets Go On Sale?

For North American shows, fans can register for the Artist Presale through Sunday, Sept. 13 at 11:59 p.m. ET. An American Express presale begins Tuesday, Sept. 15 at 9 a.m. local time, followed by the Artist Presale at 4 p.m. General sales begin Wednesday, Sept. 16 at 9 a.m. local time.

European schedules differ by city. General sales for Paris, Madrid, Rome and Dublin begin Sept. 14 at 4 p.m. local time, while Berlin goes on sale Sept. 16 at 9 a.m. London tickets reach the general public Sept. 17 at 11 a.m., following Amex and Artist presales earlier in the week.

There is an eight-ticket limit per event. Ticketmaster transfers will also remain unavailable until 72 hours before each concert, after which tickets can be transferred between accounts.

The 2027 run is also introducing a limited batch of what the tour is calling “Unpredictable Fun Tickets.” Those tickets will cost $20 in local currency for North American dates, £20 in the U.K. and €20 in European markets. The tour has not yet disclosed how many will be offered, where those seats will be located or exactly how fans will obtain them, saying additional information will follow.

Full primary-market price ranges for the new concerts have not yet been published across the tour.

## MSG Ticket Prices Remain Well Below Early Asking Levels

The announcement comes at an interesting point in Styles’ existing ticket cycle. New York was one of the most feverishly anticipated onsales of the year, with Ticketmaster saying more than **1** 1.5 million fans registered for the MSG presale, a record for an artist presale in a single market or residency-style run.

Yet the resale market ultimately settled well below the extraordinary asking prices seen around the initial sales.

A [TicketClub snapshot](https://www.ticketclub.com/blog/harry-styles-adds-25-new-2027-tour-dates-as-msg-ticket-prices-get-more-fan-friendly/) of [prices for available Harry Styles tickets](https://www.ticketclub.com/concerts/harry-styles-tickets?utm_source=ticketnews&utm_medium=content) generated on the morning of Sept. 10 shows the cheapest current ticket price for a remaining MSG concert at about $191, with several September dates carrying get-ins around or just above $200. Across the remaining New York run, the median asking price is approximately $734.

That represents a major change from the beginning of the ticket cycle. TicketNews’ Jan. 27 snapshot put the overall MSG get-in at $283 and the median at $1,434. Comparing the individual dates that remain on the schedule, current TicketClub numbers show lower get-ins for 21 of the 23 concerts and lower median asking prices for every remaining date.

TicketNews documented much of that reset in late August, when prices had already fallen sharply from their early levels as the residency finally got underway. The latest snapshot shows that considerably lower asking prices remain widely available as Styles moves through the heart of the New York run.

Harryween continues to stand apart. Styles’ Oct. 31 finale currently carries a get-in around $657 and a median asking price around $1,507, making it easily one of the most expensive remaining New York dates. Still, those figures are significantly below the $2,132 median price TicketClub observed for that coveted show in January.

The New York experience does not necessarily predict how the 2027 shows will behave. Those concerts introduce different cities, much larger venues in many markets and a collection of prominent opening acts that could make individual dates considerably more desirable than others. But with another heavily watched Styles onsale approaching, the ongoing MSG run provides a reminder that the intense market surrounding an initial ticket sale can look very different once an event gets closer.
