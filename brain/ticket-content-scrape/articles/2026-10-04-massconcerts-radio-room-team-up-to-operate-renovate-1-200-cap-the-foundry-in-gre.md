# MassConcerts / Radio Room Team Up To Operate, Renovate 1,200-Cap The Foundry In Greenville, S.C.

## Summary

John Peters of MassConcerts and Wesley Gilliam of Radio Room are partnering to take over operations of the 1,200-capacity Foundry at Judson Mill in Greenville, SC, effective Jan. 1. The transition brings renovations including an expanded stage, upgraded sound/lighting, and additional dressing rooms, building on the venue's history hosting acts like Mt. Joy and Marcus King.

### Key Points
- Partnership takes over operations of The Foundry (1,200-cap) in Greenville, SC effective Jan. 1, 2027.
- Planned upgrades: expanded stage, upgraded sound and lighting systems, additional artist dressing rooms.
- Gilliam currently operates the 500-capacity Radio Room in Greenville; The Foundry is positioned as the market's "next step" venue.
- Extends MassConcerts' venue network, which has recently expanded into Kentucky and North Carolina.

A partnership between John Peters/MassConcerts and Wesley Gilliam of Radio Room will take over operations of the 1,200-capacity The Foundry at Judson Mill in Greenville, South Carolina, effective Jan. 1.

The partnership will build on the venue’s history, which includes hosting artists like Mt. Joy, Marcus King, Lorna Shore, 49 Winchester, Hawthorne Heights and others, as a destination for live music in South Carolina,  
  
As part of the transition, The Foundry will undergo several renovations and upgrades, including an expanded stage, upgraded sound and lighting systems, and additional artist dressing rooms. Peters and Gilliam look forward to bringing an elevated concert experience to Greenville while continuing to attract a wide range of national touring acts to The Foundry at Judson Mill

“Wesley has done a great job in Greenville operating the 500-capacity Radio Room, and The Foundry will be the next step in the market for many artists,” says Peters of MassConcerts.

Gilliam adds, “MassConcerts has developed a really solid network of venues, and with the support of their team, we plan to establish The Foundry as a must-play room when touring the Southeast.”

[Peters was recently profiled in Pollstar’s latest Talent Buyer Directory,](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/18/talent-buy-directory-executive-profile-john-peters-founder-owner-massconcerts/) where he shared his history as an independent promoter in western Massachusetts and recent expansion into markets including Kentucky and North Carolina.
