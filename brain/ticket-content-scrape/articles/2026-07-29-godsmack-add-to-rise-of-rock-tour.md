# Godsmack Add To ‘Rise Of Rock’ Tour

## Summary

Godsmack added 10 fall dates to "The Rise Of Rock World Tour" across US arenas, amphitheaters and theaters, running Oct. 9-24 with support from Dorothy.

### Key Points
- Presales begin July 29; public onsale July 31.
- Tour wraps at Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, CT, on Oct. 24.

Godsmack is going to keep the rock going through the fall.

The band has announced an additional 10 dates on its “The Rise Of Rock World Tour” in U.S. arenas, amphitheaters and theaters.

The new dates begin Oct. 9 Northern Quest Resort & Casino in Airway Heights, Washington, and includes stops in Milwaukee, Cincinnati and more, before wrapping at Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut, Oct. 24.

Dorothy will support on the added dates.

VIP packages and Artist Presale begin July 29 at 10 A.M. local time, with local presales on July 30 at 10 a.m. local. All presales end July 31 at 9 a.m. local, with the public on sale beginning July 31 at 10 a.m. local.

Godsmack is repped by WME in North America and managed by Paul Geary at Shelter Music Group.
