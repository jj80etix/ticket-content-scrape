# Ariana Grande closes Eternal Sunshine tour with $108m gross

## Summary

Ariana Grande's Eternal Sunshine Tour, her first widescale outing in seven years, grossed nearly $108 million from 638,000 tickets sold. The tour ran less than half the length of her previous three outings but delivered a per-show average roughly 75% higher than her prior best.

### Key Points
- Grossed ~$108M ($92M) across the tour on 638,000 total tickets sold.
- Tour length was less than half that of her 2015/2017/2019 outings.
- Per-show average gross was ~$2.6M ($2.2M) — nearly 75% above her previous best.
- Marks her first widescale tour in seven years.
