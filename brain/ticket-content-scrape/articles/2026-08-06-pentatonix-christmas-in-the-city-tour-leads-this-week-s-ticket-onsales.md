# Pentatonix Christmas in the City Tour Leads This Week’s Ticket Onsales

## Summary

TicketNews' weekly onsale roundup covers this week's new ticket releases, led by Pentatonix's 20-plus-date "Christmas in the City" arena tour across North America. It also rounds up onsales from Lord Huron, Third Day, Megadeth, Phish, and Bryan Adams, plus a wave of NHL team schedules, more than 140 Disney On Ice performances, several touring Broadway productions, and comedy dates from Aziz Ansari and Jerry Seinfeld. The piece is a routine consumer-facing listing with no industry-structure, regulatory, or competitive-strategy content.

### Key Points

- Pentatonix's Christmas in the City Tour is the headline onsale: 20+ arena dates from November through December across the Pacific Northwest, Midwest, Northeast, South, and Southern California, ending at The Kia Forum.
- Lord Huron adds a winter cluster (Boston, Montreal, Las Vegas, Seattle); Third Day has a broader spring 2027 run across a dozen-plus markets.
- Megadeth announced a four-city UK arena run; Phish added October dates in Jacksonville and Huntsville; Bryan Adams booked a five-show Las Vegas residency engagement.
- Several NHL clubs (Dallas Stars, Chicago Blackhawks, Tampa Bay Lightning, Washington Capitals, Colorado Avalanche, Vancouver Canucks) released new home-game ticket blocks.
- Four Disney On Ice productions account for 140+ performances entering the market.
- Touring musicals on sale include The Who's Tommy, The Phantom of the Opera, Beauty and the Beast, The Book of Mormon, Mamma Mia!, Six, and Hamilton.
- Comedy onsales include Aziz Ansari's multi-market October run and a new Jerry Seinfeld fall date.
