# Garth Brooks Adds Third Hamilton Concert to ‘Blame It All On My Roots’ Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks has added a third consecutive show (a Dec 6 matinee) at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario, extending his "Blame It All On My Roots" tour stop to three nights. Tickets for all three Hamilton dates go on sale Nov 6 with no presale.

### Key Points
- Third Hamilton date added for Dec 6 as a matinee, joining previously announced Dec 4-5 shows.
- Tour has already played Indianapolis, Denver, Rosemont, Washington D.C., Orlando, Salt Lake City and Tulsa.
- Next stops are Kansas City (Nov 6-7) and four Seattle shows before Hamilton.
- Brooks is also set to headline Stagecoach festival in April 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Garth-Brooks-Hamilton.webp)

Garth Brooks has added a third concert at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario, extending his upcoming visit to three consecutive days as the “Blame It All On My Roots” arena tour continues.

The newly announced performance is scheduled for December 6 and is billed as a matinee. It joins previously announced concerts on December 4 and 5, giving Brooks a three-show engagement at the recently renovated Canadian venue.

The “Blame It All On My Roots” tour launched in August at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis. The itinerary has included Ball Arena in Denver, Allstate Arena in Rosemont, Illinois, and Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C. Other stops have included Kia Center in Orlando, Delta Center in Salt Lake City and BOK Center in Tulsa.
