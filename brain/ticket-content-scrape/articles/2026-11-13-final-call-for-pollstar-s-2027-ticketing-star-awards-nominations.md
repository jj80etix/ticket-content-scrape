# Final Call For Pollstar’s 2027 Ticketing Star Awards Nominations

## Summary

Nominations closed November 13 for Pollstar's 2027 Ticketing Star Awards, which honor box office professionals; voting runs November 20-December 4, with last year's awards presented alongside INTIX's annual conference.

### Key Points
- Nominations for the 2027 Ticketing Star Awards closed Nov. 13; voting runs Nov. 20-Dec. 4.
- Awards presented in partnership with trade group INTIX.
- 2026 awards were presented at INTIX's annual conference in Las Vegas.

Nominations close today for the Pollstar 2027 Ticketing Star Awards, honoring box office professionals who have made an impact in their organization or were a game-changer in the industry.

Submissions are being accepted through Nov. 13, 2026 at 5 p.m. PT.  
  
Voting will then begin Nov. 20 and run through Dec. 4.  
  
The 2026 Ticketing Star Awards were presented in partnership with leading trade organization INTIX and culminated with an awards presentation at INTIX’s annual conference held in February at the Paris Hotel in Las Vegas.
