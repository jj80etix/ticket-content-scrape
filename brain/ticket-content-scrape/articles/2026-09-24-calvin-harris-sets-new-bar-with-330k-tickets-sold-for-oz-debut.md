# Calvin Harris sets new bar with 330k tickets sold for Oz debut

## Summary

Calvin Harris sold out all 330,000+ tickets in pre-sale for his debut 10-show Australian headline run next February — one show each in Perth and Brisbane, five in Sydney (The Domain) and three in Melbourne (Flemington Racecourse) — setting what promoters call a new benchmark for electronic touring in the market. The article is paywalled beyond the opening details.

### Key Points
- 330,000+ tickets sold, entirely in pre-sale, across the 10-date run.
- Venues: Langley Park (Perth, x1), RNA Showgrounds (Brisbane, x1), The Domain (Sydney, x5), Flemington Racecourse (Melbourne, x3).
- Billed as Harris's first-ever headline tour and Australia debut.
- Described by promoters as a new benchmark for electronic-music touring in Australia.
