# System Of A Down Adds 4th Night At Rose Bowl Stadium Due To Overwhelming Demand

## Summary

System Of A Down has added a fourth and final Rose Bowl Stadium show (June 12, 2027) due to overwhelming demand, joining three previously sold-out nights. Tickets for the new date go on sale Oct. 27 via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Fourth Rose Bowl show added with Gojira as support, joining two sold-out June 5-6 shows and a sold-out June 10 date.
- Follows a record-setting run including a two-night Stade de France stand that grossed nearly $19.7M.
- Promoted by Live Nation and 30e.

System Of A Down will be having quite the homecoming next summer. The Grammy-winning rock band announced a fourth and final show at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California, about eight miles from its hometown of Glendale.

The new date is June 12 and will feature Gojira as a special guest. The first two shows, June 5 and 6, will feature Pierce The Veil, and Gojira will support the third and fourth nights.

Live Nation and 30e are promoting the band’s four-night stadium stand, and tickets for the final night at Rose Bowl Stadium go on sale Oct. 27 at noon local time at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/0B006556AD38634F?utm_medium=affiliate&irgwc=1&afsrc=1&clickid=Qs5Sj2VykxyZTMUWl02C0XMQUkr3Bjy7EWVGxM0&camefrom=CFC_BUYAT_219208&impradid=219208&REFERRAL_ID=tmfeedbuyat219208&wt.mc_id=aff_BUYAT_219208&utm_source=219208-Bandsintown&impradname=Bandsintown&ircid=4272).

System Of A Down is one of the most influential rock acts, selling out stadiums across the globe last summer despite not having released a new album since 2005. The band recently headlined Sick New World Festival in Fort Worth, Texas, and performed at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros, London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and Stade de France in Saint-Denis, where they grossed a whopping $19,719,544 off 145,059 tickets sold across two nights, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

The alternative metal band is booked by Creative Artists Agency and managed by David “Beno” Benveniste of Velvet Hammer Music and Management Group.
