# Bazooka Fest Expands 2026 Brooklyn Lineup With Cloud Nothings, Stephin Merritt, Destroyer

## Summary

Brooklyn's Bazooka Fest expanded its 2026 lineup with Cloud Nothings, Stephin Merritt and Destroyer, positioning the two-day event as an intimate, cross-generational indie-rock gathering.

### Key Points
- Runs Aug. 22-23 across The Meadows and The Wood Shop venues.
- Founded by former Matador Records A&R director Jake Whitener.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/bazooka-fest.jpg)

Bazooka Fest has expanded its 2026 lineup, bringing a cross-generational mix of indie-rock veterans and rising underground acts to Brooklyn on Aug. 22 and 23.

The two-day festival will take over The Meadows and The Wood Shop, with Cloud Nothings, Times New Viking and Sad13 leading Saturday’s bill. Sunday will feature Stephin Merritt of The Magnetic Fields, a solo set from Destroyer and Joanna Sternberg.

Bazooka is the project of former Matador Records A&R director Jake Whitener, whose band Sunshine Convention will also perform during the weekend. Whitener said the festival was created as an intimate alternative to larger, less personal music events.

“Bazooka really is my earnest attempt at putting together a festival for New York City that feels special and intimate for both the artists playing and the people attending,” Whitener said.

Inspired by highly curated festivals such as All Tomorrow’s Parties and Athens Pop Fest, Whitener has built a lineup that places influential artists alongside newer bands shaped by their work.

“I think having a line-up that crosses generations is so exciting because you can show people that the golden days of indie music are still happening,” he said. “I wanted Bazooka to be a place to celebrate that.”

Saturday’s expanded roster also includes Colleen Green, Winston Hightower, PARKiNG, TV Buddah, Uniflora, Lex Walton, Great Lakes, Giant Day, Garment District, $500, Vivian and Sleeper Fencel.

Sunday’s lineup is rounded out by Kiwi Jr., 2nd Grade, Josephine Network, Asher White, Chime School, Trinket, Love, Burns, Hang Him To The Scales, The Alaskas and Little Chair.
