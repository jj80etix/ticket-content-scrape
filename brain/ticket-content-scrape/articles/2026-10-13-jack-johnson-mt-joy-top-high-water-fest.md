# Jack Johnson, Mt. Joy Top High Water Fest

## Summary

High Water Fest returns to North Charleston's Riverfront Park April 17-18, 2027, topped by Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy across a 20-act, non-overlapping bill. Presale runs 10-11 a.m. Eastern on October 15, with a public onsale to follow if tickets remain.

### Key Points
- High Water Fest set for April 17-18, 2027 at Riverfront Park, North Charleston.
- Headliners: Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy; 20 acts total, no overlapping sets.
- Also features Death Cab for Cutie, Rainbow Kitten Surprise, and The Head and the Heart.
- Festival was a 2026 Pollstar Award nominee (Music Festival of the Year, Under 30K).
- Presale Oct. 15, 10-11 a.m. ET; public onsale follows at 11 a.m. if inventory remains.

## Transcript

![hw27](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/hw27.png)

High Water Fest returns to North Charleston, South Carolina’s Riverfront Park April 17 and 18 with a 20-act bill topped by Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy.

The two-day festival with no overlapping sets will also feature Death Cab For Cutie, Rainbow Kitten Surprise, The Revivalists, The Head and the Heart, Ole 60, Congress the Band, Mat Kearney, Finn Wolfhard, GoldFord, West 22nd, Mo Lowda & The Humble, Alyssa Grace, Hazlett, Marlon Funaki, Penelope Road, Ax and the Hatchetmen, Angela Autumn and Susannah Joffe.

High Water was a 2026 Pollstar Award nominee for Music Festival of the Year (Global; Under 30K Attendance).

Presale will run from 10-11 a.m. Eastern Oct. 15. 1-Day GA and all GA+, VIP & Platinum Tickets will go on sale at the same time. A public on sale will follow if tickets remain at 11 a.m. Eastern.
