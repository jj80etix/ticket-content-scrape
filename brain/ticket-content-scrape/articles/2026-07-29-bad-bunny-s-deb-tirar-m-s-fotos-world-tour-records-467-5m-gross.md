# Bad Bunny’s Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour records $467.5m gross

## Summary

Bad Bunny's *Debí Tirar Más Fotos* World Tour closed with a $467.5M gross across 55 shows and 3.1 million tickets, making it the highest-grossing tour ever to skip the US market.

### Key Points
- Becomes the highest-grossing tour by a male Latin artist, surpassing Luis Miguel's prior record.
- Madrid's 10-show stand alone grossed $96.1M on 623,000 tickets.
- Falls short of Shakira's all-time Latin-tour gross record of $479.2M.

## Transcript

## Bad Bunny’s Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour records $467.5m gross

With 3.1 million tickets across its 55 dates, the tour becomes the highest-grossing run ever not to visit the US

![Bad Bunny in London](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Bad-Bunny-in-London-©-Eric-Rojas.png)

Bad Bunny in London

Bad Bunny’s *Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour* has reported a final gross of $467.5 million (€410.5 million), according to Billboard Boxscore, making it the highest grossing tour ever not to visit the US.

The blockbuster run reached this number with 3.08 million tickets in 55 shows across eight months. Billboard also reports that the gross figure also sees the tour become the highest-grossing tour by a male Latin artist, beating the previous record held by Luis Miguel 2023-2024 world tour, which comprised over 150 shows.

Despite avoiding the US for ticketed concerts, the star’s February [Super Bowl Halftime Show](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/bad-bunny-brings-latin-stardom-to-super-bowl-stage/) took place mid-way through the tour. Bad Bunny had previously told I-D Magazine that part of the decision not to play in the US was concern over potential ICE raids around his concerts.

Following his [31-date residency](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/09/the-inside-story-of-bad-bunnys-puerto-rico-residency/) in his native Puerto Rico, the Live Nation/Rimas Nation-presented tour launched last November before visiting North and Latin America, Asia, Oceania, Europe, and the UK, ending in Brussels on 22 July.

*Debí Tirar Más Fotos* did not, however, break Shakira’s record for the highest-grossing Latin tour of all time, Billboard said. That mark belongs to the Colombian singer’s ongoing *Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour,* which had grossed $479.2 million (€420.9 million) as of 14 July, according to Billboard.

Bad Bunny’s previous ticket sales record stood at 1.9 million for his *World’s Hottest Tour,* a 43-date stadium tour from 2022. Despite the longer run, *Debí Tirar Más Fotos* still beat its 2022 predecessor in terms of nightly average sales.

The tour also set a host of national and regional records along its route, including becoming the [first Latin artist to headline a stadium in the UK](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/bad-bunny-transcends-languages-and-borders/) with two sold-out shows at London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium in June this year.

In the same month, Live Nation Spain’s Nacho Córdoba hailed the artist’s [Spanish run](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/bad-bunny-800k-spanish-cultural-coup/) as “truly unprecedented” after he played to 800,000 fans across a dozen sold-out shows in Barcelona and Madrid.

The ten-show stand in Madrid was typical of a tour which based its strategy around long stadium residencies, visiting only 18 cities for its 55 dates. Billboard reports that this Madrid stay alone grossed $96.1 million (€84.5 million) and sold 623,000 tickets.
