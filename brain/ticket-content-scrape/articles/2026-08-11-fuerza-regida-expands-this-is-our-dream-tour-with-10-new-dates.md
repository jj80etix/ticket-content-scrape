# Fuerza Regida Expands ‘This Is Our Dream Tour’ with 10 New Dates

## Summary

Música mexicana act Fuerza Regida is adding a 10-date second leg to its "This Is Our Dream Tour," running October 3 through November 2 across U.S. amphitheaters and arenas, after the first leg sold out stadium shows at Dodger Stadium and Citi Field. Presales run through Verizon, Spotify, and Citi starting August 11, with the Live Nation general onsale on August 14. The expansion follows a run of career milestones, including a first Grammy nomination and becoming the first artist to sell out both the Hollywood Bowl and Madison Square Garden in the same weekend.

### Key Points
- Second leg adds 10 U.S. dates from October 3 (Tinley Park, IL) to November 2 (Red Rocks, CO).
- First leg included sold-out stadium shows at Dodger Stadium and Citi Field with guest appearances from YG, Marshmello, and Rey Mysterio.
- Ticketing rollout: Verizon presale Aug 11, Spotify/Citi presales Aug 11, artist presale Aug 12, general onsale Aug 14 via Live Nation.
- VIP packages include premium tickets, a group photo opportunity, a pre-show lounge, and exclusive merchandise.
- Band has surpassed 48 million monthly Spotify listeners and holds multiple Billboard Latin Music and iHeartRadio awards.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/fuerza.jpg)

Fuerza Regida is extending its blockbuster “This Is Our Dream Tour,” adding 10 new U.S. dates after selling out the tour’s opening stadium run.

The newly-announced second leg kicks-off October 3 at Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre in Tinley Park, Illinois, and will take the música mexicana heavyweights through cities like Charlotte, Atlanta, Birmingham, and Albuquerque before wrapping-up November 2 at Colorado’s Red Rocks Amphitheatre.

The expansion follows a major first leg that included sold-out stops at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles and Citi Field in New York, with surprise appearances from YG, Marshmello, OhGeesy, Chino Pacas, Clave Especial, Calle 24, Chuyin and Rey Mysterio.

Tickets for the new dates begin rolling out Tuesday, August 11 with a Verizon presale at 10 a.m. local time, followed by Spotify and Citi presales at noon. An artist presale begins Wednesday, August 12 at 10 a.m. local time, while the general onsale launches Friday, August 14 at 10 a.m. local time through Live Nation.

VIP packages will also be available, with select offerings including premium tickets, a group photo opportunity with Fuerza Regida members, access to a pre-show VIP lounge and exclusive merchandise.

The added dates continue a run of major live milestones for Fuerza Regida. The group previously became the first artist to sell out both the Hollywood Bowl and Madison Square Garden in the same weekend, further cementing its rise from the música mexicana circuit into some of the biggest venues in the country.

That momentum has carried into recorded music as well. Fuerza Regida earned its first Grammy nomination for *111XPANTIA*, which reached No. 2 on the Billboard 200, while the group has also picked up an American Music Award, an iHeartRadio Music Award and five Billboard Latin Music Awards.

Formed in 2015 and led by Jesús Ortiz Paz alongside José “Pelón” García, Moisés López, Khrystian Ramos and Samuel Jáimez, Fuerza Regida now boasts more than 48 million monthly Spotify listeners and billions of streams across digital platforms.
