# BeatHub consolidates full stake in Bogotá’s Movistar Arena

## Summary

Colombia's BeatHub Entertainment has bought out Chilean partner BeLive Entertainment's remaining 50% stake in Bogotá's 14,000-capacity Movistar Arena, taking full ownership of the venue it opened in 2018. BeatHub says sole control will let it advance a "unified long-term vision" for the arena, reflecting continued consolidation and investment confidence in the Colombian live market.

### Key Points
- BeatHub now owns 100% of Movistar Arena's operating company after buying BeLive's 50% stake.
- Venue has a 14,000-person capacity and opened in 2018.
- BeatHub frames the buyout as confidence in the Colombian live-entertainment market.

### Highlights
- Full-ownership consolidation of a major Latin American arena, removing a joint-venture partner (BeLive) from the operating structure.
- Deal size/terms undisclosed in available reporting.

### Industry Problem
Venue ownership and operating rights in fast-growing Latin American live markets remain fragmented among regional promoters and operators, and consolidation deals like this one signal how quickly control can concentrate as a market matures.

### Proposed Solution
No product solution is described; this is a market-structure data point. Etix should monitor Latin American venue/promoter consolidation as a signal of where regional ticketing-partnership opportunities or competitive risk could emerge.
