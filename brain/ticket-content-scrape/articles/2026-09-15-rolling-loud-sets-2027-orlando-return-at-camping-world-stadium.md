# Rolling Loud Sets 2027 Orlando Return at Camping World Stadium

## Summary

Rolling Loud will return to Orlando's Camping World Stadium for a second consecutive year, May 7-9, 2027, with tickets going on sale Sept. 17. The 2027 lineup has not yet been announced; the festival previously moved its Florida edition to Orlando in 2026 after a decade-long run in Miami.

### Key Points
- Three-day passes start at $249 all-in, with a $10-down layaway option available.
- Early-access presale begins 11 a.m. ET Sept. 17, followed by a broader presale at noon ET.
- 2026's inaugural Orlando edition headlined by Playboi Carti, Don Toliver, and Ken Carson.
- Rolling Loud brand is also releasing a feature film, "Rolling Loud: The Movie," Oct. 2, featuring Owen Wilson and Travis Scott among others.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Rolling-Loud-Stage-1200-1200x627.webp)

Rolling Loud will return to Orlando for a second consecutive year in 2027, bringing the three-day hip-hop festival back to Camping World Stadium from May 7 through May 9.

Tickets for [Rolling Loud Florida 2027](https://rollingloud.frontgatetickets.com/?os=fuzzscan2o) will begin going on sale Thursday, Sept. 17, with an early-access presale scheduled for 11 a.m. ET. A broader presale will follow at noon ET, according to the festival announcement. Three-day passes will start at $249 all-in, with $10-down layaway plans also available.

The 2027 lineup has not yet been announced. Rolling Loud is offering GA, GA+, VIP and VIP Munchies passes, with GA and GA+ open to all ages and VIP packages restricted to fans 18 and older. The festival says prices shown through its ticketing provider include ticket taxes and fees, although shipping and layaway fees may still apply.

Rolling Loud’s return follows the festival’s first Orlando edition in May 2026, when it [moved its signature Florida event to Camping World Stadium](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/rolling-loud-sets-may-2026-dates-in-orlando/) following a decade-long run in Miami.
