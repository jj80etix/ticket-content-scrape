Carly Rae Jepsen has announced her 2027 North American headline tour, “Day And Night”. The tour, which is being promoted by Live Nation, will begin on April 15 at the Northern Alberta Jubilee Auditorium in Edmonton, AB and include 26 cities, among them her first headlining performances at the Kia Forum in Los Angeles, the Red Rocks Amphitheater in Morrison, and Madison Square Garden in New York City, before ending in Montreal at MTELUS on May 29.

The announcement comes after Jepsen released her eighth album, *Day and Night*, on Interscope Records this month, a release which has received critical acclaim and which represents her best debut since the success of her breakthrough album Kiss. As the album moves from symphonic pop through to dreamy psychedelia and then to high-octane disco, it captures the clear sense of playful abandon that Jepsen showed in her most ambitious album. Rolling Stone lauded the album by saying that it is “Jepsen’s most experimental and exhilarating work” and that Jepsen “makes falling in love feel brand-new all over again”. VULTURE called Day and Night “the most ambitious project of the Canadian singer’s 18-year career”.
