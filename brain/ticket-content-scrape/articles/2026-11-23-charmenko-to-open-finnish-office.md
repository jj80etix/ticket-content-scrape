# Charmenko to open Finnish office

## Summary

Independent promoter Charmenko is opening a Helsinki office, Charm Music Finland, led by new country manager Marko Salo, extending the company's Central/Eastern Europe-focused promoting footprint into the Nordics.

### Key Points
- Charmenko now operates in eight countries including the new Finnish office.
- Marko Salo, formerly of All Things Live, leads the operation as country manager/promoter.
- Focus spans rock, metal, pop, hip hop and electronic/world music bookings.
- The move follows strong momentum for Finnish-language pop, citing a new Nokia Arena attendance record.

## Transcript

The independent promoter is building on years of focussing its business efforts in Central and Eastern Europe

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Marko-Salo-Charmenko.png)

Marko Salo

Independent promoter Charmenko is expanding into Finland with the opening of a new office in Helsinki to be led by promoter Marko Salo.

The core focus of Charm Music Finland, the promoting operation underneath the Charmenko parent company, will be bringing international artists to Finland and promoting major headline concerts by Finnish artists, the company said.

“The company’s activities will cover a wide range of musical genres, from rock and metal to pop, hip hop, electronic music and world music,” bosses added.

The move builds on years of Charmenko focussing its business efforts largely in Central and Eastern Europe. It now has offices in seven countries as well as a longstanding cooperation with L Tips Agency, which operates in the Baltic states.

Despite the continued expansion, the company said it is aiming to operate with the “personal service mentality of a traditional independent local business”.

> “we are looking forward to working with Finnish artists”

Salo takes the role of country manager and promoter, joining from a position as a promoter at All Things Live. Commenting on his appointment, he said: “It is meaningful to be involved in making it possible for an internationally highly respected industry player to establish a presence in Finland”.

“I approach this new challenge with humility and gratitude for this opportunity. I have previously worked with Nick Hobbs and the team of top professionals at Charm Music, and I am confident that together we will be able to create outstanding concert experiences across Finland,” he continued.

“We had already worked with Marko Salo in 2025, when he organised a festival in Tampere. We stayed in touch even after he moved to All Things Live as a promoter, so it felt natural to decide to establish Charm Music Finland together,” Charmenko founder Nick Hobbs said.

“In addition to bringing international artists to Finland through our extensive global network, we are looking forward to working with Finnish artists, both domestically and internationally,” he added.

The move comes at a time of [strong momentum for Finnish-language pop music](<http://strong momentum for Finnish-language pop>) in the country’s live scene, after domestic singer Isac Elliot has set a new attendance record for Tampere’s Nokia Arena, drawing 14,700 fans.
