# Movers and shakers: UTA reveals six executive appointments

## Summary

United Talent Agency (UTA) announced six new executive appointments and 11 internal promotions across its music division, including new agents in Los Angeles and London, plus senior hires from The Team, Legends Global, and Live Nation Belgium. Part of a broader wave of recent UTA music-division moves.

### Key Points
- UTA added six new executives (agents and a business-affairs exec) across LA and London offices.
- 11 internal promotions announced alongside the new hires.
- New hires include senior talent from The Team, Legends Global, and Live Nation Belgium.
- Follows the recent elevation of James Wright to co-head of UK Music at UTA.

## Transcript

Plus: senior hires from The Team, Legends Global, and Live Nation Belgium make up the latest appointments round-up

Leading the latest round of industry appointments, United Talent Agency (UTA) has announced six new executive appointments, as well as 11 internal promotions across its music division.

The company said the moves represent “the latest in a series of advancements” for the music department, following the recent elevation of James Wright to co-head of UK Music.

Agents Anthony Brown and Becky Drouet join the firm’s London office. Brown brings over thirteen years of industry experience from CAA, where he “shaped global touring careers and built long-term strategies for international artists, specialising in hip-hop and R&B”, UTA said.

Drouet will work across touring and the corporate/ private group, following a career spanning more than eighteen years at LPO, where she booked major tours and live events for international artists.

Chris Anderson (agent), Randy Freedman (agent and music brand partnerships), Jordan Golenberg (agent), and Lauren Katzakian (executive, business affairs) have joined the Los Angeles office.

“With over a decade of experience at Universal Music Group (UMG) and elsewhere, Katzakian is the newest member of the UTA Business Affairs team focusing on touring and Music Brand Partnerships,” UTA said.

The firm added: “Freedman also arrives via UMG, and will focus his efforts within the Music Brand Partnerships division, where he brings more than a decade of experience creating talent partnerships for some of the world’s leading artists and brands. Golenberg joins from Day After Day Productions, and will bring several years of booking experience to his new role.”

Meanwhile, the company said Anderson “joins UTA following nearly two decades in artist management and talent representation, and is tasked with developing tour strategy for a diverse roster of artists at UTA”.

The agency haw also revealed its newest “coordinator class”, which will support agents and executives across multiple divisions within the music department, including touring, marketing, brand partnerships, music crossover, A&R and more.

The announcement includes promotions for Juan Aguirre, Lindsey Cole, Alia Lopotanak, and Mark Soleymani, who become the newest coordinators in the Los Angeles office, as well as for Julian Huff, Pearse Matteson, and Jenna Shatzman, who have been upped to coordinators in New York.

Cat Ingersoll is the latest to be elevated to coordinator in Nashville, And Dylan Bryant, Brooke Newton, and Emily Rhodes are the latest coordinator promotions in London.

In other agency news, **The Team** last week announced the hiring of David Valdez and Danielle Diab as director of tour marketing, both to be based in Los Angeles.

The agency said the appointments “reflect The Team’s continued expansion of its global tour marketing and ticketing capabilities”. Valdez brings experience leading marketing campaigns for major tours, venues, and festivals, including in stints at Intuit Dome, Crypto.com Arena, and AEG Presents. Diab has also served in marketing roles at Live Nation and AEG Presents.

At the same time, the company has promoted Sophie Maltby to associate director of integrated music and sports partnerships within the company’s Artist Services group.

“We’re continuing to strengthen our team with exceptional talent at every level,” said Lee Anderson, co-president of music at the agency.

“David and Danielle bring significant experience and knowledge to our tour marketing operation, while Sophie’s promotion reflects our commitment to developing talent from within. Each of these moves makes us better equipped to deliver for our artists.”

Meanwhile, **Legends Global** has appointed Aimee Carroll as president of merchandise, with the firm saying the hire will bring “two decades of brand-building leaderships across retail, fashion, and consumer goods”.

In the role, Carroll will oversee Legends Global’s worldwide retail and merchandising platform, spanning eCommerce, in-venue retail, large-scale live events, brick-and-mortar operations, licensing, and branded solutions for an extensive lineup of global partners across sports and entertainment, Legends Global said.

“With a track record of driving growth across some of the industry’s most recognised brands, Aimee combines large-company experience with a strong commercial focus and a consumer-first mentality that will deliver results for our partners,” said Legends Global president and CFO Brett Parker.

**Live Nation Belgium** has also appointed Jean Jacques Velkeniers as its new managing director, effective from 1 September.

“What we have built over the past years is the result of the dedication, passion, and hard work of an outstanding team. By welcoming Jean Jacques, we are adding valuable expertise, and I look forward to working alongside him and our management team,” said Herman Schueremans, president of Live Nation Belgium.
