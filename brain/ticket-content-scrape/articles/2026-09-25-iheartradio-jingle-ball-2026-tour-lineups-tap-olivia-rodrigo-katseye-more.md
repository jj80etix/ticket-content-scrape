# iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026 Tour Lineups Tap Olivia Rodrigo, KATSEYE, & More

## Summary

iHeartRadio announced full lineups for its 2026 Jingle Ball Tour, running December 1–17 across nine U.S. cities, headlined by acts including Olivia Rodrigo, Hilary Duff, Jonas Brothers, Charlie Puth and KATSEYE. Capital One cardholders get early access starting September 29, with general onsale via Ticketmaster on October 2.

### Key Points
- Nine-city run from Dickies Arena (Ft. Worth, Dec. 1) through Bridgestone Arena (Nashville, Dec. 17), including Madison Square Garden and TD Garden.
- Each city has its own distinct lineup rather than one traveling bill.
- Capital One cardholder presale runs Sept. 29–Oct. 1; general public onsale begins Oct. 2 via Ticketmaster.com.
- ABC will air a Jingle Ball holiday special in December, streaming on Hulu/Disney+ the next day.

IHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026, presented by Capital One, has announced lineups featuring this year’s biggest artists across nine cities this December. The multiple lineups feature stars such as RAYE, Alex Warren, Ludacris, Olivia Rodrigo, Hilary Duff, Jonas Brothers, SOMBR, Charlie Puth, KATSEYE, OneRepublic, Teddy Swims, and SIENNA SPIRO.

The tour kicks off on Dec. 1 in Ft. Worth, TX, at the Dickies Arena and then travels to Intuit Dome on Dec. 4 in Los Angeles, Chicago on Dec. 7 at the Allstate Arena, Dec. 8 at Detroit’s Little Caesars Arena, Madison Square Garden on Dec. 11 in NYC, TD Garden in Boston on Dec. 13, and more. See full tour routing below.

Eligible Capital One cardholders get first access starting Sept. 29 at 10 a.m. local time, running through Oct. 1 at 10 a.m. local time. Cardholders in select markets can also add a Capital One Access Pass for an exclusive pre-show party with a performing Jingle Ball artist. Details at [iHeartRadio.com/CapitalOne](https://news.iheart.com/featured/capital-one/jingle-ball/). General public on-sale  begins on Oct. 2 at 12 p.m. local time at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/).

For those staying home, ABC will air the *iHeartRadio Jingle Ball 2026* holiday special in December, streaming the next day on Hulu, Disney+, and VOD.
