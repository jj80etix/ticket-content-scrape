# Broadway’s ‘Now You See Me Live’ to Welcome Eden Choi and Yollin Lee

## Summary

South Korean magicians Eden Choi and Yollin Lee will join Broadway's "Now You See Me Live" as special guests during separate portions of its holiday run at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre (Nov. 11-Jan. 3, 2027), appearing alongside the production's core "Four Horsemen" cast. The illusion show, inspired by the "Now You See Me" films, is presented by Tim Lawson and Simon Painter in collaboration with Lionsgate as part of an ongoing international tour.

### Key Points
- Choi appears Nov. 11-29; Lee follows Dec. 1 through the engagement's close Jan. 3, 2027.
- Joins existing "Four Horsemen" cast: Gabriella Lester, James More, Darcy Oake and Matthew Pomeroy.
- Produced by Tim Lawson and Simon Painter (creators of "The Illusionists") with Lionsgate; part of an international tour that has played Australia, Singapore and London's West End.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/now-you-see-me-live.webp)

Eden Choi and Yollin Lee will join the upcoming Broadway engagement of “Now You See Me Live,” with the South Korean magicians appearing as special guests during separate portions of the production’s holiday run.

The illusion show begins performances November 11 at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre and continues through January 3, 2027. Choi will appear November 11–29, followed by Lee from December 1 through the final performance.

Both guests will perform alongside the Broadway production’s “Four Horsemen”: escapologist Gabriella Lester, illusionists James More and Darcy Oake, and mentalist Matthew Pomeroy. The ensemble brings together performers from South Africa, England and Canada.

Inspired by the “Now You See Me” movies, the stage production centers on the Horsemen, a group of illusionists associated with elaborate heists. The live show draws on that concept through an ensemble of performers with different specialties in magic.

Tim Lawson and Simon Painter, the creators of “The Illusionists,” are presenting the Broadway engagement in collaboration with Lionsgate. The New York dates are part of an ongoing international tour that has included performances in Australia, Singapore and London’s West End. Additional tour stops are expected to be announced.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [NowYouSeeMeLive.com](http://nowyouseemelive.com/).
