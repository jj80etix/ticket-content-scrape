# Backstreet Boys Announce 12 European Shows for 2027, Including Four Nights at London’s O2

## Summary

The Backstreet Boys announced 12 European concerts for summer 2027 as part of their "Into the Millennium – Homecoming" production, including four shows at London's O2 and eight stadium dates across Austria, the Netherlands, Germany, and Switzerland. Tickets go on general sale Dec. 4 following presales starting Dec. 1; the run follows a sold-out ~470,000-attendance German stadium residency and precedes a 12-show Toronto residency beginning February 2027.

### Key Points
- 12 European dates for summer 2027: four nights at London's O2 plus stadium shows in Vienna, Amsterdam, Berlin, Zurich, Hamburg, and Munich.
- General on-sale Dec. 4, 2026, with presales beginning Dec. 1.
- The production originated at Sphere in Las Vegas (July 2025) and is Live Nation-promoted at The O2, forming part of that venue's 20th-anniversary celebrations.
- Precedes a 12-show Toronto Rogers Centre residency starting February 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bsb.jpg)

The Backstreet Boys are returning to Europe in the summer of 2027, announcing 12 concerts across seven cities as part of their “Into the Millennium – Homecoming” production. The new dates include four performances at The O2 in London and eight stadium shows across Austria, the Netherlands, Germany and Switzerland.

The group announced its London engagement Friday before revealing an expanded European schedule that will take the five-piece through Vienna, Amsterdam, Berlin, Zurich, Hamburg and Munich. Tickets for all 12 performances will go on general sale Friday, December 4, following a series of presales beginning December 1.

The announcement follows the considerable success of the group’s European residency earlier this year. As [previously reported by TicketNews](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/backstreet-boys-sell-450000-tickets-for-germany-stadium-residency/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the Backstreet Boys had sold approximately 450,000 tickets for their 10-show engagement at Merkur Spiel-Arena in Düsseldorf by the time the residency opened in September. [Stadion Letzigrund](https://stadionletzigrund.com/en/news/topic/concert/backstreet-boys-kommen-ins-stadion-letzigrund/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which will host the group’s upcoming Zurich concert, now puts attendance for the completed German residency at approximately 470,000.

The Düsseldorf engagement was initially announced as a six-show residency before four additional performances were added in response to demand.

The production originated at Sphere in Las Vegas in July 2025, where the group has repeatedly extended its residency, including the addition of [performances surrounding New Year’s Eve](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/backstreet-boys-ring-in-new-year-additional-sphere-shows/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The Backstreet Boys will also bring the production to Toronto’s Rogers Centre for a 12-show residency beginning in February 2027 before heading to Europe.

The London concerts on June 8, 9, 11 and 12 will be the group’s only scheduled UK performances of 2027, according to [The O2](https://www.theo2.co.uk/events/detail/backstreet-boys-2027?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The Live Nation-promoted engagement will also form part of the venue’s 20th anniversary celebrations.

The production centers on the group’s 1999 album *Millennium*, featuring hits including “I Want It That Way” and “Larger Than Life,” alongside material spanning the group’s more than three decades together.

## Backstreet Boys European Tour Ticket Information

Members of the BSB Army fan club will receive the first opportunity to purchase tickets on Tuesday, December 1. Presales for the continental European dates will begin at 10 a.m. CET, while the London fan club presale will begin at 11 a.m. GMT.

According to the [group’s official announcement](https://backstreetboys.com/news/backstreet-boys-announce-into-the-millennium-homecoming-tour-dates-across-europe/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), fan club members will receive new presale codes through direct messages on the membership platform. Codes distributed for previous engagements will not be valid for the new European dates. Members will be limited to four tickets per concert during the presale.

O2 and eligible Virgin Media customers will have access to a separate Priority presale for the London performances beginning December 1 at noon GMT. Additional presale opportunities will vary by market across continental Europe.

AXS will handle primary ticket sales for the London engagement. The venue has indicated that customers purchasing through AXS or The O2’s website will need to display their mobile tickets using The O2 app.

General ticket sales for all 12 performances will begin December 4 at noon local time. VIP and travel packages, including premium seating and hotel accommodations, will also be available beginning December 1.

Ticket links for individual performances are available through the [Backstreet Boys’ official tour website](https://backstreetboys.com/events/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
