# Full Cast Set for Broadway Revival of ‘A Few Good Men’

## Summary

Lincoln Center Theater announced the complete cast for its Broadway revival of Aaron Sorkin's "A Few Good Men," led by Tom Blyth, Bradley Whitford, and newly added Krysta Rodriguez. Performances begin October 8 at the Vivian Beaumont Theater, running through January 10, 2027.

### Key Points
- Krysta Rodriguez joins as Lt. Commander JoAnne Galloway; Blyth and Whitford previously announced as leads.
- Additional cast: Stephanie Berry, Nick Blaemire, Michael Braugher, Christopher Gurr, and Billy Eugene Jones.
- Limited 13-week engagement, official opening October 29, closing January 10, 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Broadways-A-Few-Good-Men.webp)

Krysta Rodriguez is joining Tom Blyth and Bradley Whitford in Lincoln Center Theater’s upcoming production of Aaron Sorkin’s “A Few Good Men,” which has now announced its complete cast.

The courtroom drama is scheduled to begin performances October 8 at the Vivian Beaumont Theater, with an official opening set for October 29. The limited Broadway engagement is scheduled to run for 13 weeks, with performances continuing through January 10, 2027.

Rodriguez, whose credits include “Chicago” and “Smash,” will portray Lt. Commander JoAnne Galloway. She joins a cast led by Blyth, known for “The Hunger Games,” and Whitford, who appeared in “The West Wing,” another work associated with Sorkin.

The newly announced company includes Stephanie Berry as Judge Randolph, Nick Blaemire as Lt. Sam Weinberg, Michael Braugher as Cpl. Jeffrey Howard, Christopher Gurr as Dr. Stone and Billy Eugene Jones as Capt. Isaac Whitaker.

Thom Sesma will portray Capt. Matthew Markinson, Daniel Velez will play Pfc. William Santiago and John Zdrojeski will appear as Lt. Jonathan Kendrick.

The ensemble also includes Brendan Evan Behm, Alex Ekker, Drew Elhamalawy, Miles Fowler, Quentin Nguyen-duy and Stephen Michael Spencer. Peter Bradbury, Tim Creavin, Mahira Kakkar and Austin Phillips will serve as understudies.

Michael Arden is directing the production, which marks a return to Sorkin’s 1989 drama about military justice, loyalty and the consequences of challenging established authority.

The Lincoln Center Theater production has assembled a design team that includes Dane Laffrey for scenic design, Alejo Vietti for costumes, Joshua D. Reid for sound and Japhy Weideman for lighting. Cookie Jordan is handling hair and wig design, while Ricky Ubeda serves as movement director.

Kate Wilson is providing vocal coaching, and Gabriel Mann has composed original music for the production. Charles Means is the production stage manager.

Tickets and additional production information are available through [Lincoln Center Theater](https://www.lct.org/).
