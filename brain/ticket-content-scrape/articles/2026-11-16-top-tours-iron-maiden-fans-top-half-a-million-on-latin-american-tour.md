# Top Tours: Iron Maiden Fans Top Half A Million On Latin American Tour

## Summary

Pollstar's "Top Tours" column reports Iron Maiden's "Run For Your Lives World Tour" sold 505,472 tickets and grossed $46.4 million across 13 Latin American stadium shows in nine countries during its recently completed leg. The tour's cumulative worldwide totals now stand at $225.6 million and 2.15 million tickets sold since its May 2025 launch. The tour continues through New Zealand, Australia and Japan before returning to North America in April 2027.

### Key Points
- Latin American leg: 505,472 tickets, $46.4M gross across 13 shows/10 stadiums/9 countries.
- Cumulative tour totals: $225.6M gross, 2.15M tickets, 69 headlining shows since May 2025.
- Highest single-show draw: 65,002 tickets at Mexico City's Estadio GNP Seguros.
- 2027 North American run planned for April, including Sonic Temple and Boardwalk Rock festival dates.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2303941566](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-2303941566.jpg)

Iron Maiden’s “Run For Your Lives World Tour” recently completed its Latin American leg that spanned just over four weeks, from Oct. 2 through Nov. 1, with reported box-office totals reflecting just over half a million tickets sold in the region. Ticket sales from 13 concerts at 10 stadiums in nine countries totaled 505,472 while grosses topped $46.4 million.

With those box-office figures added to the tour’s overall numbers, the worldwide gross now stands at $225.6 million from a total of 69 headlining performances (not including festival appearances) since the tour began in May 2025. And the overall number of tickets sold during the past year and a half is 2,150,101, according to box-office figures reported to *Pollstar*.

The band’s world tour has visited cities in both Europe and North America since launch, with concerts booked in stadiums, indoor arenas and outdoor amphitheaters in select markets. In Latin America, though, all the concerts were stadium events.

Three of the Latin American venues hosted the band for two concerts with Estadio Nacional Julio Martínez Prádanos in Santiago, Chile, moving the most tickets at the trek’s finale. The ticket count at the stadium reached 106,987 for shows on Oct. 31 and Nov. 1. Then, Nubank Parque in São Paulo, Brazil also booked the band for two nights, Oct. 25 and 27, with ticket sales totaling 88,436, while Estadio Tomás Adolfo Ducó in Buenos Aires, Argentina welcomed 60,406 fans for shows on Oct. 20 and 21.

Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros had the highest attendance for a single performance with 65,002 tickets sold on Oct. 2. A second Brazilian stadium, Curitiba’s Arena da Baixada, followed with a ticket total of 38,518, while venues in Lima, Peru and San José, Costa Rica, also surpassed 30,000 in single-show ticket sales.

The “Run For Your Lives World Tour,” celebrating Iron Maiden’s 50 <sup>th</sup> anniversary, did not go idle after the last Latin American show, as a five-city trek through New Zealand and Australia began just six days later. After kicking off at Auckland’s Spark Arena on Nov. 7, the tour will remain Down Under through Nov. 19 with shows planned in Adelaide, Melbourne and Sydney before a final two-night stint at the Brisbane Entertainment Centre. Then, two shows at K-Arena Yokohama in Japan will complete the tour for the year.

In 2027, Iron Maiden will bring the tour back to North America in April for a final run of arena and shed dates in U.S. and Canadian cities. Currently, 14 headlining dates are booked through May 11, followed by two festival appearances. The band will be the main stage headliner on the second day of “Sonic Temple” in Columbus, Ohio and the last day of “Boardwalk Rock” in Ocean City, Maryland, both in May.
