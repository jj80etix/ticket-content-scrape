# Ticketmaster Claims It ‘Recovered’ Olivia Rodrigo Tickets From Scalpers, Will List for Face Value

## Summary

Ticketmaster says it has "recovered" tickets for Olivia Rodrigo's "The Unraveled Tour" that were purchased in violation of sale rules (i.e., by scalpers/brokers) and will re-release them directly to fans at original face value via a request-and-lottery process, excluding the Hartford, Sunrise, and Las Vegas dates. The move follows earlier fan backlash this year over Rodrigo's presale prices, which ranged from over $200 to nearly $800, echoing similar pricing controversies for artists like Bruce Springsteen and Zach Bryan.

### Key Points
- Ticketmaster claims to have "recovered" scalper-held Olivia Rodrigo tour tickets and will re-list them at original face value.
- Fans can request tickets through Aug. 21, 11:59pm ET, with results emailed by Aug. 28; excludes Hartford, Sunrise, and Las Vegas shows.
- A separate $25 "Silver Star" ticket tier opens for request on Sept. 1.
- Move follows May 2026 fan backlash over presale prices ($200–$800), part of a broader pattern of artist-pricing controversy (Springsteen, Zach Bryan).

### Highlights
- Recovery/re-release mechanism: tickets flagged as breaking sale rules are pulled and redistributed via a fan request-and-confirmation process rather than public resale.
- Pricing context: original presale prices ranged from ~$200 (upper sections) to ~$800 (floor), which drew "should be illegal" fan criticism in May 2026.
- New $25 "Silver Star" ticket tier launching Sept. 1 alongside the recovery program.
- Three markets excluded from the recovery effort (Hartford, Sunrise, Las Vegas), with no explanation given.

### Industry Problem
This is a direct, publicized case of the core Q6/Q7 problem: bulk/rule-breaking purchases (bot or broker-driven) diverting face-value inventory to the secondary market, then re-emerging as visible fan anger over "exorbitant" prices that the platform itself did not set but is blamed for.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster's response — detect rule-violating purchases post-sale, claw them back, and redistribute via a fan-facing request/lottery flow plus a low-price "Silver Star" access tier — is a concrete enforcement + goodwill playbook Etix could benchmark: post-sale fraud/bot detection paired with a transparent recovered-inventory redistribution mechanism, rather than only front-of-queue bot mitigation.

Ticketmaster revealed that it had “recovered” tickets for Olivia Rodrigo’s “The Unraveled Tour” from scalpers and will re-list them for sale at face value.

The ticketing giant took to social media on Thursday to explain that recovered tickets are “ticket purchases that broke the rules of the sales are being released directly back to fans, at the original prices set by the tour.” Fans who were unable to secure tickets yet will be prioritized and can request tickets before August 21 at 11:59 p.m. [here](https://Oliviarodrigo.request.ticketmaster.com).

Following the request period, Ticketmaster said fans will receive a confirmation email, and if the request is successful, they’ll receive a status update email on Friday, August 28.

The newly-announced “recovered” tickets exclude show dates in Hartford, Sunrise, and Las Vegas.
