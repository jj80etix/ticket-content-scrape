# ‘Shucked’ to Launch Second North American National Tour in 2027

## Summary

The Tony-nominated musical "Shucked" will launch a second North American national tour beginning January 2027 at the Embassy Theatre in Fort Wayne, Indiana, with more than 50 cities planned. The non-union tour follows the show's original Broadway run, which earned nine Tony nominations and a historic win for non-binary performer Alex Newell.

### Key Points
- Tour launches January 2027 in Fort Wayne, IN; produced by Work Light Productions, directed by Steve Edlund.
- Broadway run (2023-2024) earned 9 Tony nominations, including Alex Newell's historic Best Featured Actor win as the first non-binary Tony acting winner.
- Casting for the new tour has not yet been announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/shucked-musical.webp)

The Tony Award-nominated musical comedy “Shucked” will return to the road with a second North American national tour beginning in January 2027.

The newly announced non-union tour will launch at the Embassy Theatre in Fort Wayne, Indiana, before traveling to more than 50 cities across North America. Additional tour stops are expected to be announced at a later date.

Featuring a book by Tony Award winner Robert Horn and music and lyrics by Grammy-winning Nashville songwriters Brandy Clark and Shane McAnally, “Shucked” combines country-inspired music with comedy in an original story centered around a small Midwestern community.

Steve Edlund will direct the upcoming tour. Choreography will be by Michaeljon Slinger, while Jason DeBord will serve as music supervisor.

The production’s creative team also includes scenic designer Ann Beyersdorfer, costume designer Leon Dobkowski, lighting designer Ryan O’Gara, sound designer Jeff Human, and hair and makeup designer Adrienne Maurer. The tour will be produced by Work Light Productions.

Casting for the production will be handled by Murnane Casting, with performers to be announced at a later date.

“Shucked” originally premiered on Broadway in 2023 and earned nine Tony Award nominations. Among its honors was a win for Alex Newell, who received the Tony Award for Best Featured Actor in a Musical for their performance as Lulu. The victory also marked a historic milestone, making Newell the first non-binary performer to win a Tony Award in an acting category.
