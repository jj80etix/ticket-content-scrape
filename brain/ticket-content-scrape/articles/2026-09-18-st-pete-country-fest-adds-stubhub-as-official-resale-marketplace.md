# St. Pete Country Fest Adds StubHub as Official Resale Marketplace

## Summary

StubHub has been named Official Ticket Resale Partner for St. Pete Country Fest (Nov 20-22, Vinoy Park, St. Petersburg FL), giving the festival an officially designated secondary marketplace alongside its existing primary ticketing system. The deal extends a growing pattern of StubHub festival resale partnerships, following recent tie-ups with Rocklahoma, Born & Raised and the broader Danny Wimmer Presents festival portfolio.

### Key Points
- StubHub becomes official resale partner for St. Pete Country Fest's 2026 (second) edition, headlined by Riley Green, Bailey Zimmerman and Koe Wetzel.
- Primary ticketing stays with the festival's existing system; StubHub handles resale/secondary only.
- Adds to StubHub's recent official-resale deals: Rocklahoma, Born & Raised, and the Danny Wimmer Presents portfolio (Welcome To Rockville, Sonic Temple, Inkcarceration, Aftershock, Bourbon & Beyond, Louder Than Life).

### Highlights
- Official-resale-partner model keeps StubHub confined to secondary market while primary ticketing stays with the incumbent system.
- Pattern now spans at least 8 festivals for StubHub in 2026 (Rocklahoma, Born & Raised, 6 Danny Wimmer Presents festivals, plus St. Pete Country Fest).

### Industry Problem
Festivals increasingly want a sanctioned resale channel for sold-out ticket types and plan-change flexibility without ceding primary-ticketing control, reflecting demand-side pressure for legitimate secondary options.

### Proposed Solution
Festivals are solving this via official-partner branding rather than building in-house resale; Etix should monitor whether primary-ticketing clients start expecting a similar bundled or integrated resale option as a competitive table-stake.

StubHub has been named the Official Ticket Resale Partner of St. Pete Country Fest, giving fans an official secondary marketplace for the festival’s 2026 edition.

The festival returns to Vinoy Park in St. Petersburg, Florida, from November 20 to 22, with Riley Green, Bailey Zimmerman and Koe Wetzel headlining. More than 50 artists are scheduled to perform across five stages.

Under the new partnership, fans will be able to use StubHub to buy available resale tickets or list tickets they can no longer use. StubHub said the marketplace may also provide another option for fans looking for ticket types that have sold out through the primary market.

“Partnering with StubHub gives fans another way to search for available tickets, and gives them flexibility if their plans change,” Braxton W. of St. Pete Country Fest said in the announcement. “Creating the best possible fan experience is always our first priority, and this partnership supports that goal.”
