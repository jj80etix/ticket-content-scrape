# Backstreet Boys to Ring-In New Year with Additional Sphere Residency Shows

## Summary

Backstreet Boys are extending their "Into the Millennium" Sphere residency a fifth time with three New Year's shows, having already played 47 sold-out Sphere dates and drawn over 750,000 fans.

### Key Points
- Fan Club presale begins Aug. 18, Artist presale Aug. 19 (tied to Ticketmaster accounts, no codes needed), general onsale Aug. 21.
- Backstreet Boys were the first pop act to headline Sphere and the first artists to fly through the venue mid-performance.
- The residency ties in with the Millennium 2.0 reissue via Sony Legacy.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/bsb.jpg)

Backstreet’s Back…to ring-in the New Year!

Following a massively successful run at the immersive Las Vegas Sphere, dubbed “Into the Millenium,” Nick Carter, Brian Littrell, Howie Dorough, Kevin Richardson, and AJ McLean are returning to the Sin City for a round of shows on New Year’s Eve, New Year’s Day, and January 2, 2027.

Fans can look forward to the venue’s fully immersive LED display and Sphere Immersive Sound system, alongside the group’s signature choreography and vocal harmonies with 360-degree visuals and large-scale production elements.

The residency, which kicked-off last year, has already been extended [four](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/backstreets-back-backstreet-boys-return-las-vegas-sphere/) times. Earlier this year, the boyband revealed a [final summer extension](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/backstreet-boys-final-round-summer-residency-shows/), with dates running through late August. Already, the residency has garnered star status; the Backstreet Boys performed 47 sold-out shows at the Sphere, bringing-in over 750,000 fans.

Backstreet Boys also became the first pop act to headline the Sphere, as well as the first artists to physically fly through the venue during a performance.

Tickets for the newly-announced New Year’s dates will be available with the Backstreet Boys [Fan Club Presale](https://backstreetboys.com/fan-club/) beginning Tuesday, August 18 at 10 a.m. PT, followed by an Artist Presale Wednesday, August 19 at 10 a.m. PT. Fans can sign up for the Artist Presale **[here](https://signup.livenation.com/backstreetboys)** through Monday, August 17 at 10 p.m. PT. No codes are needed for the Artist Presale, as access is tied to fans’ Ticketmaster accounts.

A general on sale is slated for Friday, August 21 at 10 a.m. PT [here](http://backstreetboys.com/). Additional Concert & VIP Hotel Experience Packages are available via [Vibee](https://backstreetboys.vibee.com/?utm_source=hustle&utm_medium=press&utm_campaign=bsb_lv_26_announce&utm_id=bsb_lv_26&utm_term=dec_dates&tm_token=bsb_lv_26).

The residency coincides with the celebration of their fan-favorite, timeless 1999 album *Millennium*, recently commemorated with the release of  *Millennium 2.0* via Sony Legacy. The deluxe edition features remastered tracks, live recordings, demos, and their new single, “HEY.”
