# Jethro Tull Extends Ongoing ‘Curious Ruminant’ Tour Into 2027

## Summary

Jethro Tull extended its "Curious Ruminant" tour with 17 new European dates running into February 2027, marking a full year on the road.

### Key Points
- New dates start Aug. 22 in Banska Bystrica, Slovakia and Aug. 23 in Budapest, Hungary, with additional fall/winter stops across roughly 15 countries.
- The tour concludes Feb. 16, 2027 in Madrid, Spain.

Jethro Tull is bringing 17 new dates to their ongoing tour, with select new dates beginning on Aug. 22 in Banska Bystrica, Slovakia, at the Banskobystrický Amfiteater, and then on Aug. 23 in Budapest, Hungary, at Congress Hall. From there, Tull brings news dates for the fall with stops throughout September and October, and additional stops in November and December. The tour now runs until February 2027, which will mark a full year on the road, concluding on Feb. 16 in Madrid, Spain, at Teatro Lope de Vega. See full tour routing below, with the latest dates in bold. For more information or tickets, visit Jethro Tull’s official website [here](https://jethrotull.com/).
