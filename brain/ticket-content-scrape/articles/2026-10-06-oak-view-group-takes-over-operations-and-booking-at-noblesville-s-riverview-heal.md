# Oak View Group Takes Over Operations and Booking at Noblesville’s Riverview Health Arena

## Summary

Oak View Group has taken over daily operations, booking and event programming at the 3,400-seat Riverview Health Arena in Noblesville, Indiana (home to the Indiana Pacers' G League affiliate), after the venue's strong first year (60,000+ visitors, 70+ events). It's the latest in a string of OVG venue-management wins (also recently Kelowna's Prospera Place bid and Greater Sudbury's new arena), continuing the company's expansion into mid-size municipal and sports venues across North America.

### Key Points
- OVG now runs daily operations, live-event programming and booking at Riverview Health Arena, effective from the Sept. 19 TradeUp Festival.
- Venue's inaugural year drew 60,000+ visitors across 70+ events, exceeding the city's expectations.
- OVG appointed Marcellus Barksdale (ex-Learfield) as general manager.
- Part of OVG's broader pattern of winning venue-operation contracts for mid-size municipal arenas (echoes its concurrent, more contested Kelowna and Sudbury wins).

### Industry Problem
The steady drumbeat of OVG venue-management wins at mid-size municipal arenas is incremental evidence of ongoing consolidation in who controls venue operations and booking — and, as the Kelowna dispute shows, operator changes can carry unstated implications for which ticketing platform a venue uses.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor posture: each individual OVG contract is low-signal on its own, but the pattern is worth tracking as a channel through which incumbent ticketing-platform relationships at mid-size venues could shift when operators change.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Riverview-Health-Arena-Oak-View-Group.webp)

Oak View Group will oversee operations and event booking at Riverview Health Arena at Innovation Mile in Noblesville, Indiana, as city officials look to build on the venue’s first year and expand its programming.

The 3,400-seat arena is home to the Noblesville Boom, the Indiana Pacers’ NBA G League affiliate. The partnership began with the TradeUp Festival on September 19, placing OVG in charge of daily operations, live event programming and booking.

The announcement follows an inaugural year in which the arena welcomed more than 60,000 visitors and hosted over 70 events. Noblesville Mayor Chris Jensen said those results surpassed expectations and established a foundation for the partnership.

“That strong start has created incredible momentum, and we’re excited to build on it alongside Oak View Group,” Jensen said.

The city’s goals include broadening the event calendar, increasing attendance and strengthening the arena’s role as a destination for live entertainment and community gatherings.

“OVG brings tremendous experience and industry expertise, and we look forward to working together to expand programming, grow attendance, and continue positioning Riverview Health Arena as a premier destination for events in America’s Hometown,” Jensen said.

OVG appointed Marcellus Barksdale general manager of the arena. A former student-athlete at Indiana University Indianapolis, Barksdale joins the company from Learfield, where his work involved college athletics, brands, media and live events.

His background also includes co-founding SoulFeast Week, a food and culture platform focused on community, hospitality and cultural tourism.

Greg O’Dell, OVG’s president of venue management, connected the partnership to the city’s broader economic development goals.

“We look forward to partnering with the City of Noblesville in positioning Riverview Health Arena as a powerful catalyst for economic development that draws more people to Innovation Mile and the region,” O’Dell said.

The arena’s programming extends beyond its basketball tenant to include concerts, family shows, other sporting events, corporate and nonprofit gatherings, and graduations. OVG will oversee that mix of uses as it works with the city to develop the venue’s calendar.

Riverview Health Arena sits within Innovation Mile, a 600-acre innovation, technology, sports and entertainment district on Noblesville’s east side along Interstate 69. The city created the district to support economic development and growth in the area.
