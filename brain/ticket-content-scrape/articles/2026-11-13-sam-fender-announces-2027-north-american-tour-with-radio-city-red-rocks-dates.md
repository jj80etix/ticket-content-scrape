# Sam Fender Announces 2027 North American Tour With Radio City, Red Rocks Dates

## Summary

Sam Fender has announced a 10-date North American tour for spring 2027, opening April 20 in Boston and running through venues including Radio City Music Hall, Red Rocks Amphitheatre, and the Greek Theatre before wrapping in Vancouver on May 9. The run supports his third album, *People Watching*, and precedes his previously announced 2027 stadium tour.

### Key Points
- 10 dates across the US and Canada, April 20 (Boston) to May 9 (Vancouver).
- Stops include Radio City Music Hall (NYC), Scotiabank Arena (Toronto), Red Rocks Amphitheatre, and the Greek Theatre (LA).
- Precedes his previously announced Proper Massive Stadium Tour '27.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sam-Fender-2027-Tour.webp)

Sam Fender has announced a 10-date North American tour for spring 2027, bringing the British singer-songwriter to U.S. and Canadian.

The tour is slated to open on April 20 in Boston, Massachusetts, at MGM Music Hall at Fenway, with additional stops at venues such as New York’s Radio City Music Hall, Toronto’s Scotiabank Arena, Red Rocks Amphitheatre outside Denver and the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles. Other performances include Milwaukee, Tempe, Portland and Seattle, before the tour wraps up in Vancouver on May 9.

The run continues Fender’s international expansion following the release of his third album, *People Watching*, in February 2025. Fender’s U.S. and Canadian concerts precede his [Proper Massive Stadium Tour ’27](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/sam-fender-announces-2027-stadium-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in Britain and Ireland.

Notably, Fender previously [challenged proposed ticket prices and premium standing sections](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/sam-fender-2027-tour-ticket-prices/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for the British concerts, pushing for more accessible options for fans. Pricing for the newly announced North American performances has not yet been established on a comparable basis.
