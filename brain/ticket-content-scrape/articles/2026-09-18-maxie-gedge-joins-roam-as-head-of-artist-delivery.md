# Maxie Gedge Joins ROAM As Head Of Artist Delivery

## Summary

UK/EU booking agency ROAM has appointed Maxie Gedge, previously European Project Manager at Secretly Group, to the newly created role of Head of Artist Delivery, covering end-to-end roster delivery, team growth, and process development as the agency scales its operations.

### Key Points
- Maxie Gedge joins ROAM from Secretly Group (worked with Mitski, Phoebe Bridgers).
- New role: Head of Artist Delivery, overseeing UK/EU operations end-to-end.
- Follows a period of what ROAM calls "rapid growth" and continued investment in internal infrastructure.
- Gedge previously worked at PRS Foundation and Keychange; recognized on several industry diversity lists.

## Transcript

![MAXIE II ROAM](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/MAXIE-II-ROAM.jpeg)

Maxie Gedge, ROAM’s new Head of Artist Delivery. Courtesy ROAM

Live booking agency ROAM has appointed Maxie Gedge to a newly-created role as Head of Artist Delivery.

Gedge joins from Secretly Group, where she coordinated album campaigns for artists including Mitski and Phoebe Bridgers as European Project manager. The newly created Head of Artist Delivery role positions her at the strategic heart of the agency’s UK and European operations team.

Gedge’s responsibility will be the “end-to-end delivery across the roster, including team growth and process development,” according to today’s press announcement from ROAM, which is experiencing “a period of rapid growth.” Hence the agency’s “continued investment in internal infrastructure and resources, ensuring the agency can support its artist clients with consistency and care.”

A widely respected artist advocate, Gedge has programmed hundreds of shows and worked for festivals, venues, and talent development organisations across a range of genres.

Before joining Secretly Group in 2021, she worked as Communications Co-Ordinator at PRS Foundation and was Project Manager at Keychange, the international network and movement for full gender equality in the music industry.

Recognised on the shesaid.so Alternative Power 100 List, the Music Week Women in Music Roll of Honour and IQ Magazine’s LGBTIQ+ List celebrating 20 queer pioneers, Maxie has also sat on juries for MOBO Unsung, the SAY Awards, and the BRIT Awards.

Comments:

Olivia-jane Ransley, Director of Operations, ROAM: “As we grow as a business, it’s essential that the level of service artists receive from ROAM never dilutes. This role puts real ownership and structure behind that commitment, and we’re delighted to have Maxie’s experience and expertise onboard — she brings exactly the care and instinct for artists that this team is built on.”

Maxie Gedge, Head of Artist Delivery, ROAM: “I have always loved the people and roster at ROAM and I am ecstatic to be joining at such a pivotal moment for the agency. Having spent my career championing artists and helping them thrive, I know how much a solid infrastructure matters behind the scenes. I can’t wait to work with this incredible team to make sure every artist on the roster gets the consistency and support they deserve.”
