# Rod Stewart Cancels Remaining Dates of U.S. Tour Following Heart Operation

## Summary

Rod Stewart canceled the remainder of his U.S. and Mexico tour dates, including a six-night Caesars Palace Las Vegas residency, after a routine coronary stent procedure; doctors have him on a four-week recovery timeline. It follows a string of 2026 health-related disruptions, including a Red Rocks postponement to 2027 and a laryngitis cancellation that drew criticism after he was seen at a World Cup match the next day.

### Key Points
- Stewart announced the coronary stent procedure and cancellations via Instagram Tuesday night.
- Cancels remaining "One Last Time Tour" U.S./Mexico dates and the six-night Caesars Palace residency.
- Doctors say the operation was successful and recovery is on track, with a four-week recuperation window before he can perform again.
- Earlier 2026 disruptions: Red Rocks show postponed to 2027 (vocal strain), and a Chula Vista cancellation (laryngitis) followed by public criticism after he attended a World Cup match the next day.

Famed rocker Sir Rod Stewart had to call-off his remaining tour dates across the U.S. while he recovers from a heart operation.

Stewart explained the news on Instagram Tuesday night, noting that he underwent a routine coronary stent procedure. Doctors said his operation was a success and they are pleased with his recovery, noting that he is doing “very well and has returned to his normal daily activities.”

However, as he recovers, Stewart is unable to take the stage at this time.

“On the advice of his doctors, he will take the next four weeks to recuperate and regain full fitness before returning to the stage,” Stewart’s post read. “Unfortunately, this means he will be unable to continue his current tour dates.”

This includes Stewart’s upcoming shows in the U.S. and Mexico throughout August and September on his “One Last Time Tour,” as well as a six-night stint at Caesars Palace in Las Vegas.
