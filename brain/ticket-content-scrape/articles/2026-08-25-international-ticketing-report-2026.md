# International Ticketing Report 2026

## Summary

This is IQ Magazine's landing/promo page for its "International Ticketing Report 2026" print publication and its inaugural "Ticketing Stars 2026" list, profiling individual ticketing-industry professionals (at Fever, Ticketmaster, AXS France, Co-op Live, Royal Albert Hall, Tixel and others) with short career-highlight blurbs. The page is a subscription/purchase gateway and table of contents rather than a reportable article — it contains no extractable data, findings or analysis of its own.

### Key Points
- Promotes IQ Magazine's "International Ticketing Report 2026" (paid print/digital publication); content itself is paywalled.
- Introduces "Ticketing Stars 2026," a first-time list of ticketing professionals across venues, agencies and platforms (including Ticketmaster and AXS staff).
- No report findings, data or analysis are included on the page itself.
