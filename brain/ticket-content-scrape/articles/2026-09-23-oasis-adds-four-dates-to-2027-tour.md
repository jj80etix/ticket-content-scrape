# Oasis Adds Four Dates To 2027 Tour

## Summary

Oasis has added four more European dates to its 2027 tour — a third Munich show, a third Rome show and two more at Knebworth — bringing the run to 42 shows ahead of Friday's onsale. Tickets are fixed-price ($122.65–$541.05 with fees/taxes), and fans had to register in advance specifically to reduce queuing and limit scalping.

### Key Points
- New dates: third night at Munich's Allianz Arena (July 6), third at Rome's Stadio Olimpico (July 26), two more at Knebworth (Sept. 25–26).
- Fixed ticket pricing set ahead of the Sept. 25 onsale; registration required to reduce queue pressure and curb scalping.
- The tour follows Oasis's 2025 reunion run, the second-highest-grossing tour of 2025 at $405M+ from 2.2M+ tickets sold.

### Highlights
- Oasis is again pairing mandatory pre-registration with fixed onsale pricing specifically to manage queue load and deter scalpers on a high-demand stadium tour.

### Industry Problem
High-demand onsales routinely produce queue meltdowns and scalper capture of inventory; registration-gated, fixed-price sales are becoming a standard countermeasure for the biggest tours.

### Proposed Solution
Continue tracking registration/ballot + fixed-pricing as the default anti-scalping mechanism for top-tier onsales, and ensure Etix's own high-demand onsale tooling (queueing, registration gating, price integrity) can support comparable scale if a client tour requires it.

Fans in Europe are going to get more of Oasis next year. The iconic Manchester band added new dates to its 2027 tour in Italy, Germany and the U.K. ahead of the onsale.

Initially a 38-date tour, Oasis is now playing 42 shows, adding a third night at Allianz Arena in Munich on July 6, a third concert at Stadio Olimpico in Rome on July 26 and two more at Knebworth House in Stevenage, England, on Sept. 25 and 26.

**[Oasis’ Reunion Tour Hits No. 2 On Worldwide Tours Chart: ‘The Outpouring, The Emotion, You Could Cut It With A Knife’](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/12/oasis-reunion-tour-hits-no-2-on-worldwide-tours-chart-the-outpouring-the-emotion-you-could-cut-it-with-a-knife/)**

Tickets go on sale Sept. 25, and prices are fixed, with standard tickets ranging from $122.65 to $541.05, including fees and taxes. Fans were required to register for tickets to reduce queuing times and limit scalping.

The band recently released the film “Oasis: Don’t Look Back in Anger,” which chronicles Liam and Noel Gallagher’s return to the live stage in 2025. The critically acclaimed documentary premiered at the Venice Film Festival and received an eight-minute standing ovation before its worldwide theatrical release. It went on to gross more than $11.5 million worldwide, according to Box Office Mojo.

Oasis’s 2025 reunion tour was the second-highest-grossing trek of 2025, hauling in more than $405 million from 2,228,471 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. The sold-out tour received the Major Tour of the Year honor at the 37 <sup>th</sup> annual Pollstar Awards.
