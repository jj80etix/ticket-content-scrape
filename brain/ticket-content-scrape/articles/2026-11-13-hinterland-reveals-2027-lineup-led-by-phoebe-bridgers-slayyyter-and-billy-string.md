# Hinterland Reveals 2027 Lineup Led by Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter and Billy Strings

## Summary

Hinterland Music Festival announced its 2027 lineup, with Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter, and Billy Strings headlining the three-day event in St. Charles, Iowa, July 30-August 1. Several headliners, including Bridgers, will also appear at Coachella 2027.

### Key Points
- Three-day festival, July 30-August 1, 2027, in St. Charles, Iowa.
- Headliners: Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter, and Billy Strings.
- Several acts, including Bridgers, Remi Wolf, and Caroline Polachek, also play Coachella 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Hinterland-Festival-2027.webp)

Hinterland Music Festival will return to St. Charles, Iowa, in 2027 with Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter and Billy Strings headlining the three-day event.

The festival is scheduled to open Friday, July 30, with performances continuing through Sunday, August 1, with Caroline Polachek, Remi Wolf and Zach Top among the other artists on the bill.

The lineup also features Violet Grohl, Ashe, The Backseat Lovers, Westside Cowboy, underscores, Mon Rovîa and Florence Road. Haywire is scheduled to deliver a special performance in a cornfield, adding an unusual setting to the weekend’s slate of shows.

Notably, before heading to Iowa, Phoebe Bridgers, Remi Wolf and Caroline Polachek will [perform at Coachella](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/coachella-2027-lineup-tickets-dua-lipa-sza-tame-impala/) in Indio, California, April 9 to 11 and April 16 to 18, 2027. Bridgers is scheduled for both Saturdays, while Wolf and Polachek will take the stage on both Sundays.
