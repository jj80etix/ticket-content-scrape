# Gabriel Jacoby Announces 2027 Gutta Child Tour

## Summary

R&B artist Gabriel Jacoby announced a 19-date 2027 "Gutta Child Tour" across North America, running Feb. 16-March 19 with Tiger Lewis as support.

### Key Points
- Tour opens in Seattle and closes with a hometown show in Tampa.
- Follows Jacoby's 2025 EP *gutta child* and recent singles.
- General on-sale began Nov. 20 after Citi and artist presales earlier in the week.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Gabriel-Jacoby-Gutta-Child.webp)

R&B singer-songwriter Gabriel Jacoby has announced a 19-date North American headlining tour for 2027. The Gutta Child Tour will kick off February 16 in Seattle and wrap up March 19 in Tampa, Florida, with Tiger Lewis joining as support on all dates.

The tour will begin at Neumos in Seattle before heading to Portland, San Francisco, Los Angeles and Phoenix. Jacoby will then travel through Texas and Colorado, with performances scheduled in Houston, Dallas and Denver before continuing into the Midwest.

Additional stops include Minneapolis, Chicago, Detroit and Toronto, where Jacoby will perform at The Mod Club on March 6. The East Coast portion of the tour will feature shows at Brighton Music Hall in Boston on March 9, Irving Plaza in New York on March 11 and The Foundry at The Fillmore Philadelphia on March 12.

The final stretch includes Washington, D.C., Charlotte and Atlanta before the tour concludes with a hometown performance at The Orpheum in Tampa on March 19.

The upcoming tour follows Jacoby’s 2025 EP, *gutta child*, released through PULSE Records. The artist has continued releasing new music in recent months, including the singles “let’s talk about it” in October and “be alright” on November 17. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time through [Gabriel Jacoby’s official tour website](https://www.gabrieljacoby.com/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
