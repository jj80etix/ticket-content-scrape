# ATG Entertainment acquired by Ari Emanuel’s Mari in €5bn deal

## Summary

WME Group chairman Ari Emanuel will acquire UK theatre and ticketing giant ATG Entertainment through his events company Mari, in a deal reportedly worth around $6 billion (€5.2bn), pending regulatory approval. ATG — which operates 70 venues across the UK, US, Germany, and Spain along with production, hospitality, and a scaled ticketing platform — will continue under its existing brand and leadership after the deal closes.

### Key Points
- Deal value reported by the Wall Street Journal at ~$6bn / €5.2bn; financial terms not officially disclosed.
- ATG operates 70 venues including 10 West End theatres and 7 Broadway theatres (third-largest Broadway owner).
- Seller is Providence Equity Partners, ATG's backer for the past 13 years.
- Mari's existing portfolio includes ticketing platform TodayTix, Frieze, and Hyde Park Winter Wonderland.
- Transaction is subject to regulatory approval; ATG expected to keep its brand and leadership post-close.

### Highlights
- $6bn deal folds ATG's in-house ticketing platform and 70-venue portfolio into Mari, which already owns ticketing platform TodayTix — creating a combined theatre/ticketing operator at meaningful scale.
- Deal remains subject to regulatory approval across UK/US/Germany/Spain jurisdictions.

### Industry Problem
Continued consolidation of venue ownership and ticketing infrastructure under a small number of well-capitalized operators (Mari/TodayTix, Live Nation/Ticketmaster, AEG/AXS) narrows the field of independent primary-platform buyers a theatre operator like ATG can choose from.

### Proposed Solution
No product response needed short-term; worth monitoring for regulatory pushback (multi-jurisdiction approval) and whether Mari migrates ATG's venues onto TodayTix ticketing, which would signal how aggressively PE-backed roll-ups are consolidating ticketing share away from independent platforms.

## ATG Entertainment acquired by Ari Emanuel’s Mari in €5bn deal

The WME Group chairman will fold in the global venue, production, and ticketing player into the new company's growing portfolio of live offerings

![Melanie Smith](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Melanie-Smith.png)

ATG CEO Melanie Smith

WME Group chairman Ari Emanuel will acquire UK-based entertainment firm ATG Entertainment through his events company, Mari, in a reportedly multi-billion-dollar deal.

ATG operates a total of 70 venues across the UK, US, Germany, and Spain, primarily in the theatre realm, with its global business also spanning production and ticketing. Earlier this year, the company launched a dedicated live branch, called [ATG Live](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/atg-entertainment-establishes-dedicated-live-division-in-us/), in the US market.

Financial details were not disclosed, though the [*Wall Street Journal*](https://www.wsj.com/business/deals/hollywood-mogul-ari-emanuel-to-buy-theater-giant-in-6-billion-deal-5de4e228?mod=lead_feature_below_a_pos3) valued the deal around $6 billion (€5.2bn). For the past 13 years, private investment firm Providence Equity Partners – which recently finalised its [takeover of The Team](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/wasserman-sells-remaining-stake-in-the-team-to-providence/\) – has supported ATG.

“I’ve spent my whole career in entertainment, and I started out in theatre,” Emanuel said. “I’ve seen the industry reinvent itself many times, but live has only grown more powerful. Nothing connects great talent with audiences more directly.

“ATG has built one of the world’s great theatre businesses around that experience. This is a long-term bet on where live goes next,” he says.

Emanuel launched Mari last October, adding to his dealings in the live entertainment domain. He is currently the CEO of TKO Group, WWE and UFC’s parent company, and the executive chairman of agency WME’s parent company, the WME Group, which formed after [Endeavour went private](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/03/endeavor-goes-private-wme-group-formed/) in 2024.

Mari’s portfolio also includes ticketing platform TodayTix, arts platform Frieze, Hyde Park Winter Wonderland, and the Hampton Court Palace and Blenheim Palace Festivals.

ATG is expected to continue under its existing brand, with leadership to remain in place.

“Mari understands live entertainment and what matters most to ATG: our theatres, the people behind them and the relationships we have built with producers, artists and audiences,” said ATG’s global CEO, Melanie Smith, who joined the team [earlier this year](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/movers-shakers-atg-entertainment-bmac/).

“That makes Mari the right home for our next chapter. We look forward to this new era and thank Providence for their unwavering support over the past 13 years,” she said.

The deal is subject to regulatory approval.
