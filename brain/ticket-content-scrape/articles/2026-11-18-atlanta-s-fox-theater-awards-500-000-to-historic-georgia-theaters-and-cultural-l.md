# Atlanta’s Fox Theater Awards $500,000 to Historic Georgia Theaters and Cultural Landmarks Via Fox Gives

## Summary

Fox Gives, the philanthropic arm of Atlanta's Fox Theatre, announced 12 single-year grants totaling $500,000 for historic theaters and partner organizations across Georgia, split between Preservation & Facilities Grants and Technical Services Grants. The program, now in its 18th year, has invested more than $6.2 million to date as the Fox approaches its 100th anniversary.

### Key Points
- 12 grants totaling $500,000 awarded to Georgia historic theaters and partner organizations.
- Eight Preservation & Facilities Grants and four Technical Services Grants.
- Technical Services funding covers strategic planning, professional development, and technology guidance.
- Fox Gives has distributed over $6.2 million across 18 years of the program.

## Transcript

Fox Gives, the philanthropic arm of Atlanta’s Fox Theatre, announced 12 single-year grants totaling $500,000 for historic theaters and partner organizations in Georgia. The funding enables a range of projects to support important cultural assets and strengthen their operations.

Eight organizations will receive Preservation & Facilities Grants to engage community-wide participation and support for local preservation and facilities projects while fostering a sense of communal pride and ownership. Four grantees will receive Technical Services Grants, providing direct access to professional experts and resources to address specific organizational, operational and strategic needs. Support may include strategic planning, professional development, organizational growth, sustainability, leadership development and technology guidance.

“The Fox Theatre’s own history has shown us what’s possible when dedicated supporters invest in a venue and believe in its potential,” said Allan Vella, President and CEO of the Fox Theatre. “As we approach our 100th anniversary, we’re proud to carry that legacy forward through Fox Gives to help local theaters strengthen their operations and contribute to the cultural and economic vitality of their cities and regions.”

Now in its 18th year, Fox Gives has invested more than $6.2 million in historic theaters and cultural landmarks across Georgia and the Southeast. Of that total, more than $4.7 million has been awarded through single-year grants to 66 venues throughout the region. Earlier this year, Fox Gives gave $1 million in multiyear grants to the historic DeSoto Theatre in Rome and the Thomasville Municipal Auditorium, with each receiving $500,000 in long-term preservation support.

Grants from Fox Gives are designed to help address immediate needs, plan for the future and advance significant long-term projects. The program funds eligible projects focused on preservation, facilities, planning, technical assistance and other critical needs.

Leigh Burns, Director of Community Partnerships for Fox Gives, added, “Every historic theater and cultural landmark has a story to tell and a role to play in the life of its community. By supporting projects that range from critical building repairs to improved performance capabilities and long-term planning, we’re helping these organizations continue sharing the performing arts and Georgia’s unique heritage with audiences for many years to come.”

A two-time Fox Theatre grant recipient, Coastal Georgia Area Community Action Authority, Inc. (CGACAA), was awarded a $75,000 Preservation & Facilities Grant to support continued rehabilitation of the historic Colored Memorial Building Auditorium in Brunswick, Georgia. The nonprofit organization is renovating the auditorium, which was built to honor African American veterans of World War 1, as well as the school and surrounding historic buildings. Funding supports interior repairs to support the building’s structural integrity along with roof repairs.

“We are honored to be among this year’s Fox Gives recipients,” said Tres Hamilton, CEO of CGACAA. “Receiving this support allows us to build on the progress already underway. Preserving this historic landmark is important to our community, and each phase of restoration brings us closer to returning the auditorium to a place where people can gather, celebrate our history and create new memories.”

Additional 2026-2027 Preservation & Facilities Grant Recipients include:

Friends of Calhoun’s GEM Theatre/GEM Theatre, Calhoun, Georgia – $56,000

This is the first Fox Theatre grant awarded to the GEM Theatre. Funding will replace the aging HVAC system to improve energy efficiency, reduce operating costs and ensure the historic building remains a comfortable for performances, educational programs and community events.

Imperial Theatre, Augusta, Georgia – $23,500

This is the fifth grant awarded to the Imperial Theatre since 2010. The funds will provide upgrades for the backstage rigging system to improve functionality. Located in historic downtown Augusta, the Imperial Theatre continues a strong legacy of presenting a variety of performances and arts education support for the region.

Martin Centre Preservation Company, Inc./Martin Theatre, Douglas, Georgia – $40,000

This is the first Fox Theatre grant awarded to the Martin Theatre, a long time Georgia Presenter member theater in southeast Georgia. The funding will support repairs to the historic interior. This grant will be combined with additional grant funding and matching funds from the non-profit organization.

Plaza Theare Foundation, Inc./Plaza Theatre, Atlanta – $75,000

Since 2013, the Fox Theatre has funded the Plaza Theatre in four grant cycles totaling $181,190. In this phase of ongoing improvements, funding will go toward a historically accurate restoration of the 1939 building’s façade and entrance, including masonry and storefront repairs. This project also includes lighting and waterproofing improvements to help preserve the historic theater.

Sautee Nacoochee Community Association, Inc./Sautee Nacoochee Center, Sautee Nacoochee, Georgia – $25,000

The third consecutive grant from the Fox Theatre will assist in a final rehabilitation phase of the historic gymnasium at the Sautee Nacoochee Cultural Center. An updated lighting system and sound board will be installed, enhancing performances, rentals and programming at the 1930s facility while preserving the building’s character.

Theatrical Outfit, Atlanta – $36,500

This is the second Fox Theatre grant for Theatrical Outfit, located in downtown Atlanta, which is celebrating its 50th anniversary in the 2026/2027 season. The funding will upgrade stage systems and projection technology at the Balzer Theater, enhancing production quality and expanding capabilities for performances, events and rentals.

Thomasville Center for the Arts, Thomasville, Georgia – $65,000

The second consecutive grant supports the restoration of the historic East Side School’s terra cotta roof. Opened in 1915, the former school building continues to serve as a cornerstone for arts programming and cultural engagement.

2026-2027 Technical Services Grant Recipients Include:

Georgia Historic Preservation Division/Georgia Department of Community Affairs/

Georgia Chitlin’ Circuit Heritage Trail, Statewide – $25,000

This is the second grant for the multiphase Georgia Chitlin’ Circuit Heritage Trail project. The funding supports greater documentation of both remaining and no longer extant sites, focusing on underserved regions and producing an updated historic report and heritage trail brochure. These important resources help preserve African American cultural history and support future heritage tourism and educational efforts.

City of Winterville/Marigold Auditorium, Winterville, Georgia – $25,000

This is the fifth Fox Theatre grant for Marigold Auditorium located in Winterville, the smallest city in Athens-Clarke County. The project will include the development of a comprehensive 10-year strategic plan to help ensure the long-term sustainability of its historic facility and ongoing cultural programming.

Morton Theatre Corporation/Morton Theatre, Athens, Georgia – $20,000

This is the third grant for the Morton Theatre, the only surviving vaudeville theater still in operation in the United States that was uniquely built, owned and operated by an African American. Grant funding will develop a comprehensive strategic plan informed by community engagement, organizational assessment and board planning. This effort will strengthen operations, guide future growth and help preserve the historic theatre’s cultural legacy while better serving the Athens-Clarke County community.

Southface Institute, Atlanta/GoodUse Grant Program, Statewide – $34,000

Southface has partnered with the Fox Theatre since 2021 to complete more than $600,000 in grant funded projects through the GoodUse Grant Program in both Georgia and the Southeastern United States. The partnership has focused on historic theaters becoming more sustainable through energy efficiency planning and physical building updates. This grant will provide subgrants for multiple historic theaters to receive energy audits from Southface Institute technical experts, providing a road map to achieve greater energy efficiency.

A 501(c)(3) nonprofit arts organization, the Fox Theatre is one of Atlanta’s busiest live venues, welcoming more than 500,000 visitors annually with 200 performances a year in its 4,665-seat theater. Fox Gives is the philanthropic outreach arm of Fox Theatre, committed to fostering community partnerships and enriching lives through the arts.
