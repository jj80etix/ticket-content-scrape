# LA28 ticket registrations surge to over 12 million with two years to go

## Summary

LA28 ticket registrations have climbed to over 12 million (up from 5 million in February), announced alongside the launch of the LA28 Visa presale offering select cardholders early access to Drop 2 of Olympic tickets. Drop 2, powered by AXS and Eventim, runs August 10-20 with randomly assigned 48-hour purchase windows, while LA28 also confirmed plans for a multi-platform verified ticket resale network launching next year.

### Key Points
- Registrations: 12 million, up from 5 million in February 2026, from 200+ countries and all 50 US states.
- Drop 1 sold ~4 million tickets, 75% claimed by LA/Oklahoma City locals; 95% of Drop 1 tickets were priced $100 or less, with 500,000 at $28.
- Drop 2 (Aug 10-20) is powered by AXS and Eventim; notifications go out Aug 6-7, opening 48-hour purchase windows.
- Per-fan limits: up to 12 total Olympic tickets plus up to 12 football tournament tickets, with a 4-ticket-per-ceremony cap counting toward the 12-ticket max.
- LA28 Visa presale runs through July 31, 2026, and requires a Visa card.
- A multi-platform verified ticket resale network is planned for launch next year, alongside Paralympic ticket sales; the LA Rams donated $5m funding 1,000 free Community Ticket Programme tickets to 10 local organizations.

### Highlights
- 12 million registrations (from 5 million in Feb 2026) across 200+ countries and all 50 states.
- Drop 2 window: August 10-20, 2026, powered by AXS and Eventim.
- 95% of Drop 1's ~4 million tickets sold were priced $100 or under, with 500,000 at $28.
- A multi-platform verified ticket resale network is slated to launch next year.

### Industry Problem
At mega-event scale, LA28 faces the core secondary-market challenge of ensuring fair access and controlling unauthorized resale/scalping across a global, high-demand ticket base, which is why organisers are building their own verified resale network rather than ceding that demand to the open secondary market.

### Proposed Solution
LA28's answer is a purpose-built, multi-platform verified resale network launching in 2026/27, run alongside AXS/Eventim as primary distributors; for Etix this is worth monitoring as a model for how a primary platform can offer sanctioned, transferable resale at large-scale, high-demand events rather than leaving that demand to third-party marketplaces.

**Ticket registration for the Los Angeles Olympic and Paralympic Games has climbed to 12 million, up from five million in February, according to organisers.**

LA28 announced the figure to coincide with the launch of the LA28 Visa presale, which offers selected qualifying Visa cardholders early access to Drop 2 of Olympics tickets across all sports.

Fans from more than 200 countries and all 50 states have now registered for the chance to purchase tickets. During the first drop, some [four million tickets were sold](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/04/la28-sells-over-four-million-tickets-during-first-sales-phase/) – 75% of which were claimed by LA and Oklahoma City locals.

Organisers also said that 95% of tickets available in Drop 1 were priced at $100 (£75/€87) or less, with half a million costing $28.

The LA28 Visa Presale runs until tomorrow (July 31) and purchases must be completed with a Visa card.

Drop 2 will take place between August 10-20, and is powered by AXS and Eventim.

Fans will be randomly selected for a time slot and will be notified via email between August 6-7. Time slots will open for 48 hours. Guests may purchase up to 12 total Olympic event tickets, and up to 12 additional football tournament tickets. A four-ticket-per-ceremony limit will count towards the general 12-ticket maximum.

Next year, LA28 will launch a multi-platform [verified ticket resale](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/03/la28-organisers-unveil-ticket-sales-dates-verified-resellers/) network and tickets for the Paralympic Games will go on sale.
