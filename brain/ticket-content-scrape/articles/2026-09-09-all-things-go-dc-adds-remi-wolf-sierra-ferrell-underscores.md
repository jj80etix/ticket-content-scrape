# All Things Go DC Adds Remi Wolf, Sierra Ferrell, Underscores

## Summary

All Things Go added Remi Wolf, Sierra Ferrell and Underscores to its DC festival lineup, Sept. 25-27 at Merriweather Post Pavilion. The DC edition marks the festival's 12th installment and fifth straight sellout.

### Key Points
- New adds: Underscores (Friday, with Mitski/Ethel Cain headlining), Remi Wolf and Sierra Ferrell (Sunday, with Brandi Carlile/Lola Young).
- Saturday headliners are Hayley Williams, Muna and Zara Larsson.
- 12th edition of ATG DC; fifth consecutive sellout.
- ATG New York runs the same weekend at Forest Hills Stadium; a Toronto edition ran in June.

Funky soul pop singer-songwriter Remi Wolf, Americana favorite Sierra Ferrell and difficult-to-define dubstepping hyperpopper Underscores have been added to the lineup for All Things Go’s DC edition, scheduled for Sept. 25-27 at Merriweather Post Pavilion.

Underscores will perform on Friday, set to be headlined by Mitski and Ethel Cain. Wolf and Ferrell will both play Sunday with Brandi Carlile and Lola Young on top of the bill. Saturday’s headliners are Hayley Williams, Muna and Zara Larsson.

This year marks the 12th edition of ATG DC and its fifth straight sellout.

ATG New York runs the same weekend as DC at Forest Hills Stadium. A Toronto edition was held in June.
