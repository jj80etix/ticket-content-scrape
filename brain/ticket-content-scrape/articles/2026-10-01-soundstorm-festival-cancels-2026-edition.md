# Soundstorm Festival cancels 2026 edition

## Summary

Saudi Arabia's Soundstorm Festival has cancelled its 2026 edition, with promoter MDLBeast confirming a 2027 return but giving no reason for the cancellation. Content is paywalled beyond a brief lede and gives no ticketing, refund, or industry-structure detail to substantiate any research-question match.

### Key Points
- Soundstorm (Riyadh, ~100,000 daily attendance) cancelled for 2026; 2027 return promised.
- No cancellation reason or refund/patron-impact detail disclosed in available content.
- Part of a reported string of Middle East event cancellations.
