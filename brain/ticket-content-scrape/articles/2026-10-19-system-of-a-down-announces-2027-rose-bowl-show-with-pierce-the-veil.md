# System of a Down Announces 2027 Rose Bowl Show With Pierce the Veil

## Summary

System of a Down announced a June 5, 2027 concert at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena with Pierce the Veil, its first show at the venue and only announced North American date for 2027. The show joins five previously announced international stadium dates, with Faith No More as support.

### Key Points
- June 5, 2027, Rose Bowl, Pasadena, CA -- band's first show there.
- Only announced North American date for 2027; joins 5 international stadium shows with Faith No More.
- International run starts Jan. 15 (Rio de Janeiro) through Feb. 7 (Wellington, NZ).
- Artist presale Oct. 23; general onsale Oct. 26 via Ticketmaster.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/System-of-a-Down-Rose-Bowl.webp)

System of a Down has announced a June 5, 2027, concert at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California, with support from Pierce the Veil.

The concert will be the band’s first performance at the stadium and is billed as System of a Down’s only North American appearance in 2027. Pierce the Veil will open the Pasadena concert amid its “I Can’t Hear You” touring cycle.

The announcement follows the band’s summer stadium tour of Europe and the United Kingdom. System of a Down also recently marked the 25th anniversary of its 2001 album *Toxicity* with an exhibition at Beyond the Streets in Los Angeles. The exhibition featured artifacts and memorabilia from the album’s era.

The Rose Bowl date joins five previously announced international stadium concerts on System of a Down’s 2027 schedule, with Faith No More appearing as a special guest at those shows.

That run begins January 15 at Maracanã Stadium in Rio de Janeiro. The band will then perform January 22 at Accor Stadium in Sydney Olympic Park, January 27 at Marvel Stadium in Melbourne and February 1 at Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane. A February 7 concert at Hnry Stadium in Wellington, New Zealand, concludes the international stretch before the June performance in Pasadena.

An artist presale opens Friday, October 23, at 10 a.m. Pacific time, followed by general ticket sales Monday, October 26, at 10 a.m. Pacific time through Ticketmaster. Presale registration closes Wednesday, October 21, at 9 p.m. Pacific time.

For more information and additional details fans can visit [systemofadown.com](http://systemofadown.com).
