# Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy to Headline High Water Festival 2027

## Summary

Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy will headline the 2027 High Water Festival. The piece covers the festival's headliner announcement.

### Key Points
- Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy announced as 2027 High Water Festival headliners.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/High-Water-Festival.webp)

Jack Johnson and Mt. Joy will headline High Water Festival when it returns to Riverfront Park in North Charleston, South Carolina, on April 17 and 18, 2027.

The ninth edition will feature 20 acts, including Death Cab for Cutie, Rainbow Kitten Surprise, The Revivalists and The Head and the Heart. Performances will take place across two alternating stages, with no overlapping sets throughout the weekend.

Johnson will lead the Saturday, April 17, lineup, which also includes Death Cab for Cutie, The Revivalists, Ole 60 and Mat Kearney. Mt. Joy will headline Sunday, April 18, with Rainbow Kitten Surprise, The Head and the Heart, Congress the Band and Finn Wolfhard also scheduled to perform that day.

The remaining artists announced for the weekend are GoldFord, West 22nd, Mo Lowda & The Humble, Alyssa Grace, Hazlett, Marlon Funaki, Penelope Road, Ax and the Hatchetmen, Angela Autumn and Susannah Joffe.

Ticket presales begin Thursday, October 15, at 10 a.m. Eastern. Fans can register for a presale passcode through the festival’s website to access discounted two-day general admission tickets during a one-hour window.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [highwaterfest.com](http://highwaterfest.com).
