# Julien Baker Announces 2026 Rock Club Residency Tour

## Summary

Julien Baker announced an 18-show residency tour with three-night stands across six U.S. cities this fall, following a surprise sold-out September 9 show at Nashville's Third Man Records.

### Key Points
- Tour begins Oct. 15 at Kilby Court in Salt Lake City and closes with three Seattle shows Nov. 19-21.
- Format mirrors the multi-night residency approach Baker used on her 2024 solo tour.
- Longtime collaborators Matt Gilliam and Calvin Lauber join her throughout.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Julien-Baker.webp)

Julien Baker will return to intimate venues this fall for an 18-show residency tour featuring three-night stands in six U.S. cities.

The run begins October 15 at Kilby Court in Salt Lake City. Baker will then visit Cambridge, Massachusetts; Carrboro, North Carolina; Athens, Georgia; and Lawrence, Kansas before closing with three performances at Neumos in Seattle from November 19 through November 21.

Longtime collaborators Matt Gilliam and Calvin Lauber will join Baker throughout the tour.

The announcement followed a surprise September 9 performance at The Blue Room inside Nashville’s Third Man Records. The concert sold out immediately and featured several unreleased songs, offering fans an early indication of what Baker and her band have been developing.

The residency format continues an approach Baker used during her 2024 solo tour. That run included multiple-night stays at venues such as Thalia Hall in Chicago, Webster Hall in New York and London’s EartH.

Baker’s most recent solo album, *Little Oblivions*,  arrived in 2021. She reunited with Phoebe Bridgers and Lucy Dacus in 2023 for boygenius’ first full-length album, *the record*, which earned the group three Grammy Awards.
