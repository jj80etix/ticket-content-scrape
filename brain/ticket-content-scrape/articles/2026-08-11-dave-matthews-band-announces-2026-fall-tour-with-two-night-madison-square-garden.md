# Dave Matthews Band Announces 2026 Fall Tour With Two-Night Madison Square Garden Finale

## Summary

TicketNews reports that Dave Matthews Band will play a seven-show U.S. fall tour beginning November 4 in Madison, Wisconsin and closing with back-to-back Madison Square Garden shows on November 12-13. A Warehouse Fan Association presale is underway now, with general on-sale opening Friday, August 21 at 10 a.m. local venue time. The piece also covers the band's ongoing environmental partnerships and the remainder of its 2026 summer tour.

### Key Points
- Fall 2026 dates: Nov. 4 Madison, WI (Kohl Center); Nov. 6 Nashville; Nov. 7 Chicago; Nov. 9 Providence; Nov. 10 Baltimore; Nov. 12-13 New York (Madison Square Garden).
- Warehouse Fan Association presale is live now; general public on-sale begins August 21 at 10 a.m. local time.
- Band continues its "On The Road To Zero Waste Tour" partnership with Live Nation venues and REVERB, and its reforestation partnership with The Nature Conservancy, targeting seven million cumulative trees planted by end of 2026.
- Live show recordings continue via DMB Radio on SiriusXM Channel 30 and official audio releases through the nugs streaming service; Warehouse members get a complimentary three-month nugs subscription.
- Dave Matthews Band has sold more than 25 million tickets since its 1991 debut and ranked fourth on Pollstar's "25 Most Popular Touring Artists of the Millennium," the highest-ranked American headliner on that list.

## Transcript

Dave Matthews Band will return to the road this fall for a seven-show U.S. tour, beginning November 4 in Madison, Wisconsin and concluding with back-to-back performances at Madison Square Garden in New York.

The band announced the new dates Tuesday, with an online presale for members of the DMB Warehouse Fan Association already underway. Tickets will go on sale to the general public beginning Friday, August 21 at 10 a.m. local venue time.

The fall run opens November 4 at the Kohl Center in Madison before moving to Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on November 6 and Chicago’s United Center on November 7.

After a brief break, Dave Matthews Band will play Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence on November 9 and CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore on November 10. The tour will close with shows November 12 and 13 at Madison Square Garden.

The newly announced dates will follow the closing stretch of the band’s 2026 summer tour, which resumes August 28 with the first of two concerts at Fiddler’s Green Amphitheatre in Greenwood Village, Colorado.

That summer run concludes with Dave Matthews Band’s traditional Labor Day weekend residency at the Gorge Amphitheatre in Washington. Infinity Song and Stephen Wilson Jr. will support the Friday show, Jonah Kagen and Watchhouse will appear Saturday, and Sierra Hull and Jesse Welles will support Sunday.

The band is also continuing to make live recordings from its tour available to fans. Friday night concerts will air on DMB Radio on SiriusXM Channel 30, while official audio from most other shows on the band’s summer and fall tours will be available through live music streaming service nugs.

Warehouse Fan Association members are currently eligible for a complimentary three-month nugs subscription, while non-members can receive one month free.

Dave Matthews Band remains one of the most commercially successful touring acts of the modern era. The group ranked fourth on Pollstar’s list of the “25 Most Popular Touring Artists of the Millennium,” making it the highest-ranked American headliner on the publication’s list. According to the band’s announcement, Dave Matthews Band has sold more than 25 million concert tickets since its 1991 debut.

The group will also continue its environmental initiatives during the fall dates, including the third year of its “On The Road To Zero Waste Tour” partnership with Live Nation venues and its longstanding work with nonprofit REVERB. The band says its continuing partnership with The Nature Conservancy will bring its cumulative reforestation effort to seven million trees planted by the end of 2026.
