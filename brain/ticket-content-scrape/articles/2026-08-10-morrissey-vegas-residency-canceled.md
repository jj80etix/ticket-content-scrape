# Morrissey Vegas Residency Canceled

## Summary

Morrissey's four-night Encore Theater residency at Wynn Las Vegas (Aug. 14, 15, 18, 19) has been canceled for undisclosed "logistical reasons," per an email to ticketholders and a statement on his website.

### Key Points
- No refund or rebooking details were provided in the reporting.
- Morrissey remains scheduled for Riot Fest (Sept. 19), CBGB Festival, Darker Waves, and a run of fall headline dates.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 1240684929](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/GettyImages-1240684929-scaled.jpg)

Morrissey performs at the Cruel World festival at Rose Bowl in Pasadena on Saturday, May 14, 2022. (Allen J. Schaben / Los Angeles Times via Getty Images)

Morrissey’s four-night run at the Wynn Las Vegas has been canceled.

The English singer was set to perform Aug. 14, 15, 18 and 19, but ticketholders received an email saying, “Hello, it is with great sadness that we inform you that Morrissey – Live in Concert, scheduled for August 14, 15, 18, and 19 at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas, has been canceled. Due to unforeseen logistical issues, the scheduled Las Vegas residency cannot take place.”

A message posted on his website — titled “Morrissey Doesn’t Cancel Vegas” — read “The shows cannot happen for logistical reasons outside of Morrissey’s control.”

He is next scheduled to perform at Riot Fest Sept. 19 and has festival spots scheduled for CBGB Festival in Brooklyn and Darker Waves in Huntington Beach, California, along with a series of headline shows through November.
