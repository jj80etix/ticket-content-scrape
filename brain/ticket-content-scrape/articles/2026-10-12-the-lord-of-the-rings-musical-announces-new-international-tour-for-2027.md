# ‘The Lord of the Rings’ Musical Announces New International Tour for 2027

## Summary

"The Lord of the Rings" musical has announced a new international touring production for 2027, expanding the stage adaptation's reach beyond its existing runs. The announcement covers new markets and dates for the production.

### Key Points
- New international tour of "The Lord of the Rings" musical announced for 2027.
- Expands the stage production's footprint into additional territories.
- Routine touring-arts announcement with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Lord-of-the-Rings-Musical.webp)

A new production of “The Lord of the Rings” musical will launch an international tour in 2027, opening in Istanbul before traveling to cities across Europe and the Middle East.

Titled “The Lord of the Rings — The Epic Stage Adventure,” the production will premiere October 8, 2027, at Istanbul’s Zorlu Performing Arts Center. The adaptation is based on J.R.R. Tolkien’s trilogy of novels.

The announced itinerary also includes Bucharest, Sofia, Prague, Warsaw, Thessaloniki, Athens, Tallinn, Zagreb and Dubai. Additional cities will be announced, along with further venue and performance details. Fans can register through the production’s website for updates on ticket on-sale dates in each market.

Bruce Guthrie, head of film and theatre at the National Centre for Performing Arts in Mumbai, will direct. Tony and Olivier Award winner Andrzej Goulding, whose credits include “Life of Pi,” will provide scenic, video and puppetry design.

The production will be cast and rehearsed in the United Kingdom, with auditions scheduled for early 2027. Casting has not yet been announced.

“This production will thrill fans of the trilogy and fans of epic live entertainment!” producer Kevin Wallace said in the announcement.

The musical features music by Academy Award winner A.R. Rahman, Finnish folk group Värttinä and Tony Award winner Christopher Nightingale. Shaun McKenna and Matthew Warchus wrote the book and lyrics.

Kevin Wallace and William Bennett are producing for KW Productions alongside George Isaak for People Entertainment Group and Middle-earth Enterprises. Peter Huntley and Callum Runciman serve as executive producers and general managers for Smart Entertainment.

The story follows Frodo and the Fellowship on their journey through Middle-earth to destroy the One Ring and defeat the Dark Lord. The new production will bring that quest to the stage through musical storytelling and a staging that incorporates puppetry and video.

The announcement adds another production to the musical’s stage history. A previous version, “The Lord of the Rings: A Musical Tale,” opened at the Watermill Theatre in the United Kingdom in 2023 before making its U.S. premiere at Chicago Shakespeare Theater in summer 2024. That staging was directed by Paul Hart, while Guthrie will lead the newly announced production.

The official website lists the Europe and Middle East tour separately from other upcoming productions, including a February 15–April 17, 2027, engagement at Hale Centre Theatre in Utah. No North American stops have been announced for “The Epic Stage Adventure.”

Theatergoers can visit [LOTRmusical.com](http://www.lotrmusical.com/) for more information and additional details.
