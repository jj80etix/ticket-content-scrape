# ‘Live music creates meaning that virtual interactions cannot’

## Summary

A new Live Nation for Brands report, "The Return to Real," finds fans increasingly favor live music over digital entertainment, with 93% craving "real experiences" and 80% indicating a willingness to spend accordingly — framed by the report as a sponsorship/brand-loyalty opportunity.

### Key Points
- Based on global fan surveys of "thousands of people."
