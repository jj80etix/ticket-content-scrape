# Musiq Soulchild To Headline Gala & Concert Celebrating 100 Years Of Grambling State University’s Marching Band

## Summary

Musiq Soulchild will headline Grambling State University's World Famed Centennial Gala & Concert on Oct. 1, marking 100 years of the university's Tiger Marching Band as part of Grambling's yearlong 125th-anniversary "Multiversary" celebration during Homecoming 2026.

### Key Points
- Musiq Soulchild headlines Grambling State's Centennial Gala & Concert, Oct. 1, at the Fredrick C. Hobdy Assembly Center.
- Event honors 100 years of the World Famed Tiger Marching Band.
- Part of Grambling's yearlong 125th-anniversary "Multiversary" celebration and Homecoming 2026.
- Tickets and sponsorships available via the university's website.

Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter Musiq Soulchild will headline the Grambling State University World Famed Centennial Gala & Concert on Oct. 1. The event will honor 100 years of the university’s World Famed Tiger Marching Band. It will be held at the Fredrick C. Hobdy Assembly Center as part of Homecoming 2026. The celebration also marks Grambling State’s historic Multiversary: the University’s yearlong celebration of 125 years of academic excellence, and 100 years of Tiger Athletics.

Tickets and sponsorship opportunities for The World Famed Centennial Gala & Concert are available now at [gram.edu/world-famed-gala.](https://gram.edu/world-famed-gala.)

Known for his distinctive blend of R&B, soul, and funk, Musiq Soulchild has built a catalog of enduring favorites, including “Just Friends (Sunny),” “Love,” and “Halfcrazy.” His soulful sound brings a fitting musical backdrop to an evening celebrating the World Famed Tiger Marching Band’s century of artistry, tradition, and cultural influence.

“This band has carried the name of Grambling State University to more stages, more screens, and more hearts than perhaps any other single tradition in our history,” said Grambling State University President Dr. Martin Lemelle Jr. “As we celebrate our Multiversary, 125 years of academic excellence, 100 years of Tiger Athletics, and 100 years of this incredible band, there is no better way to honor that legacy than with a night like this, featuring the sound of Musiq Soulchild.”

The World Famed Centennial Gala & Concert will bring together alums, students, supporters, and friends of the University for an evening of dinner, music, and fellowship, with event proceeds and sponsorship support benefiting the band’s next century.
