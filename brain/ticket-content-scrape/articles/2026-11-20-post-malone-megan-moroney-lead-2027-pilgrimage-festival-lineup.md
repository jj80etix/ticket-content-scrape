# Post Malone, Megan Moroney Lead 2027 Pilgrimage Festival Lineup

## Summary

Post Malone and Megan Moroney will headline the 2027 Pilgrimage Music & Cultural Festival, returning to Franklin, Tennessee April 10-11 after a one-year hiatus, with a 28-artist lineup.

### Key Points
- Moroney headlines Saturday with Counting Crows and Whiskey Myers; Post Malone closes Sunday with Mt. Joy and Better Than Ezra.
- Festival canceled its 2026 edition and shifted from its traditional fall slot to April.
- Co-founded by Better Than Ezra's Kevin Griffin, running at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm since 2015.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Pilgrimage-Music-Cultural-Festival.webp)

Post Malone and Megan Moroney will headline the 2027 Pilgrimage Music & Cultural Festival, which returns to Franklin, Tennessee, April 10-11 after taking a year off.

The two-day event at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm will feature 28 artists spanning country, rock, Americana and indie music.

Moroney will headline Saturday, April 10, alongside Counting Crows, Whiskey Myers, Max McNown, Carter Faith and The War and Treaty. Post Malone will close out the festival Sunday, April 11, with Mt. Joy, Dexter and the Moonrocks, Better Than Ezra, Moon Taxi and Congress the Band also appearing that day.

Additional performers scheduled throughout the weekend include Penelope Road, Benny G, Tiger Lewis, KELS, Post Sex Nachos, The Gringos and Slater Nalley.

The lineup announcement marks another step in the festival’s return following its one-year hiatus. Pilgrimage canceled its 2026 edition, and organizers [announced plans in September to move the event from its traditional fall schedule to April](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/pilgrimage-music-cultural-festival-sets-april-2027-return/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Pilgrimage was co-founded by Better Than Ezra frontman Kevin Griffin and has been held at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm since its launch in 2015. The festival has previously featured performers including John Mayer, Noah Kahan, Dave Matthews Band, Zach Bryan, Chris Stapleton and Brandi Carlile.

The most recent edition took place in September 2025, with John Mayer, Kings of Leon and Turnpike Troubadours among the leading acts.
