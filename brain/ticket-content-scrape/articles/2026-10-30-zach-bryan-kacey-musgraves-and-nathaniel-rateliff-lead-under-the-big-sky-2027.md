# Zach Bryan, Kacey Musgraves and Nathaniel Rateliff Lead Under The Big Sky 2027

## Summary

Under The Big Sky festival announced its 2027 lineup for its eighth edition (July 16-18, Whitefish, Montana), headlined by Zach Bryan, Kacey Musgraves, and Nathaniel Rateliff. New additions include Brandon Flowers, The Wallflowers, Dawes, and The Devil Makes Three.

### Key Points
- Eighth edition runs July 16-18, 2027 at Big Mountain Ranch, Whitefish, MT
- Musgraves makes her festival debut; Bryan returns for a third appearance; Rateliff returns for the first time since headlining the 2019 inaugural event
- Returning acts include Sam Barber, Midland, Paul Cauthen, The Dead South, and local group Buffalo Traffic Jam

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Under-the-Big-Sky-1.webp)

Under The Big Sky has announced its 2027 lineup, with Zach Bryan, Kacey Musgraves and Nathaniel Rateliff leading a roster. The festival’s eighth edition will take place July 16 to 18 at Big Mountain Ranch in Whitefish, Montana.

Musgraves will make her Under The Big Sky debut, while Bryan is scheduled for his third appearance. Rateliff will return for the first time since he headlined the festival’s inaugural event in 2019.

Brandon Flowers is another new addition to the festival, along with The Wallflowers, Dawes and The Devil Makes Three. Sam Barber, Midland, Paul Cauthen and The Dead South are among the acts coming back in 2027. Buffalo Traffic Jam will also return, giving the Bozeman group another appearance at a festival held in its home state.

Additional bookings include The Infamous Stringdusters, Futurebirds, Big Richard and The White Buffalo. Allah-Las, Jess Williamson and Hogslop String Band are also scheduled to perform during the three-day gathering.

Montana artists will have a presence beyond Buffalo Traffic Jam’s set. Badger Hound, Chloe Gendrow, Sterling Drake and Tomara are part of the local contingent announced for the weekend.

Ticket sales begin Friday, November 6, with separate presale and general-sale windows. The SMS presale opens at 10 a.m. Mountain Time. General sales follow four hours later, at 2 p.m. Mountain Time that afternoon.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [underthebigskyfest.com](http://underthebigskyfest.com).
