# UTA Signs Wolf Alice

## Summary

UTA has signed the band Wolf Alice for representation.

### Key Points
- UTA signs Wolf Alice.
- Talent-representation news, no ticketing or industry-structure angle.

## Transcript

![Wolf Alice](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Wolf-Alice.jpeg)

Wolf Alice

As Wolf Alice is gearing up to support Olivia Rodrigo on “The Unraveled Tour,” the band has some touring-related news of its own. The Grammy award-nominated British rock band has signed with United Talent Agency for touring representation in North America.  

Wolf Alice’s recent live highlights include a set at Lollapalooza in Chicago and a sold-out show at New York City’s Pier 17. The quartet is set to join Rodrigo on the first leg of her “The Unraveled Tour,” which kicks off with two nights in Hartford, Connecticut, at the PeoplesBank Arena Sept. 25-26.

The band is on board for 18 sold-out dates including stops in Philadelphia (Xfinity Mobile Arena), Toronto (Scotiabank Arena), Chicago (United Center), Boston (TD Garden) and Montreal (Bell Centre).

Wolf Alice is continuing to support the band’s fourth studio album, *The Clearing*. The 2025 album, which was produced by Grammy winner Greg Kurstin, hit the top of the UK Albums chart and earned a spot on the shortlist for the 2025 Mercury Prize. Today’s announcement points out that Wolf Alice is “the only band in the Prize’s history to have a 100% nomination rate across their discography.”

Since the LP was released, Wolf Alice has been busy playing its largest global tour to date including festival sets at Glastonbury and Radio 1’s Big Weekend.

This week, Wolf Alice will mark the album’s one-year anniversary with the release of *The Clearing: B Sides* via a special 7” package. The project features three new songs from the original album’s recording sessions: “Gospel Oak,” “Hit The Sky,” and their cover of The Roches’ “Hammond Song.”

Wolf Alice’s accolades include a Mercury Prize win for their second studio album, *Visions of a Life* (2017), a BRIT Award win for their third studio album, *Blue Weekend* (2021), a BRIT Award win earlier this year for “Group of the Year.” The band was also nominated for BRIT Awards in the “Album of the Year” and “Alternative/Rock Act” categories.
