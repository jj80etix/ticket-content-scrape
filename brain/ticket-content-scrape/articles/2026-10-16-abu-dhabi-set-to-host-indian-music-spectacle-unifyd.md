# Abu Dhabi set to host Indian music ‘spectacle’ Unifyd

## Summary

A new one-day festival, Unifyd, curated by Shah Rukh Khan and headlined by Arijit Singh, Karan Aujla and Sonu Nigam, will debut at Abu Dhabi's Etihad Live on March 20, 2027, focused on Indian music and culture for South Asian diaspora audiences. Tickets will be sold exclusively through District, the ticketing platform recently named Etihad Arena's official partner, with the announcement arriving amid a string of nearby regional festival cancellations tied to GCC-region conflict.

### Key Points
- Debuts March 20, 2027 at Etihad Live (capacity up to 42,000), with 15+ artists expected.
- Produced by D'Yavol Live (Shah Rukh Khan's entertainment arm) in partnership with District, plus Abu Dhabi's Department of Culture and Tourism, Miral and Experience Abu Dhabi.
- Tickets sold exclusively via District, opening Oct. 20; prices not yet announced.
- Organizers plan to expand the festival to additional cities/markets after the Abu Dhabi debut.
- Arrives following cancellations of Untold Dubai and Saudi Arabia's Soundstorm Festival amid regional conflict.

## Transcript

## Abu Dhabi set to host Indian music ‘spectacle’ Unifyd

Arijit Singh, Karan Aujla, and Sonu Nigam will headline the one-day event at the UAE's Etihad Live next March

![Arijit Singh](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Arijit-Singh.png)

Arijit Singh

Bucking the recent trend of cancellations in the region, a new one-day festival centred on Indian talent has been announced for Abu Dhabi next March.

Curated by Bollywood superstar Shah Rukh Khan, Unifyd will bring together top Indian names like Arijit Singh, Karan Aujla, and Sonu Nigam at the UAE’s Etihad Live, an outdoor space flexible up to 42,000 people on 20 March.

The multi-stage gathering is expected to bring over 15 artists and offer cultural experiences for “audiences from India, the Middle East, and the wider global South Asian community”, according to organisers.

It will serve as the flagship event for D’Yavol Live, the entertainment arm of the Khan co-founded luxury collective D’Yavol. The inaugural festival is also being produced in partnership with District, which was named Etihad Arena’s [official ticketing partner](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/etihad-arena-unveils-ticketing-partnership-with-district-by-zomato/) earlier this year.

Abu Dhabi’s Department of Culture and Tourism and state-owned ventures Miral and Experience Abu Dhabi will also collaborate on the event, which has a distinct focus on Indian music and culture.

“I’ve spent a lifetime telling stories on screen and watching them bring people together. Music has that same extraordinary power,” Khan says.

“Unifyd, produced by D’Yavol Live along with District, will unite India’s most incredible artists on a shared stage for the first time. The festival promises to be a true spectacle in terms of its scale, energy, and grandeur.”

> “Indian culture travels further than any single genre or generation”

Along with announcing its debut in Abu Dhabi, organisers also revealed intentions to export the event to other cities and markets.

“Indian music is already global,” says Bunty Singh, a D’Yavol Live co-founder. “Unifyd gives that cultural force a festival platform that matches its scale, ambition, and influence.”

Tickets will be available exclusively through District, opening next Tuesday (20 October). Prices have not yet been revealed.

Arijit Singh, one of India’s most commercially successful singers, is also due to head out on a [seven-city stadium run](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/landmark-runs-from-diljit-dosanjh-and-arijit-singh-to-break-boundaries-in-india/) in his native market next year, the *One Night Only – India Tour.*

The festival will land in the middle of his biggest tour of the market, which spans: Ahmedabad (9 January), Chandigarh (23 Jan), Jaipur (7 February), Mumbai (20 February), Bengaluru (13 March), New Delhi (27 March), Pune (10 April), Indore (1 May), and Hyderabad (15 May).

Of the festival, District CEO Rahul Ganjoo says: “Indian culture travels further than any single genre or generation, and Unifyd is built to bring all of it together on one stage. Together with D’Yavol Live, we are creating an experience for audiences and the diaspora that goes far beyond a single night of performances, something they will remember long after the lights come up.”

Unifyd’s announcement arrives following a string of nearby cancellations, with the UAE’s [Untold Dubai](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/untold-dubai-becomes-the-latest-disrupted-middle-east-festival/) and Saudi Arabia’s [Soundstorm Festival](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/soundstorm-festival-cancels-2026-edition/) both being axed this month amid ongoing warfare in the GCC region.
