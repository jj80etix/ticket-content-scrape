# Huddersfield Giants offers free tickets for supporters ahead of stadium move reveal

## Summary

Rugby league club Huddersfield Giants are offering free admission (no booking fees, account required) to their August 8 home game against Catalans Dragons, timed to coincide with the announcement of a preferred site for the club's new stadium ahead of a 2027 move. The club must leave its current Accu Stadium in September after owner Ken Davy's plan to acquire nearby Shay Stadium as an interim home fell through, prompting his resignation as chairman.

### Key Points
- Huddersfield Giants will offer free, fee-free entry to their August 8 Super League match against Catalans Dragons.
- The free game coincides with an announcement of a favored site for the club's new permanent stadium for 2027.
- The Giants must vacate Accu Stadium (shared with Huddersfield Town FC) after this season.
- A prior deal for owner Ken Davy to buy Shay Stadium in Halifax as an interim home collapsed, and Davy has since resigned as chairman in favor of Ralph Rimmer.
- This is a club/venue relocation and fan-promotion story with no ticketing-platform, pricing, or regulatory angle.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Huddersfield Giants fans will be treated to free entry for the team’s Betfred Super League home game against Catalans Dragons on August 8, ahead an announcement from the club.**

In what the Giants have called “possibly the biggest announcement in the club’s 162-year history”, a favoured location for the side’s new home stadium for 2027 will be announced. Tickets for the game will be completely free, with no booking fees – all fans need is an account.

The Huddersfield Giants will leave the Accu Stadium at the end of the season in September, with the need to find a new home ever increasing. Owner Ken Davy pulled out of a deal last month to acquire Shay Stadium in neighbouring Halifax, which had been pinpointed as a temporary home for the Giants while the club explored plans for a new stadium of its own in Huddersfield. Davy then resigned as chairman, a role he has held since 1996, with Ralph Rimmer stepping into the position of executive chairman.

Shay Stadium is home of National League football club Halifax Town as well as second-tier rugby league team, Halifax Panthers. Calderdale Metropolitan Council had [originally approved](https://www.thestadiumbusiness.com/2025/03/18/halifax-stadium-to-be-sold-to-huddersfield-giants-owner/) a deal to sell The Shay to Davy in March, 2025.

The Giants currently share Accu Stadium with League One football club Huddersfield Town, which secured full ownership and operational control of the venue [last September](https://www.thestadiumbusiness.com/2025/09/26/huddersfield-town-secures-control-of-accu-stadium/). The rugby league side agreed a deal to remain at the stadium [for the 2026 season](https://www.thestadiumbusiness.com/2025/09/17/huddersfield-giants-set-to-stay-at-accu-stadium/) to allow more time to find a long-term home.

**‘Relaxed and very confident’**

Davy said in a [statement](https://www.giantsrl.com/article/10008/free-entry-for-giants-vs-catalans): “The pressure to find a new stage for the 2027 season hasn’t been enjoyable but I am now relaxed and very confident that next season will be very special indeed.

“We want to get as many people to the Catalans game as possible so I would like to offer an extended invite to everyone across Kirklees to join us, free of charge, at the Accu Stadium to witness this momentous announcement.”

Rimmer added that despite extensive talks with the local authority, “all avenues for an easily developable site within the town perimeter have been exhausted within the necessary timeframe”.

He continued: “However, the vision for the Giants remains the same: a consistently dominant Super League force, both on and off the field producing sell-out crowds in its own venue.

“They say that you should never miss the opportunity in a crisis and the Giants have had to grab the nettle to make this happen. We are striving to deliver elite Super League experiences to our fans, commercial partners and the rest of the competition and believe that when our plans are revealed, then all will be impressed and excited by our Giant future.”

The Giants currently sit bottom of the Super League table.
