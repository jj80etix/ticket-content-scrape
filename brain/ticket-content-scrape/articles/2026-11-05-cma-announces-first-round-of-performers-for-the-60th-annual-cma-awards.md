# CMA Announces First Round of Performers For “The 60th Annual CMA Awards”

## Summary

The CMA and ABC announced the first round of performers for the 60th annual CMA Awards, airing live Nov. 18 from Nashville's Bridgestone Arena. The lineup includes Cody Johnson, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Tim McGraw, Kacey Musgraves, and others, plus a tribute to Dolly Parton.

### Key Points
- Broadcasts live Nov. 18, 8–11 p.m. ET on ABC/Disney+, next-day on Hulu.
- Hosted by Lainey Wilson for a third consecutive year.
- Miranda Lambert leads nominees with 7 nods this year and a record 70 career nominations for a female artist.
- Ella Langley leads overall nominees with 9 nods after winning 3 CMAs last year.

The Country Music Association (CMA) and ABC have announced the first round of performers for “The 60th Annual CMA Awards.” Celebrating six decades this year’s lineup includes Cody Johnson, Miranda Lambert, Ella Langley, Tim McGraw, Kacey Musgraves, The Red Clay Strays, Zach Top and Tucker Wetmore, plus a special tribute to Dolly Parton.

Hosted by Lainey Wilson for a third consecutive year, the CMA Awards airs live from Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena on Nov. 18 (8:00-11:00 PM/ET) on ABC and Disney+ and is available next day on Hulu.

Reigning Male Vocalist of the Year Cody Johnson is up for four CMA Awards this year, including Entertainer, Male Vocalist, Single and Music Video of the Year.

Miranda Lambert earns seven nominations this year, including two for Single of the Year and one for Album of the Year as a producer, two for Musical Event of the Year, Song of the Year as a songwriter and her 19th nomination for Female Vocalist of the Year. With 70 career nominations, Lambert holds the record for the most CMA Awards nominations by a female artist.

After taking home three CMA Awards last year, Ella Langley leads this year’s nominees with nine nods, including Entertainer, Album, Female Vocalist, Musical Event and Music Video of the Year, plus two nominations each for Single and Song of the Year.

Newly inducted Country Music Hall of Fame member Tim McGraw, a Musical Event of the Year nominee this year with 40 career CMA Awards nominations, will celebrate the milestone with a performance.

Kacey Musgraves earns five nominations this year, including Album, Song, Female Vocalist, Musical Event and Music Video of the Year.

Reigning Vocal Group of the Year The Red Clay Strays are nominated again this year in the category.

Reigning CMA New Artist of the Year Zach Top is a two-time nominee this year, including Male Vocalist and Album of the Year.

Tucker Wetmore earns his second CMA Awards nomination this year in the New Artist of the Year category.

The night will also feature a tribute to Dolly Parton, honoring the music icon whose influence and legacy has touched every corner of entertainment and beyond.

Additional performers and presenters for “The 60th Annual CMA Awards” will be announced in the weeks ahead.

A limited number of CMA Awards tickets are still available. “The 60th Annual CMA Awards” is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as Executive Producer, Alan Carter is Director, and Jon Macks is Head Writer.
