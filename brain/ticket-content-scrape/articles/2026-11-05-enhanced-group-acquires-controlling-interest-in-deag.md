# Enhanced Group Acquires Controlling Interest In DEAG

## Summary

Enhanced Group Inc. has signed definitive agreements to acquire roughly 77% of Deutsche Entertainment AG (DEAG), a Berlin-headquartered live-entertainment and ticketing platform, calling it a "first step" in a broader sports-and-live-entertainment buy-and-build strategy. The combined businesses are projected to generate ~$670m in 2027 revenue and ~$26m in Adjusted EBITDA. DEAG keeps its existing management under incoming CEO Markus Breitenecker and will continue its own buy-and-build strategy, having completed 30+ acquisitions since 2019 consolidating Europe's fragmented live-entertainment market.

### Key Points
- Enhanced Group acquires ~77% controlling interest in DEAG via exchange agreements.
- Projected combined 2027 revenue ~$670m; Adjusted EBITDA ~$26m.
- DEAG retains its management team under incoming CEO Markus Breitenecker (ex-ProSiebenSat.1 COO).
- DEAG has made 30+ acquisitions since 2019 consolidating independent European promoters.
- Enhanced frames the deal as the first step in its own sports/live-entertainment buy-and-build strategy, with more acquisitions expected.
- DEAG is explicitly described as operating "proprietary ticketing infrastructure" alongside its promoter/venue network.

### Highlights
- Enhanced Group takes ~77% of DEAG; combined 2027 revenue projected at $670m.
- DEAG — a ticketing-platform operator, not just a promoter — continues its own acquisition strategy (30+ deals since 2019) under new ownership.
- Enhanced signals further sports/live-entertainment M&A ahead.

### Industry Problem

European live-entertainment and ticketing remain structurally fragmented, which is precisely what has driven DEAG's own decade of roll-up acquisitions; new, well-capitalized ownership extending that roll-up strategy — now backed by a US sports/entertainment balance sheet — could accelerate consolidation of independent ticketing infrastructure Etix might otherwise serve or compete with.

### Proposed Solution

#monitor/#evolve — track which independent European promoters/ticketing operators DEAG targets next under Enhanced's capital backing, since further roll-ups could consolidate client relationships away from independent platforms and shift where Etix finds whitespace for primary-ticketing partnerships in European markets.

Screenshot from the Enhanced website, a business that’s focused on taking “human potential further than it’s ever gone”.

Enhanced Group Inc., by its own admission “a sports, entertainment and performance products company,” announced that it has entered into definitive exchange agreements with certain shareholders of Deutsche Entertainment AG (DEAG) to acquire an approximately 77% controlling interest in DEAG, a leading Berlin-headquartered live entertainment and ticketing platform operating across Europe, with a growing presence in the U.S..

Enhanced, which believes in-real-life, human experiences cannot be replaced, expects the acquisition to transform the business “into a sports and live entertainment powerhouse, bringing together two complementary businesses in a major strategic milestone.”

“Enhanced believes demand for live, shared human experiences will only grow as AI and automation reshape work,” the press announcement continues.

Enhanced acquired approximately 77% of DEAG, which it described as “a first step.” The combination of both businesses is “expected to generate approximately $670 million in combined revenue and $26 million in Adjusted EBITDA in 2027.”

Enhanced’s owned sports properties, including the Enhanced Games and Enhanced Breakers Series, and its Live Enhanced telehealth businesses are complemented by DEAG’s live event offering across festivals, concerts and touring, spoken word and other live experiences, along with its promoter network, production capabilities, venue relationships and proprietary ticketing infrastructure.

DEAG will continue to operate under its existing management team, led by incoming CEO Markus Breitenecker, who joins from ProSiebenSat.1 Media SE, where he was Chief Operating Officer, and will pursue its established buy-and-build strategy, which Enhanced management intends to support further by contributing additional growth capital. Since 2019, DEAG has successfully completed more than 30 acquisitions, consolidating independent promoters and regional live entertainment businesses in Europe’s fragmented live entertainment market.

Enhanced’s management views the acquisition of DEAG as the first step in its own buy-and-build strategy, with further acquisitions in sports and live entertainment envisaged over the coming years.

More details from today’s announcement:

In a so-called “all-stock transaction”, Enhanced is expected to issue one Enhanced share for approximately 1.7 DEAG shares – hence approximately 11 million newly issued shares of its Class A common stock – to the participating DEAG shareholders, with no cash consideration as part of the transaction.

Enhanced is issuing the consideration shares at $8 per share, an approximately 13% premium to yesterday’s closing price of $7.11. This values 100% of DEAG’s equity at approximately $115 million, with an implied enterprise value of approximately $139 million. This represents 5.3x DEAG’s FY2027E Adjusted EBITDA of approximately $26.5 million, compared with a median of 14.1x for listed live entertainment peers (CTS Eventim 7.7x, Live Nation 14.1x, TKO Group 15.0x), an implied 63% discount. FY2027E figures are Enhanced management estimates.

The combined group is expected to generate approximately $670 million in combined revenue and $26 million in Adjusted EBITDA in 2027, representing 21.5% revenue growth and an approximately $65.5 million improvement in Adjusted EBITDA year over year, providing Enhanced with significant operating scale and a broader platform for organic growth and further acquisitions.
