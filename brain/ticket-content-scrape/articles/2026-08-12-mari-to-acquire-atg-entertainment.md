# Mari to acquire ATG Entertainment

## Summary

Ari Emanuel's Mari has agreed to acquire UK theatre and ticketing operator ATG Entertainment from Providence Equity Partners in a deal reported around $6bn (€5.2bn), pending regulatory approval. ATG's 70-venue portfolio — including 10 West End and 7 Broadway theatres, plus its in-house ticketing platform — will keep its existing brand and leadership after the deal closes.

### Key Points
- Reported deal value ~$6bn (£4.44bn/€5.2bn); official terms undisclosed.
- ATG's portfolio spans UK, US, Germany, and Spain, including 10 West End theatres and a majority stake (since 2023) in Broadway's Jujamcyn Theaters, making it Broadway's third-largest theatre owner.
- Mari, founded by Emanuel in 2025, already owns ticketing platform TodayTix Group plus Frieze, Barrett-Jackson, and other live-experience brands.
- Rumors of a sale first surfaced in May 2026, with Mari's interest confirmed in June.
- Transaction subject to regulatory approval; ATG to retain its brand, leadership, and creative independence post-close.

### Highlights
- $6bn acquisition folds a 70-venue, scaled ticketing operator into Mari, which already owns ticketing platform TodayTix — consolidating two ticketing assets under one PE-to-strategic-buyer transition.
- Deal follows Mari's 2025 acquisition of TodayTix, its second major ticketing-adjacent purchase within a year.

### Industry Problem
Rapid roll-up of venue and ticketing assets by well-capitalized buyers like Mari reduces the number of independent theatre/venue operators who might otherwise be open to partnering with non-Ticketmaster/AXS primary platforms.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor whether Mari migrates ATG's in-house ticketing onto TodayTix post-close — that would be the clearest signal of how far platform consolidation is displacing independent primary-ticketing vendors from major venue portfolios.

**Ari Emanuel’s global events and experiences company Mari has agreed to acquire ATG Entertainment from Providence Equity Partners.**

While the financial details of the agreement have not been disclosed, it has been reported the deal is worth somewhere in the region of $6bn (£4.44bn/€5.2bn).

ATG Entertainment has a 70-strong portfolio of West End and regional venues in the UK, as well as Broadway theatres in the US, and locations in Germany and Spain. It also boasts producer and promoter relationships, in-house production companies, a scaled ticketing platform, hospitality capabilities and more.

The group’s 10 West End theatres include the Lyceum, Savoy Theatre, Playhouse Theatre, Harold Pinter Theatre and more, while it is the third largest theatre owner on Broadway with seven theatres after it acquired a majority stake in Jujamcyn Theaters [in 2023](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2023/02/broadway-theatre-owner-jujamcyn-theaters-set-to-combine-with-uks-ambassador-theatre-group/).

Reports of a potential ATG Entertainment sale first surfaced in [May](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/05/atg-entertainment-parent-company-eyes-potential-sale-reports/), with Mari’s interest in the company appearing the following [month](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/06/mari-group-eyeing-up-atg-entertainment-acquisition-reports/).

“Mari understands live entertainment and what matters most to ATG: our theatres, the people behind them and the relationships we have built with producers, artists and audiences,” said Melanie Smith, global chief executive of ATG Entertainment.

“That makes Mari the right home for our next chapter. We look forward to this new era and thank Providence for their unwavering support over the past thirteen years.”

Sonia Friedman, founder and director, Sonia Friedman Productions, a producing partner of ATG Entertainment, added: “As a producer, I have always believed in the power of live theatre to bring people together, challenge perspectives and create unforgettable shared experiences. Our industry has always needed partners prepared to think long term, invest with confidence and support creativity with real ambition.

“What I find compelling about Mari is its breadth of experience across live events and its respect for creative independence. That combination gives us the chance to champion bold new work, reach broader and more diverse audiences and strengthen the future of live theatre for generations to come.”

Emanuel is also the chief executive and executive chairman of TKO Group Holdings, which owns the UFC, WWE, IMG, On Location and more, as well as the CEO of WME Group.

He set up Mari in 2025, [acquiring TodayTix Group](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2025/10/ari-emanuels-new-company-mari-acquires-todaytix-group/) not long after its inception.

Mari also owns and operates events and experiences across sports, art, entertainment and lifestyle, including Frieze, Barrett-Jackson, the Miami Open, the Mutua Madrid Open, Hyde Park Winter Wonderland and the Hampton Court Palace and Blenheim Palace Festivals.

The transaction is subject to regulatory approvals and customary closing conditions, and until completion, Mari and ATG will continue to operate as separate companies. After the deal is finalised, ATG will continue under its existing brand and leadership, retaining its commitment to creative independence.

Andrew Tisdale, vice chairman at Providence, said: “We’ve been proud to partner with ATG over the past thirteen years as it has grown into one of the world’s leading live entertainment companies.

“Together, we’ve supported its international expansion, invested in iconic venues and strengthened the business through strategic acquisitions.

“Central to that success has been ATG’s fantastic team, which has consistently expanded during our ownership and has been instrumental in driving the company’s growth. We believe ATG is well positioned for its next chapter and that Mari is the right partner to build on that success. We wish the entire ATG team all the best.”
