# Four Year Strong Sets 2027 Anniversary Tour

## Summary

Four Year Strong will tour the US in spring 2027 to mark 25 years as a band and the 20th anniversary of their album "Rise or Die Trying," playing the record in full across 15+ cities from March 20 (Buffalo) to April 30 (Boston).

### Key Points
- Tour spans March 20-April 30, 2027, across cities including Pittsburgh, Chicago, Denver, Los Angeles and Boston.
- Full performances of 2007 album "Rise or Die Trying" on every date.
- Support acts: Drug Church, Heart Attack Man, Sunbloc.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Four-Year-Strong-.webp)

Four Year Strong will celebrate 25 years as a band with a spring 2027 U.S. tour featuring full performances of *Rise or Die Trying*.

The tour is set to begin March 20 in Buffalo at Electric City. From there, the run is slated to make stops in cities such as Pittsburgh, Cleveland, Detroit, Chicago, Minneapolis, Denver, San Diego, Los Angeles, Nashville, Philadelphia and New York before wrapping up on April 30 in Boston at Citizens House of Blues.

Drug Church, Heart Attack Man and Sunbloc are set to support the run. The tour also marks the 20th anniversary of *Rise or Die Trying*, the band’s second album, released in 2007. In a statement, Four Year Strong described the tour as an opportunity to recognize both its history together and the album that helped establish its career.

“2027 is a big year for us. Not only have we had 25 years as a band, playing incredible shows and tours, doing things we never dreamed we would do, but also 20 years ago we released Rise or Die Trying, the record that started everything for us,” the band said. “It gave us the foundation for everything that came after.”

“We will be playing Rise or Die Trying in full, as well as songs that span the last 25 years, featuring lots of deep cuts and rare setlist appearances!” the group said.

Four Year Strong also highlighted the supporting lineup in its statement, expressing enthusiasm about sharing the tour with Drug Church, Heart Attack Man and Sunbloc.

“We are incredibly pumped to be doing it with Drug Church, Heart Attack Man, and Sunbloc,” the band said. “This is going to be something special, and we can’t wait to spend it with all of you!”
