# Madonna caps off WorldPride’s second-annual music festival

## Summary

Madonna closed out the second-annual WorldPride Music Festival in Amsterdam, bringing her Club Confessions show to AFAS Live alongside Kylie Minogue, Stuart Price, and Honey Dijon. The two-night, multi-stage event, staged in partnership with Insomniac Events, also featured Paris Hilton, Bebe Rexha, and Purple Disco Machine, and follows last year's debut festival in Washington D.C.

### Key Points
- Festival staged Aug. 1–2 at Amsterdam's AFAS Live (6,000-capacity venue) in partnership with Insomniac Events.
- It was Madonna's first WorldPride performance since 2019 (New York, Stonewall 50th anniversary).
- Last year's inaugural festival in Washington D.C. drew 60,000 guests over two days; the series next moves to Miami's Factory Town in February.
- Coincided with a deadly extremist attack on a Berlin Pride festival the prior week and UK Pride events including Brighton & Hove's 35th-anniversary celebration.

## Madonna caps off WorldPride’s second-annual music festival

"Mother is here, and this is gay heaven," she said on the AFAS Live stage, as she brought Club Confessions to the world's biggest LGBTQ+ event

![Kylie Minogue & Madonna at WorldPride](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Kylie-Minogue-Madonna-at-WorldPride.png)

Kylie Minogue & Madonna at WorldPride © WorldPride

Madonna closed out WorldPride Music Festival in Amsterdam over the weekend (1 August), bringing *Club Confessions* to AFAS Live to cap off the second-annual festival at the biggest LGBTQ+ event in the world.

Her set served as the finale of a two-night, multi-stage programme that also featured Paris Hilton, Bebe Rexha, Purple Disco Machine, Oliver Heldens, and more. It was the second time a full-scale festival has been staged during the annual WorldPride celebrations, with last year’s debut in Washington D.C. drawing 60,000 guests across two days.

“Mother is here, and this is gay heaven,” she said on stage. During her performances, she was also joined by Kylie Mingoue, longtime collaborator Stuart Price, and Honey Dijon.

It marked Madonna’s first performance during WorldPride since 2019, when she closed Pride Island at the New York event during the 50th anniversary of the Stonewall uprising.

Since 2000, WorldPride has been staged in a different global city, advancing visibility through marches, cultural programming, and a human rights conference. The multi-stage festival component was added last year, and looks to remain a regular feature of forthcoming events.

“Pride has always been about bringing our community together,” says Jake Resnicow, executive producer of WorldPride Music Festival. “At a moment when too many people are being told to hide or make themselves smaller, gathering openly, joyfully and without apology matters more than ever. I couldn’t be prouder of what this festival brought to life in Amsterdam – one dance floor, completely free.”

The event was staged in partnership with Insomniac Events at the 6,000-capacity venue in the Dutch capital.

“Jake has an incredible connection to this community and a big vision for what Pride can be,” says Pasquale Rotella, Insomniac’s founder. “I love collaborating with visionaries who are deeply passionate and genuinely care about the culture they represent. Jake embodies that spirit, always striving to deliver and exceed expectations, perfectly aligning with the goals of Insomniac.

“Bringing *Club Confessions* to WorldPride Amsterdam isn’t just an event; it was a vibrant celebration of love, unity, and authenticity. This was a really special experience!” he adds.

The festival is set to be staged next in Miami in February, bringing a multi-stage experience to the Factory Town venue.

WorldPride arrived a week after a deadly extremist [attack on a Pride festival](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/live/c3r2nz9ed3lt) in Berlin, where one woman was killed, and at least 29 people were injured when an attacker ploughed into a crowd with a van. Over the weekend, hundreds of thousands participated in Pride events in the UK and Europe.

In the southern part of the UK, thousands descended on Brighton & Hove to mark 35 years of Pride celebrations at Pride on the Park, which brought the likes of Raye and Diana Ross as this year’s headliners. The two-day event also featured Jessie J, Self Esteem, Leigh-Anne, G Flip, Moonchild Sanelly, RuPaul, Bimini, Mel C, and more.
