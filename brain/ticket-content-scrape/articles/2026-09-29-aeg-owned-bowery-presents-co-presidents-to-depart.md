# AEG-owned Bowery Presents’ co-presidents to depart

## Summary

Bowery Presents co-presidents John Moore and Jim Glancy are departing the AEG-owned promoter, with four internal executives (Josh Bhatti, Charley Magrew, Andrew Martin, Josh Moore) promoted to senior VPs to collectively lead the company going forward, reporting to AEG Presents' Brent Fedrizzi.

### Key Points
- Moore co-founded Bowery Presents in 2004; AEG Presents fully acquired the company in 2016. Glancy joined as partner from Live Nation in 2006.
- New leadership splits regional duties: Magrew/Martin/Moore lead New York (marketing/ticketing/sponsorship, talent, and business/legal affairs respectively); Bhatti leads New England from Boston.
- Bowery Presents has promoted artists including LCD Soundsystem, Brandi Carlile and Tyler, the Creator across NYC, Boston and Philadelphia.
- Transition framed as promoting from within the existing team rather than an external hire.

## Transcript

## AEG-owned Bowery Presents’ co-presidents to depart

John Moore and Jim Glancy are leaving the East Coast promoter, making way for a new four-person leadership team

![John Moore & Jim Glancy](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/John-Moore-Jim-Glancy-©-Danny-Clinch.png)

John Moore & Jim Glancy © Danny Clinch

The Bowery Presents’ co-presidents, John Moore and Jim Glancy, are exiting their roles at the AEG Presents-owned US promoter, making way for a new four-person leadership team.

The leading East Coast concert promoter and venue operator, which was co-founded by Moore in 2004 and fully acquired by AEG Presents in 2016, has brought artists including LCD Soundsystem, Brandi Carlile, and Tyler, the Creator to cities like New York City, Boston, and Philadelphia. Glancy joined as a partner from Live Nation in 2006.

“Founding The Bowery Presents and partnering together to grow the company has been an incredibly rewarding chapter for us,” say Glancy and Moore in a joint statement. “We’re proud of what we’ve built alongside an extraordinary team and grateful to everyone who has been part of that journey.”

As part of the transition, Josh Bhatti, Charley Magrew, Andrew Martin, and Josh Moore have all been promoted to senior vice presidents and will collectively assume leadership of the company. They will report to Brent Fedrizzi, AEG Presents’ president, North American Regional Offices, who thanked Moore and Glancy for their contributions to AEG.

“The Bowery Presents is a renowned, internationally recognised brand that has been instrumental in so many artists’ careers, and it was important for us to find the right group of people that can steward the company into this important next phase,” Fedrizzi says. “Being able to do this from within the ranks is a testament to the culture that Jim and John have built, and I couldn’t be happier for Andrew, Charley, Josh, and Josh.”

> “We’re leaving the company in capable hands”

In their new roles, Magrew, Martin, and Moore will lead the strategic direction and day-to-day leadership of the New York office, while Boston-based Bhatti will lead the New England regional business.

Magrew will lead on marketing, ticketing, sponsorship, and venue initiatives, while Moore will oversee talent, both of whom will be based in the New York office. Martin, on the other hand, will be based in Nashville and will oversee business and legal affairs.

In addition to promotion, Bowery Presents also operates and programmes several venues, including New York’s Brooklyn Steel, Terminal 5, Webster Hall, and Under the K Bridge Park, along with Boston’s Roadrunner, Philadelphia’s Franklin Music Hall, and more.

“Andrew, Charley, Josh, and Josh have made significant contributions to The Bowery Presents during their respective tenures, so it’s gratifying to see them all take this next step together. We’re leaving the company in capable hands,” Moore and Glancy say.
