# Hannah Cruz and T.R. Knight to Join Off-Broadway’s ‘Little Shop of Horrors’

## Summary

Hannah Cruz and T.R. Knight will take over the leads (Audrey and Seymour) in Off-Broadway's long-running "Little Shop of Horrors" revival at the Westside Theatre starting October 27, succeeding Betsy Wolfe and Ethan Slater.

### Key Points
- Cast change effective Oct. 27; outgoing leads' final performances are Oct. 25.
- The revival passed 2,210 performances in September, becoming the longest-running "Little Shop of Horrors" staging, surpassing the original 1982 Off-Broadway production.
- Produced by Tom Kirdahy, Robert Ahrens and Hunter Arnold.

Hannah Cruz and T.R. Knight will take over the lead roles in the Off-Broadway revival of “Little Shop of Horrors” at the Westside Theatre beginning October 27.

Cruz will play Audrey, while Knight will play Seymour. Their arrival follows the final performances of current stars Betsy Wolfe and Ethan Slater on October 25.

Producers Tom Kirdahy, Robert Ahrens and Hunter Arnold welcomed the new pairing. “There is something deeply special about Seymour and Audrey,” they said, adding that they look forward to seeing what Cruz and Knight bring to the roles.

The cast change comes as the revival continues a long run at the Westside Theatre. In September, the production passed 2,210 performances, becoming the longest-running staging of “Little Shop of Horrors.” The previous record belonged to the original Off-Broadway production, which opened in 1982.

The musical features a book and lyrics by Howard Ashman and music by Alan Menken. Michael Mayer directs the current revival, which opened in 2019 and has brought a succession of performers into its central roles. The production has received Drama Desk, Drama League and Outer Critics Circle honors for musical revival.

Another cast transition is also underway. Reg Rogers played his final performance as Mushnik on September 27, and Stephen DeRosa is scheduled to return to the role September 29. Continuing performers include Claybourne Elder as dentist Orin Scrivello and Christian McQueen as the voice of Audrey II, the plant at the center of the story.
