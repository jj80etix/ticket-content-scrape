# ‘Demand is only part of it’: Versa on unlocking Chinese touring

## Summary

Talent-buying company Versa, which passed $100 million in deals in 2026, says overseas acts touring China face permitting, cultural-approval, and financial-regulation complexity that makes local partnerships essential. The company aims to make sophisticated local buying easier for touring artists entering Chinese and East Asian markets.

### Key Points
- Versa surpassed $100M in talent-buying deals in 2026
- Cites complex permitting, cultural approvals, and financial peculiarities as barriers to Chinese touring
- Emphasizes long-term local government relationships as an under-used strategic asset for overseas tours
