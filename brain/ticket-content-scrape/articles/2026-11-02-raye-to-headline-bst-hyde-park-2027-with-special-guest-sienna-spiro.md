# RAYE to Headline BST Hyde Park 2027 With Special Guest Sienna Spiro

## Summary

RAYE was announced as the second headliner for BST Hyde Park 2027, performing July 10 with support from Sienna Spiro, following her Mercury Prize win for "This Music May Contain Hope."

### Key Points
- Joins previously announced first headliner Bon Jovi (June 24).
- General tickets go on sale Nov. 10; an Amex cardholder presale starts Nov. 3.
- RAYE has a packed run of shows beforehand, including iHeartRadio Jingle Ball dates and Spilt Milk Festival in Australia, plus 2027 Lollapalooza South America and European festival dates ahead of Hyde Park.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/RAYE-Hyde-Park.webp)

RAYE has been announced as a headliner for BST Hyde Park 2027, with the singer-songwriter scheduled to perform July 10 at the London concert series.

The appearance will mark her first time headlining the event. British singer Sienna Spiro will join the bill as a special guest, and additional supporting acts will be announced in the coming months. RAYE is the second headliner confirmed for next summer’s series, following Bon Jovi, which will perform June 24.

The announcement follows RAYE’s Mercury Prize win for her second album, *This Music May Contain Hope*. Released in March, the record reached No. 1 on the Official U.K. Albums Chart and No. 11 on the Billboard 200.

Tickets for RAYE’s show go on sale November 10 at 10 a.m. GMT through the BST Hyde Park website. An American Express cardholder presale begins November 3 at 10 a.m. GMT.

Ahead of her Hyde Park appearance, RAYE will perform at iHeartRadio Jingle Ball concerts in Fort Worth on December 1, Los Angeles on December 4 and Rosemont, Illinois, on December 7, 2026.

She will then head to Australia for Spilt Milk Festival appearances in Canberra on December 12, Gold Coast on December 13, Geelong on December 19 and Perth on December 20. Her March 2027 schedule includes Lollapalooza festivals in Argentina, Chile and Brazil, along with Colombia’s Festival Estéreo Picnic.

European dates follow at Pinkpop in Landgraaf, the Netherlands, on June 18, Werchter Boutique in Belgium on June 26 and Open’er Festival in Gdynia, Poland, on July 3, before her July 10 London show.
