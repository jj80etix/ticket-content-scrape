# TRG Arts partners with DynamO Pricing to bring dynamic pricing to arts and culture organisations

## Summary

TRG Arts, a consultancy for arts and culture organizations, has partnered with DynamO Pricing to offer its clients an automated dynamic-pricing platform that adjusts ticket prices to real demand within organization-set guardrails, with TRG citing reported revenue uplifts of up to 30% among adopters. The partnership coincides with a new live integration between DynamO Pricing and Tessitura, letting Tessitura users adopt demand-based pricing without changing their existing ticketing infrastructure, on a commission-only pricing model with no upfront or fixed fees.

### Key Points
- TRG Arts will offer DynamO Pricing as its preferred dynamic-pricing solution to its global arts/culture client base.
- Reported revenue uplifts of up to 30% among TRG clients that have adopted the DynamO platform.
- DynamO Pricing is now integrated with Tessitura, with the first joint client already signed; onboarding is described as typically completed in days.
- Commercial model is commission-only, with no upfront fees and no fixed costs, per DynamO's CRO Attila Gazdag.
- DynamO's technology is built by a cross-disciplinary team of developers, data scientists and economists, and has prior traction in sports, live events and attractions.

### Highlights
- Tessitura (a Tier 1 primary-platform competitor tracked by this pipeline) now has a live DynamO Pricing integration, with its first joint client signed — a concrete AI/dynamic-pricing feature shipped into a competitor's ecosystem.
- Reported revenue uplift of up to 30% for adopters, per TRG Arts.
- Commission-only pricing model with no upfront/fixed fees, and onboarding measured in days — lowers the adoption barrier for ticketing platforms' clients.

### Industry Problem
Arts and culture organizations historically under-price demand-variable inventory (static pricing leaves revenue on the table) but are wary of alienating audiences with aggressive dynamic pricing; this partnership frames demand-based pricing as achievable "ethically" within guardrails the organization controls, addressing both the revenue gap and the reputational risk of poorly-explained price surges.

### Proposed Solution
DynamO Pricing supplies the demand-modeling and pricing engine as a bolt-on integration (now live on Tessitura) rather than requiring clients to change ticketing platforms, monetized via commission only. For Etix, this is a benchmark AI/pricing capability to watch or match — either building native demand-based pricing guardrails into the core platform or evaluating a similar low-friction pricing-partner integration, since competitors are now able to offer this without asking clients to migrate systems.

**TRG Arts, a consultancy for arts and culture organisations, has agreed a partnership with DynamO Pricing, making the dynamic pricing platform available to its global client base.**

The partnership will see DynamO Pricing offered as the preferred dynamic pricing solution to TRG’s clients, giving them access to intelligent pricing technology that adjusts ticket prices in response to real demand, within guardrails set by each organisation.

So far, TRG clients have adopted DynamO Pricing’s automated intelligent platform, with reported revenue uplifts of up to 30%.

“We chose to partner DynamO Pricing for its proven, data-driven platform, built by a cross-disciplinary team of developers, data scientists, and economists specialising in pricing,” said Jill Robinson, chief executive of TRG Arts.

“Their approach aligns naturally with our own, combining evidence, analytical rigour and intelligent automation to help organisations optimise their pricing ethically and effectively. Having already delivered results across sports, live events and attractions, the platform gives arts and culture organisations the benefit of demand intelligence shaped by multiple industries, while ensuring they remain firmly in control of every pricing decision.”

Bence Marosi, CEO and co-founder of DynamO Pricing, added: “Arts and culture organisations should not have to choose between their audiences and their financial health. TRG Arts brings three decades of trusted counsel to this sector, and our platform brings the technology to act on that counsel in real time. With the Tessitura integration now live, thousands more organisations can put demand-based pricing to work without changing anything about their ticketing.”

Additionally, the DynamO Pricing platform is now integrated with Tessitura, with the first client having already signed on. With this integration, Tessitura users can adopt intelligent pricing without changing their existing ticketing infrastructure.

Attila Gazdag, chief revenue officer of DynamO Pricing, said: “We structured our commercial model so that our success is aligned with our clients’.

“Organisations can choose from multiple business models, led by our commission-only structure, with no upfront fees and no fixed costs. The platform is intuitive by design, and onboarding is typically completed in a matter of days.”
