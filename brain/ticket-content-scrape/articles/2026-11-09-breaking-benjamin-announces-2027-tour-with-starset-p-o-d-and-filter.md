# Breaking Benjamin Announces 2027 Tour With Starset, P.O.D. and Filter

## Summary

Breaking Benjamin announced a 16-date U.S. winter tour for early 2027 with Starset, P.O.D. and Filter as support, following up a 30-date 2026 run that wrapped in October. Tickets went on general sale Nov. 6 after presales for Citi cardmembers and Spotify Premium subscribers.

### Key Points
- Runs Feb. 16 (Cedar Rapids, IA) through March 12 (Atlantic City, NJ).
- Filter plays all but the final Atlantic City date.
- Follows a 30-date 2026 tour with Chevelle, Starset and Kami Kehoe that ended Oct. 24.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Breaking-Benjamin.webp)

Breaking Benjamin has announced a 16-date U.S. tour for early 2027, with Starset joining as special guest and P.O.D. and Filter providing additional support.

The tour is set to begin February 16 at Alliant Energy PowerHouse in Cedar Rapids, Iowa, and conclude March 12 at Ovation Hall at Ocean Casino Resort in Atlantic City, New Jersey. Filter will appear on the first 15 dates but will not perform at the Atlantic City show.

The winter run includes stops in Kalamazoo, Fort Wayne, Evansville, Peoria, Louisville, Columbus and State College throughout February. Following a February 28 performance at Mohegan Sun Arena in Connecticut, the band will head to Syracuse, Albany, Hershey, Charleston, Greenville and Savannah before wrapping up in Atlantic City.

The announcement follows Breaking Benjamin’s extensive 2026 touring schedule, which included a [30-date North American run](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/breaking-benjamin-plot-30-date-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) with Chevelle, Starset and Kami Kehoe. That tour concluded October 24 at Jiffy Lube Live in Bristow, Virginia.

Starset will return to the road with Breaking Benjamin for the second consecutive tour, while P.O.D. and Filter join the lineup for the newly announced winter shows.

The upcoming tour also follows the success of Breaking Benjamin’s latest single, “Something Wicked,” which reached No. 1 on Billboard’s Mainstream Rock Airplay chart, marking the band’s ninth career chart-topper on that ranking.

Beyond the winter tour, Breaking Benjamin is scheduled to appear at the Summer of ’99 & Beyond Cruise in April and at [Boardwalk Rock 2027](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/iron-maiden-korn-and-bad-omens-to-headline-boardwalk-rock-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in Ocean City, Maryland, on May 14. The festival’s three-day lineup also features Iron Maiden, Korn, Bad Omens, Alice in Chains and Judas Priest.

Tickets for Breaking Benjamin’s 2027 winter tour went on sale to the general public Friday, November 6, at 10 a.m. local time, following presales for Citi cardmembers, eligible Spotify Premium subscribers and other fan groups.
