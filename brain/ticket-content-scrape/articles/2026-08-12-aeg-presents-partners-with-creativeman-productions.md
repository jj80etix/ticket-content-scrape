# AEG Presents partners with Creativeman Productions

## Summary

AEG Presents has entered a strategic partnership with Creativeman Productions, Japan's largest independent music promoter, aiming to expand AEG's footprint in the Japanese live-music market. The tie-up connects Creativeman's Summer Sonic festival to AEG's global festival portfolio (Coachella, BST Hyde Park) and is framed by both sides as a growth play for artist access across Japan and the wider Asia region. No ticketing-specific terms are disclosed, but the deal signals a major international promoter deepening its presence in a market it already has infrastructure in via its planned Osaka Arena development.

### Key Points

- AEG Presents partners with Creativeman Productions (founded 1990, Japan's largest independent promoter per its own CEO) to expand joint access to Japanese and Asian audiences.
- The partnership links Creativeman's Summer Sonic festival into a network alongside AEG's Coachella (US) and BST Hyde Park (UK).
- Creativeman CEO Naoki Shimizu frames the deal as fuel for expanding festivals and artists "from Japan to Asia and the rest of the world."
- AEG Presents EVP/COO Shawn Trell cites strategic alignment and highlights Summer Sonic's global brand recognition as a draw.
- The deal follows AEG's own greenlit Osaka Arena project (with Mitsubishi Corporation Urban Development and Kanden Realty), reinforcing AEG's broader Japan market build-out.

### Highlights

- AEG Presents (international promoter, parent of ticketing arm AXS) formally allies with Creativeman, the dominant independent Japanese promoter, rather than acquiring it — a partnership rather than a buyout.
- The tie-up is explicitly framed around cross-market festival expansion (Summer Sonic + Coachella + BST Hyde Park) rather than a single-market play.
- Comes alongside AEG's separate, previously announced Osaka Arena development — two concurrent Japan market-entry moves by the same group.

### Industry Problem

International promoters and their affiliated ticketing arms are racing to secure footholds in fast-growing, high-demand markets like Japan before local incumbents or rival groups (e.g., AXS/Ticketek network expansions) lock in exclusive venue and promoter relationships.

### Proposed Solution

The item itself describes a partnership/access strategy rather than a product; the analogous move for Etix would be building or deepening reseller/localization relationships in growth touring markets ahead of competitors, and monitoring whether AEG's Japan expansion extends to ticketing-system requirements for Creativeman's events that an independent primary platform could bid for.
