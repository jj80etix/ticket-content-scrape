# Scorpions Postpone Remaining 2026 Tour Dates Until 2027

## Summary

Scorpions postponed their remaining 2026 dates — a three-show Mexico run and an eight-show Las Vegas residency — to August-September 2027, citing "unforeseen circumstances." Previously purchased Mexico tickets roll over automatically to the new dates, with refunds available via Ticketmaster Mexico or the original point of purchase.

### Key Points
- Mexico shows (Guadalajara, Mexico City, Monterrey) move to Aug. 27-Sept. 2, 2027; the "Coming Home to Las Vegas" residency (8 shows at PH Live) moves to Sept. 2027.
- Tickets remain valid for the rescheduled dates without exchange; refunds available via Ticketmaster Mexico (from Sept. 7) or the original venue box office.
- Follows an earlier April cancellation of a four-show India tour for medical reasons.
- Band plans additional 2027 European dates as part of its 60th-anniversary "Coming Home" tour.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Scorpions.jpg)

Scorpions have postponed their remaining 2026 tour dates, moving concerts in Mexico and an eight-show Las Vegas residency to August and September 2027.

The German hard rock band cited “unforeseen circumstances” for the postponements but did not provide additional information about the decision. The affected schedule includes three concerts in Mexico and eight performances at PH Live at Planet Hollywood Resort & Casino in Las Vegas.

The band shared on social media, “We are extremely disappointed but look forward to returning in 2027 along with a number of European shows with details to be announced soon. “

Scorpions were scheduled to begin the Mexico run on September 7, 2026, at Auditorio Telmex in Guadalajara. That concert will now take place on August 27, 2027. The band’s September 10 show at Palacio de los Deportes in Mexico City has been rescheduled for August 30, 2027, while the September 13 date at Auditorio Banamex in Monterrey has moved to September 2, 2027.

Tickets previously purchased for the Mexico concerts will remain valid for the corresponding rescheduled dates without requiring an exchange. Fans who do not plan to attend may request refunds through Ticketmaster Mexico. Customers who purchased tickets at a venue box office can seek refunds beginning September 7 at the original point of purchase.

The band’s “Coming Home to Las Vegas” residency was originally set to run from September 17 through October 3, 2026. The eight rescheduled performances will now be held September 9, 11, 14, 16, 18, 21, 23 and 25, 2027, at PH Live.

Buckcherry had been announced as the special guest for the original residency dates. The residency followed three previous Scorpions engagements at the venue: “Sin City Nights” in 2022, “Love at First Sting Las Vegas” in 2024 and the band’s 60th-anniversary Las Vegas residency in 2025.

The postponements affect all remaining dates on Scorpions’ 2026 schedule. The band previously canceled a four-show India tour in April because of what organizers described as “unforeseen medical circumstances affecting the band members.” Scorpions later returned to the road and completed a six-week European tour on July 31.

The group said it also plans to announce additional European concerts for 2027 as it continues its “Coming Home — 60 Years of Scorpions” tour. The rescheduled Mexico and Las Vegas dates are listed on the band’s [official tour calendar](https://www.the-scorpions.com/tour/).
