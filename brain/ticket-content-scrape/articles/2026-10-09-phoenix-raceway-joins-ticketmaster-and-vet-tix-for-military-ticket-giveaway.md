# Phoenix Raceway Joins Ticketmaster and Vet Tix for Military Ticket Giveaway

## Summary

Phoenix Raceway is partnering with Ticketmaster and the Veteran Tickets Foundation (Vet Tix) to give active-duty military and veterans tickets to the Oct. 18 NASCAR Cup Series Chase race, extending Ticketmaster's 8-year-old donated-ticket partnership with Vet Tix.

### Key Points
- Covers the Oct. 16–18 NASCAR Chase weekend at Phoenix Raceway, culminating in Sunday's Freeway Insurance 500.
- Ticketmaster/Vet Tix partnership dates to 2018 and has distributed tickets via hundreds of venues, teams and artists.
- Vet Tix has distributed 41M+ tickets since its 2008 founding to 2.8M verified veterans, service members and first responders.
- Number of tickets donated for this specific event wasn't disclosed; limited public tickets remain available.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Phoenix-Raceway-.jpg)

Phoenix Raceway is partnering with Ticketmaster and the Veteran Tickets Foundation (Vet Tix) to provide tickets to active-duty military members and veterans for the NASCAR Chase race weekend, scheduled for Oct. 16-18 in Avondale, Arizona.

The initiative will give eligible service members and veterans an opportunity to attend the Freeway Insurance 500 NASCAR Cup Series Chase race on Sunday, Oct. 18, through Vet Tix, a nonprofit organization that distributes donated tickets to military members, veterans and first responders.

The partnership extends an existing relationship between Ticketmaster and Vet Tix, which have worked together since 2018 to expand access to concerts, sporting events and other live entertainment.

“Our service members and veterans have given so much to our country, and we are proud to partner with Ticketmaster and Vet Tix to welcome them and their families to Phoenix Raceway,” said Latasha Causey, president of Phoenix Raceway. “We hope the NASCAR Chase weekend provides an opportunity to enjoy memorable moments together while knowing how deeply their service and sacrifice are appreciated.”

## Ticketmaster and Vet Tix Expand Military Ticket Access

Ticketmaster and Vet Tix have maintained a national partnership for more than eight years, helping venues, sports teams and artists distribute donated event tickets to military families.

As [TicketNews reported when the partnership launched in 2018](https://www.ticketnews.com/2018/06/ticketmaster-partners-with-vet-tix-for-free-discounted-veteran-tickets/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Ticketmaster established a real-time event ticket feed for Vet Tix and introduced a system designed to simplify ticket donations by participating venues, artists and event organizers.

That relationship has continued to expand, with Ticketmaster and Vet Tix now working with hundreds of venues, teams and artists to distribute thousands of tickets annually.

“Ticketmaster has been a tremendous partner of Vet Tix for many years, and we’re grateful to work with Phoenix Raceway and NASCAR to support Vet Tix for October’s race weekend,” said Michael A. Focareto III, founder and CEO of Vet Tix and a U.S. Navy veteran.

“This will give more veterans, service members, first responders, and their families the opportunity to experience the excitement and suspense of NASCAR at Phoenix Raceway, and to create lifelong memories they’ll carry with them long after the checkered flag.”

Since its founding in 2008, Vet Tix has distributed more than 41 million tickets to its community of 2.8 million verified veterans, active military members and first responders, according to the organization.

TicketNews has also covered other major ticket-donation initiatives involving Vet Tix, including a [partnership with FIFA and Bank of America to distribute tickets to the 2026 FIFA World Cup](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/fifa-bank-of-america-partner-with-vet-tix-to-offer-free-world-cup-tickets-to-veterans-and-first-responders/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The Phoenix Raceway announcement did not specify how many tickets would be provided through the latest initiative or whether donated inventory would extend beyond Sunday’s Cup Series race.

“Live events have a way of bringing people together, and there’s no group more deserving of that experience than those who have served our country,” said Marla Ostroff, managing director of Ticketmaster. “We’re proud to work alongside Vet Tix and Phoenix Raceway to get service members, veterans and their families into the stands for one of the most exciting weekends on the NASCAR calendar.”

## NASCAR Chase Weekend Schedule

The three-day NASCAR Chase weekend at Phoenix Raceway begins Friday, Oct. 16, with a NASCAR Craftsman Truck Series Chase race.

Saturday’s schedule includes the penultimate race of the ARCA Menards Series West season and the Timberland PRO 200 NASCAR O’Reilly Auto Parts Series Chase race.

The weekend concludes Sunday, Oct. 18, with the Freeway Insurance 500 NASCAR Cup Series Chase race.

Active military members and veterans interested in accessing donated tickets can find additional information through [Vet Tix](https://www.vettix.org/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

For other fans, Phoenix Raceway said a limited number of tickets remain available for the weekend’s events. Tickets and additional race information are available through the [official Phoenix Raceway website](https://www.phoenixraceway.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
