# Pollstar Live! 2027 Opens Registration

## Summary

Registration opened for Pollstar Live! 2027, which debuts in the greater Palm Springs area (Indian Wells, CA), running April 13-15 and presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster. The conference includes Production Live!, the Pollstar Awards, and early-bird registration pricing through Nov. 30.

### Key Points
- Dates: April 13-15, 2027, in Indian Wells, CA — Pollstar Live!'s debut in the Palm Springs area.
- Early-bird pricing: $1,199/person for Pollstar Live! (includes Production Live!), $499/person for Production Live! alone, through Nov. 30.
- Presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster; includes the annual Pollstar Awards.
- Host hotel, keynote speakers and panelists to be announced in coming months.

We’re excited to bring Pollstar Live! to the Valley, and with three full days of programming and networking, we will be able to drill down into the many facets of this complex, ever-changing business in new and exciting ways.”
