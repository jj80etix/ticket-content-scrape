# Bon Jovi Expands Forever Tour With New 2027 Stadium Shows Worldwide

## Summary

Bon Jovi added seven new 2027 Forever Tour dates, including stadium shows in Australia, the U.S. and Canada plus a BST Hyde Park festival headline slot in London. The expansion follows the band's 2026 comeback run after Jon Bon Jovi's vocal-cord surgery and rehab, including sold-out Madison Square Garden and Wembley Stadium shows. Presales begin Oct. 28 with general onsale Oct. 30 for the North American dates.

### Key Points
- New dates: Melbourne (Mar 4), Sydney (Mar 11), BST Hyde Park London (Jun 24), MetLife Stadium NJ (Jul 15), Soldier Field Chicago (Jul 21), Gillette Stadium MA (Jul 28, first since 2013), Rogers Stadium Toronto (Aug 4).
- Follows 2026 comeback: 9 sold-out MSG shows, Wembley x3, Dublin, Edinburgh.
- A July 23, 2026 MSG show ended early due to Jon Bon Jovi's sinus infection; ticket buyers were refunded.
- North American artist presale Oct. 28, general onsale Oct. 30, both 10 a.m. local.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/bon-jovi-forever-tour.webp)

Bon Jovi has announced seven new dates for its Forever Tour in 2027, taking the band’s comeback tour to stadiums in Australia, the United States and Canada, along with a headline appearance at London’s BST Hyde Park festival.

The new itinerary, announced Oct. 19, starts March 4 at Marvel Stadium in Melbourne and March 11 at Accor Stadium in Sydney Olympic Park. Following the June 24 BST Hyde Park show, the group will return to North America for concerts at MetLife Stadium in New Jersey, Soldier Field in Chicago, Gillette Stadium outside Boston and Rogers Stadium in Toronto.

The July 28 stop in Foxborough, Massachusetts, will be Bon Jovi’s first Gillette Stadium performance since 2013, according to the venue. It is one of three U.S. stadium dates on the new schedule, with the Toronto appearance scheduled Aug. 4.

“The Forever Tour has been an incredible celebration of the music and the fans who have been with us all these years,” Jon Bon Jovi said in a statement accompanying the announcement. “From Madison Square Garden to Scotland, Ireland, and England, the energy and joy we shared with our fans this summer was something truly special.”

The new dates follow Bon Jovi’s return to large-scale touring in 2026 after Jon Bon Jovi underwent vocal cord surgery in 2022 and a lengthy rehabilitation. The comeback began with nine concerts at Madison Square Garden in July before stadium appearances in Edinburgh, Dublin and three nights at Wembley Stadium in September. [TicketNews previously detailed the added New York and London shows](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/bon-jovi-adds-new-york-london-shows-to-forever-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) as the original run expanded.

## Bon Jovi 2027 Presale and Ticket Onsale Details

For the four North American dates, fans can register for the artist presale through [BonJovi.com](https://www.bonjovi.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The presale begins Wednesday, Oct. 28, at 10 a.m. local time. Other presales are scheduled throughout the week, ahead of the general onsale Friday, Oct. 30, at 10 a.m. local time.

BST Hyde Park tickets are scheduled for general sale Friday, Oct. 23, at 10 a.m. British Summer Time, with earlier presales available during the week, according to the festival announcement. Ticketing schedules for Australia vary by market, the band said, so fans should check the [official tour website](https://www.bonjovi.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for local details.
