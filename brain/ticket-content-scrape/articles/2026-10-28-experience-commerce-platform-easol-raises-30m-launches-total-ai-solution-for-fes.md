# Experience Commerce Platform Easol Raises $30M, Launches ‘Total AI Solution’ For Festival Promoters

## Summary

Experience-commerce platform Easol closed a $30M Series A extension (bringing total raised to $60M) from investors including Y Combinator, Tiger Global and Foundation Capital, and used it to launch "Teammates," an agentic AI tool letting festival/experience businesses create AI agents for support, sales and operations inside Easol's platform. Early customer examples include a ski festival identifying hundreds of guests without equipment-rental bookings and another recovering $28,000 in payments in seconds versus two days manually.

### Highlights
- $30M Series A extension (equity from Y Combinator, Tiger Global, Foundation Capital, FMZ Ventures, Notion Capital, Eight Roads; $10M debt from Viola Credit); $60M raised to date.
- Teammates agents run inside Easol's platform, drawing on live operational data to automate support, sales and revenue-recovery tasks.
- Concrete results cited: one festival found hundreds of guests with unbooked equipment rentals; another recovered $28,000 in payments in seconds (vs. two days manually).
- Easol says it has powered bookings for 2.8M guests across 135+ countries so far this year.

### Industry Problem
Festival and experience-business operators lack built-in AI tooling to catch revenue leakage (unbooked add-ons, recoverable payments) and handle routine support/ops work, forcing manual, labor-intensive processes that an AI-native platform layer can automate.

### Proposed Solution
Easol's answer is to embed agentic AI directly into its own operator platform rather than bolting on a third-party chatbot. For Etix, this is a concrete competitive/benchmark data point for how a primary platform could deploy agentic AI for client-side operations (support, revenue recovery, guest-data insights) — worth evaluating as a build/improve candidate and as grounding for Etix Assistant's own scope.

Experience commerce platform Easol completed a $30 million Series A extension funding round with $20 million equity from Y Combinator, Tiger Global, Foundation Capital, FMZ Ventures, Notion Capital, Eight Roads and a $10 million debt facility with Viola Credit. Easol has raised $60 million to date.

Launched in 2021 by festival co-founders Ben and Lisa Simpson, Easol has become the leading experience commerce platform for music festivals and experience businesses worldwide, powering experiences booked by 2.8 million guests in over 135 countries so far this year.

The funding allowed Easol to develop and launch agentic tool Teammates for festival and experience businesses.

Easol enables promoters to bring their technology in-house with full ownership of their business to manage bookings, payments, marketing and support and guest data through one connected system. That one-stop solution now enables Easol customers to create AI agents — Teammates — that work directly inside the Easol platform. Teammates can provide tailored support, sales and operations expertise to increase client efficiency.

One major ski festival’s Teammates Agent found hundreds of guests holding a pass with no equipment rental booked, and produced the guest list and the outreach to go after it. Another festival using Teammates identified $28,000 of recoverable payments in seconds, a job that had previously taken two days.

“We built Easol to give promoters control over their entire business and bring their technology in-house. AI is now allowing us to do so much more, with rising costs and tighter margins we can now support promoters in scaling their business growing revenue in ways we couldn’t have imagined when we first launched the platform in 2021,” said Ben Simpson, Co-Founder and CEO of Easol.  
“We believe the experience businesses that adopt AI will have a competitive advantage that will be transformative. They’ll be able to look after every guest like never before, grow bookings with specialist agents working as an extension of their team using their live data to operate, analyse, provide insights and take action across and keep watch over their whole business, while their teams focus on their customer relationships and delivering world class experiences.”
