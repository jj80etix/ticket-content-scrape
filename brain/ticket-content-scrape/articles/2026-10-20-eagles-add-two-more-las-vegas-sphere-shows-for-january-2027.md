# Eagles Add Two More Las Vegas Sphere Shows for January 2027

## Summary

The Eagles added two more Las Vegas Sphere residency shows (Jan. 8-9, 2027), bringing their total engagement to 74 scheduled performances — extending their record as Sphere's longest-running residency — with six total January 2027 shows now on the books.

### Key Points
- Eagles add Jan. 8 & 9, 2027 Sphere shows, bringing their total residency to 74 scheduled performances (longest-running at the venue).
- January 2027 now has 6 scheduled Eagles shows (8, 9, 15, 16, 22, 23).
- Follows a prior expansion in July adding four December 2026 dates.

## Transcript

![The Eagles residency at The Sphere in Las Vegas](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Eagles-Sphere.webp)

The Eagles residency at The Sphere in Las Vegas

The Eagles have added two more performances to their record-setting residency at Sphere in Las Vegas, announcing concerts for January 8 and 9, 2027. The additions bring the band’s engagement at the venue to 74 scheduled shows, extending its record as Sphere’s longest-running concert residency.

The newly announced performances fill an additional January weekend ahead of four previously scheduled concerts on January 15, 16, 22, and 23. The latest expansion brings the band’s January 2027 schedule to six performances, with the residency still scheduled to run through January 23.

The Eagles have repeatedly expanded their Las Vegas engagement since its launch. In July, the band [announced four additional December 2026 performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/the-eagles-extend-las-vegas-sphere-residency-through-december-2026/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), bringing the scheduled total to 68 shows. Another [four January 2027 concerts were announced in August](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/eagles-extend-sphere-residency-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), increasing the total to 72 and carrying the residency into a third calendar year.

Tickets for the newly added January 8 and 9 performances will go on sale to the general public on Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. PT, through Ticketmaster. The artist presale begins Wednesday, October 28, at 10 a.m. PT. Fans can register now through the [Eagles’ official website](https://eagles.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), with registration powered by Seated. Additional presale opportunities begin Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. PT.
