# Fcukers Plot Fall North American Headlining Shows

## Summary

New York duo Fcukers announced a fall 2026 North American headline tour in support of their debut album Ö and new EP nüyork, following summer festival dates in the Netherlands and France. The run kicks off September 1 in Grand Rapids and runs through mid-October, including stops in Toronto, Atlanta (plus a Shaky Knees festival slot), Dallas, and a two-night Austin City Limits appearance.

### Key Points
- Tour opens September 1 at Acrisure Amphitheater, Grand Rapids, MI, and runs through October 11.
- Stops include Rogers Centre (Toronto), Terminal West (Atlanta), The Echo Lounge & Music Hall (Dallas), and two Austin City Limits dates.
- Festival appearances include Shaky Knees (Atlanta) and Portola (San Francisco).
- Support acts Baby Wave, SEESOOOOO, and Total Wife join varying dates on the run.
- Tickets are on sale via the duo's official website.
- Tour supports debut record Ö (released March 27) and new EP nüyork.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/fcukers.jpg)

The New York-based duo Fcukers have announced a round of tour dates across North America this fall.

Following a pair or festival appearances in the Netherlands and France, Fcukers will head to the states, kicking-off their fall tour at Grand Rapids’ Acrisure Amphihteater on September 1. From there, they’ll appear at Toronto’s Rogers Centre, Terminal West in Atlanta, The Echo Lounge & Music Hall in Dallas, and Ventura Music Hall. They’re also slated to perform at Atlanta’s Shaky Knees festival, as well as a two-night set at Austin City Limits.

Throughout the run, Baby Wave, SEESOOOOO, and Total Wife will provide support, varying per date.

Tickets for the newly-announced dates are now available via the duo’s official [website](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/Fcukers/fall2026).
