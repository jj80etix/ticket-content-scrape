# TAIT Names Rebecca Barkin Chief Executive, Product & Platform Technology

## Summary

Concert staging and technology firm TAIT named Rebecca Barkin — most recently co-founder/CEO of blockchain-and-AI creator platform LAMINA1, with prior leadership roles at Sphere and Magic Leap — as Chief Executive, Product and Platform Technology. Barkin will set strategy and investment priorities for TAIT's technology products, focused on connecting hardware and software into scalable platforms across markets.

### Key Points
- Rebecca Barkin joins TAIT as Chief Executive, Product & Platform Technology.
- She previously co-founded LAMINA1 (an AI/blockchain creator platform with Neal Stephenson), and held senior roles at Sphere and Magic Leap.
- Her mandate is connecting TAIT's hardware/software capabilities into scalable, cross-market technology platforms.
- TAIT CEO Adam Davis framed the hire as building on the company's staging and technical-solutions reputation for live-entertainment clients.

## Transcript

![Profile Pic Crop Mid](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Profile-Pic-Crop-Mid.jpg)

Rebecca Barkin

Concert staging and live experience pioneer TAIT today announced the appointment of Rebecca Barkin as Chief Executive, Product and Platform Technology. Barkin most recently served as co-founder and CEO of LAMINA1, helping to develop an AI & blockchain-based platform supporting creators. Barkin brings more than 20 years of experience developing emerging technologies, immersive experience, having spent time in leadership roles at LAMINA1, Sphere and Magic Leap.

At TAIT, Barkin will lead the strategy, portfolio direction, and investment priorities for the company’s technology products and platforms, according to the announcement. She will focus on connecting hardware and software capabilities, developing technologies that can scale across projects and markets.

“TAIT has built its reputation on solving complex challenges and delivering experiences that few others can,” said Adam Davis, Chief Executive Officer of TAIT. “Our teams have developed extraordinary technical capabilities through years of innovation and problem-solving for our clients. Rebecca brings the product leadership, technology expertise, and commercial perspective to help us build on that foundation, develop more connected and scalable platforms, and bring the value of TAIT technology to more clients and markets.”

Most recently, Barkin served as Co-Founder and CEO of LAMINA1, where she worked alongside New York Times best-selling author and futurist Neal Stephenson to develop an AI & blockchain-based platform supporting creators in developing, managing, and commercializing intellectual property.

At Sphere, she led creative strategy and experience design, working with top talent to leverage 4D technology in delivering immersive entertainment. At Magic Leap, she led interdisciplinary teams of developers, designers, producers and strategists in developing immersive applications and AR experiences, including the award-winning interactive music experience Tónandi and collaborations with Warner Bros. and HBO.

Barkin adds, “If you work in immersive or live entertainment, you know TAIT as the company you call to achieve the impossible; from design to engineering and production, the combination of their deep experience, credibility, and broad capabilities is unmatched. In the past, I’ve had the pleasure of partnering with TAIT, and now I have the privilege of steering the vision for our suite of technology products as they support the greatest storytellers of our time.”
