# Leon Bridges Sets 2027 ‘Happiness Anytime’ North American Tour

## Summary

Leon Bridges announced the North American leg of his "Happiness Anytime Tour," opening April 7, 2027 at The Chelsea at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas and including a Fort Worth hometown show and his first headlining Madison Square Garden date. A second summer leg runs July–August 2026 across amphitheaters and arenas in the U.S. and Canada.

### Key Points
- Spring leg: Las Vegas (Apr. 7), Fort Worth's Dickies Arena (Apr. 23), Toronto's Scotiabank Arena (Apr. 27), and Madison Square Garden (May 1).
- Summer leg opens July 16 at Skyla Credit Union Amphitheatre (Charlotte) and includes Atlanta, St. Augustine, Boston, and Philadelphia.
- August dates include Red Rocks Amphitheatre, Bend, Seattle, Vancouver, Edmonton, and back-to-back Calgary shows (Aug. 18–19).
- Tour supports Bridges' newly released album *Happiness Anytime*.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Leon-Bridges.webp)

Leon Bridges will bring his “Happiness Anytime Tour” to North America in 2027, with a schedule that includes a hometown concert in Fort Worth, Texas, and his first headlining performance at New York’s Madison Square Garden.

The tour opens April 7 at The Chelsea at The Cosmopolitan of Las Vegas. Bridges will then play Dickies Arena in Fort Worth on April 23 and Toronto’s Scotiabank Arena on April 27 before reaching Madison Square Garden on May 1. The run supports his recently released album, *Happiness Anytime*.

Following the spring performances, Bridges resumes the North American itinerary July 16 at Skyla Credit Union Amphitheatre in Charlotte, North Carolina. July stops also include the Atlanta area, St. Augustine, Florida, Wilmington, North Carolina, Columbia, Maryland, Boston and Philadelphia.

The August schedule includes Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, followed by appearances in Bend, Oregon, Seattle and Vancouver. Bridges will also visit Edmonton and play consecutive nights in Calgary on August 18 and 19 before returning to the United States for shows in Montana, Iowa and Indiana.

September dates bring the tour to Columbus, Ohio, Milwaukee, Minneapolis, Nashville and Rogers, Arkansas. Bridges returns to Texas for performances at Austin’s Moody Center on September 24 and Houston’s 713 Music Hall on September 25. The announced itinerary concludes September 29 at Arizona Financial Theatre in Phoenix.

Support will vary throughout the tour. Mari Froes will open the Fort Worth, Toronto and New York concerts, with The Womack Sisters joining the Fort Worth bill, Rachel Chinouriri appearing in Toronto and El Michels Affair performing in New York. Kokoroko will support the July and August dates, while Peter Cat Recording Co. joins the September run.

The North American dates follow Bridges’ previously announced Australian performances in February and March, along with March appearances at Lollapalooza in Argentina, Chile and Brazil.

Artist presales begin October 13 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by venue and local presales beginning October 14 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets go on sale to the general public October 16 at 10 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [leonbridges.com](http://leonbridges.com).
