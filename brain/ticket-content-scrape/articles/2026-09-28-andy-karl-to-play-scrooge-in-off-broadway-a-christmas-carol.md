# Andy Karl to Play Scrooge in Off-Broadway ‘A Christmas Carol’

## Summary

Tony Award nominee Andy Karl will play Ebenezer Scrooge in the Perelman Performing Arts Center's second annual off-Broadway run of "A Christmas Carol," staged Nov. 20, 2026 - Jan. 3, 2027 in Lower Manhattan.

### Key Points
- Run dates: Nov. 20, 2026 - Jan. 3, 2027, at PAC NYC.
- Adaptation by Jack Thorne, directed/conceived by Matthew Warchus (the Old Vic staging).
- Second annual holiday run at PAC NYC; Michael Cerveris played Scrooge in the 2025 run.
- Additional casting not yet announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Off-Broadway-A-Christmas-Carol.webp)

Andy Karl will play Ebenezer Scrooge in the Perelman Performing Arts Center’s holiday production of “A Christmas Carol.”

The three-time Tony Award nominee will lead the show’s second annual run at PAC NYC, scheduled from November 20, 2026, through January 3, 2027. Additional casting has not been announced.

The production brings the Old Vic staging of Charles Dickens’ story back to Lower Manhattan. Jack Thorne adapted the work for the stage, and Matthew Warchus conceived and directs it. Michael Cerveris played Scrooge during PAC NYC’s 2025 holiday run.

Thorne and Warchus’ version follows Scrooge through Christmas past, present and future in a staging that surrounds the audience with the action. The production includes familiar carols such as “Joy to the World,” “Silent Night” and “It Came Upon the Midnight Clear.”

The show began at London’s Old Vic in 2017 and played Broadway in 2019. Its Broadway run went on to win five Tony Awards, including best original score and four design awards. The 2026 engagement continues its New York holiday presence following last year’s Off-Broadway staging at PAC NYC.

PAC NYC has also announced that this year’s performances will collect donations for Hunger Free America, a nonprofit focused on domestic hunger. The organization’s involvement adds a charitable component to a production centered on Scrooge’s change of heart and his relationships with the people around him.
