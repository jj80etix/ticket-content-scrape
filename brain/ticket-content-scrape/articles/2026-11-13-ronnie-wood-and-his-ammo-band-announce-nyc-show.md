# Ronnie Wood And His Ammo Band Announce NYC Show

## Summary

Ronnie Wood and his Ammo Band will play New York's Beacon Theatre on March 22, 2027, a rare non-stadium appearance for the Rolling Stones guitarist, joined by singer Chanel Haynes. The show follows a European and Latin American run that included two sold-out nights at Amsterdam's Paradiso.

### Key Points
- Beacon Theatre show set for March 22, 2027, with Chanel Haynes joining.
- Ammo Band includes bassist Willie Weeks and drummer Andy Newmark, both from Wood's 1974 debut solo album.
- Follows sold-out European/Latin American dates, including two nights at Amsterdam's Paradiso.

## Transcript

![Ronnie](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Ronnie-1.jpg)

Ronnie Wood And His Ammo Band are headed to the Beacon Theatre in March, giving Rolling Stones fans a rare opportunity to see the beloved guitarist take the stage outside of stadiums.

The March 22, 2027, show will feature Wood joined by Grammy-nominated singer and Rolling Stones collaborator Chanel Haynes

Wood’s Ammo Band features bassist Willie Weeks and drummer Andy Newmark. Today’s announcement notes that both musicians “played on his 1974 debut solo album, *I’ve Got My Own Album To Do*, alongside keyboardist Andy Wallace and guitarist Johnny Lee Schell.”

The Beacon Theatre show follows performances across Europe and Latin America, with two nights at Amsterdam’s Paradiso selling out within minutes. Wood also has a few 2027 shows booked in Europe including Copenhagen, Denmark, Feb. 27; Oslo, Norway March 1 and Stockholm, Sweden, March 3.

Wood is continuing to celebrate “FEARLESS: THE ANTHOLOGY 1965–2025,” which features highlights from his six-decade career that boasts The Rolling Stones, Faces, the Jeff Beck Group and The Birds, alongside his solo recordings.

“I’ve got a fantastic band around me, and we’re having a great time playing these songs. I’m loving every minute of it. Now we’re bringing the show to New York – see you at the Beacon,” Wood said in a statement.

An artist presale for the Beacon Theatre show starts Nov. 13 at 10 a.m. EST with password RWAB27. Presales from AMEX, CNB, MSG Insider and Bowery Presales start Tuesday, Nov. 17 at 10 a.m. EST and run through 11:59 EST. The general onsale begins Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. EST.
