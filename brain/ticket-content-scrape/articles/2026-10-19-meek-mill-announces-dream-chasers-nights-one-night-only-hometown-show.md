# Meek Mill Announces ‘Dream Chasers Nights,’ One-Night-Only Hometown Show

## Summary

Meek Mill will headline a one-night "Dream Chasers Nights" hometown show at Philadelphia's Xfinity Mobile Arena on Nov. 24, presented with Live Nation Urban, following a sold-out April 2026 Bridgeport, CT show that grossed $520,526. Artist presale begins Oct. 20 with general onsale Oct. 23.

### Key Points
- One-night show, Nov. 24, Xfinity Mobile Arena, Philadelphia.
- Presented in partnership with Live Nation Urban.
- Prior similar show (Bridgeport, CT, Apr. 11) sold out, grossing $520,526 per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Artist presale Oct. 20 (password: DREAMCHASERS); general onsale Oct. 23.

## Transcript

![Meek Mill Dream Chasers Nights Philly](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Meek-Mill-Dream-Chasers-Nights-Philly.png)

Meek Mill Dream Chasers Nights Philly

Meek Mill announces “Dream Chasers Nights: Meek Mill & Friends,” a one-night-only hometown show celebrating Philadelphia and “the music that shaped his journey,” going down at the Xfinity Mobile Arena on Nov. 24 in partnership with Live Nation Urban.

The Philadelphia performance carries a special significance for Meek, continuing a tradition of bringing unforgettable live experiences to his hometown. Having headlined similar events in the city multiple times, his return underscores his enduring connection to Philadelphia and the community that made him who he is today. The special one-night-only show follows Meek Mill’s April 11 show in Bridgeport, Connecticut, alongside special guests Fabolous and Young M.A., which sold out the Total Mortgage Arena and grossed $520,526, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/159424).

Tickets will go on sale starting with artist presale on Oct. 20 at 12 p.m. local time. The artist presale password is DREAMCHASERS. The general on sale begins Oct. 23 at 10 a.m. local time. For complete tour and ticket information, visit [meekmill.com](https://meekmill.com/).
