# Why constructing an ‘emotional universe’ is key to artists’ longevity

## Summary

In a (paywalled) interview, Fred Casimir and Michael Kümmerle of UK entertainment group whynow argue that artists need to build a coherent "emotional universe" — a storytelling framework — before commercial brand-building can sustain a long career. The piece is part of IQ's ongoing coverage of artist development and longevity strategy.

### Key Points
- whynow principals Fred Casimir and Michael Kümmerle make the case that storytelling must precede commercial brand strategy for artist longevity.
- Framed as part of a broader IQ editorial thread on what sustains artists' careers (alongside pieces on country music's rise and family entertainment trends).
- Full interview is behind IQ's subscriber paywall; only the framing paragraph was accessible to this harvest.
