# Stella Lefty Announces 2027 North American Headlining Tour

## Summary

Country artist Stella Lefty announced her 2027 North American headlining "Long Way Home Tour," promoted by Live Nation, running Jan. 9 (Detroit) through Feb. 20 (Chicago) in support of her debut album Long Way Home.

### Key Points
- Artist presale registration closes Aug. 25 at 10 p.m. PT ahead of an Aug. 27 presale; general on-sale is Aug. 28.
- A VIP package includes early entry, a Soundcheck Party and an autographed poster via VIP Nation.
- Follows her Aug. 21 album release, anchored by breakout single "Thinking 'bou You."

Stella Lefty is gearing up to hit the road, scheduling her 2027 North American tour in support of her debut album, *Long Way Home*. The “Long Way Home Tour,” promoted by Live Nation, will see the rising singer kicking off the tour on Jan. 9 at the Fillmore Detroit in Michigan. The tour will make stops in Brooklyn, Columbus, Toronto, Philadelphia, Houston and more before concluding in her hometown of Chicago at the Aragon Ballroom on Feb. 20.

To participate in the Stella Lefty Artist Presale on Aug. 27 at 10 a.m., you must sign up [HERE](https://signup.livenation.com/stellalefty) by Aug. 25 at 10 p.m. PT.

Remaining tickets will be available via general on-sale starting on Aug. 28 at 10 a.m. at [stella-lefty.com](https://www.stella-lefty.com/).

The tour will also offer a VIP package and experience to take fans’ concert experience to the next level. The package includes a general admission ticket, Stella Lefty’s pre-show Soundcheck Party, an autographed VIP-exclusive poster, early entry into the venue and more. For more information, visit [vipnation.com](https://www.vipnation.com/).

Lefty’s announcement arrives on the heels of the country artist’s release of her debut album, *Long Way Home*, making a splash on Aug. 21. The album is anchored by her breakout song, “Thinking ‘bou You,” and includes seven new songs alongside seven previously released singles.
