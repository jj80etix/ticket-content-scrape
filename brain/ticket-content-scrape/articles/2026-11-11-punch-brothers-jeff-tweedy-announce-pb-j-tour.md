# Punch Brothers & Jeff Tweedy Announce ‘PB&J Tour’

## Summary

Punch Brothers and Wilco frontman Jeff Tweedy announced a joint run of dates billed as the "PB&J Tour," opening February 17, 2027 in York, Pennsylvania. An artist presale is already underway, with general onsale beginning November 13.

### Key Points
- Tour stops include Charlottesville, Asheville, Ft. Lauderdale and Orlando among others
- Punch Brothers' seventh album, The Unsung Adventures of Punch Brothers, released in July
- Both acts also have independent headline dates: Punch Brothers' current North American/European run, and Tweedy's solo Ireland shows (Nov 16–20)

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed.jpg)

Hope you’re hungry for some good music – and feeling punny. Punch Brothers & Jeff Tweedy are joining forces for a run of dates called the “PB&J Tour.”  
  
An announcement about the pairing of the bluegrass band and Wilco frontman declares “Some things are just destined to be together, like PB&J. Punch Brothers and Jeff, that is.”

Punch Brothers released its seventh album, *The Unsung Adventures of Punch Brothers*, in July, marking its first collection featuring all instrumental songs and its first with fiddle player Brittany Haas, who joined the lineup in 2023.

Tweedy’s latest solo release is 2025’s *Twilight Override*, following Wilco’s 2024 LP, *Hot Sun Cool Shroud.*

The “PB&J Tour” starts Feb. 17 at Appell Center for the Performing Arts in York, Pennsylvania. Stops include Charlottesville, Asheville, Ft. Lauderdale, Orlando and more.

An artist pre-sale started today with the password “PBJ.” The general onsale begins Nov. 13 at 10 a.m. local.  
  
Each of the acts also have plenty of their own headline dates lined up, with Punch Brothers playing a run of North American dates through next week (including tonight’s show at the State Theatre in Portland, Maine) and European gigs in January. Wilco has a handful of hometown shows booked in Chicago in December and Tweedy has a few solo shows in Ireland Nov. 16-20, as well as several festival appearances.  
  
Punch Brothers is booked by THE·TEAM. Wilco and Tweedy’s solo dates are booked by High Road Touring.
