# TicketNetwork Expands International Ticket Distribution Through Connect Partnership

## Summary

TicketNetwork is integrating with Connect, a European-focused B2B platform for professional ticket resellers, letting Connect's UK/Europe sellers distribute inventory through TicketNetwork's 1,000+ partner network (consumer marketplaces, travel platforms, affiliates) while keeping pricing and inventory control. It's the latest in a string of TicketNetwork distribution partnerships (vivenu, Spektrix, GoNexus) expanding the supply feeding its B2B exchange.

### Highlights
- Connect sellers (UK/Europe, strong in Germany, Switzerland, UAE inventory) gain access to TicketNetwork's North American consumer/travel/B2B distribution channels; integration has been live since July, publicly announced Oct. 13.
- Sellers manage listings centrally in Connect via API sync to TicketNetwork's Mercury B2B exchange; sales flow back to Connect to prevent overselling.
- Follows TicketNetwork's 2026 partnerships with vivenu (Aug.), Spektrix (Aug.), and GoNexus (June) — a pattern of plugging new primary- and secondary-market supply sources into one distribution network.
- Connect separately launched "Connect B2B" direct seller-to-seller trading, with Trustap holding transaction funds.

### Industry Problem
Professional resellers and primary-ticketing-adjacent platforms want broader, multi-channel distribution without surrendering control of pricing/inventory or integrating separately with every downstream marketplace — a fragmentation problem in secondary-market distribution.

### Proposed Solution
TicketNetwork is solving this with a hub-and-spoke API model (Mercury) that aggregates diverse supply sources into one distribution network; worth monitoring whether this kind of B2B aggregation layer changes competitive dynamics in secondary distribution Etix's clients rely on.

TicketNetwork is expanding its access to international live event inventory through a new integration with Connect, a technology platform serving professional ticket resellers, agencies and hospitality providers. The agreement allows participating Connect sellers to distribute selected inventory across TicketNetwork’s network of more than 1,000 partners, including consumer ticketing websites, travel platforms, affiliate networks and business-to-business channels.

The partnership, announced Tuesday, Oct. 13, connects Connect’s largely European seller base with TicketNetwork’s substantial North American distribution network, creating additional opportunities for international event inventory to reach buyers through established sales channels.

Although the partnership was announced this week, the integration has been available to Connect sellers since July, Connect founder and CEO Josh Bailey confirmed to TicketNews. Participating sellers work through their Connect account managers, who refer them to TicketNetwork to complete the onboarding process.

For Connect, the partnership opens another path to North American buyers without requiring sellers to establish separate connections with individual marketplaces and distribution partners. For TicketNetwork, it expands access to inventory from international professional sellers, particularly for sporting events, concerts and festivals outside North America.

## Part of TicketNetwork’s Broader Distribution Expansion

The Connect agreement is the latest in a series of partnerships through which TicketNetwork has been expanding the sources of ticket inventory available across its distribution network.

In August, TicketNetwork announced a [distribution partnership with vivenu](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/ticketnetwork-vivenu-partner-on-expanded-ticket-distribution/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), allowing event organizers using the primary ticketing platform to offer selected inventory through TicketNetwork’s network while retaining control of their underlying ticketing operations.

The company subsequently announced a [similar integration with Spektrix](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/spektrix-partners-with-ticketnetwork-to-help-cultural-orgs-tap-into-new-audiences/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), extending distribution opportunities to theaters, museums, performing arts organizations and other cultural institutions.

Those agreements primarily involve inventory originating with event organizers using primary ticketing technology. The Connect partnership adds a different source of supply, connecting professional ticket resellers and other international sellers with the same broader distribution ecosystem.

TicketNetwork has also been expanding the channels through which existing inventory can reach consumers. Its [June partnership with GoNexus Group](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/ticketnetwork-gonexus-partnership-brings-ticket-inventory-deeper-into-travel-space/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) brought ticket inventory into an international travel distribution operation, allowing travel businesses to incorporate live event tickets into their offerings.

Taken together, the agreements reflect a strategy of connecting different types of ticket suppliers with a growing range of consumer and business distribution channels, without requiring those suppliers to abandon their existing ticketing systems.

For consumers, the Connect agreement could mean additional international event listings becoming available through familiar North American ticketing and travel platforms. Wider distribution does not necessarily mean every listing represents inventory exclusive to a particular marketplace, however, and final prices may differ between sales channels.

## Connect Expands Its Own B2B Capabilities

The TicketNetwork partnership also comes as Connect expands its offerings for professional sellers beyond inventory management and distribution.

Bailey told TicketNews that Connect launched a separate business-to-business trading product earlier this month, allowing sellers using the platform to purchase inventory directly from other participating sellers.

“Earlier this month we launched Connect B2B, where sellers can buy stock from other named sellers on the platform,” Bailey said. “You know exactly who you’re buying from, you can chat with them directly in Connect, and funds are held by Trustap. It’s the same thinking behind the TicketNetwork integration: helping ticket businesses grow without handing over control.”

The new product allows participating sellers to identify their trading counterparties and communicate directly within Connect, with Trustap handling the holding of transaction funds.

While Connect B2B facilitates transactions between professional sellers within the platform, the TicketNetwork integration gives those sellers another means of reaching external buyers through an established distribution network.

Both developments reflect Connect’s emphasis on maintaining centralized control of inventory, pricing and business operations while expanding the number of channels through which professional sellers can buy and sell tickets.

For Connect sellers interested in accessing TicketNetwork’s distribution network, the integration is already operational, with enrollment handled through their existing Connect account managers.
