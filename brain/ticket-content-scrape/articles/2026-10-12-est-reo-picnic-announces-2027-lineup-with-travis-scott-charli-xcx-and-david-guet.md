# Estéreo Picnic Announces 2027 Lineup With Travis Scott, Charli XCX and David Guetta

## Summary

Colombia's Estéreo Picnic festival announced its 2027 lineup, headlined by Travis Scott, Charli XCX, and David Guetta. The announcement covers the festival dates and initial artist bill.

### Key Points
- Estéreo Picnic 2027 lineup announced, headlined by Travis Scott, Charli XCX, and David Guetta.
- Standard festival-lineup announcement with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Estereo-Picnic-Festival-1.webp)

Travis Scott, Charli XCX and David Guetta will headline the 2027 edition of Estéreo Picnic, scheduled for March 19 through March 21 at Parque Simón Bolívar in Bogotá, Colombia.

Arcángel, Avenged Sevenfold and The Neighbourhood round out the leading names announced for the festival’s 16th edition. The lineup also includes The Prodigy, Fontaines D.C., Khruangbin, The War on Drugs, Raye and Cage the Elephant.

Other confirmed performers include Lola Young, Kasabian, Four Tet, The Avalanches, Angine de Poitrine, Die Spitz, Violet Grohl, Ninajirachi and Adéla.

Latin American artists will have a prominent presence at the three-day event. Venezuela’s Rawayana and Argentina’s Milo J are among the announced acts, along with Argentine band Él Mató a un Policía Motorizado.

The announcement brings together performers across hip-hop, pop, metal, alternative rock and electronic music. Founded in 2010, Estéreo Picnic has become one of Colombia’s major international music festivals, pairing global touring artists with Colombian and Latin American performers.
