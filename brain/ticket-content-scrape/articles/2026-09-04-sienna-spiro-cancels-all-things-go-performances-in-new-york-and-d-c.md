# Sienna Spiro Cancels All Things Go Performances in New York and D.C.

## Summary

Sienna Spiro withdrew from her scheduled All Things Go festival appearances in New York and D.C., citing unspecified "unforeseen circumstances," with Ethel Cain added to the NYC lineup in her place. No broader tour cancellation was announced.

### Key Points
- Spiro pulls out of both All Things Go dates (Sept. 26 NYC, Sept. 27 D.C.).
- Follows an earlier withdrawal from Newport Folk Festival this summer.
- Ethel Cain added to the NYC Saturday lineup alongside Brandi Carlile; the D.C. slot has no announced replacement.
- Spiro's October headline theater run is still on as scheduled.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/All-Things-Go-1.webp)

Sienna Spiro has withdrawn from her scheduled appearances at the 2026 All Things Go festivals in New York and the Washington, D.C., area, with Ethel Cain added to the New York lineup following the change.

Spiro had been scheduled to perform Saturday, September 26, at Forest Hills Stadium in New York and Sunday, September 27, at Merriweather Post Pavilion in Columbia, Maryland.

The English singer-songwriter announced that she would no longer appear at either festival because of unspecified “unforeseen circumstances.”

“Hello everyone, I am so sad to say that I won’t be able to perform at All Things Go this year,” Spiro wrote on social media. “I was so excited for these performances but due to unforeseen circumstances I won’t be able to make it this year.”

The New York festival runs September 25 to 27 at Forest Hills Stadium. Zara Larsson and Lola Young lead Friday’s lineup, while Carlile and Cain now top Saturday. MUNA and Carly Rae Jepsen are among Sunday’s leading acts.

The Washington-area event takes place over the same three days at Merriweather Post Pavilion. Its lineup includes Mitski, Ethel Cain, Hayley Williams, MUNA, Zara Larsson, Brandi Carlile and Lola Young.
