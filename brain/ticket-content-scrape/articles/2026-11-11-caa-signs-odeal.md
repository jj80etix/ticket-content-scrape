# CAA Signs Odeal

## Summary

CAA has signed British-Nigerian Afro-R&B artist Odeal, who has built momentum through sold-out headline shows across the UK, Europe and North America. The agency signing follows his support slot on Summer Walker's arena tour and precedes an upcoming support run with Kehlani.

### Key Points
- CAA signs Odeal (real name Hillary Dennis Udanoh), raised across the UK, Nigeria, Spain and Germany
- Toured with Summer Walker; a Climate Pledge Arena (Seattle) stop grossed $632,455 per Pollstar Boxoffice
- Upcoming support slot on Kehlani's UK arena tour
- Delivered an NPR Tiny Desk performance and two UK R&B Top 10 songs
- Managed by Charles Morgan and Beniam "Hanz" Hannes of OVMBR

CAA has signed British-Nigerian artist Odeal, a defining voice in contemporary Afro-R&B.

The singer, whose real name is Hillary Dennis Udanoh, was raised between the UK, Nigeria, Spain and Germany; his music draws on a rich mix of cultures, blending soulful songwriting with influences spanning R&B, Afrobeats and alternative sounds.

Odeal’s has amassed international recognition with a series of standout performances and touring milestones, including various sold-out headline shows in the UK, Europe and North America. According to [Pollstar Boxoffice Report](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/792451), the singer appeared on tour with Summer Walker, where a stop at Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle grossed $632,455.  

He’s delivered acclaimed performances on NPR’s Tiny Desk, and supported Summer Walker on her US arena tour, with an upcoming support slot on Kehlani’s UK arena tour. He also debuted two songs in the UK R&B Top 10.

Charles Morgan and Beniam “Hanz” Hannes of OVMBR manage Odeal. His attorney is Leeza Panayiotou from Moore SGD Law LLP.
