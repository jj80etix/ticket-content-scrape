# South By Southwest Announces First Wave Of Artists For 2027 Festival

## Summary

SXSW announced its first wave of 100 Showcasing Artists — drawn from 21 countries — for the 2027 festival and conference, running March 15-21 in Austin. The lineup, curated from thousands of submissions, will expand with further waves ahead of the event.

### Key Points
- First 100 of the 2027 SXSW Music Festival lineup announced, representing 21 countries.
- Festival/conference runs March 15-21, 2027, in Austin, TX.
- Full artist list available at SXSW's schedule site; more waves to follow.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 1473977637](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/GettyImages-1473977637-scaled.jpg)

Close-up of banner with logo for South by Southwest (SXSW) festival, Austin, Texas, March 11, 2023. (Photo by Smith Collection/Gado/Getty Images)

South by Southwest (SXSW) unveiled its first 100 artists scheduled to perform at the 2027 edition of the music festival and conference, which takes place March 15-21 in Austin, Texas. The groundbreaking artists come from 21 countries and represent various scenes, genres, and sounds, pushing music into new directions.

Among the first wave of Showcasing Artists for the SXSW Music Festival are Latin songwriter ANASOF, Japanese band bed, Boko Yout, Cowboy Hunters, Girl in the Year Above, La Fiera de Ojinaga, La Reezy, LYNN, Rome Streetz and Wild Pink.

Visit [https://schedule.sxsw.com/2027/artists](https://schedule.sxsw.com/2027/artists) to see the full list of Showcasing Artists announced so far.

“Coming off the energy and success of the 2026 SXSW Music Festival, with seven nights of groundbreaking showcases and record attendance, we are excited to keep pushing SXSW forward in 2027,” said Brian Hobbs, VP of Music, SXSW. “These first 100 artists, curated by our team from thousands of submissions, represent the quality and diversity attendees can expect when the global music industry gathers at SXSW. The excitement to get to Austin next March from artists and industry alike is as strong as it ever has been. More than ever, SXSW is the place where artists break and the business of music gets done, and we have a big 2027 in the works.”

The festival’s showcases are curated by event programmers, who collaborate with record labels, booking agencies, export offices, management and PR firms, publishers, media outlets, lifestyle brands and festivals. The artist showcases give attendees an opportunity to experience unique sets in exclusive, one-of-a-kind venues.
