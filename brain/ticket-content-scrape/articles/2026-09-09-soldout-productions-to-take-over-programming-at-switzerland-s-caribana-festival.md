# Soldout Productions to take over programming at Switzerland’s Caribana Festival

## Summary

Swiss promoter Soldout Productions will take over programming and booking for Switzerland's Caribana Festival starting in 2027, taking strategic control from longtime partner Just Because as part of a leadership reshuffle. Just Because founder Samuel Galley is stepping down from the festival's organization.

### Key Points
- Soldout Productions (Julien Rouyer, Yannick Maffli, Marc Lambelet) takes over programming/booking from the 2027 edition onward.
- Soldout had been a festival partner for three years before assuming control.
- Just Because founder Samuel Galley steps down from the organization.

### Highlights
- Control change effective for the 2027 edition and beyond.
- Follows Soldout's original 2024 strategic-partnership stake alongside festival founder Tony Lerch and Galley.

### Industry Problem
Another example of festival ownership/control consolidating into fewer promoter groups over time, even at the mid-size European festival level, reducing the number of independent decision-makers in programming.

### Proposed Solution
No solution proposed in the article; relevant mainly as a data point for tracking European festival consolidation. Worth watching whether Soldout's portfolio keeps expanding via similar takeovers.
