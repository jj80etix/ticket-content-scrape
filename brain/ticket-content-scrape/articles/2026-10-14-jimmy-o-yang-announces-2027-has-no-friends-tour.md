# Jimmy O. Yang Announces 2027 ‘Has No Friends Tour’

## Summary

Comedian Jimmy O. Yang has announced his 2027 "Has No Friends" tour. The piece lists the tour announcement and upcoming dates.

### Key Points
- Jimmy O. Yang announces 2027 "Has No Friends Tour."

## Transcript

![JOY HasNoFriends alt POST](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/JOY_HasNoFriends_alt_POST-scaled.jpg)

JOY HasNoFriends alt POST

Jimmy O. Yang is hitting the road with his 2027 “Has No Friends” Tour. The multi-city trek is scheduled to kick off at the Tampa Funny Bone in Tampa, FL, on Jan. 15. From there, the comedy tour will hit Majestic Theatre in Dallas, TX, Paramount Theatre in Denver, CO, Celebrity Theatre in Phoenix, AZ, Loew’s Jersey Theatre in Jersey City, NJ and more. The tour will end at the Wynn Las Vegas Encore Theater on May 30.

Yang’s new tour follows a streak of sold-out shows from spring 2026 as a part of his “Big & Tall Tour.” According to [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/224405), Yang grossed $461,885 across two nights at the Paramount Theatre in Seattle, WA, and grossed $535,101 after selling out Radio City Music Hall in New York, NY.

Tickets will be available on Oct. 14 at 12 p.m. PT with an artist presale using code FRIEND, while venue presales begin Oct. 15 at 12 p.m. local time. General sale begins Oct. 16 12:00 PM local time at [https://www.jimmycomedy.com/shows-2027/](https://www.jimmycomedy.com/shows-2027/)
