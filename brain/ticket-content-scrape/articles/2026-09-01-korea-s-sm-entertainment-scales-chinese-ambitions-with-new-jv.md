# Korea’s SM Entertainment scales Chinese ambitions with new JV

## Summary

South Korea's SM Entertainment is scaling its Chinese-market ambitions by launching a new Beijing joint venture, STE, with Tencent Music (TME), aimed at localizing artist development and management in China — more than a year after Tencent Music took a minority stake in SM.

### Key Points
- STE will serve as an artist development and management platform for the Chinese market.
- Comes roughly a year after HYBE sold its stake in a related China venture (per the article's teaser).
- Article is paywalled beyond the opening paragraphs, limiting further deal-structure detail.
