# Sports Illustrated Tickets Expands Falcons Partnership to Spain for NFL Madrid Game

## Summary

Sports Illustrated Tickets is extending its Atlanta Falcons partnership to Spain with a fan ticket sweepstakes ahead of the Falcons-Bengals NFL Madrid game on November 8.

### Key Points
- Announced Oct. 22 as an extension of SI Tickets' role as Official Fan Experience Partner of the NFL in Spain.
- Sweepstakes will award two tickets each to a Falcons fan and a Bengals fan; entry restricted to Spanish residents.
- Game is the NFL's second regular-season Madrid matchup, played at Bernabéu Stadium.
- Entry landing page and additional Madrid fan activities are still in development; no launch date given.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sports-Illustrated-Tickets-Falcons-Spain-10222026.webp)

Sports Illustrated Tickets is expanding its existing partnership with the Atlanta Falcons to Spain ahead of the team’s November 8 matchup against the Cincinnati Bengals in Madrid, with a ticket sweepstakes for fans living in the country.

The company announced the international extension Thursday, October 22, adding a team-specific promotion to its broader role as an Official Fan Experience Partner of the NFL in Spain. The Falcons and Bengals will meet at Bernabéu Stadium in the league’s second regular-season game in Madrid.

In response to questions from TicketNews, Sports Illustrated Tickets confirmed that the promotion will award a pair of game tickets to a Falcons fan and another two tickets to a Bengals fan. Entry will be restricted to Spanish residents.

The company said fans will enter through a dedicated landing page, but the signup page and URL have not yet launched. It is also developing additional fan activities in Madrid, although details were not ready to announce.

“This expansion with the Atlanta Falcons is a natural next step, bringing Sports Illustrated Tickets’ connection with the club to Spain for the first time,” said Joe Flores, chief marketing officer of Sports Illustrated Tickets. “We’re proud to build on what we’ve created together in the U.S. and look forward to connecting with Falcons supporters around the game in Madrid.”

The extension builds on an established domestic relationship with Atlanta rather than marking Sports Illustrated Tickets’ first involvement in Madrid. The company was a fan-experience partner around the NFL’s inaugural game in Spain in 2025 and returned as a partner for the 2026 Madrid game.

Sports Illustrated Tickets has also pursued team-specific agreements alongside its international NFL partnerships. Earlier this month, it [announced a UK partnership with the Jacksonville Jaguars](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/sports-illustrated-tickets-expands-nfl-london-role-with-jaguars-partnership/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) featuring experiences at the club’s London games. Its [broader NFL agreement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/sports-illustrated-tickets-expands-nfl-partnership-to-all-three-2026-london-games/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) covered all three London games this season, which concluded October 18.

“Sports Illustrated Tickets has been a terrific partner in helping us create memorable experiences for Falcons fans, and we’re excited to extend that relationship internationally as we head to Madrid,” said Alex Polofsky, vice president of corporate partnerships at AMB Sports and Entertainment.

The November 8 game is scheduled to kick off at 3:30 p.m. local time (9:30 a.m. ET). It will be the Falcons’ fifth regular-season game outside the United States, according to the partnership announcement. Madrid hosted its first NFL regular-season game in 2025, when the Miami Dolphins defeated the Washington Commanders.

Tickets for the Falcons-Bengals matchup are distributed through the [NFL’s official Madrid game channels](https://www.nfl.com/international/games/madrid/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The new Sports Illustrated Tickets announcement concerns a promotional giveaway and fan engagement; it does not announce a change to the game’s primary ticket distribution.

For Spanish fans interested in the giveaway, the next detail to watch is the launch of the entry page and official sweepstakes rules, neither of which had been provided as of Thursday.
