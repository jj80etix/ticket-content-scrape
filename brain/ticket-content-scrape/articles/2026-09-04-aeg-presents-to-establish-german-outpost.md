# AEG Presents to establish German outpost

## Summary

IQ Magazine's coverage of AEG Presents' new Berlin office, led by promoter Sina Hall to focus on Germany's country-music market, complementing AEG's existing MCT Agentur partnership. (Duplicate coverage of the same news as Pollstar's report.)

### Key Points
- Sina Hall departed her prior role to lead the new office.
- The office builds on AEG's 2024 partnership with MCT Agentur, whose founder Scumeck Sabottka remains a key partner.
