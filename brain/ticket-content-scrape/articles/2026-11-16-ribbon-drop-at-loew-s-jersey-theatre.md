# Ribbon Drop At Loew’s Jersey Theatre

## Summary

Loew's Jersey Theatre, a 1929 Journal Square "Wonder Theatre" landmark, held its Grand Reopening ribbon-cutting on Nov. 13 following a historic restoration led by Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment in partnership with the Jersey City Redevelopment Agency and the state's NJEDA. Local and state officials, including the mayor and a state senator, joined HBSE leadership and the nonprofit Friends of the Loew's to mark the preservation milestone.

### Key Points
- Loew's Jersey Theatre (opened 1929) completed a historic restoration and reopened Nov. 13 under Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment management.
- Project was a partnership between HBSE, the Jersey City Redevelopment Agency, and New Jersey's NJEDA.
- Friends of the Loew's, a preservation nonprofit, was recognized for decades of advocacy work behind the reopening.

## Transcript

Red ribbon opening at Lowe’s Jersey Theatre photo courtesy Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment

Loew’s Jersey Theatre set the stage for the Grand Reopening on Nov. 13 with a ribbon-cutting ceremony at the historic Journal Square landmark, which first welcomed audiences in 1929 as one of the five original “Wonder Theatres.” Mayor James Solomon opened the day with his remarks, celebrating the milestone re-opening of “The People’s Palace” and the partnership between the Jersey City Redevelopment Agency (JCRA), the State of New Jersey/NJEDA and Harris Blitzer Sports & Entertainment (HBSE) behind the historic revitalization. He also recognized the Friends of Loew’s for their decades of preservation work.

Pictured from left to right Chris Fiore, (JCRA); Bill O’Day, Hudson County Commissioner for District 2; Patrick Kelleher, Hudson County Building and Construction Trades Council; HBSE Chief Programming Officer and Head of Entertainment Sean Saadeh; Annisia Cialone, Director of the Department of Housing, Economic Development and Commerce (HEDC); Bruce Wheeler, General Manager Loew’s Jersey Theatre; HBSE Chief Executive Officer Tad Brown; Jersey City Mayor James Solomon; Hudson County Executive Craig Guy; Council President Denise Ridley; Colin Egan, Executive Director of Friends of the Loew’s; New Jersey State Senator Raj Mukherji; Jersey City Councilman Tom Zuppa; Assembly woman Katie Brennan; Jersey City Councilman Michael Griffin; and Jersey City Commissioner-Elect Pam Johnson.
