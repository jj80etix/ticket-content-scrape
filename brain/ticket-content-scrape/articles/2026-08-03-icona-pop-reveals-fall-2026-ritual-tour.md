# Icona Pop Reveals Fall 2026 ‘Ritual Tour’

## Summary

Swedish synth-pop duo Icona Pop announced a fall 2026 "Ritual Tour" spanning the U.S. and Scandinavia, timed to their upcoming fourth studio album. The run kicks off in Brooklyn in late September and wraps in Los Angeles in late November, with tickets already on sale.

### Key Points
- Tour opens September 25 at Baby's All Right (Brooklyn, NY) and closes November 21 at Moroccan Lounge (Los Angeles, CA).
- Dates include Chicago, plus stops in Sweden, Norway, and Denmark before returning to a TBD Las Vegas venue on November 20.
- New album *RITUAL* — the duo's fourth studio record and first since 2023's *Club Romantech* — releases August 14 via Ultra Records/Icona Sound Recordings, led by single "Yellow Top."
- Icona Pop debuted in 2011 and remains best known for the hit single "I Love It."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0778.jpeg)

Swedish synth-pop duo Icona Pop is returning to the road this fall for a brief tour across the U.S. and Scandinavia in support of their forthcoming record.

The run will kick-off in New York City at Brooklyn’s Baby’s All Right on September 25. From there, they’ll appear at Chicago’s Lincoln Hall before heading overseas with stops in Sweden, Norway, and Denmark. They’ll return to the states in late November for a show in Las Vegas — with a venue TBD — followed by a final set at Moroccan Lounge in Los Angeles on November 21.

Tickets are available now [here](https://iconapop.com/tour).

“Healing requires you to slow down and reflect, but you can’t work on yourself 24/7 forever,” Icona Pop said of their upcoming project. “Eventually, you have to get back out into the world and see what happens. You might mess up—but that could be exactly what you need. For us, the dance floor has always been a place where we find ourselves and each other again. It’s where we can disappear into the music and let go of everything else.”
