# Music Manager Sarah Gallagher Launches NDA Talent

## Summary

Music manager Sarah Gallagher has launched her own management company, NDA Talent, in London after nearly 17 years at Modest! Management (most recently as managing director), bringing long-time clients including Olly Murs and Five with her.

### Key Points
- New London-based management company: NDA Talent.
- Founding roster includes Olly Murs (17-year client relationship) and Five.
- Gallagher was previously managing director at Modest! Management.
- Company positions itself around a boutique, relationship-driven ethos rather than scale.

Music Manager Sarah Gallagher has announced the launch of her own company, NDA Talent, with a roster including Olly Murs and Five.

Longtime client Olly Murs, who has worked with Gallagher for 17 years, said in a statement: “Sarah has seen me through every big moment in my career and every low. I trust her completely and genuinely couldn’t imagine doing all of this without her.”  
  
Five added: “Sarah and NDA Talent handle the business side with knowledge, skill, and professionalism, but also with integrity, sensitivity, and meticulous attention to detail.”

Gallagher previously spent nearly 17 years working at Modest! Management and most recently served as managing director at the company.

NDA Talent’s website describes its ethos as “built on trust. driven by ambition.”  
  
The post goes on to say: “Life is short. We work with people we like being around — people who inspire us, who are good, honest and talented.  
  
“Creating is a privilege and should be enjoyed. We push boundaries with our clients, but always with integrity — advising, steering and executing every step of the way.  

“No big flashy stats or offices. Just focused attention, shared vision and complete trust. Partners, not managers, with a shared ambition, built for the long term.”  
  
NDA Talent is based in London. The company’s website is [https://ndatalent.com/](https://ndatalent.com/)
