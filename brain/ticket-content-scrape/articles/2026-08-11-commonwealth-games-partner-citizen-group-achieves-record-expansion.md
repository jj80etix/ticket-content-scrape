# Commonwealth Games partner Citizen Group achieves record expansion

## Summary

Edinburgh-based live event technology provider Citizen Group reports its busiest year to date, confirming four new contracts spanning its Eventree staff-accreditation platform and Citizen Ticket ticketing engine. The company's highest-profile deployment powered workforce accreditation for the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, alongside major festival clients including TRNSMT, Glasgow Summer Sessions, and the Edinburgh Summer Sessions series.

### Key Points

- Citizen Group confirmed four new contracts, its busiest year to date, spanning Eventree (accreditation) and Citizen Ticket (ticketing engine).
- Eventree powered workforce accreditation for the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games (July 23–Aug 2), processing 1,300+ accredited workers, crew, and contractors across 13 sites.
- First year Eventree supported DF Concerts' full live-music operations, including TRNSMT Festival on Glasgow Green (4,200+ workforce accreditations for an event drawing up to 150,000 attendees over three days).
- Glasgow Summer Sessions processed nearly 5,000 staff, crew, and artist accreditations via the platform.
- Eventree is also supporting the nine-show Edinburgh Summer Sessions concert series, featuring acts including Florence + The Machine, The Cure, Deftones, Lorde, and The Prodigy.
- CEO Harry Boisseau frames the UK live-music sector's reliance on large, temporary, rotating workforces (200,000+ jobs) as the core use case the software addresses.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Live event technology provider Citizen Group has confirmed four new contracts to mark its busiest year to date** **across its staff accreditation software, Eventree, and ticketing engine, Citizen Ticket.**

Amongst the client wins was the deployment across the Glasgow 2026 Commonwealth Games, which took place from July 23 to August 2.

It saw Eventree provide a central operational engine for workforce onboarding, site safety, and venue access control.

The platform processed more than 1,300 accredited workers, crew members, contractors, and Games staff across 13 operational sites and competition arenas.

“The UK live music sector supports over 200,000 jobs, many on a casual basis. Managing events which require a large, temporary, constantly rotating workforce where our software excels,” said Citizen Group chief executive Harry Boisseau.

“Supporting high-stakes live events, from an international multi-venue sporting competition like the Commonwealth Games to massive music festivals drawing tens of thousands of fans, requires sharp precision and reliability.

“Demonstrating that an Edinburgh tech company can seamlessly power operations for major events and global brands like Glastonbury and Burning Man is a proud moment for our team.”

The 2026 season also marked the first year Eventree supported DF Concerts’ largest live music operations, managing crew, contractors, and artists.

At TRNSMT Festival on Glasgow Green, Eventree managed more than 4,200 workforce accreditations for an event welcoming up to 150,000 attendees over three days.

For Glasgow Summer Sessions, the platform processed nearly 5,000 staff, crew, and artists.

Eventree is similarly supporting the nine-show Edinburgh Summer Sessions concert series, managing crew and artist logistics for acts including Florence + The Machine, The Cure, Deftones, Lorde, and The Prodigy.
