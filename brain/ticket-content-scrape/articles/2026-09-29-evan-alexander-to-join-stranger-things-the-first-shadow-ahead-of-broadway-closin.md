# Evan Alexander to Join ‘Stranger Things: The First Shadow’ Ahead of Broadway Closing

## Summary

Evan Alexander Smith joins the Broadway cast of "Stranger Things: The First Shadow" as Dr. Brenner starting September 29, taking over from John Zdrojeski (departing for "A Few Good Men") for the show's final 14 weeks through its January 3, 2027 closing.

### Key Points
- Additional new cast: Taylor Sage Evans (Karen Childress), plus ensemble members Neil McCaffrey, Drew Redington and Kala Ross.
- Production is set in 1959 Hawkins, Indiana, 24 years before the Netflix series; written by Kate Trefry with the Duffer brothers and Jack Thorne.
- Directed by Stephen Daldry with Justin Martin co-directing; the show has won three competitive Tony Awards.
- Closes January 3, 2027 at the Marquis Theatre.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/Untitled-design-2023-10-26T111807.927.jpg)

Evan Alexander Smith is joining the Broadway cast of “Stranger Things: The First Shadow” as the production enters the final stretch of its New York run.

Smith begins performances as Dr. Brenner on September 29 at the Marquis Theatre. He will remain with the production for its final 14 weeks, through the previously announced closing date of January 3, 2027. He succeeds John Zdrojeski, who is departing to join the Broadway cast of “A Few Good Men.”

Several additional performers are beginning their runs alongside Smith. Taylor Sage Evans joins as Karen Childress, while Neil McCaffrey, Drew Redington and Kala Ross enter the ensemble.

The company also includes Victor de Paula Rocha as Henry Creel, Ayana Cymone as Patty Newby, Shea Grant as Joyce Maldonado, Juan Carlos as Bob Newby and Matthew Erick White as James Hopper Jr. Rosie Benton plays Virginia Creel, with Nick Rehberger appearing as Victor Creel.

Set in Hawkins, Indiana, in 1959, “Stranger Things: The First Shadow” takes place 24 years before the events of the television series’ first season. The story follows the Creel family and teenage Henry, whose arrival in town brings a troubled past into a community already facing its own challenges.

Kate Trefry wrote the play from an original story developed with series creators Matt and Ross Duffer and Jack Thorne. Stephen Daldry directs, with Justin Martin serving as co-director.

The production received three competitive Tony Awards in 2025, recognizing its scenic design, lighting design and sound design. Its illusions and technical effects also earned a Special Tony Award.
