# Céline Dion Paris return grosses $125m across first leg

## Summary

IQ reports Céline Dion's Paris comeback residency grossed $125.5m across 16 sold-out shows at Plenitude Arena (539,000 attendees, Billboard Boxscore), marking her return after four years away from the stage. A second leg of 10 more shows is set for May 2027; the article is paywalled beyond the opening summary.

### Key Points
- AEG Presents-promoted run at Europe's largest indoor arena, averaging 33,688 attendees/night
- First leg concluded October 17; second 10-show leg added in June, returning May 2027
