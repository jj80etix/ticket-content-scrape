# Texas Election May Reshape the PUCT: Governor’s Appointment Power Will Affect Pending 765-kV Transmission Line Dockets and Data Centers

## Summary

Gina Hinojosa's 2026 win over Governor Greg Abbott is set to reshape the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT), since the governor appoints all five commissioners on staggered six-year terms. A Troutman Pepper regulatory-alert post walks through the appointment mechanics and how upcoming vacancies could affect pending 765-kV transmission-line dockets and data-center interconnection cases. The subject matter (Texas utility regulation) is unrelated to ticketing or live events.

### Key Points
- PUCT has five governor-appointed commissioners serving staggered six-year terms.
- The new governor will gain appointment power as terms expire or vacancies arise.
- Pending dockets on 765-kV transmission lines and data-center interconnection could be affected by commission turnover.
- An appointment made while the Senate is in session requires two-thirds consent.

Gina Hinojosa defeated Governor Greg Abbott in the 2026 election for governor. Because the governor appoints every member of the Public Utility Commission of Texas (PUCT), the result will no doubt affect the PUCT’s processing of all pending 765-kV transmission line cases, as well as matters involving data center interconnection.

### The Governor’s Appointment Power

The PUCT has five commissioners appointed by the governor with the advice and consent of the Senate, serving staggered six-year terms.[\[1\]](#_ftn1) Because terms are staggered, the governor appoints commissioners on a rolling basis, not all at once. The Texas Constitution treats the expiration of a term as a vacancy for purposes of the governor’s appointment power.[\[2\]](#_ftn2) The governor also fills mid-term vacancies, and an appointee to a vacancy serves the unexpired term unless otherwise provided.[\[3\]](#_ftn3) An appointment made while the Senate is in session requires the advice and consent of two-thirds of the Senate present.[\[4\]](#_ftn4) The governor also designates one commissioner as presiding officer, who serves at the governor’s pleasure.[\[5\]](#_ftn5)

### Where Each Commissioner Stands

| **Commissioner** | **Term expiration** |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioner Courtney K. Hjaltman | September 1, 2025 |
| Commissioner Kathleen Jackson | September 1, 2027 |
| Commissioner Patrick Rhode | September 1, 2027 |
| Chairman Thomas J. Gleeson | September 1, 2029 |
| Commissioner Morgan Johnson | September 1, 2031 |

Notably, Johnson and Rhode have not been confirmed by the Texas Senate. And since Hjaltman’s term has already expired, she may continue to serve until her successor is nominated and confirmed. Under the Texas Constitution, when the Senate convenes in January 2027, the governor must nominate either the recess appointee or some other person within the first 10 days of the legislative session.[\[6\]](#_ftn6) The 90th Legislative session will begin January 12, 2027; Hinojosa will take office January 19, 2027, giving her just three days to submit her nominees.

If the governor does not nominate anyone, the Senate may consider the recess appointee.[\[7\]](#_ftn7) A nominee needs two-thirds of the Senate present.[\[8\]](#_ftn8) If the Senate takes no final action by the end of a regular session, the appointee is considered rejected.[\[9\]](#_ftn9) The seat then becomes vacant and the governor must nominate another individual to fill that vacancy.[\[10\]](#_ftn10) A replacement would serve the remainder of the unexpired term.[\[11\]](#_ftn11)

The governor and lieutenant governor each have the power to appoint one member to the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) Board of Directors selection committee; the third committee member is appointed by the speaker of the Texas House of Representatives.[\[12\]](#_ftn12) The committee appoints the eight non-ex officio ERCOT board members and designates the chair and vice chair of the ERCOT board.[\[13\]](#_ftn13) There is currently one vacancy on the ERCOT board.

### The 765-kV Cases and Data Centers

Five Permian Basin Reliability Plan 765-kV dockets are pending at the PUCT. Three have been approved but remain subject to post-approval motions for rehearing, and two were remanded to the State Office of Administrative Hearings (SOAH).

| **Docket No.** | **Path** | **Applicants** | **Status** |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 59029 | Longshore Switch to Drill Hole Switch | Oncor | Approved August 28, 2026; motions for rehearing pending |
| 59315 | Dinosaur Switch to Longshore Switch | Oncor | Approved August 28, 2026; motions for rehearing pending |
| 59336 | Howard Substation to Solstice Substation | AEP Texas and CPS Energy | Approved September 18, 2026; motions for rehearing pending |
| 59182 | Big Hill Substation to Sand Lake Switch | Oncor and LCRA TSC | Remanded to SOAH for further consideration |
| 59475 | Bell County East Switch to Big Hill Substation | Oncor and LCRA TSC | Remanded to SOAH for further consideration |

Hinojosa’s campaign emphasized that “we cannot simply trample on the rights of property owners to build 765\[-\]kV transmission lines in record time because high-load users demand it.” [\[14\]](#_ftn14) She discussed the importance of stakeholder involvement, selection of the “least harm routes,” and proper notice and comment timelines.[\[15\]](#_ftn15)

As for the PUCT’s consideration of matters involving data centers, Hinojosa supported “an immediate moratorium on data centers to last until the legislature can meet to put in place real protections\[.\]” [\[16\]](#_ftn16) Hinojosa further stated that, as governor, she would “direct the \[PUCT\] to freeze the rates residential customers pay until we have laws to ensure that data centers and other high-load users pay for themselves.” [\[17\]](#_ftn17)

Currently, the PUCT and ERCOT are under a directive from Abbott to conduct a comprehensive verification and audit of data centers in the ERCOT interconnection process before allowing those interconnections to proceed. ERCOT staff has also introduced a plan to comprehensively revise transmission system planning processes and future large load interconnection “batch” study procedures. Any resulting rule changes would require ERCOT board and PUCT approval.

### Moving Forward

How Hinojosa handles PUCT commissioner appointments is likely to shape the handling of 765-kV transmission line dockets and matters involving data centers.
