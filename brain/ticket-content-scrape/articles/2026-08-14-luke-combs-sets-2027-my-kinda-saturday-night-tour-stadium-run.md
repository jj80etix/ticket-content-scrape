# Luke Combs Sets 2027 ‘My Kinda Saturday Night Tour’ Stadium Run

## Summary

Luke Combs extended his "My Kinda Saturday Night Tour" with 12 new 2027 North American stadium dates, opening April 3 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, TX and closing June 26 at Petco Park in San Diego, with fan-club presale Aug. 19 and Ticketmaster general on-sale Aug. 21.

### Key Points
- 12 new stadium dates, April 3 – June 26, 2027, extending support for album The Way I Am.
- Fan-club ("The Bootleggers") presale Aug. 19; general public on-sale via Ticketmaster Aug. 21.
- Route includes Detroit, Philadelphia, Foxborough, Kansas City, Vancouver and Denver, with a four-act opening lineup on all dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Luke-Combs-My-Kinda-Saturday-Night-2027.webp)

Luke Combs is taking his “My Kinda Saturday Night Tour” into 2027, announcing a new series of North American stadium shows that will continue his current touring run.

The newly announced leg includes 12 dates beginning April 3 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas. From there, the country singer is scheduled to make stops in cities such as Detroit, Philadelphia, Foxborough, Kansas City, Vancouver and Denver before wrapping up on June 26 in San Diego at Petco Park.

Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah and Wyatt McCubbin are set to join Combs as opening acts throughout all 12 shows.

The announcement extends a busy touring cycle for the country singer, who launched the “My Kinda Saturday Night Tour” in support of his latest album, *The Way I Am*. The record arrived in spring 2026 and serves as the latest release in Combs’ catalog.

Tickets for the 2027 shows will first become available through a series of presales. Members of Combs’ official fan club, The Bootleggers, will receive the first opportunity to purchase tickets through the fan presale.

The fan presale is scheduled to begin Wednesday, August 19, at 10 a.m. local time. A general public on-sale will follow Friday, August 21, at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Combs’ official website](https://www.lukecombs.com/).
