# Kings of Leon, Ella Langley and Kygo Join Morgan Wallen’s Sand In My Boots Festival

## Summary

TicketNews' coverage of the newly revealed "Sand In My Boots" 2027 lineup: Kings of Leon, Ella Langley, and Kygo join Morgan Wallen's Gulf Shores, Alabama festival (May 21-23), again produced with AEG Presents.

### Key Points
- Passes go on sale Friday, Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. CT.
- Returning performers from the 2025 debut include Ella Langley; new additions span country, hip-hop, and rock/pop acts.
- Festival proceeds continue to support the Morgan Wallen Foundation's youth music/sports programs.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Sand-In-My-Boots-2027.webp)

Morgan Wallen’s Sand In My Boots festival will return to Gulf Shores, Alabama, May 21 to 23, 2027, with a lineup featuring Kings of Leon, Ella Langley, Kygo and more.

The announcement marks the second edition of the three-day festival, which debuted with a sold-out event in May 2025. AEG Presents will again partner with Wallen to produce the gathering.

Other announced performers include Empire of the Sun, Nelly, Flo Rida, Gavin Adcock and Koe Wetzel. Cole Swindell, Third Eye Blind, Midland, Hudson Westbrook, Gucci Mane and Rick Ross are also scheduled to appear.

Langley is among the performers returning after appearing at the inaugural festival. The 2025 edition was headlined by Wallen, Post Malone, Brooks & Dunn and HARDY, with additional performances from Riley Green and Bailey Zimmerman.

That first lineup also included T-Pain, Diplo, Wiz Khalifa, The War on Drugs, 2 Chainz and Three 6 Mafia. The newly announced roster maintains that mix of country performers and artists from outside the genre.

The festival’s return will also include charitable work through the Morgan Wallen Foundation. The organization supports youth programs in sports and music, including efforts to supply musical instruments to schools.

Passes go on sale Friday, November 6 at 10AM CT. For additional details and to stay up-to-date on the latest information, festivalgoers can visit the official [Sand In My Boots website](https://sandinmybootsfest.com/).
