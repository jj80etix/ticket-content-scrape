# US live sector ‘thrilled’ as Music Tourism Act is signed into law

## Summary

The US Music Tourism Act has been signed into law, directing the Department of Commerce's National Travel and Tourism Office to promote domestic and international travel to U.S. music festivals, concert venues and related attractions. Lawmakers project the push could help the music tourism sector reach $11.3 billion in revenue by 2032, and the US live sector has welcomed the signing.

### Key Points
- Signs into law directing NTTO (Dept. of Commerce) to promote tourism to music-related and sports attractions.
- Lawmakers cite a projected $11.3B in music-tourism revenue by 2032.
- Covers promotion of domestic and international travel to festivals, concert venues, and other music attractions.
- Follows the bill's earlier Senate approval as the "American Music Tourism Act."
