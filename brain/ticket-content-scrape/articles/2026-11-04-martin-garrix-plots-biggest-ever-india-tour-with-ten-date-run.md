# Martin Garrix plots ‘biggest ever’ India tour with ten-date run

## Summary

DJ Martin Garrix will play a 10-date India tour in March 2027 across 12 days, produced with Sunburn Festival and billed as the country's biggest-ever tour by an international act. Stops include Ahmedabad, Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, and Pune, among others.

### Key Points
- Ten shows across 12 days in March 2027, visiting 10 Indian cities.
- Produced in partnership with Sunburn Festival.
- Venues include NICE Grounds Bengaluru, which has also hosted Guns N' Roses.
