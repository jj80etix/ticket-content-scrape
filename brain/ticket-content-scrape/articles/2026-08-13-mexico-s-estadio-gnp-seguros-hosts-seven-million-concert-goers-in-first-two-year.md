# Mexico’s Estadio GNP Seguros hosts seven million concert-goers in first two years

## Summary

Mexico City's OCESA-operated Estadio GNP Seguros has hosted over 7 million concert-goers across 121 shows in the two years since its 2024 reopening, which the venue calls proof of "Mexico's capacity to host the world's most complex productions." The 65,000-capacity stadium also highlighted sustainability results from its renovation, including 860,000 liters of reused water and nearly 2,000 tons of waste managed in 2026.

### Key Points
- 7 million+ attendees across 121 concerts since the venue's August 2024 reopening.
- Renovation enables simultaneous loading/unloading of 40+ trucks for fast production turnarounds.
- Sustainability figures cited: 860,000 liters of water reused for restrooms; ~2,000 tons of waste managed, 180+ tons recycled/recovered in 2026.
- Has hosted major international tours including Stray Kids, Harry Styles, and Shakira.

## Mexico’s Estadio GNP Seguros hosts seven million concert-goers in first two years

The OCESA-operated venue said the 121 concerts hosted since its 2024 reopening shows "Mexico's capacity to host the world's most complex productions"

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/08/Estadio-GNP-Seguros-4-scaled.jpg.webp)

Estadio GNP Seguros © OCESA

Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros hosted over 7 million fans in its first two years of concerts since reopening, as it marks the milestone this week.

Since its reopening in August 2024, the OCESA-operated venue has hosted 121 concerts, which stadium bosses said “reflect Mexico’s growth as one of the most important markets for live entertainment”.

As it marked the anniversary, the venue hailed the progress of its sustainability upgrades put in place during its renovation, revealing 860,000 litres of water were reused for the stadium’s restrooms during 2026 and that the stadium managed nearly two thousand tons of waste, “of which more than 180 tons were recycled or recovered” during this time.

“These results reflect a transformation that goes beyond the venue itself. The GNP Seguros Stadium has become a showcase of Mexico’s capacity to host the world’s most complex productions and has established itself as a must-see destination on the major international circuits,” the venue said.

It added: “The comprehensive renovation of the building incorporated infrastructure designed to meet the demands of the most complex productions and, at the same time, enhance the experience of each attendee.”

> “The GNP Seguros Stadium has become a showcase of Mexico’s capacity to host the world’s most complex productions”

Following a comprehensive refit, the 65,000-capacity stadium reopened in August 2024 as what operators called a [purpose-built music stadium](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/08/the-worlds-first-purpose-built-music-stadium/). At the time, Ocesa founder and CEO, Alejandro Soberón Kuri, said the renovations would enable the stadium to “continue being the global benchmark venue where the best national and international artists connect with their fans”.

Engineered for major international productions, the venue allows simultaneous loading and unloading of more than 40 trucks, enabling fast turnarounds between shows. Its structure is designed to support heavy touring rigs while maintaining unobstructed sight lines from every seat, maximising both capacity and the audience experience.

Having been named one of *IQ’* s 2026 Stadium Stars, since its renovation, the stadium has hosted high-profile international tours including Stray Kids’ *STRAYCITY*, Harry Styles’  *Together, Together,* and Shakira’s *Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran.*
