# Terminal V to inaugurate new open-air venue in Scotland

## Summary

Scottish electronic festival Terminal V will expand to three days and move to a new fully outdoor site, Glasgow's Clydeside Live, for its 10th edition, running June 4-6, 2027 — the first festival at the new open-air docklands venue.

### Key Points
- 2027 edition marks Terminal V's 10th anniversary and first fully outdoor Scottish event.
- Produced by EE Live; staged in Glasgow's historic docklands at the newly launched Clydeside Live site.
- Represents a scale-up from the festival's prior format to a three-day run.

Leading Scottish electronic festival Terminal V will step up its offerings for its 10th edition in 2027, scaling to three days and moving locations to Glasgow’s new Clydeside Live outdoor venue.

The EE Live-produced event is set to take place from 4-6 June 2027, and will be the festival’s first fully outdoor Scottish event. It will be staged in Glasgow’s historic docklands, with Terminal V to be the first festival held at the city’s new open-air event site.
