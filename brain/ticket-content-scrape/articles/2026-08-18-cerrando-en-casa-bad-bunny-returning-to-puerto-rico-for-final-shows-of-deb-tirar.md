# Cerrando En Casa: Bad Bunny Returning To Puerto Rico For Final Shows Of ‘Debí Tirar Más Fotos Tour’

## Summary

Bad Bunny announces final shows of his "Debí Tirar Más Fotos Tour" back home in Puerto Rico, closing out the run.

### Key Points
- Bad Bunny returns to Puerto Rico for the closing shows of the tour.
- Standard tour-date announcement with no ticketing/industry-structure angle.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2278958857](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2278958857.jpg)

Bad Bunny performs during a concert at the Riyadh Air Metropolitano on May 30, 2026, in Madrid, Spain. (Photo By Ricardo Rubio/Europa Press via Getty Images)

After an impressive run throughout Latin America, Australia, the U.K. and Europe, Bad Bunny is going back home to his beloved Puerto Rico to conclude his highly successful “Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour.”

The global superstar announced he’s performing two nights, Aug. 22 and 23, at Estadio Hiram Bithorn, a baseball stadium in San Juan, wrapping up a remarkable run that began in November 2025. Noah Assad Presents and Move Concerts are promoting the shows.

Tickets go on sale Aug. 19 at 10 a.m. For more information, visit [depuertoricopalmundo.com](https://depuertoricopalmundo.com/).

It was rumored Bad Bunny, whose real name is Benito Antonio Martínez Ocasio, would extend the run and visit other LATAM markets again, notably Mexico, where he played eight sold-out nights last December.

But a homecoming, a return to where it all started, is more fitting for Benito, whose “Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour” gave audiences a taste of Puerto Rican culture and its history with its stellar production in addition to Bad Bunny’s lyrics, which reflect on the history of the island and its people.

The sold-out tour has grossed $438,634,180 off 3,109,459 tickets sold across 55 shows, according to reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice, averaging nearly $8 million per outing. Bad Bunny recently played 10 shows at Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid, Spain, and grossed a whopping $85,547,133 off 639,656 tickets.

Bad Bunny is managed by Noah Assad of Rimas Entertainment and booked by United Talent Agency’s Jbeau Lewis.
