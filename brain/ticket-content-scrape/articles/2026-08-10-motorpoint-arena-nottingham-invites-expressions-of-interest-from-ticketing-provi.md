# Motorpoint Arena Nottingham invites Expressions of Interest from ticketing providers

## Summary

Motorpoint Arena Nottingham (10,050-capacity) has opened Expressions of Interest from ticketing providers ahead of a formal tender, moving to replace its Ticketek UK partnership (in place since Feb. 2023); the move follows Ticketek also losing Sydney's Hordern Pavilion to AXS last month and reports that parent company TEG is restructuring and cutting costs.

### Key Points
- The venue requires an established UK ticketing platform with proven UK venue-ticketing experience at 5,000-plus capacity and sports/season-ticket experience -- relevant given the venue also hosts the Nottingham Panthers (Elite Ice Hockey League).
- Suppliers must register interest by Sept. 4, 17:00 local time.
- Ticketek did land a 10-year deal with Dunedin Venues Management in March, so the Nottingham/Hordern losses aren't a total pullback for the company.

### Highlights
- This is Ticketek's second reported UK/international venue loss in as many months (Hordern Pavilion to AXS in July; now Nottingham opening an RFP).
- TEG (Ticketek's parent) was reported in June to be undergoing restructuring and cost-cutting.
- Nottingham's stated requirements (5,000-plus-cap UK experience, sports/season-ticket capability) show what a mid-size UK arena weighs when switching ticketing vendors.

### Industry Problem
This is a live win/loss data point: a mid-size UK venue is actively shopping its ticketing contract away from an incumbent whose parent company is reportedly under cost pressure, illustrating how vendor financial/operational strain can translate directly into lost venue relationships.

### Proposed Solution
For a primary platform like Etix, this is a build/improve signal: track the Nottingham tender as a competitive-bid case study, and ensure sports/season-ticket capability and UK/EU compliance are demonstrable in any comparable RFP response; it's also useful AI-context for understanding concrete reasons venues switch ticketing vendors.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Motorpoint Arena Nottingham has announced it is inviting Expressions of Interest from ticketing providers, ahead of a forthcoming ticketing services tender.**

The venue, which has a maximum capacity of 10,050 people for concerts, is currently in partnership with Ticketek UK. The deal was agreed in [February 2023](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2023/02/ticketek-boosts-uk-presence-with-motorpoint-arena-nottingham-deal/).

But now, Motorpoint Arena Nottingham has said it is looking for suppliers that are able to provide an established ticketing platform operating within the UK market; proven experience of delivering ticketing services for a UK venue with a capacity of 5,000 or more; and proven experience in sports ticketing, including season tickets.

As well as live music and entertainment, the complex that houses the arena also includes the National Ice Centre which is home to the Nottingham Panthers ice hockey team. The side competes in the Elite Ice Hockey League.

Suppliers meeting the criteria have been invited to [register](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/motorpoint-arena-nottingham-is-inviting-expressions-share-7491517250332524545-p2_e/) their interest by September 4, 17:00 local time.

“We look forward to engaging with experienced providers that can support the delivery of an exceptional ticketing experience for our customers, promoters, and partner,” the arena said in a statement.

**Moving on from Ticketek?**

It is another loss for Ticketek, albeit internationally, with the Australian-headquartered business also losing a partnership with Sydney’s Hordern Pavilion to AXS [last month](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/07/axs-named-ticketing-partner-for-sydneys-hordern-pavilion/).

[In June](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/06/ticketek-entertainment-group-reportedly-set-to-undergo-restructure/) it was also reported that Ticketek Entertainment Group (TEG) was embarking on a restructuring and cost-cutting project.

However, it is not all doom and gloom as Ticketek agreed a 10-year deal with Dunedin Venues Management in [March](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/03/ticketek-signs-10-year-deal-with-dunedin-venues-management/).
