# Willie Nelson, Neil Young To Co-Headline Shows In Bridgeport And Philadelphia

## Summary

Willie Nelson and Neil Young will co-headline two shows next month — Sept. 22 in Bridgeport, CT and Sept. 24 in Philadelphia — with support from The Chrome Hearts and Ty Myers. Tickets go on sale Aug. 21 via Nelson's website; the pairing comes as Young resumes touring after canceling his 2026 European dates.

### Key Points
- Shows: Sept. 22, Hartford Healthcare Amphitheater (Bridgeport); Sept. 24, Highmark Mann Center (Philadelphia).
- Tickets on sale Aug. 21, 10 a.m. ET via willienelson.com.
- Young canceled his 2026 European tour dates earlier this year.
- Young drew 13,001 fans and a $1.1M gross at a comparable 2025 Bethel Woods date, per Pollstar Boxoffice.

## Transcript

![Willie](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Willie.jpg)

Willie Nelson’s Outlaw Music Festival tour may be winding down, but the 93-year-old musician isn’t done performing live with his friends. Nelson is reuniting with fellow legend Neil Young for two special shows next month.

The first co-headline concert is Sept. 22 at Hartford Healthcare Amphitheater in Bridgeport, Connecticut, and the second is Sept. 24 at Highmark Mann Center for the Performing Arts in Philadelphia.

Young will be backed up by The Chrome Hearts, and Ty Myers will support both shows.

Tickets for both shows go on sale Aug. 21 at 10 a.m. ET at [willienelson.com/pages/tour](https://willienelson.com/pages/tour).

Young canceled all of his 2026 tour dates in Europe earlier this year. He began touring with the Chrome Hearts in late 2024. An Aug. 25, 2025, performance at Bethel Woods Center for the Arts in New York drew 13,001 fans, grossing $1,105,590, according to Pollstar Boxoffice reports.

In North America, Martha Vlasic at Independent Artist Group represents Young; elsewhere, his agent is Barry Dickins at International Talent Booking.
