# Foo Fighters Announce 2027 UK and European Tour With Wembley Stadium Dates

## Summary

Foo Fighters announced 15 additional 2027 European, UK and Irish dates extending their "Take Cover Tour," including two Wembley Stadium shows with support from Queens of the Stone Age, Iggy Pop, St. Vincent and Garbage. The run opens June 9 in Reykjavik and includes festival stops before closing with UK/Ireland stadium dates in September, including a Dublin show pending licensing approval. UK stadium tickets go on general sale Nov. 20, with artist/album presales starting Nov. 18.

### Key Points
- 15 new dates, June 9-Sept. 18, 2027, across Europe, UK and Ireland, including two Wembley shows (Sept. 9, 11).
- Support acts include Queens of the Stone Age, Iggy Pop, St. Vincent, Garbage and others across different stops.
- UK general onsale Nov. 20 via Ticketmaster/Gigs and Tours; presales begin Nov. 18.
- Dublin's Croke Park date is subject to licensing approval.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Foo-Fighters-UK-and-European-Tour-1.webp)

Foo Fighters have announced 15 concerts and festival appearances across Europe, the United Kingdom and Ireland for 2027, extending their *Take Cover Tour* with two nights at London’s Wembley Stadium and support from Queens of the Stone Age, Iggy Pop, St. Vincent, Garbage and other artists.

The newly announced itinerary is set to open June 9 at Nýja-Laugardalshöll in Reykjavik, Iceland. The band will play ten dates across Europe through early July before returning for Wembley shows Sept. 9 and 11. Additional concerts are scheduled in Darlington and Edinburgh, followed by a Sept. 18 appearance at Dublin’s Croke Park, which is subject to licensing approval.

The Hives will appear on the first four dates, Iggy Pop is on the June 19 Werchter Parklife bill in Belgium, and Queens of the Stone Age will join Foo Fighters at Firenze Rocks in Florence. St. Vincent and Garbage are among the guests for the September UK dates, with other performers including Slowdive, Nothing But Thieves, Yard Act and The Vaselines.

The announcement follows the band’s [2026 European and UK dates](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/foo-fighters-announce-2026-take-cover-european-and-uk-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and [North American stadium tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/10/foo-fighters-to-launch-2026-north-american-stadium-run/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) under the *Take Cover* banner.

Tickets for the four UK stadium concerts go on general sale Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. GMT through [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.co.uk/foo-fighters-take-cover-tour-2027-london-09-09-2027/event/2300656AB0260F7B?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and [Gigs and Tours](https://tickets.gigsandtours.com/news/foo-fighters-take-cover-2027-dates-announced/6208?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Artist and album presales for Wembley and Edinburgh begin Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 10 a.m. GMT, with select additional presales on Nov. 19. For Dublin, [promoter MCD](https://www.mcd.ie/artists/foo-fighters?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) lists artist and Mastercard presales beginning Nov. 18, an MCD presale Nov. 19 and a Nov. 20 general onsale at 10 a.m. local time.

Onsale arrangements for other European dates and festival appearances may differ. Fans can check the [official Foo Fighters website](https://foofighters.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for individual event links and presale details.
