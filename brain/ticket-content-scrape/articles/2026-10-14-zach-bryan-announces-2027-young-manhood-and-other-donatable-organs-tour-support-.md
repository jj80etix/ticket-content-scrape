# Zach Bryan Announces 2027 ‘Young Manhood And Other Donatable Organs Tour,” Support From John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham, Vance Joy

## Summary

Zach Bryan has announced his 2027 "Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs" tour, with support from John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham, and Vance Joy. This Pollstar report covers the same announcement as TicketNews' coverage of the tour.

### Key Points
- Tour name: "Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs," set for 2027.
- Support lineup includes John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham, and Vance Joy.

Country music superstar Zach Bryan has announced the “Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs Tour,” taking him across North America and Australia in 2027.

Promoted by AEG Presents, the tour will span 23 shows in 19 stadiums, with support from John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen, Vance Joy, Fey Fili, Gabriella Rose and Emma Ogier on select dates.

Fans can register now for presale, with Artist Presale beginning Wednesday, Oct. 21 at 10 a.m. local time, and Local Presale starting Thursday, Oct. 22 at 10 a.m. Public onsale is Friday, Oct. 23 at 10 a.m.

The “Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs Tour” is set to kick off in North America at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis on April 28. Stops include Soldier Field in Chicago, MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, Lincoln Financial Field in Philadelphia, Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta, and Highmark Stadium in Buffalo. An Australian legw ill follow, with two performances at Marvel Stadium in Melbourne, Accor Stadium in Sydney and Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane.

Zach Bryan recently made history on his “With Heaven On Tour,” where he set the record for the most attended event in Gillette Stadium, which announced [73,538 tickets sold for the Oct. 3 show](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/04/zach-bryan-sets-record-at-gillette-stadium-with-73538-fans/). That milestone adds to other blockbusters from the artist, who was the first artist to perform at Michigan Stadium, a show that set the record for largest ticketed concert in [U.S. history with 112,408 tickets reportedly](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/09/28/zach-bryan-makes-history-with-largest-us-ticketed-show-at-michigan-stadium/) sold.

Bryan is represented by WME.
