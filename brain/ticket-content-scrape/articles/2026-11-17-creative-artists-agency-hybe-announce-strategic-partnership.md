# Creative Artists Agency & HYBE Announce Strategic Partnership

## Summary

Creative Artists Agency and HYBE announced a strategic partnership to expand creative and commercial opportunities across content, brand partnerships, sports, licensing and merchandising for HYBE's K-pop artist roster. The deal centers on artist representation, IP and brand-building rather than ticketing operations or platform technology, and spans markets including Korea, Japan, the Americas, China and India. It is an agency/label partnership, not a ticketing-vendor or market-structure development.

### Key Points
- CAA and HYBE (BTS, SEVENTEEN, NewJeans, ENHYPEN labels, among others) announced a multi-market strategic partnership.
- Partnership covers content, brand partnerships, sports, licensing and merchandising — not ticketing or live-event operations specifically.
- Strategic focus markets: Korea, Japan, the Americas, China and India.

Creative Artists Agency and global entertainment lifestyle platform company HYBE have announced a strategic partnership that aims to “expand creative and commercial opportunities for HYBE, its artists, and its intellectual property (IP) and brands.”

HYBE boasts a number of labels that are home to some of the biggest K-pop artists in the world including BIGHIT MUSIC (with a roster including BTS and TOMORROW X TOGETHER, among others) BELIFT LAB (artists include ENHYPEN), SOURCE MUSIC (LE SSERAFIM), PLEDIS Entertainment (SEVENTEEN), KOZ ENTERTAINMENT (BOYNEXTDOOR), ADOR (NewJeans), ABD (TUIDE), XY LABELS (&TEAM) and JCONIC (aeon).

The multinational conglomerate, which is headquartered in Seoul, South Korea, also includes HYBE AMERICA, which “manages a diverse portfolio including Blue Highway Records, music publisher Big Machine Music and Quality Control Music in the U.S., along with HYBE’s labels in Latin America and the U.S. Hispanic market.”

“This partnership with CAA marks an important step in expanding creative possibilities for HYBE’s artists. By bringing together our complementary strengths and global networks, we aim to extend the reach and value of our artists and their IP across content, brands, and live experiences. Together, we will explore new creative opportunities that transcend borders and formats, bringing audiences closer to our artists through more immersive and meaningful experiences,” Jason Jaesang Lee, CEO of HYBE, said in a statement.  
  
CAA has a long history in South Korea including its office in Seoul and relationships across music, film and sports. The agency’s music roster features a number of South Korean artists including Rosé, aespa, NCT 127, Red Velvet, RIIZE, Suho, SHINee, Hearts2Hearts, EXO, and TVXQ!

Today’s announcement notes that CAA and HYBE will collaborate across content, brand partnerships, sports, licensing and merchandising, with the partnership “leveraging HYBE’s artist roster and extensive content IP alongside CAA’s global reach, resources, and relationships.” Strategic and commercial initiatives are planned across HYBE’s key markets including Korea, Japan, the Americas, China and India.

“HYBE has created a cultural movement. HYBE artists do not just entertain; they connect with audiences in extraordinary and innovative ways that define the future of our industry and the intersection of art, influence, and fandom. We are honored to help carry that forward, building on CAA’s experience and relationships across the region, to show the world what is possible when two organizations that believe in creative talent come together,” said Bryan Lourd, CEO and Co-Chairman, CAA.

“What stands out about HYBE is how thoughtfully it builds around each artist, from the music and storytelling to the fan experience,” said Lucy Dickins, CAA Managing Director. “Our role is to build on that foundation across brands, partnerships, and live opportunities, creating new ways for artists to expand their reach, engage audiences in new ways and shape what comes next.”

Fellow CAA Managing Director Maha Dakhil added: “This partnership represents a defining moment for our industry. HYBE has built an enduring legacy around the world. Together, we will set a new standard for what global entertainment collaboration looks like.”
