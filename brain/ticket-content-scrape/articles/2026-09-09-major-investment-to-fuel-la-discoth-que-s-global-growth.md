# Major investment to fuel La Discothèque’s global growth

## Summary

UK house music brand La Discothèque, marking its 10th anniversary, received a major investment from newly formed electronic-events portfolio company Escapism to fund global expansion into North America, Europe and Asia. It's the first of six planned investments as Escapism builds an international EDM events portfolio.

### Key Points
- Investment is the first of six planned deals for Escapism, a new electronic-events brand formed earlier in 2026.
- Funds are earmarked for La Discothèque's push into North America, Europe and Asia.
- Anniversary marked with shows at Manchester's Warehouse Project and London's Drumsheds.

### Highlights
- First of six planned Escapism investments in electronic-events brands during 2026.
- Targets expansion into North America, Europe and Asia from La Discothèque's UK home base.
- Coincides with La Discothèque's 10th anniversary, marked by Warehouse Project and Drumsheds shows.

### Industry Problem
This is another instance of consolidator capital rolling up independent live-event brands into larger portfolios (echoing Superstruct-style roll-ups), which gradually narrows the number of independent decision-makers artists, agents and festivals can work with.

### Proposed Solution
No product fix described in the item itself. For competitive tracking, worth monitoring which independent brands Escapism's next five investments target and whether the roll-up pattern extends into ticketing/venue partnerships that could affect Etix's independent-client pipeline.
