# Sarah Jakes Roberts Sets Seven-City Fall Tour Following Recovery

## Summary

Sarah Jakes Roberts announced a seven-city fall tour, "The Rogue Route," bringing her Woman Evolve community events to theaters in Detroit, Chicago, St. Louis, Memphis, Birmingham, Nashville and San Antonio from Oct. 19-28, following a year shaped by a serious April accident and recovery.

### Key Points
- Stops include Detroit's Masonic Temple, Chicago's The Auditorium, and Nashville's Ryman Auditorium.
- Woman Evolve is one of the largest gatherings for Black women of faith, built around an annual conference now expanding into a multi-city theater run.
- The tour precedes Woman Evolve's planned return to Atlanta in 2027.

## Transcript

Sarah Jakes Roberts is taking Woman Evolve back on the road this fall, announcing a seven-city run dubbed “The Rogue Route” following a year shaped by recovery, reflection and an unexpected change of course.

The tour will run from Oct. 19 through Oct. 28, bringing Jakes Roberts and the Woman Evolve community to Detroit, Chicago, St. Louis, Memphis, Birmingham, Nashville and San Antonio.

*The New York Times* bestselling author, businesswoman and co-senior pastor of The Potter’s House of Dallas said the tour grew out of a difficult personal season that forced her to rethink how she moved forward.

“This has been one of the most difficult seasons of my life. I had to go back to the drawing board and ask God what does it look like for me to respond now,” Jakes Roberts said. “I had to go rogue in ways I didn’t anticipate. The usual way of showing up, just wasn’t working.”

The announcement follows a serious accident in April that disrupted Jakes Roberts’ plans for the year and led her to prioritize her health and recovery.

Now, The Rogue Route will bring Woman Evolve’s message of faith, resilience and transformation directly to audiences across seven markets ahead of the movement’s planned return to Atlanta in 2027.

The tour begins Oct. 19 at Detroit’s Masonic Temple before heading to The Auditorium in Chicago on Oct. 20. Stops will follow at the Stifel Theatre in St. Louis on Oct. 22, the Orpheum Theatre in Memphis on Oct. 24 and Birmingham’s BJCC Concert Hall on Oct. 25. Jakes Roberts will then visit Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium on Oct. 26 before wrapping the run at the Majestic Theatre in San Antonio on Oct. 28.

Woman Evolve has built one of the country’s largest gatherings for Black women of faith through its annual conference, with attendees traveling from around the country for programming centered on spirituality, personal growth and community.

The Rogue Route takes that experience beyond a single conference destination, bringing its mix of candid conversation, connection and faith-focused programming into theaters around the country.

The “Rogue” concept stems from a central question posed by Woman Evolve: “What happens when heaven interrupts earth’s programming?” Organizers said the tour reflects that idea in practice, with unexpected changes altering Woman Evolve’s plans without changing its broader mission.

Tickets are available now via Woman Evolve’s [website](https://www.womanevolve.com/tour).
