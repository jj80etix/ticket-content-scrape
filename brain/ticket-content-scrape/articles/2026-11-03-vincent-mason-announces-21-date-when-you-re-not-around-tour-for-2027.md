# Vincent Mason Announces 21-Date ‘When You’re Not Around Tour’ for 2027

## Summary

Country artist Vincent Mason has announced his 2027 "When You're Not Around Tour," a 21-date headlining run spanning Feb 11-May 8, kicking off in St. Louis and closing in Wilmington, NC. The tour follows his sold-out 2026 "There I Go Tour" and a stint opening for Morgan Wallen.

### Key Points
- 21 dates across the South, Midwest and East Coast, including Atlanta, Charlotte, Denver and Salt Lake City.
- Support acts Benny G, Jacob Hackworth and Phil Kane join on select dates.
- Tickets go on sale Nov 6 at 10am local via Mason's official website.
- Follows a sold-out 2026 headline run and select stadium dates opening for Morgan Wallen.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Vincent-Mason-Tour.webp)

Vincent Mason has announced his 2027 “When You’re Not Around Tour,” a 21-date headlining run scheduled to begin in February.

The tour will begin February 11 at The Factory at The District in the St. Louis area. The opening stretch includes performances in Indianapolis, Grand Rapids, Cleveland and Columbus. Mason will then return to his home state of Georgia for a concert at the Tabernacle in Atlanta. Additional stops include Charlotte and Chattanooga before the tour heads to Clear Lake, Iowa, and Green Bay, Wisconsin.

The itinerary also features Florida performances at Jannus Live in St. Petersburg and FIVE in Jacksonville. Mason will head west for concerts in Wichita, Denver and Salt Lake City. Following a stop in Fayetteville, Arkansas, he will play Billy Bob’s Texas in Fort Worth and John T. Floore’s Country Store in Helotes. The final stretch includes Birmingham and Charleston before wrapping up at Greenfield Lake Amphitheater in Wilmington, North Carolina on May 8.

Benny G, Jacob Hackworth and Phil Kane will support Mason on select dates throughout the tour.
