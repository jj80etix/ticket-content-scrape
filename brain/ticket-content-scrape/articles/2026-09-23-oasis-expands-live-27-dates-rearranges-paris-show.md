# Oasis expands Live ’27 dates, rearranges Paris show

## Summary

Oasis has added four dates to its 2027 "Live '27" tour — extra shows at Knebworth Park, Rome's Olympic Stadium and Munich's Allianz Arena — and rescheduled a Paris date due to railway engineering works, bringing the run to 42 dates ahead of tickets going on sale this week. There are no public pre-sales or general onsales; access is limited to fans who registered during the earlier registration window.

### Key Points
- New dates: two more at Knebworth (Sept. 25–26, now the tour finale), plus additions in Rome (July 26) and Munich (July 6).
- Paris' Stade de France show moves from July 24 to July 20 & 23 due to nearby railway works.
- Tickets go on sale Sept. 25 via registration codes distributed from Sept. 24; no public onsale.
- The 2025 predecessor tour was the biggest concert launch in UK/Irish history, with 10M+ fans competing for 1.4M tickets.

## Oasis expands Live ’27 dates, rearranges Paris show

Ahead of the ticket sale launching Friday, the band have added a handful of new dates and rescheduled a show at the Stade de France

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/07/Oasis-@-Heaton-Park-SJM-Concerts-Copyright-Big-Brother-Recordings-scaled.jpg.webp)

Oasis at Manchester's Heaton Park © Big Brother Recordings

Due to demand, Oasis have added an extra four shows to their *Live ’27* tour, bringing the total up to 42 dates, in addition to rescheduling a date in Paris due to planned transport works.

Crediting “phenomenal demand” during the ticket registration window, two additional dates at the UK’s Knebworth Park (25-26 September, which will be the tour’s finale) and one at both Rome’s Olympic Stadium (26 July) and Munich’s Allianz Arena (6 July) will be available when tickets go on sale this week.

In addition to the new dates, promoters have had to reschedule a date at the Stade de France due to railway engineering works near the venue starting 24 July, when the second Paris show was scheduled for. Oasis will now play the venue on 20 & 23 July.

These are expected to be the [final dates](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/oasis-to-return-in-2027-with-38-date-world-tour/) for the Live Nation/SJM Concerts-promoted tour.

For the Knebworth dates – which will mark Oasis’ return to the famed venue since their historic concerts in 1996 – the promoters recently announced expanded rail/coach transit options, as well as a purpose-built Campsite Village for attendees.

There will be no public pre-sales or general on-sales for the *Live ’27* dates. Fans were invited to register for a chance to participate in the sale, with selected applicants receiving a unique code and sale instructions. Ticket sales begin this Friday (25 September), with codes to be distributed from tomorrow (24 September).

The 2025 run was the biggest-ever concert launch in UK and Irish history, as more than 10 million fans from 158 countries competed to buy the 1.4m available tickets. The sold-out, 41-date *Live ’25* tour traversed the UK/Ireland, North America, Australia, Asia, and South America, with the band playing to nearly 3 million people worldwide on that run of dates.

*Live* ‘ *27* launches at Glasgow’s Celtic Park (21, 23, 25, 28-29 May), followed by 11 nights at Manchester’s Etihad Stadium (4, 6, 8, 11-12, 15, 18-19, 22, 25-26 June) and three at Munich’s Allianz Arena (2-3, 6 July). The rock icons will then play pairs of shows at Barcelona’s Estadi Olímpic Lluís Companys (10-11 July), Amsterdam’s Johan Cruijff ArenA (16-17 July), and Paris’ Stade de France (20, 23 July), before concluding the European leg at Rome’s Olympic Stadium (26, 29-30 July).

The US leg spans two venues – Boston’s Gillette Stadium (10, 13-14 August) and Las Vegas’ Allegiant Stadium (20, 21, 24 August) – before the Mancunians return for a run of major outdoor gigs at Ireland’s Slane Castle (4-5 September) and the UK’s Knebworth (11-12, 18-19, 25-26 September).
