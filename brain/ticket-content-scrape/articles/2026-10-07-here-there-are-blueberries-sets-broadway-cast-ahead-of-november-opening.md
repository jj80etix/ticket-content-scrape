# ‘Here There Are Blueberries’ Sets Broadway Cast Ahead of November Opening

## Summary

"Here There Are Blueberries," a 2024 Pulitzer Prize for Drama finalist, will make its Broadway debut this fall at the Barrymore Theatre, with performances beginning November 2 and a limited 10-week run through January 10, 2027. The ensemble is led by Michael Cerveris, Kathleen Chalfant, Montego Glover and Conrad Ricamora, with writer Moisés Kaufman directing a co-production with La Jolla Playhouse.

### Key Points
- Broadway debut at the Barrymore Theatre; previews from Nov. 2, opening Nov. 9, through Jan. 10, 2027.
- Cast led by Michael Cerveris, Kathleen Chalfant, Montego Glover and Conrad Ricamora.
- Written by Moisés Kaufman and Amanda Gronich; Kaufman directs, co-produced with La Jolla Playhouse.
- 2024 Pulitzer Prize for Drama finalist and winner of the 2025 Lucille Lortel Award for Outstanding Play.
- Follows prior productions at Miami New Drama, La Jolla Playhouse (2022 premiere) and New York Theatre Workshop (2024).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Broadways-Here-There-Are-Blueberries.webp)

Michael Cerveris, Kathleen Chalfant, Montego Glover and Conrad Ricamora will lead the ensemble of “Here There Are Blueberries” when the Pulitzer Prize finalist makes its Broadway debut this fall.

Performances begin November 2 at the Barrymore Theatre, with opening night scheduled for November 9. The production will play a limited 10-week engagement through January 10, 2027.

The eight-member cast also includes Scott Barrow, Michelle Beck, Delia Cunningham and Brandon Flynn. Jeanne Sakata, Anna Shafer, Marrick Smith and Grant James Varjas will serve as understudies. Barrow and Chalfant return after appearing in the play’s 2024 Off-Broadway production at New York Theatre Workshop.

Written by Moisés Kaufman and Amanda Gronich, “Here There Are Blueberries” centers on a photo album that arrived at the United States Holocaust Memorial Museum in 2007. The Nazi-era images prompted an investigation by museum staff, whose work uncovered the stories behind the photographs and raised questions about the people pictured.

Kaufman conceived the production and directs its Broadway staging, which is a co-production with La Jolla Playhouse.

The Broadway engagement follows several earlier stages of development. “Here There Are Blueberries” received a workshop production at Miami New Drama before its world premiere at La Jolla Playhouse in 2022. Its New York Theatre Workshop production followed in 2024.

The play was a finalist for the 2024 Pulitzer Prize for Drama and won the 2025 Lucille Lortel Award for Outstanding Play.
