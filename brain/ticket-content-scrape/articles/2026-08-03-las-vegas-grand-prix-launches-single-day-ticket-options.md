# Las Vegas Grand Prix launches single-day ticket options

## Summary

The Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix has introduced single-day general admission, grandstand, and hospitality ticket options for the first time, giving fans more flexibility ahead of the November 20-22 race. Tickets go on sale August 13, with the tiered lineup ranging from $81 general admission to $3,900 for top-tier hospitality access.

### Key Points
- Single-day tickets go on sale August 13; general admission and T-Mobile GA start at $81 for Thursday.
- Grandstand single-day tickets start at $102 (West Harmon, Heineken) up to $206 (Heineken Silver Main Grandstand); T-Mobile Grandstands start at $145, Turn 3 at $147.
- New single-day hospitality tiers range from $382 (Club Paris) to $3,900 (Wynn Grid Club).
- Earlier in 2026 the event launched a $50 Flamingo Zone entry-level ticket, which has since sold out in both single-day and three-day formats.
- A free "100 Days to Race Day" community celebration is planned for August 15 at Grand Prix Plaza in Las Vegas.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix has launched single-day tickets, including new single-day hospitality options.**

Fans will have more flexibility in 2026, with single-day general admission and grandstand tickets available, and for the first time, select hospitality ticket options throughout the weekend. Tickets will go on sale on August 13, with single-day tickets starting at $81 (£60/€70) including taxes and fees, with access to live entertainment, interactive fan activations and more.

General admission single-day tickets for Thursday will begin at $81, as will T-Mobile general admission at the same rate. Grandstand single-day tickets will start at $102 for Thursday admission.

Breaking it down further, the West Harmon Grandstand will start at $102, as will tickets for the Heineken Grandstands. T-Mobile Grandstands single-day tickets start at $145, with Turn 3 beginning at $147. Heineken Silver Main Grandstand single-day tickets will start at $206.

Earlier this year, The Las Vegas Grand Prix launched its lowest-priced ticket offering with $50 Flamingo Zone by Caesars Rewards tickets. Both three-day and single-day Flamingo Zone general admission tickets are now sold out.

Fans will also have the opportunity to purchase single-day hospitality options, with prices starting at $382 for Club Paris. Turn 3 Club will cost $824 for one day, while Skybox with Heineken Silver Main Grandstand single-day access will start at $1,257. Trackside Tavern at Paddock Club Rooftop access will start at $1,636, while Wynn Grid Club single tickets will begin at $3,900.

The race promoter is also hosting a countdown event on August 15, with a free community celebration at Grand Prix Plaza in Las Vegas. Fans will be able to enjoy a car show, activations, special guest appearances, giveaways and more.

“The 100 Days to Race Day countdown has become a special tradition for our community and a milestone that signals race week is right around the corner,” said Emily Prazer, president and chief executive of Las Vegas Grand Prix.

“As we introduce new single-day ticket options and open sales to the public, we’re excited to give more fans opportunities to experience Formula 1 in Las Vegas. Whether joining us at Grand Prix Plaza in August or trackside in November, fans can expect the energy, entertainment and spectacle that make this event unlike any other.”

The Las Vegas Grand Prix will take place from November 20-22.
