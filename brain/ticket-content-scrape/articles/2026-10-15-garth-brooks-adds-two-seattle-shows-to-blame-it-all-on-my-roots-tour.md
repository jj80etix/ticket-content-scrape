# Garth Brooks Adds Two Seattle Shows to Blame It All on My Roots Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks added two more Seattle shows to his "Blame It All on My Roots" tour, extending the run due to demand.

### Key Points
- Two additional Seattle dates added to an existing tour.
- Routine tour-expansion coverage; no pricing/platform angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Garth-Brooks-Seattle-.webp)

Garth Brooks is bringing his Blame It All on My Roots Arena Tour to Seattle for two concerts at Climate Pledge Arena on November 13 and 14, 2026.

The announcement marks Brooks’ first concerts in the Seattle and Puget Sound region in nine years. His last visit brought three shows to Tacoma in 2017 during his comeback tour.

The Seattle engagement will feature both end-stage and in-the-round seating, continuing the configuration offered on the current arena run. Brooks described the tour’s audiences as spanning generations, with fans joining in throughout the performances.

“The thought of squeezing this show into an arena will be like catching lightning in a bottle!” Brooks said in the announcement.

The current production includes a returning Drum Pod and overhead LED screens surrounding the stage. The tour also serves as the foundation for Brooks’ Killer Live recording project, which captures performances during the run.

Tickets go on sale Friday, October 23, at 10 a.m. Pacific through Ticketmaster. The venue says the online waiting room will open 30 minutes before sales begin.

Before reaching Seattle, Brooks will play three previously announced concerts at Tulsa’s BOK Center. The Oklahoma dates include 8 p.m. performances on October 23 and 24, followed by a 2 p.m. matinee on October 25. The engagement marks his first performances at the venue since 2015.

Additionally, Brooks will also [headline the final night of Stagecoach](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/garth-brooks-riley-green-and-zach-top-to-headline-stagecoach-2027/) 2027, which runs April 23 to 25 at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California. Riley Green tops Friday’s lineup, followed by Zach Top on Saturday and Brooks on Sunday.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [garthbrooks.com](http://garthbrooks.com/).
