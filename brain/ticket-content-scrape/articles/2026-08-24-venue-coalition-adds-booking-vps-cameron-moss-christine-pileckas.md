# Venue Coalition Adds Booking VPs Cameron Moss, Christine Pileckas

## Summary

Venue Coalition, the 20-year-old venue booking network representing 150+ arenas, theaters, and stadiums across North America, named Cameron Moss and Christine Pileckas as new Vice Presidents of Booking.

### Key Points
- Moss previously worked at Ticketmaster, Live Nation, Feld Entertainment, and most recently Big Spring Entertainment.
- Pileckas most recently directed booking and marketing for Legends Global at INTRUST Bank Arena and Century II in Wichita, KS.
- Venue Coalition was founded in 2006 by Jeff Apregan, Gilles Paquin and CEO Andrew Prince.

## Transcript

![VCPileckasmoss](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/VCPileckasmoss.jpg)

Christine Pileckas and Cameron Moss, Venue Coalition

Celebrating its 20th anniversary, venue booking network Venue Coalition has announced the appointment of two new Vice Presidents of Booking in Cameron Moss and Christine Pileckas to serve its 150-plus member venues across North America.

Moss brings more than 20 years of experience to the Venue Coalition booking team, most recently holding the role of VP of Events at Big Spring Entertainment and Director of Buying for Grand Rising Curations. Moss’ previous experience includes time at Ticketmaster, Live Nation and Feld Entertainment.

“Throughout my career, I’ve built meaningful and authentic relationships at every level of this industry,” Moss said in the announcement. “Those strong connections are exactly what our member venues need to compete in today’s competitive landscape. The chance to advocate for them and open doors is what excites me most about joining the Venue Coalition team.”

Pileckas brings more than a decade of venue management experience, most recently serving as Director of Booking and Marketing with Legends Global at INTRUST Bank Arena and Century II Performing Arts Center and Convention Center in Wichita, Kansas.

“I’ve seen firsthand how the right booking strategy can transform not just a venue but an entire community,” Pileckas said in the announcement. “I’m thrilled to join the Venue Coalition team and put my depth of experience, relationships, and passion for live entertainment to work for its members. There’s nothing more rewarding than seeing a venue thrive, and I’m eager to help our members do just that.”

Venue Coalition CEO Andrew Prince, who has been with the company since its inception, added, “Reaching our 20th anniversary is a testament to the incredible community we’ve built together with our members. The best way to celebrate this milestone is to keep raising the bar and ensure our members continue to have the industries’ best support, advocacy, and expertise.”

Since its founding in 2006 by Jeff Apregan, Gilles Paquin and Prince, Venue Coalition has grown from a boutique consulting firm representing nine arenas in Canada into one of the most trusted networks in the live entertainment industry, with a growing community of over 150 member arenas, theaters, and stadiums across North America.

The addition of Cameron Moss and Christine Pileckas signals the next phase of that growth, according to the company.
