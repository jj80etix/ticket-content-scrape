# Outside Lands 2026 Livestream on Amazon Music: How to Watch

## Summary

Amazon Music will livestream select sets from Outside Lands 2026 across two simultaneous channels August 7–9, marking the first time Amazon has run dual concurrent festival streams, with headliners Charli xcx, Turnstile, The Strokes and The xx among the acts featured.

### Key Points
- Livestream runs Fri–Sun, Aug. 7–9, starting 3 p.m. PDT daily; available on Amazon Music app, Prime Video, Twitch and connected TVs.
- First time Amazon Music has run two simultaneous livestream channels for one festival.
- Featured acts include Charli xcx, Turnstile, The Strokes, The xx, Death Cab for Cutie and Baby Keem.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Outside-Lands.webp)

Festivalgoers who are unable to catch [Outside Lands](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/03/outside-lands-2026-lineup-announced-with-charli-xcx-the-strokes/) live at San Francisco’s Golden Gate Park this weekend can watch the exclusive livestream on Amazon Music.

Amazon Music will livestream select sets of the festival on Friday, August 7 through Sunday, August 9. The broadcast will begin at 3 p.m. PDT each day, available within the Amazon Music app, Prime Video, Amazon Music’s Twitch channel, and the Amazon Live Channel on Fire TV and Samsung TV Plus.

This marks the first time Amazon Music will offer two livestream channels running simultaneously. Friday’s livestream will feature headliners Charli xcx and Turnstile on Channel 1, alongside Modest Mouse and GRiZTRONICS. On Saturday, fans can look forward to live-streamed sets by acts like The Strokes, The xx, Snow Strippers, and ODD MOB & OMNOM Present HYPERBEAM, followed by Baby Keem, Death Cab for Cutie, Mariah the Scientist, and Lane 8 on Sunday.

Catch the Outside Lands 2026 livestream [here](https://www.tryamazonmusic.com/enb5xZ).
