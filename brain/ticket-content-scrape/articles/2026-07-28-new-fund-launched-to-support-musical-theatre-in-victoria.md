# New fund launched to support musical theatre in Victoria

## Summary

Victoria's government and the City of Melbourne launched a A$1M Musical Theatre Development Fund to back local writers, directors, and performers, building on the state's status as Australia's largest musical theatre market (A$204.7M in ticket sales, 38.5% of the national total). Arts-funding/policy news with no direct ticketing-platform angle.

### Key Points
- A$1M Musical Theatre Development Fund launched by Victoria's government and City of Melbourne.
- Victoria generated A$204.7M in musical theatre ticket sales — 38.5% of Australia's national total.
- Fund targets local writers, directors, performers, and lyricists, plus projects nearing commercial release.
- Part of the state's broader A$41.1bn Creative State 2028 strategy.

**Victoria’s government has partnered with the City of Melbourne to launch the Musical Theatre Development Fund.**

The fund will start with a A$1m (£525,000/$700,000/€615,000) investment and will back the Victorian sector and the careers of local writers, directors, performers, lyricists, and more.

“Musical theatre is big business for Victoria – employing thousands of Victorians and drawing tourists from around the world,” said Minister for Creative Industries Vicki Ward.

“This Fund gives Victorian stories the chance to shine on stages here and around the world.”

Victoria is said to be Australia’s musical theatre capital with the highest recorded revenue and attendance in the country.

According to the most recent Live Performance Australia report, the state’s musical theatre sector generated A$204.7m in ticket sales, 38.5% of the national total.

The fund will see projects receive dedicated investment to take their work to the next stage while also supporting projects heading towards commercial release.

This is aimed at creating a pipeline of new Victorian productions.

“Melbourne is blessed with one of the biggest and most vibrant musical theatre sectors in the world – this new fund will help us take things to the next level, backing home-grown creativity,” said Lord Mayor of Melbourne Nicholas Reece.

The Musical Theatre Development Fund is an initiative of the Victorian government’s creative industries strategy Creative State 2028, which aims to strengthen its A$41.1bn creative economy, creating local jobs, attracting investment and backing Victorian talent for success.
