# Ryan Beuschel Elevated To Co-Head At The Neal Agency

## Summary

Ryan Beuschel has been elevated to Co-Head at The Neal Agency alongside Adi Sharma and founder/CEO Austin Neal, after three years as General Manager at TNA where he built out TNA Music Publishing and artist-development arm Cervidae Records.

### Key Points
- Beuschel will co-lead office operations, overseeing touring, film/TV and brand sponsorship teams.
- He continues to manage the publishing and artist-development departments he created.
- TNA's roster includes Morgan Wallen, HARDY, Riley Green, Ella Langley and Bailey Zimmerman, among others.

Ryan Beuschel has been elevated to Co-Head alongside Adi Sharma and Founder/CEO Austin Neal. Beuschel previously served for three years as General Manager at TNA, where the company grew under his leadership, including the creation of TNA Music Publishing and the artist development arm, Cervidae Records.

Now, Beuschel will co-lead office operations and oversee teams focusing on touring, film, television and brand sponsorship opportunities, while continuing to manage the publishing and artist development departments he implemented.

“Working alongside Adi, Ryan has been instrumental in accelerating growth for our roster while significantly elevating the tools and services at our clients’ disposal,” shares Neal.

TNA Co-Head Adi Sharma adds, “Ryan’s dedication to our artists and his work expanding our agency offerings have driven some of our greatest successes. His focus provides a distinct advantage for our clients and their continued growth, and we’re honored to elevate him to co-head.”

“It’s an honor to work at an agency and with a team that thinks so differently yet remains collectively proactive for the client, and it starts with leadership’s empowerment to do just that,” Beuschel reflects. “There are so many opportunities now for artists at all levels, and we’re just getting started.”

TNA’s roster proudly represents ADHD, Palmer Anthony, Aidan Canfield, Preston Cooper, Country Night, ERNEST, Riley Green, Jacob Hackworth, HARDY, Zach John King, Rob Langdon, Ella Langley, Cody Lohden, McCoy Moore, Ole 60, Chase Rice, Schmitty, Seaforth, Nate Smith, Will Terry, Morgan Wallen, Chandler Walters, Lauren Watkins, Matthew West, Kenny Whitmire, Landon Wilks, Anne Wilson, Scott Wolverton, Jake Worthington and Bailey Zimmerman, along with entertainment brand Whiskey Jam.
