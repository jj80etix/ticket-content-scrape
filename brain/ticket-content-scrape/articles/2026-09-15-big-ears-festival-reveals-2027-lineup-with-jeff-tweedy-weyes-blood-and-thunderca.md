# Big Ears Festival Reveals 2027 Lineup With Jeff Tweedy, Weyes Blood and Thundercat

## Summary

Big Ears Festival announced its 2027 lineup, led by Jeff Tweedy, Weyes Blood, Thundercat, Robert Glasper, and Oneohtrix Point Never, returning to downtown Knoxville April 1-4 for the festival's 14th edition.

### Key Points
- More than 250 concerts, films, talks, and other events across 20+ Knoxville venues.
- Additional acts include Courtney Barnett, Sylvan Esso, Norah Jones, Bill Frisell, and the Vijay Iyer Quartet.
- Dinner Party (Glasper, Terrace Martin, Kamasi Washington, 9th Wonder) is also on the bill.
- Public on-sale begins Sept. 16; presale for prior pass-buyers began Sept. 15.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Big-Ears-Festival.webp)

Big Ears Festival will return to downtown Knoxville, Tennessee, from April 1 through April 4, 2027, with a lineup led by Jeff Tweedy, Weyes Blood, Thundercat, Robert Glasper and Oneohtrix Point Never.

The four-day festival will feature more than 250 concerts, films, talks, workshops, exhibitions and interactive experiences across more than 20 Knoxville venues. The 2027 event will mark the 14th edition of the multidisciplinary festival.

Other performers announced Monday include Courtney Barnett, Sylvan Esso, Wednesday, Jim James, Natalie Merchant, Mavis Staples, Norah Jones, Nathaniel Rateliff, Kevin Morby, Sierra Hull, Valerie June, Sleaford Mods, Mount Eerie, Meat Puppets, Melvins and U.S. Girls.

Dinner Party, the group featuring Glasper, Terrace Martin, Kamasi Washington and 9th Wonder, is also on the bill. Additional jazz performers include Bill Frisell, Brad Mehldau Trio, Chucho Valdés Royal Quartet, James Brandon Lewis Quartet, Midori Takada and the Vijay Iyer Quartet.

The lineup continues with electronic and experimental artists including Andy Stott, Ana Roxanne, Moor Mother, Mouse on Mars, Suzanne Ciani, Tyondai Braxton, Visible Cloaks and Yasuaki Shimizu.

Public on-sale begins Wednesday, September 16, at 9:00 a.m. ET. A presale for previous pass buyers begins Tuesday, September 15, at 9:00 a.m. ET.

For more information and additional details, festivagoers can visit the [official Big Ears lineup](https://bigearsfestival.org/lineup-2027/).
