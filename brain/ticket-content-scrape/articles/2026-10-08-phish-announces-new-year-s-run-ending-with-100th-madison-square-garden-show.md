# Phish Announces New Year’s Run Ending With 100th Madison Square Garden Show

## Summary

Phish will play four shows at Madison Square Garden from December 30, 2026 through January 2, 2027, with the January 2 show marking the band's 100th performance at the venue. The run extends a relationship dating back to Phish's December 30, 1994 Garden debut.

### Key Points
- Four-night run: Dec. 30–31, 2026 and Jan. 1–2, 2027; the finale is Phish's 100th MSG show.
- Opening night falls on the 32nd anniversary of Phish's first MSG performance (Dec. 30, 1994).
- History includes 17 New Year's Eve shows at the Garden and the no-repeat, 237-song 2017 Baker's Dozen residency.
- Band reached 96 career MSG performances during a five-night July 2026 engagement before this announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Phish-at-the-garden.jpg)

Phish will return to Madison Square Garden for four concerts spanning December 30, 2026, through January 2, 2027, with the final night marking the band’s 100th performance at the New York arena.

The engagement includes shows on December 30 and 31, followed by January 1 and 2. The milestone extends a relationship with the Garden that began more than three decades ago and has included annual holiday celebrations and lengthy summer engagements.

Phish first performed at Madison Square Garden on December 30, 1994, making the opening night of the upcoming run the 32nd anniversary of that debut. The band’s history at the venue includes 17 New Year’s Eve celebrations and the 2017 Baker’s Dozen residency, when the quartet played 237 songs across 13 concerts without repeating a song.

The group reached 96 Garden performances during a five-night engagement in July. Those concerts connected the running show count to the band’s history, with the 92nd performance built around 1992 and the 96th centered on 1996. The newly announced holiday dates will bring the count from 97 to 100.
