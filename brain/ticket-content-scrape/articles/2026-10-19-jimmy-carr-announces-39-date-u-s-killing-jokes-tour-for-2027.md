# Jimmy Carr Announces 39-Date U.S. ‘Killing Jokes’ Tour for 2027

## Summary

Jimmy Carr announced a 39-date U.S. "Killing Jokes" stand-up tour for 2027, running from June through November across theaters and casino venues nationwide, including a three-night finale at Wynn Las Vegas. Artist presale begins Oct. 21, a Live Nation presale follows Oct. 22, and general onsale opens Oct. 23.

### Key Points
- 39 U.S. dates, June 17 - Nov 13, 2027, spanning theaters and casino showrooms.
- Opens June 17 in Portland, ME; closes with three Encore Theater shows at Wynn Las Vegas Nov. 11-13.
- Artist presale Oct. 21, Live Nation presale (via free All Access membership) Oct. 22, general onsale Oct. 23, all at 10 a.m. local.
- Stops include NYC's Beacon Theatre, Nashville's Ryman Auditorium, and the Chicago Theatre.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Jimmy-Carr-Killing-Jokes.webp)

Jimmy Carr has announced a 39-date U.S. tour for 2027, bringing his new stand-up show, “Killing Jokes,” to theaters and casino venues across the country.

Carr’s U.S. tour begins June 17 at the State Theatre in Portland, Maine, followed by stops in Boston, Mashantucket, Scranton and Newark. He plays New York’s Beacon Theatre on June 24 before visiting Atlantic City and Portsmouth, then closing the opening stretch June 27 in Wilmington, North Carolina.

The tour resumes September 16 at the Chicago Theatre, with additional stops including Omaha, Kansas City, Louisville, Memphis and Tulsa. October brings performances across Florida, the Carolinas and Texas, followed by Knoxville’s Tennessee Theatre on October 8 and Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium on October 9. Atlanta and North Charleston complete that leg.

The final stretch opens November 3 at San Francisco’s Masonic, followed by Portland, Oregon, Sacramento, Tacoma and Spokane. Carr performs at the Wiltern in Los Angeles on November 10 before wrapping up with three shows at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas on November 11, 12 and 13.
