# Tinashe Sets Spring 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Tinashe announced "The Show Must Go On," an 18-date North American tour for spring 2027, following her new album *Popstar*.

### Key Points
- Tour runs April 14 (Dallas) through May 15 (Los Angeles), 2027.
- Opens with three consecutive Texas dates: Dallas, Austin, Houston.
- Additional stops include Kansas City, Nashville, Atlanta, Charlotte, Washington, Philadelphia, and Boston.
- Announcement follows a run of sold-out headline shows in Chicago, Brooklyn, and Los Angeles.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Tinashe-tour.webp)

Tinashe has announced “The Show Must Go On,” an 18-date North American tour scheduled for spring 2027.

The run begins April 14 at The Bomb Factory in Dallas and concludes May 15 at Shrine Expo Hall in Los Angeles. The announcement follows the release of her latest album, *Popstar*, and arrives as she begins a series of sold-out headline performances in Chicago, Brooklyn and Los Angeles.

The tour opens with three consecutive Texas dates, moving from Dallas to Austin’s Stubb’s Waller Creek Amphitheater on April 15 and Houston’s Bayou Music Hall on April 16. Additional April stops include Kansas City, Nashville, Atlanta, Charlotte, Washington, Philadelphia and Boston.

The May schedule begins in Pittsburgh before heading west through Denver and Salt Lake City. A Portland concert is scheduled for May 8, although its venue and ticket sale date will be announced later. Following Vancouver, Tinashe will perform in Seattle and Oakland before closing the tour in Los Angeles.
