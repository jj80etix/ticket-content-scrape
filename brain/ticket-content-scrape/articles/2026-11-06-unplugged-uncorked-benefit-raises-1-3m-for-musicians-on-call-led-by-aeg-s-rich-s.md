# ‘Unplugged & Uncorked’ Benefit Raises $1.3M For Musicians On Call, Led By AEG’s Rich Schaefer, City Winery’s Michael Dorf

## Summary

The fifth annual "Unplugged & Uncorked" benefit raised a record $1.3 million for Musicians On Call, drawing more than 550 guests to New York's Ziegfeld Ballroom on October 29. The event was founded and led by AEG Presents' Rich Schaefer, City Winery's Michael Dorf, and The Nordlinger Group's Glenn Nordlinger.

### Key Points
- Raised $1.3 million, a new fundraising record for the event, founded in 2021.
- More than 550 guests attended at New York's Ziegfeld Ballroom, Oct. 29.
- Led by AEG Presents' Rich Schaefer, City Winery's Michael Dorf, and Glenn Nordlinger.
- Musicians On Call says it has reached over 1.25 million people across all 50 states since its founding.

Musicians On Call’s Pete Griffin, AEG Presents’ Rich Schaefer, The Nordlinger Group’s Glenn Nordlinger and City Winery’s Michael Dorf. (Photo: Edwina Hay)

The fifth annual “Unplugged & Uncorked” benefit brought together more than 550 guests and raised more than $1.3 million for Musicians at Call with Oct. 29 at New York City’s Ziegfeld Ballroom. The sold-out evening included fine wines, exclusive experiences and philanthropy as a signature gathering of music and business industry leaders, philanthropists and supporters. The $1.3 million set a new fundraising record for the event, which was founded in 2021 by Rich Schaefer, President of Global Touring at AEG Presents; Michael Dorf, Founder of City Winery; and Glenn Nordlinger, Founder of The Nordlinger Group.

“Musicians On Call started right here in New York City with a simple idea to bring live music to people who couldn’t get to it,” said Pete Griffin, President & CEO of Musicians On Call. “What began with bedside performances at Memorial Sloan Kettering has grown into a mission that has reached more than 1.25 million people across all 50 states. We’re incredibly grateful to Rich, Michael and Glenn, and to everyone who came together to help us continue bringing comfort, connection and a sense of normalcy to patients and families during some of their hardest days.”  
  
Memorable moments from the evening included patient advocate Raynie Clark offering a firsthand account of what music did for her during her personal battle with leukemia, joined by MOC Volunteer Musician and breakout artist, ROSIE, who led the crowd in a spirited singalong of the hospital favorite “Sweet Caroline.”

The 2025 event raised more than $1 million for Musicians On Call, the announcement states.

“This is our fifth year supporting a great cause, and we love what Musicians On Call does,” Michael Dorf said. “We also love getting together and bringing people together around a shared appreciation for wine.”

“It really does feel like a family reunion,” added Rich Schaefer. “We’ve been doing this for five years, and more than half the room has attended every one of them. It’s a night everyone looks forward to, and we’re happy to be part of it.”

The 2026 Unplugged & Uncorked was possible thanks to support from Grand Cru Sponsors Legends Global, Citi and Oak View Group; Premier Cru Sponsors Maestro Cares Foundation, Michael Falcon, The Nordlinger Group, The Team, AXS, VanAndel Arena, ASM Grand Rapids, Hyundai Hope on Wheels and StubHub, and entertainment, healthcare and additional industry supporters who purchased tables.
