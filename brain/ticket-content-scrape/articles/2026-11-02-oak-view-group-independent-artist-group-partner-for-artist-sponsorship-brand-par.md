# Oak View Group, Independent Artist Group Partner For Artist Sponsorship & Brand Partnerships

## Summary

Oak View Group and Independent Artist Group announced a sponsorship/brand-partnership deal giving OVG's North American sponsorship sales network access to IAG's music and comedy touring roster. The arrangement covers title sponsorships, artist presales, brand activations, and meet-and-greets.

### Key Points
- OVG will market IAG artists' touring assets to its existing brand partners across the U.S. and Canada.
- Deal types include title sponsorships, artist sponsor presales, fan activations, and meet & greets.
- Oak View Group is Pollstar's parent company.
- OVG's Dan Griffis and IAG's Jarred Arfa/Peter Pappalaordo issued statements framing the deal as mutually beneficial.

Oak View Group and Independent Artist Group have announced a partnership to provide sponsorship and brand partnership opportunities for IAG’s music and comedy touring roster.

Additionally, the deal enables “OVG to make touring and music assets from IAG artists available to OVG brand partners for promotional opportunities through the company’s vast network of sponsorship sellers across North America,” per an official statement.

As a part of the new partnership, OVG will utilize “its large network of sponsorship sellers across the U.S. and Canada to make touring and music assets available from IAG artists to OVG’s existing and new brand relationships, including opportunities like title sponsorships, artist sponsor presales, artist/brand engagements, fan activations on site, meet & greets and more.” Oak View Group is parent company to *Pollstar*.  
  
“OVG’s sponsorship sales team is nationally recognized for forging groundbreaking, collaborative partnerships with top-tier brands, teams and talent, to maximize awareness and impact,” Dan Griffis, President, Global Partnerships, Oak View Group, said in a statement. “Music is a natural fit for OVG, and we’re excited to combine our sales expertise with the power of IAG’s unmatched roster of artists to create strategic alliances that benefit all partners and brands involved.”  
  
“Oak View Group has always been a valued partner of the agency, and we look forward to enhancing our relationship to the benefit of our artists. Building upon OVG’s extensive preexisting brand relationships and expertise to further our artists marketability makes a great deal of sense and we can’t wait to get started,” Jarred Arfa, EVP and Head of Global Music and Peter Pappalaordo, EVP, Head of Global Touring for IAG, said.
