# Pitbull Unveils 2027 ‘I’m Back’ Australia, New Zealand Headline Tour

## Summary

Pitbull announced the "I'm Back" Australia and New Zealand headline tour for September 2027, hitting arenas in Perth, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane and Auckland. It follows his sold-out 2025 "Fridayz Live" co-headline run, which grossed over $13 million combined across two Australian dates.

### Key Points
- Five-date tour: RAC Arena Perth (Sept. 11), Rod Laver Arena Melbourne (Sept. 14), Afterpay Arena Sydney (Sept. 17), Brisbane Entertainment Centre (Sept. 21), Spark Arena Auckland (Sept. 24), 2027.
- Previous 2025 Australian dates (as co-headliner) sold out and grossed $4.3M and $8.8M on two nights, per Pollstar Boxoffice Reports.
- Telstra Plus (AU) and Spark (NZ) customer presales begin Nov. 23; Frontier Members presale Nov. 25; general public on-sale Nov. 27.

Pitbull has unveiled the “I’m Back” Australia and New Zealand headline tour set for September 2027. The Florida native kicks off the tour in Australia at RAC Arena in Perth on Sept. 11, before heading to Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne on Sept. 14, Afterpay Arena in Sydney on Sept. 17 and Brisbane Entertainment Centre on Sept. 21, before heading to New Zealand to play Spark Arena in Auckland on Sept. 24.

Mr. Worldwide last played in Australia in 2025, performing to over 150k fans as co-headliner on the completely sold-out “Fridayz Live” tour. That sold-out outing, which ran for four dates in October, grossed $4,343,409 and $8,784,511, on the 24th and 25th, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/16010).

Telstra Plus Members (AU) can access presale tickets from Nov. 10 a.m. local time via telstra.com.au/plus/tickets

Spark (NZ) customer presale also starts Monday 23 November 10 a.m. local time via spark.co.nz/music.

Frontier Members can access their presale from Nov. 25 at 1 p.m. local time before tickets go on sale to the public on Nov. 27 at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets and tour info via frontiertouring.com/pitbull
