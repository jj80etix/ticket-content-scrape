# Elvis Costello Tribute Concert Sets All-Star Lineup With Ben Gibbard, Stephen Colbert and More

## Summary

Sweet Relief Musicians Fund and the Harper House Music Foundation announced "A Night to Honor Elvis Costello," an all-star benefit concert at The Masonic in San Francisco on February 13, 2027. The lineup includes Ben Gibbard, Rufus Wainwright, Stephen Colbert, Tina Fey, Nick Lowe and many others, with Costello himself expected to attend, raising money for musicians facing financial hardship.

### Key Points
- Benefit concert "A Night to Honor Elvis Costello" set for Feb. 13, 2027 at The Masonic, San Francisco.
- Organized by Sweet Relief Musicians Fund with the Harper House Music Foundation; proceeds support musicians/industry workers in financial hardship.
- Confirmed performers include Ben Gibbard, Rufus Wainwright, Martha Wainwright, Stephen Colbert, Tina Fey, Nick Lowe, Fred Armisen, Jakob Dylan and Marcus King, with more to be announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Elvis-Costello-Sweet-Relief-Benefit.webp)

Elvis Costello will be honored with an all-star benefit concert in San Francisco next year, bringing together a lineup of musicians, comedians and entertainers including Ben Gibbard, Rufus Wainwright, Stephen Colbert, Tina Fey and Nick Lowe.

Sweet Relief Musicians Fund, in partnership with Harper House Music Foundation, announced that A Night to Honor Elvis Costello will take place February 13, 2027, at The Masonic in San Francisco. Costello himself is scheduled to participate in the event, which will raise money to support musicians and music industry workers experiencing financial hardship.

The [benefit concert](https://www.sweetrelief.org/elviscostello.html?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) will feature performances and appearances by Taylor Momsen of The Pretty Reckless, Fred Armisen, Taylor Goldsmith of Dawes, Ben Gibbard of Death Cab for Cutie and The Postal Service, T-Bone Burnett, Margaret Glaspy, Suki Waterhouse, Jakob Dylan, Marcus King and Adrian Young of No Doubt.

Additional participants include Martha Wainwright, Rufus Wainwright, Jesse Malin, Charlie Sexton and Victoria Williams, who founded Sweet Relief in 1994. Organizers say more special guests and performers will be announced ahead of the concert.

Singer-songwriter and producer Joe Henry will return as musical director and bandleader, marking his fourth consecutive year leading the annual benefit.

Henry has a longstanding personal and professional connection to Costello and said the opportunity to celebrate his catalog makes this year’s concert particularly meaningful.

“Truly: he is one of the most significant and influential songwriters of my lifetime,” Henry said in the announcement.

Costello’s career spans nearly five decades, beginning with his 1977 debut album, *My Aim Is True*. He became one of the defining figures of the late-1970s punk and new wave movements before expanding into rock, country, soul, jazz and orchestral music. His catalog includes songs such as “Alison,” “Pump It Up” and “Everyday I Write the Book.”

Inducted into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2003, Costello has collaborated with artists including Paul McCartney, Burt Bacharach and Allen Toussaint.

The annual benefit concert has become an established fundraising event for Sweet Relief, which provides financial assistance to career musicians and music industry professionals facing medical expenses, disability, mental health challenges and other hardships.

The organization launched its San Francisco tribute series in 2024 with a concert honoring Ramblin’ Jack Elliott, followed by Joan Baez in 2025 and Taj Mahal in 2026.

Sweet Relief was established following Williams’ diagnosis with multiple sclerosis and the release of *Sweet Relief: A Benefit for Victoria Williams*, a 1993 tribute album featuring performances by Pearl Jam, Lou Reed, Soul Asylum and other artists.

The nonprofit has since expanded its assistance programs to include emergency financial support, mental health resources and disaster relief, including grants for music professionals affected by the 2025 Los Angeles wildfires.

Costello has supported the organization for decades, according to Sweet Relief Executive Director Aric Steinberg, who cited the enthusiasm among participating artists as a reflection of Costello’s standing within the music community.
