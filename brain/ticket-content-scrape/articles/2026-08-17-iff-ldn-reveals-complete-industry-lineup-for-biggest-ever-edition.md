# IFF LDN reveals complete industry lineup for biggest ever edition

## Summary

The International Festival Forum (IFF) London finalized its agenda for its Sept. 7-9, 2026 edition at Outernet, its new venue for the event. The gathering brings together roughly 1,000 festival bookers and agents, with a new "Asia Live" programming strand, showcases, and a keynote lineup including CAA's Emma Banks and FKP Scorpio's Stephan Thanscheidt.

### Key Points
- IFF London runs Sept. 7-9, 2026 at Outernet, relocating from its previous venue.
- New "Asia Live" strand (backed by Arts Council England) brings Asian festival heads to IFF for the first time.
- Attendance capped at 1,000, limited to agents and festival bookers.
- Agency partners include CAA, ITB, UTA, WME, and others; event partners include AXS, AEG Presents, and Fever.
- More than 20 emerging acts will showcase in nearby venues over the two main days.

## Transcript

Agenda complete, showcases transformed, and Asian festivals profiled as IFF London comes to Outernet for 7-9 September

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Copy-of-_DSC6679-scaled.jpg-1.webp)

IFF 2025

The [International Festival Forum](https://iff.rocks/?__hstc=86869575.230c770bf9f2d228db7b773715ca87b6.1782141860911.1786876397149.1786956031243.168&__hssc=86869575.2.1786956031243&__hsfp=bd3bf164d5f26e8c70918277435d987b) agenda is now complete, with 1,000 of the world’s leading agents and festival bookers getting ready to meet in London between 7-9 September 2026.

IFF, in association with Offlimits Festival, will take place in a new home for 2026 at Outernet in London’s West End, with the location next to London’s iconic Denmark Street leading to a major upgrade in IFF’s showcase and conference programme.

Across Tuesday 8 and Wednesday 9 September, more than 20 emerging acts – presented in partnership with IFF’s agency partners – will perform in venues just moments from Outernet, in front of IFF delegates and an invited audience of music buyers and industry guests. And all this comes after the special opening night *Everywhere at Once* showcase, in conjunction with Music Venue Trust and the National Lottery.

During the day, a carefully curated schedule of interviews, discussions and workshops will provide food for thought. Highlights include a keynote interview with The Team’s Tom Schroeder, leaders from Montreux Jazz Festival, and Paleo Festival Nyon unpacking what makes the pair of Swiss giants so successful and an opening session running the rule over the 2026 festival season that includes industry luminaries such as Emma Banks (CAA), Stephan Thanscheidt (FKP Scorpio), Pavla Slivova (Colours of Ostrava/Yourope) and James Wright (UTA).

A key addition to this year’s IFF London is the Asia Live strand, supported by Arts Council England, which will see a cohort of leading Asian festival heads come to IFF, many for the first time. The initiative aims to create more opportunities for booking international artists to perform across Asia while also giving attendees a deeper insight into one of the world’s most exciting live music markets.

> “IFF has always been one of the most energising gatherings of music industry professionals on the planet”

“Relocating IFF to the birthplace of modern music in London while hosting over 700 of the world’s top festivals as they build their line ups for next year and beyond feels like a killer combination,” says IFF head and ILMC MD Greg Parmley, “IFF has always been one of the most energising gatherings of music industry professionals on the planet, but this year really moves up a gear.”

As always, the main business of IFF is networking and booking shows, with a stellar list of booking agency partners setting up shop at the Outernet campus during the event. 2026 agency partners are CAA, ITB, Motion Talent, One Fiinix Live, Primary, Pure, Roam, Solo, The Team, UTA and WME.

As well as pre-booked meetings, the agenda is stacked with set-piece moments, including mixers, speed meetings and happy hours, designed to help people go home with dozens of new connections each year.

Key event partners include Offlimits Festival, AXS, AEG Presents, Outernet London, Fever, Tysers Live, and OEG.

IFF launched in London in 2015 and quickly became the annual place for the world’s leading agents and festivals to meet, discuss key industry issues and, most importantly, do business and book shows for the following years. Attendance is capped at 1,000 and is only open to agents and festival bookers to ensure maximum focus and relevance.
