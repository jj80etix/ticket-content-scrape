# P!NK Announces Sphere Residency In Las Vegas

## Summary

P!NK announced a nine-date 2027 residency at Sphere in Las Vegas, becoming the first solo female pop artist to headline the venue. The Live Nation-promoted run goes Sept. 29-Oct. 30, with Vibee offering official hotel/concert packages and VIP add-ons.

### Key Points
- Artist presale registration open now; presale begins Nov. 18, general onsale Nov. 20 (10 a.m. PT) at pinkspage.com.
- Vibee hotel packages (Venetian Resort) go on presale Nov. 11, with VIP tiers adding priority entry, lounge access and transportation.

The next star to take over the Sphere in Las Vegas has been unveiled. P!NK announced a 2027 residency at the venue, becoming the first solo female pop artist to headline the iconic building.

The three-time Grammy Award winner kicks off the Live Nation-promoted nine-date run on Sept. 29 and wraps up just before Halloween on Oct. 30.

Fans can sign up for the artist presale registration now at [pinkspage.com](https://thesphere.pinkspage.com/), and the presale begins Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. PT. Additional presales will run throughout the week leading up to the general onsale, which begins Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. PT at [pinkspage.com](https://thesphere.pinkspage.com/).

Music-led destination experience company Vibee is the official concert and hotel experience package partner for the residency, and presales for packages begin Nov. 11 at 10 a.m. PT. Vibee offers two-night stays at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas, a choice of general admission floor or reserved seating at Sphere, a commemorative laminate or lanyard, and access to on-site concierge service.

Vibee will also offer VIP packages with additional perks, including premium reserved seating and priority entry, early access, special crowd-free shopping hours, access to an exclusive lounge at the P!NK Fan Experience at The Venetian, luxury motorcoach transportation to and from the airport, a curated gift kit and more.

Visit [pink.vibee.com](https://pink.vibee.com/?utm_source=hustle&utm_medium=press&utm_campaign=pink_lv_27_announce&utm_id=pink_lv_27&utm_term=ig_10_29&tm_token=pink_lv_27) for more information about the packages.
