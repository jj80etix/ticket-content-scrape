# Pitbull and Katseye lead latest tour news with expanded runs

## Summary

IQ's latest touring round-up covers new or expanded legs from Pitbull, David Guetta, Katseye, Noah Kahan, Queens of the Stone Age, Weezer, The Neighbourhood and debut act Boy Throb. Highlights include Pitbull's Latin America run, Katseye's Asia-Pacific headline debut, and Noah Kahan's 20-date North American stadium extension.

### Key Points
- Pitbull adds 8 Latin America shows (Jan 31-Feb 21); David Guetta books a Mexico City stadium show.
- Katseye's sold-out "Wildworld Tour" heads to Asia, Australia and New Zealand for 11 dates starting Jan 27.
- Noah Kahan extends "The Last of the Bugs World Tour" with 20 North American stadium dates from April 24, including a 4-night Fenway Park run.
- QOTSA, Weezer and The Neighbourhood add Europe/Australia/NZ legs; Boy Throb announces its debut 20-date theatre tour.

## Transcript

Plus: New dates from Noah Kahan, David Guetta, QOTSA, Weezer, The Neighbourhood, and a debut from Boy Throb

Pitbull, Katseye, Noah Kahan, David Guetta, Queens of the Stone Age, and Weezer have all extended their global tours, leading the latest round-up of tour news.

**Pitbull** – Latin America dates for *I’m Back  
*Back for more, Pitbull has announced an eight-show run through Mexico and South America next year as part of his ongoing *I’m Back Tour*.

The leg launches at São Paulo, Brazil’s Nubank Parque, on 31 January, making stops in arenas in Medellín, Colombia; Lima, Peru; Santiago, Chile; Buenos Aires, Argentina, before a three-city tour of Mexico.

The stint ends with arena shows in Mexico City, Guadalajara, and Monterrey, concluding on 21 February.

He is currently touring European arenas, with stops lined up this month in Barcelona, Madrid, Zürich, Milan, Lyon, Belgrade, Bratislava, and more.

“Latin America, it’s party time!” he said.

**David Guetta** – *Monolith* Mexico City stadium show  
David Guetta has also announced a major stop in Mexico, lining up a show at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros on 8 May as part of his *Monolith Tour.*

**Katseye** –  *Wildworld* to hit Asia, Australia & New Zealand  
Global girl group Katseye have lined up 11 dates in the region as part of their sold-out *Wildworld Tour* for their headline deubt in Asia, Australia & New Zealand.

The newly announced leg launches 27 January at Japan’s Yokohama Arena, before making stops in Manila, Hong Kong, Seoul, Cebu, Singapore, Bangkok, and Taipei.

The Live Nation-backed tour will then head to Australia for a pair of shows in Sydney and Melbourne, before wrapping at Auckland’s Spark Arena on 5 March.

*Wildworld* has already passed through North America and Europe/UK, with The Team agent Mike Malak previously telling *IQ*  that balancing a [measured touring strategy](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/06/fan-first-approach-powers-katseyes-arena-breakthrough/) with hyper-engaged fandom has helped propel the group to rapid success.

**Noah Kahan** –  *Bugs* stadium tour to North America  
Twenty new dates await Noah Kahan in North America next year as he extends his T *he Last of the Bugs World Tour.*

Following behind a handful of UK/IE stadium shows, the newly announced leg will start on 24 April at Georgia’s Sanford Stadium, making stops in markets like Nashville, Houston, Austin, Las Vegas, and Buffalo, including a four-night run at Boston’s Fenway Park.

“Football stadiums with all my friends. Mind blown and hands shaking, let’s go back on tour,” Kahan says with the dates.

The artist is also running an exclusive presale for ticket holders to the Citi Field show cancelled due to weather this year.

In addition to the stadium shows, Kahan has also been confirmed for various festival dates, including Italy’s I-Days and Paris’ new [Off Track series](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/frances-new-mane-event-live-nation-launches-off-track-series/).

**Queens of the Stone Age** – *Perfecth* European leg confirmed  
QOTSA are also lining up a nine-date European leg of their *Perfecth World Tour*, taking them to stops in Germany, France, Spain, Italy, and Croatia.

The leg starts in Hannover’s Gilde Parkbühne on 13 June, and will make stops in Bordeaux, Bilbao, Sevilla, Valencia, Nimes, Pordenone, and Munich, before wrapping up at Pula Arena on 30 June.

The rockers will play a handful of dates in Asia and Australia this year, before hitting the Latam Lollapalooza circuit next year, followed by a North American headline run in 2027.

**Weezer** – Australia & New Zealand run announced  
Weezer will take *The Gathering* outing to Australia & New Zealand next year for a six-city jaunt, starting on 24 February at Perth’s RAC Arena.

The run continues through arenas in Adelaide, Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, and finishes at Auckland’s Spark Arena on 7 March.

Third Eye Blind and Fountains of Wayne will support on the leg.

The *Gathering* has already taken the band through Japan, the US, and the UK/Europe.

**The Neighbourhood** – *Wourld Tour* expands for 2027  
Following their *Wourld Tour* this year, The Neighbourhood are returning with *The Wourld Tour* *II* for 2027.

Backed by Live Nation, the headline/festival outing kicks off on 2 March at Denver’s Ball Arena and includes 16 arena stops in cities like St. Louis, Houston, Dallas, Philadelphia, Boston, Montreal, and Chicago, with the band also lined up for the three Latin Lollapaloozas and Colombia’s Festival Estéreo Picnic in the mix.

In June, the American outfit will travel to Europe, with slots lined up at the Netherlands’ Pinkpop Festival, Greece’s Release Athens, Poland’s Open’er Festival, Portugal’s NOS Alive, plus more, before finishing at Germany’s Lollapalooza Berlin on 17 July.

**Boy Throb** – Debut theatre run slated for 2027  
Finally, pink velour tracksuit-wearing boyband Boy Throb will head out on their debut run next year, *The Real Tour*, a 20-date theatre trek through North America.

It launches on 20 March at Minneapolis’ First Avenue, and will stop in Chicago, Toronto, Boston, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Austin, Houston, Los Angeles, San Francisco, and more, before concluding in Denver on 10 April.

“This time last year we were only five days old,” the band wrote online. “Today we’re announcing our very first 20-city international tour. We are a band who’s been built by its fans, and now we get to visit those fans and perform in their cities. We could not be more grateful to the Throbbers who’ve allowed us to get this far.”
