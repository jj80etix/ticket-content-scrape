# The Dream Continues: Fuerza Regida Unveil Second Leg To Historic Tour

## Summary

Fuerza Regida announced a second leg of their record-setting "This Is Our Dream Tour," moving to amphitheaters and arenas this fall after a sold-out nine-city stadium run that grossed $47.2 million from 330,228 tickets across its first eight shows.

### Key Points
- The second leg starts Oct. 3 in Tinley Park, IL and wraps Nov. 2 at Red Rocks.
- Verizon, Spotify and Citi presales run Aug. 11-13 ahead of an Aug. 14 general onsale via LiveNation.com.
- VIP packages are available via vipnation.com.
- Fuerza Regida has more than 46 million monthly Spotify listeners and was among 2025's most-streamed artists.

Building on a massively successful sold-out first leg of “This is Our Dream Tour,” Mexican American band Fuerza Regida announced additional dates to their record-setting trek, performing in amphitheaters and arenas across the country.

The band just concluded a nine-city stadium run with a sold-out show at Citi Field in New York City on Aug. 7. Totals for that concert have yet to be reported, but through their first eight outings, Fuerza Regida grossed $47,198,246 and moved 330,228 tickets, averaging about $5.9 million per night, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. The stadium shows featured surprise guests including Marshmello, Chino Pacas, Clave Especial, YG, Calle 24, Rey Misterio, 50 Cent and more.

**[Read Cover Story: Viviendo El Sueño: Fuerza Regida Manifest Landmark ‘This Is Our Dream’ Stadium Trek](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/07/living-the-dream-fuerza-regida-manifest-landmark-stadium-trek-cover/)**

The quintet is performing in more intimate venues for the second leg of the Live Nation-promoted tour this fall. Fuerza’s first date is Oct. 3 at Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre in Tinley Park, Illinois. The routing includes stops at Atlanta’s Lakewood Amphitheatre, First Financial Union Credit Amphitheater in Albuquerque, Fresno’s Save Mart Center at Fresno State and Maverik Center in West Valley City, Utah, before wrapping up Nov. 2 at the iconic Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado.

Verizon customers have access to a presale beginning Aug. 11 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by Spotify and Citi presales at noon. All presales conclude Aug. 13 at 11 p.m. local time, and the general onsale begins Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. local time. Visit [LiveNation.com](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ9179vO0/fuerza-regida-events) for more information.

VIP packages will also be available via [vipnation.com](https://www.vipnation.com/). They may include premium tickets, group photo opportunities with select members of the band, an invitation to a preshow lounge, a specially designed gift item and more.

Fuerza Regida is one of the most influential Mexican music bands in the world with more than 46 million monthly listeners on Spotify. They were among the most streamed artists across all platforms in 2025.
