# Stadium Stars 2026: US & Canada

## Summary

IQ Magazine's 2026 Stadium Stars report profiles Allegiant Stadium, AT&T Stadium, BC Place, and SoFi Stadium as part of a 20-venue list of best-in-class large-scale touring venues in the US and Canada. Each profile covers capacity, operator, production infrastructure, and recent investment (e.g., AT&T Stadium's $350m World Cup-driven renovation, BC Place's videoboard and accessibility upgrades). The piece is the first installment of the second-annual industry-nominated ranking.

### Key Points
- Profiles four of 20 venues in IQ Magazine's second-annual Stadium Stars 2026 ranking, part of its Global Stadium Report.
- Allegiant Stadium (Las Vegas, 65,000 concert capacity, operator Legends Global) highlighted for production tech, hospitality, and a track record including BTS, WrestleMania, and the Super Bowl.
- AT&T Stadium (Arlington, TX, 74,000 capacity) completed a $350m privately funded transformation ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup, and touts the NFL's most luxury suites (380) and a massive centre-hung video board.
- BC Place (Vancouver, 65,000 capacity) drew a record 1.2m visitors in 2024 and is set for its biggest-ever concert calendar (10 shows) in 2026, ahead of hosting seven FIFA World Cup matches.
- SoFi Stadium (Inglewood, CA, up to 100,000 capacity) is anchored by its Infinity Screen and subterranean bowl design within a 300-acre entertainment district.
- The venues are nominated and selected by industry peers rather than by IQ editorial staff alone.

**Allegiant Stadium** | Las Vegas, Nevada, USA

*Concert capacity*: 65,000  
*Operator*: Legends Global

In the sports and entertainment mecca that is Las Vegas sits Allegiant Stadium, which has swiftly established itself as one of the premier destinations for global touring. Positioned adjacent to the famed Strip, the venue combines a top-notch location with purpose-built infrastructure designed to host the largest concerts, championship sporting events, and international broadcasts.

From efficient load-in/load-out operations and advanced rigging capabilities to state-of-the-art production technology, luxurious hospitality, and world-class guest experiences, the Legends venue delivers an experience valued by both visitors and organisers. Its retractable grass field and artificial playing surface also provide the flexibility to accommodate any kind of event.

Opening its doors in 2020, the venue’s operators continue to invest in best-in-class hospitality, guest amenities, technology, security, and operational infrastructure, ensuring it remains at the forefront of the live industry. With a proven track record of hosting the biggest productions, including BTS, WrestleMania, and the Super Bowl, Allegiant Stadium is built for the future of live.

---

**AT&T Stadium** | Arlington, Texas, USA

*Concert capacity*: 74,000  
*Operator*: Cowboys Stadium LP

A star of the US stadium circuit, AT&T Stadium has earned its reputation as one of the world’s premier live event destinations. Strategically positioned in the Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex, it combines experiential excellence for fans and touring crews alike.

Purpose-built for efficiency, the venue features a retractable roof, a concrete floor (no turf protection required), plug-and-play production capabilities, an elite PA system, and enhanced 5G connectivity with ten times the previous capacity. It also boasts the NFL’s most luxury suites (380), 824 concession points of sale, 21 merchandise locations, and the world’s largest centre-hung 4K HDTV video board, measuring 160 feet by 72 feet.

A privately funded $350m transformation ahead of the 2026 FIFA World Cup refreshed every corner of the stadium, from premium clubs, suites, lounges, and hospitality spaces to upgraded technology and fan amenities. Home to the Dallas Cowboys NFL team and a proven host of record-breaking global events, the world’s top touring teams all tip their hat to AT&T Stadium.

---

**BC Place** | Vancouver, Canada

*Concert capacity*: 65,000  
*Operator*: BC Pavilion Corporation (PavCo)

Vancouver’s BC Place has emerged as one of North America’s fastest-growing stadium destinations, fuelled by the rapid expansion of its live music programme. The venue welcomed a record 1.2m visitors in 2024 and is set to host its biggest concert calendar ever in 2026, with ten shows.

Home to the BC Lions and Vancouver Whitecaps FC, BC Place balances elite sport with blockbuster entertainment. Combined with strong industry partnerships and a proven ability to deliver world-class productions – such as the three-night finale of Taylor Swift’s The Eras Tour – the venue has established itself as a worthy host of some of live music’s biggest moments.

Ahead of hosting seven FIFA World Cup 2026 matches, BC Place completed a major transformation, introducing a new centre-hung videoboard, upgraded Wi-Fi, expanded premium hospitality spaces, enhanced accessibility features, and renovated dressing rooms. BC Place also features the largest cable-supported retractable fabric roof of its kind in the world. Opened in 1983, the stadium’s latest investment ensures it is well positioned to capitalise on the continued growth of stadium touring while delivering world-class experiences for artists, fans, and organisers alike.

---

**SoFi Stadium** | Inglewood, California, USA

*Concert capacity*: 100,000  
*Operator*: Hollywood Park

As the centrepiece of Los Angeles’ Hollywood Park development, SoFi Stadium has redefined the modern stadium concert experience. The 70,000-capacity stadium, expandable to more than 100,000 for concerts, features a unique subterranean bowl that enhances acoustics and creates an intimate atmosphere, while the iconic dual-sided Infinity Screen and programmable LED roof offer artists unrivalled opportunities for immersive productions.

Built for major touring, SoFi Stadium combines efficient logistics with exceptional flexibility. A quarter-mile-long loading dock accommodates multiple trucks simultaneously, while the flooring system enables rapid transitions between configurations. Extensive back-of-house facilities, premium hospitality spaces, and an experienced in-house broadcast and presentation team further streamline large-scale events – such as the 2026 FIFA World Cup.

Located within a 300-acre entertainment district, alongside the YouTube Theater, retail, restaurants, and hotels, the venue delivers a complete destination experience for both artists and attendees. Continued investment in accessibility, hospitality, and food & beverage offerings has further enhanced the guest experience, as SoFi Stadium continues to set the benchmark for stadium entertainment.
