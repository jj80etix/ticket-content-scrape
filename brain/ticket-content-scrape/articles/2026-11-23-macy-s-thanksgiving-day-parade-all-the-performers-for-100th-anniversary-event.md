# Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade: All The Performers For 100th Anniversary Event

## Summary

Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade marks its 100th anniversary this year with a lineup including Queen Latifah, Teddy Swims, Megan Moroney, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots and the Rockettes, continuing a tradition dating back to 1924 that paused only during World War II.

### Key Points
- 100th anniversary parade features Queen Latifah, Teddy Swims, Megan Moroney and Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, among others.
- Supporting performances from the Rockettes, NYPD Marching Band and Macy's Great American Marching Band.
- Macy's CMO Sharon Otterman calls it a celebration of "unprecedented scale."
- The parade has run annually since 1924, except for a WWII-era pause.

Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade will celebrate its 100th anniversary and has announced a list of stars to perform at this year’s event. The milestone year will include performances from Megan Moroney, Teddy Swims, Queen Latifah, Keke Palmer, Raye, Amma, Absolutely, Jimmy Fallon & The Roots, EJAE, Indigo Girls, Natalie Merchant, Baha Men, Natasha Bedingfield, Blues Traveler, DEVO, Feid, Mckenna Grace, Icona Pop, Il Volo, Wyclef Jean, Jamie Miller, Ryan Peter Murphy, Leslie Odom Jr., Anthony Ramos, RIIZE and Smash Mouth and Jojo

Additional performances will come from the Rockettes, the New York Police Department Marching Band, Macy’s Great American Marching Band, The Divine Nine Unity Step Team, Maria Verdeja School of The Arts, Native Pride Productions, Spirit of America Cheer and Spirit of America Dance.

“For our 100th parade, we’re creating a celebration of unprecedented scale and will deliver an unforgettable Thanksgiving for millions across the country,” Sharon Otterman, Macy’s chief marketing officer, said in a statement.

The Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade began in 1924. Since then, the event has become a holiday-season staple. The parade paused only once, during World War II, when the annual commemoration took a three-year break.
