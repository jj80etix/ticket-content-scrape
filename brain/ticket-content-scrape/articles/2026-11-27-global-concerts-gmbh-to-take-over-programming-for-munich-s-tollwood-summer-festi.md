# Global Concerts GmbH to take over programming for Munich’s Tollwood Summer Festival

## Summary

DEAG subsidiary Global Concerts will take over management of Munich's Tollwood Summer Festival's Olympiapark Music Arena venue immediately, and will take over programming for the full festival from 2028, succeeding longtime programmers Mathias Schaettgen and Daniela Philippi. The festival runs summer and winter editions combining concerts and theatre at Olympiapark.

### Key Points
- Global Concerts (DEAG subsidiary) takes over Tollwood Music Arena (6,000-cap) venue management now, under managing director Andrea Blahetek-Hauzenberger.
- Full festival programming transfers to Global Concerts starting in 2028.
- Outgoing programmers Schaettgen and Philippi led the festival for over two decades, booking artists including Bob Dylan, Sting, and Take That.

## Global Concerts GmbH to take over programming for Munich’s Tollwood Summer Festival

The DEAG-subsidiary takes over the management of festival's music arena, before also taking on programming from 2028

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/Global-Concerts-scaled.jpeg.webp)

Andrea Blahetek-Hauzenberger © Global Concerts GmbH

DEAG-subsidiary promoter Global Concerts is to take over programming for Munich’s Tollwood Summer Festival from 2028, as well as management responsibilities for the Olympiapark Music Arena venue.

The festival centres around concerts at its Tollwood Music Arena site, a 6,000-cap outdoor venue, which will now be led by Global Concerts managing director Andrea Blahetek-Hauzenberger.

This venue management switch precedes Global Concerts’ takeover of programming for the wider festival, which will begin in 2028.

The festival, which stages summer and winter editions in Munich’s Olympiapark, is comprised of a a cross-genre musical bill, as well as theatre productions. Programming is currently handled by Mathias Schaettgen and Daniela Philippi of Tollwood GmbH. During their time programming the festival, Tollwood attracted artists including Bob Dylan, Sting, and Take That.

> “We are looking forward to the beginning of a new, exciting chapter starting in 2028”

“Mathias Schaettgen and Daniela Philippi shaped the music arena for over two decades. They have brought major national and international artists to Munich and made the Tollwood Music Arena known far beyond Munich. For this successful work and countless special concert evenings, Tollwood thanks both of them,” festival operator Tollwood GmbH said.

A company spokesperson added: “Andrea Blahetek-Hauzenberger is now building on this strong foundation; she wants to preserve musical diversity while simultaneously setting new levels.”

They noted that Schaettgen and Philippi were recently honoured with a Best of Live Award in the Federal Association of Concert and Event Industry (BDKV).

Global Concerts said it was “looking forward to the beginning of a new, exciting chapter starting in 2028 and to unforgettable moments in the Tollwood Music Arena”.

The summer festival is set for its next edition in June and July 2027, with appearances already confirmed from artists including Queens of the Stone Age, Simply Red, and Freundeskreis.
