# Los Gemelos De Sinaloa Embarking On 10-Date ‘Gemeniza Tour’

## Summary

Música Mexicana duo Los Gemelos De Sinaloa announced a 10-date "Gemeliza Tour" of theaters running October through December, promoted by Live Nation, in support of their new album. General on-sale begins Sept. 11 via LiveNation.com, with VIP packages sold through VIPNation.com.

### Key Points
- 10-date tour Oct. 23–Dec. 12, including stops in Phoenix, Chicago, Atlanta and Inglewood, CA.
- Live Nation-promoted; general public on-sale Sept. 11 at 10 a.m. local time.
- VIP packages available via vipnation.com (premium seats, meet-and-greet, merch).
- Duo has 7.7M+ monthly Spotify listeners; first tour since 2023.

TOUR FLYER FINAL

Música Mexicana’s favorite twins are hitting the road this fall to promote their newest LP, *The Gemeliza Album*. Los Gemelos De Sinaloa announced a 10-date tour visiting theaters across the country from October through December.

Following the release of the new album on Sept. 24, the duo kicks off the Live Nation-promoted tour Oct. 23 at Arizona Financial Theatre in Phoenix. The routing includes stops at The Criterion in Oklahoma City, Chicago’s Aragon Ballroom, Coca-Cola Roxy in Atlanta and Anaheim’s Honda Center before wrapping up Dec. 12 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California.

Juan Moises and Robert Cardenas last toured in 2023, but the Paramount, California-born brothers have kept releasing new music, growing their dedicated fanbase. Los Gemelos De Sinaloa have amassed more than 7.7 million monthly listeners on Spotify, and four of their tracks have hundreds of millions of streams, including the hit song “No Le Aflojo.”

Tickets go on sale to the general public Sept. 11 at 10 a.m. local time at [LiveNation.com](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ917Q9lf/los-gemelos-de-sinaloa-events).

VIP packages will be available and may offer premium tickets, a meet-and-greet and photo opportunity with the duo, a specially designed VIP gift item, and more. Package contents may vary. For more information, visit [vipnation.com](https://www.vipnation.com/).

Los Gemelos de Sinaloa are managed by Street Mob Records.
