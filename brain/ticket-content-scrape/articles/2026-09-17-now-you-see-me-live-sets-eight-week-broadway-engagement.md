# ‘Now You See Me Live’ Sets Eight-Week Broadway Engagement

## Summary

"Now You See Me Live," a stage-illusion show based on Lionsgate's film franchise, will run a limited eight-week Broadway engagement at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre (Nov. 11-Jan. 3, 2027) following sold-out runs in Australia, Singapore, and London.

### Key Points
- Broadway run: Nov. 11, 2026-Jan. 3, 2027, Al Hirschfeld Theatre.
- Follows sold-out engagements in Australia, Singapore, and a completed run at London's Coliseum.
- Cast includes escapologist Gabriella Lester, illusionists James More and Darcy Oake, and mentalist Matthew Pomeroy as the "Four Horsemen."
- Combines large-scale illusions and stunts with technology built for live-audience staging.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/now-you-see-me-live.webp)

“Now You See Me Live,” a stage illusion show inspired by Lionsgate’s film franchise, will arrive on Broadway this fall for a limited eight-week engagement.

Performances will begin November 11 at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre and continue through January 3, 2027. The New York production follows engagements in Australia, Singapore and London, where the show recently completed a sold-out run at the London Coliseum.

The production centers on the Four Horsemen, the team of magicians responsible for a series of elaborate performances and heists in the *Now You See Me* movies. The stage version combines large-scale illusions, individual stunts and technology developed for live audiences.

Escapologist Gabriella Lester, illusionists James More and Darcy Oake, and mentalist Matthew Pomeroy will perform as the Four Horsemen on Broadway. The international cast includes performers from South Africa, England and Canada.

We know that fans of Lionsgate’s movie franchise Now You See Me along with audiences who loved *The Illusionists* and those who just wish to experience the best of Broadway entertainment will have the time of their lives.”

Simon Painter and Adam Trent created the show, with Painter also serving as creative producer. Painter said the production was designed to expand the possibilities of a traditional stage magic performance by presenting its illusions on a larger scale.

The creative team also includes Ash Jacks McCready as creative and staging director, Kirsty White as creative director and Enzo Wenye as illusion director. Hugo Mercier-Bosseny designed the lighting, and Evan Duffy composed the music. Lionsgate executive Jenefer Brown serves as a creative consultant.

Before the Broadway engagement begins, “Now You See Me Live” is scheduled to open October 15 at the MGM Grand in Las Vegas. The production is part of an ongoing international tour, with additional locations expected to be announced.
