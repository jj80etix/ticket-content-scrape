# Fcukers Unveil Fall Tour Dates

## Summary

New York duo Fcukers announced a North American headline tour for September and October 2026, following dates supporting Harry Styles and RÜFÜS DU SOL. The trek includes stops at venues such as Ventura Music Hall, The Echo Lounge & Music Hall in Dallas, and El Paso's Lowbrow Palace, with support from Baby Wave, SEES00000, and Total Wife on select dates.

### Key Points
- Presale begins July 28 at noon local time and runs through July 29 at 8 a.m.; general onsale follows two hours later.
- The routing mixes headline club dates with festival appearances (Shaky Knees, Portola, Austin City Limits) and prior European festival stops.
- The band had a sold-out show at San Francisco's Castro Theatre in April, grossing $48,662 on 1,459 tickets sold per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Fcukers are booked by THE·TEAM and managed by Lunatic Entertainment.

As they wrap up their final show supporting Harry Styles’ “Together Together” tour in Brazil, Fcukers announced a trek of their own. The New York-based duo unveiled North American headline dates in September and October with Baby Wave, SEES00000 and Total Wife supporting on select dates.

The band will perform in venues like Ventura Music Hall in Southern California, The Echo Lounge & Music Hall in Dallas, El Paso’s Lowbrow Palace and Walter Studios in Phoenix. See full routing below.

The presale for the new shows begins July 28 at noon local time, and fans can sign up [here](https://drop.cobrand.com/d/Fcukers/fall2026). All presales conclude July 29 at 8 a.m. local time, and the general onsale begins two hours later.

It’s been a breakout year for Fcukers, who are booked by THE·TEAM and managed by Lunatic Entertainment’s Simons Parsons and Danny Rogers. The duo has supported Rüfüs Du Sol’s tour this summer and performed at festivals like Bonnaroo, Primavera Sound and Governors Ball.

Fcukers had a successful show at The Castro Theatre in San Francisco in April. The band played a sold-out concert and grossed $48,662 off 1,459 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.
