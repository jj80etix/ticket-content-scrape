# Las Vegas Grand Prix Rolls Out $81 Single-Day Tickets, $100K Community Giveaway

## Summary

The Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix (Nov. 2026) is opening single-day tickets to the public for the first time, starting at $81, alongside new one-day hospitality packages and a "100 Days to Race Day" promotional push that includes a $100,000 community giveaway and free fan activations.

### Key Points
- General single-day tickets go on public sale Aug. 13 at $81 (incl. taxes/fees); Amex cardholders get presale access Aug. 11.
- New one-day hospitality options range from $382 (Club Paris) to $3,900 (Wynn Grid Club); the previously sold-out $50 Flamingo Zone tickets are gone.
- $100,000 "100K for 100 Days" giveaway splits $20,000 each among five local Southern Nevada nonprofits.
- Free Aug. 15 fan celebration (car show, F1 Academy meet-and-greet, giveaways) plus an app-based sweepstakes for Skybox tickets, flights, and a hotel stay.

## Transcript

Photo via Formula 1

The Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix is opening the gates to more fans in 2026, launching single-day tickets, new one-day hospitality packages and a slate of promotions tied to the 100-day countdown to race weekend.

Single-day tickets go on sale to the general public Aug. 13 at 10 a.m. PDT, with prices starting at $81 including taxes and fees for Thursday admission. American Express cardholders will get presale access beginning Aug. 11 at 8 a.m. PDT [here](https://www.f1lasvegasgp.com).

For the first time, fans can also buy select hospitality experiences without committing to the full three-day weekend. General admission tickets start at $81, while grandstand seats begin at $102. Hospitality ranges from $382 for Club Paris to $3,900 for the Wynn Grid Club. The previously announced $50 Flamingo Zone single-day and three-day tickets have already sold out.

“The 100 Days to Race Day countdown has become a special tradition for our community and a milestone that signals race week is right around the corner,” Las Vegas Grand Prix President and CEO Emily Prazer said in a press release. “As we introduce new single-day ticket options and open sales to the public, we’re excited to give more fans opportunities to experience Formula 1 in Las Vegas.”

Along with the ticket drop, Grand Prix Plaza will host a free [100 Days to Race Day](https://www.f1lasvegasgp.com/100-days-out/) Celebration on Aug. 15 from 7 to 10 p.m.

The event will include a 100-vehicle car show, interactive fan activations, giveaways and a meet-and-greet with F1 Academy driver Ava Dobson. Formula 1 is also putting $100,000 into the Southern Nevada community through its “100K for 100 Days Giveaway.”

Five local nonprofit organizations will receive $20,000 each for programs supporting STEM education, youth development and sustainability. Nominations opened July 30 and will close Aug. 10.
