# OCESA Colombia unveils 6k-cap space next to Estadio Vive Claro

## Summary

OCESA Colombia has unveiled Vive Claro Music Hall, a new 6,000-capacity venue adjacent to the 40,000-capacity Estadio Vive Claro in Bogotá, timed to the stadium's first anniversary. The flexible-configuration hall is designed to fill a gap between Bogotá's theatre- and arena-tier venues, with Daniel Caesar's November 29 show confirmed as its debut booking. OCESA frames the addition as reinforcing Bogotá and Colombia as a must-stop Latin American touring market.

### Key Points
- Vive Claro Music Hall seats up to 6,000 with retractable seating and green-area access for varied show formats.
- Sits beside the 40,000-cap Estadio Vive Claro, which has hosted Green Day, Ed Sheeran, Shakira, and Guns N' Roses in its first year.
- First confirmed booking: Daniel Caesar's "Son of Spergy" Tour on November 29.
- Positioned as a mid-tier bridge alongside other Bogotá venues (2,000-cap Lourdes Music Hall, 4,000-cap Chamorro Entertainment City Hall).
- OCESA CEO Luz Angela Castro cites growing artist/production confidence in Colombia as a touring market.

## Transcript

Vive Claro Music Hall will amplify the surrounding entertainment district and help bridge the gap between Bogotá's venue tiers

![Vive Claro Music Hall rendering](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Vive-Claro-Music-Hall-rendering-©-OCESA-Colombia.png)

Vive Claro Music Hall rendering © OCESA Colombia

As Estadio Vive Claro celebrates its first anniversary, operator OCESA Colombia has unveiled a 6,000-capacity adjacent space to help bridge the gap between Bogotá’s venue tiers.

The Vive Claro Music Hall will offer multiple configurations for concerts and family shows, featuring retractable seating and access to green areas for integrated experiences, making it scalable to artists and shows of varying calibres.

Located near the 40,000-capacity stadium, the new space will amplify the offerings of the Vivo Claro Distrito Cultural, with artist and audience development at the heart of the entertainment destination, OCESA says.

“Great artists and projects need venues that grow alongside them, and audiences deserve spaces designed for memorable experiences,” says Luz Angela Castro, CEO of OCESA Colombia. “Vive Claro Music Hall closes a gap in the market and continues to position Bogotá as one of Latin America’s leading entertainment destinations,” she adds.

The first confirmed show is Daniel Caesar, who will bring his [*Son of Spergy* *Tour*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/tour-news-megadeth-expands-farewell-outing-with-eu-uk-dates/) to the venue on 29 November.

The new venue is expected to boost tourism, hospitality, and commerce in the capital city, reinforcing the value of the district as a year-round entertainment destination.

> “Colombia is no longer an ‘optional’ market – it is now a must-stop”

It looks to become an important stepping stone between theatre and arena level, expanding the city’s mid-sized venue choices, with other notable stops including the 2,000-cap Lourdes Music Hall and 4,000-cap Chamorro Entertainment City Hall.

Its stadium neighbour is also the city’s largest purpose-built music venue, with [sustainability](https://www.iqmagazine.com/magazine/the-green-machine-vive-claro-bogota/) baked into both the venue and the wider district.

“With a venue like Vive Claro, artists have the confidence of world-class production, strong audience capacity, and solid business support that make Colombia a profitable and essential part of their global tours,” Castro previously told *IQ*. “Colombia is no longer an ‘optional’ market – it is now a must-stop.”

In its first year of operation, Vive Claro has hosted artists including Green Day, Ed Sheeran, Shakira, and Guns N’ Roses, with Maná, Iron Maiden, and Stray Kids set to come through this year.
