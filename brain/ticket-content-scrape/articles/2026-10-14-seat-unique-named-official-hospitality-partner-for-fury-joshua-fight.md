# Seat Unique Named Official Hospitality Partner for Fury-Joshua Fight

## Summary

UK hospitality-ticketing platform Seat Unique has been named Official Hospitality Partner for the Tyson Fury-Anthony Joshua heavyweight fight at Principality Stadium in Cardiff this December, taking over the full premium-experiences program. The appointment builds on Seat Unique's existing role as the stadium's Official Hospitality Sales Platform since 2021.

### Key Points
- Seat Unique will power, sell, and deliver all official hospitality for the Fury-Joshua fight, Cardiff, December 2026.
- Builds on its existing relationship with Principality Stadium/Welsh Rugby Union since 2021.
- Additional VVIP experiences planned ahead of the event.
- Seat Unique's broader partner roster includes Wembley Stadium, London Stadium, Arsenal, Everton, and AEG Presents.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Seat-Unique-Named-Official-Hospitality-Partner-for-Fury-Joshua-Fight.webp)

Seat Unique has been appointed the Official Hospitality Partner for the heavyweight boxing match between Tyson Fury and Anthony Joshua, taking responsibility for the entire premium experiences program at Principality Stadium in Cardiff, Wales, this December.

Under the agreement, Seat Unique will power, sell and deliver the event’s official hospitality offerings, serving as the exclusive platform for premium access to the all-British heavyweight showdown.

The appointment builds on Seat Unique’s existing relationship with Principality Stadium, where it has served as the Official Hospitality Sales Platform since 2021. The company will now oversee the premium experiences program for one of the most anticipated boxing events in recent years.

Fury and Joshua have spent more than a decade linked to a potential matchup without ever meeting in the ring. Their December showdown in Cardiff is set to bring together two of the biggest names in British heavyweight boxing.

The Fury-Joshua appointment adds another major sporting event to Seat Unique’s growing portfolio of premium ticketing and hospitality partnerships.

The company already works with several prominent organizations across UK sports and live entertainment, including Wembley Stadium, London Stadium, Arsenal, Everton, AEG Presents, KMJ Entertainment and Arena Racing Company.

The agreement extends Seat Unique’s hospitality operations into a major heavyweight boxing event while expanding its existing commercial relationship with Principality Stadium.

As the exclusive official hospitality partner, Seat Unique will be responsible for the full premium experiences program surrounding the December fight, including the development and delivery of additional exclusive offerings.

Official premium experiences are currently available, with further announcements regarding the event’s VVIP hospitality program expected ahead of the showdown.
