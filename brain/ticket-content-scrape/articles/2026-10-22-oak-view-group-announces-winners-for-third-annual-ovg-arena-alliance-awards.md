# Oak View Group Announces Winners For Third Annual OVG Arena Alliance Awards

## Summary

Oak View Group announced the winners of its third annual OVG Arena Alliance Awards, recognizing venues and staff across its arena network ahead of the Alliance's 10th-anniversary summit in New York.

### Key Points
- Winners will be celebrated at the OVG Arena Alliance Summit Welcome Dinner on Oct. 27 in New York City.
- 2026 marks the Arena Alliance's 10th anniversary; the summit runs three days focused on collaboration and industry trends.
- More than 160 touring professionals (promoters, tour managers, production companies) voted on the winners.
- Moody Center and several named individuals (Joel Taylor, Matt Saril, Katie Dordick, Amanda Mann) were honored.

## Transcript

Oak View Group today announced the winners for the third annual OVG Arena Alliance Awards, recognizing the exceptional people and venues whose leadership, creativity and dedication help deliver extraordinary experiences for artists, touring professionals, fans and communities.

OVG is *Pollstar* ‘s parent company

The winners will be celebrated during the OVG Arena Alliance Summit at the Welcome Dinner Oct. 27, in New York City. The 2026 Summit marks the 10th anniversary of the OVG Arena Alliance and brings together leaders from across the Alliance for three days focused on collaboration, innovation and the future of the live entertainment industry.

“The Arena Alliance is built on collaboration and a shared commitment to raising the standard for the live entertainment experience,” said Amy Latimer, Chief Business Officer, Oak View Group. “These winners represent some of the very best work happening across our venues, and we’re excited to recognize the people and teams who go above and beyond every day for artists, crews, fans and their communities.”

To determine winners, OVG assembled a voting body of more than 160 touring professionals, representing promoters, tour managers, production companies and other members of the touring community. The voting group brings firsthand experience of the venues and professionals nominated and the work that goes into creating successful touring experiences.

The 2026 winners recognized across the following categories are:

VENUE OPERATOR OF THE YEAR  
Recognizing outstanding venue leadership, operational expertise, event production, team leadership and financial performance.

Amanda Mann

EVP, Chief Facilities Officer, State Farm Arena

VENUE BOOKER OF THE YEAR  
Recognizing excellence in arena, theater and stadium-level booking, including relationship building, organization, negotiation and creative thinking.

Kate Dordick  
Senior Vice President, Booking, Fiserv Forum

EVENT MANAGER OF THE YEAR  
Recognizing professionals who consistently execute complex events and serve as ambassadors for their venues, teams and cities through adaptability, preparation and attention to detail.

Joel Taylor  
Event Manager, Bridgestone Arena and Nashville Predators

MARKETING PROFESSIONAL OF THE YEAR  
Recognizing marketing leaders who demonstrate creativity, innovation and expertise across media and fan engagement while driving ticket sales and creating memorable fan experiences.

Matt Saril  
Director, Entertainment Marketing, Prudential Center/HBSE

BEST VENUE HOSPITALITY  
Recognizing the venue delivering an exceptional backstage experience through innovative culinary offerings, artist and crew gifting, thoughtful back-of-house activations, and a commitment to friendliness, responsiveness and accommodation.

Moody Center  
Austin, Texas

Now in its third year, the OVG Arena Alliance Awards are part of a broader commitment to recognizing the people and venues that continually raise the bar across the live entertainment industry. The 2026 awards will be presented as the Arena Alliance celebrates its 10th anniversary and looks toward the next decade of innovation, collaboration and growth.

For more information about the OVG Arena Alliance Summit, visit oakviewgroup.com/alliancesummit.
