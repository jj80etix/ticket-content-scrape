# Janet Jackson to Celebrate Control Anniversary With December Concert

## Summary

Janet Jackson will celebrate the 40th anniversary of her album Control with a Dec. 12 concert at Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, Florida, to be filmed for a concert movie, Janet Jackson: Control 40 — The Airtab Music Movie.

### Key Points
- The film, directed by Leslie Small, will include behind-the-scenes tour footage and is planned for a potential theatrical release ahead of streaming.
- The concert will feature Control-era hits alongside career-spanning songs like "Miss You Much" and "All for You."
- Produced by Airtab Music in partnership with Jackson and Randy Jackson.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Janet-Jackson-Control-40.webp)

Janet Jackson will celebrate the 40th anniversary of her 1986 album *Control* with a special concert at Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, Florida, on December 12.

Jackson and Airtab Music announced the “Control 40 Live Concert,” which will be filmed for an upcoming concert movie, *Janet Jackson: Control 40 — The Airtab Music Movie*. Leslie Small will direct the film, which will also include behind-the-scenes footage from Jackson’s tour.

Airtab Music will produce and present the concert, which will feature songs from *Control* as well as other albums from Jackson’s career. The performance will include “Miss You Much,” “Escapade,” “Love Will Never Do (Without You),” “If,” “Again,” “That’s the Way Love Goes,” “Together Again” and “All for You.”

The concert film is being developed for a potential global theatrical release before becoming available through major streaming platforms. The film will allow Jackson fans around the world to experience the anniversary performance beyond the live event.
