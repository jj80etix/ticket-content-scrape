# North West’s Debut Headlining Tour Canceled Days Before Scheduled Start

## Summary

North West's debut headlining "Kimokawaii Tour" with rapper Molly Santana was canceled days before its Aug. 5 Dallas start, with all Ticketmaster/AXS listings pulled and the tour website returning a 404. Both artists posted apologies to fans who had bought tickets; no explanation or refund details were given, and the cancellation follows a string of other 2026 summer tour cancellations (Roxette, David Lee Roth, Kacey Musgraves) and postponements (The Beach Boys).

### Key Points
- 13-year-old North West and rapper Molly Santana's co-headline "Kimokawaii Tour" canceled ahead of its Aug. 5 Dallas kickoff; 8-city run was to end Aug. 27 in Los Angeles.
- All tour listings removed from Ticketmaster and AXS; official tour site now shows a 404.
- Both artists apologized on Instagram; no cause given and no refund process detailed in the announcements.
- Comes amid a broader wave of summer 2026 tour cancellations/postponements across multiple acts.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/north-west.jpg)

North West will no longer be embarking on her debut headlining run.

The 13-year-old singer was scheduled to co-headline the “Kimokawaii Tour” alongside rapper Molly Santana, kicking-off on Wednesday, August 5 in Dallas. The tour was set to stop in cities like Houston, Philadelphia, New York, Boston, Chicago, Phoenix, and San Francisco before wrapping-up in Los Angeles on August 27.

However, all listings for the tour have now been pulled from Ticketmaster and AXS. The tour’s website now reads “404-Page Not Found.”

West shared news of the cancellation in an Instagram story, writing: “I was really excited to go on tour \[with\] Molly Santana, sadly it isn’t happening anymore, but I have something special for you guys, see you soon.”
