# Artists Announced For “CMA Present I Will Always Love You: A Celebration Of Dolly Parton”

## Summary

CMA and ABC announced the performer lineup for "CMA Presents I Will Always Love You: A Celebration of Dolly Parton," a two-hour tribute special airing Oct. 21 on ABC/Disney+, filmed before a live audience at Belmont University in Nashville. Performers include Reba McEntire, Carrie Underwood, Lainey Wilson, Brad Paisley and others, with proceeds supporting Dolly Parton's Imagination Library.

### Key Points
- Airs Oct. 21, 9–11 PM ET on ABC/Disney+, next-day on Hulu; filmed at Belmont University's Fisher Center.
- Performer lineup includes Reba McEntire, Carrie Underwood, Lainey Wilson, Brad Paisley, Darius Rucker, Little Big Town and others.
- CMA and Belmont University will donate proceeds to Dolly Parton's Imagination Library; Disney also supporting the Library.

The Country Music Association (CMA) and ABC revealed the lineup of artists set to perform in “CMA Presents I Will Always Love You: A Celebration of Dolly Parton,” the two-hour live television tribute to the global music icon announced last month.

Revealed on ABC’s “Good Morning America,” Ashley McBryde, Béla Fleck, Brad Paisley, Carly Pearce, Carrie Underwood, Darius Rucker, Ella Langley, Kelsea Ballerini, Kristen Bell, Kristin Chenoweth, Lady A, Lainey Wilson, Little Big Town, Lukas Nelson, Molly Tuttle, Reba McEntire, Ricky Skaggs, Sierra Hull, Vince Gill and Zac Brown will perform, with Amy Grant and Kimberly Williams-Paisley joining as special guests.

A Belmont University student ensemble will also participate in a performance, reflecting the university’s ongoing “Dolly U” programming that seeks to engage students in learning from Parton’s career, artistry and entrepreneurial legacy. 

Additional surprise tributes from friends across the entertainment world will also be featured throughout the special which airs Oct. 21, 9:00-11:00 PM/EDT on ABC and Disney+ in the U.S. and internationally, available next day on Hulu.

The special will be filmed before a live audience at Belmont University’s Fisher Center for the Performing Arts in Nashville. Robert Deaton serves as the project’s executive producer. In conjunction with the special, CMA and Belmont University will make a donation from proceeds to Dolly Parton’s Imagination Library, continuing a legacy that gives millions of children the gift of a free book each month and inspires a lifelong love of reading. In addition, Disney is supporting Imagination Library to further its mission of inspiring children to imagine their possibilities.
