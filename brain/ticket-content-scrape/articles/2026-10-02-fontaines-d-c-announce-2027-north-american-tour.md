# Fontaines D.C. Announce 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Irish rock band Fontaines D.C. announced a 19-date North American tour for 2027 in support of their album "Dopamine Chamber," running in two legs from April 23 (Boston) through October 15 (Inglewood, CA). Presale sign-up runs through October 5, with presale tickets available October 6 and general on-sale October 9.

### Key Points
- 19 dates across two legs: a spring theater/amphitheater run and a fall run including arena dates in Toronto and Inglewood.
- Tour supports the album "Dopamine Chamber."
- General on-sale is October 9; presale access requires signing up by October 5.

Irish rockers Fontaines D.C. will tour North America next year in support of their album *Dopamine Chamber.*

The 19-date run begins April 23 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston and visits mostly theaters and amps, though there are arena plays at Toronto’s Scotiabank Arena and Inglewood, California’s Kia Forum. That Forum show closes the tour — which is in two legs — Oct. 15.

Pre-sale sign-up is open until 6 p.m. Eastern Oct. 5 with pre-sale tickets available from 10 a.m, local time Oct. 6. General on-sale starts 10 a.m. local Oct. 9.

Fontaines D.C. are repped by CAA’s Carly James.
