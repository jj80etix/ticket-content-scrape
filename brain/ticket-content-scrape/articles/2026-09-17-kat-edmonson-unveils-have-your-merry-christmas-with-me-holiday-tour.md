# Kat Edmonson Unveils ‘Have Your Merry Christmas With Me’ Holiday Tour

## Summary

Singer-songwriter Kat Edmonson has announced the "Have Your Merry Christmas With Me Tour," a US holiday run from Nov. 27-Dec. 30 timed to her new holiday album of the same name, arriving Nov. 6.

### Key Points
- Tour runs Nov. 27 (St. Louis) through Dec. 30 (Seattle).
- Includes a three-night stand in Austin, TX.
- Tied to release of new holiday album "Have Your Merry Christmas With Me" (Nov. 6).
- Tickets available via Edmonson's official website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Kat-Edmonson.webp)

Kat Edmonson is taking her holiday music on the road this winter with the newly announced “Have Your Merry Christmas With Me Tour,” a U.S. trek that will take the singer and songwriter to cities across the country beginning November 27.

Edmonson will kick off the tour the day after Thanksgiving with a performance at the Blue Strawberry in St. Louis, Missouri. The tour will continue throughout December, with stops scheduled in Cincinnati, Minneapolis, Boise, Denver, New York City, Los Angeles and Houston, among other markets.

The itinerary also includes a three-night run in Austin, Texas, giving Edmonson multiple opportunities to perform for audiences in the city before the tour moves on to its final dates.

The “Have Your Merry Christmas With Me Tour” will wrap up December 30 at the Triple Door in Seattle, Washington. Tickets for the newly announced dates are available through Edmonson’s official website.

The tour is being announced alongside Edmonson’s upcoming holiday album, *Have Your Merry Christmas With Me*, which is scheduled to arrive November 6. The new record will provide the musical backdrop for the holiday-themed run, featuring Edmonson’s interpretations of songs connected to the Christmas season.
