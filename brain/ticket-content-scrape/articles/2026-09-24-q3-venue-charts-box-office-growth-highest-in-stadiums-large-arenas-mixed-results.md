# Q3 Venue Charts: Box-Office Growth Highest In Stadiums, Large Arenas; Mixed Results In Smaller Rooms

## Summary

Pollstar's Q3 Deep Data Cuts show 2026 box-office results broadly on par with the past two years, but with a clear divide by venue size: stadiums and the largest amphitheaters (30,000+ capacity) posted the strongest growth — average tickets per show up 7.5% and gross averages up 12% year-over-year — while six of eight venue-size categories saw ticket-sales-per-show declines (mostly under 2%, with clubs of 751-1,500 capacity down 9.6%). Average ticket price rose in every venue-size category, the one metric with universal upward movement.

### Highlights
- Over-30,000-capacity venues: +7.5% tickets/show, +12% gross/show YoY — the only category with increases across all metrics in both 2026 and 2025.
- 15,001-30,000 arenas: +1% ticket average (11,842 → 11,965), the only other growing category.
- Steepest decline: 751-1,500-capacity clubs, down 9.6% in tickets/show (720 → 651).
- Average ticket price increased across all eight venue-size categories — the sole metric with universal growth.

### Industry Problem
Smaller and mid-size venues/promoters are seeing softer per-show ticket demand even as average prices climb everywhere, a squeeze that falls hardest on club- and theater-tier operators while stadium-scale shows keep pulling ahead — relevant both to client (venue/promoter) pain points and to patron price sensitivity.

### Proposed Solution
No specific solution is discussed; this is market-structure data. Useful as AI/benchmark context: a primary platform serving small-to-mid venues should watch for demand softness at the sub-2,500-capacity tier and factor rising average prices into patron-facing pricing/communication strategy.
