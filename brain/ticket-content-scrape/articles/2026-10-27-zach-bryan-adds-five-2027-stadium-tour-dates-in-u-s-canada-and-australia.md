# Zach Bryan Adds Five 2027 Stadium Tour Dates in U.S., Canada and Australia

## Summary

Zach Bryan added five stadium dates to his 2027 tour, expanding stops in Chicago, Vancouver, Edmonton, Melbourne and Brisbane, each added adjacent to previously announced shows.

### Key Points
- New Chicago date: Soldier Field, May 7 (ahead of existing May 8 show).
- Canadian additions: Vancouver (BC Place, July 27) and Edmonton (Commonwealth Stadium, July 31).
- Support acts include John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen depending on market.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Zach-Bryan-.webp)

Zach Bryan has added five stadium concerts to his 2027 Young Manhood And Other Donatable Organs Tour, expanding stops in Chicago, Vancouver, Edmonton, Melbourne and Brisbane.

In Chicago, Bryan will perform at Soldier Field on May 7, one night before his previously scheduled May 8 concert. John Fogerty and Fey Fili will open both performances.

The Canadian expansion includes a July 27 show at BC Place in Vancouver and a July 31 performance at Commonwealth Stadium in Edmonton, Alberta. Each addition falls one day before the existing July 28 concert in Vancouver and August 1 in Edmonton.

Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen will join Bryan for the Canadian shows, along with Gabriella Rose.

The Australian changes extend two stadium engagements to three nights apiece. Bryan has added December 2 at Melbourne’s Marvel Stadium, ahead of the previously announced December 3 and December 4 concerts.

Brisbane’s new performance is scheduled for December 19 at Suncorp Stadium. It follows the December 17 and December 18 shows, creating a run of three consecutive nights at the venue.

Vance Joy and Emma Ogier will open the Australian concerts.

The Young Manhood And Other Donatable Organs Tour is scheduled to begin April 28 in Minneapolis. Bryan will play stadiums across North America through August before heading to Australia for the December portion of the tour.

Bryan’s continuing stadium schedule follows his 2026 *With Heaven On Tour*, which included performances across North America and Europe. Earlier this month, he [set a Gillette Stadium attendance record](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/zach-bryan-breaks-gillette-stadium-attendance-record/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) with 73,538 attendees at his October 3 concert in Foxborough, Massachusetts.

Tickets for the newly announced performances went on sale October 23. Ticketing providers vary by venue, with AXS handling Chicago, Ticketmaster handling the Canadian additions, and Australian ticketing information available through Frontier Touring.

Fans can find event-specific purchasing information and the complete updated itinerary through [Bryan’s official website](https://zachbryan.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
