# ‘Buena Vista Social Club’ to End Broadway Run in January

## Summary

"Buena Vista Social Club" will end its Broadway run on January 17, 2027, closing at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre after 27 previews and 767 regular performances since its 2025 opening. The production continues beyond Broadway via a West End opening and touring engagements in Mexico and Puerto Rico.

### Key Points
- Final Broadway performance: January 17, 2027, at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre.
- Run totals 27 previews and 767 regular performances since its 2025 opening.
- Moves to a West End opening later in 2027, plus existing Mexico and Puerto Rico engagements.
- This weekend the show runs simultaneously in four cities: Broadway, Dallas, San Juan, and Mexico City.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Buena-Vista-Social-Club.webp)

“Buena Vista Social Club” will play its final Broadway performance on January 17, 2027. The musical will conclude its engagement at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre ahead of a planned West End opening later that year.

The production opened on Broadway in 2025 and is expected to finish with 27 previews and 767 regular performances. Its closing comes as the musical expands through a tour and engagements in Mexico and Puerto Rico.

This weekend, the musical will be performed simultaneously in four cities. Productions will take the stage on Broadway, at the Music Hall at Fair Park in Dallas, at the Antonio Paoli Festival Hall at Puerto Rico’s Centro de Bellas Artes and at the Teatro de los Insurgentes in Mexico City.

Lead producer Orin Wolf addressed the closing and the musical’s expansion in a statement.

“I am deeply grateful for the time we’ve had on Broadway—and the speed with which the team has created three more productions,” Wolf said. “We are thrilled the Broadway production is on schedule to recoup as we look ahead to an exciting tour, special engagements in Mexico and Puerto Rico, the film, and so much more.”

“Buena Vista Social Club” features a book by Marco Ramirez and direction by Saheem Ali. Inspired by the Grammy-winning 1997 album, the musical follows the Cuban performers behind the recording and incorporates their music into its account of their lives.

The show received 11 Tony Award nominations and won five competitive awards. Patricia Delgado and Justin Peck earned the choreography award, while the production’s band received a separate honorary Tony. The cast recording also won a Grammy Award.

Two original cast members will return during the final months of the Broadway run. Ashley De La Rosa will resume performances as Young Haydee on November 16. Tony winner Natalie Venetia Belcon will return as Omara on November 30.

Gina Torres currently portrays Omara, and Tanairi Sade Vazquez plays Young Haydee. The principal cast also includes Rick Negron as Compay, Mel Semé as Ibrahim, Jainardo Batista Sterling as Rubén and Isa Antonetti as Young Omara.

Da’Von T. Moody plays Young Compay, Wesley Wray portrays Young Ibrahim and Jesús Pupo appears as Young Rubén. Renesito Avich plays Eliades, Chris Myers portrays Juan de Marcos and José “Candelita” Iglesias appears as The Narrator.

Music supervision is by Dean Sharenow, with orchestrations, arrangements and music direction by Marco Paguia. The design team includes Arnulfo Maldonado, Dede Ayite, Tyler Micoleau, Jonathan Deans and J. Jared Janas.
