## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ACDC-PWR-UP-Tour-Foo-Fighters.webp)

Foo Fighters will join AC/DC as special guests for the band’s September 8 concert at The Dome at America’s Center in St. Louis.

The one-night-only appearance comes after AC/DC’s scheduled support act, The Pretty Reckless, became unavailable for the St. Louis stop. Foo Fighters will step in for the performance, bringing together two Rock & Roll Hall of Fame acts for a special double bill.

AC/DC confirmed the news in a statement sharing, “Foo Fighters are taking a special one-off detour from their Take Cover Global Stadium Tour to fill in for their friends The Pretty Reckless as special guests of AC/DC for one night only in St. Louis. The performance brings two generations of rock’s biggest live acts together for a one-night-only event, with the Foo Fighters opening for the band’s St. Louis tour stop.”

Foo Fighters’ addition to the lineup comes during a break in the band’s own North American stadium schedule. The group is set to complete the first portion of its summer tour with a performance at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles on August 22. Foo Fighters will then resume the tour September 12 in Fargo, North Dakota, with Queens of the Stone Age supporting on most dates.

For Foo Fighters frontman Dave Grohl, the opportunity to share a stage with AC/DC carries personal significance. In a lengthy statement, Grohl recalled seeing AC/DC’s 1980 concert film “Let There Be Rock” when he was 11 years old and described the experience as a formative moment in his understanding of live rock music.

Grohl said in part that the film introduced him to the “raw, primal power” of rock and roll performance and helped establish the standard he would later bring to his own performances. He also credited AC/DC with influencing his approach to playing music and performing onstage.

The Foo Fighters frontman went on to describe the St. Louis performance as a longtime dream, saying his band will finally share a stage with the musicians who helped inspire his determination to give audiences everything he has during a live show.
