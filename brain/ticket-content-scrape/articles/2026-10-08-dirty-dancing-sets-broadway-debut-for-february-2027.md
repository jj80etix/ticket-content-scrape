# ‘Dirty Dancing’ Sets Broadway Debut for February 2027

## Summary

"Dirty Dancing: The Musical" will make its Broadway debut at the Lena Horne Theatre for a limited run from February 12 to September 5, 2027, timed to the film's 40th anniversary, following its North American tour. Touring leads Annalese Starzec and Blake Zelesnikar will reprise their roles and make their Broadway debuts.

### Key Points
- Broadway engagement runs Feb. 12–Sept. 5, 2027, with a gala celebration March 14.
- 27-member company includes 21 Broadway debuts; Starzec and Zelesnikar lead as Baby Houseman and Johnny Castle.
- Supporting cast includes Olivia Cece, Bryan Fenkart, Dan Klimko, Lily Kren, and others.
- Production resumes touring after the Broadway engagement concludes.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Dirty-Dancing-Broadway.webp)

“Dirty Dancing: The Musical” will make its Broadway debut in 2027, bringing the stage adaptation of the 1987 film to the Lena Horne Theatre for a limited engagement celebrating the movie’s 40th anniversary.

Performances begin February 12 and continue through September 5, with a special gala celebration scheduled for March 14. The production will arrive in New York following its North American tour and resume touring after the Broadway engagement.

Annalese Starzec will play Frances “Baby” Houseman opposite Blake Zelesnikar as Johnny Castle. Both performers, who currently lead the touring production, will make their Broadway debuts.

The 27-member company includes 21 performers making their Broadway debuts. Supporting cast members include Olivia Cece as Penny Johnson, Bryan Fenkart as Jake Houseman, Dan Klimko as Neil Kellerman and Lily Kren as Lisa Houseman. Gina Lamparella plays Marjorie Houseman, with Loren Lester as Max Kellerman, Ethan Peterson as Billy Kostecki, Erick Pinnick as Tito Suarez and Ashley LaLonde as Elizabeth.

Lonny Price directs the production, returning to a story he helped bring to the screen as Neil Kellerman in the original movie. Darrell Grand Moultrie provides choreography, while original screenwriter Eleanor Bergstein wrote the stage adaptation.

The Broadway engagement follows a touring launch in Saint Paul, Minnesota, in August. The fall itinerary includes Detroit, West Palm Beach, Boston and Toronto before the production moves to New York.

The creative team includes set designer Brett J. Banakis, costume designer Emily Rebholz, lighting designer Japhy Weideman and sound designer Kai Harada. Joseph Joubert handles music supervision and arrangements, with orchestrations by Charlie Rosen and dance arrangements by Michael Thurber.
