# Wisin Bringing ‘Universidad del Perreo’ To NY For One Night Only

## Summary

Reggaeton star Wisin will bring his "Universidad del Perreo" show to New York's United Palace in December, his only U.S. headline date, after sold-out Puerto Rico shows.

### Key Points
- Artist presale registration opens Nov. 4 at 10 a.m.; promoter presale (code CMNFAN) starts Nov. 4 at 1 p.m.; general onsale via Ticketmaster Nov. 5 at 10 a.m.
- Show is promoted by Cárdenas Marketing Network.
- Follows two sold-out nights at El Coliseo de Puerto Rico (Nov. 13-14) tied to his new album release.

## Transcript

![Wisin UDP United Palace NY](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Wisin_UDP_United-Palace-NY.jpeg)

Wisin UDP United Palace NY

Reggaeton legend Wisin is bringing his “Universidad del Perreo” to New York City this December.

“La Universidad del Perreo” shows in Puerto Rico recently sold out in just an hour, and the lone U.S. headline show, which is promoted by Cárdenas Marketing Network, this year will be hold at New York’s iconic United Palace.

Tickets for the one-night-only show go on sale Nov. 4 at 10 a.m. local time via an artist presale, and fans can sign up to register for that at [seated.com](https://app.seated.com/artists/e4d582c9-5429-4759-8942-2be319dff5f7?utm_source=chatgpt.com). The promoter presale begins Nov. 4 at 1 p.m. with the code CMNFAN, and the general onsale starts Nov. 5 at 10 a.m. at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/0000655C1286C99D).

Wisin’s “Universidad Del Perreo” celebrates not only his catalog but also the urban culture and reggaeton genre he helped build. The Puerto Rican star released his latest studio album, *La Universidad del Perreo*, in September and sold out two nights at El Coliseo de Puerto Rico. Those shows will take place Nov. 13 and 14.

The Latin music star is booked by WME and managed by Chris Duque of D27 Entertainment.
