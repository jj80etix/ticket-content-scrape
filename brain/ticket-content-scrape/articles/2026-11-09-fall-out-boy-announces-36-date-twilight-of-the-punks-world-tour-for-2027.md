# Fall Out Boy Announces 36-Date ‘Twilight of the Punks’ World Tour for 2027

## Summary

TicketNews' coverage of Fall Out Boy's newly announced 2027 "Twilight of the Punks" world tour — 36 dates across Europe, the UK and North America, opening at Prague's O2 Arena and including a June 5 stop at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. Duplicate coverage of the same announcement reported via Pollstar (see companion note).

### Key Points
- 36-date tour: 15-date Europe/UK leg (May-June) then North America from July.
- European leg runs Prague → Budapest, Milan, Madrid, Lyon, Brussels, Mönchengladbach → Glasgow, Dublin, Manchester → Amsterdam, Paris, Berlin, closing June 13 in Łódź, Poland.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/fallout-boy-twilight-of-the-punks.webp)

Fall Out Boy has announced its “Twilight of the Punks” world tour for 2027. The 36-date outing will bring the band to arenas and stadiums across Europe, the United Kingdom and North America.

The first leg consists of 15 European and UK performances, followed by a North American run beginning in July. Prague’s O2 Arena will host the opening concert. From there, the European itinerary includes Budapest, Milan, Madrid, Lyon, Brussels and Mönchengladbach before the band travels to Glasgow, Dublin and Manchester.

A June 5 concert at London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium. The London appearance is part of a route that combines stadium performances with arena bookings, bringing the band to several major European concert markets during the opening weeks.

Following those stops, the itinerary includes Amsterdam, Paris and Berlin. The European and UK portion will conclude June 13 at Atlas Arena in Łódź, Poland, completing nearly a month of performances before the tour moves across the Atlantic.
