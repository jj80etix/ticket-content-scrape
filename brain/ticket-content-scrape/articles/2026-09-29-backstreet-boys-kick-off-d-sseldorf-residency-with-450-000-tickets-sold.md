# Backstreet Boys kick off Düsseldorf residency with 450,000 tickets sold

## Summary

Backstreet Boys kicked off a ten-night residency ("Into The Millennium – Homecoming: Live in Germany") at Düsseldorf's Merkur Spiel-Arena, promoted by Live Nation and expected to draw 450,000 fans through its October 7 close, framed as a homecoming to the country that gave the band its first platinum record.

### Key Points
- Ten shows at Merkur Spiel-Arena, Düsseldorf, running through Oct. 7, 2026; ~450,000 attendees expected.
- Follows the band's Las Vegas Sphere "Into The Millennium" run (750,000 fans across 47 shows so far; 5 more dates planned around New Year).
- Continues a trend of extended pop-act residencies (Adele, Harry Styles, Shakira).
- Venue chosen partly for its retractable roof, allowing indoor/outdoor flexibility across the ten dates.

## Transcript

The ten-night engagement is strategised to capitalise on what Live Nation called the band's 'deep roots' in Germany

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/09/BSB-4-scaled.jpg.webp)

The Backstreet Boys in Düsseldorf © Justin Segura

Backstreet Boys have kicked off a ten-night residency at Merkur Spiel-Arena in Düsseldorf, which is due to welcome 450,000 fans over by the end of its run on 7 October.

The event is dubbed the *Into The Millennium- Homecoming: Live in Germany,* with promoter Live Nation saying the performances “mark a special return to the country that gave them their first-ever platinum record with *I’ll Never Break Your Heart*, a country they called home 30 years ago.”

The Düsseldorf concert series follows the band’s *Into The Millennium* Sphere show in Las Vegas, that has seen 750,00 fans attend 47 performances so far. Five further performances will follow at the Las Vegas venue across the New Year.

“When we talk about this being a homecoming, it’s because Germany is where everything clicked for us” band member Brian Littrell said, adding that the country is “where our dream stopped being hypothetical and became something real. Coming back for these shows feels full circle”.

> “German fans were among the very first to pack venues”

With the ten-night stand, the band is following a trend of extended residencies seen from top pop acts in recent years, which has included Adele, Harry Styles, and most recently, Shakira.

Speaking previously of the residency strategy for Backstreet Boys, Live Nation GSA president and managing directorAndre Lieberberg said: “Apart from pure economics, it is the sociocultural meaning of fans travelling to a destination and underlining the importance of cultural happenings, both off- and online, that fits into the growing demand.”

He added the Merkur Spiel-Arena was chosen because of “the fact that we could play the stadium ten times in the autumn, with the option of closing the roof and turning it into an indoor venue and experience”, which he said made it “a unique destination to see the band in their prime”.

“German fans were among the very first to pack venues, call radio stations, and wait outside hotels, giving the group an early glimpse of what their career would become,” promoter Live Nation said as the residency began, adding: “That same loyalty that filled clubs in the mid-90s is now filling stadiums. The response to the Düsseldorf dates underscores just how deeply the Backstreet Boys’ roots in Germany still run and why this return feels especially meaningful.”
