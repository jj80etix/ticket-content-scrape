# Athletic Brewing Scores MetLife Stadium Deal With Giants, Jets

## Summary

Non-alcoholic brewer Athletic Brewing signed a multi-year sponsorship deal with MetLife Stadium, the New York Giants, and the New York Jets, becoming the venue's and both teams' first dedicated non-alcoholic beer partner. The deal puts Athletic's Run Wild IPA and Upside Dawn Golden in stadium concessions alongside venue-wide branding (video boards, signage, kiosks) and team-branded retail packaging. It extends Athletic's live-event footprint, which the company says now spans more than 200 venues nationwide.

### Key Points
- Multi-year deal names Athletic Brewing the "Proud Non-Alcoholic Beer Partner" of MetLife Stadium, the Giants, and the Jets.
- Products sold in 19.2-ounce cans; branding appears on video boards, concourse displays, LED signage, pylons, menus, and kiosks.
- Team-branded 12-packs (Giants and Jets logos) will be sold across the Tri-State area later this year.
- Sponsorship extends to broadcast, digital, and social channels plus training camps and community events.
- Athletic frames the deal as part of a broader push into venue beverage menus as non-alcoholic demand grows; products are now at 200+ venues.

Athletic Brewing is stepping deeper into the NFL, striking a multi-year partnership with MetLife Stadium, the New York Giants and the New York Jets.

The deal makes Athletic the Proud Non-Alcoholic Beer Partner of the stadium and both teams, marking the company’s first formal sponsorship involving two NFL franchises and a professional football venue. It is also the first time MetLife Stadium, the Giants and the Jets have partnered with a dedicated non-alcoholic brewer in this capacity.

Under the agreement, Athletic’s Run Wild IPA and Upside Dawn Golden will be sold in 19.2-ounce cans throughout MetLife Stadium, giving fans another alcohol-free option on game days and during other live events.

The brand will also receive a major presence across the venue, including video boards, concourse displays, LED signage, pylons, menus, retail kiosks and digital highway signage. Product sampling and interactive fan activations are also planned for select game days.

“We’re thrilled to team up with MetLife Stadium, the New York Giants, and the New York Jets to bring Athletic’s great-tasting brews to one of the most exciting venues in sports and entertainment,” Athletic Brewing co-founder and CEO Bill Shufelt said. “This is an exciting step forward in our mission to revolutionize the way the world drinks and make modern moderation more accessible. We can’t wait for Athletic to become the game day ritual fans count on, from the tailgate to the final whistle.”

The sponsorship will extend beyond the stadium, with Athletic appearing across Giants and Jets television, radio, digital and social channels, as well as at training camps, community initiatives and select events. Athletic is also planning team-branded packaging later this year. Run Wild IPA 12-packs featuring the Giants logo and Upside Dawn Golden packs carrying Jets branding will be sold across the Tri-State area.

“Delivering an exceptional experience for every guest is our highest priority at MetLife Stadium,” MetLife Stadium president and CEO Ron VanDeVeen said.

“As the live event experience continues to evolve, we are committed to offering amenities that reflect the preferences of all guests. Partnering with a non-alcoholic brewing pioneer like Athletic allows us to broaden our beverage offerings, provide greater choice, and further shape the experience for all guests who visit MetLife Stadium throughout the year.”
