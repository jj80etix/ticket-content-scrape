# Hamburg Music rolls out event marketing fund to boost ‘international visibility’

## Summary

Hamburg Music, Germany's largest regional music-industry association, launched a marketing-funding program granting up to €1,500 (covering up to 80% of campaign costs) to small and mid-sized local companies running campaigns aimed at drawing visitors from outside the region. Applications are open through December 6, with funded campaigns due by February 14, 2027.

### Key Points
- Grants up to €1,500 per company, covering up to 80% of a campaign's net cost; companies must self-fund the remaining 20%+.
- Open to Hamburg-registered SMEs and freelancers; Hamburg Music membership is not required.
- Funded by the City of Hamburg's Authority for Culture and Media; application window closes December 6, 2026.
- Framed as part of a broader trend of governments using live-music activity to boost tourism, following the recently signed U.S. Music Tourism Act.

Hamburg's Barclays Arena

Hamburg Music has announced a marketing funding initiative to increase the “international visibility” of the city’s live music scene, granting up to €1,500 to small and mid-sized companies.

The German city’s industry association says it is looking to strengthen Hamburg’s position as a music and travel destination, with the initiative’s core aim drawing visitors from both the region and abroad.

Campaigns can be around music events, festivals, venues, and other forms of music offerings and must “contribute to attracting visitors from outside the region”, the trade body says.

“Hamburg Music supports Hamburg music companies in implementing advertising campaigns designed to draw the attention of people outside the Hamburg metropolitan region to music offerings in the city and encourage them to visit,” they say.

The subsidy will cover up to 80% of a campaign’s net costs, up to €1,500 per company, with 20% minimum to come from the company itself for any campaign.

Small and mid-sized enterprises, including freelancers, with an office or brand registered in Hamburg are eligible to apply, with membership in Hamburg Music not a requirement.

Applications are open until 6 December, with funded campaigns to be completed by 14 February, though advertised events can take place at a later date.

The program is funded by the Free and Hanseatic City of Hamburg’s Authority for Culture and Media, the central government agency for cultural policy.

Additional information and forms can be found [here](https://www.musikwirtschaft.org/de/projekte/hamburg-music-marketing-support/).

Hamburg Music is Germany’s first regional music industry association and is one of the largest in Europe, currently comprising around 160 member companies. This includes ass concerts & promotion GmbH, Barclays Arena, FKP Scorpio Konzertproduktion GmbH, and Karsten Jahnke Konzertdirektion GmbH.

Governments are increasingly turning to live music activity to strengthen their tourism draw. The US recently signed its [Music Tourism Act](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/us-live-sector-thrilled-as-music-tourism-act-is-signed-into-law/) into law, and Singapore has also invested in [attracting A-list shows,](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/02/singapore-sought-exclusivity-over-swift-gigs/) directly impacting the island’s [tourism figures](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/02/singapore-mega-concerts-record-tourism/) – two examples of the trend.
