# Lexi Jayde Announces ‘The Lover Girl Tour’

## Summary

Pop singer-songwriter Lexi Jayde announced her first headline tour in four years, supporting debut album LOVER GIRL (released Aug. 21 via Columbia Records), kicking off Oct. 18 in Philadelphia with stops including New York, Chicago, and Los Angeles.

### Key Points
- First headline tour in four years, behind debut album LOVER GIRL (Columbia Records, Aug. 21).
- Runs Oct. 18 (Philadelphia, World Stage) through Nov. 3 (Los Angeles, El Rey).
- Tickets and VIP packages on sale now; booked by WME.

## Transcript

![LexiJayde](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/LexiJayde.png)

After previously touring with artists including FINNEAS and Griff, pop singer/songwriter Lexi Jayde is ready to hit the road for her first headline run in four years.

The Los Angeles-born artist, who broke out with the viral hit “drunk text me,” is supporting her debut album, LOVER GIRL, which was released Aug. 21 via Columbia Records. The LP includes the tracks “you’re still mine,” “bubblebath,” “i’ve missed you longer,” and “misunderstood.”

LOVER GIRL is described as a diary of the last four years of her life, featuring “the unfiltered candor of a soul-baring confession and larger-than-life drama of an iconic rom-com.”

The fall tour starts with an Oct. 18 show at Philadelphia’s World Stage. Stops include New York’s Bowery Ballroom, Chicago’s Chop Shop, Los Angeles’ El Rey and more.  
  
Jayde’s tour dates are booked by WME. Check out the routing now.  
  
Tickets, as well as VIP packages, are on sale now.
