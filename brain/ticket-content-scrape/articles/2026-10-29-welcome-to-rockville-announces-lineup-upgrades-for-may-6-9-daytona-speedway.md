# Welcome To Rockville Announces Lineup, Upgrades For May 6-9 Daytona Speedway

## Summary

Danny Wimmer Presents unveiled the 16th-year Welcome To Rockville lineup — Korn, Bad Omens, Pierce The Veil and Blink-182 headlining May 6-9, 2027 at Daytona International Speedway. The festival is adding comfort and VIP upgrades alongside its usual five-stage, 150+ artist bill.

### Key Points
- 16th edition, May 6-9, 2027, Daytona International Speedway; billed as Florida's largest rock/metal/punk festival
- Headliners: Korn (Thu), Bad Omens (Fri), Pierce The Veil (Sat), Blink-182 (Sun), plus 150+ acts across five stages
- New comfort additions: three tented stages, covered bleachers, extra seating/umbrellas, new rides
- New VIP tier adds premium viewing, permanent restrooms, exclusive food/beverage and first-time Fan Zone infield access

The 16th year of Welcome To Rockville, billed as Florida’s largest rock, metal and punk festival, will include more than 150 artists, topped by Korn, Bad Omens, Pierce The Veil, Blink-182 May 6-9 at Daytona International Speedway.

Promising more bands, more shade and more space, Welcome To Rockville will also feature four packed days of music on five stages, with everyone from Alice In Chains to Lil Jon, The Offspring to Sugar Ray to Judas Priest, The Prodigy and Mac Sabbath.

Along with the vast array of music on display over the weekend, this year’s event will have three tented stages, allowing more shade and space, along with other comforts on the grounds including covered bleachers, additional seating and umbrellas, new amusement rides and other shady spots.

Also new are VIP offerings, allowing premium viewing, permanent restrooms, exclusive food and beverage options and an all new VIP lounge experience, including first-time access to The Fan Zone infield entertainment area at the speedway.

“Welcome To Rockville has grown into something bigger than we ever imagined, bringing rock

fans from around the world together in Daytona Beach,” said Chamie McCurry, General

Manager of Danny Wimmer Presents, which produces the event. “Every year, we push ourselves to raise the bar, both with a lineup that celebrates the genre in its entirety and with the experience we create for fans. In 2027, that means more tented stages, additional seating areas, a brand-new entrance, and an expanded, relocated VIP experience. We’re investing in a more comfortable, immersive festival without losing the energy that makes Rockville special. I can’t wait for fans to experience it.”

Other notable musical performances include a rare southeast U.S. show from Faith No More, a global exclusive Marilyn Manson *Antichrist Superstar* performance, U.S. exclusive Machine Head performance, as well as reunions from Circa Survive, Forever the Sickest Kids, Palisades, Cute Is What We Aim For, and Relient K. While the event is decidedly and dedicatedly rock, metal and punk, some standouts this year include rap hit-maker Lil Jon, surf-pop ‘90s stars Sugar Ray, heavy techno band The Prodigy, and a special Wednesday pre=party headliner by Ukrainian metalcore band Jinjer.

Welcome To Rockville passes, including single day, two-day, four-day, and VIP options, are on sale now. Danny Wimmer Presents contributes a portion of every pass sale to the DWP Foundation, supporting local nonprofits and charitable initiatives in the communities that host DWP festivals.
