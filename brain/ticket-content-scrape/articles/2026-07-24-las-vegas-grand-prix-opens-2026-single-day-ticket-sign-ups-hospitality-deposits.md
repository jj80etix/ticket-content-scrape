# Las Vegas Grand Prix Opens 2026 Single-Day Ticket Sign-Ups, Hospitality Deposits

## Summary

The Las Vegas Grand Prix has opened interest sign-ups for single-day general admission and grandstand tickets for the 2026 race (Nov. 19-21), along with $250 deposits for a new slate of single-day hospitality packages. The move gives Formula 1 fans a lower-commitment way to attend without buying a full weekend package, expanding several premium hospitality tiers to one-day availability for the first time.

### Key Points
- Single-day GA and grandstand ticket interest lists are now open; pricing and on-sale dates will be announced later.
- New single-day hospitality options include Wynn Grid Club, Trackside Tavern, Skybox with Heineken Silver Main Grandstand, Turn 3 Club, and Club Paris.
- A non-refundable $250 deposit secures priority access to single-day hospitality purchases and is credited toward the final price.
- Fans who placed a deposit during the earlier 2026 window keep their priority without re-depositing.
- The race weekend returns to the Las Vegas Strip Circuit Nov. 19-21, 2026.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/formula-1-1.jpg)

Formula 1 fans looking to experience the Las Vegas Grand Prix without committing to a full weekend now have more options for 2026.

The Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix has opened interest sign-ups for single-day general admission and grandstand tickets, along with $250 deposits for select one-day hospitality packages. The race weekend returns to the Las Vegas Strip Circuit from Nov. 19-21, 2026.

For the first time, several premium hospitality experiences will be offered as single-day options, giving fans access to upscale amenities and prime track views for one day of the event.

Eligible hospitality offerings include the Wynn Grid Club, Trackside Tavern at Paddock Club Rooftop, Skybox with Heineken Silver Main Grandstand, Turn 3 Club and Club Paris.
