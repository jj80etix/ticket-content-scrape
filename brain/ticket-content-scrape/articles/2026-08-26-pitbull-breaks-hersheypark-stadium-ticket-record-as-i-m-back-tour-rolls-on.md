# Pitbull Breaks Hersheypark Stadium Ticket Record as ‘I’m Back’ Tour Rolls On

## Summary

Pitbull's "I'm Back" tour with Lil Jon set a new ticket record at Hersheypark Stadium during a busy 2026 summer concert season there. The North American leg continues through late September before Pitbull heads to Europe for arena shows in November and December; he's also announced a 14th studio album, "Pitcoin," for October.

### Key Points
- Pitbull's Hersheypark Stadium show set a new ticket record for the venue.
- Hersheypark's 2026 season also hosted Guns N' Roses, Mumford & Sons, Kesha, and others.
- North American dates run through late September; European arena dates follow in Nov/Dec.
- New album "Pitcoin" expected in October 2026.

Pitbull added another box-office milestone to his “I’m Back” Tour over the weekend, with his Aug. 22 concert setting a new Hersheypark Stadium record for total tickets distributed.

The Hershey, Pennsylvania, venue confirmed the record to [ABC27](https://www.abc27.com/hershey/pitbull-concert-breaks-hersheypark-stadium-record-for-tickets-sold/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) following Saturday’s performance, which featured Lil Jon as special guest.

An exact ticket count and revenue figure for the concert have not yet been announced. Pitbull nevertheless characterized the date as another sellout in a statement provided through Live Nation.

“The love and support of the Bald-E’s and fans are record-breaking and record making,” Pitbull said. “It’s an honor, another sold-out show! Let’s keep going, Dale!”

The distinction between the ticket-distribution record and the stadium’s gross record is worth noting. ABC27 reported that Lady Gaga’s August 2022 stop on her Chromatica Ball Tour previously established the venue’s record for its highest-grossing concert. With financial results for Pitbull’s Hershey show still pending, it is not yet clear whether that revenue mark was also surpassed.

The Hershey record adds to what has already been a strong touring year for Pitbull.

Pollstar reported in July that the first 13 U.S. concerts on the “I’m Back” Tour generated $25 million in gross revenue from 234,238 tickets sold. The opening West Palm Beach show drew a sellout crowd of 17,206, while a June 7 performance at Shoreline Amphitheatre sold 20,112 tickets. The Hollywood Bowl produced the largest reported single-show gross from that group, generating $3.16 million from 17,380 tickets sold.

Produced by Live Nation, the North American portion of the tour has paired Pitbull with Lil Jon and included a mix of major amphitheaters and outdoor venues across the United States and Canada. [TicketNews covered the original “I’m Back” Tour announcement in January](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/pitbull-announces-im-back-tour-with-special-guest-lil-jon/?utm_source=chatgpt.com).

The Hershey appearance also came during a busy summer concert season at Hersheypark Stadium. The venue’s 2026 calendar has included Guns N’ Roses, Mumford & Sons, Kesha, Tyler Childers and Chris Stapleton, among other major touring acts, with Iron Maiden scheduled to close out the stadium’s summer concert slate on Sept. 15.

Pitbull, meanwhile, still has months of touring ahead. The remaining North American dates run through late September before “Mr. Worldwide” heads back overseas for another stretch of European arena shows in November and December. Pitbull has also announced his 14th studio album, *Pitcoin*, which is expected to arrive in October.
