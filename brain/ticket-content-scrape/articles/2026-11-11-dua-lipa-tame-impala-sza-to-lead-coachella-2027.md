# Dua Lipa, Tame Impala & SZA to lead Coachella 2027

## Summary

Dua Lipa, Tame Impala and SZA were confirmed as 2027 Coachella headliners, with Daddy Yankee lined up for a special performance. The Goldenvoice-promoted festival returns to Indio's Empire Polo Club across two weekends, April 9–11 and 16–18, 2027.

### Key Points
- Lipa headlines Friday, Tame Impala Saturday, SZA Sunday across both weekends
- 26th edition of the 125,000-capacity festival
