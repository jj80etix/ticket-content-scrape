# Manhunt underway after one killed, five hurt in Swiss rave shooting

## Summary

A shooting at a techno rave in Aarau, Switzerland killed one attendee and injured five others over the weekend of August 29-30; a manhunt for the shooter was underway as of this report, with police citing no leads on the perpetrator's identity or motive.

### Key Points
- One person killed (a 22-year-old Italian woman) and five injured, aged 24-27, all Swiss residents.
- Occurred less than 40km from the German border; police had no leads on suspects or motive at time of writing.
