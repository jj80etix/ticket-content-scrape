# Dropkick Murphys Announce 2027 North American St. Patrick’s Day Tour

## Summary

Dropkick Murphys announced 27 dates for their 2027 St. Patrick's Day tour across the U.S. and Canada (Feb. 5 Albuquerque – Mar. 11 Portland, Maine), with Home Front and Spiritual Cramp joining for most stops; separate Boston hometown St. Patrick's Day shows will be announced later.

### Key Points
- Route runs through Arizona, California, then Canada (Vancouver, Penticton, Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon, Winnipeg — two Winnipeg shows), back into the U.S. Midwest, then a second Canadian leg (London/Toronto/Ottawa/Quebec City/Montreal) before closing in the Northeast.
- One show (March 10, Springfield, MA) is a combined hockey-game-plus-concert ticket with the Springfield Thunderbirds.
- Follows 2025 album *For the People* and the band's split release *New England Forever* with Haywire.
- General on-sale Oct. 2 at 10 a.m. local; presale begins Sept. 30 at 10 a.m. local.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Dropkick-Murphys.webp)

Dropkick Murphys will take their annual St. Patrick’s Day tour across the United States and Canada in early 2027, with Home Front and Spiritual Cramp joining the band for most of the run.

The 27 announced dates begin February 5 in Albuquerque, New Mexico, and continue through March 11 in Portland, Maine. Boston hometown shows will be announced separately.

The opening stretch heads through Arizona and California before crossing into Canada for shows in Vancouver, Penticton, Calgary, Edmonton, Saskatoon and Winnipeg. The band then returns to the U.S. for stops including Green Bay, Wisconsin, and Detroit. A second Canadian leg includes London and Toronto, Ontario, along with Ottawa, Quebec City and Montreal. The final announced dates include Brooklyn, New York, Wilmington, Delaware, and Portland. Winnipeg and Quebec City each have two shows scheduled.

One date has a different format. Dropkick Murphys will perform after the Springfield Thunderbirds hockey game on March 10 in Springfield, Massachusetts. Admission to the concert requires a combination ticket covering both the game and the performance. Home Front and Spiritual Cramp are not scheduled for the Springfield show.
