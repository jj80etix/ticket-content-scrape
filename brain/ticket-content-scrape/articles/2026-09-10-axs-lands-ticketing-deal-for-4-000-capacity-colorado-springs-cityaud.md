# AXS Lands Ticketing Deal for 4,000-Capacity Colorado Springs CityAud

## Summary

AXS will become the ticketing partner for Colorado Springs' historic City Auditorium, now rebranded CityAud, as it undergoes a redevelopment led by promoter AVA Presents ahead of an early-2027 reopening. The deal brings AXS' primary ticketing, mobile ticketing, fan account tools, and official resale to the roughly 4,000-capacity venue.

### Key Points
- AVA Presents (Jesse Morreale) was selected by the City of Colorado Springs in 2025 to renovate and operate the century-old venue.
- AXS supplies primary ticketing, mobile ticketing, marketing/data tools, and AXS Official Resale.
- Redevelopment centers on a 4,000+ cap Auditorium Arena plus a separate 230-seat room.
- Reopening targeted for early 2027; venue previously hosted Hendrix, Willie Nelson, Van Halen, and others.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/AXS-City-Aud.webp)

AXS will serve as the official ticketing partner for Colorado Springs’ historic City Auditorium as the century-old venue undergoes a major redevelopment ahead of a planned reopening in early 2027.

The agreement with AVA Presents will bring AXS’ primary ticketing platform, mobile ticketing, fan account tools, marketing and data capabilities and AXS Official Resale to the newly branded CityAud.

The deal gives AXS another venue foothold in Colorado while adding a key operating partner for a redevelopment intended to return the long-dormant City Auditorium to the regional touring circuit.

AVA Presents, led by Colorado promoter and venue developer Jesse Morreale, was selected by the City of Colorado Springs in 2025 to manage, renovate and operate the city-owned building. The auditorium originally opened in 1923 and has hosted performers including Jimi Hendrix, Willie Nelson, Van Halen, Tina Turner, Ozzy Osbourne and Foo Fighters.

“CityAud has always been more than a building — it has been a place where Colorado Springs comes together around live music and culture,” Morreale said. “We’re building its next chapter to meet the needs of great artists, deliver an exceptional experience for fans, and restore its place as an essential stop for live entertainment in Colorado.”

Morreale said AXS’ experience working with venues, artists and fans made the company a natural fit for the project.

The redevelopment is centered on a more than 4,000-capacity Auditorium Arena with a rebuilt main stage, upgraded production and loading infrastructure and expanded backstage facilities. The property will also retain a separate 230-seat Auditorium Theater, allowing the complex to accommodate smaller performances and community programming alongside touring concerts.

Recent reporting from Colorado Public Radio said AVA Presents is investing about $3.5 million in the initial renovation, including work needed to bring the building up to contemporary fire, safety and accessibility standards. AVA has an initial five-year lease with options that could extend its tenure to 25 years.

The venue fills a potentially useful spot in the Colorado touring ecosystem. Morreale previously told Pollstar that Colorado Springs lacks comparable flexible venues in the roughly 1,200- to 4,000-capacity range, describing the city as increasingly capable of functioning as its own touring market rather than simply an extension of Denver.

“CityAud has an extraordinary history, and what Jesse and his team are creating is about honoring that history while building a venue for the next generation of artists and fans,” AXS Head of North American Venues Dean DeWulf said. “Colorado is an incredibly important live entertainment market, and we’re excited to partner with AVA Presents to give CityAud the ticketing technology and tools to support its growth from day one.”

AXS’ role will extend from ticket discovery and account management through event entry, while its inclusion of AXS Official Resale means the same platform will also provide a secondary-market channel for tickets initially sold through CityAud.

That makes the deal broader than simply providing a box-office system, giving AXS a role across both the venue’s primary ticket distribution and its designated resale ecosystem.

AVA’s involvement with the building dates to the city’s search for a private operating partner after an earlier redevelopment effort stalled. Colorado Springs formally approved leasing the venue to AVA in July 2025, with the arrangement allowing capital improvements made by the operator to offset portions of its rent.

CityAud is expected to reopen in early 2027. AVA Presents and AXS said the first group of concerts, festivals and community events is expected to be announced this fall.
