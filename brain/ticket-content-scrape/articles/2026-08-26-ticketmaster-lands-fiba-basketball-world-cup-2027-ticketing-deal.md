# Ticketmaster Lands FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 Ticketing Deal

## Summary

Ticketmaster will be the official ticketing partner for the FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 in Doha, Qatar, handling sales, mobile ticket delivery, and venue entry across all four tournament venues. The deal deploys Ticketmaster's SafeTix and TM Entry technologies, embedded in the tournament's official app, plus marketing support to drive international ticket sales. The 2027 tournament runs August 27–September 12, 2027, with tickets going on sale August 27, 2026.

### Key Points
- Ticketmaster named official ticketing partner for FIBA Basketball World Cup 2027 (Doha, Qatar).
- Uses SafeTix mobile-ticket delivery and TM Entry venue-access technology across all 4 venues.
- Ticketmaster will also provide marketing support to drive international ticket sales.
- Tournament: 32 national teams, 92 games, first FIBA World Cup in MENA region.

### Highlights
- Global-scale mega-event win for Ticketmaster: 32 teams, 92 games, 4 venues, first-ever MENA hosting.
- Deal bundles ticketing infrastructure, mobile delivery, venue entry tech, and marketing services in one contract.
- Positions Ticketmaster's tech stack (SafeTix, TM Entry) as the reference for large international sporting events.

### Industry Problem
Large multi-venue international events need a single vendor capable of unifying ticketing, secure mobile delivery, venue access control, and marketing at global scale — a bar that favors incumbents with proven infrastructure and international reach.

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster is positioning its existing SafeTix/TM Entry stack plus marketing services as a turnkey solution for mega-events. For Etix, this is a competitive-shipping signal to monitor: track how Ticketmaster's bundled ticketing+marketing offer performs on large international events and watch whether similar bundled packaging becomes an expectation from mid-size promoters and federations Etix competes for.

The deal puts Ticketmaster in charge of the ticketing infrastructure for one of international basketball’s largest events, covering sales, mobile ticket delivery and entry across all four World Cup venues.

Tickets will be delivered using Ticketmaster’s SafeTix technology and embedded directly within the tournament’s official mobile app. Ticketmaster will also deploy its TM Entry technology at competition venues and provide on-site ticketing support throughout the tournament.

FIBA said Ticketmaster will additionally use its marketing capabilities to help promote the World Cup and drive international ticket sales.

The Ticketmaster partnership arrives as FIBA prepares to begin selling the first ticket packages for the tournament.

Tickets are scheduled to go on sale Thursday, Aug. 27, 2026 — exactly one year before the World Cup tips off in Doha.

FIBA is offering a 20% early-purchase discount for the first 72 hours on selected packages, including its Lusail Series Pass and Final Phase Pass.

The Lusail Series Pass covers all 32 games scheduled for Lusail Arena, including 20 group-stage games and all 12 games of the final phase. The Final Phase Pass covers the tournament’s final 12 games, including the quarterfinals, semifinals, third-place game and championship.

A “Follow My Team Qatar” package covering the host country’s five group-phase games will also be available. FIBA plans to release similar team-specific passes as additional nations qualify for the tournament.

The concentrated Doha format is one of the distinguishing features of the 2027 World Cup. Rather than spreading games across multiple cities or countries, FIBA says the four venues will be located within roughly 30 minutes of each other, making it possible for fans to attend multiple games during a single day.

That setup also places considerable importance on the tournament’s centralized ticketing and access system, with Ticketmaster responsible for moving spectators from mobile purchase through venue entry across the event.

## Ticketmaster Expands Existing Qatar Sports Presence

The FIBA agreement also builds on Ticketmaster’s established sports-ticketing operations in Qatar rather than marking the company’s first major move into the country.

Ticketmaster has served as the ticketing partner for the Qatar Stars League, the country’s top-flight professional soccer competition, since 2018. That relationship has since expanded across all 12 league clubs, which Ticketmaster says have moved to fully digital ticketing.

The companies most recently extended the relationship beyond the 2025-26 season, with Ticketmaster continuing to provide the league with digital ticketing technology, operational support and matchday access services.

The FIBA deal extends that Qatar footprint to a major international championship expected to draw traveling basketball fans from around the world.

For FIBA, the partnership is part of preparations for what the organization is positioning as a particularly fan-accessible World Cup. Qatar won hosting rights for the event with a bid emphasizing the compact geography of Doha, transportation connections between venues and the ability to schedule the tournament without the extensive travel typically required at major international competitions.

Ticketmaster will now provide much of the digital infrastructure connecting those fans to the event when ticket sales begin Aug. 27.
