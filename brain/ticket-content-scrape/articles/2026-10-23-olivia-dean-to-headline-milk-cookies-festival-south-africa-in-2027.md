# Olivia Dean to Headline Milk + Cookies Festival South Africa in 2027

## Summary

Grammy-winning singer Olivia Dean will headline the third South African edition of Milk + Cookies Festival in January 2027, performing at two stops (Cape Town and Johannesburg) alongside Kaytranada, Gunna and local South African artists.

### Key Points
- Festival dates: Jan. 29 (Cape Town) and Jan. 31 (Johannesburg), 2027.
- Lineup also includes South African artists Elaine, Zee Nxumalo, Chelsea Sloan and Jade T.
- Additional lineup/programming to be announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Milk-and-Cookies-Festival-Olivia-Dean.webp)

Grammy-winning singer Olivia Dean will headline the third South African edition of Milk + Cookies Festival in January 2027, performing at Sandringham Farm in Cape Town on January 29 and Nasrec Expo Centre in Johannesburg on January 31.

The British singer-songwriter joins a lineup featuring South African artists Elaine, Zee Nxumalo, Chelsea Sloan and Jade T, with additional performers and programming expected to be announced in the coming weeks.

“People keep asking how Kaytranada, Gunna and Olivia Dean end up on the same bill,” said Chase Freeman, head of marketing at Milk + Cookies. “Never the same sound, always the same feeling. That’s the only brief we’ve ever had.”

Freeman said the festival’s approach emphasizes the atmosphere and cultural experience surrounding the performances as much as the individual artists.

“We book for the people in the room, not for a genre, and they come for what the room feels like as much as the name on the poster,” he said.

Milk + Cookies launched its South African edition in 2025 with electronic music producer Kaytranada, drawing a reported 20,000 attendees. Atlanta rapper Gunna followed as the 2026 headliner, with organizers reporting attendance exceeding 30,000 across Cape Town and Johannesburg.

The 2027 edition will also bring a venue change in Cape Town, moving to Sandringham Farm after the festival’s 2026 event at Kenilworth Racecourse. Johannesburg’s festival will return to Nasrec Expo Centre.
