# Singapore Grand Prix Draws 301,294 Fans, Sets Hospitality Attendance Record

## Summary

The 2026 Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix drew 301,294 spectators across its three-day weekend (Oct 9-11), the event's second-highest attendance ever and a new record for premium hospitality attendance, per organizers. The race sold out again under the reduced-capacity circuit layout in place since 2023. Early-bird 2027 tickets are already moving, and Singapore-based Sistic — now part of the AXS network — takes over as official ticketing partner starting with the 2027 race.

### Key Points
- 301,294 total attendance Oct 9-11, 2026, just 706 below the 2022 all-time record of 302,000.
- New record set for premium hospitality attendance, driven by 11+ hospitality products including the F1 Paddock Club.
- 2026 race added its first F1 Sprint and a floating Barge Stage with DJ Snake, Major Lazer Soundsystem and Rev Run.
- Super-early-bird allocation for 2027 (Oct 8-10) sold out; general early-bird sales open Oct 19.
- Sistic (part of the AXS network since its October 2025 majority-stake acquisition) becomes official ticketing partner for primary sales, hospitality, and box office starting 2027.

The 2026 Formula 1 Singapore Grand Prix attracted 301,294 spectators across its three-day race weekend, recording the event’s second-highest attendance in history while setting a new record for premium hospitality attendance.

The October 9-11 event at the Marina Bay Street Circuit was sold out, according to an [October 11 announcement from Singapore GP](https://singaporegp.sg/en/news/2026/another-sell-out-at-the-formula-1-singapore-airlines-singapore-grand-prix-2026/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The total was the highest since changes to the circuit layout in 2023 reduced spectator capacity.

Attendance was just 706 below the event’s all-time record of 302,000, established in 2022 when the race returned following a two-year pandemic-related absence.

The 2026 figure also represented a slight increase over the 300,641 spectators reported in 2025. Although the year-over-year improvement was only 653 attendees, or approximately 0.2%, the results demonstrate the event’s ability to maintain attendance near historic highs.

## Premium Hospitality Reaches New High

Beyond overall attendance, organizers reported that the 2026 Singapore Grand Prix established a new record for hospitality attendance, reflecting the continued importance of premium experiences to major Formula 1 events.

The race offered more than 11 hospitality products, including the Formula 1 Paddock Club and TWENTY3, which featured pop-up restaurants operated by chefs holding a combined 30 Michelin stars.

Organizers did not disclose the total number of hospitality guests, the year-over-year increase or revenue generated by premium packages. As a result, the announcement establishes a new attendance record for the category but does not provide enough information to quantify its financial impact.

The hospitality milestone is notable because overall attendance has remained relatively flat even as the race continues to sell out. Premium seating, dining and entertainment packages offer organizers another way to expand the commercial value of the event without necessarily increasing general-admission capacity.

The Singapore event also expanded its programming in 2026, hosting its first Formula 1 Sprint race, won by Max Verstappen. Off-track entertainment included a new floating Barge Stage featuring DJ Snake, Major Lazer Soundsystem and Rev Run, while the broader weekend lineup included Lana Del Rey, Janet Jackson, James Arthur and Mark Ronson.

Singapore’s strong attendance comes amid [broader growth across Formula 1](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/formula-1-hits-halfway-mark-record-crowds/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). As TicketNews previously reported, the series attracted nearly 3.7 million spectators during the first 11 race weekends of 2026, with all 11 events selling out and attendance increasing 6% compared with the corresponding races in 2025.

## 2027 Ticket Sales Begin as Sistic Prepares to Take Over

The attendance milestone comes as organizers prepare for another Singapore Grand Prix in 2027, with early ticket demand already emerging ahead of the event’s next general sales phase.

The official Singapore GP ticketing website confirms that its limited allocation of super-early-bird tickets for 2027 has sold out. A new early-bird sales period is scheduled to begin October 19, offering advertised savings of up to 15%.

The sellout applies to the initial discounted allocation and does not mean the entire 2027 event has sold out.

Next year’s race, scheduled for October 8-10, will also mark a significant change in the event’s ticketing operations.

As [TicketNews reported in June](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/formula-1-singapore-grand-prix-names-sistic-as-official-ticketing-partner-beginning-in-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Singapore-based ticketing provider Sistic has been appointed the official ticketing partner of the Singapore Grand Prix beginning in 2027 under a multi-year agreement.

Sistic, which became part of the AXS network following AXS’s acquisition of a majority stake in October 2025, will oversee primary ticket sales, hospitality ticketing, onsite box-office operations and related event support services.

The partnership gives Sistic and AXS responsibility for ticketing one of Asia’s highest-profile motorsports events, including a hospitality business that has just recorded its strongest attendance to date.

The 2026 turnout also provides a benchmark for the new ticketing arrangement as organizers seek to maintain demand for both traditional race admission and premium experiences.

Fans seeking tickets for the 2027 Singapore Grand Prix can find information about the October 19 early-bird sale through the [official Singapore GP ticketing website](https://singaporegp.sg/en/super-early-bird/tickets/general-tickets/all/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
