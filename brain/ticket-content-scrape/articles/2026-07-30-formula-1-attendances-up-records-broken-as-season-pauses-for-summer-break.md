# Formula 1 attendances up, records broken as season pauses for summer break

## Summary

Formula 1 hit the midpoint of its 2026 season with record-breaking attendance, having sold out every race weekend so far. Nearly 3.7 million fans attended races in the first half of 2026, a 6% year-over-year increase, with five new circuit attendance records set including an all-time F1 attendance high at the British Grand Prix.

### Key Points
- Roughly 3.7 million fans attended F1 race weekends in H1 2026, up 6% from 3.4 million in H1 2025.
- The British Grand Prix at Silverstone drew 564,000 fans over four days, the highest attendance ever for an F1 event (+13% YoY).
- Other records: Australia (484,000), Belgium (400,000), Canada (360,000), and Austria (320,000, +7% YoY); Japan's attendance rose 18% YoY.
- F1's global fan base now exceeds 830 million; 42% of the audience is female (three-quarters of new fans), and 43% is under 35.
- The season resumes after the summer break with the Dutch Grand Prix in Zandvoort, August 21-23.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Formula 1 has hit the halfway point in the season, with record-breaking crowds and sell-outs across the board.**

During the first half of the 2026 season, nearly 3.7 million fans have attended race weekends compared to 3.4 million that attended the same races in 2025. This represents a 6% year-on-year increase.

While all race weekends have sold out, there have also been five new circuit attendance records set.

Australia welcomed 484,000 fans, while Canada and Austria welcomed 360,000 and 320,000 respectively. Some 400,000 attended the Belgian Grand Prix, but the British Grand Prix really stood out with the highest attendance ever in Formula 1.

A total of [564,000 fans attended](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/07/british-grand-prix-breaks-f1-attendance-record/) across the four days at Silverstone, representing a 13% increase.

The Japanese Grand Prix saw an 18% attendance increase for this year’s race weekend compared to 2025, while Austria’s attendance was up by 7%.

According to F1, the global fan base has continued to grow every year, with more than 830 million people following the sport. Roughly 42% of the sport’s audience is female, with women also making up three-quarters of new fans. Additionally, around 43% of the fanbase is under 35 years old.

Following the summer break, Formula 1 will return with the Dutch Grand Prix in Zandvoort from August 21-23.
