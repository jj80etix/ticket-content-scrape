# Sphere, Discovery Children’s Museum Team Up for ‘Sphere Weekend’ Ahead of Wizard of Oz Anniversary

## Summary

Sphere Entertainment and DISCOVERY Children's Museum are running the second annual "Sphere Weekend" August 8-9 in Las Vegas, a free-with-admission STEAM event tied to the one-year anniversary of *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere*. The event is part of Sphere's five-year community investment commitment and pairs hands-on science activities with the venue's immersive-production branding.

### Key Points
- Two-day event, Aug. 8-9, at DISCOVERY Children's Museum, included with general admission.
- Marks the approaching first anniversary of *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere*.
- Activities include an Apple Drop Challenge, a live "Tornado in Oz" demo, a robotics/coding exercise, animation workshops, and a drone obstacle course.
- Part of Sphere's five-year youth-education investment with DISCOVERY, now in its second year.
- Hours: 10 a.m.-5 p.m. Saturday, noon-5 p.m. Sunday.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0774.jpeg)

Sphere Entertainment and DISCOVERY Children’s Museum are bringing back “Sphere Weekend” for a second year, inviting families to explore the technology and creativity behind *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere* ahead of the immersive production’s first anniversary.

The two-day event takes place August 8-9 at DISCOVERY Children’s Museum in downtown Las Vegas and is included with general museum admission. Designed to blend art, science and technology, the weekend features a series of hands-on STEAM activities inspired by Sphere’s reimagining of the beloved classic.

“Curiosity sparked by wonder can inspire future innovators,” Rich Constable, Global Head of Government Affairs and Social Impact at Sphere, said in a press release. “Through *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere* and experiences like Sphere Weekend, we hope to inspire the next generation to explore careers in STEAM-related fields. As part of our ongoing commitment to the greater Las Vegas community, Sphere is proud to continue its five-year investment in DISCOVERY’s youth education programs, now entering its second year.”

Among the featured activities are an Apple Drop Challenge, where children engineer protective devices for apples dropped from a free-fall tower; a live Tornado in Oz science demonstration; a Code the Yellow Brick Road robotics experience; digital animation workshops; DIY versions of Sphere’s mascot Orbi; and a drone obstacle course highlighting flight technology used at the venue.
