# Usher, Chris Brown Expand ‘The R&B Tour’

## Summary

Chris Brown and Usher added eight new dates to "The R&B Tour" this fall, spanning cities including East Rutherford, Baltimore, Atlanta, Los Angeles, and Miami. Presale begins August 20, with general onsale August 24.

### Key Points
- Chris Brown and Usher added 8 new fall dates to "The R&B Tour."
- New stops include East Rutherford, Baltimore, Cincinnati, Atlanta, LA, and Miami.
- Presale runs August 20; general onsale August 24.

## Transcript

![Chris Brown & Usher | Photo credit: Julian Buchan](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IMG_0493.jpeg)

Chris Brown & Usher | Photo credit: Julian Buchan

Chris Brown and Usher just expanded “ [The R&B Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/chris-brown-usher-team-up-for-massive-co-headlining-stadium-tour/) ” with eight new dates across the U.S. this fall.

“You all are STILL not playing about The R&B Tour!” Usher wrote in a social media post. “The fans have made this tour what it is. We See you. Let’s keep making it BIGGER.”

The new dates include stops in East Rutherford, Baltimore, Cincinnati, Columbia, Indianapolis, Atlanta, Los Angeles, and Miami

Tickets for the eight newly-announced stops head on sale starting with a presale on Thursday, August 20 at 10 a.m. local time; fans can sign up now through August 18 at 10 p.m. ET [here](https://www.raymondandbrowntour.com/). A general on sale will follow Monday, August 24 at 10 a.m. local time.

“The R&B Tour” first kicked-off in Denver on June 26, followed by shows in Detroit, Washington, D.C., St. Louis, Nashville, Toronto, and East Rutherford. As previously announced, the run will also include stops at venues like Los Angeles’ SoFi Stadium, and Mercedes-Benz Stadium in Atlanta before wrapping-up at Tampa’s Raymond James Stadium on December 11.

The co-headlining trek follows Usher’s record-breaking North American “USHER: Past, Present, Future” tour, which sold over 1.1 million tickets to 62 sold-out shows, as well as Brown’s sold-out “BREEZY BOWL XX WORLD TOUR” last year, which earned $300 million and drew in 2 million fans.

Brown’s legal woes fall right into the timeline of “The R&B Tour”; Brown faces a maximum of three years in prison after [pleading guilty](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/chris-brown-pleads-guilty-assault-london-nightclub/) to an assault that took place at a London nightclub in 2023. He is set to be sentenced on October 26.
