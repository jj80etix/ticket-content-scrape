# Evanescence Receive Plaque For Sold-Out EU Tour

## Summary

Evanescence received a Sold Out Award from promoter PRK DreamHaus during their Sept. 25 Berlin show, marking the success of the European leg of their 2026 World Tour — Eventim Live's first pan-European touring acquisition.

### Key Points
- Award presented at the Velodrom, Berlin, during the Sept. 25 show.
- European leg spans Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy, Switzerland, Spain, and Portugal.
- PRK DreamHaus is the national promoter for five German shows.
- Marks Eventim Live's first pan-European touring acquisition.

## Transcript

[Evanescence](https://pollstar.com/artists/evanescence-37635?_gl=1*j89ia1*_ga*MTM4MzMyNDM3Ny4xNzgyOTc2ODgw*_ga_5B6XRECYRM*czE3OTA2NjQ3ODkkbzE2MyRnMSR0MTc5MDY2NjQxOSRqNDQkbDAkaDA.) are currently touring Europe, and during their Sept. 25 show at the Velodrom in Berlin, Germany, promoter PRK DreamHaus honoured the band with a Sold Out Award in recognition of the tour’s success.

The European leg of [the “Evanescence World Tour 2026”](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/12/02/evanescence-announces-2026-world-tour-with-support-from-spiritbox-nova-twins-poppy-k-flay/) is Eventim Live’s first pan-European touring acquisition. Through its promoter network, Eventim Live is presenting the European concerts in Belgium, Germany, the Netherlands, Italy, Switzerland, Spain and Portugal. In Germany, PRK DreamHaus is the national promoter responsible for five shows.

At the time of this writing, Evanescence had three Euro dates left: Hallenstadion in Zurich, Switzerland, Sept. 29; Palau Olimpic Badalona in Barcelona, Spain, Oct. 1; Palacio Vistalegre in Madrid, Spain, Oct. 2; and MEO Arena in Lisbon, Portugal, Oct. 4.

The Swiss date is promoted by Gadget Entertainment Group, while Neo Sala’s Doctor Music handles the Spanish and Portuguese dates. Both companies are part of Eventim Live.

Ahead of their European leg, Evanescence played four shows in the UK, promoted by AEG Presents. Before that, they had extensively toured the U.S., where Live Nation promotes the band.

Highlights from the U.S. leg, taken from Pollstar Boxoffice, include a sold-out concert at Pine Knob Music Theatre in Clarkston, MI, Aug. 2, where the band moved 13,793 tickets and grossed $679,210; a sold-out Utah First Credit Union Amphitheatre in West Valley City, UT, July 25 (17,488 tickets, $812,797 grossed); or a sold out Kia Forum in Inglewood, CA (11,566, $1,064,434).

Evanescence return to the U.S. for two shows at Sick New World Texas, Oct. 24, and Red Rocks Amphitheatre Morrison, Oct. 26, before heading to Asia in November and December. The tour will hit Australia and New Zealand in March 2027.

In June 2027, Evanescence will be back in Germany to appear as one of the headliners at Rock am Ring and Rock im Park festivals.
