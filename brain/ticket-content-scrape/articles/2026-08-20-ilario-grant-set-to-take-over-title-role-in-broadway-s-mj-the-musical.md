# Ilario Grant Set to Take Over Title Role in Broadway’s ‘MJ the Musical’

## Summary

Ilario Grant will take over the title role in Broadway's MJ the Musical starting Sept. 15 at the Neil Simon Theatre, succeeding Matte Martinez, after previously playing the role in the show's Australian production and serving as a Broadway standby. The show, which won four 2022 Tony Awards, continues its Broadway run with an otherwise unchanged principal cast.

### Key Points
- Ilario Grant begins as MJ Sept. 15, succeeding Matte Martinez (final performance Aug. 30).
- Grant previously played MJ in the Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth productions.
- MJ the Musical opened Feb. 2022 and won four Tony Awards, including Best Leading Actor (Myles Frost).

Ilario Grant is set to step into the role of Michael Jackson in Broadway’s “MJ the Musical” this fall. Grant will begin performances as the title character at the Neil Simon Theatre on September 15, succeeding Matte Martinez, who is scheduled to play his final performance on August 30.

Grant previously appeared with the Broadway production as a standby for both MJ and Michael, making his Broadway debut with the musical. After his Broadway run, he took on the title role in Australia, where he opened productions in Melbourne, Brisbane and Perth.

“I’m thrilled to welcome Ilario back to New York to star as MJ,” director and choreographer Christopher Wheeldon said in a statement. “Having risen through this company and gone on to dazzle audiences as MJ in Australia, Ilario takes his rightful place on Broadway, bringing audiences his stunning interpretation of the King of Pop.”

Martinez currently leads the Broadway production as MJ. The cast also includes Apollo Levine as Rob/Joseph Jackson, Brandon Lee Harris as Michael, Christopher Sean Cooper Jr. and Jackson Robert Vann as Little Michael, Blake Channing Taylor as Little Marlon and Sasha Allen as Katherine Jackson.

“MJ the Musical” features a book by Pulitzer Prize winner Lynn Nottage, with Wheeldon directing and choreographing. David Holcenberg serves as musical supervisor, with orchestrations and arrangements by Holcenberg and Jason Michael Webb. Casting is handled by Rachel Hoffman and Lindsay Levine of The Telsey Office.

The musical began Broadway previews on December 6, 2021, before officially opening on February 1, 2022. The production went on to win four Tony Awards in 2022, including Best Choreography, Best Lighting Design of a Musical, Best Sound Design of a Musical and Best Leading Actor in a Musical for Myles Frost. Frost became the youngest actor to win the latter award.
