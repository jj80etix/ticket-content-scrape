# Michael Bublé to Open Guadalajara’s New Coliseo GNP Seguros in September

## Summary

Michael Bublé will headline the September 22 opening concert at Guadalajara's newly renovated Coliseo GNP Seguros, a former soccer stadium converted into a live-entertainment venue by promoter OCESA. Rod Stewart had originally been booked to open the venue but canceled his remaining tour dates following a medical procedure.

### Key Points
- Venue is the redeveloped former Estadio 3 de Marzo, a project by OCESA with Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara and GNP Seguros.
- Additional booked artists include Natanael Cano, Alejandro Sanz, Gorillaz, and Yuridia.
- Banamex presale begins Sept. 1; general onsale Sept. 2.
- Venue is expected to expand Guadalajara's ability to attract major international tours within the Latin American live-entertainment market.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Michael-Buble-Guadalajaras-New-Coliseo-GNP-Seguros.webp)

Michael Bublé will headline the first concert at Guadalajara’s new Coliseo GNP Seguros, with the Grammy Award-winning singer scheduled to officially open the venue on September 22.

The performance will mark the beginning of a new chapter for the former Estadio 3 de Marzo, which has undergone a major renovation to transform the longtime sports facility into a modern live entertainment destination.

Mexican concert promoter OCESA developed the project in partnership with Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara and GNP Seguros. The venue has been updated to accommodate the technical and production needs of contemporary touring acts while also improving the concert experience for audiences.

Estadio 3 de Marzo previously served as the home of Universidad Autónoma de Guadalajara’s professional soccer team for several decades. The stadium also developed a history as a concert destination, hosting artists including Luis Miguel, Shakira, Karol G, Roger Waters and Linkin Park.

The newly renovated Coliseo GNP Seguros is expected to expand Guadalajara’s ability to attract major international tours while strengthening the city’s role within the Latin American live entertainment industry. The additional venue could also provide touring artists with another option for adding Mexican dates beyond traditional stops in markets such as Mexico City and Monterrey.

Additional artists already scheduled to appear at Coliseo GNP Seguros include Mexican singer Natanael Cano, Spanish Grammy winner Alejandro Sanz, British virtual band Gorillaz and Mexican singer Yuridia.

Rod Stewart had originally been announced as the artist who would inaugurate the venue. Those plans changed after [Stewart canceled his remaining scheduled performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/rod-stewart-cancels-remaining-us-tour-heart-operation/) following a medical procedure, leaving the September 22 date available for a new opening-night headliner.

Tickets for the September 22 opening concert will first become available through a Banamex presale beginning September 1 at 2 p.m. The general public onsale is scheduled to follow on September 2.
