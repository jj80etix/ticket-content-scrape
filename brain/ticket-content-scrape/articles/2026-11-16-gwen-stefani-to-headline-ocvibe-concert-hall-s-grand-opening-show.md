# Gwen Stefani To Headline OCVIBE Concert Hall’s Grand Opening Show

## Summary

OCVIBE confirmed Gwen Stefani as the first headliner for its new 5,000-capacity Concert Hall in Anaheim, opening March 17, 2027. This is a companion announcement to the same OCVIBE opening-show news, with added quotes from OCVIBE executives and background on the surrounding $5 billion district. General onsale is Nov. 20 via Ticketmaster, with a venue/Live Nation presale Nov. 19.

### Key Points
- Duplicate/companion coverage of the Gwen Stefani OCVIBE Concert Hall opening show, March 17, 2027.
- General onsale Nov. 20; venue/Live Nation presale Nov. 19 at 10 a.m. PST.
- OCVIBE district's Katella Commons market hall expected to open in April 2027.

Not only has OCVIBE announced the opening date of its new concert hall, but it has also tabbed its first headliner. The entertainment complex is turning to local legend Gwen Stefani to usher in a new era of live entertainment in Anaheim, California, to perform the first show at the 5,000-capacity venue on March 17.

“There’s no better way to celebrate the opening of OCVIBE’s Concert Hall than by welcoming Gwen Stefani back to Orange County,” said Eric Bresler, Senior Vice President of Entertainment for OCVIBE. “Having an artist of Gwen’s caliber take the stage for our inaugural performance is an extraordinary moment for Anaheim and sets the tone for what we believe will become one of Southern California’s premier destinations for live entertainment.”

**[OCVIBE’s $5B Vision, Which Includes A 5,000-Cap Concert Hall, Comes Into Focus](http://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/04/ocvibes-5b-vision-which-includes-a-5000-cap-concert-hall-comes-into-focus/)**

Tickets for the show go on sale to the general public Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. PST at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/gwen-stefani-concert-hall-at-ocvibe-anaheim-california-03-17-2027/event/0900656FC7B77CF1). A venue and Live Nation presale begins Nov. 19 at 10 a.m. PST. To sign up for the venue presale, visit [ocvibe.com/concerthall](https://www.ocvibe.com/concerthall).

Stefani has performed at Honda Center 10 times throughout her career, including two nights when No Doubt recorded its “Live in the Tragic Kingdom” video album. She headlined a sold-out show in Sept. 2023 celebrating the Honda Center’s 30 <sup>th</sup> anniversary.

“My roots are in Orange County, so it’s such an honor to be the first artist to perform at this incredible new venue, OCVIBE,” said Stefani. “It’s really special to be part of this exciting opening and to celebrate with the community that means so much to me.”

**[‘A Compelling Story’ In The O.C.: Eric Bresler On OCVIBE’s Concert Hall & Expanding The SoCal Market](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/20/a-compelling-story-in-the-o-c-eric-bresler-on-ocvibes-concert-hall-expanding-the-socal-market/)**

The highly anticipated concert hall is part of OCVIBE, a 100-acre cultural hub that will transform Anaheim along with Honda Center, the DigAlert Grove of Anaheim and Golden Bear. The Katella Commons, a 50,000-square-foot market hall that includes restaurants from acclaimed chefs Roy Choi and Tim Hollingsworth, is expected to open in April.

“Having Gwen Stefani come home to Anaheim to open OCVIBE’s Concert Hall is an incredibly special moment,” said Bill Foltz, Chief Executive Officer for OCVIBE. “Gwen is an artist whose roots are deeply connected to this community, and we couldn’t imagine a more meaningful way to introduce OCVIBE’s Concert Hall to Orange County. Her performance will be the first opportunity for the community to experience a piece of what we’ve been working toward, and a preview of the music, culture, dining, and experiences that will bring this district to life. We can’t wait to welcome everyone.”
