# Rock the Bells Festival Returns to New Jersey with LL Cool J, SWV, Lil’ Kim

## Summary

Rock the Bells Festival returns to Newark, New Jersey's Prudential Center on November 29 for a Thanksgiving-weekend, "Home for the Holidays"-themed show headlined by LL Cool J, SWV, and Lil' Kim, with more acts to be announced. Tickets go on presale August 18 ahead of general onsale August 20.

### Key Points
- Rock the Bells Festival returns to Prudential Center, Newark, NJ on November 29.
- Headliners include LL Cool J, SWV, Lil' Kim, Method Man, and a D-Nice-curated set.
- Presale runs August 18–20 ahead of general onsale.
- Additional acts to be announced in coming weeks.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0828.jpeg)

Hip-hop icons are set to take-over the Rock the Bells Festival in Newark, New Jersey this Thanksgiving Weekend.

The festival, dubbed a “Home for the Holidays”-themed celebration, is slated to take place at the Prudential Center on November 29. Headliners will include LL Cool J, SWV, Lil’ Kim, and Method Man & Rwandan, as well as a set curated by D-Nice featuring LeToya Luckett, and David Banner and the LOX. Other acts are set to be announced in the coming weeks.

Festival tickets head on sale starting with an exclusive presale on August 18 at 10 a.m. ET through August 20 at 10 a.m. ET [here](https://www.rockthebellsfestival.com/).

Rock the Bells founder LL Cool J noted in a press release that “hip-hop has shaped the world for more than five decades, and Rock the Bells exists to honor the people who made that history and keep the culture moving upward.”
