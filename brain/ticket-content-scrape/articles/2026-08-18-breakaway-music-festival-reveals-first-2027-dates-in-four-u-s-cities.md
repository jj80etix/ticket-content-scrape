# Breakaway Music Festival Reveals First 2027 Dates in Four U.S. Cities

## Summary

Breakaway Music Festival reveals its first 2027 dates across four U.S. cities.

### Key Points
- Breakaway Music Festival announces initial 2027 dates in four U.S. cities.
- Routine festival-announcement news, no ticketing/industry-structure angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Breakaway-Music-Festival.webp)

Breakaway Music Festival is looking ahead to 2027 with the announcement of its first group of festival dates and locations, beginning in April with a return to Tampa before moving on to Atlanta, Columbus and St. Paul.

The multi-city festival series will launch its 2027 schedule with Breakaway Tampa, followed by events in Atlanta, Columbus and St. Paul during the spring and early summer. The festival has indicated that additional cities will be revealed later this year.

“Our festivals will always be the foundation of Breakaway, and in 2027 we’re investing more than ever to make each stop on the tour bigger and better than ever before,” said Adam Lynn, Breakaway Co-Founder and CEO. “This is a sneak peek at what’s ahead, with much more to be announced this fall.”

Tickets for the first four announced markets will be available to the general public beginning August 19 at 10 a.m. ET. Fans who register for presale access will have the opportunity to purchase tickets beginning one day earlier.

Breakaway’s 2027 announcement arrives while its current festival season is still underway. Upcoming 2026 stops include Breakaway Mass on August 21 and 22, Breakaway Philadelphia on September 11 and 12, and Breakaway Carolina on September 25 and 26. The season will then continue with Breakaway Utah on October 2 and 3 and Breakaway Houston on November 13 and 14.

Both Utah and Houston represent new markets for Breakaway in 2026, expanding the festival’s reach as the brand continues its multi-city format.

Notably, the festival made its debut in New York City this year, holding an event at Under The K Bridge Park earlier in the summer. The New York City event was part of a 2026 season that featured a number of prominent names from electronic music, including FISHER, John Summit, Subtronics, KETTAMA and Tiësto.
