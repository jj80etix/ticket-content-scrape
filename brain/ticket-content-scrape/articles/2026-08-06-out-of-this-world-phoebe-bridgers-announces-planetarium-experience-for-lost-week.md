# Out Of This World: Phoebe Bridgers Announces Planetarium Experience For ‘Lost Weekend’ Release

## Summary

Phoebe Bridgers is previewing her third album *Lost Weekend* through a globally coordinated series of planetarium listening events ahead of its August 14 release. Starting August 10, dozens of planetariums across North America, Europe, the UK, and Australia/New Zealand will host phone-free dome and laser-show listening sessions, alongside a Spotify fan event in Jersey City and 300+ international midnight release parties.

### Key Points
- Listening events run August 10–14 at dozens of participating planetariums worldwide, pairing the album with either a Babak Tafreshi-designed dome show or a Laser Fantasy laser show.
- Events are phone- and camera-free, with devices secured in Yondr pouches.
- A Spotify-partnered exclusive fan listening event is planned for Jersey City, NJ.
- Over 300 midnight album release parties are planned across ~30 countries.
- Performance times and ticket availability vary by individual venue.

Phoebe Bridgers’ much-anticipated third album *Lost Weekend* will debut in full inside planetariums around the world.

Beginning on Aug, 10 with multiple performances through the week ahead of the Aug. 14 release, the listening sessions pair the album with one of two original visual accompaniments: a custom dome show, designed by night-sky photographer Babak Tafreshi or an immersive laser show designed by Laser Fantasy.

Performance times and ticket availability vary by venue.

These events will be phone- and camera-free. All phones, smartwatches, recording and filming devices will be secured in Yondr pouches.

As part of the album’s launch week, Phoebe Bridgers — in partnership with Spotify & Dead Oceans — is set to host an exclusive listening event for top fans on Spotify in the Jersey City, NJ, area.

There will also be more than 300 international midnight album release parties for fans to listen to *Lost Weekend* together. Events are scheduled for the US, Canada, Scotland, England, Ireland, Wales, Belgium, Denmark, Finland, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Portugal, Spain, Australia, New Zealand, India, Japan, the Philippines, Singapore, South Korea, Thailand, Brazil, Chile, Colombia, Ecuador, Guatemala, Mexico, Peru and Uruguay. Details are available at http://phoebebridgers.lnk.to/midnight-parties.
