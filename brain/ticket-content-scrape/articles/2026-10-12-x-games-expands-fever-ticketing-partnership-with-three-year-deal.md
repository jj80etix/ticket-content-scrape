# X Games Expands Fever Ticketing Partnership With Three-Year Deal

## Summary

X Games named Fever as its Official Ticketing Platform under a new three-year, multi-market agreement covering the U.S. and select international markets as part of the expanding MoonPay X Games League. Fever will provide online and onsite ticketing, 24/7 customer support, real-time data/analytics, and audience-acquisition marketing (including its owned Secret Media Network) starting with X Games Draft, Aspen (Jan. 2027), and Calgary (Feb. 2027). The deal expands an existing relationship — Fever previously ticketed X Games California in 2023.

### Key Points
- Three-year deal; Fever becomes X Games' Official Ticketing Platform across the MoonPay X Games League.
- Scope: ticket sales management, 24/7 support, real-time data/analytics, audience acquisition via Fever's Secret Media Network.
- Initial events: X Games Draft, Aspen (Jan. 2027), Calgary (Feb. 2027), plus an unannounced summer stop.
- X Games cited Fever's combined ticketing-mechanics-plus-audience-reach pitch as the deciding factor.

### Highlights
- X Games' Global CRO explicitly credited Fever's integration of ticketing + audience acquisition + analytics as "exactly what we need," not ticketing mechanics alone.
- Deal builds on a prior single-event engagement (X Games California, 2023) into a multi-year, multi-market platform contract — a renewal/expansion win, not a cold start.
- Scope bundles marketing/audience-development services alongside core ticketing, extending beyond transaction processing.

### Industry Problem
This is a direct instance of a client (a multi-market sports/entertainment property) selecting a ticketing vendor based on bundled audience-acquisition and data capabilities rather than ticketing features alone — evidence for what wins and loses primary-platform accounts.

### Proposed Solution
Fever's pitch — pairing ticketing with owned-media audience acquisition and real-time analytics — is the model to benchmark against; for Etix, the actionable response is strengthening audience-development/marketing-analytics offerings bundled with core ticketing, since that bundle (not raw ticketing throughput) appears to be the deciding factor in this win.
