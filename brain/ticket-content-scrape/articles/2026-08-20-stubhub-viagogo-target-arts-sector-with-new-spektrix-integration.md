# StubHub, viagogo Target Arts Sector With New Spektrix Integration

## Summary

StubHub and viagogo have integrated with arts-sector ticketing platform Spektrix, letting Spektrix's 800+ theater, museum and cultural-venue clients "dual-broadcast" inventory to the StubHub/viagogo marketplaces while retaining control of pricing, inventory and fan data. The partnership carries no exclusivity requirement and no additional cost, positioning it as a low-risk distribution add-on for arts organizations that want broader reach without ceding operational control.

### Key Points
- Spektrix customers (800+ theaters, museums, venues worldwide) can broadcast inventory to StubHub/viagogo alongside their own site.
- Organizations retain control of inventory, pricing and fan data; StubHub/viagogo handle distribution, marketing, payments and buyer experience.
- No exclusivity requirement and no additional cost to participating organizations.
- Extends StubHub/viagogo's marketplace reach beyond major sports/music into arts and culture.

### Highlights
- A primary ticketing platform (Spektrix) is opting into secondary-marketplace distribution as an added-reach feature, not a replacement for direct sales.
- The deal structure explicitly protects the primary platform's pricing and data control — the model resale marketplaces are using to court primary platforms' trust.
- Extends resale marketplaces' footprint into a client segment (arts/culture) they've historically underserved.

### Industry Problem
Primary platforms serving smaller/niche organizations (theaters, museums) want broader audience reach but are wary of losing control of pricing and customer data to secondary marketplaces — a trust gap that has limited primary/secondary integration until now.

### Proposed Solution
A "dual-broadcast"-style distribution partnership — no exclusivity, no added cost, full client control of pricing/data — is a low-risk model an independent primary platform could offer or negotiate with a resale partner as an optional reach-extension feature for arts/culture clients.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/spektrix.jpg)

StubHub and viagogo have partnered with arts and culture technology provider Spektrix, giving venues and cultural organizations a new way to put ticket inventory in front of a global audience.

The integration connects Spektrix directly with the StubHub and viagogo marketplaces, allowing organizations using the platform to distribute tickets across both marketplaces while continuing to sell through their existing channels. Spektrix works with more than 800 theaters, museums, venues, attractions and other cultural organizations worldwide. Its platform combines ticketing, CRM, marketing and fundraising tools, with tens of thousands of users across the sector.

Under the new arrangement, Spektrix customers can “dual-broadcast” ticket inventory across their own platform, as well as StubHub and viagogo at the same time.

Crucially, participating organizations will retain control over their inventory, ticket pricing, and fan data. StubHub and viagogo will handle distribution, marketing, payments and the buyer experience.

The agreement is aimed particularly at arts and cultural organizations that want to broaden their ticket distribution without giving up control of their customer relationships or day-to-day ticketing operations. Tom Nolan, Head of Global Ecosystem at Spektrix, said the partnership fits with the company’s focus on helping cultural organizations reach new audiences while maintaining control of their businesses.
