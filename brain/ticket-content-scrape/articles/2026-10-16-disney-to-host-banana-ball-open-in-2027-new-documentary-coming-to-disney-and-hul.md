# Disney to Host Banana Ball Open in 2027, New Documentary Coming to Disney+ and Hulu

## Summary

Disney will host the Banana Ball Open at Walt Disney World's ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex April 15-18, 2027, bringing all six Banana Ball teams for a four-team tournament, alongside a music-driven Banana Ball documentary set to stream on Disney+ and Hulu in fall 2027. Tickets start at $35, with fans entering a lottery list through Oct. 31.

### Key Points
- Second Walt Disney World appearance for Banana Ball, following two sold-out 2026 games at the same complex.
- Tickets start at $35; interested fans join a lottery list via BananaBall.com through Oct. 31.
- Documentary follows Banana Ball's 10-year history, directed by David Beilinson, produced by Connor Schell's Words + Pictures and Pasek & Paul's Ampersand, featuring 11 original songs.
- No streaming premiere date set yet for the fall 2027 Disney+/Hulu documentary.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Banana-Ball.webp)

Disney will bring the Banana Ball Open to Walt Disney World Resort next spring, with four games scheduled April 15-18, 2027, at ESPN Wide World of Sports Complex near Orlando. The company also announced a music-driven Banana Ball documentary set to stream on Disney+ and Hulu in fall 2027.

The four-day Banana Ball Open will bring all six Banana Ball teams to Disney World, with four teams competing in the tournament. The event will also feature fan experiences, although Disney has not yet detailed the daily schedule, participating matchups or additional programming.

Banana Ball mixes baseball with music, dancing, comedy and fan participation, making the entertainment surrounding the game a central part of the experience. The 2027 tournament will mark the organization’s second appearance at Walt Disney World following two Banana Ball games held at the sports complex earlier in 2026. Disney said those games drew packed crowds.

Tickets for the Banana Ball Open start at $35. Fans interested in attending can enter the ticket lottery list through Oct. 31 at [BananaBall.com](https://bananaball.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

## Disney+ and Hulu Documentary Planned for Fall 2027

Disney’s documentary will follow Banana Ball’s 10-year journey, from its beginnings in Savannah, Georgia, to its emergence as a sports-entertainment attraction. The film will focus on the stories, personalities and community behind the organization, combining its history with original music.

The project is directed by David Beilinson and produced by Connor Schell’s Words + Pictures and Ampersand, the production company of songwriters Benj Pasek and Justin Paul.

The documentary will feature 11 original songs from Pasek and Paul, with additional songwriting contributions from Ashley Gorley, Cal Shapiro, Ben Johnson, Adam Yaron, Jake Sinclair, Brandon Bomer, Jarius Jones and Banana Ball founder Jesse Cole.
