# Rebelution, Usher & Chris Brown, John Mulaney Highlight This Week’s Hottest Concert and Comedy Tickets

## Summary

TicketNews's weekly "hottest tickets" roundup ranks the top 20 concert and comedy events for August 6–13 using MEGASeats marketplace resale-price data, led by a three-night Rebelution run at Pacific Amphitheatre and a stadium pairing of Usher and Chris Brown at MetLife Stadium. The piece is a recurring sponsored-content feature highlighting venue clusters (Pacific Amphitheatre, Red Rocks) and multi-act package shows driving demand that week.

### Key Points
- Top 20 average resale prices range from $82 (Tim McGraw & 49 Winchester, Virginia Beach) to $638 (Usher & Chris Brown, MetLife Stadium).
- Pacific Amphitheatre in Costa Mesa accounts for 6 of the top 20 spots, led by three Rebelution dates.
- Red Rocks Amphitheatre places two shows back-to-back (Of Monsters and Men, Empire of the Sun).
- John Mulaney's Wisconsin State Fair date is the only comedy show in the top 20.
- Multi-act nostalgia package tours (Blues Traveler/Gin Blossoms/Spin Doctors; ZZ Top/Cheap Trick; Lionel Richie/Earth, Wind & Fire; Train/Barenaked Ladies/Matt Nathanson) recur across the rankings.
- Rankings and pricing are sourced from MEGASeats, a resale marketplace that is also the article's advertiser/promo-code sponsor.
