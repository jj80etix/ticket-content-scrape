# TOMORROW X TOGETHER Set 2026-2027 ‘STEAL THE WIND’ World Tour

## Summary

TOMORROW X TOGETHER announced a 2026-2027 "STEAL THE WIND" world tour spanning Asia, North America, the UK and Europe. The run opens November 13 with three shows in Seoul and continues through a currently scheduled conclusion in June 2027.

### Key Points
- Opens Nov. 13 with three consecutive Seoul shows, then moves through Japan, Taiwan, Malaysia and Indonesia.
- North American leg begins May 4 in Tacoma, WA, with stops including Mexico City, Atlanta and Chicago.
- Closing European stretch includes Manchester, Paris, Amsterdam and Hong Kong, wrapping June 20.
- Ticket sale details to be announced via official TXT channels.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/TOMORROW-X-TOGETHER-STEAL-THE-WIND-World-Tour.webp)

TOMORROW X TOGETHER are heading back on the road with a new world tour spanning Asia, North America, the United Kingdom and Europe.

The K-pop group has announced its 2026-2027 “STEAL THE WIND” world tour, which will begin November 13 with the first of three consecutive performances in Seoul. The extensive run will continue into 2027 and take the group through several international markets before its currently scheduled conclusion in June.

Following the opening shows in South Korea, TOMORROW X TOGETHER will travel through Asia with stops planned in Japan, Taiwan, Malaysia and Indonesia.

The group will then bring the tour to North America in the spring. The North American portion begins May 4 in Tacoma, Washington, before continuing to cities including Mexico City, Atlanta and Chicago.
