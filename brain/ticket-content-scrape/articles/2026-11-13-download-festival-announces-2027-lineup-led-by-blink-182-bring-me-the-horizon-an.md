# Download Festival Announces 2027 Lineup Led by blink-182, Bring Me the Horizon and Evanescence

## Summary

Download Festival unveiled its 2027 lineup for its 24th edition at Donington Park, with blink-182, Bring Me the Horizon, and Evanescence headlining June 9-13. The bill marks blink-182's festival debut and makes Evanescence the festival's second female-led headline act.

### Key Points
- Headliners: blink-182 (festival debut), Bring Me the Horizon, and Evanescence.
- Runs June 9-13, 2027 at Donington Park, Leicestershire.
- Other confirmed acts include Turnstile, Faith No More, Mötley Crüe, and The Prodigy.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Download-Festival.webp)

Download Festival has unveiled its 2027 lineup, with blink-182, Bring Me the Horizon and Evanescence set to headline its 24th edition at Donington Park in Leicestershire, England.

The festival will run June 9 to 13, with Turnstile, Faith No More, Mötley Crüe and The Prodigy also scheduled to perform.

The appearance will mark blink-182’s Download debut. The band joins the bill as one of three headliners, alongside a returning Bring Me the Horizon and an Evanescence performance that will make the Amy Lee-fronted group the festival’s second female-led headline act.

Pierce the Veil, Parkway Drive, President and I Prevail are among the other confirmed performers. Down, Body Count featuring Ice-T, Primus, The Hives, Converge and GWAR also appear on the lineup.
