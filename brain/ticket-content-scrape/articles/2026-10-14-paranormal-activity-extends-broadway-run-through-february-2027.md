# ‘Paranormal Activity’ Extends Broadway Run Through February 2027

## Summary

The Broadway production of "Paranormal Activity" has extended its run through February 2027. The extension reflects continued demand for the stage adaptation of the horror franchise.

### Key Points
- Run extended through February 2027.
- Extension driven by ongoing ticket demand.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Paranormal-Activity-Broadway.webp)

“Paranormal Activity” has extended its Broadway engagement by eight weeks, keeping the horror play at the August Wilson Theatre through February 28, 2027. The production had previously been scheduled to close January 3.

Written by Levi Holloway and directed by Felix Barrett, “Paranormal Activity” brings an original story inspired by the horror film franchise to the stage. It follows James and Lou, a couple who leave Chicago for London hoping to put their past behind them. Their move fails to provide the fresh start they expected.

Cher Álvarez stars as Lou opposite Travis A. Knight as James. Shannon Cochran plays Carolanne, while Eva Kaminsky appears as Etheline Cotgrave. The company also includes understudies Caron Buinis, Caroline Hendricks and Michael Holding. Álvarez, Knight, Cochran and the three understudies are making their Broadway debuts after appearing in earlier North American productions.

The creative team includes Fly Davis, who designed the scenery and costumes, and Chris Fisher, who created the illusions. Anna Watson handles lighting design, Gareth Fry provides sound design, and Luke Halls is responsible for video and projections. Holloway previously wrote the Broadway thriller “Grey House,” while Barrett’s credits include “Sleep No More.”

The Broadway engagement follows a series of productions in North America and the United Kingdom. Before arriving in New York, the play ran at Boston’s Emerson Colonial Theatre from July 11 through July 30. That engagement followed a Toronto run at the CAA Ed Mirvish Theatre from June 9 through July 5.

Earlier stops included Chicago Shakespeare Theater, the Ahmanson Theatre in Los Angeles, Shakespeare Theatre Company in Washington, D.C., and American Conservatory Theater in San Francisco.

“Paranormal Activity” originated at Leeds Playhouse before reaching London’s West End. The London production received a 2026 Olivier Award nomination for Best Entertainment or Comedy Play.
