# Florence (SC) And the Machine: New Arena GM Vince Thomas Gears Up

## Summary

Interview with Vince Thomas, newly named General Manager of South Carolina's Florence Center (managed by Legends Global), on preparing the venue for the return of professional hockey and building on nearly two decades of event-operations experience.

### Key Points
- Thomas was previously the venue's Assistant GM and Director of Events.
- The Florence Center is a 10,000-seat arena with 79,000+ sq ft of event space, home to the Pee Dee IceCats.
- Hockey returned in Oct. 2025 after an 18-20 year absence, requiring facility retrofits including chiller-system upgrades.

## Transcript

![Current image: VinceThomasLobby](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/VinceThomasLobby-576x1024.png)

New GM Vince Thomas photo courtesy Legends Global

Industry veteran Vince Thomas was recently named General Manager of the Florence Center in South Carolina, where he has been instrumental in preparing the facility for the return of professional hockey as the former Assistant General Manager and Director of Events.

Managed by Legends Global, the Florence Center is the largest convention, entertainment and exhibition facility in northeastern South Carolina. Home of the champion Pee Dee IceCats, the venue features a 10,000-seat arena and more than 79,000 square feet of event space.

Thomas brings nearly two decades of experience leading operations across arenas, stadiums, performing arts centers, convention facilities and major entertainment destinations including the NCAA Hall of Champions and Convention Center in Indianapolis. His professional portfolio as an event manager includes supporting major national and international events including The Masters, Super Bowl LII, the NCAA, NBA and Wrestlemania.

Thomas recently talked to *Pollstar* about the Florence Center’s competitive position, tenant hockey, partnerships, elevating the guest experience and maximizing the facility’s economic and cultural impact on Florence and the region.

Pollstar: *What are you bringing to this new role*?

Vince Thomas: Primarily, I would say my experience. I’m approaching year 19 of being in the industry, managing events and managing venues.

Pollstar: *What is the foundation of your success?*

Thomas: The key component to my success and the building’s success is leveraging relationships within our community, connecting on a more intimate level with the city and county; taking full advantage of those relationships and looking to execute them the same way that Rex Berman, my predecessor, laid a really solid foundation. My goal is to build on that foundation; get a good structure on top of it to make it even more sustainable for the next 30 years.

The Florence Center has been here 33 years. I’m not trying to reinvent a wheel; just trying to challenge and push us to think about the next 33 years as opposed to the next 33 days.

![VinceThomasFlorence CenteAriele](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/VinceThomasFlorence-CenteAriele.jpg)

Florence Center includes 10,000-cap arena and 79,000 square feet of flexible meeting space. Photo courtesy Legends Global

Pollstar: *After a lengthy absence, hockey returned in 2025 with the Pee Dee IceCats, who began in the Federal Prospects Hockey League before joining the SPHL for the 2026-27 season. How do you reintroduce hockey in the market?*

Thomas: It took about 18 to 20 years to get hockey back into the building, and we were able to execute that through conversations that started in March of ‘25, announced in like May of 2025, and the first puck dropped in October of 2025. And so, getting the building ready, was kind of a whirlwind.

The city and county really put pressure on us to make sure that we had a tenant, and we were ready for them for that first season. And the team did their thing during that time. The IceCats owners have been phenomenal to work with. They had high expectations and they delivered on it by going to the championship in that first season. So, we’re just looking to build on that.

The talent level is there. The competition level is higher, and the expectations remain the same. So, from a venue standpoint, the same way that the team is responsible for putting a championship product on the ice, we have to do our due diligence to make sure that we have a championship staff ready, willing and able at every turn to ensure that every puck drop is just as successful as the previous one.

Pollstar: *How much retrofitting was necessary to prepare the venue for hockey?*

Thomas: We went through a series of upgrades, starting with our chiller system – we didn’t have one on site. We were actually renting it before I got here. So, going through that purchase and install before the first game of the season and upgrading our production.

Pollstar: *What do you have planned going forward?*

Thomas: We’re looking to focus on premium experiences and making sure that our guests not only see those things and that are in building, but that our guests get to actually *feel* it through the experiences that they have as soon as they come into the building.

Pollstar: *How do you elevate and create those kind of experiences?*

Thomas: We are looking to focus more on the programming, community outreach and making sure that fans can tell the difference. The team is ensuring that our in-game experiences are up there with professional league hockey at its highest level.

Pollstar: *How do the upgrades for hockey impact other programming?*

Thomas: The great part about having that tenant, having a having a team and having the IceCats specifically is that they set that standard. And it’s my duty as general manager to make sure that we’re following up with the quality of the shows that we’re bringing in. We’re mainly looking to complement what folks expect, right? On ice experiences and content is going to be a focal point of mine, as well as our entire booking team. It’s really limitless what we’re capable of tapping into on the arena side.

Pollstar: *How does the convention center factor in?*

Thomas: Our ballroom bookings are trending in the right direction and so maximizing the content that we’re able to do on the ballroom side means that folks get a wholesome experience. This year, as an example, we’re hosting a food truck festival the same night as a hockey game, and folks are going to get an entire experience from the parking lot through the convention center through different setups and activities and ways of engaging from vendors to clients with our guests and patrons and then capping it off by the time they make it into the arena for the hockey game.

Pollstar: *How do you describe Florence as a live market?*

Thomas: We really are a unique place for promoters, for artists and ultimately for in-town visitors. The convenient part of coming to our building is unmatched by most places. The proximity of the hotels that are around us, all the eateries, the restaurants that are next door to us. That really helps us stand out.

It’s going to be easier to get in and out of our building as we look to develop plans as we work with the city and county. So those things help us, and then naturally the experience inside. You know, I’ve got staff that have been in this building for the 33 years that it’s been open, and they’re in management and director level positions. So, we really lean on the service that helps us stand apart from everywhere.

Pollstar: *What are your priorities?*

Thomas: I really want to support this arena and this region as best as I can as a leader. I’m looking to develop and grow myself, and just because I’m in this seat, it doesn’t mean that I’m the end-all, be-all to every everything. I ultimately have a responsibility and a measure of accountability in this role. But make no mistake about it, my true impact is going to reveal itself through the staff and the team that’s working here.

![VinceThomasTEAM](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/VinceThomasTEAM-scaled.jpg)

Out of the box Pee Dee IceCats score with championship. Photo credit Stephen Lewis

Pollstar: *What about the calendar?*

Thomas: I want our calendar to look drastically different within the next year. And then scale that year-after-year. We’re building on that so that ultimately the arena can get the type of content that the community has been craving and the people here have been going out of town for. They should be able to enjoy a good night, come see a good show, be a part of a fun experience, and then go home and sleep in their bed at night.

Pollstar: *How would you describe your management style?*

Thomas: I love to take care of my team through development. I know that I’m responsible for the next leaders in our community and the next leaders in our company. I don’t take lightly the position that I’m in. I understand the role that I stepped into. I understand the shoes that I’ve stepped into as well, so it’s my responsibility to pay that forward. And development and growth is the key thing with me.

My staff will tell you one of my favorite phrases that they’ve heard me say since I’ve been here is “Embrace the challenge.” And that’s something that most people don’t look to do. I know there’s a challenge ahead. I fully recognize that every day I wake up, but I love what I do. I look to do it at the highest level possible, and I’m embracing everything that comes with it because it’ll wind up being a learning experience – and that’s ultimately going to benefit our staff and our community.

Pollstar: *You got your start slinging tables and chairs in the student center at Augusta University. You majored in Public Relations and Communications, what was attractive about events and operating venues?*

Thomas: The opportunities are limitless. Back when I was in college, I knew I wanted to be in an industry where the support was there, where I would develop and have that opportunity. But my true passion has always been sports and entertainment, so sports and music primarily. Being able to be adjacent to those things, I don’t take that for granted. We all have lifelong dreams of being on a basketball court, on a field, on a stage, but nobody thinks about the people behind the scenes, behind the stage, the housekeepers that are making sure the trash cans are on the floor for that concert, or you know the F&B staff working so hard overnight to make sure that there’s the best food presentation for the next day. The box office team, right? Nobody ever thinks about the box office team and what it takes to get a show on sale. All of these components working together really drives the motor, and ultimately, I’ve got that motor and that drive the same way that I had it back in 2008, and I I’m really fortunate to be at this spot in 2026.
