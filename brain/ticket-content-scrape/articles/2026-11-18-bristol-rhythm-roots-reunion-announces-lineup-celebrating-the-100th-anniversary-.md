# Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion Announces Lineup Celebrating The 100th Anniversary Of The Bristol Sessions In The Birthplace Of Country Music

## Summary

The 26th annual Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion (Sept. 10-12, 2027, in Bristol, TN/VA) will mark the 100th anniversary of the 1927 Bristol Sessions recordings that helped launch modern country music. The initial lineup features 19 acts — honoring the original 19 Bristol Sessions artists — including Old Crow Medicine Show's "The 2027 Bristol Sessions" tribute set, Carlene Carter and Emily Ann Roberts; tickets are already on sale.

### Key Points
- 26th annual festival, Sept. 10-12, 2027, celebrating the 1927 Bristol Sessions' 100th anniversary.
- Initial 19-artist lineup mirrors the 19 original 1927 session artists; highlights include Old Crow Medicine Show's tribute performance and Carlene Carter.
- Presented by the nonprofit Birthplace of Country Music organization.
- Tickets currently on sale.

A century ago, a recording session in Bristol, Tennessee, changed the trajectory of music, culture and legacy in the American South. Known colloquially as the Bristol Sessions, Ralph Peer’s 1927 recordings of Jimmie Rodgers, the Carter Family, and many more for the Victor Talking Machine Company became known as the “big bang” of modern country music.

On the centennial anniversary of the 1927 Bristol Sessions, the twin cities of Bristol, Tennessee, and Bristol, Virginia, will host the 26th annual Bristol Rhythm & Roots Renuion from Sept. 10-12, 2027, paying special tribute to the historic anniversary.

“One hundred years of the 1927 Bristol Sessions is a milestone that our whole region is incredibly excited to celebrate,” stated Festival Director Richard Oakley. “With every conversation we have for Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion 2027, from branding design to the lineup, we are putting the history of the 1927 Bristol Sessions front and center.”

The initial lineup announcement for Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion includes 19 performers in recognition of the 19 recording artists that took part in the 1927 Bristol Sessions. They include Old Crow Medicine Show presents “The 2027 Bristol Sessions,” Emily Ann Roberts, Wednesday, Carlene Carter, Trey Hensley, Maggie Rose, Ken Pomeroy, Magoo, Willi Carlisle, Fabrizio, Belle Frantz, River Shook, Emily Nenni, Mason Via, Slap Dragon, Lucille Klement, Adam Bolt, The Corklickers and Bratfolk.

“We have a few moments planned that will pay tribute from multiple angles, and show different sides of this story,” added Oakley of the festival’s 2027 programming. “At the top of that list is Old Crow Medicine Show presenting ‘The 2027 Bristol Sessions’ on Saturday night, from the iconic State Street Stage. We can’t reveal too much, yet we look at this as the grand finale of the 100th Anniversary. We can’t wait to share more about this performance soon.”

The Birthplace of Country Music (BCM) is the parent nonprofit organization of the Birthplace of Country Music Museum, the Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion music festival and Radio Bristol. The organization is focused on presenting a diverse cross-section of roots music, with acts like Emily Ann Roberts and Wednesday alongside Carlene Carter and Trey Hensley.

“We have been talking about this lineup for a long time already. We wanted to both pay tribute to the last 100 years of country music, while also shining a light on just how far the Sessions’ influence has spread, and set the table for what creative heights that it is going to reach next,” continued Oakley. “We didn’t worry too much about genres, or what is and isn’t considered ‘country music,’ but instead we have focused on matching the artistic spirit of 1927 Bristol Sessions.”

Tickets are on sale now for the 26th annual Bristol Rhythm & Roots Reunion.

“This whole year of celebration will be a generational moment for Bristol,” concluded Oakley. “We have so much to share from the last century, and we are proud to welcome music fans from all over the world home to Historic Downtown Bristol to learn about the 1927 Bristol Sessions.”
