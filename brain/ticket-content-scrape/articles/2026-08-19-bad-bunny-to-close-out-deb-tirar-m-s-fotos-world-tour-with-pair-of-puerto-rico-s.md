# Bad Bunny to Close Out ‘Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour with Pair of Puerto Rico Shows

## Summary

Bad Bunny added two Puerto Rico shows (Aug 22-23, San Juan's Hiram Bithorn Stadium) closing out his "Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour," which spanned five continents and 55 shows since November 2025 but notably skipped the U.S. mainland over ICE-raid concerns. Tickets go on sale Aug 19 via PR Ticket, capped at four per transaction.

### Key Points
- Two-night finale set for Aug 22-23 at Hiram Bithorn Stadium in San Juan.
- Tickets sold via PR Ticket, on sale Aug 19 at 10 a.m., limited to 4 per transaction.
- Tour launched Nov 21, 2025 in Santo Domingo after a 30-day Puerto Rico residency; 55 shows across five continents.
- Bad Bunny excluded the U.S. mainland from the tour due to concerns about ICE raids at his shows.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0843.jpeg)

Latin superstar Bad Bunny has been touring across the globe for over a year, and now, he’s set to wrap-up the ongoing trek with a pair of shows right at home in Puerto Rico.

The two-night event is set to take place across August 22 and 23 at San Juan’s Hiram Bithorn Stadium. The news follows circulating rumors over the past few weeks that the artist would return to Puerto Rico before the end of his tour, especially after Rimas Entertainment reserved the dates at the venue.

Tickets for the newly-announced dates head on sale Wednesday, August 19 at 10 a.m. via PR Ticket, where tickets are limited to four per transaction.

The “ [Debí Tirar Más Fotos World Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/05/bad-bunny-reveals-world-tour-dates-2025) ” first kicked-off on November 21 in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic after a sold-out 30-day residency at the Coliseo de Puerto Rico. The tour brought him to five continents for 55 performances, supporting his smash-hit record *Debí Tirar Más Fotos*, released last year.

The U.S., however, was [notably left off of the tour schedule](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/09/bad-bunny-leaves-u-s-off-tour-scheduled-amid-concerns-over-ice-raids/), with Bad Bunny citing concerns of Immigration and Customs Enforcement (ICE) raids outside of his shows.
