# Broadway’s ‘Just in Time’ to Welcome Back Matthew Morrison This Fall

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/Just-in-Time-1.webp)

Matthew Morrison will return to Broadway’s “Just in Time” this fall, reprising the role of Bobby Darin for a limited three-week engagement beginning October 20 at the Circle in the Square Theatre.

The Tony-nominated actor’s return follows Jeremy Jordan’s final performance as Darin on October 18. Carrie St. Louis, who plays Sandra Dee, will also conclude her run that day, marking another casting transition for the musical. Tickets for the production are currently on sale for performances through February 7, 2027.

Morrison previously starred in “Just in Time” from April 1 through April 19, stepping into the leading role after original star Jonathan Groff departed on March 29. Jordan subsequently began his engagement on April 21. The upcoming run gives audiences another opportunity to see Morrison’s portrayal following his brief spring appearance.

“Just in Time” explores Darin’s life and career through the music that helped establish him as a major American entertainer. The production transforms the Circle in the Square into an intimate nightclub setting, with a live band and an ensemble performing alongside its leading man.

The score features signature Darin songs including “Beyond the Sea,” “Mack the Knife,” “Splish Splash” and “Dream Lover.” That setting places the singer’s catalog at the center of the theatrical experience, pairing familiar recordings with the story behind his rise to fame.

Directed by Alex Timbers, the musical features a book by Tony winner Warren Leight and Isaac Oliver, with choreography by Shannon Lewis. The production is based on an original concept by Ted Chapin.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit the [official “Just in Time” website](https://justintimebroadway.com/).
