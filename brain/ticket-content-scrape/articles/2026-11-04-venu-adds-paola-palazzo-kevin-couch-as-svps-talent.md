# Venu Adds Paola Palazzo, Kevin Couch As SVPs, Talent

## Summary

Venue developer Venu has hired Paola Palazzo and Kevin Couch as senior VPs on its talent/booking team as the company builds out its pipeline of "multi-seasonal" amphitheaters, including a 20,000-capacity McKinney, Texas venue opening early 2027. Palazzo, who previously worked at Live Nation and CAA, will lead strategy and programming.

### Key Points
- Palazzo (ex-Live Nation, CAA, Brooklyn Sports & Entertainment) named SVP/Head of Talent; Couch (ex-ATG, Kennedy Center) named SVP of Talent.
- Venu's pipeline includes a 20,000-cap McKinney, TX amphitheater (early 2027) and the Regent Bank Amphitheater in Broken Arrow, OK.
- Ford Amphitheater (Colorado Springs, opened 2024) hosted a 2026 season including Lynyrd Skynyrd, Sammy Hagar, and Alison Krauss.

## Transcript

![VENU presenters](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/VENU-presenters.png)

VENU presenters

  
Two live entertainment industry veterans have joined venue developer Venu on the talent and booking side, with Paola Palazzo named senior vice president and head of talent and Kevin Couch named senior vice president of talent.

Palazzo’s experience includes time at Live Nation, CAA, Brooklyn Sports & Entertainment, Nederlander Concerts as well as founding her own Toronto-based consultancy, Work Together Inc. In her role, she will lead strategy and programming as Venu builds on the opening of its boutique Ford Amphitheater in Colorado Springs.

Couch’s experience includes programming roles at ATG Entertainment, the John F. Kennedy Center and management for Color Me Badd since 2017.

The additions come as VENU continues to build out what it is calling “a new asset class in live entertainment: multi-seasonal, more-than-music venues with multi-configuration capability for year-round programming and immersive videoboards and other technology that will bring fans deeper inside the performance.”

Venues in the Venu pipeline include a 20,000-capacity amphitheater in McKinney, Texas, scheduled to open in early 2027, and the Regent Bank Amphitheater in Broken Arrow, Oklahoma, which has holiday shows on the books for this December. Ford Amphitheater’s summer season included shows by Dwight Yoakam, For King & Country, The Spinners, Lynyrd Skynyrd, Ty Myers, Sammy Hagar, Alison Krauss & Union Station, Lauren Daigle and more. The 8,000-capacity venue opened in 2024.

Palazzo and Couch join a team that includes Hamilton Byrd, who brings more than 15 years of experience in live music and is known for championing artists early in their careers, including Zach Top, The Red Clay Strays, Mack Geiger, and The Band Loula.
