# Trio of Dutch festivals fear new entertainment tax will ‘drive events out’

## Summary

A Dutch municipality (Meierijstad) approved a new €1.50-per-visitor daily entertainment tax on major festivals, set to hit three events — Paaspop, 7th Sunday, and Harmony of Hardcore. One organizer warns the tax will "drive culture and events out" of the area.

### Key Points
- Meierijstad council approved a €1.50/visitor/day tax on festivals with "significant impact."
- Three festivals affected: Paaspop, 7th Sunday, Harmony of Hardcore.
- Vote passed November 5, 2026.
- An organizer says the levy risks pushing events out of the municipality entirely.
