# Amazon Music Live Returns For Season 5 With Performances From Noah Kahan, Don Toliver, Lainey Wilson & Doja Cat

## Summary

Amazon Music Live returns for a fifth season, airing after Thursday Night Football, with a format shift to filming artists on the road at their actual tour stops rather than in a studio. The season features Noah Kahan, Don Toliver, Lainey Wilson and Doja Cat, starting Sept. 24.

### Key Points
- Season 5 kicks off Sept. 24 with Noah Kahan's final U.S. stadium show (Seattle), followed by Don Toliver (Oct. 1, Barclays Center), Lainey Wilson (Oct. 15, Mohegan Sun) and Doja Cat (Oct. 29, Kia Forum).
- New format captures performances live from artists' actual tour stops instead of a dedicated studio audience.
- Streams on Amazon Music, Prime Video, Twitch, Amazon Live and Fire TV's Amazon Music app.
- Previous seasons (since 2022) have featured Ed Sheeran, Foo Fighters, Peso Pluma and others.

## Transcript

![amazon](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/amazon.jpg)

Amazon Music Live, a popular series that broadcasts live performances from notable artists following Thursday Night Football, is back and bigger than ever with a stacked, diverse lineup for its fifth season.

The star-studded season will feature stadium act Noah Kahan, Don Toliver, Lainey Wilson and Doja Cat, and fans will get an exclusive glimpse of the artists’ tours.

Rather than having artists perform live in front of an audience at a studio, Amazon Music Live is changing its formula this season and hitting the road to highlight some of the biggest tours in the country.

“This season of Amazon Music Live will give fans a front-row seat to some of the most talked-about tours of the year,” Kirdis Postelle, Global Head of Artist and Fan Experiences at Amazon Music, said in a statement. “From Noah Kahan’s final U.S. stadium show to Doja’s hometown birthday celebration at the Forum, Amazon Music Live will continue to bring fans closer to the artists and shows shaping music and culture right now.”

The new season kicks off Sept. 24 with Noah Kahan’s final U.S. show of his massively successful “Great Divide World Tour” at T-Mobile Park in Seattle, and the second episode on Oct. 1 will broadcast Don Toliver’s finale of the second leg of his North American “Octane Tour” at Brooklyn’s Barclays Center.

Amazon Music Live then goes to Uncasville, Connecticut, on Oct. 15 to connect fans to Lainey Wilson’s performance at Mohegan Sun, and the season concludes Oct. 29 with Doja Cat’s special birthday show at the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California.

“I’m playing The Forum the day after my birthday, and I’m spending it onstage doing what I love most. That’s already special. But the fact that Amazon Music Live is bringing that energy to everyone at home? Now it’s a party,” said Doja Cat. “I put everything into these shows—every night—and I want people to see exactly what that looks like up close.

Fans can watch each episode immediately following Thursday Night Football on Amazon Music, Prime Video, Twitch, Amazon Live and the Amazon Music app on Fire TV.

Since launching in 2022, Amazon Music Live has connected the sports and music worlds and given millions of fans at home an opportunity to experience live performances from global artists across all genres. Previous seasons have featured Ed Sheeran, A$AP Rocky, Fuerza Regida, Shaboozey, aespa, Foo Fighters and Peso Pluma.
