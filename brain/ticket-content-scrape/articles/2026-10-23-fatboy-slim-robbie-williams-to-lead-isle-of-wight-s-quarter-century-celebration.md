# Fatboy Slim, Robbie Williams to lead Isle of Wight’s quarter-century celebration

## Summary

Fatboy Slim, Robbie Williams and Kings of Leon will headline the Isle of Wight Festival's 2027 edition, marking 25 years since the festival's 2002 revival. The festival runs June 17-20, 2027, with Williams headlining Saturday and Kings of Leon closing Sunday.

### Key Points
- 2027 Isle of Wight Festival (June 17-20) marks the 25th anniversary of its 2002 revival.
- Headliners: Robbie Williams (Sat. June 18), Kings of Leon (Sun. June 20); Fatboy Slim's set time unconfirmed.
- Additional lineup to be announced.
