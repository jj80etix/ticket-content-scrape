# Godsmack Expands 2026 ‘Rise of Rock’ Tour With October Shows

## Summary

Godsmack has added a new October leg to its 2026 "Rise of Rock" tour, extending the run that already covered spring, summer, and a previously announced September leg. The added shows run from Airway Heights, Washington through Uncasville, Connecticut, with Dorothy as direct support throughout.

### Key Points
- New leg runs October 9 (Airway Heights, WA) through October 24 (Mohegan Sun Arena, Uncasville, CT).
- Dorothy supports all new October dates; the September leg also featured Stone Temple Pilots on select stops.
- Artist pre-sale uses code LEGENDS via Ticketmaster; general on-sale began Friday, July 31 at 10 a.m. local time.
- The Uncasville stop is notable to the band: Mohegan Sun Arena is where Godsmack recorded its recently released live album during the final tour with its longtime lineup.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/godsmack.webp)

Godsmack has announced a new series of U.S. tour dates for October, extending its ongoing 2026 “Rise of Rock” tour with performances across the country.

The newly announced leg begins on October 9 in Airway Heights, Washington, and will continue through cities such as Minneapolis, Milwaukee, and Cincinnati, before wrapping up on October 24 at Mohegan Sun Arena in Uncasville, Connecticut. Dorothy will serve as the direct support act on all of the newly added dates.

The final stop in Uncasville carries added significance for the band, as Mohegan Sun Arena was the venue where Godsmack recorded its recently released live album during the final tour featuring the group’s longtime lineup.
