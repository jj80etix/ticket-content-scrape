# FKA twigs to lead Meltdown as brand extends across UK

## Summary

FKA twigs will curate the next edition of London's Meltdown Festival as the brand expands with a six-city grassroots run across England, Scotland and Wales.

### Key Points
- The 32nd Meltdown edition runs June 10–20, 2027 at Southbank Centre, with FKA twigs both curating and performing.
- Six additional curators will lead the new grassroots satellite editions across new UK markets.
- Programme artists are to be announced, reflecting twigs' stated "diverse influences, cultures, and collaborative spirit."
