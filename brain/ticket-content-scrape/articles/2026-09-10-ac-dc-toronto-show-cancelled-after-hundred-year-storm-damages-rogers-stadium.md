# AC/DC Toronto show cancelled after ‘hundred year storm’ damages Rogers Stadium

## Summary

AC/DC's September 16 concert at Toronto's Rogers Stadium was cancelled after a "hundred-year storm" caused extensive structural damage, including collapsed seating blocks; the city issued an Unsafe Building Order and a replacement venue could not be secured in time.

### Key Points
- Storm damage included whole blocks of seating collapsing at Rogers Stadium.
- Toronto issued an Unsafe Building Order for the venue.
- A last-minute search for a replacement venue was unsuccessful.
