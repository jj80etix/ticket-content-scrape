# FLO Announces 2027 ‘Therapy at the Club’ Tour With Biggest Headline Shows Yet

## Summary

British R&B trio FLO announced a 22-date 2027 "Therapy at the Club" tour spanning North America, Europe and the UK, including their first headline arena show at Wembley. The Live Nation-promoted run opens Jan. 22 in Seattle and is billed as the biggest U.S. headline tour by a British girl group in nearly two decades.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Jan. 22, 2027 at Seattle's Moore Theatre and closes March 12 at OVO Arena Wembley.
- Stops include YouTube Theater (LA), Radio City Music Hall (NYC), and The Anthem (DC).
- Follows FLO's sophomore album Therapy at the Club, their second consecutive UK Top 5 album.
- Artist presale begins Oct. 7; general onsale begins Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/flo-graphic.webp)

FLO will take *Therapy at the Club* on the road in 2027 with a 22-date tour spanning North America, Europe and the UK, including some of the British R&B trio’s largest headline venues to date.

The Live Nation-promoted Therapy at the Club Tour opens Jan. 22 at the Moore Theatre in Seattle and runs through 17 North American dates before moving to Europe in March. Among the biggest stops are YouTube Theater in Los Angeles, Radio City Music Hall in New York and The Anthem in Washington, D.C., before the tour concludes March 12 with FLO’s first headline arena show at OVO Arena Wembley in London.

Promotional materials for the tour also bill the run as the biggest U.S. headline tour by a British girl group in nearly two decades. The Wembley finale, meanwhile, is being billed as FLO’s largest headline show to date.

“We’ve put our whole hearts into this album and show, so we can’t wait to share it with you,” FLO said in announcing the tour.

The new routing represents another step up from the group’s [2025 Access All Areas North American tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/11/flo-announces-all-access-areas-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which TicketNews previously covered ahead of a run through venues including Brooklyn Paramount, The Salt Shed in Chicago and the Hollywood Palladium. The 2027 itinerary moves FLO into marquee rooms including Radio City Music Hall and YouTube Theater while culminating with the group’s arena-headlining debut at Wembley.

The tour follows the August release of FLO’s sophomore album, *Therapy at the Club*. The record became the trio’s second consecutive UK Top 5 album following 2024 debut *Access All Areas*, while also producing the group’s strongest first-week sales to date in the UK.

Artist presales begin Wednesday, Oct. 7 at 10 a.m. local time, with additional North American and UK/European local presales beginning Thursday. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin Friday, Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. local time. Ticket and presale information is available through [FLO’s official website](https://www.flolikethis.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
