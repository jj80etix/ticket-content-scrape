# Atlético de Madrid sets season ticket sales record

## Summary

Spanish LaLiga club Atlético de Madrid set a season-ticket sales record for 2026-27, with over 61,500 season-ticket holders after 98% of general-admission renewals were retained. The remaining seats sold out immediately through the club's waiting list, though some VIP season tickets remain available.

### Key Points
- 61,500+ total season-ticket holders: 55,929 general admission plus 5,571 VIP, against a 68,456-capacity Riyadh Air Metropolitano.
- 98% general-admission renewal rate left only 1,200 seats open, which sold out via the waiting list.
- Members without season tickets can still access five- and ten-game packs (excluding Real Madrid and Barcelona matches).
- Follows Atlético's new five-year Fever ticketing/fan-experience partnership and a 10-year Live Nation España concert deal announced in July.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Spanish LaLiga club Atlético de Madrid has set a season ticket sales record for 2026-27, with over 61,500 season ticket holders.**

The club said that loyalty from fans has been reflected in the high number of renewals, with 98% of general admission season ticket holders last season renewing for the upcoming campaign. This means that only 1,200 seats are remaining.

These seats were then offered to the first members on the waiting list and are now sold out.

In total, 55,929 season general admission season tickets have been allocated, and 5,571 sold in VIP areas. The club believes this VIP total is still yet to grow, with VIP season tickets still available. However, all corporate suites and VIP boxes are sold out.

Atlético’s home ground Riyadh Air Metropolitano has a capacity of 68,456 spectators.

Members that were unable to secure season tickets for general admission seating still have access to other options such as five- and ten-game packs for LaLiga, which allow members to select which games they wish to attend (except for matches against Real Madrid and Barcelona) in three sections in the west stand.

Last month, Atlético [named Fever](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/07/atletico-de-madrid-adds-fever-in-official-ticketing-fan-experience-role/) as an official ticketing and fan experience partner for the next five seasons.

The club also agreed a decade-long deal with Live Nation España [in July](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/07/atletico-agrees-10-year-concert-deal-with-live-nation/), to host more concerts and events at the Metropolitano, as well as its new Ciudad del Deporte complex.
