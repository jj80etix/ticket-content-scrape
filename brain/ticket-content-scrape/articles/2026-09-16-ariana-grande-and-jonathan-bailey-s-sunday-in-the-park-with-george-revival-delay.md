# Ariana Grande and Jonathan Bailey’s ‘Sunday in the Park With George’ Revival Delayed

## Summary

A planned 2027 London revival of "Sunday in the Park With George" at the Barbican Centre has been called off after stars Ariana Grande and Jonathan Bailey withdrew. Producer Empire Street Productions retains the rights and may stage the revival in 2028 instead.

### Key Points
- The revival was set for the Barbican Centre in 2027 and is now scrapped
- Grande and Bailey, previously paired in the Wicked films, both exited the production
- Empire Street Productions may bring the revival to stage in 2028

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/Sunday-in-the-Park-with-George.webp)

A planned London revival of “Sunday in the Park With George” will not move forward in 2027 after stars Ariana Grande and Jonathan Bailey withdrew from the production.

Empire Street Productions announced that the Stephen Sondheim and James Lapine musical would no longer be presented as scheduled at the Barbican Centre. The company said it will retain the production rights and may bring the revival to the stage in 2028. Additional information is expected at a later date.

The cancellation follows the departures of Grande and Bailey, who were set to portray Dot and George, respectively. Their casting would have reunited the actors following their appearances in the two-part film adaptation of *Wicked*.

Grande left the revival in August as she prepared to take an extended break from public-facing work after completing her Eternal Sunshine Tour. A representative said the singer and actor wanted time away following sustained public attention focused on her health and appearance.

Bailey announced his withdrawal from “Sunday in the Park With George” on September 1. In a statement shared through social media, the actor described Grande as one of his closest friends and said his support for her remained firm. He also praised director Marianne Elliott, a longtime collaborator, before confirming his decision to leave the show.
