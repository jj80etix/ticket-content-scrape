# Rodney Atkins, David Leonard Lead 2026 Hamilton County Fair

## Summary

Country star Rodney Atkins will headline the 2026 Little Debbie Hamilton County Fair (Nov. 6–8) in Sale Creek, Tennessee, with GRAMMY-nominated David Leonard leading a Sunday Christian-music lineup. The event bundles concerts with rodeo, carnival rides, and agricultural exhibits under a single three-day admission price.

### Key Points
- Fair runs Nov. 6–8 at McDonald Farm, Sale Creek, TN; Atkins headlines Saturday, David Leonard/Amber Carrington lead Sunday's Christian-music bill.
- Three-day passes bundle concert admission with rides, rodeo, and attractions — notable among Tennessee county fairs for including major rides in the base ticket price.
- Discounted tickets available for veterans, active military, seniors, first responders, and teachers; children 10 and under free; parking free.
- Tickets on sale now via the fair's official website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/IMG_0770.jpeg)

Country star Rodney Atkins is heading to Tennessee this fall as the headlining act for the 2026 Little Debbie Hamilton County Fair, which returns to McDonald Farm in Sale Creek from Nov. 6-8.

Atkins will take the Dr Pepper Stage on Saturday, Nov. 7, with Hamilton County native Brandon Davis opening the show. Sunday’s Christian music lineup will feature GRAMMY-nominated and Dove Award-winning artist David Leonard alongside *The Voice* semifinalist Amber Carrington.

Presented by Little Debbie, the annual fair blends live entertainment with agriculture, family attractions, and hometown traditions. This year’s event also marks a milestone as much of McDonald Farm is set to become Tennessee’s newest state park.

“We’re proud to welcome families back to the Little Debbie Hamilton County Fair for another year of celebrating our shared traditions and the future of our county,” Hamilton County Mayor Weston Wamp said in a statement. “With much of McDonald Farm set to become Tennessee’s newest state park, this year’s fair marks an exciting new chapter for Sale Creek and all of North Hamilton County.”
