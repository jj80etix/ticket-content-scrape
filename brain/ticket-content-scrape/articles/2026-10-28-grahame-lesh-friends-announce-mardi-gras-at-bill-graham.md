# Grahame Lesh & Friends Announce Mardi Gras At Bill Graham

## Summary

Grahame Lesh & Friends will headline a one-night Mardi Gras concert at San Francisco's Bill Graham Civic Auditorium on Feb. 6, 2027, featuring Warren Haynes, Neal Francis, Cyril Neville, John Molo and the Preservation Hall Jazz Band, continuing the Grateful Dead/Phil Lesh family's longstanding Mardi Gras concert tradition at the venue. Venue presale begins Oct. 28, general onsale Oct. 30.

### Key Points
- One-night Mardi Gras show Feb. 6, 2027, at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium, San Francisco.
- Lineup includes Warren Haynes, Neal Francis, Cyril Neville, John Molo, Preservation Hall Jazz Band.
- Continues a tradition from the Grateful Dead and Phil Lesh & Friends' prior Mardi Gras shows at the same venue (2005, 2008).
- Venue presale Oct. 28 at 10 a.m. PT; general onsale Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. PT.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/unnamed.png)

Grahame Lesh & Friends will take over San Francisco’s Bill Graham Civic Auditorium for a special one-night-only Mardi Gras concert next February.

An all-star lineup of musicians will perform at the Northern California venue on Feb. 6, including Warren Haynes, Neal Francis, Cyril Neville, John Molo and Preservation Hall Jazz Band, and additional special guests will later be announced.

The special event draws from the Grateful Dead’s longstanding Mardi Gras tradition of performing Bay Area shows in the 1980s and ’90s, as well as Phil Lesh and Friends’ Mardi Gras shows at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium in 2005 and 2008 and Phil’s Terrapin Crossroads venue in the 2010s. Each show featured special guests and memorable collaborations.

A venue presale begins Oct. 28 at 10 a.m. PT, followed by the general onsale Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. PT. Visit [grahamelesh.com](https://www.grahamelesh.com/) for additional information.

Lesh continues to honor and reinterpret the Grateful Dead through his live performances and new recordings. He recently released his latest single “Jupiter,” which originated from an unfinished concept connected to Phil Lesh’s Planet Jams.

Grahame Lesh & Friends are embarking on a headline tour this week, including three sold-out shows at Masonic Lounge at Hollywood Forever in Los Angeles, and the band will perform New Year’s Eve shows at The Parker in Fort Lauderdale, Florida.
