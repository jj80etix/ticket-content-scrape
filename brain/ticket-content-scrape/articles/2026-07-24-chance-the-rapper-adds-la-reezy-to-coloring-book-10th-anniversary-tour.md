# Chance The Rapper Adds La Reezy To ‘Coloring Book 10th Anniversary Tour’

## Summary

Chance the Rapper has added New Orleans rising artist La Reezy as support for most dates on his 41-date "Coloring Book 10th Anniversary Tour," a Live Nation-promoted North American run celebrating the album's 10th anniversary. The tour kicks off Aug. 11 in Cleveland and runs through mid-October, closing in Pittsburgh.

### Key Points
- Tour promoted by Live Nation; 41 dates from Aug. 11 to Oct. 11, 2026, across the US and Canada.
- La Reezy joins all dates except the Aug. 28 Virginia Beach show.
- La Reezy released his debut album *Skiddle Bandana* in July and was named to the 2026 XXL Freshman Class.
- Tickets on sale now via ChanceStuff.com.

Chance the Rapper may be celebrating a past landmark album with the “Coloring Book 10th Anniversary Tour,” but he is also looking towards the future. The Chicago native has tapped New Orleans upstart La Reezy to join him on the trek.

The North American tour, promoted by Live Nation, kicks off on Aug. 11 at Cleveland, OH’s House of Blues. The 41-date tour continues throughout the remainder of summer and into the fall, hitting major cities like New York City, Philadelphia, PA, Toronto, ON, New Orleans, LA, Atlanta, GA, and more before finally concluding in Pittsburgh, PA at Citizens Live at The Wylie. La Reezy will support all tour dates except for the Virginia Beach show on Friday, Aug. 28th.

Tickets on sale now at [ChanceStuff.com](https://chancestuff.com/)

La Reezy has been having a banner year. The young emcee released his debut album *Skiddle Bandana* in July and was also inducted into the 2026 XXL Freshman Class. With a sound greatly influenced by his southern roots, the 21-year-old has earned co-signs from some of the industry’s heavy hitters, including Jon Batiste, Master P, Tyler, the Creator, J. Cole, and Kendrick Lamar.
