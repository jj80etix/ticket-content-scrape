# AEG and Japan’s Creativeman strike major partnership

## Summary

AEG Presents has struck a strategic partnership with Japanese promoter Creativeman Productions, Asia's largest independent music promoter, timed just ahead of Creativeman's flagship Summer Sonic festival's 25th-anniversary edition. The deal builds on AEG's push into the Japanese market — following AXS's October 2025 partnership with local ticketer Lawson Entertainment — and is described as opening the door for AXS to further expand ticketing operations in the region. AEG frames the tie-up as connecting Summer Sonic into its global festival portfolio (Coachella, BST Hyde Park), alongside its broader Japanese venue investments, including new arenas in Nagoya and Osaka.

### Key Points
- Partnership pairs AEG Presents with Creativeman Productions (founded 1990, Tokyo), positioned as opening the door for AXS to expand ticketing operations in Japan and the wider region.
- Follows AXS's October 2025 partnership with local ticketer Lawson Entertainment — a second consecutive Japan market-entry move via local partner rather than acquisition.
- Timed ahead of Summer Sonic's 25th-anniversary edition (August 14-16, 2026), one of Asia's largest music festivals, spanning Osaka and Tokyo.
- AEG is separately building physical infrastructure in Japan: the new 17,000-cap IG Arena (Nagoya) and forthcoming 18,000-cap Osaka Arena.
- Deal extends AEG's regional-subsidiary model (Goldenvoice, CMN, Frontier Touring, Marshall Arts, MCT Agentur) into a new Asian promoter partnership.

### Highlights
- Second AXS market-entry move into Japan within about a year (Lawson Entertainment, Oct 2025; Creativeman, Aug 2026), indicating a deliberate expansion strategy via local ticketing/promoter partnerships rather than a single acquisition.
- AEG is pairing ticketing-platform expansion (AXS) with venue investment (two new Japanese arenas) and promoter/festival access (Creativeman, Summer Sonic) — a three-pronged regional consolidation play.
- Creativeman is described as Asia's largest independent music promoter, giving AXS/AEG a scale foothold rather than a niche entry.

### Industry Problem
Consumer-major ticketing platforms are consolidating regional footholds by pairing promoter and local-ticketer partnerships with venue investment, a market-entry pattern that independent primary platforms without international scale or promoter relationships can't easily replicate but should track for competitive positioning cues.

### Proposed Solution
No direct product response applies; watch for AXS's actual ticketing rollout in Japan (feature set, fee model, local competitive reaction) as the trigger for reassessing Q12/Q13 posture, and monitor whether Tier 1/Tier 2 competitors pursue similar promoter-plus-local-ticketer expansion patterns into other underpenetrated markets.

## AEG and Japan’s Creativeman strike major partnership

The promoter alliance news arrives ahead of Creativeman's marquee event, Summer Sonic, celebrating 25 years later this week

![Naoki Shimizu](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Naoki-Shimizu.png)

Creativeman CEO Naoki Shimizu

AEG Presents has announced a strategic partnership with leading Japanese promoter Creativeman Productions, strengthening its foothold in the Asian market.

In addition to combining the leading entertainment companies’ experience, the partnership has opened the door for AEG’s ticket platform AXS to expand in the market and region. It follows AXS’s partnership with local ticketer [Lawson Entertainment](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/10/axs-expands-ticketing-operations-in-japan/) last October.

Founded in 1990 in Tokyo, Creativeman promotes concerts, tours, and destination events that connect global and Japanese artists with audiences across the country, scaling to become one of the most influential promoters in Asia’s live industry under the direction of CEO Naoki Shimizu.

Alongside its promotion operation, Creativeman also owns and operates a diverse festival portfolio, including its marquee event, Summer Sonic, which is set for its 25th anniversary edition later this week (14-16 August).

“Over the past 37 years, Creativeman has grown from a company with just two staff members to become the largest independent music promoter in Asia,” Shimizu says. “This strategic partnership with AEG Presents will propel our growth as we expand our festivals and artists from Japan to Asia and the rest of the world.”

One of Asia’s largest music festivals, Summer Sonic pioneered the multi-city Japanese festival by bringing international and domestic acts across Osaka and Tokyo. In its history, the festival has hosted global stars including Radiohead, Oasis, Metallica, Linkin Park, Daft Punk, and the Red Hot Chili Peppers, with names like The Strokes, Jamiroquai, Jennie, and David Byrne set to headline this year’s event.

Shimizu adds: “The partnership will also connect Summer Sonic with a portfolio of world-class festivals and creates a network that joins Coachella in the US and BST Hyde Park in the UK, serving as a cornerstone for the music industry’s continued growth.”

> “Creativeman is an important presence in Japan and across the region”

Global leader AEG also has a world-class festival portfolio, which includes the US’s [Coachella](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/coachellas-25th-anniversary-edition-proves-momentous/) Valley Music & Arts Festival, [Stagecoach](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/05/stagecoach-sell-out-an-electric-year-for-the-country-fest/), New Orleans Jazz & Heritage Festival, the UK’s [BST Hyde Park](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/07/bst-hyde-park-caps-historic-year-for-london/), All Points East, [LIDO](https://www.iqmagazine.com/featured-list/green-guardians-2025/lido/), the new [Roundhay Festival](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/jim-king-on-building-roundhay-into-aegs-next-flagship-festival/), and France’s Rock En Seine and We Love Green.

Beyond Japan, Summer Sonic also expanded overseas into [Thailand](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/01/japans-summer-sonic-expands-to-bangkok/) in 2024, reinforcing its position as Asia’s premier international music festival. The company’s other events include Sonicmania, Punkspring, Greenroom Festival, Loud Park, Super Sonic, Springroove, and many others.

“Creativeman is an important presence in Japan and across the region,” says Shawn Trell, EVP and COO, AEG Presents. “The company’s vision and business strategy align perfectly with ours, and we’re thrilled to be entering into this partnership together.

“Beyond Creativeman’s thriving concert and festival business, Summer Sonic has become a globally recognised brand on its own. We can’t wait to see what the future brings,” Trell concludes.

One of the world’s biggest promoters, AEG has been behind global tours for artists including ATEEZ, Justin Bieber, Zach Bryan, Sabrina Carpenter, Elton John, Paul McCartney, The Rolling Stones, and Taylor Swift.

AEG has also been growing its expansive venue network with a pair of arenas in [Japan](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/will-japans-arena-building-frenzy-open-the-door-for-international-tours/): the new 17,000-capacity [IG Arena](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/breaking-the-nagoya-skip-ig-arena-on-goals-to-shake-up-japanese-arena-routing/) in Nagoya and the forthcoming 18,000-cap [Osaka Arena](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2021/05/aeg-build-18000-cap-arena-osaka/), which is set to be built in the same area as the city’s Summer Sonic.

The company also has a handful of regional subsidiaries and partner brands, including festival powerhouse Goldenvoice, Latin leader [Cárdenas Marketing Network](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/03/aeg-partners-with-cmn-to-create-latin-music-giant/) (CMN), Australia’s [Frontier Touring](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2019/04/aeg-presents-announces-jv-frontier-touring/), the UK’s Marshall Arts, and Germany’s [MCT Agentur](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/12/aeg-presents-partners-with-germanys-mct-agentur/).
