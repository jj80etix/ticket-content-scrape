# Broadway’s ‘Operation Mincemeat’ Extends Engagement Through July 2027

## Summary

Broadway's "Operation Mincemeat" has extended its run at the Golden Theatre through July 4, 2027 — its 12th extension since opening in March 2025. The WWII musical comedy, originally a hit on London's West End, continues to run alongside a North American tour.

### Key Points
- 12th extension since the March 20, 2025 Broadway opening.
- Written and composed by British comedy group SpitLip.
- World tour currently running across North America alongside the Broadway production.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/Operation-Mincemeat.webp)

“Operation Mincemeat” has extended its Broadway engagement through July 4, 2027, keeping the World War II musical comedy at the Golden Theatre into next summer. The announcement marks the production’s 12th extension since it opened in New York on March 20, 2025.

The musical arrived in New York following its Olivier Award-winning West End production and continues to maintain a presence on both sides of the Atlantic, with a recently launched world tour currently traveling across North America.

“Operation Mincemeat” was written and composed by SpitLip, the British comedy group whose members are David Cumming, Felix Hagan, Natasha Hodgson and Zoë Roberts. Their musical brings a comic approach to its historical subject, using an ensemble in which performers take on multiple characters throughout the production.

The current Broadway company includes Tony Award nominee Julia Knitel as Ewen Montagu and other roles. Jeff Kready plays Hester Leggatt and additional characters, while Brandon Contreras appears as Charles Cholmondeley and others.

Jessi Kirtley portrays Jean Leslie and other roles, and Amanda Jill Robinson plays Johnny Bevan and additional characters. Together, the five performers make up the principal cast currently appearing at the Golden Theatre.

Robert Hastie directs the musical, with choreography by Jenny Arnold. Olivier Award nominee Ben Stones is responsible for both set and costume design. Lighting design is by Mark Henderson, a Tony and Olivier Award winner, and sound design is by Tony Award nominee Mike Walker.

The musical team includes Tony Award nominee Steve Sidwell, who provides the orchestrations. Olivier Award nominee Joe Bunker handles music direction and supervision, overseeing those elements of the production alongside the broader creative team. Karen Moore serves as production stage manager, and Carrie Gardner is the casting director.
