# Tour news: Hayley Williams and Sombr unveil European and UK runs

## Summary

IQ's tour-news roundup covers Hayley Williams adding an eight-date UK/Europe leg (starting Milan's Carroponte, June 7, 2027) to her ongoing solo tour, alongside briefly-noted new date announcements from Sombr, Tomorrow X Together, Take That, Miley Cyrus, and Kneecap.

### Key Points
- Hayley Williams adds 8 UK/Europe dates to The Hayley Williams Show tour for summer 2027, opening at Milan's Carroponte (June 7) before Rome, Brussels, Paris, Amsterdam, and Glasgow (Ovo Hydro/Utilita Arena) among other stops.
- Roundup also references new tour announcements from Sombr, Tomorrow X Together, Take That, Miley Cyrus, and Kneecap (details paywalled).
