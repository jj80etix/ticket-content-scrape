# Jason Isbell and Drive-By Truckers Announce 39-Date 2027 Reunion Tour

## Summary

Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit are reuniting with Drive-By Truckers — the band Isbell left in 2007 — for a 39-date 2027 "Summer of Joy Tour" running May 6 (Nashville) through Sept. 7 (Tulsa), including stops at Red Rocks, Forest Hills Stadium and the Greek Theatre. $1 per ticket benefits musician mental-health nonprofit Nuçi's Space; artist presale begins Nov. 18, with general onsale Nov. 20.

### Key Points
- 39-date co-headlining tour, May 6-Sept. 7, 2027; follows informal reunions at Isbell's ShoalsFest and a 2014 benefit show.
- Stops include Red Rocks, Forest Hills Stadium, the Greek Theatre, and two Fox Theatre (Atlanta) nights.
- $1/ticket benefits Nuçi's Space, an Athens, GA musician mental-health nonprofit.
- Artist presale Nov. 18; Live Nation presale Nov. 19; general onsale Nov. 20.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/jason-isbell-drive-by-truckers-1200.webp)

Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit are joining forces with Drive-By Truckers for a 39-date North American tour in 2027, bringing the acclaimed singer-songwriter back on the road alongside the band that helped launch his career.

Dubbed the **Summer of Joy Tour**, the co-headlining run will reunite Isbell with Drive-By Truckers, where he played guitar and contributed songwriting from 2001 until his departure in 2007.

![StubHub](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/StubHub-desktop-1.webp)

The tour is slated to open May 6 in Nashville, Tennessee, at The Pinnacle, with a second performance at the venue the following evening. The initial May dates include stops in New Orleans, St. Louis, Denver and Kansas City, before the tour resumes in July with two nights at Atlanta’s Fox Theatre.

Additional performances are scheduled at Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, Forest Hills Stadium in New York, The Salt Shed in Chicago, the Greek Theatre in Los Angeles and several other venues across the United States and Canada. The tour will conclude September 7 at Cain’s Ballroom in Tulsa, Oklahoma.

The announcement follows a series of reunion performances involving Isbell and his former bandmates Patterson Hood and Mike Cooley. The three musicians performed together at a benefit concert in Florence, Alabama, in 2014, and Isbell subsequently reunited with Drive-By Truckers for additional appearances.

Most recently, Isbell joined the group at his ShoalsFest event in Alabama in October 2026, where they performed Drive-By Truckers’ 2003 album *Decoration Day* in its entirety.

That record helped establish Isbell’s songwriting reputation with tracks including “Outfit” and the title song. Following his departure from Drive-By Truckers, Isbell built a successful solo career alongside his work with the 400 Unit, earning six Grammy Awards and becoming one of Americana’s most prominent performers.

His recent touring schedule has included an extensive [2026 North American tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/10/jason-isbell-the-400-unit-unveil-2026-north-american-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and his annual [residency at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/jason-isbell-tickets-on-sale-for-nashville-ryman-residency/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Isbell also released the stripped-down acoustic album *Foxes in the Snow* in 2025, while Drive-By Truckers’ most recent studio album, *Welcome 2 Club XIII*, arrived in 2022.

The Summer of Joy Tour will also support mental health services for musicians, with $1 from every ticket sold benefiting Nuçi’s Space. The Athens, Georgia-based nonprofit provides mental health resources, suicide prevention services and support programs for musicians.

Tickets for most performances will be available through an artist presale beginning Wednesday, November 18, at 10 a.m. local time. A Live Nation presale follows for select dates on Thursday, November 19, ahead of the general public onsale Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register for presale access through [Isbell’s official website](https://www.jasonisbell.com/shows?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), although individual venues may have different ticketing arrangements and onsale dates.
