# Dylan Gossett Unveils Spring 2027 US Leg Of ‘The Ramblin’ Tour’

## Summary

Singer-songwriter Dylan Gossett announced the US leg of his "Ramblin'" Tour for spring 2027, running March 25 through May 21 across theaters and mid-size venues, following the October release of his sophomore album. Tickets go through a tiered presale (artist presale Oct. 5, Citi presale Oct. 5) ahead of general on-sale Oct. 6.

### Key Points
- US leg runs March 25 (Charleston, SC) through May 21 (Tulsa, OK).
- Follows the October release of his second album, "Ramblin'," via Big Loud Texas/Mercury Records.
- Artist presale begins Oct. 5, Citi presale follows the same day, general on-sale Oct. 6.

Dylan Gossett, the multi-platinum singer, songwriter, and producer, has unveiled his US headline tour set for spring 2027.

The US leg of the tour will begin on March 25 at the Charleston Music Hall in Charleston, SC. Gossett will then travel to The Signal in Chattanooga, TN; the Ozark Music Hall in Fayetteville, AR; The Fillmore in Detroit, MI; the Bayou Music Center in Houston, TX,; and more. The tour will then end on May 21 at the Cain’s Ballroom in Tulsa, OK. See the full list of newly announced dates below and the current 2026 tour as well.

Tickets will be available starting with an artist pre-sale beginning on Oct. 5 at 10 a.m. local time followed by a CITI presale at 12 p.m. local time. Additional presales will run ahead of the general on-sale beginning on Oct. 6 at 10 a.m. local time at [https://www.dylangossett.com/tour/](https://www.dylangossett.com/tour/).

Last week, Gossett released his critically acclaimed sophomore album, *Ramblin* ’, via Big Loud Texas / Mercury Records.
