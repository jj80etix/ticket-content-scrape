# DEAG lifts nine-month revenue while EBITDA falls 27%

## Summary

German live-entertainment group DEAG reported nine-month 2026 revenue up 4.3% year-on-year to €336.8m, but EBITDA fell 27% to €13.8m, which it attributes to spending on new event formats and continued ticketing digitalization/automation investment. The company says it remains on track for full-year guidance; the report also notes DEAG was acquired by Enhanced Group earlier in November. DEAG sold 9.6m tickets in the period, mostly through its own platforms (myticket.de/at/co.uk, gigantic.com).

### Key Points
- Nine-month revenue: €336.8m (+4.3% YoY); EBITDA: €13.8m, down 27% from €18.8m a year earlier.
- Q3 2026 revenue €179.4m vs €167.4m prior-year; Q3 EBITDA €7.3m vs €12.2m prior-year.
- DEAG was acquired by Enhanced Group earlier this month (November 2026) — a consolidation move alongside its existing "buy-and-build" strategy.
- 9.6m tickets sold in the period, mostly via DEAG's own ticketing platforms rather than third parties.

### Industry Problem
A mid-size European promoter/ticketing group is absorbing margin pressure from platform digitalization/automation investment at the same time it is being acquired — illustrating the consolidation pressure facing independent ticketing-and-promotion operators who must fund technology modernization while competing with larger-scale platforms.

### Proposed Solution
No remedy proposed in the article. For Etix: monitor the Enhanced Group acquisition and DEAG's continued buy-and-build activity as a data point on European ticketing-sector consolidation — a trigger for a posture change would be Enhanced Group using the DEAG platforms to expand into new markets or integrate with other owned ticketing assets.

German promoter DEAG has reported a 4.3% year-on-year increase in revenue to €336.8m for the first nine months of 2026, although EBITDA fell by 27% to €13.8m in the same period.

The decline compares this EBITDA number for January to September 2026 with €18.8m for the same period in 2025, a reduction of €5m.

Despite this, the German live entertainment group said it remained on track to meet its full-year guidance. Third-quarter revenue increased to €179.4m, compared with €167.4m in the prior-year period, while Q3 EBITDA dropped to €7.3m from €12.2m.

DEAG attributed the pressure on profitability partly to expenditure on developing new event formats and establishing them in the market, alongside continued investment in digitalisation and automation, particularly across its ticketing operations. It also comes as the company was [acquired by Enhanced Group](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/11/deag-to-be-acquired-by-enhanced-group/) earlier this month.

The company sold 9.6m tickets during the first nine months, with the majority processed through its own platforms, including myticket.de, myticket.at, myticket.co.uk and gigantic.com.

> “DEAG is on track for a solid full-year performance in 2026”

DEAG continued to pursue a “buy-and-build strategy”, it said, such as in acquiring the remaining minority interests in touring promoter Wizard Live during the reporting period.

Following the reporting period, DEAG also acquired the remaining shares in handwerker promotion, having held a stake in the company since 2013. It also increased its interests in UK businesses MyTicket Services and KMJ Entertainment by 10%, securing options to acquire their remaining shares.

The results come as Markus Breitenecker takes over as Group CEO, succeeding Detlef Kornett, who [stepped down](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/markus-breitenecker-to-succeed-detleff-kornett-as-deag-ceo/) after 13 years in September. Breitenecker said of the results: “DEAG is on track for a solid full-year performance in 2026 and has good prospects. In an increasingly digital world, live experiences remain particularly important to many people and continue to grow in relevance.”

The CEO added: “Through our strong organic performance and the consistent implementation of our buy-and-build strategy, we are continuously expanding our market position. Detlef Kornett has played a pivotal role in shaping this process in recent years, and I would like to thank him sincerely for his contribution. With an experienced team and strong partners within the Group, we will continue to build on DEAG’s success with determination.”
