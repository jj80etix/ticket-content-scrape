# Top Tours: $uicideboy$ Join Live75 With Ticket Sales From ‘Grey Day’ North American Tour

## Summary

$uicideboy$ debuted at No. 13 on Pollstar's LIVE75 chart off their first 10 "Grey Day Tour" shows, selling 152,339 tickets for $10.4 million in grosses; The Weeknd and BTS held the top Global Concert Pulse spots.

### Key Points
- First 10 shows averaged 15,233 tickets and $1.04M gross per night.
- Dallas-Fort Worth produced the tour's largest crowd so far (19,216 at Dos Equis Pavilion).
- 2026 tour pace trails the act's full-year 2025 (585,272 tickets/$37.3M) and 2024 (521,165/$56.7M) totals.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 1248830338](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-1248830338.jpg)

Ruby Da Cherry and $crim of $uicideboy$ perform live on stage at the Verti Music Hall on March 19, 2023 in Berlin, Germany. (Photo by Pedro Becerra/Redferns)

$uicideboy$ debuts on LIVE75 this week with box-office sales reported from the first 10 concerts on the duo’s seventh annual “Grey Day Tour.” Produced by Live Nation, the trek through North American cities kicked off on Aug. 29 with 34 headlining shows planned through October, primarily at outdoor amphitheaters, along with one festival appearance. The lineup of show openers this year includes Destroy Lonely, Shoreline Mafia, Shakewell, DRAIN, Black Kray and $lim Gucci on select dates throughout the run.

A crowd of 16,072 fans were in attendance for the tour’s sold-out opening performance at Mystic Lake Amphitheater in Shakopee, Minnesota. Together, the first 10 shows moved a total of 152,339 sold tickets, an average of 15,233 per night. That figure lands the band at No. 13 on LIVE75, the highest ranking among concert headliners making their debut on the chart.

Grosses from the first 10 shows reached $10.4 million overall, averaging $1.04 million per show which earns a debut appearance on Global Concert Pulse as well. The group takes the No. 35 position on that chart with the second highest debut as Zac Brown Band also enters the lineup this week at No. 32.

The Dallas-Fort Worth market produced the largest crowd on the “Grey Day Tour” so far with 19,216 tickets purchased at Dos Equis Pavilion on Sep. 1. Two amphitheaters topped 17,000 in attendance as 17,804 tickets were sold at Charlotte’s Truliant Amphitheater and 17,031 at Darien Lake Performing Arts Center in Darien Center, New York. Then, The Pavilion at Star Lake in Burgettstown, Pennsylvania sold 16,483 tickets to join Mystic Lake Amphitheater as the only two sheds to land in the 16,000-ticket range for one performance. All 10 venues topped the 10,000 mark in ticket sales.

Seattle’s Climate Pledge Arena, the first of four indoor arenas on the tour, hosted the band on Sep. 28, while Save Mart Center in Fresno, California is on the books for Oct. 6. Smoothie King Center in the group’s hometown of New Orleans will host the tour on Oct. 14, and Amerant Bank Arena in Sunrise, Florida is booked on Oct. 18. Talking Stick Resort Amphitheatre in Phoenix and First Financial Credit Union Amphitheater in Albuquerque both have back-to-back shows planned in mid-October, and the finale is set for Oct. 20 at Coca-Cola Amphitheater in Birmingham, Alabama.

Last year, $uicideboy$ averaged 13,006 tickets per show in North America, selling a total of 585,272 tickets at 45 concerts with a gross of $37.3 million for the year. And in 2024, ticket sales also surpassed half a million, reaching 521,165 from 50 shows, while grosses that year hit $56.7 million. *Pollstar* ’s Boxoffice archives show that career concert grosses for the duo currently stand at $182.4 million from 2,421,543 sold tickets at 282 headlining performances during the past decade.

Elsewhere on the charts, The Weekend remains at No. 1 for another week, based on a sold-ticket average of 61,729 from 13 stadium performances on his “After Hours til Dawn Tour.” Likewise, BTS also repeats at No. 1 on Global Concert Pulse with an average gross per show of $13.9 million. The South Korean septet’s 12-show gross average earns the top ranking on the chart for a 15 <sup>th</sup> time this year.

On the Artist Power Index, Bruno Mars maintains his hold on the top ranking, but joining the top 10 is Olivia Rodrigo who debuts on the chart following the launch of “The Unraveled Tour,” her third headlining trek that kicked off on Sep. 25. Supporting her third studio album *You Seem Pretty Sad for a Girl So in Love*, released in June, the tour began in Hartford, Connecticut at PeoplesBank Arena. She will remain on the road in North America through Dec. 20, wrapping for the year at T-Mobile Arena in Las Vegas.
