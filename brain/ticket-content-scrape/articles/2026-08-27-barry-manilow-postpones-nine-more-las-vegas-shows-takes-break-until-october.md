# Barry Manilow Postpones Nine More Las Vegas Shows, Takes Break Until October

## Summary

Barry Manilow is postponing nine more Las Vegas residency shows and taking a break from performing until October, extending a pause that began with an earlier pair of cancellations tied to technical/vocal issues. The postponed dates will reportedly be rescheduled, though new dates have not yet been announced.

### Key Points
- Nine additional Westgate Las Vegas residency shows postponed; Manilow will not perform again until October.
- Follows an earlier cancellation of two shows for technical/vocal problems.
- No new dates announced yet for the postponed shows.
- Affected ticket holders are expected to be notified about rescheduling/refund options.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Barry-Manilow.webp)

Barry Manilow is stepping away from the stage until October, extending a run of recent concert disruptions by postponing nine additional performances from his Las Vegas residency.

The 83-year-old singer announced that he will take a break following a demanding summer tour that included 29 performances over 68 days. Manilow said the shows and audiences had been strong, but acknowledged that “we were pushing a little too hard.”

Manilow now plans to return to the International Theater at Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino on October 8.

The announcement means three performances previously scheduled for August 27-29 and six September shows scheduled for September 17-19 and September 24-26 will no longer take place as originally planned. Those nine dates were still listed on Manilow’s 2026 residency schedule before his announcement.

New Westgate concert dates are expected to be announced next week, suggesting at least some of the affected performances could be rescheduled rather than canceled outright.

“We’re going to take a break until we return to the Westgate Las Vegas on October 8th,” Manilow told fans, adding that he expects to be “singing like a bird” when he returns.

The latest schedule change comes just days after Manilow canceled a pair of performances at the same venue.

[As TicketNews previously reported](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/barry-manilow-cancels-pair-las-vegas-residency-shows/), Manilow canceled his August 21 and 22 residency dates after citing “technical and vocal problems.” An August 20 performance had also been interrupted by technical difficulties.

At the time of those cancellations, Manilow was still expected to return for the August 27-29 run, with another six September performances remaining on the calendar. His latest announcement wipes those dates from his immediate touring plans and gives the veteran performer more than a month away from the stage.

The run of disruptions follows what had otherwise been a busy return to touring for Manilow.

Westgate said in July that the singer had returned to the International Theater for three sold-out performances July 9-11. The shows marked a significant comeback following health-related interruptions earlier in the year.

Manilow revealed in December 2025 that doctors had discovered a cancerous spot on his left lung after he underwent testing following recurring bouts of bronchitis. The early-stage lung cancer required surgery and prompted him to postpone a number of planned performances while recovering.

TicketNews reported at the time that Manilow said the cancer had been detected early and that he intended to resume performing once medically cleared. He ultimately returned to the stage this summer and embarked on an intensive series of arena and residency performances.

The singer also postponed an August 4 concert in Lexington, Kentucky, shortly before showtime due to what his team described as “unforeseen circumstances.” That performance had previously been affected by his cancer treatment earlier in the year.

Manilow has not attributed his current break to a new medical diagnosis or complication from his cancer treatment. Instead, his announcement characterized the move as an opportunity to rest following the pace of his summer schedule.

His planned October 8 return would begin another stretch of Westgate residency performances, including shows October 8-10 and October 15-17. Additional dates are scheduled throughout November and December, including three December runs billed as part of his year-end Las Vegas schedule.

The residency has become one of the longest-running fixtures on the Las Vegas Strip. Manilow surpassed Elvis Presley’s record for performances at the International Theater in 2023 and subsequently signed what Westgate described as a lifetime residency agreement with the property.

For now, the October return remains intact, with Manilow indicating that more details about replacement dates for the affected Westgate performances should arrive in the coming days.
