# Harry Styles, Billie Eilish, Sam Fender Lead Sold-Out Glastonbury 2027 Lineup

## Summary

Glastonbury Festival unveiled its 2027 headliners — Sam Fender (Friday), Harry Styles (Saturday debut), and Billie Eilish (Sunday), with Cher in the Legends slot — for its return to Worthy Farm June 23–27 after a scheduled 2026 year off. Both the October coach-package and general-admission on-sales sold out within roughly 30–42 minutes despite a record £408 admission price, 51% above the 2020 level; Glastonbury sells exclusively through See Tickets using personalized, photo-ID, strictly nontransferable tickets that block conventional resale.

### Key Points
- 2027 headliners: Fender (Fri), Styles (Sat, festival debut), Eilish (Sun, second headline slot); Cher takes the Sunday Legends slot.
- Festival returns to Worthy Farm June 23–27, 2027, after a fallow 2026.
- Coach-package and GA on-sales both sold out in well under an hour in October despite record pricing.
- £408 admission (incl. £5 booking fee) is 51% above the £270 price set for the canceled 2020 edition.
- Tickets are sold exclusively via See Tickets, personalized to the buyer with a photograph, and strictly nontransferable.

### Highlights
- See Tickets (Q12 Tier-1 primary-platform competitor) runs Glastonbury's ticketing with photo-bound, nontransferable tickets as its anti-resale mechanism.
- GA on-sale exhausted its allocation in ~42 minutes even at a record £408 price point.
- Nontransferability blocks resale outright rather than relying on price caps or soft policy.

### Industry Problem
High-demand onsales continue to sell out in minutes even as prices climb, and incumbent primary platforms are leaning on hard identity-binding (photo ID, no transfer) rather than price-cap policy to keep inventory off secondary marketplaces.

### Proposed Solution
See Tickets' approach is pure identity-bound nontransferability with no resale allowance at all; for Etix, this is a benchmark for how a Tier-1 primary peer defends high-demand onsales against secondary leakage, and worth evaluating as an option for comparable high-demand events.

## Glastonbury Tickets Sold Out Before Lineup Announcement

The lineup arrives more than six weeks after Glastonbury 2027 tickets sold out despite reaching a record £408 for general admission, meaning ticket buyers committed to attending without knowing which artists would headline.

The initial ticket-and-coach packages sold out in less than 30 minutes on Oct. 1, followed by a general admission sale on Oct. 4 that exhausted its allocation in approximately 42 minutes.

As [TicketNews reported when the 2027 ticket prices were announced](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/glastonbury-2027-ticket-prices-record-408/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the £408 admission price includes a £5 booking fee but excludes postage and packing. It represents a £29.50 increase over the £378.50 charged for the 2025 festival.

The latest price also stands 51% above the £270 admission price established for the planned 2020 festival, which was subsequently canceled because of the pandemic.

The rapid October sellouts demonstrated that the higher asking price had not prevented another intense competition for tickets. As TicketNews [documented following the initial coach package sale](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/glastonbury-2027-coach-tickets-sell-out/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the festival’s ticketing model differs substantially from most major concerts and festivals.

Glastonbury sells tickets exclusively through See Tickets, with each admission personalized to the registered buyer and featuring the holder’s photograph. Tickets are strictly nontransferable, preventing buyers from reselling or transferring admission to another person through conventional secondary marketplaces.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit the official [Glastonbury Festival website](https://www.glastonburyfestivals.co.uk/).
