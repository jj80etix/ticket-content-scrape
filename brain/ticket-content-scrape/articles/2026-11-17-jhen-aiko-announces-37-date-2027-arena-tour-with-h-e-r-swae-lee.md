# Jhené Aiko Announces 37-Date 2027 Arena Tour With H.E.R., Swae Lee

## Summary

Jhené Aiko announced a 37-date 2027 arena tour, "Ark to Agartha: A Westside Whimsy Experience," running March 10 (St. Louis) to May 21 (Los Angeles), with Swae Lee and Karri on every date and H.E.R. joining from March 26 onward. The run follows her Billboard 200 No. 1 album Westside Whimsy; artist presale opened Nov. 17, with a Verizon Shine presale and general onsale (Live Nation) following Nov. 18 and Nov. 20 respectively.

### Key Points
- 37-date tour, March 10-May 21, 2027; Swae Lee/Karri on all dates, H.E.R. joins from March 26 (Brooklyn) onward.
- Follows Westside Whimsy, Aiko's first No. 1 on the Billboard 200 and the first female R&B album to top the chart in 2026.
- Artist presale registration opened Nov. 17; artist presale begins Nov. 18, Verizon presale same day; general onsale via Live Nation Nov. 20.
- Follows her sold-out 2024 Magic Hour Tour.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/jhene-aiko-agartha-tour-announcement-1200.webp)

Jhené Aiko has announced a 37-date North American arena tour for 2027, bringing her *Ark to Agartha: A Westside Whimsy Experience* production to venues across the United States and Canada. The Live Nation-produced trek will feature Swae Lee and Karri at every stop, with H.E.R. joining for select dates.

The tour is slated to open March 10 at Enterprise Center in St. Louis, followed by stops in Minneapolis, Chicago, Detroit, Toronto and other major markets. Among the announced concerts are March 26 at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, April 1 at Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C., April 10 at State Farm Arena in Atlanta and April 24 at Toyota Center in Houston. The run is scheduled to close May 21 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles.

H.E.R. is listed on the lineup beginning with the March 26 Brooklyn show and for every subsequent date. Swae Lee and Karri are scheduled to perform throughout the tour. Aiko previously collaborated with H.E.R. on “B.S.,” a song from her 2020 album *Chilombo*.

The announcement follows Aiko’s Sept. 11 release of *Westside Whimsy*, her first studio album in six years. The album [debuted at No. 1 on the Billboard 200](https://www.billboard.com/music/chart-beat/jhene-aiko-westwide-whimsy-number-one-billboard-200-chart-1236344193/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) dated Sept. 26, earning 74,000 equivalent album units in its opening week, according to Luminate. It marked Aiko’s first No. 1 on the chart and the first R&B album by a woman to lead the Billboard 200 in 2026.

The 20-track project includes collaborations with Kendrick Lamar, Ab-Soul, Larry June and Tyga. The new tour also follows Aiko’s 2024 Magic Hour Tour, which [expanded with additional arena dates](https://www.kaseyacenter.com/news/detail/jhene-aiko-announces-her-magic-hour-tour-coming-to-kaseya-center?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) after a run of 27 sold-out performances, according to the tour’s announcement.

Artist presale registration opened Tuesday, Nov. 17, at 10 a.m. ET through [Aiko’s official tour website](https://www.jheneaiko.com/tour?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The artist presale begins Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 2 p.m. local time, while a Verizon presale for select shows begins earlier that day at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales begin Friday, Nov. 20, at 10 a.m. local time through [Live Nation](https://www.livenation.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). VIP packages are also planned, including an experience billed as a Westside Whimsy soundbath with Aiko.
