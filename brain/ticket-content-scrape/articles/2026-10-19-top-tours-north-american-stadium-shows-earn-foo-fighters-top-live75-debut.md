# Top Tours: North American Stadium Shows Earn Foo Fighters Top Live75 Debut

## Summary

Pollstar's LIVE75 and Global Concert Pulse charts show Foo Fighters debuting in the LIVE75 top 10 (No. 4) on the strength of four sold-out North American stadium shows (146,202 tickets, averaging 36,550 per show) during the "Take Cover Tour," with a parallel top-10 Global Concert Pulse debut (No. 7, $5.2M average per-night gross). Shakira holds the LIVE75 top spot for a second week, BTS leads Global Concert Pulse for a 17th time, and Bruno Mars extends his Artist Power Index streak to 23 weeks at No. 1.

### Key Points
- Foo Fighters: LIVE75 debut at No. 4 (146,202 tickets, 4 shows); GCP debut at No. 7 ($5.2M/night average).
- Tour grossed $57M across 11 reported North American stadium shows (Aug-Sept).
- European leg included Berlin's Olympiastadion (61,955 tickets) -- the tour's highest single European attendance.
- Shakira (LIVE75 No. 1, 2nd week), BTS (GCP No. 1, 17th time), Bruno Mars (APX No. 1, 23rd time) hold top chart positions.
- Steve Lacy is the top APX chart debut at No. 47.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2298593760](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-2298593760.jpg)

The LIVE75 chart features a debut appearance in the top 10 by Foo Fighters, based on ticket sales at four sold-out concerts in North American stadiums during the band’s “Take Cover Tour.” With a total of 146,202 tickets sold, the group averaged 36,550 tickets per concert, the most of any headliner making a chart debut, to earn the No. 4 ranking.

Of the four shows, the first three were all held in Canadian cities, beginning with a Sept. 15 concert at Mosaic Stadium in Regina, Saskatchewan, with 28,167 fans in attendance. It was followed by a performance two nights later at Edmonton, Alberta’s Commonwealth Stadium that drew a crowd of 41,038, and a Sept. 20 event at Vancouver’s BC Place that recorded 39,334 in ticket sales.

The only U.S. venue was Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas. The group appeared there on Sep. 26 and performed for a crowd numbering 37,663.

The four shows were part of the tour’s most recent trek through the U.S. and Canada during August and September. With box-office figures reported from 11 stadiums during that period, North American ticket sales totaled 435,039 with a combined gross reaching $57 million.

Foo Fighters also have a top 10 debut on Global Concert Pulse, based on tour grosses from the North American shows, all of which are included in the chart’s three-month window of eligibility. The band takes the No. 7 ranking on GCP based on a per-night gross average of $5.2 million.

Before the North American leg, the group spent June and July in Europe, also with stadium shows on the schedule. One of the venues, Paris La Défense Arena, sold 39,644 tickets to French fans, and Allianz Arena in Munich, Germany hosted a crowd of 50,456, but the venue with the highest ticket count in Europe, based on box-office totals reported to *Pollstar*, was Berlin’s Olympiastadion with 61,955 in attendance.

Looking ahead, the “Take Cover Tour” is set to continue in November with a string of stadium concerts planned in Australia and New Zealand, followed by more dates Down Under in January 2027. Then two shows in India and a final swing of stadium events in South America will complete the tour next March.

Elsewhere on the two charts, LIVE75’s No. 1 ranking remains with Shakira who leads the lineup for a second consecutive week based on tickets sold at her 13-show residency in Madrid, Spain. Then, the highest ranking on Global Concert Pulse belongs to BTS as the group appears at No. 1 for a 17th time since launching the “Arirang World Tour” earlier this year.

The Artist Power Index also features a long running chart topper as Bruno Mars appears at No. 1 for a 23rd time since launching “The Romantic Tour.” His hold on the top ranking is a strong one, as he has the highest score in Airplay, one of four metrics that determine chart placement. He also has the second-best score in Streaming and the fourth highest Live rank among all concert performers who have been on tour during the past month, while ranking among the top 20 artists based on social media presence.

The top debut on APX belongs to R&B singer and guitarist Steve Lacy, ranked No. 47. He is on the road with his “Oh Yeah? Tour” through November.
