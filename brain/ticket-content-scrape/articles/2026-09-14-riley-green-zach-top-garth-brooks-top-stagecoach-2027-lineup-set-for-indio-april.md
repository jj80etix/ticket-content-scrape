# Riley Green, Zach Top, Garth Brooks Top Stagecoach 2027 Lineup, Set For Indio April 23-25

## Summary

Pollstar's Stagecoach 2027 lineup announcement includes onsale details: tickets go on sale Friday, Sept. 18, with GA three-day passes starting at $599 after early-bird pricing expires.

### Key Points
- Full lineup includes Journey, Matchbox Twenty, Pitbull and "Diamond" David Lee Roth alongside the country headliners.
- A GA six-pack bundle offers a $10-per-pass discount "while supplies last."
- Follows last year's surprise early joint announcement with Coachella at the same Empire Polo Club site.

Goldenvoice’s popular genre-defining and trendsetting country festival Stagecoach has announced its lineup, with tickets going on sale Friday, Sept. 18.

Along with each night’s headliner, the lineup includes other decidedly country or country-leaning artists like Hardy, Brothers Osborne, Willie Nelson & Family, Cody Jinks, Gretchen Wilson and Florida Georgia Line, the lineup has some notable non-country acts sitting high on the poster, including classic rock hit-makers Journey, ’90s favorites Matchbox Twenty, Mexican star Carin Leon, former Van Halen frontman and natural showman “Diamond” David Lee Roth and Mr. Worldwide himself, Pitbull.

Taking top billing at the Late Night On Mustang stage this time is Florida Georgia Line, who will be joined by Sean Paul and Flo Rida to go along with the festivities, including Guy Fieri’s Smokehouse and Diplo’s honkytonk.

GA three-day passes start at $599, following the expiration of early discounts, with numerous packages available including shuttle service as well as a GA six-pack that equals to a $10 discount per pass “while supplies last.”

This year’s announcement follows last year surprise early announcement for both Stagecoach and older, dual-weekend bellwether festival Coachella taking place at the same Empire Polo Club site in Indio, California. Both were announced in September, suggesting confidence in the lineup but also plenty of runway for fans making travel plans and saving up for the annual events.

Coachella, topped by Justin Bieber, Karol G, Sabrina Carpenter and Anyma, was a smash success with local reports claiming up to 125,000 people per day. Last year’s Stagecoach was topped by Cody Johnson, Lainey Wilson and Post Malone, with local media reporting around 80,000 people per day in attendance.
