# Miley Announces Two-Night Stand At LA’s Hollywood Bowl To Promote Her Latest Album

## Summary

Miley Cyrus (now performing under the mononym "Miley") announced two Hollywood Bowl shows on October 16 and 18 with Model/Actriz as support, timed to the September 18 release of her 10th studio album, "Bass Persuades." Tickets go on sale to the public September 10 via Ticketmaster, with an Amex presale beginning September 8.

### Key Points
- Marks Cyrus's first-ever Hollywood Bowl performance; her last headline tour was in 2015.
- Dropped her surname "Cyrus" from social media accounts as part of a rebrand tied to the new album.
- Ranked No. 19 on Pollstar's Top Women Touring Artists of the 21st Century list, with 2.6 million tickets sold and $188.2 million grossed across 193 shows.
- Booked by Creative Artists Agency; managed by Crush Music.

Miley is back. The Grammy Award-winning superstar announced her 10 <sup>th</sup> album, *Bass Persuades*, will be released Sept. 18 via Atlantic Records, and she’s celebrating the momentous occasion with two October shows at the iconic Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles with Model/Actriz as a special guest.

Before the announcement, the singer signaled a rebranding by dropping her last name, Cyrus, from her social media accounts. Releases promoting the new album and art for the two-night stand refer to her monomonously as Miley.

Tickets for the two-night stand on Oct. 16 and 18 go on sale to the general public Sept. 10 at 10 a.m. PST at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/miley-tickets/artist/1149434). An Amex presale begins Sept. 8 at 10 a.m. PST, and additional presales will run throughout the week.

Fans can sign up for the artist presale, which begins Sept. 9 at 10 a.m. PST, at [mileyofficial.com/tour](https://www.mileyofficial.com/tour).

“It’s been a while. The Hollywood Bowl is my favorite place to experience music and see my favorite artists,” Miley wrote on Instagram announcing the two-night stand. “I’ve sat in those seats many times, but I’ve never taken the stage. LA is my heart and I’m bringing it home. See you there.”

It is a rare return to the live stage for Miley, who has mostly performed at festivals over the last several years and her New Year’s Eve party. She last headlined a tour in 2015. Four years ago, the pop star headlined numerous festivals for what she called her “Attention Tour” supporting her seventh album *Plastic Hearts*.

Miley, who is booked by Creative Artists Agency and managed by Crush Music, ranked No. 19 on [*Pollstar* ’s Top Women Touring Artists of the 21 <sup>st</sup> Century](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/25/deep-data-cuts-supreme-sisterhood-the-millenniums-most-popular-women-in-touring/) list, selling 2.6 million tickets and grossing $188.2 million across 193 shows.
