# Little Big Town, HARDY and The Red Clay Strays to Headline Nashville’s Big Bash

## Summary

Little Big Town, HARDY, and The Red Clay Strays will headline Nashville's "New Year's Eve Live: Nashville's Big Bash" on December 31, with a national CBS broadcast.

### Key Points
- Held at Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park; the event's 18th annual year and 10th at this park.
- Includes the city's midnight "Music Note Drop"; additional performers to be announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Nashvilles-Big-Bash-NYE.webp)

Little Big Town, HARDY and The Red Clay Strays will headline Nashville’s New Year’s Eve celebration on December 31 at Bicentennial Capitol Mall State Park and a national broadcast on CBS.

The three acts are the first performers announced for “New Year’s Eve Live: Nashville’s Big Bash,” which will welcome 2027 with live music and the city’s midnight Music Note Drop. Additional performers will be announced later. The upcoming celebration marks Nashville’s 18th annual New Year’s Eve concert and its 10th year at Bicentennial Park.

Deana Ivey, president and CEO of the Nashville Convention & Visitors Corp shared in a statement, “New Year’s Eve is one of the nights Nashville shines brightest, and with Little Big Town, The Red Clay Strays and HARDY leading an incredible lineup this year, we’re excited to once again showcase Nashville’s music on a national stage.”

“We’re grateful for our partnership with CBS, which allows millions of viewers across the country to experience Nashville on New Year’s Eve, and hopefully inspire them to plan a visit and experience Music City for themselves,” Ivey continued.

CBS will air the special Thursday, December 31, from 8 to 10 p.m. and again from 10:30 p.m. to 1:05 a.m. Eastern and Pacific. Paramount+ Premium subscribers can stream the broadcast live, with next-day streaming also available.

Gates for the outdoor concert will open at 5:30 p.m. Central. The event is free and open to the public.
