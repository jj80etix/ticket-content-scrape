# Concert-led social network HearHear launches worldwide

## Summary

IQ reports that HearHear, a concert-focused social network billed as "the world's collective concert diary," has launched its app worldwide, giving concertgoers a centralized place to log their live-show history. The bulk of the article is paywalled; available detail is limited to the launch announcement itself.

### Key Points
- App launched worldwide, positioned as a centralized concert diary/discovery tool for fans.
- Founded by ex-Spotify employee Adam Offitzer.
