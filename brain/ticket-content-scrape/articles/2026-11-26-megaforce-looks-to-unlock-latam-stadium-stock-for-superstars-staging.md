# Megaforce looks to unlock Latam stadium stock for superstars’ staging

## Summary

IQ reports that Megaforce, a stage/production design firm, says roughly 70% of Latin American stadiums currently constrain what top-tier touring productions can do with their staging and production design. The company is positioning itself to help unlock more of that stadium stock for superstar-scale shows in the region. (Article is paywalled after the lead; full detail on Megaforce's specific proposals is not available here.)

### Key Points
- Megaforce estimates ~70% of LatAm stadiums limit production-design options for major touring acts.
- Framed as a market-opportunity piece for Megaforce's staging/production business in the region.
- No ticketing-platform, pricing, or regulatory angle in the available (paywalled) text.
