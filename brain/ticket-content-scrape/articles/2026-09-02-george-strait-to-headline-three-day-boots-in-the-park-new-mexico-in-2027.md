# George Strait to Headline Three-Day Boots In The Park New Mexico in 2027

## Summary

George Strait will headline the 2027 edition of Boots In The Park New Mexico, a three-day country festival (its first three-day edition) at Albuquerque's Balloon Fiesta Park, May 14–16, alongside Jason Aldean, Brooks & Dunn, Zach Top, and Jon Pardi. It's Strait's first Albuquerque appearance since 2013; the festival, run by Activated Events, has drawn 60,000+ fans across recent editions headlined by Luke Combs, Post Malone, and Jelly Roll.

### Key Points
- George Strait headlines Boots In The Park New Mexico 2027, expanding to three days for the first time (May 14–16, Balloon Fiesta Park, Albuquerque).
- Lineup also includes Jason Aldean, Brooks & Dunn, Zach Top, and Jon Pardi; more acts to be announced.
- Strait's first Albuquerque show since 2013.
- Festival (run by Activated Events) has drawn 60,000+ fans across editions previously headlined by Luke Combs (2025) and Post Malone/Jelly Roll (2026).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Boots-in-the-Park.webp)

George Strait will headline the 2027 edition of Boots In The Park New Mexico, leading a three-day country music lineup at Balloon Fiesta Park in Albuquerque from May 14 to 16.

The event will mark the fourth year of Boots In The Park in New Mexico and will feature Strait alongside Jason Aldean, Brooks & Dunn, Zach Top and Jon Pardi. Activated Events, the festival’s organizer, said additional performers will be announced at a later date.

The 2027 festival follows several years of strong attendance for the event. More than 60,000 fans attended Boots In The Park New Mexico across its recent editions, including events headlined by Luke Combs in 2025 and Post Malone and Jelly Roll in 2026.

“Albuquerque has shown us time and time again that New Mexico knows how to throw a country party,” Activated Events CEO Thacher said in the announcement. “For 2027, we wanted to create something truly legendary.”
