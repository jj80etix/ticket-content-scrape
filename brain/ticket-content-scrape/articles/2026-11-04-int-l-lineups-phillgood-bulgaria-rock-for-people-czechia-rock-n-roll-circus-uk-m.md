# Int’l Lineups: Phillgood Bulgaria, Rock For People Czechia, Rock N Roll Circus UK & More

## Summary

Pollstar's international festival lineup roundup covers new headliners and ticket pricing across Bulgaria (Phillgood, €199+), Czechia (Rock For People), Belgium (Rock Werchter), Portugal's Azores (Tremor), Germany (Hurricane/Southside, €199), Spain (Sónar), and the UK (Rock N Roll Circus, Wychwood, Boomtown). Most festivals are already on sale for their 2027 editions with tickets ranging roughly €160-€450.

### Key Points
- Phillgood (Bulgaria) returns July 2027 with Limp Bizkit, The Chemical Brothers, Robbie Williams; drew 60,000 in its 2026 debut.
- Rock for People (Czechia) adds Parkway Drive/Evanescence alongside blink-182 and Faith No More headliners.
- Rock Werchter (Belgium) adds Massive Attack, Nothing But Thieves, The Neighbourhood to its July 2027 bill.
- Sónar (Spain) reveals first 30 artists for its 2027 edition with a combined day/night ticket at €160-€175.
- UK's Rock N Roll Circus expands to London and Norwich in addition to its Sheffield home.

*Bulgaria*

**Phillgood Returns To Bulgaria**

Following its debut this summer Phillgood festival has announced its return to the historic Bulgarian city of Plovdiv, July 16-18, 2027, headlined by Limp Bizkit, The Chemical Brothers and Robbie Williams.

Phillgood made its debut in July 2026 with a three-day program that brought together more than 35 artists, including The Cure, Gorillaz, Moby, Kneecap, Wolf Alice, Suede, Sleaford Mods, Thievery Corporation, Daniel Avery, HAAi, Acid Arab, Kelly Lee Owens and more.

60,000 visitors attended the premiere, with more than a quarter of the festival audience traveling from outside Bulgaria, according to promoter. Alongside the sister festival Hills of Rock, both events attracted more than 60,000 visitors to Plovdiv across two weeks of live music.

Tickets for Phillgood 2027 are on sale, with three-day passes starting at €199 ($223) and day tickets for the opening night at €99 ($111).

Czechia

**Parkway Drive Join Rock For People Headliners**

Rock for People, which returns to Park 360 in Hradec Králové, Czechia, June 2-5, has revealed another huge wave of artists for 2027, with Australian metalcore giants Parkway Drive announced as the festival’s third headliner, alongside Evanescence as special guests.

They join previously confirmed headliners blink-182 and Faith No More. The latest announcement adds another 40 names to the bill, including Bastille, Joost, Beartooth, Carpenter Brut, Kreator, Hot Mulligan, Architects, Killswitch Engage, Napalm Death, The Devil Wears Prada, Daughtry, Rico Nasty and many more.

Further additions include Banshee, Chinaski, Ladrones, Pintglass, The Paradox, Temper City, The Photonics, Evening Elephants, Saint Clair, CF98, EMNW, and Netherwilds, reflecting Rock for People’s commitment to both established names and the next generation of alternative music.

Rock for People director Luděk Motyčka commented, “For us, the Rock for People line-up has always been about finding a balance. We bring in today’s biggest arena stars, but at the same time we give plenty of space to new musical trends and wilder club bands. I’m also really pleased that we were able to share this new wave of artists directly with our most loyal fans at a special gathering with the Rock for People team.”

A full festival ticket for Rock for People 2027 starts at €230 ($257), with “comfy” and VIP options available, as well.

*Belgium*

**Rock Werchter Adds Names**

Since Pollstar’s last international lineup roundup, Belgium mainstay Rock Werchter has added three more acts to its 2027 lineup: Massive Attack, Nothing But Thieves, and The Neighbourhood.

Massive Attack, who return for the first time since 2003, will headline the opening day of Rock Werchter 2027, which returns to the Festivalpark in Werchter, Belgium, July 1-4, 2027. The Neighborhood will also perform on day one, while Nothing But Thieves will take the stage on Friday, July 2.

It was previously announced that Tame Impala, SOMBR, Muse, Angèle, Sam Fender and Turnstile will be performing at Rock Werchter this summer. Tickets are on sale, a four-day ticket currently goes for €320, including service fees, a mobility contribution, and an eco-contribution, but excluding camping, which starts at €49 ($55).

*Azores / Portugal*

**Tremor Returns**

Tremor returns to São Miguel in the Azores, March 16-20, 2027, with another edition centered on musical discovery and the exchange between art and culture, landscape and community.

The first line-up announcement spans Japanese psychedelia, jazz, electronic experimentation and contemporary Brazilian music, alongside rock, dance music and collaborative artistic projects that bring different disciplines into dialogue with the island and its communities.

Japan’s Acid Mothers Temple, founded by Kawabata Makoto in 1995, are joined by Ak’chamel, a project from Texas; American quartet Horse Lords; French group Edredon Sensible; Finnish trio MUOVIPUSSI; Dora Morelenbaum from Brazil; Portuguese-Angolan pianist and composer Gisela Mabel and more.

Coro das Mulheres da Ilha is an artistic project from São Miguel that brings together women from different generations in a choir that weaves Portuguese musical traditions with Azorean identity and contemporary women’s experiences.

General passes for Tremor 2027 are sold out. Weekly passes will go on sale towards the end of 2026, followed by weekend passes in early 2027.

*Germany*

**Hurricane/Southside Unveil 32 Acts**

32 more acts have joined the lineups of Germany’s twin festivals Hurricane and Southside, returning to Sheessel and Neuhausen ob Eck, respectively, June 18-20.

Joining previously announced headliners Muse, mgk (Machine Gun Kelly) and Phoebe Bridgers, are Kontra K Placebo, Dermot Kennedy, Sublime, Beabadoobee, Alt-J, Bilderbuch, Enter Shikari, Royal Blood, Chvrches, Holly Humberstone, Felix Jaehn, and many more.

Tickets are on sale. Four-day tickets to both Hurricane and Southside currently cost €199 ($223) excluding camping.

*Spain*

**Sónar’s First 30**

Sónar has announced the first 30 artists for its 34th edition, taking place June 17–19, 2027 in Barcelona, Spain.

Bonobo, Tokischa, Yung Lean, Frost Children, Jessie Ware and Cornelius are among the first live acts confirmed, joining a DJ program that includes Carl Cox, Peggy Gou, KI/KI, Romy, Oscar Mulero and Honey Dijon presents Sinister.

The announcement also serves to connect the dots between Sónar’s past and future with Sónar by Day returning to Barcelona’s central waterfront location, reconnecting with its beginnings and bringing music and Sónar+D – the festival’s digital conference – together within the same space.

Sónar by Night will return to its established two-night format at Fira Gran Via, June 18-19, with four stages hosting major live productions and artists from across international club culture.

A tickets granting access to all of Sónar by Day, June 17-19, 2027 and all of Sónar by Night, June 18-19, 2027, currently goes for €160 ($179). Adding access to the Sonar+D digital conference to the tickets will raise the price to €175 ($196). VIP options are available.

*UK*

**Courteeners To Headline Rock N Roll Circus 2027**

Fresh from a new album and nationwide tour, Courteeners are heading back to Sheffield, England, to headline Rock N Roll Circus 2027, which returns to the Don Valley Bowl, Aug. 26-29, 2027.

Courteeners will take the stage Aug. 28, they’re joined by The Vaccines and The Lathums on the day.

The wider bill also features The Guest List, Girl In The Year Above, Little Grandad, Cowboy Hunters, Cherryholt, Saint Clair, Ist Ist and Yorkshire’s Slates and south coast band Crystal Tides, bringing together established indie heavyweights and some exciting emerging talent on the UK music scene.

With three further headliners still to be revealed across the four-day August Bank Holiday festival, organizers are promising another landmark year for the award-winning event.

Now entering its fifth year in Sheffield, Rock N Roll Circus has carved out a distinctive place in the UK’s festival calendar, combining major live music performances with spectacular circus entertainment in a setting unlike any traditional outdoor concert – dominated by a big top tent that has become somewhat of a festival icon.

Previous editions at Don Valley Bowl have welcomed some of the biggest names in music, including Queens of the Stone Age, Noel Gallagher’s High Flying Birds, The Kooks, Wunderhorse and Self Esteem.

Festival Director Ali O’Reilly, who runs the festival with her own independent company AGN Events, of which she is CEO, commented, “Anyone who saw Courteeners at Tramlines last year knows exactly what Sheffield means to this band. They absolutely blew the doors off the place, and there’s something about the connection between Liam and a Sheffield crowd that makes every show feel like a homecoming.

“To have The Vaccines and The Lathums joining them is a huge statement, but we’re just as excited about the artists coming through on Stage 2. Bands like Cherryholt, Cowboy Hunters and Ist Ist are exactly why we love putting these line-ups together. There’s something brilliant about watching a new band in an intimate setting and then heading into the Big Top for one of the biggest acts in the country.”

“2027 marks our fifth year in Sheffield, which is a real milestone for us. We’ve grown with the city, the audiences have been incredible and we’re proud of what Rock N Roll Circus has become.

“And this is just the start. We’ve still got three more headliners and plenty of surprises to come. Sheffield, we’re going to make this one count.”

The Rock N Roll Circus also premieres in London next year and returns to Norwich, with Gunnersbury Park hosting the premiere in the capital, July 22–25, featuring Madness, Fat Freddy’s Drop, Big Special, The Wailers and more tba; and Earlham Park hosting the Norwich edition, Aug. 19-22. First lineup announcement for Norwich to follow soon.

The general onsale for Rock N Roll Circus in Sheffield kicks off Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. British time.

**Wychwood Unveils Two Headliners**

Wychwood Festival has revealed a huge line-up for 2027, with indie Britpop icons Supergrass announced as Friday night headliners, joining previously confirmed synth-pop legends The Human League, who headline Saturday night.

Returning to Cheltenham Racecourse over new dates June 4-6, 2027, the family-friendly festival is preparing for another spectacular weekend of live music, comedy, arts and entertainment beneath the beautiful Prestbury Hills, UK.

Among those joining the bill are Razorlight, The Fratellis, and Natalie Imbruglia, Hard-Fi, Reverend & The Makers, Inspiral Carpets, The Molotovs, Dodgy, Dub Catalyst, and others.

No Wychwood weekend would be complete without what its makers describe as a “signature dose of musical mischief, nostalgic tributes and wonderfully unexpected entertainment,” which includes the Amy Winehouse Band celebrating the music of the late British icon; or Elvana, an eccentric collision of Elvis Presley and Nirvana. Joining the fun are Noasis, Wickedly Ariana, comedy folk favorites The Lancashire Hotpots, and returning Wychwood favorites Thrill Collins.

Wychwood was named Family Festival of the Year at the UK Festival Awards 2025, and celebrated its 20th anniversary this year. Beyond the music, the festival’s Village Green will host over 100 workshops and family activities, with a full program still to be announced. There’s a dedicated Children’s Literature Festival, and children under 10 can attend Wychwood of free.

Managing Director Graeme Merifield said, “after an incredible 20th anniversary, we’re thrilled to return with another fantastic line-up led by Supergrass and The Human League, alongside so many favourites for all generations. It’s wonderful to see our community growing while retaining everything that makes Wychwood special. With our Sunday headliner and plenty more surprises still to come, we can’t wait to welcome everyone back next summer!”

Wychwood Festival takes place June 4-6, 2027 at Cheltenham Racecourse, Gloucestershire, with a separately ticketed opening night on Thursday, June 3. The general sale launches Friday Nov. 6, at 10 a.m. Day Tickets will also be available.
