# Zach Bryan breaks Gillette attendance record with apparent free speech challenge to Kraft

## Summary

Zach Bryan broke the all-time attendance record at Gillette Stadium, drawing 73,538 fans on Oct. 3 during his "With Heaven On Tour," while wearing a "Free Palestine" T-shirt that echoed the recent controversy over Macklemore's removal from Ed Sheeran's Loop Tour at the same venue. Stadium owner Robert Kraft, who pushed for Macklemore's removal over a pro-Palestine stage comment he called "hate speech," has not responded to Bryan's display. The milestone caps a record-breaking year for Bryan, who also holds the all-time U.S. ticketed-concert attendance record.

### Key Points
- Bryan drew 73,538 fans to Gillette Stadium on Oct. 3, breaking the venue's all-time attendance record across all event types.
- Several backing musicians also wore pro-Palestinian messaging during the Oct. 2-3 shows.
- The display follows Kraft's September push to remove Macklemore from Ed Sheeran's Loop Tour over similar remarks.
- Bryan already holds the U.S. record for largest ticketed concert audience (112,408 at Michigan Stadium, Sept. 2025).
- The "With Heaven On Tour" wraps Oct. 10 at Jordan-Hare Stadium in Auburn, AL.

## Transcript

The country star broke the record in a 'Free Palestine' shirt only weeks after stadium owner Robert Kraft refused to platform Macklemore for his pro-Palestine stance

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Photo-Oct-04-2026-12-49-09-AM.jpg)

Zach Bryan has broken the attendance record for any event type at Gillette Stadium in Massachusetts, performing to over 73,000 fans twice in two days in a ‘Free Palestine’ T-shirt.

As Bryan set a venue audience record of 73,538 on 2 October as part of his *With Heaven On Tour,* the singer’s T-shirt recalled the recent fallout involving the venue, when its owner Robert Kraft forced the [removal of Macklemore from Ed Sheeran’s *Loop Tour*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/fans-political-forum-sheeran-macklemore-tour/) following pro-Palestine comments made at a different stadium.

Several of Bryan’s backing musicians also wore shirts with pro-Palestinian slogans across both concerts on 2 and 3 October.

Last month, Kraft made a public statement that he had rallied a group of fellow stadium owners to demand the removal of Macklemore as a support act from Sheeran’s tour. Kraft described [Macklemore’s remarks onstage](https://x.com/macklemore/status/2097033800811507990?ref_src=twsrc%5Etfw%7Ctwcamp%5Etweetembed%7Ctwterm%5E2097033800811507990%7Ctwgr%5E88ee3b0a5c42961d46790ce70b66cd48d927d528%7Ctwcon%5Es1_c10&ref_url=https%3A%2F%2Fvariety.com%2F2026%2Fmusic%2Fnews%2Fmacklemore-defends-free-palestine-speech-fired-ed-sheeran-tour-1236861158%2F) at New Jersey’s MetLife Stadium, which contained the phrase “free Palestine” as “hate speech”.

> “The milestone adds to a record-setting year for Bryan”

This comes after legal and insurance [experts examined the potential consequences of the *Loop Tour* fallout](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/09/what-can-the-industry-learn-from-the-sheeran-macklemore-fallout/) in conversation with  *IQ.* They said venues and promoting teams typically aim to preserve future working relationships, and that in cases such as Macklemore’s removal, promoters are unlikely to litigate the dropping of a support act. Kraft has made no statement about the display of the phrase by Bryan, who was the headline act at this weekend’s shows.

For Bryan, the record-breaking concerts come as part of what promoter AEG Presents said is his “biggest tour yet”, which spans more than 40 North American and European dates, and has been on the road since March 2026.

“The milestone adds to a record-setting year for Bryan. On September 27, 2025, he became the first artist ever to play Michigan Stadium, drawing 112,408 fans, the largest ticketed concert audience in U.S. history,” AEG Presents said of the concerts.

“That show surpassed the previous mark of 110,905, set by George Strait in Texas in 2024, and also set a single-night merchandise sales record of $5 million,” the company added.
