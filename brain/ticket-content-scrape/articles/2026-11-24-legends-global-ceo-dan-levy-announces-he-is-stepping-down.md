# Legends Global CEO Dan Levy Announces He Is Stepping Down

## Summary

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down after more than two and a half years leading the venue-management and live-entertainment services company, during which it completed and integrated its acquisition of ASM Global. Levy will stay on until a successor is named; longtime executive Chad Estis has been named president to help oversee the transition.

### Key Points
- Levy joined Legends in March 2024, became CEO in April 2024, and oversaw completion/integration of the ~$2.3B ASM Global acquisition.
- No timetable set for the transition; Levy remains in place until a successor is hired.
- Chad Estis named president, will help manage the transition.
- Prior to Legends, Levy spent 14 years at Meta in senior leadership roles.

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down according to *Sports Business Journal*. The former Meta executive will leave the company once a replacement is hired.

Legends Global has not released an official statement, but the staff announcement was made Nov. 23 via an internal memo following a decision made with the company’s board of directors. There is no set timeline to hire a successor.

Longtime executive Chad Estis (formerly EVP and Chief Revenue Officer) has been named President of Legends Global and will remain in that role after a new CEO is hired. Estis and company leaders will help guide the transition.

After 14 years in executive roles at Meta, Levy joined Legends in March 2024 as interim president and board member. He replaced Shervin Mirhashemi as CEO in April 2024.

Levy’s tenure has been defined by a period of transformational integration and strategic expansion – including oversight of the $2.3 billion ASM Global acquisition and integrating the two businesses into the rebranded Legends Global. The ASM Global acquisition was announced before Levy became CEO, although he oversaw its completion in August 2024 and the subsequent integration which dramatically expanded the company’s footprint and venue management portfolio.

In September 2025, the company reported more than 450 venues worldwide, approximately 20,000 events annually, more than 165 million guests and eight integrated business divisions spanning consulting, development, sales, partnerships, venue management, hospitality, merchandise and content and booking.

Levy also reorganized the executive leadership team including Brett Parker, who was appointed CFO and COO in March 2025 and is now President & CFO; Estis, a 20-year veteran of the company who was named EVP and Chief Revenue Officer, expanding his role in February 2025; and the addition of Josh Kritzler, President of North American Venues & Content in February 2026, consolidating oversight of venue operations, content and booking across the company’s North American portfolio of more than 250 venues.

In 2026, Levy’s administration secured significant new venue and development partnerships including the Dallas Mavericks, who selected Legends Global for owner’s representation and premium sales for the team’s future arena project; the Kansas City Royals who selected Legends Global as owner’s representative for the proposed new downtown ballpark; VENU who selected Legends Global to manage the 12,500-capacity Regent Bank Amphitheater in Oklahoma; and Mesa, Arizona, awarded the company a five-year management agreement for Mesa Amphitheatre and Mesa Convention Center.

Outside the U.S., Legends Global continued to grow its international business in 2026. Its partnership with FC Barcelona at Spotify Camp Nou entered a new phase with the launch of long-term VIP seat licensing, following the commercialization of nearly 5,000 premium seats and more than €380 million in committed revenue. In the UK, the company entered a 10-year partnership with Coventry City Football Club and Coventry Building Society Arena, backed by a joint investment of more than £15 million over three years to upgrade hospitality, technology and premium spaces.

As reported by SBJ, Levy concluded his memo to employees by writing: “As we begin the succession process, I want you to know how strongly I believe in this business and in each of you. I remember the ‘wow’ I felt at my first baseball game as a kid. I still feel the same way when I visit with each of you and our partners at the incredible events we host, including the Super Bowl, World Series, World Cup, Ryder Cup, the biggest concert tours, and major conventions. My office is covered with pictures from those visits, so I will never forget who we serve.

“Thank you for everything you’ve done to make these past few years such a success.”

Estis has been involved with Legends since it was founded in 2008 and formally became President of Global Sales in 2010. He built the Legends Global Sales division from the ground up, spearheading strategy and client acquisitions across major league and collegiate athletics.

As Executive Vice President, Estis played a key role in integrating research firm CSL International into the Legends platform. This helped establish the company’s “360-degree” service model combining sales, planning and hospitality. Following Legends’ acquisition of ASM Global, Estis was elevated to EVP & Chief Revenue Officer in February 2025. In this role, he oversaw the combined company’s sales, partnerships, college divisions, CSL International and business development before his current promotion to President.
