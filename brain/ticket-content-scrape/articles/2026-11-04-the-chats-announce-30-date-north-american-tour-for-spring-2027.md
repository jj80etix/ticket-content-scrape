# The Chats Announce 30-Date North American Tour for Spring 2027

## Summary

Australian punk band The Chats announced a 30-date North American tour for spring 2027 in support of their upcoming album "Half Cut," running April 7 to May 18 with Chain Whip and Gymshorts as support. The tour follows a 17-date Australian run this November/December and precedes previously announced UK/European dates later in 2027.

### Key Points
- North American tour opens April 7 in Berkeley, CA and closes May 18 in West Hollywood, CA.
- Artist presale begins Nov. 4; general onsale Nov. 6 via Ticketmaster.
- "Half Cut," the band's third studio album, releases March 5, 2027 via Virgin Music Group.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Chats-1.webp)

Australian punk band The Chats have announced a 30-show North American tour for spring 2027, with Chain Whip and Gymshorts joining as support.

The tour opens April 7 at the UC Theatre in Berkeley, California. The announcement adds six weeks of performances across the United States and Canada to a schedule that also includes European and United Kingdom dates later in the year.

Following Berkeley, The Chats will head north for performances in Bend and Portland, Oregon, before playing Seattle’s Showbox and Vancouver’s Commodore Ballroom. The route then moves inland through Boise, Salt Lake City and Denver, followed by concerts in Omaha and Kansas City.

The Midwest portion includes Minneapolis, Chicago and Detroit. The band will return to Canada for shows in Toronto and Montreal before heading to Boston and Brooklyn to complete the April schedule.

May dates begin in Baltimore, followed by Atlanta and a five-show Florida stretch. Those appearances will take the group to Miami Beach, Fort Myers, St. Petersburg, DeLand and St. Augustine, making Florida one of the most extensive state stops on the itinerary.

From there, the band will travel west for concerts in Austin, San Antonio, Santa Fe and Phoenix. A San Diego performance precedes the final show, scheduled for May 18 at the Roxy Theatre in West Hollywood, California.

The headline run will follow the release of the band’s third studio album, *Half Cut*, scheduled to arrive March 5 through Virgin Music Group.

Before beginning their 2027 schedule, The Chats will play a 17-date Australian tour during November and December. That run includes multiple performances in Brisbane, Sydney and Melbourne, along with stops in Tasmania, South Australia and Western Australia.
