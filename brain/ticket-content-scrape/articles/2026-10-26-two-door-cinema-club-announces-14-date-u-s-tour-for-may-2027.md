# Two Door Cinema Club Announces 14-Date U.S. Tour for May 2027

## Summary

Two Door Cinema Club has announced a 14-date U.S. tour for May 2027, performing its debut album *Tourist History* in full at each stop, running from Chicago to Dallas. The tour follows a North American anniversary run that included the band's first headlining Madison Square Garden show.

### Key Points
- 14 shows May 6 (Chicago) through May 29 (Dallas), including stops in Milwaukee, Red Rocks, Portland, Seattle, and five California cities.
- Full-album performances of *Tourist History* mark its 15th anniversary.
- Friendly Fires will support most dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Two-Door-Cinema-Club-2027.webp)

Two Door Cinema Club has announced a 14-date U.S. tour for May 2027, with the band performing its debut album, *Tourist History*, in full at each show.

The itinerary begins May 6 at Chicago’s Riviera Theater and concludes May 29 at The Bomb Factory in Dallas.

Following the Chicago opener, the group will perform May 8 at Landmark Credit Union Live in Milwaukee and May 9 at the Palace Theatre in Saint Paul, Minnesota. The Red Rocks concert follows May 11, before the band travels to Utah for its May 14 festival appearance.

The Pacific Northwest portion begins May 16 in Portland, Oregon, where the venue has not yet been announced. Two Door Cinema Club will then play Seattle’s Paramount Theatre on May 17 before heading south for five California concerts over the following week.

Those California dates begin May 19 at Channel 24 in Sacramento and continue May 20 at Oakland’s Fox Theater. The band will perform May 22 at the Hollywood Palladium in Los Angeles and May 24 at The Sound in Del Mar.

Three Texas performances will close the run. Two Door Cinema Club is scheduled to play United Heritage Hall in Austin on May 27, White Oak Music Hall in Houston on May 28 and The Bomb Factory in Dallas the following evening.

Friendly Fires will support most of the announced concerts, rejoining the Northern Irish band after appearing on its recent North American run.

The announcement follows a North American leg celebrating the 15th anniversary of “Tourist History.” That stretch focused on the East Coast and Midwest and included Two Door Cinema Club’s first headlining performance at Madison Square Garden.
