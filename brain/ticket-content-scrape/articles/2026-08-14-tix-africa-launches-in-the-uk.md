# Tix Africa launches in the UK

## Summary

Nigeria-founded ticketing and event-tech platform Tix Africa has launched in the UK, extending its currency-localized ticketing, admissions, payments and audience-engagement tools to serve the growing African diaspora events market in cities like London, Manchester and Birmingham. The company, which already operates in Nigeria and Ghana, is free for free events and charges a per-ticket fee on paid ones.

### Key Points
- Tix Africa (founded Lagos, 2019; expanded to Ghana 2023) launches in the UK to serve diaspora organizers and their London/Manchester/Birmingham-based audiences.
- Platform bundles ticketing, admissions, payments, virtual venues, promotion and audience-engagement tools, with a mobile app.
- Pricing: free for free events, per-ticket fee on paid events.
- Cited early UK activity includes work with Chef Fregz's "Fregz Dinner Table" series in London.

### Highlights
- A regional ticketing platform (Nigeria/Ghana) enters a new geography (UK) targeting a diaspora-organizer niche rather than competing head-on with major primary platforms.

### Industry Problem
Underserved organizer segments (diaspora/community events, multi-currency needs) can be a wedge for regional platforms to enter new mature markets by serving audiences larger primary platforms don't specifically target.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor Tix Africa's UK traction and pricing model as a data point on niche-market entry into a mature ticketing geography; no immediate build/improve action indicated, but worth revisiting if it starts winning larger venue or festival clients outside its diaspora niche.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Tix Africa has announced its launch in the UK.**

The ticketing and event technology platform was founded in 2019 to help organisers across Africa to sell tickets in their local currencies. The business started life in Lagos, Nigeria, with the platform integrating ticketing, admissions, payment technology, virtual venues, promotion, and audience engagement tools to simplify operations and drive sales.

It has grown to power the ticketing experience for events such as Afrofuture, as well as football clubs such as Sporting Lagos FC, and more. In 2023, Tix Africa also expanded to Ghana.

Folayemi Agusto, chief executive and co-founder of Tix Africa, explained in an [Instagram video](https://www.instagram.com/reels/DbvxXP1srwb/) the reasoning behind the move into the UK market.

“Tix is making it possible for African talents to export their events, creativity and passions, so they can host events for larger audiences here in the UK. And for those already in the UK, you can have the opportunity to work with a company like Tix to host events for our communities and from our perspectives.

“For example, over the past few months we’ve been working with Chef Fregz to host The Fregz Dinner Table series in London.”

Agusto added over the last year, while testing with early adopters, Tix has been able to work with a number of organisers as well as Chef Fregz, with the hopes of building further relationships.

In a separate [blog post](https://blog.tix.africa/https-blog-tix-africa-p-afcaaf7d-0d74-4ce4-84c1-a4b9927688ed-member_status-free-utm_source-linkedin-utm_medium-ghost-blog-utm_campaign-qib/), Agusto said: “African music, food, fashion and culture have grown far beyond the continent. Every week, creators and organisers are bringing incredible experiences to cities across the UK, building communities that feel just as vibrant as the ones back home.

“Over the past year, we’ve had the privilege of working with many of these organisers, helping them sell tickets and manage their events. Now, we’re making it official.

“We’re not launching in the UK because we’re chasing a new market. We’re launching because the communities we already serve have become increasingly global. Organisers who started in Lagos and Accra are now producing experiences in London, Manchester and Birmingham. Others have built thriving communities in the UK from the ground up. They all face the same challenge: they need technology that understands events, their audiences and what it takes to grow a community. That’s where Tix comes in.”

Tix is free for free event organisers, but takes a fee for paid events for each ticket sold. It also has an app.
