# KKR takes minority stake in BookMyShow to power ‘growth phase’

## Summary

Private equity firm KKR has taken an undisclosed minority stake in Indian ticketing and live-entertainment company BookMyShow to fund its "next phase of growth," with KKR also consulting on India's live market. The deal continues KKR's pattern of investment in India's consumer sectors and comes as BookMyShow positions itself as a "full-stack" entertainment company beyond ticketing. The piece also notes KKR's prior, more hands-off Superstruct festival-group affiliation drew public backlash over the firm's alleged ties to Israeli tech and weapons manufacturers.

### Key Points
- KKR minority stake, size/value undisclosed; both parties declined to detail terms.
- BookMyShow aims to "deepen its full-stack offering" and position India as a "global entertainment hub."
- KKR's other India PE holdings span healthcare, education and cosmetics; this is its latest India live-sector move.
- Contrast drawn with KKR's Superstruct affiliation (2024 acquisition), which triggered artist withdrawals and fan boycotts over KKR's reported ties to Israeli/weapons-linked interests.

### Highlights
- Deal marks continued PE consolidation of a major ticketing/live platform outside the US/Europe (India).
- KKR framed as advisory partner, not operational owner — a different structure than its Superstruct holding.
- BookMyShow has evolved from pure ticketing into production/promotion/talent (BookMyShow Live).

### Industry Problem
Consolidation of primary ticketing platforms under private-equity ownership is accelerating in high-growth markets, changing competitive dynamics and raising governance/reputational questions (as seen with KKR's Superstruct backlash) that independent platforms don't face.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor for signs this PE-backed growth model (capital + advisory support, full-stack expansion into production) migrates to other regions/competitors; no direct product response is needed now, but a trigger to watch is any BookMyShow move into markets where Etix competes.

## KKR takes minority stake in BookMyShow to power ‘growth phase’

The promoter says KKR will also consult on the Indian live market as it aims to make the country a 'global entertainment hub'

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/07/Ed-Sheeran_Mathematics-Tour_credit_-BookMyshow-scaled.jpg.webp)

BookMyShow-promoted Ed Sheeran concert in India © BookMyShow

Investment firm KKR has taken a “minority stake” in leading Indian promoter BookMyShow, with an undisclosed investment amount set to support the company’s “next phase of growth”.

The promoter said that following the investment it now aims to scale its live entertainment business and “deepen its full-stack offering across India”.

The investment marks KKR’s latest private equity investment in India across its portfolio, which includes holdings in the medical infrastructure, education, and cosmetics industries. When asked by *IQ,* both parties declined to disclose the value or size of the stake taken in BookMyShow.

BookMyShow said the deal comes at a time of rising discretionary spending and growing demand for “world-class live experiences”.

“The timing of this investment is particularly exciting, as we have significantly expanded our presence across the live entertainment landscape and see the opportunity for India’s entertainment economy to grow like never before,” the promoter added.

Its founder and CEO Ashish Hemrajani also recently said a flurry of 2026 tour announcements show the country is rapidly developing a profitable model of three-to-five city touring routes and becoming an [“anchor market”](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/emerging-touring-models-making-india-an-anchor-market-bookmyshow-says/).

Announcing the KKR investment, Hemrajani said: “\[KKR’s\] global perspective, deep expertise, and strong understanding of consumer businesses will be invaluable as we enter the next phase of our journey.”

This appears to differ from previous operational structures around KKR’s holdings in the live business, for example in its affiliation with festival promoter Superstruct. Superstruct CEO Alex Mahon has previously [told *IQ*](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/superstruct-continues-leadership-refresh-with-new-uk-md/) that KKR is “not involved in the group day-to-day”.

> “We see the opportunity for India’s entertainment economy to grow like never before”

KKR’s previous investments across the live industry have led to public backlash against some of its affiliated concert and festival events, with some campaigns objecting to the company’s ties to Israeli technology companies and alleged links to international weapons manufacturers.

Its June 2024 acquisition of Superstruct led to dozens of artist withdrawals, public distancing from festival organisers, as well as fan boycotts across Superstruct’s festival portfolio. Superstruct was forced to clarify in May last year that income from its events remained “entirely” within its business.

Following the announcement, KKR partner and head of India private equity, Akshay Tanna, called BookMyShow “a pioneer in delivering high-quality entertainment experiences in India”, adding that KKR is “pleased to support BookMyShow as it continues to lead the next phase of growth in India’s out-of-home entertainment sector”.

He added: “We believe BookMyShow will play an important role in advancing India’s ambition to become a global entertainment hub and a premier destination for leading artists and acts from around the world.”
