# Comedian Jimmy O. Yang Announces 2027 ‘Has No Friends’ Tour

## Summary

Comedian Jimmy O. Yang announced his 2027 "Has No Friends" stand-up tour, a routine tour announcement.

### Key Points
- New 2027 stand-up tour announced.
- Standard comedy-tour coverage; no ticketing/pricing/policy angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Jimmy-O.-Yang.webp)

Jimmy O. Yang has announced his 2027 “Has No Friends” Tour, with performances across the United States and Canada scheduled from January through May.

The comedian and actor will open the run with back-to-back nights on January 15 and 16 at Tampa Funny Bone in Tampa, Florida. The tour is set to wrap up with two shows at Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas on May 29 and 30.

Following the Tampa opening, Yang will perform in Grand Rapids and Minneapolis before a three-city Texas stretch. Those dates include Dallas’ Majestic Theatre on January 29, Austin’s ACL Live at the Moody Theater on January 30 and Houston’s Sarofim Hall at the Hobby Center on January 31.

February’s itinerary includes Hawaii performances at Maui’s Castle Theater and Honolulu’s Neal S. Blaisdell Concert Hall. Additional stops that month include Salt Lake City, Denver, Tucson, Phoenix, Washington, D.C., and Jersey City.

The West Coast schedule includes San Diego’s Civic Theatre on March 13, Portland’s Keller Auditorium on March 19 and San Francisco’s Chase Center on March 20. Yang will then head to Massachusetts for an April 1 performance at the Wilbur in Boston and an April 2 show at the Chevalier Theatre in Medford.

Other April engagements include the Chicago Theatre on April 9, New York’s Beacon Theatre on April 23 and the Dolby Theatre in Los Angeles on April 30. Atlanta’s Cobb Energy Performing Arts Centre will host the comedian May 20, ahead of the closing Las Vegas weekend.

The announcement follows Yang’s “Big & Tall” Tour. The comedian also released his third stand-up special, “Finally Home,” earlier this year. He filmed and directed the special in his hometown of Hong Kong during a five-show sold-out engagement at the Hong Kong Coliseum. Following its theatrical release, the special became available to stream on Hulu. His previous stand-up specials include “Good Deal” and “Guess How Much?”

An artist presale is scheduled to begin October 14 at noon Pacific time using the code FRIEND. Venue presales follow October 15, with the general sale announced for October 16 at noon local time. Some venues list different sale times. Fans should check the listing for their selected performance.
