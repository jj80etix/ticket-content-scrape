# AEG Presents & Greenhouse Talent Announce Partnership In Europe

## Summary

AEG Presents has formed a partnership with Greenhouse Talent, the largest independent concert promoter in Benelux, formalizing roughly 20 years of informal collaboration; Greenhouse keeps its Ghent/Breda offices and leadership.

### Key Points
- Greenhouse promotes ~1,500 events/year (music, comedy under its LiveComedy brand) across Belgium and the Netherlands with ~60 staff.
- Roster includes Hans Zimmer, Massive Attack, Rammstein, Luke Combs and others.
- Also produces Belgium's Gent Jazz festival.
- Deal terms were not disclosed. (See also the TicketNews report on this same deal, tagged #q13.)


## Transcript

![Pascal Van De Velde, drijvende kracht achter Greenhouse Talent en Gent Jazz.](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Unknown.jpeg)

Greenhouse Talent founder Pascal Van De Velde.

AEG Presents today announced that it has formed a partnership with Greenhouse Talent, one of Europe’s leading independent concert promoters.

Greenhouse Talent has long-been the go-to local promoter for AEG Presents shows and projects coming to the Benelux region, so “this investment formalizes a longstanding relationship,” according to today’s press announcement.

Greenhouse Talent will continue to operate from Ghent and Breda under its existing leadership, retaining the entrepreneurial identity, local expertise and artist relationships that have made it a leading force in Benelux.

Founded by Pascal Van De Velde in 2005, Greenhouse Talent has become the largest independent concert promoter in Benelux, with 60 employees across Belgium and the Netherlands. Its Belgian business is led by Mathilde Van geertruyden and its Dutch business by Wouter de Wilde.

The company promotes nearly 1,500 events annually featuring international artists across music, live entertainment and comedy, with the latter operating under the LiveComedy brand. Its roster includes Hans Zimmer, Massive Attack, Laufey, Ludovico Einaudi, Bryan Adams, Erykah Badu, Luke Combs, Diana Ross, Rammstein, Gabriel Iglesias, Jeff Dunham, WWE and many more.

Additionally, Greenhouse Talent produces Belgium’s Gent Jazz festival and the Netherlands Spoorpark LIVE and is the lead promoter and producer of the Dutch edition of Country to Country. Now entering its sixth year, the event has grown into the region’s largest country music festival.
