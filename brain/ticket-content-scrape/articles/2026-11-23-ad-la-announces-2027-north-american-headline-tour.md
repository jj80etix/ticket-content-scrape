# ADÉLA Announces 2027 North American Headline Tour

## Summary

ADÉLA announced her second North American headline run, the "Prima Tour," supporting her debut album PRIMA, alongside previously announced European, UK, Australian and South American dates for 2027. The pre-sale for the new NA shows opens in two waves on Dec. 1, with general on-sale Dec. 2.

### Key Points
- PRIMA Tour adds NA dates in SF, Chicago, Brooklyn, Toronto and DC following her Coachella debut.
- Pre-sale split into two time-gated waves (10am/2pm local) Dec. 1; general on-sale Dec. 2.
- Tour also spans Europe (Jan 2027), Australia/NZ (Feb) and South American festivals (Mar).
- ADÉLA is represented by CAA and managed by Good World.

To go along with her debut performance at Coachella, ADÉLA has announced dates for her second headline North American trek, “Prima Tour.”  
  
ADÉLA, whose mega-viral hit “Nicole Kidman” blew up on socials this year, was [featured in *Pollstar* ’s recent print issue](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/30/hotstar-special-2026-adela-katseye-chappell-roan-karol-g-are-among-her-growing-legion-of-fans/) as part of our [Hotstar Special 2026](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/11/03/hotstar-special-2026/).  
  
The 22-year-old Slovakian artist is celebrating her debut studio album PRIMA, which debuted at No. 4 on the Billboard Hot 200 and later hit No. 1 on the Billboard Top Album Sales chart.  
  
Her previously announced 2027 dates include her debut sold-out headline European and UK tour that launches in January in Prague and her debut Australian dates in February including appearances at Laneway Festival. She’s also lined up dates in South America with performances at Lollapalooza festivals in Argentina, Chile, and Brazil, as well as Colombia’s Estéreo Picnic.  
  
Next month she has a few Jingle Ball appearances booked in Boston and Philadelphia. She’ll head back to North America in April for Coachella, followed by newly announced headline shows in San Francisco, Chicago, Brooklyn, Toronto and Washington, D.C.

Fans can sign up for the artist pre-sale starting now through Wednesday, November 25th via https://www.adelaxo.com/#tour. Today’s announcement notes that “to ensure real fans get tickets, the pre-sale will be divided into two waves: the first will begin Tuesday, December 1st at 10 AM local time; the second will begin on December 1st at 2 PM local time. On-sale will begin Wednesday, December 2nd at 10 AM local time.”

ADÉLA is represented by CAA and she is managed by Good World’s Sam Jacobson, Brandon Creed and Dani Sibdo.
