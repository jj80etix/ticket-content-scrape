# Kate Nash Sets North American Tour Dates for Fall 2026

## Summary

Kate Nash announced a North American fall tour ahead of her sixth album "Cailín" (Jan. 22, 2027), running Nov. 3–16 across ten cities from Somerville, MA to San Diego, CA.

### Key Points
- Tour runs Nov. 3 (Somerville, MA) through Nov. 16 (San Diego, CA); 10 stops including Philadelphia, NYC, DC, Toronto, Chicago, LA.
- Artist and local/venue presales begin Sept. 30 at 10am; general on-sale Oct. 2 at 10am local time.
- New album "Cailín" due Jan. 22, 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Kate-Nash.webp)

Kate Nash is heading to North America this fall as the British and Irish singer-songwriter prepares to release her sixth studio album, *Cailín*, set for release on January 22, 2027.

The North American run is scheduled to begin November 3 in Somerville, Massachusetts at Arts at the Armory. From there, the singer is set to make stops in cities such as Philadelphia, New York, Washington D.C., Toronto, Chicago, Santa Ana, Los Angeles and San Francisco. The run is slated to wrap up on November 16 in San Diego at Music Box.

Taking to social media, Nash shared, “Hi North America! I’m coming back to play some new tunes for you & this time it’s intimate 🙂↔️ Can’t wait to see your faces!”

An artist presale begins Wednesday, September 30, at 10 a.m. Eastern Time. Local and venue presales will also begin at 10 a.m. local time. Tickets will then go on sale to the general public Friday, October 2, at 10 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Nash’s official website](https://www.katenash.com/).
