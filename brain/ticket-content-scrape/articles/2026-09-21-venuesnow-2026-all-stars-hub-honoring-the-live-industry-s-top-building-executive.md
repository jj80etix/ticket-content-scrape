# VenuesNow 2026 All-Stars Hub: Honoring The Live Industry’s Top Building Executives

## Summary

Pollstar's 2026 VenuesNow All-Stars package spotlights venue-industry executives ahead of the Sept. 28 reception preceding the VenuesNow Conference in Seattle, with newly named MSG Entertainment President Josephine Vaccarello on the cover.

### Key Points
- Honorees are selected by Pollstar's editorial team based on box-office data and operational/design leadership.
- Reception and conference are both held in Seattle; this year's list includes several Seattle-based executives.
- Full honoree roster spans executives from Live Nation, MSG, Oak View Group and multiple pro sports franchises, each profiled in companion pieces.

The annual VenuesNow All-Stars list honors the top executives transforming the venue business showcasing leadership, success and innovation. The honorees are chosen by *Pollstar* ’s editorial team with input from the industry while taking into account *Pollstar* ’s industry-leading box office, along with recognizing best-in-class venue operations, programming, development, hospitality and design.  
  
This year’s VenuesNow All-Stars will be celebrated at a reception taking place Sept. 28, the evening before the [2026 VenuesNow Conference](https://venuesnowconference.com/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=paid%20display&utm_campaign=venuesnow2026_gupta&gad_source=1&gad_campaignid=23902994572&gbraid=0AAAAAoKo5AZ6Us7t973PpOM7jZbI7qUdq&gclid=CjwKCAjwq8PVBhAKEiwA2i3SHcE2EXuv5NXA8rp18oargLFg8gHhFR07NqRkLn1ObLOjXy9zWQmwuRoC5CMQAvD_BwE) launches at the Sheraton Grand Seattle. In celebration of VNC’s return to the Emerald City, the 2026 All-Stars list also includes executives based in Seattle. This month’s print issue of *Pollstar* also features a venue-specific market focus devoted to Seattle and its live entertainment scene, which means everything from the Crocodile club to Climate Pledge Arena to Lumen Field.  
  
Josephine “Jojo” Vaccarello, the [newly-named President](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/21/josephine-vaccarello-named-president-of-msg-entertainment/) of MSG Entertainment, graces the cover of the 2026 All-Stars issue. Her 28-year journey through almost every part of MSG’s live division now includes the dazzling, game-changing Sphere in Las Vegas, an example of the continuing tech-driven evolution of the live entertainment landscape.
