# Voting Open For 2027 Pollstar Ticketing Star Awards

## Summary

Voting opened for Pollstar's 2027 Ticketing Star Awards, which honor box-office professionals, presented in partnership with INTIX and culminating at INTIX's 48th annual conference in New Orleans (Jan. 25-28, 2027).

### Key Points
- Voting deadline is Dec. 4 at 5pm PT.
- Nominees were submitted by Pollstar readers and INTIX.
- Honorees will be featured in Pollstar's February 2027 issue.
