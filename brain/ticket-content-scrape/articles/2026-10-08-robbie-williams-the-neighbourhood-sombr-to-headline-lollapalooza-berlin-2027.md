# Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood & Sombr To Headline Lollapalooza Berlin 2027

## Summary

Lollapalooza Berlin announced its first three 2027 headliners — Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood and Sombr — for the July 17-18 festival at Olympiapark, now in its 12th year. Phase 1 weekend tickets start at €179 ($200), with tiered teen and children's pricing, following last year's roughly 100,000-visitor turnout.

### Key Points
- First 2027 lineup names: Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood, Sombr; more acts to come.
- 12th edition, held at Olympiapark/Olympic Stadium, Berlin, July 17-18, 2027.
- Phase 1 pricing: €179 Regular Weekend (16+), €259 Comfort, €399 VIP; €99 Teen (12-15); free/€10 Kids tiers.
- 2026 edition drew about 100,000 visitors.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2286243167](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-2286243167.jpg)

A view of the crowd during Lollapalooza Berlin 2026. Photo by Pedro Becerra/Redferns

Lollapalooza Berlin has unveiled the first three acts to perform in the German capital July 17-18, for the festival’s 2027 edition: Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood, and Sombr.

Now in its 12th year, Lolla Berlin will once again transform the city’s Olympiapark, including the Olympic Stadium, into a world of “electrifying live shows, interactive themed experiences, and creative discoveries,” as the lineup announcement states.

Makeup brand essence returns as presenting partner of Lollapalooza Berlin. More artists and program highlights to be announced soon.

Regular Weekend Tickets (for ages 16-plus) for the upcoming festival edition are now available in Phase 1 for €179 ($200). Comfort and VIP options are available from €259 ($290) and €399 ($447), respectively.

Teen Weekend Tickets for ages 12-15 are priced at €99 ($111). Children aged 0 through 6 can attend free of charge with a complimentary Kids Ticket, while Kids Tickets for ages 7 through 11 are available for €10 ($11). Kids Tickets can only be purchased in combination with a Regular Weekend Ticket and are strictly limited.

Last year’s Lollapalooza Berlin attracted around 100,000 visitors over the weekend.
