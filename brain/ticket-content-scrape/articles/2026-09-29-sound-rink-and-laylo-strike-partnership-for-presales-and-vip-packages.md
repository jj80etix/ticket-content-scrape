# Sound Rink and Laylo strike partnership for presales and VIP packages

## Summary

VIP and presale platform Sound Rink has partnered with touring CRM firm Laylo to help artists combine fan-data sources and target "superfans" with VIP ticket packages and merch without relying on paid social advertising. The deal is aimed at boosting artists' pre-tour revenue through more precisely targeted presale offers.

### Key Points
- Sound Rink (VIP/presale platform) and Laylo (touring CRM) announced the partnership September 29.
- Goal: let artists combine separate fan databases to identify and target superfans.
- Use cases include VIP ticket packages and merch sales.
- Framed as a data-driven alternative to paid social reach for pre-tour revenue.
