# FTC Extends Public Comment on Proposed Policy Statement Regarding Personalized Pricing

## Summary

The FTC extended by seven days the public comment period on its proposed enforcement policy statement regarding personalized pricing, moving the deadline from September 18 to September 25, 2026. The policy statement addresses use of personal data to set individualized prices based on what a company believes a consumer is willing to pay.

### Highlights
- New comment deadline: September 25, 2026 (originally September 18).
- Original comment period opened August 19, 2026, via regulations.gov docket FTC-2026-1057.
- Policy statement targets "personalized pricing" — data-driven, individualized price-setting — a practice adjacent to dynamic/surge pricing models used in ticketing.

### Industry Problem
Regulators are scrutinizing data-driven personalized pricing as a consumer-protection issue, alongside existing junk-fee/all-in pricing rules — a compliance area directly relevant to any platform using dynamic or personalized pricing models.

### Proposed Solution
No enforcement action yet — this is a comment-period extension. The trigger to watch is the final policy statement (post Sept. 25) for any guidance that would constrain personalized/dynamic pricing practices; a primary platform should audit its own pricing-personalization practices against the proposed standard before it finalizes.
