# Contra boosts German urban genre focus with new live agency

## Summary

German promoter Contra launched a new boutique live agency, Pretty Noise, with management and publishing firm Sick & Tired to build an urban-genre artist roster, with singer Vicky set to launch the venture via a German tour this fall.

### Key Points
- New joint venture between Contra and Sick & Tired, led by Luis Knebl.
- Focused on urban, indie pop, electronic and leftfield genres.
- Vicky is the first announced roster artist, touring Oct.-Nov. 2026 with a bigger German run planned for 2027.

## Transcript

With new venture, Pretty Noise, Contra is aiming at building a "boutique" urban roster with the help of management and label firm Sick & Tired

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Citizens-Live-at-The-Wylie-1.png)

L-R: Lara Worch, Vicky, Luis Knebl © Contra, Pretty Noise

German live player Contra Promotion has launched a new live music agency with Sick & Tired, a German management and publishing company, in a bid to boost its domestic roster.

Known as Pretty Noise, the new division is intended as a boutique brand that will focus on urban genres, building on the previous work of Sick & Tired founder Luis Knebl, who has spent a decade working with artists in “rap, urban, and other related genres”, the companies said.

On the Contra side, Lara Worch and co-managing director Manuel Carvalho will begin working closely with Knebl on the venture, which Contra described as a “division” of its main business.

Speaking of Knebl, Pretty Noise said: “As managing director of Sick & Tired GmbH, he has been active in artist management and the label business for many years, supporting acts over the long term in their artistic and strategic development.”

> “We are to using this step to further expand its domestic department”

“As a boutique brand, Pretty Noise focuses on a carefully curated roster spanning urban, indie pop, electronic and leftfield sounds,” the two firms said.

Bochum-based Contra, which represents international touring acts such as Tokio Hotel and Electric Callboy worldwide, said it is using this step “to further expand its domestic department, which already includes artists such as Deine Cousine and Mehnersmoos”.

The first artist publicly announced for the Pretty Noise roster is Vicky, who is set to launch the agency with a German tour of mid-sized venues across October and November, with notable stops including the 2,700-cap Zoom in hamburg,

“In 2027, \[Vicky\] will embark on her biggest German tour to date together with Pretty Noise, taking her to a total of nine cities. The tour will also mark the operational launch of Pretty Noise and the beginning of the gradual development of its new roster,” the companies said, adding that “further artists and projects from Pretty Noise will be announced shortly”.
