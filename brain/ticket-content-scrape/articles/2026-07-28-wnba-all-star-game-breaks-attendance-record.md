# WNBA All-Star Game breaks attendance record

## Summary

The WNBA All-Star Game drew a record crowd of 19,783 at Chicago's United Center on July 25, surpassing the prior All-Star record and the NBA's own 2026 All-Star Game attendance. It's part of a broader pattern of WNBA attendance records this season, including a regular-season high in Montreal earlier in July.

### Key Points
- New WNBA All-Star attendance record: 19,783, beating the 2007 mark of 19,487.
- Exceeded the NBA's 2026 All-Star Game attendance of 15,783.
- Team Spoon beat Team Coop 129-122; Jonquel Jones won MVP.
- Follows a separate WNBA regular-season attendance record (20,996) set in Montreal earlier this month.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Women’s National Basketball Association (WNBA) All-Star Game set a new attendance record with a crowd of 19,783.**

The match was played on July 25 at Chicago’s United Center between Team Spoon and Team Coop, which were named after former WNBA players Cynthia Cooper and Teresa Weatherspoon.

The event surpassed the previous All-Star benchmark of 19,487, which was set in Washington D.C. in 2007.

It also exceeded the 15,783 fans who attended the NBA’s 2026 All-Star Game at the Intuit Dome in Inglewood near Los Angeles.

Team Spoon, featuring stars like Caitlin Clark and A’ja Wilson, secured a 129-122 victory over Team Coop, which boasted the likes of Paige Bueckers.

New York Liberty centre Jonquel Jones put on an MVP performance off the bench, recording 22 points, 13 rebounds, and 8 assists for Team Spoon.

The milestone event adds to another league attendance record, following the regular-season milestone set earlier this month by the Toronto Tempo, who welcomed 20,996 fans to Montreal’s Bell Centre for a match against the Dallas Wings.

The WNBA regular season is set to restart tomorrow (July 29) with five matches and runs until September 25 before the playoffs begin.
