# The Team to work with Diljit Dosanjh on first global representation

## Summary

UK-based agency The Team has signed Punjabi superstar Diljit Dosanjh for his first global representation deal, timed just ahead of Dosanjh headlining the 132,000-capacity Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad. The move follows Dosanjh's high-profile Wembley date and signals growing international agency interest in South Asian touring talent.

### Key Points
- The Team will handle Diljit Dosanjh's first-ever global representation.
- Announcement lands just before his headline show at the 132,000-cap Narendra Modi Stadium, Ahmedabad.
- Follows his recent Wembley performance, part of a broader international breakout.
- Coverage ties into IQ's wider reporting on Dosanjh's and Arijit Singh's India stadium tours as evidence of unprecedented scale in the market.
