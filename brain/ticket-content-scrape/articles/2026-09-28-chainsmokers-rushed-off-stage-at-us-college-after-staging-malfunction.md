# Chainsmokers ‘rushed off stage’ at US college after staging malfunction

## Summary

The Chainsmokers were rushed off stage during a student-run concert at Vanderbilt University after a staging malfunction, later described in some reports as a partial stage collapse. The duo relocated to perform in-the-round in the middle of the 7,000-person audience while the original stage's light show continued.

### Key Points
- Incident occurred during the first song of the set at a Lights on the Lawn student event.
- Cause not officially disclosed by organizers or the university; some reports cite a partial stage collapse.
- Duo completed the show from within the crowd rather than the original stage.
- No injuries reported in the excerpted coverage.

## Transcript

## Chainsmokers ‘rushed off stage’ at US college after staging malfunction

Despite the stage problems at the student-organised event at Vanderbilt University, the duo's performance went ahead elsewhere on the same site

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/09/Chainsmokers-in-Chicago-scaled.jpg.webp)

The Chainsmokers in Chicago

The Chainsmokers were “rushed off stage” during a student-run concert at Vanderbilt University in Tennessee after problems with the stage, organisers of the event have said.

After leaving the stage during the first song of the set, the electronic duo ultimately finished their performance in a different portion of the site in front of 7,000 fans. After being removed from the stage, the band moved their equipment to the centre of the audience space to play in-the-round. The concert’s light show continued on the original stage.

The exact nature of the stage problem was not disclosed by event organisers or the university, though some reports have described it as a [“partial collapse”](https://edm.com/news/partial-stage-collapse-the-chainsmokers-vanderbilt/). Posting footage of the concert on social media, Lights on the Lawn organisers said the stage “has issues”. *IQ* has contacted Lights on the Lawn for comment, as well as Vanderbilt University.

The concert, which came as part of the duo’s ongoing US college campus tour, was part of Vanderbilt University’s Lights on the Lawn series, annual event organised by students of the Nashville university. No injuries were reported.

> “The Chainsmokers got rushed off the stage in Nashville. \[…\] No-one knew if the show could go on”

It is unclear who was responsible for the stage structure at the concert, though the concert series’ website says that a team of students operate and organise the event “with full authority and autonomy”.

“The Chainsmokers got rushed off the stage in Nashville. \[…\] No-one knew if the stage could be fixed,” organisers wrote in a social media post on the day following the concert, accompanied by footage of the restarted show.

As with many similar US college-based events, Lights on the Lawn is organised and operated by a student team with support from the Student Affairs department of the university. The annual event “gives a team of students the opportunity to run a show with full authority and autonomy. Leaders negotiate contracts, work with Fortune 500 companies and plan a full-scale production”, the Lights on the Lawn website says.

The issues came only a day after a Chainsmokers show at the university of Tennessee was cancelled before start time following overcrowding and barrier issues which [reportedly](https://utdailybeacon.com/214205/arts-and-culture/there-was-no-way-of-stopping-it-chainsmokers-concert-cancelled-amid-crowd-safety-concerns/) caused injuries to fans. The band has also played university shows at the University of Mississippi, with a show at the University of Alabama also set for 23 October. These two shows, but not the Vanderbilt concert, are in association with booking company Refresh Music Group.

Vanderbilt’s Lights on the Lawn is a charity benefit concert, with all ticket proceeds going to Nashville-based charity the Mary Parrish Centre for victims of interpersonal violence.
