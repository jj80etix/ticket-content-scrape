# Legends Global Transforms Premium Lounge At AO Arena Manchester

## Summary

AO Arena Manchester has unveiled a rebranded Schweppes Signature Lounge, a premium hospitality space redesigned as part of Legends Global's ongoing partnership with Coca-Cola Europacific Partners. The relaunch pairs a new look and feel with a drinks menu built around Schweppes products, including exclusive cocktails, and has been open since September 2026.

### Key Points
- New premium lounge branding and drinks menu developed through Legends Global's CCEP partnership.
- Retains the existing premium hospitality package while refreshing the guest environment.
- Open now at AO Arena Manchester; upgrades available via the venue's website.

## Transcript

![Schweppes Lounge 14](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Schweppes-Lounge-14.jpg)

Schweppes Lounge 14

AO Arena Manchester has unveiled its new Schweppes Signature Lounge, transforming of one of its premium hospitality spaces as part of Legends Global’s ongoing partnership with Coca-Cola Europacific Partners (CCEP).

“The relaunch introduces a full rebrand, and a refreshed look and feel to the lounge, while retaining the premium package guests know and love. The redesign is centered on elevating the complete guest experience, pairing a stunning new environment with a new and enhanced drinks offering built around the Schweppes range,” according to Legend Global’s press announcement.

At the heart of the Schweppes Signature Lounge is a new drinks menu, including cocktails created exclusively for the space and featuring the products of the eponymous Swiss soft drink company.

The Schweppes Signature Lounge has been open at AO Arena, Manchester from September 2026. Fans wishing to upgrade their evening can visit the AO Arena website here.
