# Tour news: Tyla goes global for the first time

## Summary

IQ's touring roundup covers Tyla's first global tour, Jay-Z adding stadium dates by demand, Faith No More/System of a Down's joint Australia-NZ run, and further Beach Boys/Kacey Musgraves cancellation details.

### Key Points
- Tyla's 34-date *A\*POP World Tour* spans Europe, the UK, North America and Africa, Oct. 12-Jan. 9.
- Jay-Z adds a second London date (Tottenham Hotspur Stadium) and second LA date (SoFi Stadium) to his 30-year celebration run.
- Faith No More/System of a Down confirm a four-date stadium run in Australia/NZ for January 2027.
- Beach Boys and Kacey Musgraves cancellations are covered again, consistent with the wider "Blue Dot Fever" trend.

## Transcript

Plus: Jay-Z adds stadium dates due to demand, and announcements from Faith No More, System of a Down, Teddy Swims, Karan Aujla, and more

South African star **Tyla** has announced a 34-date global run with *The A\*Pop World Tour*, spanning Europe, the UK, North America and Africa, marking the singer’s first global run.

Coming in support of her sophomore album **A\*POP* ,* the tour follows Tyla’s 2025 We Wanna Party Tour across Asia, which included sold-out performances in Japan and the UAE. The tour opens on 12 October in Paris’s Zenith (6,300-cap) and closes 9 January at Johannesburg’s Expo Centre Nasrec (20,000-cap).

In between, European stops include AFAS Live (6,000-cap) and O2 Academy Brixton (5,000-cap), before a North American arena and theatre stretch from California’s Hard Rock Live to Las Vegas’s Fontainebleau (3,800-cap) between mid-November and mid-December.

African dates follow in Lagos, Cape Town’s Green Point Track, and Johannesburg. Running just under three months, the tour marks Tyla’s first full headline outing across all four territories.

In stadium news, **JAŸ‑Z** has added two new stadium dates to his 30‑year career celebration run, expanding the short global series following his record‑breaking three‑night sell‑out at Yankee Stadium. A second London show has been confirmed for 5 September at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium (62,850-cap), joining the previously announced 4 September performance.

In Los Angeles, a second night has been added on 24 October at SoFi Stadium, following the 23 October opener. The run already includes a Paris stop at Stade de France, with promoters attributing the added shows to “overwhelming demand”.

**Faith No More** have confirmed their live return with a four‑date 2027 stadium run co‑headlined with **System of a Down**, marking their first shows in over a decade. The short Australia–New Zealand swing opens 22 January at Sydney’s Accor Stadium (83,500-cap) before moving through Melbourne, Brisbane, and concluding in Wellington.

Spanning under two weeks, the outing marks the band’s first touring activity since 2016, following multiple cancelled plans in recent years. for System of a Down, it will follow the band’s recently concluded European stadium tour which saw them play Berlin’s Olympiastadion and London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, as well dates in Paris, Warsaw, and Milan.

**Teddy Swims** has also unveiled his largest EU and UK headline run to-date, with *The Ugly Tour* set for March–April 2027.

The 20‑date arena tour begins 2 March at Oslo’s Unity Arena and winds through major European markets before reaching the UK and Ireland. Stops include Stockholm’s Avicii Arena, Hamburg’s Barclays Arena, Amsterdam’s Ziggo Dome and Paris’s Accor Arena.

The UK leg spans Birmingham’s Utilita Arena, Glasgow’s OVO Hydro, Manchester’s Co‑op Live, and closes 21 April at London’s The O2, marking his biggest arena routing to date.

Meanwhile, Punjabi star **Karan Aujla** has announced a three‑date UK arena hop for October 2026 as part of his P‑Pop Culture tour, marking his biggest British headline shows to date.

Promoted by Live Nation, the short routing opens 1 October at Birmingham’s Utilita Arena (15,800-cap), before hitting London’s The O2 (20,000-cap) on 4 October and closing 8 October at Manchester’s Co‑op Live (23,500-cap).

In Asia, **Kanye West** will play his first-ever Indonesia show on 24 October, headlining Gelora Bung Karno Main Stadium, Jakarta (77,000 cap.) — the sole South‑east Asia stop on his 2026 Ye Live Concert Tour.

**The Beach Boys** have cancelled nearly 30 remaining summer shows so frontman Mike Love can “recharge,” the band said. The news will put a pause to the *Pet Sounds 60th Anniversary Tour* schedule after recent health‑related cancellations.

The band had been touring across North America and Europe, with dates running through 18 July before the halt. They plan to resume on 24 September at Big E Arena, Springfield, while attempting to reschedule some shows amid growing concern over Love’s onstage condition.

In other cancellation news, **Kacey Musgraves** has cancelled three dates on her upcoming *Middle Of Nowhere* tour, affecting dates on 20 August (Chicago), 29 August (Boston), and 2 September (Brooklyn), all of which were recently added after the original schedule announcement.
