# Alejandro Sanz Announces 2027 U.S. Concerts Following Latin Grammy Win

## Summary

Alejandro Sanz has announced two 2027 U.S. concerts, in New York and Las Vegas, as part of a broader 76-concert international campaign that has already notched 69 sellouts. The announcement follows his Latin Grammy win and does not yet include ticket prices or resale/transfer restrictions. A Citi cardholder presale opens Nov. 17, ahead of general onsale.

### Key Points
- Two confirmed 2027 U.S. dates (New York, Las Vegas) as part of a 76-show international run.
- Citi presale: Nov. 17-19; no pricing or resale-restriction details disclosed yet.
- Comes on the heels of Sanz's Latin Grammy album-of-the-year-category recognition.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/alejandro-sanz-2027-concerts.webp)

Alejandro Sanz has announced two U.S. concerts for May 2027, bringing his *¿Y Ahora Qué +?* tour back to Brooklyn and Miami just days after the Spanish singer-songwriter won another Latin Grammy.

The Live Nation-promoted *¿Y Ahora Qué +? US Tour 2027* currently consists of two confirmed dates, both in cities that hosted Sanz during his American tour earlier this year. The announcement did not identify any additional U.S. performances.

The news follows Sanz’s November 12 victory for Best Contemporary Pop Album at the Latin Grammy Awards in Las Vegas. His album *¿Y Ahora Qué +?* took the prize one year after its predecessor, *¿Y Ahora Qué?*, won the same category in 2025, as confirmed by the .

The 2027 shows will continue the concert production built around those recordings and Sanz’s established catalog. Organizers say the set will combine material from *¿Y Ahora Qué +?* with familiar hits from his more than three-decade career. The recent material includes “Palmeras en el Jardín,” “Bésame” with Shakira, and “Hoy No Me Siento Bien” with Grupo Frontera. No complete set list has been announced for 2027. His previous U.S. itinerary included 13 concerts in April and May 2026, with stops at both Barclays Center in Brooklyn and Kaseya Center in Miami.

The wider *¿Y Ahora Qué?* touring campaign began in Mexico in September 2025. Its first Mexican stretch included 25 concerts, among them seven performances at Mexico City’s Auditorio Nacional. The tour subsequently traveled through South America, the United States and Spain, where his summer itinerary included Estadio La Cartuja in Seville, Riyadh Air Metropolitano in Madrid and RCDE Stadium near Barcelona, before returning to Mexico in October and continuing through Central America and the Caribbean.

That history gives the newly announced dates a different shape from last spring’s [broader American run](https://ocesa-seitrack.squarespace.com/noticias/2025/11/3/alejandro-sanz-anuncia-las-fechas-para-estados-unidos-de-su-gira-y-ahora-que?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which also visited Chicago, Washington, Houston, Dallas, Los Angeles, San Jose and Las Vegas. Sanz’s representatives describe the international campaign as a 76-concert run with 69 sellouts.

## Alejandro Sanz 2027 Tickets and Presales

A Citi cardholder presale begins Tuesday, November 17, at 10 a.m. Eastern and continues through Thursday, November 19, at 11 p.m. local time. Presale eligibility and access details are available through [Citi Entertainment](https://www.citientertainment.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). General ticket sales begin Friday, November 20, at 10 a.m. local time through [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/alejandro-sanz-tickets/artist/755193?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Because both announced venues are in the Eastern Time Zone, the presale starts at 10 a.m. locally in each city. The announcement did not include ticket prices or identify any event-specific resale or ticket-transfer restrictions.
