# Georgia Tech Extends 42-Year Paciolan Ticketing Partnership Ahead of Stadium Reseating

## Summary

Georgia Tech athletics renewed its 42-year ticketing partnership with Paciolan, expanding its use of Paciolan's data, fundraising and venue-entry products. The renewal adds Paciolan iQ (a combined ticketing/fundraising/fan database for segmentation and personalized marketing), PAC io Fundraising (donor-ticketing integration) and PAC io Entry (NFC contactless access with real-time entry alerts and heat maps). It also sets up Paciolan's Ballena Seats 3D visualization tool for a full stadium reseating ahead of a $70 million Bobby Dodd Stadium renovation for the 2027 season. Terms and contract length were not disclosed.

### Key Points
- 42-year ticketing relationship renewed; Georgia Tech has sold out 65 events since the 2025-26 seasons began.
- New/expanded products: Paciolan iQ (unified ticketing+fundraising+fan data for segmentation/personalized marketing), PAC io Fundraising, PAC io Entry (NFC entry, real-time entry alerts, activity heat maps).
- Ballena Seats 3D will let season-ticket holders visualize seats during a full reseating of the renovated 50,000-capacity Bobby Dodd Stadium (up from a previously planned ~42,000).
- $70M stadium renovation includes 12,500 permanent chairback seats; first full reseating since 2003.
- Season-ticket holders must commit by Dec. 31, 2026 for 2027 seating priority; 2027 prices for non-premium seating held at 2026 levels.

### Highlights
- Paciolan iQ bundles ticketing, fundraising and fan data into one database for personalized marketing — a direct feature-parity signal for primary-platform CRM/marketing tooling.
- PAC io Entry adds NFC contactless admission plus real-time entry alerts and heat maps, following Paciolan's April 2026 facial-recognition entry partnership with Wicket.
- Ballena Seats 3D seat-visualization is being used for a large-scale reseating project, a concrete use case for 3D seat-selection tech at venue-renovation scale.

### Industry Problem
Collegiate/venue clients want a single platform that unifies ticketing, fundraising and fan data to drive personalized marketing and donor engagement, plus modern entry technology (contactless, real-time ops visibility) and seat-visualization tools for large reseating projects — capabilities that justify retaining or switching a primary ticketing vendor over decades.

### Proposed Solution
Paciolan is bundling CRM-style data/fundraising integration, NFC/biometric entry, and 3D seat visualization as retention and upsell levers for long-tenured clients. For Etix, this is a Build/Improve signal: benchmark Paciolan iQ's segmentation/personalization and PAC io Entry's real-time ops visibility against current offerings, and watch whether 3D seat-visualization becomes an expected feature for venues running large reseating or renovation projects.

Georgia Tech athletics has [renewed its ticketing agreement with Paciolan](https://ramblinwreck.com/news/2026/10/2/tech-athletics-renews-with-paciolan-as-ticketing-platform-provider?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), extending a relationship that dates to 1984 while expanding its use of the company’s data, fundraising and venue-entry technology.

Paciolan has served as Georgia Tech’s ticketing platform provider for 42 years and also supplies the athletic department with marketing, fundraising and data analytics services. Georgia Tech said it has sold out 65 events since the beginning of the 2025-26 athletic seasons, including three concerts at Bobby Dodd Stadium at Hyundai Field, while generating record ticket-sales and donation revenue.

Under the continued relationship, Georgia Tech has begun using Paciolan iQ, which combines ticketing, fundraising and consumer information in a centralized database. The platform can be used to segment fans and personalize marketing based on past purchases and other customer data.

Georgia Tech is also using PAC io Fundraising to connect donor management with its ticketing operation, along with [PAC io Entry](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/paciolan-partners-with-wicket-in-latest-facial-recognition-venue-entry-play/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Paciolan’s venue-access platform. The entry system supports NFC contactless admission and gives operators real-time information including entry alerts and heat maps showing activity at access points.

The renewal also comes ahead of a significant ticketing project at Bobby Dodd Stadium. Georgia Tech plans to use Paciolan’s Ballena Seats 3D technology as it carries out a [full reseating of the stadium for the 2027 football season](https://ramblinwreck.com/27bds-reseat?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), allowing season-ticket holders to visualize available seating before making their selections.

The reseating is required as part of a $70 million renovation of the stadium, which will include 12,500 permanent chairback seats, wider aisles, new handrails and an overhaul of premium areas. It will be the first full reseating at Bobby Dodd Stadium since its previous major renovation in 2003.

Georgia Tech expects the renovated stadium to retain a capacity of approximately 50,000, rather than the roughly 42,000 contemplated under an earlier design. The school has said most season-ticket holders should therefore have an opportunity to remain in or near their current locations.

Current season-ticket holders have until Dec. 31 to commit to 2027 tickets, with seat-selection priority determined by Alexander-Tharpe Fund Priority Points as of the same date. Individual selection times will be distributed in late January before online seat selection begins in February.
