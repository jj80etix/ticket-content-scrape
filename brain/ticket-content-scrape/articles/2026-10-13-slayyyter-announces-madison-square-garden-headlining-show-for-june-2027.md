# Slayyyter Announces Madison Square Garden Headlining Show for June 2027

## Summary

Slayyyter will headline Madison Square Garden in New York on June 21, 2027, her largest headlining show to date, with tickets going on sale October 16. The announcement follows the sold-out North American leg of her "Wor$t Girl in the World Tour," which wraps with a run of sold-out European and UK dates through early November.

### Key Points
- MSG headline show set for June 21, 2027; tickets on sale Oct. 16.
- Caps off the North American leg of the "Wor$t Girl in the World Tour."
- Sold-out European dates begin Oct. 18 in Paris, continuing through Antwerp.
- UK leg opens Oct. 30 in Manchester, closing Nov. 5 at London's Roundhouse.
- Also booked for Laneway Festival dates in Australia/NZ in February.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Slayyyter-Madison-Square-Garden.webp)

Slayyyter will headline Madison Square Garden in New York on June 21, 2027, marking her largest headlining performance to date.

The announcement follows the completion of the North American leg of her “Wor$t Girl in the World Tour,” which ended with two sold-out New York shows.

The arena announcement arrives as Slayyyter prepares to continue touring overseas. Her European dates begin October 18 at Le Trianon in Paris, followed by performances in Amsterdam, Cologne, Copenhagen and Hamburg. Additional stops include Warsaw, Prague, Berlin and Antwerp before the tour moves to the United Kingdom.

The British portion opens October 30 at Manchester’s Albert Hall. Shows in Birmingham, Glasgow and Brighton follow, with the leg concluding November 5 at London’s Roundhouse. The announced European and U.K. dates are sold out.
