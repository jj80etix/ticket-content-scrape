# Olivia Rodrigo raises $20m with inaugural Daisy Chain Fields

## Summary

Olivia Rodrigo's inaugural Daisy Chain Fields, an all-women, one-day music festival held August 29 at Irvine, California's Great Park, raised $20 million to be split equally among 10 non-profits supporting women and girls.

### Key Points
- Lilith Fair-inspired event backed by Live Nation/C3 Presents; lineup included Chappell Roan, Doechii, Stevie Nicks, Rachel Chinouriri, Garbage, and Mitski, all donating their time.
- Proceeds distributed equally across 10 women- and girls-focused non-profit organizations.
