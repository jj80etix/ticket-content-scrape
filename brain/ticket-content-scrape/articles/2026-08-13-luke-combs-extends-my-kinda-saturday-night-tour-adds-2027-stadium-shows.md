# Luke Combs Extends ‘My Kinda Saturday Night Tour,’ Adds 2027 Stadium Shows

## Summary

Luke Combs is extending his "My Kinda Saturday Night Tour" with a run of 2027 North American stadium shows following a record-setting international leg, including a sold-out three-night Wembley Stadium stand that grossed over $26.4 million. The 2027 run kicks off April 3 in Arlington, Texas, with presales beginning August 19.

### Key Points
- 2026 leg moved 1.37 million tickets across 22 shows, including sold-out back-to-back nights at Slane Castle and a sellout at Accor Arena Paris — tour records for a country artist.
- 2027 North American stadium run begins April 3 in Arlington, TX, wrapping June 26 in San Diego.
- Fan-club presale begins Aug. 19; general onsale Aug. 21.
- Combs is managed by Make Wake Artists and booked by WME.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed-4.jpg)

Luke Combs isn’t done with his massively successful “My Kinda Saturday Night Tour.” The global superstar is back on the road next spring and visiting North American stadiums in Minneapolis, Philadelphia, Vancouver, Denver and other markets.

Combs recently wrapped the first leg of “My Kinda Saturday Night Tour” with a sold-out three-night stand at London’s Wembley Stadium, July 31-Aug. 2, which grossed a total of $26,459,745 off 232,495 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

In total, the country singer’s remarkable run moved 1,371,329 tickets across 22 shows, and set multiple records: Combs was the first country solo artist to headline Wembley Stadium; he was the first to ever sell out back-to-back nights at Ireland’s Slane Castle; he was the first country star to sell out Accor Arena in Paris; and his stops at Amsterdam’s John Crujff ArenA and Sweden’s Ullevi Stadium were the largest country shows in their respective countries.

Combs begins his 2027 run April 3 at AT&T Stadium in Arlington, Texas, and the routing includes stops at Detroit’s Ford Field, Gillet Stadium in Foxborough, Kansas City’s Arrowhead Stadium, Edmonton’s Commonwealth Stadium and Albertsons Stadium in Boise before wrapping up June 26 at San Diego’s Petco Park.

Treaty Oak Revival, Avery Anna, Shenandoah and Wyatt McCubbin will be supporting Combs during the tour.

Tickets for next year’s shows will be available via presales beginning Ag. 19 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general onsale on Aug. 21 at 10 a.m. local time. Combs’ official fan club, The Bootleggers, has advanced access to tickets through the fan presale. Fans can sign up on his website, [lukecombs.com](https://www.lukecombs.com/bootlegger-membership/?queueittoken=e_lukecombs~ts_1759431335~ce_true~rt_safetynet~h_58cbf39f9e018cfffae2ed0db079540a20380cbeecbcc3b33f735bb6408012a7).

Combs is managed by Make Wake Artists and booked by WME’s Aaron Tannenbaum.
