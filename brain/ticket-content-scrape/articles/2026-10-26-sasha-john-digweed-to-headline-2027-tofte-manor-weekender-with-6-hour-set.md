# Sasha & John Digweed To Headline 2027 Tofte Manor Weekender With 6-Hour Set

## Summary

Sasha & John Digweed will headline a 6-hour set at Tofte Manor's first-ever weekender in July 2027, promoted by Labyrinth Events, following sold-out shows at the venue in 2025 and 2026. Pre-sale opens Oct. 30 with general tickets following Oct. 31.

### Key Points
- First-ever weekender at Tofte Manor, UK, marking Labyrinth's sixth summer there.
- Follows "overwhelming demand" after two prior sold-out years.
- Event combines dance music with wellness programming (yoga, sound baths, glamping).

Sasha & John Digweed will headline Tofte Manor on July 3, 2027, for Labyrinth’s first-ever weekender, with a 6-hour set. Fast becoming a cultural institution at Tofte, the UK dance legends have built a very special connection with the venue. The announcement follows “overwhelming demand from fans” after sold-out shows at Tofte Manor in 2025 and 2026. The event will also mark Labyrinth’s sixth summer at the venue.

Fans of Sasha & John Digweed can sign up for pre-sale tickets now; pre-sale opens on Friday 30th October at 12pm, and any remaining general tickets go on sale from 12pm on Oct. 31.

Nestled in the rolling UK countryside, Tofte Manor is a unique open-air venue promoted exclusively by Labyrinth Events. Where hedonism meets spirituality, the 2027 Weekender brings together world-leading dance music, holistic experiences and the estate’s natural beauty and rich history to create a cohesive experience that unfolds from day to night.

Beyond the music, the weekender celebrates the shared ethos of Labyrinth and Tofte Manor, bringing together the venue’s spirituality, the hedonism of world-leading dance music and an expanded program of holistic and wellness experiences, inviting guests to connect, explore and immerse themselves in the venue from day to night. Following a hugely successful debut last summer, Soul Corner will return with an expanded program of workshops and activities, building on its 2026 offering of guided yoga, sound baths, cacao ceremonies, dance circles and holistic treatments, including massage and reflexology. With more still to be revealed, every element has been thoughtfully considered to curate an immersive weekend that brings together music, spirituality and wellbeing.

“Sasha & John are part of the Tofte Manor story, so there was only ever one way to kick off the 2027 Weekender,” Labyrinth Events founder Nick Castleman said. “It’s our sixth summer at the venue, we’re taking everything we’ve learned over the years and we’re more excited than ever to offer new experiences including glamping for fans to experience the entire weekend. This is just the first announcement, with plenty more incredibly exciting news to come.”
