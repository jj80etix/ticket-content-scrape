# New Bosses 2026: This year’s class of rising talent revealed

## Summary

IQ Magazine has revealed its 2026 "New Bosses" list, the publication's 19th annual recognition of rising talent under 30 in the international live music business. The 20-strong cohort spans agencies, promoters, venues, and ticketing companies across multiple countries, selected from hundreds of nominations. Full profiles are available to IQ subscribers in issue 146, with daily spotlights to follow.

### Key Points
- 19th annual New Bosses cohort; 20 executives selected from hundreds of global nominations.
- Honorees span organizations including Ticketmaster (Benji Stoll), Live Nation Asia (Bryan Tan), Live Nation (Solomon Ogunbiyi), AEG (Christofer Melchior), United Talent Agency, and Coca-Cola Arena.
- Full profiles published in IQ Magazine issue 146, available to subscribers.
- Selection criteria: under 30, shaping the future of the international live music business.
