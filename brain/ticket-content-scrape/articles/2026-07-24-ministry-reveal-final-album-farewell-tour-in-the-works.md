# Ministry Reveal Final Album, Farewell Tour In-The-Works

## Summary

Industrial rock band Ministry has announced its farewell tour alongside a final studio album, *Hate To Go (Take Out Or Delivery)*, due October 30. The band will play a handful of U.S. dates this fall (including Red Rocks, Chicago's Salt Shed, and festival stops at Louder Than Life and Sick New World Texas) before a previously announced UK/European run next spring, with a full U.S. tour to follow.

### Key Points
- Ministry frontman Al Jourgensen announced the band's farewell tour and 17th and final studio album.
- Fall 2026 U.S. dates include Red Rocks, Chicago's Salt Shed, and festival appearances at Louder Than Life and Sick New World Texas.
- A previously announced UK/European tour runs April-May 2027 across more than 20 cities.
- A full U.S. tour will follow the European leg, with details still to be announced.
- The album features longtime and returning members, including original member Paul Barker on one track.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ministry.jpg)

The industrial icons of Ministry are heading out on a farewell tour to coincide with their final forthcoming record.

“Done. Sayonara. Finito. However you want to say it, Ministry is coming to an end,” the band said in a press release. “But before bidding adieu, creator and music madman Al Jourgensen has a few final things to say about the state of the world, those in charge and how we should all collectively move forward.”

Ministry has a handful of shows this fall, including gigs at Chicago’s Salt Shed, Morrison’s Red Rocks Amphitheater, and performances at Louisville’s Louder Than Life and Sick New World Texas. As previously announced, they’ll head overseas next spring for dates across Europe and the UK, wrapping-up in May.

A full U.S. tour will follow the European dates next year, though full touring details have not been announced.
