# Mike Tyson immersive experience set to debut in Las Vegas

## Summary

A new AR/VR/hologram immersive attraction based on Mike Tyson's life and career opens at Flamingo Las Vegas this autumn, with tickets ($45–$129) going on sale August 5. Producer Your ID frames Las Vegas as the flagship location for a planned multi-city rollout.

### Key Points
- "Iron Mike Legacy: The Mike Tyson Immersive Experience" opens at Flamingo Las Vegas this autumn.
- Ticket sales begin August 5, priced $45–$129.
- Produced by experiential-entertainment company Your ID, which plans further locations beyond Las Vegas.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**A new immersive experience dedicated to the life and career of former boxer and convicted rapist Mike Tyson is set to open at the Flamingo Las Vegas hotel this autumn.**

Iron Mike Legacy: The Mike Tyson Immersive Experience will use augmented reality, virtual reality, holograms and projection mapping to take visitors through the arc of Tyson’s life inside and outside the ring.

The experience will immerse visitors in what producers describe as Tyson’s triumphs, challenges and personal transformation.

Ticket sales are set to begin on August 5 with prices ranging from $45 (£33/€40) to $129.

“Las Vegas has been the backdrop for some of the most important moments of my life and career,” said Tyson.

“The city embraced me, challenged me, and helped shape the person I am today.

“That’s why it’s so meaningful to launch Iron Mike Legacy at Flamingo Las Vegas, one of the most legendary resorts on The Strip.

“This experience is deeply personal, and I’m excited for fans from around the world to step inside my story and experience the highs, the lows, and everything in between.”

Your ID, a global experiential entertainment company, is producing the attraction.

“The launch of Iron Mike Legacy at Flamingo Las Vegas marks the debut of a platform designed for global expansion,” read a statement from Your ID.

“Mike Tyson is one of the most recognised figures in sports and entertainment, and we’ve created an experience that can connect with audiences across generations and international markets.

“Las Vegas is our flagship location, but this is only the beginning.

“We look forward to announcing additional destinations as we continue building a worldwide footprint for the brand.”

The goal, the creators said, is to put visitors into important parts of Tyson’s life so they can gain a more personal insight.

In 1992, Tyson was sentenced to six years in prison for the rape of then 18-year-old Desiree Washington in Indianapolis, although he was released on parole after three years.

Tyson maintained his innocence in the trial that dominated headlines in the early 1990s and maintains his stance today.

The trial also sparked debates about racial attitudes in the criminal justice system, something which Tyson feels affected the verdict.

“I knew from the start that I’d get no justice,” he wrote in his 2013 book Undisputed Truth: My Autobiography.

“I wasn’t being tried in New York or Los Angeles; we were in Indianapolis, Indiana, historically one of the strongholds of the Ku Klux Klan.

“I had been found guilty by a jury of my ‘peers,’ only one of whom was black.”
