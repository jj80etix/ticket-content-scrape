# Grace Potter And The Trespassers Announce 2027 Tour

## Summary

Grace Potter and The Trespassers announced a 48-date 2027 "Trespasser Tour" supporting her seventh studio album, *Trespasser*. The trek runs Jan. 14 in Vermont through March 27 in Seattle, hitting venues like the Beacon Theatre and The Anthem.

### Key Points
- 48-date North American tour, Jan. 14 – March 27, 2027
- General on-sale Nov. 13; artist presale Nov. 11
- $1 from every ticket supports Potter's Grand Point Foundation, backing Vermont arts programming

Grace Potter and The Trespassers announced the 2027 “Trespasser Tour,” supporting her critically acclaimed seventh studio album, *Trespasser*. The 48-date trek will take Potter to The Beacon Theatre in New York City, The Anthem in Washington, DC, Pabst Theatre in Milwaukee, Palace Theatre in St. Paul, The Fillmore in Denver, Belly Up in Aspen and more. The tour starts on Jan. 14 in Potter’s hometown of Vermont and ends March 27 in Seattle. See full routing below.

General ticket on-sale begins Nov. 13 at 10 a.m. local time. Potter’s artist pre-sale begins Nov. 11 at 10 a.m. local time. For more information and tickets, please visit https://www.gracepotter.com/tour.

$1 from every ticket sold supports Potter’s Grand Point Foundation and its mission to strengthen and sustain the arts in rural Vermont communities. Through grants, workshops, community partnerships, and creative programming, Grand Point Foundation expands access to music and the arts, supports local artists and organizations, and creates meaningful opportunities for people of all ages to experience and participate in the arts.
