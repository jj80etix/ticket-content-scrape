# Exclusive: Mexican Music Icon Pedro Fernández Embarking On 2026 Tour Across US & Mexico

## Summary

Mexican music icon Pedro Fernández has announced his 2026 "Ave Fénix Two'r" tour, a Live Nation-promoted run spanning 13 U.S. shows plus dates in Mexico City and Guadalajara. The U.S. leg opens October 15 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood and wraps December 5 in San Diego, with a Citi presale beginning August 11 ahead of general onsale August 14 via LiveNation.com.

### Key Points

- 13 U.S. shows plus two non-Live Nation Mexico dates (Auditorio Nacional Sept 5, Auditorio Telmex Nov 7).
- U.S. leg runs Oct 15 (YouTube Theater, Inglewood) through Dec 5 (Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre, San Diego), including stops in San Jose, New York, Rosemont, Denver, Houston, and Phoenix.
- Citi presale began Aug 11 at 2pm ET; general onsale Aug 14 at 10am local via LiveNation.com.
- Tour builds on a successful 2025 "Ave Fénix Tour," which included a Rosemont Theatre show grossing $319,270 off 3,890 tickets per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Promises a "completely reimagined experience" for the three-time Latin Grammy winner's career spanning four decades, 39 studio albums, and 25 films.

America’s Aventurero is making his way back to venues in the U.S. and Mexico this fall with his “Ave Fénix Two’r,” reaffirming Pedro Fernández’s status as one of Mexican music’s most iconic figures.

Fernández will perform at two of the busiest arenas in Mexico, Sept. 5 at Auditorio Nacional in the nation’s capital and Nov. 7 at Guadalajara’s Auditorio Telmex, and play 13 U.S. shows

The Live Nation-promoted tour opens the U.S. leg Oct. 15 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California, and the routing includes stops at San Jose Civic in the Bay Area, New York’s Infosys Theater at Madison Square Garden, Rosemont Theatre near Chicago, Yaamava’ Theater in Highland, Denver’s Bellco Theatre, 713 Music Hall in Houston and Phoenix’s Arizona Financial Theatre before wrapping up Dec. 5 at Cal Coast Credit Union Open Air Theatre in San Diego.

A Citi presale begins today, Aug. 11, at 2 p.m. EST, and additional presales will run ahead of the general onsale, which begins Aug. 14 at 10 a.m. local time at [LiveNation.com](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ9171pC0/pedro-fernandez-events).

The new trek builds off his successful 2025 run, “Ave Fénix Tour,” and this year’s trek promises a “completely reimagined experience” with a world-class production and a setlist that has made Fernández an icon of música Mexicana, according to a release announcing the tour. “Ave Fénix Two’r” is a celebration of Fernández’s career spanning more than four decades, with a catalog that has defined generations, including hit songs “Yo No Fui,” “Amarte a la Antiguia,” “Mi Forma de Sentir” and “El Aventurero.”

The three-time Latin Grammy winner, born José Martín Cuevas Cobos in Guadalajara, had a successful tour last year, including a show at Rosemont Theatre on Aug. 23, that grossed $319,270 off 3,890 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.
