# John Mayer Adds June 2027 Dates To ‘Live At Sphere’ Residency

## Summary

John Mayer added three more dates to his 2027 "Live At Sphere" Las Vegas residency, citing overwhelming demand, bringing the run to 18 total shows between April and June. An artist presale for the new dates begins October 14, with general onsale following October 16 at Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Three new dates added (June 9, 11, 12) to the "Live At Sphere" residency.
- Residency now totals 18 shows, April 8 through June 12, 2027.
- Artist presale for new dates begins Oct. 14; SiriusXM presale Oct. 15.
- General onsale for new dates Oct. 16 via Ticketmaster.
- Each show blends solo, full-band, and John Mayer Trio sets with Sphere's immersive production.

UPDATE:  
Citing “overwhelming demand,” John Mayer has added three more performances to his “Live At Sphere” residency that promises to “combine immersive sound, visual spectacle, and sensory feedback to make each show a unique, unforgettable celebration.”  
  
The new dates are booked June 9 and 11-12.  
  
An artist pre-sale, powered by seated, begins Wednesday, Oct. 14 for the new dates at 10 a.m. PT at *[https://johnmayer.com/](https://johnmayer.com/).* Today’s announcement notes that fans who previously registered for the pre-sale will have access to purchase tickets for the new dates. SiriusXM is also hosting a presale, which begins Thursday, Oct. 15. To get access, fans are told to tune in to Life with John Mayer for details. The general onsale starts Friday, Oct. 16 at 10 a.m. at Ticketmaster.com.  
  
The residency is now scheduled  
April 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, 18, 28, 30  
May 1, 5, 7, 8, 12, 14, 15  
June 2, 4, 5, 9, 11, 12  
  
PREVIOUSLY:  
John Mayer has announced that he will perform live at the Sphere in Las Vegas in April 2027. The acclaimed singer-songwriter will hit the stage in the desert on April 8, 10, 11, 15, 17, and 18, with each performance enhanced by the immersive sound, visual spectacle, and sensory feedback the Sphere has become known for.

As for the music, each show will feature John’s music in three forms: solo, full band, and the John Mayer trio. According to an official press release, the solo portion will feature Mayer alone on stage, leaning heavily on his acoustic guitar work, with special performances on piano and electric guitar. The John Mayer Trio features John Mayer, Steve Jordan, and Pino Palladino.

The general on-sale will begin Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. PT at [https://ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/). Artist pre-sale registration is available now at [JohnMayer.com](https://johnmayer.com/), powered by seated, with the pre-sale beginning Oct. 7 at 10 a.m. PT.

SiriusXM listeners will have special pre-sale access to John Mayer’s newly announced dates at Sphere in Las Vegas. The exclusive SiriusXM presale begins Oct. 8, giving listeners an early opportunity to purchase tickets before they go on sale to the general public. SiriusXM listeners can tune in to Life with John Mayer for details on how to access the presale and get their chance to experience John Mayer live at Sphere.  
  
Additionally, a limited number of VIP ticket packages will be available at [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/) starting Sept. 30, at 10 AM PT. Select packages include premium seats, a date-specific lithograph signed by John, exclusive merchandise, and more. Tickets for each show will be auctioned off through charityauctionstoday.com/. 100% of proceeds from the auctions will benefit the Heart and Armor Foundation – a foundation for veterans’ health that Mayer founded in 2012.  
  
Tickets start at $129. $1 from each ticket sold will go to Mayer’s Back To You Fund, which has supported many charities, including programs supporting at-risk youth and the homeless.  
  
Mayer is represented by CAA’s Mitch Rose and Nat Farnham worldwide. Reports submitted to Pollstar Boxoffice for Mayer include two shows at London’s The O2 in March 2024 that sold a total of 32,525 tickets and grossed nearly $4.9 million.
