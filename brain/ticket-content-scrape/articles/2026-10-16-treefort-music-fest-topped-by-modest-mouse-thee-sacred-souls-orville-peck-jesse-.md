# Treefort Music Fest Topped by Modest Mouse, Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles, Courtney Barnett In Boise March 31-April 4

## Summary

Pollstar's coverage of Treefort Music Fest's 2027 lineup (15th anniversary, March 31-April 4 in Boise, Idaho) echoes the TicketNews announcement, naming Modest Mouse, Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles and Courtney Barnett as top names in a first-wave, multi-venue announcement. Tickets are sold exclusively via DICE, with five-day GA passes reported 65% sold out already.

### Key Points
- First wave described as including "a majority" of headliners, with more acts still to be announced.
- Festival spans multiple Julia Davis Park venues, including The Hideout, Gene Harris Bandshell, Kidfort Nest, and the free outdoor Cyclops Stage.
- Tickets sold exclusively on DICE's ticketing platform; five-day GA passes already 65% sold out per the announcement.
- Produced by local promoter Duck Club Entertainment.

Treefort Music Fest is gearing up for its 15th anniversary, with a first wave of artists announced as the 180-artist shindig comes together for March 31 to April 1 in Downtown Boise, Idaho.

The first wave of artists on the multi-venue includes Modest Mouse, Thee Sacred Souls, Orville Peck, Jesse Welles, Courtney Barnett, Horsegirl, Slow Pulp, All Them Witches, LP Giobbi, Joy Oladokun, Kenny MAson, Enter Shikari, Eartheater, Lime Garden, and many others.

The announcement states that the first wave includes “a majority of their highly-anticipated headliners,” suggesting more top talent is still be announced.

Along with the music, taking place within multiple venues and spaces within Julia Davis Park, including The Hideout, Gene Harris Bandshell, the Kidfort Nest area for the younger ones, as well as free outdoor Cyclops Stage. This year sees Yogafort, featuring noted yogis and guided classes.

Tickets are on sale now for Treefort Music Fest, which is exclusively on the DICE ticketing platform. Fans are encouraged to buy five-day GA passes, which are already 65% sold out according to the announcement. Various ticket packages and discounts are offered.

Other attractions include the Lookout Lounge with dedicated bar, private bathrooms and furniture.

Treefort Fest is produced by local promoter Duck Club Entertainment.
