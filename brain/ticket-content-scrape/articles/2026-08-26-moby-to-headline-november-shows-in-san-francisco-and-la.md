# Moby To Headline November Shows In San Francisco And LA

## Summary

Moby will headline two California shows this fall, following a well-received Coachella set: November 5 at San Francisco's Bill Graham Civic Auditorium and November 7 at Pasadena's Brookside at the Rose Bowl, the latter with Underworld. Presales begin August 26, with general onsale August 27.

### Key Points
- San Francisco show: November 5, Bill Graham Civic Auditorium, with Azzecca and DJ Holographic.
- Pasadena show: November 7, Brookside at the Rose Bowl, with Underworld, Fcukers, Nia Archives, DJ Holographic.
- Presale August 26; general onsale August 27.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2271875648](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2271875648.jpg)

Building off a memorable Coachella set, renowned musician Moby is returning to California this fall to headline shows in San Francisco and Los Angeles.

The five-time Grammy nominee will perform Nov. 5 at Bill Graham Civic Auditorium with special guests Azzecca and DJ Holographic as special guests. Goldenvoice and Another Planet Entertainment are promoting the show.

Two days later, Moby will be at Pasadena’s Brookside at The Rose Bowl to headline a show with Underworld, Fcukers, a DJ set from Nia Archives and DJ Holographic. Presale

Presales for both nights begin Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. PT. Tickets go on sale to the general public Aug. 27. For tickets to the San Francisco show, visit [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/event/1C006515F9551DE3). For information about the Pasadena event, visit [mobylivepasadena.com](https://mobylivepasadena.com/).

The concerts will feature “an immersive production and a high-energy set” showcasing Moby’s expansive catalog, according to a Goldenvoice release.

Moby is booked by Independent Artist Group in North America.
