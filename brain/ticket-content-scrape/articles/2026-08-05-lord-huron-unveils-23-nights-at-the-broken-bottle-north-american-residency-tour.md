# Lord Huron Unveils ‘23 Nights at the Broken Bottle’ North American Residency Tour

## Summary

Lord Huron announced "23 Nights at the Broken Bottle," a 23-date North American residency tour running December 2026 through January 2027, transforming each venue into the band's fictional "Broken Bottle" honky-tonk with no opening act. The tour opens Dec. 1 at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium and closes with back-to-back Los Angeles shows at The Wiltern.

### Key Points
- 23 dates from Dec. 1 (Ryman Auditorium, Nashville) through Jan. 11 (The Wiltern, Los Angeles), including stops in Chicago, Boston, Brooklyn, Montréal, Vancouver, and Seattle.
- Each show is billed as "An Evening With Lord Huron" with no supporting act.
- Artist presale begins Aug. 5; general on-sale opens Aug. 7 via Ticketmaster and the band's website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Lord-Huron-.webp)

Lord Huron has announced “23 Nights at the Broken Bottle,” a 23-date North American traveling residency scheduled for late 2026 and early 2027.

The tour is set to kick off December 1 at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium. From there, the singer is scheduled to make stops in cities such as Cincinnati, Detroit, Chicago, Philadelphia, Boston, Brooklyn, Las Vegas, and Seattle before wrapping up with back-to-back nights in Los Angeles at The Wiltern on January 10 and 11.

The tour will transform each venue into the “Broken Bottle,” a desert honky-tonk that appears throughout the band’s fictional universe. Each performance will be presented as “An Evening With Lord Huron,” featuring no opening act.

An artist presale will begin Wednesday, August 5, at 12 p.m. local time. General ticket sales will open Friday, August 7, at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster and [Lord Huron’s official website](https://www.lordhuron.com/#/).
