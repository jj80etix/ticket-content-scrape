# Radiohead Unveil 2027 Tour Dates In Australia & Japan

## Summary

Radiohead announced a 21-date 2027 tour covering Australia and Japan — their first Australian run in 15 years — spanning Perth, Melbourne, Sydney and Saitama. Tickets are registration-only with a four-ticket cap per registration, explicitly framed as a bot/scalper mitigation measure; Australian registration closes Sept. 10, Japan registration Sept. 27.

### Key Points
- 21 shows: 4x Perth (RAC Arena), 6x Melbourne (Rod Laver Arena), 6x Sydney (Afterpay Arena), 5x Saitama (GMO Arena), May 3–June 13, 2027.
- Ticket access is registration-gated with a 4-ticket-per-registration cap, described as a bot/scalper reduction measure.
- Frontier Touring promotes the Australian leg; the band is booked by 13 Artists (int'l) and CAA (North America).
- Follows a 2025 European arena run where a 4-night Madrid stand grossed $12.2M off 68,737 tickets (Pollstar Boxoffice).

## Transcript

![radiohead](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/radiohead.jpg)

A month removed from teasing a return to Japan at last month’s Summer Sonic festival, Radiohead confirmed it will be returning to the Asian nation this year following the band’s first Australian run in 15 years.

Radiohead announced a 21-date tour in 2027, beginning with four nights at RAC Arena in Perth on May 3, 4, 6 and 7, followed by six shows at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne on May 13, 14, 16, 17, 19 and 20. The British band concludes the Australian leg of the tour with a six-night stand at Afterpay Arena in Sydney on May 25, 26, 28, 29, 31 and June 1. Frontier Touring is promoting all Australian dates.

The quintet concludes the run with five shows at Saitama’s GMO Arena on June 8, 9 and 11-13.

[**Read: Radiohead’s Ed O’Brien: Band Planning 20 Shows A Year World Tour Starting in 2027 (Report)**](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/17/radioheads-ed-obrien-band-planning-20-shows-a-year-world-tour-starting-in-2027-report/)

Tickets are available only to those who register, to reduce competition from bots and scalpers. There will be a four-ticket limit per registration, and fans have until 3 p.m. AWST on Sept. 10 to register for the Australian dates. Registration for the shows in Japan closes on Sept. 27 at 23:59 p.m. JST. Visit [radiohead.com](https://radiohead.com/) for more information.

In 2025, Radiohead, widely considered to be one of the most influential bands over the past three decades, toured for the first time in seven years and played arenas across Europe, including a four-night stand at Madrid’s Movistar Arena where they grossed $12.2 million off 68,737 tickets across all shows, according to Pollstar Boxoffice reports.

The band is repped by 13 Artists outside of North America, where they are booked by CAA’s Carole Kinzel.
