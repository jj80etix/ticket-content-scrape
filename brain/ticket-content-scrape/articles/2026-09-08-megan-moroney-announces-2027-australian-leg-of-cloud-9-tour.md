# Megan Moroney Announces 2027 Australian Leg of Cloud 9 Tour

## Summary

Country singer Megan Moroney announced a 2027 Australian leg of her "Cloud 9 Tour," adding three arena shows in Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane, alongside her ongoing European/UK leg.

### Key Points

- Australian shows: Rod Laver Arena (Melbourne, May 8), Afterpay Arena (Sydney, May 11), Brisbane Entertainment Centre (May 14); Laci Kaye Booth opens all three.
- European/UK leg runs Sept. 13-Oct. 1, 2026 across Oslo, Stockholm, Paris, London, and other markets.
- Frontier Member/artist presale began Sept. 8 for 24 hours; general public sale opened Sept. 9 with a four-ticket limit.
- Cloud 9, the album behind the tour, won Album of the Year at the 2026 American Music Awards.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/Megan-Moroney-1.webp)

Megan Moroney is extending her “Cloud 9 Tour” into 2027 with three arena shows in Australia.

The country singer will begin the Australian leg on May 8 at Rod Laver Arena in Melbourne. She will then perform at Afterpay Arena in Sydney on May 11 before closing the run at Brisbane Entertainment Centre on May 14.

Country singer-songwriter Laci Kaye Booth will join Moroney as the special guest at all three shows.

Moroney launched the “Cloud 9 Tour” in May in support of her third studio album, *Cloud 9*. The tour’s North American schedule included performances at Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, BOK Center in Tulsa, Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles and Barclays Center in Brooklyn.

Moroney is next scheduled to bring the tour to Europe and the United Kingdom. That leg begins September 13 at Sentrum Scene in Oslo, Norway, before continuing through markets including Stockholm, Paris, London, Glasgow and Belfast.

Released earlier in 2026, *Cloud 9* won Album of the Year at the 2026 American Music Awards. The record includes “6 Months Later,” “Beautiful Things” and “Wish I Didn’t,” along with collaborations featuring Ed Sheeran and Kacey Musgraves.

The Frontier Member and artist presales began September 8 at 11 a.m. local time and will remain open for 24 hours. The general public sale begins September 9 at noon local time. A limit of four tickets per purchaser applies during both the presale and public sale.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Moroney’s official website](https://www.meganmoroney.com/).
