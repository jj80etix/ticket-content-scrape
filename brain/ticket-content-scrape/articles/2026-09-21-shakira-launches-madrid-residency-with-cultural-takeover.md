# Shakira launches Madrid residency with cultural takeover

## Summary

Shakira opened a 12-night residency at a purpose-built stadium in Madrid, her first Spanish shows in eight years, pairing headline concerts with Latin-culture programming including exhibitions, talks and workshops.

### Key Points
- Played to 50,000+ fans on opening night (Sept. 18); the residency is expected to draw around 600,000 fans through Oct. 11.
- Staged at a bespoke venue within the Iberdrola Music site in southern Madrid.
