# The Doobie Brothers Set Nine-Show Las Vegas Residency for 2027

## Summary

The Doobie Brothers will debut at Fontainebleau Las Vegas with a nine-show 2027 residency at BleauLive Theater, split between February/March and August/September dates. Tickets go on general sale Oct. 30, with Citi, artist, Live Nation, and Fontainebleau presales running Oct. 27-29.

### Key Points
- Nine-show residency: Feb. 26-27, March 5-6, and five dates in Aug./Sept. 2027.
- Follows the band's 2025 album *Walk This Road*, which reunited the full classic lineup including Michael McDonald.
- Tiered presale schedule precedes Oct. 30 general on-sale.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Doobie-Brothers-Las-Vegas.webp)

The Doobie Brothers will make their Fontainebleau Las Vegas debut in 2027 with a nine-show residency at BleauLive Theater. The engagement begins with four concerts in February and March before the band returns for five additional shows in late August and early September.

The residency is scheduled to kick off with back-to-back nights on February 26 and 27, followed by two March performances on March 5 and 6. The band is then slated to return to the Fontainebleau Las Vegas for performances on August 27 and 28, as well as September 1, 3 and 4.

The shows extend the band’s recent run of touring activity with its reunited lineup of Tom Johnston, Patrick Simmons, John McFee and Michael McDonald. The performances are the group’s first announced concert dates for 2027, following the band’s [2026 Oneness Tour with Santana](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/santana-and-the-doobie-brothers-unite-for-2026-oneness-tour-across-north-america/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The Doobie Brothers’ latest studio album, *Walk This Road*, arrived in June 2025 and marked McDonald’s return to recording new material with the group for the first time since 1980. The record reunited the four musicians in the studio, adding newer songs to a concert catalog spanning more than five decades.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. Pacific through Fontainebleau Las Vegas. Citi cardmembers get first access through a presale beginning Tuesday, October 27, at 10 a.m. Pacific. An artist presale follows Wednesday, October 28, with Live Nation and Fontainebleau presales scheduled for Thursday, October 29.
