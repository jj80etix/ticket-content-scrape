# ‘We want to be a local partner’: Zquizo launches in Argentina

## Summary

Argentine entertainment companies Artes Group, Vorterix, and Grupo Octubre have formed Zquizo, a new integrated venture combining live-show production, artist development, tour social marketing, and fan-community management, launched at an event in Buenos Aires. Plans include a new 6,000-capacity venue and physical "hubs" for fan communities as part of an ecosystem its founders describe as designed to connect artists and audiences locally. The source article is paywalled beyond its opening paragraphs, so further deal terms are not yet available.

### Key Points
- Three existing Argentine entertainment companies merged capabilities into a single new venture, Zquizo.
- Covers live production, artist development, tour social marketing, and fan-community management.
- Plans include a new 6,000-cap venue and physical fan-community hubs.
- Full terms (ownership structure, any ticketing role) are behind IQ's paywall and unavailable from the extracted content.

### Highlights
- New three-way corporate alliance consolidating production, artist development, and fan engagement under one Argentine entertainment brand.
- Physical venue (6,000-cap) and fan-community infrastructure signal vertical-integration ambitions in the Latin American market.

### Industry Problem
Illustrates continued consolidation in Latin America's live-entertainment market, with regional players bundling production, venue, and fan-relationship capabilities to compete with larger international entrants.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor whether Zquizo builds or partners for its own ticketing/fan-data layer as it scales — that would be the trigger to reassess competitive exposure in the Latin American market.
