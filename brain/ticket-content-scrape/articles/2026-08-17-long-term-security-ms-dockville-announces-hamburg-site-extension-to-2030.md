# ‘Long-term security’: MS Dockville announces Hamburg site extension to 2030

## Summary

MS Dockville, a Hamburg urban festival, has secured a site-use extension through 2030 at its Wilhelmsburg Elbe Island location, locking in the venue ahead of the festival's 20th-anniversary edition in 2027. The 2026 (19th) edition drew 40,000 fans across more than 100 acts on multiple stages. Organizers teased bringing back acts and guests from the festival's history for the anniversary show.

### Key Points
- MS Dockville renewed its site agreement at Wilhelmsburg Elbe Island, Hamburg, through 2030.
- The 2026 edition drew 40,000 attendees across 100+ acts, headlined by Tom Odell and Wolf Alice.
- The extension secures the site for the festival's 20th-anniversary edition in 2027.
- Organizers plan to bring back alumni acts, guests, and crew from two decades of the festival.

## ‘Long-term security’: MS Dockville announces Hamburg site extension to 2030

The news secures the event's 20th anniversary next year, with organisers saying the harbour and industrial site is 'inextricably linked' to the festival's identity

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/08/Tom-Odell-@MSDV_10-scaled.jpg.webp)

Tom Oddell at MS Dockville © Kopf und Steine

Hamburg-based urban festival MS Dockville has welcomed the “long-term planning security” of a site extension until 2030 at its Wilhelmsburg Elbe Island location, after drawing 40,000 fans to its 19th annual edition.

Organisers said the extension at the urban site, just ten minutes’ journey from Hamburg Central Train Station, secures the location for next year’s event, already announced as 20th anniversary edition of the event.

This year’s festival staged performances by over 100 acts across tens stages, led by Tom Odell, Wolf Alice, Hamburg native BERQ, and German indie pop band Von Wegen Lisbeth. Following this year’s event, organisers hinted at a return for acts from the festival’s history to mark next year’s anniversary, saying: “Acts, guests, crew, and stories from two decades of MS Dockville will once again be part of the festival”.

> “It’s all the more wonderful that we can now not only plan our 20th anniversary but also know that MS Dockville has a future here”

“20 years of MS Dockville also means 20 years of music history on the Elbe island. Over the years, artists have graced the festival’s stages whose names are now indispensable in the international music scene,” organisers said.

Announcing the extension, the festival team said the location “has become inextricably linked to th festival over the past two decades”.

They praised the site directly on the Reiherstieg Canal, for “its blend of harbour and industrial scenery, green spaces, art, and temporary architecture”, saying it “is not only a venue but also an essential part of the festival’s identity”.

“Twenty years of MS Dockville is quite difficult for us to summarise in just a few sentences. So much has happened on this site: people have discovered their new favourite acts, forged friendships, partied all night, experienced art, and spent countless summers with us,” a festival spokesperson said,

“It’s all the more wonderful that we can now not only plan our 20th anniversary but also know that MS Dockvile has a future here. We are very much looking forward to celebrating in 2027 with the acts, guests, crew, and everyone who has shaped this festival over the past two decades,” they added.
