# Tim McGraw Announces 2027 Las Vegas Shows

## Summary

Tim McGraw announced five new performances at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in May 2027, following a sold-out 2026 Las Vegas engagement. The announcement comes alongside his induction into the Country Music Hall of Fame and a broader slate of 2026–2027 tour stops.

### Key Points
- Five Caesars Palace shows: May 21, 22, 27–29, 2027.
- Announcement follows McGraw's Country Music Hall of Fame induction.
- Additional November 2026 Caesars Palace dates and a Phoenix rodeo stop precede the Vegas run.
- Later 2027 stops include festivals in New Jersey, Prince Edward Island, Minnesota, Ohio, and Wisconsin.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Tim-McGraw-Vegas.webp)

Tim McGraw has announced five performances at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace in May 2027, following his sold-out 2026 engagement at the Las Vegas venue.

The concerts are scheduled for May 21, 22 and the 27 through 29. The announcement follows McGraw’s induction into the Country Music Hall of Fame.

McGraw’s upcoming schedule also includes performances at The Colosseum at Caesars Palace on November 6 and November 7, 2026, followed by Hondo Rodeo in Phoenix on November 13.

Following his May Las Vegas engagement, he will appear at Barefoot Country Music Fest in Wildwood, New Jersey, on June 19, 2027. He is also scheduled for Cavendish Beach Music Festival in Cavendish, Prince Edward Island, on July 10 and WE Fest in Detroit Lakes, Minnesota, on August 5. Additional appearances include The Bash in Norwalk, Ohio, on August 13 and XRoads41 Music Festival in Oshkosh, Wisconsin, on August 14.
