# The 57th Annual GMA Dove Awards: Final Lineup Includes Brandon Lake, Elevation Worship, & More

## Summary

The Gospel Music Association announced the final performer and presenter lineup for the 57th Annual GMA Dove Awards, adding Brandon Lake, Lainey Wilson, Tye Tribbett, Hope Darst and Elevation Worship, plus an Amy Grant/Natalie Grant tribute to Dolly Parton. The show airs October 6 from Nashville's Bridgestone Arena.

### Key Points
- New performers: Brandon Lake, Lainey Wilson, Tye Tribbett, Hope Darst, Elevation Worship.
- Amy Grant and Natalie Grant will share the stage for the first time, in tribute to Dolly Parton.
- Presenters include Christine D'Clario, Chris Tomlin, Cory Asbury, Hezekiah Walker, Marvin Sapp, and cast members of "The Chosen."
- Ceremony is Oct. 6, 2026, at Bridgestone Arena, Nashville; broadcasts on TBN/TBN+ Oct. 9 and encores Oct. 16.
- Music City Fan Experiences are sold out; a 2027 waiting list is open.

The Gospel Music Association has announced the final lineup of performers for the 57th Annual Dove Awards. The lineup now includes Brandon Lake with Lainey Wilson, Tye Tribbett, Hope Darst, and Elevation Worship as some of the newest artists announced for the special evening. Other performances include Amy Grant and Natalie Grant sharing the stage for the first time to pay tribute to the late great Dolly Parton. Lastly, the final list of confirmed presenters includes Christine D’Clario, Chris Tomlin, Cory Asbury, Hezekiah Walker, Jenn Johnson, Marvin Sapp, Shawn Johnson East, and, from The Chosen, George H. Xanthis and Paras Patel.

The 57th Annual GMA Dove Awards, presented in partnership with the Cantinas Arts Foundation, are scheduled for Oct. 6, 2026, at Nashville’s Bridgestone Arena in Tennessee. Tickets are currently on sale [HERE](https://doveawards.com/tickets).

While Music City Fan Experiences are now sold out, fans interested in attending in 2027 can join the waiting list now. The broadcast will air exclusively on TBN and the TBN+ app Friday, October 9th, 2026, at 7:30 p.m. and 10 pm ET and will be simulcast on SiriusXM The Message. An encore presentation will air on TBN and the TBN+ app Friday, October 16th, at 7:30 pm and 10 pm ET.
