# Nikki M. James and Rory O’Malley Returning to ‘The Book of Mormon’

## Summary

Original Broadway cast members Nikki M. James and Rory O'Malley will return to "The Book of Mormon" for a 12-week engagement beginning Oct. 27 through Jan. 17, 2027, reprising their Tony-recognized roles of Nabulungi and Elder McKinley at the Eugene O'Neill Theatre.

### Key Points
- James and O'Malley rejoin "The Book of Mormon" Oct. 27-Jan. 17, 2027, at the Eugene O'Neill Theatre.
- James reprises her Tony-winning role as Nabulungi; O'Malley returns as Tony-nominated Elder McKinley.
- They temporarily replace current cast members Sydney Quildon and Charlie Franklin.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/The-Book-of-Mormon.webp)

Original Broadway cast members Nikki M. James and Rory O’Malley will return to “The Book of Mormon” for a limited 12-week engagement this fall.

James and O’Malley will begin performances October 27 at the Eugene O’Neill Theatre and remain with the production through January 17, 2027. James will reprise her Tony Award-winning role as Nabulungi, while O’Malley will return to the role of Elder McKinley, for which he received a Tony nomination. The characters are currently played by Sydney Quildon and Charlie Franklin, respectively.

“Returning to ‘The Book of Mormon’ feels like a true homecoming,” James said in a statement.

The actor added that returning alongside O’Malley gives her another opportunity to revisit the role that changed her career while working with members of the musical’s current company.
