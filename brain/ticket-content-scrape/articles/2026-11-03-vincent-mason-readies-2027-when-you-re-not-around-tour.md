# Vincent Mason Readies 2027 ‘When You’re Not Around Tour’

## Summary

Country artist Vincent Mason has announced his 2027 "When You're Not Around Tour," a 21-date headlining run spanning Feb 11-May 8 in St. Louis through Wilmington, NC. The announcement follows his sold-out 2026 "There I Go Tour" and stadium dates opening for Morgan Wallen, plus growing international shows in Canada, the UK and Australia.

### Key Points
- 21-date run kicks off Feb 11 in St. Louis, closes May 8 in Wilmington, NC.
- Tickets go on sale Nov 6 at 10am local via vincentmasonmusic.com.
- 2026 touring included stops in Houston, Nashville, New York and Boston, plus select Morgan Wallen stadium dates.
- Mason has also built an international following with shows in Canada, the UK and Australia.

Vincent Mason has announced the “When You’re Not Around Tour” set for 2027. The trek kicks off Feb. 11 in St. Louis at The Factory at The District, runs for 21 dates, and concludes May 8 at Greenfield Lake Amphitheater in Wilmington, North Carolina, with support from rising artists Jacob Hackworth, Benny G, and Phil Kane throughout.

Tickets go on sale Nov. 6 at 10 a.m. local time at [vincentmasonmusic.com.](https://www.vincentmasonmusic.com/#/)

After kicking off 2026 with his sold-out “There I Go Tour,” which included stops in Houston, Nashville, New York City, and Boston, he joined Morgan Wallen for select spring stadium dates on the “Still The Problem Tour.” The run added to an impressive list of touring opportunities alongside Riley Green, Tucker Wetmore, Parker McCollum, Jordan Davis, and Megan Moroney. Beyond the U.S., Mason has continued connecting with audiences in Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia, further building his international presence.
