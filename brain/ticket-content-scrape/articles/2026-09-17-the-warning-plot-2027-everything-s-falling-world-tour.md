# The Warning Plot 2027 ‘Everything’s Falling’ World Tour

## Summary

Mexican rock trio The Warning have announced the "Everything's Falling World Tour" for 2027, headlining stadiums and arenas across Mexico, Europe, the UK, US, and Canada, opening with their first headlining stadium show at Mexico City's Estadio GNP Seguros.

### Key Points
- Opens Feb. 5, 2027, at Estadio GNP Seguros, Mexico City — the band's first headlining stadium show.
- European/UK leg runs Madrid to London (OVO Arena Wembley, April 2), via Barcelona, Milan, Vienna, Berlin, Paris and more.
- Marks the band's largest hometown-area show to date.
- Tour continues into US and Canada dates beyond the announced European leg.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Warning-Everythings-Falling-World-Tour.webp)

The Warning have announced the “Everything’s Falling World Tour,” a 2027 headlining run that will visit Mexico, Europe, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada.

The Mexican rock trio will open the tour on February 5 at Estadio GNP Seguros in Mexico City. The concert will mark the band’s first headlining stadium performance and its largest hometown-area show to date.

The Warning will begin the European portion of the tour on March 9 at Palacio Vistalegre in Madrid. The itinerary continues through Barcelona, Lyon, Milan, Vienna, Prague, Munich, Düsseldorf, Berlin, Paris, Tilburg, Brussels and Luxembourg.

The group will then perform in Birmingham on March 29, Glasgow on March 30 and Manchester on April 1. The European and U.K. run will conclude on April 2 at OVO Arena Wembley in London.

The North American leg is scheduled to begin April 17 at the Pearl Concert Theater at Palms Casino Resort in Las Vegas. The 18-date run will include stops in Phoenix, San Francisco, Portland, Seattle, Vancouver, Denver, San Antonio, Oklahoma City, Houston and Atlanta.

Later dates will bring the tour to Detroit, Ottawa, Montreal, Toronto and Pittsburgh. The Warning will perform at Brooklyn Paramount in New York on May 18 before concluding the announced schedule May 23 at MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston.

The tour supports The Warning’s fifth studio album, *Everything’s Falling*, released through Lava/Republic Records.

Notably, before beginning the tour, The Warning will headline the halftime show at the NFL’s Mexico City game on November 22. The Minnesota Vikings will face the San Francisco 49ers at Estadio Banorte as part of the league’s expanded international schedule. The performance is one of seven halftime shows planned around the 2026 NFL International Games.

Tickets go on sale to the general public September 18 at 10 a.m. local time. The band’s Patreon presale and VIP package sale began September 15, while the artist presale opened September 16. Additional local presales are scheduled for September 17.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [The Warning’s official website](https://thewarningband.com/).
