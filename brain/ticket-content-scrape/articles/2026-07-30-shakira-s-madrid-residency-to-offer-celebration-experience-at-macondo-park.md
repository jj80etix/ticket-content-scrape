# Shakira’s Madrid Residency to Offer Celebration Experience at Macondo Park

## Summary

Shakira's Madrid residency, part of her "Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour," will pair 12 concert dates at the 50,000-capacity "Shakira Stadium" with an adjoining 185,000-square-metre Macondo Park experience featuring eight themed pavilions spanning music, food, literature, art, and fashion. Live Nation Spain president Pino Sagliocco and BIG studio architect Joao Alburquerque unveiled the concept, encouraging fans to arrive early and stay late to explore it.

### Key Points
- Residency runs Sept. 18, 19, 20, 22, 23, 25, 26, 27, 28 and Oct. 1, 2, 3, 2026 at Macondo Park in Madrid.
- Macondo Park spans 185,000 square metres with eight pavilions, open noon to midnight on show days.
- General admission, VIP, and Garden/Terrace tickets (with early venue access and Front Stage) are on sale.
- The tour has already set a Guinness World Record as the highest-grossing tour ever by a Hispanic artist.
- Concept designed by BIG studio architect Joao Alburquerque in partnership with Live Nation Spain.

Shakira’s Madrid residency is offering more than just a series of musical performances.

The Latin superstar will perform 12 dates in Madrid as a part of her “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour” at Macondo Park. During a press conference, Live Nation Spain president Pino Sagliocco and BIG studio architect Joao Alburquerque explained the vision behind the temporary complex set up next to the 50,000-capacity “Shakira Stadium,” spanning 185,000 meters. The space will feature eight pavilions with their own programming, focusing on music, gastronomy, literature, art, fashion, and other Latin culture experiences.

Macondo Park will be the starting point, open from 12 p.m. to 12 a.m. Sagliocco noted that they want fans to “arrive early, explore the pavilions, eat and fully immerse themselves in the experience before and after the show.”

Shakira’s “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour,” which kicked-off in February 2025, has already set a Guinness World Record as the highest-grossing tour of all time by a Hispanic Artist.
