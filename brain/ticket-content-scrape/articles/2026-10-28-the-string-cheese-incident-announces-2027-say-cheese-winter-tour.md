# The String Cheese Incident Announces 2027 ‘Say Cheese’ Winter Tour

## Summary

The String Cheese Incident announced 10 headlining "Say Cheese" Winter Tour dates for January–March 2027, running from Florida through the Southeast before closing in Colorado, including a first Steamboat Springs appearance since 2002 at WinterWonderGrass and two nights at Belly Up Aspen. Ticketing outlets vary by venue — SeatGeek at FTL War Memorial, Ticketmaster/box office at Durham PAC — distinct from the band's unrelated 2012 push for Ticketmaster-fee-free direct sales. Artist presale begins Oct. 29, general onsale Oct. 30.

### Key Points
- 10 new shows Jan. 14–23 (FL, NC, GA) and March 5–7, 2027 (WinterWonderGrass, Belly Up Aspen x2).
- First Steamboat Springs appearance since 2002, debut at WinterWonderGrass festival.
- Venue-specific ticketing outlets include SeatGeek and Ticketmaster depending on market.
- Artist presale Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. local; local presale 2 p.m.; general onsale Oct. 30 at 10 a.m.
- Announcement follows a busy 2026 including Hulaween and ongoing "Just Keep Spinning" dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/string-cheese-incident-1200.webp)

The String Cheese Incident has announced 10 headlining shows for its 2027 “Say Cheese” Winter Tour, with January dates across the Southeast followed by a return to Colorado in March. The itinerary also incorporates the band’s previously announced appearance at WinterWonderGrass in Steamboat Springs.

The run begins Jan. 14 at the Florida Theatre in Jacksonville and continues Jan. 15 at FTL War Memorial Auditorium in Fort Lauderdale. From there, the group will play back-to-back nights at Jannus Live in St. Petersburg on Jan. 16-17, accounting for four Florida performances in four days.

The tour moves north to Durham Performing Arts Center on Jan. 20 and Peace Concert Hall in Greenville, South Carolina, the following evening. Two performances at Atlanta’s Tabernacle on Jan. 22-23 close the January leg. The schedule then pauses until early March, when the Colorado-based band returns to its home state.

The March 5 performance at WinterWonderGrass was announced earlier this month as part of the festival’s 2027 lineup, which also features Trampled by Turtles and Kitchen Dwellers. The booking marks The String Cheese Incident’s first performance in Steamboat Springs since 2002 and its debut at the festival. WinterWonderGrass runs March 5-7, with the band’s listed appearance on opening day.

The group will follow the festival with two nights at Belly Up Aspen on March 6-7, closing the currently announced winter itinerary. The paired performances in St. Petersburg, Atlanta and Aspen give fans several opportunities to see the band on consecutive nights, a familiar format for an act known for changing setlists and extended improvisation.

In announcing the tour, the band’s team described “Say Cheese” as a nod to the fan community built over more than three decades of performances, encouraging concertgoers to capture and share memories from the road. The announcement follows a busy 2026 for the group, including three nights at [Hulaween in Florida](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/hulaween-completes-genre-spanning-2026-festival-lineup/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and additional “Just Keep Spinning” dates continuing this week.

That 2026 schedule includes a Wednesday, Oct. 28 performance at the Count Basie Center for the Arts in Red Bank, New Jersey, two shows at The Capitol Theatre in Port Chester, New York, over Halloween weekend, and a series of California performances in December. The final date currently listed for that run is Dec. 12 at The Sound in Del Mar.

The String Cheese Incident also has a history of pursuing direct-to-fan ticket access. [TicketNews reported in 2012](https://www.ticketnews.com/2012/05/jam-band-string-cheese-incident-fights-ticketmaster-fees/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) on the band’s efforts to offer tickets without added Ticketmaster service fees. That earlier initiative is separate from the arrangements for the new tour, which involve different ticketing outlets depending on the venue.
