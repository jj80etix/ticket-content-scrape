# Ultra Music Festival Announces 2027 Lineup With Calvin Harris, Martin Garrix and Dom Dolla

## Summary

Ultra Music Festival announced its initial 2027 lineup, headlined by Calvin Harris, Martin Garrix, and Dom Dolla, for the Miami electronic-music festival.

### Key Points
- Lineup announcement for Ultra 2027 in Miami.
- Standard festival-lineup coverage; no ticketing/pricing/policy angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Ultra-Music-Festival.webp)

Ultra Music Festival has announced the first round of performers for its 2027 edition, with Calvin Harris, Martin Garrix, Dom Dolla and Armin van Buuren among the headliners. The festival will return to Bayfront Park in downtown Miami from March 26 through March 28 for its 27th edition.

The initial announcement includes more than 25 headliners, along with several new collaborations and stage takeovers. Harris will perform at Ultra Miami for the first time since 2024. Steve Aoki is also returning, while Afrojack will present his first live set at the Miami festival.

Marshmello and Subtronics will perform together in a back-to-back set billed as a world debut. Another pairing brings Boris Brejcha together with Eric Prydz, performing under his Cirez D alias, for their U.S. debut as a duo.

Alison Wonderland and Tiffany Day are also scheduled for a joint festival debut. Nico Moreno and Novah will deliver the first U.S. performance of their back-to-back set, adding another collaboration to the opening lineup announcement.

Several artists will bring dedicated show productions to the festival. Sara Landry will present the U.S. debut of Divine Intervention, an audiovisual performance connected to her forthcoming second album. Miss Monique’s Biorhythm production will make its U.S. festival debut. Both are part of a program that includes new performance formats alongside returning headliners.

Ultra’s Resistance programming will feature Michael Bibi, I Hate Models and Indira Paganotto. Mau P’s Baddest Behaviour and Adam Beyer’s Drumcode will each host a Resistance stage takeover for the first time. The additions expand the festival’s house and techno programming for the three-day event.

The lineup follows a sold-out 2026 edition. Organizers reported that last year’s festival drew 165,000 attendees across three days, with fans traveling from more than 100 countries. Its Ultra Live broadcast reached an additional 42.5 million viewers, according to the announcement.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [ultramusicfestival.com](http://ultramusicfestival.com).
