# Ayra Starr Announces 2027 ‘Starrgirl World Tour’ In Support Of Latest Album

## Summary

Pollstar's coverage of Ayra Starr's newly announced 2027 "Starrgirl World Tour" in support of her latest album, covering the same Europe/North America dates and presale schedule (Verizon presale Oct. 13, Spotify Reserved presale, artist and Mastercard presales Oct. 14) as reported elsewhere.

Ayra Starr has announced the “Starrgirl World Tour,” a 20-city international run in support of her latest critically acclaimed new album, *Starrgirl*, which was released on Aug. 14. On the heels of her NFL halftime performance in London, the global Afrobeats superstar is gearing up for her 2027 global trek, setting Jan. 20, 2027 as the first date of her tour, kicking off in Europe at Live Music Hall in Cologne, Germany.

From there, Starr will hit major cities including Amsterdam, Paris, Milan, Zurich, and Brussels before wrapping the leg in London on Feb. 2. The tour then arrives in North America on March 18 at House of Blues in Houston, and hits cities including Atlanta, Brooklyn, Toronto, Chicago, and more, before wrapping on April 14 at The Fillmore in San Francisco. Before taking *Starrgirl* around the world in 2027, Ayra will close out this year with a special return to Africa. See full tour routing below.

Tickets for North America fans will go on sale with a Verizon presale (details below) on Oct. 13 at 10 am local time, followed by a Spotify Reserved presale on Oct. 13 at 2 pm local time. The artist presale begins Oct. 14 at 10 am local time. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale beginning on Oct. 16 at 10 a.m. local time at [Livenation.com](https://www.livenation.com/).

Tickets for Europe and UK fans will be available starting an artist and Mastercard presale beginning on Oct. 14 at 10 am local time. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale beginning on Oct. 16 at 10am local time at [Livenation.com.](https://www.livenation.com/)
