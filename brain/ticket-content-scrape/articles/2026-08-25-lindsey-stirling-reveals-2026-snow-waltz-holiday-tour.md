# Lindsey Stirling Reveals 2026 ‘Snow Waltz Holiday Tour’

## Summary

Lindsey Stirling announced her 2026 "Snow Waltz Holiday Tour," a 25-city U.S. run opening Nov. 18 in Hollywood, FL, featuring her violin performances alongside dance, aerial acrobatics, and theatrical production, with ARKAI opening.

### Key Points
- 25-city U.S. tour, opens Nov. 18 in Hollywood, FL.
- Stops include Syracuse, Philadelphia, St. Louis, Tucson, and three Las Vegas shows.
- Opening act: electroacoustic duo ARKAI.
- Show blends violin performance with dance, aerial acrobatics, and theatrical staging.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Lindsey-Stirling.webp)

Lindsey Stirling will return to the road this holiday season with her 2026 “Snow Waltz Holiday Tour,” a 25-city U.S. tour beginning in November.

The tour opens November 18 in Hollywood, Florida, and will include stops in Syracuse, Philadelphia, St. Louis and Tucson. Stirling will also perform three shows in Las Vegas. Electroacoustic duo ARKAI will serve as the opening act for all dates.

The shows will feature Stirling’s violin performances alongside dance, aerial acrobatics and theatrical production. The tour continues the format she has used for her holiday concerts since releasing *Warmer in the Winter* in 2017.

Stirling’s holiday album was recently certified Gold. The project combines seasonal songs with the electronic, classical and pop elements that have become part of her music.
