# ‘We Will Always Love You’: The World Mourns The Loss Of Dolly Parton

## Summary

Pollstar has compiled tributes from across the music and touring industry mourning the death of Dolly Parton. Figures including Paul McCartney, Taylor Swift, Beyoncé, and industry executives like UTA's Neil Warnock shared statements on her music, entrepreneurship, and philanthropy. The piece is a roundup of remembrances rather than reporting on any business or ticketing development.

### Key Points
- Compilation of tributes to Dolly Parton from artists and touring-industry figures.
- Contributors include Paul McCartney, Taylor Swift, Beyoncé, George Strait, Reba McEntire.
- UTA's Neil Warnock reflects on her career and business ambition.
- No ticketing-industry business content; purely a memorial roundup.

The world is still reeling from the loss of musical icon Dolly Parton, who not only changed country music but also had an impact on people’s lives with her words of encouragement, film and television performances, books and philanthropy.

Over the past 24 hours, the Rock and Roll Hall of Famer has been honored by the music world and beyond words from notable figures such as Paul McCartney, Taylor Swift, Beyoncé, Dionne Warwick, George Strait, Reba McEntire and many more.

*Pollstar* has gathered statements and tributes sent and posted on social media pages celebrating Parton, her music, and her legacy, which lives on in all the artists she inspired.
