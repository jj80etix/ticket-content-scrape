# WWE Expands ‘Road to Survivor Series’ Tour With Seven New Shows

## Summary

WWE added seven stops to its "Road to Survivor Series: WarGames Tour," running mid-October through November across the US and Canada, building toward Survivor Series: WarGames in Houston. Tickets go on general sale August 21 following an August 20 presale.

### Key Points
- WWE added seven tour stops from mid-October to November across the US and Canada.
- Tour builds toward Survivor Series: WarGames in Houston.
- General onsale August 21, presale August 20.
- Stops include Winnipeg, Palm Springs, Youngstown, Portland, Bangor, Fort Myers, and Huntsville.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0825.jpeg)

WWE is adding seven more stops to its fall calendar as the company builds toward Survivor Series: WarGames in Houston.

The newly announced **Road to Survivor Series: WarGames Tour** dates will take WWE across the United States and Canada from mid-October through November, with stops in Winnipeg, Palm Springs, Youngstown, Portland, Bangor, Fort Myers and Huntsville.

The run kicks off Oct. 15 at Canada Life Centre in Winnipeg before heading to California for an Oct. 22 show at Acrisure Arena in Palm Springs. WWE will then visit Covelli Centre in Youngstown, Ohio, on Oct. 29.

November brings four more dates, beginning with a pair of Maine shows at Cross Insurance Arena in Portland on Nov. 5 and Cross Insurance Center in Bangor on Nov. 6. The tour continues at Hertz Arena in Fort Myers, Florida, on Nov. 12 before wrapping the newly announced slate at VBC Propst Arena in Huntsville, Alabama, on Nov. 19.

Tickets for all seven events go on sale to the general public **Friday, Aug. 21 at 10 a.m. local time**, following an exclusive presale beginning Aug. 20. Additional ticket and event information is available through WWE’s [website](https://www.wwe.com/events).

The string of live events leads into **Survivor Series: WarGames**, scheduled for Saturday, Nov. 28 at Daikin Park in Houston.

WWE announced the Houston event in July alongside Houston First Corporation and the Houston Astros. This year’s Survivor Series will mark the event’s first trip back to Houston since 2017.
