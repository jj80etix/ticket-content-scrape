# Fever raises $250m for live entertainment tech expansion

## Summary

Live-entertainment tech platform Fever has raised $250M (~€215M) from EQT, Point72 Private Investments, Baillie Gifford and existing investors — which it calls the largest-ever raise for a live-entertainment tech company — to expand beyond its current 55 national markets and broaden its event formats and ticketing tools. Deal terms weren't disclosed.

### Key Points
- $250M raise, led/backed by EQT, Point72 Private Investments, and Baillie Gifford.
- Called the largest funding round to date for a live-entertainment tech company.
- Funds earmarked for international expansion beyond 55 current markets, new event formats, and ticketing tools.
- No valuation or deal terms disclosed.

### Highlights
- $250M raise; company already operates in 55 countries.
- Stated use of funds explicitly includes "ticketing tools," alongside event-format expansion.

### Industry Problem
Signals continued heavy capital concentration in event-tech/experiential platforms, a market-structure trend worth tracking for competitive positioning and potential adjacent-market entry into ticketing.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor Fever's next moves (market entries, ticketing-tool launches) as a leading indicator of where investor capital sees the next opportunity in live-event tech; no immediate build/improve action indicated by this item alone.
