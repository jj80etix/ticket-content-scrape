# 65% of fans travelling abroad for concerts in Asia, report finds

## Summary

A new Trip.com Group / Live Nation survey finds 65% of surveyed concertgoers in Asia traveled to another country or region for a show, framing concerts as an increasing driver of regional tourism across China, Singapore, Hong Kong, Macao, Thailand and South Korea. The article is paywalled after the lede, limiting detail on methodology or ticketing-specific findings.

### Key Points
- 65% of surveyed fans in the "Concert Travel Effect in Asia" report traveled abroad/regionally for a show
- Report combines fan surveys, travel data and ticketing insights from Trip.com Group and Live Nation (partners since 2025)
- Covers mainland China, Singapore, Hong Kong, Macao, Thailand and South Korea
