# PEEX returns for ‘new activity phase’ with Solotech partnership

## Summary

Live audio company PEEX is relaunching after a five-year hiatus via a partnership with production company Solotech, following a management buyout and new investment from roughly 20 private individuals and Dubai/Singapore-based Unity Group, with PEEX's founders retaining 72% ownership. PEEX supplies wearable audio receivers for VIP concertgoers that deliver performance-synchronized audio.

### Key Points
- PEEX relaunches after a five-year hiatus via a Solotech integration partnership.
- Management buyout plus investment from ~20 private individuals and Unity Group (Dubai/Singapore); founders retain 72% ownership.
- Product: wearable audio receivers delivering performance-synchronized audio to VIP concertgoers.
