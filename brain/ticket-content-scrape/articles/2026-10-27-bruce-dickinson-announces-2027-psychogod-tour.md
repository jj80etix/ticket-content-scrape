# Bruce Dickinson Announces 2027 ‘PsychoGod Tour’

## Summary

Bruce Dickinson announced his 2027 solo "PsychoGod Tour," his first solo outing since 2025, kicking off June 14 in Belfast and running through Europe with support from Evergrey on part of the run. Tickets go on sale Oct. 30.

### Key Points
- Opens June 14 at Ulster Hall, Belfast; dates run through at least early July across Europe.
- Supported by Swedish band Evergrey on the November/December leg.
- First solo dates since completing "The Mandrake Project Tour" in 2025.

Bruce Dickinson is hitting the road with his solo band for the “PsychoGod Tour” in 2027, kicking off June 14 at Ulster Hall in Belfast, United Kingdom. These shows will be Dickinson’s first solo outings since completing “The Mandrake Project Tour” in Brazil in September 2025.

These upcoming shows promise a wide range of new material alongside fan favorites and deep cuts spanning his entire solo career. The November/December leg of the tour will be supported by Swedish progressive metal band Evergrey. Tickets go on sale Oct. 30 at [www.brucedickinsonhq.com](http://www.brucedickinsonhq.com)

“What we do is real, and that’s so important right now,” Dickinson said. “A real drummer, a real bass player, real guitarists, real keyboards – AI can only copy; it can only pretend. It can never be in love, and it can never play. This band? They can really play!”
