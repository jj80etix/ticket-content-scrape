# Fuerza Regida Up To A Record 10 Shows At Mexico City’s Palacio De Los Deportes

## Summary

Música Mexicana band Fuerza Regida has expanded its Mexico City "This Is Our Dream Tour" stop at Palacio De Los Deportes from two nights to ten shows in eleven nights, a new record for the historic venue. The Live Nation-promoted run kicks off Jan. 28 and wraps Feb. 7, following a sold-out first U.S. stadium leg highlighted by a $9.2M gross at Dodger Stadium.

### Key Points
- 10 shows in 11 nights (Jan. 28-Feb. 7, 2027) at Palacio De Los Deportes — a venue record, up from an originally announced two-night stand.
- First leg's top date: July 18 at Dodger Stadium grossed $9,237,664 on 49,784 tickets (Pollstar Boxoffice).
- Second leg includes Tinley Park, Charlotte, Atlanta, Bakersfield, Fresno and Red Rocks.
- Tour promoted by Live Nation.

## Transcript

![fuerza](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/fuerza.jpg)

Fuerza Regida is breaking records as they achieve their dream. The influential música Mexicana band is bringing its “This Is Our Dream Tour” to Mexico City’s Palacio De Los Deportes, where the quintet will perform 10 shows in 11 nights, setting a new record at the historic venue.

The band hailing from San Bernardino, California, originally announced it as a two-night stand, Feb. 3 and 5, in the Mexican arena over a week ago, but demand has brought it up to a whopping 10 consecutive shows, a feat no other act has ever accomplished at Palacio De Los Deportes. Fuerza Regida will kick off the run Jan. 28 and conclude Feb. 7, with their only break coming Feb. 2 after the fifth show.

**[Viviendo El Sueño: Fuerza Regida Manifest Landmark ‘This Is Our Dream’ Stadium Trek (Cover)](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/11/living-the-dream-fuerza-regida-manifest-landmark-stadium-trek-cover/)**

Fuerza Regida is having quite the year with a sold-out first leg of their “This Is Our Dream Tour” in stadiums across the country. Their most successful outing was July 18 at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, where they grossed $9,237,664 off 49,784 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

They kicked off the second leg of the Live Nation-promoted tour this month with a two-night stand at Credit Union 1 Amphitheatre in Tinley Park, Illinois. The routing includes stops at Charlotte’s Truliant Amphitheater, Lakewood Amphitheatre in Atlanta, Dignity Health Arena in Bakersfield, Fresno’s Save Mart Center and Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison.
