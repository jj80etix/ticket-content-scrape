# Singapore to gain 9,000-cap ‘versatile’ arena at EXPO centre

## Summary

Singapore EXPO is adding Apex @ EXPO, a 9,000-capacity multi-purpose arena set to open later this year with a pair of K-pop shows (PLAVE and LE SSERAFIM), becoming the venue complex's second music-capable space alongside the existing 6,700-capacity Arena @ EXPO. Organizers position it as bridging a gap between Singapore's mid-tier and large-scale venue options, ahead of a planned 15,000-capacity Sands Theatre due in 2030.

### Key Points
- Apex @ EXPO opens with K-pop acts PLAVE (Oct. 17) and LE SSERAFIM (Nov. 28) christening the venue.
- Venue offers flexible seating and state-of-the-art AV/lighting/digital tech alongside an adjoining gallery space.
- Joins the existing 6,700-cap Arena @ EXPO, which has hosted Sting, Mariah Carey, Tyla, and Westlife in the past year.
- Singapore's broader venue pipeline includes the 4,000-12,000 capacity Singapore Indoor Stadium and a planned 15,000-cap Sands Theatre (2030).

## Transcript

## Singapore to gain 9,000-cap ‘versatile’ arena at EXPO centre

Apex @ EXPO will become the second music venue in Singapore EXPO, helping bridge the mid-tier and large-scale venue gap in the market

![Arena @ EXPO rendering](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Arena-@-EXPO-rendering.png)

Apex @ EXPO rendering

Singapore’s venue circuit is growing with the addition of a multi-purpose, 9,000-capacity arena at the city-state’s main convention hall, set to open with a pair of K-pop shows later this year.

Built with large-scale, immersive experiences in mind, Apex @ EXPO is designed to accommodate a range of live entertainment, from international concerts to world-class productions.

The plug-and-play venue is equipped with state-of-the-art AV, lighting, and digital technologies, and offers flexible seating configurations to accommodate audiences and productions of all calibres. Outside of the main venue is an adjoining gallery, giving organisers extra space to add on experiences.

“Over the past years, we have transformed Apex @ EXPO into a 9,000-capacity versatile venue to support a new generation of large-scale concerts and immersive live entertainment productions, while retaining its flexibility to host world-class conferences and conventions,” Sim Lim Ng, VP, commercial, tells *IQ*.

K-pop acts PLAVE and LE SSERAFIM will be among the first acts to perform at the venue on 17 October and 28 November, respectively, christening the venue as it boosts the entertainment market’s available options.

“We’re excited to welcome major K-pop icons PLAVE and LE SSERAFIM among the first acts to perform at Apex @ EXPO this October and November, marking an important milestone in the venue’s evolution and the next chapter of Singapore EXPO’s growing live entertainment offering,” Ng adds.

> “This investment reflects our broader vision of expanding Singapore EXPO’s role beyond business events”

When it comes online, it will become the second music venue within the EXPO hall, joining the 6,700-capacity Arena @ EXPO, which completed upgrade works in May 2025. In the past year, Sting, Mariah Carey, Tyla, and Westlife have all passed through its doors.

Both are nestled within Singapore EXPO, the country’s largest exhibition venue with more than 100,000 square metres of space, with organisers benefiting from the ability to scale and integrate experiences in the wider space.

“This investment reflects our broader vision of expanding Singapore EXPO’s role beyond business events, building on its position as the nation’s leading purpose-built MICE venue while establishing itself as a premier entertainment hub,” Ng concludes.

Singapore EXPO is currently the top-ranked MICE (Meetings, Incentives, Conferences, and Exhibition) destination in the Asia-Pacific region, with the new venue space to complement its status.

Apex @ EXPO will greatly expand Singapore’s venue options, with the mid-tier currently anchored by the 5,000-capacity Star Theatre and Arena @ EXPO.

The city-state’s main arena venue is the [Singapore Indoor Stadium](https://www.iqmagazine.com/publications/global-arena-guide-2026/singapore/), scalable from 4,000 to 12,000 people, located in The Kallang entertainment complex. Also in the works is a 15,000-capacity arena, called [Sands Theatre](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/07/singapore-set-for-15000-cap-arena/), expected for completion in 2030.
