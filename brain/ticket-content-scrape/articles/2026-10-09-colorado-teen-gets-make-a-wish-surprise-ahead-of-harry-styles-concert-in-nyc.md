# Colorado Teen Gets Make-A-Wish Surprise Ahead Of Harry Styles Concert in NYC

## Summary

Oak View Group CEO Chris Granger joined Make-A-Wish to surprise a Denver teen, Brianna, with news that she'll meet Harry Styles and attend his Oct. 14 Madison Square Garden show as part of OVG's national Make-A-Wish partnership.

### Key Points
- Reveal took place at Granger's Denver home, joined by Make-A-Wish Colorado and America leadership.
- Styles is mid-way through a 30-night MSG residency (Aug 26–Oct 31) as part of his "Together, Together" tour.
- OVG's Make-A-Wish partnership has driven 30+ venues to join a "Round Up for Wishes" campaign, raising over $80,000 to date.
- Pollstar is owned by Oak View Group, the subject of this promotional piece.

## Transcript

![Brianna and family, Scott Dishong, Jennifer Granger, Camille Horn and Chris Granger](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Brianna-and-family-Scott-Dishong-Jennifer-Granger-Camille-Horn-and-Chris-Granger-scaled.jpg)

Brianna and family, Scott Dishong, Jennifer Granger, Camille Horn and Oak View Group CEO Chris Granger.

Oak View Group Chief Executive Officer Chris Granger joined Make-A-Wish America Director Camille Horn and Make-A-Wish Colorado President & CEO Scott Dishong last night (Oct. 8) to surprise a Denver teen with the news that her wish to meet Harry Styles and attend his concert would come true, set for Oct. 14 at Madison Square Garden in New York City.

Recently relocated to Denver, the global headquarters for OVG, Granger and his wife, Jennifer, hosted the intimate reveal for Brianna and her family at their home in Cherry Hills Village, joined by Make-A-Wish supporters, OVG executives, Make-A-Wish Colorado board members and community leaders.

Guests embraced the Harry Styles spirit by donning feather boas in a festive nod to the artist, coming together to celebrate Brianna’s wish and the unforgettable moment her dream became a reality.

The trip will be Brianna and her family’s first-ever trip to New York City, not to mention first time meeting the global superstar that Brianna has been a fan of since sixth grade. Her top five Styles songs? “Fine Line,” “From the Dining Table,” “Sign of the Times”, “Falling” and “Matilda.”

“I’m forever grateful and so thankful that so many people came together for me and for other kids to experience similar moments because I know how much it means,” Brianna says in the announcement. “Being able to go to New York in itself is such a blessing, I can’t wait to experience Madison Square Garden, and of course see Harry. This marks a new beginning for me and shows I can do great things.”

Harry Styles’ only U.S. dates this year are at Madison Square Garden, where he’s already began a run of 30 nights that began Aug. 26 and run into Oct. 31 as part of his “Together, Together” tour. [He’ll play additional markets in 2027, thankfully.](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/10/harry-styles-expands-together-together-tour-with-12-new-markets-in-2027/)

Venue developer and management company Oak View Group, also parent company of *Pollstar*, has a national partnership with Make-A-Wish designed to recognize the life-changing impact of wishes for children with critical illnesses.

The Grangers are longtime supporters of Make-A-Wish, with previous commitments to the organization’s California and Michigan chapters including sponsoring wishes, serving on the Board, hosting fundraising events, chairing Make-A-Wish Galas and spearheading group initiatives.

“Welcoming Brianna and her family into our new Denver home was a privilege we won’t forget,” Chris and Jennifer Granger said in the announcement. “Seeing the joy they shared reminded us why wishes matter so deeply: they give families hope for a better tomorrow. We have supported the Make-A-Wish mission for many years, and we’re grateful to the Make-A-Wish Colorado team for helping us make this evening possible. As OVG grows our national partnership, we all look forward to helping grant many more wishes for children and families who need moments like these.”

Scott Dishong, President & CEO, Make-A-Wish Colorado, added “Oak View Group’s commitment to Make-A-Wish is making a difference right here in Colorado and across the country. Together, we’re creating more opportunities for children with critical illnesses to experience the hope, strength and joy that a wish can bring.”

Camille Horn, Director, Corporate Alliances, Make-A-Wish America, added “At the heart of this partnership is a shared commitment to creating moments of joy and connection. When organizations like Oak View Group and Make-A-Wish come together with a common purpose, we can make a lasting impact on children and families facing critical illnesses.”

In 2025, OVG and Make-A-Wish announced a national partnership to grant more wishes to children with critical illnesses all over the world. More than 30 venues have joined the Round Up for Wishes campaign, raising more than $80,000 that will directly support funding wishes. In May, OVG granted the wish of a Colorado-based child, with a Las Vegas experience at Allegiant Stadium to see her favorite band, BTS, during their “Arirang” World Tour.
