# Elvis Tribute Show ‘1969 LIVE!’ Sets 2027 Return After Back-to-Back Sellouts

## Summary

Following its second consecutive sellout, Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino announced "1969 LIVE! - The King Returns" will return to the International Theater on July 31, 2027 — the exact anniversary of Elvis Presley's original 1969 comeback opening night at the venue. Elvis Tribute Artist Travis Powell leads the production, which honors the residency that made the property a Las Vegas landmark.

### Key Points
- Return date (July 31, 2027) matches the exact 58th anniversary of Elvis's historic 1969 International Hotel opening.
- Tribute artist Travis Powell also returns for a separate show, "1970 LIVE! - That's The Way It Was," on Oct. 24, 2026.
- Elvis performed 636 sold-out shows at the venue during his original run.
- Tickets for both the Oct. 2026 and July 2027 shows are already on sale via Westgate's website.

After Second Consecutive Sell-Out, Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino Announces Third Show of 1969 LIVE! – The King Returns Coming in 2027 | Photo via Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino

Elvis is still packing the house in Las Vegas.

Following a second consecutive sold-out performance, Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino has announced that 1969 LIVE! – The King Returns will return to the International Theater on Saturday, July 31, 2027. The date marks the exact anniversary of Elvis Presley’s historic opening night at the former International Hotel on July 31, 1969—the performance that launched one of the most celebrated Las Vegas residencies in entertainment history.

Internationally acclaimed Elvis Tribute Artist Travis Powell will once again lead the production, recreating the energy, music and atmosphere of Presley’s legendary comeback on the same stage where it happened.

“Our job is to honor what made this property legendary while continuing to create experiences people can’t find anywhere else,” Cami Christensen, president and general manager of Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino, said in a press release. “When fans sell out the International Theater two years in a row for 1969 LIVE! to celebrate something that happened five decades ago, it tells you everything you need to know about Elvis’ impact on Las Vegas.”
