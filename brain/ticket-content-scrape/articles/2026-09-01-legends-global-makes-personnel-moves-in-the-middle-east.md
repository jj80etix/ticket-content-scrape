# Legends Global Makes Personnel Moves In The Middle East

## Summary

Legends Global announced two Middle East leadership moves effective September 2026: Mark Jan Kar is promoted to SVP, MENA and APAC Strategic Projects, while Santiago Olea moves from the King Abdullah Financial District Conference Centre in Riyadh to become General Manager of Dubai's Coca-Cola Arena.

### Key Points
- Jan Kar will oversee new business opportunities across MENA/APAC while retaining oversight of Coca-Cola Arena under Olea.
- The moves follow Legends Global's November 2025 joint venture with Saudi Arabia's Event Investment Fund to operate EIF-developed Vision 2030 venues.
- Coca-Cola Arena has hosted 550+ live events under Jan Kar's prior leadership.
- Olea previously ran Live Nation venues across Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, and Qatar before joining MBC Group and then Legends Global.

## Transcript

![Mark Santiago](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Mark_Santiago.jpg)

Legends Global has promoted Mark Jan Kar (left) to Senior Vice President, MENA and APAC Strategic Projects, and appointed Santiago Olea as General Manager for Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai. Courtesy Legends Global

Legends Global has announced two moves to bolster focus on existing and growth opportunities across the Gulf region. Mark Jan Kar, will become Senior Vice President, MENA and APAC Strategic Projects with Santiago Olea moving into the General Manager role for Coca-Cola Arena in Dubai, owned by Dubai Holding Entertainment.

Both executive appointments are effective September 2026.

Under the guidance of Legends Global EVP for Asia and MENA Paul Sergeant, Mark will now manage strategic projects across the Middle East and APAC region and oversee all new business opportunities within the region, while maintaining oversight of Coca-Cola Arena under Santiago’s leadership.

Peter Loxton, Chief Operating Officer for Asia Pacific and Middle East for Legends Global, sees this as a successful continuity of key players in a strategic region for Legends Global. “With a variety of strong opportunities across the dynamic Middle East and growing APAC market we are pleased to have Mark’s leadership continue across the region,” he commented, adding, “Last November, Legends Global announced a joint venture with Saudi Arabia’s Event Investment Fund (EIF) to support the Kingdom’s Vision 2030 and invest for its fast-growing venue industry to deliver Management Services exclusively for the EIF-developed venues as they are delivered,” expanded Loxton.”

“This partnership will become the venue operator for venue projects within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia for exhibition and convention centers, and entertainment venues to host world-class events in the culture, entertainment, tourism, and indoor sports sectors,” the press release announcing Mark Jan Kar’s promotion.

Jan Kar joined Coca-Cola Arena as Commercial Director in 2018, to then lead the live entertainment program from 2019 and became General Manager in 2021. His role was expanded in 2024 to Senior Vice President Entertainment, Content and Programming for the Middle East Region. Prior to Coca-Cola Arena, he was the Vice President of Strategic Partnerships at International Management Group (IMG), representing some of the biggest names in the world of sport and entertainment.

Under his leadership, Coca-Cola Arena delivered over 550 live events since opening.

“The exponential growth and development across the MENA region mean we can harness the power of bringing communities together through world-class live experiences, for residents and guests, our audiences, our team and the talent we welcome to our stage.

“The power of the Legends Global network means we can leverage our global team to access content, tours, partnerships and unique opportunities that appeal to our local audiences.

“We see entertainment and events across this region as a symbolic driver of, not only economic growth, but a desirable lifestyle,” said Jan Kar.

Santiago Olea joins Coca-Cola Arena from another Legends Global venue in the region, the King Abdullah Financial District Conference Centre in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia. He was appointed General Manager in 2024, looking after the 28,000 square meter conference centre, featuring a 1,215 square meter banquet hall used for conferences, concerts, gala dinners, exhibitions, product launches, award ceremonies and weddings.

Olea brings over 12 years’ experience across vital sports and entertainment venues and major events in Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Qatar, and Dubai to the team.

He joined Legends Global directly from the MBC Group, the largest media company in the Middle East and North Africa, where he was Chief Operating Officer for MBC Events helping them grow through excellence in event execution and cultivating strong, strategic alliances with vendors.

Prior to MBC, Olea managed Live Nation venues across the Middle East including the Maraya in Saudi Arabia, The Arena in Kuwait and the Ali Bin Hamad Al Attiyah Arena in Qatar. For Maraya, Santiago developed and delivered the programming strategy in support of the venue’s artistic vision attracting national and international events of all genres to the venue.

“Coca-Cola Arena, owned by Dubai Holding Entertainment, has become one of the most active and versatile venues in the world, welcoming an exceptional range of touring concerts, international sporting events, comedy shows, corporate events, exhibitions, live theatre and family entertainment,” said Olea, “It’s been adopted by locals and loved by guests and artists, and I look forward to working alongside the expert team at the Arena to deliver the next phase of growth and economic impact for the city.”
