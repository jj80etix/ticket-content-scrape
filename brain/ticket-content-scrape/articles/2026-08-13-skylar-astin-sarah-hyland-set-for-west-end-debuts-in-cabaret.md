# Skylar Astin, Sarah Hyland Set for West End Debuts in ‘Cabaret’

## Summary

Skylar Astin and Sarah Hyland will make their West End debuts this fall in the long-running "Cabaret" revival at London's Kit Kat Club, taking on the Emcee and Sally Bowles roles respectively beginning September 21. The production, which recently passed its 1,900th performance, is currently the longest-running West End staging of "Cabaret" and marks the show's 60th anniversary and the revival's fifth anniversary this November.

### Key Points
- Astin plays the Emcee through Dec. 12; Hyland plays Sally Bowles through Jan. 23, 2027.
- Several other new cast members join, including Ronan Burns and Lindsay Atherton.
- The production has passed 1,900 performances, the longest West End run of "Cabaret" in history.
- November marks the show's 60th Broadway anniversary and the revival's 5th anniversary.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Cabaret-West-End-Skylar-Astin-and-Sarah-Hyland.webp)

Skylar Astin and Sarah Hyland are set to join the West End revival of “Cabaret” this fall, taking over two of the production’s central roles at the Kit Kat Club in London.

Beginning September 21, Astin will play the Emcee, while Hyland will take on the role of Sally Bowles. The two actors will make their West End debuts with the production. Astin is scheduled to remain with the musical through December 12, while Hyland is set to continue in the role through January 23, 2027.

The incoming performers will join several other new members of the “Cabaret” company. Ronan Burns will play Ernst Ludwig, while Lindsay Atherton will portray Fraulein Kost and Fritzie. Lily Frances, Peter Houston, Samuel How, Bailey Johnson, Ellie Mitchell, Kirsty Anne Shaw, Natasha Wilde and Nicky Wong Rush are also joining the company.

Baker Mukasa will continue as Clifford Bradshaw, with Ruthie Henshall as Fraulein Schneider and Robert Hands as Herr Schultz. Joe Atkinson, Xavion Campbell-Brown, Olivia-Rose Deer, Anya Ferdinand, Danny Fogarty, Adrian Grove and Eva-Rose Tanaka will also remain with the production.

The production will also mark two milestones this year. November 20 will mark the 60th anniversary of “Cabaret”, which first opened on Broadway in 1966, as well as the fifth anniversary of the current revival.

The current revival opened in London in December 2021 and went on to win a record seven Olivier Awards. It later transferred to Broadway’s August Wilson Theatre, where it closed September 21, 2025.
