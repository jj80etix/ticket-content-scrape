# Alison Krauss & Union Station Cancel Rest of 2026 Tour on Medical Advice

## Summary

Alison Krauss & Union Station canceled the remaining 17 dates of their 2026 Arcadia Tour after doctors advised Krauss to take vocal rest, wiping out a closing stretch that included three Ryman Auditorium shows and stops in St. Louis, Austin, Las Vegas and across the Midwest and Texas. All tickets will be fully refunded.

### Key Points
- Cancellation announced the day before the tour was set to resume in Davenport, Iowa.
- 17 remaining shows canceled, including three consecutive Ryman Auditorium (Nashville) dates Sept. 18-20 and an Oct. 3 Hardly Strictly Bluegrass appearance.
- No specific diagnosis or return timetable given; band cites "vocal rest" on medical advice.
- Full refunds automatic at original point of purchase.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Alison-Krauss-Arcadia-2026-tour.webp)

Alison Krauss & Union Station have canceled the remainder of their 2026 Arcadia Tour after Krauss was advised by medical professionals to take a period of vocal rest.

The band announced the decision Thursday, one day before it had been scheduled to resume the tour Friday night in Davenport, Iowa. The cancellations wipe out 17 remaining performances through an Oct. 3 appearance at San Francisco’s Hardly Strictly Bluegrass festival.

“It is with great regret that Alison Krauss & Union Station have announced the cancellation of our upcoming tour dates,” the band said in its statement. “After consulting with medical professionals, Alison has been advised to take a period of vocal rest to ensure a full and healthy recovery.”

The statement did not identify a specific illness or provide a timetable for Krauss’ return. Instead, the group said the decision came down to protecting her health and voice.

“This decision was not made lightly as we are deeply committed to our fans,” the statement continued. “However, prioritizing health and vocal integrity is imperative at this time.”

All tickets for the canceled concerts will be fully refunded, according to the band. Fans with questions were directed to the point where they originally purchased their tickets.

The group also left open the possibility of eventually returning to the affected markets, saying, “We hope to make it back to these cities in the near future.”

The cancellation eliminates a substantial closing stretch of the tour, including three consecutive shows at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium from Sept. 18 through Sept. 20. Other scheduled stops included St. Louis, Austin, Las Vegas and several dates across the Midwest, Texas and California.
