# The Doobie Brothers Announce 2027 Residency At Fontainebleau Las Vegas

## Summary

The Doobie Brothers will make their Fontainebleau Las Vegas debut with a nine-show residency in 2027, spanning February/March and August/September dates at the BleauLive Theater. The residency follows the band's reunited lineup and 2025 album *Walk This Road*.

### Key Points
- Nine shows: Feb. 26-27, March 5-6, Aug. 27-28, and Sept. 1, 3, 4, 2027.
- General on-sale Oct. 30, with Citi and other presales starting Oct. 27-29.
- Follows reunion of founding members and 2025 album release.

Rock & Roll Hall of Fame and Songwriters Hall of Fame standouts The Doobie Brothers will make their Fontainebleau Las Vegas debut for a limited nine-show residency in 2027. The performances are scheduled for Feb. 26–27, March 5–6, Aug. 27–28 and Sept 1, 3, 4 at BleauLive Theater.  
  
With a career spanning more than five decades, The Doobie Brothers are among the most enduring and commercially successful American rock bands. Since the 1970s, the band has built a devoted following with a distinctive blend of rock, blues, country and R&B including hits such as “Listen to the Music,” “China Grove,” “Long Train Runnin’,” “Black Water” and “What a Fool Believes.”

With more than 48 million albums sold worldwide, 16 Top 40 hits, four Grammy awards and induction into the Rock & Roll Hall of Fame in 2020, The Doobie Brothers remain a major draw on tour attracting longtime fans and younger audiences discovering their extensive catalog.

The reunion of Tom Johnston, Patrick Simmons, John McFee and Michael McDonald, along with the release of their 2025 album *Walk This Road*, has helped sustain their relevance more than 55 years after the band formed and emerged out of California.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Oct. 30, at 10:00 AM/PT. Citi cardmembers will have access to a presale beginning Oct. 27, at 10:00 AM/PT, followed by additional pre-sales that will run throughout the week starting Oct. 28, at 10:00 AM/PT ahead of the general on sale.
