# TikTok Music on Stage Returns With All Things Go Festival Slot Up-for-Grabs

## Summary

TikTok LIVE is relaunching its global "Music on Stage" artist-discovery competition for 2026, following an inaugural year that generated 8.2B+ impressions and 1.4M+ Global Finals viewers. Winners get development support via TikTok-owned SoundOn and a new performance-opportunity partnership with the All Things Go Music Festival. A social-platform artist-discovery program with only a loose, indirect tie to live-event ticketing.

### Key Points
- TikTok LIVE's "Music on Stage" 2026 competition returns after 8.2B+ impressions and 230K+ #MusicOnStage posts in its debut year.
- Artists from 20+ regions compete; Cat Burns and Songkarn host regional finals.
- Winners get SoundOn production/distribution support and a shot at real-world stages.
- New tie-in: a Music on Stage winner gets a performance slot at All Things Go Music Festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/music-on-stage.jpg)

TikTok LIVE is bringing back its global Music on Stage competition for 2026, giving emerging artists a route from livestream performances to professionally released music — and, for some winners, major festival stages.

The programme returns after a breakout inaugural year that generated more than 8.2 billion impressions, inspired over 230,000 #MusicOnStage posts and drew more than 1.4 million viewers to its Global Finals.

For 2026, artists from more than 20 regions will compete through TikTok LIVE, with British singer-songwriter Cat Burns hosting the EMEA and Americas Global Finals and Thai singer-songwriter Songkarn leading the APAC competition.

The stakes extend well beyond TikTok itself. Top creators will receive professional development opportunities through TikTok-owned distribution and artist-services platform SoundOn, including the chance to record and release original music with production, distribution and promotional support.

Winners can also land real-world performance opportunities at TikTok LIVE Fest and selected music festivals, further blurring the line between social discovery and the traditional live music circuit.

One of the most prominent opportunities comes through a new partnership with All Things Go Music Festival, which will give a Music on Stage artist the chance to perform at the festival’s New York edition, running September 25-27.

Artists are competing live on TikTok throughout August for places in the regional finals on August 28, where Lizzy Plapinger — the artist behind LPX and formerly of MS MR, co-founder of Neon Gold Records and an All Things Go advisor — will serve as a guest judge.

“Creating opportunities for new voices has always been fundamental to All Things Go, making this collaboration with TikTok a natural fit,” Plapinger said. “Discovering and supporting emerging artists has been a throughline of my career, and I’m excited to help spotlight an artist for the chance to perform on the festival’s New York stage.”

TikTok has also tied Music on Stage to Breakaway Carolina Festival, with further festival opportunities expected to be announced.

Shen Gao, Head of LIVE Operations, Americas at TikTok, said the festival partnerships are designed to turn audiences built online into tangible career opportunities.

“On TikTok Live, emerging artists are building real communities and connecting with global audiences in real time, turning everyday moments into the foundation of a career,” Gao said. “Through Music on Stage, our partnership with All Things Go Music Festival gives creators the chance to share the stage alongside some of the biggest names in music.”

The competition began its audition phase on August 15 with an All-Day Global Relay Concert featuring artists from around the world. The wider programme showcases original songwriting, musicianship and live performance before progressing toward September’s Global Finals.

Burns, whose own career has been closely associated with growth on TikTok, will also serve as a guest judge.

“TikTok has played a massive part in my journey and I am so grateful to the platform,” Burns said. “The winner gets all the support in recording and releasing their own original song.”

Last year’s winners offer an early example of what that support can produce. Angelyque Solorio released her debut EP through SoundOn following her Music on Stage run, with TikTok saying the release generated more than 350,000 creator videos, helped increase her Spotify monthly listeners tenfold and accumulated more than 450,000 streams.
