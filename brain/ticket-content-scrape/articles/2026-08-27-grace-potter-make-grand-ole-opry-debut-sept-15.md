# Grace Potter Make Grand Ole Opry Debut Sept. 15

## Summary

Grace Potter will make her Grand Ole Opry debut on September 15, joining the historic Nashville institution's lineup for the first time.

### Key Points
- Grace Potter's first-ever Grand Ole Opry appearance is set for Sept. 15.

Four-time Grammy nominee Grace Potter is set to make her Grand Ole Opry debut on Sept. 15. Potter will perform songs from her new album, *Trespasser*, which is out now via Thirty Tigers. She is also scheduled to take the AmericanaFest Day Stage on Sept. 16 in celebration of the new record.

Tonight, Potter begins a run of 11 shows on the Zac Brown Band’s “Love and Fear Tour.” She kicks off her stretch of the tour with two nights at Forest Hills Stadium in New York City and then later offers support at Bon Secours Wellness Arena in Greenville, South Carolina, Allstate Arena in Rosemont, Illinois, and two nights at the iconic Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, among other markets. Tickets are available now.

Next month, Potter’s Grand Point North Festival returns to Waterfront Park in her native Burlington, Vermont. The festival, which will take place on Sept. 18, 19 and 20, features a variety of popular performers including Jackson Browne, who will make his second appearance at the annual festival; Portugal. The Man; Dawes; Dark Star Orchestra; Trampled By Turtles; The Devil Makes Three; and Potter’s most recent Grammy-nominated collaborator Maggie Rose.

Profits will go towards Potter’s Grant Point Foundation, a non-profit dedicated to supporting the arts in Vermont.
