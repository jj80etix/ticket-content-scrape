# Sam Barber Adds Dates To North American Leg Of ‘The Outlier Tour’

## Summary

Sam Barber has added new dates to the North American leg of "The Outlier Tour." The announcement covers additional stops and venues.

### Key Points
- Sam Barber expands North American "Outlier Tour" with additional dates.
- Routine tour-date announcement.

## Transcript

![unnamed (1)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-1-1.png)

unnamed (1)

Multiplatinum star Sam Barber added more dates to the North American leg of his 2027 “The Outlier Tour,” and tickets for the new shows go on sale next week.

The tour has 16 additional stops in February and May, including two-night stands at The Pinnacle in Nashville, Oklahoma City’s The Criterion, Birmingham’s Avondale Brewing Company and Iroquois Amphitheater in Louisville.

The artist presale begins Dec. 2 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general onsale on Dec. 3 at 10 a.m. American Express card members have access to an exclusive presale beginning Dec. 1 at 10 a.m. local time through Dec. 2 at 10 p.m.

Barber released Outlier, his latest EP, as well as Dyin’ Times, a surprise EP, via Atlantic Outpost earlier this month. The rising singer-songwriter won the Breakthrough Country Artist award at the 2026 American Music Awards, and he’s risen from selling out small clubs to performing at larger venues in major markets.

The country folk star performed two sold-out shows in September at The Rooftop at Pier 17 in New York City, where he grossed $374,912 off 7,170 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

Barber is booked by THE·TEAM.
