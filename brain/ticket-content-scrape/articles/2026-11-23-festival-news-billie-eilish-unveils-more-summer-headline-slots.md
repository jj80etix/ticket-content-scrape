# Festival news: Billie Eilish unveils more summer headline slots

## Summary

A roundup of 2027 festival lineup announcements across Europe and North America, led by Billie Eilish adding BST Hyde Park and Roskilde to her Glastonbury-headlining summer, alongside first-wave announcements from Boardmasters, New Orleans Jazz Fest, Oya, La Cura Fest and Forbidden Fruit.

### Key Points
- Billie Eilish headlines BST Hyde Park (Jul 3, 2027) and Roskilde (Jun 30), joining her Glastonbury slot.
- Boardmasters' 2027 first wave includes Chase & Status, Disclosure and Wolf Alice.
- New Orleans Jazz Fest 2027 (Apr 22–May 2) led by Foo Fighters, The Who and Gwen Stefani.
- Oya Festival (Oslo) adds The Strokes, Robyn and Pavement.
- Forbidden Fruit (Dublin) marks its 15th anniversary with Barry Can't Swim and Tinashe.

## Transcript

The singer has added Roskilde and BST Hyde Park to her Glastonbury billing, with announcements also coming from Boardmasters, Oya, and more

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/Credit-Henry-Hwu-scaled.jpg.webp)

Billie Eilish © Henry Hwu

Leading the latest round of festival news, Billie Eilish will headline London’s **BST Hyde Park**, where she will perform on 3 July 2027.

Bon Jovi, Muse, and RAYE are already confirmed for the series, with concerts spread across July. Eilish continues to pack summer 2027 with headline slots following her announcement at the top of the [Glastonbury](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/11/glastonbury-leads-festival-news-with-incredible-2027-lineup/) bill last week.

Jim King, CEO of AEG Presents UK and European festivals, said: “Every event has their ‘list’ of a handful of artists you dream of seeing on its stage, and for BST Hyde Park, Billie Eilish has been at the top of that list for years.”

The news came just as Eilish was also announced for Denmark’s **Roskilde** **Festival**, joining a lineup already including Tame Impala, Patti Smith, and Kacey Musgraves.

The singer will appear on the main stage of the independent, non-profit festival on 3o June 2027, with organisers saying she is a “a perfect fit both artistically and in terms of values”.

These acts form the first part of the Roskilde lineup, which will eventually number almost 200 artists. Organisers said they are expecting to welcome 130,000 participants to the 2027 edition, the festival’s 55th.

Elsewhere, **Boardmasters** has revealed its first wave of artist names for its return between 4 and 8 August 2027, unveiling Chase & Status, Disclosure, and Wolf Alice on the bill.

They are joined by artists including Ezra Collective, Becky Hill, Two Door Cinema Club, The Vaccines, Giggs, Declan Mckenna, La Roux, Holly Humberstone, The Fratellis, and Overpass.

Festival director Rob Spring called the announcement “one of the strongest first waves we’ve ever announced” for the Cornish event. The 2027 edition will also mark a “new era”, organisers said, as Dutch DJ Chris Stassy will become the festival’s first ever Thursday main stage headliner.

Over in the US, the **New Orleans Jazz and Heritage Festival** will be led by Foo Fighters, Noah Kahan, The Who, Brandi Carlile, Gwen Stefani, and James Taylor in 2027.

The AEG Presents-promoted event will span 22 April – 2 May 2027 and feature over 5,000 artists across 14 stages at Louisiana’s Fair Grounds Race Course. Also on the bill are acts including Jack White, Matchbox Twenty, and The War on Drugs.

Meanwhile, Norway’s **Oya Festival** has announced The Strokes, Robyn, Slayyter, and Angine de Poitrine for its 2027 edition in Oslo’s Tøyen Park. Also on the bill are Delillos and Pavement, with 11 artists named so far in total.

Mexican star Carín León will top a bill for his self-curated **La Cura Fest** in Mexico in 2027, leading a lineup of names also including Kacey Musgraves. Taking place on 13-14 March in Expogan Hermosillo in Sonora, the festival aims to combine Latin and country music for its second outing, after debuting in 2026.

Finally, Ireland’s **Forbidden Fruit** will return for its 15th anniversary with acts including Barry Can’t Swim, Overmono, Tinashe, and Confidence Man, organisers have announced. It will return for its 2027 outing on 5-6 June at the Irish Museum of Modern Art in Kilmainham in Dublin.
