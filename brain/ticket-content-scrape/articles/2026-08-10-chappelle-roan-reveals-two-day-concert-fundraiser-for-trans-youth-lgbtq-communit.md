# Chappelle Roan Reveals Two-Day Concert Fundraiser for Trans Youth, LGBTQ+ Community

## Summary

Chappell Roan announced a two-night benefit concert, "The Super Graphic Spectacular," at The Shrine in Los Angeles on Oct. 21-22, to support transgender youth and LGBTQ+ community organizations via her nonprofit The Midwest Princess Project.

### Key Points
- Presale runs through Aug. 11 at 11:59 p.m. ET via AXS; selected fans are notified starting Aug. 17, with ticket sale waves Aug. 18-20.
- The Midwest Princess Project has raised over $800,000 from Roan's 2025 pop-up show run.
- Roan will also perform later this month at Olivia Rodrigo's all-female "Daisy Chain Fields" festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/chappel-roan.jpg)

Chappelle Roan has announced a two-day concert fundraiser to help support transgender youth and LGBTQ+ community organizations.

“The Super Graphic Spectacular” is set to take over The Shrine in Los Angeles on October 21 and 22. The event, hosted by Roan and her nonprofit organization “The Midwest Princess Project,” will include headlining performances from Roan and collaborators, as well as guest appearances and drag artistry.

Tickets will be available starting with a presale running through Tuesday, August 11 at 8:59 p.m. PT / 11:59 p.m. ET [here](https://www.axs.com/series/34064/the-super-graphic-spectacular-tickets?skin=thesupergraphicspectacular). Fans will be notified if they are selected via email starting Monday, August 17, and tickets will be available for those selected fans in waves running from Tuesday, August 18 through Thursday, August 20.

The Midwest Princess Project has raised over $800,000 in donations and proceeds from Roan’s 2025 [pop-up run](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/07/chappell-roan-announces-nyc-la-kc-pop-up-shows/) “Visions of Damsels & Other Dangerous Things.”

Roan is scheduled to perform next at Olivia Rodrigo’s [Daisy Chain Fields](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/olivia-rodrigo-announces-daisy-chain-fields-festival-with-all-women-lineup/) festival later this month as a part of the all-female lineup alongside Doechii, Mitski, Sarah McLachlan, and Stevie Nicks.
