# Kenia Os Announces Six-Date U.S. Tour for February 2027

## Summary

Mexican singer Kenia Os has announced a six-date U.S. tour for February 2027. The announcement lists the cities and venues for the run.

### Key Points
- Kenia Os announces a six-date U.S. tour for February 2027.
- Standard touring announcement with no ticketing-industry structural angle.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Kenia-Os.webp)

Kenia Os will return to the United States in February 2027 for six concerts on her “K De Karma U.S. Tour.”

The run begins February 10 at the Majestic Theatre in Dallas and concludes February 27 at YouTube Theater in Inglewood, California. After the Dallas opener, Os will perform at The Auditorium in Chicago on February 12 and the Beacon Theatre in New York on February 13. The schedule then resumes February 24 at Houston’s 713 Music Hall before heading to Southern California for the final two concerts. The Magnolia in El Cajon will host the February 26 performance, followed by the Inglewood finale the next evening.

The tour supports *K DE KARMA*, Os’ fourth studio album, released earlier this year. The record includes “Belladona,” “Una y Otra Vez,” “En 4” and “Tokome.”

The U.S. announcement follows a May headlining performance at Estadio Banorte in Monterrey, Mexico. According to Pollstar Boxoffice figures, that concert generated approximately $1.19 million from 12,267 tickets sold. Os also appeared at Baja Beach Fest in August.
