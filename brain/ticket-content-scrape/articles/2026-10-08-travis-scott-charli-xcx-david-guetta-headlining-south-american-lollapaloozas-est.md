# Travis Scott, Charli xcx, David Guetta Headlining South American Lollapaloozas & Estereo Picnic

## Summary

Four South American festivals — Lollapalooza Argentina, Chile, and Brazil, plus Estéreo Picnic — unveiled lineups headlined by Travis Scott, Charli xcx, and David Guetta. Lolla Argentina and Chile run the same weekend (March 10–12) with largely overlapping bills, while Lolla Brazil follows the next weekend.

### Key Points
- Lollapalooza Argentina (Buenos Aires) and Chile (Santiago) both run March 10–12, 2027, sharing most headliners.
- Argentina bill adds The Killers, Tini, Queens of the Stone Age, Fontaines D.C., and Sombr; Chile swaps in acts like Francisca Valenzuela and Ana Tijoux.
- Lollapalooza Brazil follows the weekend of March 19–21.
- Shared headliners across the festivals: Travis Scott, Charli xcx, David Guetta.
- Ticket sales info was provided via each festival's own site (lollapaloozaar.com, lollapaloozacl.com).

## Transcript

![lollas](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/lollas.jpg)

Four of the biggest festivals in South America unveiled their stacked lineups Thursday, with Travis Scott, Charli xcx and David Guetta headlining each event.

Lollapalooza in Argentina and Chile take place the same weekend, March 10-12, and share similar lineups.

Lolla Argentina takes place at Hipódromo de San Isidro in Buenos Aires and includes The Killers, Tini, Queens of The Stone Age, The Neighbourhood, Rawayana, Fontaines D.C. and Sombr as headliners in addition to Travis Scott, Charli xcx and Guetta. Other acts on the bill include The Prodigy, Dillom, Malcolm Todd, Dominic Fike, Cage the Elephant, Geese, Raye, Lola Young, Slayyyter, ADÉLA, Un Poco de Ruido, Marlon Hoffstadt, Four Tet, Angine de Poitrine, Tomora, K4OS, Leon Bridges and many more.

For information about tickets, visit [https://www.lollapaloozaar.com/](https://www.lollapaloozaar.com/).

All headliners in Argentina, sans Tini, will perform at Lollapalooza Chile, which is held at Parque O’Higgins in Santiago, as well as other acts like Lola Young, Cage the Elephant and Malcolm Todd. Other artists on the bill include Francisca Valenzuela, Gondwana & Quique Neira, Alex Anwandter, Santaferia, Javier Mena, Ana Tijoux El Cuarteto de Nos and many more.

For tickets to the festival in Santiago, visit [https://lollapaloozacl.com/](https://lollapaloozacl.com/).

Lollapalooza Brazil takes place the following weekend, March 19-21, at Autódromo de Interlagos in São Paulo, and includes Mochakk as headliner. Rodrigo Amarante, Mike D 5D, Sammy Virji, Snow Strippers, Julia Wolf, Ninajirachi and many more are also scheduled to perform.

For more information about the Brazilian festival, visit [https://www.lollapaloozabr.com/](https://www.lollapaloozabr.com/).

Bogotá, Colombia’s Estéreo Picnic also boasts a diverse lineup with Arcángel, Avenged Sevenfold, The Neighbourhood, Rawayana, Milo J and Sombr billed as headliners along with Scott, Charli xcx and Guetta. Other artists on the bill include Fontaines D.C., Amelie Lens, Khruangbin, The War on Drugs, Kasabian and more.

Visit [https://www.festivalestereopicnic.com/](https://www.festivalestereopicnic.com/) for more information.
