# Jessica Chastain Announces Broadway Return in New Play ‘Lost Lear’

## Summary

Jessica Chastain will return to Broadway in spring 2027 starring in "Lost Lear," a new play by Dan Colley, alongside Corey Stoll and Michael Urie. Previews begin March 29 at the Booth Theatre, with an April 23 opening night. The play previously premiered in Dublin and won a Scotsman Fringe First Award at the Edinburgh Festival Fringe.

### Key Points
- Broadway previews begin March 29, 2027; opening night April 23 at the Booth Theatre.
- Marks Dan Colley's Broadway debut as playwright and director; Chastain's first Broadway role since 2023's "A Doll's House."
- Play previously premiered in Dublin (2022) and won a Scotsman Fringe First Award at Edinburgh (2025).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Broadway-Lost-Lear.webp)

Jessica Chastain will return to Broadway in spring 2027, starring alongside Corey Stoll and Michael Urie in “Lost Lear,” a new play written and directed by Dan Colley.

The production is scheduled to begin previews on March 29 at the Booth Theatre in New York City, with opening night set for April 23. The engagement will mark Colley’s Broadway debut as both a playwright and director.

Chastain will lead the cast as Joy, a former actress living with dementia who believes she is rehearsing for a production of William Shakespeare’s “King Lear.” Urie will portray Liam, a nurse at Joy’s care home who participates in her imagined rehearsals by taking on various supporting roles. Stoll will play Conor, Joy’s estranged son, who arrives hoping to address unresolved issues in their relationship.

“Lost Lear” originally premiered in Dublin in 2022 before gaining further recognition at the 2025 Edinburgh Festival Fringe, where it received a Scotsman Fringe First Award. The upcoming Broadway production will introduce the work to New York audiences with a new cast.

“I never imagined, when we first made ‘Lost Lear,’ that it would one day be on Broadway—and I’ve imagined some pretty wild things,” Colley said in a statement. “I’m so thrilled to have the opportunity to share this piece with New York audiences and to discover new facets of the work through collaborating with this remarkable company of artists,” he added.

The production marks Chastain’s first Broadway appearance since starring in the 2023 revival of “A Doll’s House,” which earned her a Tony Award nomination for Best Actress in a Play. She previously received a nomination for her performance in the 2012 production of “The Heiress.”

Stoll also returns to Broadway following his Tony-nominated performance in the 2023 revival of “Appropriate.” His additional stage credits include “A View From the Bridge,” “Old Acquaintance” and “Henry IV.”

Urie’s recent Broadway appearances include “Oh, Mary!,” “Once Upon a Mattress” and “Spamalot.” He is also widely recognized for his television role in “Ugly Betty.”

The creative team for “Lost Lear” includes Scott Pask as production designer, Heather Gilbert as lighting designer and Cody Spencer as sound designer. Hana S. Kim and Yana Biryukova will oversee projection design, with original music by Daniel McAuley.

Taylor Williams is handling casting, while Juniper Street Productions will serve as production manager. Christopher Taggart of 101 Productions is the general manager, and Rachel Bauder will serve as production stage manager.

For more information and additional details, theatergoers can visit [LostLearBroadway.com](http://www.lostlearbroadway.com/).
