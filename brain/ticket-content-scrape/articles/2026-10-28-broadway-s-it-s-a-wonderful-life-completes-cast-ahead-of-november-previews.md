# Broadway’s ‘It’s a Wonderful Life’ Completes Cast Ahead of November Previews

## Summary

Broadway's "It's a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play" completed its cast with standbys Jeanna de Waal and Martin Landry, joining stars Andrew Rannells, Megan Hilty, Tituss Burgess, Harry Hamlin and Lisa Rinna ahead of previews beginning Nov. 4 at the Broadhurst Theatre. The Jerry Zaks-directed limited engagement opens Nov. 18 and runs through Jan. 3, 2027.

### Key Points
- Previews begin Nov. 4; opening night Nov. 18; limited run through Jan. 3, 2027, at the Broadhurst Theatre.
- Cast includes Andrew Rannells (George Bailey), Megan Hilty (Mary), Tituss Burgess (Clarence), Harry Hamlin (Mr. Potter) and Lisa Rinna (Violet); de Waal and Landry join as standbys.
- Directed by four-time Tony winner Jerry Zaks; adapted by Joe Landry from Philip Van Doren Stern's "The Greatest Gift."
- Produced by Seaview, No Guarantees Productions, Broadway & Beyond Theatricals and The Shubert Organization.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Its-A-Wonderful-Life.webp)

Jeanna de Waal and Martin Landry have joined the Broadway company of “It’s a Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play,” completing the cast ahead of its November debut at the Broadhurst Theatre. The two actors will serve as standbys covering the production’s principal roles.

They join previously announced stars Andrew Rannells, Megan Hilty, Tituss Burgess, Harry Hamlin and Lisa Rinna. The limited holiday engagement begins previews November 4, with opening night scheduled for November 18. Performances will continue through January 3, 2027.

Directed by four-time Tony Award winner Jerry Zaks, the production presents the story through a fictional 1940s radio broadcast.

Rannells will play George Bailey, with Hilty as his wife, Mary. Burgess will portray Clarence and other characters, while Hamlin takes on Mr. Potter and additional roles. Rinna will play Violet and others. The ensemble will portray the residents of Bedford Falls within the radio studio setting.

Joe Landry adapted the play from Philip Van Doren Stern’s short story “The Greatest Gift,” which also inspired Frank Capra’s 1946 film. The story follows George Bailey as a crisis causes him to question the value of his life. An encounter with Clarence reveals how his actions have shaped the lives of his family, friends and neighbors.

The creative team includes Santo Loquasto overseeing scenic and costume design, Kenneth Posner handling lighting and Palmer Hefferan providing sound design. Andy Einhorn serves as music director, and Taylor Williams is the casting director. Producers include Seaview, No Guarantees Productions, Broadway & Beyond Theatricals and The Shubert Organization.
