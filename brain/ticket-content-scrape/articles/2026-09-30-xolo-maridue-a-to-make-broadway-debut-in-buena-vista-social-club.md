# Xolo Maridueña to Make Broadway Debut in ‘Buena Vista Social Club’

## Summary

Xolo Maridueña ("Cobra Kai," "Blue Beetle") will make his Broadway debut in "Buena Vista Social Club," playing Juan de Marcos at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre for a seven-week run starting November 17 through January 3, 2027. His engagement overlaps with Gina Torres's run as Omara Portuondo (Oct. 13–Nov. 29).

### Key Points
- Seven-week engagement, Nov. 17, 2026 – Jan. 3, 2027, Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre.
- Plays Juan de Marcos; marks his Broadway debut.
- Overlaps with Gina Torres's run in the same production.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Buena-Vista-Social-Club.webp)

Xolo Maridueña will make his Broadway debut in “Buena Vista Social Club,” joining the musical at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre for a seven-week engagement beginning November 17.

The actor, known for his roles in “Cobra Kai” and “Blue Beetle,” will play Juan de Marcos through January 3, 2027. His arrival adds another screen performer to the production’s fall lineup, which also includes an upcoming engagement by Gina Torres.

In a statement, Maridueña shared, “I’m honored to be making my Broadway debut with the incredibly talented team at *Buena Vista Social Club*.”

“Broadway has always been a dream, but getting to make that dream come true while being part of such an important and beautiful story feels like hitting the lottery twice,” Maridueña continued. “Come ready to laugh, cry, and shake your booty!”

The creative team also includes David Yazbek as creative consultant and Dean Sharenow as music supervisor. Marco Paguia provides orchestrations, arrangements and music direction, with additional arrangements by Javier Diaz and David Oquendo. The real Juan de Marcos serves as music consultant.
