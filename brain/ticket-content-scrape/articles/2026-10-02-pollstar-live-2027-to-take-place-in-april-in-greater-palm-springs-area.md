# Pollstar Live! 2027 To Take Place In April In Greater Palm Springs Area

## Summary

Pollstar Live! announced dates and location for its 2027 edition: April 13-15 in Indian Wells, in the Greater Palm Springs area, marking the conference's first desert staging and scheduled between the two Coachella weekends. The event, presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster, is billed as the live-entertainment industry's largest annual gathering, with keynotes, panels, networking, and the Pollstar Awards.

### Key Points
- Dates: April 13-15, 2027, in Indian Wells, CA — the conference's first desert-area edition.
- Presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster; scheduled between Coachella's two weekends.
- The 2026 edition's Pollstar Awards included Lars Ulrich and Eagles members as presenters/guests.

Dates and a location are set for Pollstar Live! 2027, marking the conference’s desert debut in the greater Palm Springs area.

The world’s largest gathering of live entertainment professionals promises three full days of bold ideas, meaningful connections and engaging conversations with keynote speakers, panels and plenty of networking opportunities.

Presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster, the next edition of Pollstar Live! is scheduled April 13-15 in Indian Wells, California. The event will once again take place in between the two Coachella weekends, April 9-11 and April 16-18.  
  
Stay tuned for more details including the conference hotel and speaker announcements.

Pollstar Live! 2026 featured Kevin Shivers of THE·TEAM, C3 Presents’ Charles Attal, Peter Shapiro of Dayglo Presents, Independent Artist Group’s Jarred Arfa and many more at Loews Hollywood Hotel. The conference also included the 37th annual Pollstar Awards with iHeartRadio’s Valentine as host and appearances by Lars Ulrich of Metallica and Timothy B. Schmidt and Deacon Frey of the Eagles.
