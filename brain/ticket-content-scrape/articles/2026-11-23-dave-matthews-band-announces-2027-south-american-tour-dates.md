# Dave Matthews Band Announces 2027 South American Tour Dates

## Summary

Dave Matthews Band announced six May 2027 South American tour dates spanning Brazil, Argentina and Chile — its first Argentina/Chile shows in over a decade — alongside separate early-2027 Dave Matthews/Tim Reynolds performances marking the 10th anniversary of their Riviera Maya getaway.

### Key Points
- May 1–13, 2027: São Paulo (Best of Blues and Rock fest), Rio de Janeiro, Curitiba, Buenos Aires and Santiago.
- First Buenos Aires headline show since 2010; first Chile date since 2013.
- Follows completion of the band's fall 2026 US tour (closing at Madison Square Garden).
- Separate Jan. 2027 Riviera Maya anniversary dates with Tim Reynolds.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Dave-Matthews-Band-South-America-Tour-2027.webp)

Dave Matthews Band has announced six concerts across South America for May 2027, marking the group’s return to Argentina and Chile for the first time in more than a decade.

The upcoming run will also include four performances in Brazil, with stops in São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro and Curitiba.

The tour will begin May 1 and 2 with two headlining performances at the Best of Blues and Rock festival at Auditório Ibirapuera in São Paulo. The band will then travel to Rio de Janeiro for a May 5 concert at Qualistage Arena before performing May 7 at Live Curitiba.

Following the Brazilian dates, Dave Matthews Band will perform May 10 at Movistar Arena in Buenos Aires, Argentina, and May 13 at Santander Arena in Santiago, Chile.

The Buenos Aires concert will be the group’s first performance in Argentina since appearing at the Summer Break festival in December 2013. It will also mark the band’s first headlining show in the country since October 14, 2010, when it performed at Luna Park.

That 2010 concert was later released as “Live Trax Vol. 27,” part of the band’s ongoing series of live recordings documenting performances from throughout its career.

The Santiago performance will similarly end a lengthy absence from Chile, where Dave Matthews Band last appeared in December 2013 during the Santiago Summer Fest. The group previously performed at the same Santiago arena in October 2010.

The South American announcement follows the completion of Dave Matthews Band’s fall 2026 U.S. tour, which concluded with two performances at Madison Square Garden in New York City. The concerts wrapped up the group’s touring schedule for the year.

Before returning to the road with the full band in May, frontman Dave Matthews has several additional performances scheduled for early 2027.

Matthews will reunite with longtime collaborator Tim Reynolds in January for the 10th anniversary of their [Riviera Maya concert getaway](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/dave-matthews-and-tim-reynolds-announce-2027-riviera-maya-10th-anniversary-lineup/) in Cancún, Mexico. The event will feature three acoustic performances from the duo, along with appearances by Goose, Sierra Ferrell and Stephen Wilson Jr.

Matthews is also scheduled to perform a series of solo acoustic concerts in Japan in January.

Tickets for the newly announced South American concerts will be available through presales beginning November 24, with general ticket sales scheduled to begin November 26.

For the Argentina and Chile performances, members of Warehouse, the band’s official fan association, will have access to a presale beginning at 8 a.m. ET. General sales for those concerts will open at the same time on November 26.

The Brazilian concerts will have a separate presale beginning at 10 a.m. ET on November 24 for Warehouse members and eligible previous Best of Blues and Rock attendees. General sales for the Brazilian dates will begin November 26 at 10 a.m. ET.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [davematthewsband.com](http://davematthewsband.com).
