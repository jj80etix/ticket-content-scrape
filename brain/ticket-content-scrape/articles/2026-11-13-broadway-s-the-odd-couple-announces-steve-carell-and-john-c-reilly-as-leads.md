# Broadway’s ‘The Odd Couple’ Announces Steve Carell and John C. Reilly as Leads

## Summary

Steve Carell and John C. Reilly will headline a Broadway revival of Neil Simon's "The Odd Couple," directed by Thomas Kail, opening March 23, 2027 at the Stephen Sondheim Theatre for a 15-week limited engagement. Previews begin March 3, with Carell as Felix Ungar and Reilly as Oscar Madison.

### Key Points
- Previews March 3, 2027; opening night March 23 at the Stephen Sondheim Theatre.
- 15-week limited engagement directed by Thomas Kail ("Hamilton").
- Steve Carell plays Felix Ungar; John C. Reilly plays Oscar Madison.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Broadways-The-Odd-Couple.webp)

Steve Carell and John C. Reilly will lead a Broadway revival of Neil Simon’s “The Odd Couple” this spring. The production will begin performances March 3, 2027, at the Stephen Sondheim Theatre, with an official opening scheduled for March 23.

Thomas Kail, the Tony-winning director of “Hamilton,” will direct the 15-week limited engagement. Carell will play Felix Ungar, an exacting housekeeper who moves in with his untidy friend Oscar Madison, played by Reilly.

In a statement, Kail discussed the play and its leading actors. “Neil Simon wrote one of the great American comedies, and it is an honor and a thrill to be part of this exceptional team bringing this play back to Broadway,” he said. “Underneath the extraordinary humor is something deeply human.”

“Steve and John are two remarkable actors who can make an audience laugh uproariously one moment and move them deeply in the next,” Kail added. “We aim to create a world that feels alive, immediate, and can clearly express why this play has endured for more than 60 years.”

The production’s design team includes David Korins on scenery, Brenda Abbandandolo on costumes, Stacey Derosier on lighting and Jill BC Du Boff on sound. J. Jeffrey Seller, The Season’s Mike Karns and Steven Tartick, Kail and Emanuel Azenberg are producing the revival.
