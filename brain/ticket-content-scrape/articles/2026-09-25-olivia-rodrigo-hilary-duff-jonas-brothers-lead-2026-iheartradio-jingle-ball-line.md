# Olivia Rodrigo, Hilary Duff, Jonas Brothers Lead 2026 iHeartRadio Jingle Ball Lineups

## Summary

iHeartRadio unveiled dates and lineups for its 2026 Jingle Ball Tour, led by Olivia Rodrigo, Hilary Duff and Jonas Brothers, running December 1–17 across nine U.S. cities with market-specific lineups. General tickets go on sale October 2 via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Nine-city run each with its own distinct lineup rather than a single traveling bill.
- Capital One cardholder presale runs September 29–October 1; general public onsale begins October 2 at noon local time via Ticketmaster.
- Additional touring artists include sombr, Alex Warren, OneRepublic and Teddy Swims.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/iHeart-Radio-Jingle-Ball.webp)

iHeartRadio has unveiled the dates and lineups for its 2026 Jingle Ball Tour, with Olivia Rodrigo, KATSEYE, Hilary Duff, Jonas Brothers, Charlie Puth, RAYE and a rotating group of other artists set to play nine U.S. cities in December.

The annual holiday concert series will run from December 1 through December 17, making stops in Fort Worth, Los Angeles, Chicago, Detroit, New York City, Boston, Washington, D.C., Philadelphia and Nashville.

Other artists appearing across the tour include sombr, Alex Warren, OneRepublic, Ludacris, Teddy Swims and Sienna Spiro, with each market receiving its own lineup rather than a single bill traveling together for the entire run.

In a joint statement, John Sykes, president of Entertainment Enterprises for iHeartMedia, and Tom Poleman shared, “The Jingle Ball Tour has become the holiday season’s most anticipated concert event, showcasing the biggest stars and the songs that defined the year in music.”
