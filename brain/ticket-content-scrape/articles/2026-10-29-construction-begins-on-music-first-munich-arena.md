# Construction begins on ‘music-first’ Munich Arena

## Summary

Live Nation broke ground on Munich Arena, a 20,000-capacity "music-first" venue projected to host 1.5 million spectators a year when it opens in 2029. Construction follows city council approval of a revised, 20% smaller venue footprint with refined sightlines.

### Key Points
- 20,000-capacity venue, opening 2029, projected 1.5 million annual spectators
- Live Nation frames it as strengthening Munich/Bavaria's position as a touring destination
- Construction start followed city council approval of a 20%-smaller footprint than original plans
- Live Nation describes it as Germany's first arena purpose-built for live music

Live Nation has broken ground on Munich Arena, the long-awaited 20,000-cap music-first space for the Bavarian capital.

Set to open in 2029 and with a projection of hosting 1.5 million spectators a year, Live Nation said the venue will “strengthen Munich and Bavaria’s position as a destination for live entertainment, by creating a new stage for artists touring Europe”.
