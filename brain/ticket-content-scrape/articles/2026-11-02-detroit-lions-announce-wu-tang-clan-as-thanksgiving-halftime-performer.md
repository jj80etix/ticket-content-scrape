# Detroit Lions Announce Wu-Tang Clan as Thanksgiving Halftime Performer

## Summary

Wu-Tang Clan will headline the Detroit Lions' Thanksgiving Day halftime show against the Chicago Bears on Nov. 26, with Eminem and Paul Rosenberg again executive-producing the performance.

### Key Points
- Eminem/Rosenberg also produced last year's halftime show, which featured Jack White and a surprise Eminem appearance.
- Other NFL Thanksgiving halftime acts this season: Miranda Lambert (Cowboys-Eagles), Goo Goo Dolls (Chiefs-Bills), and U2 (Packers-Rams Thanksgiving Eve game).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Wu-Tang-Clan-Haldtime-Show.webp)

Wu-Tang Clan will headline the halftime show during the Detroit Lions’ Thanksgiving Day game against the Chicago Bears on November 26.

Eminem and his longtime manager, Paul Rosenberg, will return as executive producers of the halftime show, overseeing the selection of performers. The pair also produced last year’s performance, which featured Jack White and a surprise appearance by Eminem.

In a statement, Rosenberg shared, “We often like to do things that are both exciting and unexpected. A Wu-Tang Clan Thanksgiving performance in Detroit certainly fits that description!”

“Both Eminem and myself have been Wu fans since the early 90’s along with a whole generation of Detroit football fans, and we are honored to bring them to this year’s stage,” Rosenberg continued.
