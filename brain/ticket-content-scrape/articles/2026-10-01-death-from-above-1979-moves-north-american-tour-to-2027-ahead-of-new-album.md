# Death From Above 1979 Moves North American Tour to 2027 Ahead of New Album

## Summary

Death From Above 1979 has pushed its North American tour to 2027 to coincide with a new album release. This is a routine tour-scheduling announcement with no industry-structure or platform relevance.

### Key Points
- Tour moved from original dates to 2027.
- Timed to a new album.
- No ticketing-platform or pricing angle reported.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Death-From-Above-1979.webp)

Death From Above 1979 has postponed its fall North American tour until early 2027, giving ticket holders a 30-day window to request refunds for rescheduled shows as the band finishes work on its newly announced album, *Private Lives*.

The Canadian duo’s tour had been scheduled to begin October 1 in Sacramento and run through December 6 in Pittsburgh. The replacement itinerary now opens February 2 in Tempe, Arizona, and runs through March 31 in Seattle, with most previously announced markets receiving new dates and several additional cities joining the routing.

Tickets already purchased for rescheduled performances will remain valid for the replacement dates. Fans who cannot attend can seek refunds through their original point of purchase through Oct. 22, according to the band’s website.

“Refunds will be available for 30 days” for rescheduled shows, the band said, after which existing tickets will carry over to the new dates. Shows that have been canceled entirely should be refunded through the original seller.

At least some venues are independently applying the same deadline. Minneapolis’ Fine Line, for example, says tickets for the originally scheduled Nov. 6 performance will be honored for the new March 19 date, while refund requests can be made through Oct. 22.

“When we booked our fall 2026 tour, we wanted it to be an album tour, yet we hadn’t stepped foot in a studio,” Jesse F. Keeler and Sebastien Grainger said in their postponement announcement.

Moving the shows to early 2027, they said, will allow them to finish the record and prepare the new material for the road. The band also acknowledged that the relatively short notice would create problems for some fans and apologized for the inconvenience.

*Private Lives*, Death From Above 1979’s fifth studio LP and first since 2021’s *Is 4 Lovers*, is due Jan. 29 through Last Gang/MNRK. The band announced the record this week along with its title track and an accompanying music video.

The new routing is not an exact city-for-city replacement of the fall schedule. Berkeley, Monterey and San Luis Obispo, California, and Buffalo, New York, no longer appear on the 2027 itinerary. New stops include San Francisco; Waterloo and Ottawa, Ontario; Philadelphia; Red Deer, Alberta; and Victoria, British Columbia.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit the official [Death From Above 1979 website](https://deathfromabove1979.com/).
