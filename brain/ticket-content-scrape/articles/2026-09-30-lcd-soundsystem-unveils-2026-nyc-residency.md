# LCD Soundsystem Unveils 2026 NYC Residency

## Summary

LCD Soundsystem announced its 2026 NYC Residency: 12 shows across three weekends at Knockdown Center and Brooklyn Steel, Nov. 19–Dec. 13, with the Brooklyn Steel run marking the 100th show of the band's annual residency series.

### Key Points
- Dates: Nov. 19–22 (Knockdown Center), Nov. 30–Dec. 3 (Brooklyn Steel), Dec. 10–13 (Knockdown Center).
- Nov. 30 show's full ticket proceeds will go to a charity (TBA).
- Produced by The Bowery Presents; general on-sale Oct. 9 at 10am ET via AXS.com.
- Amex Card Member presale runs Oct. 6 10am–Oct. 8 10pm ET.

LCD Soundsystem has announced its 2026 NYC Residency series, with 12 shows over three weekends beginning with Nov. 19-22 at Knockdown Center in Queens, continuing into Nov. 30-December 3 return to Brooklyn Steel, and concluding with four more shows Dec. 10-13 at Knockdown Center. This year’s four-night run at Brooklyn Steel will kick off with the 100th show of LCD Soundsystem’s annual residencies. LCD Soundsystem will donate 100% of proceeds from ticket sales for the November 30 show to a charity to be announced. According to an official press release, the 2026 residency will feature a variety of live openers, afterparties featuring DJ sets from special guests, and more. The Bowery Presents is producing LCD Soundsystem’s 2026 residency.

Tickets for all 12 shows will go on sale to the general public beginning Oct. 9 at 10:00 a.m. ET. via [AXS.com](https://www.axs.com/). Visit [youarehere.bowerypresents.com](https://youarehere.bowerypresents.com/) for presale details and further information.
