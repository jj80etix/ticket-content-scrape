# People on the move: PRS For Music, Tickets For Good, and more…

## Summary

TheTicketingBusiness's regular industry personnel roundup: Ben McOwen Wilson (ex-Google Play/TV EMEA managing director) becomes PRS For Music's new CEO effective October 12, succeeding Andrea Czapary Martin after her seven-year tenure. Also covered: Natalya Stecko's promotion to head of marketing at Tickets For Good, Taylor Young's move to director of ticket sales at Miami Athletics, and Spencer LeBoff joining music-financing platform Pipeline as head of revenue.

### Key Points
- Ben McOwen Wilson named PRS For Music CEO, starting October 12, 2026.
- Natalya Stecko promoted to head of marketing and community management at Tickets For Good.
- Taylor Young named director of ticket sales for Miami Athletics, joining from the Cincinnati Cyclones.
- Spencer LeBoff joins creative-economy financing platform Pipeline as head of revenue, from Avex Music Group.

## Transcript

![TheTicketingBusiness News](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TBN-LOGO-reversed-white.png)

TheTicketingBusiness News

**We round up the latest movements from the ticketing sector and associated industries…**

**PRS For Music** has named **Ben McOwen Wilson** as its new chief executive, replacing Andrea Czapary Martin.

McOwen Wilson is set to assume the title on October 12, following his predecessor’s seven-year stint.

He has previously held a number of key roles across technology, gaming and culture, most recently acting as the managing director for EMEA at Google Play and Google Television from September 2022.

“Ben is an outstanding leader with all the skills necessary to guide PRS for Music through the many changes in the music industry and the wider media market. Ben impressed us from the very beginning,” said chair of the PRS Members’ Council Julian Nott.

“He understands the technologies that are transforming the creative industries, but he also understands that innovation only succeeds if it works for the creators.

“We are confident that he will champion members’ interests at home and around the world, leading us boldly into the next phase of our evolution.”

**Natalya Stecko** has been promoted to head of marketing and community management at **Tickets For Good** after spending two years as marketing and communications manager at the organisation.

“I feel incredibly honoured to take on this position and so proud of our wonderful team, everything we’ve achieved together, and how far we’ve come,” she said.

“Tickets For Good has already been such an important part of my journey, and I feel very lucky to be playing a part in the company’s next phase of growth.

“There’s so much to be excited about, and I can’t wait to see what we accomplish next.”

**Taylor Young** has been named director of ticket sales for **Miami Athletics**.

He comes to Miami University after spending the past three years with the Cincinnati Cyclones.

“I’m excited to become part of the RedHawks family and can’t wait to get started working alongside such a talented department,” Young said.

“I look forward to welcoming RedHawks Nation to our athletic events throughout the 2026–27 season and helping create memorable experiences for our fans. Go RedHawks!”

**Pipeline**, the financing platform serving the music industry and wider creative economy, has appointed **Spencer LeBoff** as head of revenue.

LeBoff joins Pipeline from Avex Music Group, where he served as senior vice president of publishing and acquisitions.
