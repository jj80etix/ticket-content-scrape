# Miley Cyrus Announces Hollywood Bowl Debut With Two October Shows

## Summary

Miley Cyrus announced two Hollywood Bowl shows on October 16 and 18 — her first time performing at the venue — with Model/Actriz as support, timed to the September 18 release of her 10th studio album, "Bass Persuades."

### Key Points
- Artist presale begins Sept. 9; public onsale Sept. 11.
- Marks Cyrus's first Hollywood Bowl performance despite the venue being a longtime personal favorite.
- Cyrus's last headline tour was in 2015; recent years have centered on festival dates and New Year's Eve shows.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Miley-Cyrus-Hollywood-Bowl.webp)

Miley Cyrus has announced two shows at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles this fall.

The singer will bring “Bass Persuades Live” to the venue on October 16 and October 18, with Model/Actriz joining both concerts as a special guest. The October dates will mark Cyrus’ first time performing on the Hollywood Bowl stage.

Cyrus shared the news on social media, confirming the two dates while reflecting on her personal connection to the Hollywood Bowl.

“BASS PERSUADES live at the Hollywood Bowl. October 16th & 18th. With special guest @modelactriz,” Cyrus wrote.

“It’s been a while. The Hollywood Bowl is my favorite place to experience music and see my favorite artists. I’ve sat in those seats many times, but I’ve never taken the stage. LA is my heart and I’m bringing it home. See you there.”

In addition to announcing the Hollywood Bowl shows, Cyrus also revealed that her 10th studio album, *Bass Persuades*, will be released September 18.

An artist presale is scheduled to begin September 9 at 10 a.m., followed by the public on sale on September 11 at 10 a.m.
