# Reggae Land UK To Livestream Mainstage Concerts

## Summary

UK's Reggae Land festival, sold out for its July 31-Aug. 2 return to Milton Keynes, will livestream its main-stage sets across Facebook, Instagram and YouTube for fans who missed out on tickets.

### Key Points
- Expecting roughly 100,000 attendees across the sold-out weekend.
- Main stage streamed live both days, featuring Vybz Kartel, Shaggy, Beenie Man and others.
- A Friday date headlined by Burna Boy was added due to demand but will not be streamed.

Reggae Land returns to Milton Keynes National Bowl in England, July 31–Aug. 2, expecting some 100,000 guests across the weekend.

With both Saturday and Sunday sold out, fans who missed out on tickets can still watch the main stage action on a screen of their choosing, as the festival’s main stage will stream live across official channels all weekend, bringing Vybz Kartel, Shaggy, Beenie Man & Morgan Heritage’s debut as The King & The Royals, Barrington Levy and more to audiences worldwide.

Reggae Land’s official channels include Facebook, Instagram and YouTube, where two days of reggae and dancehall will be accessible to audiences around the world.

The stream covers all main stage performances across Saturday, Aug. 1, and Sunday, Aug. 2, beginning at 1 p.m. UK time and running into the evening.

First up on Saturday will be Jah 9 followed by Barrington Levy, Kranium, Konshens, Tarrus Riley, Beenie Man and Morgan Heritage, while Sunday begins with Mo’Kalamity and The Wizards before Inner Circle, Christopher Martin, Shaggy, Shenseea and Vybz Kartel all follow.

For orientation purposes: 1 p.m. in the UK will be 5 a.m. in Los Angeles/Vancouver; 7 a.m. in Jamaica; 8 a.m. in New York/Toronto; 12 p.m. in Accra; 1 p.m. in Lagos; 3 p.m. in Nairobi; and 4 p.m. in Dubai.

Reggae Land experienced the fastest sell-out in history of its 2026 edition, originally scheduled Aug. 1-2, 2026. To match this demand, promoters added Friday, July 31, as a third day that’ll be headlined by Burna Boy, which won’t be live streamed.
