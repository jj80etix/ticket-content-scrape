# Diana Ross Returns to Sin City with October Dates

## Summary

Diana Ross is returning to Las Vegas with new October dates. The article covers venue and on-sale details for the residency-style engagement.

### Key Points
- New October 2026 Las Vegas dates announced.
- Article covers venue and on-sale information for the shows.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/diana-moss.jpg)

The iconic R&B superstar Diana Ross is heading to the Sin City for three nights this October.

Ross confirmed three new performances of her “Diana in Motion” world tour at the Encore Theater inside Wynn Las Vegas on October 2, 3, and 4. Each show will begin at 8 p.m. This will mark her return to the theater after a sold-out trio of shows across Valentine’s Day weekend earlier this year.

Tickets for the newly-announced performances are available now via Ross’ [official website.](https://www.dianaross.com)

As previously announced, Ross will head back to North America in August, kicking-off the run at Casino Rama Resort in Ontario on August 14. From there, she’ll appear in cities like Stamford, Atlantic City, Santa Barbara, Costa Mesa, Saratoga, and Napa. before the Vegas shows.

Ross, known as the lead singer of The Supremes, has worked to create a successful solo career with timeless hits like “I’m Coming Out,” “Upside Down,” and “Ain’t No Mountain High Enough.”
