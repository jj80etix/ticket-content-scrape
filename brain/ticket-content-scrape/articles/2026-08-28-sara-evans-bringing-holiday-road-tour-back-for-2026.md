# Sara Evans Bringing Holiday Road Tour Back for 2026

## Summary

Sara Evans announced her 2026 Holiday Road Tour, a 10-city December run with support from Braden Hull. The tour opens December 2 in Cranston, RI, and closes December 18 in Des Moines, IA.

### Key Points
- 10 cities including Newton, NJ and Green Bay, WI.
- Braden Hull joins as support act.
- Framed around holiday themes of family and tradition.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Sara-Evans-Holiday-Road-Tour.webp)

Sara Evans is heading back on the road this holiday season, announcing a new run of December concerts as part of her 2026 Holiday Road Tour.

The Grammy-nominated country singer will play 10 cities during the limited engagement, with Braden Hull joining the tour as support. The run is scheduled to open December 2 at the Historic Park Theatre & Event Center in Cranston, Rhode Island, and conclude December 18 at Hoyt Sherman Place in Des Moines, Iowa.

Along the way, Evans will make stops in cities including Newton, New Jersey, and Green Bay, Wisconsin, as she brings her annual holiday production to audiences around the country.

Evans said the tour is centered on the traditions, memories and gatherings that come with the time of year.

“There’s always something so magical and special about Christmastime and the holidays every year!” Evans said. “I love getting to hit the road with my band and bring our versions of your favorite holiday songs to your hometown.”

She added that the concerts are intended to bring audiences together while celebrating the themes she associates with the season.

“The Holiday Road Tour is really about celebrating all of those things that make this time of year so special: family, tradition, memories, music, and just being together,” Evans said. “I hope people can come spend the evening with us, sing along, have a few laughs, and leave feeling a little more in the Christmas spirit.”
