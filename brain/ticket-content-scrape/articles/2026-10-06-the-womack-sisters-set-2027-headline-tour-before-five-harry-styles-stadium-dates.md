# The Womack Sisters Set 2027 Headline Tour Before Five Harry Styles Stadium Dates

## Summary

Soul trio The Womack Sisters announced a 2027 touring expansion: a 10-date North American headline run (Jan. 16–30), an 11-show European leg (Feb.–Mar.), and five stadium dates opening for Harry Styles at Chicago's Soldier Field (May–June), following their August debut album on Daptone Records.

### Key Points
- 10-date North American headline run, Jan. 16–30, 2027, New York to Minneapolis; several December warm-up shows already sold out.
- 11-show European tour, Feb. 17–Mar. 3, 2027, including two London dates.
- Five stadium dates opening for Harry Styles at Soldier Field, Chicago (May 28–June 5, 2027), part of his 40-date "Together, Together" tour.
- Follows the Aug. 14 release of their self-titled debut album (Daptone Records).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Womack-Sisters-North-America-2027.webp)

The Womack Sisters are extending their rapidly growing touring schedule into 2027, announcing a 10-date North American headline run that will take the soul trio from New York to Minneapolis in January before a European tour and five stadium dates supporting Harry Styles later in the year.

The newly announced run opens January 16 at Webster Hall in New York City and includes stops in Nashville, Charlotte, Philadelphia, Washington, Boston, Toronto, Detroit and Madison before closing January 30 at First Avenue in Minneapolis.

The January dates follow a six-show California run in December that includes two nights at Bimbo’s 365 Club in San Francisco, two at the Peppermint Club in West Hollywood, San Diego’s Music Box and the El Rey Theatre in Los Angeles. The two San Francisco dates and both Peppermint Club shows are already listed as sold out.

After the January North American dates, the sisters head overseas for an 11-show European run beginning February 17 in Manchester. The itinerary includes two London shows along with stops in the Netherlands, France, Belgium and Germany before wrapping March 3 at Berlin’s Lido. The group is also scheduled to appear at Knoxville’s Big Ears Festival in April.
