# Garth Brooks Announces 10-Show 2027 Irish Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks announced a 10-concert "Irish Tour 2027" across Ireland and Northern Ireland, his first return since five sold-out Croke Park shows in 2022. The run spans five cities in May and June 2027.

### Key Points
- 10 shows across Belfast, Galway, Kilkenny, Limerick, and Cork, May 21–June 19, 2027.
- First Ireland dates since 2022's five Dublin shows at Croke Park.
- Itinerary includes four GAA stadiums plus Belfast's Boucher Road Playing Fields.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Garth-Brooks-Irish-Tour.webp)

Garth Brooks has announced a 10-concert tour across Ireland and Northern Ireland in 2027, with performances scheduled in Belfast, Galway, Kilkenny, Limerick and Cork.

The country music star will play two shows in each city during May and June, marking his first return to Ireland since five sold-out concerts at Dublin’s Croke Park in September 2022. The newly announced “Irish Tour 2027” will begin May 21 at Boucher Road Playing Fields in Belfast and conclude June 19 at SuperValu Páirc Uí Chaoimh in Cork.

“This has been a dream of mine since the first time we played Ireland. Once you get a taste of how the Irish love music, you can’t play there enough!” Brooks said in the announcement.

Rather than returning to Dublin for another extended engagement, Brooks will take the 2027 tour to five different locations across the island. The itinerary includes four Gaelic Athletic Association stadiums, along with the outdoor concert grounds at Boucher Road in Belfast.

The tour will move through Galway’s Pearse Stadium on May 29 and 30, followed by UPMC Nowlan Park in Kilkenny on June 4 and 5. Brooks will then perform at the TUS Gaelic Grounds in Limerick on June 11 and 12 before closing the run with two concerts in Cork on June 18 and 19.

Brooks has continued to expand his touring calendar throughout 2026. His “Blame It All On My Roots” arena tour, which launched in August, has included multiple-night engagements across the United States and Canada.

The singer recently [added a third performance at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/garth-brooks-adds-third-hamilton-concert-to-blame-it-all-on-my-roots-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), extending that December stop to three consecutive days. Before traveling to Ontario, Brooks is scheduled to perform at T-Mobile Center in Kansas City. The tour will then move to Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle for four concerts. He is also scheduled to headline the Stagecoach Festival in California in April 2027.
