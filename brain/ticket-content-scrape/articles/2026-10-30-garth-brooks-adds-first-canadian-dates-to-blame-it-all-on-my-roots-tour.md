# Garth Brooks Adds First Canadian Dates to Blame It All On My Roots Tour

## Summary

Garth Brooks added his first-ever Canadian dates to the Blame It All On My Roots Arena Tour, with two shows at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario (Dec. 4-5), marking his first Hamilton performance since 2016 and the 30th anniversary of his 1996 debut there.

### Key Points
- Hamilton dates are the tour's first December stop
- Precedes stops in Kansas City (Nov. 6-7) and Seattle (Nov. 13-14)
- Article links to prior TicketNews coverage of Brooks halting his Kansas City onsale over bot-driven demand issues (not elaborated in this piece)

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Garth-Brooks-Hamilton-ON.webp)

Garth Brooks will bring his Blame It All on My Roots Arena Tour to Canada for the first time with concerts at TD Coliseum in Hamilton, Ontario, on December 4 and 5, 2026.

The two-night engagement marks Brooks’ first performances in Hamilton since 2016. It is also the tour’s first December stop. Brooks’ return coincides with the 30th anniversary of his first Hamilton performance. He made his debut in the city in the fall of 1996 at the same arena, then called Copps Coliseum. He last played the venue in the spring of 2016, when it was known as FirstOntario Centre.

Before arriving in Hamilton, Brooks is scheduled to perform at T-Mobile Center in Kansas City, Missouri, on November 6 and 7. In Seattle, Brooks will play Climate Pledge Arena on November 13 and 14.
