# Legends Global To Manage Mesa Amphitheatre And Mesa Convention Center In Arizona

## Summary

The City of Mesa, Arizona and Legends Global announced a five-year public-private partnership for Legends Global to manage operations, marketing, and strategy at Mesa Amphitheatre and the Mesa Convention Center, with the city retaining ownership of both venues.

### Key Points
- Five-year management deal covers Mesa Amphitheatre (~5,000 seats) and the Mesa Convention Center (~40,000 sq ft of event space, 300+ events/year).
- Legends Global will pursue upgrades aimed at attracting larger concerts, conventions, and events.
- A venue-management/operations deal, not a ticketing-platform or policy story.

## Transcript

![Current image: MesaCampus Picture](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MesaCampus-Picture-1024x682.jpg)

Mesa Amphitheatre and Mesa Convention Center campus photo courtesy Legends Global

The City of Mesa and Legends Global announced a five-year public-private partnership under which Legends Global will manage both Mesa Amphitheatre and the Mesa Convention Center, overseeing operations, marketing and strategy for the two downtown venues.

The partnership with Legends Global is expected to support improvements to the Mesa Amphitheatre and the adjacent Mesa Convention Center, positioning the venues for long-term growth and the ability to compete for larger concerts, conventions and events across the Valley. The City of Mesa will retain ownership of both facilities.

Built in the late 1970s, Mesa Amphitheatre seats approximately 5,000 and has been a regional draw for concerts and community events. Under the new agreement, Legends Global will work with the city on an upgrade schedule designed to elevate revenue potential, service levels and overall experience for artists and attendees.

![MesaAmp With Chairs](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/MesaAmp-With-Chairs.jpg)

Mesa Amphitheatre courtesy of Legends Global

The Mesa Convention Center is the largest event venue in the East Valley, offering nearly 40,000 square feet of meeting and exhibit space, including a 19,000-square-foot main exhibit hall, 26 breakout rooms and 40,000 square feet of outdoor function space.

The facility hosts more than 300 functions annually, from conventions and trade shows to banquets and community gatherings. Legends Global will bring its operational expertise to elevate the convention center’s service levels and event experience, complementing the improvements planned for the outdoor venue and further position downtown Mesa as a destination for meetings and events.

“Mesa Amphitheatre has a rich history of hosting unforgettable shows and City events, and the acts that have graced its stage over the years have left a lasting mark on this community,” stated Mesa Mayor Mark Freeman in the announcement. “This public-private partnership builds on that legacy. By tapping into Legends Global’s relationships and operational expertise, we can bring more people to downtown Mesa and help elevate these venues while serving our community for years to come.”

“Our goal is to expand the quantity, quality and diversity of the entertainment we bring to Mesa, which speaks to all segments of the Mesa community,” added Josh Kritzler, President, North American Venues & Content, Legends Global. “We want residents and the broader region to feel a sense of connection to and pride in both the convention center and the amphitheater. The region has a lot of positive momentum, which is energizing, and we are confident in our ability to be a key partner in achieving the vision for the city, its residents and visitors.”
