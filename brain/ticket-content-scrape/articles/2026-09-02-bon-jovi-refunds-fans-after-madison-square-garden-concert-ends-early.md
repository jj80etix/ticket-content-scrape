# Bon Jovi Refunds Fans After Madison Square Garden Concert Ends Early

## Summary

Bon Jovi is issuing full refunds — via Ticketmaster, automatically within 14 business days to the original payment method — to fans who attended the July 23 Madison Square Garden show that ended after roughly 90 minutes/15 songs (down from a typical ~19-song, longer set) when a sinus infection affected Jon Bon Jovi's voice; Train's Pat Monahan, attending as a fan, stepped in to help finish "Livin' on a Prayer." The refund announcement came over a month after the show and explicitly covers only primary-ticket purchasers; TicketNews notes no details were given for fans who bought via resale platforms.

### Key Points
- Bon Jovi is refunding all fans from the July 23 MSG show (8th of a 9-night residency), cut to ~90 minutes/15 songs after Jon Bon Jovi's sinus infection affected his voice.
- Refunds process automatically through Ticketmaster within 14 business days to the original payment method — no action needed from primary-ticket buyers.
- No replacement performance was arranged, cited as a scheduling/logistics issue.
- Announcement covers primary-ticket purchasers only; resale-platform buyers' refund treatment is unaddressed in the notice.
- The refund decision came over a month after the shortened show, not immediately.

### Highlights
- Refund scope: primary Ticketmaster purchasers only, automatic within 14 business days; resale buyers' treatment unstated.
- Show was cut to ~90 minutes/15 songs (vs. a typical ~19-song set) due to Jon Bon Jovi's sinus infection; Train's Pat Monahan filled in on vocals for part of "Livin' on a Prayer."
- Refund decision announced more than a month after the July 23 incident.

### Industry Problem
Mid-show cancellations/early endings create ambiguous refund entitlement — especially for the growing share of fans who buy via resale/secondary channels rather than primary — and the month-long gap between incident and refund decision reflects how ad hoc post-event refund policy often is industry-wide.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes a manual, delayed, primary-only refund process handled by the ticketing platform after the fact. A platform opportunity is a pre-defined, published partial/early-termination refund policy (e.g., a minimum-set-length threshold) with automatic triggering and clear treatment for resale-purchased tickets, reducing both resolution time and the "primary-only" gap that leaves secondary buyers uncertain — also useful grounding for Etix Assistant's fan-support responses.

Bon Jovi is issuing full refunds to fans who attended the band’s shortened July 23 concert at Madison Square Garden after Jon Bon Jovi’s health issues forced the performance to end early.

Ticketmaster notified primary ticket buyers that refunds would be automatically returned to their original method of payment within 14 business days. The band was unable to arrange a replacement performance because of scheduling and logistical issues, according to the notice.

The July 23 concert was the eighth night of Bon Jovi’s nine-show run at Madison Square Garden. The performance lasted approximately 90 minutes and included about 15 songs, several fewer than the roughly 19-song sets performed during other dates in the residency.

Jon Bon Jovi had been dealing with a sinus infection that affected his ability to sing. During the concert, Train frontman Pat Monahan joined the band for “Livin’ on a Prayer” and took over the lead vocals midway through the song.
