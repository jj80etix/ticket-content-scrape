# F1 music programming raises Azerbaijan’s touring profile

## Summary

iTicket Production used the Formula 1 Azerbaijan Grand Prix weekend to stage major concerts (Calvin Harris, Katy Perry) and a new open-air electronic-music offering, calling it a statement of intent to position Baku as a stop on international touring routes.

### Key Points
- Calvin Harris and Katy Perry played Baku Crystal Hall (25,000-cap) Sept. 24–25, ahead of the Sept. 26 Grand Prix.
- New "APEX After Dark Open Air" electronic program added this year with FOMO Baku, featuring ANOTR, Jamie Jones, Mahmut Orhan.
- iTicket has previously booked Anyma, Martin Garrix, Will Smith, J Balvin for past Grand Prix weekends.
- Goal: elevate Azerbaijan's profile as a recurring stop on international touring circuits.

## F1 music programming raises Azerbaijan’s touring profile

iTicket Production says it stands as a "statement" of intent as they vie to enhance the market's appeal for international routes

![Calvin Harris](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Calvin-Harris-©-Conor-McDonnell.png)

Calvin Harris © Conor McDonnell

After hosting Calvin Harris and Katy Perry as part of the Formula 1 Azerbaijan Grand Prix weekend, iTicket Production says the programming stands as a “statement” of intent to raise the market’s profile as they vie to become a stop on international routes.

The two global stars delivered shows as part of the Grand Prix weekend’s official programme, taking place at the 25,000-capacity Baku Crystal Hall on 24-25 September ahead of the race on Saturday (26 September). In years past, iTicket has also brought forth names including Anyma, Martin Garrix, Will Smith, and J Balvin for the event.

In addition, this year saw the expansion of electronic music programming to complement the sporting activity, as iTicket partnered with local nightclub FOMO Baku to curate an open-air offering, APEX After Dark Open Air, at a bespoke waterfront venue.

The purpose-built setting was arranged outside of the club, and set the stage for headliners ANOTR, Jamie Jones, and Mahmut Orhan, along with appearances from People Like Us and DJ Tennis, across the race weekend.

For iTicket Production, the growth of the programming underscored both the company and the market’s growing ability to host events of this scale, from electronic music to A-list acts.

> “We want to allow Azerbaijan and the wider region to become a regular part of the international touring map”

“This weekend was an important statement about what Baku can now deliver as an international entertainment destination,” says Mehmet Uğur Aydın, iTicket executive partner. “Within the same few days, we were able to work across intimate electronic music culture, open-air events, and major global headline productions.”

Increasingly, the company is working to develop the market and its surrounding [Central Asian markets](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/08/the-caucasus-the-next-frontier-for-touring/) as a suitable touring route rather than one-off stops around sports events. Last year, the company helped bring Justin Timberlake for a major show at Baku’s 68,000-capacity Olympic Stadium, the biggest commercial show in the venue’s history.

“Our objective with iTicket Production is not simply to bring international artists into the region,” he adds. “We want to build the infrastructure, partnerships and production standards that allow Azerbaijan and the wider region to become a regular part of the international touring map.”

Having executed the vision with their temporary space, iTicket and FOMO look uniquely positioned to do so. Since it opened, FOMO has established itself as a destination for electronic music programming in the region, with influential names like Cincity and Joseph Capriati coming through later this year.

In addition to being the country’s ticketing leader, iTicket’s portfolio also spans small- to large-scale concert promotion and production across Azerbaijan, Türkiye and Central Asia.
