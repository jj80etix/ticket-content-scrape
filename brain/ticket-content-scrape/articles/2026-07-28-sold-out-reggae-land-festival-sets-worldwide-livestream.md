# Sold-Out Reggae Land Festival Sets Worldwide Livestream

## Summary

Reggae Land Festival sold out its live event and will offer a worldwide livestream so fans who couldn't get tickets can still watch. The announcement is a standard festival capacity/access story with no ticketing-platform or industry-structure detail.

### Key Points
- Festival sold out its in-person tickets ahead of the event.
- Organizers added a global livestream option for fans without tickets.
- No pricing, platform, or ticketing-technology details reported.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/reggae-land.jpg)

Reggae Land is taking its sold-out festival worldwide.

The two-day reggae and dancehall event will livestream every performance from its main stage across the festival’s official [Facebook](https://facebook.com/@ReggaeLandUK), [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/reggaelanduk) and [YouTube](https://youtube.com/@ReggaeLandFestival) channels on Saturday, Aug. 1, and Sunday, Aug. 2. +Broadcasts begin at 1 p.m. BST each day and continue into the evening, giving fans around the globe access to sets from Vybz Kartel, Shaggy, Beenie Man, Barrington Levy, Shenseea and more.

Saturday’s main-stage schedule opens with Jah9, followed by Barrington Levy, Kranium, Konshens, Tarrus Riley and Beenie Man. The day will also feature the debut performance from The King & The Royals, the new project from Morgan Heritage.

Sunday begins with Mo’Kalamity and The Wizards before Inner Circle, Christopher Martin, Shaggy, Shenseea and Vybz Kartel take the stage.
