# Garth Brooks Expands 2026 ‘Blame It All On My Roots’ Arena Tour With Two Salt Lake City Shows

## Summary

Garth Brooks added two Salt Lake City shows (Oct. 9-10, Delta Center) to his 2026 "Blame It All On My Roots" Arena Tour, making it the sixth market on the run. Tickets go on sale Sept. 18 at a uniform $154 price plus taxes.

### Key Points
- Salt Lake City becomes the tour's sixth market; tour launched earlier this year in Indianapolis and Denver.
- Also includes stops in Rosemont, IL (4 shows), Washington DC and Orlando before Salt Lake City.
- Uniform ticket price of $154 plus taxes; on-sale Sept. 18 at 10 a.m. MT with an online waiting room.
- Production features Brooks' in-the-round "halo" LED staging and the return of his Drum Pod.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Garth-Brooks-Salt-Lake.-City.webp)

Garth Brooks has added two Salt Lake City concerts to his 2026 “Blame It All On My Roots” Arena Tour.

The country star will perform at the Delta Center on Friday, October 9, and Saturday, October 10. The newly announced dates make Salt Lake City the sixth market on the arena tour. Brooks launched the tour earlier this year with performances in Indianapolis and Denver.

**| RELATED:**[**Garth Brooks Reveals Pair of Shows in Washington, D.C. on ‘Blame It All On My Roots’ Tour**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/garth-brooks-pair-shows-washington-dc/) **|**

Before heading to Salt Lake City, Brooks will perform four concerts at Allstate Arena near Chicago from September 10 through September 13. The schedule also includes two shows at Capital One Arena in Washington, D.C., on September 18 and 19, followed by performances at Orlando’s Kia Center on September 25 and 26.

The tour marks Brooks’ return to the type of indoor venues that helped establish him as one of country music’s biggest touring performers. He performs in the round on a 360-degree stage surrounded by LED screens known as “the halo.” The production also features the return of Brooks’ Drum Pod, a staging element associated with his earlier arena tours.

**| RELATED:**[**Garth Brooks to Play Four Nights in Chicago on ‘Blame It All On My Roots’ Tour**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/garth-brooks-four-nights-chicago-2026-tour/) **|**

Tickets go on sale Friday, September 18, at 10 a.m. MT. The venue’s online waiting room will open 30 minutes before the sale begins. Tickets will be offered at a uniform price of $154, plus applicable taxes, according to the tour announcement.

For more information and additional details, fans of the “Friends in Low Places” singer can visit [garthbrooks.com](http://garthbrooks.com/).
