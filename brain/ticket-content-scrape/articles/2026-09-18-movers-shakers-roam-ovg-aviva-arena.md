# Movers & Shakers: ROAM, OVG, Aviva Arena

## Summary

IQ Magazine's latest industry movers roundup covers new appointments at ROAM (Maxie Gedge as Head of Artist Delivery), Oak View Group, and Aviva Arena, plus a key departure at Motorpoint Arena Nottingham and an addition at KOKO.

### Key Points
- Maxie Gedge joins ROAM as Head of Artist Delivery from the "recorded side" of the industry.
- Additional appointments noted at Oak View Group, Aviva Arena, and KOKO.
- One departure flagged at the UK's Motorpoint Arena Nottingham.
- Roundup format aggregates several smaller personnel moves across the live-music sector.
