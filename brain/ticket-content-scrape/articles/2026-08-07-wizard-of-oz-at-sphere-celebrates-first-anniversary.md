# Wizard of Oz at Sphere celebrates first anniversary

## Summary

The Wizard of Oz at Sphere in Las Vegas marked its first anniversary, having drawn more than 3.6 million guests and over $450m in ticket sales since its August 2025 debut; the venue is running in-venue anniversary activations Aug. 10–Sept. 30.

### Key Points
- Over 3.6 million guests and $450m+ in ticket sales in the immersive experience's first year (850+ screenings).
- Guests have traveled from all 50 US states and 140+ countries.
- Anniversary activations run Aug. 10 through Sept. 30, 2026.

## Transcript

![TheTicketingBusiness News](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TBN-LOGO-reversed-white.png)

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Wizard of Oz at Sphere in Las Vegas has celebrated its first anniversary after opening in August 2025.**

Since then, the immersive experience has welcomed more than 3.6 million guests and has made more than $450m (£335m/€390m) in ticket sales.

“The response to The Wizard of Oz at Sphere continues to exceed expectations, with millions of guests from around the world experiencing the film in a transformative new way,” said Jen Koester, president and chief operating officer at Sphere.

“It’s been rewarding to see first-time visitors and lifelong fans alike create new memories with The Wizard of Oz at Sphere, and our anniversary celebration is both a thank you to those who have made the experience a success and an invitation for new guests to experience the wonder for themselves.”

To celebrate the anniversary, guests attending The Wizard of Oz at Sphere can look forward to exclusive in-venue anniversary activations running from August 10 to September 30.

“Since its debut in August 2025, The Wizard of Oz at Sphere has become a global destination, with guests traveling from all 50 states and over 140 countries to immerse themselves with Dorothy and friends in the Land of Oz in a way only Sphere can provide,” read a statement from Sphere Entertainment Co.

“Since opening, the experience has been shown more than 850 times, transporting audiences through Sphere’s 160,000-square-foot LED media plane, Sphere Immersive Sound’s 167,000 speakers, haptic seats and 4D effects.”
