# WME Hires Agents Aleks Ljubojevic & Sophia Simon

## Summary

WME expanded its contemporary music team by hiring agents Aleks Ljubojevic and Sophia Simon to grow its electronic and international rosters. Ljubojevic brings Swedish House Mafia member Steve Angello's solo career with him.

### Key Points
- Ljubojevic, based in London, joins from Ace Agency and brings Steve Angello as a client
- Simon joins from So.Connected, the Brazilian-market artist agency she founded in 2024
- Simon's prior clients include Martin Garrix, NE-YO, Jason Mraz and Sean Paul
- Hires announced by Kirk Sommer, WME's Global Head of Music

## Transcript

![WME Aleks Ljubojevic and Sophia Simon](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WME-Aleks-Ljubojevic-and-Sophia-Simon-scaled.jpg)

Aleks Ljubojevic and Sophia Simon

WME has expanded its contemporary music team with the hire of agents Aleks Ljubojevic and Sophia Simon. The news was announced today by Kirk Sommer, Global Head of Music at WME.

Ljubojevic is bringing along Steve Angello for representation in Europe for his solo career. The electronic artist and producer rose to prominence as a member of Swedish House Mafia and continues to perform and record with the group.

Ljubojevic is based in London and reports to Stephanie LaFera, Partner and Head of EMD at WME. He most recently worked at Ace Agency in Amsterdam, where his clients included Martin Garrix, Sam Feldt, Don Diablo and others in electronic music. Today’s announcement from WME notes that he will focus on expanding the agency’s “electronic music roster and driving growth opportunities for both emerging and established talent.”

Simon is joining WME from So.Connected, an agency she founded in 2024 focused on developing international artists and music projects in the Brazilian market. Over the past few years she’s represented artists including Martin Garrix, NE-YO, Jason Mraz, Maddix, Sean Paul, Alesso, and Soulja Boy, as well as working with Ultra Records.

Simon has also spent time managing artists including co-managing Brazilian singer-songwriter and actress Giulia Be and working as a day-to-day manager at Milk & Honey. Her resume also includes “working in project coordination with GK Athletec Events assigned to The Coca-Cola Company’s Global Hospitality Team, supporting programs at the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022, the Paris 2024 Olympics, and Coachella 2024.” She is based in Spain and reports to Rob Markus, Senior Partner and Head of International at WME.

“Aleks and Sophia both have strong track records of developing talent, building international touring businesses, and creating opportunities for artists across key markets worldwide,” Sommer said in a statement. “The future of music is global, and their additions to our team continue to strengthen WME’s position as the leading agency for long-term global success.”
