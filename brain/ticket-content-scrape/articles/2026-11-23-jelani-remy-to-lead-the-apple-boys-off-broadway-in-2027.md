# Jelani Remy to Lead ‘The Apple Boys’ Off-Broadway in 2027

## Summary

"The Apple Boys" returns Off-Broadway in February 2027 at The Duke on 42nd Street, with Jelani Remy reprising his original 2018 role, running through June 18, 2027.

### Key Points
- Performances begin Feb. 16, 2027; official opening Feb. 25; the run continues through June 18.
- Jelani Remy and Teddy Yudain return to roles from the original 2018 production.
- Stage Time joins as co-producer alongside Hawkins, LaChanze, Larry Bomback and AJ Epstein.
- Tickets are on sale now via the show's official website.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Apple-Boys-Off-Broadway.webp)

Jelani Remy will lead the cast of “The Apple Boys” when the musical returns to Off-Broadway in February 2027, reprising a role he previously performed in the show’s original production.

The musical will begin performances February 16 at The Duke on 42nd Street in New York City, with an official opening scheduled for February 25. The limited engagement is scheduled to run through June 18, 2027.

Remy and Yudain are returning to roles they played in the musical’s original 2018 Off-Broadway production at HERE Arts Center. That production featured Jonothon Lyons as Warren and Amanda Ryan Paige as Hank.

Joining Remy as Jack are Josh Breckinridge as Warren, Morgan Siobhan Green as Hank and Teddy Yudain as Nathan. The newly announced company also includes Rachel Kay as the understudy for Hank, Eric Shawn as the understudy for Jack and Eric Peters as the understudy for Nathan and Warren.

Set in Gilded Age Coney Island, “The Apple Boys” follows Jack, the grandson of Johnny Appleseed, as he and his working-class friends attempt to save the family orchard from a landlord threatening to take it over. Their plan involves entering a singing competition in hopes of winning enough money to protect the property.

The musical features a book by Jonothon Lyons and music and lyrics by Ben Bonnema. David Alpert directs the production, with choreography by Marc Kimelman.

The creative team also includes Adam Koch on scenic design, Johanna Pan on costume design, Rui Rita on lighting design and Alex Neumann on sound design. Rona Siddiqui serves as music supervisor, with vocal arrangements by Matt Gallagher.

Stage Time has also joined the production as a co-producer. The organization, founded by Broadway performer PJ Adzima and entrepreneur Yuriy Pavlish, joins producers Hawkins, LaChanze, Larry Bomback and AJ Epstein..
