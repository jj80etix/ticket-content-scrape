# Live Nation Asia deepens concert tourism strategy with IHG deal

## Summary

Live Nation Asia has partnered with InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG) to bundle Live Nation-promoted concerts in Hong Kong and Taiwan with hotel stays and IHG One Rewards perks, extending its concert-tourism strategy in Greater China. It follows an earlier Live Nation Asia tie-up with Trip.com and reflects broader growth in fans traveling internationally to see touring artists.

### Key Points
- New Live Nation Asia x IHG partnership (announced Aug 17, 2026, Shanghai) bundles concerts with hotel stays and exclusive ticket giveaways for IHG One Rewards members.
- Builds on a prior Live Nation Asia x Trip.com concert-tourism package deal covering Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Thailand, South Korea, and mainland China.
- Cited market context: Lady Gaga's 2025 Singapore shows generated ~S$150M in tourism revenue; Taylor Swift/Coldplay's 2024 Singapore runs generated S$350–500M.
- Music tourism market projected to reach US$400.5B by 2032 (Credence Research).
- Business-development/travel-bundling story rather than a ticketing product or platform change — no clear match to tracked research questions.

## Transcript

A new partnership with the hotel chain is the latest bid to cater to fans travelling for concerts, amid heightened focus on music tourism around the industry

Live Nation Asia has expanded its footprint in the Greater China market through a new partnership with Intercontinental Hotel Group (IHG), in the latest tie-up that aims to provide combined live event, travel, and accommodation offerings.

Announced this week (17 August) in Shanghai, the collaboration will see Live Nation‑promoted concerts in Hong Kong and Taiwan packaged with stays at IHG Hotels and Resorts, with IHG One Rewards members gaining access to exclusive ticket giveaways, curated travel‑and‑show bundles, and promotional offers across IHG’s digital channels.

James Dick, EVP of partnerships and media at Live Nation Asia, said the partnership reflects how touring behaviour is shifting. “More fans in Asia are choosing to travel to experience their favourite artists live,” he said.

“For these fans, the experience extends beyond the show itself — it’s about the journey to the destination, where they stay and the memories they create along the way.” He added that pairing concerts with hospitality benefits creates a “more complete fan experience” and strengthens the company’s regional offering.

> “More fans in Asia are choosing to travel to experience their favourite artists live”

For Live Nation, the deal taps into a fast‑growing segment of fans who travel specifically to see artists perform — a trend that has accelerated across Asia’s cross‑border touring circuit.

In recent years, high-profile concerts and live events have been a major draw for tourists in Asia. In 2025, Lady Gaga’s four Singapore National Stadium shows generated up to [S$150 million (€100m) in tourism revenue](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/07/lady-gagas-singapore-shows-provide-tourism-boost/), while in 2024, Taylor Swift and Coldplay’s respective six-show runs in the country generated a tourism boost of between S$350-500m (€232.6-332.3m), according to  *The Straits Times*.

It follows a year after Live Nation Asia is joined forces with travel service provider Trip.com Group to offer concert tourism packages in the Asia-Pacific region. This collaboration combined presale access with flights, hotels, and local experiences in bundled concert-focused trips in Hong Kong, Macau, Singapore, Thailand, South Korea, and mainland China.

This partnership came on the heels of a report from by Credence Research that signals the music tourism market is on target to [reach US$400.5 billion (€342.2bn) by 2032](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/10/music-tourism-tipped-to-reach-400-5bn-by-2032/).
