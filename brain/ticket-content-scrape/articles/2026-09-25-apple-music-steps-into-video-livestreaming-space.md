# Apple Music steps into video livestreaming space

## Summary

Apple Music will livestream video from Goldenvoice's one-time electronic festival Head Trip (Oct. 10–11, Indio, CA) for the first time, expanding beyond its existing audio-streaming footprint into festival video livestreaming. The lineup includes Calvin Harris, Swedish House Mafia and Skrillex.

### Key Points
- Marks Apple Music's first video festival livestream, streaming main-stage sets across both days.
- Head Trip is a one-time-only Goldenvoice event held on the Coachella grounds.
- Article content is largely paywalled beyond the announcement.
