# Live Nation Takes Majority Stake in Three Major Prague Venues

## Summary

Live Nation is acquiring a majority stake in three major Prague venues -- the 20,000-capacity O2 arena, O2 Universum and Forum Karlín -- partnering with existing owner PPF Group and planning substantial long-term investment across the properties.

### Key Points
- The deal remains subject to regulatory approval and customary closing conditions.
- The venues will stay open to non-Live Nation artists/promoters, and existing management teams remain in place.
- The move extends Live Nation's Central/Eastern Europe expansion, following recent moves into Romania and Slovakia.
- The region has posted record touring numbers recently, including Iron Maiden, Eric Clapton and Metallica dates.

### Highlights
- Live Nation is set to take majority ownership of three of Prague's leading venues (O2 arena, O2 Universum, Forum Karlín).
- The deal is pending regulatory approval.
- This follows a pattern of Live Nation venue-ownership expansion across CEE markets, including Romania and Slovakia.

### Industry Problem
Live Nation continues consolidating venue ownership in fast-growing European touring markets, reinforcing the vertically-integrated promoter-venue-ticketing model that independents compete against for access and terms.

### Proposed Solution
No direct product build; this is a monitor/evolve signal on where Live Nation's venue footprint (and associated ticketing exclusivity) is expanding next, relevant to Etix's read on international market access and competitive openings.

Live Nation is making a major venue play in Prague, signing an agreement to acquire a majority stake in three of the Czech capital’s leading live entertainment properties.

The deal covers the 20,000-capacity O2 arena, 5,000-capacity O2 Universum and 4,000-capacity Forum Karlín, giving the global promoter and venue operator a significant new foothold in one of Central Europe’s busiest live entertainment markets.

Live Nation is partnering with PPF Group, which has overseen the venues for years. The companies said they plan substantial long-term investment across the properties, including venue upgrades and improvements to the fan and artist experience. The venues will remain available to artists, promoters and event organizers outside Live Nation, while their existing management teams will stay in place.

“Prague has been one of our most important markets for many years,” Paul Antonio, President of Live Nation EMEA, said in a press release. “We’ve invested here by building a strong local team, developing long-term partnerships and bringing major artists and tours to Czech fans. This partnership is the natural next step.

The region has also been drawing increasingly large audiences for international tours. “For more than two decades, O2 arena and O2 Universum have played a central role in Czechia’s cultural, sporting and social life,” Didier Stoessel, co-CEO of PPF Group, added in the release. “This investment represents the next stage in their development and will help ensure they continue to grow and evolve for many years to come.”
