# Fall Out Boy Announces 2027 ‘Twilight Of The Punks’ World Tour

## Summary

Fall Out Boy announced "Twilight of the Punks," a 36-date 2027 world tour across Europe, the UK and North America, marking the band's 25th anniversary. The Live Nation-promoted trek opens May 17 at Prague's O2 Arena, includes a June 5 stadium date at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, and is paired with the release of a new single, "ACES."

### Key Points
- 36 dates: 15-date Europe/UK leg (May 17 Prague O2 Arena to June 13 Łódź) then North America from July.
- A Day To Remember and Neck Deep support on all European/UK dates.
- Promoted by Live Nation; coincides with the band's 25th-anniversary milestone and new single "ACES."

Fall Out Boy is set for a massive 2027, with the band announcing the “Twilight of the Punks” world tour — their biggest tour yet. The tour will span 36 dates, with stops at arenas and stadiums across Europe, the UK, and North America.

Promoted by Live Nation, the “Twilight of the Punks” tour kicks off May 17 at Prague’s O2 Arena, launching a 15-date European and UK leg before heading to North America in July. Spanning 11 countries, the European and UK run will take the band to arenas in Budapest, Milan, Madrid, Lyon, Brussels, and Mönchengladbach, then to Glasgow, Dublin, and Manchester. Highlights include a massive June 5 performance at London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium, with additional shows in Amsterdam, Paris, and Berlin before the leg wraps June 13 at Atlas Arena in Łódź, Poland. A Day To Remember and Neck Deep will join as special guests on all European and UK dates. See full tour routing below.

This year also marks a major milestone as Fall Out Boy celebrates 25 years as a band, and they are kicking off their highly-anticipated next chapter with the release of “ACES,” produced and mixed by longtime collaborator Neil Avron, giving fans a taste of what’s to come. The new track channels the raw energy, infectious hooks, and unmistakable spirit of the band’s early days, delivering a nostalgic nod to the sound that helped establish the band as one of pop-punk’s most influential acts.
