# CMC Rocks QLD Sets 2027 Dates for 20th Anniversary Blowout

## Summary

Australia's CMC Rocks QLD festival will return to Willowbank Raceway March 17-21, 2027, for a 20th-anniversary edition, following a 2026 edition that drew more than 25,000 fans and $16M in economic impact for Queensland.

### Key Points
- 20th-anniversary edition confirmed at Willowbank Raceway; full lineup to be revealed later in 2026.
- Festival is already confirmed to remain at Willowbank through 2028, backed by the Queensland Government.
- 2026 edition drew 25,000+ fans and generated over $16M for the local economy.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/cmc-rocks.jpg)

CMC Rocks QLD is ready to celebrate two decades of boots, beers and blockbuster country music.

Australia’s biggest country festival will return to Willowbank Raceway from March 17-21, 2027, marking the event’s 20th year. The festival will open exclusively to campers on Wednesday and Thursday before the main action kicks into gear.

The full lineup will be revealed later this year, but organizers are already promising an anniversary edition built to honor the festival’s past while delivering plenty of new memories.

“We’ve been hard at work cooking up a 20th anniversary edition of the festival that is truly unforgettable,” Festival Director Jeremy Dylan said.

Launched in Thredbo in 2008, CMC Rocks has traveled through the Hunter Valley and Townsville before settling at Willowbank, where it has become a fixture on Australia’s live music calendar. For many fans, the annual trip is now a family tradition passed from one generation of country lovers to the next.

Festival Director and Chugg Entertainment Chairman Michael Chugg said the milestone celebration will also recognize the vision of late festival co-founder Rob Potts.

“The sense of community and country spirit we experience each year is like nothing in this world,” Chugg said in a press release. “Bring on 2027.”

The announcement follows a massive 2026 edition that drew more than 25,000 fans and generated over $16 million for Queensland’s economy.

That weekend delivered no shortage of viral and only-at-CMC moments. Tucker Wetmore made a shirtless festival debut before joining Jordan Davis for a cover of “Wagon Wheel.” Lee Kernaghan teamed with James Johnston for “Boys From The Bush,” while Dasha jumped into the crowd to perform “Austin (Boots Stop Workin’)” alongside thousands of fans.

Other highlights included collaborations from Riley Green and Carly Pearce, Tyla Rodrigues and Braxton Keith, intimate Songwriters Show appearances and Gavin Adcock’s rowdy closing set on the Tailgate Stage.

CMC Rocks has already been confirmed to remain at Willowbank through 2028, backed by the Queensland Government and Tourism and Events Queensland.

For now, the lineup guessing game begins.

CMC Rocks QLD takes place March 17-21, 2027, at Willowbank Raceway. March 17 and 18 are open exclusively to campers.
