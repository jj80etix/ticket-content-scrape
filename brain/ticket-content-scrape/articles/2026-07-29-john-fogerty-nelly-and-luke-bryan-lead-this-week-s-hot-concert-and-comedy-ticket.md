# John Fogerty, Nelly and Luke Bryan Lead This Week’s Hot Concert and Comedy Tickets

## Summary

TicketNews' weekly best-sellers roundup is led by John Fogerty, Nelly, and a Wynonna Judd/Melissa Etheridge pairing at Red Rocks, with Lollapalooza's four-day pass averaging $845.

### Key Points
- Pacific Amphitheatre in Costa Mesa hosts four of the week's top 20 shows.
- Luke Bryan and Tim McGraw each appear twice in the top-seller rankings.
- Lollapalooza's four-day pass tops the list at an average $845.

Summer amphitheater shows, major country packages and familiar rock favorites are driving the week ahead, led by [John Fogerty](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/john-fogerty-1539?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10), [Nelly](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/nelly-1938?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) and a collaborative Red Rocks appearance from [Wynonna Judd](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/wynonna-judd-1152?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) and [Melissa Etheridge](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/melissa-etheridge-646?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10).

Pacific Amphitheatre in Costa Mesa is particularly prominent, hosting four of the week’s 20 best-selling events. Fogerty’s August 5 concert leads the list, followed by Nelly and Too $hort on August 2. The venue also welcomes Beatles tribute act The Fab Four and an R&B pairing of Patti LaBelle and The O’Jays. Red Rocks Amphitheatre places two shows in the top 20, including Wynonna Judd and Melissa Etheridge with the Colorado Symphony and an August 6 appearance by Of Monsters and Men.

Big-name packages continue to give fans several recognizable acts on a single ticket. [Luke Bryan](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/luke-bryan-14160?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) appears twice, including a Missoula stadium show with [Jason Aldean](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/jason-aldean-4804?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10). Aldean also has a separate Washington date featuring Chase Matthew and Mackenzie Carpenter. Tim McGraw similarly lands two events in the rankings, led by a Fenway Park bill with The Chicks, Lady A and 49 Winchester.

Classic and alternative rock are also well represented. [Lynyrd Skynyrd](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/lynyrd-skynyrd-611?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) and [Foreigner](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/foreigner-3617?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) rank fourth, while Mötley Crüe and Tesla offer one of the week’s most accessible average prices. A three-act package featuring [Train](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/train-1084?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10), [Barenaked Ladies](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/barenaked-ladies-8068?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) and [Matt Nathanson](https://www.megaseats.com/performers/matt-nathanson-4651?utm_medium=content&utm_source=ticketnews&utm_campaign=ticketnews&promoCode=TICKETNEWS10) places dates in both Michigan and Indiana among the top sellers.
