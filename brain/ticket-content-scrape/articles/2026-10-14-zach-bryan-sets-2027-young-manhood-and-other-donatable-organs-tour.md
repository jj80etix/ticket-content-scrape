# Zach Bryan Sets 2027 ‘Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs’ Tour

## Summary

Zach Bryan has announced his 2027 "Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs" tour, a run of stadium and arena dates across North America. Support acts include John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham, and Vance Joy. The announcement follows his recent album release and continues his run of large-scale headlining tours.

### Key Points
- Tour name: "Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs," set for 2027.
- Support lineup includes John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham, and Vance Joy.
- Continues Bryan's pattern of stadium-scale headline touring.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Zach-Bryan-.webp)

Zach Bryan will return to stadiums in 2027 with his Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs Tour, announcing 23 shows across North America and Australia.

AEG Presents will promote the run, which is scheduled to visit 19 stadiums. John Fogerty, Ryan Bingham and The Texas Gentlemen, Vance Joy, Fey Fili, Gabriella Rose and Emma Ogier will join Bryan on select dates. The tour opens April 28 at U.S. Bank Stadium in Minneapolis.

Artist presales begin October 21, followed by the North American general sale October 23 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans can register for presale access through Bryan’s presale website. Individual venues are also announcing additional opportunities to purchase tickets before the public sale.

The opening stretch includes Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis on May 1, Lambeau Field in Green Bay on May 4 and Soldier Field in Chicago on May 8. Bryan will then play consecutive nights at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey, on May 14 and 15.

Additional stops include Baltimore’s M&T Bank Stadium on May 18, Nashville’s Nissan Stadium on May 22, Philadelphia’s Lincoln Financial Field on May 25 and Atlanta’s Mercedes-Benz Stadium on May 29. A June 2 performance at Highmark Stadium in Buffalo is also scheduled.

The Nashville show will take place at the new Nissan Stadium, with Fogerty and Fey Fili supporting. The venue lists a 7 p.m. Central start time and will hold its own presale October 22 at 10 a.m. Central, following the artist presale the previous day.

Bryan will head to Australia in December for six stadium concerts, with two performances each in Melbourne, Sydney and Brisbane. The dates mark his first headlining Australian tour, following a standalone appearance at Melbourne’s Flemington Racecourse in 2023.

The Australian leg begins December 3 and 4 at Marvel Stadium in Melbourne. It continues December 10 and 11 at Accor Stadium in Sydney before wrapping December 17 and 18 at Suncorp Stadium in Brisbane. Vance Joy and Emma Ogier will support those shows. Australian presales begin October 21, with staggered start times, ahead of the October 23 general sale.

The announcement arrives ahead of Bryan’s eight-song EP, *Young Manhood and Other Donatable Organs*, due October 16 through Warner Records. The release includes collaborations with Lana Del Rey and Turnpike Troubadours, along with “Robot Killed the Working Man” and “Backwards,” songs Bryan has performed during his live shows.

The new dates follow an attendance milestone on Bryan’s With Heaven On Tour. His October 3 concert at Gillette Stadium drew 73,538 fans, setting a record for any event at the Foxborough, Massachusetts, venue. It was the second of two performances there.

Bryan previously drew 112,408 fans to Michigan Stadium on September 27, 2025. That performance made him the first artist to play the venue and set the record for the largest ticketed concert audience in U.S. history.
