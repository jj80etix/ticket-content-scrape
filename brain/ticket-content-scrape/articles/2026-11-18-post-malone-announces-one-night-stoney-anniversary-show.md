# Post Malone Announces One-Night ‘Stoney’ Anniversary Show

## Summary

Post Malone is marking the 10th anniversary of his debut album *Stoney* with a one-night-only show at the Hollywood Palladium on December 9, 2026, the exact anniversary of the album's 2016 release. The celebration also includes an expanded reissue with unreleased tracks and a limited 7,000-copy vinyl pressing.

### Key Points
- One-night anniversary concert set for Dec. 9, 2026, at the Hollywood Palladium, LA.
- *Stoney* produced three RIAA Diamond-certified singles: "White Iverson," "Congratulations," "I Fall Apart."
- Expanded digital reissue (8 unreleased tracks) and physical editions (7 bonus tracks) arrive Dec. 11.
- Limited-edition 3-LP "slime green" vinyl with original intended cover art, capped at 7,000 copies worldwide.
- Ticket prices and transfer/resale policy for the show were not disclosed in the announcement.

Post Malone is celebrating the 10th anniversary of his debut album, *Stoney*, with a special one-night-only concert at the Hollywood Palladium in Los Angeles on December 9, 2026. The performance will take place exactly 10 years after the album’s original release.

The anniversary concert celebrates the album that helped establish Post Malone as a major recording artist. Released December 9, 2016, *Stoney* featured several of his breakthrough hits, including “White Iverson,” “Congratulations” featuring Quavo, and “I Fall Apart.” All three tracks have since earned RIAA Diamond certifications.

The celebration also includes an expanded anniversary edition of *Stoney*, arriving December 11. The digital release features eight previously unreleased tracks, while physical editions include seven additional recordings from the album’s original era.

Among the special releases is a limited-edition, three-LP slime green vinyl pressing featuring the album’s originally intended cover artwork by Rodney Matthews. The edition is limited to 7,000 copies worldwide.

Rather than holding a traditional ticket onsale, the event will use Ticketmaster Request, allowing fans to submit ticket requests without competing in a first-come, first-served queue.

The request window opened Wednesday, November 18, at 9 a.m. PT and closes Thursday, November 19, at 10 p.m. PT. There will be no general ticket sale for the performance, making the request process the only announced primary ticket distribution method.

Fans can [request tickets through Ticketmaster](https://postmalone.request.ticketmaster.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) before the deadline.

Under the Ticketmaster Request system, fans select their preferred ticket type and provide payment information when submitting a request. Once the window closes, tickets are allocated based on availability. Successful applicants are automatically charged, while fans whose requests cannot be fulfilled are not charged.

Submitting a request does not guarantee tickets.

According to [Ticketmaster’s explanation of the system](https://blog.ticketmaster.com/ticketmaster-request-faq/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), event organizers determine how tickets are distributed when demand exceeds availability. All fulfilled requests are final sales, although fans can modify or cancel their requests before the window closes.

The system eliminates the pressure of joining a queue at a specific onsale time, but also requires fans to commit to purchasing tickets before knowing whether their requests will be fulfilled.

Ticket prices were not disclosed in the announcement. Event-specific ticket transfer and resale policies also were not detailed.

A VIP package will be available, including a general admission ticket, commemorative VIP laminate and wristband, early venue entry, and access to pre-show merchandise shopping.

For fans hoping to attend the Hollywood Palladium performance, the immediate deadline is November 19 at 10 p.m. PT, when Ticketmaster closes the ticket request window.
