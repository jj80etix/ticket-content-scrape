# On The Steps Plans Largest Edition Yet at Sydney Opera House

## Summary

Live Nation Australia's "On The Steps" concert series returns to the Sydney Opera House Forecourt Nov 28-Dec 12, its fifth and largest edition yet, with headliners James Blake, Suki Waterhouse and Rise Against. The series renews a fundraising partnership with music-industry charity Support Act, funded through merchandise sales and voluntary ticket-purchase donations.

### Key Points
- Fifth edition expands capacity to up to 7,000 per show; organizers expect 50,000+ attendees across the series.
- Lineup includes Australian Crawl, Pub Choir, Dropkick Murphys, Wolf Alice, DMA's, Spacey Jane, and The Amity Affliction, among others.
- All official merchandise proceeds go to Support Act; Live Nation extends a voluntary donation option to all its Australian November shows.
- Spacey Jane's Dec 10-11 shows are already sold out.

## Transcript

Live Nation Australia’s On The Steps concert series will return to the Sydney Opera House Forecourt from November 28 through December 12, with James Blake, Suki Waterhouse and Rise Against among the performers.

Now in its fifth edition, the event will increase capacity to as many as 7,000 attendees per show. Organizers expect more than 50,000 people across the series, making the 2026 program the largest in its history.

The announcement also includes a renewed fundraising partnership with Support Act, the Australian charity that provides short-term financial relief and mental health support to music workers in crisis. All proceeds from official On The Steps merchandise will go to the organization.

Australian Crawl will open the concert schedule on November 28 with Boom Crash Opera. Pub Choir’s Ultimate PDA follows on November 29, featuring guests including Ben Lee and Miles Phillips, accompanied by the Australian Youth Orchestra.

The program continues in December with Dropkick Murphys, Wolf Alice, Dizzee Rascal and Dylan Gossett, alongside Blake, Waterhouse and Rise Against.

DMA’s will headline on December 3 in what is billed as the band’s biggest show to date, with Automatica supporting. Spacey Jane will play two sold-out concerts on December 10 and 11, joined by Telenova and Armlock. The Amity Affliction will close the series on December 12, with Thornhill and WAAX completing the lineup.

In addition to the merchandise fundraiser, ticket buyers can make a voluntary donation to Support Act when purchasing admission to any concert in the series. Live Nation will extend that option to all its Australian shows throughout November.

“Live music wouldn’t be possible without the people working behind the scenes,” said Mike Ross, promoter at Live Nation Australia.
