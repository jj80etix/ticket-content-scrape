# Music Venue Trust UK Strengthens Leadership Team For Next Phase Of Growth

## Summary

UK charity Music Venue Trust (MVT) has expanded its senior leadership team, completing a multi-year organizational development program as it enters its next growth phase. The charity, which champions and invests in the UK's grassroots music venue (GMV) network, now has a 21-strong team across four departments covering venue support, fundraising, partnerships, campaigns/communications, live projects, and finance. The announcement also recaps a strong 2026 for MVT, including a new Investment Projects program, the inaugural "Everywhere At Once" festival, and a successful campaign for a further 20% cut to business rates for grassroots venues.

### Key Points
- New senior management additions: Toni Coe-Brooker (Campaigns & Communications Manager) and Sophie Asquith (Venue Support Manager), both promoted internally; Chris Hurne (Head of External Affairs) and Adrian Norman (Financial Controller), both external hires.
- Denzil Thomas (Partnerships Manager) and Rebecca Walker (Live Projects Manager) round out the expanded team, alongside founder/CEO Mark Davyd and COO Beverley Whitrick.
- The org now spans 21 staff across four departments: venue support, fundraising/partnerships, campaigns/communications, and live project delivery plus finance.
- 2026 highlights cited: launch of an Investment Projects program, the nationwide "Everywhere At Once" festival (2,000 artists across 400 grassroots venues), Government recognition via the Music Plan and Music Growth Package, and a further 20% business-rates reduction for grassroots venues.
- Board co-chairs Chris Prosser and Jane Beese frame the leadership expansion as securing succession planning after 12 years under Davyd's founding leadership.

All set for the next phase of growth (from left): Chris Hurne, Toni Coe-Brooker, Denzil Thomas, Beverley Whitrick, Rebecca Walker, Sophie Asquith, Adrian Norman & Mark Davyd. Photo by Katie Frost

The UK’s Music Venue Trust (MVT) has strengthened its senior leadership team as the charity enters its next phase of growth, completing a multi-year program to build a resilient management structure that will support its long-term sustainability and ensure it continues to protect, champion and invest in the UK’s grassroots music venue (GMV) network.

The appointments reflect the scale and ambition of an organization whose influence has grown significantly in recent years. With its work expanding across advocacy, investment, funding, policy, partnerships and member services, Music Venue Trust has established a broader leadership team to ensure the expertise, governance and succession planning are in place to support the charity’s continued growth and impact.

Joining Founder & CEO Mark Davyd and Chief Operating Officer Beverley Whitrick on the senior management team are: Toni Coe-Brooker, Campaigns & Communications Manager; Sophie Asquith, Venue Support Manager; Chris Hurne, Head of External Affairs; Denzil Thomas, Partnerships Manager; Rebecca Walker, Live Projects Manager, and Adrian Norman, Financial Controller.

Toni Coe-Brooker and Rebecca Walker, a 2023 Pollstar & VenuesNow Impact International Honoree, have both been promoted into departmental leadership roles after joining Music Venue Trust in 2020 and 2021 respectively, while Chris Hurne and Adrian Norman joined the charity earlier this year to strengthen its external affairs and financial leadership.

The appointments complete a wider organizational development program that has established a 21-strong team across four departments, providing dedicated expertise in venue support, fundraising, partnerships, campaigns, communications, live project delivery and finance.

“Together, the expanded team gives Music Venue Trust the organizational capacity to meet the growing needs of its venue members while continuing to lead national campaigns, influence policy and develop new initiatives that strengthen the grassroots music sector,” according to today’s press release announcing the promotions/appointments.

The strengthened leadership team follows another landmark year for Music Venue Trust and the grassroots music venue sector. During 2026 the charity has launched its [Investment Projects program](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/07/23/pipeline-brighton-becomes-next-building-saved-by-uk-own-our-venues-campaign/), staged the nationwide [Everywhere At Once festival](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/06/30/2000-artists-at-400-grassroots-music-venues-mvt-uk-takes-stock-of-inaugural-everywhere-at-once-festival/?_gl=1%2A8077mc%2A_ga%2AMTM4MzMyNDM3Ny4xNzgyOTc2ODgw%2A_ga_5B6XRECYRM%2AczE3ODYwMjE5NTAkbzc4JGcxJHQxNzg2MDIyNjY4JGo0MCRsMCRoMA..), helped secure Government recognition of grassroots music venues through both the Music Plan and the Music Growth Package, and successfully campaigned for a further [20% reduction in business rates for grassroots music venues](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/07/28/uk-cuts-business-rates-for-clubs-live-music-venues-in-2027/).

Comments:

Chris Prosser and Jane Beese, co-chairs of the Music Venue Trust Board, “Over the past 12 years, Music Venue Trust has transformed the conversation around grassroots music venues, championing their cultural and economic importance while delivering tangible support to the sector. The progress made has only been possible because of the passion and commitment of an exceptional team. As Trustees, one of our key priorities has been ensuring that the charity itself is built on strong, sustainable foundations. With a highly experienced senior management team now in place, delivering a robust succession plan, we are confident that MVT is entering its next chapter with the expertise, resilience and leadership needed to continue protecting, strengthening and advocating for the UK’s grassroots music venue network for many years to come.”

Mark Davyd, Founder & CEO of Music Venue Trust, “Music Venue Trust has never been about one individual. Everything we’ve achieved over the past 12 years has come from building an exceptional team with the expertise and commitment to fight for grassroots music venues every day. I am incredibly proud to see colleagues like Toni and Rebecca step into senior leadership roles, alongside Chris and Adrian bringing new expertise into the organisation. Together, this leadership team ensures we’re stronger, more resilient and better equipped than ever to support the UK’s grassroots music venue community for many years to come.”
