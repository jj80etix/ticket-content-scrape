# Katseye Drops Out Of Daisy Chain Fields; Devon Again Fills In

## Summary

KATSEYE has withdrawn from Olivia Rodrigo's "Daisy Chain Fields" festival (Irvine, CA, Aug. 29) after member Megan Skiendiel sprained her ankle during tour rehearsal. Devon Again, an opener on Rodrigo's upcoming tour, has been added to the bill in KATSEYE's place.

### Key Points
- Megan Skiendiel advised by doctors to rest ahead of KATSEYE's own tour, which starts Sept. 1 in Dublin.
- Devon Again added as replacement performer.
- Same story also covered independently by TicketNews (see companion note).

## Transcript

Katseye has pulled out of Daisy Chain Fields, the all-woman festival from Olivia Rodrigo set for Irvine, California’s Great Park Aug. 29.

On social media, the group wrote that member Megan Skiendiel is recovering from a sprained ankle.

“We’re so incredibly sorry to share that we are unable to perform at Daisy Chain Fields this Saturday,” the post reads, in part, “Megan sprained her ankle yesterday during tour rehearsal and after being evaluated by doctors, she has been advised to stay off it and allow it to properly heal so we can be ready for our upcoming tour.”

The group’s tour is set to begin Sept. 1 in Dublin.

Devon Again, who will open dates for Rodrigo on her upcoming tour, has been added to the bill.
