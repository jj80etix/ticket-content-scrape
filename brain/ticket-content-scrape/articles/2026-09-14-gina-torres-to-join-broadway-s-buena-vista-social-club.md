# Gina Torres to Join Broadway’s ‘Buena Vista Social Club’

## Summary

Gina Torres joins Broadway's "Buena Vista Social Club" for a limited run (Oct. 13-Nov. 29), stepping in as Omara Portuondo while Tony winner Natalie Venetia Belcon takes a planned leave.

### Key Points
- Belcon will appear as Claudia in New York City Center's "In the Heights" gala presentation during her leave.
- Torres, a first-generation Cuban American, cited a personal connection to the show's music.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Buena-Vista-Social-Club.webp)

Gina Torres will return to Broadway this fall for a limited engagement in “Buena Vista Social Club.”

The “Suits” and “Firefly” actor will play Cuban singer Omara Portuondo from October 13 through November 29 at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre. She will temporarily replace Natalie Venetia Belcon, who is taking a planned leave of absence from the production.

Belcon will appear as Claudia in the New York City Center gala presentation of “In the Heights.” She won the 2025 Tony Award for best featured actress in a musical for originating the role of Omara in “Buena Vista Social Club.”

“I am over the moon about joining the cast of ‘Buena Vista Social Club’ and stepping into the shoes of the legendary Omara Portuondo,” Torres said in a statement.

Torres, who is a first-generation Cuban American, said the production and its music hold personal significance for her.

“I grew up listening to these songs,” she said. “They’re the heartbeat of everything that feels like home and connects me to my family and my culture.”

The engagement marks Torres’ first Broadway appearance in more than 30 years. She made her Broadway debut in David Henry Hwang’s “Face Value” and later appeared in “The Best Little Whorehouse Goes Public.” Her recent stage credits include the Off-Broadway production of “Pen Pals” alongside Carmen Cusack.

“Buena Vista Social Club” features a book by Marco Ramirez and direction by Saheem Ali. Patricia Delgado and Justin Peck, who received a Tony Award for their work on the musical, choreographed the production.

“Buena Vista Social Club” opened at the Gerald Schoenfeld Theatre in March 2025 after beginning previews the previous month. The show received 10 Tony Award nominations and won five, including honors for Belcon, choreography, orchestrations, sound design and featured actor Mel Semé.

Theatergoers can visit the official [“Buena Vista Social Club” website](https://buenavistamusical.com/) for additional information and ticket details.
