# Untold Festival Romania Counts More Than 500,000 Visitors Across Four Days

## Summary

Romania's Untold Festival drew more than 500,000 attendees across four days in Cluj-Napoca, its 11th and most diverse edition yet, headlined by Sting, Flo Rida and The Chainsmokers among others.

### Key Points
- Nine stages hosted the lineup; Sting delivered a highly-anticipated Untold debut set.
- 30,000 tickets for the 2027 edition (Aug. 5-8) had already sold at publication time, priced from €109 for early passes.
