# Inside ISO 20022: Adoption, Compliance, and Innovation in Payments

## Summary

A Troutman Pepper "Payments Pros" podcast episode features Frank Van Driessche of Federal Reserve Financial Services discussing ISO 20022, the global payment-messaging standard, covering why it matters, adoption challenges, its role in AML/sanctions-screening compliance, and the innovation potential of richer structured payment data.

### Key Points
- Guest: Frank Van Driessche, head of the ISO 20022 practice at Federal Reserve Financial Services.
- Covers adoption challenges, compliance use cases (AML, sanctions screening), and data-richness benefits of the standard.
- Discusses real-world costs/trade-offs of rolling out the standard across the payments ecosystem.
- Episode closes with outlook on domestic and international ISO 20022 adoption.

## Transcript

[Skip to content](#lxb_af-loop)

In this episode of *[Payments Pros](https://www.troutman.com/podcast/payments-pros-podcast/)*, host [Carlin McCrory](https://www.troutman.com/professionals/carlin-a-mccrory/) speaks with Frank Van Driessche, head of the ISO 20022 practice at Federal Reserve Financial Services, about the global messaging standard reshaping the payments industry.

The conversation covers what ISO 20022 is and why it matters, the practical challenges organizations face in adopting it, and how the standard supports compliance efforts such as AML and sanctions screening. Frank also discusses the innovation opportunities created by richer, more structured payment data, along with the real-world costs and trade-offs involved in rolling out a major standards change across the payments ecosystem. He wraps up with his perspective on what’s next for ISO 20022 as adoption continues to expand both domestically and internationally.
