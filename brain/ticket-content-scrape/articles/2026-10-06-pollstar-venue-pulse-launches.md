# Pollstar Venue Pulse Launches

## Summary

Pollstar launched Venue Pulse, a new biweekly e-blast newsletter covering venues of all sizes — stadiums, theaters, clubs — following the 2026 VenuesNow Conference. The inaugural edition included interviews with United Center's Danny Wirtz and Rupp Arena's first female GM, stadium renovation news, and Pollstar's venue charts; it's a free trade-press product launch, not industry data or analysis itself.

### Key Points
- New free, biweekly venue-industry newsletter from Pollstar, launched post-VenuesNow Conference 2026.
- Inaugural issue: Danny Wirtz (United Center) interview on the 1901 Project music hall, Rupp Arena's first female GM Q&A, stadium renovation roundup, and Pollstar's Q3 venue charts.
- Will regularly feature cover stories, executive profiles, venue news/hires and a "Clubland" spotlight on smaller venues.

On the heels of the [2026 VenuesNow Conference](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/09/30/venuesnow-conference-2026-hub/) in Seattle, *Pollstar* today launched Venue Pulse, a biweekly news e-blast covering all things venues – from stadiums to theaters to clubs and everything in between.

The inaugural outing included an exclusive interview with Chicago Blackhawks CEO and co-owner of United Center Danny Wirtz about the 1901 Project, which includes a 6,000-cap music hall; a Q&A with the first female general manager at Lexington’s Rupp Arena, Laura Alexander; renovation announcements at several MLB and NFL stadiums; a personal chat with WNBA star Skylar Diggins; the opening of Coliseo GNP Seguros in Guadalajara; the state of college stadium concessions; along with *Pollstar* ’s gold-standard venue charts, which for this edition looks at Q3’s venue chart

Every other week, Venue Pulse will feature in-depth cover stories along with an executive profile, charts and the latest venue news, hires and industry trends. Subscribers will also find updates on hospitality, technology and international developments along with Clubland, our spotlight on smaller venues, which this week looks at Seattle’s iconic Crocodile.
