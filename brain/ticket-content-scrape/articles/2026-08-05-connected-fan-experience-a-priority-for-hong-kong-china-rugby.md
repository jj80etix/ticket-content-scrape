# Connected fan experience a priority for Hong Kong China Rugby

## Summary

Tradable Bits partnered with Tixserve and Hong Kong China Rugby (HKCR) to deliver a mobile-first, connected fan experience for the 50th-anniversary Cathay/HSBC Hong Kong Sevens (April 17-19), combining Tixserve's authenticated digital ticketing with Tradable Bits' FanCRM and engagement platform. The integration let attendees access digital tickets, event info, exclusive content, and interactive activations from a single app while giving HKCR richer first-party fan data — building on prior campaigns that had already produced record ticket proceeds and the tournament's biggest-ever Saturday attendance. The Sevens returns April 9-11, 2027.

### Key Points
- Tradable Bits (FanCRM/engagement) + Tixserve (authenticated digital ticketing) integrated into a single HKCR Ticketing app for the 50th Hong Kong Sevens.
- Fans accessed digital tickets, event info, exclusive content, and interactive activations through one mobile journey.
- Organizers gained deeper first-party fan data and engagement insight, building on campaigns that already drove record ticket proceeds and attendance.
- Partnership framed explicitly around consolidating ticketing + fan data + engagement into a single owned channel rather than third-party platforms.

### Highlights
- Two vendors (Tradable Bits, Tixserve) combined into one mobile app spanning ticket fulfillment, content, and engagement for a major recurring sports property.
- Result cited: record-breaking ticket proceeds and the tournament's biggest-ever Saturday attendance from prior fan-data-driven campaigns.
- Event returns April 9-11, 2027, giving a concrete window to see whether the integrated approach compounds.

### Industry Problem
Rights holders and venues want a single, seamless fan journey — ticketing, content, and engagement together — plus ownership of the first-party data that journey generates, rather than fragmenting the experience (and the data) across separate ticketing and marketing/CRM vendors.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes a vendor-partnership integration (Tradable Bits + Tixserve) as the solution rather than a single unified platform. For a primary ticketing platform, the actionable response is #improve: bundle first-party fan-data capture and engagement features (follow/loyalty, in-app content, activations) directly into the ticketing app rather than requiring clients to stitch together separate CRM/engagement vendors — reducing integration friction and strengthening the platform's own data moat.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Tradable Bits partnered with Tixserve and Hong Kong China Rugby (HKCR) to launch a mobile-first connected fan experience for the 50th anniversary of the Cathay/HSBC Hong Kong Sevens which took place earlier this year.**

The partnership brought together secure digital ticket fulfilment, fan engagement and first-party fan data into a single, seamless mobile platform.

Tradable Bits is a platform built to help sports, music and entertainment brands secure their data from third-party platforms, while also helping to build fan loyalty.

Powered by the Tixserve-enabled HKCR Ticketing app, supporters were able to securely access digital tickets, event information, exclusive content and interactive activations throughout the tournament, which took place from April 17-19 at Kai Tak Stadium.

Tradable Bit’s FanCRM and engagement platform was integrated with Tixserve’s authenticated digital ticketing technology to create a frictionless digital journey. Organisers also benefitted from more valuable first-party fan data and deeper insight into supporter engagement.

The partnership built on HKCR’s previous fan campaigns which have helped to generate record-breaking ticket proceeds and the biggest Saturday attendance in the tournament’s history.

“The Hong Kong Sevens has always been about creating unforgettable experiences for fans,” said Matt Rosslee, head of digital at Hong Kong China Rugby.

“Partnering with Tixserve and Tradable Bits allowed us to deliver a connected digital journey that matched the scale and ambition of this landmark tournament while helping us build stronger, more meaningful relationships with our supporters.”

The Cathay/HSBC Hong Kong Sevens will return from April 9-11, 2027 at Kai Tak Stadium.
