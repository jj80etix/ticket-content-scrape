# Matchbox Twenty Announces 52-Date 30th Anniversary North America Tour

## Summary

Matchbox Twenty announced a 52-date, Live Nation-promoted 30th Anniversary Tour kicking off May 15, 2027, with support from acts including The All-American Rejects, The Wallflowers, and O.A.R. The announcement follows a one-night performance at LA's Fonda Theatre and precedes a Jimmy Kimmel Live! appearance.

### Key Points
- 52-date North American "30th Anniversary Tour" begins May 15, 2027.
- Stops include Bridgestone Arena, Red Rocks, Hollywood Bowl, Moody Center, RBC Amphitheatre.
- Support acts rotate: The All-American Rejects, The Wallflowers, David Gray, O.A.R., Matt Nathanson, The Lucky.
- Also booked for Stagecoach Festival on April 25.
- Artist presale starts Oct. 20; general onsale Oct. 23; band represented by CAA.

Matchbox Twenty has announced a 30th Anniversary Tour, an extensive North American run taking in 52 dates and kicking off May 15, promoted by Live Nation.

The tour will bring the Rob Thomas-fronted hit-makers to amphitheaters and arenas across the U.S. and Canada, with dates including Bridgestone Arena in Nashville, Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Colorado, the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, Moody Center in Austin, RBC Amphitheatre in Toronto and more.

The All-American Rejects, The Wallflowers, David Gray, O.A.R., Matt Nathanson, and The Lucky join as special guests on select dates.

The tour is the centerpiece of a yearlong anniversary that began last night with a special one-night-only performance at the Fonda Theatre in Los Angeles, where the band played songs from their landmark debut *Yourself or Someone Like You* and other favorites. The hype continues tomorrow when Matchbox Twenty performs on Jimmy Kimmel Live!.

The band is also tapped for Stagecoach Festival in Indio, California, on Sunday, April 25.

Matchbox Twenty’s Artist Presale starts Tuesday, Oct. 20 at 5 p.m.. Tickets will also be available starting with the Reserved by Spotify presale on Tuesday, Oct. 20 at 10 a.m. local time for eligible Spotify Premium top fans purchasing tickets in Ticketmaster venues. Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general on sale beginning Friday, Oct. 23 at 10 a.m.

Matchbox Twenty is represented by CAA.
