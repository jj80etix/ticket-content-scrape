# Sugarland to Celebrate 20th Anniversary of ‘Enjoy the Ride’ With U.S. Tour

## Summary

Sugarland announced a 26-date "Ride or Die Tour" this fall marking the 20th anniversary of their 2006 album Enjoy the Ride, kicking off October 1 in St. Augustine, FL and wrapping November 21 in Grand Prairie, TX. General on-sale began August 14.

### Key Points
- 26-date U.S. tour, Oct. 1 – Nov. 21, marking the 20th anniversary of Enjoy the Ride.
- General public on-sale began August 14.
- Route spans St. Augustine, Charleston, Greensboro, Rapid City, Duluth, Atlantic City, Reading, Toledo and more.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Sugarland-Ride-or-Die-Tour.webp)

Sugarland is returning to the road this fall with a new U.S. tour marking the 20th anniversary of their album, *Enjoy the Ride*.

The country duo of Jennifer Nettles and Kristian Bush will headline the 26-date “Ride or Die Tour,” beginning October 1 in Florida, at the St. Augustine Amphitheatre. From there, the run is slated to make stops in cities such as Greensboro, Rapid City, Uncasville, Atlantic City, Reading, Toledo and Tulsa before wrapping up on November 21 in Grand Prairie at the Texas Trust CU Theater.

The upcoming run will center on the anniversary of *Enjoy the Ride*, which was released in 2006 and became a defining release in Sugarland’s career.

“ *Enjoy the Ride* was such a turning point album,” Nettles said in the tour announcement. “It literally catapulted us onto the ride of our lives with a carnival of hits and a life on The Road. We couldn’t let its anniversary pass without marking it as something special with our fans, live and on tour.”

The album arrived during Sugarland’s rapid rise in the mid-2000s. Following the success of the duo’s 2004 debut, *Twice the Speed of Life*, *Enjoy the Ride* further expanded its audience and delivered a string of successful singles.

Bush said the live performances remain a major part of what makes returning to the stage meaningful for the duo.

“There is truly nothing more satisfying than the lights going up on the Sugarland stage and feeling our songs rumble out of the speakers,” Bush said. “I’m excited just thinking about it and even more thrilled to be celebrating the anniversary of *Enjoy The Ride*.”

Tickets go on sale to the general public August 14 at 10 a.m. local time. For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Sugarland’s official website](https://sugarlandmusic.com/).
