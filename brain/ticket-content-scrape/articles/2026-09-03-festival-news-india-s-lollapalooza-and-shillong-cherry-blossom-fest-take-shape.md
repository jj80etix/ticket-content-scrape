# Festival news: India’s Lollapalooza and Shillong Cherry Blossom Fest take shape

## Summary

IQ Magazine's festival-news roundup: Lollapalooza India's fifth edition (23-24 January 2027, Mumbai) added Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, Katseye, Fontaines D.C. and others, plus additional festival updates on Tomorrowland China and Australian New Year's events.

### Key Points
- Lollapalooza India returns to Mumbai's Mahalaxmi Race Course Jan 23-24, 2027, remaining the only Asian edition of the festival.
- Lineup additions include 5 Seconds of Summer, St. Vincent, Steve Angello, Aditya Gadhvi, and Stewart Copeland.
- Produced by Perry Farrell, C3 Presents, and BookMyShow.
