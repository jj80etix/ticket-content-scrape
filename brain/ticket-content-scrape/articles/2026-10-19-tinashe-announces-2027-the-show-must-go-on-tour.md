# Tinashe Announces 2027 ‘The Show Must Go On’ Tour

## Summary

Tinashe announced her 2027 "The Show Must Go On" tour in support of her album "Popstar," kicking off April 14 in Dallas, following three sold-out headline shows (Chicago, Brooklyn, LA) whose presale sold out almost immediately. "Popstar" debuted at No. 6 on the Billboard 200.

### Key Points
- Tour opens April 14 (Dallas), runs through May 15 (Los Angeles).
- Supports album "Popstar" (#6 Billboard 200 debut, 35,500 first-week copies).
- Preceding 3-city run (Chicago, Brooklyn, LA) sold out presale and general onsale almost immediately -- her fastest-selling presale to date.
- Artist presale Oct. 20; general onsale Oct. 21.

Tinashe has announced the 2027 “The Show Must Go On” tour, with the first stop scheduled for Dallas on April 14. The multi-platinum-selling singer, songwriter, producer and DJ will then travel to major cities, including Atlanta, Philadelphia, Vancouver, Seattle, Los Angeles and more. See full tour routing below.

The artist presale for the tour begins Oct. 20 at 10 a.m. local time, with general on-sale starting Oct. 21 at 10 a.m. local time; both are available [here](https://www.tinashenow.com/).

The tour announcement comes as Tinashe kicks off the first of her three sold-out headline shows in Chicago, Brooklyn, and Los Angeles. Pre-sale tickets sold out almost immediately, making it her fastest-selling pre-sale to date, and general on-sale tickets also sold out shortly after. The shows support her latest album, *Popstar*, one of the singer’s most successful LPs, which debuted at #6 on the Billboard 200 and sold 35,500 copies in its first week.
