# Colin Farrell Voices Formula 1’s New ‘Five Lights’ Campaign

## Summary

Formula 1 has launched "Five Lights," a global marketing campaign narrated by Colin Farrell ahead of the 2026 season restart at Zandvoort on August 21. The 90-second cinematic film centers on the pre-race five-light countdown ritual, featuring all 22 F1 drivers.

### Key Points
- F1 launched a new global marketing campaign, "Five Lights," narrated by Colin Farrell.
- Campaign features all 22 current F1 drivers and centers on the pre-race light countdown.
- Timed to the 2026 season restart at Zandvoort on August 21.
- Soundtrack is UNKLE's "Reign," featuring Ian Brown.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0831.jpeg)

Formula 1 is counting down to the return of racing with some Hollywood horsepower.

Ahead of the 2026 season restart at Zandvoort on Aug. 21, Formula 1 has launched “Five Lights,” a new global marketing campaign featuring all 22 F1 drivers and narration from Academy Award-nominated actor and longtime racing fan Colin Farrell.

At the center of the campaign is a 90-second cinematic film built around one of the sport’s most tension-filled rituals: the five-light countdown before the start of a Grand Prix.

As each light illuminates, the film moves deeper into the mindset of an F1 driver, highlighting the concentration, anticipation and pressure packed into the seconds before the lights go out and the field launches toward Turn 1.

Farrell provides the narration, while UNKLE’s “Reign,” featuring Ian Brown, serves as the soundtrack.

“Five Lights captures one of the most powerful moments in Formula 1, when anticipation, excitement and pressure all come together just before the race begins,” said Emily Prazer, Formula 1’s Chief Commercial Officer. “As we return for the second half of what has already been an incredible season, we wanted to create something that reflects the emotion of our sport and the connection our fans feel with the teams and drivers every race weekend.”

Running from Aug. 16 through Sept. 13, the campaign will stretch beyond Formula 1’s social and digital channels. F1 is also taking over six high-profile advertising locations across the U.K. and U.S., including Piccadilly Circus and The Outernet in London, Sunset Boulevard and other locations in Los Angeles, Times Square in New York and Caesars Palace on the Las Vegas Strip.

The marketing push arrives as Formula 1 heads into the second half of another record-setting season. The racing series says its global fanbase has grown beyond 830 million, while the 2026 campaign has produced 11 consecutive sellout Grands Prix and six new circuit attendance records.
