# Olivia Rodrigo to Play Secret One-Night-Only Show at Brooklyn’s Warsaw

## Summary

Olivia Rodrigo played a surprise, one-night-only show at Brooklyn's Warsaw (capacity ~1,100) on August 6, with $25 tickets sold first-come, first-served in person at the venue box office starting 10 a.m., selling out by 1 p.m. The article also recaps her upcoming "Unraveled Tour" (Sept. 2026–May 2027, including a two-night Barclays Center close), noting that when the tour first went on sale in May, fans publicly objected to presale prices ranging from roughly $200 in upper sections to nearly $800 for floor seats, with some calling the pricing "illegal."

### Key Points
- Surprise Brooklyn show: $25 tickets, in-person box-office-only, first-come first-served, sold out within ~3 hours.
- Warsaw venue capacity is approximately 1,100.
- "Unraveled Tour" runs Sept. 25, 2026 through May 2, 2027 across North America and Europe.
- North American leg closes with a two-night Barclays Center stand (Feb. 15–16, 2027).
- Prior presale (reported May 2026) drew fan backlash over pricing, with floor seats reaching nearly $800.

### Highlights
- Fans called the "Unraveled Tour" presale pricing "illegal" when it launched in May 2026, with prices spanning roughly $200 (upper bowl) to ~$800 (floor).
- Contrast case: the same artist's surprise Brooklyn show priced tickets at a flat $25, sold only in person at the venue, no dynamic/platform fees mentioned.

### Industry Problem
This item is patron-sentiment evidence (Q2) of the recurring pain point around high, opaque presale/premium pricing on major arena and stadium tours — fans perceive top-tier tour pricing as excessive or unfair even absent any regulatory finding, and the backlash recurs each time a marquee artist's tour goes on sale.

### Proposed Solution
The article describes no platform-side fix, only the artist's own low-friction, flat-price, in-person-only alternative as an implicit contrast. For an independent primary platform like Etix, this reinforces the value of transparent, all-in pricing and simple purchase flows (build/improve pricing-transparency and low-friction "surprise drop" ticketing tools) as a differentiator against the presale sticker-shock pattern patrons associate with major-platform tours.
