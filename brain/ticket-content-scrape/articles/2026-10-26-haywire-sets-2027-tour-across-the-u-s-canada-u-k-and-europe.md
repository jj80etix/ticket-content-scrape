# Haywire Sets 2027 Tour Across the U.S., Canada, U.K. and Europe

## Summary

Haywire has announced a 2027 world tour backing its new album *I Don't Wanna Say Goodbye*, with UK/European dates in January, a US run from February to April, Canadian dates in April, and festival stops in June. The announcement follows a busy 2026 touring schedule including Vans Warped Tour and a run of Australian shows.

### Key Points
- Tour spans UK, Europe, US, and Canada from January through June 2027.
- US leg runs Feb 12 (Louisville) to April 4 (Boston hometown finale).
- Canadian leg follows April 9-30; European festival dates (Rock im Park, Rock am Ring) in June.
- Tour supports the newly released album *I Don't Wanna Say Goodbye*.
- 2026 schedule still includes Sick New World, Pressure Point, Vans Warped Tour, and Australian dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Haywire-I-Dont-Wanna-Say-Goodbye-Tour-1.webp)

Haywire has announced a 2027 world tour supporting its new album, *I Don’t Wanna Say Goodbye*, with performances scheduled across the United Kingdom, Europe, the United States and Canada.

The Boston punk band will begin the year with U.K. and European shows in January before launching its U.S. tour February 12 in Louisville, Kentucky. That leg includes stops in Atlanta, Houston, Los Angeles and Seattle before wrapping up April 4 with a hometown concert in Boston.

After the Boston finale, Haywire will head to Canada for a run spanning April 9 through April 30. The band will then return to Europe in June for appearances at Germany’s Rock im Park and Rock am Ring festivals.

The upcoming tour follows the release of *I Don’t Wanna Say Goodbye*, which is available now. Its singles “Interstate Love Affair” and “Sincerely Yours” were each featured in Consequence’s Heavy Song of the Week coverage upon their respective releases.
