# Australian Grand Prix renews Ticketmaster deal

## Summary

Ticketmaster Australia has renewed its multi-year ticketing partnership with the Australian Grand Prix Corporation, extending a relationship of more than 13 years to continue providing ticketing technology, distribution and fan support for the Melbourne F1 race, which drew a record 483,934 attendees this year.

### Key Points
- Multi-year renewal continues Ticketmaster as ticketing/distribution/fan-support partner for the Australian Grand Prix.
- Partnership has run 13+ years; 2026 event set a fifth consecutive attendance record (483,934).
- No new product, fee, or technology details were disclosed alongside the renewal.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Australian Grand Prix Corporation (AGPC) has renewed its partnership with Ticketmaster Australia in a multi-year deal.**

Under the agreement, Ticketmaster will continue to deliver ticketing technology, distribution and fan support services for the Formula 1 Australian Grand Prix in Melbourne.

“Ticketmaster has been a valued partner of the Australian Grand Prix Corporation for many years, and we are pleased to extend this relationship as the Formula 1 Australian Grand Prix continues to grow in scale and popularity,” said AGPC chief commercial officer, Emma Pinwill.

“We look forward to working together to build on this momentum and deliver world-class experiences for fans in the years ahead.”

Ticketmaster Australia and the AGCP have collaborated for more than 13 years.

The motorsport event has broken its attendance record for the fifth year in a row, and most recently welcomed 483,934 people to Albert Park for this year’s edition in March.

“The Formula 1 Australian Grand Prix is one of the most iconic events on the Australian sporting calendar, and we’re proud to extend our partnership with Australian Grand Prix Corporation,” said Ticketmaster Australia managing director, Gavin Taylor.

“Demand for the event continues to grow year-on-year, and we’re focused on ensuring fans have access to a seamless, secure and trusted ticket-buying experience. We look forward to continuing to drive innovation and top-tier experiences for fans, partnering closely with the AGPC team to support the ongoing success of this world-class event.”
