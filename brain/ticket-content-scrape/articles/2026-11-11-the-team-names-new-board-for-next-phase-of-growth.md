# The Team names new board for ‘next phase of growth’

## Summary

Talent agency The Team named its first-ever public board, finalizing its sale process to private equity firm Providence Equity Partners. Music group co-president Kevin Shivers joins the board alongside CEO Michael Watts and other senior leaders.

### Key Points
- Board appointees include Kevin Shivers (music group co-president), CEO Michael Watts, and Jason Ranne (global talent representation)
- Completes Providence Equity Partners' takeover, continuing from Wasserman's prior stake sale announced in July 2026
- Framed by the agency as guiding its "next phase of growth"
