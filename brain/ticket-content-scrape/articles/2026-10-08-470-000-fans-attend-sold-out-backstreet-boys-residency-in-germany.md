# 470,000 Fans Attend Sold-Out Backstreet Boys Residency In Germany

## Summary

Backstreet Boys wrapped a completely sold-out 10-show "Into the Millennium" residency at Düsseldorf's Merkur-Spiel Arena, drawing 470,000 total fans (47,000 per show) in what Live Nation GSA called their only European dates of 2026. Each night opened with the group descending from the arena ceiling on surfboards, a callback to their 1999 "Into The Millennium" tour staging.

### Key Points
- 10 sold-out shows at Merkur-Spiel Arena, Düsseldorf; 470,000 tickets total (47,000/night).
- Band's only European performances of 2026; promoted by Live Nation GSA.
- Staging nods to the 1999 "Into The Millennium" world tour (surfboard descent into the arena).
- Band says demand "remained as strong as ever" through the final show.

## Transcript

![BSB 1](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/BSB-1.jpg)

Backstreet Boys just wrapped their completely sold-out resdency, “Into the Millennium – Homecoming – Live in Germany”. Each night, they would decend from the arena ceiling on surfboards – a nod to their 1999 “Into The Millenium” world tour.

Backstreet Boys are giving their final of ten concerts at Merkur-Spiel Arena in Düsseldorf tonight, Oct. 7, at 8 p.m. German time. And while the box office reports hadn’t been submitted at press time, Live Nation GSA stated that the residency sold a total 470,000 tickets, or 47,000 per sold-out show.

The “Into the Millennium – Homecoming – Live in Germany” residency in Düsseldorf marked Backstreet Boys’ only European performances of 2026, and their return to the country where they scored their first platinum hit with “I’ll Never Break Your Heart”.

The 26-song hit marathon opened with Backstreet Boys descending from the arena ceiling on surfboards and floating down onto the stage – a nod to their 1999 “Into The Millenium” world tour, when they would float into the arena on surfboards above the audience.

“Germany has always felt like a second home to us, and what we experienced in Düsseldorf was truly unforgettable,” a band statement reads, “seeing the entire city embrace us and our fans meant so much. We’re incredibly grateful to Live Nation, the city of Düsseldorf, and especially the 470,000 fans who made this residency so special. Germany will always have a very special place in our hearts.”

According to a press release from Live Nation, “The Backstreet Boys party resonated far beyond the arena itself, taking over Düsseldorf and the surrounding region, with the city and its people adopting the event in a truly unique way. The excitement was also echoed in some of the strongest digital engagements of the Backstreets Boys’ recent touring era. It demonstrated the special appeal of bringing together an outstanding destination and an exceptional concert residency.”

That extraordinary enthusiasm was perfectly captured by one striking newspaper headline: “Why Don’t the Backstreet Boys Stay in Düsseldorf Forever?” Indeed, demand remained as strong as ever until the very end — and fans are already asking the inevitable question: When will the Backstreet Boys come back?
