# Sports Illustrated Tickets Expands NFL London Role With Jaguars Partnership

## Summary

Sports Illustrated Tickets is expanding its NFL London presence through a new team-level partnership with the Jacksonville Jaguars, adding premium fan experiences around the team's two October London games (vs. Philadelphia at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on Oct. 11, vs. Houston at Wembley on Oct. 18). The deal builds on SI Tickets' broader September agreement covering all three of the NFL's 2026 London games.

### Key Points
- SI Tickets becomes an Official Fan Experience Partner for the Jaguars' two 2026 London games.
- At Wembley, SI Tickets presents the Jaguars' "Shadows" pregame-tunnel experience for package buyers.
- Deal sits alongside SI Tickets' existing 12-year Tottenham Hotspur partnership and prior Buccaneers tie-in.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Sports-Illustrated-Jacksonville-Jags.webp)

Sports Illustrated Tickets is expanding its NFL presence in the UK through a new partnership with the Jacksonville Jaguars that will include premium fan experiences around the team’s two London games this month.

The company will serve as an Official Fan Experience Partner of the Jaguars in the UK as Jacksonville faces the Philadelphia Eagles at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium on October 11 and the Houston Texans at Wembley Stadium on October 18.

The team-level agreement builds on a broader NFL partnership announced in September that made Sports Illustrated Tickets an Official Fan Experience Partner across all three of the league’s 2026 London games. At the time, SI Tickets said it would offer fan experiences and marketing activations around the games but had not yet disclosed specifics.

The Jaguars deal now provides some of those details.

At Wembley, Sports Illustrated Tickets will serve as presenting sponsor of the Jaguars UK “Shadows” Experience. Fans purchasing the associated SI Fan Experience package will be able to watch portions of the team’s pregame preparations from inside the Wembley Tunnel, including the Jaguars’ walkout and player introductions immediately before kickoff.

SI Tickets will also offer a second experience tied to select game tickets. Those buyers will receive a personalized Sports Illustrated-style fan cover along with a two-month digital subscription to Sports Illustrated.

The announcement did not specify pricing, the number of packages available or which ticket categories will qualify for the experiences.

“Jacksonville has built an incredible connection with fans in the UK, and we’re excited to be part of that tradition,” Sports Illustrated Tickets CEO David Lane said. “The 2026 NFL London Games give us an opportunity to bring the storytelling and energy of Sports Illustrated to life for Jaguars faithful in a market with such a strong connection to the team.”

**| RELATED:**[**Sports Illustrated Tickets Partners With Tampa Bay Buccaneers for 2026 Season**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/sports-illustrated-tickets-partners-with-tampa-bay-buccaneers-for-2026-season/) **|**

The partnership adds another layer to SI Tickets’ growing collection of UK sports relationships. The company entered a [12-year agreement with Tottenham Hotspur in 2025](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/09/sports-illustrated-tickets-inks-fan-experience-deal-with-tottenham/?utm_source=chatgpt.com) that established Sports Illustrated Tickets as the Premier League club’s Official Fan Experience Partner and created branded hospitality and fan-engagement spaces at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

The Jaguars, meanwhile, remain one of the NFL’s most established participants in the league’s international schedule. Jacksonville first played a regular-season game in London in 2013. The Texans game will mark the franchise’s 12th regular-season appearance at Wembley, according to the team’s announcement, while the Eagles matchup will be its fourth game at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium.

Both Jaguars games kick off at 2:30 p.m. local time, or 9:30 a.m. ET.

Sports Illustrated Tickets’ broader NFL London agreement also covers the October 4 Indianapolis Colts-Washington Commanders game at Tottenham. The company’s expanding NFL relationship sits alongside its role as one of the authorized ticketing channels within the league’s NFL Ticket HQ ecosystem.
