# ‘2:22 – A Ghost Story’ Sets Cast for Off-Broadway Premiere

## Summary

Danny Robins' supernatural thriller "2:22 – A Ghost Story" will make its New York premiere Off-Broadway this fall, running October 20, 2026 through January 17, 2027 at the Lucille Lortel Theatre. The cast is led by Anna Camp, Jasmine Cephas Jones, Finn Jones and Martin Marquez, with Matthew Dunster and Gabriel Vega Weissman co-directing.

### Key Points
- Performances begin Oct. 20, official opening Nov. 5, limited run through Jan. 17, 2027.
- Previously staged in seven West End engagements, two U.K. tours, and on five continents.
- Produced by Tristan Baker and Charlie Parsons for Runaway Entertainment with Isobel David and Kater Gordon.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/222-A-Ghost-Story.webp)

Danny Robins’ supernatural thriller “2:22 – A Ghost Story” is set to make its New York premiere this fall, with Anna Camp, Jasmine Cephas Jones, Finn Jones and Martin Marquez leading the Off-Broadway production.

Performances will begin October 20 at the Lucille Lortel Theatre ahead of an official opening November 5. The production is scheduled to run through January 17, 2027, for a limited engagement.

Matthew Dunster, whose credits include “Hangmen” and “The Hunger Games: On Stage,” and Gabriel Vega Weissman, known for “Guards at the Taj,” will co-direct the production.

Camp will play Lauren, while Cephas Jones will portray Jenny. Jones will make his New York stage debut as Sam. Marquez will play Ben.

“2:22 – A Ghost Story” centers on Jenny, who becomes convinced that her new home is haunted. Her husband, Sam, does not share her belief, setting up an argument over whether ghosts actually exist. When the couple’s friends join them for dinner, the group decides to remain awake until 2:22 a.m. to determine whether there is a supernatural explanation for what Jenny has experienced.

The production has previously been presented in seven West End engagements and two U.K. tours. It has also been staged in theaters across five continents, according to Robins.

The creative team includes scenic designer Anna Fleischle, costume designer Cindy Lin and lighting designer Lucy Carter. Ian Dickinson of Autograph Sound is handling sound, while Chris Fisher is creating the production’s illusions.
