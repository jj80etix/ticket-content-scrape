# Santana Announces 2027 European Tour With 13 Shows Across 10 Countries

## Summary

Santana announced a 13-show European leg of the "Planet of Free Will Global Tour" for summer 2027, spanning 10 countries from London's O2 (June 30) to Helsinki (July 31).

### Key Points
- Opens June 30 at The O2, London, as part of the venue's 20th-anniversary celebrations.
- Closes July 31 at Kaivopuisto Blues, Helsinki.
- Additional stops include Paris, Krakow, Oslo and multiple dates in Germany and Italy.

## Transcript

Santana will embark on a 13-show European tour next summer, visiting 10 countries between June 30 and July 31, 2027, as Carlos Santana and his band take their Planet of Free Will Global Tour to a mix of arenas, outdoor venues and festivals.

The [newly announced itinerary](https://santana.com/news/santana-announces-planet-of-free-will-global-tour-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) opens at The O2 in London on June 30 and ends at Kaivopuisto Blues in Helsinki on July 31. Other dates include Paris, Krakow, Oslo, three stops in Germany and two performances in Italy. London is the only U.K. concert currently listed and will be part of [The O2’s 20th-anniversary celebrations](https://www.theo2.co.uk/events/detail/santana-2027?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Carlos Santana tied the tour name to a message of personal choice and togetherness. In the October 26 announcement, he spoke of the ability to choose “unity over division, harmony over conflict, and love over fear.” The shows will draw on material from across Santana’s catalog, including music associated with *Abraxas* and *Supernatural*.

For most shows, fan presales begin Tuesday, October 27, and Santana’s announcement lists general ticket sales starting Friday, October 30, at 10 a.m. Not every stop follows that schedule: the July 7 performance at Accor Arena in Paris is set for a November 20 general onsale, while tickets for Pordenone, Lucca and Helsinki were already available when the tour was announced. Fans should check the specific event listing before making purchasing plans.

The European concerts follow a series of 2027 performances at the House of Blues inside Mandalay Bay in Las Vegas. Santana is scheduled for eight residency dates in January and another eight in May, continuing the *An Intimate Evening with Santana: Greatest Hits Live* series. TicketNews has [previously covered extensions of the Las Vegas engagement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/santana-adds-new-november-2026-las-vegas-residency-dates-at-house-of-blues/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) as well as [Santana’s 2026 North American tour with the Doobie Brothers](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/santana-and-the-doobie-brothers-unite-for-2026-oneness-tour-across-north-america/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

For the London concert, The O2 names AXS as its official ticketing partner and says buyers using the venue or AXS will access tickets through The O2 app. The venue identifies AXS Official Resale as its resale option and warns that resale tickets purchased elsewhere may not be valid for entry.

Although Santana’s announcement refers broadly to performances across Europe and North Africa, all 13 dates in the released schedule are in European countries. No North African concerts were identified in the initial itinerary.
