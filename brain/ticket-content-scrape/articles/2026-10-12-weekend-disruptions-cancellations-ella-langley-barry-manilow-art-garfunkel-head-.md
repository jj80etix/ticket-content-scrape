# Weekend Disruptions & Cancellations: Ella Langley, Barry Manilow, Art Garfunkel & Head Trip Festival

## Summary

A weekend wave of cancellations and disruptions hit several tours: Ella Langley's Oct. 9 Tulsa show was canceled hours before showtime over a called-in "shots fired and bombs" security threat (police found no credible threat; she resumed touring the next night), Barry Manilow canceled two Las Vegas shows after bronchitis cut a performance short, and Art Garfunkel postponed UK dates after a respiratory-infection diagnosis.

### Key Points
- Ella Langley's Tulsa (BOK Center) show canceled Oct. 9 over a security threat; police found no credible threat; she resumed the tour Oct. 10 in Lubbock.
- Barry Manilow (83) canceled two Las Vegas shows after being diagnosed with bronchitis mid-run; remaining shows that week stay on sale.
- Art Garfunkel (84) postponed UK "What A Wonderful World" tour dates after a respiratory-infection diagnosis.
- All three cases are isolated artist-health/security incidents with no stated ticketing-platform or policy angle.

## Transcript

Cancellations and disruptions affected notable tours and a festival this past weekend, with Ella Langley’s show making the most headlines.

The country music star’s concert scheduled for Oct. 9 at BOK Center in Tulsa, Oklahoma, was canceled hours before showtime because of a “serious security threat.”

The Tulsa Police Department told reporters that it had performed a routine sweep of the venue. A threat was called in “in the way of shots fired and bombs,” and officers visited the building a second time with explosive detection canines to search the area, Captain Richard Meulenberg said.

“We could not find a credible threat here at the BOK Center,” he added.

Langley resumed her tour the following day, Oct. 10, at United Supermarkets Arena in Lubbock, Texas, where she addressed the security threat.

“Because when it comes to keeping you safe and making sure that this show is a safe place for us to come and enjoy our music, then it will happen, but the second it feels like it’s not, I’m not doing it,” Langley said in [a video posted online by a fan](https://x.com/210TexansForTxs/status/2109141050988400775) at the show. “I’m not doing it because a concert is not worth a human life. It’s not. And so, I would have made yesterday’s decision 10 billion times over. I would have.”

**BARRY MANILOW CANCELS TWO SHOWS**

Another star who had to cancel was Barry Manilow, who walked off stage Friday night after two songs and then called off the rest of that show, as well as the following night’s performance at the International Theater at the Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino.

The 83-year-old singer “got hit with bronchitis,” representatives for the musician and the resort told *[Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/barry-manilow-cancels-las-vegas-shows-1236907619/)*. The news comes after Manilow returned from a six-week hiatus on Oct. 8. The legendary crooner has been recovering from lung cancer, which he revealed in December 2025.

Tickets are still on sale for Manilow’s shows Thursday through Saturday.

**ART GARFUNKEL POSTPONES UK DATES**

Art Garfunkel was also forced to postpone shows as a result of an illness. The 84-year-old singer and poet told *NME* that he was diagnosed with a respiratory infection, and he’s pushing back his “What A Wonderful World” tour dates in the U.K.

“During rehearsals for my upcoming concerts, I nearly postponed a scheduled appointment with my physician,” Garfunkel said in a statement sent to *[NME](https://www.nme.com/news/music/art-garfunkel-postpones-uk-tour-diagnosed-respiratory-infection-3972843)*. “Thankfully, my wonderful wife, Kim, insisted I keep it. As it turns out, I have been diagnosed with a respiratory infection, and my doctor advised me to take some time to rest and recover.”

He added: “My heart is broken that I must postpone these performances. At this season of my life, to be so warmly received by audiences and surrounded by so much love is a gift beyond words. Singing for you has always been one of the greatest joys of my life, and the thought of disappointing you saddens me deeply.”

Garfunkel was scheduled to kick off the run Oct. 13 at Watford Colosseum. The concerts at Manchester’s Opera House (Oct. 15), University of Wolverhampton at The Civic Hall (Oct. 17) and London’s Royal Albert Hall (Oct. 19) have also been postponed.

“I promise you that I will return to the stage and sing for you as soon as my health allows,” Garfunkel said. “Please don’t worry about me. I’m in good hands, and I expect to be just fine.”

**HEAD TRIP ENDS NIGHT 1 EARLY**

Southern California rarely gets severe storms, but one hit Saturday night, forcing promoter Goldenvoice to end the first night of its desert EDM festival, Head Trip, early.

Lightning storms at Empire Polo Club, the same grounds used for Coachella and Stagecoach, cut short Fisher and Chris Lake’s Under Construction set around 9 p.m. Saturday, and organizers told guests to seek shelter before canceling the rest of the night.

Fans who stuck around for the second day got to see Harris take the stage. The Scottish DJ was added to the Day 2 programming and performed before Swedish House Mafia.

The move, however, could end up costing Goldenvoice. The *[Desert Sun](https://www.desertsun.com/story/entertainment/coachella/2026/10/09/head-trip-festival-live-updates-head-trip-bag-policy-tickets-merch-lineup-calvin-harris--live/92154702007/)* reported that Swedish House Mafia’s set started late and ran 33 minutes past the midnight curfew, which could result in fines totaling $48,000.
