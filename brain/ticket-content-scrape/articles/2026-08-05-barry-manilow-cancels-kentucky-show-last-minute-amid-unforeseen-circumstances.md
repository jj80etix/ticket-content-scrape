# Barry Manilow Cancels Kentucky Show Last-Minute Amid ‘Unforeseen Circumstances’

## Summary

Barry Manilow's Tuesday show at Lexington's Rupp Arena was called off just 45 minutes before start time due to "unforeseen circumstances," with the venue confirming all purchased tickets will be honored once a new date is set. The 83-year-old singer, who previously postponed shows while undergoing stage 1 lung cancer treatment, continues his farewell tour and Westgate Las Vegas residency through the rest of 2026.

### Key Points
- Rupp Arena show canceled 45 minutes before showtime; originally rescheduled from a March 9 postponement tied to Manilow's lung cancer treatment.
- Venue says previously purchased tickets will be honored at the new (not-yet-announced) date.
- Manilow continues his farewell tour with shows in Cincinnati, Baltimore, Norfolk, Greensboro, Duluth, and Jacksonville this month, plus rescheduled Florida dates in January 2027.
- His Westgate Las Vegas residency continues through the end of 2026.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Barry-Manilow.webp)

Barry Manilow was set to take the stage at Lexington, Kentucky’s Rupp Arena Tuesday night, however, the gig was called-off just 45 minutes before the scheduled start time.

The 83-year-old singer took to social media to share the news, simply writing: “Due to unforeseen circumstances tonight’s show in Lexington has been rescheduled.” The show was originally rescheduled from March 9, which [needed to be postponed](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/12/barry-manilow-postpone-tour-lung-cancer-surger/) while Manilow underwent treatment for stage 1 lung cancer.

Rupp Arena also commented on the cancellation, noting that “all previously purchased tickets will be honored at the new date once it is announced.”

Manilow is slated to perform next at Cincinnati’s Heritage Bank Center on August 5. From there, he’ll head to Baltimore, Norfolk, Greensboro, Duluth, and Jacksonville later this month. He’s also scheduled to perform a round of rescheduled shows across Florida in Sunrise, Estero, Tampa, and Orlando in January 2027, as well as a show at Charlotte’s Spectrum Center with a date TBD.
