# Tickets for Good Reaches 1.4M Tickets Distributed as It Expands Board

## Summary

UK-based free/discounted-ticket distribution platform Tickets for Good expanded its board, adding ecommerce executive Gabrielle Hase as chair and ticketing veteran Tim Chambers (ex-Ticketmaster/Live Nation) as non-executive director, following a £3.9M Series A round. The company has now distributed 1.4 million tickets to 800,000 registered healthcare workers, teachers, and charity workers, and is launching a Music + Wellbeing research initiative with the University of Sheffield.

### Key Points
- £3.9M Series A led by NPIF II-Mercia Equity Finance.
- 1.4M tickets distributed via roughly 1,500 event partners to about 800,000 verified users.
- New board members include a former Ticketmaster/Live Nation executive.
- Company explicitly positions itself as a partner to, not a competitor of, established ticketing companies.
- Launching a Music + Wellbeing summit series with University of Sheffield research partnership.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Tickets-For-Good.webp)

Tickets for Good has expanded its board with digital commerce executive Gabrielle Hase as chair and longtime ticketing executive Tim Chambers as a non-executive director as the live-event access platform moves into its next stage of growth following a £3.9 million funding round.

The Sheffield, UK-based company also added Arjen van Mierlo, co-founder and partner in its European business, to the board while launching a new Music + Wellbeing research and events initiative.

Tickets for Good says it has now distributed 1.4 million free and discounted tickets through approximately 1,500 event partners to a base of roughly 800,000 registered healthcare workers, teachers and charity workers across the UK, Europe and U.S.

Hase brings a background in ecommerce and digital strategy, including leading Sweaty Betty’s global ecommerce operation, helping accelerate online growth at L.K. Bennett and launching TKMaxx.com. She also spent more than a decade on the board of Tate Enterprises.

His appointment gives Tickets for Good additional ticketing-sector expertise as the company seeks to deepen relationships with promoters, venues, artists and distribution partners.

Van Mierlo, meanwhile, is a co-founder and partner in Tickets for Good’s European operation and has a background in media, M&A, strategy and investment.

New and returning music partners during 2026 include All Points East, Dreamland Margate, Hollywood Vampires, LIDO Festival, The Reytons and UB40. Tickets for Good also cited continued support from artists and organizations including IDLES, Pixies, Robbie Williams and Yungblud’s Bludfest.

Founder and CEO Stephen Rimmer said the new appointments are intended to support the company’s expansion across markets and partnerships.

“Tickets for Good is based on the belief that unused tickets can open doors for people, and that belief still guides us,” Rimmer said.

The company has also promoted Amelia Garrett to Marketing & Comms Lead, George Webb to Customer Service Manager and Roland Dickinson to Ticketing Operations Manager.

## Tickets for Good Expands Music and Wellbeing Work

Alongside the board changes, Tickets for Good is formalizing its work around the relationship between live music access, wellbeing and social connection through its new TfG Music + Wellbeing research and events program.

The initiative follows a July gathering in Sheffield during the Tramlines festival weekend that brought together representatives from live entertainment, healthcare, academia, policy and community organizations. The event also provided an early look at research being conducted with the University of Sheffield.

That partnership continues Oct. 8 with a presentation from Dr. Emma Risley examining healthcare workers’ engagement with live-music access programs and barriers to broader adoption across the UK’s National Health Service. Additional panels are expected to examine how academic research and the music business can better measure music’s effects on wellbeing.

The work is scheduled to lead into a larger Music + Wellbeing: In Conversation summit in July 2027.

Tickets for Good said it is continuing to seek partnerships with music, theater and sports organizations interested in distributing underutilized inventory, while also pursuing relationships with healthcare employers and additional ticket distribution and fulfillment partners.
