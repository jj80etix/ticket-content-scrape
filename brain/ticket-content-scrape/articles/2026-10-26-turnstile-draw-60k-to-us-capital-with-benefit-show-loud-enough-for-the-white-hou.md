# Turnstile draw 60k to US capital with benefit show ‘loud enough for the White House to hear’

## Summary

Hardcore band Turnstile drew an estimated 60,000 people to a free benefit concert at the Washington Monument in Washington, D.C., reportedly the biggest headline show of the band's career. The show, organized with Human Concern International, raised more than $260,000.

### Key Points
- Free 75-minute set drew ~60,000 attendees near the White House on Oct. 24.
- Partnership with Human Concern International raised over $260,000.
- Described as Turnstile's biggest headline show to date.
