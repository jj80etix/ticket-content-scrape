# Stella Lefty Sets 2027 ‘Long Way Home’ North American Tour

## Summary

Stella Lefty announced a North American headline tour for early 2027 supporting her debut album *Long Way Home*, running January 9 (Detroit) through February 20 (Chicago). Ticket sales follow a standard artist-presale-then-general-onsale structure through Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Tour runs Jan. 9 (Detroit, The Fillmore) to Feb. 20 (Chicago, Aragon Ballroom), ~18 dates across the US and Canada.
- Supports debut album *Long Way Home*, released August 21 via Atlantic Outpost.
- Artist presale (Ticketmaster-account-linked) began Aug. 27; general onsale begins Aug. 28.
- Follows a year of festival appearances (CMA Fest, Lollapalooza, Osheaga, ACL) building her live profile.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Stella-Lefty-Long-Way-Home-Tour.webp)

Stella Lefty will hit the road for a North American headline tour in early 2027, supporting her newly released debut album, *Long Way Home*.

The tour is set to begin January 9 at The Fillmore in Detroit. From there, the singer is slated to make stops in cities such as Toronto, Montreal, Boston, Brooklyn, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Dallas, Austin, San Francisco, Denver and Nashville. The run is scheduled to wrap up on February 20 in Chicago at Aragon Ballroom.

Lefty released *Long Way Home* through Atlantic Outpost on August 21. The 14-track project combines seven previously unreleased songs with seven tracks that had already been issued, including singles “Boston” and “Thinking ‘bout You.”

Alongside her own headline dates, Lefty has continued building her live profile through appearances at major festivals. Her 2026 schedule has included performances at CMA Fest, Lollapalooza and Osheaga, with an appearance at Austin City Limits also among her festival bookings. She has additionally taken the stage alongside Mumford & Sons, Jessie Murph and Cameron Whitcomb.

Ticket sales for the 2027 tour begin with the Stella Lefty Artist Presale on August 27 at 10 a.m. local time. Fans were required to register by August 25 to participate, with presale access connected directly to their Ticketmaster accounts rather than distributed through codes.

Any remaining tickets will go on sale to the general public beginning August 28 at 10 a.m. local time through [Lefty’s official website](https://www.stella-lefty.com/).
