# Phoebe Bridgers Postpones UK Shows After Appendicitis Diagnosis

## Summary

Phoebe Bridgers postponed several intimate UK shows in Kingston, Brighton and Bristol after being diagnosed with appendicitis, following two performances at London's Union Chapel. The postponed dates were tied to the release of her new album Lost Weekend and preceded her upcoming 43-date North American arena tour, "The Lost Tour," beginning Sept. 14 in Indianapolis.

### Key Points
- UK shows in Kingston, Brighton and Bristol postponed; new dates not yet announced.
- Diagnosis announced via Instagram on her 32nd birthday (Aug. 17).
- "The Lost Tour" (43 North American dates) is still set to begin Sept. 14 at Indianapolis' Gainbridge Fieldhouse.
- Tour concludes with three Intuit Dome shows before heading to Europe (Dublin's 3Arena, Nov. 23-24).

## Transcript

Phoebe Bridgers has postponed several intimate shows in the United Kingdom after being diagnosed with appendicitis.

Bridgers took to her Instagram story, sharing,“I got appendicitis for my birthday,” she wrote. “Everything in the UK this week will have to move but I’ll make it up to you!!!!”

The singer performed two shows at London’s Union Chapel on August 17. She was scheduled to continue with performances in Kingston, Brighton and Bristol through August 21, but those dates will now be moved.

The shows were planned around the release of Bridgers’ third album, “Lost Weekend.” The upcoming performances were set to take place in smaller venues ahead of the singer’s first arena-headlining tour.

New dates for the postponed shows have not yet been announced. Banquet Records, which was scheduled to host Bridgers for the Kingston performance, said it is working with the singer’s team to determine new dates.

The postponed United Kingdom shows come ahead of Bridgers’ upcoming “The Lost Tour,” which is scheduled to begin September 14 at Gainbridge Fieldhouse in Indianapolis.

The North American portion of the tour is scheduled to include 43 shows. After opening in Indianapolis, Bridgers will perform in cities including Chicago, Columbus, Brooklyn, Philadelphia, Toronto, Boston, Nashville and Vancouver.
