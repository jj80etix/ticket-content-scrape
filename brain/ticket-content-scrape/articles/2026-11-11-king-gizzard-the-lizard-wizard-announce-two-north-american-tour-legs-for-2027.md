# King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard Announce Two North American Tour Legs for 2027

## Summary

King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard announced extensive 2027 touring plans: a European/UK summer run, followed by two North American legs (summer and fall), plus a new companion album, Entropy, out December 4. Tickets go on sale Friday, Nov. 13 via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Summer North American leg opens Aug. 5 (two nights, Hollywood Forever Cemetery, LA); fall leg runs Oct. 6–22, closing in Chicago
- European/UK dates run June 5–23, including Primavera Sound and London's Eventim Apollo
- Entropy, a companion to August's Alien Metal, arrives Dec. 4 via the band's own (p)doom records

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/King-Gizzard-The-Lizard-Wizard-2027.webp)

King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard have announced summer and fall North American tour dates for 2027.

The run is slated to open August 5 in Los Angeles, California, at Hollywood Forever Cemetery, where the Australian band will perform two consecutive nights. The summer itinerary also includes San Diego, Portland, Vancouver and Quincy, Washington, before wrapping August 27 at The Armory in Minneapolis. Four additional shows in Colorado Springs, Denver, Dillon and Reno have been added to the schedule.

The fall leg begins October 6 at Longhorn Backyard Amphitheater in Dallas, Texas. Stops in Atlanta, New Orleans, Toronto and Cleveland will follow, with the run concluding October 22 at Wintrust Arena in Chicago. Before the North American dates, the group will tour Europe and the United Kingdom during summer 2027.

The announcement also included details of *Entropy*, a companion to August’s *Alien Metal*, arriving December 4 through the band’s (p)doom records label.
