# Shakira’s Madrid Residency Draws More Than 700,000 Fans Across 13 Shows

## Summary

Shakira's Madrid residency drew more than 700,000 fans across 13 shows, underscoring continued strong demand for residency-format touring in major European markets.

### Key Points
- 13 shows in Madrid drew a combined 700,000+ attendees.
- Residency format continues to perform strongly for major touring artists.
- No ticketing-platform, pricing, or policy angle reported.

## Transcript

![Shakira | Photo via 165 Entertainment](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/shakira-1.webp)

Shakira | Photo via 165 Entertainment

Shakira has concluded her European residency in Madrid after 13 performances that attracted more than 700,000 fans, according to Live Nation.

Produced by Live Nation Spain, the residency took place at Macondo Park, a purpose-built 220,000-square-meter venue created specifically for the engagement. The concerts attracted fans from across Europe and beyond, with Live Nation describing the run as one of the largest live music events ever staged in Spain.

According to [Live Nation’s announcement](https://news.livenationentertainment.com/news/shakira-plays-to-more-than-700-000-fans-during-madrid-residency?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), the residency is expected to generate more than €200 million in economic activity for Madrid, reflecting the contribution of concertgoers to local businesses and tourism. The figure is a projection rather than a reported total of economic activity generated.
