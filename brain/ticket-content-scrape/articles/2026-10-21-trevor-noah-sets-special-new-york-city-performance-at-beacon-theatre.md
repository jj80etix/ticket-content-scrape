# Trevor Noah Sets Special New York City Performance At Beacon Theatre

## Summary

Trevor Noah will give a special all-new-material performance at New York's Beacon Theatre on December 15, with tickets sold via an artist presale (Oct. 22) and Ticketmaster presales/general on-sale (Oct. 23).

### Key Points
- Artist presale starts Oct. 22, 10am local; general on-sale Oct. 23, 10am ET via Ticketmaster.com.
- Announcement comes as Noah kicks off a sold-out Asia tour (Hong Kong, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur) plus a four-night Dubai run in November.

Trevor Noah is preparing for a special performance at New York City’s Beacon Theatre on Dec. 15. The show will feature all-new material from the Emmy Award-winning host of “The Daily Show.”

Tickets for the exclusive show will be available starting with the Artist presale on Oct. 22 at 10 a.m. local time on [Trevornoah.com](https://Trevornoah.com). Additional presales will run ahead of the general on sale beginning Oct. 23 at 10 am ET at [Ticketmaster.com](http://Ticketmaster.com).

Noah’s announcement comes as the comedian prepares to kick off his sold-out Asia tour, with stops in Hong Kong, Singapore and Kuala Lumpur. Noah also has a four-night run in Dubai in November.

The South African native most recently completed his West Coast comedy run in July, selling out a slew of shows, including a stop in Bend, Oregon, at the Hayden Homes Amphitheater, which grossed $325,837, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/227046).
