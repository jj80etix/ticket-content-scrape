# ‘Spring Awakening’ Revival Reveals Complete Off-Broadway Cast

## Summary

Full principal casting has been announced for the Off-Broadway revival of "Spring Awakening" at Studio Seaview, directed by Danya Taymor, with performances beginning November 27 and an official opening December 17.

### Key Points
- Tyler Jordan Wesley (Melchior) and Ashlyn Maddox (Wendla) lead, with Ryo Kamibayashi (Moritz) and Amalia Yoo (Ilse) rounding out principal leads.
- Danya Taymor directs; choreography by Celia Rowlson-Hall (her theatrical debut); music supervision by Or Matias.
- Original 2006 Broadway production (music by Duncan Sheik, book/lyrics by Steven Sater) won eight Tony Awards including Best Musical.
- Performances run at Studio Seaview starting Nov 27, opening Dec 17.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Spring-Awakening-Off-Broadway.webp)

Complete principal casting has been announced for the upcoming Off-Broadway revival of “Spring Awakening,” directed by Tony Award winner Danya Taymor.

Performances will begin November 27 at Studio Seaview, with an official opening scheduled for December 17.

Tyler Jordan Wesley will play Melchior opposite Ashlyn Maddox as Wendla. The principal company also includes Ryo Kamibayashi as Moritz and Amalia Yoo as Ilse.

The remaining principal cast features Jack Bateman as Georg, Jada Simone Clark as Thea, Leif Coomer as Ernst, Nando Morland as Otto, Antonio Porciello as Hänschen, Khadija Sankoh as Martha and Grace Slear as Anna.

The creative team includes scenic designers Christine Jones and Brett Banakis, costume designer Kaye Voyce, lighting designer Brian MacDevitt, sound designer Cody Spencer and special-effects designer Jeremy Chernick. Cynthia Meng will serve as music director and conductor.

Featuring music by Duncan Sheik and a book and lyrics by Steven Sater, “Spring Awakening” is based on Frank Wedekind’s 1891 play. The musical first opened on Broadway in 2006 with a cast that included Jonathan Groff, Lea Michele and John Gallagher Jr. It won eight Tony Awards, including Best Musical, Best Original Score and Best Book of a Musical.
