# Boardmasters Draws 158,000 Fans, Sets 2027 Return to Cornwall

## Summary

Boardmasters drew a record 158,000 attendees across five days at Watergate Bay, Cornwall, headlined by Fatboy Slim, Lily Allen and Kasabian, and confirmed its return to Newquay Aug 4-8, 2027 with Super Early Bird tickets already on sale.

### Key Points
- 2027 dates: Aug 4-8, Newquay; general on-sale begins Aug 14 (Super Early Bird already live).
- 2026 headliners: Fatboy Slim, Lily Allen, Kasabian; the Boardmasters Open surf competition was upgraded to a World Surf League QS 4,000 event.
- New ocean-protection initiative with Oceana UK; wellness/accessibility programming including a sensory calm space.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0838.jpeg)

Boardmasters wrapped another blockbuster edition on the Cornish coast, welcoming a cumulative 158,000 attendees across five days of music, surfing and seaside culture — and the festival is already looking ahead to 2027.

Fatboy Slim, Lily Allen and Kasabian topped the 2026 bill at Watergate Bay, while the Boardmasters Open brought world-class surfing back to Fistral Beach.

The festival will return to Newquay from August 4-8, 2027, with Super Early Bird tickets already available and the general on-sale beginning at 10 a.m. on Friday, August 14 [here](https://boardmasters.com/join-the-newsletter-2/).

Fatboy Slim kicked off the headline run on Friday, transforming the Main Stage into a sprawling outdoor dancefloor. Lily Allen took over Saturday night with a performance centered around her *West End Girl* album before Kasabian closed the Main Stage on Sunday.

The weekend’s Main Stage lineup also featured The Kooks, Loyle Carner and Nia Archives, along with Rudimental, REN, Tinie Tempah, Example and Dizzee Rascal.

Local talent was in the spotlight, too. Fletchr Fletchr opened the Main Stage on Friday, while Falmouth folk artist Essy Sparrow opened Sunday’s action after becoming the inaugural winner of the Boardmasters Breaking competition in partnership with BBC Introducing.

Elsewhere around the festival, Pendulum, Goldie and The Darkness appeared on the Mordros stage, while Surf Club 81 hosted sets from General Levy, Lens and Woody Cook against its cliffside Atlantic backdrop.

Ewan McVicar, Hamdi, Hannah Wants and Girls Don’t Sync were among the dance acts appearing at Unleashed, while the Keg & Pasty stage showcased Cornish artists, sea shanties and stand-up comedy.

“This year’s Boardmasters was everything we could have dreamed of when we started planning each edition, five days of incredible music, world-class surfing and that unmistakable Cornish spirit and sunshine that makes this festival what it is,” Festival Director Rob Spring said in a press release.

“Watching people come together on our coastline, from the Main Stage all the way down to the sensory calm space, is exactly why we do this,” Spring added. “We’re already excited to get started on 2027 and we can’t wait to welcome everyone back next year for what’s shaping up to be our biggest year yet.”

Music was only part of the draw.

The Boardmasters Open at Fistral Beach stepped up to a World Surf League QS 4,000 event this year, with France’s Sam Piter taking the Men’s Shortboard title and Spain’s Janire Gonzalez Etxabarri winning the Women’s Shortboard competition.

France’s Louis Marchiset earned his first Longboard Qualifying Series title in the Men’s division, while Alice Lemoigne of Reunion Island secured her seventh Boardmasters title in the Women’s competition.

Boardmasters also launched a new ocean protection initiative with Oceana UK. The View stage combined acoustic performances with protest artwork created by Falmouth University students calling for an end to destructive bottom trawling in UK Marine Protected Areas.

The festival’s wellness, food and accessibility programs rounded out the weekend, including yoga, sound and ice baths, local Cornish traders and a sensory calm space for neurodiverse guests.
