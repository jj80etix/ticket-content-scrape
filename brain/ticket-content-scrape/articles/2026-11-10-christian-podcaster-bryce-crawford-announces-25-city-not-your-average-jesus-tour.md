# Christian Podcaster Bryce Crawford Announces 25-City ‘Not Your Average Jesus Tour’

## Summary

Christian podcaster and evangelist Bryce Crawford announced a 25-city "Not Your Average Jesus Tour" of theaters this spring, billed as an immersive faith-focused gathering rather than a traditional service. The tour, promoted by TPR, runs March 11 (Tacoma) through April 18 (Los Angeles, the Wiltern).

### Key Points
- Promoted by TPR, which also books Christian-music tours for Forrest Frank, CeCe Winans and Brandon Lake.
- Venues include the Fox Theater (Atlanta) and the new venue The Truth (Nashville).
- Exclusive presale begins Nov. 12; public onsale Nov. 16 at 10 a.m. local time.

## Transcript

![Bryce](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Bryce.jpg)

Bryce Crawford, Christian evangelist and host of The Bryce Crawford Podcast, is bringing his “unfiltered, raw faith discussions” to theaters across the continent this spring for his “Not Your Average Jesus Tour.” Described as not a traditional church service — the show is billed as an “immersive live gathering built around authentic truth, real community, and meeting people right where they are.”

“We’re inviting Jesus into the room on this tour,” Crawford says in the announcement. “Our hope is that people leave these nights turning to Jesus for the first time or turning back with fresh eyes.” Drawing from Crawford’s viral street conversations, the tour aims to inspire and equip individuals to deepen their relationship with Christ, according to the announcement.

The tour is promoted by TPR, the promoter behind smash Christian music concert tours by artists including Forrest Frank, CeCe Winans, Brandon Lake and many others.

“The Not Your Average Jesus Tour” kicks off on Thursday, March 11 in Tacoma, with stops in cities including Portland, Denver, Atlanta, Tampa, Nashville and more before wrapping April 18 in Los Angeles at the historic Wiltern. The routing includes other large historic theaters such as the Fox Theater in Atlanta as well as the brand-new The Truth in Nashville. An exclusive presale begins Thursday, Nov. 12, with public onsale staring Monday, Nov. 16 at 10 a.m. local time.

Crawford, based in Los Angeles, says his life was changed following a transformative, supernatural encounter with Jesus at a Waffle House, after which he committed to full-time ministry. Crawford’s podcast is currently No. 2 on Spotify in the Religion & Spirituality category and frequently charts as a Top 100 USA Podcast on Spotify.

Crawford is managed by Divij Vaswani at Division Media and represented by 33 & West’s JJ Cassiere.
