# David Arquette Plots ‘Behind the Scream’ Screening Tour Amid Film’s 30th Anniversary

## Summary

Actor David Arquette announced a 14-date "Behind the Scream" tour marking Scream's 30th anniversary, screening the 1996 film followed by a Q&A at each stop, running October 8 (Roanoke, VA) through Halloween (Torrington, CT).

### Key Points
- 14-date tour, Oct. 8 – Oct. 31, pairs a Scream screening with an Arquette Q&A at each venue.
- Stops include Jacksonville, Akron, Bloomington, Austin and Schenectady, among others.
- Timed to Scream's 30th anniversary and the 2026 release of Scream 7.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/behind-the-scream.jpg)

In honor of *Scream* ‘s 30th anniversary this October, actor David Arquette has revealed a 14-date tour across the U.S., which will include a screening of the slasher film followed by a Q&A.

The 14-date “Behind the Scream” tour will kick-off on October 8 at the Berglund Performing Arts Theatre in Roanoke, Virginia on October 8. From there, he’ll head to Jacksonville, Akron, Bloomington, and Austin, appearing at theaters along the way like the Fine Arts Theatre at The Monument in Rapid City, Paramount Theatre Cedar Rapids, Lowell Memorial Auditorium, and Proctors Theatre in Schenectady. The brief run wraps-up in Torrington, Connecticut at Warner Theatre’s Oneglia Auditorium on Halloween.

Each tour date will begin with a showing of the 1996 horror film, followed by a talk with Arquette, where he will share behind-the-scenes stories from his *Scream* career. Audience members will also be able to participate in a Q&A.

Arquette appeared in the first five films of the *Scream* franchise, directed by Wes Craven, playing police officer Dwight “Dewey” Riley. His character was killed-off in the fifth film and 2022 reboot by Ghostface.
