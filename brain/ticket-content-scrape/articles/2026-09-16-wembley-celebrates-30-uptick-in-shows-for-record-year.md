# Wembley celebrates 30% uptick in shows for record year

## Summary

Wembley Stadium capped a record year with 34 concerts, a 30% year-over-year increase, drawing more than 2.5 million fans. The season included a 12-night Harry Styles run, the longest solo-artist stand in the venue's history.

### Key Points
- 34 shows in 2026, up 30% year-over-year, a record for the venue
- Over 2.5 million fans attended across the season
- Harry Styles' 12-night residency set a venue longevity record
- Diljit Dosanjh was among the season's featured headliners
