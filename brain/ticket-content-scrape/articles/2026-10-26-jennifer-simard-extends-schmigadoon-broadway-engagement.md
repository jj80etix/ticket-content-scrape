# Jennifer Simard Extends ‘Schmigadoon!’ Broadway Engagement

## Summary

Jennifer Simard has extended her run as Mildred Layton in Broadway's "Schmigadoon!" through November 29 at the Nederlander Theatre, while Juan Pablo Di Pace makes his Broadway debut as a temporary cast replacement through November 22. The Tony-winning musical continues its extended engagement through April 2027.

### Key Points
- Simard's extension runs through Nov. 29; Di Pace covers for Ivan Hernandez Oct. 24-Nov. 22.
- "Schmigadoon!" won four Tony Awards this season, including Best Musical.
- Production runs at the Nederlander Theatre through April 4, 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Schmigadoon.webp)

Jennifer Simard has added three weeks to her run in Broadway’s “Schmigadoon!” and will continue playing Mildred Layton through November 29 at the Nederlander Theatre.

Simard performs alongside Alex Brightman and Sara Chase as Josh Skinner and Melissa Gimble.

The extension coincides with a temporary change in another principal role. Juan Pablo Di Pace’s Broadway debut engagement as Doc runs October 24 through November 22. He is covering for original cast member Ivan Hernandez during a planned leave of absence. Hernandez is scheduled to return November 24.

The principal cast also includes Ann Harada as Florence Menlove, Brad Oscar as Mayor Menlove, Isabelle McCalla as Emma Tate, Maulik Pancholy as the Reverend Layton and the Leprechaun, Max Clayton as Danny Bailey, McKenzie Kurtz as Betsy and Ayaan Diop as Carson.

“Schmigadoon!” opened April 20 at the Nederlander Theatre and is scheduled to continue its extended Broadway engagement through April 4, 2027.

The production won four Tony Awards this year, tying “The Lost Boys” and “Ragtime” for the most wins among the season’s musicals. In addition to Best Musical, “Schmigadoon!” received awards for Cinco Paul’s book and original score, and for orchestrations by Doug Besterman and Mike Morris.

Christopher Gattelli directs and choreographs the Broadway production after overseeing its world premiere at Washington, D.C.’s Kennedy Center in 2025. Gattelli also choreographed the television series.
