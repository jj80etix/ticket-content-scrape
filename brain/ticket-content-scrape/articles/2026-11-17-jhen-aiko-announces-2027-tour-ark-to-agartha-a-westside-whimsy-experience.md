# Jhené Aiko Announces 2027 Tour: ‘Ark To Agartha: A Westside Whimsy Experience’

## Summary

Jhené Aiko announced a 37-date 2027 North American arena tour, "Ark to Agartha: A Westside Whimsy Experience," produced by Live Nation, running March 10 (St. Louis) through May 21 (Los Angeles) with Swae Lee and Karri on every date and H.E.R. joining select shows. The run follows her sold-out 2024 Magic Hour Tour and her 2026 Westside Whimsy album; artist presale starts Nov. 17-18, with a Verizon Shine loyalty presale and VIP packages also offered, ahead of Nov. 20 general onsale.

### Key Points
- 37-date arena tour, March 10-May 21, 2027; Live Nation-produced.
- Swae Lee and Karri on all dates; H.E.R. joins beginning March 26 (Brooklyn).
- Artist presale Nov. 17-18; Verizon Shine loyalty-program presale also offered; general onsale Nov. 20.
- VIP packages include a "soundbath" experience and pre-show lounge access via VIP Nation.
- Follows the sold-out 2024 Magic Hour Tour and her Westside Whimsy album, which topped the Billboard 200.

Jhené Aiko is taking her No. 1 album on the road with an arena tour called “Ark to Agartha: A Westside Whimsy Experience,” produced by Live Nation. The 37-date trek will find the songstress traveling across North America, kicking off in March. 10 on St. Louis, at the Enterprise Center. Aiko will close the tour on May 21 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. Special guest H.E.R. will join on select dates, while the tour will feature Swae Lee and Karri on all dates. See full tour routing below.

The tour builds on the remarkable success of Aiko’s 2024 27-market “Magic Hour Tour,” which marked her first headlining tour in seven years and sold out arenas across North America in support of an album released nearly five years earlier. According to [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/171391), Jhené sold out the entire tour with her Dec. 5 stop at the CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore and her Dec. 3 stop at the UBS Arena in Elmont, New York, which grossed $1,099,592 and $924,657, respectively.

Fans can sign up [HERE](https://www.jheneaiko.com/tour) for the artist presale beginning Nov. 17 at 10 a.m. ET. The artist presale begins Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. local time and Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time at [LiveNation.com](http://LiveNation.com).

Verizon will offer customers an exclusive presale for “Jhené Aiko Presents: Ark to Agartha – A Westside Whimsy Experience” in the U.S. through Verizon’s loyalty program, Verizon Shine. The presale for select shows runs from Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. Local Time to Thursday, November 19 at 10 PM Local Time. Verizon Shine awards customers with once-in-a-lifetime experiences, alongside daily drops including tickets to concerts and sporting events, exclusive merchandise, dining vouchers, gift cards and more. Opt in to Verizon Shine via the My Verizon App, and learn more about Verizon Shine here.
