# U2 Unveils European & UK Dates For ‘Carnaval De Luz Presents: The Greatest Show On Earth’ Tour

## Summary

U2 unveiled full UK and European stadium dates for its 2027 "Carnaval De Luz Presents: The Greatest Show On Earth" tour, running from Stockholm to Milan with Wolf Alice supporting. Tickets route through U2.com with staggered subscriber, Visa-cardholder and artist presales ahead of general onsale.

### Key Points
- Stadium stops: Stockholm, Paris, Lisbon, Amsterdam, London (Tottenham Hotspur Stadium), Berlin, Milan; more Spain dates to follow
- Follows a Latin America leg of the same tour
- Wolf Alice will support across the European run
- Presale sequence: U2.com subscriber presale Nov. 2, Visa cardholder access Nov. 4

## Transcript

U2 is bringing the Carnaval De Luz to the U.K. and Europe next spring and summer, with stadium shows in Sweden, France, the Netherlands and more.

After a run through Latin America, the 2027 European leg of “Carnaval De Luz presents: The Greatest Show On Earth” kicks off April 26 at Stockholm’s Strawberry Arena, then heads to Stade de France in Paris, Lisbon’s Estadio da Luz, and Johan Cruijff ArenA in Amsterdam. The band then visits London’s Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and Berlin’s Olympiastadion before wrapping up at Stadio San Siro in Milan, Italy. More dates in Spain will be announced soon.

Wolf Alice will be supporting U2’s European run.

Tickets for the U.K. and European concerts will first be available via [U2.com](https://www.u2.com/). A subscriber presale begins Nov. 2 at 10 a.m. local time. Visa cardholders can also access presale tickets from 10 a.m. local time until 10 p.m. on Nov. 4, while supplies last. Cardholders can purchase tickets at [visa.com/u2](https://www.visa.com/en-gb/partnerships/music/u2) during the presale window. Fans can also sign up for an artist presale via Ticketmaster that begins Nov. 5. Fans must sign up [here](https://signup.ticketmaster.co.uk/u2) by Nov. 1 at 10:59 GMT.

In addition to the U.K. and European leg, U2 announced additional dates in Latin America due to popular demand. Bono, The Edge, Adam Clayton and Larry Mullen Jr. added a fourth show at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros taking place March 2 and another night at São Paulo’s Estádio MorumBIS. The iconic band will also perform a third night at Estadio River Plate in Buenos Aires. Beck will support those shows.

U2’s last formal run was in 2023 and 2024 when the band opened Sphere in Las Vegas, a 40-date run that grossed $244,478,903 off 662,532 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice. The quartet will announce a return to North America in the coming weeks.

The band is managed by Full Stop Management.
