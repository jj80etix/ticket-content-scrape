# All Time Low Sets ‘So Wrong, It’s Right’ 20th Anniversary Show at Radio City

## Summary

All Time Low will play a one-night 20th-anniversary performance of *So Wrong, It's Right* at Radio City Music Hall on February 19, 2027, performing the full 2007 album plus catalog favorites. Presales begin September 29, with general onsale October 2.

### Key Points
- Show marks the 20th anniversary of the band's breakout album, released September 25, 2007.
- Artist presale September 29, local presales September 30–October 1, general onsale October 2 (all 10 a.m. ET).
- Band has several U.S. dates before the anniversary show, including Oceans Calling in Ocean City, MD (later canceled by weather).
- Band also joins Jonas Brothers at the Hollywood Bowl November 18–19 before the Radio City show.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/All-Time-Low-Radio-City-Music-Hall.webp)

All Time Low will celebrate the 20th anniversary of *So Wrong, It’s Right* with a one-night-only performance at New York City’s Radio City Music Hall next year.

The pop-punk band will take the stage February 19, 2027, performing the 2007 album in full along with additional songs from its catalog, according to an announcement Friday.

Artist presale tickets will become available September 29 at 10 a.m. ET, followed by local presales September 30 and October 1 at 10 a.m. ET. The general onsale begins October 2 at 10 a.m. ET.

Released September 25, 2007, *So Wrong, It’s Right* became one of the defining releases of All Time Low’s early career and includes “Dear Maria, Count Me In,” “Six Feet Under the Stars,” “Remembering Sunday” and “Poppin’ Champagne.”

“Twenty years later and ‘So Wrong, It’s Right’ is still some of our most impactful work as a band — it laid a foundation for us to grow into the band we are today and so many of these songs still hold a very important place in our live shows and with the fans,” singer and guitarist Alex Gaskarth said in the announcement.

Gaskarth said the album also became part of fans’ formative years at a time when the members of All Time Low were still young themselves.

“This is a celebration of where it all began, but also how far we’ve come,” he said.

Additionally, All Time Low also has a handful of U.S. dates remaining before the Radio City anniversary show. The band plays the Wellmont Theater in Montclair, New Jersey, on September 25 with Kiss the Scientist, followed by an appearance at Oceans Calling in Ocean City, Maryland, on September 26 and The Big E in West Springfield, Massachusetts, on September 27.
