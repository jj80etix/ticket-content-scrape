# AEG and O2 Extend London Venue’s Naming Rights Deal Through 2038

## Summary

AEG and O2/Virgin Media have extended their naming-rights partnership for London's The O2 through 2038, calling it the largest naming-rights renewal of its kind in UK history. The renewed deal expands the "Priority Tickets" early-access perk by 50% and, for the first time, extends Priority access to O2/Virgin Media customers at the venue's smaller indigo at The O2 space, ahead of The O2's 20th anniversary in 2027.

### Key Points
- 10-year naming-rights extension (through 2038) ahead of The O2's 20th anniversary in 2027; financial terms undisclosed but described as "major multi-million-pound."
- O2 Priority ticket access increased 50% for The O2 and extended for the first time to indigo at The O2.
- The O2 has hosted 100M+ visitors and 6,800+ events since opening in 2007.
- Partnership also includes connectivity infrastructure (5G DAS, Virgin Media gigabit Wi-Fi).

### Highlights
- A 10-year renewal with an explicit 50% increase in reserved early-access ticket inventory shows a telecom-sponsor loyalty/access program scaling up rather than winding down after nearly 20 years.
- Extending Priority access down to the venue's smaller indigo space is a deliberate funnel strategy — building loyalty with emerging artists' audiences before they reach arena-size shows.

### Proposed Solution
AEG/O2's approach ties a telecom carrier's subscriber base directly to guaranteed early ticket access at scale, rather than a points-based loyalty program. This is an evolve signal for Etix: watch whether sponsor- or carrier-linked priority-access models (not just point programs) become a more common loyalty mechanism clients request, particularly as a way to pre-qualify and reward a venue's or promoter's most consistent audience.

AEG and O2 have extended their naming rights partnership for London’s The O2 through 2038, securing the venue’s name while expanding early ticket access for eligible customers.

The agreement adds another 10 years to the partnership ahead of the venue’s 20th anniversary in 2027. Financial terms were not disclosed, though AEG described the extension as “a major multi-million-pound” agreement and the largest naming rights renewal of its kind in U.K. history.

The deal includes a 50% increase in the number of tickets available for shows at The O2 through Priority Tickets. O2 and Virgin Media broadband customers will also receive Priority access to performances at indigo at The O2 for the first time.

“The renewal of our partnership with O2 reflects the enduring strength of a relationship built on trust, innovation and a shared ambition to deliver exceptional live entertainment experiences,” said Paul Samuels, president of global partnerships at AEG International.
