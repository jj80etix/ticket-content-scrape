# Legends Global CEO Dan Levy to step down

## Summary

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down after more than two and a half years leading the venue-management and live-entertainment services company, during which it completed and integrated its acquisition of ASM Global. Levy will stay on until a successor is named; longtime executive Chad Estis has been named president to help oversee the transition.

### Key Points
- Levy joined Legends in March 2024, became CEO in April 2024, and oversaw completion/integration of the ~$2.3B ASM Global acquisition.
- No timetable set for the transition; Levy remains in place until a successor is hired.
- Chad Estis named president, will help manage the transition.
- Company has not publicly announced the change as of reporting; website still listed prior titles.

Legends Global CEO Dan Levy is stepping down after just over two years leading the firm, *IQ* can confirm.

He joined premium experiences brand Legends in March 2024 as interim president, taking over as [CEO](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2024/04/legends-installs-ex-meta-executive-dan-levy-as-ceo/) a month later and helping steer the company through its $2.4 billion acquisition and subsequent merger with ASM Global, which was [finalised early last year](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/09/legends-global-takes-flight-this-is-one-company/).

Announcing his departure with an internal memo yesterday (23 November), Levy will stay on board until a replacement is hired.

In the mix, it was also announced that EVP and CRO Chad Estis will now become president of Legends Global. He will also be involved in guiding the team through its CEO transition.

“As we begin the succession process, I want you to know how strongly I believe in this business and in each of you,” Levy said in the memo. “I remember the ‘wow’ I felt at my first baseball game as a kid. I still feel the same way when I visit with each of you and our partners at the incredible events we host, including the Super Bowl, World Series, World Cup, Ryder Cup, the biggest concert tours, and major conventions. My office is covered with pictures from those visits, so I will never forget who we serve.

“Thank you for everything you’ve done to make these past few years such a success,” he concluded.

Legends Global’s network encompasses more than 450 venues worldwide, with recent announcements including Vietnam’s VinFast – Trong Dong Stadium, expected to be the [world’s largest](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/worlds-largest-stadium-to-make-vietnam-key-routing-spot-for-live-legends-global-says/), and London’s [Outernet](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/01/legends-global-partners-with-cutting-edge-outernet-london/), along with a host of sporting partnerships.

Levy joined the company after having spent almost 15 years at Meta/Facebook, and took over from Shervin Mirhashemi as CEO in April 2024. When he spoke to *IQ* last year, he said technology was the “ [biggest area for advancement](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/01/dan-levy-legends-global-interview/) ” in the entertainment industry.
