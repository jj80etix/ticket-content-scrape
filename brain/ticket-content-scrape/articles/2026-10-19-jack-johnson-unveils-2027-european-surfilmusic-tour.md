# Jack Johnson Unveils 2027 European ‘Surfilmusic’ Tour

## Summary

Jack Johnson will bring his "Surfilmusic" tour to Europe in summer 2027, playing 10 shows across seven countries from June 23 to July 15. Several dates feature Donavon Frankenreiter and G. Love as a duo, with Ben Harper joining for the tour's 20th-anniversary O2 London stop. The European leg follows the conclusion of Johnson's North American run and precedes additional international dates.

### Key Points
- 10 shows, June 23 - July 15, 2027, across Germany, Belgium, Netherlands, France, England, Spain, Portugal.
- Opens at Munich's Tollwood Festival; closes at Ageas Cooljazz in Cascais, Portugal.
- July 4 O2 London show (20th-anniversary celebration) adds Ben Harper to the bill.
- Presales began Oct. 19; general onsale Oct. 22 (England/France) and Oct. 23 (other markets).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Jack-Johnson.webp)

Jack Johnson will take his “Surfilmusic” tour to Europe in summer 2027, announcing 10 performances across seven countries.

The European leg kicks off June 23 at Munich’s Tollwood Festival and is scheduled to wrap up July 15 at Ageas Cooljazz in Cascais, Portugal. The tour also includes stops in Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, France, England and Spain.

Frankenreiter and G. Love will perform together in a duo format on select dates. Their appearances begin June 24 in Berlin and continue through Brussels, Amsterdam, Paris and Johnson’s three English shows. Harper will also join the bill for the July 4 concert at London’s O2, which forms part of the arena’s 20th anniversary celebrations.

Presales began October 19 in select markets, with additional presale opportunities scheduled through October 23. General ticket sales open October 22 for the English and French performances. Shows in Germany, Belgium, the Netherlands, Spain and Portugal go on sale October 23.
