# Guns N’ Roses Added to ‘Aerosmith & Friends’ Lineup

## Summary

Guns N' Roses is joining an expanded "Aerosmith & Friends" guest lineup for two Hollywood Bowl shows (Oct. 29 and Nov. 1), part of Aerosmith's return to live performance after retiring from touring in 2024 following Steven Tyler's vocal injury.

### Key Points
- Guest lineup also includes the Black Crowes, Run-DMC, Heart, Billy Idol, Sammy Hagar and Eric Church, among others.
- Don Was serves as music director; nightly lineups will vary and remain subject to change near showtime.
- Aerosmith canceled its "Peace Out" farewell tour in 2024 after Tyler's September 2023 larynx fracture; these two shows are standalone returns, not a broader tour.
- General public tickets go on sale via Ticketmaster Oct. 10 at 10 a.m. PT; Aero Force One fan club presale ran Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Aerosmith-Friends.webp)

Guns N’ Roses will join Aerosmith for two concerts at the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles on October 29 and November 1, expanding the guest lineup for the band’s return to full-length live shows.

The band joins a lineup that includes the Black Crowes, Run-DMC, Heart, Billy Idol, Sammy Hagar and Eric Church. Other announced participants include Tom Morello, Red Hot Chili Peppers drummer Chad Smith, Alice in Chains members Jerry Cantrell and Mike Inez, and Godsmack singer Sully Erna.

Andrew Watt, Marcus King, Extreme guitarist Nuno Bettencourt, Train frontman Pat Monahan, Steve Stevens, Adam Wakeman, Audley Freed and Kenny Aronoff are also scheduled to participate. Aerosmith has said additional guests and surprise appearances will be announced.

Don Was will serve as music director for the concerts, which will celebrate Aerosmith’s catalog. Although Guns N’ Roses have been announced for both dates, the overall guest lineup will differ between performances. Final nightly lineups are expected at or near showtime and remain subject to change.

The Hollywood Bowl dates follow an extended interruption to Aerosmith’s live schedule. The band’s “Peace Out” farewell tour stopped after three concerts when singer Steven Tyler fractured his larynx during a September 9, 2023, performance in Elmont, New York.

Aerosmith initially postponed the remaining shows and later announced rescheduled dates. In August 2024, the band canceled the tour and retired from touring, saying a full recovery from Tyler’s vocal injury was not possible. The newly announced concerts establish two return performances, with no broader tour outlined in the announcement.

Tickets go on sale to the general public through Ticketmaster on Saturday, October 10, at 10 a.m. Pacific time. Aero Force One members have an exclusive artist presale window on Friday, October 9, from 10 to 11 a.m. Pacific time.
