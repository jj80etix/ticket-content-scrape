# Australian Indie Rock Duo Royel Otis Signs with WME

## Summary

Australian indie rock duo Royel Otis expanded its representation deal with WME to cover North America, South America and all territories outside Europe. The move follows a breakout two years that included Coachella, Glastonbird-circuit festivals and headline arena/amphitheater dates.

### Key Points
- WME's Kirk Sommer leads the expanded representation; Brett Murrihy continues to represent the band in Australia, Asia and the Middle East
- Debut album PRATTS & PAIN (2024) won four ARIA Awards; follow-up hickey released in 2025
- 2026 highlights include a sunset Coachella set, Foo Fighters stadium support, and headline shows at Prospect Park, Salt Shed and the Greek Theatre

Australian indie rock duo Royel Otis has expanded its relationship with WME to include representation in North America, South America, and all territories outside of Europe by a team led by Kirk Sommer, Global Head of Music for WME. Brett Murrihy, Head of Asia Pacific for WME, will continue to represent the band in Australia, Asia and the Middle East.

Royel Otis’ early charmers like “Oysters in My Pocket” and “Sofa King” made the duo breakout acts and set the stage for their debut album, *PRATTS & PAIN* (2024). The album was recorded in south London with Dan Carey (Wet Leg, Fontaines D.C.) and named after the pub down the road where they’d finish lyrics over a pint. It won four ARIA Awards, including Best Group and Best Rock Album. Their triple j Like A Version of Sophie Ellis-Bextor’s “Murder on the Dancefloor” went to #1 at U.S. alternative commercial radio and #2 in triple j’s Hottest 100. Their SiriusXM take on the Cranberries’ “Linger” gave them their first Billboard Hot 100 entry. hickey followed in 2025, and lead single “moody” held #1 on Billboard’s Adult Alternative Airplay chart for seven weeks.

In 2025, Royel Otis performed at Glastonbury, Governors Ball, Lollapalooza and Fuji Rock, and played three nights at O2 Academy Brixton and their biggest hometown shows yet. This year, they expanded their international reach, playing mainstage Lollapalooza in Argentina, Chile and Brazil, then Estéreo Picnic and Tecate Pa’l Norte. In 2026, they also made their sunset Coachella debut, played stadiums with Foo Fighters, and headlined nights at Prospect Park, Salt Sheds and the Greek Theatre.
