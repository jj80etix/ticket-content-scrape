# Off-Broadway’s ‘Spring Awakening’ Revival Adds Lilli Cooper and George Abud

## Summary

The Off-Broadway revival of "Spring Awakening" has added Lilli Cooper and George Abud to its cast ahead of previews beginning November 27 at Studio Seaview. The limited engagement, directed by Danya Taymor, has already been extended through April 4, 2027, with tickets for the added dates now on sale.

### Key Points
- Lilli Cooper and George Abud join the Off-Broadway "Spring Awakening" revival cast.
- Previews begin Nov. 27; opening night Dec. 17 at Studio Seaview.
- Run already extended through April 4, 2027; tickets for added dates on sale now.
- Directed by Tony winner Danya Taymor, choreography by Celia Rowlson-Hall.
- Existing cast includes Tyler Jordan Wesley, Ashlyn Maddox, and Ryo Kamibayashi.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Spring-Awakening-Off-Broadway.webp)

Lilli Cooper and George Abud have joined the cast of the upcoming Off-Broadway revival of “Spring Awakening,” which begins performances November 27 at Studio Seaview in New York. Cooper will play the Adult Women, while Abud will take on the Adult Men.

Opening night is scheduled for December 17. The limited engagement has already been extended through April 4, 2027, with tickets for the additional performances now on sale.

Tony Award winner Danya Taymor directs the revival, with choreography by Celia Rowlson-Hall and music supervision by Or Matias. The production brings Steven Sater and Duncan Sheik’s musical back to an Off-Broadway setting, following its original New York staging and two Broadway productions.

The previously announced company includes Tyler Jordan Wesley as Melchior, Ashlyn Maddox as Wendla and Ryo Kamibayashi as Moritz. Khadija Sankoh will play Martha, the role Cooper originated.
