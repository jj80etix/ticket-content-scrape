# Modest Mouse Kicks Guitarist Off Tour After He Allegedly Sent Inappropriate Messages to Minor

## Summary

Modest Mouse has removed guitarist Simon O'Connor from its ongoing tour after screenshots surfaced showing him sending inappropriate messages to a 16-year-old fan via social media; O'Connor apologized and said he mistakenly believed he was messaging someone else. Seth Jabour of Les Savy Fav will fill in as the tour resumes Aug. 4, running through a Shaky Knees festival appearance on Aug. 20.

### Key Points
- Guitarist Simon O'Connor (with the band since 2021) removed from the tour following allegations he DM'd inappropriate content to a 16-year-old.
- Seth Jabour (Les Savy Fav) is filling in on guitar for the remainder of the run.
- O'Connor publicly apologized, saying he mistook the fan for a different, previously provocative account.
- Tour resumes Aug. 4 and wraps Aug. 20 at Atlanta's Shaky Knees festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Modest-Mouse-2026.webp)

Modest Mouse guitarist Simon O’Connor has officially been booted from the band’s ongoing tour after news surfaced that he allegedly sent inappropriate messages to a minor on social media.

The band shared the news in an Instagram post, noting that “Simon O’Connor will not be joining us on the upcoming tour.”

“The good news is that Seth Jabour of Les Savy Fav will be jumping in to play guitar,” the band said. “We look forward to being out there with you all.”

Earlier this week, screenshots began to circulate online that showed O’Connor sending inappropriate messages to a young fan. The claims started in the r/ModestMouse subreddit, where a fan claimed that O’Connor direct messaged her a sticker and then sent an explicit meme before deleting the picture and later apologizing for the message.
