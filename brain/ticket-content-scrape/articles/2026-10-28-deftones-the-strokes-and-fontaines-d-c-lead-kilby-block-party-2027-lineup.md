# Deftones, The Strokes and Fontaines D.C. Lead Kilby Block Party 2027 Lineup

## Summary

Kilby Block Party's 2027 lineup is led by Deftones, The Strokes and Fontaines D.C., running May 14–16 at Utah State Fairpark in Salt Lake City, with Slayyyter, Jungle, IDLES and Spoon also featured. Ticket presale begins Oct. 28, general onsale Oct. 29.

### Key Points
- 2027 festival runs May 14–16 at Utah State Fairpark, Salt Lake City.
- Top-billed acts: Deftones, The Strokes, Fontaines D.C., Slayyyter, Jungle, IDLES.
- Presale begins Oct. 28 at noon MT; general onsale Oct. 29 at 10 a.m. PT.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Kilby-Block-Party-.webp)

Kilby Block Party has announced its 2027 lineup, with Deftones, The Strokes and Fontaines D.C. among the top acts scheduled to perform May 14 to 16 at Utah State Fairpark in Salt Lake City.

Slayyyter, Jungle and IDLES also lead the bill for the three-day festival. Other announced performers include Spoon, Kim Gordon, Wednesday, Lil Yachty, Julien Baker and Atlas Sound. Wolf Parade, Iceage, Westside Cowboy, Weyes Blood, Hot Chip and Phantogram are also scheduled to appear.

**| RELATED:**[**Innings Festival 2027 Unveils Headliners The Strokes, Pitbull and Alanis Morissette**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/innings-festival-2027-unveils-headliners-the-strokes-pitbull-and-alanis-morissette/) **|**

The lineup continues with Kurt Vile and the Violators, Silversun Pickups, Joyce Manor, Two Door Cinema Club, Twin Peaks, Mercury Rev, Ratboys and Lip Critic, among others.

Elsewhere on the bill, Jungle, Hot Chip and Phantogram add to the festival’s electronic and dance-oriented offerings, while Lil Yachty brings a hip-hop presence.

A ticket presale is scheduled to begin Wednesday, October 28, at noon Mountain Time, with advance registration available through the festival’s presale form. General ticket sales follow Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. Pacific Time.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [kilbyblockparty.com](http://kilbyblockparty.com).
