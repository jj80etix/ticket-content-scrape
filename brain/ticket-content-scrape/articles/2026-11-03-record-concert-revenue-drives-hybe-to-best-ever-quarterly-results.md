# Record concert revenue drives HYBE to best-ever quarterly results

## Summary

South Korean entertainment company HYBE reported its best-ever quarterly results, with concert revenue up 196% year-over-year to KRW 726.8 billion (€483.3M), driven largely by BTS's U.S. and European Arirang World Tour legs. Merchandise and licensing revenue also hit a quarterly record at KRW 334.6 billion.

### Key Points
- Q3 operating profit reached KRW 188.5 billion on KRW 1.526 trillion in turnover.
- BTS drew roughly 70,000 fans per show during the quarter; KATSEYE sold out all 31 shows across 27 cities on its tour.
- HYBE credits part of the merchandise growth to Weverse Pickup, which lets fans order online and collect at the venue.
- KATSEYE will expand to an 11-city Asia/Oceania arena tour starting January 2027, including Lollapalooza India.

Concert revenue for the South Korean giant rose 196% year-on-year, powered by BTS' Arirang Tour

South Korean entertainment giant HYBE has reported record quarterly revenue and operating profit, driven by its concert revenue almost tripling year-on-year in Q3.

The full-service company that blends management, promotion, label services, and marketing for acts including BTS and KATSEYE, reported KRW 188.5 billion (€125.7 million) in earnings for the period, based on KRW 1.5257 trillion (€1.017 billion) in turnover in Q3. It attributed much of this success to US and European legs of BTS’ ongoing *Arirang* World Tour.

Across all of its acts, concert revenue reached KRW 726.785 billion (€483.3 million), 196% up from KRW 244.986 billion (€162.9 million) in the same period last year, and up from KRW 647.699 billion (€430.7 million) in Q2 of this year, when BTS’ *Arirang* Tour began.

“Concert revenue reached a quarterly record of KRW 726.8 billion (€483.3m), led by BTS’ European and U.S. legs of its world tour, which drew approximately 70,000 fans per show during the quarter,” HYBE said, adding: “Tours by ENHYPEN, LE SSERAFIM, &TEAM, KATSEYE, CORTIS and other HYBE Music Group artists also contributed to the quarter’s performance.”

> “Concert revenue reached a quarterly record of KRW 726.8 billion (€483.3m)”

Merchandise revenues also enjoyed a record quarter, after one million fans bought two millions units of merchandise across the 31 shows of BTS’ US run.

“Merchandise and licensing revenue reached a quarterly record of KRW 334.6 billion (€222.5 million), including KRW 107 billion (€71.2 million) from BTS’ North American tour merchandise,” the company said, attributing this uptick to “localised products, a diversified production network and Weverse Pickup, which allows fans to order online and collect merchandise at concert venues.

It added it “plans to apply these locally managed planning, production and supply chain capabilities to touring artists across its labels”.

Outside of BTS, KATSEYE also sold out all 31 shows across 27 cities on its European and North American tour. HYBE said the group will expand its touring activities to Asia and Oceania with an 11-city arena tour beginning in January 2027, and will headline Lollapalooza India.
