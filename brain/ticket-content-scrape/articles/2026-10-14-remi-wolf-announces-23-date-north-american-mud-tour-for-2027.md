# Remi Wolf Announces 23-Date North American ‘Mud Tour’ for 2027

## Summary

Remi Wolf has announced a 23-date North American "Mud Tour" for 2027. The piece covers the tour announcement and routing.

### Key Points
- Remi Wolf announces 23-date "Mud Tour" across North America for 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Remi-Wolf-Mud-Tour.webp)

Remi Wolf has announced a 23-date North American headlining tour for 2027, with performances scheduled across the United States and Canada.

“The Mud Tour” opens February 23 at The Anthem in Washington, D.C., and wraps up May 22 at Seattle’s WaMu Theater.

Following the Washington opener, Wolf will perform at Philadelphia’s The Met on February 26 and Radio City Music Hall on February 27. The tour continues to Boston’s MGM Music Hall at Fenway on March 2 before crossing into Canada for a March 5 appearance at Massey Hall.

March dates also include Detroit’s Masonic Temple Theatre, Chicago’s The Salt Shed and Minneapolis’ The Armory. Performances in Milwaukee, St. Louis and Cincinnati follow before the first leg closes with concerts at Nashville’s The Truth on March 19 and Atlanta’s Coca-Cola Roxy on March 20.

Wolf returns to the road May 6 at Austin’s Moody Amphitheater at Waterloo Park. The Texas stretch continues with shows at Houston’s 713 Music Hall on May 7 and Toyota Music Factory in Irving on May 8.

The second leg then heads to Tulsa, Kansas City and the Des Moines area before a May 16 performance at Red Rocks. The final week includes KettleHouse Amphitheater in Montana on May 19 and Vancouver’s PNE Forum on May 21, followed by the Seattle finale.

Emma Ogier will support the February and March performances, while Annabelle Dinda joins Wolf for the May dates. The announcement accompanies the October 9 release of *Mud* through Island Records.

Before the North American tour begins, Wolf has a December run in Australia. That schedule includes Spilt Milk festival appearances in Canberra, Gold Coast, Geelong and Perth, alongside headlining performances at Sydney’s Enmore Theatre on December 15 and Forum Melbourne on December 17. Leyla Ebrahimi will support the two theater shows.

Tickets go on sale to the general public October 16 at 10 a.m. local time. An artist presale begins October 13 at 10 a.m. local time and runs through October 15 at 10 p.m. A separate U.S. presale through the Verizon Shine loyalty program began October 12 at 10 a.m. local time.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [remiwolf.com](http://remiwolf.com).
