# New venue set to open in Kuala Lumpur

## Summary

The Jeffrey Cheah Performing Arts Centre, a new 1,200-seat venue in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, is set to open August 15 with a programme spanning theatre, music, dance, and comedy. The centre also includes a 150-seat experimental theatre and will open with a benefit performance supporting Child Aid Asia Malaysia.

### Key Points
- Main venue seats 1,200, plus a 150-seat experimental theatre, hosted at Sunway City in Kuala Lumpur.
- Named for Sunway Group founder Jeffrey Cheah.
- Opening show "AniMemori For Real" benefits Child Aid Asia Malaysia.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Jeffrey Cheah Performing Arts Centre is nearing its opening night on Saturday (August 15) in the Malaysian capital of Kuala Lumpur.**

The 1,200-seat venue hosted a tour for industry professionals prior to the beginning of its artistic programme.

In addition to the main proscenium theatre, it also features a 150-seat experimental theatre with the two designed to serve the full spectrum of performing arts from large-scale international touring productions to original Malaysian works.

The venue is named after Tan Sri Dato’ Seri Sir Dr. Jeffrey Cheah KBE AO, founder of Sunway Group and the Jeffrey Cheah Foundation.

The first show at the centre is AniMemori For Real, which celebrates the music and stories that have shaped generations of anime fans.

It is being held in support of Child Aid Asia Malaysia with a portion of every ticket sold going towards the charity which aims to empower underprivileged children through education and community development.

“We wanted the opening season to reflect who we are and who we aspire to be – a home for Malaysian artists and a gateway to the best of global performance,” said producing artistic director Melissa Teoh, as reported by [The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/lifestyle/culture/2026/04/29/jeffrey-cheah-performing-arts-centre-in-sunway-city-kl-set-to-open-this-august#goog_rewarded).

“Our programme reflects this dual commitment, bringing together works rooted in our cultural landscape alongside international productions that broaden perspectives and set new benchmarks for excellence.

“We believe a performing arts centre should reflect the richness of the community it serves. Our work spans theatre, music, dance, film, comedy and new forms, creating space for diverse voices and audiences.”
