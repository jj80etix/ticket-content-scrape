# Neon City Festival In Las Vegas Expands Lineup, Adding Big Boi, Cobra Starship & More

## Transcript

![image](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/image.jpeg)

Neon City Festival, a large-scale free event in Las Vegas, just got bigger. The festival added Big Boi, Cobra Starship, Freddie Gibbs, Jayce Manor, Three 6 Mafia and more to the stacked bill.

The artists join previously announced headliners Kaskade Subtronics and The Fray, and they all will be performing across four stages—at Downtown Las Vegas Events Center and on Freemont Street Experience’s Main Street, First Street and Third Street—Nov. 20-22. Additional acts will be revealed in a later announcement.

“This year’s lineup brings together artists people have grown up listening to and new voices they may be discovering for the first time,” said Jeff Victor, CEO of Neon City Festival. “That mix is part of what makes Neon City Festival special. You can come downtown for a favorite act, discover a local band and experience an entire weekend of live music without purchasing a festival ticket.”

In its third year, Neon City Festival features local food and beverage, art installations and immersive entertainment. The 2026 edition is presented in partnership with Fremont Street Experience, Audacy and Las Vegas Convention and Visitors Authority.

Neon City Festival is also welcoming We The Beat and Unity Events as partners to build community collaboration.

For additonal information about the event and updates, visit [neoncityfestival.com](https://www.neoncityfestival.com/).
