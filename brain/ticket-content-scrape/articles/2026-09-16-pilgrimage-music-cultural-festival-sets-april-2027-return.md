# Pilgrimage Music & Cultural Festival Sets April 2027 Return

## Summary

Pilgrimage Music & Cultural Festival returns to Franklin, Tennessee, on April 10-11, 2027, after a one-year pause, shifting from its traditional fall slot to spring. It will be the 12th edition of the festival at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm.

### Key Points
- Returns April 10-11, 2027, at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm near Nashville
- The festival skipped 2026 and is moving from a fall to a spring date
- Lineup, ticket prices, and onsale dates have not yet been announced

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Pilgrimage-Music-Cultural-Festival.webp)

Pilgrimage Music & Cultural Festival will return to Franklin, Tennessee, in April 2027, ending a one-year pause and moving the event from its customary fall position on the calendar.

Organizers announced that the two-day festival will take place April 10 and 11 at The Park at Harlinsdale Farm. The 230-acre former horse farm, located about 20 miles south of Nashville, has served as Pilgrimage’s home since the event began in 2015.

The 2027 gathering will be the festival’s 12th edition. A performer lineup, ticket prices and on-sale dates have not yet been released. Fans can register through the festival’s website to receive text updates as additional information becomes available.

Pilgrimage did not stage an event in 2026. When the break was announced, organizers said the time away would be used to prepare new sights, sounds and surprises for the festival’s return. The April dates also represent a notable scheduling change for an event traditionally held in September.

Notably, Pilgrimage has faced interruptions before the 2026 hiatus. Severe weather forced organizers to end the 2018 festival early, while the 2020 edition was canceled during the COVID-19 pandemic. The event later resumed at Harlinsdale Farm and continued through 2025.

The festival last welcomed fans in September 2025. John Mayer and Kings of Leon led that year’s bill, which also included Turnpike Troubadours, Young the Giant, Father John Misty, Sam Fender, Grace Potter, Better Than Ezra, Colony House, Maggie Rose and other acts.

Better Than Ezra frontman and Franklin resident Kevin Griffin co-founded Pilgrimage with W. Brandt Wood and Michael Whelan. Griffin conceived the festival after seeing the natural amphitheaters and open landscape of Harlinsdale Farm while on a morning run. The founders sought to build an event that joined music with the history and character of Middle Tennessee.
