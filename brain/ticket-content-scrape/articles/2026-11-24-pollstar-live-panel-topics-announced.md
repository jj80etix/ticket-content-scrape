# Pollstar Live! Panel Topics Announced

## Summary

Pollstar Live! has announced the panel topics for its upcoming industry conference. The piece lists the sessions planned for the event.

### Key Points
- Pollstar Live! conference panel topics announced.
- Standard industry-conference programming news.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-2.png)

Details for the next edition of Pollstar Live! are coming together with topics unveiled for the 2027 conference.

Presented by Marsh and Ticketmaster, Pollstar Live! 2027 is scheduled April 13-15 in Indian Wells, California and marks the conference’s debut in the greater Palm Springs area.

The world’s largest gathering of live entertainment professionals promises three days of programming, networking and celebration.

This week’s announcement notes that veterans from the live entertainment industry will share insights, strategies, and expertise to make the most of 2027 and set a strong foundation for 2028. Attendees can expect impactful panels, strategic sessions, and on-trend takeaways to put into play right away.

Topics Include:

Bookings in Large, Outdoor, & International Venues  
Indie Venue Trends  
Production Budgeting  
Marketing with New Media  
Venue and Tour Relations  
Ticketing Trends  
Tour Merchandise Strategy  
Tech & AI  
Sustainability in Touring  
Laws Impacting Live Entertainment

Registration is open now at Pollstar.Live, with early bird pricing available through Nov. 30.

The early bird rate is $1,199 per person for Pollstar Live! and $499 per person for Production Live! The pass for Pollstar Live! includes Production Live!

Host hotel details will be announced soon, with keynote speakers, panelists and experiences throughout Greater Palm Springs announced in the coming months.

Visit [pollstar.live/](https://pollstar.live/) to register and for more information.
