# David Lee Roth Cancels Majority of Summer Tour Dates

## Summary

David Lee Roth canceled the majority of his remaining summer tour dates. The report covers which shows are affected without a stated reason from Roth or his camp.

### Key Points
- Majority of remaining summer 2026 tour dates called off.
- No public explanation given for the cancellations at time of reporting.
- Article lists the specific affected tour stops.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/David-Lee-Roth.webp)

David Lee Roth has cancelled a handful of upcoming live performances this summer.

The former Van Halen frontman was scheduled to appear at venues like Louisville’s Iroquois Amphitheater, Astro Amphitheater in Nebraska, and The Edge Pavilion in Laughlin on the “Don’t Love Me, Rent Me” tour, though the dates were quietly taken down from Roth’s schedule last week. The only remaining upcoming performance is a gig at the Sturgis Motorcycle Rally in South Dakota on August 7.

Roth has not publicly commented on the cancellations at this time.

Sammy Hager, who later denied Van Halen, took to social need and publicly addressed Roth, noting that he received a call from Michael Anthony about the tour getting cancelled and “I wanna say from the bottom of my heart, I’m sorry.”
