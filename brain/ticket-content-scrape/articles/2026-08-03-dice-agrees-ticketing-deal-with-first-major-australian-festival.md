# Dice agrees ticketing deal with first major Australian festival

## Summary

Dice has been named exclusive primary ticketing partner for Wildlands, marking the discovery-platform's first major Australian festival deal. Wildlands, produced by Untitled Group — Australia's largest independently owned live music and events company — runs New Year's Eve in Brisbane and January 2, 2027 in Perth, with a lineup including Skepta and John Summit. Presale opened August 3 with general onsale August 6.

### Key Points
- Dice is exclusive primary ticketing partner for Wildlands Festival (Brisbane, Dec. 31; Perth, Jan. 2, 2027).
- Untitled Group produces the festival; its portfolio also includes Beyond The Valley, Pitch Music & Arts, and Ability Fest.
- Presale began August 3; general onsale begins August 6.
- Lineup includes Skepta, John Summit, Disco Lines, Hannah Laing, Mia Archives, and Ewan McVicar.
- Quoted: Courtney Tahar (head of ticketing, Untitled Group) and Andrew Foggin (CCO, Dice).

### Highlights
- Dice, a Q12 Tier 1 primary-ticketing competitor, wins its first major Australian festival contract, expanding beyond its core UK/Europe/US footprint.
- The deal is with Australia's largest independent live-events promoter (Untitled Group), signaling Dice's push into new geographic markets via independent-promoter relationships rather than major-label/venue chains.

### Industry Problem
Independent festival promoters are choosing discovery-oriented primary ticketing platforms over legacy incumbents, suggesting demand for ticketing partners that promoters see as aligned with fan experience and community-building rather than pure transaction processing.

### Proposed Solution
Dice's answer is direct expansion into new territories through exclusive deals with independent promoters, leaning on its discovery/fan-experience positioning; Etix should monitor whether Dice's Australian entry signals a broader push into markets where Etix also competes for independent-promoter business, and track deal terms as a competitive benchmark.

**Dice has been appointed the exclusive primary ticketing partner for Widlands Festival, marking the discovery platform’s first major Australian festival deal.**

Dice gets that – and that’s exactly why we wanted them involved,” said Courtney Tahar, head of ticketing at Untitled Group.

Untitled Group is Australia’s largest independently owned live music and events company, with its portfolio of events including Beyond The Valley, Pitch Music & Arts and Ability Fest, as well as Wildlands.

“Australia has one of the most passionate live music audiences in the world, and Wildlands reflects that energy. Partnering with Untitled Group on this is a big moment for Dice, and we’re looking forward to connecting their fanbase through our platform,” added Andrew Foggin, chief commercial officer at Dice.
