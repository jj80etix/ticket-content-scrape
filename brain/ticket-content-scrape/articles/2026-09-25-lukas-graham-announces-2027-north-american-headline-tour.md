# Lukas Graham Announces 2027 North American Headline Tour

## Summary

Lukas Graham announced a 13-date 2027 North American headline tour in support of his fifth studio album "Good Times," running March 2 (Dallas) through April 11 (Chicago). Tickets go on sale September 25 through Graham's website, with $1 per ticket benefiting Plan International.

### Key Points
- Tour follows Graham's withdrawal from Ed Sheeran's "Loop Tour" in solidarity with Macklemore amid the tour's Israel/Palestine-related controversy.
- 13 dates include New York, Toronto, Boston, Philadelphia, Detroit and Chicago.
- Onsale is direct via the artist's website rather than a major ticketing platform's public sale.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Lukas-Graham.webp)

Lukas Graham will return to North America for a 13-date headline tour next spring in support of his fifth studio album, “Good Times.” The run opens March 2 in Dallas and concludes April 11 in Chicago, with stops in New York, Toronto, Boston and other cities.

The tour follows the August release of “Good Times.” The 13-track album includes “To Know A Girl” and “Before You Go” and draws on folk and Celtic influences tied to Graham’s upbringing in Copenhagen and his Irish heritage.

The dates begin with six March shows, running from Dallas through a March 10 performance at Irving Plaza in New York. After a break, Graham resumes the tour April 2 in Boston. The final stretch includes Philadelphia, Toronto, Detroit, Indianapolis and Minneapolis before closing at House of Blues Chicago.

The announcement comes after Graham [withdrew from Ed Sheeran’s “Loop Tour”](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/ed-sheeran-tour-fallout-macklemore-aaron-rowe/) last week in solidarity with Macklemore. Graham and several other supporting artists left the tour after a dispute involving Macklemore’s onstage statements in support of Palestine.
