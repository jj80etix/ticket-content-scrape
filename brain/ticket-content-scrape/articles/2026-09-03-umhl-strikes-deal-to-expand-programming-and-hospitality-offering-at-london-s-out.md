# UMHL strikes deal to expand programming and hospitality offering at London’s Outernet

## Summary

UMG hospitality subsidiary UMHL will take over programming and operations at The Lower Third venue within London's Outernet complex, part of a partnership that also includes a new UMusic Hotel opening on Denmark Street in Q4 2026.

### Key Points
- UMHL takes over programming/operational duties plus some digital programming in Outernet's immersive space.
- The UMusic Hotel Denmark Street is set to open alongside the venue complex in Q4 2026.
- Deal expands UMG's hospitality arm's footprint into live-venue operations.
