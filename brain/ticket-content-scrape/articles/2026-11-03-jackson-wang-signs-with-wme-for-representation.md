# Jackson Wang Signs With WME For Representation

## Summary

Singer-entrepreneur Jackson Wang, a former GOT7 member and the first Chinese solo artist to headline Coachella's main stage, has signed with WME for representation. Wang continues to be represented by his own label TEAM WANG Records and by Roc Nation.

### Key Points
- Wang's hit "LMLY" has topped 103M Spotify streams; he has collaborated with Ciara, Major Lazer, Gucci Mane and others.
- An April 2026 Barclays Center show grossed $1.48M on 10,344 tickets sold, per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- He also holds fashion partnerships with Louis Vuitton, Cartier, Armani and Nike/Jordan, and founded TEAM WANG Records and TEAM WANG Design.

## Transcript

![Jackson Wang](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Jackson-Wang-scaled.jpg)

Jackson Wang

Jackson Wang, a singer, songwriter, fashion icon and entrepreneur, signed with WME for representation, the agency announced.

Born in Hong Kong, Wang broke onto the music scene as a member of South Korean boy band GOT7, and became a global star when he released his solo debut, MIRRORS, in 2019. He became the first Chinese singer-songwriter to enter Mediabase’s U.S. Top 40 with “100 Ways” and his hit track “LMLY” has amassed more than 103 million streams on Spotify, leading to collaborations with Ciara, Swae Lee, Major Lazer, Galantis, Gucci Mane, Joji, GoldLink and more.

Wang has also performed all over the world and made history in 2022 as the first Chinese solo artist to perform on the main stage at Coachella. He is in an Apple campaign tied to his latest single and music video, “Thank You,” which was released in September.

The Chinese star has sold out arenas across the globe, including a show at Brooklyn’s Barclays Center on April 10 that grossed $1,481,898 off 10,344 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

In addition to touring the globe, Wang has developed partnerships with fashion brands such as Louis Vuitton, Cartier, Armani, Nike and Air Jordan.

He founded TEAM WANG Records and TEAM WANG Design, ventures that earned him recognition on Forbes China’s 30 Under 30 feature list in 2023.

Wang is also represented by TEAM WANG Records and Roc Nation.
