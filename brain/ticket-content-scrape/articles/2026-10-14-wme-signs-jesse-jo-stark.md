# WME Signs Jesse Jo Stark

## Summary

Talent agency WME has signed musician Jesse Jo Stark for representation. The item is a brief signing announcement.

### Key Points
- WME signs Jesse Jo Stark for representation.

## Transcript

![Jjs 1 scaled](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Jjs-1-scaled-1-e1791988178783.jpg)

Courtesy of Pablo Gonzalez

WME has signed Jesse Jo Stark for worldwide representation. Stark is a multi-hyphenate who wears various hats as a singer-songwriter, fashionista, actress, and Chrome Hearts co-creative director and designer. Stark is preparing a new album, *MUSE*, set for release a Nov. 6.

According to an official press release, the record is a raw “and deeply intimate body of work that finds Stark settling into her heart and emerging with some of her most honest songwriting to date,” with the first single, “colorado,” released in early October.

Jesse Jo Stark is currently on her North American tour. For dates and more information, go to: [https://jessejostark.com/](https://jessejostark.com/).
