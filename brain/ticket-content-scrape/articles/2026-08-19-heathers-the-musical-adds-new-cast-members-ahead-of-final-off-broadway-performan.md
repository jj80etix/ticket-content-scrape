# ‘Heathers The Musical’ Adds New Cast Members Ahead of Final Off-Broadway Performances

## Summary

Off-Broadway's "Heathers the Musical" is refreshing its cast (new performers start Sept 9) ahead of its final performances Nov 8 at New World Stages, including returning guest star Anneliese van der Pol as Ms. Fleming and Kate Rockwell back for the closing week.

### Key Points
- New cast begins Sept 9; production closes Nov 8, then launches a 2027 North American tour starting in Baltimore.
- Anneliese van der Pol joins as Ms. Fleming through Oct 26; Kate Rockwell returns for the final week.
- Morgan Dudley, Nicholas A. Wilkinson, Jimmy Ray Bennett, and Brian Martin also join the cast.
- Current company members Bayardelle, Davis, Ostermeyer, and Bashian give their final performances Sept 6.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/Heathers-the-musical.webp)

“Heathers The Musical” is adding several new performers to its Off-Broadway revival as the production moves toward its final performances at New World Stages.

The new cast members will begin performances September 9, while the current production is scheduled to close November 8. Anneliese van der Pol will join the production as Ms. Fleming. Her engagement is set to run through October 26. Kate Rockwell will return to the role for the show’s final week.

Morgan Dudley will take over as Heather Duke. Nicholas A. Wilkinson will play Kurt Kelly, while Jimmy Ray Bennett will assume the role of Ram’s Dad. Brian Martin will join the cast as Kurt’s Dad.

The incoming performers will replace several members of the current company. Jeannette Bayardelle, Jackera Davis, Cade Ostermeyer and Adam Bashian are scheduled to give their final performances September 6.

The revival currently stars Isabella Esler as Veronica, Zan Berube as Heather Chandler and John Cardoza as J.D. Ava DeMary plays Heather McNamara, while Chris Marsh Clark portrays Ram Sweeney and Aniya Thompson appears as Martha Dunstock.

Bayardelle currently plays Ms. Fleming, Davis portrays Heather Duke, Ostermeyer plays Kurt Kelly and Bashian appears as Ram’s Dad. Bennett, who is joining the new company in a different role, currently plays Kurt’s Dad.

The Laurence O’Keefe and Kevin Murphy musical opened its current Off-Broadway engagement July 10, 2025. The production returned to New World Stages, the same venue where “Heathers The Musical” previously played following its 2013 Los Angeles premiere.

Andy Fickman directs the production, reuniting with members of the creative team behind the London production. Gary Lloyd serves as choreographer, with additional choreography by Stephanie Klemons for the New York engagement.

David Shields designed the set and costumes, with Siena Zoë Allen providing additional costume design. Ben Cracknell is the lighting designer, while Dan Samson serves as sound designer. Will Joy is the music supervisor and Mona Seyed-Bolorforosh is the music director.

Following its Off-Broadway run, “Heathers The Musical” is scheduled to launch a North American tour in Baltimore next year.
