# Wilco Announces 2027 Tour Dates

## Summary

Wilco announced a batch of 2027 tour dates spanning North America and Europe, plus a Marrakech, Morocco getaway in March, with presales starting Thanksgiving week. The news follows an earlier announcement of a February 2027 "PB&J Tour" pairing Jeff Tweedy with Punch Brothers.

### Key Points
- North America leg opens April 20, 2027, in Huber Heights, OH, with stops including Cincinnati, Houston, Austin, Dallas, Denver, Tucson, LA, and five Fillmore (SF) nights.
- European dates follow in June across Spain, France, Germany, Denmark, Czech Republic, Switzerland, and Italy.
- Includes a standalone March 25-27, 2027 run of shows in Marrakech, Morocco.
- Several December 2026 Chicago hometown shows are already sold out.
- Presale begins November 25 with on-sale November 27 for most dates.

Wilco is celebrating Thanksgiving by announcing a bunch of 2027 tour dates on both sides of the Atlantic, with ticket presales starting on Thursday.  
  
The news follows an announcement earlier this month that Punch Brothers & Wilco frontman Jeff Tweedy are joining forces for a run of dates in February 2027 called the “PB&J Tour.”  
  
Wilco has a handful of hometown shows booked in Chicago in December and then the alternative rock band’s 2027 tour plans start with its Marrakech, Morocco, getaway March 25-27. The new tour dates kick off April 20 at Rose Music Center in Huber Heights, Ohio. Stops on the North America routing include Cincinnati, Houston, Austin, Dallas, Denver, Tucson, Los Angeles and five nights at San Francisco’s The Fillmore.  
  
Wilco heads to Europe in June, with dates lined up in Spain, France, Germany, Denmark, Czech Republic, Switzerland, Italy and more.

Tweedy’s latest solo release is 2025’s *Twilight Override*, following Wilco’s 2024 LP, *Hot Sun Cool Shroud.*
