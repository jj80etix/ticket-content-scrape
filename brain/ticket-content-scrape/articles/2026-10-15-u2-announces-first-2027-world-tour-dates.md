# U2 Announces First 2027 World Tour Dates

## Summary

U2 announced the first six dates of its 2027 "The Greatest Show on Earth" world tour, with Beck opening, covering stadium shows in Mexico City, São Paulo, and Buenos Aires beginning February 23. The tour supports U2's forthcoming album *Carnaval de Luz*, due November 13, and marks the band's first stadium run since 2019.

### Key Points
- Dates: Feb 23 & 26 Mexico City, Mar 10 & 12 São Paulo, Mar 23 & 25 Buenos Aires; European/North American dates and Ireland shows to follow.
- Fan-club presales run Oct 19-21 via U2.com; Ticketmaster artist presale for Mexico City opens Oct 22, general sales Oct 22-23.
- Additional dates expected in coming weeks.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/U2-The-Greatest-Show-On-Earth-1.webp)

U2 has announced the first six dates of its 2027 “The Greatest Show on Earth” world tour, with Beck joining the band for stadium concerts in Mexico City, São Paulo and Buenos Aires.

The run is set to begin February 23 at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros. The initial schedule includes two performances in each city, with the Latin American run concluding March 25 in Buenos Aires.

Additional European and North American dates are expected in the coming weeks. U2 also confirmed that plans for performances in Ireland are underway. Mexico City, São Paulo and Buenos Aires will be the tour’s only Latin American stops in 2027, according to the announcement.

The concerts will mark U2’s first stadium performances since the Joshua Tree Tour ended at Mumbai’s DY Patil Stadium on December 15, 2019. Organizers said the new production will feature songs spanning the band’s catalog. U2’s more recent live work included a 40-show residency at Sphere in Las Vegas.

The tour supports *Carnaval de Luz*, U2’s forthcoming 16th studio album, scheduled for release November 13. The 12-track release arrives during the band’s 50th anniversary year and follows its previous studio album, 2017’s “Songs of Experience.”

U2 accompanied the tour announcement with the release of “The Greatest Show on Earth,” the album’s third single.

Ticket sales begin with a U2.com subscriber presale on October 19 at 10 a.m. local time for members of the Feedback group. Members of the Hype group gain access October 20 at 10 a.m. local time. Both presales close October 21 at 10 p.m. local time.

Mexico City will have a separate Ticketmaster artist presale beginning October 22 at 10 a.m. local time. Registration closes October 18 at 11:59 p.m. Eastern time. General ticket sales for São Paulo and Buenos Aires begin October 22 at 10 a.m. local time. Mexico City’s general sale follows October 23 at 10 a.m. local time.
