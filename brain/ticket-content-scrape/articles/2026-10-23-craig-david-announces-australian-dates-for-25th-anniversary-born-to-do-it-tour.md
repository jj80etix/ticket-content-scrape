# Craig David Announces Australian Dates For 25th Anniversary ‘Born To Do It Tour’

## Summary

Craig David will bring his "Born To Do It Tour" to Australia in May 2027, marking the album's 25th anniversary with a full-band run of six dates. Frontier Members get presale access Oct. 26, with general on-sale Oct. 28.

### Key Points
- Tour celebrates the 25th anniversary of Craig David's "Born To Do It" album.
- Six Australian dates kick off May 22, 2027 in Brisbane and close in Perth.
- Promoted by MG Live and Frontier Touring.
- Presale: Oct. 26 (Frontier Members); general on-sale: Oct. 28.

Craig David, in partnership with MG Live and Frontier Touring, is bringing his seminal album, “Born To Do It,” to Australia in May 2027 with a full band to celebrate the LP’s 25th anniversary. David will kick off the tour on May 22 at Brisbane’s Fortitude Music Hall. The limited run will continue for five dates before concluding at the Perth HPC.

Frontier Members can access their presale from Oct. 26 at 12 p.m. local time before tickets go on sale to the public from Oct. 28 at 2 p.m. local time.

Tickets and tour info via [frontiertouring.com/craigdavid](https://www.frontiertouring.com/craigdavid)

“I’m so excited to be coming back to Australia with my band in May 2027 to perform and celebrate the 26th anniversary of my life-changing debut album ‘Born To Do It,’ Craig David says. “It’s going to be such a vibe playing songs from that album along with other classic tracks from my other albums. Can’t wait to see you all there!”
