# Koe Wetzel and Parker McCollum Bring Back ‘Naughty or Nice Tour’ for Five Texas Shows

## Summary

Koe Wetzel and Parker McCollum are reviving their "Naughty or Nice Tour" for five Texas shows this December, the first time the co-headlining holiday run has returned in seven years. The run opens December 2 in Helotes and closes December 10 in Fort Worth, with support from Jackson Wendell and Logan Jahnke. Artist presales begin October 7, with general onsale October 9.

### Key Points
- Five-date co-headlining run Dec. 2-10 across Texas (Helotes, Houston, Corpus Christi, Round Rock, Fort Worth).
- First "Naughty or Nice Tour" revival since it began in 2017.
- Support acts: Jackson Wendell and Logan Jahnke.
- Artist presale Oct. 7-8 (password NAUGHTY); general onsale Oct. 9 at 10 a.m. CT.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Koe-Wetzel-Parker-McCollum-Naughty-or-Nice-Tour.webp)

Koe Wetzel and Parker McCollum will reunite this December for their Naughty or Nice Tour, bringing the holiday outing back for the first time in seven years.

The five-date co-headlining run opens December 2 with back-to-back nights at Floore’s Country Store in Helotes, Texas. From there, the singers will head to Corpus Christi for a performance at the Concrete Street Amphitheater on December 4 followed by a stop in Round Rock on December 5 at Round Rock Amp.

The run is set to wrap up on December 10 at Billy Bob’s Texas in Fort Worth. Houston.

Jackson Wendell and Logan Jahnke will support the tour, which follows a year of separate arena outings for both headliners. The December shows bring Wetzel and McCollum back together in their home state for a holiday tradition that began in 2017.

“It felt right to end the year in Texas and make it a party,” Wetzel said in the announcement.

McCollum reflected on how much their careers have changed since the first run, saying the opportunity to revive the tour carries particular significance.

“Proud to say we’ve both come a long way since then, and getting to bring this back after some years off is really special,” McCollum said.

Artist presales begin Wednesday, October 7, at 10 a.m. local time and continue through Thursday, October 8, at 10 p.m. local time. Fans can use the password NAUGHTY to access the presale. Promoter and local presales open October 8 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general public sale Friday, October 9, at 10 a.m. CT.
