# Iron Maiden, Korn and Bad Omens to Headline Boardwalk Rock 2027

## Summary

C3 Presents' Boardwalk Rock expands to three days for its 2027 return to Ocean City, MD (May 14-16), headlined by Iron Maiden, Korn and Bad Omens, after debuting as a two-day event in 2025 and taking 2026 off.

### Key Points
- Boardwalk Rock 2027: May 14-16, Ocean City, MD; expands from 2 days (2025) to 3 days; event skipped 2026.
- Headliners by day: Korn (Fri, May 14), Bad Omens (Sat, May 15); Iron Maiden also headlines.
- Full bill also includes Alice in Chains, Judas Priest, Breaking Benjamin, Good Charlotte, Marilyn Manson, The Prodigy, Cypress Hill, Slash ft. Myles Kennedy & the Conspirators, Clutch, Sevendust, and others.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Boardwalk-Rock-2027.webp)

Iron Maiden, Korn and Bad Omens will headline Boardwalk Rock when the festival returns to Ocean City, Maryland, May 14 to 16, 2027.

Produced by C3 Presents, the beachside event will expand to three days after debuting as a two-day festival in 2025 and taking 2026 off. The newly announced lineup also includes Alice in Chains, Judas Priest, Breaking Benjamin, Good Charlotte, Marilyn Manson and The Prodigy.

Korn will headline the opening day on Friday, May 14. Breaking Benjamin, The Prodigy, Cypress Hill and Slash featuring Myles Kennedy and the Conspirators are also scheduled to perform. Friday’s lineup continues with Clutch, Sevendust, Theory of a Deadman, Filter, All Them Witches, Corrosion of Conformity, Eve 6, Local H, Nonpoint and Soil.

Bad Omens will lead Saturday, May 15, with Good Charlotte, Marilyn Manson, Motionless in White, Bullet for My Valentine and Poppy also on the bill. Tesla, Superheaven, Buckcherry, Drowning Pool, Sponge, Showing Teeth, Tetrarch and Asava round out the day’s announced performers.

Iron Maiden will close the festival on Sunday, May 16. The final day also features Alice in Chains and Judas Priest, alongside Sleeping with Sirens, The Pretty Reckless, Down and The Warning. Loathe, The Hu, Apocalyptica, GWAR, Dirty Honey, The Flys and Mac Sabbath complete Sunday’s lineup.

Iron Maiden’s appearance was announced ahead of the full festival lineup as part of the band’s 2027 “Run for Your Lives” North American tour. The Ocean City performance is scheduled to conclude that run, following another festival appearance at Sonic Temple in Columbus, Ohio, on May 14. Bullet for My Valentine will support the band’s nonfestival dates.
