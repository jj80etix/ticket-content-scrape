# Sasha Keable Announces 31-Date Act Right World Tour for 2027

## Summary

Sasha Keable has announced her first world tour, a 31-date "Act Right Tour" spanning Europe, the UK, and North America in spring 2027, with EJ Ross supporting on all dates. The run opens March 2 in Copenhagen and closes April 29 in Los Angeles.

### Key Points
- 31-date debut world tour runs March 2 (Copenhagen) through April 29 (Los Angeles).
- European/UK leg wraps March 16 at London's O2 Academy Brixton; North American leg opens March 22 in Minneapolis.
- EJ Ross serves as support act for the entire tour.
- Tour follows the momentum of Keable's *ACT RIGHT* EP and its deluxe edition, *ACT II*.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Sasha-Keable-.webp)

British-Colombian R&B singer-songwriter Sasha Keable has announced her first world tour, a 31-date international run spanning Europe, the United Kingdom and North America in spring 2027. The Act Right Tour will feature special guest EJ Ross on all dates.

The tour begins March 2 at Pumpehuset in Copenhagen, Denmark, before visiting Berlin, Brussels, Paris, Amsterdam and several other European cities. The opening leg concludes March 16 at London’s O2 Academy Brixton.

The North American run kicks off March 22 at Minneapolis’ Varsity Theater, with performances scheduled in Chicago, Detroit, Toronto, Boston, New York, Philadelphia, Atlanta, Houston and other markets. The tour wraps up April 29 at Pacific Electric in Los Angeles.

The announcement marks another milestone for Keable, whose growing international profile has been fueled by her *ACT RIGHT* EP and its deluxe edition, *ACT II*.
