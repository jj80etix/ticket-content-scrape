# Bella Poarch Announces First Headlining Tour

## Summary

Bella Poarch announced her first-ever headlining tour in support of debut album Picnic at the Cemetery, with dates across North America, the UK and Europe starting Jan. 30 in Atlanta.

### Key Points
- Tour supports debut album Picnic at the Cemetery, released September 2026.
- Starts Jan. 30 at Masquerade Purgatory, Atlanta; includes NYC, Toronto, Chicago, LA, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Berlin.
- Artist presale Oct. 7; general onsale Oct. 9.

Bella Poarch has announced dates for her first-ever headlining tour, with dates in North America, the UK and Europe. The 29-year-old Filipina-American singer, songwriter and producer is supporting her debut album, *Picnic at the Cemetery*, which was released in September.

*Picnic at the Cemetery* is described as “a cinematic coming-of-age album rooted in Bella’s experiences of girlhood, heartbreak and healing.” An announcement from her PR rep adds “Through surreal fairytale imagery and her hand-drawn characters, ‘The Bigheads,’ Bella turns difficult childhood memories into a vivid world of her own. Featuring songs including ‘Ribcage,’ ‘My Boy,’ and ‘Stay Gone,’ the album explores reclaiming the past and finding solace in yourself.”

Poarch, who first broke through on the charts with her 2021 platinum-certified single, “Build a B\*tch,” has already racked up a fanbase featuring 125 million followers across social platforms, with more than 4.5 billion streams – and now she’s ready to hit the road.

The headline tour starts Jan. 30 at Masquerade Purgatory in Atlanta, and includes stops in New York, Toronto, Chicago, Los Angeles, London, Amsterdam, Paris, Berlin and more.

“This album is so personal to me, and getting to share it with you on my first headlining tour means everything. I’ve dreamed of singing these songs together in the same room. I can’t wait to see my Bigheads and make new memories with you,” Poarch said in a statement.

An artist presale begins Wednesday, October 7 at 9:00 a.m. local time, followed by the general ticket onsale on sale Friday, October 9 at 9:00 a.m. local time. For tickets and additional information, visit [bellapoarch.ph/tour](https://bellapoarch.ph/tour).

Poarch’s team includes Creative Artists Agency’s Rachel Pestik, Mike Marquis and Matt Galle.
