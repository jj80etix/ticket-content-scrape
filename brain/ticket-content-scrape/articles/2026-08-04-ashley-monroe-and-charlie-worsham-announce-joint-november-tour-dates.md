# Ashley Monroe And Charlie Worsham Announce Joint November Tour Dates

## Summary

Ashley Monroe and Charlie Worsham announced a joint three-city tour in November, visiting Washington D.C., New York City and Chicago, with tickets on sale now. The pairing follows years of collaboration between the two Nashville singer-songwriters, arriving alongside new solo releases from each artist.

### Key Points
- Three-show run: Nov. 4 Washington D.C. (The Birchmere), Nov. 5 New York (Bleeker Bell), Nov. 12 Chicago (Garcia's).
- Monroe released her concept album *Dear Nashville* earlier this year; Worsham releases his solo album *Once Upon A Second Time Around* on Aug. 7.
- Both artists describe the joint dates as a long-planned pairing after years of writing and performing together.

Ashley Monroe and Charlie Worsham announced joint dates in November. The limited three-show run visits Washington, D.C., New York City and Chicago. Tickets are on sale now.

“Charlie is one of the most talented people I’ve ever met and his energy is infectious,” stated Monroe in the announcement. “We’ve written songs together over the years, played and sang together with Vince Gill and recently started cooking up something extra special in the studio. We figured it was about damn time we took this show on the road.”

“Not only am I a card-carrying Ashely Monroe fan, she’s one of my longest-standing collaborators and oldest friends in Nashville,” added Worsham. “I can’t wait to see what music magic we dream up for these shows, and I know for a fact we’ve already tapped a couple world-class musicians to join us. Don’t be surprised if you hear everything from Conway/Loretta covers to stoner gospel in these sets.”

Earlier this year Monroe released her eight-song concept album *Dear Nashville*. The project was co-written and co-produced with Luke Laird. On Aug. 7, Worsham will release his first solo, independent album *Once Upon A Second Time Around*. The release features guest appearances from Lainey Wilson, Stephen Wilson Jr. and The Travelin’ McCourys.

Ashley Monroe And Charlie Worsham Dates:

Nov. 4 – Washington, D.C., The Birchmere

Nov. 5 – New York, NY, Bleeker Bell

Nov. 12 – Chicago, IL, Garcia’s
