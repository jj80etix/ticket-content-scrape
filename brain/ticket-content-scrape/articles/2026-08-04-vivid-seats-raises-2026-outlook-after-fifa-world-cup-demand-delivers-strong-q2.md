# Vivid Seats Raises 2026 Outlook After FIFA World Cup Demand Delivers Strong Q2

## Summary

Vivid Seats topped its own Q2 expectations, posting $659.4M in Marketplace Gross Order Value and $129.9M revenue on FIFA World Cup-driven demand, and raised its full-year 2026 GOV guidance to $2.3B-$2.6B and adjusted EBITDA guidance to $34M-$40M. The company still posted a $14.3M net loss for the quarter.

### Key Points
- Q2 2026: Marketplace GOV $659.4M, revenue $129.9M, net loss $14.3M, adjusted EBITDA $12.6M.
- Raised full-year 2026 guidance: GOV to $2.3B-$2.6B (low end up $100M), adjusted EBITDA to $34M-$40M (from $30M-$40M).
- CEO Lawrence Fey credits "extraordinary demand created by the FIFA World Cup" for the quarter's outperformance.
- Company points to continued investment in technology, marketplace data, and operational efficiency as driving the raised outlook for H2 2026.

### Highlights
- Full-year 2026 GOV guidance raised to $2.3B-$2.6B, adjusted EBITDA guidance raised to $34M-$40M, both citing World Cup tailwinds.
- Q2 net loss of $14.3M despite the guidance raise — the secondary marketplace remains unprofitable on a GAAP basis even in a strong demand quarter.

### Industry Problem
Secondary-marketplace financial performance is increasingly tied to mega-events (FIFA World Cup) rather than steady organic growth, illustrating how volatile and event-dependent resale-marketplace revenue can be.

### Proposed Solution
No product action indicated; monitor Vivid Seats' guidance execution through H2 2026 and watch whether post-World Cup demand normalization forces guidance back down — a signal of how much resale volume is now event-driven versus baseline.

CEO Lawrence Fey said that the company is “encouraged by the progress we’ve made through the first half of the year.”
