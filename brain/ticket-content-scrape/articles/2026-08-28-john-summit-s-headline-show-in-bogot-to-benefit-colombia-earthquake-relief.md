# John Summit’s Headline Show In Bogotá To Benefit Colombia Earthquake Relief

## Summary

DJ John Summit will donate 100% of ticket sales from his Sept. 10 Bogotá show to Techo's Colombia earthquake relief efforts, kicking off a Latin America run that also includes São Paulo, Buenos Aires and a Rock in Rio debut.

### Key Points
- Bogotá show (Sept. 10) proceeds go entirely to Techo's earthquake recovery work.
- LATAM run continues through São Paulo (Sept. 11), Buenos Aires (Sept. 12) and Rock in Rio (Sept. 13).
- Follows release of sophomore album *CTRL ESCAPE*; also has a Dec. 12 North American headline show at LA Memorial Coliseum.
