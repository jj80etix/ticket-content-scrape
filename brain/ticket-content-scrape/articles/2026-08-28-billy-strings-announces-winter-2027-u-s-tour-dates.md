# Billy Strings Announces Winter 2027 U.S. Tour Dates

## Summary

Billy Strings extended his touring schedule into 2027, announcing 10 US shows for February across North Carolina, Georgia and Tennessee, adding to an already extensive 2026 slate.

### Key Points
- Feb. 2027 run: five nights in Asheville, NC; two in Duluth, GA; three in Nashville (two at Bridgestone Arena, one at Ryman Auditorium).
- Ryman show access is gated: fans must buy a Bridgestone ticket to enter the Fair AXS request for the Ryman date.
- More than 20 previously announced 2026 dates remain, including Ionia Freak Fair (Aug. 28-29) and a September Denver/Southeast run.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Billy-Strings-Winter-2027.webp)

Billy Strings is extending his touring schedule into 2027, announcing 10 U.S. shows for February that will take the bluegrass star through North Carolina, Georgia and Tennessee.

The newly announced winter run will begin February 4 at ExploreAsheville.com Arena in Asheville, North Carolina, where Strings is scheduled for five performances through February 9. He will then head to Duluth, Georgia, for two nights at Gas South Arena on February 12 and 13.

The tour concludes with three shows in Nashville. Strings will play Bridgestone Arena on February 19 and 20 before moving to the Ryman Auditorium for the final show on February 21.

Notably, access to the Ryman performance will work differently from the other dates. Fans must purchase a ticket for either of the Bridgestone Arena concerts to be eligible to participate in the Fair AXS ticket request for the February 21 Ryman show.

The announcement adds another stretch of dates to an already extensive schedule for Strings. He still has more than 20 previously announced performances remaining in 2026, beginning with the two-day Ionia Freak Fair at the Ionia County Fairgrounds in Michigan on August 28 and 29.

From there, Strings resumes touring in September with three Denver shows, including two nights at Ball Arena and a September 20 performance at the Paramount Theatre. His fall schedule also includes dates in Southern California, Oakland, Eugene and Everett before returning to the Southeast and East Coast in October.  

Among the remaining dates are two October shows at the Tivoli Theatre in Chattanooga, Tennessee, followed by two nights at The Orion Amphitheater in Huntsville, Alabama, and a pair of Halloween-weekend performances at CFG Bank Arena in Baltimore.

Strings will close his 2026 touring year with five December concerts. The run begins with shows at the Saenger Theatre and Smoothie King Center in New Orleans before heading to Texas for performances in Sugar Land, Fort Worth and Austin. The December 12 show at Austin’s Moody Center is currently his final scheduled performance of 2026.  

The winter tour announcement coincides with the August 28 release of Strings’ new album, *So Much for Goodbyes*, through Reprise Records.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Strings’ official website](https://www.billystrings.com/).
