## U2’s latest world tour dates confirmed as North American stadiums revealed

The band has unveiled 15 dates as the latest batch of concerts on their 2027 global tour

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/10/PHOTO-U2-Street-Of-Dreams-cover-image-no-text-CREDIT-Viviane-Sassen-scaled.jpg.webp)

U2 © Viviane Sassen

U2’s *Carnaval De Luz presents:**Greatest Show on Earth* tour has confirmed its North American stadium dates, as 15 concerts across the US and Canada have been unveiled between August and October 2027, bringing the upcoming tour to a total of 53 shows.

The leg will begin on 13 August at Miami’s Hard Rock Stadium, before moving to Atlanta’s Mercedes-Benz Stadium and Baltimore’s M&T Bank Stadium.

A double-header at New Jersey’s MetLife Stadium is set for 26 and 28 August, before the band goes on to play Chicago’s Soldier Field and another two-night engagement at Rogers stadium in Toronto, before ending at Massachusetts’ Gillette Stadium on 14 September.

After a nearly two-week break, the run continues with AT&T Stadium in Dallas, two nights at Los Angeles’ SoFi Stadium, Las Vegas’ Allegiant Stadium, Santa Clara’s Levi’s Stadium, and finally a show at BC Place in Vancouver.

> “U2’s 2027 return to a stadium setting promises an ambitious live experience”

These dates are the latest batch of concerts revealed for the world tour, and come after the band initially unveiled only Latin American dates spanning February and March 2027, while saying the full tour could extend into 2028 with further legs around the globe.

In late October, Live Nation revealed the [European dates](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/u2-reveals-dates-for-european-and-uk-leg-of-2027-world-tour/) for the global run, which include stadiums in London, Berlin, Paris, and more. Each leg announced so far has subsequently added dates.

*Carnaval De Luz presents: Greatest Show On Earth* will be the band’s first world tour in eight years, and comes as the band celebrates its half-century anniversary. U2 has a long history of ambitious global stadium tours, often making use of elaborate staging. With their monumental *360° Tour* (2009–2011), the band became the first band to scale an in-the-round stadium production worldwide, going on to sell 7 million tickets worldwide and becoming the highest grossing tour in history at the time.

“U2’s 2027 return to a stadium setting promises an ambitious live experience marked by the signature innovation that has defined their live shows for five decades, with a performance spectacle embracing human connection and intimacy at a grand scale, featuring a set list drawn from across the band’s extraordinary catalogue,” Live Nation said.
