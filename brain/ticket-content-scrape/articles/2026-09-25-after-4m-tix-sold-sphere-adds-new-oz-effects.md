# After 4M+ Tix Sold, Sphere Adds New ‘Oz’ Effects

## Summary

"The Wizard of Oz at Sphere" has sold more than 4 million tickets and surpassed $500 million in revenue, and is adding new effects — animatronic winged monkeys, themed scents and pyrotechnics — to the ongoing Las Vegas production.

### Key Points
- Production has sold 4M+ tickets and topped $500M in revenue since opening.
- New additions include animatronic flying monkeys, apple-scent effects and "Glinda Glitter" moments.
- Tickets remain on sale through early 2027 via thesphere.com.

“The Wizard of Oz at Sphere” has sold more than 4 million tickets and surpassed $500 million in revenue, the venue announced Friday.

The already wildly successful production is adding new effects: animatronic winged monkeys, themed scents — including on the apples that fall from the ceiling, pyrotechnic fireworks and “Glinda Glitter” that accompanies the arrival of the good witch.

Tickets for “The Wizard of Oz at Sphere” are on sale now through the beginning of 2027 at thesphere.com.
