# Global Stadium Report 2026

## Summary

IQ Magazine's Global Stadium Report 2026 is a themed content roundup on the stadium-touring boom, linking out to pieces on its "Stadium Stars 2026" venue rankings, the venues behind Bad Bunny's record tour, US college stadiums filling touring capacity gaps, in-the-round production trends, and agency views on the stadium market's growth.

### Key Points
- Profiles 20 venues in the "Stadium Stars 2026" list.
- Covers US college stadiums increasingly hosting mega-tours amid venue shortages.
- Explores 360°/in-the-round production trends and their ticketing/capacity trade-offs.

This is a link roundup with only teaser-length blurbs for each linked story; no single item has enough substantive detail here to summarize further.
