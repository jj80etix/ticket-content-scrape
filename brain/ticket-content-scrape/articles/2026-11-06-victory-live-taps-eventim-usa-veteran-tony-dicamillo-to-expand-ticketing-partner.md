# Victory Live Taps Eventim USA Veteran Tony DiCamillo to Expand Ticketing Partnerships

## Summary

Victory Live hired Tony DiCamillo, a 25-year ticketing-industry veteran most recently of Eventim USA, as SVP of business development to expand the Atlanta-based ticketing-technology company's partnerships with sports teams, venues, and promoters. DiCamillo previously held the same role at See Tickets and was president of ExtremeTix, whose acquisition by Etix he helped guide.

### Key Points
- Tony DiCamillo joins Victory Live as SVP of business development, reporting to CRO Kate Howard.
- Prior roles: SVP business development at Eventim USA and See Tickets; president of ExtremeTix.
- Guided ExtremeTix's acquisition by Etix during his tenure there.
- Move follows Victory Live's May 2026 multi-year secondary-ticketing agreement with Minor League Baseball/Diamond Baseball Holdings.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/11626-victory-live-announce.webp)

Victory Live has appointed Tony DiCamillo as senior vice president of business development, bringing more than 25 years of ticketing industry experience to the company’s efforts to expand partnerships with sports teams, venues, promoters and other live event rights holders.

The Atlanta-based ticketing technology and analytics company announced the appointment Thursday, Nov. 5. DiCamillo will report to Chief Revenue Officer Kate Howard, focusing on new commercial relationships and helping clients use Victory Live’s ticketing technology, data analytics and distribution capabilities.

“Tony understands the ticketing business from the perspective of the teams, venues and event organizers we serve,” Howard said. “He has built commercial teams, opened new markets and helped clients navigate changes in technology and operations.”

DiCamillo joins Victory Live from Eventim USA, where he served as senior vice president of business development after holding the same position at See Tickets. His work included expanding ticketing relationships across music, sports, cultural events, attractions and air shows, along with securing agreements with major automotive shows across the United States.

Previously, DiCamillo served as president and a board member of ExtremeTix, overseeing the company’s operations, technology, sales and customer service. During his tenure, he managed teams of more than 65 employees and helped guide the company’s acquisition by Etix.

He began his career in sports and entertainment with the Houston Rockets before moving into box office and venue management at Houston’s Compaq Center. His background spans frontline ticketing operations, enterprise sales and executive leadership.

The appointment comes as Victory Live continues expanding its relationships with event organizers through ticket distribution, technology and data analytics partnerships.

In May, the company [secured a multi-year agreement with Minor League Baseball and Diamond Baseball Holdings](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/victory-live-lands-milb-partnership-expand-ticketing-reach/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) to serve as a preferred secondary ticketing services partner. The arrangement allows participating clubs to distribute inventory across multiple major ticket marketplaces, including Ticketmaster, StubHub, SeatGeek and Vivid Seats, while maintaining their existing primary ticketing arrangements.

Victory Live has also expanded its sports analytics business. The [Las Vegas Raiders named the company their official data and analytics partner](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/10/las-vegas-raiders-name-victory-live-official-data-analytics-partner/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in October 2025, while an [October 2026 agreement with the Orange Bowl](https://www.victorylive.com/6928-2/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) will provide ticketing data and analytics services for the college football event, which is scheduled to host a College Football Playoff semifinal in January.

Founded in 2021 by Sam Soni, Victory Live operates a technology platform combining event management, ticketing software, data insights and multi-channel distribution. Its Victory Live One platform supports ticketing operations across sports, concerts and theater, while its business-to-business marketplace provides connectivity between ticket sellers and distribution channels.

DiCamillo’s new role will center on developing additional rights-holder relationships and identifying opportunities to apply those capabilities to ticketing operations and revenue growth.

“Teams and event organizers need technology that works with their operations, information they can act on and more ways to reach ticket buyers,” DiCamillo said. “Victory Live brings those capabilities together with people who understand the demands of live events. I’m excited to work with Kate and the team to build partnerships that help clients grow their businesses and serve their fans.”
