# Sun Sets On Original Red Hat Amphitheater

## Summary

Raleigh's original Red Hat Amphitheater closed out its final season on Oct. 23 after 37 concerts and nearly 150,000 guests in 2026, ahead of a move to a new permanent venue one block south opening in June 2027. The current site will be razed for a convention center expansion.

### Key Points
- Final season included Sting, Deep Purple, O.A.R. and Marcus King Band, plus NHL Hurricanes championship watch parties
- 37 concerts, ~150,000 guests in the venue's last season at its original site
- New Red Hat Amphitheater opens June 2027 on an adjacent site

## Transcript

![Current image: RedhatFinalbow](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/RedhatFinalbow-1024x768.jpg)

Original Red Hat Amphitheater in Raleigh wraps season and sets the stage for new venue in 2027. Photo courtesy Red Hat Amphitheater/The Complex

Red Hat Amphitheater celebrated the final show in its original location on Oct. 23 before moving to its new permanent home one block south.

An eventful 2026 season brought 37 concerts and almost 150,000 guests to the outdoor venue in downtown Raleigh, North Carolina. In addition to its usual run of concerts, the amphitheater also played host to official watch parties for the NHL Carolina Hurricanes during their historic championship season.

The final season at the original venue brought a wide variety of high-profile performers including Sting and Deep Purple, venue regulars like O.A.R. who also performed during the venue’s first season, up-and-comers including Sam Barber and Ole 60, and favorites like the Marcus King Band. King has a personal attachment to venue. He met his wife Briley Hussey at Red Hat in July 2021. In June, the venue took a break from concerts to welcome nearly 7,000 Hurricanes fans to watch Raleigh’s hometown team bring a championship to the City of Oaks.

“This year has been full of great examples of what makes Red Hat Amphitheater such a special, important place for Raleigh,” says Kerry Painter, Executive Director of The Complex. “We know there are so many people who have great memories of special moments here and love the unique character of this venue. This year has beautifully encapsulated all this place means to Raleigh, and we’re so grateful for what a bright future this place has set us up for in our new amp.”

The new Red Hat Amphitheater is set to open in June for the 2027 season. The current spot is going to be razed for the expansion of Atlantic Union Bank Convention Center.
