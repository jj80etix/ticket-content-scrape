# T-Mobile Arena’s Outdoor Plaza Gets New Name Through Paramount+ Partnership

## Summary

T-Mobile Arena's outdoor plaza in Las Vegas is getting a new name through a sponsorship partnership with Paramount+.

### Key Points
- Naming-rights/sponsorship deal covers the arena's outdoor plaza.
- Paramount+ becomes the plaza's new sponsor.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/T-Mobile-Arena-Paramount-Plaza-.webp)

T-Mobile Arena is giving its outdoor gathering space a new name as part of a multiyear partnership with Paramount+. The Las Vegas venue’s two-acre plaza will now be known as Paramount+ Plaza.

The agreement makes Paramount+ an official partner of T-Mobile Arena and the arena’s exclusive streaming partner. AEG Global Partnerships brokered the deal. Financial terms were not disclosed.

Located outside T-Mobile Arena on the Las Vegas Strip, the plaza serves as a gathering place for fans attending concerts, sporting events and other live entertainment. The space was previously known as Toshiba Plaza.

Paramount+ Plaza will make its debut during fight week for the Garcia vs. Benn boxing event on Saturday, September 12. The plaza will host a public ceremonial weigh-in on Friday, September 11. Fight-week programming will include the Cube, a Paramount+ customer lounge featuring an immersive activation based on the series “MobLand.”

On Saturday, September 12, the plaza’s video screens will show Noche UFC live from Glendale, Arizona, before doors open for Garcia vs. Benn. The boxing event will stream live on Paramount+ around the world, excluding the United Kingdom and Ireland, at no additional cost to subscribers.

The partnership expands Paramount+’s involvement in combat sports. The streaming service is UFC’s exclusive streaming partner in the United States, while T-Mobile Arena serves as the organization’s primary arena destination in Las Vegas.

“Paramount+ is home to some of the most compelling stories, franchises and live sports in entertainment, and this partnership gives us an exciting new way to bring that content beyond the screen and directly to fans,” Domenic Di Meglio, chief marketing officer and data officer for Paramount+, said in a statement.

“Paramount+ Plaza will give us a dynamic, year-round presence in the heart of Las Vegas where we can create memorable experiences around our programming and connect with audiences as they come together for some of the biggest moments in sports and entertainment,” Di Meglio added.

Sally Bae, AEG’s senior vice president of global partnerships in Las Vegas, said Paramount+’s presence at the arena reflects the company’s broader connection to entertainment and sports. “Paramount+ sits at the intersection of premium entertainment, live sports and culture,” Bae said.

The plaza has played a role in major Las Vegas celebrations since T-Mobile Arena opened in April 2016. Past events at the space have included the Vegas Golden Knights’ Stanley Cup victory parade, Las Vegas Aces championship rallies and the city’s official Bruno Mars Day celebration.

Dan Quinn, MGM Resorts’ senior vice president of entertainment, arena and venue operations, said the new name marks another stage in the plaza’s development.

“Paramount+ Plaza represents an exciting next chapter for one of the most visible and important entertainment gathering spaces on the Las Vegas Strip,” Quinn said.

“The plaza has been an integral part of the T-Mobile Arena experience for the last 10 years and this partnership creates new opportunities to deliver even more energy and entertainment to the millions of visitors who visit the destination each year,” Quinn added.
