# Mar Solís To Headline First U.S. Tour This Fall

## Summary

Mexican pop singer Mar Solís announced her first US tour, a five-date Live Nation-promoted run across California, Texas and Illinois this fall.

### Key Points
- Tour kicks off Sept. 27 in San Diego and wraps Oct. 15 in Chicago.
- Tickets sold via LiveNation.com, with general onsale July 31.

Singer-songwriter Mar Solís is embarking on her first U.S. tour this fall, with five intimate shows in California, Texas and Illinois.

The Live Nation-promoted “TU TU TU TOUR” kicks off Sept. 27 at House of Blues San Diego, followed by a show at Pacific Electric in Los Angeles on Oct. 3. The 26-year-old Mexican pop singer then makes her way to Texas for shows at The Echo Lounge & Music Hall in Dallas and The Presi McAllen before wrapping up Oct. 15 at House of Blues Chicago.

The tour marks a turning point for the Solís, who recently released the single “Fuiste Tú” and is ready to headline U.S. shows for the first time with shows that blend “intimacy, expressive power and a production tailored to prioritize a deep connection with the audience,” according to the release announcing the trek. The name of the tour comes from the song “Tu Tu Tu,” which will be released July 31 on all digital platforms.

Tickets for the shows are now available via an artist presale, and the general onsale begins July 31 at 10 a.m. local time at [LiveNation.com](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ917-6sV/mar-solis-events).
