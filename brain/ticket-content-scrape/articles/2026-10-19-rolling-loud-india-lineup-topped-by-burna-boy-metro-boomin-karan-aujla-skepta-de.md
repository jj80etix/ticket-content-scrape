# Rolling Loud India Lineup Topped By Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla, Skepta Dec. 12-13

## Summary

Rolling Loud India's Dec. 12-13 Mumbai lineup (Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla, Skepta and others) is being sold through the District app using a reserve-now-pay-later structure, with only 50% of ticket value due at purchase -- a BNPL-style payment approach for live-event tickets. Co-founder Matt Zingler and District CEO Rahul Ganjoo framed the lineup as blending international and Indian hip-hop talent for the festival's second Indian edition.

### Key Points
- Lineup: Burna Boy, Metro Boomin, Karan Aujla, Skepta, plus ScHoolboy Q, GoldLink, Big Sean and others.
- Tickets sold via the District app with 50% of ticket value due at time of purchase, balance due later.
- Second Indian edition; 2025's debut featured Central Cee, Wiz Khalifa, Jaram Aujla, Don Toliver.
- Rolling Loud also returns to Orlando (May) and San Bernardino (late 2027) in the U.S.

### Highlights
- District app ticketing applies a reserve-now-pay-later model: 50% of ticket price due at purchase, remainder due later.
- Deployed on a major international festival (30-artist bill, two days, Mumbai) rather than a niche or local event.
- Framed explicitly by the ticketing platform as lowering the up-front cost barrier for fans.

### Industry Problem
Full upfront payment can suppress ticket conversion, especially for higher-priced events and in markets like India where installment/BNPL payment habits are already well established among consumers.

### Proposed Solution
District's split-payment model lets fans commit with a partial deposit and defer the rest -- a pattern Etix could evaluate for premium-priced events to reduce checkout abandonment, potentially via a BNPL partner integration (Affirm/Klarna-style) at checkout.

Rolling Loud India has announced its lineup, topped by Bruna Boy, Metro Bomin, Karan Aujla and Skepta Dec. 12-13 in Mumbai.

The event marks first-time Indian performances for multiple artists on the bill, notably including Burna Boy, Metro Boomin and Skepta.

“One thing that really stood out to us in India was how open the fans were to everything,” Rolling Loud co-founder and CEO Matt Zingler said in the announcement. “If the music connects, they show up for it. That’s influenced how we’ve approached this year’s lineup – bringing international and Indian artists together across both days and making it feel like one festival, not two different worlds.”

The event, billed as all ages, also features major talent including Schoolboy Q, GoldLink, Big Sean, DJ Five Venoms and many others. Tickets are live on the District app, where fans are encouraged to reserve now and pay later, with 50% of ticket value due at time of purchase.

Rahul Ganjoo, CEO, District, said, “Walk into a cypher in Delhi or a small gig in Mumbai and you will see who people actually turn up for. And it is rarely the same list of artists you’d see on social media. Hip-hop in India has always moved through cyphers, small shows, and people putting each other on, and that’s where we looked when we built this year’s bill. At the first edition, the crowd didn’t thin out between the international sets and the Indian ones, and that told us the audience was already a step ahead of the industry. This line up is built on what we saw.”

It’s the second time Rolling Loud arrives in India, with 2025’s event taking place in late November with a lineup featuring Central Cee, Wiz Khalifa, Jaram Aujla and Don Toliver. Back in the U.S. Rolling Loud has been announced for a second time in Orlando, at Camping World Stadium in May, and returns to Southern California at the NOS Events Center in San Bernardino in late 2027.
