# Second US Sphere venue greenlit with $215m funding approval

## Summary

Prince George's County Council unanimously approved up to $215 million in public bond financing for Sphere Entertainment's second US Sphere venue, a $1.4 billion, 6,000-seat project planned for National Harbor, Maryland, clearing the final local approval hurdle.

### Key Points
- Prince George's County Council unanimously approved up to $215m in bonds for the National Harbor Sphere.
- Total project cost estimated at $1.4 billion; venue will seat 6,000.
- Sphere Entertainment is expected to repay the bond financing.
- This follows the January 2026 announcement naming the DC metro area as the second US Sphere location.
