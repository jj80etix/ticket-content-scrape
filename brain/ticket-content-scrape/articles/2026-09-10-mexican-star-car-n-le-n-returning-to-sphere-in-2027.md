# Mexican Star Carín León Returning To Sphere In 2027

## Summary

Carín León will return to Sphere in Las Vegas in 2027, per Pollstar's coverage of the same booking reported elsewhere.

### Key Points
- Confirms a 2027 Sphere return date for Carín León.
- Duplicate coverage of the same booking reported by other outlets.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/unnamed.jpg)

Carín León is coming back to Sphere in 2027. Following a sold-out weekend at the iconic Las Vegas venue, the Mexican music star announced a limited residency at Sphere next year, Sept. 16-18, to celebrate Mexico’s Independence Day.

The announcement comes just before the singer form Hermosillo concludes the second weekend of his historic residency as Sphere. León is the first Latin artist to headline the venue.

The presale for the 2027 residency, produced by AEG Presents and Cárdenas Marketing Network, begins Sept. 15 at 10 a.m. PT. Fans can register now and sign up for early access at [carinleonlive.com](https://carinleonlive.com/). The general onsale begins Sept. 17 at 10 a.m. PT.

**[Read: Carín León On Making History In Vegas & Elevating Música Mexicana](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/10/03/from-hermosillo-to-the-sphere-carin-leon-on-making-history-in-vegas-elevating-musica-mexicana/)**

A limited number of VIP travel packages are now available, and they include reserved seating, luxury hotel accommodations at The Venetian Resort Las Vegas, special merchandise and more. Visit [https://carin-leon-aeg.myshopify.com/](https://carin-leon-aeg.myshopify.com/) for more information.

León’s first weekend at Sphere was a massive success, with the Sonora native bringing out special guests each night. Cody Johnson, The Warning and Jorge Medina joined León for the opener on Sept. 4, Ricky Muñoz of Intocable made an appearance the following night and Kany García was present on Sept. 6.

Last weekend’s three shows were a success, grossing $9,986,405 off 47,094 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice reports.

The historic run at Sphere is another milestone for León, who recently curated his own festival, La Cura Fest, in his hometown of Hermosillo and has bridged cultures by weaving country, heartland rock, gospel and other genres into regional Mexican. The Mexican standout was featured on the [cover of *Pollstar*](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/07/22/from-the-opry-to-the-stones-carin-leon-embarks-on-breakout-u-s-tour/) in July of 2024, and his [manager, Jorge Juárez, graced the cover of *Pollstar* ’s Artist Management Directory](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/10/10/executive-profile-how-jorge-juarez-helped-make-regional-mexican-music-global-with-carin-leon/).

WME‘s Kirk Sommer, the agency’s global co-head of music, represents León in all areas.
