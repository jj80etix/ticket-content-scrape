# ADÉLA Announces 2027 PRIMA North American Tour Dates

## Summary

ADÉLA announced North American PRIMA Tour dates for April 2027, including stops in San Francisco, Chicago, Brooklyn, Washington D.C. and Toronto, timed around her two Coachella weekends. Artist presale begins in two waves Dec. 1, with general on-sale Dec. 2.

### Key Points
- NA leg runs April 13–30, 2027, anchored around Coachella appearances April 10 and 17.
- Two-wave artist presale Dec. 1 (10am/2pm local), general on-sale Dec. 2 at 10am local.
- Follows ADÉLA's earlier 2026 Red Bottoms Tour and Sept. 4 debut album PRIMA release.
- Broader 2027 schedule includes Europe (Jan), Australia/NZ (Feb) and South American festivals (Mar).

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ADELA-2027.jpg)

Slovak pop singer ADÉLA has announced a new run of North American tour dates for April 2027 in support of her debut album, *PRIMA*.

The North American leg of the PRIMA Tour will begin April 13 with two consecutive performances at The Warfield in San Francisco. The singer will then visit Chicago’s Radius on April 20 before heading to Brooklyn Paramount for shows April 23 and 24. A performance at The Anthem in Washington, D.C., follows April 27, with the run concluding at Toronto’s History on April 29 and 30.

The newly announced dates are scheduled around ADÉLA’s appearances at the Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival in Indio, California, on April 10 and 17.

Tickets for the new concerts will first become available through two artist presale waves on Tuesday, December 1. The first wave begins at 10 a.m. local time, with a second wave following at 2 p.m. local time. Additional venue and promoter presales will also be available for select performances.
