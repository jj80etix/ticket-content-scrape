# Jessie Reyez Announces Fall Headline Tour

## Summary

Jessie Reyez announced her "A Little Vengeance Tour," her biggest headlining run to date, spanning arenas and theaters including the Kia Forum and Barclays Center, in support of her fourth album A Little Vengeance. The Live Nation-promoted tour runs Nov. 3 (Seattle) through Nov. 24 (Toronto).

### Key Points
- Artist presale begins Tue., Aug. 25 at 10 a.m. local time; general on-sale Fri., Aug. 28.
- A portion of proceeds benefits The Jessie Reyez Fund, supporting women and underrepresented communities in music.
- Follows praise from Rolling Stone and prior collaborations including "Ain't U Tired" featuring Muni Long.

## Transcript

![unnamed](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/unnamed-9.jpg)

Jessie Reyez has unveiled the routing for “A LITTLE VENGEANCE TOUR,” featuring her biggest venues to date. The tour includes a mix of arena dates and theater gigs in major cities including stops at the Kia Forum and Barclays Center.

The Grammy-nominated and 6x Juno-winning singer-songwriter is supporting her critically acclaimed fourth album A LITTLE VENGEANCE. The album includes the singles “99%,” “UR HEARTBEAT (WHO DO U THINK ABOUT AT 2 AM?)”; “AIN’T U TIRED” featuring Muni Long, and “N.Y.F.F..”

Reyez has earned praise from critics like Rolling Stone, which declared “Jessie Reyez’s soft, guttural vocals and uninhibited lyrics have captivated fans across the industry.”

Promoted by Live Nation, the fall tour kicks off on Nov. 3rd in Seattle at WAMU Theater. Dates are booked through a Nov 24 show in Toronto, Ontario at Coca-Cola Coliseum. Check out the itinerary below.

Fans’ first chance to get tickets is via an artist presale that begins Tuesday, August 25th at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the general on-sale on Friday, August 28 at 10 AM local time. A portion of the proceeds from this tour will go to The Jessie Reyez Fund.

The nonprofit, which was launched with strategic partner PLUS1, aims to “expand access to opportunities in the music industry for women and historically underrepresented communities, while also supporting music-focused initiatives that strengthen underserved communities through education, wellbeing, cultural connection and preservation.” Today’s announcement adds that, “the work has already begun in the background through select tour and partnership integrations, including a collaboration with Nando’s that resulted in 500 donated meals. Following the fund’s official launch, Jessie looks forward to sharing more about how the fund will continue to use music as a force for connection, empowerment and lasting impact.”
