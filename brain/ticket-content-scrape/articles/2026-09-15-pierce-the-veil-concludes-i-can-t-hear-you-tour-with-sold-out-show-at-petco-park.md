# Pierce The Veil Concludes ‘I Can’t Hear You Tour’ With Sold-Out Show At Petco Park

## Summary

Pierce The Veil closed out their "I Can't Hear You World Tour" with a sold-out, career-biggest headline show at Petco Park in San Diego, capping a run that moved over 760,000 tickets across 70 shows since starting in May 2025.

### Key Points
- Petco Park finale drew more than 42,339 fans and grossed $4,379,623 per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- The trek included a sold-out Madison Square Garden date and a three-night Kia Forum stand, plus shows across Australia, Latin America, and Europe.
- Tour total: over 760,000 tickets across 70 shows.
- CAA books the band outside Europe/UK; managed by Opera Ghost Management.

Pierce The Veil concluded its Jaws of Life era in grand fashion last weekend, playing at their beloved San Diego Padres’ stadium with more than 42,339 fans in attendance.

The rock band wrapped up the “I Can’t Hear You World Tour,” the trio’s most ambitious trek, with its biggest headline show to date at Petco Park, grossing a whopping $4,379,623, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

**[See Cover Story: Pierce The Veil Ascend To New Heights, Big Venues With ‘I Can’t Hear You Tour’](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/10/10/pierce-the-veil-ascend-to-new-heights-big-venues-with-i-cant-hear-you-tour/)**

Pierce The Veil began its most recent tour in May 2025 and played arenas and theaters across the globe, including a sold-out concert at Madison Square Garden in New York and a three-night stand at Inglewood’s Kia Forum, as well as shows in Australia, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Germany, Belgium, the U.K. and the Netherlands.

The SoCal band was featured on the cover of Pollstar last October. Creative Artists Agency books Pierce The Veil in all territories except Europe and the U.K., and James Throgmorton of Opera Ghost Management manages them.
