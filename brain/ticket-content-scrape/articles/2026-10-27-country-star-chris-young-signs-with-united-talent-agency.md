# Country Star Chris Young Signs With United Talent Agency

## Summary

United Talent Agency signed Grammy-nominated country star Chris Young for global representation across touring, media, film and brand partnerships.

### Key Points
- UTA now represents Young across live touring, media, filmed entertainment and brands.
- Young's most recent album (2025) has surpassed 80 million global streams.
- Standard artist-representation signing; no ticketing-industry angle.

## Transcript

Chris Young Primary Press Photo

United Talent Agency announced the signing of Grammy-nominated singer-songwriter Chris Young for global representation in all areas. The country star will work with the agency to secure opportunities across live touring, media, filmed entertainment, brand partnerships and more.

“I’m very excited to move into this new chapter at UTA,” Young said. “I really feel like there is a plan and a vision for growth and moving forward.”

Young broke onto the country scene in 2006 when he won the “Nashville Star” competition, and he then released his self-titled debut later that year. The multi-platinum singer is responsible for hit songs “Getting’ You Home,” “I’m Comin’ Over,” “Famous Friends,” and many more, and his most recent album, *I Didn’t Come Here to Leave*, which was released in 2025, has amassed more than 80 million global streams.

“Chris is a world-class singer, songwriter, and live performer,” UTA Agent Brian Hill said. “We’re excited to welcome him to the UTA family, and we look forward to building upon the incredible foundation he’s already established. This is only the beginning!”

The CMT Award winner is currently on the road for his “An Acoustic Evening” trek and will soon embark on his “An Acoustic Christmas” tour. In addition to headlining shows across the country and becoming a member of the historic Grand Ole Opry, Young has collaborated with artists like Miranda Lambert, Jon Pardi, Ashley McBryde and Scotty McCreery.
