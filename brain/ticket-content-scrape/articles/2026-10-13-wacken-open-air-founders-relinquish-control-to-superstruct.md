# Wacken Open Air Founders Relinquish Control To Superstruct

## Summary

Wacken Open Air founders Holger Hübner and Thomas Jensen have sold down further shares in operating company ICS GmbH to festival consolidator Superstruct Entertainment, which first invested in 2019 and now holds roughly 90% of the business. The founders' stake fell from about 15.5% to 5%, costing them their blocking minority, though they say they'll stay on to run the festival.

### Key Points
- Superstruct's stake in Wacken operator ICS GmbH rises to roughly 90%.
- Founders' combined stake drops from ~15.5% to ~5%, losing their blocking minority.
- Further sale was reportedly built into Superstruct's original 2019 deal terms.
- Founders credit Superstruct's support for the festival's survival through COVID lockdowns.
- Wacken drew ~85,000 attendees to its 35th-anniversary edition this year.

### Highlights
- Superstruct ownership of Wacken's ICS GmbH climbs to ~90% from an initial 2019 investment.
- Founders' combined stake shrinks from 15.5% to 5%, eliminating governance veto power.
- Founders stay on operationally but without blocking-minority control.

### Industry Problem
Large private-equity-backed consolidators (Superstruct) continuing to absorb founder-controlled festivals signals ongoing market-structure consolidation on the promoter/event side of live entertainment — concentration that can shift ticketing-vendor decisions to whatever platform relationships the acquirer standardizes across its portfolio.

### Proposed Solution
The item describes no ticketing response directly; the relevant posture is to monitor which ticketing vendors Superstruct's portfolio festivals standardize on as consolidation advances, since a wave of roll-up decisions could either open or close vendor-switch opportunities for independent primary platforms.

The founders of Wacken Open Air, Holger Hübner and Thomas Jensen, have given up more of their shares in operating company ICS GmbH to Superstruct, [which first invested in the world-famous German festival in 2019](https://news.pollstar.com/2019/08/18/superstruct-entertainment-invests-in-wacken-open-air-promoter-ics/), according to a [local news report](https://www.tagesschau.de/inland/regional/schleswigholstein/wacken-open-air-huebner-und-jensen-verkaufen-weitere-anteile,wacken-936.html).

According to an investigative report by NDR Schleswig-Holstein, both men now hold around 5% in ICS, reduced from around 15,5% they held initially kept after the Superstruct deal.

**[See: Sepultura Says ‘Goodbye Germany’ At 35th Wacken Open Air](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/08/05/sepultura-says-goodbye-germany-at-35th-wacken-open-air/)**

Jensen told the NDR upon request that further sale of their shares had always been part of the deal, bringing Superstruct’s share in ICS up to around 90%. He also confirmed that Superstruct’s support during the lockdowns has been necessary for the festival’s survival.

While both men reaffirmed that they’d continue to contribute their vast experience running Wacken going forward, the reduced amount of shares means they’ve lost their blocking minority during votes.

Wacken Open Air is widely considered one of the biggest and best metal gatherings worldwide. Taking place in the little German village of Wacken, the event attracted some 85,000 guests to its 35th anniversary this year, July 29–Aug. 1.
