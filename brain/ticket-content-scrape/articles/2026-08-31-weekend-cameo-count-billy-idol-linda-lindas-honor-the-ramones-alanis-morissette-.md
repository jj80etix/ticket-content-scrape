# Weekend Cameo Count: Billy Idol, Linda Lindas Honor The Ramones; Alanis Morissette At Olivia Rodrigo’s Daisy Chain Fields

## Summary

Weekend roundup of notable guest cameos across the touring circuit, including Billy Idol and The Linda Lindas honoring The Ramones at a 50th-anniversary tribute show, and Alanis Morissette joining Olivia Rodrigo's Daisy Chain Fields.

### Key Points
- Billy Idol and The Linda Lindas performed at The Official Ramones 50th Anniversary Tribute at Hollywood Forever Cemetery.
- Alanis Morissette made a surprise appearance at Olivia Rodrigo's Daisy Chain Fields festival.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2292794326](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2292794326-1.jpg)

Eloise Wong and Lucia de la Garza of The Linda Lindas perform onstage during The Official Ramones 50th Anniversary Tribute at Hollywood Forever Cemetery on Aug. 30, 2026, in Los Angeles, California. (Photo by Sean Sabhasaigh/Getty Images for The Ramones)

From L.A. to the O.C., Southern California was full of music stars last weekend with a 50 <sup>th</sup> anniversary celebration of the Ramones and Olivia Rodrigo’s inaugural Daisy Chain Fields festival.

The Official Ramones 50 <sup>th</sup> Anniversary Tribute was hosted by John Travolta at the Hollywood Forever Cemetery in Los Angeles on Sunday, and it featured a one-night-only performance from Cretin Family, a supergroup that included Green Day’s Billie Joe Armstrong, Travis Barker of blink-182, Rancid’s Tim Armstrong and C.J. Ramone, who played bass for the Ramones from 1989-1996.

The band performed covers of Ramones classics such as “Rockaway Beach” and “Sheena Is a Punk Rocker,” and they were joined by some special guests throughout the set. Actor, writer and musician Fred Armisen joined Cretin Family to sing “Danny Says,” and beloved local band The Linda Lindas graced the stage to play “California Sun” and “I Wanna Be Sedated.”

The final three songs of the set featured rock icon Billy Idol, who sang “Beat on the Brat,” Generation X’s “Ready Steady Go” and the Ramones classic “Blitzkrieg Bop.”

![GettyImages 2292659701](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2292659701.jpg)

Sarah McLachlan and Olivia Rodrigo perform on the Dandelion Stage during the Daisy Chain Fields Festival 2026 at Great Park Sports Complex on Aug. 29, 2026 in Irvine, California. (Photo by Monica Schipper/Getty Images)

Daisy Chain Fields In Irvine, California, also had a punk vibe with a Riot Grrrl moment when local band Emily’s Sassy Lime joined Bikini Kill to perform a rendition of the “Rebel Girl.”

Girl group KATSEYE, who had to drop out of the festival because a member injured her ankle during rehearsals, still made a brief appearance on Saturday. Three members of the group surprised fans and introduced Devon Again, who filled in for KATSEYE.

The biggest cameos of the night came, of course, during Olivia Rodrigo’s set. The 23-year-old star was joined by Sarah McLachlan for a rendition of “Building a Mystery,” and Alanis Morissette joined Rodrigo to perform her hit song “Ironic.” The festival concluded with the legendary Stevie Nicks standing alongside Rodrigo for a duet of “Landslide.”
