# Australian live bodies launch nationwide venue map for tour planning

## Summary

A coalition of Australian live-music industry bodies has launched the Australian Music Touring Network (AMTN), a free interactive map letting agents and artists browse venue capacity, genre fit, accessibility details and booking contacts nationwide, plus a tour-routing calculator. The project, led by the Australian Music Industry Network, builds on MusicNSW's earlier Regional Touring Network and aims to boost touring into regional towns.

### Key Points
- Venues of any size can register for free; profiles show capacity, genre balance, accessibility and technical specs.
- Includes a tour-itinerary feature calculating travel time between stops.
- Expands nationally on MusicNSW's existing Regional Touring Network model.
- Aligns with other efforts (e.g., booking agency Lowtide) to grow touring beyond Australia's major cities.

## Transcript

The interactive tool from a group of live sector organisations lets agents and artists plot tour routes and find venue specifications in one place

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/11/Australian-music-industry-network-scaled.jpg.webp)

© Australian Music Industry Network

Australian live music industry organisations have launched a free-to-use national venue directory which aims to let agents and artists plan tours with its interactive tools.

The service is known as the Australian Music Touring Network (AMTN) and launched with its website this week. It is the work of the Australian Music Industry Network (AMIN), a group that brings together the country’s eight state and territory peak bodies for contemporary music.

The site features an interactive map of Australia, with users able to click on a venue to view its profile. This shows details including capacity, genre balance, and accessibility information. This leads users to a booking portal with information about backline and technical specifications for the venue.

It also contains a tour itinerary feature, which calculates travel time between stops.

AMIN described the project as “a living map-based directory of live music venues right across the country, with so much information right at your fingertips,” saying that it is “built for artists, venues, and tour managers with community knowledge at its core”.

“Original live music venues of all sizes, from city rooms to regional pubs, community halls, to intimate cafes, can sign up for free and claim their place on the map,” AMIN said.

> “As we build, the Australian local live music ecosystem begins to come to life”

Part of the plan behind the project is to promote the organisation of touring outside of the major cities of Australia. This comes after new booking agency Lowtide told *IQ* it is targeting [“unlocking” the touring potential in the country’s regional towns](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/lowtide-agency-launches-with-mission-to-unlock-australian-regional-touring) to supplement the established city circuit.

AMIN first announced the building of the site in September this year by inviting venues to register their details on it. It is intended as an evolution of the Regional Touring Network (RTN), previously developed by MusicNSW.

MusicNSW managing director Joe Muller said of the launch: “I’m delighted to see MusicNSW’s Regional Touring Network inspiring the development of the Australian Music Touring Network.

“The success of the RTN and the feedback we’ve received from artists about its value underpins this national expansion, and I look forward to all of Australia having access to this invaluable resource.”

AMIN added: “As we build, the Australian local live music ecosystem begins to come to life in this living, map-driven directory that puts the right information in the right hands and opens up the connections that successful touring is built on.”
