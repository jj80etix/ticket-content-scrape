# Triangle Group raises $15m, onboards former Live Nation APAC president

## Summary

Singapore-based Triangle Group, an end-to-end platform for building out Asia-Pacific live touring and festival infrastructure, has raised $15m in combined equity and venture debt and brought on Roger Field — former president of Live Nation Asia Pacific and CEO of Live Nation Australia/New Zealand — as executive director and operating partner. The raise ($10m equity seed led by DSG Consumer Partners plus a $5m venture debt facility from Genesis Alternative Ventures) and the senior Live Nation hire signal a well-capitalized new entrant positioning itself as a regional alternative to global touring/promotion infrastructure in APAC.

### Key Points
- Triangle Group raised $15m total: a $10m equity seed round (DSG Consumer Partners, Golden Gate Ventures, the Oberoi Family Office, angel investors) plus a $5m venture debt facility from Genesis Alternative Ventures.
- Roger Field, formerly Live Nation APAC president and Live Nation Australia/NZ CEO (13-year Live Nation tenure), joins as executive director and operating partner after a year as advisor/investor.
- Founded as Creation Collective by Greg Hargrave (CEO) and Zaran Vachha (managing director); headquartered in Singapore.
- Triangle positions itself as handling "end-to-end" touring/festival infrastructure for APAC, including bearing the financial risk of owning a tour or festival, with a market-by-market operating model rather than a "global template."

### Highlights
- $15m raise ($10m equity + $5m venture debt) announced 6 August 2026, led by DSG Consumer Partners with Genesis Alternative Ventures providing debt.
- Roger Field (ex-Live Nation APAC president, ex-CEO Live Nation Australia/NZ) joins as executive director/operating partner, a significant senior-talent move out of Live Nation into a well-funded independent competitor.
- Company explicitly frames its value proposition as regional-operator expertise vs. Live Nation's "global template" approach to APAC touring.

### Industry Problem
This evidences continued capital inflow and executive talent migration into independent, non-Live-Nation touring/festival infrastructure in the fast-growing Asia-Pacific market — a market-structure signal that well-funded regional challengers are emerging outside the dominant incumbent's orbit (Q13).

### Proposed Solution
Triangle's own solution is building region-specific, end-to-end touring/festival infrastructure (including risk-bearing) rather than a one-size-fits-all global platform. For Etix, this is a monitor/evolve signal: track whether Triangle or similar APAC-focused entrants build or partner into primary ticketing (not just touring/promotion), which would change the competitive posture in a market segment currently outside Etix's core footprint.

**Triangle Group, an end-to-end entertainment platform with the aim of scaling Asia-Pacific’s live touring and festivals infrastructure, has raised $15m (£11.2m/€13m) and brought in former Live Nation APAC president Roger Field.**

The $15m in combined equity and venture debt consists of a $10m equity seed round led by DSG Consumer Partners, along with Golden Gate Ventures, the Oberoi Family Office and prominent angel investors, and a $5m venture debt facility from Genesis Alternative Ventures.

Headquartered in Singapore and originally founded as Creation Collective by Greg Hargrave and Zaran Vachha, Triangle aims to build relationships on both sides, with the artist and the consumer, and manages all aspects of live touring and festivals, including the financial risk of owning a tour or festival.

“We set out to build seamless, end-to-end infrastructure for artists and festivals coming to Asia,” said Hargrave, who also acts as chief executive of Triangle.

“A raise this size in this market, and the region’s most senior executive choosing to join us, are the clearest proof yet that vision is real, and ready to scale.”

Managing director Vachha added: “We build around the specific artist in terms of who their audience is, which markets are ready, when to step up and when to hold. We’re not booking a one-off; we’re routing a region.”

To aid continued growth, Roger Field, formerly president of Live Nation Asia Pacific and chief executive of Live Nation Australia and New Zealand, has also joined the business after one year as an advisor and investor. He has taken on the role of executive director and operating partner.

Field has more than three decades of experience in live entertainment with roles in ticketing, venue management, and concert and festival promotion. His 13-year tenure at Live Nation culminated in becoming CEO of Live Nation Australia and New Zealand, and then president of Live Nation Asia Pacific from 2020.

“Running Live Nation across this region taught me one thing: Asia-Pacific doesn’t need a global template; it needs an operator who understands every market individually. That’s the business we’re building at Triangle, where the standards of a global player are applied with a regional focus,” he said.
