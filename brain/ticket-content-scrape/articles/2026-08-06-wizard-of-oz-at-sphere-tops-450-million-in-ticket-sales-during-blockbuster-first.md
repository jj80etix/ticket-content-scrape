# ‘Wizard of Oz’ at Sphere Tops $450 Million in Ticket Sales During Blockbuster First Year

## Summary

Sphere Entertainment announced that *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere* has surpassed $450 million in ticket sales and drawn over 3.6 million guests in its first year at the Las Vegas venue. The immersive, tech-driven reimagining of the classic film opened August 28, 2025, and has staged more than 850 performances for visitors from all 50 states and 140+ countries. The piece is a box-office milestone press release, marking a one-year anniversary with special in-venue activations running August 10–September 30.

### Key Points
- The show has generated $450M+ in ticket sales and 3.6M+ attendees since its August 2025 opening.
- More than 850 performances have been staged in the first year.
- Visitors have come from all 50 US states and 140+ countries.
- The production uses Sphere's 160,000-sq-ft LED display, 167,000-speaker sound system, haptic seating, and 4D effects (675,000+ falling apples, 8,000 butterfly flights, etc.).
- Anniversary activations (Aug 10–Sept 30) include exclusive photo moments and themed food/beverage items.
- Production was a collaboration between Sphere Studios, Warner Bros. Discovery, Google, and Magnopus.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/wizard-of-oz-1.webp)

*The Wizard of Oz at Sphere* has generated more than $450 million in ticket sales and welcomed over 3.6 million guests during its first year, Sphere Entertainment announced Thursday.

The immersive production opened at the Las Vegas venue on Aug. 28, 2025, and has since staged more than 850 performances. Visitors have traveled from all 50 states and more than 140 countries to experience Sphere’s technology-driven reimagining of the classic film.

“The response to *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere* continues to exceed expectations, with millions of guests from around the world experiencing the film in a transformative new way,” Jen Koester, president and chief operating officer of Sphere, said in a press release. “It’s been rewarding to see first-time visitors and lifelong fans alike create new memories with *The Wizard of Oz at Sphere*, and our anniversary celebration is both a thank you to those who have made the experience a success and an invitation for new guests to experience the wonder for themselves.”

The production surrounds audiences with Sphere’s 160,000-square-foot LED display, 167,000-speaker immersive sound system, haptic seating and large-scale 4D effects. Those effects have produced some eye-catching totals during the show’s first year, including more than 675,000 falling apples, 8,000 butterfly flights, 6,500 pounds of blown leaves and 3,200 winged monkey flights.
