# Karol G To Stream Performance On TikTok Live Premiere

## Summary

Karol G will perform a one-hour livestreamed set via TikTok LIVE Premiere, promoting her latest album "No Me Arrepiento De Sentir Tanto." The event leverages Karol G's 56 million TikTok followers and the platform's established LIVE format for major artist performances.

### Key Points
- One-hour TikTok LIVE Premiere set, 8 p.m. CST, streamed globally with live fan comments.
- Promotes her August album release, featuring Drake, Bruno Mars, and others.
- TikTok positions LIVE Premiere as its flagship format for major artist moments.

## Transcript

![karol](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/karol.jpg)

Global megastar Karol G will be going live on TikTok tonight to perform music from her latest album, *No Me Arrepiento De Sentir Tanto*, which was released through Bichota Records and Interscope in August.

The Colombian singer will perform a one-hour set for TikTok LIVE Premiere at 8 p.m. CST, and fans can stream it wherever they are. Karol G’s latest album shot up to the top of the charts and featured collaborations with Drake, Bruno Mars, Judeline, Rusowsky and Greg Gonzalez of Cigarettes After Sex.

Karol G has amassed more than 56 million followers on TikTok, and the platform has helped the artist expand her reach globally as well as promote Latin culture. Her smash hit “Si Antes Te Hubiera Conocido” was one of the songs of the summer in 2024 and turned up in more than 1.7 million videos. When *Tropicoqueta* was released in June 2025, fans joined the in-app campaign that included an AI Karol G effect, themed profile frames and a leaderboard ranking countries by views of content.

“Latin artists are among the most powerful drivers of music discovery on TikTok, and KAROL G is at the center of that movement,” said Eduardo Praça, TikTok Artist Partnership US Latin & LATAM. “Her fans on TikTok don’t just listen, they create, celebrate and show up for every chapter of her journey. With TikTok LIVE we’re bringing together creativity, fandom, and live connection to give fans a front row seat to their favorite artists’ biggest moments.”

TikTok LIVE Premiere is the social media platform’s flagship LIVE series built to host the biggest names in music, entertainment, sports, and culture. Comments run alongside the performance, allowing fans worldwide to react together to the same moment.

Fans can tune in at 8 p.m. CST on [Karol G’s official TikTok account](https://www.tiktok.com/@karolg?lang=en).
