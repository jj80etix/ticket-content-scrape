# Dylan Gossett Extends ‘The Ramblin’ Tour’

## Summary

Dylan Gossett extended "The Ramblin' Tour" with new spring 2027 U.S. headline dates following his sophomore album Ramblin', adding stops from Charleston to Tulsa on top of previously booked Australia/New Zealand and Europe/UK legs.

### Key Points
- New U.S. leg runs March 25-May 21, 2027, opening in Charleston, SC and closing in Tulsa, OK.
- Supports sophomore album Ramblin' (Big Loud Texas/Mercury Records).
- Also includes December Australia/NZ dates and Feb.-Mar. 2027 Europe/UK dates.
- Will perform at Stagecoach festival April 23.
- General onsale begins Oct. 6.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Dylan-Gossett.webp)

Dylan Gossett will return to the road next spring with U.S. headline dates for “The Ramblin’ Tour.”

The newly announced dates are set to begin March 25, 2027, at Charleston Music Hall in South Carolina. From there, the singer is slated to make stops in cities such as Charlotte, Memphis, Birmingham, Philadelphia, Detroit, Kansas City, and Austin before wrapping up the tour on May 21 in Tulsa at Cain’s Ballroom.

Leon Majcen and Clay Street Unit will support select dates.

The new leg of the tour follows previously announced dates across the U.S. this fall, including stops in College Station, Waco, Chicago and Pittsburgh in October, followed by Florida performances in November.
