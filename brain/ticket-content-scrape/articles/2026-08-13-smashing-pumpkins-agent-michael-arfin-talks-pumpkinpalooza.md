# Smashing Pumpkins Agent Michael Arfin Talks ‘Pumpkinpalooza’

## Summary

Pollstar interviews IAG agent Michael Arfin about "Pumpkinpalooza," a week of Chicago festivities surrounding The Smashing Pumpkins' return to Lollapalooza, including an underplay show at the Riviera Theatre and merch/F&B pop-ups. Arfin also previews the band's fall "Rats In A Cage Tour," an arena run celebrating the 30th anniversary of *Mellon Collie and the Infinite Sadness*.

### Key Points
- Underplay show at the Riviera Theatre (July 29) served as a rehearsal ahead of the band's Lollapalooza headline set on July 31.
- Lollapalooza set featured guest appearances from Yungblud, Olivia Rodrigo, and former bassist Melissa Auf der Maur.
- Fall "Rats In A Cage Tour" launches Sept. 30 in Columbus, OH, with arena dates through mid-November across North America.
- Arfin describes demand as the strongest since the three original members reunited.

## Transcript

![yAP1U4BQ](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/yAP1U4BQ.jpg)

The Smashing Pumpkins take the stage for an underplay show at the Riviera Theatre in Chicago July 29, 2026. Photo by Tyler Curtis.

The Smashing Pumpkins celebrated its grand return to Lollapalooza – the Grammy-winning alternative rock band’s first appearance at the famed festival since it was a touring entity in 1994 – with a week of festivities in their Chicago hometown dubbed “Pumpkinpalooza.” The Windy City fun included a July 29 underplay show at the historic Riviera Theatre, the 2,500-capacity venue that was the site of their release party for 1995’s *Mellon Collie and the Infinite Sadness*.

![0bP AimA](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/0bP-AimA.jpg)

A fan shows off special crossover merch sold during “Pumpkinpalooza.”

“As a Chicagoan, born and raised, I take great pride in contributing to our city’s immense and colorful cultural fabric,” Billy Corgan said in a statement. “And so this year’s already historic Lollapalooza is that chance to make new memories, new friends, and have a bit of Midwest fun, too as only we might.”

The band hosted a merchandise pop-up on Wabash Ave, a few blocks from Lollapalooza’s home at Grant Park, “selling crossover designs merging the Pumpkins and the Chicago Cubs,” according to the *Chicago Sun Times*.  
  
F&B offerings at the Lolla festival grounds included Madame Zuzu’s Celestial Lounge, which gave fans the experience of visiting Billy Corgan and Chloé Mendel Corgan’s Highland Park teahouse, with a menu featuring matcha, vinyl and exclusive merchandise.  
  
And over at Chicago cocktail lounge The Aviary, the good times continued with the debut of a limited-edition speciality cocktail Infinite Mellons, inspired by the band’s Lolla homecoming.  
  
A review of The Smashing Pumpkins’ Lollapalooza set on July 31 by the *[Chicago Sun Times](https://chicago.suntimes.com/lollapalooza/2026/07/31/smashing-pumpkins-review-lollapalooza-olivia-rodrigo-yungblud)* proclaimed that “The Smashing Pumpkins came to shakedown 1994 … Headlining the festival again 32 years later was about recognizing that past while moving into a new era and a new audience for the band — and proving they very much still had a place on the lineup.” The headline set boasted appearances by Yungblud (who joined the band for a rendition of the tune “Luna”), Olivia Rodrigo (an acoustic duet of “Thirty-Three”) and the band’s former bassist Melissa Auf der Maur (“The Everlasting Gaze”).

The Pumpkins will be back on stage this fall for “The Rats In A Cage Tour,” launching Sept. 30 in Columbus, Ohio, at the Schottenstein Center. Arena dates are booked through November including stops at Brooklyn’s Barclays Center, Chicago’s United Center, Austin’s Moody Center and the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California. The band – which features original members Billy Corgan, Jimmy Chamberlin, and James Iha, along with Jack Bates on bass and Kiki Wong on guitar – will be playing a set celebrating *Mellon Collie And The Infinite Sadness*, along with additional hits, deep cuts, and fan-favorites.

![P073026D 2229a](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/P073026D-2229a.jpg)

Olivia Rodrigo appears as a special guest during The Smashing Pumpkins’ headline set at Lollapalooza at Grant Park in Chicago on July 31, 2026. Photo by Tyler Curtis.

*Pollstar* caught up with IAG’s Michael Arfin, who serves as Executive Vice President, Global Touring at the agency and has directed Pumpkins’ touring for the last several years, to learn more about “Pumpkinpalooza.”

**Pollstar: How did the idea for “Pumpkinpalooza” come about?**

**Michael Arfin:** Most things start with the band and Billy coming up with these incredible ideas and these branding opportunities. That’s where it usually begins and there’s a great team surrounding them including Red Light Management, the agency, publicist, everybody really helps bring all of these things to fruition from the booking to the special guests and the pop-ups and everything.

![nEwQk 1g](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/nEwQk-1g.jpg)

Photo by Tyler Curtis.

**It feels like this was a wonderful love letter to the city and the fans. Can you talk about choosing the Riviera Theatre as the venue for the underplay show?**

The Riv has a sentimental place and history with the band. … They have a ton of history in Chicago and the Riviera’s been there forever. So it made sense for a lot of reasons.

**How did the show go?**

The show was not only special for the fans but was also a warm up for the Lolla set. So they used that as a rehearsal as well.

One of the highlights of the Riviera show was when Barry Williams from the Brady Bunch came out and they did a cover (“It’s a Sunshine Day”) with The Pumpkins. That was, I think, a really unique, odd, but very special moment that fans really enjoyed. Everyone was ecstatic and really content with the results.

![LNHJ3e9w](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/LNHJ3e9w.jpg)

B arry Williams (right) joins The Smashing Pumpkins at the Riviera Theatre in Chicago July 29, 2026. Photo by Tyler Curtis.

**Anything you can share about the fall tour?**

The fall tour is a continuation of the celebration of the 30th anniversary of their *Mellon Collie* album in addition to playing some amazing other tracks that people know and love. We’re very excited about the result thus far. The demand has been the strongest to date for The Pumpkins since we’ve had the three original members back … The amount of work and effort and focus that they’ve put into rebuilding this amazing brand with an incredible catalog, it’s all coming together and the results are showing themselves. The fruits of the labor are showing. So, it’s great to be rolling into this next tour with momentum and the band just playing at the top of their game.

**What’s been one of the highlights of working with the band the past decade?**

As far as highlights, I think working with the band overall is a highlight. They’re an artist that I’m a massive fan of, that I grew up listening to. So for me it’s just a full circle, incredible experience – from the underplay stuff that we put together to Billy’s solo career to Pumpkins on the grandest stage. It’s really a pleasure to work with the team and how committed and creative they still are.
