# AEG Presents Names Chris Hammond Pacific Northwest SVP Ahead of Portland Venue Opening

## Summary

(Same Chris Hammond/AEG Presents promotion as covered by Pollstar, via TicketNews.) AEG Presents has named veteran talent buyer Chris Hammond SVP of the Pacific Northwest, moving him from Las Vegas to Seattle ahead of a new AEG concert hall opening in Portland.

### Key Points
- Hammond promoted from VP of Talent (Las Vegas) to SVP, Pacific Northwest.
- Move precedes a new AEG venue opening in Portland next year.
- Hammond spent 8 years with AEG Presents in Las Vegas, including programming at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas.
- Reports to Brent Fedrizzi, President of North American regional offices.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/AEG-Presents.webp)

AEG Presents has promoted veteran talent buyer Chris Hammond to senior vice president of the Pacific Northwest, putting the longtime Las Vegas executive in charge of booking and operational strategy for the company’s growing regional venue portfolio as it prepares to open a new concert hall in Portland next year.

The appointment moves Hammond from his position as vice president of talent in AEG Presents’ Las Vegas office to a new role based in Seattle. He will report to Brent Fedrizzi, president of North American regional offices for AEG Presents.

Hammond has spent the past eight years with AEG Presents in Las Vegas, most recently leading the company’s regional talent-buying team. His responsibilities have included shaping programming at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas and managing relationships with agents and artists in one of the country’s busiest live entertainment markets.

“Chris has demonstrated that he is ready to take this next step with the company,” Fedrizzi said. He credited Hammond’s ability to develop artist relationships while contributing to the financial performance of the Las Vegas operation.

The promotion comes as AEG Presents prepares for a significant expansion of its Pacific Northwest venue business, highlighted by the planned opening of a 4,250-capacity concert hall in Portland’s Lloyd District in 2027.

The venue, [first announced in 2024](https://www.ticketnews.com/2024/07/aeg-presents-to-open-new-venue-in-portland/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), is being developed in partnership with Portland-based independent promoter Monqui Presents. Located at the former Nordstrom site near Lloyd Center, the 68,000-square-foot facility will accommodate audiences ranging from approximately 2,000 to 4,250 through flexible configurations. Its opening is currently targeted for early 2027.

“I’m really excited for what lies ahead,” Hammond said. “The Pacific Northwest’s music scene speaks for itself…it’s globally recognized. And the live business is a critical part of that ecosystem.”

“Add to that a new venue coming online in Portland next year and it’s shaping up to be quite an exciting time for us. I can’t wait to dig in with the team and get to work.”

The Portland project will enter a market preparing for the arrival of another major concert venue. Live Nation is partnering with local developers Beam Development and Colas Development Group on [Steelhead, a 3,500-capacity venue](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/04/portlands-steelhead-music-venue-unveils-design-eyes-2027-launch/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in the city’s Central Eastside neighborhood, currently expected to open in summer 2027.

The two projects will add substantial new capacity for midsize touring acts in Portland, potentially attracting concerts that might otherwise bypass the market while creating additional competition for bookings between two of the country’s largest concert promoters.

Hammond’s appointment also follows [AEG Presents’ expansion of its national venue development team](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/aeg-presents-expands-venue-development-team/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), announced in July. That reorganization included several promotions and new hires supporting the company’s development pipeline, which includes projects in Portland and Austin scheduled to open in 2027.

Before joining AEG Presents in 2018, Hammond built a career spanning professional sports and live entertainment. He began in baseball as general manager of the West Palm Beach Expos, then a Class A affiliate of the Montreal Expos. According to AEG, he became the youngest active general manager in professional baseball at age 22.

In 2003, Hammond founded Rock ‘n Roll Wine, a business combining wine experiences with live music. Over approximately 15 years, the company produced more than 600 events across six states. Hammond was also recognized among Las Vegas’ “40 Under 40” business leaders by *In Business Magazine* in 2007.

His new responsibilities will place him at the center of AEG Presents’ booking and venue operations in the Pacific Northwest as the promoter prepares to bring its newest Portland concert facility online.
