# Ticketmaster Expands Middle East Sports Portfolio With Dubai Racing Club Deal

## Summary

Dubai Racing Club has named Ticketmaster its official ticketing partner for the 2026-27 season at Meydan Racecourse (including the Nov. 6-Mar. 12 Dubai Racing Carnival and the Mar. 27, 2027 Dubai World Cup, worth $30.5M in total prize money), switching from incumbent provider Vivaticket. Ticketmaster will run the club's branded ticketing site under a white-label model, continuing its push into Middle East sports ticketing.

### Key Points
- Dubai Racing Club switches ticketing providers from Vivaticket to Ticketmaster for the 2026-27 season.
- Deal covers the full Dubai Racing Carnival (16 race meetings, Nov. 6-Mar. 12) and the Dubai World Cup (Mar. 27, 2027; $30.5M total purse).
- Ticketmaster will operate a club-branded, white-labeled ticketing site/experience rather than a Ticketmaster-branded storefront.
- Framed by Ticketmaster Middle East's MD as part of continued regional expansion.

### Highlights
- A concrete vendor-switch win for Ticketmaster against an international competitor (Vivaticket) in a non-North-American sports vertical.
- White-label branding kept entirely as the client's own, rather than Ticketmaster's.

### Industry Problem
This is a data point on why clients switch primary-ticketing vendors (win/loss signal) — a prestige international sports/racing client moved away from an established provider, suggesting technology and white-label flexibility and global account reach are differentiators worth tracking.

### Proposed Solution
Worth a follow-up read on why Vivaticket lost the account (not covered in this article) to extract concrete win/loss lessons; in the meantime, it reinforces that white-label flexibility and deep client-branding control are table stakes competitors are winning major accounts on — a relevant benchmark for Etix's own white-label/client-branding capabilities.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Dubai-Racing-Club-appoints-Ticketmaster.webp)

Dubai Racing Club has appointed Ticketmaster as its official ticketing partner for the 2026-27 season at Meydan Racecourse, including the Dubai World Cup and Dubai Racing Carnival.

Under the new agreement, Ticketmaster will operate Dubai Racing Club’s branded ticketing website, providing ticket purchasing and management services for events throughout the racing season.

The deal marks a change in ticketing providers for the organization, which previously worked with Vivaticket. The company was identified as the exclusive ticketing partner in Dubai Racing Club’s official hospitality materials for the 2026 Dubai World Cup.

“Every season, we look for new ways to make an evening at Meydan Racecourse even more enjoyable for our guests – whether they are here for the racing, the hospitality, the dining or simply the atmosphere,” said Ali Al Ali, board member and CEO of Dubai Racing Club. “We’re pleased to welcome Ticketmaster as our official ticketing partner, helping make it even easier for fans in the UAE and around the world to join us for another exciting season.”
