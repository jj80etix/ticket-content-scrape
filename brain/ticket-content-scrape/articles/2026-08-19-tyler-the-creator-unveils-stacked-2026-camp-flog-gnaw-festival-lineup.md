# Tyler, The Creator Unveils Stacked 2026 Camp Flog Gnaw Festival Lineup

## Summary

Tyler, The Creator revealed the lineup for the Nov 14-15 Camp Flog Gnaw Festival at Dodger Stadium, featuring Turnstile, Steve Lacy, Future, JID, and dozens more; tickets sold out shortly after the initial on-sale, with only a waitlist remaining.

### Key Points
- Nov 14-15 at Dodger Stadium; lineup includes Turnstile, Steve Lacy, Future, JID, Vince Staples, Wallows, and more.
- Tickets already sold out; fans directed to the festival's official waitlist.
- Follows Tyler's 2025 "Chromakopia: The World Tour," which grossed over $2M on a single July 2025 Raleigh date per Pollstar Boxoffice.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2148574058](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/GettyImages-2148574058.jpg)

Tyler, the Creator performs at the Coachella Stage during the 2024 Coachella Valley Music and Arts Festival at Empire Polo Club on April 13, 2024 in Indio, California. (Photo by Arturo Holmes/Getty Images for Coachella)

Tyler, the Creator is back with his annual Camp Flog Gnaw Festival, set to run from Nov. 14 & 15 at Dodger Stadium, with yet another stacked lineup. Artists who have been confirmed for the fan-favorite festival include newcomers, veterans, and legends alike, such as Lola Young, Quadeca, Lupe Fiasco, 2hollis, JID, Steve Lacy, Isaiah Rashad, Concrete Boys, Earl Sweatshirt & MIKE, Gabriel Jacoby, Natanya, Don Toliver, Syd, threetwenty, Left Brain, Thee Sacred Souls, Domo Genesis, Liim, Magdalena Bay, Jane Remover, Mike G, NSQK, Turnstile, Audrey Nuna, Latin Mafia, Leon Thomas, Casey Veggies, Wallows, Ama, 310babii, Future, Nomi, Tyler, The Creator, The Alchemist, Not For Radio, Vince Staples, GENA, Wyatt Waddell, and 54 Ultra.

Tickets for Camp Flog Gnaw 2026 initially went on sale earlier this summer. However, if you’re looking to get a hold of tickets now that the lineup is here, you’re out of luck because they have since sold out. At this point, those still hoping to attend Flog Gnaw can sign up to join the waitlist on the festival’s official [website](https://www.campfloggnaw.com/).

Tyler, the Creator most recently released two stellar albums in 2024’s *Chromakopia* and 2025’s *Don’t Tap The Glass.* The multi-hyphenate embarked on the “Chromakopia: The World Tour” to support both LPs beginning in February 2025 and concluded in March. According to [Pollstar Boxoffice reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/175138), the sold-out trek was commercially successful for Tyler, which featured a July 12, 2025 stop at Raleigh, NC’s Lenovo Center, averaging $2,017,298 on the night.
