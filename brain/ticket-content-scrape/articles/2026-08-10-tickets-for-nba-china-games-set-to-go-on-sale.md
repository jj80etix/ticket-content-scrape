# Tickets for NBA China Games set to go on sale

## Summary

Tickets for the 2026 NBA China Games (Dallas Mavericks vs. Houston Rockets preseason games, Oct. 9 and 11 at Macao's Venetian Arena) go on sale Aug. 12 via Cotai Ticketing, alongside a separately ticketed Fan Day.

### Key Points
- Fan Day tickets start at MOP 488; match tickets start at MOP 588.
- Fan Day features K-pop and rap performers plus player activities, hosted by rapper-actor MC Jin.
- Follows a 2025 Macau series between the Suns and Nets; a Nuggets-Pacers game is also set for Mexico City in November.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Tickets for the NBA China Games 2026 are set to go on sale on Wednesday (August 12) through Cotai Ticketing.**

The series will see the Dallas Mavericks face the Houston Rockets in preseason fixtures on October 9 and October 11 at the Venetian Arena in Macao.

Tickets for NBA Fan Day are also set to go on sale at the same time for MOP 488 (£45/$60/€52) while match tickets will start at MOP 588.

The events will also feature performances from artists KiiiKiii, Keung To and K-pop star Evan.

Fan Day will be hosted by rapper-actor MC Jin and have a variety of fan engagement activities featuring Mavericks and Rockets players, entertainment and mascots, including a skills challenge, three-point contest, shooting stars challenge, and a slam dunk show featuring professional dunkers.

The fixtures follow on from a two-match series held in Macau last year between the Phoenix Suns and the Brooklyn Nets which saw the teams win one apiece.

They are the only international preseason matches scheduled for this year, while a regular season game between the Denver Nuggets and Indian Pacers is set to take place on November 7 at Mexico City Arena.
