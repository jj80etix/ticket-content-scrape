# Ticketmaster Outage Disrupts Event Entry as Fans Report Widespread Access Problems

## Summary

A Ticketmaster outage on Nov. 19 delayed entry at South Dakota's state high school volleyball tournament and caused widespread reports of failed purchases, transfers and ticket retrieval across the US, UK, Canada and Ireland. Outage trackers showed a spike peaking at 544 complaints in a 15-minute window; service was largely restored within about 90 minutes, but the cause was not publicly explained. It's the latest in a string of digital-ticket-access failures, following October ticket-transfer problems and a mid-November Microsoft outage that disrupted ticket delivery via Outlook.

### Key Points
- Outage began ~4:30pm Central Nov. 19, just before a 5pm event session; mostly resolved by 6pm.
- IsItDownRightNow logged 544 complaints in its busiest 15-minute window; elevated reports also came from the UK, France and Ireland.
- Customers reported failed purchases, inaccessible tickets, login errors and stuck transfers via Reddit and status-monitoring sites.
- Ticketmaster has not published a cause, scope or duration for the incident.
- Follows October's reported transfer failures and a Nov. 12-13 Microsoft outage that blocked email-delivered tickets/codes.

### Highlights
- 544 complaints logged in a single 15-minute window (IsItDownRightNow), with elevated reports in the US, UK, Canada, France and Ireland.
- Outage window: ~4:30pm-6pm Central, Nov. 19, 2026, overlapping a live event's entry time.
- Third reported digital-ticket-access failure in two months (Oct. transfer bug, Nov. 12-13 Microsoft/Outlook outage, this Nov. 19 outage).

### Industry Problem
Mobile-only/account-dependent ticket delivery creates a single point of failure: when login, transfer, or wallet-loading systems go down, patrons with valid tickets can be denied entry or unable to complete purchases, with no disclosed fallback beyond venue "mobile resolution windows."

### Proposed Solution
Ticketmaster's own guidance (pre-load tickets, use phone wallets) is a patron-side mitigation, not a platform fix. An independent primary platform like Etix could differentiate by offering offline-capable wallet passes, redundant (SMS/email/PDF) ticket delivery, and a documented incident-communication and venue-fallback protocol to reduce entry-day risk exposure.

A Ticketmaster outage delayed entry for spectators attending South Dakota’s state high school volleyball tournament Thursday evening, with lines reportedly stretching around the Denny Sanford PREMIER Center in Sioux Falls as customers elsewhere reported problems accessing tickets, completing purchases and transferring tickets.

The disruption occurred November 19, as fans arrived for the evening session of the South Dakota High School Activities Association’s state volleyball championships. According to [Dakota News Now](https://www.dakotanewsnow.com/2026/11/20/ticketmaster-outage-affects-south-dakota-state-volleyball-tournament/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), some spectators described entrance lines wrapping around the arena while Ticketmaster experienced service problems.

Outage reports tracked by Downdetector began surging shortly after 4:30 p.m. Central, just ahead of the scheduled 5 p.m. session. Most services were reportedly restored shortly before 6 p.m., and the tournament continued without further reported problems.

The championships began Thursday and run through Saturday, November 21, with multiple sessions scheduled at the arena. Tickets are sold through Ticketmaster, although the SDHSAA also permits [general-admission ticket purchases at the venue](https://sdhsaa.com/ticket-information-volleyball/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

[Outage-monitoring service IsItDownRightNow](https://isitdownrightnow.app/services/ticketmaster/outages/2026-11-19/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) recorded a substantial increase in Ticketmaster-related problem reports Thursday, with its busiest 15-minute period registering 544 complaints. Elevated reporting was recorded in the United States, United Kingdom, Canada and Ireland.

Those figures represent user-submitted complaints rather than independently verified outages. They do not establish the number of customers affected or whether every reported problem shared the same technical cause.

Customers also described difficulties across several Ticketmaster functions.

In a [Reddit discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/Ticketmaster/comments/1y0mm5j/anyone_having_issues_buying_tickets/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), users reported being unable to complete purchases, access previously purchased tickets or process transfers.

One customer said tickets for a hockey game that evening would not load. Another reported being unable to retrieve tickets on either a phone or laptop, while other users described login errors and failed transfers.

A customer attending a concert at Madison Square Garden in New York said a Ticketmaster representative had acknowledged an outage and directed the customer to the venue’s mobile ticket resolution window. That account has not been independently verified, and there was no confirmation of a broader admission disruption at the New York venue.

Other customers reported problems accessing Ticketmaster from the United Kingdom and France, suggesting the complaints were not limited to one domestic market.

Third-party monitoring service StatusGator separately [identified a November 19 incident](https://statusgator.com/services/ticketmaster?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) involving tickets failing to load in customers’ order histories.

A detailed public explanation from Ticketmaster identifying the cause, scope and duration of the disruption was not immediately available.

## Another Reminder of Digital Ticketing Vulnerabilities

The incident adds to a series of recent technical problems affecting consumers’ ability to access tickets they have already purchased.

In October, [TicketNews reported widespread Ticketmaster ticket-transfer failures](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/ticketmaster-transfer-problems-october-2/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) affecting fans attending concerts and sporting events across the United States.

Customers described transfers stuck between accounts, unavailable tickets and error messages preventing recipients from accepting tickets. Ticketmaster’s support team subsequently acknowledged the problems and provided updates on the processing of pending transfers.

More recently, a [November 12-13 Microsoft outage disrupted digital ticket access](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/microsoft-outage-digital-ticketing-access-risks/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) for customers dependent on Outlook and other Microsoft services to receive transfer notifications, electronic tickets or authentication codes.

That disruption demonstrated how external technology failures can interfere with ticket access even when the underlying ticketing platform remains operational.

Thursday’s Ticketmaster incident presents a somewhat different problem, with customers reporting difficulties directly involving Ticketmaster’s website, application and ticket-management functions.

The implications are particularly significant for events that rely on mobile credentials rather than traditional printable tickets. When ticket access depends on successfully logging into an account, receiving a transfer or loading a barcode, a service interruption can interfere with admission even when the customer already owns a valid ticket.

Ticketmaster recommends that customers access their mobile tickets in advance or save eligible tickets to their phone’s digital wallet, allowing credentials to remain available without an internet connection.

The company’s [mobile-entry instructions](https://help.ticketmaster.com/hc/en-us/articles/9786597785617-How-do-I-use-Mobile-Entry-tickets?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) also direct customers who cannot access tickets on event day to seek assistance at a venue’s mobile resolution window, bringing their order number, matching photo identification and payment information.

Those precautions may help with certain connectivity or account-access problems, but they do not necessarily address failures involving pending transfers, tickets that have not yet been retrieved or venue-side admission systems.

For the South Dakota volleyball tournament, it remains unclear whether alternative admission procedures were implemented during Thursday’s disruption.

The championships are scheduled to continue Friday and Saturday, with daytime and evening sessions at the PREMIER Center. Whether the tournament organizers or Ticketmaster will provide additional information about the outage and any measures taken to prevent further admission delays remains to be seen.
