# Stagecoach 2027 to Honor Dolly Parton With Opening-Day Tribute

## Summary

Stagecoach 2027 will open with a tribute to Dolly Parton on its opening day. The announcement is part of the festival's lineup/programming rollout for next year.

### Key Points
- Stagecoach 2027 opening day will feature a Dolly Parton tribute.
- Routine festival programming announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Stagecoach.webp)

Stagecoach will dedicate the opening day of its 2027 country music festival to Dolly Parton, encouraging fans to celebrate the late country music icon when the event returns to the Empire Polo Club in Indio, California, next spring.

Friday, April 23, will officially be known as “Dolly Day,” with the announcement coming during Monday’s episode of NBC’s *The Voice*. The festival will run through Sunday, April 25.

*The Voice* host Carson Daly revealed that festival organizers are encouraging attendees to wear Parton-inspired attire on the opening day. Country singer and coach Riley Green helped introduce the announcement by showing off a sleeveless shirt featuring Parton’s image.

Green is scheduled to headline the opening night of Stagecoach, while fellow *The Voice* coach Kelly Clarkson will make her Stagecoach debut during the festival.

Stagecoach [announced its 2027 lineup in September](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/garth-brooks-riley-green-and-zach-top-to-headline-stagecoach-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), with Zach Top and Garth Brooks joining Green as the festival’s three headliners. Willie Nelson & Family, HARDY, Brothers Osborne, Turnpike Troubadours, Charley Crockett, Shaboozey, Jo Dee Messina and Gretchen Wilson are also scheduled to perform. The festival will feature artists from outside the country music genre, including Journey, Pitbull, Matchbox Twenty, David Lee Roth and Melissa Etheridge.

Florida Georgia Line will also return to Stagecoach for a reunion performance as part of the festival’s late-night programming, marking the duo’s first appearance at the event since headlining in 2018.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit the [official Stagecoach website](https://www.stagecoachfestival.com/passes/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
