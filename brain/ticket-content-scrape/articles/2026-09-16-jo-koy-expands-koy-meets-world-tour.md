# Jo Koy Expands Koy Meets World Tour

## Summary

Jo Koy is extending his Koy Meets World Tour with more than 30 additional U.S. shows scheduled for early 2027. The new run begins January 15 and wraps May 8, with presales starting this week ahead of general onsale.

### Key Points
- Tour resumes Jan. 15, 2027 at Ovens Auditorium, Charlotte, NC
- Runs through Atlanta, Orlando, Denver, Kansas City, Indianapolis, Milwaukee, and Los Angeles
- Concludes May 8, 2027 at Prudential Center, Newark, NJ
- Notable stops: Kia Center Orlando (Jan. 30), Dickies Arena Fort Worth (Feb. 27), Kia Forum Inglewood (Apr. 24)
- Presales begin this week

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Jo-Koy.webp)

Jo Koy is extending his Koy Meets World Tour with more than 30 U.S. shows scheduled for early 2027.

The newly announced run begins January 15 at Ovens Auditorium in Charlotte, North Carolina. It will travel through cities including Atlanta, Orlando, Denver, Kansas City, Indianapolis, Milwaukee and Los Angeles before concluding May 8 at Prudential Center in Newark, New Jersey.

Other notable stops include Kia Center in Orlando on January 30, Dickies Arena in Fort Worth on February 27, Bellco Theatre in Denver on March 19 and the Kia Forum in Inglewood, California, on April 24.
