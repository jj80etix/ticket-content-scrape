# Festival news: Global Citizen reveals lineup for Central Park outing

## Summary

IQ's festival news roundup covers upcoming lineups and announcements across five festivals: Global Citizen's Central Park outing (26 Sept, headlined by Ms. Lauryn Hill, Wyclef Jean, Lenny Kravitz, John Legend), Vans Warped Tour Mexico, Melbourne's NFL-tied Kickoff Festival, Breakaway Festival's 2027 city stops, and EFG London Jazz Festival. It also reports the postponement of OFFLIMITS Festival in Abu Dhabi (Shakira, Nick Jonas) amid a wave of Middle East live-event cancellations, with automatic refunds promised.

### Key Points
- Global Citizen Festival returns to NYC's Central Park 26 Sept, partnered with Live Nation and the NYC Parks Dept.
- Vans Warped Tour Mexico (12-13 Sept) led by New Found Glory, Bowling for Soup, Dropkick Murphys.
- Breakaway Festival announces its first four 2027 city stops (Tampa, Atlanta, Columbus, St. Paul).
- OFFLIMITS Festival (Abu Dhabi) postponed to March 2027; automatic refunds issued amid a regional wave of Middle East event cancellations (incl. Christina Aguilera's Etihad Arena show).

Plus: Lineup and location announcements from Warped Tour Mexico, Kickoff Festival, Breakaway Festival, and more

Global Citizen Festival Amazônia

In the latest round of festival news, **Global Citizen** has revealed a lineup led by Ms Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean, Lenny Kravitz, and John Legend, for its 26 September event in New York’s Central Park.

The one-day festival, partnered with Live Nation as well the New York Parks Department, will also bring to the bill acts including Lainey Wilson, Shaboozey, and Tomorrow X Together, alongside host duties taken up by actor Hugh Jackman.

The festival is the work of its eponymous production company and campaign organisation, which aims to fundraise for global education, agricultural, and electricity infrastructure projects. The New York event comes on the heels of the company producing the first ever [FIFA World Cup Final half time show](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/inaugural-world-cup-halftime-show-illustrates-lives-globalisation/) in July.

“It’s time to gather once again on the Great Lawn, in the middle of New York City, to tell the world an end to extreme poverty is possible,” said Hugh Jackman, who is also a Global Citizen ambassador.

Elsewhere, **Vans Warped Tour Mexico** has revealed its lineup for its event on 12-13 September

Promoted by OCESA, the punk and alternative festival will be led by international artists including New Found Glory, Bowling for Soup, Dropkick Murphys, and Less Than Jake, alongside a raft of emerging Mexican acts in the genre. These include Flowers and Fire, DIE, Bloody Benders, and Omega Hardcore.

“True to its historical DNA of resistance, alternative culture, and community, the festival reaffirms its commitment to being a genuine incubator for emerging talent. Beyond the big names, this edition serves as a crucial platform for the Mexican underground scene,” organisers said.

> “ Beyond the big names, this edition serves as a crucial platform for the Mexican underground scene”

In other festival news, Peking Duck, Grinspoon, and Sneaky Sound System are among the artists announced on the bill of Melbourne’s **Kickoff Festival**, coming up between 10-12 September. Each act will headline one of the festival’s days.

The event is launched in conjunction with the NFL ahead of the league’s first ever game to be held in Australia. The three-day festival will be held across the Melbourne Park Precinct. Joining the headliners on the bill are Kita Alexander, Hot Dub Time Machine, and Public Figures.

In the US, **Breakaway Festival** has revealed the first four city stops that it will visit between April and June 2027. The event will make two-day stops, with Tampa first up, beginning on 16 April, followed by Atlanta beginning on 14 May, Columbus, Ohio on 28 May, and St Paul, Minnesota on 25 June.

No lineup information is available yet, but Breakaway’s 2026 lineups were led by global electronic music artists including FISHER, John Summit, KETTAMA, Subtronics, Tiësto.

Meanwhile, the **EFG London Jazz Festival** has also revealed a lineup which promises ten days of performance across jazz, soul, and global music for its November edition.

Produced by Serious, the event will take place between 13-22 November at the World Heart Beat Embassy Gardens. Organisers say ticket revenue will go towards the venue’s namesake charity, helping to fund bursaries, instruments, and performance opportunities for young musicians.

“For the fourth-year running, the Nine Elms venue will host performances by some of the most distinctive voices in contemporary jazz, including Jean Toussaint, Tony Kofi, Ricky Ford, Julian Joseph, Luiza Zan, Bálint Gyémánt Trió, Amira Kheir, Christine Tobin, and Chris Coull Quartet featuring Imogen Ryall,” the festival said.

And in cancellation news, **OFFLIMITS Festival** has postponed its 2026 outing, which was set to feature Shakira and Nick Jonas in Abu Dhabi. It comes as other Middle East live events have been withdrawn in recent days, including a Christina Aguillera concert that was scheduled for 25 September at Etihad Arena.

OFFLIMITS Organisers said all refunds would be issued automatically, and *IQ* understands the event will return in March 2027 with the same programme.
