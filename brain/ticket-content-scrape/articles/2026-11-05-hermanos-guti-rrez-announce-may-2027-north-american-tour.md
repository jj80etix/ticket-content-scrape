# Hermanos Gutiérrez Announce May 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Hermanos Gutiérrez announced a 12-show North American tour for May 2027 supporting their latest album, Los Ojos Del Cóndor, including the duo's first-ever performance at Radio City Music Hall.

### Key Points
- Tour opens May 5 in St. Paul, MN and closes May 23 in Atlanta.
- First-ever Radio City Music Hall show on May 15.
- American Express presale Nov. 6; artist presale Nov. 9; general on-sale Nov. 13 via Ticketmaster.
- Follows the duo's 2026 North American leg and precedes European/UK dates through early 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Hermanos-Gutierrez.webp)

Hermanos Gutiérrez will return to North America in May 2027 for a 12-show tour supporting their latest album, *Los Ojos Del Cóndor*.

Brothers Estevan and Alejandro Gutiérrez will open the run May 5 at the Palace Theatre in St. Paul, Minnesota. The itinerary continues through Chicago, Toronto, Detroit, Montreal and Boston before reaching Radio City Music Hall on May 15, marking the duo’s first performance at the venue.

The remaining stops include Philadelphia, Washington, D.C., Asheville, North Carolina, and Nashville. The tour closes May 23 at The Eastern in Atlanta.

The announcement follows the conclusion of Hermanos Gutiérrez’s 2026 North American leg. The brothers will head to Europe later this month for concerts continuing through December, followed by performances across the United Kingdom, Ireland and Scandinavia in March 2027.
