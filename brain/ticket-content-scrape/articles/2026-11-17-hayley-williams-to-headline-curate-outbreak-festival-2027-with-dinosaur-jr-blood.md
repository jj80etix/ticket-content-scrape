# Hayley Williams to Headline, Curate Outbreak Festival 2027 With Dinosaur Jr., Blood Orange and More

## Summary

Hayley Williams will headline both nights of the UK's Outbreak Festival 2027 (June 19-20, Manchester) and co-curated a 34-artist lineup spanning punk, emo, hip-hop and R&B, including Dinosaur Jr., Blood Orange, Julien Baker and Rico Nasty. Presale registration closes Nov. 17 GMT, with codes going out Nov. 18 ahead of a 10 a.m. GMT presale and Nov. 19 general onsale; £1 per ticket benefits Medical Aid for Palestinians and Not A Phase.

### Key Points
- Two-night, two-distinct-lineup format; Williams headlines both as "The Hayley Williams Show."
- Lineup broadens Outbreak's hardcore/punk roots into hip-hop and R&B, drawing some fan pushback.
- £1/ticket split between Medical Aid for Palestinians and Not A Phase; separate Manchester food-bank donation drive.
- Presale Nov. 18, 10 a.m. GMT; general onsale Nov. 19, 10 a.m. GMT; single-day and two-day passes available at launch.

Hayley Williams will headline both nights of Outbreak Festival 2027 and has helped curate a lineup featuring Dinosaur Jr., Blood Orange, American Football, Julien Baker, Rico Nasty and Wednesday. The festival returns June 19-20 to B.E.C. Arena in Manchester, England.

The Paramore singer’s performances are billed as *The Hayley Williams Show*, with separate supporting lineups each night. Organizers unveiled 34 artists in a [Nov. 16 announcement](https://www.instagram.com/p/Dfz7nQhxbEm/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) and said more performers will be revealed.

Founded in Manchester in 2011 as a hardcore and punk event, Outbreak has gradually widened its musical scope. The 2026 lineup mixed bands such as Alexisonfire, Trapped Under Ice and Suicidal Tendencies with Joyce Manor and Snail Mail. Williams’ 2027 selections take that crossover further, incorporating hip-hop and R&B alongside punk, emo and alternative rock.

**Saturday, June 19,** brings Blood Orange, Julien Baker, Rico Nasty and Mannequin Pussy alongside Williams. Former Modern Baseball singer Bren Lukens is also on the bill, as are ear, Dev Lemons, Lime Garden, Chalk, Soul Glo, Jejune, PISS, Snõõper, First Day Back, Reclus.É and Appleseed.

**Sunday, June 20,** features Dinosaur Jr., American Football, Wednesday and Citizen. Rival Schools, Failure, feeble little horse, Drug Church, Drain, Mary in the Junkyard, Angel Du$t, Sweet Pill, Spiritual Cramp, H2O, Holder, Empty Shell Casing and Emma Ogier complete the current Sunday lineup. Williams returns for the second headline performance.

The bill maintains a presence for hardcore and post-hardcore through acts including Soul Glo, Drain, Drug Church, Rival Schools and H2O, even as the wider lineup reaches new audiences. The change has also prompted [criticism from some longtime fans](https://www.nme.com/news/music/hayley-williams-to-headline-her-curated-outbreak-2027-manchester-line-up-with-blood-orange-julien-baker-dinosaur-jr-american-football-and-loads-more-3976183?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) who would prefer a stronger emphasis on the festival’s original musical focus.

For Williams, the booking extends [*The Hayley Williams Show*](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/hayley-williams-reveals-the-hayley-williams-show/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which expanded across North and Latin America in 2026 following the release of her 2025 solo album *Ego Death at a Bachelorette Party*. Rico Nasty, who appears on Outbreak’s Saturday bill, also supported Williams on U.S. dates during the tour. Williams played two Manchester Academy shows during her earlier 2026 European run.

Organizers also plan a separate concert on **Friday, June 18**, but have not announced that show’s lineup. Two-day pass holders will be able to upgrade their tickets to include it once details are released.

Outbreak said **£1 from each ticket sold** will be divided equally between Medical Aid for Palestinians and Not A Phase. The event will separately collect donations for Manchester Central Foodbank.

## Outbreak Festival 2027 Tickets

[Presale registration](https://www.outbreak-fest.co.uk/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) remains open until midnight GMT on Tuesday, Nov. 17. Codes and links are scheduled to arrive Wednesday, Nov. 18, between 9 and 9:30 a.m. GMT, with the presale beginning at **10 a.m. GMT**. Any remaining tickets will go on general sale Thursday, Nov. 19, at **10 a.m. GMT**. Organizers said both single-day tickets and two-day passes will be available from the start.
