# EICMA and WorldSBK reach ‘momentous agreement’ with ticketing benefits

## Summary

WorldSBK and EICMA (the world's largest annual motorcycle exhibition) expanded their partnership: round 10 of the Superbike World Championship at Cremona (Sept 25-27) will be renamed the EICMA Italian Round, and anyone holding or buying an EICMA exhibition ticket gets a €10 discount on race admission. EICMA's 83rd edition runs Nov. 5-8 in Milan and drew nearly 600,000 visitors last year.

### Key Points
- WorldSBK round 10 (Cremona Circuit, Sept 25-27) renamed the "EICMA Italian Round."
- EICMA ticket holders/buyers get a €10 (£8.50/$11.50) discount on Cremona race admission.
- EICMA's 83rd edition: Nov. 5-8, Fiera Milano Rho; last edition drew ~600,000 visitors, 734 exhibitors, 43,000+ trade professionals from 167 nations.

![TheTicketingBusiness News](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TBN-LOGO-reversed-white.png)

TheTicketingBusiness News

**WorldSBK and the world’s largest annual motorcycle exhibition EICMA have launched an expanded collaboration that will see the release of an exclusive ticket promotion.**

The agreement will see round 10 of this year’s Superbike World Championship at the Cremona Circuit from September 25-27 renamed to the EICMA Italian Round.

Anyone who already holds a ticket for the 2026 EICMA exhibition, or purchases one, will be entitled to a €10 (£8.50/$11.50) discount on admission to the EICMA Italian Round at the Cremona Circuit.

The 83 <sup>rd</sup> edition of the exhibition is set to take place in Milan from November 5-8 at Fiera Milano Rho.

“This expanded collaboration marks another step in bringing racing, manufacturers, industry stakeholders and motorcycle enthusiasts closer together,” said head of WorldSBK commercial and marketing department Francesco Valentino.

“EICMA is the world’s most influential gathering for the motorcycle industry, making it the ideal platform to showcase what makes WorldSBK unique: from the production-derived motorcycles at the heart of our Championship to the passion of our fans and partners.

“From the EICMA Italian Round in Cremona to our presence at the exhibition in Milan, we’re creating new opportunities for audiences to experience Pure Motorsport while further strengthening the relationship between our Championship and the global motorcycle community.”

In its latest edition, the exhibition welcomed almost 600,000 visitors, featured 734 exhibitors representing more than 2,000 brands from 50 countries, and drew over 43,000 trade professionals from 167 nations.
