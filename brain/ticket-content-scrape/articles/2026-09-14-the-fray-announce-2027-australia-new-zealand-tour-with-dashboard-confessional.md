# The Fray Announce 2027 Australia, New Zealand Tour With Dashboard Confessional

## Summary

The Fray will tour Australia and New Zealand in February 2027 with Dashboard Confessional and Bastille, mixing headline dates with four editions of the band's own Lookout Festival.

### Key Points
- "Summer of Light Tour" runs Feb. 5 (Melbourne) to Feb. 17 (Auckland).
- Lookout Festival stops: Torquay, Perth, Lake Macquarie and Sandstone Point.


## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Fray.webp)

The Fray will return to Australia and New Zealand in February 2027 for a series of headline concerts and outdoor festival appearances featuring Dashboard Confessional, Bastille and several Australian acts.

The band’s “Summer of Light Tour” begins February 5 at Margaret Court Arena in Melbourne. Headline dates will follow at Waves in Wollongong and Hordern Pavilion in Sydney before the run concludes February 17 at Eventfinda Stadium in Auckland, New Zealand.

Dashboard Confessional and Australian country-pop singer Rachael Fahim will open the Melbourne, Sydney and Auckland concerts. Fahim will also appear in Wollongong, where Dashboard Confessional is not scheduled to perform.

The Fray will fill the gaps between the headline shows by leading four editions of Lookout Festival. The outdoor events are scheduled for Torquay, Perth, Lake Macquarie and Sandstone Point.

Bastille, Dashboard Confessional and Little Birdy will join The Fray at all four festival stops. Killing Heidi will perform at every Lookout date except the February 7 event at Kings Park and Botanic Garden in Perth.

The announcement follows The Fray’s sold-out six-show Australian tour in 2025, which celebrated the 20th anniversary of the band’s debut album, *How to Save a Life*.

The upcoming run will support *A Light That Waits*, The Fray’s first full-length album of new material in more than a decade. It is also the group’s first album as the core trio of King, Dave Welsh and Ben Wysocki.
