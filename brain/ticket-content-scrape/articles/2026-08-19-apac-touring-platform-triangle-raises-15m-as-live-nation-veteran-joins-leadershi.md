# APAC touring platform Triangle raises $15m as Live Nation veteran joins leadership

## Summary

Singapore-based touring/festival producer Triangle Group raised a $15M seed round (DSG Consumer Partners equity plus Genesis Alternative Ventures venture debt) and added former Live Nation APAC/Australia-NZ president Roger Field to its leadership, aiming to simplify multi-market touring across Asia-Pacific for mid-tier international artists.

### Key Points
- $15M raise: $10M equity (DSG Consumer Partners) plus $5M venture debt (Genesis Alternative Ventures); called the largest institutional investment in Asia's "experience economy" by a homegrown operator.
- Roger Field (ex-Live Nation APAC president, ex-Live Nation Australia/NZ CEO) joins full-time after a year as advisor/investor.
- Triangle operates from Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, Indonesia; pitches a single regional counterparty vs. market-by-market local promoters.
- Echoes a similar "local expertise vs. global template" pitch emerging from Latin American promoters.

## Transcript

The funding and appointment of Roger Field aim to support the company's stated goal of 'taking the chaos out of touring Asia'

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/smush-webp/2026/08/Group-shot-scaled.jpg.webp)

L-R: Roger Field, Matthew Qi-Tao, Greg Hargrave, Zaran Vachha © Triangle

Touring and festival producer Triangle Group has announced a $15m (€12.9m) fundraising round as well as the appointment of former Live Nation APAC president Roger Field.

The funding comprises an $10m (€8.6m) equity seed round led by DSG Consumer Partners, plus a $5m (€4.3m) venture debt from Genesis Alternative Ventures. Announcing the financing, Triangle called it “the largest institutional investment in Asia’s experience economy by a homegrown operator”.

The Singapore-headquartered Triangle operates across Asia-Pacific with teams based in Singapore, Australia, Malaysia, and Indonesia. Its stated goal is to “take the chaos out of touring Asia”.

Field, who also previously served as CEO of Live Nation Australia/New Zealand, now joins Triangle full-time after a year as an advisor and investor. He joins a leadership team consisting of CFO Matthew Qi-Tao, and founders Greg Hargrave and Zaran Vachha.

“Running Live Nation across this region taught me one thing: Asia-Pacific doesn’t need a global template; it needs an operator who understands every market individually,” Field said. “That’s the business we’re building at Triangle—applying global standards with a regional focus. There’s a larger piece of the pie we can claim by making it genuinely easier to tour the region.”

> “Asia-Pacific doesn’t need a global template; it needs an operator who understands every market individually”

“For mid-tier artists with real Asian fanbases, touring the region has always been a nightmare,” the company said, adding: “Local promoters are brilliant—they know their city, their audience, their scene. But they can only offer one show in one city. Fly halfway around the world for a single night? The math doesn’t work. So the tours don’t happen, and both artists and fans miss out.”

The firm said it aims to address issues caused by a lack of local market knowledge when global players offer end-to-end regional solutions with a “global template”, echoing a [proposition wielded also by some promoters in Latin America](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/08/southern-cone-tours-need-eyes-on-the-ground/).

Explaining what it can offer international acts touring Asia-Pacific, Triangle said it “understands the operational nuances of each individual market—visa rules, tax systems, purchasing power, languages, cultures—in ways a global operator simply cannot”.

“Every city in Asia is completely different,” explains co-founder Zaran Vachha. “When we send a proposal to a London agent, they’re dealing with one counterparty who actually knows Jakarta, Manila, and Melbourne.”
