# Bachman-Turner Overdrive Embarking On ‘No Speed Limit Tour’ In 2027

## Summary

Bachman-Turner Overdrive, led by founding member Randy Bachman, announced the "No Speed Limit Tour" hitting U.S. theaters starting Jan. 7, 2027 at Hard Rock Live in Orlando, following a run touring with Burton Cummings as The Guess Who.

### Key Points
- Tour kicks off Jan. 7, 2027 at Hard Rock Live in Orlando.
- Follows a prior tour pairing Bachman with Burton Cummings as The Guess Who.
- Visits U.S. theaters through early 2027.

## Transcript

![BTO 250216 0309 web](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/BTO_250216_0309_web.jpg)

Bachman-Turner Overdrive is hitting the road early next year and visiting U.S. theaters. Following a successful tour with Burton Cummings as The Guess Who, founding member Randy Bachman is embarking on the “No Speed Limit Tour,” which kicks off Jan. 7 at Hard Rock Live in Orlando.

“I was a rock n’ roll child at 14 after quitting classical violin and seeing Elvis on TV,” Bachman said in a statement. “I feel fortunate and amazed to tour the first half of 2026 with Burton Cummings as The Guess Who – it’s exciting to see all the zillions of fans everywhere grooving once again to those hits. And now, I also get to tour across the U.S. with BTO as we announce our No Speed Limit Tour for next year! I’m thankful to all the rockers who keep on rockin’ and inspire me!”

The routing includes stops at Jacksonville’s The Florida Theatre, Tobin Center in San Antonio, Southern California’s Cerritos Center for the Performing Arts, Wellmont Theater in Montclair, Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium, and Hoyt Sherman Place in Des Moines before wrapping up March 20 at Black Bear Casino Resort in Carlton, Minnesota. See full routing below.

Tickets go on sale Aug. 28 at [btoband.com/tour](https://www.btoband.com/tour).

Bachman-Turner Overdrive is an influential rock and roll band with hits including “Takin’ Care Of Business” and “You Ain’t Seen Nothin’ Yet.” The band returned to touring in 2023 with a revamped lineup comprising guitarist Tal Bachman, KoKo Bachman on drums, Lance Lapointe on bass, and Brent Knudsen on guitar.

The band last toured the States in 2025, and one of its most successful outings was an Oct. 23 show at Wind Creek Event Center in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania, where Bachman-Turner Overdrive grossed $192,072 off 1,659 tickets, according to Pollstar Boxoffice reports.

Bachman-Turner Overdrive is booked by WME’s Brodie Becker and David Levine in the U.S. and The Feldman Agency’s Jeff Feldman in Canada.
