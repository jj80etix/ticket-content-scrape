# Icona Pop Unveil Fall Tour Dates

## Summary

Swedish synthpop duo Icona Pop announced a short fall US tour (Brooklyn, Chicago, Las Vegas, Los Angeles) to promote their upcoming album Ritual, out Aug. 14 via Ultra Records/Iconic Sound Recordings.

### Key Points
- Four dates: Sept. 25 Brooklyn, Oct. 2 Chicago, Nov. 20 Las Vegas, Nov. 21 Los Angeles.
- Tour supports new album Ritual (Aug. 14 release) and its lead single "Yellow Top."
- Tickets on sale now to the general public via the band's website; booked by CAA.

Icona Pop will be visiting U.S. venues this fall to promote their upcoming album *Ritual*, which will be released Aug. 14 via Ultra Records/Iconic Sound Recordings.

The Swedish synthpop duo announced shows in New York City, Chicago, Los Angeles and a special outing in Las Vegas.

Tickets for the concerts are now on sale to the general public at [iconapop.com/tour](https://www.iconapop.com/tour).

Icona Pop, comprising Caroline Hjelt and Aino Jawo, recently gave fans a preview of the forthcoming album by releasing its latest single, “Yellow Top.” Ritual captures the duo at their authentic, “celebrating resilience, friendship, vulnerability and the freedom found on the dancefloor,” according to a release announcing the tour.

“Healing requires you to slow down and reflect, but you can’t work on yourself 24/7 forever. Eventually, you have to get back out into the world and see what happens,” Icona Pop said about the project. “You might mess up—but that could be exactly what you need. For us, the dance floor has always been a place where we find ourselves and each other again. It’s where we can disappear into the music and let go of everything else.”

Icona Pop is booked worldwide by Creative Artists Agency.
