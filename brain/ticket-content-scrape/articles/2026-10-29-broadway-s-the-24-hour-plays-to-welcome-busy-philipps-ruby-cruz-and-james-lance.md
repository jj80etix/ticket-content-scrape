# Broadway’s ‘The 24 Hour Plays’ to Welcome Busy Philipps, Ruby Cruz and James Lance

## Summary

The 24 Hour Plays on Broadway added Busy Philipps, Ruby Cruz, James Lance and Shannon Purser to its November 2 benefit lineup at the Todd Haimes Theatre. The annual event will honor director Thomas Kail.

### Key Points
- New cast additions join previously announced participants including Justin Bartha, Hugh Dancy and Amber Tamblyn
- Event creates six original short plays from first draft to performance within 24 hours
- This year's benefit honors director Thomas Kail
- Organizers say more participants will be announced ahead of the Nov. 2 performance

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/the-24-hour-plays.webp)

Busy Philipps, Ruby Cruz, James Lance and Shannon Purser have joined the cast of The 24 Hour Plays on Broadway, which returns to the Todd Haimes Theatre on November 2.

Patrick Heusinger, Mickey Sumner and Constance Zimmer are also newly announced for the annual benefit.

The performance will honor director Thomas Kail. Participants will create six original short plays, taking each work from its first draft to a Broadway performance within 24 hours. The evening brings together actors, writers and directors connected to different stages of Kail’s career.

The additions join previously announced participants Justin Bartha, David Burtka, Chris Chalk, David Cross, Hugh Dancy, Jin Ha, Josh Hamilton, Alano Miller, Keith Nobbs, Daphne Rubin-Vega and Amber Tamblyn. Organizers have indicated that additional participants will be announced.

Six writers will develop the evening’s new material: Eboni Booth, Sarah Burgess, Julia Jordan, Steven Levenson, David Lindsay-Abaire and Jonathan Marc Sherman. Their stage credits include “Primary Trust,” “Dry Powder,” “Murder Ballad,” “Dear Evan Hansen,” “Kimberly Akimbo” and “Things We Want,” respectively.

The process starts at 7 p.m. the night before the performance, when the participants gather for an orientation. After the actors leave, the writers work overnight to produce 10-minute plays. The performers receive their scripts the following morning and spend the day rehearsing with their directors before presenting the finished pieces that evening.

Directing duties will be shared by Sherri Eden Barber, Tiffany Nichole Greene, David Ruttura, Kate Sullivan, Patrick Vassel and Deanna Weiner. All six are drawn from Kail’s network of associate and assistant directors. Greene serves as resident director of “Hamilton,” while Vassel is the musical’s supervising director.
