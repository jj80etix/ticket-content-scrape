# Papa Roach Announces 2027 U.S. Tour With Ice Nine Kills

## Summary

Papa Roach announced a 30-date 2027 U.S. tour continuing its "Rise of the Roach" run, joined by Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key, opening May 11 in Biloxi and closing June 27 in Auburn, WA. The band will again donate $1 per ticket to the American Foundation for Suicide Prevention.

### Key Points
- 30 U.S. dates, May 11 - June 27, 2027; stops include Barclays Center, Red Rocks, Golden 1 Center.
- Follows a 34-stop 2025 "Rise of the Roach" run.
- Spotify Reserved presale Oct. 20, artist presale same day at noon, Citi presale Oct. 21-22.
- General onsale Oct. 23; $1/ticket donated to AFSP.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Papa-Roach-.webp)

Papa Roach has announced a 30-date U.S. tour for 2027, with Ice Nine Kills and Ninth Key joining the band for the next leg of its “Rise of the Roach” tour.

Frontman Jacoby Shaddix shared in the announcement, “We can’t wait to get out there and infest America again. Bringing our horrifyingly entertaining friends Ice Nine Kills is overdue, and we’re excited to introduce our fans to Ninth Key, a band we’re big fans of. These shows are going to be unforgettable,” Shaddix continued.

The tour opens May 11 at Mississippi Coast Coliseum in Biloxi, Mississippi, and is scheduled to wrap up June 27 at White River Amphitheatre in Auburn, Washington. Stops include Barclays Center in Brooklyn, Moody Center in Austin, Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado, and Golden 1 Center in Sacramento.

The itinerary moves through the Southeast before reaching Baltimore on May 19 and Brooklyn on May 21. June dates include Boston, Camden, Chicago and St. Louis, followed by shows in Texas, the Southwest and California. The final weekend includes two Washington performances, in Ridgefield on June 26 and Auburn on June 27.
