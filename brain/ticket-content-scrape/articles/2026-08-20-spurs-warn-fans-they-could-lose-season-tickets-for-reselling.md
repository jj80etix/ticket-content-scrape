# Spurs Warn Fans They Could Lose Season Tickets for Reselling

## Summary

The San Antonio Spurs sent season-ticket holders a "membership update" warning that reselling on unauthorized secondary marketplaces could result in account cancellation, directing fans instead to the team's app or other approved channels. The notice cites vague "membership intent" language without a defined resale-volume threshold, leaving fans uncertain how many resales are permissible. This follows an earlier crackdown this year in which a 15-year season-ticket holder had his four seats revoked and was banned from team properties for reselling via unauthorized online raffles.

### Key Points
- Aug. 13 email warns of cancellation for resale "inconsistent with membership intent" — no numeric limit disclosed.
- Approved channels: the Spurs' own app and Ticketmaster's Account Manager exchange.
- Earlier 2026 crackdown revoked a 15-year holder's 4 seats over unauthorized-platform raffles; other affected members say they don't know what rule they broke.
- Team's terms already allow cancellation for "repeatedly engaging" in resale, but the threshold remains undefined.

### Highlights
- Enforcement action already taken against at least one fan (4 seats revoked, 1-year property ban).
- Vague "membership intent" standard creates ambiguity for the fanbase.
- Authorized resale is funneled through Ticketmaster's Account Manager exchange.

### Industry Problem
Teams enforcing resale restrictions with vague, undefined thresholds create real confusion and ill will among patrons — even fans using the team's own "authorized" channel have been penalized, undermining trust in resale rules generally.

### Proposed Solution
A primary platform could differentiate by publishing clear, quantified resale-behavior thresholds (e.g., a stated transaction/volume cap) inside its exchange product, and by giving venues self-serve dashboards to set and communicate policy transparently rather than relying on ambiguous "intent" language enforced after the fact.

The San Antonio Spurs are cracking-down on resellers — and warned fans that if they violate the team’s rules, their season tickets will be cancelled.

According to the [*San Antonio Express-News*](https://www.expressnews.com/news/article/spurs-season-ticketholders-warning-resale-revoke-22388431.php), the team sent a “membership update” email on Aug. 13, advising ticket holders against using “unauthorized secondary market sites,” and said to resell tickets only on the Spurs’ app or via other team-approved channels.

The email added that if season tickets holders “engage in a pattern or volume of ticket transfers or resale inconsistent with membership intent, your account may be cancelled.” However, the team did not specify what “membership intent” entails, nor how many resale tickets would pose an issue.

Fans are now unsure about how many tickets they can actually resell. Per the Spurs’ membership terms and conditions, season-ticket accounts are subject to cancellation if they are “identified as repeatedly engaging in the resale of tickets,” though an actual limit is not mentioned.
