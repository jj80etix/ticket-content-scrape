# O2 Academy Sheffield UK To Reopen February 2027

## Summary

Live Nation subsidiary Academy Music Group will reopen O2 Academy Sheffield in February 2027 after a multi-year closure for remedial works, including a new roof, HVAC, and a new L-Acoustics sound system. The venue, one of the UK's storied touring-circuit rooms, has hosted acts from The Clash to The Killers since 1968.

### Key Points
- AMG (Live Nation) invested in a new roof, air conditioning, and service-area upgrades since the venue closed in September 2023.
- Reopening slated for February 2027 across the 2,350-cap main room and 600-cap O2 Academy2.
- New L-Acoustics K3 loudspeaker system installed in partnership with Adlib.
- Part of a broader AMG venue-investment push, following a $12M Brighton Hippodrome investment announced in June 2026.

The UK’s Academy Music Group (AMG), a Live Nation subsidiary, announced that the O2 Academy Sheffield is hoisting up the mirror ball once again and will reopen in February 2027.

AMG invested substantially in remedial works since the venue was temporarily closed in September 2023, including the installation of a new roof, air-conditioning and extensive works to service areas in both the 2,350-capacity main room and the 600 capacity O2 Academy2.

The venue also partnered with Adlib on the installation of a new L-Acoustics K3 loudspeaker system, delivering consistency and familiarity that touring artists and crew expect.

The history of O2 Academy Sheffield is synonymous with the city’s music scene. Originally opened in 1968 as the infamous Top Rank Suite, the venue hosted some iconic and now legendary gigs from The Police, The Clash, Joy Division and David Bowie, before rebranding to dance institution the Roxy Disco, which dominated the club scene and became the region’s biggest nightspot in the eighties and nineties.

The venue was acquired by AMG and reopened in 2008 following an extensive refurbishment and an opening week that highlighted the great and the good of South Yorkshire. Shining a spotlight on some of the most notable artists from Sheffield whose music was influenced by their hometown, Reverend & The Makers and The Human League were among the first acts to take to the stage. The venue quickly became a much-loved institution once again for music fans with shows from the likes of Elbow, The Prodigy, Kasabian, Self Esteem, The Streets, Bring Me The Horizon, The Killers, Wolf Alice, Stormzy, Milburn and The Black Keys.

Liam Boylan, CEO, Academy Music Group, said: “AMG is the backbone of the UK touring circuit, and O2 Academy Sheffield is an important venue in the region and one that promoters and artists will have missed being able to include on their routing whilst it has been closed. Our venue estate has not been complete without this landmark building and it’s wonderful to be able to announce its return to the group.”

John Hughes, Managing Director of Adlib, said: “We’re delighted to be working with the AMG team on this landmark venue in Sheffield. Having welcomed legendary artists to the stage, it has a brilliant musical heritage that we’re helping to carry forward. By integrating new audio and lighting systems in collaboration with our partners L-Acoustics and DiGiCo, we’re ensuring the venue is ready for the demands of today’s productions, while giving artists, engineers and audiences a great experience.”

Justin Smith, Divisional Manager, Academy Music Group, said: “Having worked at the venue since opening night almost two decades ago, the wider team and I can’t wait to get artists back on our stage and welcome loyal customers through the doors again. We have invested in some fantastic improvements including updating the audio capabilities at the venue. We want artists who return to play here to have access to the best technical equipment and ensure our audience is given the very best live experience. Our programme, as ever, is varied and the new L-Acoustics system really delivers across different genres and event types.”
