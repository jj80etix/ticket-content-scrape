# The obligation to have competent personnel in safety roles is indisputable

## Summary

On the fifth anniversary of the Astroworld tragedy, executives behind the Showstop Procedure reflect on the live industry's progress on crowd-safety training. The accredited course, launched at ILMC 2025 with the Pink Bows Foundation, certified staff who worked across venues, stadiums, and festivals this summer, covering events reaching over 100 million spectators. The piece frames safety competency as a settled moral, legal, and financial obligation rather than an open question.

### Key Points
- Marks the fifth anniversary of the 2021 Astroworld crowd crush.
- Showstop Procedure, launched at ILMC 2025 with the Pink Bows Foundation, is billed as the only internationally accredited showstop training.
- Trained staff worked across venues, stadiums, and festivals this summer, covering events for over 100 million spectators.
- Over 1,000 event-safety professionals have completed the certification since launch.
