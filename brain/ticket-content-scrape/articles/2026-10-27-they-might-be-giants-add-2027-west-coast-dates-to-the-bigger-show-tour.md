# They Might Be Giants Add 2027 West Coast Dates to ‘The Bigger Show Tour’

## Summary

They Might Be Giants added 2027 West Coast dates to "The Bigger Show Tour," covering six cities with varying set lists night to night. General tickets go on sale Oct. 30 through Ticketmaster, with artist presale Oct. 29.

### Key Points
- Run opens March 5 at San Francisco's Fillmore (three nights), continues through San Diego, LA, Portland, Boise and Seattle (four nights) into June.
- Sets vary nightly: one focused on a single album, the other a mixed-catalog set.
- Tickets sold through Ticketmaster; artist presale Oct. 29, public sale Oct. 30.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/They-Might-Be-Giants-2027-Tour.webp)

They Might Be Giants will continue “The Bigger Show Tour” in 2027, adding a new series of West Coast performances.

The newly announced dates will include multiple nights in six cities: San Francisco, San Diego, Los Angeles, Portland, Boise and Seattle, with the run beginning March 5 at the Fillmore in San Francisco, where the band will perform three consecutive nights through March 7.

The tour will then move to San Diego and Los Angeles for two-night engagements in each city. After a break, the group will return to the road in June for three performances in Portland, followed by a Boise show and four nights at Seattle’s Neptune Theatre.

For cities featuring multiple performances, the group will change the set list from night to night. The first set will focus on a different album from the band’s extensive catalog, while the second will combine popular songs, lesser-known tracks and more recent releases.

General ticket sales for the newly announced dates will begin Friday, October 30, through Ticketmaster. An artist presale is scheduled to start Thursday, October 29, at noon Eastern Time.

The 2027 announcement comes as They Might Be Giants wraps up the current leg of “The Bigger Show Tour.” The remaining 2026 schedule includes multi-night engagements in Austin, Houston, Dallas and Washington, D.C. The band will conclude the year with New Year’s performances in Montclair, New Jersey, and New Haven, Connecticut. Both shows will feature Jonathan Coulton.

The expanded touring schedule follows the release of the band’s latest album, *The World Is to Dig*. The record marks the duo’s first full-length album since 2021’s *BOOK*.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [theymightbegiants.com](http://theymightbegiants.com).
