# Yuridia Announces 2027 U.S. Dates for ‘Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa Tour’

## Summary

Mexican singer Yuridia announced 2027 U.S. tour dates for "Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa Tour," following two sold-out stadium shows in Mexico City where she became the first Mexican female solo artist to headline Estadio GNP Seguros.

### Key Points

- U.S. run begins Feb. 4, 2027 at Seattle's Paramount Theatre and closes March 28 in National Harbor, MD.
- Citi and eligible Verizon customers get presale access starting Sept. 8; a Spotify presale for Premium subscribers follows Sept. 9; general onsale is Sept. 11 with a six-ticket Ticketmaster limit.
- Some dates (Highland, CA; Rosemont, IL) are not Live Nation venues.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Yuridia.webp)

Yuridia will bring her “Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa Tour” to the United States in early 2027, following a pair of sold-out stadium concerts in Mexico City.

The U.S. run will begin February 4 at the Paramount Theatre in Seattle. From there, the singer is scheduled to make stops in cities such as Reno, San Jose, Phoenice, Las Vegas, San Diego, El Paso and Brooklyn before wrapping up on March 28 in National Harbor, MD at The Theater at MGM National Harbor.

Citi cardmembers and eligible Verizon customers will receive access to presales beginning September 8 at noon local time. A Spotify presale is scheduled to open September 9 at 10 a.m. local time for eligible U.S. Premium subscribers.

The general ticket sale begins September 11 at 10 a.m. local time. Ticketmaster has set a six-ticket limit for the public sale, although purchasing rules and sale arrangements may vary by venue. The Highland, California, and Rosemont, Illinois, concerts are not Live Nation dates.

The tour announcement arrives shortly after Yuridia performed two consecutive sold-out shows at Estadio GNP Seguros in Mexico City on September 3 and 4. She became the first Mexican female solo artist to perform at the stadium, with the two concerts opening the Mexican portion of the “Las Cartas Sobre La Mesa Tour.”
