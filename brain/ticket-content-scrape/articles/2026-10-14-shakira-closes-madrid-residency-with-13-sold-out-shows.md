# Shakira Closes Madrid Residency With 13 Sold-Out Shows

## Summary

Shakira closed her Madrid residency with 13 sold-out shows. The piece recaps the residency's run and attendance.

### Key Points
- Shakira's Madrid residency concluded after 13 sold-out shows.

## Transcript

![Photo 12 10 2026 21 11 43](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Photo-12-10-2026-21-11-43-scaled.jpg)

Shakira performing on the final night of her Madrid residency, Oct. 11. Picture by Nicolas Gerardin

[Shakira](https://pollstar.com/artists/shakira-9762) has brought her historic European residency in Madrid, Spain, to a close, completing 13 monumental shows and welcoming more than 700,000 fans to the Spanish capital in one of the most ambitious live music events ever staged in Europe.

[Shakira debuted at No. 1 on Pollstar’s LIVE75 chart for September](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/12/top-tours-shakira-hits-the-charts-with-stadium-sized-ticket-sales-from-madrid-residency/), driven by box-office results from the first nine shows of her 13-date European residency as part of the “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour.” The first nine performances sold a combined 452,097 tickets, averaging 50,233 fans per show to secure the top spot.

Globally, the tour has now surpassed four million tickets sold and $534 million in reported box-office revenue, further cementing its position as the highest-grossing Latin tour of all time.

Some more highlights that have contributed to these numbers, taken from Pollstar Boxoffice, include 55,709 tickets sold, and $8,066,986 grossed at her Feb. 27 performance at Estadio GNP Seguros in Mexico City, Mexico; or 129,389 tickets sold at a $15,488,500 gross across five shows at Estadio Jorge Magico Gonzalez in San Salvador, El Salvador, Feb. 7-15.

Shakira has appeared at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros a total of 13 times on this tour, selling 724,306 tickets for a whopping $84.1 million at the box office. The national stadiums in Lima, Peru and Santiago, Chile each hosted the tour a total of four nights, while numerous venues had two or three shows on the books.

Shakira’s world tour launched in February of last year in support of her album *Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran*, released in March 2024 through Sony Music Latin. Grosses currently total $534.4 million from 118 reported concerts since launch, all booked at venues in the Americas prior to the shows in Spain.

Throughout the Madrid residency, the global superstar shared the stage with an extraordinary lineup of artists, including Dua Lipa, Laura Pausini, Alejandro Sanz, Maluma, Beéle, Manuel Turizo, Dani Martín, and Bizarrap, celebrating the power and global influence of Latin music.

![@iamcu4tro Photo Oct 12 2026, 11 07 20 PM](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/%40iamcu4tro-Photo-Oct-12-2026-11-07-20-PM-scaled.jpg)

Shakira’s purpose-built stadium designed by world-renowned architecture firm BIG and named in Shakira’s honor. Picture by @iamcu4tro

Madrid became the epicenter of this achievement with the creation of Shak’s City, a 185,000-square-meter cultural destination known as Macondo Park, featuring a purpose-built stadium designed by world-renowned architecture firm BIG and named in Shakira’s honor. Through ES LATINA, Shakira transformed a concert residency into an immersive celebration of music, art, literature, fashion, and gastronomy, bringing together more than 1,500 artists and performers from around the world and delivering over 400 hours of cultural programming, while generating significant economic and cultural impact across the city.

The residency also generated an economic impact exceeding $200 million, driving greater demand for tourism and accommodation bookings than major events such as Pope Leo XIV’s visit and Formula 1.

The residency also set a new record for [Amazon Music’s most-watched livestream music performance by a female artist](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/10/04/shakiras-madrid-concert-is-amazon-musics-most-livestreamed-performance-by-a-female-artist/?_gl=1%2A1ahoopd%2A_ga%2AMTM4MzMyNDM3Ny4xNzgyOTc2ODgw%2A_ga_5B6XRECYRM%2AczE3OTE5Nzc5NDEkbzIxNCRnMSR0MTc5MTk3ODM4MiRqNTgkbDAkaDA.).

The “Las Mujeres Ya No Lloran World Tour” is produced by Live Nation. Shakira is managed by Nadine Eliya at Shak Entertainment, and repped worldwide by WME’s Keith Sarkisian.
