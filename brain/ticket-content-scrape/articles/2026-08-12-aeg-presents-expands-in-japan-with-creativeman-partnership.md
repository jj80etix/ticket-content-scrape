# AEG Presents Expands in Japan With Creativeman Partnership

## Summary

AEG Presents is expanding in Japan through a new strategic partnership with Creativeman Productions, the country's largest independent concert promoter, aimed at growing live-music opportunities across Japan and giving AEG's ticketing arm AXS a stronger regional foothold. The deal connects Creativeman's Summer Sonic festival brand into AEG's global festival network alongside Coachella and BST Hyde Park.

### Key Points
- Creativeman (founded 1990) runs Summer Sonic, Sonicmania, Punkspring, and Loud Park, and has already expanded Summer Sonic to Bangkok.
- Partnership explicitly positioned to expand AXS's presence in Japan/Asia.
- Summer Sonic has hosted Radiohead, Oasis, Metallica, Linkin Park, and Red Hot Chili Peppers.
- AEG Presents EVP/COO Shawn Trell called Creativeman "an important presence in Japan and across the region."

### Highlights
- Deal is explicitly framed as a vehicle to expand AXS's ticketing footprint into Japan/Asia via Creativeman's promoter relationships.
- Extends the pattern of major North American promoters bundling ticketing-platform expansion with regional promoter partnerships rather than direct market entry.

### Industry Problem
Ticketing-platform share in growth markets like Asia is increasingly won through promoter alliances rather than direct competition, meaning independent platforms without comparable promoter relationships face a structural disadvantage entering those markets.

### Proposed Solution
Monitor how aggressively AXS and other Tier 2 competitors use promoter partnerships (rather than product features) to expand share in growth markets — informs how Etix should think about international/promoter-partnership strategy versus direct-to-consumer expansion.

AEG Presents is expanding its presence in Japan through a new strategic partnership with leading concert promoter Creativeman Productions.

The deal will bring the companies together on live music opportunities across Japan, combining AEG Presents’ global reach with Creativeman’s established concert and festival business. The partnership will also provide a platform for AEG-owned ticketing company AXS to expand across the region.

Creativeman, founded in 1990, is behind some of Japan’s biggest music events, including Summer Sonic, Sonicmania, Punkspring and Loud Park.

Its flagship Summer Sonic festival takes place across Tokyo and Osaka and has hosted artists including Radiohead, Oasis, Metallica, Linkin Park and Red Hot Chili Peppers. The brand has also expanded internationally with Summer Sonic Bangkok.
