# Macklemore announces Free Palestine Tour

## Summary

Macklemore announced a hastily organized three-date "Free Palestine Tour" — Dublin's 3Arena (Oct 26), Paris's Accor Arena (Oct 29) and London's OVO Wembley (Oct 30) — after being dropped from Ed Sheeran's Loop Tour stadium dates over his pro-Palestine remarks. Proceeds go to charitable causes, and the run is promoted by Live Nation in the UK and France. The source article is paywalled beyond its opening details.

### Key Points
- Three arena dates: Dublin 3Arena (Oct 26), Paris Accor Arena (Oct 29), London OVO Wembley (Oct 30).
- Organized after Macklemore's removal from Ed Sheeran's Loop Tour stadium shows.
- Proceeds donated to charitable causes.
- Promoted by Live Nation in the UK and France.
