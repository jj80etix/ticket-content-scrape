# Rock in Rio draws 700,000 fans with diverse headliner pool

## Summary

Brazil's Rock in Rio drew more than 700,000 fans across two weekends in its 41st year, featuring the festival's first K-pop and electronic headliners — Stray Kids and Calvin Harris — alongside Foo Fighters and Maroon 5.

### Key Points
- Held at Rio's Cidade do Rock (Olympic Park), Sept. 4-7 and 11-13.
- First time K-pop and electronic acts headlined the historically rock-focused festival.
