# Movers & Shakers: HYBE, PRS For Music, Sphere Ent

## Summary

IQ's personnel roundup covers HYBE naming a new chief people officer, PRS for Music appointing a new CEO, Sphere Entertainment hiring a global marketing head, and moves at Georg Leitner Productions, Music Venue Trust and Semmel Concerts.

### Key Points
- HYBE appoints Jenni Pfaff as chief people and transformation officer.
- PRS for Music names Ben McOwen Wilson (ex-YouTube/Google Play EMEA) as CEO, effective October.
- Sphere Entertainment hires Karen Laureano-Rikardsen as global head of corporate marketing.
- Music Venue Trust adds a head of external affairs; Semmel Concerts' Sina Hall departs after 13 years.

## Transcript

Plus: Georg Leitner Productions expands UK presence, MVT adds external affairs leader, and major player departs Semmel Concerts...

![Jenni Pfaff](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Jenni-Pfaff.png)

Jenni Pfaff

HYBE is expanding its efforts to build up the entertainment firm’s global leadership with a new appointment, leading the roundup of movement across the international live music business.

**HYBE  
**Off the back of posting its “ [all-time high](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/hybe-revenue-doubles-year-on-year-in-all-time-high-quarter/) ” quarter results, HYBE has announced the appointment of Jenni Pfaff as chief people and transformation officer.

Pfaff will step up from her role as chief people and transformation officer in the US division of the South Korean-based entertainment powerhouse to lead its global personnel operations in the newly created position. She will report to HYBE CEO Jason Jaesang Lee and remain based in Los Angeles.

In the position, she will help HYBE build the company’s talent development and organisational structure across the global business.

“I’m honoured to step into this global role as HYBE enters its next chapter of growth,” she says. “What makes HYBE so special is the incredible talent across our multi-label ecosystem, pushing the envelope on innovation each day.

“My priority is to build a globally connected people strategy that supports that creative independence while bringing our teams together around a shared culture of trust, transparency, and a real commitment to living our core values,” Pfaff concludes.

She brings experience from Warner Chappell Music, PwC, Activision Blizzard, Northrop Grumman, and Pfaff HR.

Isaac Lee, chairman and CEO of HYBE AMERICA, says: “She has been a constant champion of empowering our individual team members to bring new ideas, perspectives and collaboration to the forefront for the betterment of our company. Our organisation is stronger because of her contributions, and I’m thrilled to see her dedication rewarded with this appointment.”

> “It is a privilege to lead this organisation at a time when the voice of the creator must be louder than ever”

**PRS For Music  
**The UK’s royalty collection organisation, PRS for Music, has appointed Ben McOwen Wilson as its next CEO.

Wilson brings more than 25 years’ experience across the creative industry, and has recently worked as an executive leading YouTube and Google Play’s expansion across the EMEA region. He will take up the role on 12 October.

Ben joins at a pivotal moment for the music industry, bringing a proven track record of navigating complex digital transformations and championing creator rights. During his career, he has worked with government and industry through the UK’s Creative Industries Council and the Creative Industries Federation on issues including intellectual property, innovation, and growth.

His appointment comes at a time of rapid change for the music industry, with AI, gaming, digital platforms, and music consumption patterns reshaping the industry.

“PRS for Music is built on trust, transparency, and a relentless commitment to its members,” Wilson says. “It is a privilege to lead this organisation at a time when the voice of the creator must be louder than ever.”

“In the age of AI and accelerated digital distribution, protecting the integrity and value of human creativity is our most urgent priority. My focus will be on ensuring PRS serves as a catalyst for our members’ success, ensuring they remain at the centre of a sustainable and growing global music ecosystem,” he says.

Julian Nott, Chair of the PRS Members’ Council, added: “Ben impressed us from the very beginning. He understands the technologies that are transforming the creative industries, but he also understands that innovation only succeeds if it works for the creators. We are confident that he will champion members’ interests at home and around the world, leading us boldly into the next phase of our evolution.”

> “It is critical that we continue to tell our story in compelling and impactful ways”

**Sphere Entertainment  
**Sphere Entertainment has appointed Karen Laureano-Rikardsen as global head of corporate marketing and communications.

Her appointment comes amid Sphere’s push to expand its network of venues, anchored by its flagship Las Vegas space, with sites slated to open in Abu Dhabi and in Maryland in the coming years.

She brings experience from Wall Street firm Cantor Fitzgerald, Credit Suisse, Dresdner Kleinwort Wasserstein, and Hill & Knowlton. Laureano-Rikardsen will be based in New York and will report to Jennifer Koester, Sphere president and COO.

“As we continue to expand Sphere’s presence and bring our next-generation immersive entertainment experiences to audiences around the world, it is critical that we continue to tell our story in compelling and impactful ways,” Koester says.

“Karen brings a strong combination of global marketing and communications experience, sound judgement and proven leadership in high-profile environments. She will play a vital role in shaping how we communicate our vision and innovation,” she concludes.

Laureano-Rikardsen adds: “I look forward to working alongside the talented team of creatives, engineers, technologists, and business leaders to help fuel the company’s continued expansion.”

> “We are proud to have Manfred as a sort of new home base for GLP in London”

**Georg Leitner Productions  
**Senior music executive Manfred Seipt will join Georg Leitner Productions (GLP) to help expand the Vienna-based booking agency in the UK.

With more than 30 years’ experience in the international live entertainment industry, Seipt brings experience across artist management, touring, event production, and business development  
to his new role. As a former IMG Artists and Universal Music Group senior manager and consultant, he has worked across a variety of events, including global tours, festivals, major sporting events, and large-scale cultural productions.

“We are glad to have Manfred now in the GLP team who brings in extensive international experience, a broad industry network and a proven track record in booking and delivering world-class artists as well as complex live entertainment projects for our partners worldwide,” says GLP CEO Harald Buechel.

Georg Leitner, founder and chairman, adds: “While Vienna will remain our headquarters, we are proud to have Manfred as a sort of new home base for GLP in London. He shall ensure that our successful networks, especially in the Eastern territories and Central Asia, will get noticed and all London-based live music executives have a short-cut direct contact to GLP within the UK.”

The boutique agency represents acts including Kool & The Gang, Foreigner, the Earth Wind & Fire Experience, Dire Straits Legacy, and José Carreras.

> “Chris brings a deep understanding of how government works”

**Music Venue Trust**  
The UK’s MVT has appointed Chris Hurne as its new head of external affairs.

He joins MVT to lead the organisation’s external activity, with a focus on strengthening relationships with government, policymakers, industry stakeholders and partners as MVT continues its work to protect and support the UK’s grassroots music venue network.

Hurne brings extensive experience in the policymaking and government strategy sectors, having spent nearly a decade working across the UK Government’s creative industries portfolio, most recently at the Department for Business and Trade, where he led engagement with the Creative & Media, Sport & Leisure sectors.

“Chris brings a deep understanding of how government works, how policy is developed, and how meaningful relationships between industry and decision-makers are built,” says COO Beverley Whitrick. His experience supporting the growth of the UK’s creative industries, combined with his knowledge of the music sector, makes him a hugely valuable addition to our work.

“Grassroots music venues are a vital part of the UK’s cultural infrastructure, but they continue to face significant challenges. Having someone with Chris’s experience working across government and industry will help us strengthen our voice, build new partnerships and ensure that the importance of grassroots music venues is understood by those making decisions about the future of music,” she concludes.

Hurne adds: “I’m delighted to be joining Music Venue Trust at such an important time for grassroots music venues, which is where artists begin their journeys, audiences discover new music, and communities come together. Having spent much of my career supporting the UK’s creative sectors, I know how important a strong and sustainable ecosystem is. I’m looking forward to working with government, industry partners and the wider music community to champion these vital spaces and help secure their future.”

> “Fans from across Europe come together, celebrate the music, and create a special atmosphere that defines the festival year after year”

**Semmel Concerts  
**After 13 years at Germany’s Semmel Concerts, Sina Hall has announced she will leave the company.

Most recently serving as senior project manager and head of international booking, Hall was integral to the growth of the country genre in the German market. She also served as the festival director for C2C (Country to Country) Germany.

This year’s edition saw more than [9,000](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/03/c2c-festival-100-000-attendees-uk-editions/) attendees flock to Berlin’s Uber Eats Music Hall for three days of music, with Zach Top, Jordan Davis, and Scotty McCreery leading a lineup of 55 artists.

“C2C in Berlin grows every year, along with its community,” Hall said earlier this year. “Fans from across Europe come together, celebrate the music, and create a special atmosphere that defines the festival year after year.”

Her next position has not yet been announced.
