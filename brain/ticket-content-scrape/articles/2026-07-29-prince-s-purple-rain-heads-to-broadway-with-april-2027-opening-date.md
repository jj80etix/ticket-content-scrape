# Prince’s ‘Purple Rain’ Heads to Broadway with April 2027 Opening Date

## Summary

Prince's *Purple Rain* will transfer to Broadway's Majestic Theatre, opening April 12, 2027, following its 2025 premiere in Minneapolis.

### Key Points
- Previews begin March 12, 2027; directed by Saheem Ali and written by Peter Duchan.
- Prince collaborators Bobby Z and Morris Hayes serve as music advisors.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Purple-Rain.webp)

Prince’s iconic 1984 film and record Purple Rain will officially head to the Main Stem next year following a run in Minneapolis.

The show first debuted at the Hennepin Theatre Trust’s State Theatre in 2025. Now, “Purple Rain” will begin previews at New York City’s Majestic Theatre on March 12, 2027, followed by an official opening on April 12. Saheem Ali will direct the show, which is written by playwright Peter Duchan and choreographed by Ebony Williams. Prince collaborators Bobby Z and Morris Hayes were brought on as music advisors.

Producer Orin Wolf noted in a statement to Variety that “Prince is, without doubt, one of the rarest, most brilliant musicians we’ve ever had.”

“What he accomplished with ‘Purple Rain’ in 1984 — the song, the album and the film — is a testament to the art of making music and showcases it at the highest level,” Wolf said. “It will be every bit as exciting for audiences today as it was when we first heard these songs on our boom boxes.”

*Purple Rain* helped catapult Prince further into stardom; the two-part project topped the Billboard 200 for 24 weeks and went 13-times platinum.
