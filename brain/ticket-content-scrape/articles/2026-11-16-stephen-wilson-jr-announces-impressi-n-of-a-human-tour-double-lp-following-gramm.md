# Stephen Wilson Jr. Announces ‘Impressiøn Of A Human Tour,’ Double LP, Following Grammy Noms

## Summary

Stephen Wilson Jr. has announced a new double LP and the "Impressiøn Of A Human Tour" kicking off March 24, following his first Grammy nominations and a Rock & Roll Hall of Fame Induction Ceremony performance. The tour runs through a June 20 close at Red Rocks, with festival stops including Stagecoach and Hinterland. The run uses Ticketmaster's Face Value Exchange, making tickets non-transferable and resellable only at face value on Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Tour kicks off March 24 in Oklahoma City, closes June 20 at Red Rocks; public onsale Nov. 20.
- Uses Ticketmaster's Face Value Exchange — non-transferable tickets, face-value-only resale on Ticketmaster.
- Two first-time Grammy nominations announced alongside the tour and album news.

Following his “Wonderwall” performance at the Rock & Roll Hall Of Fame Induction Ceremony as part of Oasis’ induction and his frist Grammy nominations announced Monday, alternative country standout Stephen Wilson Jr. has announced a new double LP and a concert tour kicking off in the spring following a current run of sold-out dates.

The “Impressiøn Of A Human Tour” kicks off at the Criterion in Oklahoma City on March 24, with dates mostly in the South and into April at venues and markets including 713 Music Hall in Houston, Hellbender By The Orange Peel in Asheville, Wind Creek Event Center in Bethlehem, Pennsylvania and will conclude at Red Rocks in Morrison, Colorado, June 20.

Festival appearances stretch well into the summer and include Tortuga in Fort Lauderdale, Stagecoach in Southern California and Hinterland in Iowa.

Public onsale is Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time. Presales begin Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. local time. VIP packages will be available beginning Thursday, Nov. 19 at 10 a.m. local time. Stephen Wilson Jr. is using Ticketmaster’s Face Value Exchange, meaning tickets will be non-transferable and can only be resold on Ticketmaster at face value.

The new tour follows ongoing sold-out dates on Wilson Jr.’s “Gary The Torch Tour,” which has upcoming dates at venues including Citizens Live at the Wylie in Pittsburgh, Coca Cola Roxy in Atlanta, Choctaw Casino in Oklahoma and more.

The momentum continues for Wilson Jr. as today he announced his sophomore double albumm Impressiøn of a Human // Children of the Cørnerman will be released Feb. 26, 2027, via Big Loud Records. With today’s announcement, Wilson Jr. became a first-time Grammy nominee, with “Hardwired” feat. Miranda Lambert nominated for Best Country Song and “Gary” nominated for Best Country Solo Performance.

On Wednesday, Nov. 18, Wilson Jr. and Chris Stapleton will perform “I’m A Song” at the 60th annual CMA Awards, where he is one of the year’s top nominees with four different nods.

He will return to Jimmy Kimmel Live! to perform “Exorcisms on the Weekend” from his forthcoming album, one year after making his debut on the show with a performance of “Gary,” the announcement notes.

Although Oasis did not attend the Rock Hall Ceremony, Wilson Jr.’s somber take on the band’s classic singalong hit “Wonderwall” got praise from one particularly notable party.

“I liked it honestly,” Oasis frontman Liam Gallagher tweeted. “it had a real organic feel about and it brought out my melancholy side.”

Wilson Jr. is represented by United Talent Agency and managed by Range Music / Range Media Partners.
