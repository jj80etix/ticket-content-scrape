# Plymouth Arena seeks new ticketing partner

## Summary

Plymouth Arena (4,000 capacity, currently ticketed by Ticketek) has opened an Expressions of Interest process for a new ticketing partner, seeking a UK-proven platform with accessibility sales tools and membership/CRM management experience. Providers have until September 11 to respond. It's the second UK venue this week under a Ticketek partnership to publicly seek a new ticketing provider, following a similar move by Motorpoint Arena Nottingham.

### Key Points
- Plymouth Arena (formerly Plymouth Pavilions), 4,000 capacity, currently ticketed by Ticketek since an October 2023 deal, is seeking a new provider by Sept. 11.
- Requirements include a UK-proven platform, accessibility sales tooling, 4,000+ capacity venue experience, and membership/CRM management (renewals, benefits, communications).
- Motorpoint Arena Nottingham — also a Ticketek venue — issued a similar Expressions of Interest this same week.

### Highlights
- Two UK arena venues under Ticketek contracts (Plymouth Arena, Motorpoint Arena Nottingham) both opened ticketing-provider searches in the same week — a concentrated churn signal against one incumbent.

### Industry Problem
Mid-size UK arena venues are actively shopping ticketing partners, citing needs (accessibility tooling, membership/CRM depth) that go beyond basic ticket sales — suggesting incumbent platforms in this tier aren't fully meeting client expectations on those fronts.

### Proposed Solution
No specific competitor solution is described; this is a live RFP signal. For Etix, these are concrete near-term business-development leads (two UK arena venues actively tendering) and evidence for Q20 win/loss analysis — the stated requirements (accessibility, membership management, CRM) indicate what a primary platform needs to demonstrate to win this class of tender.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Plymouth Arena has revealed it is seeking a new ticketing partner, with companies invited to express their interest.**

Formerly known as Plymouth Pavilions, the arena is located in the English city of Plymouth and has a fully standing capacity of 4,000, and a maximum seated capacity of 2,500 people.

The venue is set to host a packed calendar of live events in the second half of 2026 from comedy through to live music. Artists such as Van Morrison, The Proclaimers, Jack Savoretti and Tony Hadley are all due to perform, while comedians such as Chris Ramsey and Bill Bailey are also on the schedule.

Plymouth Arena management said it [was keen to hear](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/plymouth-arena_plymouth-arena-is-seeking-a-ticketing-partner-activity-7492910586767323137-cMSM/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop&rcm=ACoAAAOmU0cBY25gKPnfUGk91ml9Hhb7bCp03aY) from providers that have a proven ticketing platform operating in the UK as well as a proven accessibility sales tool; experience working with venues of a 4,000 or more capacity; and experience in membership management, including member communications, renewals, benefits administration and CRM.

The venue is currently ticketed by Ticketek, which agreed a deal with the venue in October 2023.

“We’re looking forward to connecting with experienced providers who share our commitment to delivering an exceptional ticketing experience and building a successful long-term partnership with Plymouth Arena,” the venue stated.

Ticketing service providers have until the end of the day on Friday, September 11, to express their interest.

It is the second UK venue this week currently in partnership with Ticketek to announce it is looking for a new ticketing partner.

Motorpoint Arena Nottingham [also announced](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/2026/08/motorpoint-arena-nottingham-invites-expressions-of-interest-from-ticketing-providers/) it was inviting Expressions of Interest (EOI) from ticketing providers, ahead of a forthcoming ticketing services tender.
