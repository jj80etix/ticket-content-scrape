# Independent Canadian promoter MRG launches Australian office

## Summary

Canadian independent promoter The MRG Group is opening a Sydney-based Australian division, MRG Live Australia, led by former decade-long Live Nation Australia arts-and-entertainment head Graham Kennedy, citing similarities between the Canadian and Australian mid-sized touring markets.

### Key Points
- MRG Live Australia based in Sydney; Graham Kennedy (ex-Live Nation Australia, ex-The Wiggles global touring exec) named GM/senior promoter.
- MRG, founded 2008, produces 1,000+ events annually as "Canada's largest independent experience company."
- Expansion builds on prior MRG Australian projects: The Next Step tour, Tyler Hilton/Kate Voegele, MKTO, Colin Mochrie.

## Transcript

Former Live Nation executive Graham Kennedy will take the helm as senior promoter as the company aims to capitalise on market similarities with its native Canada

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Graham-Kennedy-MRG-Group.png)

Graham Kennedy © MRG Group

Canadian independent promoter The MRG Group (MRG) has announced the launch of MRG Live Australia, which the company said marks “a significant milestone in the company’s international growth strategy”.

MRG said the expansion establishes a “permanent presence in one of the world’s most vibrant touring markets”, and the division is to be based in Sydney and led by former Live Nation executive Graham Kennedy, who joins the company as general manager and senior promoter.

MRG describes itself as “Canada’s largest independent experience company active in producing and promoting experiences on a global stage”. Since launching in 2008, it has grown to produce over 1,000 events annually.

The company said the choice of Australia comes in a bid to capitalise on market similarities between the nation and its home Canadian market.

“Australia is a market we’ve watched closely for years, and MRG’s success there, with *The Next Step* tour, Tyler Hilton and Kate Voegele, MKTO, Colin Mochrie, and Asad Mecci, showed us the appetite was real,” said Matthew Gibbons, founder and president of MRG.

He continued: “The Australian and Canadian entertainment landscapes mirror each other in a lot of ways: mid-sized markets with passionate audiences and a strong culture of touring. That familiarity gives us a real advantage, and bringing on someone with Graham’s track record is exactly how we want to grow: methodically, with the right people, in the right markets.”

> “The Australian and Canadian entertainment landscapes mirror each other in a lot of ways: mid-sized markets with passionate audiences and a strong culture of touring”

Kennedy most recently spent over ten years as head of arts and entertainment at Live Nation Australia, where he promoted “numerous successful national and international tours”, MRG said. Prior to that, he served as senior manager of global live events for The Wiggles, a role in which he oversaw the group’s worldwide touring operations.

Kennedy commented: “I’m excited to join MRG at such an important time in the company’s evolution. MRG has built an outstanding reputation for innovation, entrepreneurial thinking, and delivering exceptional live experiences. I’m looking forward to establishing our Australian presence, building new relationships, and bringing more world-class tours and entertainment to audiences across the region.”

MRG also said that Kennedy and the promoter’s COO Stephane Lecuyer have more than a decade of history in working together across projects including a recent Australia and New Zealand touring run of the dance TV show *The Next Step.*

“It was a tremendous experience collaborating with Graham on our Australian The Next Step dates,” said Jacob Smid, managing director of MRG Live. “We’re looking forward to continuing that partnership over the long-term across music, comedy, and family touring in the market. Graham’s knowledge, network, and experience will be invaluable as we set ourselves up for success.”
