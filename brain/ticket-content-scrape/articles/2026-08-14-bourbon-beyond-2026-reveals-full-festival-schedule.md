# Bourbon & Beyond 2026 Reveals Full Festival Schedule

## Summary

Bourbon & Beyond released its full 2026 performance schedule for the September 24-27 Louisville festival, headlined by Foo Fighters, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton and Dave Matthews Band across five stages including a dedicated culinary stage.

### Key Points
- Four-day festival, Sept. 24-27, Louisville, KY, across five music stages plus the Fork & Flask culinary stage.
- Headliners: Foo Fighters, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton, Dave Matthews Band.
- Full day-by-day stage lineups released, including Queens of the Stone Age, Kacey Musgraves, Goose and more.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/Bourbon-Beyond-2026.webp)

Bourbon & Beyond has announced the full performance schedule for its 2026 festival, giving fans a look at which artists will take the stage throughout the four-day event. The festival is scheduled to take place September 24 through September 27 in Louisville, Kentucky.

The 2026 edition will feature headlining performances from Foo Fighters, Mumford & Sons, Chris Stapleton and Dave Matthews Band. The lineup also includes Queens of the Stone Age, Goose, Kacey Musgraves, Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit, Hootie & the Blowfish and Counting Crows.

Performances will take place across five stages: the Oak Stage, Barrel Stage, 100 Proof Stage, Revival Stage and The Bluegrass Situation Stage. Organizers have also announced programming for the festival’s Fork & Flask culinary stage.

Thursday’s opening-day lineup includes Foo Fighters, The War On Drugs, Gary Clark Jr. and Live on the Oak Stage. The Barrel Stage will feature Queens of the Stone Age, Goose, Cheap Trick and 4 Non Blondes. The 100 Proof Stage lineup includes The Fray, Plain White T’s, Nine Days and Howie Day.

Saturday’s schedule features Chris Stapleton and Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit on the Oak Stage. Charley Crockett, Durand Jones & The Indications and Bryan Martin are also scheduled to perform. The Barrel Stage will feature The Red Clay Strays, Jessie Murph, Cameron Whitcomb and Avery Anna.

Noah Cyrus will perform on the 100 Proof Stage, while Squeeze, Adam Ant, Cracker and Haircut 100 are among the artists scheduled for the Revival Stage. The Infamous Stringdusters will headline the Bluegrass Situation Stage lineup alongside Yächtley Crëw and The Fretliners.

The festival will wrap up Sunday with Dave Matthews Band closing the Oak Stage. Counting Crows, Portugal. The Man, Amos Lee and Uncle Lucius will also perform on the stage. Hootie & the Blowfish will lead the Barrel Stage lineup, with Fitz And The Tantrums, Max McNown and Jesse Welles also scheduled to appear.

Sunday’s additional performances include Better Than Ezra, Our Lady Peace, Tonic and The Verve Pipe on the 100 Proof Stage. Lisa Loeb, Joan Osborne and Paula Cole will perform on the Revival Stage, while Daniel Donato’s Cosmic Country, The Steel Wheels and Vickie Vaughn Band are among the acts appearing on The Bluegrass Situation Stage.
