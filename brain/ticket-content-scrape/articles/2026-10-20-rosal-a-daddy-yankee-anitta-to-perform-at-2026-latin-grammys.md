# Rosalía, Daddy Yankee, Anitta To Perform At 2026 Latin Grammys

## Summary

The Latin Recording Academy announced the performer lineup for the 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards, set for Nov. 12 at MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, featuring Rosalía, Daddy Yankee, Anitta and others, with Roselyn Sánchez returning to host. Rosalía leads nominations with seven nods for her album Lux.

### Key Points
- 27th Annual Latin Grammys: Nov. 12, MGM Grand Garden Arena, Las Vegas.
- Performers include Anitta, Rosalía, Daddy Yankee, Banda Los Recoditos, Jorge Drexler, Silvana Estrada, Kany García, Juanes, Carín León, Mon Laferte, Vivir Quintana, Rawayana, Elena Rose, Xavi.
- Roselyn Sánchez returns as host.
- Rosalía is the top nominee with 7 nominations for album Lux, including Song/Album of the Year.

The Latin Recording Academy has announced the performers for the 27th Annual Latin Grammy Awards, which is set to take place at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas on Nov. 12.

The current lineup of performers includes Anitta, Rosalía, Daddy Yankee, Banda Los Recoditos, Jorge Drexler, Silvana Estrada, Kany García, Juanes, Carín León, Mon Laferte, Vivir Quintana, Rawayana, Elena Rose and Xavi. Additionally, internationally renowned actress, producer, and Latin GRAMMY nominee Roselyn Sánchez will return to host.

Rosalía is one of this year’s most nominated artists with seven Latin Grammys for her latest album, Lux, including categories such as Song of the Year and Album of the Year. Other top nominees include Mon Laferte, receiving six noms for *Femme Fatale*, and Jorge Drexler, who snagged seven nominations for his LP *Taracá*.

The 27th Annual Latin GRAMMY Awards will air live from the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas on Nov. 12. The three-hour telecast will air on Univision, beginning at 8 p.m. ET/PT (7 p.m. Central), preceded by a one-hour pre-show starting at 7 p.m. ET/PT (6 p.m. Central).
