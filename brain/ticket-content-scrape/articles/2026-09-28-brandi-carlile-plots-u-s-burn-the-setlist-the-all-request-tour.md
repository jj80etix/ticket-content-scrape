# Brandi Carlile Plots U.S. ‘Burn the Setlist (The All Request Tour)’

## Summary

Brandi Carlile announced a 15-date U.S. tour, "Burn the Setlist (The All Request Tour)," in which nightly setlists are determined entirely by live audience requests. The November-December run focuses on smaller theaters rather than the arenas she played through most of 2026, and will be phone-free via Yondr pouches.

### Key Points
- 15 dates, Nov. 5 (Portland, OR) through Dec. 15-17 (Port Chester, NY).
- Performed as a trio with longtime collaborators Phil and Tim Hanseroth.
- Expands an audience-request segment first used on "The Human Tour."
- Multi-night stops in Tucson, Milwaukee, Minneapolis, and Louisville.
- Fully phone-free format using Yondr pouches.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Brandi-Carlile-Burn-The-Setlist.webp)

Brandi Carlile is turning the audience into the setlist maker for a new run of intimate theater shows this fall, announcing the 15-date “Burn the Setlist (The All Request Tour)” for November and December.

The tour will feature Carlile performing as a trio with longtime collaborators Phil and Tim Hanseroth, with every night’s setlist determined entirely by live, in-the-moment requests from fans. The concept expands on an audience-request segment Carlile incorporated into “The Human Tour” earlier this year, but makes those requests the basis for the entire show.

The run begins November 5 at Revolution Hall in Portland, Oregon, and concentrates on smaller theaters rather than the arenas and amphitheaters Carlile played during much of her 2026 touring schedule. Multi-night stops are scheduled in Tucson, Milwaukee, Minneapolis and Louisville before the tour concludes with three performances at The Capitol Theatre in Port Chester, New York, December 15 to 17.

“Burn the Setlist” will be a phone-free tour, with attendees required to place their devices in Yondr pouches during the performance. Fans will retain possession of the locked pouches throughout the show.
