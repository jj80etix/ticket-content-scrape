# Pentatonix Set 2026 Holiday Tour Dates Across the U.S. and Canada

## Summary

Pentatonix announced their 2026 "Christmas in the City Tour," a 28-date North American arena run from November 5 (Tacoma) through December 22 (Los Angeles), with tickets going through tiered pre-sales before an August 14 general on-sale via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Tour runs Nov. 5 (Tacoma Dome) to Dec. 22 (Kia Forum, LA); 28 dates across the US and Canada.
- Pre-sale sequence: Patreon (Aug. 10), artist pre-sale (Aug. 11), Live Nation All Access (Aug. 12), general on-sale Aug. 14 via Ticketmaster.
- Tour supports new single "Get Happy / Happy Days Are Here Again," continuing the group's holiday-music series; partnered with nonprofit Save the Music for select-show donations.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Pentatonix-Christmas-in-the-City-Tour.webp)

Pentatonix have announced plans for their 2026 “Christmas in the City Tour,” a North American arena tour featuring 28 dates across the United States and Canada.

The Grammy-winning vocal group will begin the holiday tour on November 5 at the Tacoma Dome in Tacoma, Washington. The run will continue with stops in cities including Vancouver, Cleveland, Albany, Nashville, Atlanta, Austin, Glendale and San Diego before wrapping up on December 22 in Los Angeles at the Kia Forum.

Tickets will be available through several pre-sale opportunities before the general public on-sale. Patreon members will have the first chance to purchase tickets beginning Monday, August 10, at 10 a.m. local time. An artist pre-sale will follow on Tuesday, August 11, while a Live Nation All Access pre-sale for select dates will begin Wednesday, August 12.

The general public ticket sale will begin Friday, August 14, at 10 a.m. local time through Ticketmaster.

Pentatonix have also partnered with the music education nonprofit Save the Music for the 2026 tour. Fans attending select shows will have the opportunity to donate to the organization during the ticket purchasing process.

In addition to announcing the tour, Pentatonix released a new single titled “Get Happy / Happy Days Are Here Again.” The song is featured as part of the group’s continued holiday music releases and follows the 2025 album *Christmas in the City*.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit the [official Pentatonix website](https://www.home.ptxofficial.com/).
