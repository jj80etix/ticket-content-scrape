# Nickelback Announces 70-Date 2027 World Tour With Don Broco

## Summary

Nickelback announced a 70-date "Everything Under The Sun" world tour for 2027 spanning North America, the UK, Europe and Australia, with Don Broco as support on most dates, in support of its 11th studio album of the same name (out Oct. 30).

### Key Points
- Tour runs Feb. 6 (Pittsburgh) through Oct. 16, 2027 (Pokolbin, Australia), across 70 shows on four continents.
- Follows Nickelback's "Get Rollin'" tour, which the band calls its fastest-selling and highest-attended to date.
- General onsale begins Oct. 16 via Live Nation; artist presale starts Oct. 13, with region-specific card presales (Verizon Shine in the US, Amex in Canada, Mastercard/Vodafone in UK/Europe/Australia).
- Also offered through Reserved by Spotify, which uses listening activity and location data to select eligible Premium subscribers for a dedicated presale window.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/nickelback-2026-tour-announcement-1200.webp)

Nickelback has announced a 70-date world tour for 2027, with shows across North America, the United Kingdom, Europe and Australia and English rock band Don Broco joining as support on most dates. The Everything Under The Sun Tour begins Feb. 6 in Pittsburgh and continues through October.

The tour supports Nickelback’s 11th studio album, *Everything Under The Sun*, scheduled for release Oct. 30 through Virgin Music Group. It will be the Canadian band’s first studio album since 2022’s *Get Rollin’*, which was followed by a world tour that the band describes as its fastest-selling and highest-attended to date.

North American stops include Chicago’s United Center on Feb. 18, Washington’s Capital One Arena on March 8, Toronto’s Scotiabank Arena on March 21, Vancouver’s Rogers Arena on April 9, Las Vegas’ T-Mobile Arena on April 16 and Houston’s Toyota Center on April 29. The schedule also includes a May 7 concert at CDMX Arena in Mexico City.

Nickelback will appear at Hondo Rodeo Fest at Raymond James Stadium in Tampa, Florida, on Feb. 27. Don Broco is not scheduled to perform at that event, according to the tour announcement.

The UK and European leg begins May 22 in Leeds, with dates in London, Dublin, Amsterdam, Cologne, Munich, Milan and other cities before concluding July 13 in Copenhagen. The Australian run opens Oct. 2 in Perth and ends Oct. 16 in Pokolbin, following concerts in Adelaide, Melbourne, Geelong, Brisbane and Sydney.

Don Broco’s involvement extends an existing musical collaboration between the two bands. Nickelback collaborated with Don Broco on “Nightmare Tripping,” the title track of the English band’s album for Fearless Records. The supporting act is known for mixing rock, metal, post-hardcore and electronic sounds.

Nickelback has released several songs ahead of its new album, including “Rattle The Cage” featuring guitarist John 5, “Leave Me Behind” and “Make Me Love You.” The band’s catalog includes “How You Remind Me,” “Photograph,” “Rockstar” and “Someday.” TicketNews previously covered the band’s [Get Rollin’ tour announcement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2023/01/nickelback-announces-get-rollin-tour-with-brantley-gilbert/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in 2023.

## Nickelback 2027 Tour Tickets and Presales

Artist presales for North America, the UK, Europe and Australia begin Tuesday, Oct. 13, at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales are scheduled to begin Friday, Oct. 16, at 10 a.m. local time through [Live Nation’s Nickelback tour listings](https://www.livenation.com/artist/K8vZ9171EXf/nickelback-events?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Additional presales vary by region and eligibility.

In the United States, Verizon customers enrolled in Verizon Shine will have a presale for select shows beginning Oct. 13 at 10 a.m. local time and ending Oct. 15 at 10 p.m. local time. American Express card members in Canada can access a presale beginning Oct. 13 at noon local time. Mastercard presales begin Oct. 14 in the UK and several European markets, while Australian buyers will have Mastercard and Vodafone presale opportunities that day.

Another North American option is Reserved by Spotify. Eligible Spotify Premium subscribers ages 18 and older in the U.S. and Canada may be invited to purchase up to two tickets set aside for selected fans through Ticketmaster. Spotify says it uses listening activity, including streams, saves and shares, along with location, to determine eligibility. Offers are delivered through email and the Spotify app; an ordinary Premium subscription does not automatically guarantee access.

The program, [which TicketNews covered when it launched](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/spotify-launches-reserved-ticket-presale-program-for-top-u-s-fans/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), gives selected listeners a dedicated buying window ahead of the general onsale. Spotify’s [guidance for Reserved](https://newsroom.spotify.com/2026-06-18/reserved-by-spotify-guide/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) says the offer applies to eligible tour dates and seating availability can vary by show. Receiving a Reserved offer does not guarantee a specific show or seat.

VIP packages will also be offered, with options that may include premium seats, pre-show lounge access and exclusive merchandise. Package contents vary, and the announcement did not provide standard ticket prices.
