# ‘Communities beyond the continent’: Tix Africa launches in the UK

## Summary

Nigeria-founded ticketing and event-technology platform Tix Africa is expanding into the UK, following the growth of African promoters and diaspora audiences producing events there. The company, which has powered ticketing for Spotify, Global Citizen, and other major African live events over six years, frames the move as following its existing organiser and audience base rather than chasing a new market cold. The launch coincides with the release of Tix Africa's inaugural industry data report, The Tix Index.

### Key Points
- Tix Africa (founded Nigeria, 2019) is launching UK operations, citing African promoters and diaspora communities increasingly producing events in London, Manchester, and Birmingham.
- The company has six years of ticketing/event-tech experience across Nigeria and Ghana, working with clients including Spotify, Global Citizen, Flytime Festival, and AfroFuture.
- Launch coincides with The Tix Index, Tix Africa's first proprietary data report on Nigerian ticketing/consumer behavior.

### Highlights
- New primary-ticketing platform entrant into the UK market, extending an existing African organiser/audience base rather than launching cold.

### Industry Problem
General-purpose primary platforms have historically underserved diaspora and culturally-specific organiser communities (local-currency support, audience understanding); Tix Africa's growth suggests unmet demand for platforms purpose-built around a specific audience/organiser base rather than geography alone.

### Proposed Solution
No direct product ask; this is a monitor-only market-entry signal. Etix should track whether Tix Africa's UK expansion brings it into direct competition for diaspora-audience or culturally-specific organiser clients Etix also serves.

## ‘Communities beyond the continent’: Tix Africa launches in the UK

The Nigeria-founded ticketing platform says the expansion follows the growth of African promoters and diaspora audience in the UK

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/Crowd-©-Tom-Grut.png)

**African ticketing and event technology platform Tix Africa is expanding into the UK, marking a milestone in the international growth of African-built live event infrastructure.**

The move comes after six years of building ticketing and event management technology for some of the continent’s largest live experiences, including events for Spotify, Global Citizen, Flytime Festival, Art X Lagos and AfroFuture, alongside thousands of organisers across Nigeria and Ghana.

Announcing the expansion, Tix Africa said the UK launch was driven by changing audience behaviour, and not by mere geographic growth.

“African creators, organisers, and audiences are increasingly building communities beyond the continent. As migration continues to reshape where culture is created and experienced, we believe the infrastructure supporting those experiences should evolve alongside it,” said Folayemi Agusto, co-founder and CEO of Tix.

As organisers scale abroad, the technology backing ticket sales, audience management, event operations and customer insights needs to travel with them. IQ recently [reported](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/07/mainland-block-party-marks-million-ticket-milestone/) on Mainland Block Party’s move to the UK after crossing one million ticket sales across Nigeria, Ghana, US and UK.

“The world may have discovered Africa through its music. Now it’s time for the world to discover Africa through its event experiences. If the last few years are any indication, Africa’s next great cultural export won’t just be what the world listens to. It will be what the world shows up for,” Agusto added.

> “African creators, organisers and audiences are increasingly building communities beyond the continent”

The UK expansion also coincides with the release of The Tix Index, the company’s inaugural industry report based on three years of proprietary platform data. Drawing on ticketing activity across Nigeria, the report examines consumer behaviour, audience demographics, geographic demand and spending patterns.

Founded in Nigeria, Tix Africa has quietly established itself as one of the continent’s leading ticketing platforms, powering thousands of events across music, business, sport, and culture.

While African music has become a global export over the past decade, Tix Africa’s expansion suggests that the infrastructure supporting the continent’s live entertainment industry may now be treading a similar path. Instead of following artists alone, African event technology companies are following the organisers, promoters, and diaspora communities they already serve across multiple markets.

Announcing the launch this week, Agusto said: “When we launched Tix in 2019, our ambition wasn’t to build a ticketing platform for a single city or even a single country. We wanted to help organisers across Africa to sell tickets in their local currencies and to build a platform that truly understood their needs.Today, that same ambition is taking us beyond the continent.

“We’re not launching in the UK because we’re chasing a new market. We’re launching because the communities we already serve have become increasingly global.

“Organisers who started in Lagos and Accra are now producing experiences in London, Manchester and Birmingham. Others have built thriving communities in the UK from the ground up. They all face the same challenge: they need technology that understands events, their audiences and what it takes to grow a community,” the CEO said.
