# Jonathan Spector’s ‘Birthright’ Sets Broadway Premiere for Spring 2027

## Summary

Jonathan Spector's play "Birthright" will transfer to Broadway's Hudson Theatre in spring 2027, with previews starting Feb. 23 and opening March 15, following earlier Off-Broadway and regional productions.

### Key Points
- Director Teddy Bergman and the full Off-Broadway creative team return for the transfer.
- It's Spector's second Broadway production after "Eureka Day," which won the 2025 Tony for Best Revival of a Play.
- Originated at Miami New Drama and MCC Theater before the Broadway move.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Broadways-Birthright-1.webp)

Jonathan Spector’s play “Birthright” is heading to Broadway in spring 2027 following its Off-Broadway engagement earlier this year.

The production will begin previews at the Hudson Theatre on February 23, with an official opening scheduled for March 15.

The Broadway transfer follows the play’s earlier productions at Miami New Drama and MCC Theater. Teddy Bergman, who directed the Off-Broadway staging, will return to lead the Broadway production.

Bergman, who has worked with Spector throughout the play’s development, discussed the production’s themes and its upcoming move to Broadway. “Working with Jonathan on his extraordinary play has been one of the greatest artistic experiences of my life,” Bergman said in a statement.

The Broadway engagement will mark Spector’s second production on Broadway. His first was “Eureka Day,” which opened in 2024 and went on to win the 2025 Tony Award for Best Revival of a Play. The production helped establish Spector’s presence on Broadway ahead of the upcoming premiere of “Birthright.”

The creative team from the Off-Broadway production will also return for the Broadway transfer. Scott Pask will oversee scenic design, with costumes by Clint Ramos and lighting by Natasha Katz. Lee Kinney will handle sound design, while David Bengali will provide projection design.
