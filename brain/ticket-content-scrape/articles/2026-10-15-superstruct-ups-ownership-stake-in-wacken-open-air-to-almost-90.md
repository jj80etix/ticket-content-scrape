# Superstruct ups ownership stake in Wacken Open Air to almost 90%

## Summary

Festival roll-up Superstruct Entertainment has increased its ownership stake in Wacken Open Air's operating company to almost 90%, up from 69% at the end of 2025, as part of a long-term phased-transfer agreement dating to its original 2019 investment. Co-founders Thomas Jensen and Holger Hübner, now holding reduced stakes, say they remain committed to the festival's operational strategy alongside Superstruct.

### Key Points
- Superstruct's stake in International Concert Services (ICS, WOA's parent) rises to just under 90%, up from 69% ownership of WOA Festival GmbH at end-2025.
- Jensen and Hübner's personal stakes dropped from 15.5% to 5% as part of the phased transfer agreed in 2019.
- Superstruct holds stakes across ~80 festivals in its portfolio, including 100% ownership of Tramlines and Boardmasters.
- WOA, one of Germany's largest metal festivals, returns July 2027 with Electric Callboy, Five Finger Death Punch and Helloween.

## Transcript

Superstruct has increased its ownership stake in Wacken Open Air (WOA) to almost 90%, with co-founder Thomas Jensen revealing the move comes as part of a “long-term” agreement dating back to the company’s first involvement with the festival.

After Jensen and co-founder Holger Hübner reduced their stakes from 15.5% down to 5% holdings in the event, Jensen tells *IQ* the pair are “fully committed” to continuing to develop WOA, one of Germany’s largest music festivals.

He also reveals that since Superstruct first partnered with WOA in 2019, an agreement has been in effect for Superstruct to increase its shares in International Concert Services, a promoter and producer founded by Jensen and Hübner, which operates WOA as its flagship event. Superstruct first invested in ICS in 2019.

“A phased transfer of ICS shares to Superstruct Entertainment was part of the long-term agreement from the outset and has been gradually implemented. As managing directors, we remain fully committed to the ongoing development of Wacken Open Air together with the whole team and the metal community,” Jensen tells *IQ.*

“We have had a lot of positive experiences working with Superstruct Entertainment and their festival network over the years,” he adds.

> “Operational aspects of Wacken Open Air are handled by the whole team”

Superstruct’s new stake of just under 90% in ICS is up from its 69% ownership of operating company WOA Festival GmbH, part of the ICS business group, at the end of 2025.

Superstruct holds stakes of varying sizes in festivals across its portfolio of around 80 events, including 100% holdings in companies behind festivals like Tramlines and Boardmasters, according to the filings.

Jensen tells *IQ*: “Without this partnership, Holger and I might sometimes have taken a different path. But if we’re honest, most of the decisions that didn’t work out were the ones we made on our own, which makes us all the more pleased to have Superstruct Entertainment as a partner.”

Jensen and Hübner will remain involved in the festival’s strategy, with Jensen telling *IQ: “* Planning and decision-making regarding the operational aspects of Wacken Open Air are handled by the whole team, a practice that has long been in place, and the addition of Andre Jürgens to the management team has brought in valuable extra expertise.”

WOA returns in July 2027 with a bill which includes Electric Callboy, Five Finger Death Punch, and Helloween.
