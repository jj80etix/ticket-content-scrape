# Stray Kids plot return to global stadium circuit in latest tour news

## Summary

IQ Magazine's tour-news roundup leads with Stray Kids' newly revealed 29-date "Run It" global stadium tour for 2027, alongside fresh 2027 dates from Garth Brooks, P!NK, Maluma and Kanye West.

### Key Points
- Stray Kids: Live Nation-promoted Run It World Tour opens Jan. 16-17 in Bangkok, hits Singapore/Yokohama/Osaka, then 14 North American dates (Seattle to MetLife Stadium) and a July EU/UK leg ending at San Siro July 31.
- Builds on the group's 2026 StrayCity festival-style concert run across Latin America.
- Roundup also covers new Garth Brooks (Ireland), P!NK, Maluma and Kanye West dates.

## Transcript

Plus: New dates from Garth Brooks, P!NK, Maluma, Kanye West, Blond:ish, Kings of Leon, Nick Cave, and Bon Jovi

![Stray Kids at Rock in Rio](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Stray-Kids-at-Rock-in-Rio-©-Diego-Padhila.png)

Stray Kids at Rock in Rio

Stray Kids, Garth Brooks, P!NK, Maluma, and Kanye West have all announced a series of new tour dates for 2027, leading the latest round-up of tour news.

**Stray Kids** –  *Run It* global outing revealed  
One of the K-pop genre’s [best-selling groups](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/08/stray-kids-set-new-global-benchmark-for-k-pop/), Stray Kids will return to the road in 2027 with the 29-date *Run It World Tour*.

The stadium outing will launch with two nights at Bangkok’s Impact Arena (16-17 January), with dual plays in Singapore, Yokohama, and Osaka lined up in Asia.

From there, the JYP Entertainment group will hit the North American circuit for 14 shows, starting in Seattle’s Lumen Field (16 May) and making stops in cities like Vancouver, San Diego, Los Angeles, Houston, Miami, Chicago, Tonto, and wrapping up at East Rutherford’s MetLife Stadium (29 June).

An EU/UK leg is set for July, with the band to play stadiums in Paris, London, Munich, Vienna, Barcelona, Arnhem, and Milan, wrapping up at Italy’s San Siro Stadium on 31 July.

The Live Nation-led venture builds on the group’s [StrayCity campaign](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/05/stray-kids-straycity-to-bring-festival-production-to-special-concert-run/) across Latin America this year.

**Garth Brooks** – Country to take over Ireland in 2027  
Country star Garth Brooks will embark on a five-city, 10-date tour of Ireland next year, his first headline shows in the country since 2022.

The Aiken Promotions-backed run begins at Belfast’s Boucher Road (21-22 May), and will run through Galway’s Pearse Stadium (29-30 May), Kilkenny’s UMPC Nowlan Park (4-5 June), Limerick’s TUS Gaelic Grounds (11-12 June), before finishing at Cork’s Supervalu Páirc Uí Chaoimh (18-19 June).

“This has been a dream of mine since the first time we played Ireland,” Brooks said. “Once you get a taste of how the Irish love music, you can’t play there enough!”

In 2022, he [sold out five nights](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2021/11/garth-brooks-sells-out-croke-park/) at Croke Park in Dublin, shifting roughly 400,000 tickets. Dublin is notably absent from the announced routing.

He is currently playing arenas in North America with the *Blame It All On My Roots* tour.

**Maluma** –  *Loco X Volver World Tour* pending  
Colombian rapper Maluma has announced a 60-date world tour for 2027, set to span Latin America, North America, Europe, and Africa, though dates and venues are to be announced.

The first three dates are the only confirmed details of the *Loco X Volver* outing so far. It starts at Colombia’s Bogotá Arena on 5 February, followed by back-to-back shows at Argentina’s Movistar Arena on 19-20 February.

Confirmed cities include Mexico City, Santiago, Lima, Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, New York, Chicago, Madrid, Paris, Berlin, Rabat, and Pristina.

“There are places that shaped my story and people who made every one of my dreams possible,” Maluma says. “Loco X Volver was born from a deep desire to go back to where it all began, to reunite with those who have grown up alongside me, and to reach new corners of the world so we can keep writing our story together.”

**P!NK** – Next Sphere headliner announced  
P!NK will soon become the Sphere’s first solo female pop artist to headline the next-generation venue.

Her residency, promoted by Live Nation, will start 29 September, with dates lined up on 1-2, 6, 8-9, 27, and 29-30 October 2027.

**Kanye West** – First 2027 date rolled out  
Ye has announced a show at Mexico City’s Estadio Banorte on 17 March, the controversial rapper’s first confirmed date for 2027.

It is presented by Ikon Presents and 515 Entertainment, both of which have been heavily involved in his return to touring.

**Blond:ish** – Four-city India tour confirmed  
Electronic star Blond:ish has lined up a tour of India in December, set to span Bengaluru, Delhi, Hyderabad, and Goa.

The quickfire run is set for 25-28 December, promoted by Team Innovation, with venues to be announced.

**Kings of Leon** – North American outing lined up  
Off the back of their latest album, *O My Beloved*, Kings of Leon have announced a 30-date North American run for 2027.

Spanning outdoor amphitheatres and arenas, the outing starts on 8 May at Virginia’s Jiffy Lube Live, making stops in cities like Philadelphia, Toronto, Vancouver, Seattle, Los Angeles, Phoenix, Austin, Orlando, and New York, where it is set to conclude at Madison Square Garden.

Special guests Band of Horses and Dexter and The Moonrocks will support on select dates.

**Nick Cave** – *Talk and Music* format revivedAustralian singer Nick Cave is reviving a 2019 tour format, *Conversations With Nick Cave: An Evening of Talk and Music*, to hit the road next year. The format features a mix of unvetted audience questions and intimate piano performances of Cave’s extensive catalogue.

The 25-date North American theatre run starts on 9 April at Vancouver’s Queen Elizabeth Theatre, and will make stops in major cities like Seattle, San Francisco, Los Angeles, Chicago, Toronto, Boston, Brooklyn, Nashville, and will wrap up on 21 May at New Orleans’ Saenger Theatre.

**Bon Jovi** – Pair of dates added to *Forever  
*Finally, rockers *Bon Jovi* will play a second night at Toronto’s Rogers Stadium in August, and have added a new date at Indiana’s Notre Dame Stadium, in the latest developments on the  *Forever Tour*.

The pair of shows join previously announced 2027 dates in Tokyo, Melbourne, Sydney, London, East Rutherford, Chicago, and Foxborough.
