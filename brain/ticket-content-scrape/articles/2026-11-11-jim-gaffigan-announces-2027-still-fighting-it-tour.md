# Jim Gaffigan Announces 2027 ‘Still Fighting It Tour’

## Summary

Jim Gaffigan announced his 2027 "Still Fighting It Tour," a 31-date North American run spanning theaters, arenas and casino venues across more than 20 cities. The tour follows a run of November/December shows, with a presale Nov 12 and general onsale Nov 13.

### Key Points
- 2027 leg opens January 6 at Encore Theater, Wynn Las Vegas, and runs through June 6 in La Crosse, WI
- Precedes stops in Nashville, Texas and Florida through the end of 2026
- Venue presale (code COMEDY) begins Nov 12, 10am; general onsale Nov 13, 10am
- Only Canadian stop is London, Ontario (April 2, 2027)

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Jim-Gaffigan-Still-Fighting-It-Tour.webp)

Jim Gaffigan has announced his 2027 “Still Fighting It Tour,” with 31 performances planned across North America. The comedian’s all-new tour will visit more than 20 cities, bringing him to theaters, arenas and casino venues across the United States and Canada.

The tour is slated to open on January 6 in Las Vegas, Nevada, at the Encore Theater at Wynn Las Vegas. Gaffigan will perform two nights at the venue before heading to Albuquerque, New Mexico, for a show at the Isleta Resort & Casino showroom.

From New Mexico, Gaffigan will head to The Venue at Thunder Valley in Lincoln, California, before Midwest stops in Oshkosh, Wisconsin; Rockford, Illinois; and Duluth, Minnesota. He then returns west for performances at Mountain America Center in Idaho Falls, Idaho, and casino venues in Auburn and Ridgefield, Washington.

The tour continues to Florida with shows in Tallahassee and Hollywood, followed by Rivers Casino in Portsmouth, Virginia, and Eccles Theater in Salt Lake City. Gaffigan will next visit Santander Arena in Reading, Pennsylvania, before crossing into Canada for a performance at Canada Life Place in London, Ontario, the only Canadian stop announced.

A venue presale begins Thursday, November 12, at 10 a.m. with the code COMEDY. General public ticket sales follow Friday, November 13, at 10 a.m.
