# Former IQ news editor Jon Chapple passes away

## Summary

Former IQ Magazine news editor Jon Chapple has died at age 37 following a long battle with cancer. Chapple led IQ's news desk from 2016-2021, launched its daily Index news service, and helped create the ILMC Futures Forum for young live-music professionals.

### Key Points
- Served as IQ news editor January 2016 - August 2021.
- Oversaw launch of IQ Index, the publication's daily news service.
- Helped launch the ILMC Futures Forum in March 2019.
- Diagnosed with stage-four bowel cancer in January 2022; ran a 2023 fundraising campaign for further treatment.

Former *IQ* news editor Jon Chapple has passed away aged 37, after a lengthy battle with cancer.

Chapple worked as *IQ’* s news editor from January 2016 to August 2021, in which time he oversaw the launch of *IQ* ‘s daily news service, Index, and played a key role in relaying critical information to the live music business during the Covid pandemic.

He was also part of the agenda team for the International Live Music Conference (ILMC), and in March 2019 helped launch a new one-day event at ILMC for young live music professionals, Futures Forum.

Chapple was diagnosed with stage-four bowel cancer in January 2022, aged 32. In May 2023, he launched a [fundraising campaign](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2023/05/former-iq-news-editor-jon-chapple-fundraising-cancer-care/), hoping to raise £75,000 to receive cancer vaccines in Germany, after being denied further surgery via the NHS.

“ Jon was a hugely talented, warm, and funny individual who played a major role in *IQ* ‘s development for many years. If you needed an example of how highly Jon was regarded, when he started a GoFundMe page in 2023 to pay for cancer treatment, the business kindly rallied around to contribute over £100,000 within days,” said ILMC managing director Greg Parmley.

“Jon was a tenacious journalist, and he battled his illness with that same strength of will until the very end. The whole ILMC team is incredibly sad to hear of his passing.”

Around the same time, Chapple also wrote a [comment piece](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2023/05/a-gut-punch-living-with-stage-four-bowel-cancer/) on his journey since leaving *IQ* and on his experience with the disease.

> “The whole ILMC team is incredibly sad to hear of his passing”

In 2019, Chapple was named music business journalist of the year at the International Music Journalism Awards (IMJA). And in 2023, he was recognised among Inavate’s 40 Under 40 for the EMEA region.

Prior to joining *IQ*, he was deputy editor of audio industry title PSNEurope, and also wrote for the European Arena Yearbook, the International Ticketing Yearbook, the Spanish Live Music Yearbook (Anuario de la Música en Vivo), Genius!, PSNLive, Shindig!, PopMatters and various Titan Magazines titles.

Following his time at *IQ,* Chapple worked in live event communications at 3WM Communications, a boutique pro-AV PR agency.

His book, *Sri Krishna Prem: A Wing and a Prayer*, a biography of the British-born guru and yogi, was published by Blazing Sapphire Press in December 2024.

Chapple is survived by his wife Nikki and their baby, Winifred.

A celebration of life service will be held at Southend Crematorium at 2pm on Thursday 15 October.
