# Front Gate Tickets links with face-value resale platform CashorTrade

## Summary

Ticketmaster subsidiary Front Gate Tickets will extend its ticket-verification process to resales processed through independent face-value marketplace CashorTrade for major festivals including Lollapalooza, Austin City Limits, Bourbon & Beyond, Lost Lands, and Ultra Music Festival. CashorTrade caps resale at the original purchase price (inclusive of fees) and already has a direct integration with Ticketmaster; the partnership comes weeks after a group of secondary sellers sued to block Washington, D.C.'s new resale price-cap law, arguing "original purchase price" is something marketplaces can't reliably know.

### Key Points
- Front Gate (Ticketmaster subsidiary) will verify authenticity and purchase price for CashorTrade resales at five major festival brands
- CashorTrade: face-value-capped, fan-to-fan marketplace; already integrated with Ticketmaster directly; other partners include Afton Tickets, VenuePilot, TicketSauce
- Comes amid active litigation (DC resale-law challenge) over whether "original purchase price" caps are enforceable/knowable
- Positioned by both companies as reducing fraud while keeping resale at face value

### Highlights
- Price-verification technology enables face-value caps where secondary sellers claim platforms "cannot know" the original price — directly rebutting the DC lawsuit's core argument
- Deal spans five named major festivals (Lollapalooza, ACL, Bourbon & Beyond, Lost Lands, Ultra)

### Industry Problem
Fan-to-fan resale at inflated prices and ticket-authenticity fraud remain core patron pain points; regulators and legislators are simultaneously trying to cap resale prices (DC law) while sellers argue price-verification is technically infeasible — a live compliance/product question for any primary platform's resale posture.

### Proposed Solution
Front Gate's approach — primary-platform verification data licensed to an independent face-value resale marketplace — is a concrete, working answer to the "can't know the original price" objection. Etix could evaluate a similar verified-price-capped resale integration (build/improve) as both a fraud-reduction and compliance-readiness move ahead of similar price-cap legislation spreading to other states.

Ticketmaster subsidiary Front Gate Tickets has announced it will offer resale for some of North America’s biggest festivals through independent resale platform CashorTrade, endorsing the US platform’s mission of face-value, fan-to-fan resale.

Front Gate will offer its ticket verification for passes resold through the marketplace for events including Lollapalooza, Austin City Limits, Bourbon & Beyond, Lost Lands, and Ultra Music Festival, verifying tickets’ authenticity and purchase price, inclusive of fees.

“Every new integration is another door we open for fans who just want to see a show and pay the original, face value price,” says Brando Rich, CashorTrade CEO/ founder. “Festival fans plan their whole year around these weekends. Now, when plans change, they can hand a pass to another fan at face value, verified at the source.”

Referred to as a social network for resale, Cashortrade is the world’s self-professed “first fair trade marketplace where real fans ethically buy, sell, and trade tickets at face value”.

Its service directly links primary and secondary sellers, allowing for the resale price to be capped at the known value, a unique business model in the US resale domain.

Earlier this month, this concept was part of a [direct complaint](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/10/secondary-us-sellers-sue-to-halt-legally-flawed-d-c-resale-laws/) from a cohort of secondary sellers to stop the enforcement of Washington, D.C.’s recently passed ticket resale law. The plaintiffs argued that price caps are “arbitrary” as the “original purchase price” is something that marketplaces “did not set, do not receive, and generally cannot know”.

CashorTrade also has a direct integration with Ticketmaster, which the company says makes it the “only face-value platform officially integrated with the world’s largest ticketing company”.

The independent company also says its resale technology was pioneered with Ticketmaster. Other partners include Afton Tickets, VenuePilot, and TicketSauce.

“Festival goers should be able to buy resale tickets for festivals knowing they’re getting the real thing,” says Andrew Sider, Ticketmaster EVP of festivals and general admission. “By extending Front Gate Tickets’ verification process to CashorTrade, we’re giving fans another trusted place to buy and sell legitimate tickets at face value or below.”
