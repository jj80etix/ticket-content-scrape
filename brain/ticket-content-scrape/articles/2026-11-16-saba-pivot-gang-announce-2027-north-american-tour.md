# Saba, Pivot Gang Announce 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Saba and Pivot Gang announced "The All My Friends Tour," their first trek since 2019, running March 5-24, 2027 across North America. Before the tour, Pivot Gang will play a hometown memorial show at Chicago's Thalia Hall on Dec. 19 honoring late member John Walt. The announcement follows the group's first new music video in over two years.

### Key Points
- Tour runs March 5 (Atlanta) to March 24 (Los Angeles), 2027; first tour since 2019.
- Dec. 19 hometown show at Thalia Hall in memory of member Walter "John Walt" Long Jr.

Saba and Pivot Gang are hitting the road in 2027 for “The All My Friends Tour,” their first trek since 2019. The outing kicks off on March 5 in Atlanta at Terminal West and concludes on March 24 at the Echoplex in Los Angeles.

However, before the collective hits the road, Pivot Gang is set to play a special hometown show at Thalia Hall on Dec. 19 in honor of John Walt Day. The annual concert is held in memory of Pivot Gang rapper Walter “John Walt” Long Jr., who was murdered in 2017.

The announcement arrives just a couple of weeks after the Chicago-based group released “Location,” the act’s first music video in over two years. Outside of the collective, Saba has remained very active in recent years. In 2025, the rapper dropped the solo EP *Coffee!* and a collaborative album with No ID, *From the Private Collection of Saba and No ID,* which earned critical acclaim upon release.
