# Victorian government supports festivals in latest round of funding

## Summary

The Victorian state government (Australia), in partnership with Music Victoria, is funding 16 festivals through round three of its Live Music Festivals Fund, distributing over A$640,000. Recipients span established events (Port Fairy Folk Festival, Riverboats Music Festival) and newer/regional ones, with a focus on communities affected by the January 2026 bushfires. The fund has now supported nearly 50 festivals since 2024, and the state plans to launch a related venues program, "10,000 Gigs: The Victorian Gig Fund."

### Key Points
- Round three grants total more than A$640,000 (~£335,000/€391,000/$452,000) across 16 festivals.
- Notable recipients: Port Fairy Folk Festival, Riverboats Music Festival, Queenscliff Music Festival, Ability Fest, LuliePalooza, The Dot, Our Survival Day.
- Priority given to regional festivals impacted by the January 2026 bushfires (Town Folk Festival, Interstellar Groove Festival, High Country Hop).
- Program has funded nearly 50 festivals total since 2024, aligned with the state's Creative State 2028 strategy.
- Victoria will soon launch "10,000 Gigs: The Victorian Gig Fund" to support music venues.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Victorian government, in partnership with Music Victoria, has backed 16 festivals across the Australian state through the latest round of the Live Music Festivals Fund.**

Round three of the fund will provide grants totalling more than A$640,000 (£335,000/€391,000/$452,000) to a mix of metropolitan and regional events.

Recipients include the likes of Port Fairy Folk Festival, Riverboats Music Festival and Queenscliff Music Festival, as well as newer events such as Abbotsford’s LuliePalooza, Footscray electronic festival The Dot and Mt Martha’s First Peoples music festival Our Survival Day. Inclusive music festival Ability Fest will also receive funding.

The majority of festivals supported by round three of the Live Music Festivals Fund are located in regional communities, including those that were impacted by the January 2026 bushfires such as Castlemaine’s Town Folk Festival, Tallarook’s Interstellar Groove Festival and Beechworth’s High Country Hop.

**Securing futures**

Since 2024, the Live Music Festivals Fund has provided grants to nearly 50 festivals. The investment has helped to secure jobs for artists and crew, while creating memorable live music experiences for fans.

The programme will continue to prioritise inclusive, accessible and diverse events in line with the state government’s creative industries strategy Creative State 2028.

As well as the Live Music Festivals Fund, the government supports live music through the Music Works grants, and will soon launch the music venues programme 10,000 Gigs: The Victorian Gig Fund.
