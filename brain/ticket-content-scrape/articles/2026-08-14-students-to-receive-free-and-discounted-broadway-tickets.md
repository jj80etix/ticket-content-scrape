# Students to receive free and discounted Broadway tickets

## Summary

New York City, in partnership with the Theatre Development Fund, is offering NYC high school students a lottery for 2,000 free/low-cost Broadway tickets (including Buena Vista Social Club and Stranger Things: The First Shadow) plus a free two-month TDF membership giving discounted access to Broadway, Off-Broadway and cultural institutions; the lottery closes Aug. 17.

### Key Points
- 2,000 free/discounted Broadway tickets available via lottery to NYC high schoolers, entries close Aug. 17.
- Winners get up to two tickets plus a free two-month TDF membership (discounted Broadway/Off-Broadway/museum/dance access).
- Announced by Mayor Zohran Mamdani and TDF, which says it has served ~70 million theatregoers since 1968.

## Transcript

![TheTicketingBusiness News](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/TBN-LOGO-reversed-white.png)

TheTicketingBusiness News

**New York Mayor Zohran Mamdani and the Theatre Development Fund (TDF) have provided New York City high school students with the opportunity to receive 2,000 free and low-cost Broadway tickets.**

Students can now enter a lottery to receive tickets to shows such as Buena Vista Social Club, The Lost Boys, and Stranger Things: The First Shadow.

The lottery will remain open until August 17 at 10am, and selected students will be able to claim up to two tickets.

“In a city overflowing with extraordinary art and culture, every young person should have the opportunity to experience those moments, regardless of what’s in their bank account,” said Mamdani.

“That’s why, in partnership with TDF, we’re offering more than 2,000 free tickets to some of Broadway’s most exciting shows, along with discounted tickets to many more.

“We want every high school student to have the opportunity to explore this city, experience incredible art and discover something that stays with them long after the curtain falls.”

Every student who enters the lottery will automatically receive a free, two-month TDF membership, giving them access to discounted tickets to Broadway, Off-Broadway, and Off-Off Broadway shows, as well as music, museums and dance.

TDF Membership will also give New York City high school students the opportunity to visit some of the city’s cultural institutions for free or at discounted rates.

“At TDF, we believe that theatre is the birthright of every New Yorker, and since our founding in 1968, we have welcomed close to 70 million theatregoers with low-cost and free admissions to Broadway and NYC theatre,” said Deeksha Gaur, TDF’s executive director.

“We believe that individuals and societies are healthier when residents have access to art. That is why we are deeply grateful to Mayor Mamdani for partnering with TDF on this essential opportunity for New York City high schoolers.

“We—along with our incredible industry partners—look forward to facilitating a lifelong relationship with the arts for these young New Yorkers, leading to joyful, transformative experiences while strengthening the fabric of our great city.”
