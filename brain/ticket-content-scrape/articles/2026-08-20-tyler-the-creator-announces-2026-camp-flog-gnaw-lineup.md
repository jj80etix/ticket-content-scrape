# Tyler, The Creator Announces 2026 Camp Flog Gnaw Lineup

## Summary

Tyler, The Creator announced the full lineup for the 2026 Camp Flog Gnaw Carnival, taking place Nov. 14-15 at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles, featuring Steve Lacy, Turnstile, Future and Don Toliver among dozens of acts. The event follows Tyler's Chromakopia and Don't Tap the Glass album cycles and his 2025 "Chromakopia" world tour.

### Key Points
- Two-day festival, Nov. 14-15, Dodger Stadium, Los Angeles.
- Headline-tier acts include Steve Lacy, Turnstile, Future, Don Toliver, JID, Lupe Fiasco and Vince Staples.
- Extensive supporting lineup spans dozens of additional acts across multiple stages.
- Founded and curated by Tyler, The Creator; runs alongside his recent album releases.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Camp-Flog-Gnaw-Carnival.webp)

Tyler, The Creator has announced the lineup for the 2026 edition of Camp Flog Gnaw Festival. The two-day event will take place November 14 and 15 at Dodger Stadium in Los Angeles.

The lineup includes Steve Lacy, Turnstile, Future, Don Toliver, JID, Lupe Fiasco, Vince Staples, Wallows, Syd and Isaiah Rashad. Tyler, The Creator is also scheduled to perform at the festival.

Other artists on the bill include Lola Young, Quadeca, 2hollis, Concrete Boys, Earl Sweatshirt & MIKE, Domo Genesis, The Alchemist, Leon Thomas, Magdalena Bay, Jane Remover, Thee Sacred Souls, Audrey Nuna, Latin Mafia, Casey Veggies and 310babii.

The festival will also feature performances from Gabriel Jacoby, Natanya, threetwenty, Left Brain, Liim, Mike G, NSQK, Nomi, GENA, Wyatt Waddell, Ama, Not For Radio and 54 Ultra.

The 2026 event will mark another edition of the festival founded and curated by Tyler, The Creator. Camp Flog Gnaw has featured a variety of hip-hop, R&B, alternative and other musical acts since its launch.

Tyler, The Creator’s appearance comes after the release of two albums in recent years. He released *Chromakopia* in 2024, followed by *Don’t Tap The Glass* in 2025. He supported the albums with the “Chromakopia: The World Tour,” which began in February 2025 and concluded in March.
