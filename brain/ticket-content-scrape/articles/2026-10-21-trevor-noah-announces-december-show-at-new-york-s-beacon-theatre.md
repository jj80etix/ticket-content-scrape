# Trevor Noah Announces December Show at New York’s Beacon Theatre

## Summary

Trevor Noah will perform "An Evening of Stand Up with Trevor Noah" at New York's Beacon Theatre on December 15 (TicketNews's companion coverage of the same announcement reported by Pollstar).

### Key Points
- Noah previously staged an extended 10-show Beacon Theatre run in Oct. 2023, and appeared there again in Oct. 2024 for a book-related event.
- Follows a busy international stretch: four Hong Kong shows (Oct. 26-29), three in Singapore (Nov. 3-5), plus Kuala Lumpur dates.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Trevor-Noah.webp)

Trevor Noah will perform at New York City’s Beacon Theatre on December 15, adding a late-year stand-up appearance to his 2026 schedule.

Billed as “An Evening of Stand Up with Trevor Noah,” the performance brings Noah back to a venue where he previously staged an extended run. His 2023 Off the Record Tour included 10 Beacon Theatre performances between October 9 and October 18. He also appeared at the theater in October 2024 for “A Conversation: Into the Uncut Grass,” an event centered on his book.

The New York performance follows a busy international stretch for Noah, who is preparing for a series of shows across Asia and the Middle East. His upcoming schedule includes four performances in Hong Kong from October 26-29, followed by three shows in Singapore from November 3-5 and two performances in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia, on November 9-10.

Noah will then travel to the Middle East for four shows at Dubai Opera on November 25, 26, 28 and 29, followed by performances in Doha, Qatar, on December 4 and Bahrain on December 5.
