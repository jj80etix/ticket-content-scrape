# Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood and Sombr Announced for Lollapalooza Berlin 2027

## Summary

Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood and sombr are the first announced artists for Lollapalooza Berlin 2027 (July 17–18, Olympiastadion/Olympiapark), with Williams making his Lollapalooza Berlin debut as headliner.

### Key Points
- Berlin was Lollapalooza's first European host city (debuted 2015 at Tempelhofer Feld; moved to Olympic grounds in 2018).
- Individual performance dates for the three confirmed artists haven't been set; more lineup news is expected in coming months.
- Makeup brand essence returns as presenting partner.
- Comes alongside Lollapalooza India's fifth edition (Jan. 23–24, 2027, Mumbai), headlined by Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, KATSEYE and Fontaines D.C.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Lollapalooza-Berlin.webp)

Robbie Williams, The Neighbourhood and sombr are the first artists announced for Lollapalooza Berlin 2027, which will take place July 17 to 18 at Olympiastadion and Olympiapark in the German capital.

The announcement begins the festival’s lineup rollout, with additional performers and programming details expected in the coming months. Williams will make his Lollapalooza Berlin debut as a headliner. Individual performance dates for the three confirmed artists have not been announced.

The festival will again use the Olympic Stadium and surrounding park for a weekend combining live performances with interactive attractions and creative programming. Makeup brand essence will return as presenting partner.

Berlin became Lollapalooza’s first European host city when the festival debuted at Tempelhofer Feld in 2015. It moved to the Olympic grounds in 2018, establishing the site as its long-term home.

The Berlin announcement follows news of Lollapalooza India’s fifth edition, scheduled for January 23–24, 2027, at Mumbai’s Mahalaxmi Racecourse. Olivia Dean, Limp Bizkit, KATSEYE and Fontaines D.C. will headline, marking the first performances in India for all four acts. The lineup also includes 5 Seconds of Summer, St. Vincent and Steve Angello, with performances spread across four stages.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit the [official Lollapalooza Berlin website](https://www.lollapaloozade.com/en).
