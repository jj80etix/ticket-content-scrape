# Outside Days 2027 Announces Headliners Jack Johnson, Jungle and Sierra Ferrell

## Summary

Outside Days has announced its 2027 lineup for the fourth annual festival at Denver's Auraria Campus, running June 3-6 with music June 4-6. Jack Johnson, Jungle and Sierra Ferrell will headline, joined by a broader bill including Big Wild, Trevor Hall and Dr. Dog. The event, organized by Outside Interactive Inc. with Colorado's Outdoor Recreation Industry Office, pairs live music with outdoor recreation programming.

### Key Points
- Headliners: Jack Johnson, Jungle, Sierra Ferrell; festival runs June 3-6, 2027 in Denver.
- Capital One/REI Co-op cardholder presale runs Nov. 16-19; general onsale Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. MT.
- Fourth annual edition, presented with Colorado's Outdoor Recreation Industry Office.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Outside-Days-1.webp)

Outside Days has announced its 2027 music lineup, with Jack Johnson, Jungle and Sierra Ferrell set to headline the fourth annual festival at Denver’s Auraria Campus. The event will run June 3–6, with three days of live music scheduled for June 4–6.

The lineup also features Big Wild, Trevor Hall, Dr. Dog, Almost Monday, Jalen Ngonda, Chance Peña, Medium Build, Amistat, Andy Frasco & The U.N., Steph Strings, Brothers of Brass, DOGTAGS and The Galentines, with additional performers expected to be announced.

Organized by Outside Interactive Inc. in partnership with Colorado’s Outdoor Recreation Industry Office and presenting sponsors Capital One and REI Co-op, the festival combines live performances with outdoor recreation activities, adventure films, speakers and industry programming.

“Outside exists to actually get people outdoors, and nothing shows that mission in action like Outside Days. Four years in, it’s where tens of thousands of people show up for the music, the ideas, the community, and each other,” said Robin Thurston, founder and CEO of Outside Interactive Inc. “Colorado has been the perfect backdrop, and we can’t wait to welcome everyone back to Denver this June.”
