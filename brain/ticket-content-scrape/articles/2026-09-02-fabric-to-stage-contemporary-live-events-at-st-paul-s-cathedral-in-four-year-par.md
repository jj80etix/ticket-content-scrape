## Fabric to stage contemporary live events at St Paul’s Cathedral in four-year partnership

The club and promoter has become the London landmark's "exclusive partner" to programme live music until 2030 after two previous live events in the cathedral

![](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/St-Pauls-Fabric.jpg)

St Paul's Cathedral © Fabric London

Famed London club and promoter Fabric has revealed a four-year partnership with the city’s St Paul’s Cathedral to promote contemporary live music events in the heritage site.

The partnership will begin with a 29 October concert with an as-yet undisclosed lineup. It will follow two previous intimate live events put on by Fabric in the cathedral in recent years, with the club and promoter saying: “We return to one of London’s most extraordinary spaces for another night of live music, and this time we’re making it official.”

“Until 2030 Fabric will be the cathedral’s exclusive partner for contemporary music events, bringing an evolving programme of artists and performances into the space,” Fabric said.

Despite being an electronic-focused club venue, Fabric’s previous St Paul’s events have mixed genres. It first brought a live music event to the site in 2024 with a performance from Australian artist RY X. The following year, Patti Smith played an intimate concert at the cathedral, which organisers at Fabric said “felt entirely at home in a place like this”.

> “It’s an extraordinary and unexpected coming together of two very different parts of London”

The club and promoter said: “What began with RY X and Patti Smith now becomes a long-term commitment to creating new musical experiences inside one of London most iconic buildings”.

St Paul’s has hosted occasional live events throughout its history, but has largely focused on classical and religious music.

“Fabric has always been about bringing people together through music and creating experiences that stay with people,” said the club’s co-founder Cameron Leslie.

“The opportunity to do that at St Paul’s Cathedral over the next four years is incredibly exciting for us. It’s an extraordinary and unexpected coming together of two very different parts of London, and we’re looking forward to building on the success of the events we’ve already held and creating something genuinely special and befitting of this incredible space,” he added.
