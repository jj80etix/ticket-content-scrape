# CEE ticketing leader PLG group picks up Serbia’s Ticket Vision

## Summary

Central/Eastern European ticketing consolidator PLG group has completed its acquisition of Serbia's leading ticketing platform, Ticket Vision — its first investment in Serbia and the latest in a long string of regional roll-up acquisitions. Ticket Vision will keep its brand and management team; PLG now operates in eight European markets.

### Key Points
- PLG group acquires 100% of Belgrade-based Ticket Vision (founded 2005), deal closed Nov. 5
- Ticket Vision keeps its brand and CEO Nataša Šošević; no team changes disclosed
- PLG now spans Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, Czechia, Slovakia, Poland, Romania and Serbia
- Follows PLG's recent acquisition of Romanian platform iaBilet; prior buys include Ticketstream, Ticketportal, bilete.ro, Ticketing Nation, goout.net
- PLG frames the deal around a "unified platform" + largest CEE consumer audience as the sales-channel pitch to organizers, alongside continued AI product investment

### Highlights
- First PLG investment in Serbia, extending its roll-up strategy to an 8th European market
- PLG explicitly ties M&A to building the largest unified CEE audience/sales-channel, not just technology
- Continues a now-familiar pattern: regional ticketing consolidators (PLG, and peers) acquiring local incumbents rather than building greenfield

### Industry Problem
Fragmented national/regional ticketing markets in CEE are consolidating rapidly as a handful of well-capitalized roll-ups acquire local leaders, concentrating audience data and sales-channel leverage in fewer hands — a market-structure dynamic largely outside the primary US/UK markets Etix competes in today, but indicative of where consolidation economics in ticketing head next.

### Proposed Solution
No specific product response needed now; worth tracking PLG's (and similar roll-ups') acquisition pace and AI-investment claims as a leading indicator for how consolidated ticketing groups compete on audience reach versus independent platforms.

## CEE ticketing leader PLG group picks up Serbia’s Ticket Vision

It is the firm's first investment in Serbia and the latest in a series of acquisitions expanding the firm's footprint in the region

![Sven Nuutmann](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Sven-Nuutmann.png)

Sven Nuutmann

International ticketing firm PLG group has completed a total acquisition of Serbia’s leading ticketing company Ticket Vision d.o.o. as it continues to expand its footprint in Central and Eastern Europe.

Founded in 2005, Belgrade-based Ticket Vision is one of the country’s leading ticketing platforms. The operation will continue under its established brand, with Nataša Šošević to continue as chief executive and the management structure to remain.

It marks PLG Ggroup’s first investment in Serbia and an “important milestone in our group’s continued expansion,” says Sven Nuutmann, PLG group founder and CEO.

“We strongly believe in the long-term growth potential of the Serbian economy and the development of its entertainment, cultural and sporting events market,” he says.

He adds that brand recognition and consumer trust give the firm a “competitive advantage” as they expand their reach.

Šošević says: “With the strength, resources and strategic vision of PLG group behind us, we have an excellent opportunity to build on our achievements and take the company to the next level.”

Formerly known as Piletilevi Group, PLG group has steadily increased market dominance in a number of CEE markets, most recently with Romanian ticketing platform iaBilet.

> “We firmly believe that technology alone does not sell tickets”

In recent years, the company has added Ticketstream, Ticketportal, bilete.ro, entertix.ro, and myticket.ro, while further back, PLG has also acquired the likes of Ticketing Nation, goout.net, Kicket, and Biletomat.

PLG group plans to continue investing in AI-powered solutions and developing its technology, in line with other firms [ramping up](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/11/ticketers-turning-to-ai-to-offer-tour-planning-services/) their efforts in the domain.

“Our goal is to provide customers with the best possible features and functionality. AI tools have brought a major breakthrough in product and IT development across the group. At the same time, we firmly believe that technology alone does not sell tickets,” Nuutmann says. “What truly drives ticket sales is a strong and loyal customer base, with technology making the purchasing experience smoother and more seamless.”

“A unified platform across the group, combined with the largest consumer audience in Central and Eastern Europe, offers event organisers a unique and highly effective sales channel,” he says.

PLG group now operates in eight European markets: Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania, the Czech Republic, Slovakia, Poland, Romania, and Serbia.

The deal was completed on Friday (5 November).
