# Hive Launches AI Marketing Assistants

## Summary

Live-event marketing platform Hive launched five AI assistants — Just Announced, Onsale, Newsletter, Countdown, and Post Show — that auto-draft email/SMS campaigns for venues, promoters, and festivals using ticketing data, event schedules, and past campaigns. Final approval and distribution stay with the human marketer, and the assistants learn each organization's brand voice from edit/approval feedback over time. The launch follows Hive's $27M Frontier Growth investment aimed at expanding AI and automation capabilities.

### Key Points
- Five lifecycle-stage AI assistants launched together, each tied to a specific event-marketing moment.
- Assistants draft campaigns only; humans retain final approval and distribution control.
- System learns brand voice and preferences from each organization's edit/approval/rejection history.
- More assistants covering other parts of the event lifecycle are in development.
- Follows Hive's $27M Frontier Growth raise to accelerate AI/automation and integrations.

### Highlights
- Human-in-the-loop design: drafting only, no autonomous sending.
- Learning loop uses approval/edit/rejection feedback to refine future drafts over time.
- Positioned to reduce manual, repetitive marketing-campaign work industrywide.

### Industry Problem
Smaller venue/promoter marketing teams are stretched thin manually producing recurring campaign types (onsale pushes, reminders, newsletters) across a busy event calendar.

### Proposed Solution
Hive's answer is lifecycle-stage AI drafting assistants gated by human approval; a useful benchmark for Etix Assistant's own marketing-automation roadmap (Q14) and worth evaluating for client-facing marketing tooling (Q1).

## Transcript

![image (4)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/image-4.png)

Live events marketing platform Hive announced it is debuting a suite of AI assistants to help festivals, venues and promoters to help develop and manage marketing campaigns.

The assistants will prepare campaigns using upcoming shows, past campaigns and brand preferences, incorporating artist information, event dates, ticket status and relevant links, drawing on ticketing information and audience data. Final approval and distribution remains with the human user.

The assistants learn each organization’s voice from its previous campaigns and ongoing feedback, using past sends to reflect established messaging, tone and brand preferences. As marketers review, edit, approve or decline drafts, that feedback informs subsequent campaigns, helping the assistants become more aligned with each team’s approach over time.

“Live-event marketers know their audiences and their shows. What they often need is more time to get the work done,” said Ian Roberts, CEO and Co-Founder of Hive. “Our Marketing Assistants bring together the event data and brand context already in Hive to prepare campaigns for their teams to review. Marketers stay in control, and their feedback helps shape the next draft.”

At launch, there are five assistants: Just Announced, Onsale, Newsletter, Countdown and Post Show. Each develops campaigns tailored to a specific stage of an event.

Other assistants are being developed to handle other parts of the event life cycle.

The announcement follows Hive’s $27 million investment from Frontier Growth to accelerate AI and automation development, deepen integrations and support global expansion.
