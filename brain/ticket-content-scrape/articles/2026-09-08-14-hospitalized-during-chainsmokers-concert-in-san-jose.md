# 14 Hospitalized During Chainsmokers Concert In San Jose

## Summary

Fourteen people were hospitalized after a Chainsmokers concert at Discovery Park in San Jose, with police citing alcohol and drug intoxication as a factor among the medical emergencies.

### Key Points

- San Jose Fire Department deployed an ambulance task force, two advanced life support units, and a battalion chief starting around 8:30 p.m.
- The concert began at 5:30 p.m.; some attendees were treated on-site before 14 were taken to local hospitals.
- Police did not disclose full details but confirmed multiple cases involved alcohol/drug intoxication.

Fourteen people were taken to the hospital Friday during a concert by The Chainsmokers in San Jose, California.

The electronic duo were performing at the city’s Discovery Park in an event that began at 5:30 p.m.. The San Jose Fire Department said it began responding to calls for service around 8:30. The city’s police department said some people were treated at the park but paramedics took 14 to local hospitals.

The SJFD said it deployed an ambulance task force, two advanced life support units and a battalion chief to the event.

While the fire department would not disclose the nature of the medical emergencies, police said multiple people were treated for alcohol and drug intoxication.
