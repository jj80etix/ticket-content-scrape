# Matt Rife Announces First Stadium Comedy Show With 2027 Ohio Homecoming

## Summary

Comedian Matt Rife announced his first-ever stadium show, "Matt Rife: Homecoming," at Ohio Stadium in Columbus on July 17, 2027, as part of his Stay Golden World Tour. It marks a career milestone performance in his home state, with special guests still to be announced.

### Key Points
- Matt Rife: Homecoming set for July 17, 2027 at Ohio Stadium (Columbus, Ohio), 7 p.m. ET start.
- First-ever stadium performance for Rife, part of the ongoing Stay Golden World Tour.
- Special guests not yet announced.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Matt-Rife-Ohio-Stadium-.webp)

Comedian Matt Rife is preparing for the biggest performance of his career, announcing a special hometown stadium show at Ohio Stadium in Columbus, Ohio, on Saturday, July 17, 2027.

The event, billed as Matt Rife: Homecoming, will mark the comedian’s first-ever stadium performance, bringing his *Stay Golden World Tour* back to his home state for a milestone show at the iconic Ohio State University football venue. The performance is scheduled to begin at 7 p.m. ET and will feature special guests who have yet to be announced.

“This event means everything to me,” Rife said in the [official event announcement](https://www.schottensteincenter.com/events/detail/matt-rife?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). “It surpasses even my wildest dreams that started right here in Columbus. As an Ohio native, The Shoe holds a special place in my heart. To spend a night there making thousands of my hometown people laugh, enjoy themselves, and forget about their problems for an evening will be incredibly special.”

Rife added, “It’s going to be a night unlike any other. Let’s make history and have the time of our lives!”

“Columbus is proud to claim one of the most iconic comedians in the country, and there’s no better way to bring Matt home than host him at the most iconic stadium in the country … Ohio Stadium!” Thomas said.

His remaining 2026 dates include shows at UBS Arena in Elmont, New York, on November 20 and KeyBank Center in Buffalo on November 21, followed by stops in Toronto, Las Vegas, Highland, Phoenix and New Orleans.

## Matt Rife Ohio Stadium Tickets and Presale Information

Tickets for Matt Rife’s July 17, 2027, homecoming show will become available through several presales beginning Monday, November 23, 2026, ahead of the general public onsale.

The Matt Rife Fan Club and Citi cardmember presales begin Monday, November 23, at 10 a.m. ET. Fans interested in participating in the fan club presale must register through [Rife’s official website](https://mattrifeofficial.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) by Sunday, November 22, at 6 p.m. ET.

Additional presales, including artist, venue, Live Nation and Ohio State football season-ticket-holder opportunities, begin Tuesday, November 24, at 10 a.m. ET.

The general public onsale begins Wednesday, November 25, at 10 a.m. ET, with tickets available through [Ticketmaster](https://www.ticketmaster.com/matt-rife-homecoming-with-very-special-columbus-ohio-07-17-2027/event/05006572F58BE1A3?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
