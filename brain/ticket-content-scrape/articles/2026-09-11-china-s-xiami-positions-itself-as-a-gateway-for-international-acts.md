# China’s Xiami positions itself as a ‘gateway’ for international acts

## Summary

China's Xiami Music & Arts Festival is positioning itself as a "gateway" for international acts entering the historically insulated Chinese market, with nearly 40% of this year's fifth-edition lineup — including The Cardigans, Tortoise and The Horrors — coming from abroad. The article is paywalled beyond the opening summary.

### Key Points
- Fifth edition drew a 30,000-capacity audience; ~40% of acts were international.
- Festival frames itself as a market-development bridge for foreign artists in China.
