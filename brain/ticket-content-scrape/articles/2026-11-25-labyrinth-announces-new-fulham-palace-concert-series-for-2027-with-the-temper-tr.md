# Labyrinth Announces New Fulham Palace Concert Series for 2027 With The Temper Trap

## Summary

Independent UK promoter Labyrinth is launching a new outdoor concert series, "Labyrinth at the Palace," at London's historic Fulham Palace starting June 2027, with Australian band The Temper Trap as first headliner. The series adds to Labyrinth's roster of historic-property events and gives Fulham Palace, an independent charity, an added revenue source.

### Key Points
- New series debuts June 2027 at Fulham Palace's main lawn in West London.
- The Temper Trap is the first announced headliner (June 12), also playing Manchester's Albert Hall June 11.
- Expands Labyrinth's portfolio of historic-venue events, alongside Old Royal Naval College and Tofte Manor.
- Presale begins Friday, November 27 at 10 a.m. GMT via Labyrinth's site.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Labyrinth-at-the-Palace.webp)

Independent UK concert promoter Labyrinth is expanding its portfolio of outdoor music events with the launch of Labyrinth at the Palace, a new concert series set to debut at London’s historic Fulham Palace in June 2027.

Australian indie rock band The Temper Trap is the first headliner announced for the series, with a performance scheduled for Saturday, June 12. The band will also perform at Albert Hall in Manchester on June 11 as part of its return to the UK.

The new concert series will take place on the main lawn of Fulham Palace in West London, with additional artists expected to be announced in the coming months.

The addition of Fulham Palace expands Labyrinth’s portfolio of events at historic UK properties, which includes the Old Royal Naval College in Greenwich and Tofte Manor in Bedfordshire.

The partnership will provide an additional source of revenue for Fulham Palace, which operates as an independent charity dedicated to preserving the historic property and maintaining public access.

Fans can register for the ticket presale through [Labyrinth’s official website](https://www.labyrinthevents.com/events/temper-trap-sungazer-ldn?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The presale is scheduled to begin Friday, November 27, at 10 a.m. GMT.

Additional information about the concert series is available on the [Labyrinth at the Palace website](https://www.labyrinthevents.com/at-the-palace?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
