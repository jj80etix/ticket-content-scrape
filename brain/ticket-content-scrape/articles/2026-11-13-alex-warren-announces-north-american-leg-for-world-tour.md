# Alex Warren Announces North American Leg For World Tour

## Summary

Alex Warren announced the North American leg of his "Finding (More) Family On The Road World Tour," a 26-city run starting April 5, 2027 in Oklahoma City and wrapping May 16 in Birmingham, Alabama. Bebe Stockwell and Faouzia will support select dates, with artist presale beginning November 18 and general on-sale November 20.

### Key Points
- 26-city North American tour, April 5 - May 16, 2027.
- Support acts Bebe Stockwell and Faouzia on select dates.
- Artist presale (CO:BRAND) begins Nov. 18; general on-sale begins Nov. 20.

Alex Warren has announced the North American leg of his “Finding (More) Family On The Road World Tour,” with a new 26-city run starting April 5 at Paycom Center in Oklahoma City. Warren will then play New York, Tampa, Raleigh and more before wrapping up in Birmingham, Alabama, at Coca-Cola Amphitheater on May 16. Bebe Stockwell and Faouzia will join as support on select dates

Artist presale registration is available now at CO:BRAND, with the presale beginning Nov. 18 at 10 a.m. local. Additional presales will follow ahead of the general on-sale beginning Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time. For more information, visit: https://www.alexwarrenofficial.com.

Alex Warren is continuing his partnership with PLUS1 throughout the “Finding (More) Family On The Road Tour,” directing $1 from every ticket sold to local children’s hospitals in the communities where he performs. Since launching the partnership in 2025, Alex and his fans have helped generate more than $424,000 for organizations supporting children across North America.

The Finding (More) Family On The Road World Tour announcement caps off an extraordinary year for Warren, having sold out his 2026 arena tour through North America, the UK, Europe, Japan, Singapore and Australia. The tour earned rave reviews, with the Chicago Sun-Times saying, “\[Warren\] digs deep into the human psyche with his folk-pop canon, eloquently putting to song a candid conversation about navigating one of the most universal topics of all: grief.”

The North American leg of the “Finding (More) Family On The Road World Tour” will launch ahead of his EU/UK run beginning June 20 at Cardiff’s Blackweir Fields before stopping in Belfast, Dublin, Glasgow, Exeter and more, followed by two headline shows at Crystal Palace Park Live in London on July 3 and July 4. He will wrap the EU/UK leg with his Portugal debut on July 18 at the Figueira da Foz. See below for all tour and festival dates.
