# Pollstar’s Fifth Annual Impact International: UK/Euro Reception In London Was One To Remember

## Summary

Pollstar's fifth annual Impact International UK/Euro reception was held Nov. 19 at London's Sky Garden, honoring industry figures including keynote guest Neil Warnock MBE of UTA.

### Key Points
- Event capped at ~150 invited guests at Sky Garden Bar 155 meters above London.
- Warnock, UTA's Global Head of Touring, gave a keynote on his career and shared Dolly Parton anecdotes.
- Organized by Pollstar with Oak View Group's events team; attendees called it the best edition yet.

## Transcript

![DSC5557](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/DSC5557-scaled.jpg)

Neil Warnock MBE, co-head of UK Music and Global Head of Touring at UTA, takes part in a keynote conversation during Pollstar’s annual Impact International: UK/Euro reception in London. He’s joined on stage by Pollstar ‘s UK and European editor Gideon Gottfried.

*Pollstar’s* annual Impact International UK/Euro reception celebrated its fifth edition Thursday, Nov. 19, at London’s Sky Garden Bar 155 meters above the city.

The spectacular venue turned out to be a perfect fit for this reception, which once again celebrated some of this industry’s best, boldest and brightest.

Guest arrived promptly at 7 p.m., making their way up to the 35th floor via the elevators, and taking the final steps up to level 36 on foot – via west-facing steps that offered views of some of London’s landmarks: the City of London, its Temple district and St. Paul’s Cathedral, all lit up even brighter than usual due to the many Christmas decorations all around town.

At the top, guests were welcomed with Champagne and quickly began mingling, networking and meeting old and new friends. *Pollstar* ‘s Director, International Business Development & Partnerships Brij Gosai officially kicked off the night just after 7.30 p.m., highlighting the fact that 2026 was a mini-anniversary for the Impact International reception, which turned five, and how far it has come since its humble beginnings in the small upstairs room at the legendary Ronnie Scott’s Jazz Club in 2022.

He then handed over the mic to *Pollstar* ‘s UK and European editor Gideon Gottfried, who inducted this year’s honorees, calling each of them up on stage to receive their personal *Pollstar* cover edition and have their picture taken in front of a screen highlighting all of this year’s honorees.

**[See: Pollstar’s 2026 Impact International: UK/Euro Honors](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/11/16/pollstars-2026-impact-international-uk-euro-honors/)**

The last honoree to come on stage was Neil Warnock MBE, co-head of UK Music and Global Head of Touring at UTA. He remained on stage for a short but sweet keynote conversation, which has become a tradition at this event.

Warnock talked about his love for the agent’s job; shared a couple of anecdotes from working with the late Dolly Parton, one of his long-time clients; explained how the agent’s role, and live entertainment in general, has changed since his early days; and shared some wisdom for the next generation of agents coming through. A real highlight of the night, as was to be expected given Warnock’s incredible legacy.

With the official ceremony part ending around 8 p.m. the bar remained open until late, with final guests having to be gently ushered out by staff just after midnight.

The venue looked spectacular thanks to the meticulous planning and execution of Erin Grady, Vice President, Global Events & Conferences, Oak View Group, and her team. *Pollstar* COO Jon Guynn and Brendan Buglione, Senior Director, Global Partnerships, OVG Media & Conferences, were also on site from the U.S., contributing in various ways to making the fifth annual *Pollstar* Impact International UK/Euro reception one to remember.

The magic of this event lies in its size. Deliberately limited to a capacity of around 150 invited guests, there’s a real opportunity to catch up, make introductions, talk shop and share lots of hugs without it all feeling rushed or too formal.

Based on the feedback from guests on the night, it was the best edition yet. A hugely motivating verdict, and a reason for the team to keep working hard on it – as well as this business to save the date in their calendars for next year.
