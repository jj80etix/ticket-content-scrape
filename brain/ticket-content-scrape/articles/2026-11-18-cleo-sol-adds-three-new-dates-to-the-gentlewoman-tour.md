# Cleo Sol Adds Three New Dates To ‘The Gentlewoman Tour’

## Summary

Cleo Sol has added three new dates to her 2027 "Gentlewoman Tour" due to demand, including debut shows in Boston, Detroit, and Houston. The Live Nation-promoted tour kicks off April 13 at Madison Square Garden, following her sold-out three-night Radio City Music Hall run that grossed over $1.68 million.

### Key Points
- Three new 2027 "Gentlewoman Tour" dates added: Boston (Apr 14), Detroit (Apr 23), Houston (Apr 26) — all market debuts.
- Tickets go on sale Nov. 20 at 9 a.m. local via Ticketmaster.
- Tour opens April 13 at Madison Square Garden; promoted by Live Nation.
- Addition follows a sold-out three-night Radio City Music Hall stand grossing $1,682,151 per Pollstar Boxoffice Reports.

CleoSol 2026 Added Dates

Due to popular demand, Cleo Sol has added three new dates to her 2027 “Gentlewoman Tour.” The newly added shows will find the soul singer traveling to MGM Music Hall at Fenway in Boston on April 14, the Fox Theatre in Detroit on April 23, and the Smart Financial Center at Sugar Land in Houston — making her debut in all three markets.

Tickets will be available starting Nov. 20 at 9 a.m. local time at ticketmaster.com.

Live Nation is promoting the “Gentlewoman Tour,” slated to kick off on April 13 at the world-famous Madison Square Garden in New York City. Sol will then travel to Baltimore, Toronto, Chicago, Atlanta, Los Angeles and Oakland.

The announcement follows Cleo Sol’s sold-out three-night stand at Radio City Music Hall last March, which grossed $1,682,151, per Pollstar Boxoffice Reports.
