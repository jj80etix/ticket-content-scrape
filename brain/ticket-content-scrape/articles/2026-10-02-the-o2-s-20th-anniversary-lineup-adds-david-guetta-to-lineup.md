# The O2’s 20th Anniversary Lineup Adds David Guetta to Lineup

## Summary

London's The O2 added David Guetta and Maisie Peters to its growing 20th-anniversary concert series lineup for 2027, with Guetta's June 5 "Monolith" show billed as his only indoor UK and only London appearance that year. Tickets for Guetta's show go on general sale October 2 through the venue's official ticketing partner, AXS.

### Key Points
- Guetta plays The O2 June 5, 2027, in his only indoor UK/London date that year; Peters headlines May 8.
- AEG's anniversary series also adds Shreya Ghoshal, five sombr dates, Billie Jean King/John McEnroe, Don Omar, and Jorja Smith.
- General on-sale for Guetta's show is October 2 via AXS, following an O2/Virgin Media priority presale.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/David-Guetta-The-O2.webp)

David Guetta will bring his Monolith show to London’s The O2 on June 5, 2027, joining Maisie Peters in a growing lineup celebrating the venue’s 20th anniversary.

The AEG-owned venue will launch its anniversary concert series in May, with programming continuing throughout the summer. Plans include multiday residencies, exclusive performances and special one-night shows featuring established performers and emerging headliners.

Presented by Labyrinth Events, Guetta’s performance is billed as his only indoor U.K. concert and only London appearance in 2027. The booking follows his European Monolith dates, including performances in Germany, Belgium and Portugal and three stadium shows at Stade de France in Paris.

“London has always been a very special city for me, so playing my biggest show there to date means a lot,” Guetta said.

Peters, the first artist announced for the anniversary program, will play her debut headline concert at The O2 on May 8, 2027. Her appearance places a first-time headliner at the beginning of a series designed to recognize the venue’s history while introducing its next generation of arena performers.

“Being the first artist announced for The O2’s 20th birthday celebration is honestly such an honour,” Peters said.

The anniversary schedule has expanded beyond the two artists. AEG’s latest announcement also lists Shreya Ghoshal on June 4, followed by five sombr performances on June 21, 22, 24, 25 and 29. Billie Jean King and John McEnroe are scheduled for June 27, with Don Omar appearing July 5 and Jorja Smith on July 17.
