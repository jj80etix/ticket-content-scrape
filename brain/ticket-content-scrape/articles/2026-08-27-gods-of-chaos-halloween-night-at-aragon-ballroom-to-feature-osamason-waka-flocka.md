# Gods of Chaos: Halloween Night At Aragon Ballroom To Feature OsamaSon, Waka Flocka Flame

## Summary

Chicago's Aragon Ballroom announced "Gods of Chaos," a Halloween night show featuring OsamaSon and Waka Flocka Flame.

### Key Points
- Halloween night event at Aragon Ballroom (Chicago) billed "Gods of Chaos."
- Lineup includes OsamaSon and Waka Flocka Flame.

Described as a “high adrenaline rush of rage, horror, metal and hip-hop,” Gods of Chaos returns to Chicago on Halloween night.

Taking place at the Aragon Ballroom in the Windy City, the event is also to include Speed, 2Slimey, Smokey, Buckshot, Big Ass Truck, Warlord Colossus, Private School, Boltcutter and Chamber.

Tickets are on sale now, starting at $53.25 standing room only, with VIP options available at $117.75. The show is 17 and over, with doors opening at 4 p.m. and performance beginning at 5.

The show is promoted by Live Nation.

The 2025 Gods of Chaos Xmas at the Aragon Ballroom featured Insane Clown Posse, Gucci Mane, Harm’s Way, Trash Talk, Haywire and Kent Osborne, while last Halloween’s [Gods of Chaos was topped by Three 6 Mafia,](https://news.pollstar.com/2025/07/28/three-6-mafia-tops-halloween-gods-of-chaos-festival-at-chicagos-aragon-ballroom/) also including Pouya, Project Pat, 1900RugRat, Lik Wyte and others, also at the Aragon Ballroom in Chicago.
