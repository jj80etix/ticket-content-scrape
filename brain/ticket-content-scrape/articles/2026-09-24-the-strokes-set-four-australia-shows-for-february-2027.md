# The Strokes Set Four Australia Shows for February 2027

## Summary

The Strokes will play four Australian shows in February 2027 — Brisbane Entertainment Centre (Feb 13), two nights at Melbourne's Rod Laver Arena (Feb 16-17), and headlining the new Sydney Summertime series at Centennial Park (Feb 20) — supporting their Rick Rubin-produced album Reality Awaits. Courtney Barnett and Promiseland open all four dates, with Beck added for the Sydney show. General on-sale is September 29.

### Key Points
- Dates: Brisbane (Feb 13), Melbourne x2 (Feb 16-17), Sydney/Centennial Park (Feb 20).
- First Australian headline shows since 2022.
- Supports 7th studio album Reality Awaits (produced by Rick Rubin).
- Presales begin Sept 28; general on-sale Sept 29, 1pm local.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/The-Strokes-Australia-.webp)

The Strokes will return to Australia in February 2027 for four performances in Brisbane, Melbourne and Sydney.

The dates extend the band’s “Reality Awaits” tour, and is scheduled to begin February 13 at Brisbane Entertainment Centre. The band will then play two nights at Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena on February 16 and 17 before closing February 20 at Centennial Park as the headliner of the inaugural Sydney Summertime concert.

Courtney Barnett and Promiseland will join The Strokes at all four shows. Beck is also on the Sydney bill, giving the new outdoor concert series a four-act opening lineup. Promiseland is the project of Johann Rashid, who is signed to Strokes frontman Julian Casablancas’ Cult Records.

The Brisbane and Melbourne dates will be The Strokes’ first Australian headline shows since 2022. The band appeared in the country more recently at Adelaide’s Harvest Rock festival in 2025, but that performance was exclusive to the event.
