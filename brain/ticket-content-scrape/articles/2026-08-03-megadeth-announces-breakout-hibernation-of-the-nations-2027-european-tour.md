# Megadeth Announces ‘Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations’ 2027 European Tour

## Summary

Megadeth have announced "Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations," a 24-date European leg of their multi-year global farewell tour, running March 9 to April 13, 2027 from Belfast to Lisbon. Black Label Society and Testament join for the full run, and the tour follows Megadeth's first-ever No. 1 album on the Billboard 200. Tickets go on sale in three phases starting Aug. 4.

### Key Points
- 24 arena dates across the UK and mainland Europe, March 9, 2027 (Belfast) to April 13, 2027 (Lisbon).
- Full tour support from Black Label Society and Testament.
- Cyber Army fanclub presale Aug. 4, artist presale Aug. 5, general on-sale Aug. 7 (all 10 a.m. local).
- Tour supports Megadeth's 17th and final studio album, its first to top the Billboard 200.
- 2026 European boxoffice comps cited: Hannover sellout (41,181 tickets, $4.68M) and Valencia (9,092 tickets, $725,707).
- Band has logged 440 headline boxoffice reports since 1999, totaling 1.7M tickets and $91.5M grossed.

Grammy Award-winning thrash metal pioneers Megadeth have announced “Breakout: Hibernation Of The Nations,” a European chapter of their multi-year global farewell headline tour.

Kicking off March 9, 2027 in Belfast, Northern Ireland, the 24-date arena run will bring Megadeth to fans across the United Kingdom and mainland Europe before concluding April 13 in Lisbon, Portugal.

Joining Megadeth for the entire tour are fellow metal titans Black Label Society and Testament, creating a solid heavy metal touring package.

The tour follows the success of Megadeth’s seventeenth and final studio album, which debuted at number one on the Billboard 200 — the band’s first-ever chart-topping album — and reaffirmed their place as one of heavy metal’s most enduring and influential forces.

The European leg will visit 12 of the 22 global markets where the album reached the Top 5, underscoring Megadeth’s continued international appeal.

Rather than a single farewell run, the band’s final journey has been designed as a multi-year, multi-continent celebration, giving fans around the world several opportunities to experience Megadeth live.

Tickets for the band’s fanclub, the Cyber Army, go on sale Tuesday, Aug. 4 at 10 a.m. local time; the artist presale launches Wednesday, Aug. 5 at 10 a.m. local, and the general sale kicks off Friday, Aug. 7, at 10 a.m.

Megadeth have only just been in Europe. Pollstar Boxoffice highlights from this year’s visit to the continent include a sold-out Heinz von Heiden Arena in Hannover, Germany, June 2, where the band moved 41,181 tickets, grossing $4,683,899; a May 25 performance at Roig Arena in Valencia, Spain (9,092 tickets, $725,707 grossed).

Megadeth is managed by CTK Enterprises’ Danny Nozell, Steve Ross, Kyle McClain and Justis Mustaine, and booked by UTA’s Neil Warnock. With 440 headline reports submitted to Pollstar’s Boxoffice dating back to 1999, the band has sold a total of 1.7 million tickets, grossing $91.5 million.
