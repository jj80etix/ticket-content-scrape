# Moby, Underworld Set November Show at Brookside at the Rose Bowl

## Summary

Moby and Underworld will headline a one-day electronic music show at Brookside at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena on Saturday, Nov. 7, promoted by Goldenvoice. Fcukers, Nia Archives, and DJ Holographic round out the bill.

### Key Points
- Show date: Saturday, Nov. 7, at Brookside at the Rose Bowl, Pasadena, CA.
- Lineup: Moby (headline), Underworld, plus Fcukers, Nia Archives (DJ set), and DJ Holographic.
- Presale begins Wednesday, Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. PT; general on-sale Thursday, Aug. 27 at 10 a.m. PT through AXS.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/moby-underworld-1200x675.webp)

Moby will return to Southern California this fall for a headline performance at Brookside at the Rose Bowl, with Underworld joining him for a one-day electronic music bill in Pasadena.

The Goldenvoice-promoted show is scheduled for Saturday, Nov. 7, with Fcukers, Nia Archives performing a DJ set and DJ Holographic also appearing.

Tickets go on sale to the general public Thursday, Aug. 27 at 10 a.m. PT through AXS. A presale begins Wednesday, Aug. 26 at 10 a.m. PT.

The concert will take place on the open-air, grass-covered golf course adjacent to Rose Bowl Stadium. AXS lists the event as all standing-room and all ages, with the venue opening and music beginning at 4 p.m. The show will take place rain or shine.

Goldenvoice says the event will use a single stage, meaning there will be no overlapping sets between the five artists on the bill.

The Pasadena announcement gives Moby another California headline appearance following his return to Coachella earlier this year, where he played a full-band set in the Mojave tent that drew heavily from his catalog, including “Porcelain,” “Natural Blues,” “Why Does My Heart Feel So Bad?” and “Extreme Ways.”

Moby is also scheduled to perform at San Francisco’s Bill Graham Civic Auditorium on Thursday, Nov. 5, two days before the Pasadena event. That 18-and-over show is currently listed through Ticketmaster.

The California dates come during a relatively active year of live performances for Moby, who has spent much of 2026 playing festival and European dates. He is also scheduled to appear at Sea.Hear.Now in Asbury Park, New Jersey, on Sept. 20.

Moby released his latest studio album, *Future Quiet*, earlier this year. The project includes a new version of “When It’s Cold I’d Like to Die” featuring Jacob Lusk of Gabriels, revisiting a song originally released on Moby’s 1995 album *Everything Is Wrong*.

His Pasadena co-headliner Underworld adds another major electronic act to the bill. The British duo of Karl Hyde and Rick Smith have remained a fixture of electronic and dance music for decades, with “Born Slippy.NUXX” among their most recognizable recordings.

Fcukers, Nia Archives and Detroit-based DJ Holographic round out a lineup that extends across several generations of electronic and dance music.

Brookside at the Rose Bowl has increasingly served as a large-format outdoor concert and festival site separate from the neighboring Rose Bowl Stadium itself. Goldenvoice holds an exclusive booking arrangement at the venue and has used the property for events including Cruel World and Just Like Heaven.

Parking for the Nov. 7 Moby show is scheduled to open at 3 p.m., with the event running under an 11 p.m. curfew. Tickets and additional event information are available through AXS and MobyLivePasadena.com.
