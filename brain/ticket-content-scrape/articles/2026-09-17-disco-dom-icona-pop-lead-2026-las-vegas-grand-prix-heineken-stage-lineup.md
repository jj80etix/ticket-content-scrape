# Disco Dom, Icona Pop Lead 2026 Las Vegas Grand Prix Heineken Stage Lineup

## Summary

The 2026 Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix (Nov. 19-21) will feature Disco Dom and Icona Pop headlining its Heineken Stage, alongside LP Giobbi, Eli & Fur, and other acts, plus live Q&As with all 22 F1 drivers.

### Key Points
- Heineken Stage runs during the Nov. 19-21 race weekend in the East Harmon Zone.
- Headliners: Disco Dom (Saturday, Nov. 21) and Icona Pop, alongside LP Giobbi and Eli & Fur.
- All 22 F1 drivers scheduled for live Q&A sessions on the stage.
- Also features the "Now You See Me Live" illusion production and other variety acts.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Las-Vegas-Grand-Prix-Heineken-Stage.webp)

Disco Dom and Icona Pop will headline the Heineken Stage during the 2026 Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix, adding another slate of live music and entertainment to the Nov. 19-21 race weekend.

The Heineken Stage, located in the East Harmon Zone, will also feature sets from LP Giobbi and Eli & Fur, along with performances from The Australian Bee Gees Show, PACK Drumline, Super Cr3w and the illusion production *Now You See Me Live*. All 22 Formula 1 drivers are also scheduled to appear on the stage for live Q&A sessions throughout the weekend.

Disco Dom, the disco-focused project from French DJ and producer Dombresky, will perform Saturday, November 21, with LP Giobbi also appearing that night.

Icona Pop will deliver a DJ set Friday, November 20. The Swedish duo, best known for “I Love It,” will be joined by London electronic duo Eli & Fur and *Now You See Me Live*, the stage illusion production scheduled to begin its Las Vegas run in October.

Thursday’s entertainment will include The Australian Bee Gees Show, PACK Drumline and Las Vegas dance crew Super Cr3w.

“The Heineken Stage brings together two of the things fans love most about Formula 1: world-class entertainment and access to the sport’s biggest stars,” Las Vegas Grand Prix Vice President of Marketing Austin Brown said in announcing the lineup. “Guests can enjoy an incredible lineup of live performances while also getting an up-close opportunity to hear from all 22 drivers.”

Formula 1 has increasingly positioned live entertainment as a significant part of the Las Vegas Grand Prix experience. The 2025 Heineken Stage featured Shaggy and Dillon Francis, while other stages across the circuit hosted Louis Tomlinson, Kane Brown, Kaskade, Steve Aoki, T-Pain and Zedd.

The 2026 weekend will also extend beyond the circuit. Backstreet Boys are scheduled to perform the official Formula 1 Las Vegas Grand Prix Afterparty at Sphere on Saturday, Nov. 21, following an earlier sold-out run at the venue. That event is being sold separately from standard race admission.

The 2026 Formula 1 Heineken Las Vegas Grand Prix runs November 19 to 21 on the Las Vegas Strip Circuit.
