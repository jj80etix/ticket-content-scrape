# Jason Isbell And The 400 Unit & Drive-By Truckers Announce ‘Summer of Joy Tour’

## Summary

Jason Isbell and the 400 Unit, joined by Drive-By Truckers, announced the 39-date "Summer of Joy Tour" for 2027 (May 6 Nashville - Sept. 7 Tulsa), reuniting Isbell with the band he played in from 2001-2007. Presale begins Nov. 18 through Nov. 19, with general onsale Nov. 20; $1 per ticket benefits musician mental-health nonprofit Nuçi's Space.

### Key Points
- 39-date tour booked by WME's Andrew Colvin, May 6-Sept. 7, 2027.
- Reunites Isbell with Drive-By Truckers following informal reunions at ShoalsFest and a 2014 benefit.
- Stops include Red Rocks, Forest Hills Stadium, and the Greek Theatre.
- $1/ticket benefits Nuçi's Space; presale Nov. 18-19, general onsale Nov. 20.

After wrapping his annual residency at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium and another successful edition of his ShoalsFest in Alabama, Jason Isbell has announced plans for 2027 with dates set for the “Summer of Joy Tour,” with his band, the 400 Unit, along with Drive-By Truckers.

The six-time Grammy winner, who was a member of Drive-By Truckers’ lineup from 2001 through 2007, reunited with the Southern rock band last month at ShoalsFest, where they performed their 2003 album, *Decoration Day*.

Isbell is continuing to support his 2025 album, Foxes in the Snow, which he released on his own Southeastern Records. An announcement from his team notes that the album’s “stripped back, bare-bones format put his immense talent for evocative storytelling and the complete mastery of his craft on full display.”

The “Summer of Joy Tour” starts May 6 with a show at Nashville’s The Pinnacle and runs through a Sep. 7 gig at Tulsa, Oklahoma’s Cain’s Ballroom. Stops on the tour include iconic venues such as Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado; Forest Hills Stadium in Forret Hills, New York; and The Greek Theatre in Los Angeles.

Fans can sign up now for access to a pre-sale that begins Wednesday, Nov. 18th at 10 a.m. local time through Thursday, Nov. 19 at 10 p.m. local time. The general onsale starts Friday, Nov. 20 at 10 a.m. local time.

A dollar from every ticket sold on the tour will go to Nuçi’s Space, a nonprofit based in Athens, Georgia, with a mission to prevent suicide and a focus on musicians. The nonprofit has a health and resource center for musicians and offers programs and services in Health & Wellness, Musicians Services, and Youth Support.  
  
Isbell’s tour dates are booked by WME’s Andrew Colvin.
