# Brooks & Dunn, Eric Church and Ashley McBryde to Headline CMC Rocks QLD’s 20th Anniversary

## Summary

Brooks & Dunn, Eric Church and Ashley McBryde will headline CMC Rocks QLD's 20th-anniversary festival in Willowbank, Queensland, March 19-21, 2027, with support from Kameron Marlowe, Parker McCollum, Turnpike Troubadours and others. It's Brooks & Dunn's first Australian trip in nearly 20 years and Church's Australian debut. Tickets go on sale September 25 via AXS.

### Key Points
- Anniversary edition runs March 19-21, 2027, at Willowbank; camping opens March 17-18.
- Lineup also includes Kaylee Bell, Lane Pittman, Rachael Fahim and other AU/NZ acts.
- 2026 edition drew 25,000+ attendees, generating an estimated $13-15M for Ipswich businesses.
- General on-sale September 25, 10am AEST via AXS.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/CMC-Rocks-QLDs-20th-Anniversary.webp)

Brooks & Dunn, Eric Church and Ashley McBryde will headline CMC Rocks QLD in March 2027 as the Australian country music festival celebrates its 20th anniversary.

The lineup also features Kameron Marlowe, Parker McCollum, Turnpike Troubadours, Blake Whiten, 49 Winchester, Randall King and Lanie Gardner. Kaylee Bell, Lane Pittman, Rachael Fahim and other Australian and New Zealand artists are among the performers.

Brooks & Dunn will make their CMC Rocks QLD debut on their first trip to Australia in nearly 20 years, according to organizers. Festival director and Chugg Entertainment chairman Michael Chugg said the booking has a connection to the festival’s history.

“Brooks & Dunn were one of the first US acts that our festival founder, the late Rob Potts, ever toured in Australia almost twenty years ago, so it feels fitting to have them headline CMC Rocks’ 20 Year bash in 2027,” Chugg said. “We have lots in store for next year, it will be a mega celebration — our biggest and most ambitious festival yet.”

The festival will also bring back its Songwriters Show, an acoustic session in which four artists share songs and stories. CMC Rocks began in Thredbo in 2008 and later moved through the Hunter Valley and Townsville before establishing its current home in Willowbank. Ipswich Mayor Teresa Harding said more than 25,000 people traveled to Ipswich for the 2026 festival, generating an estimated $13 million to $15 million for local businesses and tourism operators.

“CMC Rocks has become one of Ipswich’s signature events, and we are thrilled to welcome some of the biggest names in country music and thousands of visitors back to our city,” Harding said.

Storme Warren, Mike Carr and D.J. Grizzly Adams will serve as stage hosts for the 2027 festival.

The main festival runs from March 19 to 21 in Willowbank, Queensland. The site opens to campers on March 17 and 18. Tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, September 25, at 10 a.m. AEST through AXS.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [cmcrocks.com](https://www.cmcrocks.com/).
