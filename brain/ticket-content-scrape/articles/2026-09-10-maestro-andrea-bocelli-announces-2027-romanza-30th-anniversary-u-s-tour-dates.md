# Maestro Andrea Bocelli Announces 2027 Romanza 30th Anniversary U.S. Tour Dates

## Summary

Andrea Bocelli announced 2027 U.S. tour dates marking the 30th anniversary of his "Romanza" album, part of an ongoing Romanza Anniversary tour cycle.

### Key Points
- Tour marks 30 years since the release of "Romanza."
- New dates cover multiple U.S. cities in 2027.
- Continues Bocelli's pattern of anniversary-themed touring.

## Transcript

![bocelli](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/bocelli.jpg)

Iconic Italian tenor Maestro Andrea Bocelli announced new U.S. dates for his “2027 Romanza 30 <sup>th</sup> Anniversary World Tour,” which celebrates his acclaimed 1997 breakthrough album *Romanza*.

The tour, in partnership with global wealth management and investment banking firm Stifel, will feature many of his famed songs live, including timeless songs like “Con Te Partirò.”

Produced by AEG Presents, the tour includes nine show dates in major cities across the U.S., including Charlotte, Baltimore, Atlanta, Austin, Dallas, Houston, Orlando and Hollywood, Florida. The tour kicks off Jan. 29 at Spectrum Center in Charlotte, North Carolina, and concludes with two performances at Hard Rock Live in South Florida on Feb. 12-13. All shows will be conducted by Maestro Steven Mercurio.

General tickets go on sale Sept. 18 at 10:00 AM local time. Ticket presales will begin on Sept. 15 at 10:00 AM local time for American Express Card Members, followed by an Andrea Bocelli social fan presale on Sept. 16 at 10:00 AM local time.

American Express is providing eligible card members access to Amex Presale Tickets, available for purchase beginning Sept. 15 at 10:00 AM local time through Sept. 17 at 10:00 PM local time, before the general public on sale, while supplies last. Terms apply.

The announcement follows a busy year for Bocelli as he celebrates the 30th anniversary of *Romanza*. Originally released in 1997, *Romanza* launched Bocelli onto the global stage, selling nearly 20 million copies worldwide and becoming one of the best-selling contemporary classical albums of all time.

In celebration of the anniversary, Bocelli will release two new collections: R *omanza (30 <sup>th</sup> Anniversary)* due out Sept. 25 via Sugar/UMe and *Rarities: Collectors Edition*, due out Nov. 20 via Decca. The collection brings together all 16 tracks from the original album, including global hits “Time to Say Goodbye” with Sarah Brightman and “Vivo per Lei” featuring Giorgia, as well as a previously unreleased extended version of “Con te Partirò.” The limited-edition box set of *Rarities* brings together three previously unreleased albums featuring personal recordings released for the first time. Pre-orders are now available.

The new dates were announced as Bocelli is in the midst of his “Romanza 30th Anniversary World Tour*,”* which began earlier this week in Seattle and continues across North America this fall. Upcoming performances include his return to Madison Square Garden for two nights in New York City, the Hollywood Bowl in Los Angeles, Bell Centre in Montreal, TD Garden in Boston and his debut at the iconic Red Rocks Amphitheatre in Morrison, Colorado.

Bocelli has sold nearly 90 million records and amassed more than 25 billion streams since rising to fame after winning the 1994 Sanremo Music Festival. Renowned for his interpretations of major operatic works under legendary conductors including Lorin Maazel, Seiji Ozawa and Zubin Mehta, he has earned six Grammy and six Latin Grammy nominations and received a star on the Hollywood Walk of Fame.

Bocelli has performed for four U.S. Presidents, four Popes, the British Royal Family and at major global events around the world, including the 2026 FIFA World Cup Final Draw, the Opening Ceremony of the Milan-Cortina 2026 Winter Olympic Games, and FIFA World Cup Opening Ceremonies in Mexico City, where he and EJAE delivered a performance of “DNA,” the official FIFA World Cup 2026 anthem.
