# The Red Clay Strays Extend ‘Grateful Tour’

## Summary

The Red Clay Strays extended their "Grateful Tour" into 2027 with new arena and amphitheater dates across the U.S. and Canada, running January through September.

### Key Points
- New leg runs Jan. 22-Sept. 19, 2027, opening in Raleigh and closing in Austin.
- Includes three Canadian dates (Saskatoon, Edmonton, Winnipeg) in March.
- Multiple presale tiers begin Oct. 5-6; general onsale Oct. 9.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/The-Red-Clay-Strays-Grateful.webp)

The Red Clay Strays have announced a new round of North American dates for their “Grateful Tour,” extending the trek into 2027 with arena and amphitheater performances across the United States and Canada.

The new leg begins January 22 at Lenovo Center in Raleigh, North Carolina, and concludes September 19 at Moody Center in Austin, Texas. Avery Anna, The Bends and St. Paul & The Broken Bones will join the band on select dates.

The winter itinerary includes Allstate Arena in Rosemont, Illinois, on January 28, Enterprise Center in St. Louis on January 29 and CHI Health Center in Omaha, Nebraska, on January 31. February stops include Cleveland, Indianapolis, Charleston, West Virginia, Estero, Florida, and Jacksonville, Florida.

Canadian performances are scheduled for Saskatoon on March 11, Edmonton on March 12 and Winnipeg on March 14, with The Bends opening those shows. The band will then return to the United States for concerts in Uncasville, Connecticut, and Allentown, Pennsylvania, before the Mobile engagement.

The summer schedule begins July 23 at Maine Savings Amphitheater in Bangor, Maine. Additional stops include BankNH Pavilion in Gilford, New Hampshire, CMAC in Canandaigua, New York, and Pine Knob Music Theatre in Clarkston, Michigan.

August brings performances at the Greek theaters in Berkeley and Los Angeles, along with concerts in Fresno, Tucson, Bend, Boise and the Denver area. September dates include Walmart AMP in Rogers, Arkansas, The Orion Amphitheater in Huntsville, Alabama, and Paycom Center in Oklahoma City before the tour reaches Corpus Christi and Austin.

Ticket presales begin Monday, October 5. The OG Strays fan club presale opens at 10 a.m. local time, followed by the Verified Strays presale at 1 p.m. local time. A presale for Red Clay Strays app users begins Tuesday, October 6, at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales start Friday, October 9, at 10 a.m. local time.

The announcement arrives while the band continues its 2026 tour. Upcoming performances include two-night engagements at Dickies Arena in Fort Worth, Texas, on October 7 and 8 and Bridgestone Arena in Nashville on October 22 and 23. The current year’s schedule runs through December 5 at MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [redclaystrays.com](http://redclaystrays.com).
