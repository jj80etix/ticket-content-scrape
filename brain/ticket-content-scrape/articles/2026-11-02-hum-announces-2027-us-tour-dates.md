# HUM Announces 2027 US Tour Dates

## Summary

HUM announced a 12-date 2027 U.S. tour beginning March 3 in Atlanta and closing July 17 in San Francisco, marking their first tour run since returning to the stage in 2026.

### Key Points
- The Life and Times support on all dates; Pelican and Spotlights added on East/West Coast dates respectively.
- Tickets available via Ticketmaster.
- Band has a history of strong regional draws, including an $18,750-grossing sellout at St. Louis's Delmar Hall.

## Transcript

![HUM by Chris Green](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/HUM-by-Chris-Green-scaled.jpg)

HUM by Chris Green

HUM announced a 2027 United States tour, set to begin March 3 in Atlanta at The Eastern. The tour will run for 12 dates, concluding July 17 at The Warfield in San Francisco. The US tour will mark the band’s first run of shows since their return to the stage in 2026. See full tour routing below.

HUM will be joined by Kansas City’s The Life and Times on all dates, with additional support from Pelican and Spotlights on East and West Coast dates, respectively.

Fans can grab tickets [here](https://www.ticketmaster.com/hum-tickets/artist/775086).

HUM has a history of successful tours and shows, with the band’s career including a sold-out show at Delmar Hall in St. Louis, MO, which grossed $18,750 that night, per [Pollstar Boxoffice Reports](https://pollstar.com/tour-histories/artists/31636).
