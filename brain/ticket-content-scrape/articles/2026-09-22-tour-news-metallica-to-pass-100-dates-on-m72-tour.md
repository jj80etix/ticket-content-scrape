# Tour News: Metallica to pass 100 dates on M72 Tour

## Summary

IQ Magazine's tour-news roundup leads with Metallica extending its M72 World Tour with 12 new North American stadium dates between early May and mid-June 2027, following two sold-out London Stadium shows. Limp Bizkit and Avatar will support the newly announced leg, which pushes the tour past 100 total dates. The roundup also references upcoming announcements from Calvin Harris, Kenny Chesney, and Hans Zimmer.

### Key Points
- Metallica adds 12 North American stadium dates (May-June 2027) to the M72 World Tour, extending it past 100 shows.
- Stops include Vancouver, El Paso, San Diego, San Antonio, Fayetteville, Kansas City, Raleigh, Indianapolis, Buffalo, Madison, Cleveland, and Salt Lake City.
- Limp Bizkit and Avatar will support the new North American leg.
- The tour most recently played two nights at London Stadium.
