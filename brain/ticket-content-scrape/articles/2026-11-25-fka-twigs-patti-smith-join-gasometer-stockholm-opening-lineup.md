# FKA twigs, Patti Smith Join Gasometer Stockholm Opening Lineup

## Summary

FKA twigs and Patti Smith will perform their own music in new orchestral arrangements as part of ALEPH, the opening production of Gasometer, a new cultural venue in Stockholm opening March 2027 inside a converted gasholder.

### Key Points
- Gasometer opens March 2027 in Stockholm's Gasworks district, featuring two performance spaces for concerts, opera, and other formats.
- ALEPH, the opening production, features new orchestral arrangements of FKA twigs' and Patti Smith's music by composer Jacob Mühlrad.
- Venue is housed in Gasholder 2, designed by Swedish architect Ferdinand Boberg.

## Transcript

![Gasometer 1 Alessandro Moggi lo res](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Gasometer-1-Alessandro-Moggi-lo-res.jpeg)

The Gasholder 2 located in the Gasworks district of Stockholm, designed by Swedish architect Ferdinand Boberg. The building will open as the Gasometer in March 2027, featuring two performance spaces for concerts, opera, performing arts, live productions, galas, conferences, exhibitions and other large cultural and event formats. Picture by Alessandro Moggi

FKA twigs and Patti Smith will perform their own music in new orchestral arrangements by Jacob Mühlrad, heard for the first time in ALEPH, the opening production of Gasometer, Stockholm’s new cultural landmark.

Mühlrad has composed, arranged and curated the music of ALEPH, which he has created together with visual artist and scenographer Alexander Wessely. ALEPH runs for four nights, March 17-20, 2027.

ALEPH will also feature the premiere of a new work by Marina Abramović, for which “Mühlrad’s music is the connecting thread and Wessely shapes the visual world,” according to the press announcement of the opening lineup. ALEPH will also feature the work of Sir Wayne McGregor, Resident Choreographer of the UK’s Royal Ballet, and acclaimed British artist Celeste.

Conceived in four acts, ALEPH brings together classical and contemporary music, performance, movement and visual art. The production will mirror the journey of Gasometer itself, from industrial to cultural powerhouse.

In addition as the Royal Swedish Opera makes Gasometer its home for the next five years, Alan Gilbert, Music Director of the Royal Swedish Opera, will conduct the Royal Swedish Orchestra, Chorus and Soloists whilst percussionist Adélaïde Ferrière, vocalist Matilda Sterby and clarinetist Martin Fröst also join the line-up.

Gasometer was originally built to hold the gas that powered a city for a century. When the industrial fire died, it stood as a silent monolith. ALEPH, created by Mühlrad and Wessely is its “resurrection, a transformation where industrial power returns as music, spatial light and movement,” the press announcement continues.
