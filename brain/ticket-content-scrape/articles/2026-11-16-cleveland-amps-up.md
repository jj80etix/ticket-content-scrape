# Cleveland Amps Up

## Summary

Dan Gilbert's Bedrock broke ground Nov. 10 on a new 6,200-capacity outdoor amphitheater on Cleveland's Cuyahoga riverfront, part of a $3.5B, 35-acre redevelopment. Live Nation will operate and book the venue, which is being designed by DLR Group with Live Nation's Blueprint Studio and is slated to open summer 2028.

### Key Points
- 6,200-cap amphitheater on ~4.1 acres, developed by Bedrock with Rock Entertainment Group and Live Nation; opening targeted for summer 2028.
- Live Nation will operate the venue and handle booking; design by DLR Group with Live Nation's Blueprint Studio.
- Projected at nearly $59M in annual economic impact; construction cost undisclosed.
- Groundbreaking drew Cleveland Cavaliers/Rock Entertainment leadership, Live Nation execs, the mayor, county executive, and Ohio's governor.

## Transcript

![Current image: BedrockCleveland01 Aerial (1)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/BedrockCleveland01-Aerial-1-1024x576.jpg)

Rendering of planned Cleveland amphitheater courtesy DLR Group

The Rock and Roll Capital of the World broke ground on a new outdoor performance space.

The new Cleveland Riverfront Amphitheater, being developed by Dan Gilbert’s Bedrock, is a 6,200-capacity outdoor music venue planned along the Cuyahoga River in downtown Cleveland. The venue is part of Bedrock’s ambitious $3.5 billion, approximately 35-acre riverfront redevelopment project.

Groundbreaking took place Nov. 10 bringing together an influential group of sports, entertainment, business, and civic leaders, including Nic Barlage, CEO of Rock Entertainment Group and the Cleveland Cavaliers; Jared Fleisher, CEO of Bedrock; executive leadership from Live Nation; Mayor Justin M. Bibb; Cuyahoga County Executive Chris Ronayne; and Governor Mike DeWine.

The venue is expected to open in summer 2028. It will be built on approximately 4.1 acres adjacent to the Cleveland Cavaliers’ new Cleveland Clinic Global Peak Performance Center, a sports performance and medical facility. The venue is being developed in partnership with Rock Entertainment Group – Gilbert’s sports and entertainment organization that oversees the Cleveland Cavaliers, Cleveland Monsters and other sports and entertainment properties – along with Live Nation, which will operate the venue and handle booking.

The architectural design is being led by DLR Group, with support from Live Nation’s Blueprint Studio. Plans call for two levels of seating, a canopy covering the lower seating bowl, VIP hospitality areas, outdoor dining and drinking spaces and dedicated artist and production facilities at a waterfront location with views of the Cuyahoga River and Cleveland’s skyline.

The amphitheater alone is projected to generate nearly $59 million in annual economic impact, although its individual construction cost has not been publicly disclosed.
