# Ultra Music Festival Unveils Initial 2027 Lineup: Calvin Harris, Martin Garrix, Alison Wonderland, Steve Aoki, SOFI TUKKER & More

## Summary

Pollstar's version of the Ultra Music Festival 2027 lineup announcement, covering the same Calvin Harris/Martin Garrix/Alison Wonderland/Steve Aoki/SOFI TUKKER-led bill as reported elsewhere.

### Key Points
- Initial 2027 Ultra Music Festival lineup confirmed.
- Duplicate-topic lineup announcement; no ticketing/pricing/policy angle.

## Transcript

![ultra](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ultra.png)

The Phase 1 lineup is here for Ultra Music Festival 2027, promising to deliver the best in electronic music “with a genre-crossing roster of superstar headliners, debut performances and special collaborations.”

The 27th edition of Ultra Music Festival takes place at Miami’s Bayfront Park March 26-28.

The lineup boasts AFROJACK’s debut live set at Ultra Miami, as well as debuts from DJ Pee.Wee (aka Anderson.Paak), German producer BUNT. and rising house artist it’s murph.

Other highlights include Calvin Harris’ first return to Ultra Miami since 2024, along with Martin Garrix, Dom Dolla, Armin van Buuren and Steve Aoki. Today’s announcement adds that the lineup also boasts “festival veterans like bass innovator Liquid Stranger, electronic pioneer Boys Noize, German techno artist Klangkuenstler, and masked hard dance enigma HOLY PRIEST.”

Confirmed B2B pairings include the world debut of Marshmello B2B Subtronics and the U.S. debut of Boris Brejcha B2B Eric Prydz Presents Cirez D. Alison Wonderland and Tiffany Day will also team up for their first-ever festival B2B performance.

Plus, Ultra’s underground brand RESISTANCE will host debut Ultra Miami stage takeovers from Mau P’s Baddest Behaviour and Adam Beyer’s Drumcode, as well as the U.S. debut of Nico Moreno B2B NOVAH. Also of note, organizers have booked the U.S. festival debut of Miss Monique Presents BIORHYTHM, described as “an immersive audiovisual show exploring the relationship between technology and nature.”

SOFI TUKKER fans will be pleased to hear that the group has signed on for “the first-ever curated Live Stage takeover in Ultra Miami history, helmed by the festival debut of the duo’s live show, SOFI TUKKER Presents Animal Talk.”

Tickets are on sale now.  
  
Visit [https://ultramusicfestival.com/](https://ultramusicfestival.com/) for more information.
