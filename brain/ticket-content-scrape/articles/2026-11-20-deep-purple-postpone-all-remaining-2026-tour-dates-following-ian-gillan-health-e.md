# Deep Purple Postpone All Remaining 2026 Tour Dates Following Ian Gillan Health Emergency

## Summary

Deep Purple postponed its six remaining December 2026 South American and Mexican tour dates as singer Ian Gillan continues recovering from a health emergency that already halted the band's European/UK leg.

### Key Points
- Gillan was hospitalized in Leipzig on Oct. 31; a subsequent procedure in early November was reported successful.
- Affected shows were in Brazil, Chile, Argentina and Mexico; no replacement dates announced yet.
- Some postponed European dates have been rescheduled to February 2027.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Deep-Purple.webp)

Deep Purple has postponed its six remaining 2026 concerts in South America and Mexico as singer Ian Gillan continues to recover from a medical emergency that already forced the band to halt its European and U.K. tour.

The band confirmed the additional postponements Thursday, Nov. 19, ending uncertainty over a December run that had remained on the schedule after the initial European announcement. The affected concerts were planned for Brazil, Chile and Argentina, followed by three dates in Mexico.

In a [statement published on Deep Purple’s website](https://deeppurple.com/blogs/news/important-announcement-from-ig?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), Gillan said, “Regrettably, these postponements will also need to extend to the South American and Mexico shows we have booked this year.” He apologized for the disruption and said he expects to return to touring early next year. The band’s team, he added, expects to announce replacement dates for early 2027 soon.

The update follows a medical incident in Leipzig, Germany, that required Gillan to receive urgent hospital attention on Oct. 31. Deep Purple initially postponed concerts in Leipzig and Hamburg before suspending the rest of its European and U.K. itinerary. Those developments were detailed in [TicketNews’ earlier coverage](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/deep-purple-ian-gillan-tour-postponed/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), which noted that the status of the December Latin American shows remained unconfirmed.

Gillan, 81, subsequently reported that a medical procedure in early November had been successful, but said he needed time to rest and regain his strength. Neither the singer nor the band has publicly identified the underlying medical condition. The new announcement did not provide additional information about his health or a confirmed date for his return to the stage.

As of Nov. 20, the original December dates were still displayed on Deep Purple’s online tour calendar, even though Gillan’s newer statement makes clear the shows will not take place as scheduled. No replacement dates for the six Latin American concerts have been announced.

## New Dates and Ticket Refunds

Several postponed European concerts now appear on the band’s calendar for February 2027, with the next listed performance scheduled for Feb. 2 at Arkéa Arena in Bordeaux, France. Other February listings include Strasbourg, Frankfurt, Berlin, Hamburg, Amsterdam, Dortmund, Lyon, Nantes and Nuremberg. These listings reflect the current schedule, not a new medical update or a guarantee that touring will resume on a particular date.

Ticket arrangements are being handled at the event level. For the Dec. 5 São Paulo show, [Suhai Music Hall says](https://suhaimusichall.com.br/en/evento/deep-purple/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) previously purchased tickets will remain valid for the rescheduled performance without an exchange. Fans who do not want to attend the new date can request a refund through [Eventim](https://www.eventim.com.br/artist/deep-purple/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) from Nov. 20 through Dec. 7, 2026.

The São Paulo notice says eligible refunds will include service fees and other applicable charges, with processing times dependent on the original payment method. Requests submitted after Dec. 7 will not be accepted under that event’s published policy.

Ticket holders for the concerts in Chile, Argentina and Mexico should consult the original seller or local promoter for event-specific instructions. The São Paulo refund deadline should not be assumed to apply to those performances.
