# Maluma Announces ‘Loco X Volver World Tour 2027’

## Summary

Maluma announced the "Loco X Volver World Tour 2027," spanning more than 60 cities globally and produced by Fenix Entertainment and Cardenas Marketing Network. The tour opens February 5, 2027 in Bogotá and will feature a new stage concept built around his recently released album *Loco X Volver*.

### Key Points
- Tour opens Feb. 5, 2027 in Bogotá, Colombia; spans 60+ cities worldwide.
- Produced by Fenix Entertainment and Cardenas Marketing Network (CMN).
- Stops include Buenos Aires, Mexico City, Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, Madrid, Paris, and Berlin.
- New stage/visual production built around the *Loco X Volver* album, released in May.

Maluma has announced the “Loco X Volver World Tour 2027,” which will take him to more than 60 cities worldwide. Produced globally by Fenix Entertainment and Cardenas Marketing Network (CMN), the tour will open Feb. 5 in Bogotá, Colombia, and include stops in Buenos Aires, Mexico City, Santiago, Lima, Miami, Los Angeles, Toronto, Madrid, Paris, Berlin, and Rabat, among others.

With a new stage concept and a visual production created especially for this tour, Maluma is preparing a show that will change the way he presents his music live and connects with his audience.

The tour takes its name from *Loco X Volver*, the album Maluma released in May, which marks a new artistic era. The show will bring the album’s songs and creative world to the stage, alongside the biggest hits of his career.
