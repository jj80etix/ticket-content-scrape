# KATSEYE Add 2027 Dates In Asia, Australia & New Zealand

## Summary

KATSEYE extended its "Wildworld Tour" into 2027 with new Asia, Australia and New Zealand dates following sold-out shows across Europe, the UK and North America. The leg kicks off January 27 in Yokohama and includes staggered presales for EYEKONS members, artist fans and the general public.

### Key Points
- New leg runs Jan. 27 (Yokohama Arena) through stops in Manila, Hong Kong, Seoul, Bangkok, Melbourne and Auckland
- Weverse/EYEKONS presale access begins Nov. 4 for Australia/NZ; most Asian-market presales begin Nov. 10-12
- General onsale for Australia/NZ begins Nov. 6; most Asian markets general onsale Nov. 13
- VIP experience packages also being offered for the new dates

KATSEYE is extending its “Wildworld Tour” into 2027 with dates across Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.

The group recently performed sold-out shows in Europe, the U.K., and North America, with shows running through Nov. 28.

The new leg of the tour kicks off Jan. 27 at Yokohama Arena in Japan, and the routing includes stops at Manila’s SM Mall of Asia Arena, AsiaWorld-Arena in Hong Kong, Seoul’s KSPO Dome, IMPACT Arena in Bangkok, Melbourne’s Rod Laver Arena and Spark Arena in Auckland. See full routing below.

For the shows in Australia and New Zealand, paid EYEKONS members will have access to Weverse beginning Nov. 4. Fans can sign up for membership at [Weverse](https://shop.weverse.io/en/shop/USD/artists/206/sales/63466) and must [apply for the presale](https://weverse.io/katseye/notice/40429) by Nov. 1, 11:59 p.m. NZDT. Fans can also sign up for the artist presale on Nov. 5, and the general onsale begins Nov. 6.

For most Asian markets, the Weverse presale begins Nov. 10, and the KATSEYE.World presale is Nov. 12, followed by the general onsale on Nov. 13.

For more information, visit KATSEYE’s [tour sign-up page](https://link.fans/katseye-toursign-up).

VIP experiences will also be available for purchase via KATSEYE Weverse Shop during the general onsale.

The pop group saw great success during its recent U.K. and European run, including a stop at Co-op Live in Manchester that grossed $1,981,991 from 17,589 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

KATSEYE is booked worldwide by THE·TEAM.
