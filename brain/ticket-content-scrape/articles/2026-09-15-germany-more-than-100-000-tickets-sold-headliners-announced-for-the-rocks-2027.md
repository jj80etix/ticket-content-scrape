# Germany: More Than 100,000 Tickets Sold, Headliners Announced For The Rocks 2027

## Summary

Germany's Rock am Ring and Rock im Park festivals have already sold more than 100,000 weekend tickets for their 2027 editions (June 4-6), with blink-182, Die Toten Hosen, and Bring Me The Horizon leading the shared lineup announced today.

### Key Points
- Over 60,000 tickets sold for Rock am Ring (Nürburgring) and 40,000+ for Rock im Park (Nuremberg) so far.
- Full weekend tickets: €269 ($310) for Rock am Ring; €358 ($414) with camping for Rock im Park.
- Die Toten Hosen return for their 10th Rock am Ring and 9th Rock im Park appearance.
- Follows two consecutive sold-out editions, including 2025's anniversary shows.

Following two consecutive sold-out editions, including for the big anniversaries in 2025, ticket sales for Rock am Ring and Rock im Park 2027 are once again reaching record-breaking levels: more than 100,000 weekend tickets had already been sold at the time of this writing, over 60,000 tickets for Rock am Ring, and more than 40,000 for Rock im Park.

“Demand for Germany’s biggest festivals has never been stronger,” conclude promoters PRK DreamHaus, eventimpresents, and FKP Scorpio in a joint press release announcing the 2027 lineup today.

Rock am Ring takes place at the Nürburgring in Germany’s Eifel area, while Rock im Park takes over the Zeppelinfeld in Nuremberg. The dates are June 4-6, 2027.

The twin festivals largely share the same lineup, which, in 2027, will be led by blink-182, Die Toten Hosen and Bring Me The Horizon. Today’s announcement also features Faith No More, Parkway Drive, Billy Talent, Evanescence, Turnstile, Falling In Reverse, The Prodigy and more. Full alphabetical breakdown of the currently confirmed lineup below.

Full weekend tickets for Rock am Ring are currently on sale for €269 ($310), excluding camping. A Rock im Park weekend ticket including camping costs €358 ($414). A limited number of Day Tickets for Rock im Park also go on sale today. while Rock am Ring doesn’t offer single-day tickets.

Ticket upgrades, premium camping, and VIP options for both festivals are also available.

The three headliners for next year’s festivals span three generations. When Die Toten Hosen return in 2027 for their tenth Rock am Ring and ninth Rock im Park  
appearance, they’ll bring songs from their final studio album of their career, Trink aus, wir müssen gehen (Drink up, it’s time to leave).

Hosen frontman Campino commented, “Rock am Ring and Rock im Park have been an integral part of Die Toten Hosen’s history ever since we were first invited to play there in the 1990s. We’ve fought legendary battles there, celebrated wild parties, torn ligaments, made friends and woken up in other people’s beds and tents more than once. The two twin festivals hold a special place in our hearts, and we’re incredibly happy to be stopping by once more as we head into the home stretch.”

Matt Schwarz, promoter of Rock am Ring and Rock im Park: “After two completely sold-out festival years, seeing more than 100,000 people already put their trust in us for 2027 fills us with tremendous gratitude. Rock am Ring and Rock im Park are defined by their unique community. Together with our fans and these extraordinary artists, we want to write the next chapter in the history of Rock am Ring and Rock im Park in 2027.”
