# Usher, Chris Brown Cancel Four US Stadium Dates

## Summary

Usher and Chris Brown canceled four upcoming stadium dates on their Live Nation-promoted "R&B Tour" to protect the health of the touring party and crew, affecting shows in Baltimore, Columbia, Columbia, and Indianapolis. The cancellations come days before Brown is due in a UK court for sentencing on an affray plea tied to a 2023 London nightclub incident.

### Key Points
- Four stadium dates canceled: Baltimore (Oct. 17), Columbia SC (Oct. 24 and Oct. 29), Indianapolis (Oct. 31).
- Promoter cites health and well-being of touring party and crew.
- Tour has grossed $280.86M across 36 shows as of Oct. 3, per Pollstar Boxoffice.
- Cancellations precede Chris Brown's Oct. 25 UK sentencing hearing for an affray plea.

## Transcript

![GettyImages 2290105418](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/GettyImages-2290105418-scaled.jpg)

Usher and Chris Brown recently announced the cancellation of four dates of their “R&B Tour” to “prioritize the health and well-being of the touring party and crew,” according to a message from the event organizer.

Those who purchased tickets for upcoming shows at Baltimore’s MT&T Bank Stadium (Oct. 17), Paycor Stadium in Columbia (Oct. 24), Columbia’s Williams-Brice Stadium (Oct. 29) and Lucas Oil Stadium in Indianapolis (Oct. 31) were informed of the cancellations last Friday and received a statement from the tour organizer. “The R&B Tour” is promoted by Live Nation.

“Following an extensive run of sold-out stadium shows on one of the year’s biggest tours, the event promoter has made the difficult decision to cancel select upcoming dates on the R&B Tour to prioritize the health and well-being of the touring party and crew,” the message read, according to [a post on X](https://x.com/TB_Vanguard/status/2108766993620910313). “The demands of a tour of this length and scale are significant, and these adjustments will allow the tour to continue safely and sustainably through the remaining performances.”

The cancellations happen to be announced before Brown is scheduled to appear before a U.K. court. The global star pleaded guilty to affray in July following a London nightclub attack in 2023. The artist is awaiting sentencing and has a court date set for Oct. 25.

“The R&B Tour” has been a massive hit for the artists and Live Nation, with the trek grossing a whopping $280.86 million across 36 shows through Oct. 3, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.
