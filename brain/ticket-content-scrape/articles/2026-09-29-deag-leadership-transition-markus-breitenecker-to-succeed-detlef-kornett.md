# DEAG Leadership Transition: Markus Breitenecker To Succeed Detlef Kornett

## Summary

DEAG (Deutsche Entertainment AG) announced that media executive Markus Breitenecker will join its Executive Board November 1, 2026 and succeed CEO Detlef Kornett, who departs at year-end after 13 years leading the company's international expansion. Breitenecker will also acquire a shareholding in DEAG as part of the transition.

### Key Points
- Breitenecker (57), formerly of ProSieben/Sat1/Puls 4 Austria, becomes chairman of DEAG's Executive Board Nov. 1, 2026.
- Kornett leaves at contract expiry end of 2026, having served as Co-CEO since 2023 and Chairman since 2024.
- Under Kornett, DEAG's overseas business grew to more than 50% of consolidated turnover; UK revenue reached ~€200M annually.
- FY2025 revenue hit a record ~€490M ($573M); expansion included MC2 Live acquisition (Italy, 2024) and Get Rock Live launch (Spain, 2023).
- DEAG frames the move as focusing on further international expansion and strengthening market position.

Deutsche Entertainment AG (DEAG) is setting the course for its next phase of growth: Experienced media executive Markus Breitenecker (57) will succeed DEAG CEO Detlef Kornett, and join DEAG’s Executive Board on Nov. 1, 2026. Breitenecker will simultaneously acquire a shareholding in the company. Kornett will leave the company at the end of 2026 upon expiry of his contract, following thirteen successful years on DEAG’s Executive Board.

During this period, Detlef Kornett played a decisive role in shaping DEAG’s development, serving as Co-CEO since 2023 and as Chairman of the Executive Board since 2024. That’s also the year in which [DEAG founder Prof. Peter Schwenkow handed over the management of the company to Kornett](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/03/28/after-46-years-at-the-helm-schwenkow-hands-over-deag-management/).

Under Kornett’s leadership, the DEAG Group significantly expanded its international presence and further strengthened its market position. DEAG’s overseas business now accounts for more than 50% of consolidated turnover. Under Kornett’s leadership, the UK developed into DEAG’s second home market, generating some €200 million annually.

In financial year 2025, the Group generated revenue of approximately €490 million ($573 million at the time), [the highest in its corporate history](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/04/24/deag-reports-record-revenue-earnings-in-financial-year-2025/). Kornett also oversaw DEAG’s expansion into Italy, through [the acquisition of MC2 Live in 2024](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/10/30/the-lineup-of-events-is-impressive-deag-ceo-detlef-kornett-on-acquiring-mc%C2%B2-live-entering-italy-growing-strategically/), and Spain through [the launch of Get Rock Live in collaboration with experienced Spanish live promoter Pierre Sabbag](https://news.pollstar.com/2023/12/13/get-rock-live-deag-launches-in-spain/).

DEAG’s spoken work division, a focus under Kornett’s leadership, has become a key pillar of the group’s business. “DEAG’s next phase of development will build on this strong foundation,” according to DEAG’s press release announcing the leadership transition.

Kornett, whose lifelong love for live music was sparked by German Krautrock band Guru Guru when he was 12 years old, is a [2024 Pollstar Impact International honoree](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/11/13/impact-international-uk-euro-2024-honoree-detlef-kornett/).

With Markus Breitenecker, DEAG gains “a highly experienced media and entertainment executive with extensive expertise in building and developing new business areas,” the announcement continues.

Among other roles, Breitenecker served on the Executive Board of ProSiebenSat.1 Media SE, where he acted as Chief Operating Officer (COO). Together with the other members of DEAG’s Executive Board, he will focus in particular on driving the Group’s continued international growth and further expanding its market position across existing and new business areas.
