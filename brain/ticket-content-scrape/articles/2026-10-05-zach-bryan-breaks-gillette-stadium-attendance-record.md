# Zach Bryan Breaks Gillette Stadium Attendance Record

## Summary

Zach Bryan drew a record 73,538 fans to Gillette Stadium on Oct. 3, the largest crowd for any event in the venue's history, during a two-night stand on his "With Heaven On Tour."

### Key Points
- Record set during Bryan's third and fourth career Gillette Stadium appearances.
- Comes about a year after his 112,408-attendee show at Michigan Stadium, the current U.S. ticketed-concert attendance record.
- Tour wraps Oct. 10 at Jordan-Hare Stadium, Auburn, AL.

## Transcript

![Zach Bryan performing at Gillette Stadium | Photo courtesy of AEG Presents](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Zach-Bryan-Gillette-Stadium.webp)

Zach Bryan drew 73,538 fans to Gillette Stadium on October 3, setting a record for the largest crowd at any event in the Foxborough, Massachusetts, venue’s history.

The performance closed a two-night stand at the stadium during his With Heaven On Tour. Bryan performed October 2 and 3 with special guests Gregory Alan Isakov and Gabriella Rose. The concerts marked his third and fourth career appearances at the stadium, which serves as home to the New England Patriots and New England Revolution.

The Gillette record comes just over a year after Bryan reached another attendance milestone at Michigan Stadium in Ann Arbor.

On September 27, 2025, Bryan became the [first artist to headline a concert at the venue](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/09/zach-bryan-sets-u-s-concert-record-with-michigan-stadium-show/), drawing 112,408 fans. The performance established the U.S. record for the largest ticketed concert audience at the time, surpassing George Strait’s June 2024 show at Kyle Field in College Station, Texas.

The Foxborough stop is part of Bryan’s largest tour to date, spanning more than 40 dates across North America and Europe. The trek began in March 2026, following a series of sold-out stadium performances in 2025.

Bryan is set to wrap up his With Heaven On Tour on October 10 at Jordan-Hare Stadium in Auburn, Alabama.
