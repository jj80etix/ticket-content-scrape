# Hans Zimmer Announces 2027 North American ‘The Next Level’ Tour

## Summary

Hans Zimmer will bring "Hans Zimmer Live — The Next Level" to North America for a 30-date arena tour (Feb. 17-April 11, 2027), pairing orchestral and electronic elements with a 19-piece ensemble and a newly designed stage show.

### Key Points
- 30-date North American arena tour, Feb. 17 (Hartford)-April 11, 2027 (Portland).
- Performs with a 19-piece ensemble; production blends orchestral and electronic elements.
- Zimmer describes the goal as making fans "feel like they're at a rave party or riding a roller coaster."
- Same tour as covered separately by Pollstar; this is the TicketNews report.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Hans-Zimmer.webp)

Hans Zimmer will bring his “Hans Zimmer Live — The Next Level” production to North America in 2027 for a 30-date arena tour across the United States and Canada.

The run will begin February 17 at PeoplesBank Arena in Hartford, Connecticut. It will continue for nearly two months before concluding April 11 at Moda Center in Portland, Oregon.

Zimmer will perform alongside a 19-piece ensemble throughout the tour. The production combines orchestral arrangements with electronic music, lighting effects and a newly designed stage presentation intended to expand upon his previous live shows.

“I want fans to feel like they’re at a rave party or riding a roller coaster,” Zimmer said of the concert experience.

The composer added that each performance represents a new opportunity for discovery and said the production was built around moving beyond established expectations.

The opening stretch will include performances at UBS Arena in New York, Prudential Center in Newark, Xfinity Mobile Arena in Philadelphia and TD Garden in Boston. Zimmer will then travel to Canada for concerts in Ottawa, Quebec City and Hamilton.

March dates include Detroit, Chicago, Cincinnati, Charlotte, Orlando, Miami, Tampa, Atlanta, Nashville, Kansas City, Oklahoma City, Fort Worth, Denver, Salt Lake City and Phoenix.

The final portion begins April 1 at Crypto.com Arena in Los Angeles. Additional California stops are scheduled for Anaheim, San Francisco and Sacramento. Zimmer will then perform at Rogers Arena in Vancouver and Climate Pledge Arena in Seattle before closing the tour in Portland.
