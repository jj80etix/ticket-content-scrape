# Ticketplus prices IPO at $8 per share

## Summary

Chile-headquartered ticketing platform Ticketplus priced its IPO at $8/share, raising $15m gross, with shares beginning trading on NYSE American under "TP" on August 7. The company runs a full-stack ticketing platform across 11 Latin American countries plus a US HQ in Miami.

### Key Points
- IPO: 1,875,000 ordinary shares at $8, ~$15m gross proceeds; underwriters hold a 45-day option on 281,250 additional shares.
- Trading began August 7 on NYSE American (ticker: TP); offering expected to close ~August 10.
- Founded 2014; operates in Chile, Argentina, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Mexico, Paraguay, Peru, Uruguay, and the US (Miami HQ opened last year).

### Highlights
- First publicly-traded Latin American ticketing platform of its kind to list on a major US exchange in this cycle — a market-structure signal for the region.
- $15m raise is modest in size but establishes public-market visibility for a full-stack regional competitor.

### Industry Problem
Latin America's ticketing market has been fragmented and under-capitalized relative to US/European primary platforms; going public gives a regional full-stack player a capital-raising path to scale infrastructure and potentially compete for cross-border and enterprise deals.

### Proposed Solution
No direct product move to respond to — this is a capital-markets event, not a feature launch. Worth monitoring Ticketplus's use of IPO proceeds (geographic expansion, M&A) as a leading indicator of competitive pressure in LatAm markets Etix may target.

**Santiago, Chile-headquartered Ticketplus has revealed the pricing of its initial public offering (IPO) of 1,875,000 ordinary shares.**

The shares will be priced at $8 (£6/€7) for a total of $15m of gross proceeds to the company, before deducting underwriting discounts, commissions and other offering expenses.

Ticketplus also granted a 45-day option to the underwriters to purchase up to 281,250 additional ordinary shares at the IPO offering price, minus underwriting discount and commissions.

The company’s ordinary shares are expected to begin trading on NYSE American today (August 7) under the symbol TP. The offering is expected to close on or around August 10, subject to customary closing conditions.

Founded in 2014, Ticketplus operates a proprietary, full-stack technology platform powering live entertainment across Latin America. It has a footprint in 11 countries including Chile, Argentina, Colombia, Costa Rica, Dominican Republic, Ecuador, Mexico, Paraguay, Peru, the United States, and Uruguay.

Ticketplus officially launched its US headquarters in Miami last year.
