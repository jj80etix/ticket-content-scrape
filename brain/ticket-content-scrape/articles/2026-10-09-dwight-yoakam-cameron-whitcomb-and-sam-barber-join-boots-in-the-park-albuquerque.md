# Dwight Yoakam, Cameron Whitcomb and Sam Barber Join Boots In The Park Albuquerque

## Summary

Dwight Yoakam, Cameron Whitcomb and Sam Barber join the completed daily lineups for Boots In The Park in Albuquerque, a three-day country festival (May 14–16, 2027) headlined by George Strait, Jason Aldean and Zach Top.

### Key Points
- Festival runs May 14–16, 2027 at Balloon Fiesta Park, following a September reveal of the headliners.
- Yoakam closes out Sunday alongside Strait and Brooks & Dunn; Whitcomb plays Saturday with Aldean; Barber plays Friday's opening day with Top.
- Daily bills now stand at six acts Friday, six Saturday, five Sunday.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Boots-in-the-Park.webp)

Dwight Yoakam, Cameron Whitcomb and Sam Barber are among the newly announced performers joining Boots In The Park in Albuquerque, New Mexico, next spring.

The additions complete the festival’s daily lineup for May 14 to 16, 2027, at Balloon Fiesta Park, where George Strait, Jason Aldean and Zach Top will headline the expanded three-day event.

Corey Kent, Zach John King and Braxton Keith have also joined the bill, alongside Avery Anna, Carter Faith, Drake Milligan, John Morgan, Ian Harrison and Ashley Monroe. The full announcement follows [September’s initial reveal](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/george-strait-to-headline-three-day-boots-in-the-park-new-mexico-in-2027/), which established Strait, Aldean, Top, Brooks & Dunn and Jon Pardi as the festival’s leading acts.

Yoakam will perform Sunday, May 16, joining a closing-day lineup led by Strait and featuring Brooks & Dunn. Kent and Monroe are also scheduled that day, rounding out a five-act bill. The grouping puts three of the festival’s veteran acts on the same program, with Strait’s set bringing the Albuquerque event to a close.

Whitcomb and King will appear Saturday, May 15, when Aldean headlines. Morgan and Harrison have also been added to that day’s schedule, joining previously announced performer Pardi. Saturday’s six-act lineup places four of the newly confirmed artists ahead of the evening’s established names.

Barber and Keith are scheduled for Friday, May 14, alongside Anna, Faith and Milligan. Top will headline the opening day. With the latest additions, the announced daily bills include six performers on Friday, six on Saturday and five on Sunday, giving attendees the artist breakdown for each date well ahead of the festival’s return.

For more information and additional details, festivalgoers can visit [bootsinthepark.com](http://bootsinthepark.com).
