# On The Steps Announces 2026 Sydney Opera House Lineup

## Summary

The Live Nation Australia-produced "On The Steps" concert series returns to the Sydney Opera House Forecourt Dec. 1-12, 2026, its fifth consecutive year, with nine shows featuring Rise Against, James Blake, DMA'S, Wolf Alice, Dizzee Rascal, Spacey Jane, and more.

### Key Points
- Fifth annual "On The Steps" series at Sydney Opera House Forecourt, Dec. 1-12, 2026.
- Nine shows in the run; lineup includes Rise Against, James Blake, DMA'S, Wolf Alice, Dizzee Rascal, and Spacey Jane.
- Produced by Live Nation Australia; Rise Against opens the series Dec. 1 alongside Dropkick Murphys.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/On-The-Steps-Concert-Series-.webp)

The On The Steps concert series will return to the Sydney Opera House Forecourt this December with a lineup featuring Rise Against, James Blake, DMA’S, Wolf Alice, Dizzee Rascal, Spacey Jane and more.

The Live Nation Australia series will take place from December 1 through December 12, with nine shows scheduled during the run. The 2026 season marks the fifth consecutive year of On The Steps at the Sydney Opera House.

Rise Against will open the series on December 1 with a performance alongside Dropkick Murphys. James Blake will follow on December 2.

DMA’S will perform on December 3 with support from Automatic. Wolf Alice will headline the December 4 show, joined by Folk Bitch Trio and Swapmeet. Dizzee Rascal will headline on December 5. The lineup for that show also includes Example, ArrDee and Swaglord Savannah.

Country singer-songwriter Dylan Gossett is scheduled to perform December 6. Spacey Jane will then take the stage for two shows on December 10 and 11, with Telenova and Armlock supporting. The Amity Affliction will close out the announced lineup on December 12.

Additional tickets for the series were released after the NSW Government increased the outdoor event capacity for major concerts at the Sydney Opera House Forecourt. The new capacity allows major concerts to host up to 7,000 people.

The increase has also made additional tickets available for Spacey Jane’s two shows, which had previously sold out.

The capacity change comes as Sydney continues to expand its options for large-scale outdoor live music. The Sydney Opera House Forecourt will host more fans during the 2026 On The Steps season as a result of the new limit.

“On The Steps is always a privilege to deliver, and it’s incredible that in our fifth year we’re able to welcome more fans than ever to each show,” said Mike Ross, promoter at Live Nation Australia. “We’re proud of this first release of acts playing the series, and are working on more shows to be announced in coming months.”

On The Steps will also continue its fundraising efforts for Support Act, the Australian music industry charity. The organization provides crisis relief, mental health and wellbeing support to musicians, managers, crew members, music workers and organizations across the Australian music industry.

The series will support Support Act’s Aus Music T-Shirt Day campaign during October and November. Further information about the fundraising initiative is expected to be announced.

Live Nation expects more than 50,000 people to attend On The Steps performances during the 2026 season. Additional shows and artists are expected to be announced in the coming months.
