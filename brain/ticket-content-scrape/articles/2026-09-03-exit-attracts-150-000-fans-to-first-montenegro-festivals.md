# EXIT attracts 150,000 fans to first Montenegro festivals

## Summary

EXIT festival's first international events in Montenegro — free festivals Long Beach (Ulcinj, July) and Sea Dance (Budva, August) — drew 150,000 attendees from over 100 countries, part of the festival's world-tour expansion and a bid to position Montenegro as a tourist destination.

### Key Points
- Lineup included Charlotte de Witte, HUGEL, John Newman, Boris Brejcha, Argy, Stephan Bodzin, and Eurovision winner Dara.
- Over 120 local Montenegrin artists were platformed alongside international names.
- Events were free to attend, hosted as part of EXIT's continuing world-tour series.
