# Jack Johnson, Brandi Carlile Headlining 2027 Zootown Festival

## Summary

Brandi Carlile and Jack Johnson will headline the third annual Zootown Music Festival, June 18–19, 2027, at the Missoula Fairgrounds in Montana, with more than 20 artists including Alabama Shakes, Sierra Ferrell and Courtney Barnett. "First Cast" tickets sold out without a lineup announcement; presale begins Oct. 30, with layaway plans available for as little as 10% down.

### Key Points
- Zootown Music Festival's third edition runs June 18–19, 2027, in Missoula, MT.
- Headliners Brandi Carlile and Jack Johnson (Johnson's Montana debut); 20+ artist lineup includes Alabama Shakes, Sierra Ferrell, Courtney Barnett.
- "First Cast" tickets sold out pre-lineup; general presale Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. MT, with layaway plans (as little as 10% down).
- Produced by Switchback Presents, Knitting Factory Presents and Shore Sound Entertainment.
- Festival weekend overlaps with Missoula Pride (June 18–20).

## Transcript

![image](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/image-1.jpeg)

Brandi Carlile and Jack Johnson will be headlining the 2027 edition of Zootown Music Festival next summer in Missoula, Montana.

Johnson will be making his Montana debut at the third edition of the festival, which takes place June 18 and 19 at the Missoula Fairgrounds. There will be more than 20 artists performing at the two-day event, including Caamp, Alabama Shakes, Sierra Ferrell, Whiskey Myers, Courtney Barnett, Kitchen Dwellers and many more.

Zootown has established itself as a premier destination festival, drawing fans from every state and giving them the opportunity to experience what the area has to offer, including biking, fishing, whitewater rafting, trail rides, hikes and more. Details about the festival and excursions are available on the event website, [ZootownFestival.com](https://zootownfestival.com/).

After a successful 2026 edition, “First Cast” tickets for next year’s edition sold out quickly despite no lineup announcement. Presale for single- and two-day passes begins Oct. 30 at 10 a.m. MT at [ZootownFestival.com](https://zootownfestival.com/). A limited number of tickets will be available for in-person purchase at Rockin Rudy’s at a special locals-only rate.

General onsale begins Oct. 30 at noon MT, and layaway plans will be available for all tickets with as little as 10% down.

Switchback Presents, Knitting Factory Presents, and Shore Sound Entertainment produce Zootown Festival. Past editions have featured The Lumineers, The Chicks, Tedeschi Trucks Band, Rainbow Kitten Surprise, Hozier, Kacey Musgraves and more.

The festival also kicks off Missoula’s Pride weekend celebrations, which run June 18-20. For more information about Pride events, visit [MissoulaPride.com](https://www.missoulapride.com/).
