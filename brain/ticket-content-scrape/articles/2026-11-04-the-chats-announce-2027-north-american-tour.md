# The Chats Announce 2027 North American Tour

## Summary

Pollstar reports The Chats' newly announced 30-date 2027 North American tour (April 7-May 18), part of a packed year of touring that also includes a 17-date Australian run this November/December and previously announced UK/European dates in fall 2027. Full tour routing, including Australian, North American, and European legs, is detailed in the report.

### Key Points
- North American leg: April 7 (Berkeley, CA) through May 18 (West Hollywood, CA), 30 dates.
- Australian tour: 17 dates Nov.-Dec. 2026 across Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Tasmania, Adelaide, and Perth.
- UK/Europe leg: September-October 2027, including three nights at London's Electric Ballroom.

The Chats are bringing a 2027 30-date headlining tour to North America, kicking off April 7 in Berkeley, California, at the UC Theatre, and wrapping up May 18 in West Hollywood at the Roxy Theatre. See full tour routing below.

The North American dates are part of an extensive year of touring that will also see The Chats return to the UK and Europe in September and October 2027, with stops in Helsinki, Berlin, Paris, Amsterdam, Dublin, Manchester and three nights at London’s Electric Ballroom, among others. Before the year is out, the band will also head out on a 17-date Australian tour this November and December, playing intimate venues across the country ahead of *Half Cut’s* release.

Tickets are available [here](https://thechats.com/#tour-dates).
