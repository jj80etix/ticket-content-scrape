# Stadium attendance jump drives Live Nation to $10bn Q3 revenue

## Summary

Live Nation's Q3 2026 revenue rose 19% year-over-year to $10.1bn, driven by a record 61 million concert attendees, with stadium shows accounting for more than half of global audience growth. International attendance grew 29% versus 13% domestically, and adjusted operating income rose 14% to $1.18bn.

### Key Points
- Q3 revenue: $10.1bn, +19% YoY.
- Concert attendance: 61 million, +19% YoY (a record).
- International attendance +29%; North American attendance +13%.
- Adjusted operating income +14% to $1.18bn.
- Stadium shows drove more than half of global audience growth.

### Highlights
- Q3 revenue $10.1bn (+19% YoY); record 61m attendees.
- International markets growing faster than domestic (29% vs. 13%).

### Industry Problem

Live Nation's continued double-digit growth, increasingly concentrated in international stadium touring, underscores the scale advantage that anchors the antitrust scrutiny it already faces — the same market position at issue in DOJ's ongoing case against the company.

### Proposed Solution

No build/improve action is implied by the item itself; this is a #monitor signal — track whether Live Nation's international stadium-touring growth outpaces remedies in the DOJ settlement, which would suggest structural dominance persisting regardless of the litigation's outcome.
