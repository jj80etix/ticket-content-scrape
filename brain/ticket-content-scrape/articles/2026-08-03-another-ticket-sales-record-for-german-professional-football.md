# Another ticket sales record for German professional football

## Summary

German professional football set a new ticket-sales record in the 2025-26 season, with the Bundesliga and Bundesliga 2 combined selling over 21.4 million tickets. Average attendance rates and ticket prices both rose year-over-year, driven partly by the return of two historically well-attended clubs to the top flight.

### Key Points
- 36 clubs sold 21,404,258 tickets combined in 2025-26, averaging 34,974 tickets per match.
- Bundesliga alone sold 12,718,097 tickets (~41,562/match), up 9% year-over-year; average stadium attendance rate hit 96.10%, and 16 of 18 clubs exceeded 90% attendance.
- League credits the return of 1. FC Köln and Hamburger SV — historically high-draw clubs — as a significant contributor.
- Bundesliga 2 sold 8,686,161 tickets, averaging 28,386 per match.
- Average Bundesliga ticket price (incl. VAT and transport) rose to €30.77 from €28.78; Bundesliga 2 average price dipped slightly to €24.34 from €24.70.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The 2025-26 German Bundesliga and Bundesliga 2 season set yet another record for ticket sales.**

Thirty-six professional clubs sold a total of 21,404,258 tickets last season, with an average of 34,974 tickets sold per match.

The Bundesliga, the highest tier of German men’s professional football, achieved ticket sales of 12,718,097. This corresponds to roughly 41,562 tickets per match – a 9% increase on the previous season. The league said that the return of 1. FC Köln and Hamburger SV to the top flight was a significant contributor to the sales total, as the two clubs traditionally have some of the highest attendance figures in German football.

The average attendance rate at Bundesliga stadiums, based on ticket sales, amounted to 96.10% according to the league, which is slightly above the previous year’s high of 95.91%. A total of 16 out of the 18 Bundesliga clubs achieved an attendance rate of more than 90%.

In the Bundesliga 2, 8,686,161 tickets were sold with an average of 28,386 tickets sold per match.

“With more than 21 million tickets sold, German professional football impressively confirms its great appeal,” said Bundesliga chief executive, Marc Lenz.

“The Bundesliga stands for sporting quality, a fantastic stadium atmosphere and, by international standards, spectator-friendly ticket prices. This combination makes the stadium experience accessible to many people and an integral part of their everyday lives.”

**Cost of attendance**

The average price of a ticket for a Bundesliga match, including VAT and public transport charges, was €30.77 (£26.35/$35.47) in the 2025-26 season compared to the previous year of €28.78. In the Bundesliga 2, the average price was €24.34, slightly below the previous year’s figure of €24.70. A standing ticket in the Bundesliga cost an average of €12.27 compared to €12.04 in 2024-25, and in the 2. Bundesliga €13.20 (2024-25: €12.55).

[Statistics](https://report.bundesliga.de/2526/en/current-reports/spectator-report.html) are based on the number of tickets sold, concluding complimentary and free tickets.
