# The Funeral Portrait Announce 2027 ‘A Hex on the Nation’ Tour

## Summary

The Funeral Portrait announced a 20-date North American headlining tour for March 2027, with Archers, Creeper, If Not For Me and FKA Rayne supporting on every date. The run plays rock clubs and midsize venues across the US and Canada.

### Key Points
- "A Hex on the Nation Tour" runs March 2 (Asheville, NC) through March 28 (Nashville, TN)
- 18 US dates plus two Canadian stops (Toronto, Montreal)
- Citi cardmember presale began Nov 10; general onsale Friday, Nov 13, 10am local via Live Nation
- Follows single "My Future Prediction" and 100M+ streams for album Greetings From Suffocate City

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/The-Funeral-Portrait-2027.webp)

The Funeral Portrait have announced a 20-date North American headlining tour for March 2027, with Archers, Creeper, If Not For Me and FKA Rayne joining the Atlanta-based alternative rock band for every show.

The *A Hex on the Nation Tour* will begin March 2 at The Orange Peel in Asheville, North Carolina, and wraps up March 28 at Brooklyn Bowl in Nashville, Tennessee. The tour includes 18 U.S. dates and two Canadian performances, with stops in New York, Boston, Detroit, Chicago, Toronto and Montreal.

The 2027 tour will bring all five bands to a mix of established rock clubs and midsize concert venues, including Irving Plaza in New York, The Stone Pony in Asbury Park, House of Blues Chicago and the Opera House in Toronto.

Led by frontman Lee Jennings, The Funeral Portrait have built a following through their theatrical live performances and a sound combining alternative rock, post-hardcore and metal influences.
