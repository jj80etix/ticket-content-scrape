# Untold Festival closes with more than 500,000 attendees

## Summary

Untold Festival in Cluj-Napoca, Romania wrapped its 2026 edition with more than 500,000 attendees across four days and nine stages, featuring headliners including Sting, Lewis Capaldi, Carl Cox, Kygo, and The Chainsmokers. The 11th edition sold out its first 20,000 tickets for the 2027 edition (August 5-8) within minutes of going on sale, with prices starting at €109 and rising to €249. Organizers are reportedly exploring international expansion into Latin America, including a possible new festival in Colombia and discussions in Brazil, the US, Canada, and several Asian and European markets.

### Key Points
- 2026 edition drew over 500,000 attendees over four days at Cluj Arena, its 11th year running.
- Lineup spanned Flo Rida, Alan Walker, MEDUZA³ LIVE, Afrojack B2B R3HAB, Steve Aoki, Sting, Lewis Capaldi, Carl Cox, Kygo, and The Chainsmokers, alongside regional Romanian/Balkan acts.
- 2027 dates already set for August 5-8, with tickets on sale starting at €109 (rising to €249); first 20,000 tickets sold out within minutes.
- Organizers are reportedly in advanced discussions on a global expansion strategy, including a new festival launch in Colombia and projects in Brazil, the US, Canada, Japan, China, India, South Korea, Egypt, and Spain.
