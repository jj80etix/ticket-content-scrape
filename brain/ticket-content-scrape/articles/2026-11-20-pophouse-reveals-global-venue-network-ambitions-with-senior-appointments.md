# Pophouse reveals global venue network ambitions with senior appointments

## Summary

Pophouse Entertainment, the investment and production firm behind ABBA Voyage, has hired Greg Lombardo (ex-Netflix) as president of its Avatar Network and Kristina Heney (ex-Oak View Group) as president of commercial & marketing, as it pushes into a "next chapter" of global expansion. Lombardo will lead creative development and the build-out of a global network of avatar venues; Heney will unify commercial growth, marketing, data strategy, ticketing, and sponsorship across Pophouse's brands.

### Key Points
- Pophouse's portfolio spans ABBA Voyage, Iron Maiden, Tina Turner, Sia, Cyndi Lauper, Avicii, and an upcoming KISS avatar show (2028 launch).
- Lombardo joins after seven years at Netflix to lead strategy and delivery of avatar venues worldwide.
- Heney joins after three years at Oak View Group to lead commercial growth, marketing, data strategy, ticketing, and sponsorship.
- ABBA Voyage, Pophouse's flagship show, has sold over 3 million tickets and added more than £2bn to the UK economy since its 2022 debut.
- Pophouse has also been acquiring catalogue and name/image/likeness rights, most recently for Sia.

## Transcript

![Greg Lombardo & Kristina Heney](https://www.iqmagazine.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Greg-Lombardo-Kristina-Heney.png)

Greg Lombardo & Kristina Heney

Pophouse Entertainment, the pioneering global entertainment and music investment firm behind ABBA Voyage, has added a pair to its senior leadership team to help usher in its “next chapter” of global expansion.

Greg Lombardo is set to join as president, Pophouse Avatar Network, and Kristina Heney will join as president, commercial & marketing. They will be Los Angeles- and New York-based based, respectively.

The duo are expected to play “central roles” in the growth of the company’s brands, experiences, and productions, across a portfolio that also includes Iron Maiden, Tina Turner, Sia, Cyndi Lauper, Avicii, and [KISS](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/04/kiss-confirm-2028-for-avatar-show-launch/), whose avatar show will launch in 2028.

Joining from a more than seven-year run at Netflix, Lombardo will take the lead on creative development, production, and operations for Pophouse’s technology-led live productions.

He will also be tasked with the strategy, development, and delivery of avatar venues, with the company saying it is looking to build a “global network of venues designed to host a variety of immersive music experiences”.

And Heney joins from a three-year stint at Oak View Group, and will lead a “unified approach” to commercial growth, marketing, data strategy, ticketing and sponsorship across Pophouse’s brands, productions, and experiences.

“Pophouse was founded on the belief that the world’s most iconic artists can reach new generations through bold, imaginative experiences,” CEO Jessica Koravos says.

“Building on our partnerships with legendary artists and productions, including KISS, Iron Maiden, Tina Turner, Sia, Cyndi Lauper, and Avicii, ABBA Voyage, Mamma Mia! The Party and our exhibitions, we aim to carry these legacies to audiences worldwide.

“Greg and Tina are two of the most accomplished leaders in live entertainment, and they bring exactly the combination of commercial discipline and creative ambition we need for the next chapter. I am delighted to welcome them both to our leadership team,” she concludes.

In the past year, Pophouse has snapped up a variety of catalogue, name, image and likeness rights, with Sia the most recent example.

The company’s flagship project, the ABBA Voyage concert series, [launched in May 2022](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2022/07/behind-the-madness-of-abba-voyage/) at the purpose-built 3,000-cap ABBA Arena in London’s Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, under the direction of producers Svana Gisla and Ludvig Andersson and director Baillie Walsh.

Since its debut, the show has delivered a more than [£2 billion (€1.7bn) boost to the UK economy](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2025/12/abba-voyages-economic-contribution-to-uk-tops-2bn/) and sold over 3 million tickets.
