# Josh Radnor and Beth Behrs to Lead Broadway’s ‘Schmigadoon!’ in 2027

## Summary

Josh Radnor and Beth Behrs will take over the lead roles in Broadway's "Schmigadoon!" for a six-week run starting Jan. 19, 2027, succeeding original stars Alex Brightman and Sara Chase.

### Key Points
- New engagement runs Jan. 19-Feb. 28, 2027 at the Nederlander Theatre.
- Michelle Visage (RuPaul's Drag Race) joins Dec. 4 as a separate cast replacement.
- "Schmigadoon!" won 4 Tony Awards in 2026, including Best Musical.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Schmigadoon.webp)

Josh Radnor and Beth Behrs will take over the leading roles in Broadway’s “Schmigadoon!” for a limited six-week engagement beginning January 19, 2027, at the Nederlander Theatre in New York City.

Behrs, known for her roles in “2 Broke Girls” and “The Neighborhood,” will play Melissa Gimble, while Radnor, best known for starring in “How I Met Your Mother,” will portray Josh Skinner. Their engagement will continue through February 28.

The two actors will succeed original Broadway stars Alex Brightman and Sara Chase, who will give their [final performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/alex-brightman-and-sara-chase-to-leave-broadways-schmigadoon-in-january/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) on January 17. Brightman and Chase originated their roles in the musical’s 2025 world premiere at the Kennedy Center in Washington, D.C., before transferring with the production to Broadway.

The production has also featured several cast changes during its Broadway run. Michelle Visage of “RuPaul’s Drag Race” is scheduled to make her Broadway debut as Mildred Layton on December 4, replacing Jennifer Simard. Visage’s engagement continues through January 31, allowing her to perform alongside Radnor and Behrs during the beginning of their run.

The company also includes Ann Harada as Florence Menlove, Brad Oscar as Mayor Menlove, Isabelle McCalla as Emma Tate, Maulik Pancholy as the Reverend Layton and Leprechaun, Max Clayton as Danny Bailey and McKenzie Kurtz as Betsy.

The engagement will mark Behrs’ Broadway debut. Meanwhile, Radnor returns to Broadway following previous appearances in “The Graduate” and “Disgraced.”

Based on the Apple television series created by Cinco Paul and Ken Daurio, “Schmigadoon!” follows New York doctors Josh and Melissa as they embark on a backpacking retreat intended to repair their relationship. Instead, they discover a magical town where everyday life unfolds like a Golden Age Broadway musical.

The stage adaptation features a book, music and lyrics by Paul, with direction and choreography by Christopher Gattelli. The musical began Broadway previews on April 4, 2026, and officially opened April 20.
