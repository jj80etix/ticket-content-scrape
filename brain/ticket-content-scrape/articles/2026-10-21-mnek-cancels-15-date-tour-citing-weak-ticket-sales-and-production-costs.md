# MNEK Cancels 15-Date Tour, Citing Weak Ticket Sales and Production Costs

## Summary

British singer-producer MNEK canceled his 15-date UK/European "BULLDOZER!!" tour three weeks before its start, citing weak ticket sales, budget problems, and production readiness concerns.

### Key Points
- Tour was set to run Nov. 7-Dec. 3 across the UK and Europe (Bristol, Manchester, London, Glasgow, Dublin, Brighton, Barcelona, Zurich, Paris, Cologne, Amsterdam, Berlin, Copenhagen, Oslo).
- Would have been his first full headline tour since 2019, supporting his new album *BULLDOZER!!* (released Sept. 18).
- Tickets started around £30; MNEK said fans will be refunded "ASAP."
- Follows other recent cancellations amid soft ticket demand: JP Saxe's 2025 North American tour and the Pussycat Dolls' 2026 reunion dates.
- MNEK, primarily known as a songwriter/producer (Beyoncé, Dua Lipa, Zara Larsson), said most of his income comes from writing/production rather than touring as a headliner.

British singer-songwriter and producer MNEK has canceled his 15-date U.K. and European tour, citing weak ticket sales, a troubled budget and concerns that the production was not ready less than three weeks before the first show.

The Grammy-nominated artist, who has written and produced music for Beyoncé, Dua Lipa and Zara Larsson, announced the decision Tuesday in a [statement posted on X](https://x.com/MNEK/status/2112591980387852435?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). THE!! BULLDOZER!! TOUR!! had been scheduled to begin Nov. 7 in Bristol, England, and continue through Dec. 3.

“To be completely frank, the budget has been fudgey.. the tickets aren’t selling enough …and the show just isn’t ready to the standard I want it to be,” MNEK wrote.

He said he was joining other artists forced to cancel tours because of “high production costs, over-ambitious planning, and weighing up whether being at a loss is worth it or not.” MNEK also acknowledged that the show itself was not ready, rather than attributing the decision entirely to ticket demand.

“I was really looking forward to performing BULLDOZER!! on tour,” he wrote. “I’m super proud of the album and I worked really hard on it – but I have no interest in doing this tour if I can’t do it right.”

The canceled itinerary included Bristol’s Thekla, Manchester’s Gorilla, London’s KOKO and smaller venues in Glasgow, Dublin and Brighton before heading to Barcelona, Zurich, Paris, Cologne, Amsterdam, Berlin, Copenhagen and Oslo. It would have marked [his first full headlining tour since 2019](https://www.thelineofbestfit.com/news/mnek-announces-first-uk-eu-headline-tour-in-seven-years?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), supporting *BULLDOZER!!*, released Sept. 18 as his first studio album in eight years.

Although prices differed by city, [BBC Newsbeat reported](https://www.bbc.co.uk/news/articles/cwe8lz018pe1o?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) that tickets started at approximately £30. The relatively modest advertised entry price distinguishes this club-and-theater run from high-priced arena tours that have struggled to sell. It does not establish why demand was insufficient, and MNEK did not disclose ticket-sales figures or specific production costs.

MNEK, whose real name is Uzoechi Emenike, has been a successful collaborator for other artists, including on Zara Larsson’s “Never Forget You” and Joel Corry’s “Head & Heart.” But his success behind the scenes has not translated into the same level of demand for his own headlining concerts. In [a September interview with The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/music/2026/sep/25/mnek-music-interview-uzoechi-osisioma-emenike-bulldozer-album?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), he said “most, if not all, of my money is made from being a writer and producer.” His 2018 solo debut, *Language*, peaked at No. 81 on the U.K. albums chart.

The cancellation follows other tours that have struggled to match expectations. [JP Saxe canceled a 25-date North American tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/08/jp-saxe-cancels-tour-amid-low-ticket-sales/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in 2025 after falling short of needed ticket sales, while [the Pussycat Dolls scrapped nearly all of their planned North American reunion dates](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/05/pussycat-dolls-cancel-north-american-reunion-tour-dates-citing-honest-look-at-sales/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) this year amid concern about soft sales and arena-sized bookings. Unlike those arena dates, MNEK’s tour was primarily booked into clubs and smaller music venues.

Other musicians have acknowledged the financial pressure of touring. Becky Hill responded to MNEK’s announcement by writing, “Touring is devastatingly expensive but you’ll find a way,” according to BBC Newsbeat. Blur drummer David Rowntree, chair of the Featured Artists Coalition, has likewise warned that touring has become increasingly difficult to make financially viable.

MNEK thanked the fans who had purchased tickets and said they would be refunded “ASAP.” He said he hopes to try again when he can give the album and its audience the show he believes they deserve. No replacement dates have been announced.
