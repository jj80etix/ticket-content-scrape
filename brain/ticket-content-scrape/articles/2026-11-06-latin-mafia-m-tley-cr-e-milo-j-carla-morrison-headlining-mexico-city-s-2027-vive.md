# Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison Headlining Mexico City’s 2027 Vive Latino

## Summary

Mexico City's Vive Latino festival revealed its 27th-edition lineup, headlined by Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison, and Ed Maverick, taking place March 13-14, 2027 at Estadio GNP Seguros. Presale begins November 11 for Banamex cardmembers, with general onsale November 12 via Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- Dates: March 13-14, 2027, Estadio GNP Seguros, Mexico City.
- Headliners: Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison, Ed Maverick.
- Other acts: Residente, Turnstile, Placebo, Kasabian, Ximena Sariñana.
- Promoted by OCESA, presented by Amazon; presale Nov. 11, general onsale Nov. 12 via Ticketmaster.

The 27 <sup>th</sup> edition of the popular Mexican festival Vive Latino will be headlined by Latin Mafia, Mötley Crüe, Milo J, Carla Morrison and Ed Maverick. The rock-heavy, eclectic festival celebrates Ibero-American culture and takes place March 13 and 14 at Mexico City’s iconic Estadio GNP Seguros.

**[¡Vive Latino, Viva México! OCESA’s Festival Emblematic Of The Nation’s Cultural Shift](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/03/29/vive-latino-viva-mexico-ocesas-festival-emblematic-of-the-nations-cultural-shift/)**

Other acts on the bill include 29-time Latin Grammy winner and festival favorite Residente, Turnstile, Ximena Sariñana, Placebo, Kase.O, Mi Banda El Mexicano, Cultura Profética, La Gusana Ciega, Bandalos Chinos, Inspector, Ataque 77, Kasabian, Zurdok and many more.

Presale tickets for the festival, which is promoted by OCESA and presented by Amazon, are available for Banamex cardmembers beginning Nov. 11 at 2 p.m. local time, and the general onsale begins Nov. 12 at 2 p.m. local time. Visit [Ticketmaster.com](https://www.ticketmaster.com/Vive-Latino-boletos/artist/1209767) for tickets.
