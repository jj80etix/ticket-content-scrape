# Dinosaur Jr. Extends ‘There Near’ Tour Into 2027 With Starcrawler

## Summary

Dinosaur Jr. is extending its "There Near" North American tour into 2027, adding new dates with support act Starcrawler behind the band's recently released album of the same name.

### Key Points
- New run begins February 11 at Brooklyn Steel, with a second Brooklyn show the following night.
- Tour continues through cities including Philadelphia, Silver Spring, and Carrboro.
- Starcrawler joins as support for the extended dates.
- Extension follows the release of the band's new album, "There Near."

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Dinosaur-Jr.webp)

Dinosaur Jr. is extending its North American tour into 2027, adding a new slate of shows behind the band’s recently released album, *There Near*.

The new run begins February 11 at Brooklyn Steel in Brooklyn, where Dinosaur Jr. will play a second show the following night. The tour then heads through Philadelphia, Silver Spring, Carrboro, Jacksonville, St. Petersburg, Orlando, Athens and Nashville before moving through the Midwest and concluding March 13 at House of Blues in Boston.

Los Angeles rock band Starcrawler will join Dinosaur Jr. for most of the newly announced dates, including stops in Philadelphia, Nashville, Chicago, Detroit and Toronto. Tickets for the 2027 shows are on sale now.

**| RELATED:**[**Dinosaur Jr. Adds Fall North American Tour Dates**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/07/dinosaur-jr-adds-fall-north-american-tour-dates/) **|**

The extension gives Dinosaur Jr. another month of North American dates following a previously announced fall tour that runs from October 9 through November 7. The fall run is set to begin at Brooklyn Bowl in Las Vegas before visiting venues across the West Coast, Pacific Northwest, Mountain West and Texas. That run is set to wrap up at Tipitina’s in New Orleans.

The touring activity comes alongside the August 28 release of *There Near*, Dinosaur Jr.’s 13th studio album and its first since *Sweep It Into Space* arrived in 2021.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Dinosaur Jr. official website](https://www.dinosaurjr.com/).
