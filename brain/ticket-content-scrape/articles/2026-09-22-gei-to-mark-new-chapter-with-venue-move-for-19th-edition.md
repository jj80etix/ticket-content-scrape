# GEI to mark new chapter with venue move for 19th edition

## Summary

The Green Events & Innovations Conference (GEI), the live industry's leading event-sustainability gathering, will relocate to London's The O2 for its 19th edition on Feb. 23, 2027, as organizer A Greener Future looks to scale up the one-day conference. The move puts GEI inside a venue whose CEO cites its own 20-year sustainability track record.

### Key Points
- GEI's 19th edition moves to The O2 (indigo and O2 Blueroom spaces), London, Feb. 23, 2027.
- Organizer A Greener Future aims to scale up the conference in its new home.
- The O2 cited for its own sustainability investments over 20 years of operation.
- GEI focuses on event-industry sustainability, climate, and social-action topics.

## Transcript

The Green Events and Innovations Conference will be staged within two spaces in London's The O2 next February

Organisers of the Green Events & Innovations Conference (GEI) have announced the sustainability conference’s next chapter, revealing a move to London’s The O2 for its 19th edition next year.

The one-day industry conference, set for 23 February 2027, will be staged across indigo at The O2 and the O2 Blueroom within the leading arena, as organisers A Greener Future (AGF) aim to scale up the event.

GEI is the planet’s leading conference for event sustainability, and brings together a cohort annually to tackle pressing topics around event sustainability, climate change, and social action.

“The Green Events & Innovations Conference is evolving once again,” says AGF founder and CEO Claire O’Neill. “The expansion of GEI will allow for even greater action and amplification of the important issues of climate, nature and people through the lens of the live sector – from music to sport. We’re delighted to have secured a new home with The O2, which, across its 20 years of operations, has invested strongly in green technology and led the way for the entertainment sector with its sustainability initiatives.”

*IQ* recently caught up with [O’Neill](https://www.iqmagazine.com/magazine/creating-a-greener-future/), discussing how she helped build the foundation of the global live music industry’s sustainability practices.

The [18th edition of GEI](https://www.iqmagazine.com/2026/02/seven-takeaways-from-gei-2026/) drew more than 450 delegates from 26 countries, with topics ranging from clean power, green energy in venues, zero-emission touring, water’s impact, fan expectations, and audience emissions.

Previous speakers have included eco-warriors and artists Brian Eno, Moby, Aurora, Blaine Harrison, Jarvis Cocker, Madame Gandhi, Jacob Collier, and Declan McKenna, and frontline changemakers like Carly McLachlan (Tyndall Centre), Dale Vince OBE (Ecotricity/Grid Faeries), Erik Distler (AEG), Minister for Climate Kerry McCarthy (MP Bristol East), and Cathy Runciman (EarthPercent).

The event first took place at Buckinghamshire’s Missenden Abbey before moving to London’s Royal Festival Hall, and has most recently been staged at the Royal Lancaster Hotel in partnership with the International Live Music Conference (ILMC).

ILMC delegates will be able to purchase a dicounted pass to GEI, which will be held the day before the 2027 edition of the live entertainment industry’s leading annual gathering of professionals, with GEI delegates also able to access ILMC’s Soho Calling showcase festival.

AEG International’s director of sustainability Sam Booth says: “We’re pleased to welcome the Green Events & Innovations Conference to The O2 in 2027. Sustainability remains an important focus across our business, and GEI has played a significant role in bringing the sector together to share ideas, drive innovation and accelerate progress. We look forward to hosting delegates under our iconic tent and supporting industry conversation that shapes a sustainable future.”

GEI is supported by Ecotricity Business, Ecotalk, The O2, AEG, Skydiamond, and ILMC.

More information is available [here](http://agreenerfuture.com).
