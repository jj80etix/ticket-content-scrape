# Mercury Prize UK Shortlist Unveiled

## Summary

The BBC unveiled the twelve-album shortlist for the 2026 Mercury Prize, spanning artists including Dave, Florence + The Machine, Paul McCartney, RAYE and Olivia Dean. The ceremony returns to Newcastle for 2026, taking place Oct. 22 at Utilita Arena Newcastle with live performances from nominated artists, broadcast by BBC Music; tickets for the awards show are already on sale.

### Key Points
- Twelve nominated albums announced July 30 on BBC Radio 6 Music/BBC Sounds, including Dave, Florence + The Machine, Paul McCartney, JADE, Knats, Nia Archives, Olivia Dean, RAYE and Suede.
- Olivia Dean's nominated album, The Art of Loving, previously swept the 2026 Brit Awards.
- Award ceremony set for Thursday, Oct. 22, 2026 at Utilita Arena Newcastle, in partnership with Newcastle City Council.
- BBC Music serves as broadcast partner; tickets to the ceremony are already on sale.

The twelve “Albums of the Year” nominated at this year’s Mercury Prize were announced today, on BBC Radio 6 Music and BBC Sounds by Jamz Supernova. The shortlist includes Dave, Florence + The Machine, Paul McCartney, RAYE, and Olivia Dean, who already swept [the 2026 Brit Awards](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/03/02/brit-awards-2026-feel-right-at-home-in-manchester-sound-better-than-ever/) with her current album, *The Art Of Loving*.

The 2026 Mercury Prize 12 “Albums of the Year” are:

Dave – *The Boy Who Played the Harp*  
Dove Ellis – *Blizzard*  
Florence + The Machine – *Everybody Scream*  
JADE – *THAT’S SHOWBIZ BABY!  
*Knats – *A Great Day In Newcastle*  
Kojey Radical – *Don’t Look Down  
*Nia Archives – *Emotional Junglist  
*Olivia Dean – *The Art of Loving  
*Paul McCartney – *The Boys of Dungeon Lane  
*RAYE – *THIS MUSIC MAY CONTAIN HOPE.  
*Suede – *Antidepressants*  
War Child Records – *HELP(2)*

The Mercury Prize returns to Newcastle as the host city for 2026, in partnership with Newcastle City Council. The awards show will be held on Thursday, Oct. 22, at Utilita Arena Newcastle and will feature live performances from many of the nominated artists, culminating in the announcement of the overall winner of the 2026 Mercury Prize.

The Prize’s broadcast partner, BBC Music, will cover the event. Tickets for the 2026 Mercury Prize awards ceremony are on sale.
