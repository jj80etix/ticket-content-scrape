# Aaron Tveit Rejoins ‘Moulin Rouge! The Musical’ for Final Broadway Performances

## Summary

Aaron Tveit will return to Broadway's "Moulin Rouge! The Musical" for 15 performances from August 18–29, reprising his Tony-winning role as Christian during the show's final weeks at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre. Christian Douglas takes over for the production's final Broadway performance on August 30.

### Key Points
- Tveit performs Aug. 18–29 (15 shows); Christian Douglas plays the final Broadway performance Aug. 30.
- Tveit won the 2021 Tony for Best Leading Actor in a Musical for originating the role; the show has won 10 Tony Awards total, including Best Musical.
- Cast for the run includes Ricky Rojas, Kelsie Watts, AK Naderer, Eric Anderson, André Ward, David Harris, and Arianna Rosario.
- The production has drawn more than 12 million audience members across 15 productions worldwide, including current runs in London's West End, Germany, Korea, and the Netherlands.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Moulin-Rouge.webp)

Aaron Tveit will return to Broadway’s “Moulin Rouge! The Musical” later this month, reprising his Tony Award-winning role as Christian for the production’s final weeks at the Al Hirschfeld Theatre.

Tveit will perform in 15 performances from August 18 through August 29. Christian Douglas will then return as Christian for the show’s final Broadway performance on August 30.

Tveit originated the role of Christian when “Moulin Rouge! The Musical” opened on Broadway and won the 2021 Tony Award for Best Performance by a Leading Actor in a Musical for his performance. Most recently, he appeared on Broadway as Freddie Trumper in “Chess.”

During his limited engagement, Tveit will join a cast that includes original cast member Ricky Rojas as Santiago, Kelsie Watts as Satine, AK Naderer as Nini, Eric Anderson as Harold Zidler, André Ward as Toulouse-Lautrec, David Harris as The Duke of Monroth, and Arianna Rosario as the Satine alternate.
