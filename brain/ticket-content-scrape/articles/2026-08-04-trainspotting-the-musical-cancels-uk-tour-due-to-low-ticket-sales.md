# Trainspotting: The Musical cancels UK tour due to low ticket sales

## Summary

The West End production of *Trainspotting: The Musical* has cancelled its planned UK tour, citing weak ticket sales tied to the current economic climate. The tour was set to launch October 19 and visit Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Sheffield, Leeds, Nottingham and other cities; affected ticket-holders will receive full refunds through their point of purchase. The West End run at Theatre Royal Haymarket continues through September 5.

### Key Points
- UK tour cancellation announced ahead of planned October 19 launch; full refunds to be issued via original point of purchase.
- Producers cited "the current economic climate" resulting in lower-than-expected ticket sales.
- Planned stops included Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Sheffield, Leeds, Nottingham and more.
- West End production at Theatre Royal Haymarket continues its run through September 5.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The UK tour of *Trainspotting: The Musical* has been cancelled due to low ticket sales.**

The production, which launched in the West End this year at Theatre Royal Haymarket, comes 30 years after the iconic film directed by Danny Boyle. Written by Irvine Welsh, based on his novel, the show is presented by Phil McIntyre Live.

The musical follows the characters Renton, Sick Boy, Begbie, Spud, Tommy and Kelly, with the score including tracks associated with the original film along with material written specifically for the stage.

*Trainspotting: The Musical* was initially scheduled to head to Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Sheffield, Leeds, Nottingham and more.

In a [statement](https://www.rollingstone.co.uk/music/trainspotting-the-musical-uk-tour-cancelled-due-to-low-ticket-sales-63328/) confirming the cancellation, the production said: “The forthcoming UK tour of  *Trainspotting The Musical*, which was due to begin on 19 October, has been cancelled.

“We are very grateful to our cast, crew and creative team for their dedication to the production, but the current economic climate has resulted in lower than expected ticket sales. Ticket-holders will be contacted by their point of purchase with full refund details. We apologise for any inconvenience and disappointment this may cause.”

The West End production will continue its run through to September 5.
