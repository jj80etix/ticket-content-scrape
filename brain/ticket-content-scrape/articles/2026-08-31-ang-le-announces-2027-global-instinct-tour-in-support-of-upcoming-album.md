# Angèle Announces 2027 Global “Instinct Tour” In Support Of Upcoming Album

## Summary

Belgian singer Angèle announced a global "Instinct Tour" for 2027 in support of her upcoming album, kicking off in spring at Reims Arena in France.

### Key Points
- Tour begins March 3, 2027 at Reims Arena, France.
- Billed as a global tour supporting Angèle's forthcoming album.

Angèle has announced she is hitting the road for a brand-new international tour, kicking off in spring 2027. Beginning March 3, the Belgian singer will open the global tour at Reims Arena in France. From there she will hit Paris, Brussels, Montreal, New York, Amsterdam and more before concluding at Lausanne’s Vaudoise Arena.

The announcement arrives fresh on the heels of the singer revealing that her third album, *INSTINCT,* is scheduled to release on Oct. 16. Her new single “Une seule vie”, released on August 28, has seen success on social media with fans taking to TikTok to show their support for the track. The 23-date tour will support her new LP; it marks a new milestone in Angèle’s career and is her most ambitious live venture to date.

Tickets will be available starting Sept. 2 at 10 a.m. local time with Artist, Citi and Amex presales (details below). Additional presales will run throughout the week ahead of the general onsale beginning on Friday, September 4 at 10 a.m. local time and vary by city.

Citi cardmembers will have access to presale tickets in Brooklyn and Los Angeles only beginning Sept. 2 at 10 a.m. local time until Sept. 3 at 10 a.m. local time through the Citi Entertainment program. For complete presale details, visit www.citientertainment.com.

American Express® Card Members can purchase tickets for Montreal only before the general public beginning Sept. 2 at 10 a.m. local time through Sept. 3 at 10 a.m. local time.

Mastercard cardholders will benefit from exclusive presale access to Angèle’s concerts in France, Belgium and the Netherlands. The Mastercard presale will begin on Sept. 4 at 10 a.m. (local time) and will run until Sept. 5 (local time). For more information, visit priceless.com/music.

Live Nation France is proud to support Angèle on this landmark tour, which marks the next chapter in an exceptional journey that has made her one of the most influential European artists of her generation. With the Instinct Tour, she continues the international expansion of her career and further cements her status as one of the rare French-speaking artists who can bring audiences together across Europe and beyond.
