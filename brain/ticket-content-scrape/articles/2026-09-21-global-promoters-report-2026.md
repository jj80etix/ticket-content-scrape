# Global Promoters Report 2026

## Summary

IQ Magazine's Global Promoters Report 2026 landing page lists the report's contents rather than reporting news: a keynote interview with AEG president of global touring Rich Schaefer on late-buying fans and deal dynamics, plus 76 profiled touring markets including a new Central Asia/Caucasus section. The rest of the page is a catalog of IQ's other annual report titles.

### Key Points
- Report profiles 76 touring markets, adding Central Asia and the Caucasus (Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Georgia, etc.) as a new region.
- Rich Schaefer (AEG, president of global touring) is interviewed on late-buying fan behavior and walking away from deals.
- Page functions as a report storefront/TOC with links to IQ's other 2025-26 annual reports (stadiums, arenas, touring, festivals).
