# Gnarls Barkley Announce First Tour in 18 Years

## Summary

Gnarls Barkley (CeeLo Green and Danger Mouse) have announced their first tour in 18 years, a nine-date North American run called "To Whom It Still Concerns," promoted by Live Nation and running November 5-28, 2026.

### Key Points
- Nine dates from Nov 5 (San Francisco) through Nov 28 (Chicago), also hitting LA, Atlanta, Detroit, Boston, Silver Spring MD, Toronto and New York.
- First tour since 2008, following new album *Atlanta* (released March 2026), described as the duo's third and final studio album.
- Citi Cardmember/Reserved by Spotify presales begin Sept 21; Live Nation/venue presales Sept 22; artist presale Sept 23 (registration closes Sept 20).
- Tour promoted by Live Nation.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/gnarls-barkley-tour-image-1200.webp)

Gnarls Barkley are heading back on the road for the first time in 18 years, with CeeLo Green and Danger Mouse announcing a nine-date North American tour for November.

The “To Whom It Still Concerns Tour,” promoted by Live Nation, begins Nov. 5 at The Masonic in San Francisco and runs through Nov. 28 at Chicago’s House of Blues. Stops along the way include Los Angeles, Atlanta, Detroit, Boston, Silver Spring (Maryland, outside Washington D.C.), Toronto and New York.

The run marks the duo’s first tour since 2008 and follows their return earlier this year with *Atlanta*, their first album since *The Odd Couple* and what Gnarls Barkley have described as their third and final studio album. *Atlanta* was released in March after the duo broke an 18-year recording hiatus with lead single “Pictures.”

“The Gnarls Barkley ‘To Whom It Still Concerns’ Tour operates like an open book, exploring the universal matters of life, death and everything in between,” Green said in announcing the shows.
