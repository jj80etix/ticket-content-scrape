# Popular VTuber Hoshimachi Suisei Signs With WME

## Summary

Japanese virtual artist Hoshimachi Suisei, who has nearly 3 million YouTube followers and surpassed 100 million cumulative streams on Billboard Japan, has signed with WME for global representation. The announcement came on the final night of her sold-out Japan tour at Marine Messe Fukuoka.

### Key Points
- Signed with WME for global representation.
- Breakout hit "BIBBIDIBA" surpassed 180M YouTube views and 100M Billboard Japan streams.
- Named to Forbes Japan's 30 Under 30 list and runs her own agency, Studio STELLAR.

## Transcript

![Hoshimachi Suisei](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Hoshimachi-Suisei.jpg)

Hoshimachi Suisei

Hoshimachi Suisei, a Japanese virtual artist with nearly 3 million YouTube followers, has signed with WME for global representation. The popular VTuber announced the news on the final night of her sold-out tour at Marine Messe Fukuoka Hall A in Japan.

Suisei broke out with her hit track “BIBBIDIBA,” which went viral globally and amassed more than 180 million views on YouTube. It was the first song by a VTuber to surpass 100 million cumulative streams on Billboard Japan.

The standout artist earned a Bronze Award at the Clio Music Awards in 2025, where she performed a song from Mobile Suit Gundam GQuuuuuuX. She was featured on the cover of Forbes Japan’s 30 Under 30 list in 2025 and established an independent agency, Studio STELLAR.

With such a following across YouTube, X, Spotify and TikTok, Suisei has cemented herself as a leader in the VTuber music movement, continuing to transcend the boundaries between the virtual and physical worlds. She recently concluded her largest arena tour to date and will embark on her first co-headlining tour and an Asia tour in 2027.

One of the artist’s most successful outings was Sept. 9 at K-Arena Yokohama in Japan, where Suisei grossed $1,319,377 off 18,405 tickets sold, according to Pollstar Boxoffice.

WME continues to expand its global roster and is signing artists from the Asia Pacific region. The agency recently signed JISOO, and its roster includes EJAE, Jackson Wang, Audrey Nuna, Number\_i and virtual talent Ado
