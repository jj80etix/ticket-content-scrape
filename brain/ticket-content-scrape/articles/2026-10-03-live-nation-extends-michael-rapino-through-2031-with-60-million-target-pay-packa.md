# Live Nation Extends Michael Rapino Through 2031 With $60 Million Target Pay Package

## Summary

Live Nation extended CEO Michael Rapino's employment agreement through Dec. 31, 2031, roughly doubling his target annual compensation starting in 2027 by adding two new $15 million annual equity grants on top of his existing salary, bonus and performance-share targets. The board unanimously approved the amended agreement (Rapino abstaining); he also received a separate $20 million upfront RSU grant. The move locks in Rapino's leadership of the concert-promotion and Ticketmaster parent company for five more years and follows a shift in shareholder sentiment from the ~53% "say on pay" rejection in 2023 to ~87% approval in June 2026.

### Key Points
- New target compensation starting 2027: at least $60 million/year ($3M salary, $17M cash bonus, $10M performance RSAs, $15M time-based RSUs, $15M performance share units), roughly double the prior $30M target.
- Separate $20 million upfront RSU grant (117,805 units) vesting 40% in 2027, 20% in 2028-29, 10% in 2030-31.
- Agreement signed Sept. 30, 2026, effective Oct. 1; extends his contract (previously expiring end of 2027) through Dec. 31, 2031.
- Severance: termination without cause or "good reason" exit pays 2x salary+bonus+performance shares in cash, plus immediate equity acceleration; change-in-control also accelerates equity.
- Shareholder "say on pay" support has swung from ~47% approval in 2023 to ~87% in June 2026.

Live Nation Entertainment has extended CEO Michael Rapino’s employment through the end of 2031 under a new agreement that roughly doubles his target annual compensation beginning next year, adding $30 million in new yearly equity awards to an already substantial pay package.

The [amended employment agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1335258/000133525826000041/lyv-20260930.htm?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), signed Sept. 30 and effective Oct. 1, keeps Rapino as Live Nation’s president, CEO and a member of its board through Dec. 31, 2031. His previous contract was scheduled to expire at the end of 2027.

Beginning in 2027, Rapino’s stated annual target compensation will total at least $60 million: a $3 million salary, $17 million target cash performance bonus, at least $10 million in annual performance shares, $15 million in time-based restricted stock units and another $15 million in performance share units.

The first three components were already included in [Rapino’s 2022 employment agreement](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1335258/000133525822000114/lyv-20220701.htm?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). The new contract adds the two $15 million annual equity grants, effectively doubling his contractual target compensation from at least $30 million to at least $60 million per year.

## New Equity Awards Double Target Package

Live Nation characterized the new package as 70% performance-based compensation, 25% time-based equity and 5% guaranteed salary.

Rapino’s $3 million annual salary and $17 million cash performance-bonus target remain unchanged from the contract he signed in 2022. He also retains an annual performance-based restricted-stock opportunity with a target value of at least $10 million.

What changes substantially is the amount of additional equity Rapino can receive each year.

Beginning in 2027, he will receive a $15 million annual grant of time-based RSUs. Those awards will vest in 20% increments on each of the following five anniversaries, assuming Rapino remains employed by the company.

Live Nation will also grant him $15 million in target-value performance share units annually. Those awards have three-year performance periods and are tied to Live Nation’s total shareholder return relative to companies in the S&P 500.

Rapino can receive twice the target number of shares if Live Nation’s relative shareholder return reaches at least the 75th percentile of the comparison group. Performance at the 50th percentile produces a target payout, while the award falls to 25% at the 25th percentile and zero below that level.

That means the $15 million annual PSU award can ultimately produce shares worth considerably more or less than its stated target grant value depending on Live Nation’s performance and stock price.

The distinction is important for the headline number. The $60 million is a target compensation package, not a guaranteed $60 million annual paycheck. Equity values, company performance and vesting conditions can cause Rapino’s eventual realized compensation to differ substantially.

Still, if the stated target amounts remain unchanged, the five calendar years from 2027 through 2031 represent at least $300 million in annual target compensation, before counting the separate $20 million upfront grant.

## Rapino Gets $20 Million Upfront Stock Grant

Live Nation granted Rapino the additional $20 million in RSUs when the new agreement took effect.

A [separate Form 4 filed Friday](https://investors.livenationentertainment.com/sec-filings/section-16-filings/content/0001337041-26-000021/wk-form4_1790975357.html?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) shows the award consisted of 117,805 restricted stock units, each representing the right to receive one Live Nation share.

Forty percent is scheduled to vest on Oct. 1, 2027; another 20% in each of 2028 and 2029; and 10% in each of 2030 and 2031.

The grant value was calculated using Live Nation’s average closing share price over the 10 trading days immediately preceding the award rather than the stock price on a single trading day.

The contract also contains significant provisions governing Rapino’s unvested equity if his employment ends before 2031.

If Live Nation terminates him without cause or Rapino leaves for qualifying “good reason,” he would receive a lump-sum cash payment equal to twice the combined value of his salary, most recent performance bonus and most recently earned annual performance shares. His unvested equity would also immediately accelerate, with outstanding performance share units vesting at target.

A change in control would similarly accelerate his unvested equity, although performance share units would be calculated based on performance through the date of the transaction.

## Rapino Pay Has Previously Drawn Shareholder Pushback

The latest agreement follows several years in which Rapino’s compensation and equity holdings have grown alongside Live Nation.

Rapino received approximately $32.55 million in reported compensation for 2025, including a $3 million salary, about $10 million in stock awards, more than $17 million in non-equity incentive compensation and more than $2 million in other compensation.

His 2022 compensation was considerably larger because of equity granted when his previous employment agreement was signed. Live Nation reported approximately $139 million in total compensation for Rapino that year, including about $116.7 million in stock awards.

That package prompted unusually strong shareholder opposition. At Live Nation’s 2023 annual meeting, [more than 53% of votes cast rejected the company’s executive-compensation proposal](https://www.ticketnews.com/2023/06/live-nation-investors-slam-excessive-pay-for-rapino-executives/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in the advisory “say on pay” vote.

Shareholder sentiment has since moved substantially in the other direction. At the company’s June 2026 meeting, approximately 188 million shares voted for Live Nation’s executive-compensation proposal and 28.7 million voted against it, amounting to roughly 87% support among shares voting either for or against.

Rapino also already holds a significant personal stake in the company. TicketNews reported in August that he directly owned approximately 4.2 million Live Nation shares following another scheduled vesting of earlier stock awards, a stake valued at roughly $761 million using the share price reflected in that transaction.

The new agreement increases the amount of Rapino’s compensation tied to Live Nation’s stock performance while securing his leadership of the concert promotion and Ticketmaster parent company through another five full calendar years.

The new $60 million annual target structure begins in 2027. Rapino’s 2026 cash bonus and annual performance-based equity award will continue to be governed by his previous employment agreement.
