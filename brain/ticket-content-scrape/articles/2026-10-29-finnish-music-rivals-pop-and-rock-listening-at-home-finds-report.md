# Finnish music rivals pop and rock listening at home, finds report

## Summary

A new "Music Listening in Finland" industry report found Finnish-language pop ties with rock and English-language pop as the country's most popular genre, each liked by 53% of the population. The findings point to growing appeal of local-language music across the broader European market.

### Key Points
- Finnish-language pop, rock and English-language pop are statistically tied at 53% popularity
- Finnish schlager and metal follow at 40% each
- Rap/hip hop, indie rock and K-pop are named as growing in demand
- Report framed as part of a wider trend of local-language music gaining traction across Europe
