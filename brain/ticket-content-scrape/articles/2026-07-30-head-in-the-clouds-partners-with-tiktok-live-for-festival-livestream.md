# Head In The Clouds Partners with TikTok Live For Festival Livestream

## Summary

Goldenvoice and 88rising have partnered with TikTok Live to officially livestream this year's Head in the Clouds Music and Arts Festival, held Aug. 8 at Brookside at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena. The livestream will include festival commentary, artist interviews, and a special edition of TikTok's "TikTok Live: On Air" show, plus a TAAF digital series produced with Wong Fu Productions. The festival is headlined by KATSEYE alongside XG, Dabin.kr, UMI, LNGSHOT, and KiiiKiii, spotlighting the global reach of the Asian diaspora.

### Key Points
- TikTok Live is the official livestream partner for Head in the Clouds 2026, taking place Aug. 8 at Brookside at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena.
- Programming includes festival commentary, artist interviews, and a dedicated episode of "TikTok Live: On Air."
- The Asian American Foundation (TAAF) will debut "The United States of Asian+America," a digital series made with Wong Fu Productions, as part of the stream.
- The 2026 lineup is headlined by KATSEYE, with XG, Dabin.kr, UMI, LNGSHOT, and KiiiKiii also performing.
- Tickets to the in-person festival remain available at the time of publication.

Goldenvoice and 88rising have partnered with TikTok Live for the official livestream of this year’s Head in the Clouds Music and Arts Festival, taking place Aug. 8 at Brookside at the Rose Bowl in Pasadena, California.

The livestream will feature a variety of original programming including festival commentary and artist interviews in addition to a special edition of the platform’s interactive podcast-style show “TikTok Live: On Air.”

The 2026 Head in the Clouds lineup highlights the global reach of the Asian diaspora. The festival will be headlined by KATSEYE, who will be joined by Japanese hip/hop/R&B group XG, South Korean rapper Dabin.kr, singer/songwriter UMI, Jay Park-produced K-Pop boy group LNGSHOT and South Korean girl group KiiiKiii among others.

As part of the livestream, The Asian American Foundation (TAAF) will share “The United States of Asian+America,” a digital series produced in partnership with Wong Fu Productions that highlights he untold stories of AAPI pioneers who helped shape America and paved the way for organizations like music and media company 88rising. Tickets for the festival are still available.
