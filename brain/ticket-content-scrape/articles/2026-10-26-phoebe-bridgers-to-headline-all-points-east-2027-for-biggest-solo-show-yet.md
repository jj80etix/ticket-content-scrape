# Phoebe Bridgers to Headline All Points East 2027 for Biggest Solo Show Yet

## Summary

Phoebe Bridgers will headline London's All Points East festival on August 27, 2027 — billed as the biggest solo headlining show of her career — alongside Caroline Polachek, Kelsey Lu, Danielle Ponder, and jasmine.4.t. The booking follows her busy 2026, including a new album and expanded "The Lost Tour."

### Key Points
- First headliner announced for All Points East 2027, at Victoria Park.
- Follows release of third solo album *Lost Weekend* and an expanded arena tour.
- General on-sale Oct. 29; festival and Amex presales begin Oct. 28.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Phoebe-Bridgers-All-Points-East.webp)

Phoebe Bridgers will headline London’s All Points East festival on August 27, 2027, at Victoria Park, in what organizers are calling the biggest solo headlining show of her career.

Bridgers is the first headliner announced for the 2027 edition of the festival, with Caroline Polachek, Kelsey Lu, Danielle Ponder and jasmine.4.t also confirmed for the date. Additional performers will be announced in the coming months.

The festival appearance follows a busy year for Bridgers, who released her third solo studio album, *Lost Weekend*, in August. She also [announced The Lost Tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/phoebe-bridgers-announces-2026-the-lost-tour-across-north-america-uk-and-europe/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content), with arena dates across North America, the United Kingdom and Europe. The tour was subsequently [expanded with additional performances](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/06/phoebe-bridgers-expands-the-lost-tour/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) in several markets, including San Francisco, Los Angeles, Dublin and London.

The North American leg is getting ready to wrap up with two San Francisco shows scheduled for October 27 and 28 at Chase Center, followed by three Los Angeles shows at Inuit Dome on October 30, 31 and November 1. Bridgers will then head to Europe. Her upcoming schedule includes two nights at London’s O2 Arena on December 1 and 2, with Isaac Wood supporting the UK and European dates.

The All Points East booking will bring Bridgers back to London less than nine months after those arena performances. She is also scheduled to perform at Primavera Sound in Barcelona.

Tickets for Bridgers’ August 27 performance will go on general sale Thursday, October 29, at 10 a.m. GMT. A festival presale begins Wednesday, October 28, at 10 a.m. GMT, with an American Express cardmember presale also available.

Full ticket information and presale registration are available through the [All Points East website](https://www.allpointseastfestival.com/tickets/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).
