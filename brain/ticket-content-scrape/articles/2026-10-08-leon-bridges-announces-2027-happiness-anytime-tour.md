# Leon Bridges Announces 2027 ‘Happiness Anytime Tour’

## Summary

Leon Bridges announced "The Happiness Anytime Tour," launching April 2027 in support of his new album *Happiness Anytime*, with stops including Madison Square Garden, Seattle's Climate Pledge Arena, and a close at Phoenix's Arizona Financial Theatre on Sept. 29, 2027. Support acts include The Womack Sisters, Rachel Chinouriri, and Kokoroko.

### Key Points
- Tour runs April–September 2027; general tickets on sale Oct. 16, artist presale Oct. 13, venue/local presales Oct. 14.
- $1 per headline ticket benefits The Big Good, a Texas-region nonprofit co-founded by Bridges.
- Album *Happiness Anytime* was created with Jungle's J Lloyd and Lydia Kitto.
- Support lineup varies by date: The Womack Sisters, Rachel Chinouriri, Mari Froes, Kokoroko, Peter Cat Recording Co., El Michels Affair.

Leon Bridges has announced “The Happiness Anytime Tour,” kicking off in April 2027, to support his latest album. Bridges will travel to Las Vegas to perform at The Chelsea at Cosmopolitan, then hit Madison Square Garden in NYC, Seattle’s Climate Pledge Arena, the TD Pavilion at Highmark Mann in Philadelphia, the Walmart Arkansas Music Pavilion in Rogers, AR, and more. The musician will then conclude the tour at Arizona Financial Theatre in Phoenix, AZ on Sept. 29, 2027.

The tour will also feature supporting acts such as The Womack Sisters, Rachel Chinouriri, Mari Froes, Kokoroko, Peter Cat Recording Co., and the El Michels Affair. See full tour routing below.

Tickets go on sale Oct 16 at 10 a.m. local time. Before then, fans can sign up for the artist presale, which goes live on Oct 13 at 10 a.m. local time, followed by venue and local presales on Oct 14 at 10 a.m. local time.

$1 per ticket from all headline shows will be donated to The Big Good, the 501c3 organization that partners with local organizations working to meet critical, unmet needs across the Texas region, co-founded by Bridges and legendary football coach Gary Patterson.

As for the album *Happiness Anytime*, the LP was created in collaboration with Jungle’s J Lloyd and Lydia Kitto, and is about “movement, cutting through the stresses of modern life and grounding you in the rhythms of the present,” according to an official press release.
