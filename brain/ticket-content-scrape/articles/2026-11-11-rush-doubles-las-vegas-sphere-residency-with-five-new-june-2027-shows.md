# Rush Doubles Las Vegas Sphere Residency With Five New June 2027 Shows

## Summary

Rush doubled its upcoming Las Vegas Sphere residency to 10 shows spanning May and June 2027, adding five June dates after strong demand for the original announcement. The expanded "Elevate From the Norm" run will feature founding members Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson alongside touring additions Anika Nilles and Loren Gold.

### Key Points
- Residency now totals 10 shows: five in May, five in June 2027, all at Sphere, Las Vegas
- General public onsale Friday, Nov 13, 10am Pacific via Rush's official residency site
- Set may include full-album performances of 2112 and Moving Pictures
- Shows will honor late drummer/lyricist Neil Peart, who died in 2020

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/RUSH-Sphere.webp)

Rush has expanded its upcoming Las Vegas Sphere residency, adding five performances in June 2027 following strong demand for the band’s previously announced concerts.

The rock group announced the additional shows, doubling its *Elevate From the Norm* residency to 10 performances spanning May and June. The expansion comes less than a week after Rush [announced its original Sphere engagement](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/11/rush-sphere-residency-may-2027/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The general public onsale for all performances is scheduled for Friday, November 13, at 10 a.m. Pacific. Tickets and packages are available through [Rush’s official residency website](https://www.rush.com/sphere/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

The residency will feature founding Rush members Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson alongside drummer Anika Nilles and keyboardist Loren Gold, who joined the musicians for their *Fifty Something Tour* following the band’s return to live performances.
