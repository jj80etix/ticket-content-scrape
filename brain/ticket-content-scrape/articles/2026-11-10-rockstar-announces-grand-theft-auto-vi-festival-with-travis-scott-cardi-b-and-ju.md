# Rockstar Announces Grand Theft Auto VI Festival With Travis Scott, Cardi B and Justice

## Summary

Rockstar Games announced "Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival," a Miami music festival marking the game's release, headlined by Travis Scott, Cardi B and Justice on Nov. 21 at Mana Wynwood.

### Key Points
- Presented with PlayStation and produced by the team behind III Points festival
- Lineup also includes Metro Boomin, Tainy, Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso and others across two stages
- Entry via presale lottery registration (deadline Nov. 10), with selected entrants able to buy tickets Nov. 11

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Grand-Theft-Auto-The-Festival.webp)

Rockstar Games has announced a Miami music festival to mark the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. Travis Scott, Cardi B and Justice will lead the November 21 event at Mana Wynwood.

“Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival” will feature artists from the game and its official soundtrack across two stages. Presented in partnership with PlayStation, the festival is being produced by the creators of III Points.

Scott’s performance is billed as Cactus Jack presents, with Don Toliver, Kodak Black and Sofaygo also appearing under that banner. Cardi B will perform, while French electronic duo Justice is scheduled for a DJ set.

The lineup also includes Ca7riel & Paco Amoroso, Metro Boomin, Rauw & Friends, Suero and Tainy. Several artists will share back-to-back sets, including Nick León with DJ Python and Prospa with Skream. The Dare and Fcukers are also booked for a back-to-back DJ set.
