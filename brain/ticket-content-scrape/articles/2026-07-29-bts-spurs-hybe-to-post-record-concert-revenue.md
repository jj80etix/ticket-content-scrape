# BTS spurs HYBE to post record concert revenue

## Summary

HYBE's concert revenue overtook recorded-music revenue for the first time in Q2, surging 630% quarter-over-quarter on the strength of BTS's Arirang World Tour, which also set touring records.

### Key Points
- Concert revenue reached KRW 647.7bn, up 630% QoQ and 243.3% YoY.
- Total revenue hit KRW 1.45 trillion, up 105.5% YoY, with operating margin at 11.8% — a swing back to profit after a Q1 loss.
- BTS grossed $127.8M on 641,000 tickets across 12 May shows, the largest monthly gross by a group since Billboard's chart began in 2019.
- The tour continues through March 2027 across the Americas, Asia, and Oceania.

## Transcript

TheTicketingBusiness News

**South Korean entertainment company HYBE, which is behind K-pop group BTS, has earned more revenue from concerts than recorded music in Q2 this year.**

The organisation’s revenue more than doubled year-over-year to a record KRW 1.45 trillion (£750m/$1bn/€875m) in the second quarter of this year.

That was up 105.5% year-on-year and 107.6% quarter-on-quarter, in what HYBE said was a record quarterly result.

Concert revenue was the standout figure, surging 630.0% QoQ and 243.3% YoY to KRW 647.7bn.

Revenue from artist direct-involvement activities, which covers recorded music, concerts, and advertising, rose by 132.2% YoY to KRW 1.04 trillion, marking an all-time high for the company.

![](https://www.theticketingbusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/hybe-financial-statement-q2-2026-1024x564.webp)

Credit: HYBE

BTS’ Arirang World Tour, launched in April after the first quarter reporting period had closed, is thought to have had an instrumental impact on the concert revenue figures.

Operating profit reached KRW 170.9bn, up 159.3% YoY, with an operating margin of 11.8% to mark a return to profit for HYBE after it posted an operating loss of KRW 196.6bn in the first quarter of the year.

BTS had the highest-grossing and best-selling tour in May, surpassing The Rolling Stones’ biggest monthly gross by a group since Billboard began its Top Tours chart in 2019.

The group grossed $127.8m and selling 641,000 tickets over 12 shows between May 2 and 28.

This was the group’s second straight month in the top spot for tours, and its fourth month reaching number one since May 2019.

Originally released in March, BTS’ Arirang album returned to the Top 10 of the Billboard 200 album charts this week.

Earlier this month, the group also co-headlined the FIFA World Cup final halftime show at MetLife Stadium before resuming the world tour on August 1 at the same venue in New Jersey.

The tour is then set to continue to March 16 next year with dates in Canada, Colombia, Peru, Chile, Argentina, Brazil, Taiwan, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia, Australia, Hong Kong, and the Philippines.
