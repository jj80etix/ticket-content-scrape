# Miranda Lambert to Headline Cowboys’ Thanksgiving Halftime Show

## Summary

Miranda Lambert will headline the Dallas Cowboys' Thanksgiving Day halftime show on November 26, marking the 30th anniversary of the Salvation Army's Red Kettle Kickoff. The televised AT&T Stadium performance supports a campaign that has raised over $3.4 billion over three decades.

### Key Points
- Performance airs on Fox during the Cowboys-Eagles Thanksgiving game.
- Marks the 30th year of the Red Kettle Kickoff halftime tradition, started in 1997 by Reba McEntire.
- Past headliners include Destiny's Child, Carrie Underwood, and Post Malone.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Miranda-Lambert-Halftime-Show.webp)

Miranda Lambert will headline the Dallas Cowboys’ Thanksgiving Day halftime show November 26, performing at AT&T Stadium during the team’s game against the Philadelphia Eagles.

The Texas native will lead the Salvation Army Red Kettle Kickoff Halftime Show as the annual event marks its 30th anniversary. Fox will broadcast the game beginning at 3:30 p.m. Central time.

In a statement Lambert pointed to the charitable purpose behind the performance and the opportunity to spend Thanksgiving in Texas.

“There’s something special about how music brings people together, especially when it’s to spread joy and hope to families who need it most,” Lambert said. “The Salvation Army and the Dallas Cowboys have built an incredible tradition with the Red Kettle Kickoff, and I can’t wait to be back home in Texas this Thanksgiving to help carry it forward.”

The Red Kettle Campaign has raised more than $3.4 billion over the past three decades. Donations support assistance in the communities where the contributions are collected, with the campaign addressing needs during the holidays and throughout the year.

Charlotte Jones, the Cowboys’ chief brand officer and co-owner and a former national advisory board chairman for The Salvation Army, said the halftime event remains an important part of the organization’s Thanksgiving schedule.

“The Red Kettle Kickoff has become one of the Dallas Cowboys’ proudest traditions and one my family and our entire organization look forward to every Thanksgiving,” Jones said.

“For 30 years, incredible artists have appeared on our halftime stage to help The Salvation Army bring hope to millions of people in need. We’re grateful to Miranda Lambert for joining that legacy and helping us continue this meaningful tradition.”

Reba McEntire headlined the inaugural Red Kettle Kickoff in 1997. Since then, the event has featured performers across country and pop, including Destiny’s Child, Carrie Underwood, the Jonas Brothers and Selena Gomez. Lainey Wilson and Post Malone have also led the Cowboys’ Thanksgiving halftime entertainment.

Dolly Parton performed in 2023, taking the field in an outfit modeled after the Dallas Cowboys Cheerleaders’ uniforms.

Lambert’s appearance follows the October 2 release of her 10th solo studio album, *Crisco*. The record debuted at No. 4 on Billboard’s Top Country Albums chart, extending her uninterrupted run of top 10 entries on that chart to 11.

Her Thanksgiving booking also adds another music performance to the NFL’s holiday schedule. U2 was announced last week as the halftime act for the league’s first Thanksgiving Eve game, featuring the Green Bay Packers and Los Angeles Rams at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood, California.
