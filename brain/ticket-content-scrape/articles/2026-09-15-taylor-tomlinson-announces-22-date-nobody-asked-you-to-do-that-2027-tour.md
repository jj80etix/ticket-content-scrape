# Taylor Tomlinson Announces 22-Date ‘Nobody Asked You To Do That’ 2027 Tour

## Summary

Taylor Tomlinson announced the first 22 dates of her 2027 stand-up tour "Nobody Asked You To Do That," running Feb. 4 through May 22, 2027, with a presale beginning Sept. 16 and general on-sale Sept. 18.

### Key Points
- Tour opens in Spokane, WA and closes at the Taft Theatre in Cincinnati.
- Includes a May 1 stop at Nashville's Ryman Auditorium; Salt Lake City show has a separate Sept. 25 on-sale.
- Tomlinson's first book, Actually, Nevermind, publishes Sept. 22 via Gallery Books.
- She currently has four Netflix stand-up specials to her name.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Taylor-Tomlinson.webp)

Taylor Tomlinson is heading back on the road in 2027, announcing the first 22 dates of her new stand-up tour, *Nobody Asked You To Do That*.

The first leg begins February 4 at the First Interstate Center for the Arts in Spokane, Washington, and runs through May 22 at the Taft Theatre in Cincinnati. Other stops include Salt Lake City, Raleigh, San Antonio, Cleveland, Baltimore, Indianapolis, Pittsburgh and Detroit, along with a May 1 performance at Nashville’s Ryman Auditorium.

A tour presale begins Wednesday, September 16 at 10 a.m. local time using the code NOBODY. Remaining tickets go on sale to the general public Friday, September 18 at 10 a.m. local time.

The Salt Lake City show at Eccles Theater has a separate onsale schedule, with tickets becoming available September 25 at 10 a.m. local time.

Tomlinson enters the new tour with four Netflix stand-up specials already to her name: *Quarter-Life Crisis*, *Look At You*, *Have It All* and her most recent release, *Prodigal Daughter*. Her comedy frequently draws on her experiences with family, relationships, faith and mental health.

Additionally, the tour announcement arrives just ahead of another new project from Tomlinson. Her first book, *Actually, Nevermind*, is scheduled for publication September 22 through Gallery Books. The collection of autobiographical essays centers on subjects Tomlinson has changed her mind about and the contradictions she has encountered navigating adulthood.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit [Tomlinson’s official website](https://taylortomlinsoncomedy.com/).
