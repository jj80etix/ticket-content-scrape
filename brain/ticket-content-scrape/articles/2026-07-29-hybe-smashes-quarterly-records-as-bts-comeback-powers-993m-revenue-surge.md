# HYBE Smashes Quarterly Records as BTS Comeback Powers $993M Revenue Surge

## Summary

HYBE posted a record $993M Q2 revenue, up 105.5% year-over-year, driven by BTS's Arirang tour comeback and continued growth on its Weverse fan platform.

### Key Points
- Q2 revenue KRW 1.45 trillion ($993M), operating profit up 159.3% YoY.
- BTS's Arirang tour and album fueled the surge; 119 shows played in Q2 with 200+ more planned for H2.
- Weverse MAUs rose 8% QoQ to 14.43 million; average revenue per paying user jumped 24%.
- HYBE's wider roster (Tomorrow X Together, Enhypen, BOYNEXTDOOR and others) also posted strong album sales.

HYBE has posted the biggest quarter in its history, fueled by BTS’ blockbuster comeback, accelerating tour activity and continued growth across its global fan platform Weverse.

The K-pop powerhouse generated 1.45 trillion won ($993 million) in second-quarter revenue, up 105.5% year over year. Operating profit climbed 159.3% to 170.9 billion won.

The results marked the first time HYBE’s quarterly revenue surpassed 1 trillion won and operating profit topped 100 billion won.

BTS played a major role in the record-breaking quarter after launching its world tour in April and releasing its fifth studio album, *ARIRANG, 아리랑*. According to Luminate’s 2026 Midyear Report, the album led U.S. vinyl and CD sales during the first half of the year.

HYBE said the release also helped South Korea become the world’s third-largest music exporter, trailing only the United States and United Kingdom.

Live performances provided another major boost. HYBE Music Group artists staged 119 shows during the quarter, with more than 200 additional performances scheduled for the second half of 2026. The company now expects to comfortably surpass last year’s total of 279 shows.

HYBE’s wider roster also delivered strong album sales. Tomorrow X Together, Enhypen, &Team, BoyNextDoor, TWS and Cortis each surpassed 1 million copies during the first half of the year, according to Circle Chart.

Meanwhile, Weverse recorded its strongest engagement to date. Monthly active users rose 8% quarter over quarter to 14.43 million, while total payment volume increased 12% and average revenue per paying user jumped 24%. The platform also added communities for Filipino pop groups BINI and SB19, taking its total beyond 200.
