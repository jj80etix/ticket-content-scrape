# The Country Music Association Reveals Early Winners of “The 60th Annual CMA Awards” Airing Live Tonight From Bridgestone Arena In Nashville

## Summary

The Country Music Association revealed early winners ahead of "The 60th Annual CMA Awards," airing live tonight from Bridgestone Arena in Nashville. HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen, and Tim McGraw won Musical Event of the Year for "McArthur," while Ella Langley took Music Video of the Year for "Choosin' Texas."

### Key Points
- Musical Event of the Year: HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen, Tim McGraw for "McArthur."
- Music Video of the Year: Ella Langley for "Choosin' Texas."
- Winners revealed live on ABC's "Good Morning America" from Nashville.
- Main ceremony airs live at 8 p.m. ET on ABC, Disney+, and next-day on Hulu, hosted by Lainey Wilson.

HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw earn CMA Musical Event of the Year for “McArthur,” produced by Jay Joyce

The celebration of “The 60 <sup>th</sup> Annual CMA Awards” started a little early with the Country Music Association revealing early winners in two categories: HARDY, Eric Church, Morgan Wallen and Tim McGraw earn CMA Musical Event of the Year for “McArthur,” produced by Jay Joyce while Ella Langley takes home the win for CMA Music Video of the Year for “Choosin’ Texas,” directed by Langley, Caylee Robillard and Wales Toney.

The announcement took place live on ABC’s “Good Morning America,” with Lara Spencer sharing the news from downtown Nashville’s Honky Tonk Central. Viewers also got an exclusive look inside Bridgestone Arena with returning CMA Awards host Lainey Wilson, who gave a behind-the-scenes look as final preparations for the big night get underway.

“The 60th Annual CMA Awards” airs live at 8:00 PM/ET on ABC, Disney+ and Next Day on Hulu. The gala is a production of the Country Music Association. Robert Deaton serves as Executive Producer, Alan Carter is Director, and Jon Macks is Head Writer. First held in 1967, the CMA Awards is the longest-running, annual music awards program on network television. Each year, the show honors outstanding achievement in Country Music – celebrating the artists, songwriters, musicians, and industry professionals whose work has shaped the genre. Winners and nominees are determined by CMA’s Professional voting membership, reflecting the voices of their peers across the industry.
