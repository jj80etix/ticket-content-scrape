# Deep Purple Postpone Remainder of European and UK Tour Following Ian Gillan Hospitalization

## Summary

Deep Purple postponed the remainder of its European and UK tour after frontman Ian Gillan was hospitalized in Leipzig for an urgent medical issue. No rescheduled dates have been announced yet.

### Key Points
- Affects 4 remaining German dates (Nov. 3-7) plus 11 shows in the Netherlands, France and the UK.
- Gillan's medical team advised against continuing the tour; replacement dates not yet set.
- Shows in Leipzig (Oct. 31) and Hamburg (Nov. 1) were already postponed before the broader announcement.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Deep-Purple.webp)

Deep Purple has postponed the remainder of its European and UK tour following frontman Ian Gillan’s hospitalization. The band is working to reschedule the affected concerts, but replacement dates have not been announced.

Gillan explained the circumstances in a November 2 statement. “I had a medical problem whilst in Leipzig which required urgent attention at the hospital,” he said.

The singer had hoped to finish the tour, but said continuing would carry serious health risks. Following advice from his medical team and management, the band postponed its remaining European and UK performances until further notice.

Deep Purple initially postponed its October 31 concert in Leipzig and November 1 appearance in Hamburg. The broader announcement affected four additional German shows scheduled between November 3 and November 7, followed by 11 performances in the Netherlands, France and the UK.

The UK portion included concerts in Newcastle, Glasgow, Birmingham and Manchester before two London appearances. The tour was scheduled to conclude November 25 at Royal Albert Hall.

The band’s announcement also addressed ticket arrangements.

“All tickets purchased will be valid for the rescheduled dates,” the statement said.

Deep Purple and its team are seeking a suitable window to stage the postponed performances. No timeline for announcing the revised schedule has been provided.

Venues have also issued notices to customers. Glasgow’s OVO Hydro confirmed that its November 19 concert will be rescheduled and advised fans to retain their tickets. Manchester’s AO Arena has listed its November 22 performance as postponed.

Ticket holders seeking refund information should contact their original point of purchase, as policies may differ by event and ticket provider.

Gillan, 81, subsequently shared a recovery update on November 7, confirming that he had undergone treatment the previous day.

“I had a procedure yesterday which was successful,” he said.

The singer said he would need more time to rest, recover fully and regain his fitness before returning to performing. He thanked fans for their patience and apologized for the disruption to the band’s schedule.

Neither Gillan nor Deep Purple has disclosed the nature of his medical condition or the procedure. His return to the stage has not been scheduled.

Deep Purple’s tour calendar continues to list six Latin American concerts in December, beginning December 5 in São Paulo, Brazil, and concluding December 19 in Mexico City. Those performances were not included in the European and UK postponement announcement. The band has not separately confirmed whether the December concerts will proceed.
