# Alicia Keys Signs With CAA

## Summary

Seventeen-time GRAMMY winner Alicia Keys has signed with talent agency CAA. The move covers an artist with over 65 million records sold and 5 billion-plus streams, whose credits include the Broadway musical "Hell's Kitchen" (13 Tony nominations, two wins, and a 2025 GRAMMY for Best Musical Theater Album). Keys remains managed by Roc Nation and has unspecified new projects reportedly in development.

### Key Points
- Alicia Keys signs with CAA for representation.
- Career totals: 65M+ records sold, 5B+ streams since her 2001 debut album "Songs in A Minor."
- "Hell's Kitchen" (Broadway, opened April 20, 2024 at the Shubert Theater) earned 13 Tony nominations, 2 wins, and the 2025 GRAMMY for Best Musical Theater Album.
- Keys is a founding member of She Is The Music, a nonprofit for women's advancement in the music industry.
- Continues to be managed by Roc Nation.

Alicia Keys, the 17-time GRAMMY Award-winning singer, songwriter, musician, and producer, has signed with CAA. Keys’ career hasn’t slowed down since she first arrived with her debut album, *Songs in A Minor*, in 2001. In the years since then, the icon has sold over 65 million records and amassed over 5 billion streams.

Additionally, Alicia Keys created *Hell’s Kitchen*, a Broadway musical, featuring music and lyrics by the songstress, inspired by her life growing up in NYC in the 1990s, which opened on Broadway at the Shubert Theater on April 20, 2024. *Hell’s Kitchen* was met with critical acclaim and commercial success and went on to receive 13 Tony Award nominations, with two Tony Award wins, and the 2025 GRAMMY Award for Best Musical Theater Album.

Alicia is a founding member of She Is The Music, a global nonprofit to advance women’s careers in music through professional development, long-term sustainability, and community, while creating greater opportunity and equity across the industry, and an advocate for a new vision called The People’s Bill of Rights 250.

And, as per an official press release, Alicia Keys “has an exciting slate of new projects on the horizon.”

Alicia is managed by Roc Nation.
