# Coachella & Goldenvoice sue Walmart for ‘repeated’ trademark infringements

## Summary

Coachella and promoter Goldenvoice have sued Walmart, alleging the retailer repeatedly sold merchandise bearing the "Coachella Marks" without a license. Detail on the specific merchandise, damages sought, and case venue is behind IQ's paywall; the available text covers only the trademark-infringement claim and that Walmart is named as "the world's largest retailer."

### Key Points
- Coachella/Goldenvoice allege repeated, unlicensed use of Coachella trademarks on Walmart-sold merchandise.
- Framed as part of a pattern of trademark enforcement by Coachella (prior suits cited: Urban Outfitters, Afrochella, Filmchella).
- No ticketing, pricing, or regulatory angle — this is brand/IP litigation, not ticketing-market litigation.
