# Blink-182, Korn, Pierce The Veil, Bad Omens Headline Welcome to Rockville 2027

## Summary

Welcome To Rockville revealed its full 2027 lineup, with Blink-182, Korn, Pierce The Veil and Bad Omens headlining the four-day festival May 6-9 at Daytona International Speedway. Organizer Danny Wimmer Presents announced more than 150 acts across five stages, including several reunions and anniversary sets.

### Key Points
- Daily headliners: Korn (Thu, with Alice in Chains, Faith No More), Bad Omens (Fri), Pierce The Veil (Sat, with Good Charlotte, The Offspring), Blink-182 (Sun, with MGK, Papa Roach)
- 150+ artists across five stages
- Other notable names: The Prodigy, Judas Priest, Marilyn Manson, Cypress Hill, Stone Temple Pilots, Meshuggah
- Full lineup and ticket info available on the festival's official site

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Welcome-to-Rockville-2027-1200.webp)

Welcome to Rockville has unveiled its 2027 lineup, with Blink-182, Korn, Pierce The Veil and Bad Omens set to headline the four-day festival May 6-9 at Daytona International Speedway in Daytona Beach, Florida.

Organizer Danny Wimmer Presents announced the complete bill Thursday, Oct. 29, featuring more than 150 artists across five stages. The lineup brings together rock, metal, punk and alternative acts, along with several reunions, anniversary performances and festival-exclusive sets.

Korn will lead Thursday, May 6, joined by Alice in Chains and Faith No More. Friday, May 7, will feature Bad Omens, $uicideboy$ and Ice Nine Kills. Pierce The Veil will headline Saturday, May 8, alongside Good Charlotte and The Offspring, while Blink-182 will close the weekend Sunday, May 9, with MGK and Papa Roach among the day’s other top-billed acts.

Other performers announced for the weekend include The Prodigy, Judas Priest, Marilyn Manson, Knocked Loose, Danzig, Bilmuri, I Prevail, Cypress Hill, 311, Stone Temple Pilots, The Pretty Reckless, Poppy, Black Veil Brides, Meshuggah and Machine Head. The [festival’s official lineup page](https://welcometorockville.com/lineup/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) has additional artist information.

## Reunions, Anniversary Sets and Special Appearances

Faith No More is scheduled to make its first North American appearance of the 2027 reunion at Rockville. The band also has performances booked in Brazil, Australia and New Zealand earlier in the year, according to its [official tour schedule](https://www.fnm.com/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Organizers are also billing Marilyn Manson for an exclusive *Antichrist Superstar* performance and Machine Head for an exclusive U.S. appearance. Skid Row is expected to return to the stage after a three-year absence as part of its 40th-anniversary celebration. Meshuggah will also mark its 40th anniversary at the festival, an appearance included in the band’s [2027 North American tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/10/meshuggah-2027-north-american-tour-dates/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content).

Circa Survive, Forever the Sickest Kids, Palisades and Cute Is What We Aim For are among the groups slated for reunion performances. A Heartwell Ending is set to play its first show in 19 years, while Relient K is booked for a rare festival appearance.

The 2027 festival will also feature changes to the grounds intended to improve comfort during the four-day event. Previously announced stage upgrades include panoramic video at the Apex stage, enhanced visuals at Octane, a larger and fully shaded Vortex stage, a tented Inferno stage with upgraded audio and video, and additional viewing opportunities at Garage. Three of the five stages will be tented.

Other additions include covered bleachers, more seating and umbrellas, new amusement rides, a new entrance and the Bonus Round Barcade. VIP areas will expand and relocate, with additional shade, permanent restrooms near both main stages and access to the Speedway’s Fan Zone. The premium Daytona Owners Club package will offer an expanded outdoor area, dedicated parking and shuttle access, and an air-conditioned lounge.

“We’re investing in a more comfortable, immersive festival without losing the energy that makes Rockville special,” said Chamie McCurry, general manager of Danny Wimmer Presents. McCurry said the investment includes more tented stages, additional seating and an expanded VIP experience.

## Welcome to Rockville 2027 Tickets and Passes

Single-day general admission passes are currently listed at $155, two-day GA passes at $290 and four-day GA passes at $445. Those advertised prices include fees and taxes, according to the [official festival ticket page](https://welcometorockville.com/passes/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content). Prices are subject to change as ticket tiers sell through.

Four-day VIP passes are listed at $845, while the four-day Daytona Owners Club package costs $2,400. Students with a valid.edu email address can access a $120 all-in single-day GA offer. The festival also lists installment-payment plans starting with a $1 down payment; that amount is the initial payment, not the total ticket price.

Jinjer will headline the Wednesday, May 5, pre-party at Daytona International Speedway, with admission included for campers and eligible hotel-package holders, according to organizers. Camping, hotel and parking options are offered separately from standard festival passes.

The festival reported attendance of 210,000 in 2026. Welcome to Rockville moved from Jacksonville to Daytona International Speedway in 2021, and [a long-term agreement announced last year](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/08/welcome-to-rockville-extends-daytona-stay-2035/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) is set to keep the event at the venue through 2035. Last year’s [2026 lineup](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/11/welcome-to-rockville-2026-guns-n-roses-foo-fighters/?utm_source=TicketNews&utm_medium=content) was led by Guns N’ Roses, Foo Fighters, Bring Me The Horizon and My Chemical Romance.
