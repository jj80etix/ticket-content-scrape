# Anthony Ramos Sets Three-Date NYC Residency At Nublu

## Summary

Anthony Ramos announced a three-date salsa residency, "Noches de Familia," at New York's Nublu in November and December, following the release of his new single "Wasn't Even Lookin'."

### Key Points
- Dates: Nov. 25, Dec. 2, Dec. 9 at Nublu, NYC.
- Coincides with new single "Wasn't Even Lookin'," produced by Sergio George.

Anthony Ramos, the Grammy Award-winning and Golden Globe and Emmy-nominated multihyphenate, has announced a three-date residency at New York City’s Nublu, called “Noches de Familia,” where the superstar will is set to perform live salsa. Ramos will grace the stage at Nublu starting Nov. 25, continuing Dec. 2, and concluding Dec. 9, where the actor and producer will “ bring his signature style, salsa moves and dynamic live performance to the stage,” as per an official press release. See full residency dates below.

Fans looking to attend the dynamic show can purchase tickets [here](https://anthonyramos.komi.io/).

Ramos is fresh off dropping his newest single, “Wasn’t Even Lookin’” via Ramos Music & The Orchard. Salsa legend Sergio George produced the track, which expands on Ramos’ English-language homage to salsa music and the sounds and culture that serve as the genre’s bedrock. George and Ramos previously collaborated on a string of fresh tracks, including “New York City” and “I Was the One”. Additionally, “Wasn’t Even Lookin’” has an official music video directed by Rio Noir.
