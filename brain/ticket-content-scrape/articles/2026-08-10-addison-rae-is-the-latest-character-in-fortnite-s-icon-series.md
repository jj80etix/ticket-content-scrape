# Addison Rae is the Latest Character in Fortnite’s ‘Icon Series’

## Summary

Addison Rae has joined Fortnite's "Icon Series," with skins and cosmetics tied to her "Fame is a Gun" music video and headlining tour released August 6 by Epic Games.

### Key Points
- The Addison Rae Outfit includes reactive skin variants (White, Fame Brite, Soda Black) plus themed Back Blings and Pickaxes.
- She joins prior Icon Series artists Lady Gaga, Ariana Grande, Doja Cat, Sabrina Carpenter, Billie Eilish and Chappell Roan.
- Rae says she grew up playing Fortnite and expects the tie-in to be a "big flex" for her younger brothers, who still play.

## Transcript

![Addison Rae in Fortnite | Photo via Epic Games](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/addison.jpg)

Addison Rae in Fortnite | Photo via Epic Games

Addison Rae fans can now score a Battle Royale win playing as the pop singer in Fortnite.

Fortnite owner Epic Games announced that Rae’s Fortnite skins dropped on August 6, bringing looks to the game from her music video “Fame is a Gun,” as well as outfits from her [global headlining tour](https://www.ticketnews.com/2025/06/addison-rae-sets-2025-headlining-tour-in-support-of-debut-album/). The Addison Rae Outfit includes a pink jacket, top, and skirt with a sunglasses toggle; the skin is reactive and comes in White, Fame Brite (pink and yellow) and Soda Black.

Fans can also look forward to using Turtle-on-Moon Back Bling, Addison’s Sundae Back Bling, the two-handed Fame Scepter Pickaxe, and the reactive Addison’s Biggest Fan Pickaxe.

“I used to play Fortnite quite a lot, I got introduced to it in high school,” Rae said in a statement, adding that her little brothers still play the game, so this will “be a big flex card.”
