# Superbloom ends after five editions due to venue constraints

## Summary

Munich's Superbloom festival is ending after five editions as promoter Goodlive cites venue constraints, with the Olympic Park site undergoing renovations projected to last until 2029. The article (paywalled beyond the lede) gives no further detail on replacement plans.

### Key Points
- Superbloom concludes after five editions.
- Cause cited is venue constraints tied to Munich's Olympic Park renovation, expected to run until 2029.
