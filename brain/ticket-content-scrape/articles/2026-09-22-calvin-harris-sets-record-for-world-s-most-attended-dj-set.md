# Calvin Harris sets record for world’s most-attended DJ set

## Summary

Calvin Harris earned a Guinness World Record for the largest-ever attendance at a DJ set, drawing an estimated 1.6 million people to a free pre-Carnival street performance in Sao Paulo, Brazil on Feb. 8, 2026, performing atop a trio eletrico parade float.

### Key Points
- Calvin Harris sets Guinness World Record for largest DJ-set audience: approximately 1.6M attendees.
- Performance at Sao Paulo's Bloko Scol pre-Carnival celebration, Feb. 8, 2026.
- Performed aboard a trio eletrico mobile stage platform.
