# Katy Perry delivers monumental Mexico show to 310k fans

## Summary

Katy Perry played one of the largest shows of her career to a reported crowd of over 300,000 fans at the National Fair of San Luis Potosí in Mexico.

### Key Points
- Performance drew a reported 310,000 attendees, among the largest crowds of Perry's career.
- Took place as part of the National Fair of San Luis Potosí, Mexico.
