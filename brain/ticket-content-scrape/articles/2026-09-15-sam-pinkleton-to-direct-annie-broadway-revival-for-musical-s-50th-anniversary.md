# Sam Pinkleton to Direct ‘Annie’ Broadway Revival for Musical’s 50th Anniversary

## Summary

"Annie" is returning to Broadway for its 50th anniversary with a fall 2027 revival directed by Tony winner Sam Pinkleton, marking the musical's first Broadway production since 2012. Theater, casting, and ticket details have not yet been announced.

### Key Points
- Pinkleton won the 2025 Tony for Best Direction of a Play for "Oh, Mary!" and currently directs Broadway's "The Rocky Horror Show" revival.
- Revival arrives 50 years after Annie's original 1977 Broadway debut, which ran nearly six years and won seven Tony Awards including Best Musical.
- Broadway has hosted two prior revivals, in 1997 and 2012.
- A separate 50th-anniversary production opens at Goodspeed Musicals in Connecticut Oct. 30-Dec. 27, 2026, unrelated to the Broadway revival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/broadway-annie.webp)

“Annie” is heading back to Broadway for its 50th anniversary, with Tony Award-winning director Sam Pinkleton set to lead a new production scheduled for fall 2027.

The revival will mark the musical’s first Broadway production since the 2012 revival and will arrive 50 years after “Annie” made its Broadway debut in 1977. A theater, exact performance dates, casting and ticket-sale information have not yet been announced.

Pinkleton takes on the family musical following a run of high-profile Broadway projects. He won the 2025 Tony Award for Best Direction of a Play for [Cole Escola’s “Oh, Mary!”](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/01/broadway-hit-oh-mary-sets-2026-u-s-national-tour/) and currently directs the [Broadway revival of “The Rocky Horror Show”](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/02/the-rocky-horror-show-broadway-revival-reveals-full-cast/) at Studio 54.

“Annie” features music by Charles Strouse, lyrics by Martin Charnin and a book by Thomas Meehan. Based on Harold Gray’s “Little Orphan Annie” comic strip, the musical follows an orphan living in Depression-era New York City whose life changes after she is taken in by billionaire Oliver Warbucks.

The original Broadway production opened at the Alvin Theatre, now the Neil Simon Theatre, in April 1977. It won seven Tony Awards, including Best Musical, Best Original Score and Best Book of a Musical, and ultimately ran for nearly six years.

Broadway has since hosted two major revivals. The first opened in 1997, followed by another production in 2012.

The Broadway anniversary production will also follow renewed attention around the musical’s origins. Goodspeed Musicals, where “Annie” received its pre-Broadway premiere in 1976, is staging its own 50th anniversary production in Connecticut from October 30 through December 27, 2026. That production is separate from the Broadway revival announced for 2027.

Further details for “Annie,” including the Broadway theater, cast, opening date and ticket onsale schedule, are expected to be announced at a later date.
