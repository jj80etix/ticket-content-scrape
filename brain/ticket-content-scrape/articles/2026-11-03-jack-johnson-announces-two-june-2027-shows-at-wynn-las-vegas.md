# Jack Johnson Announces Two June 2027 Shows at Wynn Las Vegas

## Summary

Jack Johnson has announced two June 2027 shows at Wynn Las Vegas' Encore Theater, his first Vegas return in four years, with G. Love supporting both nights. The dates fit into a broader 2026-27 touring calendar spanning Australia/NZ, South America, Europe and festival dates.

### Key Points
- June 2-3, 2027 shows at the 1,480-capacity Encore Theater; tickets go on sale Nov 6.
- Promoted by AEG Presents as a 25-years-of-music retrospective set featuring archival footage.
- Currently touring Australia/NZ (through Nov 22) with Ben Harper and John Butler; heads to South America in February 2027.
- Additional 2027 festival slots include High Water Festival, Tollwood, Eden Sessions and BARTS Festival.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Jack-Johnson-Las-Vegas.webp)

Jack Johnson has announced two concerts at Wynn Las Vegas’ Encore Theater on June 2 and 3, 2027, marking his first return to the venue in four years.

Johnson will perform with his band at the 1,480-capacity theater, with G. Love joining both nights as a special guest. The booking follows Johnson’s “SURFILMUSIC” amphitheater performances across North America.

In a statement, promoter AEG Presents said Johnson “will present an expansive set celebrating more than 25 years of making music, pairing chart-topping songs with stories and archival footage from his early days as a surfer and filmmaker.”

Tickets for both shows go on sale Friday, November 6, 2026, at 10 a.m. Pacific time.

Johnson’s upcoming schedule includes an Australia and New Zealand run from November 6 through November 22, 2026, with Ben Harper and John Butler joining the shows. He will head to South America in February 2027 for concerts in Buenos Aires, Santiago, Rio de Janeiro, Florianópolis and São Paulo, with Donavon Frankenreiter and G. Love appearing at the Brazilian dates and Ben Harper & The Innocent Criminals also joining in São Paulo.

Johnson will return to Las Vegas’ Encore Theater on June 2 and 3 before his European dates in Brussels, Amsterdam, Paris and London.

His 2027 festival schedule includes High Water Festival in North Charleston on April 17, Zootown Music Festival in Missoula on June 19, Tollwood Festival in Munich on June 23, Citadel Music Festival in Berlin on June 24, Eden Sessions in Cornwall on July 2 and 3, BARTS Festival in Barcelona on July 12 and Ageas Cooljazz in Cascais on July 15.
