# Vibee bets big on experiential music travel as fandom deepens

## Summary

IQ interviewed Vibee president Harvey Cohen about how the Live Nation-owned destination-experience company is turning deepening artist fandom into a business, citing examples like selling 87,000 experience packages in 48 hours for Bad Bunny's Puerto Rico residency and drawing roughly 15,000 people daily to Las Vegas Sphere pop-ups. Most of the interview's detail is behind IQ's paywall, limiting what's available on Vibee's current strategy and roadmap.

### Key Points
- Vibee, a Live Nation company, launched in 2023 to build experiential travel/packages tied to artist fandom.
- Sold 87,000 experience packages within 48 hours for Bad Bunny's Puerto Rico residency.
- Drew ~15,000 people daily for pop-up experiences around Las Vegas Sphere shows.
- Full interview detail is paywalled beyond the opening summary.
