# Rush Announces 5-Date Sphere ‘Elevate From The Norm’ Vegas Residency May 21-30

## Summary

Rush announced a 5-date Sphere residency in Las Vegas, "Elevate From The Norm," running May 21–30, 2027, as an extension of the band's ongoing "Fifty Something" reunion tour. Tickets go on sale Nov. 13.

### Key Points
- Dates: May 21, 23, 26, 28, 30, 2027; tickets on sale Nov. 13.
- Billed as a celebration of Rush's catalog and the legacy of late drummer Neil Peart.
- Follows the band's ongoing multi-continent "Fifty Something" comeback tour.
- First Rush dates since the 2015 "R40" tour with Peart.

## Transcript

![unnamed (32)](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/unnamed-32.png)

Rush’s triumphant “Fifty Something” comeback tour, currently in progress and set to hit multiple continents into spring 2027, will give way to at least five dates at the Sphere in Las Vegas, billed as the “Elevate From The Norm” residency.

Dates are May 21, May 23, May 26, May 28 and May 30, with tickets going on sale Nov. 13. The shows are described as “a set of fan favorites and greatest hits from across their catalogue in celebration of RUSH’s music, legacy, and the life of the late drummer and lyricist Neil Peart.” The title of the residency is a reference to “Vital Signs,” the closing track from the band’s standout *Moving Pictures* album from 1981.

The Sphere dates are an added treat for Rush fans, who are getting a full run of shows from an eager frontman in Geddy Lee, who had publicly hinted at wanting to perform and record, and a rejuvenated guitarist Alex Lifeson, who had suggested he was done as a member of Rush, dealing with arthritis and overall out of practice as the sole guitarist. The band’s final tour with Peart was 2015’s “R40” tour celebrating 40 years of the band.

Lee says in the Sphere announcement, “When I first heard of Sphere, I thought to myself, ‘wow, what a shame that RUSH is no longer touring, because this venue sounds tailor made for the kind of visual attitude with which we have always designed our shows.’ Normally we spend months designing the visual effects for our shows and this scenario seemed like the cherry on top of the ice cream for our design team. But life is nothing if not surprising and now, here we are, living and touring in the present tense and we are able to make that particular dream come true. We are presently working hard to expand our touring concepts and concoct some new madness to suit this incredible venue and musically perform the kind of show we’ve been doing in some of our “one off” cities. Meaning playing all of 2112 and all of Moving Pictures along with an array of other songs. We are so effin’ excited to make use of this incredible opportunity to please our fans both visually and viscerally- from wherever they might fly in from. Gonna be so much fun!!”

Peart, the band’s singular drummer since 1975’s *Fly By Night* album, died in 2020, after which many thought was the end of the band. For “Fifty Something,” drummer Anika Nilles was brought on board as full time percussionist, receiving rave reviews since the tour kicked off in Los Angeles in June. The band is also joined by keyboardist Loren Gold on the road.

Lee in 2024 undertook a speaking tour based on his memoir, “My Effin’ Life,” which included guest hosts each night such as super-fans like actor/musician Jack Black, actor Paul Rudd, Soundgarden’s Kim Thayil and former Hole and Smashing Pumpkins member Melissa Auf der Maur.

[An an interview with *Pollstar* at the time, Lee](https://news.pollstar.com/2024/01/29/rushs-geddy-lee-back-in-the-limelight-with-my-effin-life-tour-and-maybe-more/) hinted at more live performances, but nothing on the scale of “Fifty Something” or the Sphere.

“I have some big decisions to make and I’m pondering,” Lee said at the time. “I would like to get back to writing music and see where that takes me. I would like to do some things in music before I shut the door on that option. So I just need some time now.”

Rush is represented by Independent Artist Group and managed by Q Prime.

Today, Rock & Roll Hall of Fame® inductees and RUSH co-founders Geddy Lee (on bass, keys, and vocals) and Alex Lifeson (on guitar and vocals) have announced RUSH’s five-date residency at Sphere in Las Vegas. The band will perform on May 21, 23, 26, 28, and 30. Each night the band will play a set of fan favorites and greatest hits from across their catalogue in celebration of RUSH’s music, legacy, and the life of the late drummer and lyricist Neil Peart.

Geddy Lee shares about the residency:

“When I first heard of Sphere, I thought to myself, ‘wow, what a shame that RUSH is no longer touring, because this venue sounds tailor made for the kind of visual attitude with which we have always designed our shows.’ Normally we spend months designing the visual effects for our shows and this scenario seemed like the cherry on top of the ice cream for our design team. But life is nothing if not surprising and now, here we are, living and touring in the present tense and we are able to make that particular dream come true. We are presently working hard to expand our touring concepts and concoct some new madness to suit this incredible venue and musically perform the kind of show we’ve been doing in some of our “one off” cities. Meaning playing all of 2112 and all of Moving Pictures along with an array of other songs. We are so effin’ excited to make use of this incredible opportunity to please our fans both visually and viscerally- from wherever they might fly in from. Gonna be so much fun!!”
