# Bruno Mars Expands ‘The Romantic Tour’ Into 2027, With Shows In Australia, New Zealand And Asia

## Summary

Bruno Mars added 26 new dates to "The Romantic Tour" for 2027, expanding into Australia, New Zealand, Asia, and a fifth Mexico City show. The Live Nation-promoted trek now spans Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines, and South Korea, running January–May 2027, with artist presales via Ticketmaster beginning September 2.

### Key Points
- 26 new 2027 dates added across Australia, New Zealand, Asia, and a 5th Mexico City show.
- Tour runs January 4 (Japan) through May 26, 2027 (South Korea).
- Tyla joins as special guest for Australia/New Zealand; Anderson.Paak returns as DJ for all shows.
- Artist presale for Australia/NZ via Ticketmaster starts September 2; general onsale September 4.

Bruno Mars will be bringing more romance to additional markets next year. The global superstar announced the expansion of “The Romantic Tour,” with new dates in Australia, New Zealand, Asia and Mexico City.

Mars added 26 dates to his Live Nation-promoted tour, including a fifth show at Mexico City’s Estadio GNP Seguros on Dec. 11. The 2027 leg of his trek now includes stops in Japan, Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, Thailand, Taiwan, Hong Kong, the Philippines and South Korea, kicking off at Jan. 4 at Valtelin Dome in Japan and wrapping up May 26 at Goyang Stadium in South Korea.

**[How ‘Romantic’: Could Bruno Mars Have 2026’s Biggest Tour? (Cover Story)](https://news.pollstar.com/2026/05/15/how-romantic-could-bruno-mars-have-the-biggest-tour-of-2026/)**

Two-time Grammy winner Tyla will be a special guest at the Australia and New Zealand shows, and Silk Sonic collaborator Anderson.Paak returns for all shows as DJ Pee.Wee.

Tickets for the new Mexico City date go on sale Aug. 31 at 2 p.m. local time.

For the Australia and New Zealand shows, there will be an artist presale Sept. 2 that fans must sign up via [Ticketmaster](https://signup.ticketmaster.com.au/brunomars) by Aug. 31 at 11:59 p.m. All remaining tickets go on sale to the general public Sept. 4.

For the Asia dates, fans can sign up for the artist presale at [brunomars.com](https://www.brunomars.com/) through Sept. 7. Presale and general onsale dates and times vary by market.

For tour and ticket information, visit [brunomars.com](https://www.brunomars.com/).

Extraordinary demand for “The Romantic Tour” prompted the artist and his team to expand the trek, which delivered the largest single-day ticket sales in Live Nation history across North America, Europe and the U.K. Mars’ headline tour, his first in nearly a decade, also set a Ticketmaster record with 2.1 million tickets sold in a single day. With the new dates, “The Romantic Tour” expands to more than 115 stadium concerts across the globe

Mars, who is booked by WME’s John Marx and Ben Totis, is on the road promoting his fourth solo album, The Romantic, which has been a hit on streaming charts. He kicked off the tour with a two-night stand at Allegiant Stadium in Las Vegas, and he’s visited stadiums such as Arlington’s Globe Life Field, Nissan Stadium in Nashville, Detroit’s Ford Field, Soldier Field in Chicago and Toronto’s Rogers Stadium before going to Europe. Mars returned to the States this month for a four-night stand at MetLife Stadium in East Rutherford, New Jersey.

The 16-time Grammy-winning superstar is managed by Aaron Elharar of Gorilla Management.
