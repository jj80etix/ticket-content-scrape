# Brazilian authorities order resellers to stop selling BTS tickets

## Summary

Brazil's National Consumer Secretariat (Senacon) ordered StubHub, Viagogo and three local resale platforms to stop selling tickets for BTS's ongoing São Paulo concerts, part of a broader crackdown that extends to all new ticket resales nationwide. The order coincides with BTS's three-show Arirang World Tour stop in São Paulo.

### Key Points
- Platforms named: StubHub, Viagogo International, BuyTickets, Global Entertainment Software and Ticketswap Brazil
- Directive tied to BTS's São Paulo shows (Oct. 28, 30, 31) but applies to new resale activity across Brazil generally
- Part of Senacon's escalating enforcement of Brazil's consumer-protection and ticket-pricing-transparency rules
- Companies face fines if they fail to comply with the suspension order

### Highlights
- A second-source confirmation of the same-day Brazilian resale suspension, timed explicitly to a high-demand K-pop tour stop
- Five named platforms span both global majors (StubHub, Viagogo) and Brazilian-domestic services
- Underlines that regulators are willing to act during live, in-progress concert runs, not just after the fact

### Industry Problem
Regulators are increasingly willing to intervene in real time against secondary marketplaces during active high-demand tours, adding acute compliance and reputational risk for resale platforms operating internationally.

### Proposed Solution
Not addressed by the companies in this report. For Etix, the takeaway is the same as the related, more detailed TicketNews coverage of this action: monitor how Brazil's enforcement proceeds and what disclosure standard it ultimately requires, since similar frameworks could spread to other markets.
