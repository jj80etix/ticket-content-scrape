# Jelly Roll, Madness and Simply Red Lead New U.K. Festival Announcements

## Summary

A new round of UK festival announcements includes Jelly Roll, Madness, and Simply Red headlining expanded or newly launched events: Country to Country adds a Manchester date for 2027, Rock N Roll Circus makes its London debut with Madness, and Taunton's new Somerset Sounds debuts with Simply Red. Bristol's Simple Things also completed its November 2026 lineup.

### Key Points
- Country to Country (Mar. 12-14, 2027) expands to Manchester's AO Arena alongside London's O2 and Glasgow's OVO Hydro; general on-sale Oct. 2.
- Rock N Roll Circus debuts in London (Gunnersbury Park, July 22-25, 2027) with Madness headlining July 24; general on-sale Oct. 2.
- Somerset Sounds launches in Taunton (Aug. 22, 2027) with Simply Red headlining; on-sale already open.

Jelly Roll, Madness and Simply Red are among the headliners confirmed in a new round of U.K. festival announcements, with plans including Country to Country’s expansion into Manchester, Rock N Roll Circus’ London debut and the launch of Somerset Sounds in Taunton.

The announcements outline several additions to the 2027 live music calendar. Meanwhile, Bristol’s Simple Things has completed its lineup for its upcoming November event.

Country to Country will return March 12 to 14, 2027, with Ashley McBryde, Riley Green and Jelly Roll headlining a rotating lineup across London’s O2, Glasgow’s OVO Hydro and Manchester’s AO Arena. Manchester joins the festival for the first time.

All three artists will make their C2C headlining debuts, while Jelly Roll’s appearances will mark his first U.K. headline shows. Other performers include Midland, Cole Swindell, Cooper Alan, Jackson Dean, Dasha, Chase Matthew, Carter Faith and Emily Ann Roberts.

The CMA Spotlight Stage will also expand to all three cities, featuring emerging performers including Greylan James, Lanie Gardner, Owen Riegling and Belles. “This lineup shows just how global our genre has become, with artists from around the world sharing the stage,” said incoming CMA CEO Brittany Schaffer.

C2C’s official presale began September 30. General ticket sales open October 2 at 10 a.m. U.K. time.

The booking brings the Camden-born group to the festival’s first capital edition. Madness’ catalog includes “Our House,” “It Must Be Love,” “Baggy Trousers” and “House of Fun.”

Local residents received presale access September 29, followed by an exclusive presale September 30. General ticket sales begin October 2 at 10 a.m. U.K. time.

In Taunton, Simply Red will headline the inaugural Somerset Sounds at the Cooper Associates County Ground on August 22, 2027. Heather Small and Soul II Soul are also confirmed, with more performers expected.

The appearance will close Simply Red’s “Summer Funk ’27” U.K. and European tour and is billed as the band’s only South West performance of 2027.

**| RELATED:**[**Somerset Sounds Concert Series Set for 2027 Launch**](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/09/somerset-sounds-concert-series-set-for-2027-launch/) **|**

Somerset Sounds is a joint venture between Somerset County Cricket Club and promoter Full Fat 360. The event builds on the ground’s existing concert history, which includes appearances by Elton John, Rod Stewart and Lionel Richie.

General ticket sales opened September 24 following a September 22 presale.

Before those summer events, Simple Things will return to venues across Bristol on November 7, 2026, for its 12th edition. The festival’s completed program introduces guest-curated selections from Cobrah, Squid, Shabaka and Jeremy Deller.

The wider lineup includes The Avalanches, Panda Bear & Sonic Boom, Mykki Blanco, Moor Mother, Hannah Diamond and Gold Panda. Tickets are on sale, with the festival again produced by the teams behind Crack Magazine, Bristol Beacon and Team Love.
