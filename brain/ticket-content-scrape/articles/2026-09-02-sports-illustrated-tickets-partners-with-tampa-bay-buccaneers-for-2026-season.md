# Sports Illustrated Tickets Partners With Tampa Bay Buccaneers for 2026 Season

## Summary

Sports Illustrated Tickets becomes the Tampa Bay Buccaneers' official fan-experience partner starting the Sept. 20 home opener, debuting its "Faces in the Crowd" activation — a network of 4K cameras that capture fan reactions and let fans convert being filmed into a free SI Fan Cover or a commemorative game ticket. The activation is rolling out to 10 stadiums (6 of them NFL venues) across the US and Europe in the 2026 season; the Buccaneers deal follows SI Tickets' earlier partnership with NWSL's Gotham FC and also grants SI Tickets broader in-stadium ticketing/marketing rights beyond the camera feature.

### Key Points
- SI Tickets named Buccaneers' official fan-experience partner beginning the Sept. 20, 2026 home opener vs. the Cleveland Browns at Raymond James Stadium.
- "Faces in the Crowd" activation: a 4K camera network captures fan reactions, convertible into a free Sports Illustrated Fan Cover or commemorative ticket.
- Rolling out to 10 stadiums total in 2026 (6 NFL venues) across the US and Europe.
- Follows SI Tickets' prior fan-experience/ticketing deal with NWSL's Gotham FC.
- Deal also includes broader in-stadium ticketing/marketing rights for SI Tickets, not just the camera activation.

### Highlights
- "Faces in the Crowd": a 4K camera-driven fan-recognition feature turning attendance into a personalized, shareable keepsake (SI-branded cover or commemorative ticket).
- Scale: 10 stadiums in the 2026 season, 6 of them NFL venues, spanning the US and Europe.
- Pattern: SI Tickets is stacking pro-sports fan-experience/ticketing partnerships (Gotham FC, now Buccaneers) as a go-to-market wedge.

### Industry Problem
Competitors are differentiating primary-ticketing/fan-experience partnerships with novel in-venue engagement features (gamified personalization, shareable keepsakes) rather than competing purely on ticketing fees or checkout — raising the bar for what venue/team partners expect from a ticketing/fan-experience vendor relationship.

### Proposed Solution
SI Tickets' answer is a camera-driven personalization/keepsake feature bundled into its ticketing partnership. Etix should track adoption and fan reception of "Faces in the Crowd" across its 10-venue rollout and evaluate whether a lighter-weight, venue-side fan-recognition or shareable-keepsake feature is worth building for clients competing for premium sponsorship/fan-engagement dollars.

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Sports-Illustrated-Tickets-Partners-With-Tampa-Bay-Buccaneers.webp)

The Tampa Bay Buccaneers have entered into a new partnership with Sports Illustrated Tickets that will make the ticketing company the NFL team’s official fan experience partner.

The collaboration will begin at the Buccaneers’ 2026 home opener on September 20, when Tampa Bay hosts the Cleveland Browns at Raymond James Stadium. The game will also mark the debut of Sports Illustrated Tickets’ “Faces in the Crowd” activation at the venue.

The activation is designed to capture fan reactions during games through a network of strategically positioned 4K cameras installed throughout the stadium. Fans whose reactions are captured will have the opportunity to turn those moments into a free Sports Illustrated Fan Cover or a commemorative game ticket.

“Faces in the Crowd” will be available at 10 stadiums across the United States and Europe during the 2026 season. Six of those venues will be home stadiums for NFL teams.

The Buccaneers said the partnership is intended to provide additional ways for fans to preserve memories from attending games.

“We are always looking for new ways to enhance the gameday experience and create memorable moments for our fans,” said Atul Khosla, chief commercial officer of the Buccaneers. “Sports Illustrated Tickets’ Faces in the Crowd gives fans a unique way to capture their gameday experience and turn it into a lasting keepsake that celebrates their connection to the Buccaneers.”

The new agreement comes as the Buccaneers continue to expand their commercial and technology partnerships ahead of the 2026 NFL season. The team recently announced a multi-year partnership with Priority Commerce that will introduce a new ticket payment process for Buccaneers fans.

The 2026 NFL season is scheduled to begin on September 9, with the Seattle Seahawks hosting the New England Patriots at Lumen Field. Tampa Bay will open its home schedule 11 days later against Cleveland.
