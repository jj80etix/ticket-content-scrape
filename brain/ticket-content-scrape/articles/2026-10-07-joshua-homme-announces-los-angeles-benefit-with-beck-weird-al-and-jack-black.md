# Joshua Homme Announces Los Angeles Benefit With Beck, Weird Al and Jack Black

## Summary

Joshua Homme is hosting a benefit concert, "Joshua Homme and Friends," at The Wiltern in Los Angeles on November 16, featuring Beck, "Weird Al" Yankovic, St. Vincent, Norah Jones, The Kills and members of Queens of the Stone Age, with proceeds supporting disaster-relief nonprofit CORE and The Sweet Stuff Foundation. Comedy guests include Bill Burr and Sarah Silverman, with Jack Black making a special appearance. Tickets go on general sale October 9 through Ticketmaster.

### Key Points
- One-night benefit at The Wiltern, LA, Nov. 16, benefiting CORE and The Sweet Stuff Foundation.
- Lineup: Beck, "Weird Al" Yankovic, St. Vincent, Norah Jones, The Kills, plus QOTSA members and Chad Smith (RHCP).
- Comedy guests: Bill Burr, Sarah Silverman, Dean Delray, Dan Soder; Jack Black presents an award.
- Artist presale Oct. 7-8; general onsale Oct. 9 via Ticketmaster.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Joshua-Homme-and-Friends-Benefit.webp)

Joshua Homme will bring musicians, comedians and special guests to The Wiltern in Los Angeles on November 16 for a benefit supporting CORE and The Sweet Stuff Foundation.

Billed as “Joshua Homme and Friends,” the one-night event will feature Beck, “Weird Al” Yankovic, St. Vincent, Norah Jones and The Kills, alongside members of Homme’s own Queens of the Stone Age.

The lineup includes Queens of the Stone Age guitarist Troy Van Leeuwen and bassist Michael Shuman, as well as Red Hot Chili Peppers drummer Chad Smith and producer and musician Andrew Watt. Justin Willman and John McEnroe are also scheduled to participate.

Comedy guests include Bill Burr, Sarah Silverman, Dean Delray and Dan Soder, giving the evening a lineup that extends beyond its musical performances. Jack Black will make a special appearance to present an award, according to the announcement.

“CORE is an immediate responder to crises globally,” Homme said. “They act decisively with care & kindness when others hesitate.”

The Sweet Stuff Foundation assists career musicians, recording engineers and their families dealing with illness and disability. Limited-edition screen prints by Queens of the Stone Age collaborators Boneface and Emek will also raise money for the foundation.
