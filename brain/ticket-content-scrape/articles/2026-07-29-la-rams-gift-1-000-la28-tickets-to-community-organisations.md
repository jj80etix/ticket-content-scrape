# LA Rams gift 1,000 LA28 tickets to community organisations

## Summary

The LA Rams donated 1,000 tickets to 10 community organizations as part of a $5m gift to the LA28 Community Ticket Program, giving local youth groups access to Olympic events.

### Key Points
- Part of the Rams' $5m donation to the LA28 Community Ticket Program.
- Ten recipient organizations include Boys & Girls Clubs of Metro LA and YMCA of Metro LA.
- Recipients will attend flag football (its Olympic debut) at Exposition Park Stadium or swimming at SoFi Stadium.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**The Los Angeles Rams have provided 1,000 tickets for the 2028 Olympic Games to 10 local community organisations.**

The organisations mark the first recipients of the NFL team’s $5m (£3.8m/€4.3m) donation to the LA28 Community Ticket Program.

“The Rams are excited to be able to partner with LA28,” said Rams head coach Sean McVay.

“I think one of the cool things about this organisation, starting with our owner and chairman Mr. Kroenke, is that he’s always interested in seeing how we can use this platform to give back, be a part of this community to make positive impacts and be a part of positive change.”

The recipient groups are A Place Called Home, After-School All-Stars, Al Wooten Jr. Youth Center, Boys & Girls Clubs of Metro LA, Brotherhood Crusade, Heart of Los Angeles, Project Blue, Social Justice Learning Institute, Swim Up Hill and YMCA of Metro LA.

Ticket recipients will attend either flag football in its Olympic debut at Exposition Park Stadium or swimming at SoFi Stadium, the home venue of the Rams, which is also set to serve as one of the main ceremonies venue at the Games.

“We are very proud to jointly present these local organizations with the opportunity to make their communities part of the LA28 Games,” said Erikk Aldridge, LA28 vice president of impact and legacy.

“The Rams’ donation to our Community Ticket Program is a great starting point, helping us create access to Games competitions directly for local neighbourhoods and youth development organisations.”
