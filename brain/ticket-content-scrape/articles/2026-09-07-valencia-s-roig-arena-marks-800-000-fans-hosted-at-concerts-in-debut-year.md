# Valencia’s Roig Arena marks 800,000 fans hosted at concerts in debut year

## Summary

Valencia's Roig Arena marked its first year in business by hosting 800,000 fans across 125 concerts, accounting for just under half of the venue's total 1.7 million attendees since its September 2025 opening — beating its own pre-opening prediction of 1 million total-event attendance by over 40%. Venue bosses credited the diversity of its concert programming, led by artists including Quevedo.

### Key Points
- 800,000 concert attendees across 125 shows in the venue's debut year.
- Total footfall across all events (concerts + other) reached 1.7 million, exceeding a 1-million pre-opening forecast by 40%+.
- Opened September 2025.
