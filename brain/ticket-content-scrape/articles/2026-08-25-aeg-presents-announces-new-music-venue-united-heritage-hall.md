# AEG Presents Announces New Music Venue United Heritage Hall

## Summary

AEG Presents unveiled a new 4,000-capacity venue, United Heritage Hall, opening in Austin, TX in Spring 2027 through a partnership with United Heritage Credit Union, Westfield, Partners Group, and Presidium. The venue anchors River Park, a 109-acre mixed-use development in Austin's Riverside neighborhood.

### Key Points
- 4,000-capacity venue, ~65,000 sq ft, opening Spring 2027 in Austin, TX.
- Partnership between AEG Presents and United Heritage Credit Union, developed with Westfield, Partners Group, and Presidium.
- Anchors River Park, a 109-acre mixed-use development near downtown Austin and the airport.
- Design draws on 1920s Art Deco and mid-century influences; includes luxury suites and VIP seating.

AEG Presents has unveiled a brand-new 4,000-capacity live music venue, United Heritage Hall, in Austin, TX. The venue is set to open in Spring 2027 through a partnership between AEG Presents and United Heritage Credit Union. Furthermore, AEG Presents developed United Heritage Hall in partnership with Westfield, Partners Group, and Presidium. According to an official press release, the planned venue will “pair world-class production with an intimate, fan-first experience, creating a new destination for live music in one of the world’s most celebrated music cities.”

The approximately 65,000-square-foot venue draws on “1920s Art Deco, the optimism of mid-century design, and a bold vision for the future” to fuse influences across generations that pay homage to Austin and all of the city’s culture.

United Heritage Hall will host concerts “spanning global touring artists, emerging talent, and Austin’s celebrated local music community.” The venue will feature state-of-the-art sound and lighting, exceptional sightlines, premium hospitality offerings, luxury suites, VIP seating, and thoughtfully designed artist spaces.

United Heritage Hall will anchor River Park, the 109-acre mixed-use development in Austin’s rapidly growing Riverside neighborhood. The venue will play a central role in River Park’s vision for a vibrant, walkable district that brings new music, culture, and entertainment opportunities to Austin’s east side. Located just minutes from downtown and Austin-Bergstrom International Airport, the venue is poised to become a lasting fixture in Austin’s cultural landscape, honoring the city’s musical legacy while helping shape its future.

“We are incredibly honored to announce United Heritage Hall in partnership with our amazing friends at United Heritage Credit Union. From day one, they believed in our vision of a venue that marries Austin’s rich musical legacy with the next chapter of its live music story,” commented Robin Phillips, VP, Southwest Region, AEG Presents. “United Heritage Hall is the culmination of our team’s tireless work anchored by an unwavering belief that this city deserves another truly iconic room. We cannot wait to open the doors and welcome everyone!”

“United Heritage Credit Union will celebrate 70 years of serving our members and communities in 2027, making the opening of United Heritage Hall especially meaningful,” said Michael Ver Schuur, President and CEO of United Heritage Credit Union. “For seven decades, we have grown alongside Central Texas and invested in the people and places that make this region extraordinary. United Heritage Hall represents both a celebration of that heritage and an investment in Austin’s future with a gathering place bringing together artists, music fans, and communities for generations to come.”
