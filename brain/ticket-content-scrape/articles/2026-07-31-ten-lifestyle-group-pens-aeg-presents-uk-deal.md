# Ten Lifestyle Group pens AEG Presents UK deal

## Summary

Ten Lifestyle Group, a customer-experience and loyalty platform for premium brands, has signed a UK partnership with select AEG Presents venues and festivals — Indigo at The O2, Watford Colosseum, University of Wolverhampton at The Halls, and the C2C: Country to Country festival. Inventory is distributed exclusively to Ten's closed-user member base through its proprietary Ten Box Office platform rather than via general public sale, extending an existing multi-year Ten/AEG relationship.

### Key Points
- Ten Lifestyle Group partners with AEG Presents on UK venues/events: Indigo at The O2, Watford Colosseum, Uni of Wolverhampton at The Halls, and the C2C festival.
- Tickets are distributed through Ten Box Office, Ten's proprietary platform, exclusively to its closed-user member ecosystem rather than general public sale.
- The deal extends an existing multi-year relationship between Ten and AEG.
- Framed as "enhanced access" for premium/loyalty members to high-demand events.

### Highlights
- Named venues: Indigo at The O2, Watford Colosseum, University of Wolverhampton at The Halls, plus the C2C festival.
- Distribution model: closed-user, members-only access via Ten Box Office rather than open public sale.

### Industry Problem
Reflects a structural shift where premium/loyalty platforms carve out guaranteed inventory ahead of general on-sale, reducing publicly available supply for high-demand events and reinforcing a two-tier access model for fans.

### Proposed Solution
The item proposes no patron-facing fix beyond the loyalty value proposition; for Etix, it signals a long-horizon question of whether a client-facing loyalty/membership tier with dedicated inventory carve-outs is worth evaluating, and whether such deals affect perceived on-sale fairness — worth monitoring how prevalent members-only carve-outs become before building one.

TheTicketingBusiness News

**Ten Lifestyle Group has agreed a partnership with select AEG Presents venues, events and festivals in the UK.**

The agreement sees Ten, a customer experience and loyalty platform for premium brands, become an official partners across AEG Presents venues and events including Indigo at The O2, Watford Colosseum and University of Wolverhampton at The Halls, as well as the C2C: Country to Country festival.

The partnership will provide eligible Ten members with enhanced access to a curated range of high-demand events and tours.

Ticket inventory will be distributed via Ten Box Office, Ten’s proprietary ticketing platform, which enables eligible members to discover and book events across theatre, music, family shows and sports.Ten Box Office allows members to access official ticket inventory and selected opportunities made available through Ten’s closed-user member ecosystem, rather than through general public sale.

“We are delighted to be partnering with AEG Presents on these venues and events. Ten has worked with the AEG for a number of years, and we are pleased to extend the relationship,” said Lucy Chillingworth, global director of live entertainment for Ten Lifestyle Group.

“Offering our members with access to official tickets is central to Ten’s entertainment proposition and this partnership further strengthens our ability to deliver trusted, high-quality live experiences. We are excited by the opportunity ahead and look forward to building a long-term, expansive relationship.”
