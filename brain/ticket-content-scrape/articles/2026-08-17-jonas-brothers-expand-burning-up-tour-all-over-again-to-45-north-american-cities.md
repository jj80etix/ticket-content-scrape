# Jonas Brothers Expand ‘Burning Up Tour All Over Again’ to 45 North American Cities

## Summary

The Jonas Brothers expanded "The Burning Up Tour All Over Again" into a 45-city North American run this fall, promoted by Live Nation, following three sold-out Madison Square Garden shows. The tour kicks off September 25 in Boston and wraps December 21 in Newark.

### Key Points
- Jonas Brothers expanded their tour to 45 North American cities, Sept 25–Dec 21.
- Follows three sold-out Madison Square Garden shows.
- Promoted by Live Nation; stops include Toronto, DC, Miami, Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, LA, and more.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/IMG_0819.jpeg)

The Jonas Brothers are ready to bring “Burnin’ Up” across North America all over again.

Following the response to three sold-out Madison Square Garden shows, Kevin, Joe and Nick Jonas have expanded “ **The Burning Up Tour All Over Again** ” into a 45-city North American run this fall.

Promoted by Live Nation, the newly announced tour kicks off Sept. 25 at TD Garden in Boston and will hit Toronto, Washington, D.C., Miami, Atlanta, Chicago-area Rosemont, Nashville, Dallas, Los Angeles, Las Vegas, Seattle, Denver and more. The trek wraps Dec. 21 at Prudential Center in Newark, N.J.

The tour revives the spirit of the Jonas Brothers’ original “Burning Up Tour,” the 2008 arena run that arrived as the band was becoming one of pop music’s biggest acts.

“Our last tour reminded us just how much this music means to us and to the fans who’ve been with us from the start,” the Jonas Brothers said in a press release. “The response was so overwhelming announcing the MSG shows that we knew we couldn’t stop at New York.”

“We’re taking The Burning Up Tour All Over Again on the road so we can relive it with all of you, city by city,” the group added. “We can’t wait to run it back with all of you.”

The brothers are bringing some familiar faces along for the ride. Magnus Ferrell, Franklin Jonas and All Time Low will serve as special guests on select dates, while Deleasa is listed as support throughout the newly announced run.

The tour arrives during a busy stretch for the Jonas Brothers. The trio was recently announced as the halftime act for the NFL’s [first regular-season game in Australia](https://www.ticketnews.com/2026/08/jonas-brothers-to-perform-at-first-nfl-regular-season-game-in-australia/), while *Camp Rock 3* made its Disney Channel and Disney+ debut nearly two decades after the original film. Kevin, Joe and Nick were also inducted as Disney Legends at D23 on Aug. 16.

Tickets for the new dates will first become available through the Spotify Reserved presale in the U.S. beginning **Tuesday, Aug. 18 at 8:28 a.m. local time**. Additional presales will follow throughout the week.
