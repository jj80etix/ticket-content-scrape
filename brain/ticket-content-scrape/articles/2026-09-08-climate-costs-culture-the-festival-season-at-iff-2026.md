# Climate, costs & culture: The Festival Season at IFF 2026

## Summary

At the International Festival Forum (IFF) 2026 in London, promoters and agents from FKP Scorpio, CAA, Colours of Ostrava, and UTA discussed the past European festival season, covering climate impacts, festival identity, alternative programming, and booking timelines lengthening.
