# Noah Kahan Sets U.K., Ireland Dates for 2027 ‘The Last of the Bugs World Tour’

## Summary

Noah Kahan announced the first dates of his 2027 "The Last of the Bugs World Tour," covering the U.K. and Ireland with stadium shows at Glasgow's Hampden Park, Dublin's Aviva Stadium and London's Wembley Stadium. More dates are expected to follow.

### Key Points
- Presale begins Tue., Aug. 25 at 10 a.m. local time; general on-sale Fri., Aug. 28.
- Follows milestones on his current "The Great Divide World Tour," including a 65,000-fan Rose Bowl show and four consecutive sold-out nights at Fenway Park.
- Tour name draws from the expanded edition of his album The Great Divide.

## Transcript

![](https://www.ticketnews.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Noah-Kahan-The-Last-of-the-Bugs-World-Tour.webp)

Noah Kahan has announced the first dates for his upcoming “The Last of the Bugs World Tour,” with the initial shows scheduled for July 2027 in the U.K. and Ireland.

The announced dates include performances at Hampden Park in Glasgow, Aviva Stadium in Dublin and Wembley Stadium in London. Kahan said additional dates will be announced.

Taking to social media, Kahan shared, “We’ll be heading to Glasgow, Dublin, and London to play some big old rooms for the bugs across the Atlantic Ocean on The Last Of The Bugs World Tour in 2027,” he wrote.

The singer also announced the schedule for tickets. Fans can register for the presale through Kahan’s tour website, with the presale scheduled to begin Tuesday, Aug. 25, at 10 a.m. local time. General ticket sales will begin Friday, Aug. 28, at 10 a.m. local time.

“This is only the first of the last of the bugs… stay tuned for more,” Kahan added.

The new tour follows Kahan’s current “The Great Divide World Tour,” which is supporting his fourth studio album, *The Great Divide*. Upcoming North American stops include Sandy, UT, Vancouver, BC, and Seattle, WA.

Notably, the announcement comes after several major milestones during Kahan’s current touring run. Last weekend, he performed his largest headline show to date at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California, where 65,000 fans attended. Additionally, in July, Kahan also became the first artist to headline four consecutive sold-out concerts at Boston’s Fenway Park.

Kahan released *The Great Divide*, his fourth studio album, as he continued to expand his touring schedule. The album’s expanded edition, *The Great Divide (The Last of the Bugs)*, provides the name for the upcoming 2027 tour.

For more information and additional details, fans can visit Kahan’s [official website](https://noahkahan.com/).
