# Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter, Billy Strings To Headline Hinterland 2027

## Summary

Hinterland festival unveiled its 2027 lineup, with Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter and Billy Strings headlining the three-day Iowa countryside event (July 30–Aug. 1) outside Des Moines. Presale for the camping festival begins November 13.

### Key Points
- Slayyyter closes Friday; Bridgers headlines Saturday (her first Hinterland return since 2022); Strings closes Sunday
- More than two dozen acts across three days, including Remi Wolf, Caroline Polachek and Zach Top
- Presale begins Nov. 13, 11am CT via hinterlandiowa.com

## Transcript

Festival announcements are upon us, and Hinterland is the latest to unveil its lineup. Phoebe Bridgers, Slayyyter and Billy Strings will be headlining the popular Iowa countryside festival next summer.

Hinterland, which takes place July 30-Aug. 1, 2027, in Saint Charles, featured more than two dozen acts across three days, with Slayyyter closing out Day 1 following performances from Remi Wolf, Ashe, Caroline Polachek, Tiffany Day, Florence Road, Violet Grohl and judith.

![HMF27 F2](https://static.pollstar.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/HMF27_F2-scaled.png)

Bridgers returns to the festival for the first time since 2022 to headline Saturday’s slate, which includes Malcolm Todd, The Backseat Lovers, underscores, Joshua Slone, Emma Olgier, Westside Cowboy and Kinji.

Strings, who also headlined four years ago, will close out the final day of the festival, and other acts scheduled to perform that Sunday are Zach Top, Stephen Wilson Jr., Avery Anna, Mon Rovîa, Medium Build, Wade Forster and a special performance by Haywire in a cornfield.

Presale for the camping festival, which is just outside Des Moines, begins Nov. 13 at 11 a.m. CT. [hinterlandiowa.com](https://www.hinterlandiowa.com/).
