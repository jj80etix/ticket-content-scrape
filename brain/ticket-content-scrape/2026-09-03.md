# Harvest 2026-09-03

## Highlights

- **California AB 1349 clears State Senate with disputed "ghost-ticketing" carve-out** — a late Senate amendment lets some resale listings proceed without upfront ownership proof, prompting lead backer NIVA to reverse course and call for Gov. Newsom to veto; bill passed Aug 31, decision now with the governor.
- **PPI asks federal judge to reject DOJ/Live Nation settlement, wants Ticketmaster broken into several companies** — filed one day before the Sept 4 Tunney Act comment deadline; argues Ticketmaster's 80%+ primary-ticketing share means behavioral remedies (already tried once, in 2010) won't restore competition.
- **Ontario fines SeatGeek C$25,000 under its resale price-cap law** — first monetary enforcement of the province's proof-of-original-price requirement; SeatGeek says the underlying compliance problem (sellers, especially season-ticket holders, can't produce that proof) remains unresolved.
- **FTC extends personalized-pricing policy statement comment period to Sept 25** — regulator scrutiny of data-driven individualized pricing continues to build alongside existing junk-fee rulemaking.
