## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **Brazil's Senacon ordered StubHub, viagogo and three other platforms to halt all new ticket resales nationwide within 72 hours** (issued Oct. 29, timed to BTS's São Paulo tour stop), citing pricing-transparency and seller-identification failures under the "Fan Decree"; daily fines up to R$100,000 for noncompliance, previously completed purchases unaffected (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-10-29-brazil-orders-stubhub-viagogo-and-three-others-to-halt-new-ticket-resales|Oct 29]], [[articles/2026-10-29-brazilian-authorities-order-resellers-to-stop-selling-bts-tickets|Oct 29]]
- **FTC formally demanded a jury trial (filed Oct. 27) in its suit against Live Nation/Ticketmaster**, proposing an April 24, 2028 trial date against the companies' preferred Oct. 2, 2028 bench trial; filing combines BOTS Act broker-evasion allegations with all-in-pricing deception claims (Q9, Q10). → [[articles/2026-10-30-ftc-formally-demands-jury-trial-in-live-nation-ticketmaster-lawsuit|Oct 30]]
- **Rep. Raskin introduced the Antitrust Accountability and Transparency Act (House companion to Klobuchar's Senate bill)**, citing Live Nation's Oct. 21 amended Tunney Act filing admitting Trump attended the March 5 meeting that finalized the DOJ settlement — no vote scheduled (Q10). → [[articles/2026-10-30-raskin-introduces-antitrust-reform-bill-citing-live-nation-trump-settlement-cont|Oct 30]]
- **Nadler called the DOJ-Live Nation settlement "grotesquely politicized"** after the same Oct. 21 filing also confirmed Trump personally called CEO Michael Rapino in February re: the Kennedy Center and the antitrust case (Q10). → [[articles/2026-10-27-nadler-calls-live-nation-settlement-grotesquely-politicized-following-new-trump-|Oct 27]]
- **New UVA Darden/Wharton research finds seller-controlled resale systematically produces less consumer surplus than unrestricted resale**, tying the finding directly to Live Nation/Ticketmaster's Face Value Exchange advocacy, the contested DOJ settlement resale-eligibility terms, and Massachusetts's pending resale-cap bill; researcher recommends commission caps over price caps (Q2, Q6, Q10, Q11). → [[articles/2026-10-28-research-explains-why-ticket-sellers-embrace-resale-controls-that-can-reduce-con|Oct 28]]
