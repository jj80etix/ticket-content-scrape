# Weekly Digest — 2026-W45 (Nov 2–8)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **FTC and eight states/localities sued fintech Toast over hidden fees and deceptive subscription terms** (filed Oct. 29); Toast's defense that ROSCA doesn't cover B2B contracts is an open question any B2B-recurring-revenue platform, including primary ticketing platforms' client agreements, should watch (Q9). → [[articles/2026-11-04-ftc-and-eight-states-sue-toast-over-hidden-fees-and-deceptive-subscription-pract|Nov 4]]
- **Democrats flipped 5 state AG seats (GA, IA, KS, OH, TX) on Nov. 3**, moving the national AG split to 29D-22R; incoming AGs take office Jan. 2027 — their staffing and first filings are the leading indicator for how fast multistate consumer-protection (junk-fee) and resale/transferability enforcement shifts (Q9, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-04-2026-state-ag-election-day-updates|Nov 4]], [[articles/2026-11-04-after-the-state-ag-elections-what-s-next-for-enforcement|Nov 4]]
- **UK Commons rejected (286-62, Nov. 4) a Sporting Events Bill amendment that would have capped sports-ticket resale at face value**; critics say the narrow sports-only focus ignores Live Nation/Ticketmaster's disputed 66% share of UK primary tickets, and the government's broader Ticket Tout Ban Bill remains the planned vehicle for sweeping resale reform, still with no introduction date (Q10, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-05-uk-lawmakers-reject-sports-ticket-resale-price-cap-amendment-as-broader-ban-loom|Nov 5]]
- **NATP (ticket-resale trade group) called for ticket-authentication/transfer access to be opened to any qualified marketplace**, not just issuer-selected partners like CashorTrade, positioning the proposal alongside DOJ's pending Live Nation-Ticketmaster settlement's open-distribution terms (Q10, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-05-natp-calls-for-open-ticket-authentication-beyond-ticketmaster-s-selected-partner|Nov 5]]
