# Weekly Digest — 2026-W39 (Sep 21–27)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **Ontario fines StubHub Canada C$20,000** for above-cap resale and missing proof of original ticket price; StubHub plans to appeal, citing no clear mechanism for verifying face value — follows SeatGeek Canada's unappealed C$25,000 penalty under the same framework (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-09-22-stubhub-fined-under-ontario-ticket-resale-cap-verification-dispute-continues|Sep 22]]
- **DOJ–Live Nation/Ticketmaster Tunney Act comment period closed Sept. 4; ruling pending** before Judge Arun Subramanian. Multiple objectors kept pressing through the week: AEG says the settlement leaves ~85% market share intact and opens only ~170 events to bid; TicketNetwork wants resale-conduct conditions stripped from primary-distribution eligibility; Live Nation's Berchtold publicly defended the deal and floated Supreme Court review of the underlying case (Q10, Q11). → [[articles/2026-09-22-the-biz-berchtold-talks-globally-at-goldman-sachs-ln-competitors-object-to-settl|Sep 22]], [[articles/2026-09-22-ticketnetwork-targets-resale-conditions-in-doj-s-live-nation-ticketmaster-settle|Sep 22]], [[articles/2026-09-23-live-nation-s-berchtold-says-its-concert-scale-gives-its-venues-an-edge-while-di|Sep 23]]
- **Massachusetts AG forces a one-off Ticketmaster refund window** for Ed Sheeran's Sept. 25–26 Gillette Stadium shows (deadline 24 hrs pre-show) after ~200 complaints over the near-total opening-lineup collapse — AG argues the standard no-refund-for-opening-act-changes policy became "unfair" at this scale (Q9, Q2). → [[articles/2026-09-24-ticketmaster-commits-to-ed-sheeran-refunds-after-massachusetts-ag-demand|Sep 24]]
- **Gov. Healey files a second Great Divide Act track (H.5679)** inside the MA supplemental budget, adding a 10% resale-marketplace fee cap absent from the Senate-passed version; neither bill precisely defines "original ticket price," echoing Ontario's verification dispute above. Ticket Policy Forum (StubHub, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats et al.) opposes it (Q11, Q6). → [[articles/2026-09-25-healey-doubles-down-on-ticket-resale-price-cap-in-massachusetts-supplemental-bud|Sep 25]]
- **Rangers Pass's no-resale restriction is untested under NY law** — Section 25.12's sports-membership-pass exemption applies only at venues ≥30,000 seats, and MSG (~19,500) doesn't clearly qualify; a bill lowering the threshold to 13,500 remains stuck in committee (Q11, Q2). → [[articles/2026-09-23-rangers-launch-75-lower-bowl-ticket-pass-undercutting-prices-paid-by-season-memb|Sep 23]]
