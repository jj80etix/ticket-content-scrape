# Weekly Digest — 2026-W30 (Jul 20–26)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **Massachusetts "Great Divide Act" advances, weakened** (Q9, Q10, Q11) — Proposed **July 20** as Gov. Healey's closeout-budget amendment (110%-of-face resale cap, 10% fee cap, speculative-listing ban); backed by New England artists citing clout **July 23**; **passed the MA Senate** as Amendment 20 to bond bill S.3178 on **July 24**, but the Senate stripped the 10% reseller fee cap before passage — only the resale cap, speculative-ticketing ban, and deceptive-marketing provisions survive. Now heads to conference committee against the House's bond bill (no ticketing provisions); decision expected before the legislative session closes **July 31**. Leaves Ticketmaster's primary-market dynamic/platinum pricing untouched, and lands while remedies are still pending in the MA AG's April 2026 antitrust verdict against Live Nation/Ticketmaster. → [[articles/2026-07-20-massachusetts-governor-aims-to-cap-resale-prices-with-the-great-divide-act|Jul 20]], [[articles/2026-07-23-new-england-artists-offer-clout-to-industry-backed-push-for-ticket-resale-cap-in|Jul 23]], [[articles/2026-07-24-mass-senate-advances-ticketing-reform-after-artist-push|Jul 24]]
- **21 states seek Tunney Act discovery into DOJ–Live Nation settlement** (Q10) — States say they weren't notified of settlement talks until Jan. 29, 2026 and want discovery into the deal's substance; hearing on this plus Live Nation's bid to overturn the April antitrust verdict expected **end of July**. → [[articles/2026-07-20-states-seek-further-information-on-live-nation-s-doj-settlement|note]]
- **UK CMA edges closer to a formal Live Nation market inquiry** (Q10) — CMA was reported gearing up (**Jul 20**) and then reported lining up meetings with Live Nation critics (**Jul 21**), following a May 2026 parliamentary report alleging Live Nation controls 58–66% of UK primary tickets; a decision to open a formal inquiry is expected before end of 2026. → [[articles/2026-07-20-uk-watchdog-reportedly-gearing-up-major-inquiry-into-live-nation-s-market-power|Jul 20]], [[articles/2026-07-21-live-nation-faces-possible-investigation-in-uk-as-cma-reportedly-lines-up-meetin|Jul 21]]
- **FIFA World Cup ticketing investigations continue post-tournament** (Q9) — NJ and NY AGs' joint probe (subpoenaed May 27) into FIFA's variable pricing and a late-added premium tier persists past the July 19 final, alongside separate CA and TX AG investigations; FIFA reportedly raised prices ~34% on average across 90+ of 104 matches; no penalty timeline set. → [[articles/2026-07-20-fifa-ticketing-investigation-will-continue-after-world-cup-new-jersey-ag-says|note]]
- **Federal HOLDBACKS Act introduced** (Q9) — Rep. Pou's bill (H.R. 9762, introduced July 16) would force primary sellers to disclose inventory/pricing pre-onsale and ban manufactured-scarcity tactics; referred to committee, text not yet public. → [[articles/2026-07-20-pou-s-holdbacks-act-targets-deceptive-hidden-ticket-inventory-manufactured-scarc|note]]
- **National Consumers League pushes "gold standard" federal reform** (Q9, Q11) — At a July 22 House Energy & Commerce hearing, NCL renewed its push for the BOSS and SWIFT Act (all-in pricing + holdback disclosure) and endorsed the MAIN Event Ticketing Act (stronger BOTS Act anti-bot reporting), alongside the House-passed (409-15) TICKET Act still pending in the Senate. → [[articles/2026-07-23-national-consumers-league-pushes-for-gold-standard-ticketing-reform-at-house-of-|note]]
- **State AGs broaden focus to algorithmic pricing generally** (Q9) — A July 22 legal alert flags rising state AG scrutiny of algorithmic/dynamic pricing outside ticketing — directly adjacent to ticketing's own dynamic-pricing exposure. → [[articles/2026-07-22-states-are-cracking-down-on-algorithmic-pricing-are-you-ready|note]]
