# Weekly Digest — 2026-W46 (Nov 9–15)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **FTC and four states (CO, FL, IL, NE) moved for a jury trial in the BOTS Act case against Live Nation/Ticketmaster**, alleging Ticketmaster "turned a blind eye" to brokers controlling 6,345 accounts/246,407 tickets and rejected stronger ID verification in 2021 because it was "too effective" (Q10, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-10-the-biz-hybe-s-record-quarter-umg-earnings-jury-demand-in-ln-bots-trial|Nov 10]]
- **Democrats' Jan. 2027 takeover of Congress is expected to pair with intensified multistate AG investigations into junk fees, subscriptions and algorithmic pricing** — advisory counsel flags affordability/pricing as a top combined-scrutiny target once committees and subpoena power change hands (Q9). → [[articles/2026-11-09-democratic-control-of-congress-and-the-rise-of-parallel-congressional-and-state-|Nov 9]]
- **The EU Commission formally proposed its Digital Fairness Act** — real-time all-in pricing on virtual queues, a ban on drip pricing and fake sold-out "dark patterns," plus new fining power up to 6% of global turnover; not yet law, now heads to Parliament/Council (Q9, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-11-eu-s-digital-fairness-act-proposal-advances-ambitious-ticketing-reform|Nov 11]]
- **Texas Sen. Zaffirini filed SB 42** (session convenes Jan. 12, 2027; effective Sept. 1, 2027 if passed), requiring all-in fee disclosure and a post-selection price-lock — but leaves pre-selection dynamic pricing and tiered inventory, the actual driver of effective price, untouched (Q9). → [[articles/2026-11-10-proposed-texas-ticketing-bill-would-curb-checkout-price-hikes-but-leave-surge-pr|Nov 10]]
- **A judge compelled Live Nation's $35M whistleblower suit (ex-VP Rumanes) into arbitration** but retained a role in discovery disputes; allegations include mischaracterizing ticket "facility/maintenance" fees as revenue and consent-decree compliance questions (Q10). → [[articles/2026-11-10-judge-sends-live-nation-s-35m-whistleblower-case-to-arbitration-flags-discovery-|Nov 10]]
