# Weekly Digest — 2026-W47 (Nov 16–22)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **UK CMA provisionally finds viagogo engaged in drip pricing** across three viagogo domains (conduct alleged Apr 2025–present); viagogo is contesting rather than settling — the first case to reach this stage under the CMA's new direct-enforcement powers without a settlement. Potential penalty up to 10% of global turnover or £300,000; next update not expected before spring 2027 (Q9). → [[articles/2026-11-19-uk-regulator-escalates-viagogo-hidden-fee-investigation-with-provisional-finding|Nov 19]]
- **Ten music-industry groups, including Irving Azoff's Music Artists Coalition, filed amicus briefs backing D.C.'s RESALE Act** (110% resale cap + 10% fee) against challenges from SeatGeek/Vivid Seats/Gametime/TickPick and ticket brokers; a preliminary-injunction hearing was held Nov. 19 before Judge Carl J. Nichols, with the law's Jan. 1, 2027 effective date hanging on the ruling (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-19-music-industry-groups-including-azoff-s-coalition-back-d-c-resale-cap-in-court|Nov 19]]
- **NHL, Ticketmaster, SeatGeek and Live Nation are all lobbying Alberta on Bill 34**, which would mandate all-in resale price disclosure and give the province rulemaking authority over resale prices/fees — no cap proposed yet, pending stakeholder consultation, but Ontario's parallel resale cap has already produced C$67,500+ in fines against SeatGeek Canada and StubHub over original-price-verification disputes (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-17-nhl-ticketmaster-seatgeek-lobby-alberta-as-ticket-resale-rules-take-shape|Nov 17]]
- **Live Nation/Ticketmaster fired back in the first-ever BOTS Act prosecution of a ticketing platform**, arguing FTC enforcement is selective/arbitrary and that tracing bot-bought resale tickets back to the original purchase is effectively impossible — a defense that, if it succeeds, could limit future BOTS Act enforcement against primary/resale hybrids (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-11-17-the-biz-q3-earnings-takeaways-ln-bites-back-in-bots-act-suit-hybe-s-new-front|Nov 17]]
