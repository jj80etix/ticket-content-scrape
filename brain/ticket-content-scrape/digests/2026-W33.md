# Weekly Digest — 2026-W33 (Aug 10–16)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **New Jersey bans surveillance pricing** (Q9) — Gov. Sherrill signed the Fair Price Protection Act (**Jul 23**, effective **Aug 1, 2027**) banning personalized/algorithmic pricing on groceries and adjacent goods, plus a one-year moratorium on new electronic shelf labels from **Feb 1, 2027**; joins MD, CT, and (pending signature) NY in a fast-growing state patchwork enforceable via treble damages and AG penalties up to $20K/violation. → [[articles/2026-08-11-new-jersey-bans-surveillance-pricing-for-groceries-and-other-products|Aug 11]]
- **State AG enforcement outlook flags pricing transparency as the top 2026 priority** (Q9, Q11) — Troutman Pepper mid-year review cites DC's $9.9M Live Nation settlement and the FTC's $10M StubHub junk-fee refund as anchors, with a wave of new algorithmic-pricing and general junk-fee statutes (NY, MD, CT, IL, LA) widening the compliance surface. → [[articles/2026-08-12-2026-state-ag-enforcement-key-focus-areas-for-the-remainder-of-the-year|Aug 12]]
- **California's AB 1720 resale-cap bill shelved** (Q6, Q11) — the 10%-above-face resale cap was held in Senate Appropriations (**Aug 13**) after StubHub reportedly spent $3.4M lobbying against it, despite Live Nation/NIVA/artist-group support; narrower companion bill AB 1349 (targeting bots/speculative sales) advanced instead. California joins states without a resale cap. → [[articles/2026-08-13-california-shelves-ticket-resale-price-cap-after-months-of-lawmaker-skepticism|Aug 13]], [[articles/2026-08-14-california-ticket-resale-cap-bill-fails-to-advance|Aug 14]]
- **Vivid Seats CEO contests the resale-cap wave, flags D.C.'s Jan 2027 effective date** (Q6, Q9, Q11) — on Vivid Seats' Q2 call, CEO Lawrence Fey argued D.C.'s RESALE Act (effective **Jan 1, 2027**: 110% resale cap, all-in pricing, surveillance-pricing ban, $25K reseller surety bond), Vermont's Act 109 (effective Jul 1, 2026), and Maine's 2025 package all cap secondary prices while leaving primary-market pricing untouched — an asymmetry he says pushes demand into "shadow markets." → [[articles/2026-08-15-vivid-seats-ceo-pushes-back-on-ticket-resale-price-caps-questions-d-c-approach|Aug 15]]
