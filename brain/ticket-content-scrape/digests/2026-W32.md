# Weekly Digest — 2026-W32 (Aug 3–9)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **HOLDBACKS Act introduced** (Q9, Q11) — Rep. Nellie Pou's H.R. 9762 (**Aug 4**) would require primary sellers to disclose public-onsale ticket counts at least 7 days before onsale and bar concealing/misrepresenting availability to inflate prices, FTC-enforced. → [[articles/2026-08-04-artists-20-ticket-programs-show-why-not-all-ticket-holdbacks-are-created-equal|Aug 4]]
- **BOTS Act: Elite Events settlement finalized** (Q11) — FTC's $300K penalty (of a $10.7M total, largely suspended) against ticket broker Elite Events/SmartScalpers for using fictitious/employee accounts, virtual cards, IP proxies, and multi-session browsers to bypass purchase limits across 2,400+ events; permanent ban on the tactics pending judicial approval. → [[articles/2026-08-03-ticket-broker-elite-events-settles-with-ftc-for-300k|Aug 3]], [[articles/2026-08-04-ticket-broker-elite-events-settles-with-ftc-for-300k-over-bots-act-violations|Aug 4]]
- **Massachusetts Amendment 20 advances quietly** (Q9, Q11) — resale-only cap at 110% of original price, cleared via a budget conference committee on **Aug 5** with no dedicated hearing (unlike California's more scrutinized AB 1720); primary pricing and holdbacks remain untouched, leaving an 82–88% primary/secondary price gap once Ticketmaster's official resale is disabled. → [[articles/2026-08-05-bts-ticket-prices-plummet-for-massachusetts-concerts-but-only-outside-ticketmast|Aug 5]]
- **Connecticut audit corroborates Live Nation antitrust pattern** (Q10) — state audit confirmed OVG bypassed required competitive bidding to award Ticketmaster the 2023 XL Center (Hartford) contract, independently supporting the paid-advocacy claims in *DOJ v. Live Nation*; the pending federal settlement would terminate the OVG-Ticketmaster incentive deal and let affected venues rebid. → [[articles/2026-08-06-connecticut-audit-confirms-ovg-bypassed-bidding-rules-for-ticketmaster-deal-at-h|Aug 6]]
- **Live Nation states' discovery bid denied, without prejudice** (Q10) — Judge Arun Subramanian rejected (**Aug 4**) the states' request for discovery into the DOJ-Live Nation settlement for lacking specific requests; states may refile "narrow and targeted" asks. Post-trial motions to overturn the states' jury verdict were argued July 31, ruling still pending. → [[articles/2026-08-06-judge-denies-states-discovery-request-into-live-nation-settlement-leaves-door-op|Aug 6]]
- **FTC subscription-billing crackdown** (Q9) — TRO against 15 corporations/8 individuals (Cyprus/Ukraine entities with US payment-rail access) covering asset freezes and expedited discovery reaching third-party banks/processors; signals continued scrutiny of undisclosed recurring charges and cancellation friction relevant to any platform's billing disclosures. → [[articles/2026-08-06-ftc-s-subscription-crackdown-what-payment-processors-need-to-know|Aug 6]]
- **California lobbying surge as Fans First Act stalls** (Q6, Q11) — the bill (fee disclosure, resale cap at face+10%, fee cap at 10% of face) was narrowed to venues under 3,000-cap/indie only and sent to the Senate Appropriations "suspense file" on **Aug 3**, a common holding pen for bills to die — but not before StubHub's Q2 CA lobbying spend hit $2.56M (3x its prior high) and a previously-dormant "Ticket Policy Forum" spent ~$1.1M opposing it. Watch for a scoped-down revival. → [[articles/2026-08-07-secondary-ticketers-lobbying-efforts-surge-as-california-tackles-reform|Aug 7]]
