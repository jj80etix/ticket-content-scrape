# Weekly Digest — 2026-W38 (Sep 14–20)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **NIVA files formal Tunney Act comments on the DOJ–Live Nation settlement** (Q10, Q13) — proposes capping Live Nation at 50% of any headline tour's domestic dates, forced Ticketmaster/artist-management divestiture, and penalty funds routed to independent-venue relief; flags the settlement's 8,000-seat "Major Concert Venue" threshold and festival exclusions as loopholes. → [[articles/2026-09-14-niva-calls-for-ticketmaster-breakup-50-cap-on-live-nation-tour-control|Sep 14]]

- **Raskin and Nadler file Tunney Act comments** (Q10) urging Judge Subramanian to probe whether a Trump–Rapino meeting shaped DOJ's abandonment of a forced Ticketmaster divestiture. → [[articles/2026-09-14-raskin-nadler-urge-judge-to-probe-corrupt-and-improper-influence-behind-live-nat|Sep 14]]
- **Tunney Act comment period closed Sept. 4; ruling expected mid-September–mid-October** (Q10) — Pollstar rounds up late objections from AEG, SeatGeek, Louis Messina, and NIVA, all arguing the settlement doesn't restore competition. → [[articles/2026-09-15-the-biz-objections-roll-in-as-judge-reviews-ln-settlement-umg-wraps-buyback|Sep 15]]
- **17 state AGs + D.C. file a Tunney Act comment** (Q10) arguing the settlement's open-distribution system and "Divestiture Venues" provision (none currently Live Nation-owned) could entrench rather than erode Ticketmaster's power. → [[articles/2026-09-16-state-ags-say-doj-s-live-nation-settlement-could-make-ticketmaster-more-powerful|Sep 16]]
- **House Energy & Commerce Committee advances H.R. 2713 (MAIN Event Ticketing Act), 36-0** (Q11) — expands FTC/BOTS Act enforcement authority and adds security/incident-reporting duties for ticket issuers; the Coalition for Ticket Fairness warns the bill's "circumvention" definition could sweep in broad private purchase-limit rules. Advances toward a floor vote. → [[articles/2026-09-16-house-committee-advances-amended-main-event-ticketing-bill-advocates-warn-agains|Sep 16]]
- **New Jersey AG Jennifer Davenport names junk fees a top office priority** (Q9), per a Troutman Pepper "Regulatory Oversight" podcast interview. → [[articles/2026-09-16-inside-the-new-jersey-ag-s-office-with-ag-jennifer-davenport|Sep 16]]
- **FTC/Amazon accelerate Prime-settlement consumer redress** (Q9, precedent for platform-refund enforcement) — per-consumer cap raised from $51 to $200, payments switch to automatic disbursement starting **Oct. 1, 2026**, with a final supplemental round by **April 2027**. → [[articles/2026-09-17-ftc-announces-additional-payments-to-consumers-stemming-from-ftc-s-amazon-prime-|Sep 17]]
- **Massachusetts AG's office logs 25+ consumer complaints (up from 9 a day earlier) over Ed Sheeran Gillette Stadium refunds** ahead of next week's shows, as Ticketmaster grants unrequested full refunds in Philadelphia amid softening Loop Tour resale prices (Q2, Q6, Q11) — active, fast-moving consumer-protection situation. → [[articles/2026-09-18-ed-sheeran-refunds-begin-in-philadelphia-as-softening-ticket-market-puts-pressur|Sep 18]]
- **New Orleans' Howlin' Wolf sues StubHub in federal court** alleging "false scarcity" and fictitious former-price tactics (Q2, Q6, Q11) — cites a Sept. 7 show where StubHub advertised "Only 1% of tickets left!" while the venue had sold zero of its own inventory. → [[articles/2026-09-18-new-orleans-howlin-wolf-latest-venue-to-sue-stubhub-citing-false-scarcity-tactic|Sep 18]]
