## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **Ticketmaster Ireland criminally charged by the CCPC over "Platinum" ticket pricing during the 2024 Oasis onsale** (Q10, Q2) — first criminal action tied to tiered-pricing disclosure; case adjourned to an **Oct. 5, 2026 hearing**, which could produce the first court ruling on the legality of "Platinum" pricing. → [[articles/2026-09-08-irish-regulator-accuses-ticketmaster-of-misleading-oasis-ticket-buyers-with-plat|Sep 8]]
- **DOJ v. Live Nation/Ticketmaster Tunney Act review draws a fourth and fifth wave of objections** (Q10) — AEG and promoter Louis Messina argue the settlement would leave Ticketmaster controlling ~85% of major-venue events; promoter Tommy Dorfman says its venue-rebate and festival-definition carve-outs let Live Nation "preserve the leverage" under different terms; the American Antitrust Institute asks Judge Subramanian to probe whether a reported Rapino-Trump meeting and Kennedy Center discussions amount to an undisclosed side deal. Comment period closed Sept. 4; ruling still pending. → [[articles/2026-09-08-taylor-swift-promoter-says-live-nation-shut-him-out-as-aeg-calls-for-ticketmaste|Sep 8]], [[articles/2026-09-09-longtime-live-nation-foe-says-doj-settlement-preserves-the-leverage-that-sank-hi|Sep 9]], [[articles/2026-09-10-antitrust-institute-urges-judge-to-probe-political-motives-behind-live-nation-se|Sep 10]]
- **Live Nation/Ticketmaster arbitration clauses face two new appellate tests** (Q10) — the Second Circuit is asked to undo a June order compelling arbitration on resale-ticket antitrust claims, and a separate Front Gate Tickets dispute over unilateral contract-amendment rights has been referred to the Nevada Supreme Court. → [[articles/2026-09-10-live-nation-s-ticket-arbitration-battles-head-to-second-circuit-nevada-supreme-c|Sep 10]]
- **Belgium's Competition Authority cleared Live Nation's Pukkelpop acquisition but imposed a binding 10-year (extendable to 15) structural separation** keeping ticket pricing and programming with the festival's independent operator (Q10, Q13) — relies on the EU's 2023 *Towercast* precedent; a template other regulators could apply to Live Nation elsewhere. → [[articles/2026-09-11-belgian-authorities-green-light-live-nation-s-pukkelpop-acquisition-with-conditi|Sep 11]]
- **Seven Pennsylvania DAs now pursuing the Vivid Seats hidden-fee lawsuit** (Q9) — six more counties (Bucks, Montgomery, Lackawanna, Pike, Wyoming, Luzerne) joined Monroe County's drip-pricing/dark-pattern case; amended complaint docketed Aug. 24, expansion announced Sept. 9. → [[articles/2026-09-10-seven-pennsylvania-das-now-pursuing-vivid-seats-hidden-fee-lawsuit|Sep 10]]
- **Quebec's expanded resale law took effect Sept. 12, 2026** (Q11) — caps secondary ticket prices at the authorized seller's price unless the event producer/presenter grants a written exception, with no requirement that any exception be offered to competing marketplaces on equal terms; the Ticket Policy Forum warns this could entrench Ticketmaster's primary-market power. → [[articles/2026-09-11-quebec-resale-law-lets-event-rights-holders-decide-who-can-sell-above-price-cap|Sep 11]]
