# Weekly Digest — 2026-W34 (Aug 17–23)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **FTC opens comment period on personalized-pricing enforcement policy** (Q9) — draft policy statement targets undisclosed data-driven price variation; doesn't ban the practice but puts businesses on notice that non-disclosure can be an unfair/deceptive practice. **30-day comment window** opens once published in the Federal Register (docket FTC-2026-1057) — nearest-term actionable date this week. → [[articles/2026-08-19-ftc-seeks-comment-on-enforcement-policy-statement-regarding-personalized-pricing|Aug 19]]
- **FTC + Connecticut land $4M deceptive-fees settlement, with an all-in-pricing consent order** (Q9) — targets an auto dealer (Manchester City Nissan), not ticketing, but the order requires prominent disclosure of maximum total price and express per-charge consumer consent — the same theory underpinning the FTC's live-event Junk Fees Rule. → [[articles/2026-08-19-ftc-connecticut-secure-4-million-settlement-with-manchester-city-nissan-over-dec|Aug 19]]
- **FTC settles $2.1M junk-fee/dark-pattern case against bill-payment platform Doxo** (Q9) — undisclosed "delivery fees" plus deceptive recurring-subscription enrollment; confirms the FTC's junk-fee enforcement push is active and reaching well beyond ticketing. → [[articles/2026-08-17-bill-payment-firm-doxo-to-pay-2-1-million-to-settle-ftc-allegations-it-deceived-|Aug 17]]
- **Ellie Goulding sues former managers, alleging they were barred from steering her away from Live Nation** (Q10) — doesn't name Live Nation as a defendant, but ties into the wider vertical-integration scrutiny following April's jury finding of illegal monopoly maintenance against Live Nation/Ticketmaster. → [[articles/2026-08-18-ellie-goulding-lawsuit-says-managers-were-barred-from-steering-artists-away-from|Aug 18]]
- **California's Fans First Act (10%-above-face resale cap, sub-3,000-cap venues) dies in Senate committee** (Q6, Q11) — StubHub and allies spent a record $2.56M lobbying in Q2 alone (3x its prior high), including ~$1.1M funneled through a previously-inactive front group running voter mailers; California continues without a resale price cap, echoing the AB 1720 outcome from the prior week. → [[articles/2026-08-18-the-biz-what-we-learned-from-q2-earnings-california-ticketing-bill-killed-more|Aug 18]]
