# Weekly Digest — 2026-W42 (Oct 12–18)

## Regulatory/Legal Watch

- **Secondary-market platforms sue to block D.C.'s RESALE Act before its Jan. 1, 2027 effective date.** The Ticket Policy Forum, SeatGeek, Vivid Seats, Gametime and TickPick filed suit arguing the law's 10%-above-face resale cap, 10% marketplace-fee cap, and original-price-disclosure mandate violate the Commerce Clause, First Amendment, Takings Clause, Section 230 and DC Home Rule Act — while leaving primary-market pricing (including Ticketmaster's) uncapped. Deadline to watch: Jan. 1, 2027 (Q9, Q11). → [[articles/2026-10-14-ticket-marketplaces-sue-d-c-to-block-resale-caps-arguing-one-sided-regulations-a|Oct 14]], [[articles/2026-10-15-online-resellers-sue-to-block-dc-s-resale-act|Oct 15]], [[articles/2026-10-16-secondary-us-sellers-sue-to-halt-legally-flawed-d-c-resale-laws|Oct 16]]
- **Ontario's escalating-penalty regime hits SeatGeek again: C$47,500 cumulative.** An Oct. 8 round of fines (C$22,500) under Reg. 175/26's second-offense tier keeps pressure on resale platforms over original-price verification — SeatGeek says primary sellers like Ticketmaster control the records resellers need to prove compliance; StubHub (C$20,000, separately) is appealing (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-10-15-ontario-escalates-seatgeek-fines-as-ticket-resale-compliance-questions-persist|Oct 15]]
- **DOJ sues San Diego broker REPS & Co. for BOTS Act violations** — 18.5M+ tickets across 83,500+ events via thousands of accounts since 2022, $1.3B spent, $120.8M+ resale profit; a live example of federal bulk-buying enforcement reaching deep into broker operations (Q6, Q11). → [[articles/2026-10-16-feds-sue-san-diego-broker-who-allegedly-bought-18-5m-tickets-for-bots-act-violat|Oct 16]]
- **FTC + Utah/Nevada AGs sue Lens.com over hidden fees and deceptive auto-renewal** (filed Oct. 2) — a fresh coordinated federal-state drip-pricing case outside ticketing that reinforces the enforcement model (FTC Act §5 + ROSCA) increasingly applied to junk-fee cases generally (Q9). → [[articles/2026-10-13-bipartisan-state-coalition-joins-ftc-to-allege-deceptive-pricing-and-auto-renewa|Oct 13]]
- **Live Nation data breach disclosed to Vermont AG (Oct. 1); three class actions filed.** SSNs/government IDs exposed for "a small number" of individuals. Same note flags the U.S. Copyright Office's AI-streaming-fraud inquiry (comments open through Nov. 23) and NYC's new "click to cancel" mandate for subscriptions (Q10). → [[articles/2026-10-12-the-biz-class-action-suits-roll-in-after-ln-data-breach-disclosure-feds-probe-st|Oct 12]]
