# Harvest 2026-09-22

## Highlights

- StubHub Canada was fined C$20,000 (two C$10,000 penalties, issued Sept. 9) under Ontario's ticket resale price-cap law — one for selling above the cap, one for lacking proof of the original ticket price — and says it will appeal; the case follows an unappealed C$25,000 SeatGeek Canada fine under the same framework and exposes an unresolved question of how secondary platforms are supposed to verify original face value.
- TicketNetwork's Sept. 4 Tunney Act comments argue DOJ's proposed Live Nation-Ticketmaster settlement lets Ticketmaster condition rivals' access to its new open primary-ticketing infrastructure on how those rivals run their separate resale businesses; the public comment period on the settlement has now closed, with the court's review of final terms still pending.
- AEG, independent promoter Messina Touring, and NIVA all filed objections arguing the DOJ-Live Nation/Ticketmaster settlement doesn't go far enough — AEG says it leaves Ticketmaster with roughly 85% market share and opens only ~170 events to bid — while Live Nation's Joe Berchtold said the underlying antitrust case could ultimately reach the Supreme Court.
